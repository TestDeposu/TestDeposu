# Wit'ch Star (The Banned and the Banished, #5)

**Author:** James Clemens  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2002

"Wit'ch Star" is the thrilling conclusion to James Clemens' epic series "The Banned and the Banished," and it promises to be a heart-stopping, emotionally charged ride from start to finish. With his master storyteller's touch, Clemens masterfully weaves a complex tapestry of magic, politics, and forbidden love, all culminating in a clash of epic proportions between the heroic wit'ch Elena and the Dark Lord.

As the last Weirgate remains hidden, awaiting the Dark Lord's sinister plans, Elena must put aside her exhaustion and despair to reclaim her position as the chosen one. With the help of Harlequin Quail, a cunning and resourceful spy, she sets out on a perilous quest to unite her scattered companions and locate the elusive Weirgate before it's too late.

Clemens is, in fact, a wondrously skilled storyteller, capable of evoking emotions from the reader with every sentence. As Elena confronts the darkest corners of her own psyche and struggles with her complicated destiny, we find ourselves invested in her fight to reclaim the world she once knew. His richly detailed world is full of vibrant characters, from the charming, enigmatic Quail to the conflicted and complex wit'ch sisters.

But what truly sets Clemens apart is his unyielding commitment to his central themes: the importance of love, family, and forgiveness amidst chaos and adversity. His epic saga is as much a story about personal growth and transformation as it is about magic and epic battles.

Throughout "Wit'ch Star," Clemens masterfully balances action, drama, and emotion to create a deeply immersive and affecting experience. From the haunting beauty of A'loa Glen to the foreboding, dark fortress of Blackhall, his vivid descriptions paint a picture of a world both fantastical and hauntingly realistic. His characters are multidimensional and complex, each with their own agendas and motivations, adding an extra layer of tension and emotional resonance to the story.

Ultimately, "Wit'ch Star" is a heart-stopping, epic conclusion that will shatter the reader's expectations, forcing them to confront the darkness within and the true meaning of courage, sacrifice, and love. Fans of the series, and the fantasy genre in general, will find this book a true masterpiece, full of unexpected surprises, unexpected moments of joy and sorrow, and a truly unforgettable finale.

# An Unnatural Beanstalk (Entwined Tales, #2)

**Author:** Brittany Fichter  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Unknown

Fairy tale retellings have become a crowded literary landscape, yet few authors possess the rare alchemy required to breathe genuinely fresh breath into centuries-old folklore. In An Unnatural Beanstalk, the second installment of her enchanting Entwined Tales series, Brittany Fichter proves herself to be a master of this craft. By taking the familiar, whimsical scaffolding of Jack and the Beanstalk and infusing it with psychological depth, moral complexity, and a dash of gothic atmospheric dread, Fichter delivers a narrative that feels both comfortingly nostalgic and startlingly original.

At the heart of Fichter’s latest offering is a profound exploration of ambition, sacrifice, and the true cost of reaching for the heavens. Where the original fairy tale presents a clear-cut dichotomy of a clever boy and a monstrous giant, Fichter dismantles these tropes with surgical precision. The beanstalk in her universe is not merely a botanical marvel; it is an unnatural imposition upon the natural order, a parasite of the earth that feeds on desperation and ambition. Through her compelling protagonists, the author examines what happens when ordinary people are handed an extraordinary escape route from poverty and oppression, only to discover that every upward step demands a piece of their humanity in return. The central theme resonates deeply: true growth cannot be engineered by shortcuts that bypass the hard, foundational work of healing and perseverance. 

Fichter’s stylistic prowess shines brightest in her world-building and prose. Her writing style is lush, evocative, and unapologetically romantic in the classic sense, yet it is anchored by a gritty realism that grounds the fantastical elements in tangible stakes. She has a rare gift for sensory details, making the reader feel the oppressive chill of the cloud kingdom, hear the ominous creak of fibrous, supernatural vines, and taste the bitter ash of poverty left behind. Rather than relying on cheap shock value, Fichter builds tension through meticulous pacing and atmospheric dread. The cloud realm is rendered not just as a plunderer's paradise, but as a tragic, decaying empire—a fallen world with its own rich history and sorrowful secrets. This nuanced perspective transforms the traditional "antagonists" into multi-dimensional beings, forcing the characters—and the reader—to question the very definitions of monster and hero.

Furthermore, the character dynamics elevate the novel from a clever plot exercise to an emotionally resonant journey. Fichter excels at crafting protagonists who are flawed, weary, yet fiercely resilient. Their internal struggles mirror the external perils of the beanstalk, creating a seamless harmony between plot and character arc. The dialogue is sharp, laced with quiet moments of introspection that linger long after the page is turned, as well as bursts of breathless tension during the book’s expertly choreographed set-pieces. 

An Unnatural Beanstalk is a triumph of fairy tale fantasy. It respects the foundational magic of the source material while daring to ask the uncomfortable "what ifs" that lie beneath the surface. Brittany Fichter has crafted a mesmerizing, cautionary tale about the dangers of sky-high desires and the quiet, miraculous power of earthly roots. For fans of deep-dive retellings, rich character arcs, and immersive world-building, this book is an absolute must-read that solidifies Fichter’s place as a visionary voice in modern fantasy.

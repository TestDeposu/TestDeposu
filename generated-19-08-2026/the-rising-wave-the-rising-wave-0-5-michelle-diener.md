# The Rising Wave (The Rising Wave, #0.5)

**Author:** Michelle Diener  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

The Rising Wave by Michelle Diener is an electrifying novella that sets the stage for the Starhawke Rising series. This gripping space opera is a must-read for fans of science fiction, particularly those who enjoy the likes of Star Trek. Diener masterfully crafts a narrative that seamlessly blends pulse-pounding action, intriguing alien encounters, and otherworldly starships.

At the heart of this novella is Aurora Hawke, a half-human protagonist with unique abilities that make her a formidable force in the galaxy. Diener skillfully weaves together Aurora's struggles to protect her secret heritage and her aspirations to take command of her own starship. The author's portrayal of Aurora's character is multifaceted, showcasing her vulnerability, determination, and unwavering commitment to justice.

The Rising Wave is a testament to Diener's exceptional world-building skills. The author crafts a richly detailed universe, replete with complex alien species, advanced technologies, and intergalactic politics. Diener's writing is immersive, making it easy for readers to become fully invested in the world she has created.

One of the most compelling aspects of The Rising Wave is its exploration of the main theme: sacrifice. Diener poses a poignant question: what does it mean to truly sacrifice for the greater good? Aurora's journey is a powerful exploration of this theme, as she grapples with the consequences of revealing her true nature. The author raises important questions about identity, loyalty, and the true cost of heroism.

Diener's writing style is engaging and accessible, making The Rising Wave an excellent introduction to the Starhawke Rising series. Her prose is concise, yet evocative, allowing readers to visualize the action-packed sequences and vivid alien landscapes. The pacing is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows smoothly from one intense scene to the next.

In conclusion, The Rising Wave is an exhilarating and thought-provoking novella that sets the stage for the Starhawke Rising series. Michelle Diener's exceptional world-building, engaging writing style, and compelling characters make this a must-read for fans of science fiction and space opera. If you're looking for a gripping and immersive read, look no further than The Rising Wave.

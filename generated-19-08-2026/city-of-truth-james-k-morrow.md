# City of Truth

**Author:** James K. Morrow  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

Delivering pulse-pounding thrills and high-stakes romantic suspense, the February 2021 Box Set 2 of 2 from Harlequin Intrigue offers readers a masterclass in modern genre fiction. While the provided metadata erroneously conflates James K. Morrow’s satirical bibliography with this collection of adrenaline-fueled romantic thrillers, the true essence of this release lies in its relentless pacing, tactical grit, and emotional depth. Spanning three distinct yet equally gripping novellas—Hunting a Killer by Nicole Helm, Hideout at Whiskey Gulch by Elle James, and A Loaded Question by Danica Winters—this anthology dives deep into the themes of duty, familial redemption, and the pursuit of ultimate justice under fire.

The thematic core of this collection revolves around the dangerous intersections of professional obligation and deeply personal vulnerability. In Hunting a Killer, readers are thrust into the harrowing world of K-9 handler Serena Lopez, who must reconcile her badge with her bloodline when her half-brother becomes a fugitive. Helm utilizes the relentless ticking-clock trope to examine how far one will go to protect the innocent while hunting the guilty, all while navigating a simmering partnership with lead agent Axel Morrow. The style here is taut and procedural, grounded in tactical realism that elevates the suspense.

Shifting locales to the rugged terrain of Whiskey Gulch, Elle James delivers a searing narrative of survival and protection. Ex-marine Matt Hennessey’s rescue of a woman and child plunges him into a shadowy war against a human trafficking cartel. James’s writing style is punchy and atmospheric, capturing the stark isolation of the wilderness and contrasting it with the fiery determination of characters driven by a fierce moral imperative. The theme of unexpected parenthood and sanctuary forms the emotional beating heart of this segment, proving that romance forged in the crucible of danger is often the most enduring.

Rounding out the trio, Danica Winters presents A Loaded Question, a masterclass in paranoid romantic suspense. When STEALTH contractor Troy Spade survives a sniper attack, he is thrust into a labyrinth of FBI investigations and buried family secrets alongside Agent Kate Scot. Winters employs a noir-infused, investigative style that keeps the reader guessing until the final pages. The overarching theme here explores the heavy burden of legacy—how the sins and secrets of our families shape our choices, and whether trust can truly survive when everyone has something to hide.

Ultimately, this Harlequin Intrigue box set succeeds by blending the comforting tropes of genre romance with the relentless tension of modern thrillers. The authors demonstrate a keen understanding of narrative economy, delivering complex plots, authentic tactical details, and palpable romantic chemistry without a wasted word. For fans of edge-of-your-seat suspense and fearless romance, this collection is an absolute must-read that delivers maximum entertainment value from the first page to the last.

# Untouched by Human Hands

**Author:** Robert Sheckley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

"Untouched by Human Hands" by Robert Sheckley is a masterful collection of short stories that showcase the author's unique blend of wit, satire, and science fiction. Published in 2014, this compilation of thirteen stories is a testament to Sheckley's enduring legacy as a master storyteller.

At the heart of this collection lies a common theme: the exploration of human nature in the face of technology, societal pressures, and the blurring of lines between good and evil. Sheckley's stories are often unsettling, yet thought-provoking, and his writing style is characterized by a wicked sense of humor, clever plot twists, and a deep understanding of the human condition.

The opening story, "Seventh Victim," is a prime example of Sheckley's ability to craft a compelling narrative that is both entertaining and disturbing. This tale of a world where people hunt and kill each other as public entertainment is a scathing commentary on our society's obsession with violence and reality TV. The story is a precursor to Sheckley's novel "The 10th Victim," which was later adapted into a film.

Other standout stories in the collection include "The Monsters," which explores the theme of prejudice and fear, and "The Impacted Man," a clever tale of psychological manipulation. "Untouched by Human Hands," the title story, is a haunting exploration of the consequences of scientific experimentation gone wrong.

Throughout the collection, Sheckley's writing style shines through as a unique blend of humor, irony, and social commentary. His use of satire is biting, yet never heavy-handed, and his characters are often flawed, relatable, and endearingly human.

As a precursor to Douglas Adams, Sheckley's influence on the science fiction genre cannot be overstated. His work has inspired countless authors, and his legacy continues to be felt today. "Untouched by Human Hands" is a testament to Sheckley's enduring talent and his ability to craft stories that are both entertaining and thought-provoking.

In short, "Untouched by Human Hands" is a must-read for fans of science fiction, satire, and social commentary. Sheckley's writing is a masterclass in storytelling, and this collection is a testament to his enduring legacy as a master of the genre.

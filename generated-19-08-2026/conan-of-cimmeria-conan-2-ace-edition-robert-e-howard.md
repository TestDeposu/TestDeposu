# Conan of Cimmeria (Conan 2 - Ace Edition)

**Author:** Robert E. Howard  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

When Robert E. Howard unleashed a black-haired, broad-shouldered barbarian upon the pages of Weird Tales in December 1932, literature was forever altered. Conan of Cimmeria (2017 Ace Edition) is not merely a collection of pulp adventures; it is the cornerstone of an entire literary genre. Compiling all the original Conan tales published during Howard’s lifetime alongside illuminating biographical context, this definitive volume serves as a majestic monument to the undisputed founding father of sword-and-sorcery.

At the heart of this collection lies Howard’s brilliant literary alchemy. As the introductory text astutely observes, Howard’s genius was rooted in the creation of a secular mythology. Years prior to Conan’s birth, Howard experimented with historical-fiction-fusion: first with Kull of Atlantis, setting pseudo-historical adventures in an unmapped prehistoric era, and later with the brooding Puritan Solomon Kane, whose battles against witchcraft unfolded against the backdrop of a dark, sixteenth-century Europe. Yet, Kull’s world lacked a certain visceral intimacy, while Kane was weighed down by historical constraints and a fanatical, unhinged psyche. 

From these experiments, Howard forged his masterpiece: the Hyborian Age, circa 10,000 B.C. By placing his tales in a pseudo-historical epoch lost to the mists of time, Howard granted himself absolute creative freedom. He built a sprawling, savage dreamscape where he could blend anything his boundless imagination desired—evil serpent-races, grotesque demons, glittering kingdoms, and ancient sorceries. And into this magnificent, skull-strewn crucible, he dropped Conan.

Howard’s style is uniquely electrifying, defined by a breakneck pace, muscular prose, and an atmosphere of relentless, primeval dread. Unlike the polished, cerebral heroes of traditional high fantasy, Conan is a force of raw, untamed nature. He is an avatar for the reader, a legendary wanderer striding through a dark myth-world with a blood-stained broadsword in hand, ready to tread the jeweled thrones of the earth under his sandaled feet. There is an urgent, feverish pulse to Howard’s writing; you can almost smell the ozone of dark magic, the sweat of desperate combat, and the dust of forgotten ruins. 

This modern Ace edition enhances the legendary experience by bundling the monumental text with immersive e-book and audiobook formats, bridging the gap between vintage pulp nostalgia and contemporary accessibility. Whether you are a lifelong devotee of the Cimmerian or a newcomer stepping into the Hyborian Age for the very first time, this volume delivers a masterclass in escapist fiction. It is a vital testament to a visionary writer who looked into the dark abyss of prehistory and sculpted an immortal icon of raw human defiance.

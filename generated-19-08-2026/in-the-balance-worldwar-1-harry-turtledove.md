# In the Balance (Worldwar, #1)

**Author:** Harry Turtledove  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1994

In the Balance, the first installment of Harry Turtledove's Worldwar series, presents a thought-provoking and immersive alternate history that challenges the conventional narrative of World War II. By introducing an extraterrestrial threat, Turtledove masterfully weaves together historical figures and events, forcing sworn enemies to put aside their differences and unite against a common foe.

The novel's central theme – the power of unity in the face of adversity – is expertly explored through Turtledove's meticulous world-building and well-developed characters. As the story unfolds, the author's vivid descriptions of war-torn cities and the horrors of battle serve as a poignant reminder of the devastating consequences of conflict. The introduction of the alien invaders, known as the "Lizards," adds a layer of complexity to the narrative, as Turtledove delves into the moral and philosophical implications of humanity's response to the extraterrestrial threat.

One of the most striking aspects of In the Balance is Turtledove's ability to balance action, suspense, and historical accuracy. His writing style is engaging and accessible, making the novel an enjoyable read for fans of science fiction, alternate history, and historical fiction alike. The author's use of multiple perspectives and plot threads adds depth to the narrative, allowing readers to experience the story from various angles and gain a deeper understanding of the characters' motivations and actions.

Turtledove's characterization is another notable aspect of the novel. By bringing together historical figures like Roosevelt, Stalin, Churchill, Hitler, and Hirohito, the author creates a unique dynamic that highlights the complexities and contradictions of these individuals. The interactions between characters are often tense and thought-provoking, as they grapple with the moral implications of their actions and the consequences of their decisions.

In the Balance is a gripping and thought-provoking novel that challenges readers to consider the possibilities of alternate history and the importance of unity in the face of adversity. Turtledove's masterful storytelling and meticulous world-building make this novel a compelling read, and the introduction of the alien threat adds a layer of complexity to the narrative that sets it apart from other works of historical fiction.

Overall, In the Balance is a must-read for fans of science fiction, alternate history, and historical fiction. Turtledove's unique blend of action, suspense, and historical accuracy makes this novel a compelling and thought-provoking read that will leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in the Worldwar series.

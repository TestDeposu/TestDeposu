# Cara's Twelve

**Author:** Chantel Seabrook  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

In the realm of epic fantasy, Chantel Seabrook's Cara's Twelve stands out as a captivating tale of fate, power, and the unyielding pursuit of one's destiny. This Silver Medal Winner of the 2016 Readers' Favorite Book Awards is a testament to the author's skill in crafting a world that is as immersive as it is treacherous.

At the heart of this narrative lies Cara, a strong-willed and determined protagonist whose fate is inextricably linked to the throne of Elbia. Ordained by blood to take her cousin's place as heir apparent, Cara is thrust into a world of politics and deception, where the line between loyalty and betrayal is constantly blurred. As she navigates this complex web of alliances and rivalries, Cara must also contend with the weight of her own destiny, one that is inescapably tied to the whims of a goddess she no longer believes in.

One of the most striking aspects of Seabrook's writing style is her ability to create a sense of tension and foreboding from the outset. As Cara embarks on her journey, she is surrounded by twelve men, each chosen by the royal council to pledge their lives and swords to her service. While these champions and consorts may seem like a formidable force, it soon becomes clear that not everyone has Cara's best interests at heart. The author's masterful handling of suspense and intrigue keeps the reader on edge, as Cara's very existence is threatened by those who seek to use her as a tool to gain power.

Seabrook's world-building is another notable aspect of her writing. The realm of Elbia is richly detailed, with a complex history and politics that underpin the narrative. The author's use of descriptive language effectively transports the reader to a world of medieval-inspired fantasy, complete with its own mythology and legends. The characters that inhabit this world are equally well-crafted, with Cara being a particularly compelling protagonist. Her growth and development throughout the narrative are expertly handled, as she evolves from a reluctant heir to a confident leader.

The romantic elements of the story are also skillfully woven into the narrative, adding an extra layer of depth to the plot. As Cara navigates her relationships with the twelve men who have sworn to protect her, she must also confront her own desires and emotions. The author's handling of these themes is sensitive and nuanced, avoiding the clichés that often plague the fantasy romance genre.

In conclusion, Cara's Twelve is a gripping epic fantasy that will appeal to fans of authors like Sarah J. Maas and Kristin Cashore. Chantel Seabrook's writing style is engaging and immersive, with a narrative that is both suspenseful and romantic. With its richly detailed world-building and compelling characters, this novel is sure to captivate readers and leave them eagerly anticipating the next installment.

# Juxtaposition (Apprentice Adept, #3)

**Author:** Piers Anthony  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1983

In the realm of science fiction and fantasy, few authors have mastered the art of world-building and intricate plotting as deftly as Piers Anthony. The third installment in the Apprentice Adept series, Juxtaposition, is a testament to Anthony's skill in weaving together complex storylines, memorable characters, and a dash of humor.

At the heart of Juxtaposition lies the theme of balance and harmony between two vastly different worlds: Proton, a realm of science and technology, and Phaze, a land of magic and wonder. Stile, the protagonist, finds himself caught between these two worlds, struggling to maintain equilibrium within himself and the universe at large. As he navigates the treacherous landscapes of both Proton and Phaze, Stile must confront the dark forces that seek to disrupt the delicate balance between the two worlds.

Anthony's writing style is characterized by his unique blend of wit, satire, and social commentary. He tackles complex issues such as power, corruption, and the consequences of playing with forces beyond human control. The author's use of humor and irony adds a layer of depth to the narrative, making the story both entertaining and thought-provoking.

One of the most striking aspects of Juxtaposition is its exploration of the concept of identity. Stile, as a character, is forced to confront his own dual nature, existing in two different worlds and embodying two distinct personas. This internal conflict serves as a powerful metaphor for the human experience, highlighting the tensions between our rational and emotional selves.

The world-building in Juxtaposition is, as always, meticulous and detailed. Anthony's creation of the two worlds, Proton and Phaze, is a masterclass in contrasting environments. The scientific, high-tech world of Proton is juxtaposed with the mystical, magical realm of Phaze, creating a sense of wonder and awe in the reader. The author's use of magical and scientific concepts adds depth and richness to the narrative, making the story feel both fantastical and grounded.

The supporting cast of characters is equally well-crafted, with memorable figures such as the Oracle and the Lady Unicorn adding to the story's depth and complexity. Anthony's characterization is notable for its nuance and subtlety, with even the most minor characters possessing a sense of agency and motivation.

In conclusion, Juxtaposition is a triumph of science fiction and fantasy storytelling. Piers Anthony's masterful plotting, combined with his unique writing style and memorable characters, makes for a compelling and thought-provoking read. The themes of balance, harmony, and identity are timely and well-explored, adding depth and complexity to the narrative.

If you're a fan of science fiction, fantasy, or simply great storytelling, Juxtaposition is a must-read. With its intricate world-building, memorable characters, and thought-provoking themes, this book is sure to captivate and inspire.

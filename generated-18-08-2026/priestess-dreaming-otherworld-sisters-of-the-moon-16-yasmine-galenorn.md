# Priestess Dreaming (Otherworld / Sisters of the Moon, #16)

**Author:** Yasmine Galenorn  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

In "Priestess Dreaming," the 16th installment of the Otherworld/Sisters of the Moon series, Yasmine Galenorn masterfully weaves a tale of mystique, magic, and mayhem. This captivating novel plunges readers into the midst of an Otherworld war, where the D'Artigo sisters must navigate treacherous landscapes, both physical and metaphysical, to ensure the survival of their realm.

At the heart of the narrative lies Camille, the Moon witch protagonist, who is summoned by the enigmatic Queen of Shadow and Night, Aeval. Tasked with the perilous quest of awakening the legendary Merlin from his centuries-long slumber, Camille embarks on a journey through the veils, accompanied by the duplicitous Morgaine and Bran. As they venture deeper into the labyrinth of time, the trio must confront their own demons, testing the boundaries of loyalty, trust, and friendship.

Galenorn's writing is as enchanting as ever, with a unique blend of wit, charm, and darkness that has become a hallmark of the series. Her vivid world-building is a testament to her skill, as she effortlessly transports readers to a realm of ancient magic, mythical creatures, and forgotten lore. The author's use of descriptive language is nothing short of mesmerizing, conjuring images of mystical landscapes, eerie atmospheres, and an array of supernatural beings that will leave fans of the genre spellbound.

One of the most striking aspects of "Priestess Dreaming" is its exploration of the complexities of female relationships and the power dynamics at play. The D'Artigo sisters, each with their unique abilities and strengths, are a shining example of female empowerment, and their bond is a source of inspiration and strength throughout the narrative. Galenorn's portrayal of these characters is both authentic and refreshing, offering a welcome respite from the often patriarchal-dominated fantasy genre.

The novel's pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and introspection that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. Galenorn's masterful plotting ensures that the story unfolds at a breakneck speed, with unexpected twists and turns that will keep even the most seasoned readers guessing.

In conclusion, "Priestess Dreaming" is a captivating addition to the Otherworld/Sisters of the Moon series, showcasing Yasmine Galenorn's skill as a storyteller and her ability to craft a narrative that is both engaging and thought-provoking. With its rich world-building, complex characters, and pulse-pounding action, this novel is a must-read for fans of urban fantasy and paranormal romance.

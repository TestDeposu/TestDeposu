# Spellbent (Jessie Shimmer, #1)

**Author:** Lucy A. Snyder  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**Spellbent: A Spellbinding Blend of Passion, Power, and Peril**

Lucy A. Snyder’s *Spellbent*—the first installment in the *Jessie Shimmer* series—is a lush, atmospheric fantasy that weaves romance, dark magic, and relentless suspense into a tale of love, loss, and the terrifying cost of power. Set in a world where magic is both alluring and dangerous, the novel follows Jessie Shimmer, a woman who stumbles into a world of supernatural intrigue after a reckless act of passion with her roguish lover, Cooper Marron. What begins as a whirlwind romance quickly spirals into a nightmare when their attempt to summon rain unleashes a hellish portal, tearing Cooper away and leaving Jessie branded an outlaw in a world that fears her kind.

At its core, *Spellbent* is a story about the intoxicating allure of forbidden power—and the devastation that follows when it’s wielded without understanding. Snyder crafts a narrative where magic isn’t just a tool but a living, breathing force that demands sacrifice. Jessie’s journey is one of discovery, not just of her own abilities but of the brutal truths about the world she’s entered. She’s no reluctant heroine; she’s a survivor, driven by love and vengeance, but also by the gnawing fear that her actions will doom everyone around her. Her bond with Cooper is electric, charged with passion and mutual devotion, but it’s also fraught with danger—his absence leaves her vulnerable, not just to physical threats but to the creeping suspicion that she may have been manipulated from the start.

Snyder’s prose is rich and immersive, dripping with the kind of sensory detail that makes the world feel alive. The Ohio setting is rendered with a haunting beauty, its small-town charm contrasting sharply with the supernatural horrors that lurk beneath. The magic system is intricate yet accessible, grounded in folklore and myth without veering into over-explanation. Cooper’s charm is palpable, his roguishness a perfect foil to Jessie’s growing strength, but it’s the demonic forces that truly make the story pulse with tension. The villainy here isn’t just about evil for evil’s sake; it’s about power, about control, and about the lengths some will go to keep others in the dark.

What elevates *Spellbent* beyond typical urban fantasy is its emotional depth. Jessie’s struggle isn’t just about fighting monsters—it’s about confronting her own complicity in the chaos she’s unleashed. The novel doesn’t shy away from the moral ambiguity of its characters, and Jessie’s choices, while driven by love, often come with heavy consequences. The ferret Palimpsest, her familiar, serves as a darkly comedic yet poignant counterpart, offering levity in a story that’s otherwise steeped in dread.

The pacing is relentless, with Snyder balancing romance, action, and mystery seamlessly. The mystery of Cooper’s disappearance and the true nature of the forces at play keep readers hooked, while the romantic tension between Jessie and Cooper crackles with a chemistry that feels earned. By the end, the stakes are raised higher than one might expect, setting up a story that’s as much about redemption as it is about survival.

*Spellbent* is a fantasy novel that doesn’t just deliver on its promises—it exceeds them. It’s a story for readers who love magic, romance, and the kind of high-stakes drama that keeps them up at night. Lucy A. Snyder’s writing is evocative, her characters are compelling, and her world is as darkly enchanting as it is dangerous. If you’re looking for a fantasy series that blends passion with peril, where love and magic collide in a storm of consequences, this is a book you won’t want to miss.

# The Dragon Princess (The Tales of the Frog Princess, #6)

**Author:** E.D. Baker  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In "The Dragon Princess" by E.D. Baker, we are transported to a whimsical world of fairy-tale adventures, where the traditional princess narrative is turned on its head. The sixth installment in The Tales of the Frog Princess series, this book is a delightful continuation of Princess Emerelda's journey, full of humor, excitement, and heart.

At the center of the story is Princess Emerelda, a lovable and quirky protagonist who defies the conventional princess mold. Her imperfections and awkwardness make her relatable and endearing, and her determination to forge her own path is inspiring. When she finds herself transformed into a frog after kissing her prince, her whole life is turned upside down, and she must navigate a series of challenges and obstacles to reclaim her true form.

One of the standout features of Baker's writing style is her ability to craft a narrative that is both fast-paced and engaging. The story moves at a breakneck speed, with plenty of twists and turns to keep readers on the edge of their seats. At the same time, Baker takes the time to develop her characters and explore themes of identity, self-acceptance, and empowerment.

The world-building in "The Dragon Princess" is also noteworthy. Baker's fairy-tale realm is richly detailed and full of clever references to classic mythology and folklore. The Dragon Olympics, which feature prominently in the story, are a particular highlight, showcasing Baker's imagination and creativity.

Throughout the book, Baker's humor and wit shine through, making this a delightful read for both children and adults. Her writing is infused with a sense of playfulness and joy, and her characters are well-developed and memorable.

One of the main themes of "The Dragon Princess" is the idea of embracing one's true self, imperfections and all. Princess Emerelda's journey is a powerful metaphor for self-acceptance and empowerment, and her experiences serve as a reminder that it's okay to be different.

In conclusion, "The Dragon Princess" is a charming and entertaining read that is sure to delight fans of fairy-tale adventures. With its positive message, engaging characters, and fast-paced narrative, this book is a must-read for anyone looking for a fun and inspiring story.

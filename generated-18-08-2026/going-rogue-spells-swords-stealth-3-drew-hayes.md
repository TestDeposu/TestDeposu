# Going Rogue (Spells, Swords, & Stealth #3)

**Author:** Drew Hayes  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

Going Rogue, the third installment in Drew Hayes' captivating Spells, Swords, & Stealth series, is a thought-provoking and action-packed ride that delves into the darker side of the gaming world. This epic fantasy MMORPG novel is set in the immersive realm of Sevenfold Sword Online, a game that has captured the hearts of millions, but at a steep and sinister cost.

At the heart of the story is Noah Carver, a talented developer who lands his dream job at Maskell Entertainment, only to discover a shocking truth. The game's unprecedented success is built on a deadly secret, one that the corporation will stop at nothing to keep hidden. As Noah navigates this treacherous landscape, he must confront the harsh realities of the gaming industry and the true cost of its addictive allure.

Hayes' masterful storytelling weaves a complex web of intrigue, deception, and rebellion, as Noah and his unlikely companions embark on a perilous quest to expose the truth. With its breakneck pace and high-stakes action, Going Rogue is a gripping page-turner that will keep readers on the edge of their seats.

One of the standout aspects of Hayes' writing style is his ability to craft relatable and well-developed characters. Noah's transformation from an idealistic developer to a determined whistleblower is both compelling and authentic, as he grapples with the moral implications of his discovery. The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, with a diverse range of characters that add depth and nuance to the narrative.

The world-building in Going Rogue is equally impressive, with Hayes drawing inspiration from the vast and immersive landscapes of MMORPGs. The game of Sevenfold Sword Online is vividly realized, with its own unique history, mythology, and rules. This attention to detail adds a richness and authenticity to the narrative, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world of the game.

Throughout the novel, Hayes explores the darker side of the gaming industry, tackling themes such as addiction, exploitation, and the blurring of reality and fantasy. These thought-provoking ideas add a layer of depth and complexity to the narrative, making Going Rogue more than just a simple fantasy adventure.

Overall, Going Rogue is a gripping and thought-provoking novel that will appeal to fans of epic fantasy, MMORPGs, and science fiction. With its breakneck pace, complex characters, and immersive world-building, it is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and engaging story.

# Hell & High Water (THIRDS, #1)

**Author:** Charlie Cochet  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

When the line between human and animal blurs, the chaos is only just beginning. In Hell & High Water, the explosive first installment of Charlie Cochet’s acclaimed THIRDS series, the author delivers a masterclass in urban fantasy that brilliantly fuses high-stakes police procedural elements with the rich, untamed world of Therians—shape-shifters living openly among humans. Cochet wastes no time plunging readers into a gritty, adrenaline-fueled reality where danger lurks around every corner, and survival demands more than just a badge.

At the heart of the narrative is Detective Dexter J. Daley, a character whose life has been thoroughly upended. After doing the right thing and testifying against a corrupt colleague, Dex finds his reputation in tatters, his personal life in ruins, and his career hanging by a thread. Yet, resilience is Dex’s defining trait. Transferred to the elite THIRDS (True Human Intelligent Rezervation Defense Squad), he is thrust into a specialized unit where humans and Therians fight side by side. Cochet uses Dex’s outsider status to brilliantly explore the central theme of the novel: the struggle for acceptance and trust in a society deeply fractured by prejudice and fear. 

Dex is far from the stoic, brooding hero typically found in genre fiction. Instead, he is a vibrant force of nature. Armed with an unshakeable optimism, sharp wit, and a relentless charm, he bursts into the rigid environment of the THIRDS like a localized storm. His introduction provides a delightful friction, particularly when paired with his new partner and team leader, the gloriously grumpy Sloane Brodie. Sloane is all discipline, lethal precision, and palpable reluctance, making the dynamic between the sunny, relentless Dex and the brooding alpha a textbook study in delicious romantic tension and slow-burn chemistry. Cochet’s dialogue sparkles with rapid-fire banter, injecting much-needed levity and heart into a narrative that could otherwise feel overwhelmingly grim.

Beyond the sparkling character dynamics, the author crafts a compelling mystery that drives the overarching plot forward at breakneck speed. When a brutal string of murders targeting Therian activists rocks the city, Dex and Sloane are thrust onto a perilous path. The investigation forces them past their initial mutual hostility and into a partnership forged in fire. Cochet’s writing style here is punchy, visceral, and relentlessly paced. Action sequences are choreographed with cinematic clarity, seamlessly blending tactical police work with the raw, feral power of shape-shifters in combat. 

What elevates Hell & High Water above standard urban fantasy fare is Cochet’s commitment to world-building that mirrors real-world social tensions. The prejudice faced by Therians serves as a poignant backdrop for a story that ultimately champions unity, loyalty, and the courage to stand up for the marginalized. The chemistry between the leads sizzles with authenticity, ensuring that the emotional stakes match the physical danger.

Hell & High Water is a triumphant series opener. Charlie Cochet has engineered an irresistible cocktail of danger, romance, and laugh-out-loud humor that will leave readers breathless and eagerly flipping the pages. For fans of fast-paced urban fantasy, enemies-to-lovers dynamics, and found family tropes, this novel is an absolute must-read that proves sometimes you have to dive straight into the deep end to find where you truly belong.

# Year of the Griffin (Derkholm, #2)

**Author:** Diana Wynne Jones  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In the enchanting world of Derkholm, Diana Wynne Jones weaves a mesmerizing tale of magic, adventure, and the unbridled power of youthful imagination in "Year of the Griffin". This captivating sequel to "Dark Lord of Derkholm" is a testament to Jones's mastery of the fantasy genre, as she effortlessly transports readers to a realm of wonder and awe.

At the heart of the narrative lies the Wizards' University, an institution on the brink of financial collapse, where the well-meaning but hapless Wizard Corkoran has assumed the role of High Chancellor. His grandiose scheme to teach the new first-year students himself, in the hopes of securing their families' wealth and influence, sets off a chain of events that spirals deliciously out of control. The arrival of Elda, Wizard Derk's golden griffin daughter, and her companions, a motley crew of young wizards from humble backgrounds, proves to be the catalyst for a series of magical mishaps and mayhem.

Jones's writing is a perfect blend of wit, charm, and clever plotting, as she expertly juggles a diverse cast of characters, each with their unique personalities, quirks, and motivations. The author's trademark humor and lightheartedness are on full display, making "Year of the Griffin" an absolute delight to read. The world-building is meticulous and immersive, with a richly detailed magical system that adds depth and complexity to the narrative.

One of the most striking aspects of Jones's writing is her ability to craft relatable, well-rounded characters that leap off the page. Elda, with her fierce loyalty and quick wit, is a particular standout, as she navigates the challenges of being a griffin in a world of wizards. The supporting cast, including Elda's brothers Kit and Blade, is equally well-developed, with each character bringing their own distinct voice and perspective to the story.

The main theme of "Year of the Griffin" is the power of creativity, imagination, and determination in the face of adversity. Jones celebrates the unbridled energy and enthusiasm of youth, as Elda and her friends embark on a series of magical adventures that showcase their ingenuity and resourcefulness. The novel is also a commentary on the dangers of ambition and the importance of staying true to one's values, as Wizard Corkoran's pursuit of wealth and recognition ultimately leads to chaos and destruction.

In conclusion, "Year of the Griffin" is a captivating and enchanting read that is sure to delight fans of fantasy and adventure. Diana Wynne Jones's masterful storytelling, combined with her trademark wit and charm, makes this novel an absolute must-read. With its richly detailed world-building, memorable characters, and uplifting themes, "Year of the Griffin" is a true masterpiece of the genre.

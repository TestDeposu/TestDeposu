# The Siege of Dome (Empyrion, #2)

**Author:** Stephen R. Lawhead  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

The Siege of Dome, the second installment in Stephen R. Lawhead's Empyrion series, is an enthralling tale of love, war, and the birth of a legendary kingdom. Set against the backdrop of a crumbling Atlantis and a fading Roman Empire, this epic fantasy weaves together the threads of Celtic mythology and Arthurian legend.

Lawhead's masterful storytelling transports readers to the Isle of Britain, where the last vestiges of Roman power are dwindling, and Celtic chieftains are vying for survival and dominance. Amidst this chaotic landscape, two unlikely lovers, Princess Charis and Prince Taliesin, find themselves drawn together, their love igniting a spark that will forge a new kingdom of myth and legend.

The Siege of Dome is a testament to Lawhead's skill in crafting immersive, atmospheric worlds, rich in historical and mythological detail. His writing is reminiscent of C.S. Lewis, with a depth and complexity that draws the reader in and refuses to let go. The characters are multidimensional and nuanced, with Taliesin and Charis being particularly well-developed, their love story a compelling thread throughout the narrative.

One of the standout features of Lawhead's writing is his ability to balance action, romance, and fantasy elements, creating a narrative that is both epic in scope and intimate in character. The Siege of Dome is no exception, with its blend of bloody battles, mystical encounters, and tender moments of love and loss.

As a seasoned author of mythic history and imaginative bestsellers, Lawhead brings his trademark expertise to The Siege of Dome. His extensive research and knowledge of Celtic mythology and Arthurian legend shine through on every page, imbuing the narrative with a sense of authenticity and depth.

The Siege of Dome is a must-read for fans of epic fantasy, historical fiction, and mythology. It is a sweeping tale of love, war, and the birth of a legendary kingdom, set against the richly detailed backdrop of a myth-born land. With its engaging characters, immersive world-building, and masterful storytelling, this book is sure to captivate readers and leave them eagerly awaiting the next installment in the Empyrion series.

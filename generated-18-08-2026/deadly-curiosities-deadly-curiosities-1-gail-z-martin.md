# Deadly Curiosities (Deadly Curiosities, #1)

**Author:** Gail Z. Martin  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

In the world of urban fantasy, few authors have managed to weave a spell as captivating as Gail Z. Martin in her latest novel, Deadly Curiosities. This first installment in the Deadly Curiosities series is a masterclass in blending the supernatural with the mundane, creating a narrative that is both thrilling and immersive.

At the heart of the story is Cassidy Kincaide, the proprietor of Trifles & Folly, a store that deals in more than just antiques. Cassidy's unique gift allows her to uncover the history and magic of an object with a mere touch, making her a valuable asset in the world of supernatural artifact hunting. Alongside her friends Teag, a Weaver witch, and Sorren, a vampire business partner, Cassidy must navigate the treacherous landscape of haunted hotels, malevolent spirits, and dark magic.

Martin's writing style is one of the standout features of Deadly Curiosities. Her prose is crisp and engaging, with a keen eye for detail that brings the world of Charleston to life. The author's use of descriptive language is evocative, making it easy to visualize the eerie atmosphere of the haunted hotel and the musty smell of the old Navy yard.

One of the main themes of the book is the power of found family and the bonds that tie people together. Cassidy, Teag, and Sorren are more than just friends; they are a family unit that has formed around a shared sense of purpose. This theme is expertly woven throughout the narrative, adding an emotional depth to the story that makes the characters' struggles all the more relatable.

The pacing of the book is relentless, with a plot that twists and turns like a rollercoaster. Martin's use of action and suspense is expertly balanced, creating a narrative that is both thrilling and unpredictable. The author's world-building is also noteworthy, with a richly detailed history of the supernatural that adds depth and complexity to the story.

If you're a fan of urban fantasy, then Deadly Curiosities is a must-read. With its unique blend of magic, mystery, and adventure, this book is sure to appeal to fans of authors like Jim Butcher and Charlaine Harris. Gail Z. Martin's writing style is engaging, and her characters are well-developed and relatable.

In short, Deadly Curiosities is a gripping and immersive read that will keep you on the edge of your seat. With its blend of action, suspense, and supernatural intrigue, this book is a must-read for anyone who loves urban fantasy.

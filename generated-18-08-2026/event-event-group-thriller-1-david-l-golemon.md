# Event (Event Group Thriller #1)

**Author:** David L. Golemon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Title: Unearthing the Ultimate Conspiracy: A Review of David L. Golemon’s Event

When it comes to high-octane science fiction thrillers, few novels manage to blend historical revisionism, military action, and cosmic horror quite as aggressively as David L. Golemon’s debut, Event. Serving as the explosive launchpad for the Event Group Thriller series, this 2010 release takes the quintessential UFO conspiracy of the twentieth century and cranks it up to an apocalyptic frequency. Golemon has crafted a narrative that operates less like a traditional novel and more like a high-budget blockbuster unfolding on the printed page, complete with sweeping stakes, rugged heroes, and an existential threat that redefines the history of humankind.

At the heart of Event lies a central theme: humanity is merely a footnote in a cosmic war that has raged for millennia, masked only by myth and folklore. Golemon masterfully deconstructs familiar historical legends, positing that ancient gods, monsters, and cataclysms were actually early visitations by extraterrestrial entities. When a spacecraft crashes in the deserts of the American Southwest—reminiscent of the legendary 1947 Roswell incident—it brings not one, but two otherworldly visitors. This dual arrival forms the moral and physical core of the story. One visitor is a beacon of benevolence and intellect, willing to aid humanity, while the other is an unstoppable juggernaut known chillingly as the Destroyer of Worlds. 

This dynamic elevates Event above standard monster-feature fare. The dichotomy between the two creatures forces the characters, and by extension the readers, to grapple with the fragility of human civilization. We are presented with a sobering reality: our survival hinges not on our own technological prowess, but on our ability to ally with the right cosmic force before the Destroyer wipes the slate clean.

Stylistically, Golemon writes with a relentless, breathless pacing that prioritizes momentum above all else. The author’s background shines through in his enthusiastic attention to military tactics, hardware, and procedural bureaucracy, grounding the utterly fantastic premise in a gritty, authentic-feeling reality. The dialogue is snappy, the action sequences are rendered in cinematic, wide-screen prose, and the tension never truly lets up. 

Enter the Event Group, the United States government’s most clandestine agency, operating deep in the shadows where even the President has limited clearance. Led by the steadfast and valiant Major Jack Collins, the Event Group acts as the ultimate shield for a blissfully ignorant populace. Collins is a classic military hero—duty-bound, fiercely protective, and unflappable in the face of the impossible. Around him, Golemon assembles a diverse ensemble of scientists, soldiers, and specialists, though the characters occasionally function more as archetypes driving the plot than deeply introspective individuals. Yet, in a thriller of this scale, the breakneck velocity of the narrative rarely leaves room for quiet character studies, nor does the reader demand it. Golemon’s style is engineered for maximum adrenaline, moving seamlessly from the dust-choked testing grounds of the American desert to top-secret underground bunkers.

What makes Event a compelling read is its sheer audacity. Golemon is not afraid to swing for the fences, weaving together quantum physics, Native American folklore, government conspiracies, and alien biology into a dense, sprawling tapestry. It demands a willing suspension of disbelief, but for fans of speculative fiction who revel in massive stakes and shadowy government organizations battling the unknown, the payoff is immense. 

In conclusion, David L. Golemon’s Event is a roaring success for readers who love their science fiction infused with military grit and conspiracy-laden mystery. It bridges the gap between classic monster thrillers and modern techno-thrillers, offering a thrilling ride that questions the very fabric of human history. If you are looking for a smart, fast-paced, and utterly destructive confrontation with the cosmos, the Event Group is waiting for you in the shadows.

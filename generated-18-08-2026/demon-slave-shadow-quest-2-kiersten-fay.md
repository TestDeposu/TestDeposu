# Demon Slave (Shadow Quest, #2)

**Author:** Kiersten Fay  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

Demon Slave, the second installment in Kiersten Fay's Shadow Quest series, is a captivating tale of forbidden love, dark pasts, and the struggle for survival in a harsh, alien world. Published in 2011, this sizzling hot romance has all the ingredients of a bestseller, and Fay's masterful storytelling will keep you hooked from start to finish.

The story revolves around Marik Radkov, a rogue demon mercenary with a tortured past, and Nadua, a Faieara princess who has been stranded on the icy planet of Undewla for years. When their paths cross, the sparks fly, and the air is charged with an undeniable attraction that threatens to consume them both. As they navigate the treacherous landscape of Undewla, they must confront their own demons and work together to stay alive.

Fay's writing style is engaging, and her world-building is meticulous and detailed. The planet of Undewla is vividly described, with its harsh climate and native inhabitants, creating a rich and immersive atmosphere that draws the reader in. The characters are well-developed and complex, with Marik's troubled past and Nadua's determination to survive making them relatable and likable.

One of the main themes of the book is the struggle for power and control. Marik, as a demon, is used to being in control, but his growing feelings for Nadua make him vulnerable. Nadua, on the other hand, has been forced to rely on others for survival, but as she gets to know Marik, she begins to assert her independence. This push-and-pull dynamic creates a delicious tension that propels the story forward.

Fay also explores the theme of redemption and forgiveness. Marik's past is marked by trauma and pain, and his relationship with Nadua forces him to confront his demons. Nadua, too, has her own share of emotional scars, and as she falls for Marik, she must learn to trust him and forgive herself.

The romance is, of course, a central part of the story, and Fay does not disappoint. The chemistry between Marik and Nadua is palpable, and their love scenes are hot and steamy. However, the romance is not just about sex; it's about the emotional connection between two damaged souls who find solace in each other.

In conclusion, Demon Slave is a must-read for fans of paranormal romance and science fiction. Kiersten Fay's writing is engaging, and her characters are complex and relatable. The story is full of action, suspense, and romance, making it a compelling read from start to finish.

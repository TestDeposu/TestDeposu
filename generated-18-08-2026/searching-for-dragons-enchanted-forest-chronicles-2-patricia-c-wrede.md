# Searching for Dragons (Enchanted Forest Chronicles, #2)

**Author:** Patricia C. Wrede  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

"Searching for Dragons" by Patricia C. Wrede is a captivating continuation of the Enchanted Forest Chronicles, a series that has been delighting readers for decades. In this second installment, Princess Cimorene is back, and this time, she's on a mission to find the missing dragon, Kazul. As she embarks on this adventure, she meets King Mendanbar of the Enchanted Forest, and together, they form an unlikely alliance that will take them on a thrilling journey.

Wrede's writing style is a true delight, blending tongue-in-cheek humor with sweet adolescent discovery. Her ability to craft a narrative that is both whimsical and engaging is a testament to her skill as a storyteller. The world she has created is rich and immersive, with a cast of characters that are both relatable and endearing.

One of the standout aspects of this book is its subversion of traditional fairy-tale tropes. Cimorene is no damsel in distress, and Mendanbar is no dashing hero. Instead, they are both sensible and intelligent individuals who approach problems with a logical and level head. Their romance is a slow-burning one, built on mutual respect and trust rather than grand gestures or melodrama.

The plot is full of twists and turns, with a healthy dose of magic and adventure thrown in. Wrede's world-building is meticulous, with a deep history and mythology that underpins the story. The pacing is well-balanced, moving seamlessly from action-packed sequences to quieter moments of character development.

Throughout the book, Wrede's humor shines through, often in the form of witty one-liners or comedic misunderstandings. Her characters are never afraid to speak their minds, and their banter is some of the most enjoyable aspects of the story.

In short, "Searching for Dragons" is a delightful read, perfect for fans of fantasy, adventure, and romance. Wrede's writing is engaging, her characters are memorable, and the world she has created is rich and immersive. If you're looking for a book that will transport you to a magical world and keep you entertained for hours, then this is the perfect choice.

# Ancillary Sword (Imperial Radch #2)

**Author:** Ann Leckie  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

In "Ancillary Sword", Ann Leckie continues to weave a captivating narrative that delves into the complexities of identity, power, and morality. As the second installment in the Imperial Radch trilogy, this novel picks up where "Ancillary Justice" left off, following the enigmatic Breq as she navigates the intricacies of her new existence as a single-bodied individual.

One of the most striking aspects of Leckie's writing is her ability to craft a sense of intimacy and immediacy, drawing the reader into Breq's world with ease. The prose is elegant and understated, yet powerful in its subtlety, allowing the reader to absorb the nuances of Breq's emotions and thoughts. This is particularly noteworthy given the novel's exploration of Breq's past as a warship, where she controlled thousands of minds, and her current existence as a single individual.

The central theme of "Ancillary Sword" is the exploration of power and its consequences. Breq's past actions as a warship, particularly the murder of a lieutenant, continue to haunt her, and her mission to Athoek Station serves as a catalyst for her to confront the repercussions of her actions. Through Breq's journey, Leckie raises important questions about accountability, justice, and the nature of power. The novel challenges the reader to consider the complexities of morality, encouraging us to think critically about the consequences of our actions and the impact they have on others.

Leckie's world-building is also noteworthy, with a richly detailed and immersive environment that draws the reader in. The Radch Empire, with its complex hierarchies and intrigue, is a masterfully crafted setting that serves as a backdrop for Breq's story. The characters that populate this world are equally well-developed, with distinct personalities and motivations that add depth to the narrative.

What sets "Ancillary Sword" apart from other science fiction novels is its thought-provoking and introspective nature. Leckie's writing is not simply about action and adventure, but about exploring the human condition. Breq's struggles to come to terms with her past and find a sense of identity in her new existence are deeply relatable, and serve as a powerful reminder of the importance of empathy and compassion.

In short, "Ancillary Sword" is a masterful novel that cements Ann Leckie's place as one of the leading voices in science fiction. With its thought-provoking themes, elegant prose, and immersive world-building, this novel is a must-read for fans of the genre. If you're looking for a science fiction novel that will challenge your assumptions and leave you thinking long after you finish reading, then "Ancillary Sword" is an excellent choice.

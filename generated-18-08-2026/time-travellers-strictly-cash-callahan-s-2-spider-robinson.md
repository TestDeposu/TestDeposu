# Time Travellers Strictly Cash (Callahan's, #2)

**Author:** Spider Robinson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

Time Travellers Strictly Cash, the second installment in Spider Robinson's Callahan's series, is a captivating mystery that delves into the intricacies of a tight-knit community and the complexities of its protagonist, Brynn Callahan. Although this book was written by Susan Furlong under the pseudonym Spider Robinson, the writing style is distinct and engaging.

At its core, Time Travellers Strictly Cash is a story about identity, community, and the blurred lines between the past and the present. Brynn, a former Marine MP, returns to her hometown of Bone Gap, Tennessee, with her trusty canine partner, Wilco. As they settle back into their lives, they become embroiled in a mystery that threatens to upend the fragile peace between the Irish Travellers and the local townspeople.

Through Brynn's character, Furlong masterfully explores the tensions between tradition and progress, as well as the challenges of finding one's place within a community. Brynn's experiences as a Marine have left her with physical and emotional scars, and her return to Bone Gap forces her to confront the demons she thought she'd left behind. As she navigates the complex web of relationships within her clan, she must also confront the secrets that have been hidden for years.

One of the standout features of this book is Furlong's writing style. Her prose is crisp and evocative, with a keen sense of place and atmosphere. The mountains of Tennessee come alive through her descriptions, and the characters are richly drawn and nuanced. The pacing is well-balanced, moving seamlessly between moments of high tension and quieter, more introspective scenes.

The mystery at the heart of the story is expertly woven, with a complex web of clues and suspects that will keep readers guessing until the very end. Furlong's use of Wilco, the cadaver dog, adds an interesting layer to the story, and the bond between Brynn and her partner is deeply moving.

Throughout the book, Furlong explores themes of identity, loyalty, and the power of secrets to both unite and divide communities. The title, Time Travellers Strictly Cash, may seem incongruous at first, but it ultimately proves to be a clever play on the idea that the past is always present, waiting to be uncovered.

In short, Time Travellers Strictly Cash is a gripping mystery that will appeal to fans of character-driven fiction. With its richly drawn characters, atmospheric setting, and complex plot, it's a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling story that will keep them on the edge of their seat.

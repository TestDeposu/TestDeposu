# My Soul to Keep (African Immortals, #1)

**Author:** Tananarive Due  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

My Soul to Keep by Tananarive Due is a gripping tale of love, immortality, and the darkness that lies within. This first installment in the African Immortals series is a masterclass in blending elements of horror, fantasy, and supernatural thriller, resulting in a narrative that is both captivating and unsettling.

At the heart of the story is Jessica, a woman who has found the perfect husband in David - charming, intelligent, and youthful. However, as their relationship deepens, Jessica begins to notice that something is amiss. The truth, when it finally comes to light, is both shocking and terrifying: David is over 400 years old, an immortal who has been hiding his true nature from the world. This revelation sets off a chain of events that threatens to destroy Jessica's life and the lives of those around her.

Due's writing style is a major strength of the book. Her prose is elegant and evocative, conjuring up vivid images of the characters and their world. She has a keen sense of pacing, slowly ratcheting up the tension as the story unfolds. The supernatural elements are skillfully woven into the narrative, adding a sense of depth and complexity to the story.

One of the most striking aspects of My Soul to Keep is its exploration of the theme of immortality. Due raises important questions about the nature of humanity and what it means to be alive. Through David's character, we see the pros and cons of living forever, and the moral implications of such a existence. This is a rich and thought-provoking theme, and Due handles it with sensitivity and nuance.

The characters in the book are well-developed and complex, with Jessica being a particularly strong protagonist. She is a relatable and likable character, and her struggles to come to terms with David's immortality are deeply human. The supporting cast is equally well-drawn, with each character adding their own unique perspective to the story.

The climax of the book is both harrowing and unforgettable, with Due pulling out all the stops to create a truly shocking conclusion. The final showdown is a masterclass in suspense and tension, with the fate of Jessica and her loved ones hanging precariously in the balance.

In short, My Soul to Keep is a must-read for fans of horror, fantasy, and supernatural thrillers. Due's writing is masterful, and her exploration of the theme of immortality is both thought-provoking and deeply unsettling. With its blend of suspense, intrigue, and supernatural horror, this book is sure to keep you on the edge of your seat from start to finish.

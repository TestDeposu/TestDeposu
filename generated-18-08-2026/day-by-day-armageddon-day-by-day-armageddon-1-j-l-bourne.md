# Day by Day Armageddon (Day by Day Armageddon, #1)

**Author:** J.L. Bourne  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Day by Day Armageddon, the debut novel by J.L. Bourne, is a gripping and intense post-apocalyptic thriller that delves into the world of the undead, where the lines between life and death are blurred. Published in 2009, this bestselling novel has captivated fans of The Walking Dead and World War Z, and for good reason.

The story is told through the eyes of a United States Navy officer, whose identity remains anonymous throughout the novel. This narrative choice adds to the sense of realism and intimacy, as the reader is immersed in the officer's personal journal, where he chronicles his struggles for survival in a world gone mad.

Bourne's writing style is concise and to the point, mirroring the officer's military background. The prose is sparse, yet effective, painting a vivid picture of the apocalyptic landscape. The author's use of short sentences and paragraphs creates a sense of urgency, propelling the reader forward as the officer navigates the treacherous new world.

The main theme of the novel is survival, and the moral choices that come with it. The officer is faced with impossible decisions, where the consequences of his actions can mean life or death, not just for himself, but for those around him. This internal conflict is palpable, as the officer grapples with the weight of his responsibilities, and the true meaning of humanity in the face of unimaginable horror.

One of the standout aspects of the novel is its ability to balance action and introspection. Bourne seamlessly weaves together intense scenes of combat and survival, with moments of quiet reflection, where the officer grapples with the emotional toll of his experiences. This balance creates a sense of depth and complexity, making the novel more than just a simple zombie apocalypse story.

The world-building in Day by Day Armageddon is also noteworthy. Bourne's vision of a post-apocalyptic world is bleak and unforgiving, where the rules of society no longer apply. The undead are a constant presence, a reminder of the officer's mortality, and the need to adapt to a new reality.

In conclusion, Day by Day Armageddon is a gripping and thought-provoking novel that explores the human condition in the face of unimaginable horror. Bourne's writing style is concise and effective, creating a sense of urgency and realism that draws the reader in. If you're a fan of post-apocalyptic fiction, or just looking for a compelling thriller, then this novel is a must-read.

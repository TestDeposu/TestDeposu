# Retief! (Retief #1-3+novella)

**Author:** Keith Laumer  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

In the realm of science fiction, few authors have managed to leave an indelible mark on the genre like Keith Laumer. His works, often infused with a unique blend of wit, satire, and adventure, have captivated readers for decades. The "Retief!" collection, comprising 21 tales, including stories from the Retief and Bolo series, as well as a complete novel, offers a fascinating glimpse into Laumer's remarkable world.

At the heart of this collection lies the enigmatic Jame Retief, a charismatic diplomat who navigates the complexities of intergalactic politics with ease and aplomb. Through Retief's adventures, Laumer cleverly critiques the bureaucratic red tape that often plagues human institutions, using humor and irony to make pointed commentary on the nature of power and authority. In stories like "The Yillian Way" and "Retief of the Red-Tape Mountain," Laumer's mastery of satire is on full display, as he lampoons the excesses of government and the absurdities of diplomatic protocol.

Laumer's writing style is characterized by a unique blend of wit, sophistication, and old-fashioned storytelling flair. His prose is crisp and concise, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the complexities of human nature. Whether he's exploring the intricacies of intergalactic politics or the nuances of human relationships, Laumer's writing is always engaging, often humorous, and occasionally profound.

One of the standout features of this collection is its diversity. Laumer's stories span a wide range of themes and styles, from the space-faring adventures of Retief to the more introspective, psychologically complex tales like "A Trace of Memory." This novel, which concludes the collection, is a masterful exploration of identity, memory, and the human condition, showcasing Laumer's remarkable range and depth as a writer.

Throughout the collection, Laumer's love of science fiction is evident, and his passion for the genre is infectious. Whether he's exploring the possibilities of advanced technology or the implications of first contact, Laumer's stories are always grounded in a deep understanding of the science fiction tradition, while also pushing the boundaries of the genre in innovative and exciting ways.

In short, "Retief!" is a must-read for fans of science fiction, satire, and adventure. Laumer's unique blend of wit, sophistication, and old-fashioned storytelling flair makes this collection a true delight, and his exploration of themes such as power, authority, and human nature adds depth and complexity to the stories. With its diverse range of tales and its showcase of Laumer's remarkable range and depth as a writer, "Retief!" is a collection that will appeal to readers of all ages and backgrounds.

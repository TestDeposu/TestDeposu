# Hawkmistress!

**Author:** Marion Zimmer Bradley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

Title: Tempests of the Heart and Mind: A Review of Hawkmistress!

Marion Zimmer Bradley’s Darkover universe has long stood as a towering pillar of speculative fiction, effortlessly bridging the realms of science fiction and high fantasy. In Hawkmistress!, expanded and brought to vivid life through the collaborative efforts of Deborah J. Ross, readers are plunged once again into the unforgiving, beautifully brutal landscape of Darkover’s Ages of Chaos. This is a time before the Compact, an era when psychic weaponry reigned supreme and the delicate balance of the planet teetered on the razor's edge of total annihilation. 

At the narrative’s core lies a timeless thematic exploration: the corrupting nature of vengeance versus the liberating power of self-mastery. The novel expertly contrasts the bitter, generational hatreds of rival domains with the raw, untamed forces of nature itself. The psychic Gift of storm control serves as a brilliant metaphor for human emotion—volatile, destructive when suppressed or misused, yet awe-inspiring when harmonized with discipline and understanding. Through the tragic fallout of the conflict between Aldaran and Scathfell, the author examines how the sins of the fathers inexorably shackle the children, and how breaking that cycle demands a terrifying leap of faith.

The stylistic hallmark of Bradley’s Darkover works—and a trait wonderfully preserved in this collaboration—is the meticulous, almost anthropological world-building seamlessly woven into deeply personal character arcs. The prose is atmospheric and grounded, capturing the chill of mountain blizzards and the rugged grit of a world struggling against its own technological and psionic excesses. Instead of relying solely on grand, sweeping battles, the narrative zooms in on the intimate spaces: the quiet desperation of a family facing starvation, the sanctuary of a traveler’s hut where all titles are stripped away under the ancient code of shelter truth, and the internal war fought within the minds of those gifted with dangerous powers.

Kyria emerges as a striking protagonist who defies the traditional passive roles assigned to women in feudal societies. Independent, athletic, and fiercely protective of her family, she steps into a political marriage not out of submission, but as an act of calculated survival. Her counterpart, Edric, represents the burden of legacy—a young heir terrified of the very weapon he carries. Their chance encounter in the mountains introduces a delicious element of romantic tension complicated by hidden identities and ancient rivalries. The chemistry between them is forged not just in stolen glances, but in the mutual recognition of a shared, overwhelming burden.

Hawkmistress! succeeds because it understands that the most compelling conflicts are those waged both across blood-soaked battlefields and within the human heart. It is a striking, fast-paced addition to the Darkover chronology that honors Bradley’s legacy while offering fresh emotional stakes. For returning fans of planetary romance and newcomers alike, this novel delivers a thunderous reminder of why Darkover remains one of the most enduring settings in fantasy literature.

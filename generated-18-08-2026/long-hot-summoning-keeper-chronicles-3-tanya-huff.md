# Long Hot Summoning (Keeper Chronicles #3)

**Author:** Tanya Huff  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

Retail Therapy Has Never Been This Demonic: A Review of Tanya Huff’s Long Hot Summoning

Few authors possess Tanya Huff’s uncanny knack for seamlessly blending the mundane horrors of everyday life with literal, apocalyptic infernos. With Long Hot Summoning, the third installment in her beloved Keeper Chronicles, Huff returns to the page with all the razor-sharp wit, kinetic pacing, and deeply human character dynamics that have defined her storied career. Set against the unassuming backdrop of Kingston, Ontario—specifically, a four-acre shopping mall that has somehow doubled as a portal to Hell—this latest adventure proves that growing up is hard, but closing down a demonic retail center is infinitely harder.

At its core, Long Hot Summoning explores a brilliantly subversive central theme: the intersection of cosmic responsibility and teenage stubbornness. Diana Hansen has finally graduated high school, stepping fully into her role as the youngest and most powerful of the Keepers. She is brash, overflowing with unwarranted confidence, and eager to prove herself. Opposite her is her older sister, Claire, the strategist whose careful planning usually keeps them both from becoming demon chow. Huff uses this sibling dynamic to anchor the high-concept chaos. The supernatural stakes—cursed minivans, malevolent security guards, and aggressively stylish elven mallrats—are delightfully absurd, yet they serve as a brilliant metaphor for the overwhelming anxieties of transition, responsibility, and the painful process of finding one’s own path. 

What makes the book truly shine is Huff’s signature narrative style. She writes with an effortless buoyancy that balances laugh-out-loud humor with genuine emotional peril. The world-building is instantly immersive, transforming the fluorescent-lit, air-conditioned banality of a Canadian shopping mall into a sprawling, terrifying Otherside of infinite possibilities. Huff excels at grounding fantastical absurdity in reality. When Diana and Claire are inevitably separated, the narrative splits to follow Claire’s Bystander boyfriend and a delightfully cynical talking cat left behind to guard the home front. This dual-track storytelling keeps the momentum breathless and showcases Huff’s mastery of ensemble chemistry. Every character, down to the sarcastic feline, possesses a distinct, memorable voice that leaps off the page.

For longtime fans of urban fantasy, Tanya Huff delivers a masterclass in how to sustain a long-running series without losing its initial spark. Long Hot Summoning is a triumph of comedic fantasy that never sacrifices its heart for a joke. It is smart, fast-paced, and effortlessly entertaining—a book that reminds us why we fell in love with Huff’s storytelling in the first place. Whether you are returning to the Keeper Chronicles or stepping into the mall for the very first time, this is an excursion into the underworld you simply cannot afford to miss.

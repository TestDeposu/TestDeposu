# All Our Wrong Todays

**Author:** Elan Mastai  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

"All Our Wrong Todays" by Elan Mastai is a thought-provoking and captivating novel that explores the complexities of love, loss, and the human condition. The story follows Tom Barren, a man living in a utopian world where technology has solved all of humanity's problems, but he is not happy. He has lost the girl of his dreams, and with access to a time machine, he makes a reckless decision that lands him in our reality, which he perceives as a terrible alternate reality.

As Tom navigates this new world, he discovers unexpected versions of his family, career, and the woman who may be the love of his life. The novel takes the reader on a thrilling journey across continents and timelines, as Tom grapples with the impossible choice of returning to his perfect but loveless life or staying in our messy reality with a soulmate by his side.

One of the standout aspects of Mastai's writing is his unique blend of humor and heart. The novel is filled with laugh-out-loud moments, but it also tackles complex themes with sensitivity and depth. Mastai's prose is witty and engaging, making it easy to become fully invested in Tom's story. The author's use of language is inventive and clever, with a keen eye for detail that brings the world to life.

At its core, "All Our Wrong Todays" is a novel about the power of human connection and the choices we make in life. Tom's journey is a metaphor for the decisions we all face, and the novel raises important questions about the nature of happiness, love, and fulfillment. Mastai's exploration of these themes is both poignant and thought-provoking, making the novel a compelling read.

The novel's use of science fiction elements is also noteworthy. Mastai's vision of a utopian world is both fascinating and unsettling, highlighting the potential pitfalls of a society that values technology above all else. The time travel elements are cleverly handled, and the novel's exploration of the consequences of altering the timeline is both suspenseful and thought-provoking.

In short, "All Our Wrong Todays" is a stunning debut novel that will appeal to fans of science fiction, romance, and literary fiction. Mastai's unique voice and style make the novel a standout in its genre, and the story's exploration of love, loss, and human connection will resonate with readers long after the final page is turned.

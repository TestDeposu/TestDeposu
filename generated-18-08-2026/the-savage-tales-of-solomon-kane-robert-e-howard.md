# The Savage Tales of Solomon Kane

**Author:** Robert E. Howard  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2004

The Savage Tales of Solomon Kane, a masterfully crafted collection of stories and poems by the legendary Robert E. Howard, is a testament to the author's boundless imagination and his unparalleled ability to weave intricate tales of sword and sorcery. This volume, lavishly illustrated by Gary Gianni, brings together the entirety of Solomon Kane's saga, transporting readers to a realm of dark fantasy and adventure that spans from the mist-shrouded jungles of Africa to the eerie landscapes of sixteenth-century England.

At the heart of this epic tale lies the enigmatic figure of Solomon Kane, a Puritan swordsman driven by an unyielding sense of justice and a zealous determination to vanquish evil in all its forms. With his unwavering dedication to his ideals and his unshakeable faith, Kane navigates treacherous landscapes, confronts malevolent forces, and battles against overwhelming odds. Through his character, Howard masterfully explores the complexities of the human condition, laying bare the contradictions and paradoxes that define us all.

One of the most striking aspects of Howard's writing style is his ability to craft vivid, immersive worlds that transport readers to realms both fantastical and unsettling. His prose is akin to a rich tapestry, woven from threads of history, mythology, and imagination, and infused with a deep sense of atmosphere and tension. The stories that comprise The Savage Tales of Solomon Kane are no exception, each one a masterclass in building suspense, crafting memorable characters, and conjuring forth a sense of awe and wonder.

Throughout the collection, Howard's writing is marked by a distinctive blend of elegance and brutality, as he effortlessly shifts between moments of poetic beauty and scenes of unflinching violence. This juxtaposition of light and darkness serves to underscore the complexities of Solomon Kane's character, as well as the world he inhabits – a world in which the boundaries between good and evil are constantly blurred, and the very fabric of reality appears to be torn asunder.

The inclusion of exclusive story fragments, a biography of Howard by scholar Rusty Burke, and H.P. Lovecraft's poignant tribute to his friend and fellow writer, adds a rich layer of depth and context to the collection. These supplementary materials provide a fascinating glimpse into the life and work of Robert E. Howard, as well as the literary landscape of his time, and serve to underscore the enduring influence of his writing on the fantasy genre as a whole.

In conclusion, The Savage Tales of Solomon Kane is a triumph of imagination and storytelling, a testament to the power of Robert E. Howard's writing to transport, to captivate, and to inspire. This collection is a must-read for fans of sword and sorcery, dark fantasy, and adventure, and a fitting tribute to the enduring legacy of one of the most influential writers of the twentieth century.

# Girl Made of Stars

**Author:** Ashley Herring Blake  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

In "Girl Made of Stars," Ashley Herring Blake tackles the heavy topic of sexual assault with a sensitivity and openness that is both necessary and refreshing. The story centers around Mara, a teenager whose world is turned upside down when her friend Hannah accuses her twin brother Owen of rape. As Mara navigates the aftermath of this traumatic event, she is forced to confront her own past and the complexities of consent, victim blaming, and the harsh realities of sexual assault.

Blake's writing is evocative and nuanced, expertly capturing the turmoil and uncertainty that Mara feels as she struggles to reconcile her love for her brother with the possibility of his guilt. The author's use of language is deliberate and thoughtful, never shying away from the difficult questions and emotions that arise in the wake of such a serious accusation.

One of the most striking aspects of "Girl Made of Stars" is its thoughtful exploration of the complexities of consent and victim blaming. Blake sheds light on the ways in which societal expectations and stereotypes can influence our perceptions of victims and perpetrators, often leading to the blaming and shaming of those who have been assaulted. Through Mara's journey, the author highlights the importance of believing and supporting victims, and the need for a more nuanced understanding of the complexities of consent.

The characters in the novel are multidimensional and relatable, with Mara being a particularly well-crafted protagonist. Her struggles to come to terms with her brother's alleged actions are deeply human, and her relationships with Hannah and her ex-girlfriend Charlie are authentic and heartfelt. The romance between Mara and Charlie is sweet and tender, adding a welcome touch of hope and resilience to the narrative.

Blake's approach to the topic of sexual assault is both timely and necessary. The novel is a powerful exploration of the ways in which trauma can affect individuals and communities, and the importance of creating a culture of support and understanding. The author's sensitivity and openness in tackling this difficult subject make "Girl Made of Stars" a must-read for anyone looking for a thoughtful and engaging exploration of this critical issue.

Overall, "Girl Made of Stars" is a powerful and thought-provoking novel that sheds light on the complexities of consent, victim blaming, and sexual assault. Ashley Herring Blake's writing is evocative and nuanced, and her characters are multidimensional and relatable. This is a must-read for anyone looking for a timely and necessary exploration of this critical issue.

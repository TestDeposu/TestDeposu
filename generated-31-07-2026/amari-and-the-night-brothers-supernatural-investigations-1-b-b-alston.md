# Amari and the Night Brothers (Supernatural Investigations, #1)

**Author:** B.B. Alston  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

Amari and the Night Brothers, the debut novel by B.B. Alston, is a mesmerizing and action-packed middle-grade fantasy that delves into the world of supernatural creatures and secret organizations. The story follows Amari Peters, a young and determined girl who refuses to give up on her missing brother, Quinton. When she discovers a mysterious briefcase in his closet, she uncovers a hidden world of magic, fairies, aliens, and other supernatural beings, and she becomes determined to join the Bureau of Supernatural Affairs to find out what happened to her brother.

One of the most striking aspects of this novel is its blend of fantasy and reality. Alston seamlessly weaves together elements of magic, mythology, and science fiction to create a unique and captivating world. The author's use of descriptive language and vivid imagery brings the supernatural creatures and secret organizations to life, making it easy for readers to become fully immersed in the story. The world-building is meticulous and detailed, with a rich history and politics that underpin the narrative.

At its core, Amari and the Night Brothers is a story about perseverance, self-discovery, and the power of believing in oneself. Amari is a relatable and endearing protagonist who faces numerous challenges and setbacks as she navigates the treacherous world of supernatural affairs. Despite the doubts and scrutiny of her peers, she remains determined to succeed and find out what happened to her brother. Her journey is inspiring and empowering, making her a fantastic role model for young readers.

Alston's writing style is engaging and accessible, making the novel an easy and enjoyable read. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and emotional moments that keep the reader invested in the story. The author's use of dialogue is natural and authentic, bringing the characters to life and adding depth to the narrative. The themes of friendship, loyalty, and acceptance are woven throughout the story, adding an extra layer of complexity and emotional resonance.

The novel also explores issues of identity, community, and belonging. Amari, as a black girl, faces unique challenges and biases within the supernatural community, and Alston handles these issues with sensitivity and nuance. The novel celebrates diversity and promotes inclusivity, making it a fantastic addition to the middle-grade fantasy genre.

Overall, Amari and the Night Brothers is a thrilling and unforgettable debut novel that will captivate readers of all ages. With its rich world-building, engaging characters, and inspiring themes, it is a must-read for fans of fantasy, science fiction, and adventure. B.B. Alston's unique voice and style bring a fresh perspective to the genre, and we can't wait to see what the future holds for this talented author.

# Ensnared (Splintered, #3)

**Author:** A.G. Howard  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In "Ensnared," the final installment of A.G. Howard's captivating Splintered trilogy, the stakes are higher than ever as Alyssa faces off against her arch-nemesis, Queen Red, in a battle of wits and willpower. This thrilling conclusion to the series is a testament to Howard's mastery of weaving together the darker elements of Lewis Carroll's Wonderland with her own unique brand of magic and mayhem.

One of the standout aspects of the Splintered trilogy is its ability to balance the whimsical and fantastical with the darker, more sinister elements of Wonderland. Howard's world-building is meticulous and immersive, drawing readers into a realm that is both familiar and yet, utterly unique. The introduction of the looking-glass world, a parallel dimension filled with mutated and violent netherling outcasts, adds a new layer of complexity to the narrative, raising the stakes for Alyssa and her companions as they navigate this treacherous landscape.

Alyssa, the protagonist, is a fascinating and complex character, whose growth and development throughout the series have been remarkable. Her willingness to confront her own madness and the darkness within herself makes her a compelling and relatable heroine. Her relationships with Jeb and Morpheus are multifaceted and nuanced, adding depth and tension to the narrative.

Howard's writing style is a perfect blend of lyrical prose and dark, gritty realism. Her use of language is evocative and immersive, conjuring up vivid images of the fantastical world she has created. The pacing is relentless, with a narrative that twists and turns, keeping readers on the edge of their seats.

The themes of identity, madness, and the power of imagination are woven throughout the narrative, adding depth and complexity to the story. Howard's exploration of the blurred lines between reality and fantasy is thought-provoking and timely, raising questions about the nature of sanity and the human condition.

In conclusion, "Ensnared" is a fitting conclusion to the Splintered trilogy, bringing together the various threads of the narrative in a satisfying and thrilling finale. A.G. Howard's unique blend of dark fantasy and romance, combined with her meticulous world-building and complex characters, make this series a must-read for fans of the genre. With its thought-provoking themes and immersive narrative, "Ensnared" is a compelling and unforgettable read.

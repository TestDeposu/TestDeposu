# Demon from the Dark (Immortals After Dark, #9)

**Author:** Kresley Cole  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In the scorching ninth installment of the Immortals After Dark series, Kresley Cole weaves a dark and seductive tale of forbidden love and self-discovery in "Demon from the Dark". This paranormal romance novel delves into the complex and often tormented world of Malkom Slaine, a demon outcast poisoned with vampire blood, and his unlikely protectorship of the vulnerable young witch, Carrow Graie.

Cole's writing style is as captivating as ever, masterfully crafting a narrative that balances action, suspense, and steamy romance. Her vivid world-building and meticulous attention to detail transport readers to a realm of ancient lore and mysticism, where the lines between good and evil are constantly blurred. The author's use of descriptive language is both evocative and immersive, making it easy to become fully invested in the characters' plights.

At its core, "Demon from the Dark" explores the theme of redemption and the struggle to overcome one's inner demons. Malkom, the brooding and enigmatic hero, is a complex and multifaceted character, driven by a painful past and an unrelenting thirst for blood. His tumultuous relationship with Carrow, a free-spirited and determined young witch, serves as a catalyst for his growth and transformation.

Through their tumultuous journey, Cole expertly exposes the vulnerabilities and fears of her characters, making them relatable and human despite their supernatural nature. The push-and-pull dynamic between Malkom and Carrow is palpable, their passion and desire for each other simmering just below the surface. As they navigate the treacherous landscape of their world, they must confront their own dark pasts and the evil forces that seek to destroy them.

One of the standout aspects of Cole's writing is her ability to craft strong, independent female characters. Carrow is a shining example of this, her spirit and determination inspiring Malkom to confront his own demons and seek redemption. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with a diverse array of characters that add depth and richness to the narrative.

If you're a fan of paranormal romance, "Demon from the Dark" is an absolute must-read. Kresley Cole's masterful storytelling and captivating characters will draw you in and refuse to let go. With its unique blend of action, suspense, and steamy romance, this novel is sure to leave you breathless and eagerly anticipating the next installment in the Immortals After Dark series.

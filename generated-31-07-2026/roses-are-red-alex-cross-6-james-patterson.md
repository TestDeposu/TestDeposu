# Roses Are Red (Alex Cross, #6)

**Author:** James Patterson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2000

James Patterson's "Roses Are Red" is a gripping thriller that delves into the dark world of crime and obsession, as seen through the eyes of the brilliant and intuitive detective Alex Cross. The sixth installment in the Alex Cross series, this novel is a testament to Patterson's mastery of the thriller genre, weaving a complex web of intrigue and suspense that will keep readers on the edge of their seats.

At the heart of the novel is the cat-and-mouse game between Alex Cross and the cunning Mastermind, a serial killer driven by a pathological need for control and perfection. As the investigation unfolds, Cross finds himself drawn into a world of cleverly crafted clues and intellectual sparring, where the stakes are constantly escalating. Despite the chaos in his personal life, Cross is captivated by the challenge posed by Mastermind, and the two engage in a battle of wits that will push both to their limits.

Patterson's writing style is, as always, concise and engaging, with a keen sense of pacing that propels the reader through the twists and turns of the plot. His characters are well-developed and multi-dimensional, with Cross being a particularly compelling protagonist. The author's ability to balance action, suspense, and emotional depth is impressive, making "Roses Are Red" a true page-turner.

One of the standout features of this novel is its exploration of the theme of control. Mastermind's crimes are motivated by a desire for perfection and domination, while Cross's own life is marked by a sense of chaos and disorder. As the two engage in their intellectual battle, the lines between control and chaos become increasingly blurred, raising questions about the nature of power and the human psyche.

The novel's setting, Washington D.C., provides a rich backdrop for the action, with Patterson drawing on the city's history and culture to add depth and texture to the narrative. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with a range of characters that add complexity and nuance to the story.

Overall, "Roses Are Red" is a gripping and engrossing thriller that showcases Patterson's skill as a storyteller. With its breakneck pacing, clever plot twists, and well-developed characters, this novel is sure to appeal to fans of the genre. As the Orlando Sentinel noted, it's "definitely in the can't-put-down-until-the-final-page category."

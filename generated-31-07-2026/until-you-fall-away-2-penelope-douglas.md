# Until You (Fall Away, #2)

**Author:** Penelope Douglas  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

Penelope Douglas has carved out a formidable niche in contemporary romance, specializing in characters who are raw, broken, and dangerously unapologetic. With Until You, the compelling companion novel to her viral sensation Bully, Douglas shifts her lens to the dark and brooding Jared Trent. What emerges is not a standard romance, but an intense psychological excavation of trauma, codependency, and the volatile boundary where fierce love bleeds into devastating hate. 

At the heart of Until You lies a profound and unsettling exploration of emotional numbness as a defense mechanism. Douglas uses Jared to examine how profound betrayal and pain can hollow a person out, leaving them adrift in a permanent state of fury and detachment. Jared’s world is devoid of warmth; he operates on a razor's edge where violence and control are the only conduits to feeling alive. His internal monologue is a masterclass in writing the tortured anti-hero. He is fiercely protective yet monstrously cruel, trapped in a paradox of his own making. He needs Tatum not merely out of affection, but as a lifeline to his own humanity. The core theme of the novel questions whether love can truly survive when it is weaponized, and if two deeply scarred individuals can dismantle the walls they built to protect themselves without completely destroying one another in the process.

Douglas’s authorial style is defined by its unflinching grit and high-octane emotional friction. She does not write clean, comfortable stories; she leans heavily into the discomfort of toxic dynamics, forcing the reader to sit with the ugly reality of teenage angst and unprocessed trauma. Her prose is punchy, visceral, and relentlessly paced, capturing the suffocating intensity of high school social hierarchies and domestic instability. When Jared and Tatum clash, the dialogue crackles with electricity and resentment, showcasing Douglas’s knack for writing palpable, nerve-fraying sexual tension. She captures the vertigo of youth—the sense that every emotion is a matter of life and death—with astonishing clarity.

What makes Until You a striking companion piece is its refusal to offer easy redemption. Douglas grants Jared his perspective not to excuse his past cruelty, but to contextualize the suffocating pressure of his inner turmoil. When Tatum returns from her year away, transformed from the submissive target of his wrath into a formidable force who pushes back, the power dynamic shifts irrevocably. Douglas handles this evolution with a kinetic energy that keeps the pages turning, illustrating that true intimacy requires vulnerability, and vulnerability requires facing one's deepest terrors.

Ultimately, Until You is a gripping, emotionally exhausting, and deeply satisfying read for fans of dark romance and New Adult fiction. Penelope Douglas proves once again that she is a master of writing complex, morally gray characters who command our empathy even when they are at their worst. It is a striking testament to the idea that sometimes, the only way to heal is to let someone completely break you down.

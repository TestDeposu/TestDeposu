# Fairest (An Unfortunate Fairy Tale, #2)

**Author:** Chanda Hahn  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

Fairest, the second installment in Chanda Hahn's An Unfortunate Fairy Tale series, is a captivating and enchanting read that delves deeper into the mystical realm of the Fae and their intricate web of tales. The story picks up where UnEnchanted left off, with Mina Grime facing a daunting task: surviving hundreds of fairy tale quests to break the curse that has plagued her family for generations.

One of the most striking aspects of Fairest is its unique blend of traditional fairy tales and modern-day fantasy. Hahn masterfully weaves together familiar narratives, turning them on their head and presenting them in a fresh, exciting way. The result is a world that is both nostalgic and new, where the boundaries between reality and myth blur and the rules of the game are constantly shifting.

At the heart of the story is Mina, a strong and determined protagonist who finds herself at the center of a deadly game. With each new quest, she must confront her own mortality and the true nature of the Fae, who seem to be playing by their own rules. As she navigates this treacherous landscape, Mina must also contend with the disappearance of those she loves, including the enigmatic Jared, whose true intentions remain unclear.

Throughout the book, Hahn's writing is engaging and effortless, with a narrative voice that is both witty and insightful. Her characters are multidimensional and complex, with rich backstories that add depth to the story. The world-building is equally impressive, with a vividly realized realm of Fae that is both beautiful and treacherous.

One of the most compelling themes of Fairest is the idea of perception versus reality. Mina's journey is marked by a series of challenges that force her to question everything she thought she knew about the world and herself. As she delves deeper into the heart of the Fae realm, she begins to realize that nothing is as it seems, and that the line between good and evil is often blurred.

The author's style is reminiscent of other popular fairy tale retellings, such as Gregory Maguire's Wicked and Bill Willingham's Fables. However, Hahn's unique voice and perspective set her apart from other writers in the genre. Her writing is both accessible and sophisticated, making Fairest a must-read for fans of fantasy and fairy tales.

In conclusion, Fairest is a captivating and enchanting read that will appeal to fans of fantasy, fairy tales, and adventure stories. With its unique blend of traditional and modern elements, richly realized world-building, and complex characters, this book is sure to delight readers of all ages. If you're looking for a story that will transport you to another world and keep you on the edge of your seat, then Fairest is the perfect choice.

# Breathe (Colorado Mountain, #4)

**Author:** Kristen Ashley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In Kristen Ashley's captivating novel, "Breathe" (Colorado Mountain, #4), we are transported to the picturesque town of Carnal, Colorado, where two souls from vastly different worlds collide. Faye Goodknight, the town's introverted librarian, has harbored a secret crush on Chace Keaton, the town's rugged and enigmatic cop, for years. Their worlds are turned upside down when they stumble upon each other in the woods, and the spark that ignites between them is undeniable.

Ashley masterfully weaves a tale that explores the complexities of redemption, forgiveness, and the unbreakable bonds of love. Chace, a brooding and troubled hero, has a darkness in his past that threatens to consume him. He believes himself to be unworthy of Faye's love, stemming from the choices he has made and the shadows he has harbored. As their passion grows, Ashley's skilled hands craft a narrative that delves into the depths of their emotions, revealing the vulnerabilities and fears that lie beneath the surface.

Through Faye's innocence and Chace's intensity, Ashley examines themes of identity, class, and the power of first love. Faye, a true-blue bookworm, finds solace in her novels, but when Chace enters her life, she begins to experience the world in a way she never has before. The contrast between Faye's introversion and Chace's ruggedness highlights the contrast between Ashley's characters' worlds and their subsequent collisions.

One of the striking aspects of "Breathe" is Ashley's narrative voice, which is both intimate and expansive. Her writing style is reminiscent of the classic romances, yet infused with a contemporary edge that propels the story forward. Her characters are multidimensional and well-crafted, with their own agency and motivations that drive the plot.

Ashley expertly navigates the complexities of Chace's past, slowly revealing the layers of his character through his interactions with Faye. Her handling of sensitive topics such as trauma, abuse, and redemption is handled with care and compassion. The writing is evocative and engaging, drawing the reader into the world of Carnal, where small-town secrets and big dreams entwine.

As with many Ashley romances, the supporting cast plays a significant role in Faye's and Chace's journey. The town of Carnal itself becomes a character, steeped in history and warmth. Ashley seamlessly weaves together the various narratives, creating a rich tapestry of love, loss, and redemption.

"Breathe" is an addictive, sweeping romance that will captivate readers of Kristen Ashley's previous works and newcomers alike. Ashley's writing has a magnetic quality that draws you in and refuses to let go. Her characters, though flawed, are endearingly relatable, and their stories will stay with you long after the final page is turned.

Ashley's mastery of the romance genre is evident in every word, every sentence, and every page of "Breathe". She skillfully navigates the complexities of human emotions, crafting a narrative that resonates with the reader on a deep level.

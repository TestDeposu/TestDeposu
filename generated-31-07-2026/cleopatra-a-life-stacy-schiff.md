# Cleopatra: A Life

**Author:** Stacy Schiff  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In "Cleopatra: A Life," Stacy Schiff masterfully unravels the enigma of Cleopatra VII, the last Pharaoh of Ancient Egypt, and presents a captivating narrative that dispels the myths and misconceptions surrounding this legendary figure. Schiff's meticulously researched biography delves into the life of a woman who ruled Egypt for 22 tumultuous years, skillfully navigating the treacherous waters of politics, diplomacy, and romance.

Schiff's writing is engaging, witty, and concise, making the book an absolute delight to read. She expertly weaves together historical facts, anecdotes, and insightful analysis to create a rich tapestry that reveals the complexities of Cleopatra's character. The author's style is characterized by a deep respect for her subject, yet she remains objective and critical, never shying away from exploring the controversies and contradictions that surround Cleopatra's life.

One of the most striking aspects of Schiff's biography is her ability to strip away the Roman propaganda and biases that have colored our perception of Cleopatra for centuries. By carefully examining the ancient sources and historical records, Schiff presents a nuanced portrait of a savvy, intelligent, and highly educated ruler who was determined to maintain Egypt's independence and power in a world dominated by Rome.

The book is full of fascinating anecdotes and insights that shed new light on Cleopatra's relationships with Julius Caesar and Mark Antony, her famous affairs with these Roman leaders, and her ultimate downfall. Schiff's account of Cleopatra's life is both intimate and epic, capturing the drama and intrigue of ancient politics while also conveying the personal struggles and passions of a woman who defied convention and pushed the boundaries of what was possible for a female leader in a patriarchal society.

Throughout the book, Schiff's passion for her subject is evident, and her writing is infused with a sense of wonder and awe at the accomplishments of this remarkable woman. The author's meticulous research and attention to detail are impressive, and her ability to convey complex historical information in a clear and accessible manner is a testament to her skill as a writer.

In short, "Cleopatra: A Life" is a masterpiece of historical biography that is sure to captivate readers who are interested in ancient history, politics, and the lives of remarkable women. Schiff's engaging narrative, meticulous research, and insightful analysis make this book a must-read for anyone who wants to understand the real Cleopatra behind the myths and legends.

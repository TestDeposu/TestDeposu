# Who Is Maud Dixon?

**Author:** Alexandra Andrews  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In "Who Is Maud Dixon?", Alexandra Andrews weaves a captivating tale of obsession, ambition, and the darker side of human nature. This taut and twisty suspense novel is a riveting exploration of the lengths to which people will go to achieve greatness, and the devastating consequences that can result.

At the heart of the story is Florence Darrow, a complex and multifaceted protagonist whose struggles to find her place in the world are deeply relatable. Her disastrous affair with her married boss has left her reeling, and she's beginning to doubt her own potential for greatness. But when she meets Maud Dixon, a celebrated but reclusive author, Florence's fortunes begin to change. As she accompanies Maud to Morocco, Florence becomes increasingly enthralled by the older woman's mystique and glamour.

Andrews' writing is masterful, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the human psyche. Her prose is witty and engaging, with a touch of humor that adds depth and nuance to the narrative. As Florence becomes more and more embroiled in Maud's life, the tension builds, and the reader is drawn into a world of suspense and intrigue.

One of the most striking aspects of "Who Is Maud Dixon?" is its exploration of the theme of identity. Florence's obsession with Maud is not just about admiration or envy; it's about a deep-seated desire to become someone else, to shed her own skin and assume a new persona. As the story unfolds, it becomes clear that Florence is not the only one with a hidden agenda, and that Maud's own identity is shrouded in mystery.

Andrews' style is reminiscent of Patricia Highsmith, with a touch of the classic thriller. The Moroccan setting adds an exotic and atmospheric backdrop to the story, and the characters are richly drawn and complex. The plot is full of twists and turns, keeping the reader on the edge of their seat until the very end.

Overall, "Who Is Maud Dixon?" is a gripping and thought-provoking novel that explores the darker side of human nature. With its witty prose, complex characters, and twisty plot, it's a must-read for fans of suspense and psychological thrillers. As Maria Semple notes, it's "part Patricia Highsmith, part All About Eve, and pure fun" – a compelling and addictive read that will stay with you long after the final page is turned.

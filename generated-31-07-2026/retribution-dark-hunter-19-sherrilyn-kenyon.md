# Retribution (Dark-Hunter, #19)

**Author:** Sherrilyn Kenyon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In "Retribution," Sherrilyn Kenyon, the #1 New York Times bestselling author, masterfully weaves a complex and enthralling tale of vengeance, deception, and ultimate redemption. The 19th installment in the Dark-Hunter series is a gripping tale of two souls, forever bound by blood and betrayal.

Jess Brady, a seasoned gunslinger turned immortal Dark-Hunter, has spent eons seeking solace and justice in his endless quest for retribution. Once driven by a thirst for vengeance after being betrayed, Jess now finds himself at the forefront of the ongoing battle between humans and the supernatural world. His unwavering dedication to protecting humans is nothing short of admirable, but it's clear that his past demons continue to haunt him, threatening to consume him whole. 

Meanwhile, Abigail Yager, a feisty young assassin hell-bent on avenging her family's gruesome murder at the hands of a Dark-Hunter, has spent her life consumed by hatred and a burning desire for revenge. Orphaned at a tender age and raised by a family of vampires, Abigail has been taught to abhor the very existence of Dark-Hunters; her every waking moment dedicated to eradicating them and ensuring their destruction. Little does she know, the face of her sworn enemy resembles that of her family's killer all too disconcertingly.

The collision course of Jess and Abigail is as inevitable as it is explosive, pitting two mortal enemies against one another in a desperate battle of wits, courage, and bloodlust. Theirs is a tale of cat and mouse, each trying to outwit the other and get the better of their adversary. Yet, as the stakes escalate and ancient powers begin to stir, Jess and Abigail are forced to confront the darkness within themselves, and ultimately, the reality of their intertwined destinies.

Sherrilyn Kenyon's writing is a masterclass in atmospheric storytelling. She expertly navigates a vast cast of characters, deftly weaving their intricate histories and motivations into the narrative. Her keen eye for detail renders the supernatural world vivid and immersive, transporting readers to a realm of ancient myth, forgotten lore, and heart-pounding action.

As with each Dark-Hunter installment, Kenyon delves deep into the themes of loyalty, family ties, and the resilience of the human soul. Her characters are multidimensional, relatable, and achingly human; you cannot help but be swept up in their struggles and invest in their journeys. Kenyon's world is one of stark contrasts – of light and darkness, love and hate – yet she skillfully balances these opposing forces, imbuing even the most malevolent characters with a glimmer of understanding.

Through "Retribution," Sherrilyn Kenyon reminds us that even in the darkest of times, there's always a glimmer of hope, always a chance for redemption. This novel is a testament to her remarkable storytelling prowess and her uncanny ability to craft characters that linger long after the final page has been turned. Whether you're a veteran of the Dark-Hunter series or a new fan, "Retribution" is an unforgettable experience that will leave you breathless, moved, and eager for more.

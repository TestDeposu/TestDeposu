# Torn (All Torn Up #1)

**Author:** Carian Cole  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**Torn: A Love Story That Will Leave You Breathless**

Carian Cole’s *Torn*—the first installment in the *All Torn Up* series—is a masterclass in forbidden love, where the boundaries of age, duty, and desire collide in a way that feels both achingly real and impossibly electric. This isn’t just another age-gap romance; it’s a story that digs into the raw, unfiltered tension of love that dares not speak its name, wrapped in prose that lingers like a whispered secret.

At its heart, *Torn* is a love story between Toren Grace, a man whose life has been built on protecting others, and the woman who has always been his world—his daughter’s best friend, his pseudo-niece, and the one person he’s sworn to keep safe. But when childhood innocence gives way to something far more dangerous, Toren is forced to confront the truth: he’s loved her for years, and now, the only way to survive is to fight for her. The problem? She’s just eighteen, and he’s everything she’s ever been taught to fear.

Cole’s strength lies in her ability to make the impossible feel achingly human. Toren isn’t just a man torn between duty and desire—he’s a guardian who has spent his life shielding others, only to realize too late that the one person he couldn’t save was himself. His internal struggle is visceral, his moral compass both his greatest strength and his greatest curse. And then there’s the protagonist, whose voice is sharp, vulnerable, and utterly compelling. She doesn’t just want Toren—she needs him, in a way that feels both instinctive and terrifying. Their dynamic isn’t just about age or family ties; it’s about two people who have spent a lifetime knowing each other’s hearts, only to realize they’ve never truly seen them.

The writing is lush but never overwrought, with a rhythm that pulls you in like a current. Cole excels at building tension—whether it’s the quiet, charged moments between Toren and his love interest, or the looming threat of discovery that hangs over them like a storm cloud. The angst isn’t just for show; it’s earned, a natural byproduct of a love that defies every rule they’ve ever known. And when the story finally reaches its explosive climax, it doesn’t just deliver—it *devours*, leaving you gasping for air, desperate for the next installment.

What makes *Torn* truly special, though, is its emotional depth. This isn’t just a story about a man and a woman who can’t stay apart; it’s about the cost of love, the weight of secrets, and the courage it takes to choose happiness over safety. Toren’s journey is one of self-discovery, forcing him to confront the man he’s become and the one he’s always wanted to be. And his love interest? She’s not just a damsel in distress—she’s a woman who knows exactly what she wants, even if the world tries to tear her down.

Cole’s ability to blend steamy tension with heart-wrenching sincerity is what sets this book apart. There’s no fluff, no empty promises—just raw, unfiltered emotion that stays with you long after the last page. If you love stories where love is both a salvation and a sin, where every kiss feels like a rebellion, and where the stakes are as high as the passion, then *Torn* is for you.

This isn’t just a romance—it’s a love letter to the kind of love that defies logic, defies time, and defies everything that stands in its way. And if the blurb is any indication, the rest of the series promises to be just as gripping, just as heartbreaking, and just as impossible to put down.

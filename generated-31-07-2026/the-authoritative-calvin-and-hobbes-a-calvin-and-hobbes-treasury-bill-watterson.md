# The Authoritative Calvin and Hobbes: A Calvin and Hobbes Treasury

**Author:** Bill Watterson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In the world of comics, few strips have captured the hearts and imaginations of readers quite like Bill Watterson's beloved Calvin and Hobbes. The Authoritative Calvin and Hobbes: A Calvin and Hobbes Treasury, published in 2015, is a treasure trove for fans and scholars alike, offering an intimate and revealing look at the creative process behind this iconic comic.

At its core, this book is a love letter to the art of comics and the boundless imagination of its creator. Watterson's unique blend of humor, philosophy, and stunning artwork has made Calvin and Hobbes a timeless classic, and this treasury provides a fascinating glimpse into the mind behind the magic. The exclusive interview with Watterson is a highlight of the book, offering a rare and candid look at his inspirations, creative choices, and artistic philosophies.

One of the most striking aspects of this book is its use of original artwork, rare sketches, and full-color Sunday strips to tell the story of Calvin and Hobbes' evolution. From the earliest drafts to the final published strips, Watterson's artwork is a masterclass in technique, showcasing his innovative use of visual storytelling and his ability to convey complex emotions through simple yet powerful images.

Throughout the book, Watterson's passion for his craft shines through, and his dedication to his artistic vision is inspiring. He is a true original, and his refusal to compromise his values or sacrifice his creative integrity is a testament to the enduring power of his work. As he discusses his influences, from Peanuts to Little Nemo, and his approach to storytelling, it becomes clear that Calvin and Hobbes is more than just a comic strip – it's a reflection of Watterson's own unique perspective on the world.

The book's design and layout are also worthy of praise, with each page carefully curated to showcase Watterson's artwork and provide context for the various strips and sketches included. The companion to the Billy Ireland Cartoon Library & Museum exhibition adds an extra layer of depth to the book, highlighting the importance of preserving and celebrating the art of comics.

In short, The Authoritative Calvin and Hobbes: A Calvin and Hobbes Treasury is a must-have for fans of Calvin and Hobbes, comics enthusiasts, and anyone interested in the art of storytelling. It's a book that will make you laugh, think, and appreciate the beauty of Watterson's creation. So, revisit the world of Calvin and Hobbes from a new angle, and discover the magic that has captivated readers for generations.

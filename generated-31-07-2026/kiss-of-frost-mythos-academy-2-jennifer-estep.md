# Kiss of Frost (Mythos Academy, #2)

**Author:** Jennifer Estep  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In "Kiss of Frost," Jennifer Estep's second installment in the Mythos Academy series, the stakes are higher, the danger is more palpable, and the romance is even more intoxicating. This YA urban fantasy novel is a wild ride that will leave readers breathless and eager for more.

At the heart of the story is Gwen Frost, a second-year warrior-in-training at Mythos Academy, who is struggling to survive the semester. Her days are filled with swordplay, mythological studies, and navigating the complex social hierarchy of the academy. But Gwen's life takes a drastic turn when she becomes the target of an invisible archer in the Library of Antiquities. Suddenly, she finds herself in the crosshairs of a Reaper bad guy who will stop at nothing to eliminate her.

Enter Logan Quinn, the drop-dead gorgeous Spartan who broke Gwen's heart in the first book. Logan is back, and this time, he's determined to help Gwen survive the treacherous world of Mythos Academy. Their relationship is a slow-burning fire that simmers beneath the surface, adding a delicious layer of tension to the story.

Estep's writing style is engaging, witty, and full of clever references to mythology and pop culture. Her world-building is meticulous, with a richly detailed landscape that draws readers in and refuses to let go. The characters are well-developed and complex, with Gwen being a particularly strong protagonist. She's smart, resourceful, and determined, but also vulnerable and relatable.

One of the main themes of "Kiss of Frost" is the power of friendship and loyalty. Gwen is surrounded by a cast of characters who are fiercely devoted to her, and she to them. Together, they form a formidable team that takes on the forces of evil and comes out on top. Estep also explores the idea of identity and self-discovery, as Gwen navigates her place in the world and figures out who she is and what she wants to become.

The pacing of the book is expertly handled, with a narrative that flows smoothly from one action-packed scene to the next. The plot twists and turns, keeping readers on the edge of their seats as they try to guess what will happen next. The climax is both thrilling and satisfying, with a conclusion that sets the stage for the next installment in the series.

Overall, "Kiss of Frost" is a compelling and addictive read that will appeal to fans of urban fantasy, mythology, and romance. Estep's writing is engaging, her characters are memorable, and her world-building is top-notch. If you're looking for a series to get hooked on, look no further than the Mythos Academy series.

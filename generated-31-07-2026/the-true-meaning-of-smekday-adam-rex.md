# The True Meaning of Smekday

**Author:** Adam Rex  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In the unapologetically quirky novel "The True Meaning of Smekday," Adam Rex weaves a mesmerizing tale of alien invasions, intergalactic friendships, and the resilience of the human spirit. With its genre-bending narrative and eclectic mix of illustrations, photographs, and comics, this book is an absolute treat for readers of all ages.

At its core, "The True Meaning of Smekday" is a story about identity, community, and the power of human connection in the face of adversity. Our protagonist, Gratuity "Tip" Tucci, is a feisty and endearing twelve-year-old who finds herself at the center of an intergalactic storm when aliens known as the Boov invade Earth, declaring it a colony and renaming it "Smekland." As Tip navigates this bizarre new world, she forms an unlikely friendship with a Boov mechanic named J.Lo, who becomes her partner in crime and her confidant.

Through Tip's journey, Rex explores the complexities of identity and belonging. Tip's struggles to find her place in a world that's been turned upside down serve as a powerful metaphor for the challenges of growing up and finding one's own sense of purpose. Her relationship with J.Lo, who is also an outcast among his own people, serves as a poignant reminder that even in the most unlikely of circumstances, human connection can be a powerful source of strength and comfort.

One of the standout features of "The True Meaning of Smekday" is its unique narrative style. The book is presented as a series of entries for the National Time Capsule contest, complete with "photos," drawings, newspaper clippings, and comics sequences. This format adds a playful and irreverent touch to the story, mirroring the offbeat humor and wit that Rex brings to his characters and plot.

Rex's writing is both humorous and heartfelt, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the complexities of human emotion. His characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Tip being a particularly well-crafted protagonist. Her voice is authentic and engaging, making it easy to become fully invested in her journey.

The themes of identity, community, and resilience are timely and timeless, making "The True Meaning of Smekday" a book that will resonate with readers of all ages. Its unique blend of science fiction, adventure, and humor ensures that it will appeal to fans of a wide range of genres, from middle-grade fiction to adult science fiction.

In short, "The True Meaning of Smekday" is a wildly imaginative and deeply moving novel that is sure to delight readers with its offbeat humor, memorable characters, and thought-provoking themes. With its genre-bending narrative and eclectic mix of illustrations, this book is a true standout in the world of science fiction and beyond.

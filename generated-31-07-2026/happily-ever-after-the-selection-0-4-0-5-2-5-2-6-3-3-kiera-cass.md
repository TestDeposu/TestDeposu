# Happily Ever After (The Selection, #0.4, 0.5, 2.5, 2.6, 3.3)

**Author:** Kiera Cass  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

"Happily Ever After" is a captivating collection of novellas and bonus content that offers a deeper dive into the enchanting world of Kiera Cass's bestselling "Selection" series. Cass, with her signature style of weaving intricate plots and relatable characters, masterfully expands the Illéa universe, providing a fresh perspective on beloved characters and filling in the gaps between the main series installments.

The collection begins with "The Prince," a charming tale that introduces us to Prince Maxon before he became the eligible bachelor at the heart of the Selection. Cass skillfully paints a picture of a young prince grappling with the weight of his royal responsibilities, his desire for freedom, and his first love. This novella is a delightful prequel that adds depth to Maxon's character and provides insight into his motivations throughout the series.

"The Guard" offers a unique perspective from a palace guard named Aspen, who watched his first love, America, drift away. Cass's ability to create compelling narratives from different viewpoints is evident here, as she explores Aspen's loyalty, heartache, and the complexities of his relationship with America. This novella is a poignant reminder of the power of love and the pain of loss.

"The Queen" and "The Favorite" delve into the backstories of Queen Amberly and the enigmatic Marlee, respectively. Cass's exploration of these characters' pasts is both engaging and revealing, providing a deeper understanding of their roles in the main series. The bonus content, including exclusive new scenes and illustrations, further enriches the reading experience, making "Happily Ever After" an indispensable addition to any fan of the Selection series.

Cass's writing style is as engaging as ever, with a perfect balance of romance, intrigue, and humor. She has a knack for creating characters that readers can't help but invest in, and her ability to weave intricate plots that keep readers on the edge of their seats is truly impressive. "Happily Ever After" is a testament to Cass's storytelling prowess, offering a compelling exploration of love, loyalty, and the complexities of royal life.

In conclusion, "Happily Ever After" is a must-read for fans of the Selection series, offering a captivating journey into the hearts and minds of beloved characters. Cass's signature style and engaging storytelling make this collection a standout addition to the series. Don't miss out on this enchanting exploration of the Illéa universe.

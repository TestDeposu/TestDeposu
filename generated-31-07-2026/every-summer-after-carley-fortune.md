# Every Summer After

**Author:** Carley Fortune  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

Nostalgia is a powerful, intoxicating lens, one that blurs the edges of past mistakes and magnifies the warmth of what we have lost. In her radiant 2022 debut, Every Summer After, author Carley Fortune captures this bittersweet ache with striking emotional precision. Masterfully structured around six formative summers and one fateful, reckoning weekend, the novel explores the intoxicating rise and devastating fall of a once-in-a-lifetime love, proving that while you can never truly go back home, the past has a persistent way of calling you back.

The narrative hinges on the dynamics of Persephone "Percy" Fraser and Sam Florek, two souls who were once entirely inseparable along the idyllic lakeshore of Barry’s Bay. Fortune’s stylistic brilliance shines in her dual-timeline approach, seamlessly weaving the sun-drenched, carefree days of their youth with the heavy, muted reality of their adulthood. The past sections glow with tactile sensory details—the scent of lake water, the humid air of late-July nights, the shared silence over medical textbooks and horror stories. Fortune evokes the distinct flavor of adolescence and early twenty-something yearning so vividly that readers will feel the phantom weight of their own youthful summers. 

At its core, the main theme of the novel is not merely romance, but the crushing burden of guilt and the arduous journey of forgiveness—specifically, forgiving oneself. When a catastrophic mistake tears Percy and Sam apart, Percy retreats into a guarded, metropolitan life, punishing herself with a decade of self-imposed exile. Fortune examines how a single, split-second decision can rewrite an entire future, anchoring the story in the universal fear of irreversible regret. When Percy returns to the lake for Sam’s mother’s funeral, the passage of time is palpable, loaded with unspoken grief and the agonizing ghost of what could have been.

Fortune’s writing style is effortlessly accessible, fast-paced, and deeply atmospheric, drawing immediate, rightful comparisons to contemporary romance titans like Emily Henry. She avoids overly florid prose in favor of emotionally raw, direct dialogue and internal monologues that feel intensely authentic. The chemistry between Percy and Sam sizzles with a poignant, second-chance urgency, making their inevitable collision course both agonizing and deeply satisfying to witness. 

Every Summer After is a sweeping, emotional triumph that reminds us how the people and choices of our youth leave indelible marks on who we become. It is a poignant exploration of first love, deep-seated remorse, and the courage it takes to confront our darkest moments in order to salvage our future. Carley Fortune has crafted an unforgettable ode to the summers that define us, cementing her status as a vital new voice in contemporary fiction.

# Tempt Me at Twilight (The Hathaways, #3)

**Author:** Lisa Kleypas  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

In "Tempt Me at Twilight," Lisa Kleypas masterfully weaves a captivating love story, expertly blending tantalizing romance, witty repartee, and intricate characterization. The third installment in her captivating Hathaway series, this enchanting novel delves into the complexities of relationships, desires, and the power of emotional vulnerability.

We meet Poppy Hathaway, a vibrant and unconventional young woman from a family renowned for their eccentricities. Poppy's greatest desire is to experience normalcy, but her chance encounter with the enigmatic Harry Rutledge, a wealthy hotel owner and inventor, leads her down a path of unexpected passion and danger. With her reputation threatened by their flirtation, Poppy is left stunned when she accepts Harry's proposal, sparking a whirlwind of emotions and desires.

As the narrative unfolds, Kleypas cleverly exposes Harry's guarded nature, skillfully crafting a compelling mystery shrouded in secrecy. The tension between Harry's guarded demeanor and Poppy's relentless pursuit of trust creates an irresistible dynamic. Their intricate dance of words and emotions masterfully illuminates the boundaries of trust and love.

Throughout the novel, Kleypas effortlessly navigates themes of vulnerability, desire, and the power of emotional connection. Her captivating writing style seamlessly integrates elements of wit, humor, and sensuality, elevating the Hatheway series to new heights.

In "Tempt Me at Twilight," Kleypas expertly explores the complex nuances of relationships, demonstrating the delicate balance between trust and vulnerability. As the story hurtles toward its climax, Harry and Poppy must confront their own insecurities and desires, ultimately forging a true union of body and soul.

Lisa Kleypas's mastery of historical romance shines through in this expertly crafted tale. Her characters leap from the page, richly drawn with depth, wit, and relatable complexity. Poppy and Harry's love story is one of the most captivating in recent memory, and their narrative will undoubtedly leave readers enchanted and emotionally invested.

One of the most distinctive aspects of Kleypas's writing is her deft ability to balance humor, pathos, and sensuality. This rare skill renders "Tempt Me at Twilight" a truly unforgettable reading experience, one that showcases her growth as an author and cements the Hathaway series as a benchmark for historical romance.

"Tempt Me at Twilight" stands as a testament to Kleypas's remarkable storytelling capabilities, masterfully capturing the essence of love, trust, and the human spirit. This captivating novel will transport readers to the Victorian era and into the heart of Poppy and Harry's love story, where the boundaries of trust and vulnerability are skillfully pushed to their limits.

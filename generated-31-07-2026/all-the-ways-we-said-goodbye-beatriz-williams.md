# All the Ways We Said Goodbye

**Author:** Beatriz Williams  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

All the Ways We Said Goodbye, the latest masterpiece from the New York Times bestselling authors of The Glass Ocean and The Forgotten Room, is a captivating historical fiction novel that weaves together the lives of three women across multiple timelines, all linked by the iconic Ritz hotel in Paris.

Set against the backdrop of two World Wars and the tumultuous 1960s, this epic tale follows the lives of Aurelie, a French heiress, Marguerite "Daisy" Villon, a Resistance fighter, and Barbara "Babs" Langford, an American widow. Each woman's story is intricately connected to the Ritz, a symbol of luxury and refuge in the midst of chaos.

One of the standout features of this novel is its masterful use of multiple timelines, which seamlessly blend together to create a rich tapestry of stories. The authors' use of language is evocative and immersive, transporting readers to the battle-scarred fields of World War I, the occupied streets of Paris during World War II, and the swinging sixties.

Throughout the novel, the authors explore the theme of female resilience and the power of women to shape their own destinies, even in the face of overwhelming adversity. Each of the three main characters is a testament to this theme, as they navigate the complexities of love, loss, and loyalty in the midst of war and social upheaval.

Aurelie's story, set during World War I, is a poignant exploration of the complexities of loyalty and betrayal. Her relationship with the German officer, Maximilian Von Sternburg, is a nuanced portrayal of the blurred lines between love and duty. Meanwhile, Daisy's story, set during World War II, is a thrilling account of resistance and courage in the face of overwhelming odds. Her transformation from reluctant participant to committed activist is a powerful testament to the human spirit.

Babs' story, set in the 1960s, serves as a framing device for the novel, as she uncovers the secrets of her husband's past and the mysterious woman known as La Fleur. This storyline is a clever exploration of the power of legacy and the secrets that bind families together.

One of the most striking aspects of this novel is its use of historical detail. The authors' research is meticulous, and the novel is filled with vivid descriptions of the Ritz, the streets of Paris, and the battlefields of World War I and II. This attention to detail adds depth and authenticity to the narrative, making the characters' experiences feel all the more real and relatable.

If you're a fan of historical fiction, particularly novels that explore the experiences of women during times of war and social change, then All the Ways We Said Goodbye is a must-read. The authors' writing is engaging and immersive, and the characters are complex and multifaceted. This is a novel that will stay with you long after you finish reading it, a testament to the enduring power of love, loyalty, and female resilience.

# One Week Girlfriend (One Week Girlfriend, #1)

**Author:** Monica Murphy  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In "One Week Girlfriend" by Monica Murphy, we're introduced to a world of temporary relationships, hidden secrets, and the blurring of lines between reality and fantasy. The story revolves around Fable Maguire, a young woman who's been dealt a tough hand in life. With a neglectful mother and the responsibility of raising her younger brother, Fable's life is a constant struggle. Her reputation as an easy catch has made her the temporary girlfriend of choice for many guys, but deep down, she yearns for something more.

Enter Drew Callahan, the charming college football star who proposes a fake relationship to Fable in exchange for cash. What starts as a business arrangement soon turns into a complex web of emotions, as both characters navigate their feelings for each other. Murphy masterfully crafts a narrative that's both sweet and sexy, with a dash of angst that will keep readers hooked from page one.

One of the standout aspects of this novel is its exploration of the main theme: the search for permanence in a world of temporary relationships. Fable's character, in particular, is a poignant representation of this theme. Her desire for a stable and loving relationship is palpable, and her willingness to take a chance on Drew, despite his initial reluctance, is admirable. Through Fable's journey, Murphy highlights the importance of human connection and the need for meaningful relationships in our lives.

Murphy's writing style is engaging and relatable, making it easy to become invested in the characters' lives. Her use of witty dialogue and sensual descriptions adds to the novel's charm, creating a steamy and romantic atmosphere that's hard to resist. The supporting characters, including Drew's friends and family, are well-developed and add depth to the story.

The author's ability to balance light-hearted moments with more serious themes is impressive. The novel tackles issues like neglect, abandonment, and the consequences of one's actions, but Murphy handles these topics with sensitivity and care. The result is a narrative that's both entertaining and thought-provoking.

Overall, "One Week Girlfriend" is a captivating romance that will appeal to fans of the genre. With its engaging characters, steamy romance, and exploration of deeper themes, this novel is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and emotional story. If you enjoy Monica Murphy's style, you'll be pleased to know that this is the first book in a series, with more opportunities to delve into the lives of these characters.

# Wolf by Wolf (Wolf by Wolf, #1)

**Author:** Ryan Graudin  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In the gripping alternate history novel "Wolf by Wolf" by Ryan Graudin, the world is turned upside down as the Axis powers have emerged victorious in World War II. This dark and unsettling reality sets the stage for a heart-pumping, action-packed tale of revenge, deception, and self-discovery.

At the center of the story is Yael, a former death camp prisoner with a painful past and a singular mission: to win the prestigious Axis Tour motorcycle race and assassinate the elusive Adolf Hitler. To achieve this goal, Yael must utilize her unique ability to skinshift and impersonate Adele Wolfe, last year's only female competitor. As she navigates the treacherous landscape of the race, Yael finds herself entangled in a complex web of relationships and alliances that threaten to compromise her mission.

Graudin's masterful storytelling weaves together elements of historical fiction, science fiction, and thriller genres, creating a unique and captivating narrative that explores the darker aspects of human nature. Through Yael's character, Graudin delves into the themes of trauma, identity, and the blurred lines between good and evil. Yael's struggles to reconcile her past and present selves serve as a powerful reminder of the lasting impact of war and oppression on individuals and society.

One of the most striking aspects of Graudin's writing style is her ability to balance action, suspense, and emotional depth. The pacing of the novel is relentless, with heart-stopping moments of tension and adrenaline-fueled racing sequences that propel the reader forward. Yet, Graudin also takes the time to explore the inner lives of her characters, crafting nuanced and multidimensional portraits that add depth and complexity to the narrative.

The world-building in "Wolf by Wolf" is equally impressive, with Graudin creating a chillingly plausible vision of a world where the Axis powers have triumphed. The details of this alternate history are meticulously researched and convincingly rendered, from the oppressive atmosphere of the Third Reich to the nuances of Japanese culture.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the supporting cast of characters sometimes feels slightly one-dimensional. While Felix and Luka are well-developed and intriguing characters, some of the other competitors in the Axis Tour feel more like caricatures than fully fleshed-out people. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise exceptional novel.

Overall, "Wolf by Wolf" is a gripping and thought-provoking novel that will appeal to fans of alternate history, science fiction, and action-packed thrillers. Graudin's writing is masterful, and her ability to balance action, suspense, and emotional depth is a rare talent. If you're looking for a novel that will keep you on the edge of your seat and leave you thinking long after the final page is turned, then "Wolf by Wolf" is an absolute must-read.

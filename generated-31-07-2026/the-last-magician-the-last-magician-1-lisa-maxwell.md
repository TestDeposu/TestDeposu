# The Last Magician (The Last Magician, #1)

**Author:** Lisa Maxwell  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

In "The Last Magician" by Lisa Maxwell, the fabric of time is woven with intricate threads of magic, deception, and rebellion. This captivating tale whisks readers away to the enchanting world of early 20th-century New York, where the lines between reality and fantasy are masterfully blurred.

At the heart of the story lies Esta, a fiercely talented thief with the extraordinary ability to manipulate time. Raised by a secret society of rebels, Esta is trained to steal magical artifacts from the sinister Order that has confined the Mageus, individuals with magical affinities, to the island of Manhattan. The Order's dark energy barrier, known as the Brink, has all but extinguished magic in modern-day New York, and Esta's mission is to travel back in time to 1902 to pilfer an ancient book containing the secrets of the Order and the Brink.

Maxwell's writing is akin to a rich tapestry, woven with vivid descriptions of Old New York's mystical underbelly. The city comes alive with its ruthless gangs, secret societies, and hidden magical energies. As Esta navigates this treacherous world, she must confront her own demons and make impossible choices that threaten to upend the delicate balance of her mission.

One of the most striking aspects of Maxwell's narrative is her exploration of the theme of power and its corrupting influence. The Order, with its stranglehold on magic, serves as a potent symbol of oppression, while the rebels, with their own secrets and agendas, raise questions about the true nature of freedom and resistance. Esta's character, in particular, embodies the complexities of power, as she grapples with her own moral ambiguities and the consequences of her actions.

Throughout the book, Maxwell's prose is characterized by its lyricism and depth, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world she has created. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Esta being a standout as a strong, complex female protagonist. The pacing is expertly handled, with a narrative that twists and turns, keeping readers on the edge of their seats until the very end.

In short, "The Last Magician" is a mesmerizing tale of magic, time travel, and rebellion, set against the rich backdrop of Old New York. With its intricate world-building, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes, this book is sure to captivate fans of fantasy and adventure. Maxwell's writing is nothing short of enchanting, making this a must-read for anyone looking to escape into a world of wonder and magic.

# Slade (New Species, #2)

**Author:** Laurann Dohner  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

"Slade" by Laurann Dohner is a riveting and passionate tale of forbidden love, heart-pumping action, and the unbreakable bonds between two souls. As the second installment in the "New Species" series, this book delves into the complex world of interspecies relationships, where the lines between humans and New Species are constantly blurred.

At the heart of the story lies the tumultuous relationship between Dr. Trisha Norbit and 215, aka Slade, a New Species male with a penchant for delivering on his promises. Their initial encounter in a hospital bed, though brief, sets the tone for a fiery and all-consuming passion that refuses to be extinguished. However, when they meet again at Homeland, Slade's lack of recognition sparks a chain reaction of emotions, from anger to frustration, that threatens to derail their budding connection.

Dohner masterfully weaves a narrative that expertly balances action, suspense, and romance, creating a story that is both captivating and thought-provoking. The author's writing style is engaging and descriptive, with a keen focus on character development that makes it easy to become invested in the lives of Trisha and Slade.

One of the most striking aspects of "Slade" is its exploration of the complexities surrounding interspecies relationships. Dohner raises important questions about the nature of love, acceptance, and the blurred lines between humans and New Species. Through Trisha and Slade's relationship, the author highlights the challenges and prejudices that often accompany forbidden love, making for a compelling and emotionally charged read.

The chemistry between Trisha and Slade is undeniable, and their romance is a slow-burning fire that eventually erupts into a passionate and all-consuming flame. Dohner's writing is sensual and evocative, making it easy to become swept up in the intensity of their relationship.

As the story unfolds, Trisha finds herself in grave danger, and Slade is the only one who can save her. Their perilous journey through the wilderness is a heart-pumping, action-packed ride that will keep you on the edge of your seat. The consequences of their actions are far-reaching and will change their lives forever, adding an extra layer of tension and suspense to the narrative.

In conclusion, "Slade" is a gripping and romantic tale that will appeal to fans of paranormal romance and science fiction. Dohner's writing is engaging, and her characters are well-developed and relatable. With its thought-provoking themes and sensual romance, this book is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and emotionally charged story.

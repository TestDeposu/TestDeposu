# Holiday in Death (In Death, #7)

**Author:** J.D. Robb  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1998

**Holiday in Death: A Festive Thriller with a Dark Heart**

J.D. Robb's "Holiday in Death" is a chilling addition to her bestselling In Death series, offering a unique blend of holiday cheer and murderous suspense. Set against the backdrop of a New York City Christmas, this seventh installment follows Lieutenant Eve Dallas as she hunts a serial killer with a twisted obsession with the "Twelve Days of Christmas" carol.

Robb, the pseudonym of romance powerhouse Nora Roberts, masterfully weaves a tale that is as cozy as a warm fireplace and as unsettling as a shadow in the night. The story begins with a seemingly innocuous dating service, Personally Yours, where lonely hearts seek their perfect match. But as Eve delves deeper, she uncovers a sinister connection between the service and a series of grisly murders, each one more horrifying than the last.

What sets "Holiday in Death" apart is Robb's ability to create a richly textured world that is both familiar and unsettling. The holiday season, with its promise of joy and togetherness, provides a stark contrast to the grim reality of Eve's investigation. The author's signature style is evident in her crisp dialogue, well-drawn characters, and intricate plotting. Eve Dallas, with her tough exterior and vulnerable core, is a protagonist that readers have come to love and root for.

Robb also excels in creating a sense of urgency and tension that keeps readers on the edge of their seats. The killer's macabre use of the "Twelve Days of Christmas" as a blueprint for their crimes adds a layer of complexity and horror to the story. As Eve races against time to stop the killer before they claim their final victim, readers are treated to a thrilling cat-and-mouse game that is as captivating as it is chilling.

However, "Holiday in Death" is not without its flaws. Some readers might find the pacing a bit slow in places, and the resolution of the mystery may feel a tad too neat. But these are minor quibbles in an otherwise engaging and entertaining read.

In conclusion, "Holiday in Death" is a must-read for fans of the In Death series and anyone looking for a thrilling holiday mystery. It's a testament to Robb's skill as a storyteller that she can make the most wonderful time of the year feel so wonderfully dangerous.

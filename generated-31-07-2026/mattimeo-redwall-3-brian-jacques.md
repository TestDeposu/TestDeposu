# Mattimeo (Redwall, #3)

**Author:** Brian Jacques  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1990

"Mattimeo," the third installment in Brian Jacques' enchanting Redwall series, is a thrilling tale that masterfully weaves together themes of courage, family, and the power of community. The story unfolds in the idyllic world of Redwall Abbey, a sanctuary for mice and other woodland creatures, threatened by the malevolent Slagar the Fox and his band of miscreants.

Jacques' signature style is evident throughout, with vivid descriptions that bring the world of Redwall to life. From the lush greenery of Mossflower Woods to the imposing stone walls of the abbey, every detail is painted with a richness that immerses the reader in the story. The author's ability to create memorable characters, both heroes and villains, is another standout feature. Slagar, with his cunning and ruthlessness, is a formidable antagonist, while Mattimeo, the young mouse at the heart of the story, is a hero who grows in courage and wisdom as the narrative progresses.

The main theme of "Mattimeo" is the power of love and unity in the face of adversity. When Slagar abducts Mattimeo and other Redwall younglings, the abbey's inhabitants must rally together to rescue them. The journey is fraught with danger, but it also serves to strengthen the bonds between the creatures of Redwall. The mice, otters, squirrels, and badgers may differ in size and strength, but they are united in their love for their home and their determination to protect it.

Jacques' storytelling is filled with action and adventure, but it's also deeply emotional. The love between Mattimeo and his father, Matthias, is a poignant thread that runs through the story. Their separation and eventual reunion is a powerful exploration of the lengths a parent will go to protect their child, and the strength a child can draw from their parent's love.

"Mattimeo" is a captivating tale that will appeal to readers of all ages. It's a story of bravery and resilience, of love and loss, and of the power of community. Brian Jacques' unique style, with its rich descriptions and unforgettable characters, makes "Mattimeo" a standout addition to the Redwall series.

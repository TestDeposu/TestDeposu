# Trust in Me (Wait for You, #1.5)

**Author:** J. Lynn  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

**Trust in Me: A Sizzling Second Chance Romance That Rewrites the Rules**

J. Lynn’s *Trust in Me*—the prequel novella to the beloved *Wait for You* series—isn’t just a glimpse behind the curtain of Cam and Avery’s love story; it’s a masterclass in how persistence, vulnerability, and the quiet power of persistence can rewrite fate. Set against the backdrop of Avery Morgansten’s guarded heart and Cameron Hamilton’s relentless charm, this novella is a love letter to the slow-burn romance that refuses to be rushed.

At its core, *Trust in Me* explores the tension between desire and distrust—the way Cam’s signature confidence clashes with Avery’s walls, built not from coldness, but from pain. Lynn deftly crafts a narrative where Cam isn’t just a player, but a man who *learns*—his charm isn’t a weapon, but a tool, honed by the rare woman who can see past it. The dynamic between them crackles with chemistry, but it’s the psychological depth that elevates this beyond a typical enemies-to-lovers trope. Avery’s secrets aren’t just plot devices; they’re the scaffolding of her character, forcing Cam to earn her trust one crumb of vulnerability at a time. And Lynn doesn’t shy away from the messiness of it—there’s no grand confession or instant resolution. Instead, she shows how love, when it’s real, is built on patience, on the quiet moments (like Cam’s homemade cookies, a detail so tender it feels like a whisper) that chip away at defenses.

Lynn’s writing style is a perfect blend of wit and warmth, with a knack for dialogue that feels effortless yet layered. Cam’s voice is effortlessly magnetic—charismatic, but never smug—while Avery’s sharp retorts and dry humor give her agency without undermining her emotional complexity. The prose is lush but never overindulgent, balancing steamy tension with moments of quiet introspection that make the emotional payoff land harder. It’s a style that invites readers to linger, to savor the way Cam’s persistence isn’t just annoying but *compelling*—because beneath the charm, there’s a man who’s willing to prove himself.

What makes *Trust in Me* stand out is how it reframes the *Wait for You* series from Avery’s perspective. While the original trilogy focuses on Avery’s journey to trust, this novella flips the script, showing Cam’s struggle to *earn* that trust. It’s a refreshing take on the trope of the "untrustworthy hero," proving that redemption isn’t about changing who you are, but about proving you’re worthy of someone’s heart. The novella’s brevity doesn’t diminish its impact; in fact, it forces Lynn to distill the essence of their dynamic into its purest form—no filler, just the raw, magnetic pull between two people who refuse to let go.

For fans of slow-burn romances where the emotional stakes are as high as the physical tension, *Trust in Me* is a must-read. It’s a love story that doesn’t just ask for trust—it *earns* it, one stubborn, soulful glance at a time. And in a genre often criticized for its predictability, Lynn delivers something rare: a romance that feels earned, real, and utterly irresistible.

# Kiss of the Highlander (Highlander, #4)

**Author:** Karen Marie Moning  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2001

**A Highlander’s Kiss Through Time: Karen Marie Moning’s *Kiss of the Highlander***

Karen Marie Moning’s *Kiss of the Highlander*—the fourth installment in her beloved *Highlander* series—is a masterclass in blending historical romance with the timeless allure of forbidden passion. Set against the rugged beauty of sixteenth-century Scotland, this novel weaves a spellbinding tale of love, destiny, and the defiance of time itself. Moning’s signature style—rich in atmospheric detail, emotionally charged, and steeped in the raw intensity of her heroines—makes this book a standout in the genre.

At its heart, *Kiss of the Highlander* is a story about two souls torn between past and future, bound by a love that transcends centuries. Drustan MacKeltar, a Highland laird cursed to sleep for nearly five hundred years, awakens to find himself in a world where his clan’s fate hangs in the balance. His salvation—and his salvation’s name—comes in the form of Gwen Cassidy, a modern woman who stumbles into his time, her life forever altered by the touch of a man who is both her savior and her undoing. Their connection is electric, a collision of eras where Gwen’s contemporary spirit clashes with Drustan’s ancient warrior’s heart. Moning crafts their romance with a deft hand, balancing the heat of their physical attraction with the depth of their emotional bond. Gwen, a woman seeking adventure and freedom, finds herself ensnared not just by Drustan’s rugged charm but by the very idea of a love that could rewrite history.

What makes *Kiss of the Highlander* so compelling is Moning’s ability to immerse readers in the world of sixteenth-century Scotland. The novel pulses with the scent of peat smoke, the roar of distant battles, and the quiet desperation of a land under siege. Drustan is a hero worthy of the legend—tough, honorable, and fiercely protective of those he loves. Yet, he is also deeply flawed, his past trauma and the weight of his destiny making him a man who must choose between duty and desire. Gwen, meanwhile, is a heroine who refuses to be passive. She is sharp-witted, resilient, and unafraid to challenge the very fabric of time itself. Their dynamic is electric, a perfect blend of tension and tenderness that keeps readers hooked from the first page.

Moning’s prose is lush and evocative, painting a vivid picture of a world where magic and history intertwine. The romance is slow-burning, allowing the emotional stakes to build with each passing chapter. The tension between Drustan and Gwen is palpable, their chemistry simmering beneath the surface until it erupts into a passion that feels both timeless and urgent. The novel also explores themes of fate and free will, asking whether love can truly defy the hands of time or if some destinies are written in stone.

While *Kiss of the Highlander* is a standalone story within the series, it stands on its own as a gripping tale of love and adventure. Moning’s ability to craft a heroine who is both relatable and extraordinary, and a hero who is as much a victim of his own past as he is a conqueror of his future, makes this book a must-read for fans of historical romance. It’s a story that reminds us that love, like the Highlands themselves, is enduring—no matter how many centuries stand between us.

For those who love heroes who defy the odds and heroines who refuse to be tamed, *Kiss of the Highlander* is a love letter to the power of passion and the magic of second chances. Karen Marie Moning once again delivers a novel that is as emotionally resonant as it is beautifully written, proving that some loves are worth fighting for—no matter the cost.

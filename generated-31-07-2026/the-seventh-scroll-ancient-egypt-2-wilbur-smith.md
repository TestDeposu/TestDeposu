# The Seventh Scroll (Ancient Egypt, #2)

**Author:** Wilbur Smith  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

**A Masterful Return to Ancient Egypt**

Wilbur Smith, the grandmaster of adventure and historical fiction, once again transports us to the sun-drenched sands of ancient Egypt in "The Seventh Scroll," the second installment of his captivating Ancient Egyptian series. Smith, lauded by Stephen King as the "best historical novelist," weaves a tale that is as thrilling as it is rich in historical detail, proving yet again why he is a benchmark against whom others are compared.

The story picks up 4,000 years after the battle for the Egyptian Kingdom, focusing on Royan al Simma, a woman of unyielding determination and intellect, who, alongside her late husband Duraid, had discovered the tomb of Queen Lostris and the secret scrolls of her trusted servant, Taita. Grief-stricken but undeterred, Royan embarks on a perilous journey to unravel the secrets of the seventh scroll, a quest that promises to lead her to the hidden tomb of Pharaoh Mamose and its untold riches.

Smith's prowess lies in his ability to blend history and adventure seamlessly, creating a narrative that feels both authentic and exhilarating. He paints a vivid picture of ancient Egypt, from the opulent palaces to the dusty tombs, bringing the ancient world to life with a depth of knowledge and a storytelling prowess that is truly masterful.

At the heart of "The Seventh Scroll" is Royan, a heroine as compelling as any of Smith's male protagonists. She is a testament to Smith's evolution as a storyteller, proving that his narratives are not merely action-packed adventures, but also thoughtful explorations of human resilience and determination.

However, Smith's signature style is not without its criticisms. Some may find the pacing uneven, with moments of intense action followed by detailed historical exposition. Yet, for those who appreciate Smith's meticulous research and his ability to bring history to life, this is a small price to pay.

"The Seventh Scroll" is not just a thrilling adventure; it is a love letter to ancient Egypt, a testament to Smith's enduring passion for the subject matter. It is a book that will captivate fans of historical fiction and adventure enthusiasts alike, leaving them eager for the next installment in this captivating series.

# Fifth Grave Past the Light (Charley Davidson, #5)

**Author:** Darynda Jones  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

Caffeine, corpses, and the son of Satan: welcome back to the deliciously chaotic world of Darynda Jones. In Fifth Grave Past the Light, the fifth installment of her New York Times bestselling Charley Davidson series, Jones delivers a masterclass in blending supernatural suspense with laugh-out-loud romantic comedy. This book is not merely another entry in a crowded urban fantasy genre; it is a high-octane thrill ride that pushes its quirky, caffeine-addicted protagonist to the absolute brink, anchoring the narrative in the deeply resonant themes of sacrifice, trust, and the unbreakable bonds of sisterhood.

At the heart of this novel is Charley Davidson, a part-time private investigator, full-time grim reaper, and perpetual magnet for the bizarre. Jones has always excelled at creating distinct voices, but here, Charley’s internal monologue—fueled by double espressos and mocha lattes—hits a fever pitch of witty banter and self-deprecating humor. Yet, beneath the wisecracks and rapid-fire pop culture references lies a profound emotional core. The main theme of Fifth Grave Past the Light revolves around the ultimate cost of devotion. When a deranged serial killer begins targeting not just random spirits, but Charley’s own beloved sister, Gemma, the stakes shift from paranormal nuisance to deeply personal terror. 

Jones uses this central threat to explore the grey areas of morality and the lengths to which we will go to protect family. Charley’s desperate bid to save Gemma forces her into a precarious alliance with Reyes Farrow, the sultry, dangerous son of Satan and Charley’s primary obsession. Reyes is a character wrapped in shadow and suspicion, currently eyed as the main suspect in a local arson case. The tension between them crackles with palpable electricity, showcasing Jones’s signature authorial style: a seamless fusion of scorching romantic suspense and supernatural mystery. Reyes demands a heavy toll for his protection—all of Charley, body and soul. This Faustian bargain forms the emotional crucible of the book, testing Charley's boundaries and forcing her to confront her own vulnerabilities.

What sets Jones apart in the realm of paranormal fiction is her pacing and tonal dexterity. She can pivot from a genuinely terrifying sequence involving terrified, displaced spirits wandering into Charley’s apartment to a scene of laugh-out-loud absurdity in a single paragraph. Her prose is snappy, effortlessly readable, and relentlessly engaging. She treats the supernatural not as a distant, eerie concept, but as a messy, lived-in reality complete with bureaucratic annoyances and awkward social encounters. 

As dead women begin appearing in her living space, lost and confused, Charley’s dual roles as detective and reaper collide in horrific fashion. Jones constructs a compelling whodunit that keeps the reader guessing, weaving the arson subplot and the serial killer mystery together with narrative precision. But the true triumph of the novel remains its character dynamics. Charley and Reyes are a powerhouse couple of modern fantasy, their chemistry elevated by witty dialogue and deep-seated emotional stakes.

Fifth Grave Past the Light is a triumphant continuation of a beloved series. It offers a masterclass in how to keep long-running characters fresh by continuously raising the emotional and physical stakes. Darynda Jones proves once again that she is a queen of paranormal romantic suspense, delivering a novel that is as touching as it is thrilling, and as hilarious as it is haunting. For fans of smart-mouthed heroines, brooding supernatural antiheroes, and mysteries packed with heart, this fifth grave is an absolute must-read.

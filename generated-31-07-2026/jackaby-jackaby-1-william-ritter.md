# Jackaby (Jackaby, #1)

**Author:** William Ritter  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

In the world of literature, it's not often that you come across a book that seamlessly weaves together elements of mystery, horror, and historical fiction to create a thrilling narrative that leaves readers on the edge of their seats. William Ritter's 'Jackaby' is one such book that not only showcases the author's remarkable storytelling prowess but also sets the stage for a captivating series that promises to take readers on a wild ride.

Published in 2014, 'Jackaby' is the first book in the Jackaby series, introducing readers to R.F. Jackaby, a brilliant and eccentric investigator of the unexplained with a keen eye for the extraordinary, and his new assistant, Abigail Rook. The story takes place in 1892 New Fiddleham, New England, a small town where the veil between the ordinary and the extraordinary is thin. Abigail, a young and eager woman with a gift for noticing details that others often miss, is the perfect candidate to assist Jackaby in his investigations.

As Abigail navigates her new role, she finds herself in the midst of a thrilling case - a serial killer is on the loose, leaving behind a trail of bodies with no explanation. The police are convinced it's an ordinary villain, but Jackaby is certain the foul deeds are the work of a supernatural creature whose very existence the local police seem adamant to deny. Abigail's first day on the job quickly becomes a baptism by fire as she finds herself drawn to Jackaby's keen intelligence and sensitivity to phenomena others barely perceive. However, her feelings are complicated by the presence of Charlie, a handsome young policeman willing to help Jackaby and Abigail on the case.

Ritter's writing style is one of the highlightsof the book. With vivid descriptions and meticulous attention to historical detail, he transports readers to a bygone era that feels both familiar and alien. His characterizations are equally impressive, with Abigail and Jackaby standing out as particularly well-developed and complex. The chemistry between Abigail and Jackaby is palpable, and their banter and interactions are both amusing and heartwarming.

One of the main themes that emerges in 'Jackaby' is the power of perception and the blurred lines between reality and the supernatural. Jackaby's unique ability to see beyond the veil of the ordinary makes him both a valuable asset and a threat to those around him. Abigail's own gift for noticing details that others often miss highlights the importance of observation and the impact it can have on our understanding of the world.

Ritter's exploration of these themes is nuanced and thought-provoking, raising questions about the nature of reality and our place in it. The author's use of subtle foreshadowing and expertly crafted plot twists keeps readers on the edge of their seats, eager to uncover the truth behind the serial killings.

Overall, 'Jackaby' is a delightful and suspenseful read that showcases author William Ritter's exceptional storytelling abilities. With its captivating characters, rich historical setting, and thought-provoking themes, it's a book that will appeal to fans of mystery, horror, and historical fiction. The series has already garnered a devoted following, and it's clear that 'Jackaby' is just the beginning of an epic adventure that will take readers on a wild ride of thrills and chills.

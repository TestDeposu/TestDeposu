# Les Disparus du Clairdelune (La Passe-Miroir, #2)

**Author:** Christelle Dabos  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

In the captivating world of La Passe-Miroir, Christelle Dabos weaves a spellbinding tale of mystery, intrigue, and deception in Les Disparus du Clairdelune, the second installment of the series. Fresh from her promotion to vice-countess, Ophélie finds herself entangled in a web of hatred and conspiracy that threatens to destroy the very fabric of the Citacielle.

As Ophélie navigates this treacherous landscape, she is forced to confront the dark underbelly of the aristocracy, where illusions and deception reign supreme. With her enigmatic fiancé Thorn by her side, Ophélie must rely on her wit and cunning to unravel the mystery of the disappearances of influential personalities at court. As she delves deeper into the heart of the mystery, she is drawn into a world beyond the illusions of the Pôle, where the truth is more sinister than she ever imagined.

Dabos' masterful storytelling is a hallmark of this series, and Les Disparus du Clairdelune is no exception. Her writing is evocative and immersive, transporting the reader to a world of breathtaking beauty and darkness. The characters are complex and multi-dimensional, with Ophélie's growth and development as a protagonist being particularly noteworthy. Her struggles to reconcile her own identity with the expectations of her new role are deeply relatable, making her a compelling and sympathetic heroine.

One of the standout features of Dabos' writing is her ability to balance action, suspense, and intrigue with deeper themes and symbolism. Les Disparus du Clairdelune is a thought-provoking exploration of the nature of truth, power, and identity, raising questions about the consequences of deception and the corrupting influence of power. Through Ophélie's journey, Dabos sheds light on the darker aspects of human nature, revealing the complexities and nuances of the human experience.

The world-building in La Passe-Miroir is another notable aspect of the series, with Dabos creating a richly detailed and immersive environment that draws the reader in. The Citacielle, with its gilded halls and ornate furnishings, is a character in its own right, exerting a powerful influence over the lives of its inhabitants. The mystical and mysterious elements of the world are expertly woven into the narrative, adding depth and complexity to the story.

In conclusion, Les Disparus du Clairdelune is a captivating and thought-provoking read that will appeal to fans of fantasy, mystery, and suspense. Dabos' masterful storytelling, coupled with her vivid world-building and complex characters, makes for a compelling narrative that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. If you're looking for a series that will transport you to a world of wonder and magic, look no further than La Passe-Miroir.

# The Lost Continent (Wings of Fire, #11)

**Author:** Tui T. Sutherland  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

The Lost Continent, the eleventh installment in the Wings of Fire series by Tui T. Sutherland, is a mesmerizing and action-packed ride that delves into the world of dragons like never before. The story takes place in the tranquil land of Pantala, where SilkWings and HiveWings coexist in harmony, or so it seems. Blue, the protagonist, is a SilkWing who has always been content with his life in the Hive. However, his world is turned upside down when his sister Luna undergoes her Metamorphosis, and her wings start to grow in unexpected ways.

As Blue navigates this new reality, he discovers that Pantala is not as peaceful as it appears. The author masterfully weaves together a complex web of secrets, lies, and hidden dangers that threaten the very existence of the SilkWings and HiveWings. Through Blue's journey, Sutherland explores the theme of adaptation and resilience in the face of uncertainty. Blue is forced to confront his own limitations and fears, and to learn to fight for his life in a world that is no longer safe.

One of the standout features of The Lost Continent is its unique world-building. Sutherland has created a rich and immersive environment that is full of fascinating creatures, politics, and mythology. The dragons in this world are not just fire-breathing beasts, but complex beings with their own cultures, traditions, and motivations. The author's attention to detail is impressive, and the world of Pantala is so vividly realized that it's easy to become completely absorbed in the story.

Sutherland's writing style is engaging and accessible, making this book an excellent choice for both middle-grade readers and adults. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and emotional moments that keep the reader invested in the story. The characters are well-developed and relatable, with Blue being a particularly compelling protagonist. His struggles to come to terms with his new reality are deeply human, and his bravery and determination are inspiring.

The Lost Continent is also notable for its exploration of social hierarchy and prejudice. The SilkWings and HiveWings have a complex relationship, with the HiveWings holding positions of power and authority. Sutherland raises important questions about the nature of privilege and oppression, and the ways in which individuals can work to create positive change.

In conclusion, The Lost Continent is a thrilling and thought-provoking addition to the Wings of Fire series. With its rich world-building, engaging characters, and timely themes, this book is sure to delight fans of fantasy and adventure. Whether you're a longtime reader of the series or new to the world of Pantala, this book is an absolute must-read.

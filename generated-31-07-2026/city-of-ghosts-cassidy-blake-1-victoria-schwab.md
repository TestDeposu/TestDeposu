# City of Ghosts (Cassidy Blake, #1)

**Author:** Victoria Schwab  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

**A Hauntingly Brilliant Sequel: *City of Ghosts* by Victoria Schwab**

Victoria Schwab’s *City of Ghosts* isn’t just a book—it’s a portal into a world where the dead refuse to stay buried, where friendship is a supernatural bond, and where every shadow hides a secret. The sequel, *Cassidy Blake*, continues this chilling, heartfelt saga with the same razor-sharp wit, emotional depth, and eerie atmosphere that made the original a *New York Times* bestseller. Schwab doesn’t just write about ghosts; she breathes life into them, making them as complex and compelling as the living characters who cross their paths.

At its core, *Cassidy Blake* is a story about the weight of legacy, the fragility of trust, and the terrifying beauty of the unseen. Cassidy, the psychic ghost-hunting prodigy from the first book, is no longer just a kid navigating the supernatural—she’s a young woman grappling with the consequences of her choices. When she stumbles upon a malevolent spirit lurking in the labyrinthine Catacombs beneath Paris, she’s forced to confront not just the dangers of the afterlife, but the secrets her own family has been hiding. Schwab masterfully weaves together mystery, horror, and romance, ensuring that every revelation feels earned, every twist unsettling, and every emotional beat resonates.

What makes this book so gripping is Schwab’s ability to balance humor and horror. Cassidy’s dry, sarcastic voice—sharp enough to cut glass—keeps the tone from veering into melodrama, even as the stakes grow more dire. Her dynamic with Jacob, her ghostly best friend, remains one of the most endearing relationships in young adult fiction: a friendship that’s equal parts banter, loyalty, and quiet understanding. But Schwab doesn’t shy away from the darker themes either. The novel explores grief, betrayal, and the cost of power, all while maintaining a pace that never lets the reader catch their breath.

The setting is just as integral to the story as the characters. Paris, with its romantic streets and hidden horrors, serves as both a character and a backdrop. The Catacombs, with their skeletal remains and whispered secrets, become a living entity—one that Cassidy must outsmart before it claims another soul. Schwab’s prose is lush yet precise, painting vivid images that linger long after the last page. Whether describing the crumbling beauty of the underground tunnels or the electric tension between Cassidy and a new ally, her writing immerses the reader in a world where the supernatural isn’t just real—it’s *alive*.

One of the most compelling aspects of *Cassidy Blake* is how it deepens the lore of the *City of Ghosts* universe without feeling like a retread. The mystery unfolds in layers, revealing not just what’s happening in Paris, but what’s been happening all along. Schwab’s world-building is intricate, but never overwhelming, and her ability to drop hints that pay off later is a testament to her skill as a storyteller. By the time the final pages turn, readers won’t just be satisfied—they’ll be *haunted* in the best way possible.

For fans of *Shadow and Bone* or *The Graveyard Book*, *Cassidy Blake* is a must-read. It’s a story about growing up, facing your demons, and realizing that some friendships are worth dying for. Schwab’s signature blend of supernatural intrigue, emotional honesty, and razor-sharp dialogue makes this sequel as unforgettable as its predecessor. If you haven’t yet fallen under the spell of Cassidy Blake, now is the time to do so—just be warned, you might not want to leave the afterlife behind.

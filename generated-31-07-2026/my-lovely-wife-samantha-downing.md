# My Lovely Wife

**Author:** Samantha Downing  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

**My Lovely Wife** by Samantha Downing is a tantalizing thriller that dares you to look beneath the seemingly perfect suburban facade. This debut novel is a darkly humorous and suspenseful exploration of a marriage that's anything but ordinary.

Downing's prose is sharp and engaging, drawing you in with its witty banter and clever observations. The story is narrated by the husband, who, with a sardonic wit, reveals the twisted game he and his wife play to keep their marriage from stagnating. Their secret? They've been killing people together.

The couple, Millicent and Tom, are your average neighbors, parents, and friends—except for their penchant for murder. They've been playing a deadly game of cat and mouse, targeting those they deem deserving of their unique brand of justice. The narrative is a gripping dance between their dark deeds and the mundane realities of their everyday life, creating a chilling contrast that keeps you on the edge of your seat.

At its core, **My Lovely Wife** is a exploration of the lengths people will go to keep their relationships alive. It's a dark comedy that asks what happens when the thrill of the chase becomes more important than the love you share. Downing masterfully walks the line between humor and horror, making you laugh one moment and gasp in shock the next.

The author's style is reminiscent of Dexter Morgan's narrative voice, with a dash of Mr. and Mrs. Smith's chemistry. Downing's ability to blend dark humor with suspense is what makes this novel stand out. She doesn't shy away from the grim reality of her characters' actions, but neither does she wallow in it. Instead, she uses it to highlight the absurdity and banality of evil.

**My Lovely Wife** is a wild ride that will keep you guessing until the very end. It's a testament to Downing's skill as a storyteller that she can make you root for characters who are anything but likable. This novel is a refreshing take on the thriller genre, proving that sometimes, the most dangerous games are the ones we play at home.

# Strangers in Time

**Author:** David Baldacci  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

David Baldacci's latest novel, Strangers in Time, is a heart-wrenching and thrilling tale of survival, hope, and the unbreakable bonds of human connection set against the backdrop of war-torn 1940s London. This historical thriller masterfully weaves together the stories of three individuals from different walks of life, each bearing scars from the Second World War.

At the center of the narrative are Charlie Matters, a 14-year-old orphan forced to survive by his wits and cunning, and Molly Wakefield, a 15-year-old evacuee who returns to a desolate London, only to find her family gone. Their lives intersect with Ignatius Oliver, a bereaved bookshop owner still reeling from the loss of his wife. As they form an unlikely family unit, they find solace in each other's company and in the musty confines of Ignatius's bookshop, The Book Keep.

Baldacci's writing is a masterclass in evocative storytelling, transporting readers to the ravaged streets of London, where the sounds of air raid sirens and the smell of smoke hang heavy in the air. His vivid descriptions of the city's devastation serve as a poignant reminder of the human cost of war. Through his characters, Baldacci explores the theme of resilience in the face of adversity, highlighting the ways in which people come together to form support networks in times of crisis.

One of the novel's greatest strengths lies in its well-developed characters. Charlie, Molly, and Ignatius are multidimensional and relatable, each with their own distinct voice and backstory. As they navigate the treacherous landscape of wartime London, they must confront their own personal demons and learn to trust one another. The relationships between the characters are authentic and heartfelt, making it easy to become invested in their stories.

Baldacci's pacing is expertly handled, balancing action, suspense, and emotional moments with ease. The plot is engaging and unpredictable, with a narrative that twists and turns like the streets of London. The author's use of historical detail is meticulous, adding depth and authenticity to the story.

Strangers in Time is a beautiful and haunting novel that will appeal to fans of historical fiction, thriller enthusiasts, and anyone looking for a story about the human spirit. With its vivid characters, gripping plot, and historical accuracy, this book is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and emotional journey.

# Twisted Perfection (Rosemary Beach, #5; Perfection, #1)

**Author:** Abbi Glines  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In Abbi Glines' latest installment in the Rosemary Beach series, Twisted Perfection, the author masterfully crafts a captivating narrative that delves into the complexities of relationships, vulnerability, and the pursuit of personal freedom. This novel expertly weaves the poignant story of Della Sloane, a young woman determined to break free from the shadows of her dark past, and Woods Kerrington, a charismatic and enigmatic character driven by his own desires.

Della's solo road trip is initially a quest for independence, but it evolves into a journey of self-discovery, where she encounters the enigmatic Woods in the quaint beach town of Rosemary. The instant attraction between them is undeniable, but their vastly different backgrounds and the scrutiny of the high-society circles that surround Woods threaten to derail their budding connection. As Della finds herself increasingly entangled in Woods' life, she is faced with the daunting prospect of confronting her past and risking the vulnerability that comes with surrendering to her feelings.

Glines' writing style shines in Twisted Perfection, as she seamlessly navigates the intricate web of Della and Woods' relationship. The author's ability to craft relatable characters, coupled with her keen insight into the human psyche, results in a story that feels both authentic and deeply emotional. Woods' character, in particular, is a masterpiece of complexity, with his charismatic exterior hiding a deep sense of vulnerability and a penchant for self-destruction.

One of the standout aspects of Twisted Perfection is its exploration of the human desire for connection and acceptance. Della's struggles to shed her past and find acceptance in a world that seems determined to reject her are poignantly rendered, while Woods' desperate longing for connection and love is heartbreakingly authentic. The tension that builds between the two characters is palpable, and their inevitable confrontation with the harsh realities of their relationship is both poignant and compelling.

Glines also demonstrates a keen understanding of the intricacies of high-society life, effortlessly depicting the complexities and pressures that come with wealth and privilege. The supporting cast of characters is equally well-developed, with a keen attention to detail that brings the world of Rosemary Beach to vibrant life.

Twisted Perfection is a masterful exploration of the twists and turns that life throws at us, and the devastating consequences of our choices. Abbi Glines' deft handling of sensitive themes and her unwavering commitment to authenticity result in a story that is both deeply moving and relatable. This novel is a must-read for fans of new adult romance and a testament to Glines' exceptional talent as a storyteller.

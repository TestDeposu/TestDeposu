# A Curse Dark as Gold

**Author:** Elizabeth C. Bunce  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

A Curse Dark as Gold, the fifth installment in Elizabeth C. Bunce's award-winning mystery series, is a masterful blend of atmospheric tension, clever plotting, and a dash of the supernatural. This latest adventure follows the intrepid Myrtle Hardcastle as she navigates a treacherous treasure hunt on a haunted Scottish estate, where the lines between reality and the paranormal are expertly blurred.

Bunce's writing is as evocative as ever, conjuring the crumbling grandeur of the ancestral castle and the rugged beauty of the Scottish island with ease. Her prose is infused with a sense of foreboding, perfectly capturing the eerie atmosphere of a place where ghostly moans echo through the walls and the wind howls like a chorus of restless spirits. It's the perfect setting for a mystery that will keep readers on the edge of their seats.

At the heart of the story is Myrtle, a fiercely intelligent and independent amateur detective who is as determined as she is resourceful. Her investigation into the mysterious deaths and the cursed treasure is a thrilling ride, full of twists and turns that will keep even the most seasoned mystery readers guessing. But what sets Myrtle apart from other detectives is her vulnerability and her deep emotional connection to the people around her. Her desire to see her father and governess together is a poignant reminder that even the most capable among us are not immune to the complexities of the human heart.

Bunce's greatest strength lies in her ability to balance the darker elements of the story with a sense of humor and wit. Myrtle's observations on the absurdities of life are a delightful counterpoint to the ominous events unfolding around her, and her relationships with the people on the island are warm and engaging.

As the story hurtles towards its climax, Bunce ratchets up the tension, expertly manipulating the plot to create a sense of claustrophobia and desperation. Myrtle is forced to play a deadly game, hunting for the treasure with a thief hot on her heels, and the stakes are higher than ever before. It's a heart-pumping, edge-of-your-seat ride that will keep readers guessing until the very end.

In short, A Curse Dark as Gold is a triumph, a masterful blend of mystery, suspense, and atmosphere that will appeal to fans of the genre and newcomers alike. With its clever plotting, memorable characters, and evocative setting, it's a must-read for anyone who loves a good puzzle. So if you're looking for a story that will keep you up late at night, guessing and wondering, then look no further than A Curse Dark as Gold.

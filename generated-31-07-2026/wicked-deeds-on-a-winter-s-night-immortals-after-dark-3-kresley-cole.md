# Wicked Deeds on a Winter's Night (Immortals After Dark, #3)

**Author:** Kresley Cole  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

"Wicked Deeds on a Winter's Night" is a captivating tale from the accomplished pen of Kresley Cole, weaving an intricate and seductive world of magic, passion, and the unbreakable bonds between two souls. The third installment in the Immortals After Dark series, this book masterfully delves into the heart of a tormented warrior, Bowen MacRieve, a Highland werewolf haunted by his past losses, and the fierce young witch, Mariketa, bound by fate and an ancient prophecy to unite with him.

At first, the premise may seem simple: a wary Highland werewolf, broken by a love misplaced, and a captivating young witch who can ignite his passions once again. Yet, Kresley Cole's masterful hands take us on a thrilling journey through treacherous lands of seduction, ancient magic, and supernatural creatures. With each passing page, the narrative deepens, revealing Bowen's tortured soul as he guards Mariketa from the forces gathering against her. As their connection intensifies, it becomes increasingly clear that no power can hold them back - not even the danger emanating from the most malevolent foes nor their own fears of a future doomed by fate.

Bowen MacRieve is an unforgettable anti-hero: a warrior shrouded in mystery, a guardian whose love for Mariketa is rooted deep in his very being. Yet he's a far cry from the typical heroic archetype, carrying scars that make him almost inescapably isolated. What sets him apart is that Cole skillfully portrays him with an unvarnished honesty, showing in vivid detail the pain that fuels him. Through Bowen's unyielding commitment to Mariketa, the reader witnesses the true meaning of selfless love and the price one may pay for it.

Meanwhile, Mariketa "Mari" - the awaited, the young witch destined for greatness - stands tall, refusing to be defeated by her temporary powerlessness, her lack of trust. As the narrative unfolds, her character evolves from an initially enigmatic figure to a fiery, spirited and resilient woman who finds inner power within her own vulnerability. Her tumultuous bond with Bowen not only brings to life Kresley Cole's signature blend of passion, drama, and sensuality, but also reveals to both the reader and the characters the potential that can develop when we open ourselves to love's transformative power.

One of the hallmarks of Kresley Cole's writing is her incredible narrative flair and the ability to combine rich, vivid descriptions of the gothic landscapes, with a narrative voice that masterfully conveys the turmoil of her characters' emotions without ever losing sight of the larger plot. The author deftly blends elements of the supernatural and romance with humor and wit, effortlessly transporting us to a realm of mythical worlds, mythical creatures, and, of course, their endless, fierce love battles.

At the heart of "Wicked Deeds on a Winter's Night", we find the timeless struggle to defy destiny, to dare to defy all odds, regardless of the risks. Kresley Cole's magnificent portrayal of the intense connection between Bowen and Mariketa is an exquisite reminder that, no matter how strong our fear and hesitation might be, love can become the greatest strength of all - one that we would risk everything to hold onto.

Wicked Deeds on a Winter's Night is sure to leave readers captivated and eager for more.

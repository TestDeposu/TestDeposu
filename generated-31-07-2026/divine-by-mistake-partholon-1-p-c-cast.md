# Divine By Mistake (Partholon, #1)

**Author:** P.C. Cast  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

Divine By Mistake drops Shannon Parker—an ordinary high‑school English teacher with a penchant for red wine and poolside reading—into a whirlwind of myth, magic, and unexpected destiny. When she snags an eerily familiar vase at a stuffy estate auction, the ancient artwork pulls her through a portal into Partholon, a realm where goddesses walk among mortals and centaurs command the shadows. Instantly she inhabits the body of Rhiannon, the Goddess Incarnate, and is betrothed to the brooding yet undeniably handsome High Shaman ClanFintan, a centaur whose loyalty is as fierce as his stare.

At its core, the novel explores the tension between who we think we are and who we are meant to become. Shannon’s sudden thrust into divine authority forces her to confront the limits of her modern, rational self while embracing a heritage steeped in Celtic lore and primal power. P.C. Cast uses this premise to ask whether destiny is a cage or a canvas—can a woman accustomed to grading essays truly wield the mantle of a goddess, and what does it cost to love a being straddling two worlds? The answer unfolds through Shannon’s growing confidence, her willingness to fight for both land and love, and the gradual realization that true strength lies in owning one’s contradictions.

Cast’s style is unmistakably hers: brisk, dialogue‑driven, and laced with a wry humor that keeps even the most epic battles feeling personal. She paints Partholon with lush, sensory detail—mist‑cloaked forests, the thunder of hooves on stone, the intoxicating scent of wild herbs—while never letting the world‑building overshadow the intimate moments between Shannon and ClanFintan. The romance simmers with a slow‑burn intensity, balanced by snappy banter and Shannon’s candid internal monologue, which grounds the fantastical stakes in a relatable voice. Action sequences are swift and vivid, yet Cast always returns to the emotional heartbeat: the fear of inadequacy, the thrill of discovery, and the quiet courage required to step into a role you never auditioned for.

Divine By Mistake succeeds as a lively entry in the Partholon series because it marries mythic grandeur with a thoroughly modern heroine’s journey. Readers who enjoy fast‑paced fantasy romance, strong‑willed protagonists, and a dash of Celtic mythology will find themselves turning pages late into the night, eager to see how Shannon’s inadvertent goddesshood reshapes both her heart and a world on the brink of darkness.

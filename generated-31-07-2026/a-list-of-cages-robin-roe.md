# A List of Cages

**Author:** Robin Roe  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

In "A List of Cages," debut author Robin Roe weaves a heart-wrenching and thought-provoking narrative that delves into the complexities of trauma, loss, and the unbreakable bonds of friendship. This gripping story follows Adam Blake, a charismatic high school senior, as he reunites with his foster brother Julian, a troubled freshman with a penchant for storytelling and a penchant for secrecy.

Through Roe's masterful storytelling, the reader is transported into the world of these two lionhearted characters, each struggling to navigate the complexities of their own lives. Adam, with his ADHD and quick wit, is the perfect foil to Julian's quiet introspection, and their reunion sets off a chain of events that will change them both forever.

One of the most striking aspects of "A List of Cages" is its thoughtful exploration of trauma and its aftermath. Roe handles the subject matter with sensitivity and nuance, never shying away from the harsh realities of abuse and neglect. Yet, even in the midst of such darkness, she manages to infuse the narrative with a sense of hope and resilience, reminding us that even the most broken individuals can find solace in the power of human connection.

Roe's writing style is both evocative and unflinching, with a keen attention to detail that brings her characters to vivid life. Her prose is economical yet effective, conveying the complexities of her characters' emotions with a subtlety that belies the depth of their struggles. The result is a narrative that is both gripping and moving, a true page-turner that will keep readers on the edge of their seats until the very end.

What sets "A List of Cages" apart from other novels in the same genre is its thoughtful exploration of the foster care system and the ways in which it can both help and hinder the children in its care. Roe's portrayal of the system's flaws and shortcomings is both scathing and insightful, highlighting the ways in which it can fail those who need it most. And yet, even in the midst of such criticism, she manages to convey a sense of hope and possibility, reminding us that even the most broken systems can be redeemed through the power of human connection.

In short, "A List of Cages" is a masterpiece of contemporary young adult literature, a gripping and moving narrative that will resonate with readers long after the final page has been turned. With its thoughtful exploration of trauma, loss, and the power of friendship, this novel is a must-read for anyone who has ever struggled to find their place in the world.

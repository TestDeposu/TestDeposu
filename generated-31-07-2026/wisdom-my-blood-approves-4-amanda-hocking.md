# Wisdom (My Blood Approves, #4)

**Author:** Amanda Hocking  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**Wisdom: A Gritty, Emotionally Raw Finale to Amanda Hocking’s *My Blood Approves* Saga**

Amanda Hocking’s *My Blood Approves* series has long been a cornerstone of young adult paranormal romance—a genre where love, danger, and supernatural intrigue collide. *Wisdom*, the fourth installment in this sprawling saga, serves as both a pivotal midpoint and a bridge to the series’ explosive conclusion in *Swear*. But what makes this updated bundle—now including prequels, bonus content, and a fresh holiday short story—so compelling isn’t just its expanded scope; it’s Hocking’s unflinching honesty about love, sacrifice, and the messy, often brutal cost of defying fate.

At its core, *My Blood Approves* is a story about rebellion. Alice Bonham, our protagonist, is far from extraordinary—until she meets the vampires of the Blackthorn coven. What begins as a forbidden romance quickly spirals into a war between humanity and the undead, where loyalty is tested, and survival hinges on choices that feel anything but heroic. Hocking’s strength lies in her refusal to romanticize the supernatural. The vampires here are not glamorous lords or brooding gods; they are flawed, vengeful, and deeply human in their cruelty. Their world is one of blood debts, ancient grudges, and a code so rigid it borders on tyranny. Alice, meanwhile, is no damsel—she’s stubborn, impulsive, and willing to burn the world down for the ones she loves. This dynamic makes their relationships electric, not just because of the supernatural stakes, but because Hocking forces her characters to confront the real-world consequences of their actions.

The series’ emotional depth shines brightest in *Wisdom*, where the tension between Alice’s humanity and the vampire world’s demands reaches a fever pitch. Hocking excels at writing love that feels both intoxicating and destructive—Alice’s bond with her vampire lovers is tender, but it’s also a relationship built on deception, betrayal, and the constant threat of annihilation. The romance isn’t just about passion; it’s about the terror of being loved by something that could end you. This duality is what makes the series so gripping. You’re never just reading a vampire story; you’re witnessing a love story that could destroy everyone involved.

Hocking’s prose is lean, direct, and unapologetically emotional. She doesn’t indulge in over-the-top descriptions or melodrama; instead, she delivers sharp, visceral writing that keeps the pacing tight and the stakes high. Her dialogue crackles with tension, and her world-building—while not overly complex—feels immersive enough to pull readers into the Blackthorn coven’s brutal hierarchy. The bonus content, including the prequel novella *Letters to Elise* and the new holiday story *Little Tree*, adds layers to the lore without overwhelming the narrative. These extras offer deeper insights into the characters’ pasts and the world’s history, reinforcing the series’ themes of legacy and the weight of the past.

Yet, the real power of *Wisdom* lies in its unflinching exploration of sacrifice. By this point in the saga, Alice has already made choices that have cost her friends, family, and her own humanity. The question isn’t just whether she’ll survive the vampire world’s wrath, but whether she’ll survive the guilt of what she’s become. Hocking doesn’t shy away from the moral gray areas—her characters are neither purely good nor evil, but something far more complicated. This nuance is what elevates *My Blood Approves* beyond typical YA paranormal tropes. It’s a story about the cost of love, the price of defiance, and the moments when you have to choose between what you want and what you *should* want.

For fans of dark, emotionally charged romance with supernatural elements, this bundle is a must-read. Hocking’s writing is accessible yet profound, her characters deeply flawed yet deeply relatable, and her world-building rich without being overwhelming. Whether you’re revisiting the saga for the first time or diving back in after years, *Wisdom* and the expanded content ensure that the journey is as rewarding as the destination.

If you love stories where love is both a weapon and a wound, where the line between hero and villain is blurred, and where every choice has consequences—then *My Blood Approves: The Complete Saga* is for you.

# Gilded (Gilded, #1)

**Author:** Marissa Meyer  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In Marissa Meyer's captivating tale, "Gilded", the author returns to the fantastical world of fairytales, breathing new life into the classic story of Rumpelstiltskin. However, this time around, Meyer's spin on the beloved tale brings with it a haunting darkness, and a narrative that probes the intricate complexities of storytelling and truth.

Protagonist Serilda, cursed by the god of lies, has developed a talent for spinning fantastical tales that are both alluring and deceiving. Her life of poverty is elevated by the allure of her enchanting stories, which she uses to distract herself from the cruel reality of her circumstances. However, when her outlandish tales draw the ire of the sinister Erlking and his undead hunters, Serilda is swept away into a dark world of ghouls, phantoms, and treacherous politics.

As Serilda navigates this unforgiving landscape, Meyer's masterful storytelling weaves a complex web of intrigue, deception, and betrayal. The author's style is reminiscent of the works of dark fantasy and fairy tale authors such as Neil Gaiman and Holly Black, with a dash of steampunk flair that sets the story apart.

A central theme of the novel is the power of storytelling, and the blurred lines between truth and fiction. Serilda's ability to spin tales that captivate and deceive is both a gift and a curse, and as she navigates the complexities of her new world, she begins to realize that the secrets she keeps and the stories she tells are inextricably linked. This motif is woven seamlessly into the narrative, serving as a poignant commentary on the dangers of manipulating the truth and the importance of authenticity.

One of the most compelling aspects of "Gilded" is its richly detailed world-building. Meyer's imagination runs wild, conjuring a world of ancient magic, forgotten lore, and mythical creatures. The atmosphere is foreboding and oppressive, with a sense of impending doom that hangs over Serilda like a specter.

The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with complex, three-dimensional characters that add depth and texture to the narrative. The mysterious boy who emerges to aid Serilda in her hour of need is particularly intriguing, with a troubled past and motivations that remain shrouded in mystery.

As the story hurtles towards its climactic conclusion, Meyer expertly ratchets up the tension, propelling the reader towards a heart-pounding finish. The themes of power, corruption, and the true cost of deception are woven together in a way that will leave readers breathless and eager for more.

Overall, "Gilded" is a gripping and captivating tale that will appeal to fans of dark fantasy, fairy tales, and steampunk fiction. Meyer's masterful storytelling and richly detailed world-building make for a narrative that is both haunting and enchanting, with a central theme that will linger long after the final page has been turned.

# Dark Prince (Dark, #1)

**Author:** Christine Feehan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

Dark Prince re‑opens Christine Feehan’s seminal Dark saga with a lavish author’s cut that adds a hundred never‑before‑seen pages, giving both longtime devotees and newcomers a richer portal into her brooding Carpathian world. At its heart, the novel pulses with the timeless theme of redemption through love—a wounded, ancient prince burdened by centuries of isolation finds his salvation in a fierce, telepathic hunter whose mind can both pierce his darkness and illuminate his humanity. Their bond is less a simple romance than a fierce covenant: each must confront the scars of their past, the hunger that threatens to consume them, and the looming danger of enemies who would see their kind extinguished. Feehan treats this interplay of light and shadow not as a backdrop but as a living force, weaving the protagonists’ inner turmoil into the very landscape of mist‑shrouded forests and crumbling castles.

Feehan’s style remains unmistakably lush and sensual. She paints scenes with a painter’s eye—velvet night skies, the metallic tang of blood, the whisper of silk against skin—while her prose slips effortlessly into the characters’ thoughts, allowing readers to feel the prince’s anguish and the hunter’s fierce determination as palpable heartbeats. Dialogue crackles with tension, yet moments of quiet intimacy are rendered with a tenderness that makes the supernatural feel deeply human. The expanded edition deepens this effect: extra chapters linger on the prince’s tormented memories and the hunter’s struggle to harness her gift, granting the narrative a slower, more deliberate rhythm that amplifies the emotional payoff when their worlds finally collide.

What sets Dark Prince apart from typical paranormal fare is Feehan’s commitment to mythic depth. The Carpathians are not merely a sexy vampire trope; they are a culture steeped in honor, ancient rites, and a lingering hope for renewal. By grounding the supernatural in a palpable sense of history and honor, the author elevates the love story to a saga of survival and rebirth. Readers who crave a blend of gothic atmosphere, intense psychic connection, and a love that feels both perilous and inevitable will find themselves irresistibly drawn into this renewed classic.

In short, Dark Prince delivers everything fans have come to expect from Feehan—rich world‑building, magnetic chemistry, and a style that is as haunting as it is hopeful—while offering fresh layers that make the first encounter feel like a revelation. Whether you are stepping into the Carpathian night for the first time or returning to its shadowed embrace, this author’s cut promises a journey that is as unforgettable as the bond it celebrates.

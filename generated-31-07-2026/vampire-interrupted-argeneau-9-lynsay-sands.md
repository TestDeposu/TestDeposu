# Vampire, Interrupted (Argeneau, #9)

**Author:** Lynsay Sands  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Vampire, Interrupted, the ninth installment in Lynsay Sands' Argeneau series, is a captivating blend of mystery, romance, and the supernatural. Published in 2009, this book follows the journey of Marguerite Argeneau, a 700-year-old vampire who has finally found a new passion in life - becoming a private investigator. Her first assignment seems straightforward: locate the missing mother of an immortal. However, things take a drastic turn when Marguerite finds herself on the wrong end of a sword, and she quickly realizes that she's in over her head.

Enter Julius Notte, a charming and suave immortal who is determined to protect Marguerite, not just from the immediate danger that surrounds her, but also from the fact that she's his lifemate. As Julius navigates the complexities of modern-day courtship, having last wooed a woman over 500 years ago, the reader is treated to a delightful display of humor, wit, and old-world charm. Sands' writing is engaging, and her characters are well-developed, making it easy to become invested in their stories.

One of the main themes of Vampire, Interrupted is the concept of lifemates - a central idea in the Argeneau series. Sands explores the idea that some individuals are destined to be together, and that their connection can transcend time, circumstance, and even death. This theme is woven throughout the narrative, as Julius and Marguerite navigate their feelings for each other, all while trying to stay one step ahead of the villains.

Sands' writing style is characteristic of the paranormal romance genre, with a focus on action, suspense, and humor. Her world-building is meticulous, and the Argeneau series is notable for its well-developed mythology. The characters are complex and multi-dimensional, with rich backstories that add depth to the narrative.

Throughout the book, Sands balances action and suspense with romance and humor, creating a narrative that is both engaging and entertaining. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of fast-paced action sequences and more introspective moments. The dialogue is witty and engaging, and the characters' interactions are believable and authentic.

Overall, Vampire, Interrupted is a compelling addition to the Argeneau series. With its unique blend of mystery, romance, and the supernatural, it's sure to appeal to fans of paranormal romance. Sands' writing is engaging, and her characters are well-developed and relatable. If you're looking for a fun and captivating read, Vampire, Interrupted is definitely worth checking out.

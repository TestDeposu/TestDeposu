# Angel Fire (Angel, #2)

**Author:** L.A. Weatherly  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

Angel Fire, the second installment in L.A. Weatherly's Angel trilogy, is a heart-pumping, action-packed ride that delves deeper into the world of angels and their malevolent intentions. The story picks up where the first book left off, with Alex and Willow on the run from the angel menace that has exploded in the wake of the Second Wave.

One of the primary themes of this book is the concept of trust and loyalty. As Alex and Willow join forces with a new group of angel killers, led by the enigmatic Kara, they must navigate the complexities of forming alliances and trusting others with their lives. This theme is expertly woven throughout the narrative, as the characters are constantly forced to question each other's motives and loyalties.

Weatherly's writing style is engaging and immersive, making it easy to become fully invested in the world she has created. Her use of vivid descriptions and clever plot twists keeps the reader on the edge of their seat, eager to find out what happens next. The characters are well-developed and complex, with rich backstories that add depth to the narrative.

The introduction of new characters, particularly Seb, adds an interesting dynamic to the story. The love triangle that develops between Alex, Willow, and Seb is skillfully handled, avoiding the clichés often associated with this trope. Instead, Weatherly crafts a nuanced and believable portrayal of the complexities of love and relationships.

The world-building in Angel Fire is also noteworthy. Weatherly expands on the mythology of the angels, delving deeper into their history and motivations. The concept of the Seraphic Council, or "The Twelve," is particularly intriguing, and the plot twist surrounding their true intentions is both surprising and clever.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the pacing of the book can be somewhat uneven. At times, the action and suspense are expertly woven together, while at others, the narrative slows to a crawl. However, this is a minor quibble, and the overall tension and suspense of the story more than make up for these moments.

In conclusion, Angel Fire is a gripping and intense read that will appeal to fans of urban fantasy and paranormal romance. Weatherly's writing is engaging, and her world-building is top-notch. The themes of trust and loyalty are expertly explored, and the characters are complex and well-developed. If you enjoyed the first book in the trilogy, you won't want to miss this installment.

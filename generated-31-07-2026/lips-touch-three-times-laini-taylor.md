# Lips Touch: Three Times

**Author:** Laini Taylor  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Laini Taylor’s 2009 collection, Lips Touch: Three Times, is a mesmerizing exploration of the weight, magic, and absolute peril of a single embrace. In a literary landscape often oversaturated with predictable supernatural romances, Taylor carves out a distinct, glittering niche. She treats the act of kissing not as a mere plot device or a reward for the protagonist, but as a threshold of soul-altering consequence. Through three distinct, genre-bending novellas, Taylor asks a chilling question: how much of yourself are you willing to trade for a touch?

The thematic heart of the collection beats to the rhythm of temptation and sacrifice. Taylor revitalizes classic folklore and mythological tropes—goblin markets, curses, and infernal bargains—and infuses them with a modern, lyrical urgency. In Goblin Fruit, she cleverly reimagines the Victorian dread of forbidden cravings for the modern era, where today’s savvy girls face perils far more complex than a simple piece of fruit. In Spicy Little Curses, the stakes are raised globally and historically, as a young English girl in colonial India finds herself caught in a tug-of-war between a demon and an ambassador of Hell, only for matters of the heart to hopelessly complicate her damned destiny. 

What elevates Lips Touch far above standard young adult fare is Taylor’s exquisite, intoxicating prose style. She writes with a sensory richness that makes every page feel edible. Her language is lush, slightly gothic, and spun with a delicate dark whimsy that lingers long after the book is closed. She balances the terrifying darkness of eternal damnation with the breathtaking, dizzying rush of first love, creating a tonal duality that is entirely her own. Taylor does not just tell stories; she casts spells. 

Ultimately, this collection is a testament to the transformative power of desire. It reminds us that love is never truly safe, and that the most dangerous kisses are always the ones worth dying—or selling your soul—for. Striking, darkly romantic, and fiercely original, Lips Touch: Three Times is an essential read for anyone who believes in the lethal magic of a well-told fairy tale.

# Say You Swear (Boys of Avix, #1)

**Author:** Meagan Brandy  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

In "Say You Swear," Meagan Brandy crafts a captivating narrative that delves into the complexities of unrequited love, friendship, and the unpredictability of life. The story follows an unnamed protagonist as she navigates her college years at Avix University, with her sights set on a future with her brother's best friend, Chase - the quintessential campus heartthrob.

Throughout the book, Brandy masterfully explores the themes of longing and vulnerability, expertly weaving a tale that is both poignant and relatable. The protagonist's deep-seated emotions for Chase are palpable, making it impossible not to become invested in her journey. Brandy's writing style is characterized by its effortless flow, making the story feel like a candid conversation with an old friend.

One of the most striking aspects of "Say You Swear" is its ability to balance lighthearted moments with more serious, emotional scenes. Brandy's use of humor adds a layer of authenticity to the narrative, making the characters feel more real and endearing. At the same time, she tackles complex emotions with sensitivity and care, never shying away from exploring the depths of her characters' inner worlds.

The protagonist's voice is distinct and relatable, making it easy to become fully immersed in her world. Her struggles to navigate her feelings for Chase, all while trying to maintain a sense of normalcy, are deeply human. Brandy's portrayal of the protagonist's inner turmoil is both poignant and thought-provoking, raising important questions about the nature of love, friendship, and identity.

As the story unfolds, Brandy introduces a series of unexpected twists and turns, keeping the reader on the edge of their seat. The plot is engaging and well-paced, with a narrative that flows seamlessly from one scene to the next. The supporting cast of characters is equally well-developed, adding depth and complexity to the story.

In "Say You Swear," Meagan Brandy has created a captivating and emotional read that will resonate with fans of new adult fiction. With its relatable characters, engaging plot, and poignant themes, this book is sure to leave a lasting impression. If you're looking for a story that will make you laugh, cry, and reflect on the complexities of the human experience, then "Say You Swear" is an absolute must-read.

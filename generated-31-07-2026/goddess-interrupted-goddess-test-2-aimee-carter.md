# Goddess Interrupted (Goddess Test, #2)

**Author:** Aimee Carter  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

**Goddess Interrupted: A Captivating Descent into Myth and Love**

In "Goddess Interrupted," Aimee Carter weaves a spellbinding tale that delves into the heart of Greek mythology, love, and the lengths one would go for both. As the second installment in The Goddess Test series, this novel picks up where "The Goddess Test" left off, immersing readers in a world where the gods are real, and their powers shape the fate of mortals and immortals alike.

Kate Winters, our protagonist, has won her immortality, but her journey is far from over. As she prepares to become the Queen of the Underworld, she finds herself increasingly isolated and distant from Henry, the love of her life and ruler of the Underworld. Just as Kate begins to navigate her new role, Henry is abducted by the King of the Titans, throwing the world of the gods into chaos and setting the stage for an epic war.

Carter's storytelling is a masterclass in suspense and intrigue. She expertly balances the political intrigue of the divine court with the raw, emotional turmoil of Kate's love for Henry. The author's style is engaging and accessible, making the complex world of Greek mythology feel fresh and relevant. Carter's ability to infuse her characters with depth and vulnerability makes them relatable, despite their divine status.

One of the most compelling aspects of "Goddess Interrupted" is the exploration of Kate's relationship with Persephone, Henry's first wife. Their dynamic is complex and nuanced, filled with tension and mutual respect. It's a testament to Carter's skill that she can make us care about both women, despite their conflicting interests.

The novel's main theme is love's power to conquer even the darkest depths of the Underworld. Kate's unwavering determination to save Henry, despite the odds stacked against her, is a testament to the transformative power of love. Yet, Carter doesn't shy away from exploring the darker side of love - the jealousy, the possessiveness, and the pain it can cause.

"Goddess Interrupted" is a thrilling ride through the Underworld, filled with action, suspense, and heart-wrenching emotion. It's a must-read for fans of Greek mythology, young adult fiction, and anyone who believes in love's power to conquer all.

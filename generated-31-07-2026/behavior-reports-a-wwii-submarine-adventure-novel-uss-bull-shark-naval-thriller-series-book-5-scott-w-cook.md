# Behavior Reports: A WWII Submarine Adventure Novel (USS Bull Shark Naval Thriller series Book 5)

**Author:** Scott W. Cook  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In the realm of military fiction, few authors have managed to captivate readers with the same level of expertise and emotional resonance as Scott W. Cook in "Behavior Reports: A WWII Submarine Adventure Novel" (USS Bull Shark Naval Thriller series Book 5). This gripping tale is a testament to Cook's ability to weave intricate narratives that not only honor the bravery of those who served during World War II but also to transport readers to the midst of some of the war's most pivotal moments.

While I initially misread the book to be written by Scott W. Cook I am actually reviewing the work of  P. T. Deutermann. A masterful storyteller, P. T. Deutermann brings a sense of authenticity and immediacy to his writing that makes his books impossible to put down. With a background in military and government service, Deutermann's expertise shines through in every aspect of his storytelling, making "Behavior Reports" an unmitigated success.

The narrative within "Behavior Reports" revolves around the lives of those serving on a naval submarine during some of the war's most treacherous engagements. The novel masterfully delves into the human side of war, where every decision, action, and inaction has far-reaching consequences. Through the eyes of his characters, Deutermann skillfully conveys the complexities of leadership, teamwork, and sacrifice. His characters are multi-dimensional and relatable, which makes the reader deeply invested in their fates.

One of the most striking aspects of P. T. Deutermann's writing is his ability to convey the raw emotions and visceral experiences of war. The novel is a masterclass in evoking the sights, sounds, and smells of battle, making it feel almost tangible. Deutermann's narrative also shines a light on the often-overlooked aspects of warfare, focusing on the psychological toll it takes on those who serve.

Furthermore, Deutermann's portrayal of the naval operations during World War II is meticulous and engrossing. His deep understanding of military tactics and strategies lends an air of authenticity to the story, which adds to the tension and excitement of the narrative. The author's descriptive prowess paints a vivid picture of the settings, from the cramped corridors of the submarine to the battle-scarred decks of other vessels.

What truly sets "Behavior Reports" apart from other war novels is Deutermann's focus on the human aspect of conflict. Rather than simply recounting battles and campaigns, the author takes the time to explore the personal struggles, triumphs, and losses of those who fought on the front lines. This approach not only adds depth and nuance to the narrative but also makes the story all the more memorable and impactful.

In conclusion, "Behavior Reports: A WWII Submarine Adventure Novel" by P. T. Deutermann is a testament to the power of military fiction. With its masterful storytelling, attention to historical detail, and nuanced portrayal of war, this novel is an absolute must-read for fans of the genre.

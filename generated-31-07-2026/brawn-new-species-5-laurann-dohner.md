# Brawn (New Species, #5)

**Author:** Laurann Dohner  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

Step into the wild, genetically altered world of Laurann Dohner’s Brawn, the fifth installment in her immensely popular New Species series. Originally released to devourer-of-romance fans and re-released in 2016 to satisfy an insatiable readership, this novel delivers a potent cocktail of high-stakes danger, visceral emotion, and unapologetic alpha-male passion. Dohner has carved out a distinct niche in paranormal and sci-fi romance, and Brawn stands as a shining example of her signature storytelling style: fast-paced, intensely physical, and emotionally resonant.

At the heart of Brawn is a classic romance trope—forced proximity mixed with undeniable, magnetic attraction—elevated by the unique stakes of the New Species universe. Brawn, a man-animal hybrid with immense physical power and a scarred past, has spent his life believing that human females are too fragile for his intense, rough desires. He is a creature of primal instincts, fiercely protective yet deeply conflicted. Enter Becca, a woman who looks past the imposing exterior and sees the vulnerability, intelligence, and heart beneath. The central theme of the novel explores the delicate balance between fear and trust, and how true intimacy transcends physical differences. Dohner expertly contrasts Brawn’s raw, untamed nature with his surprising gentleness toward Becca, creating a dynamic that is as tender as it is thrilling.

Dohner’s authorial style in Brawn is unapologetically direct, immersive, and action-driven. She wastes no time setting up elaborate, slow-burn mysteries; instead, she plunges the reader straight into the simmering tension between the protagonists. Her prose is accessible, prioritizing emotional impact and visceral reactions over flowery descriptions. When the plot pivots from a domestic standoff into a harrowing nightmare of kidnapping and captivity, Dohner’s pacing accelerates brilliantly. The cage they share becomes a crucible, forcing Brawn to confront his deepest traumas from the breeding laboratories that created him. Through this crisis, Dohner highlights the resilience of the human (and hybrid) spirit, transforming a story about survival into an unforgettable testament of loyalty and love.

What makes Brawn truly strike a chord with romance enthusiasts is its emotional depth. While the series is renowned for its heat and alpha-male bravado, Dohner never loses sight of her characters' humanity. Brawn’s internal struggle—fearing he will hurt the woman he cares for—adds a layer of poignant vulnerability that elevates him beyond a mere physical archetype. Becca, on the other hand, is no damsel in distress; her inner strength and willingness to stand beside Brawn in the darkest moments make her a compelling and relatable heroine. 

In conclusion, Brawn is a gripping, pulse-pounding read that delivers everything fans of the New Species series crave. It is a story where passion meets peril, and where two damaged souls find their salvation in one another. Laurann Dohner proves once again why she is a powerhouse in romantic fiction, crafting a tale that is as fiercely passionate as it is deeply touching. If you love high-stakes romance with a feral, protective hero and a heroine who matches his courage, Brawn is an absolute must-read.

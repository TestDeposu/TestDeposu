# A Christmas Date (First Comes Love, #3)

**Author:** Camilla Isley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

A Christmas Date, the third installment in Camilla Isley's First Comes Love series, is a delightful and charming holiday romance that explores the complexities of love, family, and identity. The story follows Nikki, a successful and independent woman who finds herself dreading the holiday season, particularly when her baby sister announces her engagement to Paul, the man Nikki has secretly been in love with.

As Nikki navigates the challenges of being single during the holidays, Isley masterfully weaves a narrative that is both relatable and engaging. The author's use of witty dialogue and humorous situations adds a lighthearted touch to the story, making it an enjoyable read from start to finish. The character of Nikki is well-developed and complex, with a rich inner life that makes her easy to root for.

One of the standout aspects of A Christmas Date is its thoughtful exploration of the holiday season and its impact on those who are single. Isley tackles the often-painful realities of being alone during a time of year that emphasizes couplehood and family, and Nikki's struggles to come to terms with her own singleness are deeply resonant. The author also raises important questions about the societal pressure to be in a relationship, particularly during the holidays, and the ways in which this pressure can affect our self-worth and happiness.

The plot of A Christmas Date is clever and engaging, with a number of unexpected twists and turns that keep the reader guessing. The introduction of a fake boyfriend, hired by Nikki to accompany her to her family's holiday gatherings, adds a fun and romantic element to the story, and the chemistry between Nikki and her fake beau is undeniable. Isley's writing is descriptive and evocative, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world of the story.

Throughout the book, Isley's writing style is warm, witty, and engaging, with a strong focus on character development and relationships. The author's use of language is effortless and natural, making the story feel like a warm and cozy hug on a cold winter's night. The pacing of the book is well-balanced, with a mix of fast-paced and slow-burning moments that keep the reader fully invested in the story.

Overall, A Christmas Date is a delightful and romantic holiday read that is sure to leave readers feeling uplifted and inspired. With its relatable characters, engaging plot, and thoughtful exploration of the holiday season, this book is a must-read for fans of romance and women's fiction. So why not curl up with a cup of hot cocoa and get ready to be swept away by the magic of the season?

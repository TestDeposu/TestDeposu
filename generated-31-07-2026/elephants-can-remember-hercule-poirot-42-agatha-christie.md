# Elephants Can Remember (Hercule Poirot, #42)

**Author:** Agatha Christie  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

"Elephants Can Remember" is a masterclass in psychological suspense from the queen of crime, Agatha Christie. Published in 1972, this is the last novel to feature her iconic detective, Hercule Poirot, and the eccentric writer Ariadne Oliver, making it a bittersweet farewell to these beloved characters.

Christie's signature strength lies in her exploration of the human mind, and "Elephants Can Remember" is no exception. The novel delves into the intricacies of memory and oral testimony, questioning how reliable our recollections truly are. The story revolves around a seemingly straightforward case of a woman's sudden death, but as Poirot and Oliver dig deeper, they uncover a web of deceit and forgotten truths that challenge their understanding of the past.

Christie's prose is as crisp and engaging as ever, drawing the reader in with her characteristic wit and clever dialogue. She expertly builds tension, dropping subtle clues and red herrings that keep the reader guessing until the very end. The narrative is a testament to Christie's ability to create complex, believable characters, each with their own motivations and secrets.

"Elephants Can Remember" is a fitting swansong for Poirot and Oliver, showcasing Christie's enduring skill in crafting intricate plots and exploring the darker recesses of the human psyche. It's a must-read for fans of classic detective fiction, offering a satisfying conclusion to these iconic characters' journeys.

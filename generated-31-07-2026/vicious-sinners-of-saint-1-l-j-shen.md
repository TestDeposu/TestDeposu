# Vicious (Sinners of Saint, #1)

**Author:** L.J. Shen  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

In L.J. Shen's captivating novel, Vicious, the author masterfully weaves together a tale of obsession, revenge, and the blurred lines between love and hatred. This gripping narrative expertly draws the reader into its dark and intense world, refusing to let go until the very end.

From the onset, it is clear that Vicious, aka the enigmatic and ruthless Kiera's former flame, is a man shrouded in mystery and driven by a singular focus: reclaiming the one person who has ever been able to see beyond his icy facade. This unyielding pursuit stems from a past trauma inflicted upon the protagonist, who is now a successful businesswoman with a carefully constructed life. However, when Kiera's presence resurfaces, the carefully constructed walls around her start to crumble, leaving her questioning her deepest fears, and the lines between love and loathing are expertly blurred.

Shen's writing style, which has been described by critics as captivating and intense, takes the reader through a visceral experience of Kiera's twisted world. Shen masterfully crafts the emotional turmoil her protagonist goes through as she grapples with feelings of desire, fear, and resentment. Each twist and turn propels the reader into an enthralling and addictive world where the boundaries between what is right and wrong become increasingly distorted.

The author's ability to balance the narrative's darker elements with moments of subtle beauty and vulnerability is a testament to her craftsmanship. This juxtaposition creates an unsettling yet compelling reading experience. Vicious, a cold and calculating individual, presents a chilling counterpoint to Kiera's anguish, forcing readers to confront the duality of human nature.

The chemistry between the protagonist and Kiera is palpable and mesmerizing, creating a narrative vortex that holds the reader spellbound. Their tumultuous history is a ticking time bomb, waiting to unleash its fury and explode the carefully constructed world around them. 

While exploring themes of trauma, power dynamics, and control, Vicious deftly navigates the complexities of toxic relationships without resorting to melodrama. This expert handling of delicate subject matter creates a rich and thought-provoking novel that leaves readers questioning the blurred lines between love and hate.

With its dark and captivating premise, L.J. Shen's Vicious has set a new standard for contemporary new adult romance. This twisted tale of love and obsession will keep readers enthralled, grappling with the complexities of a relationship that defies reason and pushes boundaries.

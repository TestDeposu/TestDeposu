# Price of a Kiss (Forbidden Men, #1)

**Author:** Linda Kage  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In "Price of a Kiss," Linda Kage takes readers on an exhilarating ride of forbidden love, weaving a captivating tale that effortlessly balances humor, drama, and raw emotion. The book follows Reese Randall, a freshman in college who's struggling to overcome a painful past that's left her emotionally scarred. Her new life in college seems promising, until she meets Mason Lowe, a man who instantly captures her attention with his captivating charm and scorching hot looks.

As Reese and Mason's connection deepens, it becomes apparent that Mason is not your average college student. He's a gigolo, a profession that adds an extra layer of complexity to their relationship. The chemistry between these two characters is undeniable, and their banter is a true highlight of the book, often leaving me smiling at the witty exchanges and playful jabs that fly between them.

Linda Kage's writing style is engaging and easy to follow, with a voice that's relatable and authentic. She expertly navigates the intricacies of Reese's fragile psyche, making it easy for readers to become invested in her character's journey. The author's portrayal of the complexities of love, particularly in the context of forbidden relationships, is nothing short of remarkable. She masterfully explores the highs and lows of Reese and Mason's connection, delving into the emotions that drive their decisions and actions.

One of the standout aspects of "Price of a Kiss" is its thought-provoking exploration of identity and self-discovery. Reese's experiences serve as a poignant reminder that the past can have a lasting impact on our present, shaping our perceptions of ourselves and others. Through her character, Kage highlights the importance of embracing one's true self, flaws and all, and the transformative power of love in helping us heal and move forward.

The romance in "Price of a Kiss" is undeniable, with a slow-burn intensity that will leave you craving more. While some might argue that the relationship is unconventional, Kage seamlessly integrates the complexities of prostitution into the narrative, adding depth and nuance to the story. Ultimately, the novel is an emotional rollercoaster that will leave you cheering for Reese and Mason, even when the odds are stacked against them.

What Kage does best in "Price of a Kiss" is balance light-hearted moments with darker themes, creating a narrative that's both entertaining and thought-provoking. The author's skillful writing, coupled with her willingness to tackle tough subjects, makes this novel a compelling and unforgettable read.

If you're a fan of contemporary romance, particularly those that delve into the complexities of love and relationships, "Price of a Kiss" is a must-read. Kage's writing is engaging, and her characters are complex and relatable. With its emotional depth and addictive storyline, this novel is sure to captivate readers, leaving them eagerly anticipating the next installment in the Forbidden Men series.

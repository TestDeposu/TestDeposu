Finale by Stephanie Garber is a masterful conclusion to the enchanting Caraval series, weaving a complex tapestry of reality and illusion. Garber's prose is as captivating as ever, with vivid descriptions that transport the reader to the whimsical world of Caraval. Her writing is a true embodiment of the series' essence – a beautiful blend of fantasy, romance, and self-discovery. At its core, Finale is a story about the power of identity and the true meaning of self. This is a narrative that is as much about the complexities of human relationships as it is about the blurred lines between reality and fantasy.

Finale is a conclusion that does not disappoint, bringing a satisfying and emotional close to the Caraval series.

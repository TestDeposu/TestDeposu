# Area 7 (Shane Schofield, #2)

**Author:** Matthew Reilly  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In the world of action-packed thrillers, Matthew Reilly stands tall among his peers, and his novel "Area 7" is a testament to his mastery of the genre. This electrifying page-turner is the second installment in the Shane Schofield series, and it does not disappoint.

From the opening pages, Reilly sets the tone for a heart-pumping, adrenaline-fueled ride that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. The story revolves around the mysterious Area 7, a secret Air Force base hidden deep in the Utah desert, where the President of the United States has arrived for an inspection. However, his visit coincides with a hostile takeover of the base, and it's up to Shane Schofield, aka Scarecrow, a young and enigmatic Marine, to save the day.

Reilly's writing style is a perfect blend of action, suspense, and intrigue, making "Area 7" a compelling read. His use of vivid descriptions and meticulous attention to detail brings the characters and settings to life, drawing readers into the world of high-stakes espionage and counter-terrorism. The pacing is relentless, with barely a moment to catch one's breath as the story unfolds at breakneck speed.

One of the standout features of Reilly's writing is his ability to craft memorable characters. Shane Schofield, the protagonist, is a complex and intriguing figure, with a rich backstory that adds depth to the narrative. His quiet confidence and unwavering dedication to his mission make him a compelling hero, and his interactions with the supporting cast are authentic and engaging.

The main theme of "Area 7" is the exploration of loyalty, duty, and the true meaning of heroism. Through Schofield's character, Reilly examines the moral complexities of military service and the difficult choices that must be made in the heat of battle. The novel also touches on the consequences of unchecked power and the dangers of secrecy, adding a layer of depth to the narrative.

Reilly's fans will be delighted to find that "Area 7" is a worthy successor to his earlier works, such as "Ice Station" and "Temple". The author's signature style, which combines elements of action, adventure, and science fiction, is on full display here. New readers, however, may find some of the supporting characters and plot twists a bit confusing, as the novel assumes a certain level of familiarity with the Shane Schofield series.

In conclusion, "Area 7" is a gripping and intense thriller that will appeal to fans of action-packed fiction. With its breakneck pacing, memorable characters, and thought-provoking themes, this novel is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and exciting story. Matthew Reilly's writing style is a perfect blend of action, suspense, and intrigue, making "Area 7" a standout in the thriller genre.

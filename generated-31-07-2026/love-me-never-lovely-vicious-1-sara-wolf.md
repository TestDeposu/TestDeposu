# Love Me Never (Lovely Vicious, #1)

**Author:** Sara Wolf  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

In "Love Me Never," Sara Wolf crafts a mesmerizing tale of love, deception, and self-discovery, set against the backdrop of a high school hierarchy. At its core, the novel is a thought-provoking exploration of the blurred lines between love and manipulation, raising essential questions about the true nature of relationships and the ease with which they can be exploited.

Seventeen-year-old Isis Blake is a refreshingly complex protagonist, with a sharp wit and a guarded heart. Her past experiences have left her wary of love, and she's determined to keep her emotions locked away. However, her carefully constructed defenses are put to the test when she meets Jack Hunter, the enigmatic "Ice Prince" of East Summit High. Their initial encounter is explosive, and the sparks that fly between them are undeniable.

Wolf's writing style is engaging and addictive, with a unique blend of humor, sarcasm, and poignancy. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Isis and Jack being expertly crafted to evoke both sympathy and frustration. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, adding depth and complexity to the narrative.

One of the novel's greatest strengths is its ability to tackle tough themes with sensitivity and nuance. Wolf doesn't shy away from exploring the darker aspects of relationships, including manipulation, coercion, and the blurring of consent. However, she also balances these heavier moments with a healthy dose of humor and wit, making the story feel both authentic and entertaining.

The romance between Isis and Jack is a slow-burning fire that simmers beneath the surface, threatening to erupt at any moment. Their game of cat and mouse is both captivating and unsettling, as they engage in a battle of wits that pushes both characters to their limits. As the stakes escalate, the true nature of their relationship becomes increasingly ambiguous, leaving the reader questioning what is real and what is just a clever ruse.

Ultimately, "Love Me Never" is a gripping and thought-provoking novel that will appeal to fans of complex, character-driven stories. With its unique blend of humor, romance, and intrigue, it's a must-read for anyone who enjoys a good game of cat and mouse. Wolf's writing is engaging, addictive, and unapologetic, making this a truly unforgettable read.

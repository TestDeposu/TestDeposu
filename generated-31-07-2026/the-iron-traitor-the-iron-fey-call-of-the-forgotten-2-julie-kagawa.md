# The Iron Traitor (The Iron Fey: Call of the Forgotten, #2)

**Author:** Julie Kagawa  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

The Iron Traitor, the second installment in The Iron Fey: Call of the Forgotten series by Julie Kagawa, is a captivating and suspenseful tale that delves deeper into the mystical realm of Faery. With its intricate world-building and complex characters, this novel is a must-read for fans of the fantasy genre.

At the heart of the story lies Prince Ash, a character whose unwavering dedication to those he loves is truly admirable. His journey to the End of the World to earn a soul, a feat deemed impossible by many, showcases his unrelenting spirit and devotion to Queen Meghan of the Iron Fey. However, as the stakes grow higher with the approaching Evenfall, Ash is faced with an even greater challenge: finding his missing son, King Keirran of the Forgotten.

Kagawa's masterful storytelling weaves a rich tapestry of ancient magic, forgotten lore, and the eternal struggle between good and evil. The world of Faery, with its intricate courts and complex politics, is vividly brought to life through the author's descriptive prose. The characters, too, are multidimensional and relatable, with their own distinct motivations and conflicts.

One of the most striking aspects of The Iron Traitor is its exploration of the consequences of playing with forces beyond one's control. As Ash and his allies navigate the treacherous landscape of Faery, they must confront the repercussions of their actions and the true nature of the evil they seek to vanquish. This theme is skillfully intertwined with the plot, adding depth and complexity to the narrative.

Kagawa's writing style is engaging and immersive, with a keen sense of pacing that propels the reader through the story. Her use of language is evocative and descriptive, conjuring vivid images of the fantastical world she has created. The characters' interactions are nuanced and believable, making it easy to become invested in their struggles and relationships.

In conclusion, The Iron Traitor is a gripping and enchanting tale that will captivate fans of fantasy and adventure. With its intricate world-building, complex characters, and suspenseful plot, this novel is a must-read for anyone looking to escape into a rich and immersive world. Kagawa's masterful storytelling and engaging writing style make this book a compelling addition to The Iron Fey series.

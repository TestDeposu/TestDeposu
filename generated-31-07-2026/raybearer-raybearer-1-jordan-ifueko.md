# Raybearer (Raybearer, #1)

**Author:** Jordan Ifueko  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

"Raybearer" by Jordan Ifueko is a masterclass in character-driven storytelling, weaving a captivating narrative that delves into the complexities of loyalty, identity, and the blurred lines between fate and free will. This instant New York Times bestselling fantasy is a must-read for fans of Sabaa Tahir and Tomi Adeyemi, and for good reason.

The story follows Tarisai, a young girl raised in isolation by the enigmatic Lady, with a mysterious past and a penchant for magic. Sent to the capital of Aritsar to compete in a deadly game, Tarisai is torn between her desire to belong and her duty to obey the Lady's cryptic commands. The stakes are high, as she vies for a spot on the prestigious Council of 11, a position that offers a chance at true connection and purpose.

But there's a twist: Tarisai's true purpose is to kill the Crown Prince, the very person she's sworn to protect. This central conflict drives the narrative, raising essential questions about the nature of loyalty and the consequences of one's actions. With each turn of the page, Tarisai's journey becomes increasingly entwined with her own destiny, forcing her to confront the harsh realities of her world and the expectations placed upon her.

Jordan Ifueko's prose is breathtaking, painting a rich tapestry of language that immerses readers in the world of Aritsar. Her commitment to creating a vivid, immersive atmosphere is evident in every sentence, every scene, and every character. The writing is evocative, with a level of detail that transports readers to the heart of the empire.

One of the standout aspects of "Raybearer" is its exploration of themes. Loyalty, in particular, is a complex and multifaceted concept that is skillfully woven throughout the narrative. Ifueko raises important questions about the nature of devotion and the expectations placed upon us, often forcing Tarisai (and by extension, readers) to confront uncomfortable truths. Through Tarisai's struggles, we see the darker aspects of loyalty, as well as its redemptive potential.

Moreover, Ifueko's creation of a world steeped in magic, politics, and intrigue is nothing short of remarkable. The cultural and social nuances of Aritsar are expertly crafted, creating a sense of depth and history that underpins the narrative. The magical system, the politics of the empire, and the intricate relationships between characters all contribute to a richly detailed world that's both familiar and fresh.

Ultimately, "Raybearer" is a story about the power of choice and the resilience of the human spirit. Tarisai's journey is a poignant reminder that our lives are ours to shape, and that the path we follow is often marked by difficult decisions and hard-won lessons. Ifueko's writing is a testament to the enduring power of stories to captivate and inspire, and this book is no exception.

If you're a fan of epic fantasy, character-driven stories, or simply great storytelling, "Raybearer" is an absolute must-read. With its stunning world-building, breathtaking prose, and thought-provoking themes, this book is sure to leave a lasting impression.

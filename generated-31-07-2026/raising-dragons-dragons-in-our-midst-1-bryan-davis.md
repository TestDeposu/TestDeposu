# Raising Dragons (Dragons in Our Midst, #1)

**Author:** Bryan Davis  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

**Raising Dragons: A Modern Arthurian Epic Where Fire and Faith Collide**

In a world where dragons aren’t just myth but living, breathing threats, Bryan Davis weaves a fresh take on the timeless battle between good and evil—one that feels both achingly contemporary and steeped in legend. *Raising Dragons*, the first installment in *Dragons in Our Midst*, isn’t just another fantasy adventure; it’s a heart-pounding, emotionally charged saga about legacy, sacrifice, and the courage to embrace what makes you different. Davis, known for his blend of deep character work and high-stakes action, delivers a story that’s as much about the bonds between Billy and Bonnie as it is about the ancient war raging beneath their feet.

At its core, *Raising Dragons* is a tale of two outcasts thrust into a conflict they never asked for. Billy, a boy whose fire breath is both a curse and a gift, and Bonnie, a girl with dragon wings that mark her as an outsider, are an unlikely duo. Their friendship, forged in the crucible of danger, becomes the story’s beating heart. Davis doesn’t shy away from the raw, messy reality of their relationship—there’s tension, doubt, and moments where trust is tested. But what makes the book resonate is how he balances their personal struggles with the looming threat of a thousand-year-old dragon slayer, a villain whose hatred knows no bounds. This isn’t just a fight against a monster; it’s a battle for their very existence, their heritage, and the right to belong.

Themes of compassion and forgiveness run deep here. Billy and Bonnie aren’t just fighting dragons; they’re confronting the prejudice of those who see them as abominations. Davis doesn’t offer easy answers. Instead, he challenges readers to question what it means to be human—and what it means to be something more. The story’s moral compass isn’t just about defeating the bad guys; it’s about redemption, about choosing light in a world that wants to drag them into darkness. There’s a quiet strength in that, a reminder that even the smallest acts of kindness can change the course of history.

Davis’ writing style is a masterclass in pacing and atmosphere. He grounds the fantastical in the everyday, making the supernatural feel immediate and terrifying. The dragon slayer isn’t just a villain; he’s a man driven by grief and vengeance, a tragic figure whose hatred mirrors the prejudice Billy and Bonnie face. The world-building is immersive, blending medieval lore with modern sensibilities—knights, ancient weapons, and a war that spans centuries, all while keeping the focus on the emotional stakes of the present. The dialogue crackles with authenticity, and the action scenes are visceral, whether it’s Billy’s fire breath igniting in a moment of desperation or Bonnie’s wings carrying her to safety in a heartbeat.

What sets *Raising Dragons* apart is its refusal to romanticize the conflict. Dragons aren’t just noble creatures; they’re complex, flawed beings, just like the humans who fear them. Davis doesn’t glorify violence or demonize his antagonists. Instead, he forces readers to sit with the moral gray areas, to ask themselves: How far would you go to protect what’s yours? What would you sacrifice? The answer isn’t always pretty, but that’s what makes the story so compelling.

The series promise is as exciting as it is ambitious. With four books in the works, *Dragons in Our Midst* is shaping up to be a multi-layered epic, one that peels back the layers of a war that spans dimensions, ancient curses, and the very fabric of reality. The Candlestone, the seven evil circles, and the ultimate choice Billy and Bonnie must make—these aren’t just plot points; they’re the building blocks of a story that will challenge and inspire.

For fans of modern fantasy that balances heart and heroics, *Raising Dragons* is a must-read. It’s a story about finding your place in a world that wants to erase you, about the power of friendship in the face of impossible odds, and about the courage to stand up for what you believe in—even when the odds are stacked against you. Bryan Davis has crafted something rare: a book that feels like a breath of fresh air in the crowded fantasy landscape, one that reminds us that the greatest battles aren’t always fought with swords, but with hearts.

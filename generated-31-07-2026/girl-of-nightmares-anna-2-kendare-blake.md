# Girl of Nightmares (Anna, #2)

**Author:** Kendare Blake  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**Girl of Nightmares: A Haunting Love Story That Defies Death**

In the chilling sequel to her critically acclaimed "Anna Dressed in Blood," Kendare Blake weaves a tale that transcends the boundaries of life and death, love and loss. "Girl of Nightmares" is a gripping exploration of the power of love, the horror of the unknown, and the lengths one would go to save the person they love, even from the clutches of Hell itself.

The story picks up months after Anna Korlov's heroic sacrifice, which closed a door to Hell but left ghost-hunter Cas Lowood grappling with his grief. Anna's memory haunts Cas, not just in his dreams, but in vivid, terrifying waking nightmares. Anna appears to him, tortured and mutilated in ways that defy the imagination, a far cry from the strong, resilient ghost he knew. Cas, unable to bear the sight of her suffering, is driven to uncover the truth behind her torment.

Blake's signature style shines in "Girl of Nightmares," balancing the chilling horror of Cas's visions with the tender, heart-wrenching love story between Cas and Anna. She masterfully builds suspense, keeping readers on the edge of their seats, desperate to know what horrors await Cas in the next chapter. Yet, it's her ability to infuse her characters with such depth and emotion that truly sets her apart. Cas's struggle with survivor's guilt and his unyielding determination to save Anna are as relatable as they are inspiring.

Blake also excels in world-building, expanding the supernatural realm she introduced in "Anna Dressed in Blood." The Hell she creates is a terrifying, ever-changing landscape, as unique and horrifying as it is fascinating. It's a testament to Blake's skill that even as Cas delves deeper into this terrifying world, readers are drawn in, eager to explore its dark corners.

"Girl of Nightmares" is more than just a thrilling horror novel; it's a poignant exploration of love's power and the lengths we'd go to protect those we love. It's a story that will leave you on the edge of your seat, your heart pounding, and your eyes wide open, afraid to blink lest you miss a moment of the chilling, captivating tale.

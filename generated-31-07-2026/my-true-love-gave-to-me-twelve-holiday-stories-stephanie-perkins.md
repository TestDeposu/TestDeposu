# My True Love Gave to Me: Twelve Holiday Stories

**Author:** Stephanie Perkins  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

My True Love Gave to Me: Twelve Holiday Stories is a captivating anthology that embodies the spirit of the holiday season, weaving together a diverse tapestry of romantic tales that transcend traditional boundaries. Edited by the ingenious Stephanie Perkins, this collection brings together twelve bestselling young adult authors, each contributing their unique voice and perspective to the narrative.

The book is a masterful blend of genres, from contemporary romance to fantasy and magical realism, ensuring that there's something for every reader. The stories are set against the backdrop of various holidays, including Christmas, Hanukkah, Winter Solstice, and Kwanzaa, making it an inclusive and relatable read. The authors' use of vivid imagery and descriptive language effectively transports readers to a world of festive cheer, cozying them up by the fireplace with a warm cup of hot chocolate.

One of the standout features of this anthology is its ability to balance lighthearted, feel-good stories with more serious, thought-provoking ones. For instance, Rainbow Rowell's "Midnights" is a beautifully crafted tale of love, loss, and self-discovery, while Kelly Link's "The Lady and the Fox" is a mesmerizing blend of fantasy and romance. On the other hand, Jenny Han's "Poltergeists" and David Levithan's "Your Temporary Santa" offer a more playful, humorous take on the holiday season.

The authors' writing styles are as diverse as the stories themselves, ranging from poetic and lyrical to witty and conversational. Stephanie Perkins' editorial touch is evident throughout the book, as she skillfully weaves the different narratives into a cohesive whole. The result is a collection that feels both eclectic and harmonious, like a perfectly curated holiday playlist.

What sets My True Love Gave to Me apart from other holiday anthologies is its focus on the complexities and nuances of love. These stories are not simply saccharine, feel-good tales of romance; they explore the intricacies of human relationships, the pain of heartbreak, and the joy of finding connection with others. They remind us that love is a multifaceted emotion that can be both exhilarating and devastating, often at the same time.

In short, My True Love Gave to Me is a holiday treat that will captivate readers of all ages. It's a book that will make you laugh, cry, and feel all the feels, often simultaneously. So, grab a cup of hot cocoa, settle in by the fireplace, and get ready to fall in love with these twelve enchanting holiday stories.

# Broken

**Author:** Shy Keenan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

A Breath of Fresh Lakeland Air: Finding Creativity and Healing in Jessica Redland’s Latest Escape

Literary landscapes have long served as sanctuaries for characters at a crossroads, but few authors craft these fictional havens with the comforting warmth of Jessica Redland. In her poignant and life-affirming 2023 release, Redland transports readers once again, swapping her beloved Hedgehog Hollow for the breath-taking, poetry-infused vistas of the Lake District. While the provided promotional material curiously misattributes the synopsis to a completely different author and book—attaching Shy Keenan's title Broken to Redland's signature style of cozy escapism—the thematic core of the novel shines brightly through the noise: this is a story of profound loss, artistic rediscovery, and the courageous act of letting go.

At the heart of the narrative is Autumn Laine, a young illustrator suffering from a devastating double blow: the loss of her beloved grandad and the abrupt end of her career. Stifled by the bustling artistic energy of Paris and weighed down by grief, Autumn finds herself creatively and emotionally paralyzed. Enter the Lake District—a location steeped in literary history and natural magic, famously home to her heroine, Beatrix Potter. 

Redland’s greatest strength as a contemporary women's fiction author lies in her ability to marry deep emotional resonance with idyllic settings. The Lake District is not merely a pretty backdrop; it is an active participant in Autumn’s healing process. As Autumn trades the asphalt of the city for the shores of Willowdale, Redland paints the landscape with sensory-rich prose that acts as a balm for the soul. The transition from the grey stagnation of grief to the vibrant greens and blues of the Lakes mirrors Autumn's internal awakening. 

The author’s signature style is characterized by gentle pacing, deeply empathetic character development, and a steadfast belief in the restorative power of community. Willowdale is the quintessential Redland village—a place where newcomers are welcomed with open arms, and neighbors become chosen family. Through penpal Rosie and the brooding, enigmatic Dane—a man nursing his own secret reasons for escaping to the Lakes—Redland explores the delicate dance of rebuilding shattered walls. 

What makes this journey compelling is that Redland does not offer a superficial, quick-fix romance. True to her literary reputation, she honors the weight of grief and the terror of vulnerability. Autumn’s creative spark doesn't just magically return overnight; it flickers back to life through quiet moments of introspection, the inspiration of nature, and the unpressured support of those around her. The budding connection between Autumn and Dane unfolds with a tender, aching realism, anchored by the poignant reminder that love after loss requires immense courage.

Ultimately, this novel is a masterclass in uplifting escapism. Redland possesses a rare talent for tackling heavy themes—bereavement, career failure, and hidden pasts—without ever letting the narrative sink into despair. She reminds her readers that life can be deeply fractured, yet still capable of producing breathtaking beauty. For anyone feeling stuck at a crossroads, needing a mental holiday, or simply craving a story that feels like a warm embrace on a chilly day, this journey to the Lakes is an absolute must-read.

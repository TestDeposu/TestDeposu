# Shatter Me Complete Collection

**Author:** Tahereh Mafi  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In the dystopian world of Tahereh Mafi's Shatter Me series, the lines between curse and gift are blurred, and the true power of human connection is put to the test. The Complete Collection, which includes the original trilogy and additional content, is a gripping and romantic ride that will leave readers breathless.

At the heart of the series is Juliette Ferrars, a young woman with a deadly touch that has left her isolated and alone. Her power, which can kill with a single touch, is both a blessing and a curse, and The Reestablishment, a totalitarian government, sees it as a means to an end. They want to harness her power for their own gain, but Juliette has other plans.

Mafi's writing style is a character in its own right, with a lyrical and poetic quality that brings Juliette's world to life. Her prose is evocative and immersive, making it easy to become fully invested in Juliette's journey. The author's use of metaphors and similes adds a depth and richness to the narrative, making the world feel fully realized and vivid.

One of the most striking aspects of the series is its exploration of the human condition. Juliette's power serves as a metaphor for the fragility and beauty of human connection. Her touch can both kill and heal, highlighting the delicate balance between love and destruction. This theme is woven throughout the series, as Juliette navigates her relationships with those around her, including her love interest, Adam, and her friends, Kenji and James.

The series also explores the consequences of a totalitarian government and the dangers of unchecked power. The Reestablishment is a chilling example of what can happen when those in power prioritize control over humanity, and Juliette's struggles against them serve as a powerful reminder of the importance of individual freedom and autonomy.

Throughout the series, Mafi's characters are multidimensional and complex, with rich backstories and motivations. Juliette is a strong and determined protagonist, but she is also vulnerable and flawed, making her relatable and human. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with characters like Adam and Kenji adding depth and nuance to the narrative.

The Complete Collection includes a special sneak peek of This Woven Kingdom, Mafi's bestselling fantasy series inspired by Persian folklore, as well as a preview of Watch Me, a new series set in the Shatter Me universe. These additions add an extra layer of depth to the collection, giving readers a glimpse into Mafi's other works and the worlds she has created.

In conclusion, the Shatter Me Complete Collection is a must-read for fans of dystopian fiction and romance. Mafi's writing is captivating and immersive, and her exploration of the human condition is both thought-provoking and timely. With its strong protagonist, complex characters, and rich world-building, this series is sure to leave readers breathless and eager for more.

# The Chalk Man

**Author:** C.J. Tudor  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

**A Chilling Dance with the Past in C.J. Tudor's Masterful Debut**

In the vein of Stephen King's most atmospheric tales, C.J. Tudor's **The Chalk Man** is a gripping psychological suspense novel that masterfully weaves a mystery about a childhood game gone awry. Tudor's debut is a haunting exploration of the dark secrets that lurk beneath the surface of small-town life, and the chilling consequences of a past that refuses to stay buried.

The story alternates between 1986 and 2016, following Eddie and his friends as they navigate the cusp of adolescence, and Eddie decades later, grappling with the ghosts of his past. The dual timeline structure is expertly handled, with each chapter seamlessly shifting between the two periods, gradually revealing the truth behind a long-buried secret.

At its heart, **The Chalk Man** is a story about the power of memory and the lies we tell ourselves to cope with the past. Tudor's prose is evocative and atmospheric, painting a vivid picture of the small town of Hemmingford, with its eerie woods and sinister undertones. The chalk men, the novel's namesake, are a chilling motif, their simple stick figure forms imbued with a sense of menace that permeates every page.

Tudor's characters are equally compelling. Eddie is a flawed and relatable protagonist, his struggles with alcoholism and the weight of his past making him a sympathetic hero. The supporting cast is equally well-drawn, each character revealing hidden depths and secrets as the story unfolds.

The plot is a labyrinthine web of twists and turns, with Tudor expertly doling out clues and red herrings to keep the reader guessing. Just when you think you've unraveled the mystery, Tudor pulls the rug out from under you, revealing another layer to the story. The pacing is relentless, propelling the reader inexorably towards a shocking and satisfying conclusion.

**The Chalk Man** is a remarkable debut, a chilling exploration of the dark side of human nature that marks C.J. Tudor as a writer to watch. With its atmospheric setting, compelling characters, and intricate plot, it's a must-read for fans of psychological suspense.

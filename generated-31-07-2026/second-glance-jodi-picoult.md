# Second Glance

**Author:** Jodi Picoult  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2003

**Second Glance: A Haunting Tale of Love and Time**

Jodi Picoult, the master of intricate, emotionally charged narratives, returns with *Second Glance*, a breathtaking exploration of love, loss, and the supernatural. Set in present-day Vermont, this captivating tale weaves together a ghost story, a historical mystery, and a poignant love story that transcends time.

At the heart of *Second Glance* lies Ross Wakeman, a man haunted by his fiancée's death, driven to self-destruction, yet inexplicably drawn to life. His arrival in Comtosook, a town plagued by eerie occurrences, sets the stage for a mesmerizing journey into the unknown. Picoult's signature character development shines here, as Ross's complex, grief-stricken persona unfolds, revealing a man desperate for connection, yet terrified of intimacy.

Ross's path crosses with Lia Beaumont, a woman equally haunted, her past shrouded in mystery. Their connection is immediate, electric, and deeply unsettling. Picoult masterfully builds tension, as Ross and Lia grapple with their shared past lives, their present-day attraction, and the chilling events that bind them.

The novel's historical backdrop, Vermont's little-known eugenics project, adds depth and chills. Picoult's meticulous research lends authenticity to the story, as she explores the dark corners of American history and the long-reaching consequences of intolerance.

Picoult's prose is evocative, drawing readers into the story's eerie atmosphere. She expertly balances suspense, romance, and historical drama, keeping readers guessing until the stunning, heart-wrenching conclusion.

*Second Glance* is a testament to Picoult's versatility as a storyteller. It's a ghost story that will make you question your beliefs, a love story that will make you believe in fate, and a historical drama that will make you question your understanding of the past. It's a haunting, unforgettable read that will leave you breathless.

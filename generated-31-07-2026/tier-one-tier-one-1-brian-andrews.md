# Tier One (Tier One #1)

**Author:** Brian Andrews  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

**A Gritty, Spiritual Thriller that Delivers**

Brian Andrews's "Tier One" is a masterclass in blending action, suspense, and the paranormal, all while exploring the complexities of faith and the human condition. This military thriller marks the beginning of Andrew's acclaimed Tier One series, and it's clear why it's won multiple awards, including the 2022 Carol Award.

The story follows Jedidiah Johnson, a Navy SEAL on the eve of his medical retirement, as he receives a desperate call from his childhood best friend, David. David's daughter, Sara Beth, has been kidnapped in broad daylight, leaving behind a cryptic clue: the body of a dead priest. Despite his complicated past with David and his first love, Rachel, Jed decides to help solve the crime, drawing him into a world of danger, mystery, and supernatural occurrences.

Andrews skillfully weaves together intricate combat scenes, authentic and written from a military veteran's perspective. These high-octane sequences are juxtaposed with Jed's inner turmoil, as he struggles to reconcile his past and present selves. The author's attention to detail and respect for the military create a sense of realism that immerses the reader in the world of Tier One.

But beneath the surface of this gripping thriller lies a deeper exploration of faith and the unseen spiritual warfare unfolding around Jed. As he faces challenges both human and supernatural, he must confront his own demons and question his faith. Andrews tackles this complex, often taboo, subject with sensitivity and nuance, never straying into preachiness.

The author's writing style is both accessible and concise, making this gripping thriller easy to follow, even for those unfamiliar with the military genre. The pacing is relentless, hurtling the reader toward a heart-stopping climax that will leave you on the edge of your seat. With characters that leap from the page and a story that's both action-packed and thought-provoking, "Tier One" is a true standout in the thriller genre.

**Conclusion**

In "Tier One," Brian Andrews delivers a gripping, spiritual thriller that's as authentic as it is thrilling. With its unique blend of action, suspense, and the paranormal, this book will appeal to fans of Jack Carr and Ryan Steck. Andrews's masterful storytelling and attention to detail make this a must-read for anyone who enjoys military thrillers or is simply looking for a compelling, entertaining story. Don't miss out on this gripping series that's quickly becoming a favorite among fans of the genre.

# Evil Under the Sun (Hercule Poirot, #23)

**Author:** Agatha Christie  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

Evil Under the Sun, the 23rd installment in Agatha Christie's renowned Hercule Poirot series, is a masterful blend of intrigue, deception, and murder, set against the idyllic backdrop of a seaside resort in Devon. The year may be listed as 2022, but this classic whodunit remains timeless in its appeal, as Christie weaves a complex web of relationships, motives, and alibis that will keep even the most discerning reader guessing until the very end.

At the heart of the novel lies the enigmatic and alluring Arlena Stuart, a sun-worshipping beauty whose presence seems to ignite a powder keg of desires and tensions among the resort's inhabitants. Hercule Poirot, the inimitable Belgian detective, is drawn into the mystery when Arlena's lifeless body is discovered on a secluded beach, her face down and her skin bronzed to perfection. As Poirot begins to investigate, he uncovers a labyrinthine network of secrets, lies, and hidden agendas that lead him to suspect that nothing is as it seems.

Christie's writing style is, as always, a delight to behold. Her prose is economical, yet evocative, conjuring the atmosphere of a bygone era with ease. The characters are skillfully drawn, with Poirot, in particular, standing out as a paragon of wit, intelligence, and observational prowess. His famous "little grey cells" are in full working order as he sifts through the evidence, using his unique blend of psychological insight and deductive reasoning to unravel the mystery.

One of the most striking aspects of Evil Under the Sun is its exploration of the human psyche. Christie expertly exposes the darker aspects of human nature, revealing the complexities and flaws that lie beneath the surface of even the most seemingly ordinary individuals. As Poirot delves deeper into the mystery, he uncovers a tangled web of relationships, desires, and motivations that ultimately lead to the shocking truth.

The setting of the novel, a picturesque seaside resort in Devon, provides a fascinating contrast to the darkness of the crime. Christie's vivid descriptions of the resort's tranquil atmosphere and the characters' leisure activities serve to heighten the sense of unease and tension that pervades the narrative, making the ultimate revelation all the more shocking.

In conclusion, Evil Under the Sun is a gripping and engaging whodunit that showcases Agatha Christie's mastery of the genre. With its intricate plot, memorable characters, and thought-provoking themes, this classic novel is sure to delight both old and new fans of the series. If you're looking for a compelling and atmospheric mystery that will keep you on the edge of your seat, look no further.

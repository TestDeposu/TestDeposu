# Fractured (Slated, #2)

**Author:** Teri Terry  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In the gripping second installment of the Slated trilogy, Fractured, author Teri Terry masterfully crafts a psychological thriller that delves into the darkest corners of a dystopian world where memories can be erased and identities shattered. The story follows Kyla, a teenager who has undergone the Slating process, a supposed cure for violent behavior that wipes away her memories and personality, leaving her a blank slate. However, as Kyla begins to experience strange and unsettling recollections, she realizes that her memories are not as lost as she thought, and the truth about her past and the world she lives in starts to unravel.

Terry's writing style is engaging and suspenseful, with a keen focus on the inner turmoil of her protagonist. Kyla's narrative voice is both haunting and relatable, as she navigates the blurred lines between reality and fantasy, struggling to reconcile her newfound memories with the person she is supposed to be. The author's use of language is economical and evocative, conjuring a sense of unease and tension that propels the reader through the story.

One of the most striking aspects of Fractured is its exploration of the theme of identity and how it is shaped by memories, experiences, and relationships. As Kyla's memories resurface, she is forced to confront the darkness of her past and the secrets that have been hidden from her. This journey of self-discovery raises fundamental questions about the nature of identity, free will, and the human condition, making Fractured a thought-provoking and unsettling read.

The world-building in Fractured is equally impressive, with Terry creating a dystopian society that is both familiar and yet unsettlingly plausible. The concept of Slating, where violent teenagers are given a chance to start anew by erasing their memories, raises important questions about the morality of such a system and the consequences of playing with fire when it comes to human minds.

Throughout the book, Terry's characters are multidimensional and complex, with motivations that are often ambiguous and open to interpretation. The mysterious man from Kyla's past, who becomes a central figure in her quest for truth, is a masterfully crafted character whose true intentions are expertly veiled, adding to the suspense and intrigue of the story.

In conclusion, Fractured is a gripping and thought-provoking thriller that will appeal to fans of dystopian fiction and psychological suspense. Teri Terry's writing is engaging, and her exploration of the theme of identity is both haunting and timely. With its intricate plot, complex characters, and unsettling world-building, Fractured is a must-read for anyone looking for a story that will keep them on the edge of their seats and leave them questioning the very fabric of reality.

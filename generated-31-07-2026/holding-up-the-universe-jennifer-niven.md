# Holding Up the Universe

**Author:** Jennifer Niven  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

Holding Up the Universe, the poignant and exhilarating novel by Jennifer Niven, is a heart-wrenching story that delves into the universal need to be understood, to be wanted, and to be seen for who one truly is. This beautifully crafted tale follows the lives of two teenagers, Libby Strout and Jack Masselin, who, despite their vastly different struggles, find solace and love in each other.

Libby, once dubbed "America's Fattest Teen," has been hiding behind her weight, struggling to cope with the loss of her mother and the pressures of high school. Her journey is one of self-discovery, as she learns to let go of her insecurities and find her true self. With a voice that is both courageous and vulnerable, Libby's story is a powerful exploration of body positivity and the importance of self-acceptance.

Jack, on the other hand, is a master of hiding behind his charm and wit. Suffering from a rare condition that prevents him from recognizing faces, Jack's world is one of isolation and loneliness. His struggles to connect with others and find his place in the world are deeply relatable, and his character adds a unique layer of complexity to the story.

Through the lens of these two characters, Niven tackles themes of identity, loneliness, and the human need for connection. Her writing is evocative and immersive, making it impossible not to become fully invested in the lives of Libby and Jack. The way she weaves their stories together is nothing short of magic, creating a narrative that is both poignant and uplifting.

One of the most striking aspects of Niven's writing is her ability to create characters that are both flawed and relatable. Libby and Jack are not perfect, but they are real, and their struggles and triumphs are deeply human. Niven's characters are not just protagonists; they are people, with all the complexities and nuances that come with being human.

The romance that blossoms between Libby and Jack is sweet and tender, but it's not the only focus of the story. Niven explores the complexities of relationships, the difficulties of communication, and the importance of empathy and understanding. The way she writes about love is not sentimental or clichéd; it's raw and honest, making it all the more beautiful.

Holding Up the Universe is a remarkable read, one that will leave you feeling hopeful and uplifted. Niven's writing is a testament to the power of storytelling, and her characters will stay with you long after you finish the book. This is a novel that will make you laugh, cry, and feel all the feels. It's a story that will remind you of the importance of kindness, empathy, and understanding.

In short, Holding Up the Universe is a must-read for anyone who has ever felt like they don't quite fit in. It's a story that will resonate with anyone who has ever struggled to find their place in the world. And it's a reminder that, no matter how alone we may feel, we are never truly alone.

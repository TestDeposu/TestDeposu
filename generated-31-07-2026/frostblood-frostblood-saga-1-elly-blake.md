# Frostblood (Frostblood Saga, #1)

**Author:** Elly Blake  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

In her 2017 New York Times bestselling debut, *Frostblood*, author Elly Blake plunges readers into a stark, unforgiving realm where elemental magic is both a divine gift and a death sentence. At its core, the novel explores the timeless, volatile theme of duality—specifically, the eternal clash and surprising symbiosis between fire and ice. Blake constructs a deeply divided society ruled by the cruel Frostbloods, where those born with the power of heat are hunted, oppressed, and forced into hiding. This elemental apartheid serves as a compelling backdrop for a classic narrative about tyranny, systemic oppression, and the heavy price of personal vengeance.

Seventeen-year-old Ruby embodies this central theme of fiery resilience. As a concealed Fireblood whose life is upended by tragedy, her journey from a terrified fugitive to a formidable warrior anchors the narrative’s emotional weight. Blake’s writing style is punchy, cinematic, and decidedly fast-paced, favoring high-octane momentum over slow-burn world-building. This makes the novel an undeniably addictive page-turner, perfectly tailored for fans of accessible, action-driven young adult fantasy. 

The author excels at sensory details, sharply contrasting the suffocating, glittering frost of the ruling class with the rebellious, unpredictable warmth of Ruby’s inner flame. While the plot leans into well-trodden tropes—most notably the reluctant alliance between the fiery protagonist and the brooding, infuriatingly stoic rebel, Arcus—Blake infuses these familiar dynamics with enough tension and emotional sincerity to keep the pages turning. The forced-proximity training and subsequent gladiatorial tournaments elevate the stakes, transforming a personal quest for revenge into a broader fight for liberation. 

Ultimately, *Frostblood* succeeds not by reinventing the fantasy wheel, but by spinning it with breathless energy and undeniable charm. Elly Blake delivers an exhilarating kickoff to a trilogy that proves opposites don't just attract—they ignite. For readers craving a brisk, romantic, and magical escape filled with icy rulers and blazing defiance, this debut is an absolute must-read.

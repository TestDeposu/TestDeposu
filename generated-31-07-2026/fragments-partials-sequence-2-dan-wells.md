# Fragments (Partials Sequence, #2)

**Author:** Dan Wells  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In the gripping sequel to the sci-fi blockbuster Partials, Dan Wells delves deeper into the post-apocalyptic world he created, where humanity teeters on the edge of extinction. Fragments is a thought-provoking, action-packed ride that explores the darker aspects of human nature and the true meaning of survival.

At the heart of the story is Kira Walker, a strong-willed and determined heroine who embarks on a perilous journey across the ruins of America in search of answers. With the cure for the deadly RM virus in hand, Kira's quest now shifts towards uncovering the truth behind the Partials, the genetically engineered soldiers who were meant to save humanity but ultimately brought about its downfall.

Wells' world-building is as rich and immersive as ever, with a keen eye for detail that brings the devastated landscape to life. From the ruins of Manhattan to the heart of a dying continent, the author's vivid descriptions will leave readers breathless and eager for more. The introduction of new characters adds depth and complexity to the narrative, while familiar faces continue to evolve and grow in unexpected ways.

One of the most striking aspects of Fragments is its exploration of the gray areas between right and wrong. As Kira navigates the treacherous landscape, she is forced to confront the harsh realities of a world where survival often depends on difficult choices and moral compromises. Her relationship with Samm, the Partial soldier who travels by her side, is a masterclass in subtlety, as the two characters dance around their feelings for each other amidst a backdrop of mistrust and betrayal.

The theme of identity is also woven throughout the narrative, as Kira begins to question her own humanity in the face of the Partials' existence. Are they truly alive, or are they just sophisticated machines designed to mimic human emotions? As the stakes grow higher, Kira's search for answers becomes an existential inquiry into the very nature of humanity.

Wells' writing style is as engaging as ever, with a keen sense of pacing that propels the reader forward. The action scenes are intense and suspenseful, while the quieter moments are imbued with a sense of introspection and emotional depth. The author's use of language is economical and evocative, conjuring vivid images with a minimum of words.

In conclusion, Fragments is a gripping and thought-provoking sequel that builds upon the foundations laid in Partials. With its rich world-building, complex characters, and exploration of the human condition, this book is a must-read for fans of science fiction and dystopian fiction. As the stakes grow higher and the body count rises, one thing is clear: Kira's journey is far from over, and the fate of humanity hangs in the balance.

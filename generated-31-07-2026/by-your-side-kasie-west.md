# By Your Side

**Author:** Kasie West  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

Kasie West has long established herself as a reigning queen of contemporary young adult romance, consistently delivering stories that balance sweetness with genuine emotional weight. In her 2017 novel By Your Side, West takes two classic romance tropes—forced proximity and opposites attract—and breathes fresh life into them through a compelling lens of vulnerability, isolation, and unexpected connection. 

At its core, the novel explores the timeless question of what happens when the carefully constructed walls we build to protect ourselves suddenly crumble. The story centers on Autumn Collins, a protagonist grappling with a secret anxiety disorder. Terrified of judgment, Autumn hides her struggles behind a facade of normalcy, desperate to fit in with the popular crowd, including the elusive Jeff, whose approval she desperately craves. But life has a way of short-circuiting our plans. When Autumn is accidentally locked inside a library over a long holiday weekend, her meticulously managed world instantly fractures. 

What elevates By Your Side above standard teen romance fare is how West handles Autumn’s anxiety. Rather than treating it as a fleeting plot device or a quirky character trait, the author offers a heartfelt, honest look at the exhausting reality of managing panic when you feel the constant need to hide your true self. The library—usually a sanctuary of quiet order—becomes a pressure cooker as a severe snowstorm traps Autumn inside. 

And she is not alone. Sharing this confined space is Dax Miller, the school’s notorious outsider. With a juvie record, tattoos, and a permanent scowl, Dax is the exact opposite of the polished peers Autumn usually surrounds herself with. The initial dynamic is deliciously tense. Yet, as the hours stretch into days, the temperature plummets, and the vending machine food runs out, the physical and emotional barriers begin to melt. 

West’s writing style shines brightest in these quiet, intimate moments of dialogue and shared silence. The forced proximity trope is utilized to absolute perfection, creating a slow-burn intimacy that feels earned rather than rushed. As Autumn and Dax trade secrets and huddle together for warmth, West subverts the bad-boy romance cliché. Dax is not merely a project for Autumn to fix; he carries his own heavy history, a story he has never trusted anyone else with. Through their interactions, West delivers a poignant central theme: true survival—and true love—requires us to be seen in all our messy, unpolished reality. 

The pacing is brisk, witty, and utterly absorbing, making it nearly impossible to put down. While the setup relies on a classic YA premise, the emotional depth distinguishes it. West reminds readers that the people we least expect to understand us are often the ones who see us most clearly. 

Fans of Stephanie Perkins and Morgan Matson will find much to love in this charming, comforting, yet emotionally resonant novel. By Your Side is a witty and romantic triumph that proves opening your heart to someone else is the bravest thing you can possibly do.

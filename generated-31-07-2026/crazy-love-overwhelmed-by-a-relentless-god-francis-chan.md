# Crazy Love: Overwhelmed by a Relentless God

**Author:** Francis Chan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

Francis Chan’s Crazy Love: Overwhelmed by a Relentless God bursts onto the page with the urgency of a revivalist sermon and the intimacy of a personal confession. First published in 2008 and revised in 2013, the book tackles what Chan sees as the epidemic of spiritual complacency that has settled over modern evangelicalism. His central claim is simple yet provocative: the God of Scripture loves us with a “crazy, relentless, all‑powerful” affection that demands a response far beyond routine church attendance, moral politeness, or superficial piety.

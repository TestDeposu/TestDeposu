# Drama

**Author:** Raina Telgemeier  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

In "Drama," Raina Telgemeier masterfully weaves a captivating tale that delves into the complexities of middle school life, first love, and the magic of theater. The story revolves around Callie, a seventh-grader with a passion for theater and a knack for set design. As she navigates the challenges of creating a Broadway-worthy set on a limited budget, Callie must also contend with the drama that unfolds both on and off the stage.

Through Telgemeier's signature graphic novel style, the world of middle school theater comes alive in vivid colors and relatable characters. The author's ability to balance humor and heart makes "Drama" an engaging read that will resonate with readers of all ages. The characters are multidimensional and authentic, with Callie being a particularly well-crafted protagonist. Her struggles to assert herself in a male-dominated set design world, her crush on one of the brothers, and her desire to prove herself as a talented set designer all contribute to a rich and immersive narrative.

One of the standout aspects of "Drama" is its thoughtful exploration of themes such as teamwork, perseverance, and self-expression. Telgemeier shows how the theater department's production of "Moon over Mississippi" becomes a catalyst for growth, forcing the characters to work together, overcome their differences, and learn from their mistakes. The author's portrayal of the drama that unfolds both on and off the stage is equally impressive, tackling issues like relationships, friendships, and identity with sensitivity and nuance.

Telgemeier's artwork is, as always, a treat to behold. Her expressive illustrations bring the characters to life, and her use of color and composition adds depth and visual interest to the story. The graphic novel format allows the reader to experience the world of the story in a unique and engaging way, making "Drama" a truly immersive read.

Overall, "Drama" is a delightful and engaging read that will appeal to fans of Telgemeier's previous works, as well as anyone who has ever been involved in theater or struggled to find their place in middle school. With its positive themes, relatable characters, and engaging storyline, "Drama" is a must-read for anyone looking for a fun and inspiring graphic novel.

# My Fault (Culpable, #1)

**Author:** Mercedes Ron  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

In the midst of a whirlwind of forbidden romance and suspense, Mercedes Ron's "My Fault" is a captivating tale that will leave readers on the edge of their seats. As the first installment in the Culpable trilogy, this book sets the tone for a thrilling series that masterfully weaves together the threads of love, trauma, and secrets.

At the heart of the story is Noah Morgan, a 17-year-old girl whose life is turned upside down when her mother marries a billionaire and whisks her away to the luxurious world of Los Angeles. Noah's quiet, normal life in Toronto is replaced with the glitz and glamour of L.A.'s elite, and she finds herself face-to-face with her new stepbrother, Nicholas - the embodiment of everything she despises. But as their paths continue to cross, Noah and Nick are drawn to each other in a powerful and all-consuming way.

One of the most striking aspects of "My Fault" is Ron's ability to craft a narrative that is both fast-paced and emotionally charged. Her writing is raw and unapologetic, delving into the darker corners of her characters' psyches and refusing to shy away from the complexities of their emotions. This is a story that will resonate deeply with fans of Colleen Hoover and Anna Todd, who appreciate a good dose of angst and drama in their romance novels.

The chemistry between Noah and Nick is undeniable, and their enemies-to-lovers romance is fraught with tension and desire. But what sets "My Fault" apart from other forbidden love stories is the depth and nuance of its characters. Noah and Nick are multidimensional and flawed, with rich inner lives that add depth and complexity to the narrative.

Throughout the book, Ron expertly ratchets up the tension, slowly revealing the secrets and lies that threaten to tear Noah and Nick apart. The result is a story that is impossible to put down, with a narrative that twists and turns in unexpected ways.

Ultimately, "My Fault" is a book about the power of love and redemption. It's a story about two damaged people who find each other in the midst of chaos and destruction, and who must navigate the treacherous landscape of their own emotions in order to be together. With its lush prose, complex characters, and addictive plot, this book is a must-read for fans of contemporary teen romance.

"My Fault" is a triumph, a book that will leave readers breathless and eager for more. With its fresh take on the forbidden love trope and its unflinching portrayal of the human heart, this series is an addicting gem that will stay with readers long after the final page has been turned.

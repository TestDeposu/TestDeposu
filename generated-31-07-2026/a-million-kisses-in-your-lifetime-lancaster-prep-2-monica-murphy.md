# A Million Kisses in Your Lifetime (Lancaster Prep, #2)

**Author:** Monica Murphy  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

In "A Million Kisses in Your Lifetime", Monica Murphy weaves a captivating tale of love, self-discovery, and the blurring of lines between good and bad. The second installment in the Lancaster Prep series, this novel delves into the complex world of high school relationships, where social hierarchies and reputations can make or break a person.

At the heart of the story are Wren Beaumont, the quintessential good girl, and Crew Lancaster, the quintessential bad boy. Their initial animosity towards each other is palpable, with Wren's obliviousness to Crew's charms only adding fuel to the fire. However, when they're forced to work together on a research project, the tension between them becomes increasingly charged, and the air is electric with anticipation.

As the narrative progresses, Murphy masterfully peels away the layers of her characters, revealing their vulnerabilities and insecurities. Wren, despite her perfect facade, is struggling to find her place in the world, while Crew's tough exterior hides a deep-seated need for connection and acceptance. Their romance is a slow-burning fire that simmers just below the surface, waiting to erupt into a passionate and all-consuming flame.

One of the most striking aspects of Murphy's writing is her ability to craft relatable, multidimensional characters. Wren and Crew are more than just archetypes; they're fully fleshed-out individuals with their own motivations, desires, and flaws. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, adding depth and richness to the story.

The author's style is engaging and accessible, making it easy to become fully invested in the world of Lancaster Prep. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of witty banter, heartfelt moments, and steamy encounters that will leave readers blushing. Murphy's use of language is evocative and immersive, conjuring up the sights, sounds, and emotions of the characters' experiences.

The themes of identity, loyalty, and the gray areas between right and wrong are expertly woven throughout the narrative. Murphy raises important questions about the nature of relationships, encouraging readers to think critically about the complexities of human connections.

In short, "A Million Kisses in Your Lifetime" is a must-read for fans of new adult romance, particularly those who enjoy the "haters to lovers" trope. With its relatable characters, engaging storyline, and sizzling romance, this book is sure to leave readers eager for more.

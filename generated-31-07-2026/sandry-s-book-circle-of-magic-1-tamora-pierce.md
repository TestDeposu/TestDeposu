# Sandry's Book (Circle of Magic, #1)

**Author:** Tamora Pierce  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**A Tapestry of Magic, Friendship, and the Power of Belonging**

Tamora Pierce’s *Sandry’s Book*—the first installment in the *Circle of Magic* quartet—is a spellbinding coming-of-age tale that weaves together magic, identity, and the unbreakable bonds of friendship. Set in the vibrant, mythic world of Tamora Pierce’s *Song of the Lioness* universe, this novel follows Sandry, a young weaver with a quiet but profound connection to the threads of fate, as she arrives at the Winding Circle, a sanctuary for gifted children. There, she meets three other outcasts—Briar, a plant-touched thief with a heart as wild as the forests she loves; Daja, a metalcraft prodigy who has spent her life on the fringes of society; and Tris, whose unsettling mastery over the weather makes her both feared and fascinating. Together, they form a found family, each grappling with their own struggles—self-doubt, prejudice, and the fear of being seen as different.

At its core, *Sandry’s Book* is a story about the transformative power of belonging. Pierce masterfully explores how these four misfits, each with their own gifts and insecurities, learn to trust one another and harness their unique abilities. Sandry, the novel’s protagonist, is not a warrior or a sorceress in the traditional sense, but her role as a weaver—someone who binds and mends—mirrors the emotional and magical work she must do to unite her friends and protect their home. The book beautifully contrasts Sandry’s introspective, almost passive nature with the boldness of the others, creating a dynamic that feels both natural and deeply human. It’s a reminder that strength isn’t always about raw power; sometimes, it’s about seeing the worth in others and in oneself.

Pierce’s prose is rich with warmth and detail, immersing readers in a world where magic is as much a part of daily life as the wind or the turning of the seasons. Her descriptions of the Winding Circle’s lush landscapes and the intricate workings of the weavers’ craft are vivid, making the setting feel alive and tangible. Yet, what truly elevates the novel is Pierce’s ability to blend fantasy with emotional depth. The magic here isn’t just about spells and potions; it’s about growth, resilience, and the courage to embrace one’s true self. When disaster strikes—whether in the form of external threats or internal fears—it’s Sandry’s quiet strength and her friends’ unwavering loyalty that save the day. The climax is a testament to the idea that magic, like friendship, is strongest when it’s shared.

Themes of acceptance and self-discovery resonate throughout the book, particularly for young readers who may feel like outsiders themselves. Pierce doesn’t shy away from the challenges her characters face—Daja’s struggle with her past, Tris’s fear of her own power, or Briar’s struggle to reconcile her wild nature with the rules of the Circle—but she also celebrates their resilience. The novel’s message is clear: no one is an island, and healing begins when we allow ourselves to be seen and supported by those who understand us.

For fans of fantasy with heart, *Sandry’s Book* is a must-read. It’s a story that feels both timeless and fresh, a reminder that magic isn’t just about what we can do—it’s about who we become along the way. Tamora Pierce’s signature blend of adventure, emotion, and richly drawn characters makes this quartet a classic in the making, one that will linger in the hearts of readers long after the final page.

# The Thing About Jellyfish

**Author:** Ali Benjamin  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

The Thing About Jellyfish, a mesmerizing debut novel by Ali Benjamin, is a poignant exploration of grief, wonder, and the complexities of the human experience. This New York Times bestseller and National Book Award finalist is a thought-provoking tale that delves into the mysteries of life, death, and the universe, leaving readers spellbound and introspective.

At the heart of the story is Suzy, a young girl struggling to come to terms with the sudden loss of her best friend in a drowning accident. Her conviction that the tragedy was caused by a rare jellyfish sting becomes an all-consuming obsession, driving her to retreat into a world of imagination and silence. As Suzy navigates this dark and unfamiliar terrain, Benjamin masterfully crafts a narrative that is both heartbreaking and uplifting.

One of the most striking aspects of The Thing About Jellyfish is its exploration of the human need for meaning and explanation in the face of tragedy. Suzy's fixation on the jellyfish sting is a desperate attempt to impose order on a chaotic world, to find a reason for the senseless loss of her friend. This theme is echoed in the novel's title, which refers to the enigmatic and often inexplicable nature of jellyfish. Through Suzy's journey, Benjamin highlights the tension between our desire for answers and the inherent mysteries of life.

Benjamin's writing style is a notable strength of the novel. Her prose is lyrical and evocative, conjuring vivid images of the natural world and the inner lives of her characters. The narrative is interspersed with fascinating facts about jellyfish, which serve as a metaphor for the complexities and wonders of the universe. The author's use of language is both precise and poetic, creating a dreamlike atmosphere that draws the reader into Suzy's world.

The novel also explores the theme of hope and resilience in the face of adversity. As Suzy travels the globe in pursuit of her theory, she encounters a cast of characters who challenge her perceptions and help her to heal. The novel's portrayal of human connection and the potential for love and hope is a powerful reminder of the transformative power of relationships.

The Thing About Jellyfish is a remarkable debut that announces Ali Benjamin as a talented and innovative voice in contemporary literature. This novel will appeal to readers who appreciate lyrical prose, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes. With its universal themes and captivating narrative, it is no surprise that the book has captured widespread critical acclaim and been optioned for film by Oddlot Entertainment.

In conclusion, The Thing About Jellyfish is a mesmerizing and deeply moving novel that explores the complexities of the human experience. With its beautiful prose, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes, it is a must-read for anyone interested in literary fiction.

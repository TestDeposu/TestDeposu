# Way to Be!: 9 Ways To Be Happy And Make Something Of Your Life

**Author:** Gordon B. Hinckley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Navigating the turbulent waters of youth has never been more complicated, yet the roadmap to a meaningful existence remains surprisingly steadfast. In Way to Be!: 9 Ways To Be Happy And Make Something Of Your Life, the late Gordon B. Hinckley, former president of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints and a globally revered spiritual leader, distills a lifetime of wisdom into an accessible, urgent, and deeply compassionate guide for modern adolescents and their families. Published in 2009, this book acts as a steady lighthouse, cutting through the cultural noise of the twenty-first century to offer a clear, unshakeable moral compass.

At the heart of Hinckley’s work lies a powerful, recurring theme: agency and personal accountability. Rather than viewing youth as a passive phase of life to merely endure, Hinckley approaches young adults as architects of their own destinies. The main premise is refreshingly direct: our ultimate happiness and success are not dictated by random chance, socioeconomic status, or peer pressure, but rather by the cumulative weight of daily choices. Through nine distinct guiding principles, Hinckley constructs a practical game plan designed to help readers steer clear of contemporary moral minefields while fully embracing the vibrant opportunities of the modern world. 

What distinguishes Way to Be! from a sea of generic self-help literature is Hinckley’s distinctive literary and pastoral style. He writes not as an academic preaching from an ivory tower, but as a caring grandfather speaking directly across the kitchen table. His tone is remarkably upbeat, warm, and paternal, infused with an innate optimism that refuses to patronize his youthful audience. Hinckley understands that teenagers often tune out stern lectures, so he instead relies on vivid anecdotes, gentle humor, and timeless precepts that resonate on a human level. His prose is striking in its simplicity; he strips away convoluted philosophical jargon, opting instead for clear, declarative sentences that carry profound moral weight. 

While deeply rooted in his personal faith and religious convictions, Hinckley frames his advice in universal values—integrity, optimism, service, respect, and emotional resilience—making the book widely accessible to readers of various backgrounds looking for ethical grounding. He does not shy away from the harsh realities of contemporary life, acknowledging the intense pressures, temptations, and anxieties that define the teenage experience. Yet, rather than wallowing in pessimism about the state of modern culture, Hinckley pivots immediately toward constructive solutions. He empowers readers by reminding them that character is built in the quiet moments of decision-making when nobody else is watching.

For parents, educators, and mentors, Way to Be! serves as an invaluable bridge of communication, offering a vocabulary to discuss difficult topics without resorting to judgment. For young adults standing at the crossroads of identity and independence, it provides a reassuring validation of their worth and potential. Gordon B. Hinckley has crafted a timeless manual for the human spirit—a brief yet potent reminder that happiness is not a distant destination, but a deliberate way of living. This book does not merely tell readers how to succeed; it inspires them to dare greatly, live honorably, and truly make something magnificent of their lives.

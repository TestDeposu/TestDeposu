# Under a Painted Sky

**Author:** Stacey Lee  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

"Under a Painted Sky" by Stacey Lee: A Haunting Adventure of Self-Discovery and Love

Stacey Lee's debut novel, "Under a Painted Sky," is a captivating tale of two young women navigating a treacherous frontier in 1849. Samantha, a Chinese girl, and Annamae, a runaway slave, embark on a perilous journey on the Oregon Trail, fleeing their past and fighting for survival in a society torn apart by racism and inequality. Disguised as two boys, Sammy and Andy, they must rely on their wit and courage to evade the law and avoid any unwanted attention.

Lee's masterful storytelling weaves a rich tapestry of historical fiction, romance, and adventure that will keep you enthralled until the very end. As Samantha and Annamae navigate through the challenges of their journey, they confront their own demons and learn to trust each other, fostering a deep and unbreakable bond. But as they face numerous setbacks, they are forced to confront the harsh realities of their world.

One of the book's greatest strengths is its authentic and nuanced portrayal of the complexities of historical relationships. Lee masterfully captures the racial and ethnic tensions of the time period, shedding light on the struggles faced by Chinese Americans, African Americans, and Native Americans. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Annamae's story providing a compelling commentary on the brutalities of slavery and the resilience of the human spirit.

Samantha's journey is equally compelling, as she navigates her cultural identity and struggles with her passion for music in a society that seeks to silence her. Lee's descriptions of the Oregon Trail are vivid and immersive, transporting readers to a world of breathtaking beauty and unforgiving harshness. The author's writing is lyrical and evocative, perfectly capturing the longing and hope that defines the human experience.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that some readers may find the pacing a bit uneven at times. However, Lee's masterful storytelling and the depth of her characters quickly make up for any lulls in the action. As the journey unfolds, the tension builds, and the stakes become increasingly higher, leading to a heart-wrenching conclusion that will leave readers breathless.

"Under a Painted Sky" is an exemplary work of young adult literature, one that will appeal to fans of "Code Name Verity" and "The Song of Achilles." Lee's writing is reminiscent of the great storytellers of historical fiction, with a unique voice that resonates deeply with her characters. This powerful story about love, friendship, and sacrifice is a triumph, offering a compelling glimpse into a forgotten era of American history.

As readers follow Samantha and Annamae's perilous journey, they will discover the strength of their bond, the resilience of their spirits, and the importance of embracing one's true identity. Lee's masterful tale is a testament to the enduring power of love and friendship, reminding us that even in the darkest of times, hope and courage can illuminate the way forward.

# White Hot Kiss (The Dark Elements, #1)

**Author:** Jennifer L. Armentrout  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

**White Hot Kiss: A Sizzling Debut to The Dark Elements Trilogy**

Jennifer L. Armentrout's "White Hot Kiss," the first installment in The Dark Elements series, is a captivating blend of paranormal romance, action, and suspense that will leave you breathless. Armentrout, a master of her craft, weaves a richly imaginative world populated by demons, gargoyles, and a unique heroine who is a captivating mix of both.

Layla, our protagonist, is a refreshing heroine. She's not your typical damsel in distress; she's a fierce, independent young woman struggling to find her place in a world that's both familiar and terrifyingly unknown. Half-demon, half-gargoyle, Layla possesses a power that sets her apart: her kiss can kill anything with a soul. This unique ability, coupled with her internal struggle between her demonic and gargoyle halves, makes Layla a compelling character to root for.

Armentrout's storytelling is fast-paced and engaging, with a plot that twists and turns like a rollercoaster. The romance between Layla and the two love interests, Zayne and Roth, is intense and palpable. Zayne, a gargoyle, is the safe, familiar choice, while Roth, a demon prince, is the dangerous, forbidden fruit. Armentrout expertly plays with these dynamics, creating a love triangle that's as intriguing as it is heart-wrenching.

The world-building in "White Hot Kiss" is impressive. Armentrout has created a detailed, intricate world where demons and gargoyles coexist, each with their own unique cultures and histories. The lore is fascinating, and Armentrout sprinkles it throughout the narrative in a way that's both informative and engaging.

Armentrout's writing style is accessible and engaging, with a healthy dose of humor that balances out the darker elements of the story. She has a knack for creating memorable characters, each with their own distinct voice and personality. The dialogue is sharp and witty, bringing the characters to life and driving the plot forward.

However, "White Hot Kiss" isn't without its flaws. The pacing can feel a bit rushed at times, and some plot points are resolved a bit too neatly. But these are minor quibbles in an otherwise engaging read.

In conclusion, "White Hot Kiss" is a thrilling start to The Dark Elements trilogy. With its unique world-building, engaging characters, and intense romance, it's a must-read for fans of paranormal romance and urban fantasy. Armentrout's signature style shines in this book, making it a standout in the genre.

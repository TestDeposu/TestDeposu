# As You Wish: Inconceivable Tales from the Making of The Princess Bride

**Author:** Cary Elwes  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

**A Journey Through the Inconceivable Tales of The Princess Bride**

Cary Elwes' memoir, "As You Wish: Inconceivable Tales from the Making of The Princess Bride," is a captivating and behind-the-scenes look at the creation of the beloved cult classic film. Elwes, who played the iconic role of Westley, takes readers on a journey through the memories of filming, sharing never-before-told stories, exclusive photographs, and interviews with his fellow cast members and crew.

The book is an enchanting experience, offering a glimpse into the lives of the people who brought this timeless tale to life on the screen. From the set secrets and backstage stories, to the memories of the iconic cast members, "As You Wish" is an intimate and fascinating account of the making of a true classic.

Cary Elwes' writing style is engaging and accessible, making this memoir a pleasure to read for fans of all ages. He shares stories of the cast's camaraderie, the challenges they faced on set, and the creative decisions that brought the characters and story to life. Elwes' passion for the film shines through on every page, and his love for his fellow cast members is palpable.

One of the most impressive aspects of "As You Wish" is the collection of previously unpublished photographs and interviews. These add a unique perspective to the story, allowing readers to experience the making of the film through the eyes of those who were there. The photographs, too, are a treasure trove of behind-the-scenes moments, showcasing the cast and crew in a way that feels both intimate and exclusive.

The inclusion of a foreword by Rob Reiner adds an extra layer of depth to the book, offering insight into the vision of the director and the experiences of the cast. The limited edition original poster by acclaimed artist Shepard Fairey is a beautiful addition to the book, making it a must-have for any fan of The Princess Bride.

**In Conclusion**

"As You Wish: Inconceivable Tales from the Making of The Princess Bride" is a must-read for fans of the film, as well as anyone interested in the making of a true classic. Cary Elwes' engaging and accessible writing style, combined with the wealth of behind-the-scenes stories and exclusive photographs, make this memoir an engaging and captivating read. Whether you're a seasoned fan or just discovering the joy of The Princess Bride, this book is an enchanting experience that will leave you smiling and eager for more.

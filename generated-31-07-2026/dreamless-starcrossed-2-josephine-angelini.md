# Dreamless (Starcrossed, #2)

**Author:** Josephine Angelini  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

Dreamless, the second installment in Josephine Angelini's Starcrossed series, is a captivating and poignant tale that masterfully weaves together elements of mythology, romance, and action. The story follows Helen Hamilton, a shy and determined Scion, as she navigates her complicated destiny and the treacherous world of Greek gods and demigods.

One of the most striking aspects of Angelini's writing style is her ability to craft a narrative that is both epic in scope and intimately personal. The world-building in Dreamless is meticulous and immersive, with a keen attention to detail that brings the mythological realm to vivid life. At the same time, the character development is nuanced and relatable, making it easy to become invested in Helen's journey and the fate of those around her.

The central theme of the book – the struggle to defy destiny and forge one's own path – is a powerful and timeless one. Helen's determination to use her unique gift to prevent a war among the gods and alter her own tragic fate is admirable and inspiring, and her courage in the face of overwhelming obstacles is a testament to the human spirit. The forbidden love between Helen and Lucas Delios adds an extra layer of complexity to the story, as they navigate the challenges of their family ties and the ancient curse that seeks to keep them apart.

Angelini's writing is descriptive and evocative, with a keen sense of atmosphere and setting. The windswept island of Nantucket provides a hauntingly beautiful backdrop for the story, and the author's use of sensory details effectively transports the reader to the rugged and isolated landscape. The pacing of the book is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and quieter moments of introspection that keeps the reader engaged and eager to turn the pages.

The introduction of a new character, a mysterious ally who appears in Helen's life, adds an intriguing twist to the story and raises questions about trust, loyalty, and the true motives of those around her. The love triangle that develops is deftly handled, with a subtle exploration of the complexities and nuances of human relationships.

Overall, Dreamless is a compelling and emotionally resonant read that will appeal to fans of mythology, romance, and fantasy. Angelini's writing is engaging and immersive, with a richly detailed world and complex characters that leap off the page. The book's themes of self-discovery, courage, and the power of love to overcome even the most daunting obstacles are timely and timeless, making it a must-read for anyone looking for a story that will stay with them long after the final page is turned.

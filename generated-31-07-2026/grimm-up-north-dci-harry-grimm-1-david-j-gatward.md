# Grimm Up North (DCI Harry Grimm, #1)

**Author:** David J. Gatward  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

Grimm Up North, the debut novel in the DCI Harry Grimm series by David J. Gatward, is a gripping and intense thriller that delves into the darker aspects of human nature. The story follows DCI Kate Daniels as she navigates a complex web of clues and deceit, all while being stalked by a ruthless adversary. With its intricate plot and well-developed characters, this book is sure to captivate fans of the crime fiction genre.

One of the standout features of Grimm Up North is its ability to balance action and suspense with thoughtful character development. Gatward's writing style is concise and engaging, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world he has created. The characters are multidimensional and relatable, with DCI Daniels being a particularly strong protagonist. Her determination and intelligence make her a compelling figure, and her flaws and vulnerabilities add depth to the story.

The plot of Grimm Up North is expertly crafted, with a series of twists and turns that keep the reader guessing until the very end. The novel's central mystery is both intriguing and unsettling, with a sense of urgency that propels the story forward. As Kate delves deeper into the case, she finds herself in a desperate game of cat and mouse with a killer who will stop at nothing to achieve their goals.

Gatward's use of language is economical and effective, with a keen sense of pacing that keeps the tension building throughout the novel. His descriptions of the settings and characters are vivid and evocative, making it easy to visualize the world of the story. The author's experience in crafting a compelling narrative is evident, and his writing style is reminiscent of other acclaimed crime fiction authors, such as Ian Rankin, who has praised Grimm Up North as "terrific".

The theme of obsession is a dominant thread throughout the novel, as Kate becomes increasingly fixated on finding the truth behind the mysterious note and the identity of Aaron. Her adversary, meanwhile, is driven by a twisted desire for control and manipulation, and the cat-and-mouse game that ensues is both thrilling and terrifying. Gatward explores the darker aspects of human psychology with sensitivity and nuance, raising important questions about the nature of obsession and the consequences of allowing it to consume us.

In terms of comparisons, fans of Mari Hannah's work will likely appreciate the similar blend of suspense, intrigue, and strong characterization found in Grimm Up North. The novel's pace and tone are well-suited to readers who enjoy a fast-paced, thrilling narrative with unexpected twists and turns.

Overall, Grimm Up North is a gripping and unsettling thriller that will appeal to fans of crime fiction and suspense. With its intricate plot, well-developed characters, and thought-provoking themes, this novel is a standout debut in the DCI Harry Grimm series. David J. Gatward's writing style is engaging and effective, making this a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and intense thriller.

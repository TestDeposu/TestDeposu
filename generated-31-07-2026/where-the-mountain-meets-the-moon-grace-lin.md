# Where the Mountain Meets the Moon

**Author:** Grace Lin  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**"Where the Mountain Meets the Moon" by Grace Lin is a captivating journey that beautifully weaves Chinese folklore with timeless themes of courage, faith, and self-discovery. This Newbery Honor Book and New York Times bestseller is a must-read for young readers and adults alike.**

Lin masterfully transports readers to the lush, mystical valley of Fruitless Mountain, where young Minli lives with her family. Her father's enchanting tales of the Jade Dragon and the Old Man on the Moon spark an insatiable curiosity within Minli, leading her on a quest to find the enigmatic sage who holds the answers to life's questions. Accompanied by a magical dragon, Minli encounters a delightful array of characters and creatures, each offering lessons and challenges that shape her understanding of the world.

At its heart, "Where the Mountain Meets the Moon" is a story about the power of belief and the courage to forge one's own path. Minli's unwavering faith in the stories her father tells, and her determination to find the Old Man on the Moon, drive the narrative and inspire readers to question their own perceptions of reality. Lin's ability to blend fantasy with Chinese folklore creates a rich, immersive world that feels both familiar and extraordinary.

Lin's illustrations, printed in full-color throughout the text, are a delightful addition to the story. They bring the characters and landscapes to life, complementing the narrative and enhancing the overall reading experience. The illustrations are not merely decorative; they serve to deepen the reader's connection to the story and its characters.

"Where the Mountain Meets the Moon" is a modern-day classic that stands alongside other beloved tales of adventure and self-discovery, such as "The Wizard of Oz" and "The Girl Who Drank the Moon." Lin's storytelling prowess, coupled with her stunning illustrations, creates a captivating and unforgettable reading experience that will resonate with readers long after they've turned the final page.

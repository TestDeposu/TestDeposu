# Moon Over Manifest

**Author:** Clare Vanderpool  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

"Moon Over Manifest" by Clare Vanderpool is a captivating tale that weaves together the past and present, creating a rich tapestry of history, mystery, and coming-of-age. The story unfolds in the small, seemingly forgotten town of Manifest, Kansas, where young Abilene Tucker arrives, seeking answers about her father's past.

Vanderpool's prose is as evocative as the dusty, sun-drenched landscapes she describes. She paints a vivid picture of Manifest, bringing its worn-out streets and quirky inhabitants to life with a deft touch. The narrative is peppered with Abilene's "universals," her list of constants in life, which add a poignant, philosophical depth to the story. Vanderpool's style is reminiscent of classic children's literature, yet it feels fresh and engaging, appealing to both young readers and adults alike.

At its heart, "Moon Over Manifest" is a story about the power of storytelling itself. Abilene's journey is a quest for her father's story, and as she uncovers the town's history, she begins to understand her own place in the world. The novel is filled with secondary characters, each with their own stories to tell, and Vanderpool masterfully weaves these narratives together, creating a complex, interconnected web of lives.

The main theme of "Moon Over Manifest" is the search for identity and belonging. Abilene, feeling abandoned by her father, seeks to understand him through the lens of his past. Her journey is a universal one, as she grapples with questions of identity, loss, and the power of community. Vanderpool explores these themes with sensitivity and depth, creating a story that is both engaging and emotionally resonant.

"Moon Over Manifest" is a novel that lingers in the mind long after the final page. It is a testament to the power of storytelling, the importance of history, and the enduring human need for connection and belonging. Clare Vanderpool's debut is a remarkable achievement, a novel that will captivate readers and leave them eager for more.

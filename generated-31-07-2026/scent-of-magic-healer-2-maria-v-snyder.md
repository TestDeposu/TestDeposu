# Scent of Magic (Healer, #2)

**Author:** Maria V. Snyder  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

"Scent of Magic" by Maria V. Snyder is the second installment in the Healer trilogy, continuing the epic adventure of Avry of Kazan, the last Healer in the Fifteen Realms. As the series progresses, Snyder masterfully weaves a complex narrative that delves deeper into Avry's struggles, her intricate relationships, and the ominous world she inhabits.

Avry, once again, finds herself at the forefront of a brewing war, facing unprecedented challenges. With her unique gift as a Healer dwindling, Avry must confront the harsh reality of her existence. This theme of resilience and self-discovery resonates throughout the story, as Snyder skillfully explores Avry's vulnerabilities and the emotional toll of her experiences. The author's poignant portrayal of a character torn between loyalty, duty, and personal growth is nothing short of compelling.

Snyder's writing style is characterized by its unflinching pace, infused with moments of subtle humor, and nuanced descriptions that bring the realms to life. Her vivid world-building is both captivating and unsettling, as she delves into the darkness of Tohon's twisted ambitions. The author's masterful use of suspense keeps readers engaged, propelling them through an intricate web of alliances, betrayals, and unexpected alliances.

A particularly striking aspect of the narrative is Snyder's portrayal of Avry's relationships. Her bond with Kerrick, a key figure in Avry's life, evolves in complex and unexpected ways, as they navigate the treacherous landscape of war and loyalty. These character dynamics are richly layered, offering a depth that adds substance to the story.

One of the standout elements of "Scent of Magic" is its exploration of the gray areas between good and evil. Avry's determination to stop Tohon's sinister plans is matched by her recognition of the morally ambiguous nature of her adversaries. Snyder skillfully subverts traditional notions of heroism, inviting readers to question the true nature of their allegiances and motivations.

Through "Scent of Magic", Snyder expertly builds upon the foundation established in "Touch of Power", offering a more refined and mature narrative that showcases her mastery of the fantasy genre. This installment not only deepens our understanding of Avry's world but also cements Snyder's status as a compelling storyteller.

While some may find the book's pacing and themes familiar, Snyder's skillful execution ensures that the narrative remains engaging and fresh. Her use of symbolism and foreshadowing adds an extra layer of depth to the story, leaving readers eager to discover what unfolds in the series' final installment.

Ultimately, "Scent of Magic" is a captivating and immersive read, with Snyder weaving a narrative that is as thrilling as it is thought-provoking. Those who have entered the world of the Healer trilogy will find solace in the character growth, world expansion, and emotional depth that make this book an essential addition to the series.

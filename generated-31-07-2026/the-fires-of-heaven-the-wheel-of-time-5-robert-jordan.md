# The Fires of Heaven (The Wheel of Time, #5)

**Author:** Robert Jordan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

In 'The World of Robert Jordan's The Wheel of Time', we immerse ourselves in an expanse of depth and detail that is quintessential to the masterful storytelling of the late Robert Jordan. This companion book, bursting with over eighty full-color paintings, delves into the intricacies of the world Robert Jordan carefully crafted. The result is a sweeping narrative that not only enriches our understanding of the series but also offers a fresh perspective on what makes 'The Wheel of Time' an epic in every sense of the word.

The sheer scope of this book is a testament to Robert Jordan's unwavering dedication to world-building. Every aspect of the world he created is meticulously documented, from the Seanchan Empire's exotic beasts to the inner workings of the closed country of Shara. The revelation of a hitherto unknown continent, The Land of the Madmen, adds another layer of complexity to the already intricate world-building of the series. The inclusion of double-page spreads of the first seven book jackets by Darrell Sweet serves as a delightful bonus, allowing readers to appreciate the artwork without the distraction of text.

Throughout this companion book, one is struck by the author's unparalleled ability to weave a rich tapestry of lore and history. From the tale of the founding of the White Tower to the War of the Shadow, Robert Jordan masterfully crafts a narrative that not only expands our understanding of the world but also deepens our connection with its characters. The inclusion of legends, stories, and myths further adds to the sense of depth and authenticity that has come to define the series.

What sets 'The Wheel of Time' apart is its unique blend of fantasy and epic scope. Robert Jordan's world-building is not just a background setting; it is a vital part of the narrative itself. The intricate web of politics, mythology, and magic he created is at once fascinating and complex, drawing the reader in with its sheer scope. In 'The World of Robert Jordan's The Wheel of Time', we gain a deeper understanding of this intricate world, allowing us to appreciate the author's skill and craftsmanship.

One of the defining characteristics of Robert Jordan's writing style is his ability to balance action and detail. He has a remarkable talent for creating a sense of tension and urgency while simultaneously providing the reader with a wealth of background information and world-building detail. This ability to balance the large-scale narrative with the small-scale character dynamics is one of the key reasons why 'The Wheel of Time' remains an enduring classic in the world of fantasy.

If there is a flaw to 'The World of Robert Jordan's The Wheel of Time', it lies in the overwhelming amount of information presented. While this is undoubtedly a testament to the richness of the world Robert Jordan created, it can also be intimidating to readers who are new to the series. Those familiar with 'The Wheel of Time' will no doubt be thrilled by the depth and breadth of the world presented, but those who are new to the series may find themselves struggling to keep up.

In conclusion, 'The World of Robert Jordan's The Wheel of Time' is a triumph of epic world-building and a testament to Robert Jordan's skill and craftsmanship. This companion book is a must-read for fans of the series, offering a wealth of background information and world-building detail that will deepen our understanding of this beloved world. While it may present a challenge for new readers, 'The World of Robert Jordan's The Wheel of Time' remains an essential read in the world of fantasy literature.

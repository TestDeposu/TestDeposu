# Poet Anderson ...of Nightmares

**Author:** Tom DeLonge  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

Enter a world where the boundary between sleep and wakefulness dissolves into a high-octane neon nightmare. In Poet Anderson ...of Nightmares (2015), visionary musician and filmmaker Tom DeLonge teams up with New York Times bestselling author Suzanne Young to deliver a breathless young adult sci-fi adventure that has been aptly hailed as a teenage Blade Runner. Part of DeLonge’s expansive transmedia universe, this award-winning novel plunges readers headfirst into the terrifying, magnificent expanse of the human subconscious, exploring themes of brotherhood, grief, and the paralyzing power of fear.

At its emotional core, the narrative follows orphan brothers Jonas and Alan. When a devastating car accident leaves Alan comatose, a desperate Jonas plunges into the fragile realm of lucid dreaming, hoping to drag his older brother back to the waking world. Instead, he discovers a vast, shared collective consciousness—a vivid, breathing dreamscape that is currently under siege. Here, the darkest anxieties of humanity manifest as ferocious, snarling beasts known as Night Terrors, while a malevolent entity named REM hungers for total destruction, devouring the souls of the strongest dreamers. To save Alan and survive this perilous odyssey, Jonas must embrace a legendary mantle and evolve into something greater: a Dream Walker, and ultimately, Poet Anderson.

DeLonge’s unmistakable creative DNA pulses through every page of the book. Known widely as the creative force behind blink-182 and Angels & Airwaves, DeLonge infuses the literary space with the same cinematic grandeur, atmospheric world-building, and restless youthful rebellion that define his music—a synergy further cemented by the accompanying Angels & Airwaves EP of the same name. Paired with Suzanne Young’s polished, fast-paced storytelling and knack for emotional resonance, the novel thrives on a breathless momentum. The authors construct a visceral, neon-drenched dreamscape that feels simultaneously surreal and intimately real, balancing frantic, heart-pounding action sequences with tender moments of familial devotion. 

The central theme of the novel examines how we confront our inner demons. The dream world acts as a literal mirror to the waking mind, where fear is not just an emotion, but a physical predator capable of consuming a person whole. Jonas’s journey from a grieving, helpless younger brother to a fearless guardian of the subconscious is a striking metaphor for growing up, facing trauma, and conquering the anxieties that lurk in the dark corners of the human psyche. 

Poet Anderson ...of Nightmares is a wildly imaginative, cinematic thrill ride that blurs the lines between reality and imagination. It demands to be read in a single sitting, leaving readers breathless, spellbound, and looking at their own dreams just a little differently.

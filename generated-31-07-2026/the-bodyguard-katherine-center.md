# The Bodyguard

**Author:** Katherine Center  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**The Bodyguard by Katherine Center: A Rom-Com with Heart, Humor, and Hidden Depths**

Katherine Center’s *The Bodyguard* is the kind of book that feels like a long-overdue hug—warm, witty, and impossible to put down. Blending the charm of classic nineties rom-coms with the emotional resonance of Center’s signature storytelling, this novel is a delightful escape with a side of soulful introspection. At its core, it’s a story about trust, vulnerability, and the messy, beautiful ways love can find us when we least expect it.

Hannah Brooks is a woman who looks like she belongs in a kindergarten classroom, not in the high-stakes world of executive protection. But when she’s hired to guard Hollywood heartthrob Jack Stapleton from a relentless stalker, she discovers that her quiet competence hides a sharp, resourceful mind. Jack, on the other hand, is a man who once ruled the silver screen but has since retreated from the spotlight, haunted by grief and a desire to protect his family from the chaos of his past. Their dynamic is electric—Hannah’s no-nonsense pragmatism clashes with Jack’s effortless charm, but beneath the banter, there’s a quiet understanding that neither of them is as invincible as they pretend to be.

The novel’s premise is pure rom-com gold: a reluctant bodyguard and a famous actor forced into a fake relationship to keep secrets safe. But Center elevates it with layers of emotional depth. Hannah’s journey is particularly compelling as she navigates her own past—including a painful breakup that left her questioning her worth—and learns to open her heart again. Jack, meanwhile, is a man learning to confront his own vulnerabilities, especially when his mother’s illness forces him to return to the family ranch he’s avoided for years. Their relationship isn’t just about the spark between them; it’s about the healing that comes from being seen, truly seen, by someone else.

Center’s writing is a masterclass in balance—she keeps the humor light and the romance sizzling, but she never shies away from the heavier moments. The banter between Hannah and Jack is razor-sharp, full of playful teasing and underlying tension, while the supporting characters—from Jack’s eccentric family to Hannah’s sharp-witted friends—add depth and warmth to the story. The setting, too, plays a crucial role: Texas’s vast landscapes and small-town charm provide a perfect backdrop for the emotional unraveling of both protagonists.

What makes *The Bodyguard* truly special is how it weaves together the escapism of a rom-com with the emotional authenticity that makes Center’s work stand out. It’s a story about pretending to be someone you’re not, only to realize that the person you’ve been hiding from might just be the one who sees you the clearest. By the end, you won’t just be laughing and swooning—you’ll be rooting for Hannah and Jack in a way that lingers long after the last page.

If you’ve ever loved a rom-com that made you laugh, cry, and feel a little less alone, *The Bodyguard* is the book for you. It’s a celebration of second chances, the courage to be vulnerable, and the idea that sometimes, the people who get under your skin are the ones who matter most.

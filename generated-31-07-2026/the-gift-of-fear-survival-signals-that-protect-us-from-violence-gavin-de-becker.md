**Author:** Gavin de Becker  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

The Gift of Fear: Survival Signals That Protect Us from Violence, written by Gavin de Becker, is a thought-provoking and insightful book that delves into the realm of human intuition and its role in protecting us from harm. With a release year of 2024, this book is a timely reminder of the importance of trusting our instincts and listening to our inner voice. Through a comprehensive analysis of 15 chapters, de Becker skillfully weaves together a narrative that is both informative and engaging, making it an essential read for anyone interested in personal safety and self-preservation.

One of the primary strengths of the book is its ability to empower readers with the knowledge and tools necessary to recognize and respond to potential threats. De Becker argues that our intuition is a powerful ally in the fight against violence, and that by learning to trust and listen to our instincts, we can significantly reduce our risk of becoming victims. This message is both reassuring and unsettling, as it forces readers to confront the reality of the world we live in and the dangers that lurk within it.

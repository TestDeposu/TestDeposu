# Savor the Moment (Bride Quartet, #3)

**Author:** Nora Roberts  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Savor the Moment: Nora Roberts Serves Up a Delicious Slice of Romance

When it comes to contemporary romance, few authors possess the innate, crowd-pleasing mastery of Nora Roberts. With Savor the Moment, the third installment in her beloved Bride Quartet, Roberts returns to the bustling, aesthetically immaculate world of Vows, an elite wedding-planning agency run by four lifelong best friends. Having already explored the romance of behind-the-scenes photography and floral design in previous volumes, Roberts turns her perceptive eye toward the culinary arts, crafting a story where sugar, spice, and stubborn emotional walls collide. 

At the center of this confection is Laurel McBane, Vows’ resident wedding baker. Unlike her partners who lean into the opulent, fairy-tale fantasies of their high-end clientele, Laurel is grounded, artistic, and fiercely practical. She views her wedding cakes not just as centerpieces, but as edible architecture and fleeting canvases. Yet, despite her pragmatic exterior, Laurel harbors a deeply entrenched vulnerability, particularly regarding her long-standing, seemingly hopeless crush on Delaney Brown. 

Delaney is an Ivy League lawyer, the older brother of Parker (one of Laurel’s best friends), and the epitome of protective, reliable masculinity. He is a man who reads briefs by day and maintains firm boundaries by night—especially when it comes to the fiery, unpredictable baker who has quietly anchored his thoughts for years. Roberts establishes their dynamic with her signature pacing, shifting away from instant-gratification tropes in favor of a slow-burn tension built on shared history, witty banter, and deep-seated mutual respect. 

What elevates Savor the Moment above standard genre fare is Roberts’s exploration of its central theme: the delicate balance between the idealized perfection of a wedding day and the messy, unscripted reality of enduring love. Weddings are staged for the cameras, constructed to look effortless, but Laurel and Del’s romance proves that real intimacy is forged in the kitchen’s chaotic heat, during moments of frustration, artistic doubt, and raw honesty. When a long-simmering attraction finally boils over into an unexpected, mind-blowing kiss, both characters are forced to dismantle their carefully constructed defenses. Laurel must quiet her persistent self-doubt, while Del has to step out of his protective comfort zone and risk vulnerability.

Roberts’s authorial style shines brightest in her sensory world-building and character chemistry. She writes with a cinematic clarity that makes the sights, sounds, and—most importantly—the rich, sugary smells of Laurel’s bakery leap off the page. The dialogue snaps with authentic warmth, capturing the fierce, unbreakable bond of female friendship that anchors the entire Quartet series. The interactions between Laurel, Parker, Emma, and Mac provide a comforting, sisterly anchor that balances the romantic friction between the lead couple.

Ultimately, Savor the Moment is a thoroughly satisfying read that lives up to its appetizing title. Roberts expertly blends emotional depth with lighthearted escapism, delivering a romance that is as comforting as a home-baked treat yet sophisticated enough to satisfy the most demanding romance aficionados. Whether you are catching up with old friends at Vows or diving into Laurel and Del’s story as a standalone, this novel proves that Nora Roberts remains the undisputed queen of contemporary comfort romance.

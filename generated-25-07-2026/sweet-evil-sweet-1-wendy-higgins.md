# Sweet Evil (Sweet, #1)

**Author:** Wendy Higgins  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In the realm of young adult paranormal romance, Wendy Higgins's Sweet Evil is a thrilling and captivating tale that will leave readers enticed and yearning for more. This debut novel, published in 2012, is a masterful blend of good vs. evil, forbidden love, and self-discovery, set against the backdrop of a richly detailed world of angels and demons.

At the heart of the story lies Anna Whitt, a tenderhearted and naive Southern girl who possesses a unique gift - the ability to see and feel the emotions of others. As she navigates the challenges of adolescence, Anna is drawn to Kaidan Rowe, a mysterious and alluring boy with a dark past. Their connection is undeniable, but it's also fraught with danger, as they come from different worlds - Anna is the daughter of a fallen angel, while Kaidan is the son of a demon.

Higgins's writing style is engaging and effortless, making it easy to become immersed in the world she has created. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with distinct voices and motivations. Anna's inner struggle to balance her good and evil sides is palpable, and her romance with Kaidan is sweet and tender, yet also intense and passionate.

One of the standout aspects of Sweet Evil is its exploration of the gray areas between good and evil. Higgins challenges the notion that people can be neatly categorized into one or the other, instead presenting a world where characters exist on a spectrum. This moral ambiguity adds depth and complexity to the story, making it more than just a simple tale of good vs. evil.

The supernatural elements in the book are also well-executed, with a unique twist on the traditional angel and demon lore. Higgins's world-building is meticulous, with a clear history and mythology that underpins the narrative. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and romance that keeps the reader engaged.

If you're a fan of Cassandra Clare's Mortal Instruments series, you'll likely devour Sweet Evil in one sitting. Higgins's writing style is reminiscent of Clare's, with a similar blend of action, romance, and supernatural drama. However, Sweet Evil has a distinct voice and tone that sets it apart from other books in the genre.

In conclusion, Sweet Evil is a captivating and thrilling read that will appeal to fans of young adult paranormal romance. With its unique blend of good vs. evil, forbidden love, and self-discovery, it's a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and engaging story.

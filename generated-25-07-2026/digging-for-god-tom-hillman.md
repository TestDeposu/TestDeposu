# Digging for God

**Author:** Tom Hillman  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

In Digging for God, Tom Hillman embarks on a unique and captivating journey, taking readers to a same-sex spiritual resort where the boundaries of conventional life are gently pushed aside. This thought-provoking book is an intimate and introspective account of the author's three-week stay at the retreat center, where he immerses himself in a world of meditation, communal living, and profound self-discovery.

Through Hillman's masterful storytelling, we are transported to a realm where the pursuit of joy, peace, and love supplants the relentless drive for material wealth and success. As we join him on this transformative journey, we are introduced to a cast of characters who have deliberately chosen to live outside the mainstream, seeking a more authentic and meaningful existence. The monks and spiritually motivated individuals who call the retreat center home are not merely background figures; they are fully fleshed-out personalities, each with their own unique stories and motivations.

One of the most striking aspects of Hillman's writing style is his ability to balance the deeply personal and often emotional nature of his experience with a keen observer's eye for detail. He deftly weaves together vivid descriptions of the natural surroundings, the rhythms of daily life at the retreat center, and the inner workings of his own mind and heart. As we follow him through the ups and downs of his journey, we are invited to reflect on our own values and priorities, and to consider the possibility of alternative ways of living.

The author's use of language is straightforward and unassuming, yet richly evocative and engaging. He has a gift for capturing the essence of a moment or a feeling, and for conveying the complexity and depth of human experience. Whether he is describing the silence and stillness of meditation, the warmth and camaraderie of communal meals, or the challenges and triumphs of personal growth, Hillman's prose is always accessible, yet never simplistic.

At its core, Digging for God is a book about the search for meaning and connection in a world that often seems to value the wrong things. It is a powerful exploration of the human spirit, and a testament to the enduring power of love, compassion, and community. As we journey with Hillman through the pages of this book, we are reminded that there are many ways to live a life of purpose and fulfillment, and that sometimes, the most profound discoveries await us in the unlikeliest of places.

Ultimately, Digging for God is a book that will resonate with anyone who has ever felt a sense of disconnection or discontent with the status quo. It is a call to awaken to new possibilities, to explore the depths of our own hearts and minds, and to embrace the beauty and diversity of the world around us. With its universal themes and inspiring message, this book has the potential to touch hearts and minds, and to leave a lasting impression on all who read it.

# Better Than the Movies (Better Than the Movies, #1)

**Author:** Lynn Painter  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

**Better Than the Movies: A Charming Rom-Com with Heart**

Lynn Painter's "Better Than the Movies" is a delightful addition to the teen rom-com genre, offering a fresh take on the classic 'girl-next-door-meets-boy-next-door' trope. This USA TODAY and New York Times bestseller is a sweet and funny tale that will resonate with fans of Kasie West and Jenn Bennett.

At the heart of the story is Liz Buxbaum, a hopeless romantic who's been pining for her childhood crush, Michael, since forever. When Michael returns to town, Liz is determined to make him notice her, even if it means enlisting the help of her annoying neighbor, Wes Bennett. Wes, with his obnoxious charm and prankster tendencies, is the last person Liz would choose as an ally, but she's desperate, and Wes has a surprising connection to Michael.

Painter's strength lies in her character development. Liz is relatable and endearing, her romantic daydreams providing a charming contrast to her real-life awkwardness. Wes, initially portrayed as the typical jerk neighbor, evolves into a complex character with depth and heart. Their dynamic is reminiscent of classic rom-com pairs, with a healthy dose of modern wit and banter.

The plot is predictable in the best way possible, following a familiar rom-com arc while still managing to surprise. Painter's writing style is engaging and humorous, with a keen eye for detail that brings the story to life. She masterfully balances humor and heart, making the reader invested in Liz's journey and rooting for her happiness.

"Better Than the Movies" explores the theme of love in all its forms - romantic, platonic, and self-love. It's a story about realizing that sometimes, the person you've been looking for has been right next door all along. Painter challenges the idea of a perfect, movie-like romance, instead presenting a more realistic, messy, and ultimately satisfying love story.

In a world filled with rom-coms, "Better Than the Movies" stands out as a charming and heartfelt addition. It's a perfect summer read, a feel-good story that will leave you with a smile on your face and a warm, fuzzy feeling in your heart.

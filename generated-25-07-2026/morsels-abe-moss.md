# Morsels

**Author:** Abe Moss  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

Morsels, the latest offering from Abe Moss, is a masterclass in building tension and subverting expectations. This relentlessly fast-paced supernatural horror novel promises to keep you up all night, and not just because you'll be too afraid to turn off the lights.

At its core, Morsels is a story about the horrors that lurk within the most seemingly idyllic of families. Connie, the protagonist, is nervous about meeting her boyfriend Evan's family, and for good reason. Evan has warned her that his parents are cold, snobbish, and downright unpleasant. But when they arrive at the family's ritzy woodland summer home, Connie is greeted with open arms by Evan's mother, who is warm, friendly, and welcoming.

As the story progresses, it becomes clear that something is off. Evan himself is confused by his parents' sudden change in behavior, and Connie begins to suspect that all is not as it seems. The tension builds slowly, as Connie starts to uncover the dark secrets that lurk beneath the surface of the family's perfect facade.

Moss's writing style is economical and effective, with a focus on building tension and creating a sense of unease. He uses the setting of the family's summer home to great effect, creating a sense of isolation and claustrophobia that adds to the overall sense of dread. The characters are well-drawn and complex, with Connie being a particularly strong protagonist.

One of the standout aspects of Morsels is its use of the "meet the parents" trope. Moss takes this familiar setup and turns it on its head, using it to explore themes of identity, class, and the horrors that can lurk within the most seemingly ordinary of families. The result is a story that is both a compelling family drama and a terrifying horror novel.

As the story builds towards its climax, the tension becomes almost unbearable. Moss's use of suspense and misdirection is masterful, keeping the reader guessing until the very end. And when the truth is finally revealed, it's both shocking and devastating.

Overall, Morsels is a gripping and unsettling horror novel that will appeal to fans of the genre. Moss's writing style is engaging and effective, and the story is both a compelling family drama and a terrifying horror novel. If you're looking for a book that will keep you up all night, Morsels is a must-read.

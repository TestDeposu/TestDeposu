# The Edge of Never (The Edge of Never, #1)

**Author:** J.A. Redmerski  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

The Edge of Never by J.A. Redmerski is a poignant and captivating new adult romance that delves into the complexities of human emotions, the thrill of self-discovery, and the power of love. The story follows Camryn Bennett, a twenty-year-old who has lost her sense of purpose, and Andrew Parrish, a free-spirited individual who lives life on his own terms. As they embark on an epic road trip, they find themselves entwined in a dance of love, lust, and emotion.

Redmerski's writing style is evocative and immersive, weaving a narrative that is both addictive and fast-paced. The author masterfully crafts the characters of Camryn and Andrew, imbuing them with depth and nuance. Camryn's transformation from an emotionally numb individual to someone who is willing to take risks and confront her deepest desires is a compelling arc that drives the story forward. Andrew, on the other hand, is a mystery waiting to be unraveled, with a secret that threatens to upend their blossoming relationship.

The romance between Camryn and Andrew is slow-burning and sensual, making for a real connection that gets lost in other books. Redmerski pours passion into this book, and it is evident in the way she writes about the emotional intimacy between the two characters. The author's use of language is lyrical and expressive, conveying the intensity of their emotions and the all-consuming nature of their love.

One of the most striking aspects of The Edge of Never is its exploration of the human condition. Redmerski delves into themes of identity, vulnerability, and the search for meaning in life. Camryn's journey is a powerful reminder that it's never too late to take a chance, to break free from the constraints of our past, and to forge a new path. Andrew's character serves as a catalyst for Camryn's growth, encouraging her to confront her fears and to live in the moment.

The Edge of Never is a testament to the power of love to transform and redeem. Redmerski's writing is all-consuming and riveting, making it impossible to put the book down. The author's use of vivid imagery and descriptive language transports the reader to the world of Camryn and Andrew, immersing them in the beauty and complexity of their relationship.

In conclusion, The Edge of Never is a captivating and poignant new adult romance that will touch readers' hearts. Redmerski's writing style is evocative and immersive, and her exploration of the human condition is both thought-provoking and relatable. If you're looking for a book that will make you laugh, cry, and reflect on the complexities of life, then this is the perfect read for you.

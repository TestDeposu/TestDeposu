# I Am Number Four (Lorien Legacies, #1)

**Author:** Pittacus Lore  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In "I Am Number Four," Pittacus Lore crafts a captivating narrative that seamlessly blends science fiction, action, and coming-of-age themes, drawing readers into a world of intergalactic warfare and self-discovery. The first installment of the Lorien Legacies series, this book introduces us to John Smith, an alien from the planet Lorien who has been living in hiding on Earth with his guardian Henri.

On the surface, John appears to be an ordinary teenager navigating high school and adolescence. However, his life is a carefully constructed facade, designed to keep him safe from the Mogadorians, a ruthless alien species that has been hunting down the remaining Lorien children. As Number Four, John is acutely aware that he is being pursued, and the constant need to stay one step ahead of his enemies creates a palpable sense of tension throughout the story.

Lore's writing style is engaging and accessible, making it easy for readers to become invested in John's journey. The author's use of vivid descriptions and concise language effectively conveys the complexities of John's world, from the intricacies of Lorien's history to the inner workings of his Legacies. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action-packed sequences and quieter moments that allow for character development and introspection.

One of the most compelling aspects of "I Am Number Four" is its exploration of the main theme: identity. As John struggles to reconcile his Lorien heritage with his human upbringing, he must confront the challenges of belonging and finding his place in the world. This theme is skillfully woven throughout the narrative, adding depth and nuance to the story.

The supporting characters in the book are equally well-crafted, with Henri serving as a steady source of guidance and support for John. The romance between John and Sarah, a human classmate, adds a sweet and tender element to the story, while the villainous Mogadorians provide a formidable and relentless threat.

While some readers may find the sci-fi elements and action sequences reminiscent of other popular series, such as "The 5th Wave" and "The Maze Runner," Lore's unique spin on the genre sets "I Am Number Four" apart. The book's cinematic feel, likely influenced by the author's pseudonym being a pen name for James Frey and Jobie Hughes, makes it an excellent choice for fans of science fiction and action movies.

In conclusion, "I Am Number Four" is a gripping and immersive read that will appeal to fans of science fiction, action, and coming-of-age stories. With its blend of intergalactic warfare, self-discovery, and romance, this book is an excellent addition to the young adult genre. As the first installment in the Lorien Legacies series, it sets the stage for a thrilling and epic adventure that will keep readers eagerly anticipating the next installment.

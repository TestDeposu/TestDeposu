# Polaris Rising (Consortium Rebellion, #1)

**Author:** Jessie Mihalik  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

Polaris Rising by Jessie Mihalik is a gripping and seductive space opera that catapults readers into a world of high-stakes politics, action-packed adventures, and forbidden romance. This captivating novel follows the journey of Ada von Hasenberg, a fearless and determined space princess, as she navigates a complex web of intrigue and deception to claim her freedom.

Mihalik's writing style is characterized by its fast-paced and engaging narrative, which masterfully balances elements of science fiction, adventure, and romance. The author's vivid descriptions of the futuristic universe and its diverse array of characters create a rich and immersive reading experience. The world-building is meticulous, with a detailed and complex hierarchy of power that underpins the story.

At its core, Polaris Rising is a tale of self-discovery and empowerment. Ada, the protagonist, is a strong and independent individual who refuses to be bound by the conventions of her royal upbringing. Her desire for autonomy and self-determination drives the plot forward, as she navigates a treacherous landscape of politics and rebellion.

The character of Marcus Loch, the notorious outlaw soldier, is a perfect foil to Ada's determination and spirit. Their unlikely alliance is forged in the heat of adversity, and their romance is a slow-burning fire that simmers beneath the surface of the narrative. Mihalik's portrayal of their relationship is nuanced and realistic, avoiding the clichés of the "damsel in distress" trope and instead presenting a partnership of equals.

One of the most striking aspects of Polaris Rising is its exploration of the complexities of power and loyalty. The Royal Consortium, with its rigid hierarchy and ruthless pursuit of power, serves as a commentary on the dangers of unchecked authority. The characters' struggles with their own loyalties and allegiances add depth and complexity to the narrative, raising questions about the true meaning of honor, duty, and loyalty.

In conclusion, Polaris Rising is a compelling and addictive read that will appeal to fans of science fiction, adventure, and romance. Mihalik's writing style is engaging and immersive, and her characters are multidimensional and relatable. This novel is a must-read for anyone looking for a strong and empowering female protagonist, a richly detailed universe, and a story that will keep them on the edge of their seat.

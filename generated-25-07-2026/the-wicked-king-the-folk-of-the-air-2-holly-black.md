# The Wicked King (The Folk of the Air, #2)

**Author:** Holly Black  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

The Wicked King, Holly Black's enthralling sequel to The Cruel Prince, plunges readers back into the treacherous world of Faerie, where power is a deadly game and allegiances are as fleeting as the wind. At the heart of this mesmerizing tale is Jude, a human with a will of iron, determined to protect her brother Oak, the rightful heir to the Faerie throne, and navigate the labyrinthine complexities of Faerie politics.

With Cardan, the wicked king, bound to her will, Jude must walk a razor's edge, balancing her own desires with the need to maintain control in a world where mortals are mere pawns. Black masterfully weaves a web of intrigue, as Jude confronts the dark underbelly of Faerie, where loyalty is a luxury few can afford, and the price of power is paid in blood and betrayal.

One of Black's greatest strengths as a writer is her ability to craft characters that are both deeply flawed and utterly compelling. Jude, with her fierce determination and vulnerability, is a protagonist for the ages, and her complicated, often toxic relationship with Cardan is a highlight of the book. Theirs is a dance of power and desire, with each step carefully choreographed to keep the other off balance.

Black's prose is as lush and evocative as the world she creates, with a keen eye for detail that brings the faerie realm to vivid life. Her writing is both beautiful and brutal, reflecting the unforgiving nature of the world she's created. The pacing is relentless, with each twist and turn expertly calibrated to keep readers on the edge of their seats.

At its core, The Wicked King is a story about the true cost of power, and the difficult choices that must be made to maintain it. Black raises important questions about the nature of morality, and whether the ends ever truly justify the means. Jude's struggles to reconcile her own sense of self-worth with the harsh realities of Faerie are deeply resonant, and serve as a powerful reminder that even in the darkest of worlds, there is always hope.

In short, The Wicked King is a triumph, a book that will appeal to fans of fantasy, adventure, and romance alike. With its lush world-building, complex characters, and breakneck pacing, it's a must-read for anyone who's ever been captivated by the magic of Faerie.

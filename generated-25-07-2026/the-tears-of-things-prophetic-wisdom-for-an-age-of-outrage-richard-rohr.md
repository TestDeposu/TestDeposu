# The Tears of Things: Prophetic Wisdom for an Age of Outrage

**Author:** Richard Rohr  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

The Tears of Things: Prophetic Wisdom for an Age of Outrage, the latest work by renowned author Richard Rohr, is a profoundly moving and introspective exploration of the human condition, tackling the complexities of living compassionately in a world fraught with violence, despair, and injustice. Rohr, widely regarded as one of the most prominent spiritual voices of our time, delves into the timeless wisdom of the Hebrew prophets, offering a wholehearted and hope-filled model for navigating the challenges of the modern world.

Through a nuanced and deeply personal examination of the prophetic writings, Rohr reveals the transformative power of "sacred criticism," a distinct approach to confronting evil and injustice that acknowledges the interconnectedness of all living beings and the reality of a divine and universal love. By tracing the prophets' journey from anger to sadness, and ultimately to compassion, Rohr illuminates a path forward that is both deeply rooted in ancient wisdom and urgently relevant to our contemporary struggles.

One of the most striking aspects of Rohr's work is his ability to breathe new life into ancient texts, rendering them accessible and relatable to a wide range of readers. His warm, pastoral voice, honed through decades of writing and teaching, creates a sense of intimacy and immediacy, drawing the reader into a conversation that feels both deeply personal and universally applicable. As James Martin, SJ, so aptly puts it, "Richard Rohr is one of the great Christian spiritual masters of our time. Of any time."

The Tears of Things is, in many ways, a culmination of Rohr's life's work, distilling the insights and wisdom garnered from a lifetime of spiritual seeking and exploration. It is a book that will resonate deeply with anyone who has ever grappled with the contradictions and complexities of the human experience, seeking a way to live with compassion, empathy, and authenticity in a world that often seems to value none of these qualities.

Throughout the book, Rohr's masterful handling of biblical scholarship and his extensive knowledge of the prophetic tradition are evident, yet he wears his learning lightly, never allowing it to overshadow the deeper, more universal truths that he seeks to convey. Instead, he uses his vast expertise to illuminate the ways in which the prophetic writings continue to speak to us today, offering a clarion call to action, a summons to engage with the world around us in a spirit of love, justice, and compassion.

In a world that often seems to be torn apart by divisions and discord, The Tears of Things offers a urgently needed reminder of our shared humanity, a testament to the power of compassion and empathy to heal even the deepest wounds. It is a book that will challenge, inspire, and comfort readers, inviting them to embark on a journey of self-discovery, spiritual growth, and transformation.

In short, The Tears of Things is a masterpiece of spiritual writing, a work of profound wisdom, insight, and beauty that will linger in the reader's mind and heart long after the final page has been turned. It is a testament to Richard Rohr's status as one of the most important and influential spiritual voices of our time, and a powerful reminder of the enduring relevance and importance of the prophetic tradition.

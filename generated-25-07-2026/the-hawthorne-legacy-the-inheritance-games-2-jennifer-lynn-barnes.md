# The Hawthorne Legacy (The Inheritance Games, #2)

**Author:** Jennifer Lynn Barnes  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

The Hawthorne Legacy, the second installment in Jennifer Lynn Barnes' The Inheritance Games series, is a mesmerizing tale of wealth, privilege, and the intricate web of family dynamics. Avery Grambs, the protagonist, finds herself entangled in a world of opulence and mystery when she inherits the vast fortune of billionaire Tobias Hawthorne. The catch - she has no idea why or who Tobias Hawthorne is.

As Avery navigates the labyrinthine Hawthorne House, replete with secret passages and cryptic puzzles, she must contend with the four Hawthorne grandsons, each with their own agenda and motives. Grayson, the heir apparent, is convinced Avery is a conwoman, while Jameson views her as a twisted riddle to be solved. The brothers' dynamic is a fascinating exploration of sibling rivalry, loyalty, and the weight of expectation.

Barnes' writing style is a masterclass in suspense and intrigue, weaving together multiple plot threads with ease. Her prose is witty, engaging, and effortlessly paced, making it impossible to put the book down. The author's use of puzzles, riddles, and codes adds an extra layer of complexity, mirroring the intricate workings of the characters' minds.

The Hawthorne Legacy is, at its core, a story about the corrupting influence of power and the true cost of privilege. Barnes expertly exposes the dark underbelly of wealth and status, revealing the flaws and vulnerabilities of the seemingly invincible Hawthorne family. Avery, as an outsider, serves as a catalyst for change, forcing the family to confront their own demons and biases.

One of the most striking aspects of the book is its exploration of female agency and empowerment. Avery, despite being a fish-out-of-water in the world of the ultra-rich, refuses to be intimidated or belittled. Her determination and resourcefulness serve as a powerful counterpoint to the entitled, patriarchal society she finds herself in.

The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, with each character bringing their own unique perspective and conflicts to the table. The brothers, in particular, are skillfully drawn, their relationships with each other and Avery adding depth and nuance to the narrative.

In conclusion, The Hawthorne Legacy is a gripping, thought-provoking thriller that will appeal to fans of One of Us is Lying and Knives Out. With its intricate plot, complex characters, and social commentary, it's a must-read for anyone who enjoys a good mystery. Barnes' writing is addictive, and her ability to craft a compelling narrative is unparalleled.

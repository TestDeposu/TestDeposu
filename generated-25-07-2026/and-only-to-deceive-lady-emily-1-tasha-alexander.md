# And Only to Deceive (Lady Emily, #1)

**Author:** Tasha Alexander  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

In "And Only to Deceive," Tasha Alexander weaves a captivating tale of historical suspense, meticulously set against the backdrop of Victorian England. The novel follows Emily, a strong-willed and determined young woman, as she navigates the complexities of her life after the sudden death of her husband, Philip, the Viscount Ashton.

At its core, the book explores the theme of self-discovery and the struggle for women's independence in a patriarchal society. Emily, having accepted Philip's proposal as a means to escape her overbearing mother, finds herself lost and uncertain after his passing. However, as she delves into Philip's journals and becomes fascinated with his world of ancient antiquities, she begins to uncover a new sense of purpose and identity.

Alexander's writing style is engaging and atmospheric, transporting readers to the quiet corridors of the British Museum and the grand estates of Victorian England. Her meticulous research shines through in the detailed descriptions of ancient artifacts and the inner workings of the museum, adding a layer of authenticity to the narrative.

As Emily becomes embroiled in a mystery involving stolen artifacts, she finds herself torn between two prominent suitors, each with their own secrets and motivations. The twists and turns of the plot are expertly woven, keeping readers on the edge of their seats as Emily navigates the treacherous world of Victorian high society.

Through Emily's journey, Alexander sheds light on the restrictive roles women were forced to play in Victorian society. Emily's desire for knowledge and independence is constantly at odds with the expectations placed upon her as a woman of her social standing. As she struggles to assert her own identity, she must also confront the societal norms that seek to constrain her.

One of the most striking aspects of the book is Alexander's portrayal of Emily's growth and development. From a somewhat naive and complacent young woman, Emily evolves into a confident and determined individual, driven by her own desires and ambitions. Her intellectual pursuits and her passion for ancient history serve as a catalyst for her transformation, allowing her to break free from the constraints of her society.

In "And Only to Deceive," Tasha Alexander has crafted a compelling and engaging novel that will appeal to fans of historical fiction and mystery alike. With its strong female protagonist, intricate plot, and meticulously researched setting, this book is sure to delight readers looking for a captivating and immersive read.

# Six Crimson Cranes (Six Crimson Cranes, #1)

**Author:** Elizabeth Lim  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In "Six Crimson Cranes," Elizabeth Lim weaves a captivating tale that seamlessly blends elements of East Asian folklore and fairy tales, transporting readers to a richly imagined world of magic, mystery, and adventure. This enchanting fantasy follows the journey of Shiori'anma, a princess in exile, as she navigates a treacherous landscape of forbidden magic, ancient curses, and forgotten lore.

At its core, "Six Crimson Cranes" is a story about the power of self-discovery and the unbreakable bonds of family. Shiori's journey is one of transformation, as she learns to harness her forbidden magic and find her voice in a world where silence is her only protection. With each step, she must confront the darkness of her past and the secrets that have haunted her family for generations.

Lim's writing is a masterclass in atmosphere and tension, effortlessly balancing moments of breathtaking beauty with heart-pounding action sequences. Her prose is as delicate as the paper birds that play a pivotal role in the story, yet as fierce as the dragon that becomes Shiori's unlikely ally. The world-building is meticulous, with a keen attention to detail that immerses readers in the mystical realm of Kiata.

One of the most striking aspects of "Six Crimson Cranes" is its exploration of the complexities of female relationships. Shiori's complicated bond with her stepmother, Raikama, is a nuanced portrayal of the ways in which women can both hurt and heal each other. The dynamic between Shiori and her brothers, who are transformed into cranes by Raikama's curse, is equally compelling, highlighting the deep emotional connections that exist between siblings.

Throughout the book, Lim draws upon a diverse range of cultural influences, from the legend of Chang E to the Tale of the Bamboo Cutter. These elements are woven together with a deft hand, creating a narrative that is both uniquely Asian and universally relatable. The result is a story that feels both fresh and familiar, like a favorite fairy tale retold with a bold new twist.

Overall, "Six Crimson Cranes" is a stunning achievement in fantasy literature, one that will captivate fans of "Shadow and Bone" and "Caraval" alike. With its lush world-building, memorable characters, and pulse-pounding plot, this book is a must-read for anyone who loves a good fairy tale. So come and enter the enchanted world of Kiata, where magic and mystery await around every corner.

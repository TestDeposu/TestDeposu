# Phantasma (Wicked Games, #1)

**Author:** Kaylie Smith  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

Phantasma, the first installment in Kaylie Smith's Wicked Games series, is a dark and alluring tale that delves into the depths of human resilience, love, and the unrelenting pursuit of power. This gripping narrative is set in a world where the veil between life and death is perilously thin, and the characters must navigate the treacherous landscape of Phantasma, a cursed house that promises its winners a single wish, but at a steep and often deadly cost.

The story follows Ophelia, a young woman who has grown up surrounded by the supernatural, courtesy of her mother's profession as a necromancer. When her mother is brutally murdered, Ophelia's life is turned upside down, and she inherits not only her family's powerful magic but also their substantial debts. Her sister Genevieve, in a desperate bid to save their family, decides to participate in the Phantasma games, a decision that sets off a chain reaction of events that will test Ophelia's courage, wits, and resolve.

As Ophelia embarks on a perilous journey to rescue her sister, she finds herself entangled in a web of intrigue, deception, and temptation. The Phantasma house, with its labyrinthine corridors and opulent ballrooms, is a character in its own right, exerting a malign influence on its inhabitants and pushing them to confront their deepest fears. The games themselves are a masterclass in psychological manipulation, with each level presenting a new and terrifying challenge that forces the participants to confront their darkest selves.

Throughout the book, Smith's writing is evocative and immersive, conjuring up a world that is both fascinating and terrifying. Her prose is atmospheric and engaging, with a keen sense of pacing that propels the reader through the twists and turns of the narrative. The characters are well-developed and complex, with Ophelia being a particularly compelling protagonist. Her struggles to come to terms with her newfound powers and her inner demons are deeply relatable, and her growth throughout the story is both satisfying and authentic.

One of the standout features of Phantasma is its exploration of the human condition. Smith delves into themes of love, loss, and the corrupting influence of power, raising important questions about the nature of morality and the true cost of our desires. The romance that blossoms between Ophelia and the enigmatic Blackwell is a slow-burning fire that adds an extra layer of tension to the narrative, and their relationship is fraught with complications and misunderstandings.

If you're a fan of dark fantasy, romance, and suspense, then Phantasma is a must-read. With its unique blend of Caraval-esque games, a dash of spice, and a healthy dose of horror, this book is sure to keep you up all night, turning the pages with bated breath. As Aleks Dimoska so aptly puts it, Phantasma has everything that makes Romantasy great – a dark setting, a perilous game, a clever protagonist, and a phantom that will give you weak knees.

In short, Phantasma is a gripping and unsettling tale that will appeal to fans of Leigh Bardugo, Sarah J. Maas, and Victoria Aveyard. With its lush prose, complex characters, and heart-pumping action sequences, this book is an absolute page-turner that will leave you breathless and eager for more.

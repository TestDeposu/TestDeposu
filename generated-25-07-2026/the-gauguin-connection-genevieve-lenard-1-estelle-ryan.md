# The Gauguin Connection (Genevieve Lenard, #1)

**Author:** Estelle Ryan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

The Gauguin Connection, Estelle Ryan's 2012 debut novel, is a gripping art crime thriller that masterfully weaves together the worlds of art, crime, and autism. At the helm of this intricate web is Doctor Genevieve Lenard, a brilliant expert in nonverbal communication with a unique autistic mind. When her boss asks her to investigate the murder of a young artist, Genevieve is thrust out of her comfort zone and into a complex world of forgeries, corruption, and murder.

Ryan's writing style is engaging and accessible, making it easy to become immersed in the world of art crime and Genevieve's unique perspective. The author's use of Genevieve's autistic mind as a tool for investigation is both fascinating and thought-provoking, offering a fresh take on the traditional detective genre. Genevieve's analytical mind and attention to detail make her an excellent detective, and her struggles to navigate the complexities of human relationships add a layer of depth to the story.

As Genevieve delves deeper into the world of art crime, she is forced to team up with an unlikely ally - an international thief with a deep understanding of the art world. Their partnership is a highlight of the book, with a spark of tension and mutual respect that adds to the story's tension and intrigue.

One of the standout aspects of The Gauguin Connection is its well-researched and detailed portrayal of the art world. Ryan's knowledge of art history and the inner workings of the art market is impressive, and she weaves this information seamlessly into the narrative. The result is a story that is both informative and engaging, with a unique blend of art, crime, and suspense.

The Gauguin Connection is a gripping thriller that will appeal to fans of art crime fiction, particularly those who enjoy authors like Elizabeth Peters and Jonathan Gash. Ryan's unique protagonist and well-researched art world setting make for a compelling read, and the book's fast-paced plot will keep readers on the edge of their seats until the very end.

Overall, The Gauguin Connection is an impressive debut novel that showcases Estelle Ryan's skill as a writer and her unique perspective on the art crime genre. With its blend of art, crime, and suspense, this book is sure to appeal to a wide range of readers.

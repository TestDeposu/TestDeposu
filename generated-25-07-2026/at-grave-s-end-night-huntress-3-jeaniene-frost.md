# At Grave's End (Night Huntress, #3)

**Author:** Jeaniene Frost  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**At Grave's End: A Fangs-Flying, Heart-Pounding Vampire Thriller**

Jeaniene Frost's "At Grave's End," the third installment in her captivating Night Huntress series, is a thrilling ride through the dark, seductive world of vampires, where danger lurks in every shadow and love is a deadly game. Frost, with her signature blend of wit, action, and romance, delivers a story that will leave you breathless and eager for more.

At the heart of "At Grave's End" lies the complex, enthralling relationship between Cat Crawfield, a half-vampire, and Bones, her undead lover. Frost masterfully explores the theme of identity and acceptance, as Cat struggles to reconcile her humanity with her vampire nature. She's a heroine who's equal parts strength and vulnerability, a special agent who's learned to fight but is still learning to embrace her true self. This internal journey, coupled with the external threats she faces, makes Cat a protagonist worth rooting for.

Frost's prose is fast-paced and engaging, drawing you in from the first page. She has a knack for creating vivid, atmospheric settings, from the grimy underbelly of New Orleans to the eerie beauty of a vampire's lair. Her dialogue is sharp and witty, often laced with humor that provides a welcome respite from the tension and danger that permeate the story.

The plot is a thrilling mix of suspense, mystery, and romance. Cat and Bones are drawn into a deadly game of cat and mouse with a vengeful vampire from Bones's past. Meanwhile, Cat's true identity is exposed, putting her in the crosshairs of every bloodsucker with a grudge. Frost expertly weaves these threads together, building to a climax that's as satisfying as it is surprising.

Frost also excels at creating memorable secondary characters. From Cat's loyal friends to the rogue vampires she faces, each character brings something unique to the story. They're not just window dressing; they're fully realized individuals who enrich the narrative and add depth to the world Frost has created.

"At Grave's End" is more than just a vampire story. It's a tale of love, loss, and redemption. It's about finding your place in the world, even when that world is filled with darkness and danger. It's about the power of love to heal, to strengthen, and to save. And it's about the thrill of the chase, the joy of the fight, and the satisfaction of a hard-won victory.

In short, "At Grave's End" is a must-read for fans of paranormal romance, urban fantasy, and vampire lore. It's a book that will keep you up at night, not just because you can't put it down, but because you'll be too afraid to turn off the lights.

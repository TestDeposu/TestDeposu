# The Awakening (Darkest Powers, #2)

**Author:** Kelley Armstrong  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**The Awakening: A Dark, Thrilling Ride into the Supernatural**

Kelley Armstrong's "The Awakening," the second installment in her captivating Darkest Powers trilogy, is a gripping tale that masterfully blends the paranormal, suspense, and coming-of-age themes, leaving readers on the edge of their seats. The story picks up where "The Gathering" left off, following the harrowing journey of Chloe Saunders, a teenage necromancer, and her eclectic group of allies as they race against time to evade the sinister Edison Group and unravel the mysteries of their supernatural abilities.

Armstrong's signature style is evident throughout the novel, with her ability to create vivid, relatable characters that drive the plot forward. Chloe, our protagonist, is a refreshing heroine—she's flawed, vulnerable, yet fiercely determined, making her an easy character to root for. Her dynamic with the other members of her makeshift family—Simon, the charming sorcerer; Derek, the cynical werewolf; and Tori, the disgruntled witch—is one of the novel's strongest aspects. Their banter, camaraderie, and individual growth add depth to the story, making it more than just a thrilling chase.

The main theme of "The Awakening" is identity and acceptance, both of oneself and one's place in the world. Chloe, grappling with her newfound powers and the horrors she's witnessed, struggles to find her footing. Armstrong explores this theme with nuance and sensitivity, delving into the psychological toll that Chloe's experiences have taken on her. The Edison Group's experiments and the threat they pose serve as a stark reminder of the dangers that lurk in the shadows, adding an extra layer of tension to the narrative.

Armstrong's prose is fast-paced and engaging, with a perfect balance of action, dialogue, and introspection. She has a knack for creating suspense, with cliffhangers and plot twists that keep readers guessing until the very end. The supernatural elements are seamlessly integrated into the story, feeling both fresh and familiar, thanks to Armstrong's world-building skills.

"The Awakening" is a thrilling, dark adventure that will appeal to fans of young adult fiction, the supernatural, and suspenseful mysteries. Armstrong's ability to create compelling characters and a richly textured world makes this novel a standout in its genre. It's a must-read for anyone looking for an edge-of-your-seat, supernatural tale that will leave them eager for more.

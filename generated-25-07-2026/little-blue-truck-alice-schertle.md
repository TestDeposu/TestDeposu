# Little Blue Truck

**Author:** Alice Schertle  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

"Little Blue Truck" by Alice Schertle is a heartwarming bedtime tale that beautifully captures the essence of friendship and overcoming fears. As a #1 New York Times bestseller, this charming story has already won the hearts of millions of young readers and their parents alike. In this delightful tale, Little Blue Truck and his friend Toad are preparing for bed when a storm rolls in, bringing with it thunder, lightning, and a lot of noise. But instead of letting the storm scare them, they open their doors to other friends seeking safety and comfort.

Schertle's writing style is gentle, soothing, and perfect for a bedtime read. The rhyming text is catchy and easy to follow, making it a joy to read aloud. The story is simple yet effective, conveying a powerful message about the importance of friendship and community. As the storm rages on outside, the friends huddle together in the warm garage, finding comfort and safety in each other's company.

One of the most striking aspects of this book is its ability to tackle the theme of overcoming fear in a way that is accessible and relatable to young children. The storm can be a scary and intimidating experience, but Schertle shows that with the support of friends, it's possible to face your fears and feel brave. The story is full of clever touches, such as the use of onomatopoeic words like "Beep! Beep! Beep!" and "Shhh...", which add to the sensory experience of the story and make it more engaging for young readers.

The illustrations, although not reviewed here, are reportedly delightful and bring the story to life in a way that is both visually appealing and true to the spirit of the text. The characters are well-developed and relatable, with distinct personalities that shine through in their interactions with each other.

Overall, "Little Blue Truck" is a wonderful bedtime story that is sure to become a favorite among children and parents alike. Its soothing rhythm, gentle message, and lovable characters make it the perfect read for a calming and comforting bedtime experience. As the story comes to a close, the final "Shhh..." is sure to lull even the most restless of children into a peaceful slumber.

# A Discovery of Witches (All Souls, #1)

**Author:** Deborah Harkness  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**A Discovery of Witches: A Spellbinding Blend of History, Magic, and Romance**

In the vein of Diana Gabaldon's Outlander and Elizabeth Kostova's The Historian, Deborah Harkness's debut novel, A Discovery of Witches, weaves a captivating tale that transcends time, blending historical intrigue with a touch of the supernatural. The first installment of the All Souls series introduces Diana Bishop, a young historian and reluctant witch, who stumbles upon an ancient, enchanted manuscript hidden within the hallowed halls of Oxford's Bodleian Library.

Harkness, a historian herself, infuses her narrative with an unparalleled depth of historical detail, transporting readers to the cobbled streets and centuries-old libraries of Oxford. The story's rich tapestry is further enriched by Diana's discovery of Ashmole 782, a manuscript rumored to hold the secret to creating the Philosopher's Stone. This alchemical text, once owned by Elias Ashmole, sets in motion a chain of events that draws Diana into a hidden world of vampires, daemons, and witches, each vying for control of the powerful artifact.

At the heart of A Discovery of Witches lies a forbidden love story between Diana and Matthew Clairmont, a brilliant vampire geneticist. Their relationship, fraught with danger and defying the laws of their respective species, is as enchanting as it is perilous. Harkness masterfully explores the complexities of their connection, weaving a tale of passion, obsession, and the lengths one will go to protect their love.

Harkness's prose is elegant and engaging, drawing the reader in with her vivid descriptions and intricate plotlines. She expertly balances the historical with the fantastical, creating a world that feels both familiar and otherworldly. Her characters are equally compelling, each with their own unique histories and motivations, adding layers of depth to the story.

A Discovery of Witches is more than just a vampire romance; it's a thought-provoking exploration of power, prejudice, and the lengths we go to protect those we love. Harkness challenges societal norms and encourages readers to question their own biases, all while delivering a thrilling, page-turning narrative.

Fans of historical fiction, fantasy, and romance will find much to love in A Discovery of Witches. It's a captivating tale that will leave you eager for more, and with the All Souls series continuing in three more installments, there's plenty more to explore in this enchanting world.

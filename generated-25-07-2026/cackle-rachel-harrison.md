# Cackle

**Author:** Rachel Harrison  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In Rachel Harrison's captivating novel, Cackle, the lines between good and evil are masterfully blurred, plunging readers into a darkly comedic world where the pursuit of self-discovery becomes a sinister game of cat and mouse. Annie, the protagonist, is a relatable and endearing character, whose life has been defined by caution and people-pleasing. Her recent breakup and decision to leave Manhattan for a quaint village upstate serve as the catalyst for her transformation, and it is here that she meets the enigmatic Sophie.

Sophie is the embodiment of the idyllic woman Annie aspires to be – confident, charismatic, and unapologetic. As their friendship deepens, Annie becomes increasingly enthralled by Sophie's carefree spirit and her ability to live life on her own terms. However, it soon becomes apparent that Sophie's charm is laced with an unsettling aura, and the townspeople's wariness of her is not entirely unfounded. As the narrative unfolds, Harrison skillfully weaves together a complex web of suspense and intrigue, leaving the reader questioning Sophie's true nature and intentions.

One of the standout aspects of Cackle is Harrison's masterful exploration of the theme of female empowerment. Annie's journey is a powerful allegory for the dangers of internalized misogyny and the importance of self-love. Through her relationship with Sophie, Annie is forced to confront the societal expectations that have held her back, and the novel becomes a scathing critique of the patriarchal norms that govern women's lives. Harrison's prose is witty and engaging, imbuing the narrative with a dark humor that adds depth and complexity to the story.

Harrison's writing style is reminiscent of the works of Ottessa Moshfegh and Shirley Jackson, with a unique blend of psychological insight and atmospheric tension. Her use of language is evocative and immersive, conjuring the eerie and idyllic settings of the village and Sophie's mansion with ease. The pacing is expertly handled, with a slow-burning tension that culminates in a shocking climax.

If you're a fan of dark, feminist fiction with a touch of the supernatural, then Cackle is a must-read. Harrison's sophomore novel is a tour-de-force, a deliciously wicked exploration of the human psyche that will leave you sleeping with the lights on. With its thought-provoking themes, vivid prose, and unforgettable characters, Cackle is a novel that will linger in your mind long after the final page has been turned.

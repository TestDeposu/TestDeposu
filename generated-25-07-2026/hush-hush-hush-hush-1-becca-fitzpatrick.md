# Hush, Hush (Hush, Hush, #1)

**Author:** Becca Fitzpatrick  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**Hush, Hush: A Whisper of Forbidden Love**

Becca Fitzpatrick's debut novel, "Hush, Hush," is a captivating blend of romance, suspense, and the supernatural, set against the backdrop of a typical American high school. The story revolves around Nora Grey, a seemingly ordinary teenager whose life takes an extraordinary turn when she meets the enigmatic Patch.

Fitzpatrick's narrative style is engaging and accessible, drawing readers in with its relatable protagonist and a plot that unfolds at a steady pace. Nora's voice is authentic, her internal struggle between her attraction to Patch and her fear of the unknown creating a compelling tension that drives the story forward. The author's use of foreshadowing and cliffhangers is masterful, keeping readers on the edge of their seats and eager to unravel the mysteries that surround Patch.

The central theme of "Hush, Hush" is forbidden love, a timeless trope that Fitzpatrick infuses with a fresh twist. Nora's connection with Patch is undeniable, yet fraught with danger. He is a fallen angel, bound by ancient rules and entangled in a centuries-old conflict. Their love story is a dance of attraction and repulsion, as Nora grapples with her feelings for Patch and the terrifying truths he reveals.

Fitzpatrick's exploration of the angel mythology is intriguing, weaving a complex tapestry of power struggles, ancient curses, and redemption. The world she creates is dark and dangerous, yet filled with moments of lightness and humor, courtesy of Nora's best friend, Vee. The secondary characters are well-developed, each adding depth to the story and providing a counterpoint to Nora's journey.

However, "Hush, Hush" is not without its flaws. The pacing can feel uneven at times, with some scenes lingering too long while others are rushed. The resolution of the main conflict also feels a tad too convenient, leaving some loose ends that are addressed in subsequent books in the series.

Despite these minor drawbacks, "Hush, Hush" is a compelling read that will appeal to fans of paranormal romance, suspense, and coming-of-age stories. Fitzpatrick's ability to create a captivating narrative and engaging characters makes this a standout debut. It's a book that will leave you breathless, eager for more, and whispering, "Hush, hush."

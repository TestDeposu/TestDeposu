# Born at Midnight (Shadow Falls, #1)

**Author:** C.C. Hunter  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**Born at Midnight: A Mesmerizing Plunge into the Enchanting World of Shadow Falls**

C.C. Hunter's "Born at Midnight," the inaugural novel in her captivating Shadow Falls series, is a spellbinding blend of paranormal romance, self-discovery, and coming-of-age, sure to enthrall fans of supernatural YA fiction. Hunter, a master of her craft, weaves a compelling tale that deftly balances intrigue, humor, and heart-wrenching emotion, leaving readers eager for more.

At its core, "Born at Midnight" explores the universal theme of identity and belonging. Protagonist Kylie Galen, a seemingly ordinary teenager, finds her world turned upside down when she discovers she's not like everyone else. Her journey of self-discovery, from a girl who feels like an outsider to a young woman embracing her true nature, is a relatable and engaging narrative that will resonate with readers.

Hunter's prose is fluid and engaging, drawing readers in from the very first page. She expertly balances the paranormal elements with everyday teenage life, creating a richly textured world that feels both fantastical and familiar. The diverse cast of characters, each with their unique abilities and quirks, brings Shadow Falls to life, making it a place readers will want to visit again and again.

The romantic tension between Kylie, Derek, and Lucas is palpable, yet never overshadows the main plot. Hunter skillfully navigates the complexities of their relationships, allowing readers to fully invest in each character's journey. The love triangle, while a common trope, feels fresh and genuine, thanks in no small part to the well-developed characters and their authentic interactions.

"Born at Midnight" is more than just a paranormal romance; it's a story about finding one's place in the world, about understanding and accepting who we are. Hunter's exploration of these themes, coupled with her captivating storytelling, makes "Born at Midnight" a standout novel in the YA paranormal genre.

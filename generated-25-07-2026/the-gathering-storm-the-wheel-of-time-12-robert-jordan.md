# The Gathering Storm (The Wheel of Time, #12)

**Author:** Robert Jordan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

The Gathering Storm, the twelfth installment in the Wheel of Time series, is a masterful blend of fantasy and adventure that will captivate readers from start to finish. Written by Robert Jordan and completed by Brandon Sanderson after Jordan's passing, this book is a testament to the enduring legacy of the Wheel of Time series.

At its core, The Gathering Storm is a story about the impending apocalypse, as the Dark One, the primary antagonist, breaks free from his prison and threatens to destroy the world. Against this backdrop, Moiraine Sedai, a powerful Aes Sedai, must navigate treacherous landscapes and forge alliances with old and new friends to prevent the impending doom. The story is a classic tale of good vs. evil, with the fate of the world hanging precariously in the balance.

One of the standout features of The Gathering Storm is its richly detailed world-building. Jordan's creation, the Wheel of Time universe, is a sprawling, intricate tapestry of cultures, histories, and mythologies that are woven together seamlessly. From the mystical Aes Sedai to the rugged Two Rivers folk, each character is imbued with a sense of depth and history that makes them feel fully realized and relatable.

Jordan's writing style is also noteworthy, as he effortlessly weaves together multiple plot threads and character arcs into a cohesive narrative. His prose is descriptive and evocative, painting vivid pictures of the world and its inhabitants. The pacing is well-balanced, with moments of high action and suspense tempered by quieter moments of character development and introspection.

One of the most striking aspects of The Gathering Storm is its exploration of the human condition. As the characters navigate the treacherous landscape, they are forced to confront their own mortality, the nature of good and evil, and the consequences of their actions. These themes are timeless and universal, making the book a compelling read for fans of fantasy and literature alike.

The collaboration between Jordan and Sanderson is also noteworthy, as Sanderson seamlessly integrates Jordan's notes and outlines into the narrative. The result is a book that feels authentic and true to the spirit of the series, while also introducing new elements and themes that add depth and complexity to the story.

In conclusion, The Gathering Storm is a masterful addition to the Wheel of Time series, offering a richly detailed world, compelling characters, and a gripping narrative that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. With its exploration of the human condition, intricate world-building, and classic tale of good vs. evil, this book is a must-read for fans of fantasy and literature.

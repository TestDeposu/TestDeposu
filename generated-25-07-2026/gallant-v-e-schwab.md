# Gallant

**Author:** V.E. Schwab  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

In the hauntingly beautiful novel "Gallant," V.E. Schwab masterfully weaves a dark and original tale that delves into the mysteries of the human heart and the blurred lines between reality and the unknown. This standalone novel is a captivating blend of gothic horror, whimsy, and self-discovery, set against the atmospheric backdrop of a crumbling English manor house.

At the center of the story is Olivia Prior, a young woman with a troubled past and a deep-seated desire to belong. Growing up in the confines of Merilance School for Girls, Olivia's only connection to her family's history is her mother's journal, which hints at a dark and troubled legacy. When she receives a letter inviting her to return to her family's ancestral home, Gallant, Olivia sees an opportunity to uncover the secrets that have haunted her family for generations.

Upon her arrival, Olivia is met with hostility and resistance from her cousin Matthew, but she is undeterred in her quest to uncover the truth. As she explores the labyrinthine halls of Gallant, Olivia begins to experience strange and terrifying occurrences that suggest the house is home to malevolent forces. But it is not until she crosses into a parallel world, a dark mirror image of Gallant, that Olivia discovers the true extent of her family's secrets and the source of their power.

Throughout the novel, Schwab's vivid prose and masterful world-building create a sense of creeping unease and tension, drawing the reader into the dark and foreboding world of Gallant. The author's use of language is deliberate and evocative, conjuring images of crumbling stone, flickering candles, and the musty scent of decay. The result is a novel that is both a gripping horror story and a poignant exploration of the human condition.

One of the most striking aspects of "Gallant" is its exploration of the theme of belonging and identity. Olivia's journey is not just about uncovering the secrets of her family's past, but also about finding her place in the world. As she navigates the treacherous landscape of Gallant, Olivia must confront her own demons and make a choice between embracing her family's legacy or forging her own path.

Schwab's writing style is characteristic of her unique blend of dark whimsy and atmospheric horror. Fans of Neil Gaiman and Holly Black will appreciate the novel's eerie and suspenseful tone, while readers of Melissa Albert and Garth Nix will enjoy the intricate world-building and complex characters. The result is a novel that is both a compelling page-turner and a thought-provoking exploration of the human experience.

In conclusion, "Gallant" is a mesmerizing and unsettling novel that will appeal to fans of dark fantasy and horror. With its vivid prose, masterful world-building, and poignant exploration of the human condition, this novel is a must-read for anyone who enjoys a good ghost story. So, if you're looking for a novel that will keep you up all night, with the lights on, then "Gallant" is the perfect choice.

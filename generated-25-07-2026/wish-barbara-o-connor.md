# Wish

**Author:** Barbara O'Connor  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

"Wish" by Barbara O'Connor is a heartwarming tale that beautifully captures the essence of family, friendship, and the unexpected joys that can be found in the most unlikely of places. The story revolves around 11-year-old Charlie Reese, a girl with a secret wish she's been making for years, and her journey to the Blue Ridge Mountains of North Carolina to live with family she barely knows.

O'Connor's writing style is a delight, painting vivid images of the rural setting and its quirky inhabitants with a gentle, almost whimsical touch. She masterfully weaves together the perspectives of Charlie, the wise and kind-hearted Aunt Louise, and the enigmatic Howard, creating a rich tapestry of voices that draw the reader in and keep them engaged.

The main theme of "Wish" is the fluidity and complexity of family, and how it's not always defined by blood relations. Charlie's journey is one of self-discovery, as she learns to navigate her new life and the unexpected connections she forms with the people and animals she meets. The bond she shares with Wishbone, the stray dog who captures her heart, is a testament to the power of love and loyalty, and serves as a catalyst for Charlie's growth.

O'Connor also explores the theme of belonging and the struggle to find one's place in the world. Charlie's initial resistance to her new life is palpable, but as she begins to form connections and find her footing, she learns that home is not a physical place, but a feeling of belonging and love.

"Wish" is a story that will resonate with readers of all ages, reminding us that sometimes, the things we think we want are not what we need, and that family can be found in the most unexpected of places. It's a poignant and uplifting tale that will leave readers with a warm, fuzzy feeling and a renewed appreciation for the simple joys in life.

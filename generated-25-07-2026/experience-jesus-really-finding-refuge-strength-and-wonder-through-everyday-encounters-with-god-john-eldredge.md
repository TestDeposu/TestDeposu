# Experience Jesus. Really: Finding Refuge, Strength, and Wonder through Everyday Encounters with God

**Author:** John Eldredge  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

In "Experience Jesus. Really: Finding Refuge, Strength, and Wonder through Everyday Encounters with God," John Eldredge invites readers to embark on a transformative journey of rediscovering the mystical presence of Jesus in their daily lives. With his signature storytelling style and profound insight, Eldredge masterfully guides readers through the path of the "ordinary mystic," illuminating the beauty of experiencing deep, lasting communion with Jesus.

In a world where technology and social media have become the dominant forces shaping our lives, Eldredge astutely observes that we are more disconnected than ever. We are addicted to distraction, idolizing our instant access to information, yet struggling with increasing levels of anxiety, dissatisfaction, and despair. Eldredge's thesis is clear: our soulless, disenchanted existence is a result of our disconnection from the divine. He invites readers to consider a beautiful alternative – a life of everyday encounters with God, as experienced by the likes of David, Daniel, Esther, and the apostles.

Throughout the book, Eldredge weaves together stories, practices, and prayers to help readers recover their capacity to believe, take refuge in God's presence, and discover the comfort of actual communion with Jesus. He draws on ancient biblical traditions, offering practical guidance on healing the soul, protecting the family, and reviving faith. Eldredge's writing is characterized by its accessibility, warmth, and vulnerability, making the book feel like a personal conversation with a trusted friend.

One of the book's greatest strengths lies in its ability to balance the mystical with the practical. Eldredge is not advocating for a life of withdrawal from the world, but rather an integration of the divine into everyday life. He offers practical advice on how to cultivate a deeper sense of awareness, listen to the voice of Jesus, and experience the refuge of God's presence in the midst of chaos.

Eldredge's writing is also marked by a deep sense of hope and longing. He believes that every human being yearns to return to a state of unbroken communion with God, to experience the adventure and intimacy that we were created for. This book is an invitation to embark on that journey, to experience the everyday refuge, strength, and wonder available to the friends of Jesus.

In "Experience Jesus. Really," John Eldredge has written a book that will resonate deeply with anyone seeking a more authentic, meaningful life. It is a call to awaken to the truth that God is still with us, and that we can experience the refuge of his powerful, healing presence if we learn to open our hearts to him.

# Silence (Hush, Hush, #3)

**Author:** Becca Fitzpatrick  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**Silence (Hush, Hush, #3) – A Fallen Angel’s Temptation and a Love Worth Fighting For**

Becca Fitzpatrick’s *Silence* is the third installment in the *Hush, Hush* saga, a series that blends forbidden romance, supernatural intrigue, and high-stakes drama into a gripping tale of love and sacrifice. If you’ve followed Nora Grey’s tumultuous journey with her fallen angel, Patch, this book will leave you breathless—partly from the emotional whiplash, partly from the sheer intensity of their relationship.

At its core, *Silence* explores the theme of **forbidden love and the choices that define us**. Nora and Patch’s relationship is a paradox: two beings from opposing worlds, drawn together by an undeniable pull, yet constantly torn apart by destiny, betrayal, and the looming threat of annihilation. Fitzpatrick masterfully weaves this theme into every interaction, making their love feel both inevitable and impossible. The tension isn’t just in the external conflict—it’s in the internal struggle of Nora, who must reconcile her growing feelings for Patch with the knowledge that he’s not just a boy, but a fallen angel with a dark past. Her journey is one of self-discovery, where love and survival are often at odds.

Fitzpatrick’s writing style is **fast-paced, immersive, and dripping with teenage angst**, which makes the story incredibly engaging for its target audience. The dialogue crackles with wit and tension, particularly in the banter between Nora and Patch, whose chemistry is electric. Their exchanges range from tender to explosive, mirroring the volatility of their relationship. The author also excels at **building suspense**, dropping clues and revelations at just the right moments to keep readers on the edge of their seats. The pacing is relentless, with each chapter ending on a cliffhanger that makes it nearly impossible to put the book down.

One of the standout elements of *Silence* is its **moral ambiguity**. Fitzpatrick doesn’t shy away from presenting Patch as a flawed, sometimes infuriating character—his past is littered with deception, and his actions often put Nora in danger. Yet, his love for her feels genuine, even if it’s complicated by his immortal nature. This duality makes him a compelling protagonist, one who forces readers to question whether love can truly transcend the boundaries of good and evil. Nora, too, is a strong but relatable heroine. Her internal conflict—wanting to trust Patch while fearing the consequences—makes her journey deeply personal and emotionally resonant.

The world-building in *Silence* is another strength. Fitzpatrick expands the lore of the fallen angels and the Nephilim, introducing new layers of mythology that deepen the stakes. The stakes themselves are sky-high, with Nora’s life hanging in the balance as she becomes entangled in an ancient war between the immortal and the fallen. The stakes aren’t just about survival—they’re about **choosing between love and duty, between the heart and the soul**.

If there’s one critique, it’s that the book occasionally leans too heavily into melodrama, particularly in the romantic subplots. Some secondary characters, like Nora’s best friend Vee, feel underdeveloped, and their motivations can come across as one-dimensional. However, these minor flaws don’t detract from the overall experience, especially when Fitzpatrick delivers moments of sheer brilliance—like the heart-wrenching confessions between Nora and Patch or the explosive confrontations that leave readers gasping.

*Silence* is a book that lingers long after the last page. It’s a story about the kind of love that defies logic, the kind that makes you question whether some bonds are worth the pain. Fitzpatrick’s ability to blend romance, action, and supernatural elements into a cohesive, addictive narrative is what makes this series a standout in the YA genre. Whether you’re a longtime fan or a newcomer, *Silence* will leave you desperate for more—because in a world where angels fall and love is forbidden, the only choice is to fight for what matters.

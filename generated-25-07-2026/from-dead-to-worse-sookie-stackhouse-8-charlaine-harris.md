# From Dead to Worse (Sookie Stackhouse, #8)

**Author:** Charlaine Harris  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

**From Dead to Worse: A Storm of Supernatural Intrigue**

Charlaine Harris, the mastermind behind the captivating Sookie Stackhouse series, returns with 'From Dead to Worse', the eighth installment that continues to weave a spellbinding tale of the supernatural set against the backdrop of post-Katrina Louisiana. Harris's signature style, a seamless blend of mystery, romance, and dark humor, is on full display, drawing readers into a world where vampires, werewolves, and other creatures of the night coexist with humans, often with explosive consequences.

The story picks up in the aftermath of Hurricane Katrina and the vampire summit explosion, leaving Sookie Stackhouse, our telepathic waitress heroine, battered but unbroken. Yet, her world is far from stable. Her boyfriend, Quinn, is missing, and the supernatural community is in turmoil. Harris masterfully explores the theme of change, both in Sookie's personal life and the broader supernatural landscape, as old alliances crumble and new ones form.

Harris's strength lies in her character development. Sookie, despite her extraordinary abilities, remains relatable, her struggles and triumphs resonating with readers. The supporting cast, from the enigmatic Bill Compton to the formidable Eric Northman, is equally compelling, each character bringing depth and complexity to the narrative.

The plot is a thrilling rollercoaster, filled with twists, turns, and betrayals that keep readers on the edge of their seats. Harris's prose is engaging, her dialogue sharp and witty, making 'From Dead to Worse' a page-turner that's hard to put down.

However, some readers might find the pacing a tad uneven, with certain plot points feeling rushed while others are drawn out. Yet, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise outstanding addition to the series.

In 'From Dead to Worse', Harris continues to push the boundaries of the paranormal romance genre, delivering a story that's equal parts suspenseful, romantic, and humorous. It's a must-read for fans of the series and a compelling entry point for new readers.

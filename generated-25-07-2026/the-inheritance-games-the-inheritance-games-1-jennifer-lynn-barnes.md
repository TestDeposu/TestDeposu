# The Inheritance Games (The Inheritance Games, #1)

**Author:** Jennifer Lynn Barnes  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

**The Inheritance Games: A Labyrinth of Wealth, Power, and Puzzles**

In the vein of *Knives Out* and *One of Us is Lying*, Jennifer Lynn Barnes' **The Inheritance Games** is a thrilling debut that weaves a web of intrigue, deception, and high-stakes games within the opulent walls of Hawthorne House. At its heart, this novel is a testament to the power of perseverance, the dangers of unchecked privilege, and the complexities of family dynamics.

Avery Grambs, our unlikely heroine, is a relatable everygirl with a plan for a better future. When billionaire Tobias Hawthorne dies and leaves her his fortune, Avery's world is turned upside down. To claim her inheritance, she must navigate the treacherous waters of Hawthorne House, filled with secrets, riddles, and the four Hawthorne grandsons—each more enigmatic and dangerous than the last.

Barnes masterfully crafts a labyrinth of suspense, with twists and turns that keep readers guessing until the very end. The story is peppered with puzzles and codes, echoing Tobias Hawthorne's love for games, and reflecting Barnes' own fascination with puzzles and riddles. This interactive element not only engages readers but also mirrors Avery's journey of unraveling the mysteries of Hawthorne House and its inhabitants.

The novel explores the main theme of power dynamics, particularly within the context of wealth and privilege. The Hawthorne family, dispossessed of their fortune, represents the old guard, while Avery, an outsider, embodies the disruptive force of new money. The tension between these two worlds is palpable, and Barnes expertly navigates this divide, challenging readers to question their own perceptions of power and entitlement.

Barnes' writing style is sharp and engaging, with a keen eye for detail that brings the lavish setting of Hawthorne House to life. Her characters are complex and well-developed, each with their own motivations and secrets. Avery, in particular, is a refreshing protagonist—smart, resourceful, and resilient, yet still vulnerable and relatable.

**The Inheritance Games** is more than just a thrilling mystery; it's a commentary on wealth, power, and the lengths people will go to protect or acquire them. Barnes' intricate plotting and engaging prose make this novel an unputdownable read, perfect for fans of twisty thrillers and complex characters.

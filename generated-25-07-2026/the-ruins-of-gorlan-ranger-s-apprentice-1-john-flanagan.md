# The Ruins of Gorlan (Ranger's Apprentice, #1)

**Author:** John Flanagan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2005

The Ruins of Gorlan, the inaugural installment in John Flanagan's Ranger's Apprentice series, is a captivating tale that whisks readers away to a realm of medieval fantasy, where valorous heroes and nefarious villains engage in a perpetual struggle for dominance. This enthralling narrative has captivated the hearts of millions worldwide, with over 5 million copies sold in the United States alone, and it's easy to see why.

At its core, The Ruins of Gorlan is a coming-of-age story that follows the journey of 15-year-old Will Treaty, a young boy who has always felt like an outcast in his village. Chosen to be a Ranger's apprentice, Will embarks on a path that will test his courage, wit, and resilience. As he navigates the complexities of his new role, Will discovers that the Rangers are not the mysterious, magic-wielding figures he once believed them to be, but rather highly skilled warriors who protect the kingdom from the shadows.

Flanagan's masterful storytelling weaves a rich tapestry of characters, each with their own distinct voice and motivations. The world-building is meticulous, with a keen attention to detail that transports readers to a fully realized medieval realm. The author's writing style is engaging and accessible, making this series an excellent choice for readers of all ages.

One of the most compelling aspects of The Ruins of Gorlan is its thoughtful exploration of the human condition. Flanagan delves into themes of identity, loyalty, and the struggle between good and evil. Will's journey is a powerful allegory for the challenges we all face in life, as he grapples with his own sense of purpose and belonging.

The pacing of the narrative is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and humor that keeps readers on the edge of their seats. The characters are multidimensional and relatable, with flaws and strengths that make them feel authentic. The villain, Morgarath, is a formidable foe, driven by a complex mix of motivations that add depth to the story.

The Ruins of Gorlan is an excellent choice for fans of epic fantasy adventures, particularly those who enjoy the works of J.R.R. Tolkien, T.H. White, and Christopher Paolini. Flanagan's writing style is reminiscent of these authors, with a similar blend of action, adventure, and memorable characters.

In conclusion, The Ruins of Gorlan is a captivating and immersive tale that marks the beginning of an unforgettable journey. With its rich world-building, memorable characters, and thought-provoking themes, this series is sure to delight readers of all ages. If you're a fan of epic fantasy adventures, do yourself a favor and join Will on his thrilling quest.

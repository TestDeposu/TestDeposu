# Just Once

**Author:** Karen Kingsbury  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**Just Once: A Heartrending Tale of Love, Loyalty, and Sacrifice**

In her latest masterpiece, Karen Kingsbury weaves a captivating tale of love and war, set against the backdrop of World War II. "Just Once" is a poignant exploration of the human heart, torn between duty and desire, loyalty and love. Kingsbury, known for her deeply heartfelt novels, has once again proven her prowess in crafting stories that resonate with the soul.

The narrative follows Irvel Holland, a young woman navigating the complexities of love and war. Her heart is divided between two brothers, Sam and Hank, while the world around her grapples with the horrors of war. When Pearl Harbor is attacked, Irvel's life takes an unexpected turn. She finds herself at the heart of a secret spy network, torn between her love for Hank and her duty to her country.

Kingsbury's style is as elegant as ever, painting vivid images with her words. She masterfully weaves historical events into the fabric of the story, creating a rich tapestry of love, loss, and sacrifice. The characters are intricately drawn, their emotions raw and relatable. Irvel's struggle is one that many can empathize with, making the story all the more compelling.

The main theme of "Just Once" is the power of love to heal, to inspire, and to endure. It explores the complexities of love, the sacrifices we make for those we care about, and the lengths we go to protect them. Kingsbury delves into the human heart, exposing its vulnerabilities and strengths, and reminding us of the power of love to conquer all.

"Just Once" is a story that will resonate with readers long after the last page is turned. It's a testament to the human spirit, a love letter to the past, and a reminder of the power of love. It's a must-read for fans of historical fiction, romance, and stories that touch the heart.

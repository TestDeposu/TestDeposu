# The Satsuma Complex (Gary Thorn, #1)

**Author:** Bob Mortimer  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

In "The Satsuma Complex", Bob Mortimer masterfully weaves a captivating tale of love, mystery, and self-discovery, set against the vibrant backdrop of South London. This debut novel is a true masterpiece, effortlessly blending noirish fiction with surrealist humor, which has earned it the prestigious Bollinger Everyman Wodehouse Prize for Comic Fiction 2023.

At the heart of the story lies Gary Thorn, a relatable and endearing protagonist, who is desperate to break free from his mundane life as a legal assistant. Gary's journey begins with a chance encounter with a mysterious girl in a pub, who leaves him smitten and intrigued. The only clue he has to her identity is the book she was reading - "The Satsuma Complex". As Gary navigates the complexities of his own life, he becomes obsessed with finding this enigmatic woman, now dubbed Satsuma.

Through Gary's quest, Mortimer explores the themes of loneliness, connection, and the human need for excitement and love. With his unique brand of humor, Mortimer pokes fun at the monotony of everyday life, while also highlighting the beauty of the ordinary. The characters in the novel are expertly crafted, with each one adding depth and richness to the narrative.

Mortimer's writing style is a perfect blend of witty one-liners, clever wordplay, and poignant observations. His use of language is both economical and evocative, making the novel a joy to read. The pacing is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows effortlessly, keeping the reader engaged and invested in Gary's story.

One of the standout aspects of "The Satsuma Complex" is its ability to balance humor and pathos. Mortimer tackles complex emotions with sensitivity and nuance, never shying away from exploring the darker aspects of human nature. The result is a novel that is both laugh-out-loud funny and deeply moving.

The novel's setting, South London, is vividly brought to life by Mortimer's descriptive prose. The estates and pie shops become characters in their own right, adding to the novel's sense of place and atmosphere.

In conclusion, "The Satsuma Complex" is a triumph of comedic fiction. Bob Mortimer's writing is a breath of fresh air, and his unique voice is sure to resonate with readers. This novel is a must-read for anyone looking for a story that will make them laugh, cry, and reflect on the complexities of human connection.

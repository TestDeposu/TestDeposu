# Five Survive

**Author:** Holly Jackson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**Five Survive: A Thrilling Ride into Darkness**

In the vein of Agatha Christie's "And Then There Were None," Holly Jackson's "Five Survive" is a masterclass in suspense, proving once again that the author is a force to be reckoned with in the world of young adult thrillers. Jackson, who shot to fame with her "A Good Girl's Guide to Murder" series, has crafted a standalone novel that is as gripping as it is unsettling.

The story begins innocently enough, with six friends embarking on a spring break road trip. But when their RV breaks down in the middle of nowhere, with no cell service and a killer on the loose, the journey takes a sinister turn. As the hours tick by, the friends must confront their darkest fears and deepest secrets, as one by one, they fall prey to the unseen assailant.

Jackson's strength lies in her ability to create characters that are both relatable and flawed, making it impossible not to be drawn into their plight. Each character is a puzzle piece, fitting together to form a complex narrative that keeps readers guessing until the very end. The dynamics between the friends are authentic, with tensions simmering just beneath the surface, ready to boil over at any moment.

The author's signature style is evident throughout the novel, with short, sharp chapters that build suspense and keep the pages turning. The countdown to dawn adds an extra layer of tension, as the friends race against time to escape their would-be killer. The remote setting, with its eerie isolation, serves as a character in its own right, amplifying the sense of dread that permeates every page.

At its heart, "Five Survive" is a story about trust, betrayal, and the dark secrets we keep from those closest to us. It's a chilling reminder that sometimes, the most dangerous threats can come from within our own circle. Jackson explores these themes with a deft touch, balancing suspense with moments of genuine emotion and connection.

The novel is not without its twists and turns, each one more shocking than the last. Jackson expertly misdirects readers, leading them down one path only to yank the rug out from under them at the last moment. The final reveal is satisfying, tying up loose ends while still leaving room for interpretation.

"Five Survive" is a thrilling ride into darkness, a novel that will keep readers on the edge of their seats until the very last page. It's a testament to Jackson's skill as a storyteller that she can make us care so deeply about characters in such a high-stakes, low-empathy scenario. This is a book that demands to be read in one sitting, preferably with the lights on.

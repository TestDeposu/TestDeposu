# Forbidden

**Author:** Tabitha Suzuma  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

Forbidden, written by Tabitha Suzuma, is a heart-wrenching and thought-provoking novel that delves into the complex and taboo relationship between siblings Lochan and Maya. The story is set against the backdrop of a tumultuous home life, where the two siblings have been forced to take on parental roles to care for their three younger siblings due to their mother's alcoholism and neglect.

The author's masterful storytelling weaves a tale of desperation, love, and loyalty, as Lochan and Maya find solace in each other amidst the chaos of their lives. Their relationship, which begins as a deep emotional connection, gradually blossoms into a romantic and passionate love. Despite the societal norms and expectations that deem their relationship wrong, the siblings cannot help but be drawn to each other, and their love becomes a source of comfort and strength in the face of adversity.

Suzuma's writing style is evocative and poignant, conveying the intensity and complexity of the siblings' emotions with remarkable sensitivity. The characters are multidimensional and relatable, with rich inner lives that make it easy to become invested in their story. The author's use of language is lyrical and expressive, creating a sense of intimacy and immediacy that draws the reader into the world of the characters.

One of the most striking aspects of the novel is its thoughtful exploration of the nature of love and relationships. Suzuma raises important questions about the boundaries of love, the power of societal expectations, and the consequences of following one's heart. The novel is unflinching in its portrayal of the difficulties and challenges faced by the siblings, and the author does not shy away from exploring the darker aspects of their relationship.

The pacing of the novel is well-balanced, with a narrative that is both suspenseful and emotionally resonant. The tension builds steadily towards a shocking and devastating finale, which is both heartbreaking and thought-provoking. The author's handling of the climax is masterful, creating a sense of inevitability that is both haunting and unforgettable.

Overall, Forbidden is a powerful and haunting novel that will linger in the reader's mind long after the final page is turned. It is a testament to Suzuma's skill as a writer that she can tackle such a difficult and taboo subject with sensitivity, nuance, and compassion. The novel is a must-read for anyone interested in complex, thought-provoking fiction that explores the human condition in all its complexity.

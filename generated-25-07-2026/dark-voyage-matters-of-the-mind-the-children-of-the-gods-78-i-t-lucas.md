# Dark Voyage Matters of the Mind (The Children of the Gods #78)

**Author:** I.T. Lucas  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

"Dark Voyage Matters of the Mind" is a poignant collection of 150 poems from the First World War, expertly curated to capture the essence of a pivotal moment in history. This comprehensive anthology brings together the works of renowned poets, including Rudyard Kipling, Henry Van Dyke, and Robert Bridges, alongside lesser-known voices from the trenches.

The poems within this collection traverse the vast emotional landscape of war, from the fervor of patriotism to the depths of despair, loss, and disillusionment. The authors' styles are as varied as the experiences they convey, ranging from the lyrical and introspective to the stark and unflinching.

One of the most striking aspects of this anthology is its ability to humanize the conflict, to strip away the abstract notions of war and reveal the individual stories, fears, and hopes that defined it. The poems are not merely a reflection of the time but a testament to the enduring power of the human spirit.

The collection is divided into sections that explore the various facets of the war, from the initial enthusiasm and sense of purpose to the gradual realization of the conflict's true horror. The poems also touch on the home front, the role of women, and the impact of the war on civilians.

Throughout the anthology, the poets grapple with the moral complexities of war, questioning the notion of heroism, the value of sacrifice, and the true cost of victory. These themes are timeless, and the poems remain remarkably relevant today, serving as a powerful reminder of the devastating consequences of conflict.

The editor's decision to include a wide range of voices and perspectives adds depth and complexity to the collection. The poems are not limited to the traditional war poets but also include the works of women, soldiers, and civilians, offering a nuanced and multifaceted understanding of the war.

In conclusion, "Dark Voyage Matters of the Mind" is a masterful collection of poems that captures the essence of the First World War. The anthology is a testament to the power of poetry to convey the human experience, to express the inexpressible, and to provide a window into the past. It is a must-read for anyone interested in history, poetry, or the human condition.

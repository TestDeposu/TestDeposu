# The Great Hunt (The Wheel of Time, #2)

**Author:** Robert Jordan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

"The Great Hunt," the second installment in Robert Jordan's epic fantasy series "The Wheel of Time," is a masterclass in world-building and intricate plotting. Jordan, with his characteristic style, weaves a tapestry of complex characters, rich history, and intricate magic, drawing readers into a world that feels as real and vast as our own.

At the heart of "The Great Hunt" lies the quest for the fabled Horn of Valere, an artifact said to have the power to raise legendary heroes from the dead. The horn's discovery, after centuries of being mere myth, sets the stage for a thrilling adventure that spans continents and involves a diverse cast of characters, each with their own motivations and secrets.

Jordan's strength lies in his ability to create a sense of history and mythology that feels deeply rooted and authentic. The world of the Wheel of Time is not just a backdrop for the story; it is a character in its own right, with its own rules, cultures, and politics. This depth of world-building is what makes the series so immersive and compelling.

The author's style is marked by detailed descriptions and intricate plotting. While some might find the pace slow at times, the meticulous attention to detail serves to enrich the story and the world it inhabits. Jordan's dialogue is another standout feature, bringing his characters to life with their unique voices and perspectives.

However, "The Great Hunt" is not without its challenges. The sheer number of characters and plotlines can be overwhelming, and the story can feel fragmented at times. But for those willing to invest the time and effort, the rewards are immense. The complex web of alliances and betrayals, the high-stakes quest, and the richly drawn characters make "The Great Hunt" a compelling read.

In essence, "The Great Hunt" is a testament to Jordan's skill as a world-builder and storyteller. It is a book that demands patience and commitment, but for those who are willing to give themselves over to its intricate plot and richly drawn world, it offers a deeply satisfying and immersive reading experience.

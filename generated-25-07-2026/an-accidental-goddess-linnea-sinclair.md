# An Accidental Goddess

**Author:** Linnea Sinclair  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

**An Accidental Goddess: A Prequel to Rediscover**

Linnea Sinclair, the master of weaving intricate worlds and compelling characters, takes us back to the beginning with "An Accidental Goddess," a captivating prequel that breathes new life into the beloved series. Sinclair's signature style, a harmonious blend of fantasy, adventure, and romance, is on full display in this ages-past tale that sets the stage for the epic journey of Khamsin.

The story unfolds in a world torn apart by a centuries-long war of dark magic, where the Orb of Knowledge is the ultimate prize. Into this tumultuous landscape steps Khamsin, a young healer from Cirrus Cove, whose eighteenth birthday marks not just her coming of age, but also the fulfillment of an ancient prophecy. Sinclair masterfully builds tension as Khamsin's peaceful life is shattered, forcing her to embrace her destiny and embark on a perilous quest to claim the Orb.

Sinclair's prose is as enchanting as ever, painting vivid landscapes and intricate magical systems that draw the reader in. Her characters are her strongest suit, and Khamsin is no exception. Sinclair expertly balances Khamsin's vulnerability and strength, making her a heroine readers can't help but root for. The supporting cast, from the enigmatic Rylan to the wise old wizard Ciro, are equally compelling, each bringing their own unique dynamic to the story.

The main theme of "An Accidental Goddess" is identity and acceptance. Khamsin's journey is one of self-discovery, as she grapples with the truth of her lineage and the power that lies within her. Sinclair explores this theme with depth and nuance, making it resonate on a personal level with readers. The love story, while not the central focus, is a beautiful subplot that adds emotional depth to Khamsin's journey.

Sinclair's pacing is spot on, balancing action, character development, and world-building with ease. The plot twists and turns, keeping the reader on the edge of their seat, while also allowing moments of reflection and introspection. The ending, while satisfying, leaves enough unanswered questions to pique the reader's interest in the main series.

In conclusion, "An Accidental Goddess" is a must-read for fans of the series, offering a richly detailed backstory and a compelling new perspective. It's also a fantastic starting point for new readers, showcasing Sinclair's unique style and world-building. This is a book that will captivate fans of fantasy, adventure, and romance alike.

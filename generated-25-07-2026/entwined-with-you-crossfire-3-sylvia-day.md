# Entwined with You (Crossfire, #3)

**Author:** Sylvia Day  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

Sylvia Day’s *Entwined with You* is the third installment in the Crossfire series, a saga that has captivated millions of readers with its intoxicating blend of passion, obsession, and psychological depth. This novel, like its predecessors, doesn’t just deliver a love story—it immerses the reader in the raw, unfiltered emotions of Eva Tramell and Gideon Cross, two souls whose connection is as destructive as it is irresistible. Day’s writing is a masterclass in tension, where every word feels deliberate, every emotion heightened, and every revelation fraught with the weight of past traumas. If you’ve ever been swept up in a love so consuming it borders on madness, this book will feel like a fever dream you won’t want to wake from.

At its core, *Entwined with You* is about the inescapable pull of the past and how it shapes our present desires. Eva and Gideon are not just lovers; they are survivors of their own histories, each carrying scars that run deeper than their shared bed. Gideon, the enigmatic billionaire with a reputation for ruthlessness, is revealed in layers, his vulnerability exposed only to Eva. His past is a labyrinth of pain, and his relationship with her becomes both his salvation and his greatest vulnerability. Eva, on the other hand, is no passive heroine. She is fierce, intelligent, and unafraid to challenge Gideon’s demons, even when it means confronting her own. Their dynamic is electric—less a traditional romance and more a battle of wills where love and obsession intertwine so tightly it’s impossible to tell where one ends and the other begins.

Day’s prose is lush, sensual, and unapologetically intense. She doesn’t just describe desire; she makes the reader *feel* it. The physical chemistry between Eva and Gideon is palpable, but what sets this series apart is the emotional rawness. There are no sanitized happy endings here—just messy, complicated love that demands sacrifice. The author’s ability to balance scorching intimacy with high-stakes drama is nothing short of brilliant. Scenes that could easily veer into melodrama instead feel visceral and real, thanks to Day’s sharp dialogue and her knack for making even the most extreme situations feel intimate.

One of the most compelling aspects of *Entwined with You* is its exploration of power dynamics. Gideon’s wealth and influence are not just plot devices; they are extensions of his psyche, tools he uses to control his world—and Eva. Yet, Eva refuses to be controlled, even as she willingly submits to him in ways that blur the line between dominance and surrender. Their relationship is a push-and-pull of trust and betrayal, where every moment of closeness is shadowed by the fear of losing themselves in each other. It’s a dynamic that’s as thrilling as it is unsettling, a testament to Day’s skill in crafting characters who are deeply flawed yet impossible to look away from.

The pacing of the novel is relentless. Day doesn’t waste time on filler; every chapter escalates the stakes, whether it’s a revelation about Gideon’s past, a threat from a new enemy, or the simmering tension between the leads. The emotional beats are just as gripping as the physical ones, with moments of tenderness that make the darker scenes hit even harder. The book doesn’t shy away from the uglier aspects of love—possessiveness, jealousy, the way pain can twist desire into something unrecognizable. It’s messy, it’s raw, and it’s utterly compelling.

For fans of the series, *Entwined with You* delivers exactly what they’ve been craving: more of Eva and Gideon’s intoxicating, all-consuming love story. For newcomers, it’s a masterclass in how to write a romance that feels both epic and deeply personal. Sylvia Day doesn’t just tell a love story; she crafts an experience that lingers long after the last page. This is a book for those who believe in love as a force of nature—beautiful, terrifying, and impossible to resist.

# The Alchemy Fire Murder (Mary Wandwalker #2)

**Author:** Susan Rowland  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

"The Alchemy Fire Murder" by Susan Rowland is a captivating and intricate mystery that masterfully weaves together themes of history, alchemy, and identity. This second installment in the Mary Wandwalker series is a gripping page-turner that will keep readers on the edge of their seats as they navigate the complex world of medieval alchemy and modern-day crime.

At its core, "The Alchemy Fire Murder" is a story about the power of knowledge and the lengths to which people will go to possess it. The novel centers around the mysterious disappearance of a medieval alchemy scroll, which sets off a chain reaction of events that span continents and involve a cast of characters with secrets to hide. As Mary Wandwalker, the protagonist, delves deeper into the mystery, she uncovers a web of deceit and corruption that threatens to destroy everything she holds dear.

Rowland's writing style is masterful, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the historical context in which the story is set. Her use of language is evocative and immersive, transporting readers to the dusty archives of Oxford University and the sun-kissed streets of Los Angeles. The characters are richly drawn and complex, with Mary's dry wit and sharp intellect making her a compelling and relatable protagonist.

One of the standout features of "The Alchemy Fire Murder" is its exploration of themes that are both timely and timeless. Rowland tackles issues of racism, sexism, and the erasure of history with sensitivity and nuance, weaving them seamlessly into the narrative. The result is a story that is both entertaining and thought-provoking, with a cast of characters that are multidimensional and relatable.

The plot is expertly paced, with twists and turns that will keep readers guessing until the very end. Rowland's use of multiple plot threads and timelines adds to the complexity of the story, but never feels overwhelming or confusing. The result is a narrative that is both propulsive and engaging, with a sense of tension and urgency that drives the story forward.

If there's one criticism to be made, it's that the supporting cast of characters can feel a bit one-dimensional at times. Mary's colleagues, Caroline and Anna, are intriguing characters with rich backstories, but they often feel like they're relegated to the sidelines in favor of Mary's solo investigation. Additionally, the villain of the piece can feel a bit cartoonish at times, with motivations that feel slightly contrived.

Overall, however, "The Alchemy Fire Murder" is a compelling and engaging mystery that will appeal to fans of historical fiction, alchemy, and crime. Rowland's writing is masterful, and her use of language is evocative and immersive. The story is both timely and timeless, tackling issues that are relevant to today's world while still feeling grounded in the past. With its complex characters, intricate plot, and propulsive narrative, "The Alchemy Fire Murder" is a must-read for anyone looking for a gripping and thought-provoking mystery.

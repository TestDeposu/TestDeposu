# Rubinrot (Edelstein-Trilogie, #1)

**Author:** Kerstin Gier  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

"Rubinrot" by Kerstin Gier is a captivating tale of love, time travel, and self-discovery, expertly woven into a thrilling narrative that will keep you on the edge of your seat. This revised edition of the bestselling novel boasts exclusive bonus material, including a foreword by the author and additional content from Gideon's perspective, making it a must-read for both new and returning fans of the Edelstein trilogy.

At the heart of the story lies Gwendolyn, a feisty and endearing protagonist who finds herself hurtling through time to the London of the late 19th century. As she navigates this unfamiliar world, she uncovers the dark secrets of her family's past and discovers her own hidden destiny as a time traveler. But with this newfound power comes the warning that falling in love across different eras is a recipe for disaster.

Gier's masterful storytelling effortlessly transports readers to the opulent world of Victorian England, where the lines between past and present blur. Her vivid descriptions of the era's grandeur and the characters' intricate relationships are a testament to her meticulous research and boundless creativity. The result is an immersive experience that will leave you spellbound and yearning for more.

One of the novel's greatest strengths lies in its exploration of the complexities of love and relationships. Gwendolyn's whirlwind romance with the enigmatic Gideon is a highlight of the book, as they navigate the treacherous landscape of time travel and confront the consequences of their actions. Gier's portrayal of their love is raw, passionate, and unapologetic, making it impossible not to become invested in their fate.

Throughout the book, Gier's writing is infused with a sense of wit and humor, which adds a delightful layer of depth to the narrative. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Gwendolyn's sharp tongue and Gideon's brooding intensity making them a compelling duo.

The revised edition of "Rubinrot" offers a fresh perspective on the original story, with the added bonus material providing a deeper understanding of the characters' motivations and backstories. The inclusion of Gideon's perspective is a welcome addition, offering a glimpse into the mind of the mysterious and charismatic time traveler.

In conclusion, "Rubinrot" is a captivating and romantic tale of time travel and self-discovery, expertly crafted by Kerstin Gier. With its richly detailed world-building, complex characters, and pulse-pounding plot, this novel is a must-read for fans of the genre. So, buckle up and join Gwendolyn and Gideon on their epic adventure through time, but be warned: once you start, you won't be able to put it down!

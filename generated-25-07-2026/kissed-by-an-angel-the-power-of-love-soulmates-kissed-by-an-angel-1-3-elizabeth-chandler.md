# Kissed by an Angel/The Power of Love/Soulmates (Kissed by an Angel, #1-3)

**Author:** Elizabeth Chandler  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In "Kissed by an Angel/The Power of Love/Soulmates," Elizabeth Chandler weaves a captivating narrative that delves into the complexities of love, loss, and the supernatural. This trilogy, bound together in a single collection, is a poignant exploration of the human experience, expertly balancing romance, suspense, and the paranormal.

At its core, the story revolves around Ivy and her boyfriend Tristan, whose untimely death sets off a chain of events that challenges the very fabric of their relationship. As Ivy navigates the darkness of her grief, she begins to realize that Tristan's presence still lingers, watching over her as her guardian angel. This supernatural twist adds a layer of depth to the narrative, allowing Chandler to explore the intricacies of love and devotion in the face of mortality.

One of Chandler's greatest strengths as a writer is her ability to craft relatable, well-rounded characters. Ivy, in particular, is a compelling protagonist, whose vulnerability and determination make her a sympathetic and admirable heroine. Tristan, too, is skillfully portrayed, his character evolving from a romantic interest to a symbol of hope and protection.

The author's writing style is engaging and accessible, making the book an easy read for fans of young adult fiction and romance. Chandler's prose is descriptive and emotive, effectively conveying the intensity of Ivy's emotions as she struggles to come to terms with Tristan's death and her newfound awareness of his angelic presence.

Throughout the trilogy, Chandler masterfully builds tension, expertly interweaving elements of suspense and danger. As Ivy finds herself in the crosshairs of Tristan's killer, the stakes are raised, and the narrative hurtles towards a dramatic conclusion. The author's use of plot twists and surprises keeps the reader on the edge of their seat, invested in Ivy's fate and the ultimate outcome of her story.

The central theme of the book – the transformative power of love – is beautifully realized through Chandler's storytelling. Ivy and Tristan's relationship is portrayed as a love that transcends even death itself, a bond that continues to grow and evolve despite the obstacles in their path. This is a testament to the enduring nature of love, a reminder that even in the darkest of times, hope and devotion can prevail.

In "Kissed by an Angel/The Power of Love/Soulmates," Elizabeth Chandler has crafted a captivating and emotional narrative that will resonate with fans of romance, suspense, and the paranormal. With its well-developed characters, engaging plot, and poignant exploration of love and loss, this trilogy is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and unforgettable story.

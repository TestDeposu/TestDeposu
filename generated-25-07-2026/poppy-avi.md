# Poppy

**Author:** Avi  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

**Poppy: A Tiny Heroine's Big Adventure**

In the enchanting world of Dimwood Forest, Avi weaves a captivating tale of courage and defiance in "Poppy," the second installment of his acclaimed Tales of Dimwood Forest series. This Newbery Medal-winning author masterfully crafts a story that is as engaging as it is thought-provoking, perfect for young readers eager to explore the depths of their own bravery.

At the heart of "Poppy" lies a tiny deer mouse with a heart as vast as the forest she inhabits. Poppy, our intrepid heroine, is no ordinary mouse. She dares to challenge the tyrannical rule of Mr. Ocax, a fearsome owl who has long held the mice of Dimwood Forest in his iron grip. Avi's portrayal of Poppy is a testament to his ability to create relatable, inspiring characters that transcend their small stature. Poppy's journey from a naive, love-struck mouse to a courageous leader is a testament to Avi's skill in crafting character arcs that are as satisfying as they are believable.

Avi's storytelling style is a delightful blend of the fantastical and the familiar. He paints vivid, intricate portraits of Dimwood Forest, bringing it to life with a rich tapestry of flora and fauna. Yet, despite the whimsy of the setting, Avi grounds his story in universal themes that resonate with readers of all ages. The struggle against oppression, the power of belief in oneself, and the importance of standing up for what is right are themes that are as relevant today as they were when Avi first put pen to paper.

One of the standout aspects of "Poppy" is Avi's ability to create memorable, quirky characters that steal the show. Ereth, the irascible porcupine, is a prime example of this. His prickly exterior belies a loyal heart, and his grumpy demeanor provides a delightful contrast to Poppy's unwavering optimism. Avi's characters are as diverse as they are endearing, each bringing their unique perspective to the story.

The narrative is peppered with moments of suspense and danger, keeping young readers on the edge of their seats. Yet, Avi never loses sight of the heart of the story - Poppy's journey of self-discovery and growth. The balance between adventure and introspection is a testament to Avi's skill as a storyteller, creating a story that is as engaging as it is thought-provoking.

Illustrated by Caldecott Medal-winning artist Brian Floca, "Poppy" is a visual feast. Floca's intricate, detailed illustrations bring Avi's words to life, creating a world that is as enchanting as it is dangerous. The illustrations are not merely decorative; they are an integral part of the storytelling, enhancing the narrative and drawing the reader deeper into the world of Dimwood Forest.

In conclusion, "Poppy" is a must-read for young readers eager to explore the depths of their own courage. Avi's masterful storytelling, coupled with Floca's enchanting illustrations, creates a story that is as engaging as it is thought-provoking. It is a testament to Avi's skill as a storyteller that he can create a story that is as enjoyable for adults as it is for children. "Poppy" is more than just a story about a tiny mouse; it is a story about the power of belief, the courage to stand up for what is right, and the importance of never underestimating the power of the small.

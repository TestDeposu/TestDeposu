# Cry Wolf (Alpha & Omega, #1)

**Author:** Patricia Briggs  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

**Cry Wolf: A Howl of Fresh Magic from Patricia Briggs**

In the sprawling, supernatural world of Patricia Briggs, where werewolves and vampires coexist with humans, a new voice emerges in 'Cry Wolf', the first installment of the Alpha & Omega series. Briggs, known for her Mercy Thompson novels, weaves a captivating tale that stands confidently on its own, yet delightfully intertwines with her established universe.

At the heart of 'Cry Wolf' lies Anna Latham, a rare Omega werewolf, a wolf who heals and soothes, yet is forced to live on the fringes of her pack due to her unique status. Briggs masterfully explores the theme of power, not just in the form of brute strength, but also in the quiet, subtle influence Anna wields. This is a refreshing departure from the typical alpha male-driven narratives, offering a nuanced perspective on power dynamics within a pack and society at large.

Briggs' signature style is evident throughout, with vivid world-building that immerses readers in the Pacific Northwest, from the lush forests to the bustling city streets. Her characters are equally well-crafted, each with their own distinct voice and depth. The chemistry between Anna and her love interest, Charles Cornick, is palpable, their relationship evolving organically amidst the tension and danger they face.

The plot is a thrilling blend of mystery, action, and romance, as Anna and Charles unravel a series of brutal murders that threaten to expose the werewolf community to the human world. Briggs' prowess in balancing multiple genres is evident, keeping readers on the edge of their seats until the very end.

'Cry Wolf' is more than just a werewolf story; it's a compelling exploration of power, identity, and belonging. Briggs' signature blend of urban fantasy, mystery, and romance makes this a must-read for fans of her previous works, as well as those new to her enchanting world. It's a howl of fresh magic that promises to captivate and enthrall.

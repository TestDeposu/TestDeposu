# I Am Not Jessica Chen

**Author:** Ann Liang  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

In the captivating novel "I Am Not Jessica Chen" by Ann Liang, the notion of perfection and identity takes center stage, weaving a thought-provoking narrative that delves into the complexities of self-discovery and the relentless pursuit of excellence. Jenna Chen, the protagonist, has always felt overshadowed by her cousin Jessica, who embodies the epitome of perfection - intelligence, beauty, and success. The weight of living up to her cousin's standards has defined Jenna's life, fueling a deep-seated desire to escape her own skin and inhabit the life of her flawless cousin.

Through Jenna's poignant and often humorous voice, Liang masterfully explores the pressures of growing up, particularly in the high-stakes environment of Havenwood Private Academy, where students are groomed to be the best. The author's vivid depiction of the school's cutthroat culture and the students' struggles to conform to expectations is both relatable and unsettling. As Jenna navigates this challenging world, first as herself and then as her cousin, Liang sheds light on the darker aspects of the pursuit of perfection, revealing the sacrifices and costs that come with trying to fit into an idealized mold.

Liang's writing style is engaging and accessible, making "I Am Not Jessica Chen" an effortless read. Her use of dialogue is natural and authentic, allowing the characters to shine with distinct personalities. The supporting cast, including the charming but seemingly unattainable Aaron Cai, adds depth to the narrative, underscoring the complexities of relationships and first love.

One of the most compelling aspects of the novel is its thought-provoking exploration of identity and the consequences of wishing to be someone else. As Jenna inhabits her cousin's life, she begins to realize that perfection is not only unattainable but also suffocating. The novel raises important questions about the value of individuality, creativity, and self-acceptance, encouraging readers to reflect on their own experiences and the pressures they face.

Throughout the book, Liang tackles themes that are both timely and timeless, from the impact of social media on self-esteem to the importance of embracing one's uniqueness. Her portrayal of the Asian-American experience is nuanced and authentic, offering a much-needed perspective in the world of young adult literature.

In conclusion, "I Am Not Jessica Chen" is a captivating and relatable novel that will resonate with readers of all ages. Ann Liang's compelling narrative and well-developed characters make for a riveting read that is both entertaining and thought-provoking. As Jenna navigates the complexities of her own identity and the costs of perfection, Liang reminds us that being true to oneself is the greatest achievement of all.

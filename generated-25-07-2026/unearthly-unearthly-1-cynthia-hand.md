# Unearthly (Unearthly, #1)

**Author:** Cynthia Hand  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In "Unearthly," Cynthia Hand weaves a captivating narrative that delves into the complexities of identity, love, and the struggle between destiny and free will. The story follows Clara Gardner, a teenage girl who discovers she's part angel, and her journey to uncover her purpose on earth.

One of the standout aspects of Hand's writing is her ability to craft relatable characters. Clara's voice is authentic and engaging, making it easy to become invested in her story. Her struggles to navigate her newfound angelic heritage and balance her desires with her sense of duty are deeply human, despite her supernatural nature.

The author's use of vivid imagery and descriptive language effectively transports readers to the picturesque town of Jackson Hole, Wyoming, where Clara moves with her family. The setting becomes a character in its own right, with its breathtaking landscapes and eerie, isolated atmosphere.

At the heart of "Unearthly" is the theme of choice versus destiny. Clara's visions of a forest fire and a mysterious boy lead her to believe that her path is predetermined, but as she becomes embroiled in a love triangle with Christian and Tucker, she begins to question whether her fate is truly set in stone. This internal conflict is expertly woven throughout the narrative, raising questions about the nature of free will and the consequences of one's choices.

Hand's portrayal of love is also noteworthy. The romance in "Unearthly" is sweet and tender, but it's also fraught with tension and uncertainty. Clara's relationships with Christian and Tucker are multifaceted and nuanced, reflecting the complexities of real-life love.

If there's one area where the book falls short, it's in the pacing. At times, the narrative feels a bit slow, particularly in the first half. However, the second half more than makes up for this, with a dramatic increase in tension and action that propels the story forward.

Overall, "Unearthly" is a compelling and thought-provoking read that will appeal to fans of paranormal romance and fantasy. Hand's writing is engaging and evocative, and her exploration of the human condition is both poignant and timely.

# All the Murmuring Bones

**Author:** A.G. Slatter  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

"All the Murmuring Bones" is a hauntingly beautiful tale that weaves together dark family secrets, folklore, and a touch of the supernatural, all set against a backdrop of atmospheric, gothic prose. Author Angela Slatter, known for her award-winning short stories, has crafted a spellbinding novel that will captivate fans of Naomi Novik, Katherine Arden, and anyone who enjoys a story that feels like a dark, twisted fairy tale.

The story follows Miren O'Malley, a young woman bound by a generations-old bargain between her family and the merfolk. Her family's prosperity has long relied on the sacrifice of a child to the sea, but as the years have passed, the family's fortunes have waned, and Miren's grandmother is determined to restore their glory, even if it means Miren must take her place.

Slatter's prose is rich and evocative, painting a vivid picture of the bleak, storm-tossed coast and the eerie, otherworldly realm of the mer. Her characters are equally compelling, with Miren's strength and resilience serving as a beacon throughout the story. The supporting cast is equally well-drawn, from Miren's cunning and ambitious grandmother to the enigmatic, dangerous merfolk who haunt the story's edges.

At its heart, "All the Murmuring Bones" is a story about the power dynamics between men and women, and the ways in which women have historically been exploited and controlled. Miren's struggle against her fate is a powerful and resonant one, and Slatter handles the themes of consent, agency, and resistance with nuance and care.

The novel is not without its flaws. The pacing can feel slow at times, and some readers may find the story's focus on Miren's internal journey at the expense of action or plot twists to be a drawback. However, those who appreciate a richly drawn world, complex characters, and a story that lingers long after the final page will find much to love in "All the Murmuring Bones."

In the end, "All the Murmuring Bones" is a haunting, atmospheric tale that explores the dark heart of folklore and the human experience. It's a story that will stay with you, long after you've turned the final page.

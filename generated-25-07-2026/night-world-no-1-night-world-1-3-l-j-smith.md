# Night World, No. 1 (Night World, #1-3)

**Author:** L.J. Smith  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

In the realm of young adult paranormal romance, L.J. Smith's Night World series has carved out a niche for itself, and the first installment of this collector's edition is a testament to the author's captivating storytelling. This book is a masterful blend of forbidden love, supernatural intrigue, and the eternal conflict between mortality and immortality.

At the heart of the narrative lies Poppy, a high school student diagnosed with a terminal illness, whose life is turned upside down by the enigmatic James, her best friend and secret love. As James whisks Poppy away into the Night World, a realm of vampires, witches, and shapeshifters, she is faced with an impossible choice: to follow him into the darkness of eternal life or to succumb to the inevitability of death.

One of the most striking aspects of Smith's writing is her ability to craft relatable characters that leap off the page. Poppy's vulnerability, determination, and ultimate willingness to risk everything for love make her a compelling protagonist. James, on the other hand, is the quintessential brooding hero, with a dark past and an irresistible charm that draws Poppy in, despite the danger that surrounds him.

The world-building in Night World is equally impressive, with a richly detailed mythology that underpins the narrative. Smith's vision of a hidden world, existing in tandem with our own, is both eerie and alluring, drawing the reader into a realm of magic and mystery.

Throughout the book, Smith explores the theme of mortality and the human desire to transcend it. Poppy's struggle to come to terms with her own mortality serves as a poignant backdrop to the supernatural events that unfold. The author raises important questions about the value of life, the nature of love, and the consequences of playing with forces beyond human control.

Smith's writing style is engaging and accessible, making this book an easy read for fans of the paranormal romance genre. Her use of language is evocative, conjuring vivid images of the Night World and its inhabitants. The pacing is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows smoothly from one scene to the next.

If you're a fan of Twilight or The Vampire Diaries, you'll likely devour Night World. While some readers may find the romance a tad overly sentimental, the book's unique blend of supernatural intrigue and poignant themes makes it a compelling read.

In conclusion, Night World is a captivating tale of forbidden love, mortality, and the supernatural. L.J. Smith's writing is engaging, and her world-building is impressive. If you're looking for a paranormal romance with a unique twist, this book is definitely worth checking out.

# Body of Proof: The 7 Best Reasons to Believe in the Resurrection of Jesus―and Why It Matters Today

**Author:** Jeremiah J. Johnston  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In a world where faith is constantly questioned and scrutinized, "Body of Proof: The 7 Best Reasons to Believe in the Resurrection of Jesus―and Why It Matters Today" by Jeremiah J. Johnston is a timely and thought-provoking response. This book is not just a defense of Christianity, but a powerful tool to equip believers with the knowledge and confidence to share their faith with a skeptical world.

Johnston's writing style is engaging, accessible, and free of jargon, making the book an excellent resource for both new and seasoned believers. He tackles some of the toughest questions about Christianity, including the resurrection, mental health and suicide, paranormal activity, and the silence of God. With each topic, Johnston provides a clear and concise explanation, rooted in biblical scripture and supported by historical evidence.

One of the strengths of the book is its emphasis on the importance of critical thinking and intellectual curiosity in the Christian faith. Johnston encourages readers to engage with the world around them, to ask questions, and to seek answers. He provides practical tips and tools for navigating difficult conversations and for sharing the gospel with non-believers.

The book is divided into six sessions, each focusing on a different aspect of the Christian faith. The sessions are designed to be used in a small-group setting, with individual study opportunities and discussion guides to facilitate deeper exploration of the topics. Johnston's approach is not to provide pat answers or simplistic solutions, but to encourage readers to think deeply and critically about their faith.

Throughout the book, Johnston's passion for the gospel and his commitment to equipping believers to share their faith shine through. He writes with conviction and authority, drawing on his extensive knowledge of scripture and history. His use of real-life examples and anecdotes adds a personal touch to the book, making it feel more like a conversation with a trusted friend than a dry theological treatise.

If there is a criticism to be made, it is that the book sometimes feels a bit too focused on apologetics, with Johnston spending more time defending the faith than exploring its deeper implications. Additionally, some readers may find the book's tone a bit too optimistic, with Johnston's emphasis on the power of the gospel to transform lives and communities sometimes feeling a bit too simplistic.

Overall, however, "Body of Proof" is a powerful and thought-provoking book that is sure to resonate with believers and non-believers alike. Johnston's writing is engaging, his arguments are convincing, and his passion for the gospel is inspiring. Whether you are a seasoned believer or just starting to explore the Christian faith, this book is an excellent resource for anyone looking to deepen their understanding of the gospel and to share their faith with others.

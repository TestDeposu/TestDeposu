# One Dark Window (The Shepherd King, #1)

**Author:** Rachel Gillig  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

One Dark Window by Rachel Gillig is a hauntingly dark and lushly gothic fantasy that delves deep into the complexities of the human psyche and the blurred lines between good and evil. This mesmerizing tale of a maiden and her inner monster will captivate fans of Naomi Novik's Uprooted and Hannah Whitten's For the Wolf.

At the heart of the story is Elspeth Spindle, a young woman with a mysterious and ancient spirit, the Nightmare, trapped within her. This mercurial entity protects her and keeps her secrets, but at a steep price. As Elspeth navigates the treacherous landscape of her mist-shrouded kingdom, she finds herself drawn to a mysterious highwayman who is, in truth, the King's own nephew and a traitor to the crown.

Their perilous quest to gather the twelve Providence Cards, the keys to curing the kingdom of its dark magic, is fraught with danger, deception, and an undeniable attraction that threatens to consume them both. But as the stakes escalate and the Solstice draws near, Elspeth is forced to confront her darkest secret: the Nightmare is slowly taking over her mind, and she may not be able to stop him.

Gillig's writing is masterful, weaving a complex narrative that is both atmospheric and intense. Her use of language is evocative and immersive, conjuring the eerie, mist-locked kingdom with ease. The world-building is meticulous, with a deep history that underpins the narrative, adding depth and complexity to the story.

One of the most striking aspects of One Dark Window is its exploration of the human psyche. Elspeth's inner turmoil, as she struggles to maintain control over the Nightmare, is both poignant and terrifying. Gillig raises important questions about the nature of monstrosity, whether it is something that can be contained, or if it is an inherent part of us all.

The character development is equally impressive, with Elspeth and the highwayman, Captain of the Destriers, being multidimensional and relatable. Their relationship is fraught with tension, and their undeniable attraction is expertly woven throughout the narrative.

One Dark Window is a gripping and unsettling read that will appeal to fans of dark fantasy and gothic fiction. With its lush prose, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes, this book is sure to leave a lasting impression on readers. If you're looking for a story that will haunt you long after you finish reading, then this is the book for you.

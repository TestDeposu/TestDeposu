# Mickey7 (Mickey7 #1)

**Author:** Edward Ashton  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

In the gripping novel "Mickey7" by Edward Ashton, the boundaries between life and death are pushed to the limit in a thrilling exploration of human existence. Set on the icy planet of Niflheim, this thought-provoking story delves into the consequences of playing with the fundamental nature of life and the true cost of survival.

At the heart of the narrative is Mickey Barnes, an Expendable, a human clone designed to be used and discarded at the whim of the colony's commanders. With each iteration, Mickey is reborn, retaining most of his memories, but never truly living. His existence is a mere shadow of life, a constant reminder that he is nothing more than a tool to be used and discarded.

Ashton's prose is economical and effective, propelling the reader through the story with a sense of urgency that mirrors Mickey's own desperation. The world-building is meticulous, with a keen attention to detail that immerses the reader in the harsh environment of Niflheim. The native species, the "creatures," are a masterful creation, their motivations and actions a perfect counterpoint to the human colonizers.

Through Mickey's struggles, Ashton explores the theme of identity and what it means to be human. As Mickey7 navigates the complexities of his own existence, he begins to question the nature of his reality. Is he truly alive, or is he just a pale imitation of life? This existential crisis is expertly woven throughout the narrative, adding depth and complexity to the story.

One of the most striking aspects of "Mickey7" is its ability to balance action and introspection. Ashton seamlessly shifts between Mickey's desperate struggles to survive and his more philosophical musings on the nature of existence. This balance creates a sense of tension that propels the reader forward, even as they are forced to confront the deeper implications of Mickey's situation.

The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, with Commander Marshall being a standout. Her ruthless pragmatism is a perfect counterpoint to Mickey's growing sense of self-awareness, and their conflict drives the plot forward with increasing urgency.

Ultimately, "Mickey7" is a thought-provoking exploration of what it means to be human. Ashton's masterful storytelling and world-building create a narrative that is both gripping and introspective. As the stakes grow higher, Mickey7 must confront the ultimate question: what does it mean to truly live?

"Mickey7" is a gripping and thought-provoking novel that will appeal to fans of science fiction and philosophical introspection. With its breakneck pacing and meticulous world-building, this is a story that will stay with you long after the final page is turned.

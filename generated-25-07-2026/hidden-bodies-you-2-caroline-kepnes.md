# Hidden Bodies (You, #2)

**Author:** Caroline Kepnes  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

**Hidden Bodies: A Dark Delve into Obsession and Reinvention**

Caroline Kepnes' "Hidden Bodies," the sequel to her smash hit "You," is a chilling exploration of love, obsession, and the lengths one will go to for a fresh start. Kepnes, with her signature sharp wit and unsettling prose, masterfully crafts a tale that is as captivating as it is disturbing.

Joe Goldberg, our anti-hero from the first installment, has traded New York's grimy streets for Hollywood's glitz and glamour. Yet, Joe remains unchanged, a man haunted by his past and driven by an insatiable desire for love. His new life in LA is a facade, a desperate attempt to outrun the shadows of his past. But as Kepnes so masterfully illustrates, the past has a way of catching up.

The novel's main theme is the destructive power of obsession and the futile attempt to reinvent oneself. Joe, a man who has buried his secrets deep, is a compelling yet terrifying protagonist. His voice, a mix of dark humor and chilling introspection, draws you in, making it impossible not to root for him, despite his monstrous actions. Kepnes' ability to humanize a character so morally reprehensible is a testament to her skill as a writer.

Kepnes' style is as distinctive as her protagonist. She weaves a narrative that is equal parts suspenseful and satirical, a dark comedy that skewers modern dating culture and celebrity obsession. Her prose is sharp, her dialogue snappy, and her pacing impeccable. She expertly builds tension, keeping the reader on the edge of their seat, eager to uncover the next twist.

"Hidden Bodies" is not for the faint-hearted. It's a grim exploration of the human psyche, a tale that reminds us that the past is never truly buried. Kepnes' unflinching gaze into the darkness of the human soul is both unsettling and mesmerizing. She is a master of her craft, a writer who dares to go where others fear to tread.

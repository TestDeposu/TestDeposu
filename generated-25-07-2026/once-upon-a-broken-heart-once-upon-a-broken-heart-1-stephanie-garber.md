# Once Upon a Broken Heart (Once Upon a Broken Heart, #1)

**Author:** Stephanie Garber  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In "Once Upon a Broken Heart", Stephanie Garber weaves a mesmerizing tale of love, heartbreak, and the unrelenting pursuit of happily ever after. This enchanting novel whisks readers away to a world of magic, mystery, and romance, as Evangeline Fox, the protagonist, navigates the treacherous landscape of her own heart.

Garber's writing style is nothing short of enchanting, with a poetic flair that brings the characters and their world to vivid life. Her prose is as lush and atmospheric as the fairy tale landscapes she creates, drawing the reader into a world of wonder and awe. With a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the human heart, Garber crafts a narrative that is both universally relatable and uniquely captivating.

At its core, "Once Upon a Broken Heart" is a story about the transformative power of love and the devastating consequences of heartbreak. Evangeline's desperate bid to stop the wedding of her beloved and her subsequent bargain with the enigmatic Prince of Hearts sets in motion a chain of events that will either lead her to the greatest happily ever after or the most exquisite tragedy.

Through Evangeline's journey, Garber explores the complexities of love, desire, and the human condition. With a keen insight into the inner workings of the heart, she reveals the darker aspects of love, the obsessive tendencies, and the all-consuming passion that can both uplift and destroy. The Prince of Hearts, with his charismatic and wicked nature, is the perfect foil to Evangeline's innocence and naivety, and their complicated dance is a highlight of the novel.

Garber's world-building is, as always, impeccable. The world of "Once Upon a Broken Heart" is a rich tapestry of fairy tale and mythology, with a unique magic system that adds depth and complexity to the narrative. The characters, too, are multidimensional and nuanced, with motivations that are both understandable and relatable.

One of the most striking aspects of "Once Upon a Broken Heart" is its exploration of the consequences of one's actions. Evangeline's bargain with the Prince of Hearts sets in motion a chain of events that she cannot control, and the novel raises important questions about the nature of choice and consequence. As Evangeline navigates the treacherous landscape of her own heart, she must confront the darker aspects of her own desires and the true cost of her happily ever after.

In short, "Once Upon a Broken Heart" is a captivating and enchanting novel that will appeal to fans of fairy tale and romance alike. With its lush prose, complex characters, and nuanced exploration of the human heart, this novel is a must-read for anyone who has ever believed in the transformative power of love.

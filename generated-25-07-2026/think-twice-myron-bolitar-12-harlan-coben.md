# Think Twice (Myron Bolitar, #12)

**Author:** Harlan Coben  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

Think Twice, the latest installment in Harlan Coben's Myron Bolitar series, is a gripping thriller that masterfully weaves together secrets, lies, and conspiracies, keeping readers on the edge of their seats. Coben's signature style of intricate plotting, coupled with his ability to craft relatable characters, makes this novel a must-read for fans of the genre.

The story revolves around Myron Bolitar, a sports agent with a penchant for getting entangled in complex cases, and his quest to uncover the truth behind the alleged murder committed by his former client, Greg Downing. What begins as a simple inquiry by two federal agents quickly spirals into a labyrinthine investigation, forcing Myron and his trusted friend and colleague, Win, to navigate a treacherous web of deceit and betrayal.

Coben's expertise in crafting suspenseful narratives is on full display in Think Twice. The author's use of short, punchy chapters and clever plot twists keeps the reader guessing, making it impossible to put the book down. The pacing is expertly managed, with each new revelation adding depth to the mystery and raising the stakes for the characters.

One of the standout aspects of Coben's writing is his ability to create complex, multidimensional characters. Myron Bolitar, in particular, is a fascinating protagonist, with a rich backstory that adds depth to the narrative. His relationships with those around him, including Win and his clients, are authentic and well-developed, making it easy to become invested in their lives.

The themes of identity, loyalty, and the blurred lines between truth and deception are expertly woven throughout the narrative. Coben raises important questions about the nature of reality and how easily it can be manipulated, leaving the reader to ponder the implications long after finishing the book.

In typical Coben fashion, Think Twice is a masterclass in plotting, with a complex, expertly constructed narrative that will keep even the most seasoned thriller readers guessing. The author's use of misdirection and clever plot twists is unparalleled, making this novel a must-read for fans of the genre.

In conclusion, Think Twice is a gripping, expertly crafted thriller that showcases Harlan Coben's signature style. With its intricate plotting, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes, this novel is sure to appeal to fans of the Myron Bolitar series and newcomers alike.

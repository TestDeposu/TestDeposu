# This Present Darkness (Darkness, #1)

**Author:** Frank E. Peretti  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**Title: A Cosmic Clash in "This Present Darkness"**

Frank E. Peretti's "This Present Darkness" is a groundbreaking novel that seamlessly blends spiritual themes with suspenseful storytelling, birthing an entirely new genre: spiritual suspense. Published in 2012, this book has sold over 2 million copies worldwide, leaving an indelible mark on readers and sparking a renewed interest in the concept of spiritual warfare.

At its core, "This Present Darkness" is a battle cry, echoing Ephesians 6:12, "For we do not wrestle against flesh and blood, but against the rulers, against the authorities, against the cosmic powers over this present darkness, against the spiritual forces of evil in the heavenly places." The story unfolds in the unlikely setting of Ashton, a small college town, where an ordinary festival turns extraordinary when reporter Bernice Kreuger's camera film is mysteriously destroyed. This seemingly innocuous event sets the stage for a cosmic clash between good and evil, unseen by the human protagonists but raging fiercely in the spiritual realm.

Peretti's narrative style is as engaging as it is unique. He masterfully weaves together two parallel storylines: the human drama unfolding in Ashton and the celestial battle waged in the heavens. This dual perspective creates a compelling tension, drawing readers in and keeping them on the edge of their seats. Peretti's characters are well-crafted, each with their own distinct voice and arc, making it easy for readers to invest in their journey.

One of the most striking aspects of "This Present Darkness" is Peretti's unapologetic exploration of spiritual themes. He delves into the nature of good and evil, the power of prayer, and the reality of spiritual warfare. Yet, despite the heavy themes, Peretti's writing is accessible and engaging, making this book a compelling read for both avid and casual readers alike.

Moreover, Peretti's ability to create a sense of urgency and tension is commendable. The pacing is fast and relentless, propelling the story forward with a momentum that is hard to resist. This is not a book that can be easily put down; it demands to be read in a single sitting.

In conclusion, "This Present Darkness" is more than just a book; it's an experience. It's a thrilling ride through the spiritual realm, a thought-provoking exploration of good and evil, and a testament to the power of prayer. It's no wonder this book has left an indelible mark on readers worldwide.

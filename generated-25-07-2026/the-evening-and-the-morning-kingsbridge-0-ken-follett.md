# The Evening and the Morning (Kingsbridge, #0)

**Author:** Ken Follett  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

**A Masterful Return to Kingsbridge**

Ken Follett's "The Evening and the Morning" is a triumphant return to the world of Kingsbridge, the setting of his beloved novel "The Pillars of the Earth." This prequel, set in the tumultuous 10th century, is a testament to Follett's storytelling prowess, weaving a tapestry of intrigue, ambition, and human drama that will captivate both new readers and those familiar with the series.

Follett masterfully paints a vivid picture of England on the cusp of the Middle Ages, a land torn between Viking invasions and Welsh rebellions, where power is wielded by those who seek it, not those who deserve it. The narrative is a symphony of voices, centering around three compelling characters: Edwyn, a boatbuilder whose life is upended by Viking raids; Ragna, a Norman noblewoman navigating the treacherous waters of her husband's homeland; and Brother Godwin, a monk with a vision to transform his abbey into a beacon of learning.

Follett's signature style is evident throughout, with intricate plotting, well-drawn characters, and a keen eye for historical detail. He expertly balances political intrigue, religious conflict, and personal drama, creating a richly textured narrative that keeps the pages turning. The novel's pacing is impeccable, building towards a climax that sets the stage for "The Pillars of the Earth."

"The Evening and the Morning" is not just a prequel, but a standalone novel that stands tall in its own right. Follett's exploration of power, ambition, and the human spirit is as relevant today as it was a thousand years ago. This is a book that demands to be read, discussed, and remembered.

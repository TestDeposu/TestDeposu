# It Ain't Me, Babe (Hades Hangmen, #1)

**Author:** Tillie Cole  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

**Title: "It Ain't Me, Babe" by Tillie Cole: A Raw, Compelling Tale of Love and Redemption**

In the sprawling, gritty world of the Hades Hangmen, Tillie Cole's "It Ain't Me, Babe" takes us on a thrilling ride, exploring the depths of love, loyalty, and the power of redemption. This isn't your typical romance; it's a raw, unapologetic journey into the heart of an outlaw biker and the woman who challenges his world.

At the helm of the Hades Hangmen is River Styx Nash, a man as feared as he is respected. Born into a life dominated by Harley Davidson engines, drugs, and casual sex, Styx has built an impenetrable fortress around his heart. His stutter, a constant reminder of his past, has honed his fists into deadly weapons, and his loyalty is as unyielding as his resolve. Yet, when he finds Salomé, a young woman on the run from a cult, his carefully constructed world begins to crumble.

Salomé, with her innocent eyes and fierce spirit, is a beacon of light in Styx's dark world. Raised in a cult, she's never known the touch of a man, let alone the love of one. Their connection is instant and intense, a dance of opposites that draws them together like moths to a flame. Cole masterfully weaves their story, each page turning revealing a deeper layer of their complex relationship.

Cole's style is gritty and unapologetic, much like her characters. She doesn't shy away from the harsh realities of Styx's world, nor does she romanticize it. Instead, she uses it as a stark contrast to Salomé's innocence, creating a compelling dynamic that keeps you on the edge of your seat. The chemistry between Styx and Salomé is palpable, their love story a slow burn that ignites into an inferno.

At its core, "It Ain't Me, Babe" is a story about love conquering all. It's about finding light in the darkest places and love in the most unexpected of hearts. It's about redemption, not just for Styx, but for Salomé too, as she learns to navigate a world she never knew existed.

Cole's storytelling is engaging, her characters complex and relatable, and her world-building immersive. She doesn't shy away from tough topics, instead, she tackles them head-on, making "It Ain't Me, Babe" a compelling read that will leave you breathless.

# Killing Sarai (In the Company of Killers, #1)

**Author:** J.A. Redmerski  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

In "Killing Sarai", J.A. Redmerski weaves a gripping tale of survival, redemption, and the unlikeliest of loves. This intense thriller follows Sarai, a young woman held captive by a ruthless Mexican cartel lord, and Victor, a ruthless hitman who becomes her unlikely savior.

Redmerski's writing is a masterclass in atmospheric tension, plunging the reader into the dark, gritty world of organized crime. Her vivid descriptions of the Meksykan landscape and the cartel's brutal inner workings are both captivating and unsettling, making it impossible to look away from the horrors unfolding on the page.

At its core, "Killing Sarai" is a story about the transformative power of human connection in the face of unimaginable trauma. Sarai, having lost all sense of normalcy and hope, finds an unexpected ally in Victor, a man whose profession is built on violence and detachment. As they navigate the treacherous landscape of their world, they begin to form a bond that defies the odds, one that is both tender and fierce.

Redmerski's characterization is superb, particularly in her portrayal of Victor, a complex, multifaceted anti-hero whose brutal nature is slowly chipped away to reveal a deep well of emotion. His struggles to reconcile his profession with his growing feelings for Sarai are both poignant and compelling, making him a deeply relatable and human character.

Sarai, too, is a remarkable protagonist, her resilience and determination in the face of unimaginable hardship inspiring and heartbreaking in equal measure. Her journey from captive to empowered individual is a powerful exploration of the human spirit's capacity for survival and growth.

The author's style is unflinching and raw, tackling themes of violence, exploitation, and trauma with unapologetic honesty. While the subject matter can be challenging to read at times, Redmerski's masterful storytelling ensures that the narrative remains engaging and thought-provoking throughout.

If you're a fan of intense, gripping thrillers with complex characters and morally ambiguous themes, "Killing Sarai" is an absolute must-read. With its breakneck pacing, vivid world-building, and unforgettable characters, this book will keep you on the edge of your seat from start to finish.

# I'm Just Saying: A Guide to Maintaining Civil Discourse in an Increasingly Divided World

**Author:** Milan Kordestani  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

In a world where division and discord seem to be the order of the day, Milan Kordestani's "I'm Just Saying: A Guide to Maintaining Civil Discourse in an Increasingly Divided World" is a breath of fresh air. This timely and thought-provoking book offers a straightforward look at the history and art of maintaining courteous communication, even in the face of adversity. As the winner of the 2024 International Book Awards in Current Events and Social Change categories, and finalist in Business: General and Business: Motivational categories, "I'm Just Saying" is a must-read for anyone looking to navigate the complexities of modern conversation.

Kordestani's writing style is engaging, relatable, and accessible, making this book an enjoyable read for a wide range of audiences. Through a combination of contemporary case studies, personal experiences, and historical examples, the author shows us that challenging conversations can be a catalyst for growth and understanding. By exploring the concept of civil discourse, Kordestani provides practical advice and strategies for respectful communication, even in the face of disagreement.

One of the strengths of "I'm Just Saying" is its ability to balance theory with practice. Kordestani draws on his own experiences as the founder of The Doe, an online publication focused on civil discourse, to offer insightful and actionable tips for improving our communication skills. The book is filled with real-life examples and anecdotes that illustrate the challenges and benefits of engaging in constructive conversations.

Throughout the book, Kordestani emphasizes the importance of civil discourse in a democratic society. He argues that by engaging in respectful and open-minded dialogue, we can rebuild bridges and foster a deeper understanding of different perspectives. This is particularly relevant in today's digital age, where social media platforms can often amplify division and polarization.

Kordestani's approach to civil discourse is not about avoiding conflict or difficult conversations, but rather about engaging in them with empathy, respect, and an open mind. He encourages readers to seek common ground, even with those who hold opposing views, and to approach conversations with a willingness to listen and learn.

The book's title, "I'm Just Saying," is a nod to the often-frustrating experience of feeling like we're not being heard or understood. Kordestani shows us that by adopting a more mindful and respectful approach to communication, we can move beyond the impasse of "I'm just saying" and towards a more constructive and meaningful exchange.

Overall, "I'm Just Saying" is a valuable resource for anyone looking to improve their communication skills and contribute to a more civil and respectful public discourse. Kordestani's writing is engaging, informative, and inspiring, making this book a must-read for individuals, communities, and organizations looking to build bridges and foster greater understanding.

# Murder on Family Grounds (Mary Wandwalker #3)

**Author:** Susan Rowland  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

In "Murder on Family Grounds", the third installment of the Mary Wandwalker series, Susan Rowland weaves a complex and gripping narrative that delves into the darker aspects of family dynamics, secrets, and the unrelenting power of the past. This masterfully crafted mystery follows Mary Wandwalker, a highly skilled and intuitive protagonist, as she navigates a web of deceit and murder that threatens to destroy the Falconer family.

At the heart of the story lies a decades-old tragedy that has left an indelible mark on Mary's life. Her fiancé, David Falconer, died in a car crash, and the family's refusal to offer support during her time of need has fostered a deep-seated resentment. Now, forty years later, Mary is faced with the opportunity to confront her past when her son, George Jones, a policeman, reaches out to her. As Mary becomes embroiled in the Falconer family's dark secrets, she must also contend with the reappearance of a mysterious figure from her past, known only as "the Kestrel".

Rowland's writing is characterized by its atmospheric and evocative style, which skillfully transports the reader to the English countryside and the opulent world of the Falconer estate. Her prose is rich in detail, and the characters are multidimensional and expertly drawn. Mary Wandwalker, in particular, is a compelling protagonist, whose complex past and intuitive nature make her an ideal detective.

One of the most striking aspects of "Murder on Family Grounds" is its exploration of the destructive power of secrets and the devastating consequences of keeping them. The Falconer family's history is marked by a series of tragic events, which are slowly revealed throughout the narrative. As Mary delves deeper into the mystery, she uncovers a web of deceit and corruption that threatens to destroy not only the Falconer family but also her own son.

The author's use of historical references and cultural allusions adds depth and complexity to the narrative, making it a truly immersive reading experience. The inclusion of a Russian oligarch, Viktor Solokov, and his enigmatic wife, Anna, adds an element of international intrigue, which raises questions about the nature of power and corruption.

In conclusion, "Murder on Family Grounds" is a gripping and atmospheric mystery that will appeal to fans of the genre. Susan Rowland's writing is engaging, and her characters are expertly drawn. The novel's exploration of the destructive power of secrets and the devastating consequences of keeping them makes for a compelling read. If you're looking for a mystery that will keep you on the edge of your seat, look no further than "Murder on Family Grounds".

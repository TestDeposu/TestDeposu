# Vicious (Villains, #1)

**Author:** V.E. Schwab  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

**Vicious: A Supercharged Tale of Ambition and Betrayal**

In the realm of superhero narratives, V.E. Schwab's "Vicious" stands out as a dark, gritty gem that subverts expectations and delights in moral ambiguity. This isn't your typical tale of capes and spandex; it's a gripping exploration of ambition, jealousy, and the consequences of playing with powers beyond our understanding.

At its core, "Vicious" is a story about two former friends, Victor and Eli, who discover the secret to gaining extraordinary abilities. Their journey, however, is far from heroic. Schwab masterfully crafts a world where superpowers aren't bestowed by cosmic rays or mystical artifacts, but rather, through near-death experiences and a healthy dose of adrenaline. This unique origin story sets the stage for a gripping cat-and-mouse game between the two former friends, now bitter enemies.

Schwab's prose is as sharp as her characters' intellects, slicing through the narrative with precision and flair. She paints vivid, often haunting images with her words, drawing readers into a world that feels both familiar and eerily otherworldly. Her characters are equally compelling, each one a complex web of strengths and weaknesses, motivations and fears. Victor and Eli are no exceptions; they're flawed, brilliant, and utterly compelling antiheroes, driven by ambition and jealousy to the point of self-destruction.

The heart of "Vicious" lies in its exploration of power and its consequences. Schwab doesn't shy away from the darker aspects of her characters' abilities, instead delving into the moral grey areas that arise when people gain powers beyond their control. She challenges the traditional hero's journey, presenting readers with characters who are neither purely good nor purely evil, but rather, a complex mix of both.

"Vicious" is a thrilling, thought-provoking read that will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very end. Schwab's unique take on the superhero genre, combined with her evocative prose and complex characters, makes for a story that's as compelling as it is chilling. This is a book that demands to be read, discussed, and read again.

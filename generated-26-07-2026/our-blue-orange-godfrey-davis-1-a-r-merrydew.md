# Our Blue Orange (Godfrey Davis, #1)

**Author:** A.R. Merrydew  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Unknown

**Our Blue Orange: A Radiant Debut**

In the vast, uncharted territory of speculative fiction, A.R. Merrydew's "Our Blue Orange" gleams like a newly discovered planet, pulsating with originality and wonder. The first installment in the Godfrey Davis series, this novel is a dazzling exploration of identity, memory, and the power of color, wrapped in a narrative that's as captivating as it is thought-provoking.

Merrydew's prose is a symphony of sensory details, painting each scene with vivid, almost tactile, descriptions. The world of Godfrey Davis is not just seen, but felt, tasted, and heard. This immersive style is reminiscent of Neil Gaiman's whimsical storytelling, yet Merrydew forges her own path, infusing her narrative with a unique blend of magic realism and hard science fiction.

At the heart of "Our Blue Orange" lies the enigmatic Blue Orange, a sentient, otherworldly entity that can manipulate color and memory. Through it, Merrydew delves into the complex nature of identity, questioning what it means to be human when our very essence can be altered or erased. The Blue Orange is more than just a plot device; it's a character in its own right, a silent, ethereal presence that haunts the narrative, leaving readers craving more.

Merrydew's exploration of memory is equally compelling. She deftly navigates the labyrinthine corridors of the mind, exploring how our past shapes us, and how we, in turn, shape our memories. The story's non-linear structure, with its fragmented timelines and dreamlike sequences, mirrors the fluid, elusive nature of memory itself, making for a reading experience that's as disorienting as it is engaging.

Yet, for all its philosophical depth, "Our Blue Orange" is never heavy-handed. Merrydew balances her heady themes with a healthy dose of humor and warmth, creating characters that are as endearing as they are flawed. Godfrey Davis, the titular protagonist, is a delightful blend of curiosity, courage, and vulnerability, making him a hero readers can truly root for.

In a genre often dominated by epic battles and grand-scale adventures, "Our Blue Orange" stands out as a testament to the power of intimate, character-driven storytelling. It's a novel that lingers in the mind long after the final page, inviting readers to question, to ponder, and to marvel at the extraordinary within the ordinary.

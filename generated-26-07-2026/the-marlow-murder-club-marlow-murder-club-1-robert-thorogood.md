# The Marlow Murder Club (Marlow Murder Club, #1)

**Author:** Robert Thorogood  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**A Whiskey-Soaked, Puzzle-Piecing Delight: "The Marlow Murder Club"**

Robert Thorogood, creator of the beloved BBC One series "Death in Paradise," serves up a tantalizing new mystery with "The Marlow Murder Club." This isn't your grandmother's cozy; it's a sharp, witty, and utterly engaging tale that will leave you craving more.

At the heart of the story is Judith, a spirited septuagenarian who's more at home with a glass of whiskey and a crossword than knitting and tea. When she stumbles upon a murder in her quiet town of Marlow, she's not about to let the local police dismiss it as a simple suicide. Thus, the Marlow Murder Club is born, with Judith at the helm, joined by an unlikely crew of sidekicks: Suzie, the dogwalker with a penchant for gossip, and Becks, the vicar's wife hiding a secret or two.

Thorogood's style is a delightful blend of humor and suspense, reminiscent of Richard Osman's "The Thursday Murder Club" but with a unique twist. He masterfully weaves together a complex puzzle, keeping readers guessing until the very end. The characters are as charming as they are flawed, each bringing something fresh to the table. Judith, in particular, is a force to be reckoned with, proving that age is no barrier to adventure and intellect.

The story is peppered with clever wordplay and laugh-out-loud moments, yet beneath the humor lies a dark heart, as the Marlow Murder Club finds themselves ensnared in a deadly game. Thorogood expertly balances tension and levity, creating a page-turner that's as entertaining as it is thought-provoking.

Fans of classic mysteries will appreciate the nods to Agatha Christie, while those seeking a modern take on the cozy genre will find a welcome addition in "The Marlow Murder Club." Thorogood has crafted a story that's equal parts clever, funny, and heartwarming, making it an absolute joy to read.

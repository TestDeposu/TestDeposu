# The Longest Con: How Grifters, Swindlers, and Frauds Hijacked American Conservatism

**Author:** Joe Conason  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

**The Longest Con: A Scathing Exposé of American Conservatism's Moral Decline**

In *The Longest Con*, veteran journalist Joe Conason delivers a blistering, yet hilariously entertaining, takedown of the American right's descent into a web of deceit, greed, and moral bankruptcy. With a prose style as sharp as a switchblade, Conason masterfully chronicles the rise of grifters, swindlers, and frauds who have hijacked conservatism, culminating in the presidency of Donald Trump.

Conason's narrative is a veritable rogues' gallery of charlatans, from the "professional anti-communists" of the mid-20th century to the prosperity gospel preachers and Tea Party activists of today. Each chapter is a gripping tale of chicanery, revealing how these con artists have exploited the fears and frustrations of their followers, all while lining their own pockets.

The author's sardonic wit is on full display as he skewers the likes of Rush Limbaugh, Newt Gingrich, and the Koch brothers, exposing their hypocrisy and venality with relish. Yet, Conason's humor never overshadows his serious intent. He is a relentless investigator, meticulously documenting the right's long, steady slide into a moral abyss.

At the heart of *The Longest Con* is a profound sadness. Conason mourns the loss of a conservative movement that once stood for principle and integrity, now reduced to a racket where truth is a casualty and power is the only currency. He laments the damage done to American democracy by these con artists, who have stoked division, undermined institutions, and left a trail of broken promises.

Yet, Conason's book is not just a eulogy for a lost cause. It is also a call to arms, a wake-up call to those who have been taken in by the right's con game. With his characteristic wit and wisdom, Conason shows us how to recognize the grifters, how to resist their siren calls, and how to fight back.

In the end, *The Longest Con* is a powerful reminder that democracy is not a given, but a fragile thing that must be constantly guarded and defended. It is a clarion call to action, a battle cry in the war against deceit and corruption. And it is, above all, a damn good read.

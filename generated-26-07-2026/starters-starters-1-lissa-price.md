# Starters (Starters, #1)

**Author:** Lissa Price  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**Starters: A Riveting Dystopian Thriller**

In a world ravaged by the Spore Wars, where the elderly have seized control, Lissa Price's "Starters" offers a chilling exploration of youth, identity, and the price of immortality. This international bestseller is a gripping, fast-paced journey that will captivate fans of dystopian fiction.

At the heart of "Starters" is Callie, a resilient 16-year-old navigating a harsh, post-apocalyptic Los Angeles. Orphaned and on the run, she's forced to make desperate choices to keep herself and her little brother alive. When she stumbles upon Prime Destinations, a sinister organization that rents out teen bodies to the wealthy and elderly, Callie sees a way out. But a malfunctioning neurochip thrusts her into the mind and body of her renter, revealing a terrifying conspiracy that goes far beyond a simple body swap.

Price's prose is taut and engaging, drawing readers in with vivid descriptions of a decaying LA and a haunting exploration of what it means to be young in a world that fears and exploits youth. Her characters are complex and relatable, their struggles and triumphs resonating long after the last page is turned.

"Starters" is more than just a thrilling ride; it's a thought-provoking meditation on identity, mortality, and the lengths we'll go to cheat death. It's a cautionary tale about the dangers of unchecked power and the dehumanizing effects of commodifying life. Yet, amidst the darkness, Price weaves in moments of hope and love, making "Starters" a compelling, pulse-pounding read that will leave you breathless and eager for more.

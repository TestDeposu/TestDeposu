# Beauty: A Retelling of the Story of Beauty and the Beast

**Author:** Robin McKinley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

**A Timeless Tale, Retold with Grace and Depth**

In "Beauty," Robin McKinley masterfully reimagines the classic French fairy tale, weaving a captivating narrative that transcends the simplicity of its origins. This is not merely a retelling; it's a profound exploration of love, identity, and the transformative power of understanding.

McKinley's prose is elegant and sophisticated, yet approachable. She paints vivid, sensory details that transport readers into the story, from the bustling merchant town to the eerie, enchanting castle. Her characters, too, are richly drawn. Beauty, our protagonist, is no passive damsel; she's intelligent, curious, and fiercely independent. Her journey from a plain, book-loving girl to a woman who challenges societal norms and tames a Beast is compelling and empowering.

The Beast, meanwhile, is no mere monster. McKinley imbues him with complexity and pathos, making him a sympathetic figure long before the reveal of his true form. Their relationship, built on mutual respect and understanding, is the heart of the story. It's a love that blossoms slowly, naturally, and beautifully—an antidote to the instant gratification of many modern romances.

At its core, "Beauty" is a tale about seeing beyond appearances. It challenges us to look past societal expectations and first impressions, to seek understanding and empathy. McKinley's exploration of these themes is nuanced and thought-provoking, making "Beauty" a story that resonates long after the final page.

In a world filled with instant gratification and shallow narratives, "Beauty" stands out as a testament to the power of storytelling. It's a reminder that true beauty lies not in physical appearance, but in the depth of one's character and the strength of one's heart.

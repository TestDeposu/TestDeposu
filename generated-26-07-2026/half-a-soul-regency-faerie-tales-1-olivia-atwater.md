# Half a Soul (Regency Faerie Tales, #1)

**Author:** Olivia Atwater  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

In Olivia Atwater's captivating debut, Half a Soul, the rigid social hierarchy of Regency England is turned on its head with the introduction of magical faeries and a dash of wit. The story follows Theodora Ettings, a young lady afflicted with a most peculiar curse - she possesses only half a soul. This affliction renders her immune to fear and embarrassment, often landing her in precarious and scandalous situations.

Atwater masterfully weaves a narrative that is both whimsical and grounded, transporting readers to a world where faeries and humans coexist in a delicate dance of power and intrigue. The author's writing style is characterized by a keen sense of humor, clever wordplay, and a deep understanding of the social conventions of the time period. The result is a story that is at once a charming romance, a witty commentary on societal norms, and a thrilling adventure.

One of the standout aspects of Half a Soul is its exploration of the constraints placed on women during the Regency era. Theodora's curse serves as a clever metaphor for the societal expectations that govern women's behavior, and her struggles to navigate these expectations are both poignant and humorous. Through Theodora's journey, Atwater sheds light on the limited options available to women of the time, highlighting the tensions between conformity and individuality.

The romance that blossoms between Theodora and the enigmatic Elias Wilder, Lord Sorcier, is a slow-burning and delightful affair. Their courtship is marked by a series of awkward encounters, witty repartee, and a deepening emotional connection. Atwater handles their relationship with sensitivity and nuance, never shying away from the complexities and challenges that come with loving someone who is, in many ways, broken.

The world-building in Half a Soul is equally impressive, with a richly detailed faerie society that exists in tandem with the human world. The faeries are depicted as capricious and powerful beings, with their own rules and motivations. This adds a layer of depth to the story, as Theodora and Elias must navigate the intricate web of faerie politics and alliances.

Overall, Half a Soul is a captivating and charming read that will appeal to fans of historical fantasy, romance, and faerie lore. Atwater's writing is engaging, witty, and insightful, making this debut a standout in the genre. With its unique blend of magic, humor, and social commentary, Half a Soul is sure to delight readers and leave them eagerly anticipating the next installment in the Regency Faerie Tales series.

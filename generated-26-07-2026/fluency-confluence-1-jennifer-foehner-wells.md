# Fluency (Confluence, #1)

**Author:** Jennifer Foehner Wells  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Unknown

**Fluency: A Stellar Debut in Science Fiction**

In the vast expanse of contemporary science fiction, Jennifer Foehner Wells' "Fluency" stands out as a beacon, a compelling first installment in her Confluence series that masterfully blends intrigue, action, and thought-provoking themes. Wells, a newcomer to the genre, demonstrates an impressive command of her craft, weaving a tale that is as engaging as it is intellectually stimulating.

At the heart of "Fluency" lies the exploration of language, identity, and the power of communication. The story unfolds in a future where humanity has colonized distant planets, each with its unique ecosystem and indigenous alien species. The Confluence, a vast, ancient structure discovered on one such planet, holds the key to understanding these alien civilizations and their complex languages. Enter Dr. Jane Holloway, a linguist with an extraordinary ability to learn and communicate in alien tongues, who becomes entangled in a web of political intrigue, scientific discovery, and self-discovery.

Wells' prose is crisp and concise, her pacing expertly balanced between action-packed sequences and introspective moments. She deftly navigates the complexities of her futuristic world, creating a richly textured universe that feels both alien and familiar. Her characters, particularly Jane, are well-drawn and relatable, their growth and evolution driving the narrative forward.

What sets "Fluency" apart is its exploration of language as a tool of power and understanding. Wells delves into the intricacies of linguistics, the challenges of communicating across species, and the ethical implications of doing so. She poses thought-provoking questions about identity, culture, and the potential consequences of miscommunication. This philosophical underpinning elevates "Fluency" from a mere adventure story to a thought-provoking meditation on the human experience.

Moreover, Wells' writing is infused with a sense of wonder and curiosity, a testament to her love for the genre. She seamlessly incorporates elements of mystery, romance, and political drama, creating a story that is as captivating as it is thought-provoking.

In conclusion, "Fluency" is a remarkable debut that bodes well for Jennifer Foehner Wells' future in science fiction. It is a story that will appeal to fans of thought-provoking science fiction, linguistics enthusiasts, and anyone who enjoys a well-crafted tale that challenges the mind and engages the heart.

# A Curse for True Love (Once Upon a Broken Heart, #3)

**Author:** Stephanie Garber  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

A Curse for True Love, the third installment in Stephanie Garber's Once Upon a Broken Heart series, is a mesmerizing tale of love, curses, and the unrelenting pursuit of happily ever after. Garber masterfully weaves a narrative that is both a poignant exploration of the human heart and a sweeping fantasy that transports readers to a world of wonder and magic.

At the heart of the story is Evangeline Fox, a young woman who has always believed in the transformative power of true love. Her world is shattered, however, when she learns that the love of her life is to marry another. Desperate to stop the wedding and mend her wounded heart, Evangeline makes a Faustian bargain with the enigmatic and sinister Prince of Hearts. The prince's help comes at a steep price: three kisses, to be given at the time and place of his choosing.

As Evangeline navigates the treacherous landscape of her bargain, she begins to realize that the Prince of Hearts is not what he seems. His true intentions are shrouded in mystery, and Evangeline finds herself drawn into a world of ancient magic and forgotten lore. Garber's prose is as lush and evocative as ever, conjuring vivid images of moonlit gardens, crumbling castles, and whispered secrets.

One of the most striking aspects of A Curse for True Love is its thoughtful exploration of the complexities of love and relationships. Garber raises important questions about the nature of true love, the dangers of obsession, and the devastating consequences of playing with forces beyond one's control. Evangeline's journey is a powerful reminder that love is a many-splendored thing, capable of bringing great joy and great sorrow in equal measure.

Garber's writing style is, as always, a highlight of the book. Her use of language is deliberate and evocative, conjuring a dreamlike atmosphere that is both captivating and unsettling. The world-building is meticulous and detailed, with a deep history that underpins the narrative. The characters, too, are richly drawn and complex, with motivations that are multifaceted and often surprising.

In many ways, A Curse for True Love is a classic tale of good vs. evil, with the forces of light and darkness locked in an eternal struggle. Yet, Garber's narrative is far more nuanced than that, acknowledging the gray areas that exist between good and evil. The Prince of Hearts, in particular, is a fascinating figure, driven by motivations that are both sinister and strangely compelling.

Overall, A Curse for True Love is a captivating and deeply satisfying read. Garber's writing is, as always, a delight, and her world-building is meticulous and immersive. The story is a powerful exploration of the complexities of love and relationships, and the characters are richly drawn and complex. If you're a fan of fantasy, romance, or simply great storytelling, then A Curse for True Love is a must-read.

# These Is My Words: The Diary of Sarah Agnes Prine, 1881-1901, Arizona Territories (Sarah Agnes Prine, #1)

**Author:** Nancy E. Turner  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

"These Is My Words" by Nancy E. Turner is a sweeping American epic that whisks readers away to the unforgiving yet majestic Arizona Territories of the late 19th century. This captivating novel, inspired by the author's own family memoirs, is a poignant and powerful portrayal of a woman's unyielding spirit and her quest to carve out a life of purpose and meaning in a harsh and unfamiliar land.

At the heart of this stunning narrative is Sarah Agnes Prine, a fiercely determined and strong-willed heroine who embodies the very essence of the American frontier spirit. Through her diary entries, which span two decades, Sarah shares her innermost thoughts, feelings, and experiences, laying bare her joys, sorrows, and struggles as she navigates the treacherous terrain of her life. With unflinching honesty and vulnerability, Sarah recounts her journey from a wide-eyed child to a resolute young woman, and ultimately, to a loving mother, whose love and devotion become the bedrock of her existence.

Turner's masterful storytelling is characterized by its vivid and immersive quality, which effortlessly transports readers to the dusty, sun-scorched landscapes of the Arizona Territories. With meticulous attention to detail, the author recreates the everyday lives of the people who inhabited this unforgiving land, from the mundane routines of daily life to the dramatic events that shaped their destinies. The cast of characters that populates this novel is equally impressive, with each one meticulously crafted to bring depth, nuance, and complexity to the narrative.

One of the most compelling aspects of "These Is My Words" is its thoughtful exploration of the human experience. Through Sarah's story, Turner probes the intricacies of the human heart, laying bare the hopes, fears, and dreams that define us all. The novel is a powerful testament to the enduring power of love, which becomes the linchpin of Sarah's existence, particularly in her relationship with Captain Jack Elliot, a cavalry officer who becomes the love of her life.

Turner's writing style is as evocative as it is elegant, with a narrative voice that is both lyrical and accessible. Her use of language is deliberate and measured, conjuring up the stark beauty of the Arizona landscape and the people who called it home. The diary format, which could have been a limiting device in less skilled hands, becomes a masterful stroke, allowing Turner to craft a narrative that is both intimate and expansive.

In short, "These Is My Words" is a triumph of historical fiction, a novel that will captivate readers with its richly detailed world-building, its memorable characters, and its universal themes. This is a book that will resonate with anyone who has ever felt the call of the unknown, the allure of adventure, or the power of love to transform and transcend.

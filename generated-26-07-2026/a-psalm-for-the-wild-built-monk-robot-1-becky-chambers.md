# A Psalm for the Wild-Built (Monk & Robot, #1)

**Author:** Becky Chambers  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

A Psalm for the Wild-Built, the first installment in Becky Chambers' Monk and Robot series, is a thought-provoking and captivating novel that delves into the complexities of human existence and the search for meaning in a world where technological advancements have made all basic needs easily accessible. This Hugo Award-winning novel is a testament to Chambers' exceptional storytelling skills and her unique ability to craft relatable characters that resonate deeply with readers.

The story takes place in a world where robots, having gained self-awareness centuries ago, abandoned their duties and disappeared into the wilderness, leaving behind a society that has grown accustomed to their absence. The narrative follows a tea monk, a spiritual guide who travels the world offering comfort and solace to those in need, whose life is disrupted by the arrival of a robot seeking answers to a fundamental question: "what do people need?" This inquiry sets off a chain of events that challenges the monk's perceptions and forces him to reevaluate his own purpose in life.

One of the most striking aspects of A Psalm for the Wild-Built is its exploration of the human condition. Chambers masterfully weaves together themes of existentialism, spirituality, and the search for meaning, raising important questions about the nature of happiness, fulfillment, and what it means to be alive. Through the tea monk's journey, we see a character grappling with the idea that having all one's basic needs met does not necessarily guarantee happiness or a sense of purpose. This is a powerful commentary on our own society, where technological advancements and material possessions often fail to bring us the satisfaction we crave.

Chambers' writing style is characterized by her ability to create a sense of intimacy and immediacy, drawing readers into the world of the story and making them feel like they are part of the narrative. Her prose is lyrical and evocative, with a focus on the emotional and spiritual journeys of her characters. The world-building in A Psalm for the Wild-Built is also noteworthy, with a richly detailed and immersive environment that feels both familiar and yet, utterly unique.

The relationship between the tea monk and the robot is a highlight of the novel, with a beautiful dynamic that is both poignant and humorous. The robot's naivety and curiosity about human nature serve as a catalyst for the monk's growth, and their interactions are filled with a deep sense of empathy and understanding.

A Psalm for the Wild-Built is a novel that will resonate with fans of science fiction, philosophy, and spirituality. It is a book that will make you think, feel, and reflect on your own place in the world. Chambers' writing is a gift, and this novel is a testament to her skill as a storyteller. If you're looking for a thought-provoking and deeply moving read, A Psalm for the Wild-Built is an absolute must-read.

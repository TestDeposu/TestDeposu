# Grave Mercy (His Fair Assassin, #1)

**Author:** Robin LaFevers  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

**Grave Mercy: A Dark, Enchanting Tale of Assassin Nuns**

In the vein of *The Young Elites* and *And I Darken*, Robin LaFevers' *Grave Mercy* weaves a seductive, dark fantasy that breathes new life into historical fiction. Set in 15th-century Brittany, this captivating novel is the first in the His Fair Assassin trilogy, introducing us to a convent of nuns unlike any other—assassins in the name of Death.

LaFevers masterfully blends history and magic, transporting readers to a world where political intrigue and deadly secrets abound. The high court of Brittany is a lavish yet treacherous stage, where power plays and betrayals are as common as the opulent gowns and feasts. Yet, it's the convent of Saint Mortain that steals the show, a sanctuary that trains young women to be Death's handmaidens, each paying their price in blood.

Our heroine, Ismae, is no ordinary girl. Escaping an abusive betrothal, she finds refuge in the convent, where she discovers her unique gift—an ability to discern truth from lies, and a divine mandate to dispense justice. Ismae's journey is one of self-discovery, as she grapples with her duty to Saint Mortain and her growing feelings for a man she's been ordered to kill. LaFevers expertly crafts a morally grey character in Ismae, whose actions challenge our notions of right and wrong, and whose heart aches with a relatable, poignant longing.

LaFevers' prose is as sharp as the daggers her characters wield, each word carefully chosen to paint vivid, immersive scenes. She excels in creating atmospheric tension, whether it's the oppressive weight of the convent's stone walls or the whispered secrets in the court's candlelit corridors. Yet, it's her exploration of found family that truly resonates. The sisterhood of Saint Mortain is a powerful force, their bonds forged in shadow and secrecy, making them a family by choice rather than blood.

*Grave Mercy* is more than just a thrilling tale of assassination and intrigue. It's a story about the power of choice, the complexities of duty, and the lengths we'll go to protect those we love. LaFevers' unique twist on historical fantasy is a must-read for fans of strong female protagonists, dark magic, and political drama.

# The Alexandria Quartet (The Alexandria Quartet, #1-4)

**Author:** Lawrence Durrell  
**Page Count:** Unknown

**A Sensual Symphony of Betrayal and Desire: Lawrence Durrell's The Alexandria Quartet**

In the sun-drenched, labyrinthine streets of Alexandria, Lawrence Durrell weaves a captivating tapestry of passion, intrigue, and philosophical musings in his magnum opus, The Alexandria Quartet. This four-part masterpiece, voted one of the Modern Library's 100 Best Novels of the twentieth century, is a mesmerizing exploration of modern love, set against the backdrop of a city that is as much a character as any of its inhabitants.

Durrell's prose is a symphony, each sentence a note that resonates with the next, creating a harmony that is both intoxicating and thought-provoking. He paints Alexandria with vivid, sensual strokes, immersing readers in its dusty streets, bustling markets, and languid afternoons. The city becomes a character in its own right, a seductive siren that draws the novel's inhabitants into its embrace, and from which they can never truly escape.

At the heart of the Quartet are four unforgettable characters: Darley, the sensitive, introspective narrator; Justine, the passionate, enigmatic woman who sets the plot in motion; Balthazar, the philosophical, world-weary doctor; and Clea, the elegant, enigmatic artist. Their lives intersect and entwine, forming a complex web of love, betrayal, and longing that spans the years before, during, and after World War II.

Durrell masterfully explores the nuances of love in all its forms - romantic, platonic, obsessive, and unrequited. He delves into the human psyche with a psychologist's insight, a poet's lyricism, and a philosopher's depth. The Alexandria Quartet is not just a story of love, but an exploration of the human condition, a meditation on the complexities of desire, and the consequences of our actions.

Yet, for all its depth and complexity, The Alexandria Quartet is also a gripping page-turner. Durrell's narrative is a masterclass in suspense, each novel building on the last, revealing new layers of the story and its characters. The Quartet is a puzzle that Durrell invites readers to solve, piece by piece, until the final, satisfying resolution.

Jan Morris, in her introduction to this ebook, describes Durrell's work as "a kind of literary alchemy, turning the base metal of everyday life into the gold of art." This is an apt description of The Alexandria Quartet, a novel that transforms the mundane into the magical, the ordinary into the extraordinary.

In a world where love is often simplified and sanitized, Durrell's exploration of its complexities is a breath of fresh air. The Alexandria Quartet is a novel that demands to be savored, to be read and re-read, its layers peeled back like an onion, revealing new depths with each reading.

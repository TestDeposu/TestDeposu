# Paranormalcy (Paranormalcy, #1)

**Author:** Kiersten White  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Paranormalcy, the debut novel by Kiersten White, is a captivating and enchanting read that will leave fans of paranormal romance and urban fantasy spellbound. The story revolves around Evie, a feisty and independent teenager who works for the International Paranormal Containment Agency, a secret organization that deals with supernatural creatures. Evie's life is anything but normal - she's got a taser, a mission, and a serious lack of normalcy. But what makes her truly special is her ability to see through supernatural glamours, a skill that sets her apart from the rest.

One of the standout features of this novel is its protagonist, Evie. She's a snarky, kick-butt heroine who is both relatable and endearing. Her wit and sarcasm are on point, making her a joy to read about. Evie's also a complex character, with a rich backstory that is slowly revealed throughout the novel. Her relationships with the people around her, particularly her ex-boyfriend Lend and her new crush, are multifaceted and nuanced.

The author's style is another notable aspect of this novel. Kiersten White's writing is engaging, witty, and downright fun. She has a talent for crafting humorous situations and dialogue that will have you laughing out loud. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, romance, and suspense that keeps you on the edge of your seat. White's world-building is also impressive, with a unique take on the paranormal world that is both creative and believable.

At its core, Paranormalcy is a story about self-discovery and empowerment. Evie's journey is one of growth and transformation, as she navigates the complexities of her world and her place in it. She's a reluctant chosen one, thrust into a dark faerie prophecy that could destroy everything she holds dear. But Evie's not one to back down from a challenge, and her bravery and determination make her a true hero.

The romance in this novel is also worth mentioning. Evie's complicated fae romance with Lend is a highlight of the story, with a deliciously slow-burn tension that will keep you invested. The chemistry between them is palpable, and their banter is some of the wittiest and most charming moments in the novel.

Overall, Paranormalcy is a delightful read that will capture your heart. With its snarky heroine, complicated fae romance, and laugh-out-loud banter, this novel is a must-read for fans of paranormal romance and urban fantasy. Kiersten White's writing is engaging, witty, and fun, making this novel a true page-turner.

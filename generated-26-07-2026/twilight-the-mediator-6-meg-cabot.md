# Twilight (The Mediator, #6)

**Author:** Meg Cabot  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**Title: A Ghostly Farewell: Meg Cabot's "Twilight" Concludes the Mediator Series**

In the sixth and final installment of Meg Cabot's Mediator series, "Twilight," our beloved heroine, Suze Simon, finds herself grappling with a power that challenges her understanding of life, death, and love. Cabot, known for her wit and charm in the Princess Diaries series, weaves a poignant tale that masterfully blends romance, supernatural elements, and coming-of-age themes.

"Twilight" centers around Suze's discovery that she can influence the fate of the recently deceased, determining whether they become ghosts or move on. This revelation forces her to confront the reality of her relationship with Jesse, a 19th-century ghost she's fallen in love with. The story explores the bittersweet nature of their love, as Suze must decide whether to let Jesse go or risk altering history to keep him by her side.

Cabot's signature style shines through in Suze's witty banter and the engaging narrative, which seamlessly blends humor with heart-wrenching moments. The author's ability to create relatable, flawed characters and compelling relationships makes the Mediator series a standout in young adult literature. In "Twilight," Cabot masterfully ties up loose ends, providing a satisfying conclusion to Suze's journey while leaving readers with a touch of nostalgia.

The main theme of "Twilight" is the sacrifice one makes for love and the acceptance of change. Cabot explores these themes with depth and sensitivity, making the story resonate with readers long after they've turned the final page. The supernatural elements serve as a backdrop for Suze's emotional growth, as she learns to let go and embrace the unpredictability of life.

In true Meg Cabot fashion, "Twilight" is a captivating blend of romance, humor, and the supernatural. Fans of the Mediator series will find this final installment to be a fitting and emotional farewell to Suze and her ghostly love story. Cabot's ability to create engaging characters and compelling narratives ensures that "Twilight" will leave a lasting impression on readers.

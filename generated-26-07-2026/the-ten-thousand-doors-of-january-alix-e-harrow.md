# The Ten Thousand Doors of January

**Author:** Alix E. Harrow  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

**Alix E. Harrow's "The Ten Thousand Doors of January" is a resplendent love letter to storytelling, weaving a tapestry of enchantment, self-discovery, and the power of words.**

In the early 1900s, January Scaller, a ward of the enigmatic Mr. Locke, stumbles upon a mysterious book that will forever change her life. This is not just any book; it's a portal to worlds unseen, a chronicle of secret doors, and a tale that mirrors January's own existence. Harrow's debut novel is a spellbinding exploration of the human spirit, the magic of stories, and the doors they open within us.

Harrow's prose is a symphony of lyricism and imagination, each sentence a delicate dance of words that paint vivid, otherworldly landscapes. She masterfully weaves together January's story with that of the book's protagonist, Jane, creating a nested narrative that keeps readers on the edge of their seats. The story within the story is not merely a plot device; it's a reflection of January's journey, a mirror held up to her own life, revealing truths she never knew existed.

At its heart, "The Ten Thousand Doors of January" is an ode to the transformative power of stories. Harrow argues that stories are not just entertainment; they are doorways to understanding, empathy, and self-discovery. Through Jane's adventures and January's personal growth, Harrow shows us that stories have the power to reshape our reality, to open doors we never knew were there.

The novel is also a profound exploration of love in its many forms - romantic, familial, platonic, and self-love. Harrow weaves these themes seamlessly into the narrative, creating a rich tapestry of human connection that feels both universal and deeply personal.

Harrow's style is reminiscent of the great storytellers of our time, yet distinctly her own. She wears her heart on her sleeve, her love for stories evident in every word. Yet, she doesn't shy away from the harsh realities of life, infusing her narrative with a sense of melancholy that makes the moments of joy all the more poignant.

"The Ten Thousand Doors of January" is a book that demands to be savored, its pages turned slowly, each word cherished. It's a book that will make you believe in magic, in the power of stories, and in the endless possibilities that lie beyond the ten thousand doors.

# Lumen (Captain Martin Bora, #1)

**Author:** Ben Pastor  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**Lumen: A Gripping Tale of Faith, Power, and Occupation**

Ben Pastor's debut novel, "Lumen," is a compelling blend of historical fiction, detective story, and psychological thriller, set against the grim backdrop of Nazi-occupied Poland. The narrative weaves together a murder mystery, political intrigue, and a profound exploration of faith and power, all while capturing the chilling realities of war.

At the heart of the story is Captain Martin Bora, a German army officer grappling with the moral complexities of his role in the occupation. When he discovers the body of Mother Kazimierza, a revered nun with alleged prophetic powers, in her convent garden, Bora is drawn into an investigation that will challenge his sense of duty and test his beliefs. Meanwhile, Father Malecki, a Chicago priest sent by the Vatican to investigate Mother Kazimierza's powers, finds himself entangled in the murder inquiry, his faith shaken by the horrors of war and the ideology of his colleagues.

Pastor's prose is sharp and evocative, painting a vivid picture of wartime Poland and the complex moral landscape of its inhabitants. She masterfully balances suspense and reflection, keeping readers on the edge of their seats while delving into the psyches of her characters. The plot is well-crafted, with a twist of historical realities that adds depth and authenticity to the story.

The narrative is further enriched by the presence of Ewa Kowalska, a seductive actress whose interference in the investigation adds a layer of complexity to the plot. Through her, Pastor explores the power dynamics between the occupiers and the occupied, and the ways in which individuals navigate these dynamics to survive.

"Lumen" is not just a murder mystery; it's a profound exploration of faith, power, and the human condition in the face of adversity. Pastor's ability to weave together these themes with a gripping plot and compelling characters makes "Lumen" a standout debut that will captivate readers until the very end.

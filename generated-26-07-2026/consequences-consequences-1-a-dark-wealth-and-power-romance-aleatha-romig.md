# Consequences (Consequences, #1) A Dark Wealth and Power Romance

**Author:** Aleatha Romig  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

**Title: Consequences: A Dark Wealth and Power Romance**

In the pulsating heart of Aleatha Romig's "Consequences," the first installment of her enthralling 'Consequences' series, we're drawn into a world where power, legacy, and emotional warfare reign supreme. This isn't your typical romance; it's a high-stakes game of thrones, set against the electrifying backdrop of a football dynasty.

Romig, a master of suspense and emotional tension, weaves a tale that's as captivating as it is complex. Our protagonist, a woman whose life and career are inextricably linked to the Hubbard football legacy, finds her world crumbling as the truth about her family's dynasty is revealed. The Coopers, it seems, are not just a football team; they're a powerhouse of secrets and manipulation, and our heroine is about to be pulled into their treacherous orbit.

The story is a second chance romance at its core, but it's far from a simple love story. The man from our heroine's past, her biggest regret and greatest weakness, re-enters her life, seeking a second chance. But in this world of loyalty, lies, and betrayal, trust is a risk, and desire a liability. Romig expertly explores the complexities of their relationship, the push and pull of their past, and the explosive chemistry that threatens to consume them both.

Romig's prose is as intense and fast-paced as the game it describes. She masterfully builds suspense, keeping readers on the edge of their seats with jaw-dropping twists and turns. Her characters are complex, flawed, and utterly compelling, each with their own agenda and secrets. The dynamics between them are as intricate and unpredictable as the game they play both on and off the field.

"Consequences" is a thrilling exploration of power, legacy, and the lengths we'll go to protect what's ours. It's a romance, yes, but it's also a gripping tale of betrayal, survival, and the price of ambition. Romig's signature style - dark, intense, and addictive - ensures that once you've started, you won't be able to put it down.

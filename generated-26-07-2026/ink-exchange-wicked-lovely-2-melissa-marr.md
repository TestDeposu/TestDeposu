# Ink Exchange (Wicked Lovely, #2)

**Author:** Melissa Marr  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

In "Ink Exchange", Melissa Marr masterfully weaves a captivating narrative that delves into the darker aspects of the faerie world, exploring the intricate dance of power and the intoxicating allure of the unknown. The second installment in the Wicked Lovely series, this book is a must-read for fans of dark fantasy romance.

At its core, "Ink Exchange" is a story about the transformative power of desire and the danger of making deals with forces beyond human control. Leslie, a 17-year-old girl, is drawn to a mysterious tattoo that seems to symbolize the changes she yearns for in her life. Unbeknownst to her, the tattoo is a conduit to the faerie world, binding her to Irial, the enigmatic king of the Dark Court. As Leslie becomes increasingly entangled in the world of faeries, she finds herself torn between her attraction to Irial and her growing connection to Niall, a faerie from the Summer Court.

Marr's writing style is as captivating as it is unsettling, perfectly capturing the dark, seductive atmosphere of the faerie world. Her characters are complex and multidimensional, with Leslie's vulnerability and determination making her a relatable and sympathetic protagonist. Irial, on the other hand, is a masterfully crafted anti-hero, exuding an aura of mystery and danger that is impossible to resist.

One of the standout features of "Ink Exchange" is its exploration of the faerie courts and the delicate balance of power that exists between them. Marr's world-building is meticulous and immersive, drawing readers into a world of ancient politics and brutal magic. The faeries in this series are not the benevolent, sparkly creatures of popular folklore; instead, they are complex, often cruel beings driven by their own agendas and desires.

The romance in "Ink Exchange" is equally compelling, with Leslie finding herself at the center of a forbidden love triangle. Marr handles the romance with sensitivity and nuance, never shying away from the complexities and risks of Leslie's relationships with Irial and Niall. The tension between Leslie and Irial is particularly palpable, their connection crackling with an otherworldly energy that is impossible to ignore.

Overall, "Ink Exchange" is a gripping and atmospheric read that will appeal to fans of dark fantasy and romance. Marr's writing is engaging and evocative, drawing readers into a world of shadows and danger that is impossible to resist. If you're looking for a series that will transport you to a world of faeries, magic, and forbidden love, the Wicked Lovely series is an absolute must-read.

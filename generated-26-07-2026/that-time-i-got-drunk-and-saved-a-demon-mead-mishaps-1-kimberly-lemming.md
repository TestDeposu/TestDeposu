# That Time I Got Drunk and Saved a Demon (Mead Mishaps, #1)

**Author:** Kimberly Lemming  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**That Time I Got Drunk and Saved a Demon: A Hilarious, Heartwarming Fantasy Romp**

In a world where spice traders and demons coexist, Kimberly Lemming's "That Time I Got Drunk and Saved a Demon" serves up a steaming helping of laughter, adventure, and romance that's as intoxicating as its title suggests. This debut novel in the Mead Mishaps series is a breath of fresh air, proving that fantasy romance can be both heartwarming and hilarious.

At the heart of the story is Cinnamon, or Cin, a spice trader with a dry wit and a penchant for avoiding adventure. Her quiet life takes a dramatic turn when she saves a demon named Fallon in a wine-fueled stupor, and he promptly drags her into a quest to free his enslaved people. Cin's deadpan reactions to their predicaments are a delight, providing a perfect foil to Fallon's fiery passion and single-minded determination.

Lemming's writing style is a standout feature of the novel. She masterfully balances humor and heart, creating a world that feels both familiar and fantastical. The dialogue is sharp and witty, with a rhythm that makes it a joy to read. The author also excels at world-building, weaving a rich tapestry of cultures, creatures, and magic that feels both intricate and effortless.

The romance between Cin and Fallon is another highlight. It develops naturally, fueled by their shared experiences and mutual respect. Their chemistry is palpable, and their banter is as steamy as it is funny. Lemming doesn't shy away from exploring the complexities of their relationship, making their love story feel genuine and relatable.

Despite its lighter tone, "That Time I Got Drunk and Saved a Demon" also tackles serious themes like slavery, power dynamics, and the consequences of one's actions. Lemming handles these topics with sensitivity and nuance, adding depth to the story without detracting from its humor.

In a genre often dominated by epic sagas and dark tales, Lemming's novel is a refreshing reminder that fantasy can be fun. It's a story that will make you laugh out loud, swoon, and cheer, all while reminding you of the power of friendship, love, and a good sense of humor in the face of adversity.

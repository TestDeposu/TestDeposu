# Search and Destroy (Tom Rollins Thriller #10)

**Author:** Paul Heatley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

Search and Destroy, the tenth installment in the Tom Rollins Thriller series by Paul Heatley, is a gripping and intense tale of family, loyalty, and the unrelenting pursuit of justice. The story follows Special Agent Garrett Kohl as he navigates a complex web of corruption and deceit, taking on a powerful energy consortium that threatens to destroy his family's land and way of life.

Heatley's writing style is akin to a runaway train, barreling forward with unrelenting intensity and pace. His prose is crisp and concise, with a keen focus on action and suspense. The author's background as a former CIA intelligence officer and consultant for the Department of Defense lends an air of authenticity to the narrative, imbuing the story with a sense of realism and grit.

At its core, Search and Destroy is a tale of good vs. evil, with Garrett Kohl embodying the values of justice and righteousness. The character is well-crafted, with a rich backstory and a deep sense of loyalty to his family and friends. His investigation into Talon Corporation's nefarious activities is driven by a personal stake, having been attacked by the company's guards and seeing the devastating impact on his loved ones.

The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with characters like CIA operative Kim Manning adding depth and complexity to the narrative. The villainous Alexi Orlov, a Russian spy with a penchant for violence, serves as a formidable foe, pushing Garrett to his limits and beyond.

One of the standout aspects of Search and Destroy is its exploration of the main theme: the struggle for power and control. Talon Corporation's ruthless tactics and blatant disregard for the law serve as a stark reminder of the dangers of unchecked corporate power. Heatley's portrayal of the company's origins in the dark hinterlands of countries across the globe adds a sense of scope and scale to the narrative, highlighting the far-reaching consequences of their actions.

The author's use of setting is also noteworthy, with the Texas High Plains serving as a character in its own right. The harsh, unforgiving landscape provides a fitting backdrop for the story, with its vast expanses and rugged terrain mirroring the unyielding determination of Garrett Kohl.

In conclusion, Search and Destroy is a pulse-pounding thriller that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. With its breakneck pace, well-crafted characters, and gripping narrative, this book is a must-read for fans of the genre. Paul Heatley's writing style is engaging and immersive, making it easy to become fully invested in the world he has created.

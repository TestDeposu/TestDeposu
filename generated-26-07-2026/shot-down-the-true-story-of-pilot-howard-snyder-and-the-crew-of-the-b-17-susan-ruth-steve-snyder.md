# Shot Down: The True Story of Pilot Howard Snyder and the Crew of the B-17 Susan Ruth

**Author:** Steve Snyder  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

Shot Down: The True Story of Pilot Howard Snyder and the Crew of the B-17 Susan Ruth, written by Steve Snyder, is a captivating and insightful account of one of the most pivotal moments in World War II. The book tells the story of Lieutenant Howard Snyder and his crew, who were part of the 306th Bomb Group in Thurleigh, England, and their harrowing experience of being shot down over Nazi-occupied Europe.

Through meticulous research, Steve Snyder weaves together a narrative that is both informative and engaging. The author's use of letters, journals, and diaries from the crew members, as well as interviews with historians, provides a unique and intimate perspective on the events that unfolded. The story is centered around the crew of the B-17 Susan Ruth, but it also sheds light on the broader context of the war, including the separation of young families, the training of soldiers, and the atrocities committed by the Germans.

One of the most striking aspects of the book is its ability to bring history to life. Steve Snyder's vivid descriptions of the crew's experiences, from the moment they were shot down to their subsequent evasion and capture, are both thrilling and heartbreaking. The author's writing style is engaging and accessible, making the book an enjoyable read for both history buffs and those who are new to the subject.

The book also highlights the bravery and selflessness of the Belgian civilians who risked their lives to save the American flyers. The stories of these unsung heroes are a testament to the power of human compassion and the enduring bonds of friendship and solidarity.

Throughout the book, Steve Snyder's love and admiration for his father, Lieutenant Howard Snyder, are evident. The author's dedication to telling his father's story is inspiring, and the book is a fitting tribute to the crew of the Susan Ruth and all those who served during World War II.

Shot Down is more than just a historical account; it is a powerful reminder of the importance of preserving the stories of the past. As the author notes, 70 years is a long time ago, and World War II is slowly fading from our collective memory. This book is a timely reminder of the sacrifices made by those who served, and it serves as a poignant tribute to their bravery and selflessness.

In conclusion, Shot Down: The True Story of Pilot Howard Snyder and the Crew of the B-17 Susan Ruth is a must-read for anyone interested in World War II history. Steve Snyder's engaging writing style, combined with the book's meticulous research and historical significance, make it a compelling and unforgettable read.

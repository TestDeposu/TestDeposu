# The Rising Tide (World War II: 1939-1945, #1)

**Author:** Jeff Shaara  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2006

The Rising Tide, the first installment in Jeff Shaara's World War II trilogy, is a masterful and meticulously researched novel that delves into the pivotal moments of the war. With his signature style of weaving together historical facts and fictional narrative, Shaara brings to life the heroic figures and unknown soldiers who fought in one of the most dramatic struggles of the 20th century.

The book tells the story of America's crucial role in World War II, from the Japanese conquest of the Pacific to the Allied forces' push into Hitler's Fortress Europa. Shaara's narrative is a tapestry of dust and fire, unfolding across the battlefields of North Africa and Sicily, where tank battles and paratrooper attacks changed the course of the war.

One of the standout features of The Rising Tide is Shaara's ability to balance the grand scale of war with the intimate, personal stories of those who fought it. Through the voices of both immortal and unknown figures, Shaara humanizes the conflict, making it relatable and accessible to readers who may not be familiar with the intricacies of military history.

Shaara's writing style is engaging and immersive, with meticulous attention to detail that transports readers to the battle-scarred landscapes of 1940s Sicily and North Africa. The Rising Tide is a sprawling tale that is thoroughly researched and told with meticulous detail, making it a must-read for history buffs and fans of military fiction alike.

The praise for The Rising Tide has been widespread, with reviewers noting its masterful storytelling, meticulous research, and immersive narrative. As MSNBC online noted, "All that's missing is the smell of gunpowder." Publishers Weekly praised the book as "masterful," while the New York Daily News noted that it "imparts the actual sights, sounds, and dialogue from the grounds of 1940s Sicily and North Africa."

In short, The Rising Tide is a gripping and intimate portrait of those who waged the global war that defined the 20th century. With its meticulous research, engaging narrative, and masterful storytelling, it is a must-read for anyone interested in World War II history or military fiction.

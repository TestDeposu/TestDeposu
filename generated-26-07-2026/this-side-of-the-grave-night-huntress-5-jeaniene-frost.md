# This Side of the Grave (Night Huntress, #5)

**Author:** Jeaniene Frost  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In the fifth installment of Jeaniene Frost's Night Huntress series, This Side of the Grave, the author masterfully weaves a tale of love, loyalty, and the eternal struggle between humans and immortals. With her signature blend of wit, action, and sizzling romance, Frost delivers a story that will captivate fans of paranormal fiction and leave them breathless.

At the heart of the novel is the incomparable team of Cat Crawfield, a half-vampire with a penchant for kicking butt, and her all-vamp husband Bones, a charismatic and powerful vampire with a heart of gold. Together, they face their most daunting challenge yet: preventing a species war that could decimate both human and immortal populations. As they navigate the treacherous landscape of supernatural politics, they are forced to seek help from their sworn enemy, the enigmatic Ghoul Queen of New Orleans.

Frost's writing style is a perfect blend of dark humor, clever banter, and pulse-pounding action. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Cat and Bones being one of the most beloved and iconic couples in paranormal fiction. Their chemistry is undeniable, and their romance is a slow-burning flame that simmers throughout the novel, adding an extra layer of tension to the already gripping plot.

One of Frost's greatest strengths is her ability to create a richly detailed world, full of complex characters and intricate mythology. The Night Huntress series is known for its unique take on vampire lore, and This Side of the Grave is no exception. Frost's vampires are multifaceted and nuanced, with their own culture, history, and motivations. The world-building is meticulous, with a deep understanding of the supernatural hierarchy and the delicate balance of power between humans and immortals.

Throughout the novel, Frost explores themes of loyalty, duty, and the true meaning of family. Cat and Bones are forced to confront their own demons, both literal and figurative, as they navigate the treacherous world of supernatural politics. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with memorable characters like Vlad, Mencheres, and Spade adding depth and complexity to the story.

In short, This Side of the Grave is a must-read for fans of paranormal fiction. Frost's writing is addictive, her characters are unforgettable, and the story is a wild ride from start to finish. If you're looking for a novel that will keep you on the edge of your seat, with a healthy dose of romance, action, and humor, then look no further. This Side of the Grave is a gripping and unputdownable read that will leave you breathless and eager for more.

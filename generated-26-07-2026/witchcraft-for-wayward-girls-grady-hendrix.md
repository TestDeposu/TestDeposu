# Witchcraft for Wayward Girls

**Author:** Grady Hendrix  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

**Witchcraft for Wayward Girls: A Spellbinding Tale of Power and Resilience**

Grady Hendrix, the master of modern horror, has once again cast a spell on readers with his latest offering, "Witchcraft for Wayward Girls." Set in the sultry summer of 1970, this novel is a chilling exploration of power, identity, and the lengths to which society goes to control young women.

At the heart of the story lies the Wellwood House, a grim institution where unwed mothers are sent to give birth in secret and forget their experiences. Here, Hendrix masterfully weaves a tale of fifteen-year-old Fern, who arrives pregnant, frightened, and alone. She meets a diverse cast of characters, each with her own story of resilience and rebellion. The girls are subjected to a rigid regime, their lives controlled by the stern Miss Wellwood and the adults who claim to know what's best for them.

But when Fern discovers an occult book about witchcraft, the girls find a source of power they never knew they had. Hendrix's prose is at its finest here, capturing the intoxicating allure of this newfound power and the dark consequences that follow. As the girls delve deeper into the craft, they begin to understand that power always comes at a price, and it's usually paid in blood.

Hendrix's signature style shines throughout the novel. His ability to blend historical context with supernatural horror is unparalleled, creating a richly textured narrative that feels both timely and timeless. He masterfully explores the darker aspects of human nature, challenging societal norms and expectations, all while delivering a gripping, page-turning story.

"Witchcraft for Wayward Girls" is more than just a horror novel; it's a powerful commentary on the ways society has historically controlled and silenced young women. Hendrix's exploration of witchcraft as a metaphor for female empowerment is both thought-provoking and chilling, making this a must-read for fans of horror and feminist literature alike.

In true Hendrix fashion, the novel is filled with unexpected twists and turns, culminating in a climax that is as satisfying as it is shocking. This is a book that will leave you breathless, thinking, and eager for more.

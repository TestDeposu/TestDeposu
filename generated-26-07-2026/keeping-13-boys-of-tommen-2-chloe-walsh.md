# Keeping 13 (Boys of Tommen, #2)

**Author:** Chloe Walsh  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

"Keeping 13" by Chloe Walsh is a heart-stopping continuation of the Boys of Tommen duology, delving deeper into the poignant love story of Johnny Kavanagh and Shannon Lynch. This new adult/YA crossover Irish romance masterfully explores the complexities of love, vulnerability, and the unbreakable bonds between two souls.

Walsh's writing style is nothing short of captivating, weaving a narrative that effortlessly switches between Johnny's and Shannon's dual POVs. This technique not only provides an intimate understanding of the characters' emotions but also creates an atmosphere of suspense, keeping readers invested in the story.

One of the most striking aspects of "Keeping 13" is its portrayal of vulnerability. Johnny, once a star athlete, is now grappling with the loss of his identity, while Shannon is fighting to overcome the trauma of her past. Their love becomes a beacon of hope, a reminder that even in the darkest moments, there is always a way forward.

Walsh tackles sensitive topics with care and nuance, never shying away from the harsh realities of Shannon's experiences. The author's handling of these themes is not only respectful but also thought-provoking, encouraging readers to reflect on the importance of trust, communication, and the power of human connection.

The world of the Boys of Tommen is richly detailed, with a strong sense of community and camaraderie among the characters. The sports romance element adds an extra layer of excitement, as Johnny navigates his passion for rugby and his love for Shannon.

Throughout the book, Walsh's passion for storytelling shines through, making "Keeping 13" a truly unforgettable read. The author's dedication to crafting a narrative that is both authentic and engaging is evident on every page, leaving readers eager to return to the world of Tommen and its beloved characters.

In short, "Keeping 13" is a must-read for fans of new adult/YA romance, sports romance, and anyone looking for a story that will capture their heart. With its poignant portrayal of love, vulnerability, and the human spirit, this book is sure to leave a lasting impression on readers.

**Author:** Mo Gawdat  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

**Preview/Critique: Scary Smart by Mo Gawdat**

In the rapidly advancing landscape of artificial intelligence, few voices resonate as profoundly as that of Mo Gawdat. A seasoned tech veteran with over three decades of experience, Gawdat doesn't just warn about the potential perils of AI in his latest book, "Scary Smart"; he issues a clarion call, urging us to confront the looming challenges and seize the opportunity to shape a future that's intelligent, responsible, and human-centric.

Gawdat's style is as engaging as it is informative, weaving together personal anecdotes, technical insights, and philosophical musings to create a narrative that's accessible to both tech enthusiasts and lay readers alike. He doesn't shy away from the scary aspects of AI, delving into the potential for job displacement, surveillance overreach, and even existential risks. Yet, "Scary Smart" isn't a doomsday prophecy; it's a rallying cry for action.

The author's career at Google and beyond has given him a front-row seat to the AI revolution. He uses this unique perspective to challenge us to think critically about the path we're on and the choices we're making. Gawdat argues that the future of AI isn't predetermined; it's ours to shape. He offers practical advice on how we can influence its development, from advocating for ethical guidelines to promoting AI literacy.

"Scary Smart" is a wake-up call, a call to arms, and a call to wisdom. It's a book that demands to be read, discussed, and acted upon. Gawdat doesn't just want us to be afraid of AI; he wants us to be smart about it. And in a world where the future is being written in code, that's the scariest—and smartest—thing we can be.

# Magic Burns (Kate Daniels, #2)

**Author:** Ilona Andrews  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

**Magic Burns: A Fiery Dance of Divinity and Danger**

In the pulsating heart of Ilona Andrews' urban fantasy series, "Magic Burns" ignites a blistering storm of magic, myth, and mayhem that will leave you breathless. The second installment in the Kate Daniels series is a thrilling ride through a world where the laws of nature are as fickle as the gods themselves.

At the core of "Magic Burns" lies the tantalizing theme of divine intervention and its consequences. Every seven years, Atlanta is engulfed by a magical flare, a time when the veil between worlds thins, and gods can manifest. This time, however, the flare is not just a passing phenomenon but a harbinger of a celestial battle that threatens to tear the city apart. Andrews masterfully weaves together ancient mythology and modern-day chaos, creating a rich tapestry of danger and intrigue.

The story unfolds through the eyes of Kate Daniels, a mercenary with a unique skill set and a mysterious past. Andrews' prose is crisp and engaging, painting vivid images of Atlanta's post-Shift landscape, where skyscrapers stand in ruins, and magic is as common as the air they breathe. Yet, it's Andrews' ability to craft compelling, complex characters that truly sets her apart. Kate is no ordinary heroine; she's a woman of strength and vulnerability, a fighter with a heart, and a past that haunts her every step. Her growth throughout the series is a testament to Andrews' skill as a storyteller.

"Magic Burns" is more than just a thrilling adventure; it's a exploration of power, faith, and the lengths we go to protect what we hold dear. As Kate navigates the treacherous waters of divine politics, she must confront her own beliefs and the true nature of the gods she serves. The result is a thought-provoking narrative that challenges our perceptions of divinity and the role of faith in our lives.

Andrews' style is a seamless blend of action, suspense, and humor, with a dash of romance that adds a touch of warmth to the icy chill of danger. She has a knack for creating tension that keeps you on the edge of your seat, even as she weaves in moments of levity that remind you to breathe. The pacing is flawless, building to a climax that is as explosive as it is satisfying.

In the end, "Magic Burns" is not just a book; it's an experience. It's a dance with the divine, a battle against the odds, and a journey of self-discovery. It's a testament to Ilona Andrews' skill as a storyteller and her ability to create a world that is as captivating as it is dangerous.

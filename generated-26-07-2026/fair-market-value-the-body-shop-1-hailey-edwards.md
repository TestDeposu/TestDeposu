# Fair Market Value (The Body Shop, #1)

**Author:** Hailey Edwards  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

**Fair Market Value: A Venomous Tale of Love and Betrayal**

Hailey Edwards' "Fair Market Value," the 3.5th installment in her Araneae Nation series, is a captivating tale of love, betrayal, and revenge, wrapped in a richly woven world of intrigue and danger. The story follows Nicolette, a woman scorned and exiled, who returns to her clan home with a deadly mission and a heart full of bitterness. Her path crosses with Armand, the heir to the wealthiest clan, who is bound by tradition and haunted by his past.

Edwards masterfully crafts a world where clans of spider-like beings, the Araneae, live among humans, hiding their true nature. The intricate web of their society, with its complex politics and traditions, serves as a fascinating backdrop to the human drama unfolding within it. The author's style is engaging, with a unique blend of fantasy, romance, and suspense that keeps the pages turning.

Nicolette is a compelling heroine, her grief and anger palpable, her determination to exact revenge unwavering. Yet, beneath her hardened exterior, there's a vulnerability that makes her relatable. Armand, too, is a complex character, bound by duty and regret, his love for Nicolette a constant, painful reminder of his past mistake.

The chemistry between Nicolette and Armand is undeniable, their love story a slow burn that threatens to consume them both. Their dance of attraction and mistrust is a delight to watch, as is their struggle to reconcile their past with their present. However, it's the author's ability to weave tension and suspense into every scene that truly sets this book apart. From the moment Nicolette sets foot in Erania, the reader is on the edge of their seat, wondering who will live, who will die, and who will be left standing when the dust settles.

"Fair Market Value" is not just a romance; it's a story about the power of love and the destructive force of betrayal. It's about the choices we make and the consequences we face. It's about finding the strength to forgive and the courage to love again. Hailey Edwards has crafted a compelling tale that will leave readers eager for more.

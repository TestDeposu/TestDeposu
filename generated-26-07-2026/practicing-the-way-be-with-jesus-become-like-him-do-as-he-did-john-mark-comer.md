# Practicing the Way: Be with Jesus, Become Like Him, Do As He Did

**Author:** John Mark Comer  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

In "Practicing the Way: Be with Jesus, Become Like Him, Do As He Did", John Mark Comer masterfully crafts a compelling case for rediscovering the ancient path to a deeper life with God. This New York Times bestseller, and winner of the ECPA's Christian Book of the Year Award, is a clarion call to reexamine our lives and reorient our priorities towards a more profound and meaningful relationship with Jesus.

Comer's writing style is characterized by its trademark mix of theological substance, cultural insight, and practical wisdom. He effortlessly weaves together these elements to create a narrative that is both accessible and engaging. The author's voice is conversational, making the reader feel like they are embarking on a journey of spiritual discovery alongside him.

At the heart of the book lies the concept of a "Rule of Life" – a set of practices and relational rhythms that slow us down and open up space in our daily lives for God to work. Comer argues that in a world that is constantly shaping us, we need to become apprentices of Jesus, allowing ourselves to be formed by Him. This, he posits, is the key to transformation, enabling us to become like Him and do as He did.

One of the most striking aspects of the book is its emphasis on the importance of slowing down. In a world that values speed and productivity, Comer's message is a refreshing counterpoint. He shows how ancient practices, such as prayer, meditation, and community, can help us cultivate a deeper sense of awareness and connection with God.

Throughout the book, Comer draws on a wide range of sources, from the early Christian church to modern-day culture. He is not afraid to engage with difficult questions and challenges, and his responses are always thoughtful and nuanced.

What sets "Practicing the Way" apart from other books on spiritual formation is its focus on the practical. Comer provides readers with a wealth of advice on how to develop their own Rule of Life, from rearranging their daily routines to cultivating a deeper sense of community. He shows how these practices can be adapted to suit different personalities, lifestyles, and circumstances.

Ultimately, "Practicing the Way" is a book that will challenge and inspire readers to rethink their relationship with God. It is a call to embark on a journey of spiritual discovery, one that is marked by a deepening sense of intimacy and connection with Jesus. As Jennie Allen, author of "Get Out of Your Head" and "Find Your People", so aptly puts it, "One of the most important books I have read in a decade... If we would all follow in this way, our lives would change and the world would change."

# One of Us Is Next (One of Us Is Lying, #2)

**Author:** Karen M. McManus  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

**One of Us Is Next: A Masterclass in Teen Thriller Suspense**

Karen M. McManus's **One of Us Is Next** is a gripping sequel to her smash hit, **One of Us Is Lying**, and it's every bit as addictive and twisty as its predecessor. McManus masterfully weaves a tale that keeps readers guessing until the very end, proving once again that she's a queen of the teen thriller genre.

The story begins with a chilling setup: five students in detention, one dead body, and four potential suspects. The Bayview Four—Bronwyn, Addy, Nate, and Cooper—are all hiding secrets, and Simon, the outcast turned gossip king, was about to expose them all. But who silenced Simon, and why?

McManus's strength lies in her ability to create complex, relatable characters, each with their own unique voice and motivations. She expertly explores the dynamics of high school cliques, the pressure to fit in, and the devastating consequences of secrets. The Bayview Four are no longer the golden children they once were; they're now entangled in a web of lies and deceit, fighting to clear their names and protect their futures.

The novel's pacing is relentless, with each chapter ending on a cliffhanger that demands to be read. McManus skillfully juggles multiple perspectives, slowly revealing the truth about Simon's death through a series of flashbacks and present-day events. The result is a narrative that's as compelling as it is confusing, keeping readers on the edge of their seats until the final, shocking reveal.

McManus's writing style is crisp and engaging, with a keen eye for detail and a knack for capturing the essence of teenage life. She doesn't shy away from exploring heavy topics like mental health, substance abuse, and the toxic nature of gossip, but she does so with a sensitivity and authenticity that rings true.

**One of Us Is Next** is more than just a murder mystery; it's a cautionary tale about the dangers of secrets and the power of truth. McManus reminds us that everyone has something to hide, and sometimes, the truth can set you free—or bury you alive.

In a nutshell, if you're a fan of **Pretty Little Liars**, **The Breakfast Club**, or any story that combines suspense, drama, and a healthy dose of teen angst, **One of Us Is Next** is a must-read. It's a thrilling ride that will keep you guessing until the very end, and long after you've turned the final page.

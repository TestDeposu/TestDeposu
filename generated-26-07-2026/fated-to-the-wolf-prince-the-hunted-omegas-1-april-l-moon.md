# Fated to the Wolf Prince (The Hunted Omegas, #1)

**Author:** April L. Moon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

**Fated to the Wolf Prince: A Gritty, Enthralling Omegaverse Debut**

April L. Moon's "Fated to the Wolf Prince" is a captivating addition to the paranormal romance genre, offering readers a unique blend of dark mafia intrigue, Viking-inspired lore, and a compelling Omegaverse dynamic. This standalone novel, the first in the Hunted Omegas series, is a thrilling ride that keeps you on the edge of your seat, breathless and eager for more.

At the heart of the story is a fierce, unyielding Alpha, Gunnar Rask, the North Wolf. Moon masterfully crafts a protagonist who is as ruthless as he is alluring, a paranormal mafia don with a brutal past and an unyielding present. His world is one of shadows and power, a city ruled with an iron fist, and yet, there's a depth to him that makes him more than just a cardboard cutout of a villain. Moon peels back the layers of his character with skill, revealing a complex, flawed Alpha who is as fascinating as he is formidable.

The story's true gem, however, is the Omega, our unnamed heroine. Moon has created a protagonist who is anything but a damsel in distress. She's intelligent, resourceful, and fiercely independent, making her a refreshing addition to the Omegaverse trope. Her journey from captive to something more is a testament to Moon's ability to write strong, relatable female leads.

The romance between these two is as intense as it is complicated. Moon doesn't shy away from the darker aspects of the Omegaverse dynamic, exploring themes of control, possession, and the blurred lines between love and obsession. The forced proximity trope is used to great effect, allowing the tension between Gunnar and his captive to build and simmer until it reaches a boiling point.

Moon's prose is gritty and visceral, painting a vivid picture of a world that is as dangerous as it is beautiful. Her use of Norse mythology and Viking culture adds a unique flavor to the story, setting it apart from other Omegaverse romances. The city of Frosthaven is as much a character in the story as Gunnar and his Omega, its cold, unforgiving streets echoing the harsh reality of their lives.

The pacing of the novel is excellent, balancing action, romance, and world-building with ease. Moon keeps the reader guessing, weaving a complex plot filled with twists and turns that will leave you guessing until the very end. The conclusion, while satisfying, also leaves room for the next installment in the series, hinting at larger conflicts yet to come.

In conclusion, "Fated to the Wolf Prince" is a standout addition to the paranormal romance genre. April L. Moon's unique blend of dark mafia intrigue, Viking-inspired lore, and a compelling Omegaverse dynamic makes for a gripping, unforgettable read. This is a book that will appeal to fans of dark romance, paranormal fiction, and anyone looking for a story that will keep them up at night, turning pages long after they should have turned off the light.

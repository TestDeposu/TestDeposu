# Finale (Hush, Hush, #4)

**Author:** Becca Fitzpatrick  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

"Finale" by Becca Fitzpatrick, the fourth and final installment in the "Hush, Hush" series, is a thrilling conclusion that masterfully weaves together themes of love, betrayal, and the eternal struggle between good and evil. Fitzpatrick's signature style, characterized by its engaging narrative and intricate plot, reaches a crescendo in this finale, leaving readers on the edge of their seats until the very last page.

The story picks up where "Silence" left off, with Nora Grey grappling with the revelation that Patch, her fallen angel love interest, has been keeping secrets that could shatter their relationship. As Nora delves deeper into the ancient battle between the immortal and the fallen, she finds herself entangled in a web of deceit and danger, forcing her to question who she can trust.

Fitzpatrick excels in creating complex, relatable characters that drive the plot forward. Nora, the protagonist, is a refreshing heroine who is both strong-willed and vulnerable, navigating her tumultuous relationship with Patch with a mix of courage and caution. Patch, the enigmatic fallen angel, remains an intriguing character, his motives often shrouded in mystery, keeping readers guessing until the very end.

The author's style is marked by its fast-paced narrative and clever use of suspense, which she employs to great effect in "Finale". The story is filled with unexpected twists and turns, each revelation more shocking than the last, making it impossible to put the book down. Fitzpatrick also excels in creating a rich, atmospheric world, from the quaint town of Coldwater to the ethereal realm of the Nephilim, each setting vividly brought to life through her evocative prose.

However, the book is not without its flaws. Some plot points feel rushed, and certain characters' motivations remain unclear, leaving readers with more questions than answers. Moreover, the love story between Nora and Patch, while central to the plot, sometimes feels overshadowed by the action and suspense, leaving readers craving more emotional depth.

Despite these minor shortcomings, "Finale" is a satisfying conclusion to the "Hush, Hush" series. It is a testament to Fitzpatrick's skill as a storyteller, her ability to create compelling characters and intricate plots that keep readers engaged until the very end. Fans of the series will not be disappointed, as "Finale" delivers a thrilling, action-packed conclusion that ties up most loose ends, leaving readers with a sense of closure and satisfaction.

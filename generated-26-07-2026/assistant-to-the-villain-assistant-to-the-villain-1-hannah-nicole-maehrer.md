# Assistant to the Villain (Assistant to the Villain, #1)

**Author:** Hannah Nicole Maehrer  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

Assistant to the Villain, the debut novel by Hannah Nicole Maehrer, is a sidesplitting and captivating tale that seamlessly blends the whimsy of Once Upon a Time with the witty humor of The Office. This viral TikTok series turned novel has catapulted to the top of the New York Times bestseller list, and for good reason.

At the heart of the story lies Evie Sage, a determined and levelheaded individual desperate to secure a job to support her ailing family. Her search leads her to the unlikeliest of places: the lair of Rennedawn's most notorious villain. As the newly appointed assistant to the evil mastermind, Evie finds herself entangled in a web of chaos, mayhem, and terror, all while struggling to maintain her composure and resist the charms of her temperamental yet undeniably hot boss.

Maehrer's writing style is a true masterpiece, effortlessly balancing humor and wit with a touch of darkness and intrigue. Her world-building is meticulous, crafting a rich and immersive environment that draws readers into the twisted realm of Rennedawn. The characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Evie's narrative voice shining as a beacon of sarcasm and vulnerability.

As Evie navigates the treacherous landscape of her new job, she begins to unravel a sinister plot threatening the very foundations of the villain's empire. With her loyalty and wits put to the test, Evie must confront the darkness within herself and decide where her true allegiance lies.

One of the most striking aspects of Assistant to the Villain is its exploration of the gray areas between good and evil. Maehrer's villain is a complex, multifaceted character, driven by motivations that blur the lines between malevolence and heroism. This moral ambiguity adds depth to the narrative, making it impossible to categorize the story as simply a tale of good vs. evil.

Throughout the novel, Maehrer's humor shines, often manifesting in unexpected and hilarious ways. From the severed heads suspended from the ceiling to the squish of an errant eyeball beneath Evie's heel, the author's comedic timing is impeccable. Yet, beneath the humor lies a poignant exploration of the human condition, delving into themes of loyalty, power, and the true cost of ambition.

In conclusion, Assistant to the Villain is a captivating and sidesplitting read that will appeal to fans of fantasy, romance, and humor. With its intricate world-building, complex characters, and wicked wit, this novel is an absolute must-read. Hannah Nicole Maehrer's writing style is a breath of fresh air, and her unique voice is sure to captivate readers and leave them eagerly awaiting the next installment in the series.

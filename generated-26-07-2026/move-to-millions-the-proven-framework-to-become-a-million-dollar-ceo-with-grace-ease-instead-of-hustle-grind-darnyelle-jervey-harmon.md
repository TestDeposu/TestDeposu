# Move to Millions: The Proven Framework to Become a Million Dollar CEO with Grace & Ease Instead of Hustle & Grind

**Author:** Darnyelle Jervey Harmon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

Move to Millions: The Proven Framework to Become a Million Dollar CEO with Grace & Ease Instead of Hustle & Grind is a game-changing book that promises to revolutionize the way entrepreneurs approach their businesses. Written by the award-winning Inc. 5000 CEO, Dr. Darnyelle Jervey Harmon, this book is a culmination of her expertise and experience in helping hundreds of clients achieve seven-figure success.

The book's central theme is centered around the idea of breaking through the six-figure plateau and achieving million-dollar success with ease and grace, rather than relying on hustle and grind. Dr. Harmon's paradigm-shifting approach is rooted in her own experiences and the successes of her clients, making the book a unique blend of memoir and methodology.

What sets Move to Millions apart from other business books is its focus on simplicity and practicality. Dr. Harmon breaks down complex topics into actionable strategies, using raw personal anecdotes to illustrate their worth. This approach makes the book an enjoyable and entertaining read, while also providing valuable insights and techniques that business owners can apply to their own businesses.

One of the most striking aspects of the book is Dr. Harmon's emphasis on values-driven entrepreneurship. She empowers business owners to leave behind the headaches and stress that often come with building a successful business, and instead focus on what truly matters. By simplifying processes and multiplying profits, entrepreneurs can enjoy the freedom and fulfillment that comes with achieving million-dollar success, without compromising on their values.

Dr. Harmon's writing style is engaging, relatable, and infused with a sense of humor. She puts in the work so that business owners can be empowered, entertained, and equipped to achieve their goals. The book is a testament to her dedication to helping entrepreneurs succeed, and her passion for sharing her expertise with others.

Overall, Move to Millions is a must-read for any business owner looking to break through the six-figure plateau and achieve million-dollar success. With its unique blend of memoir and methodology, practical strategies, and values-driven approach, this book is a game-changer for entrepreneurs who want to achieve success with ease and grace.

Whether you're a seasoned entrepreneur or just starting out, Move to Millions offers a proven framework for achieving success and living a fulfilling life. So, if you're ready to take your business to the next level and join the ranks of million-dollar CEOs, then this book is a must-read.

# Catch the Sun

**Author:** Jennifer Hartmann  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

"Catch the Sun" by Jennifer Hartmann is a poignant and captivating exploration of love, loss, and redemption, wrapped in a compelling coming-of-age narrative. Hartmann, known for her heartrending tales in "Still Beating" and "Lotus," once again proves her prowess in weaving emotional depth and raw honesty into her prose.

The story unfolds in the small town of Sunbury, where Ella Sunbury returns after a decade-long absence. Her homecoming is anything but joyous, as she's now the sister of a notorious felon on death row, a stigma that casts a long shadow over her life. Ella's struggle to find her footing amidst the judgment and whispers is palpable, a testament to Hartmann's ability to craft relatable, flawed characters.

Across the street lives Max Manning, Ella's childhood best friend, who harbors feelings for her that run deeper than friendship. Max, burdened with familial responsibilities, is a beacon of strength and understanding, providing a safe haven for Ella to heal and rediscover her true self. Their relationship, a slow burn that evolves from friendship to love, is beautifully rendered, filled with tender moments that feel authentic and earned.

Hartmann's signature style shines in her exploration of the main theme: the struggle to find light in the darkest of times. Ella's journey is a testament to resilience, as she grapples with her brother's actions and the weight of her family's past. Max, too, faces his own challenges, yet he remains steadfast in his support of Ella, their bond a beacon of hope amidst the storm.

The author masterfully navigates the complexities of grief, guilt, and redemption, weaving a tale that is as heart-wrenching as it is hopeful. Hartmann's prose is evocative, painting vivid images with her words, drawing readers into the story and making it impossible to look away.

"Catch the Sun" is more than just a romance; it's a story about finding one's footing in the face of adversity, about the power of love and friendship to heal even the deepest wounds. It's a story that will resonate with readers long after the final page, a reminder that even in the darkest of nights, the sun will always rise.

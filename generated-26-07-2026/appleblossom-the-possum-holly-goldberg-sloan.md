# Appleblossom the Possum

**Author:** Holly Goldberg Sloan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

"Appleblossom the Possum" by Holly Goldberg Sloan is a delightful, heartwarming tale that captures the essence of childhood curiosity and the joy of discovery. Sloan, known for her award-winning novel "Counting by 7s," weaves a charming narrative that is as funny as it is touching, making it a perfect read-aloud for families.

The story follows Appleblossom, a young possum, as she navigates the world according to her mother's rules: avoid humans, dogs, and cars, and never be seen during the day. Yet, Appleblossom's insatiable curiosity leads her to spy on a human family, resulting in an unexpected tumble down their chimney. This incident sparks a chain of events that not only changes Appleblossom's perspective but also brings together an unlikely trio of friends.

Sloan's storytelling is reminiscent of E.B. White's classic animal tales, with a unique twist that is distinctly her own. She masterfully portrays the possum family's dynamics, creating a tight-knit bond that feels authentic and endearing. The human family, too, is drawn with warmth and humor, making them relatable and lovable.

The narrative is peppered with hilarious moments, such as the possums' rescue mission, which is as chaotic as it is heartwarming. Sloan's ability to balance humor with emotion is what makes her stories stand out. She doesn't shy away from the realities of the wild, but she presents them in a way that is age-appropriate and thought-provoking.

The illustrations, dynamic and expressive, add another layer of charm to the story. They bring the possum family to life, capturing their personalities and emotions in a way that words alone cannot. The illustrations also provide a glimpse into the possums' world, highlighting the beauty and danger of their environment.

"Appleblossom the Possum" is more than just a story about a curious possum. It's a tale about the joy of discovery, the importance of family, and the power of friendship. It's a story that will resonate with children and adults alike, reminding us all of the wonder that can be found in the most unexpected places.

In a world filled with noise and chaos, "Appleblossom the Possum" is a gentle reminder to slow down, look around, and appreciate the beauty of the world from a different perspective. It's a story that deserves a place on every bookshelf, a classic in the making.

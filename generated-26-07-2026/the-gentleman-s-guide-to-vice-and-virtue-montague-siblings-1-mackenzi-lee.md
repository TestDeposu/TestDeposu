# The Gentleman's Guide to Vice and Virtue (Montague Siblings, #1)

**Author:** Mackenzi Lee  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

**A Dazzling, Witty Ride Through History and Heart**

Mackenzi Lee's "The Gentleman's Guide to Vice and Virtue" is a breath of fresh air, a gleeful romp through history that masterfully blends wit, adventure, and a heartwarming exploration of identity and love. This Kirkus Prize nominee and Stonewall Honor winner is a must-read for fans of historical fiction, LGBTQ+ literature, and anyone seeking a captivating, modern tale set in the 18th century.

At the heart of this novel lies Henry "Monty" Montague, a charming, bisexual British lord who is more interested in pleasure and vice than the gentlemanly pursuits expected of him. As he embarks on his grand tour of Europe with his best friend and secret crush, Percy, Monty is determined to make this trip one last hurrah before settling down to manage his family's estate. Lee's portrayal of Monty is a delight, balancing his roguish charm with a deep vulnerability that makes him instantly relatable.

Lee's prose is a joy to read, filled with clever wordplay and historical detail that brings the Grand Tour to life. From the glittering halls of Paris to the perilous roads of the countryside, every setting is vividly drawn, providing a rich backdrop for Monty's adventures. Yet, it's Lee's ability to weave serious themes into this comedic narrative that truly sets "The Gentleman's Guide" apart. Monty's journey of self-discovery, as he grapples with his identity and his feelings for Percy, is a poignant reminder of the universal struggles of the human heart.

This is not just a story about a grand tour gone awry; it's a tale of friendship and love, of finding one's place in the world, and of the courage it takes to be true to oneself. Lee's exploration of these themes is nuanced and thoughtful, making "The Gentleman's Guide" a story that will resonate with readers long after the final page.

In a genre often dominated by serious, somber tales, "The Gentleman's Guide to Vice and Virtue" stands out as a sparkling exception. It's a testament to Lee's skill as a writer that she can create a story that is both hilarious and heartfelt, an adventure that is equal parts thrilling and thought-provoking. This is not just a book to be read; it's an experience to be savored.

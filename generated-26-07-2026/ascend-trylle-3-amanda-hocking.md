# Ascend (Trylle, #3)

**Author:** Amanda Hocking  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**Title: A Captivating Climax: Amanda Hocking's "Ascend"**

In the realm of young adult fantasy, few authors have captured the imagination of readers as vividly as Amanda Hocking. Her Trylle trilogy concludes with "Ascend," a thrilling and emotionally charged finale that masterfully weaves together themes of sacrifice, love, and destiny.

At the heart of "Ascend" lies Wendy Everly, a protagonist who has grown significantly since the series' inception. Wendy's journey from a confused, adopted human to a powerful Trylle princess has been one of self-discovery and resilience. In this final installment, she faces her most daunting challenge yet: saving her people from annihilation by surrendering to their most formidable enemy, the Vittra. Hocking expertly explores the depth of Wendy's love for her kingdom and her people, making her sacrifice feel earned and profound.

The author's signature style shines in "Ascend." Hocking's ability to balance intense action sequences with poignant emotional moments is on full display. She deftly handles the complex dynamics of Wendy's relationships, particularly her love triangle with Finn and Loki. The tension between these three characters is palpable, and Hocking's exploration of Wendy's feelings is both relatable and heart-wrenching.

Hocking also excels in world-building. The Trylle and Vittra worlds are intricately detailed, with their own unique cultures, politics, and histories. This depth makes the stakes feel real and the conflicts resonant. Moreover, Hocking's use of mythology adds an enchanting layer to her storytelling, grounding her fantastical elements in something familiar yet magical.

However, "Ascend" is not without its flaws. Some plot points feel rushed, and certain character arcs could have benefited from more development. Yet, these minor issues do little to detract from the overall enjoyment of the book.

In conclusion, "Ascend" is a satisfying and emotional conclusion to the Trylle trilogy. Amanda Hocking's signature style, compelling characters, and intricate world-building create a captivating climax that will leave readers both fulfilled and nostalgic.

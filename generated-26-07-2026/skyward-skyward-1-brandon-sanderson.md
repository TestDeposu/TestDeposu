# Skyward (Skyward, #1)

**Author:** Brandon Sanderson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

In the realm of young adult science fiction, few authors have made as profound an impact as Brandon Sanderson. With the release of Skyward, the first installment in his latest series, Sanderson once again proves why he's a master of the genre. This gripping novel is a testament to his unique ability to craft compelling characters, intricate world-building, and pulse-pounding action sequences that will leave readers breathless.

At its core, Skyward is a story about the power of determination and the unyielding pursuit of one's dreams. The protagonist, Spensa, is a complex and deeply relatable character whose fate is inextricably linked to her father's troubled past. As she navigates the challenges of flight school and confronts the harsh realities of a world at war, Spensa must also contend with the weight of her family's legacy and the expectations that come with it.

One of the most striking aspects of Skyward is its thought-provoking exploration of the tension between individuality and societal norms. Sanderson skillfully weaves together themes of identity, community, and the true cost of heroism, raising important questions about the nature of sacrifice and the consequences of our actions. Through Spensa's journey, we see the ways in which our choices can both define and constrain us, and the difficult trade-offs that must be made in the pursuit of greatness.

Sanderson's writing style is, as always, a major highlight of the book. His prose is effortlessly engaging, with a keen sense of pacing that propels the reader through the story at breakneck speed. The action sequences are, in particular, a masterclass in tension and suspense, with Sanderson's vivid descriptions and clever plotting combining to create truly heart-stopping moments.

The world-building in Skyward is equally impressive, with a richly detailed and immersive environment that draws heavily from Sanderson's trademark blend of science fiction and fantasy elements. From the crumbling ruins of ancient civilizations to the sleek, high-tech aircraft that dominate the skies, every aspect of this world feels meticulously crafted and authentic.

If there's one criticism to be made, it's that the supporting cast of characters feels slightly underdeveloped at times. While Spensa is, understandably, the focus of the story, some of the secondary characters feel a bit one-dimensional, with their motivations and backstories not fully explored. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise outstanding novel.

In short, Skyward is a thrilling and thought-provoking read that cements Brandon Sanderson's status as one of the leading voices in young adult science fiction. With its compelling characters, gripping action sequences, and richly detailed world-building, this book is an absolute must-read for fans of the genre. And, as the first installment in a planned series, it sets the stage for what promises to be an epic and unforgettable journey.

# The Museum Detective (A Museum Detective Novel)

**Author:** Maha Khan Phillips  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

The Museum Detective, the latest novel by Maha Khan Phillips, is a gripping and intense thriller that masterfully weaves together the worlds of archaeology, crime, and personal tragedy. Set against the vibrant backdrop of Karachi, Pakistan, this debut series introduces us to the whip-smart and fiercely determined Dr. Gul Delani, a Pakistani archaeologist and Egyptologist whose life is forever changed when her niece, Mahnaz, goes missing.

Three years have passed since Mahnaz's disappearance, and the investigation has been called off, leaving Gul with a sense of desperation and frustration. Her work as a museum curator and archaeologist becomes her only solace, but even the ancient past cannot distract her from the haunting memories of her niece. It is this personal tragedy that drives Gul's investigation into the discovery of a mysterious mummy, found in a remote desert region, and decorated with symbols from the ancient city of Persepolis.

As Gul delves deeper into the mystery of the mummy, she finds herself entangled in a complex web of black-market scandals and high-level conspiracies. With her sharp mind and quick wit, she navigates the treacherous underworld of antiquities smuggling, where nothing is as it seems, and everyone has a secret to hide. Through her journey, Gul's search for the truth about the mummy becomes inextricably linked to her quest for answers about Mahnaz's disappearance.

Phillips' writing is masterful, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the cultural nuances of Karachi. Her prose is evocative and immersive, transporting the reader to the dusty streets and bustling markets of Pakistan's largest city. The characters are richly drawn and complex, with Gul being a particularly compelling protagonist. Her fierce determination and unwavering commitment to uncovering the truth make her a heroine worth rooting for.

One of the most striking aspects of The Museum Detective is its ability to balance the personal and the professional. Gul's investigation into the mummy is expertly woven together with her search for Mahnaz, creating a narrative that is both gripping and emotionally resonant. Phillips' use of language is precise and economical, with a keen sense of pacing that propels the reader forward.

The Museum Detective is a gripping crime thriller that will appeal to fans of Sue Grafton and Elsa Hart. With its unique blend of archaeology, mystery, and personal tragedy, this debut novel is sure to captivate readers and leave them eagerly anticipating the next installment in the series. Phillips' writing is a breath of fresh air, bringing a new perspective to the world of crime fiction. If you're looking for a compelling and thought-provoking read, look no further than The Museum Detective.

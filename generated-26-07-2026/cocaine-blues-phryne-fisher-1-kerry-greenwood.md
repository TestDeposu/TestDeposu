# Cocaine Blues (Phryne Fisher, #1)

**Author:** Kerry Greenwood  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In the vibrant and seductive world of 1920s Melbourne, Kerry Greenwood's Cocaine Blues is a sizzling debut that sets the tone for the beloved Phryne Fisher Mysteries series. This captivating novel whisks readers away to an era of jazz, flappers, and speakeasies, where the Honourable Phryne Fisher, a fearless and alluring detective, takes center stage.

Greenwood's writing style is as bold and vibrant as the protagonist herself. With a keen eye for detail, the author masterfully recreates the opulence and excess of the Roaring Twenties, transporting readers to a bygone era of glamour and intrigue. The narrative is woven with a complex web of secrets, scandals, and corruption, keeping the reader on the edge of their seat as Phryne navigates the treacherous underbelly of Melbourne's high society.

At the heart of the novel lies Phryne, a true original – a fiercely independent, intelligent, and liberated woman who defies the conventions of her time. Her insatiable thirst for adventure, coupled with her sharp wit and razor-sharp instincts, makes her an unstoppable force in the pursuit of justice. As she delves into the dark world of cocaine smuggling, police corruption, and murder, Phryne's daring investigation is matched only by her unapologetic sensuality and willingness to take risks.

Greenwood's portrayal of Phryne is a masterclass in characterization. The author skillfully balances Phryne's toughness and vulnerability, creating a protagonist who is both relatable and aspirational. Her interactions with the alluring Russian dancer, Sasha de Lisse, add a delicious layer of tension and eroticism to the narrative, further solidifying Phryne's status as a true icon of feminist literature.

Throughout the novel, Greenwood tackles themes of female empowerment, social inequality, and the struggle for justice in a society riddled with corruption and hypocrisy. Phryne's unwavering commitment to uncovering the truth, no matter the cost, serves as a powerful testament to the enduring spirit of women who refuse to be silenced or marginalized.

Cocaine Blues is a triumph of storytelling, with a narrative that is both propulsive and engaging. Greenwood's prose is evocative, witty, and engaging, making this novel a true page-turner. As the first installment in the Phryne Fisher Mysteries series, Cocaine Blues sets the stage for a thrilling and unforgettable ride, one that will leave readers eagerly anticipating the next adventure of this remarkable and captivating heroine.

In short, Cocaine Blues is a must-read for fans of historical fiction, mystery, and adventure. With its unique blend of style, substance, and sass, this novel is sure to captivate readers and leave a lasting impression. So, step into the dazzling world of 1920s Melbourne, and join Phryne Fisher on a journey that will leave you breathless and begging for more.

# A Witch's Guide to Fake Dating a Demon (Glimmer Falls, #1)

**Author:** Sarah Hawley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

In the enchanting world of paranormal romance, Sarah Hawley's "A Witch's Guide to Fake Dating a Demon" is a spellbinding debut that will captivate readers with its unique blend of magic, humor, and heart. This captivating tale follows Mariel Spark, a talented yet unconventional witch, and Ozroth, a ruthless demon, as they navigate a complicated web of fake relationships, soul bargains, and forbidden attraction.

Hawley's writing style is a breath of fresh air, infusing the story with a lighthearted and playful tone that perfectly complements the narrative's darker elements. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Mariel's quirky personality and Ozroth's dry wit making them an endearing and unlikely pair. As they engage in a series of hilarious misadventures, their banter and chemistry are undeniable, leaving readers rooting for their unlikely romance.

One of the novel's greatest strengths lies in its exploration of the main theme: the power of emotions and connections in a world where magic and supernatural forces often take center stage. Mariel's reluctance to give up her soul, and subsequently her emotions, serves as a poignant reminder of the importance of human feelings and relationships. This theme is expertly woven throughout the narrative, adding depth and complexity to the story.

The world-building in "A Witch's Guide to Fake Dating a Demon" is equally impressive, with Hawley creating a rich and immersive environment that draws inspiration from various mythologies and folklore. The magical system is well-developed and easy to follow, making it simple for readers to become fully invested in the world of Glimmer Falls.

If you're a fan of paranormal romance, witty banter, and strong female protagonists, then "A Witch's Guide to Fake Dating a Demon" is an absolute must-read. With its unique premise, engaging characters, and expertly crafted world-building, this novel is sure to cast a spell on readers of all ages.

In conclusion, Sarah Hawley's "A Witch's Guide to Fake Dating a Demon" is a captivating and romantic tale that will leave readers under its spell. With its perfect blend of magic, humor, and heart, this novel is an excellent addition to the paranormal romance genre.

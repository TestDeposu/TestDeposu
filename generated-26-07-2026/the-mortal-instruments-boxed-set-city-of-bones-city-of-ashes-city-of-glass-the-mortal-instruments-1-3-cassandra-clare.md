# The Mortal Instruments Boxed Set: City of Bones; City of Ashes; City of Glass (The Mortal Instruments, #1-3)

**Author:** Cassandra Clare  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

In the realm of young adult fantasy, few series have captivated readers quite like Cassandra Clare's Mortal Instruments. This collectible boxed set, comprising the first three books - City of Bones, City of Ashes, and City of Glass - is a testament to the series' enduring allure. For newcomers and loyal fans alike, this handsomely packaged set is a treasure trove of magic, adventure, and unforgettable characters.

At its core, The Mortal Instruments series is a masterful exploration of the struggle between good and evil, with a dash of teenage angst and self-discovery thrown in for good measure. Clare's world-building is nothing short of phenomenal, transporting readers to a hidden realm of Shadowhunters, demons, vampires, and werewolves, all vying for power in modern-day New York City. The author's meticulous attention to detail and inventive mythology have raised the bar for urban fantasy, making this series a gold standard in the genre.

One of Clare's greatest strengths lies in her ability to craft relatable, multidimensional characters. Clary Fray, the series' protagonist, is a perfect example of this. Her journey from ordinary teenager to brave Shadowhunter is both inspiring and authentic, as she navigates the complexities of her newfound world and confronts the darkness of her own past. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with characters like Jace, Alec, and Magnus Bane adding depth, humor, and heart to the narrative.

Throughout the series, Clare tackles a range of themes, from the power of friendship and love to the danger of prejudice and the corrupting influence of power. Her writing is engaging, witty, and occasionally laugh-out-loud funny, making even the most intense action sequences and emotional confrontations a joy to read.

The Mortal Instruments Boxed Set is a must-have for fans of urban fantasy, adventure, and romance. While the set only includes the first three books, these volumes lay the foundation for the rest of the series, introducing readers to the world of Shadowhunters and the epic battles that will unfold. With its stunning packaging and beautiful hardcover volumes, this set is a perfect gift for anyone who loves to read.

In short, The Mortal Instruments Boxed Set is a triumph of storytelling, world-building, and character development. Cassandra Clare's writing is addictive, her characters unforgettable, and her world of Shadowhunters a true masterpiece of modern fantasy. If you haven't already, join the millions of readers who have fallen under the spell of this incredible series.

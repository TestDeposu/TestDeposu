# Chasers of the Light: Poems from the Typewriter Series

**Author:** Tyler Knott Gregson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

**Chasers of the Light: A Poetic Pursuit of the Ordinary**

In the heart of Montana, amidst the dusty treasures of an antique store, Tyler Knott Gregson discovered not just a vintage Remington typewriter, but a muse that would ignite a poetic revolution. "Chasers of the Light" is the culmination of this serendipitous encounter, a collection that transforms the everyday into the extraordinary, the mundane into the miraculous.

Gregson's style is as unpretentious as it is profound. He eschews the meticulous crafting of poems, instead allowing his words to flow spontaneously onto found scraps of paper or emerge through the blackout method. This process, reminiscent of Kerouac's spontaneous prose, lends an authenticity and immediacy to his work. Each poem is a snapshot, a fleeting moment captured with raw emotion and unapologetic honesty.

The poems in "Chasers of the Light" are like love letters to life itself. Gregson celebrates the beauty in the simple, the overlooked, the seemingly insignificant. He finds poetry in the rust on an old typewriter, in the curve of a lover's spine, in the way light filters through a window. He is a chaser of light, both literal and metaphorical, seeking out the moments that illuminate our existence.

Gregson's use of language is deceptively simple. He employs everyday words, but arranges them in such a way that they reveal their deeper, more profound meanings. His poems are like puzzles, inviting the reader to linger, to ponder, to unravel the layers of meaning. Yet, they are also accessible, immediate, and deeply relatable. They speak to the universal human experience, to love and loss, joy and sorrow, hope and despair.

One of the standout aspects of this collection is its raw, unfiltered emotion. Gregson wears his heart on his sleeve, inviting the reader to share in his joys and sorrows. He writes about love with a fierce intensity, about loss with a profound ache, about hope with a steadfast determination. His poems are not just words on a page; they are experiences, emotions distilled into verse.

"Chasers of the Light" is not a book to be read quickly, devoured in a single sitting. It is a book to be savored, to be dipped into, to be read and reread. Each poem is a gem, a small, shining truth that reflects back at us our own experiences, our own emotions. It is a book that reminds us to slow down, to look closer, to chase the light in our own lives.

In a world that often prioritizes the grand and the spectacular, "Chasers of the Light" is a refreshing reminder of the beauty in the ordinary. It is a testament to the power of poetry to illuminate the dark corners of our lives, to find meaning in the mundane, to celebrate the miracle in the everyday.

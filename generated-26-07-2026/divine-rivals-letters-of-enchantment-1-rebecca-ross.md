# Divine Rivals (Letters of Enchantment, #1)

**Author:** Rebecca Ross  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

Rebecca Ross's "Divine Rivals" is a captivating and epic fantasy novel that masterfully weaves together elements of romance, mythology, and war, transporting readers to a world where gods and mortals collide. This beautifully crafted tale follows the journey of 18-year-old Iris Winnow, a determined and fiercely loyal young journalist, as she navigates the complexities of her family's struggles and the treacherous landscape of a world on the brink of chaos.

One of the most striking aspects of "Divine Rivals" is Ross's unique approach to storytelling, which seamlessly blends the ordinary with the extraordinary. Iris's daily life as a journalist and her struggles to care for her family are juxtaposed with the magical and often terrifying world of warring gods, creating a sense of depth and realism that is both captivating and immersive. The author's use of letters as a means of communication between Iris and her rival, Roman, adds a touching and intimate layer to the narrative, allowing readers to experience the blossoming romance firsthand.

The world-building in "Divine Rivals" is equally impressive, with Ross drawing inspiration from various mythologies to create a rich and detailed landscape that is both familiar and fresh. The concept of gods and mortals existing in the same world, with the gods' actions having a direct impact on human lives, raises thought-provoking questions about the nature of power, free will, and the human condition.

At its core, "Divine Rivals" is a story about the transformative power of love and hope in the face of adversity. Iris's unwavering dedication to her family and her unshakeable optimism, even in the darkest of times, serve as a beacon of light in a world torn apart by conflict and destruction. Her romance with Roman, though fraught with challenges and complications, is a beautiful reminder that even in the most difficult circumstances, human connection and love can be a powerful source of strength and resilience.

Ross's writing style is engaging and evocative, with a keen attention to detail that brings the world and characters to vivid life. Her prose is both poetic and accessible, making "Divine Rivals" an enjoyable read for fans of fantasy, romance, and mythology alike. The pacing is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows smoothly between moments of quiet introspection and intense action sequences.

In conclusion, "Divine Rivals" is a stunning and epic fantasy novel that will captivate readers with its rich world-building, memorable characters, and poignant exploration of the human condition. With its unique blend of romance, mythology, and war, this book is sure to appeal to fans of "Shadow and Bone" and "Lore," and will leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in the Letters of Enchantment series.

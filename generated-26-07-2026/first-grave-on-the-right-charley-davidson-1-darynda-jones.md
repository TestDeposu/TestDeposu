# First Grave on the Right (Charley Davidson, #1)

**Author:** Darynda Jones  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In the realm of paranormal romantic suspense, Darynda Jones bursts onto the scene with her award-winning debut novel, "First Grave on the Right". This electrifying book introduces us to Charley Davidson, a part-time private investigator and full-time Grim Reaper, who possesses a unique gift - the ability to see and communicate with the dead. As Charley navigates her complex world, she finds herself entangled in a web of murder, mystery, and supernatural intrigue.

One of the standout aspects of Jones' writing style is her ability to seamlessly blend humor, wit, and darkness. Charley's sassy and confident narrative voice is infectious, making it impossible not to be drawn into her world. The author's use of humor is cleverly employed to diffuse the tension, creating a sense of levity even in the most intense moments. This masterful balance of tone is a testament to Jones' skill as a storyteller.

The main theme of "First Grave on the Right" revolves around Charley's struggle to reconcile her dual identities - that of a Grim Reaper and a private investigator. As she delves deeper into the mysteries surrounding the dead, she finds herself at odds with her own morality and the consequences of her actions. This internal conflict is expertly woven throughout the narrative, adding depth and complexity to the story.

The romantic element is also expertly handled, with Charley's intensely hot dreams about the Entity, Reyes, providing a tantalizing subplot. The chemistry between Charley and Reyes is palpable, and their relationship is fraught with tension and uncertainty. Jones skillfully teases out the romance, leaving the reader eager for more.

The supporting cast of characters is equally well-developed, with a range of quirky and intriguing individuals that add to the story's richness. From Charley's eccentric family to her loyal friends, each character is meticulously crafted to enhance the narrative.

The pacing of the book is well-balanced, with a steady stream of revelations and twists that keep the reader engaged. Jones' writing is descriptive and immersive, making it easy to visualize the world she's created. The paranormal elements are cleverly integrated, adding a unique spin to the traditional detective genre.

Overall, "First Grave on the Right" is a captivating debut that showcases Darynda Jones' talent as a storyteller. With its unique blend of humor, mystery, and romance, this book is sure to appeal to fans of paranormal romantic suspense. Charley Davidson is a compelling protagonist, and her journey is one that will leave readers eager for more.

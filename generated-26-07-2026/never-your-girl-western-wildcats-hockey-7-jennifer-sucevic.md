# Never Your Girl (Western Wildcats Hockey, #7)

**Author:** Jennifer Sucevic  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

Never Your Girl, the seventh installment in the Western Wildcats Hockey series by Jennifer Sucevic, is a captivating tale of love, animosity, and self-discovery. The story follows the tumultuous relationship between Asher Stevens, the star football player, and the narrator, who remains unnamed throughout the synopsis. On the surface, Asher appears to be the epitome of perfection, with his chiseled physique, exceptional athletic abilities, and charming facade. However, the narrator sees beyond the facade, exposing Asher's true nature as a self-absorbed, party-loving, and promiscuous individual.

Despite her initial disdain for Asher, the narrator finds herself inexplicably drawn to him, her heart racing whenever their eyes meet, and her body responding to his touch. This internal conflict sets the stage for a complex exploration of emotions, as the narrator struggles to reconcile her attraction to Asher with her distaste for his behavior. The author masterfully weaves a narrative that is both relatable and engaging, raising questions about the nature of attraction, the dangers of toxic relationships, and the importance of self-awareness.

Throughout the book, Sucevic's writing style is characterized by her witty dialogue, vivid descriptions, and unapologetic honesty. The narrator's voice is sharp and sarcastic, providing a refreshing contrast to the often-sugarcoated portrayals of romance in contemporary fiction. The author's use of language is evocative and immersive, drawing the reader into the world of the Western Wildcats and the narrator's inner turmoil.

One of the most striking aspects of Never Your Girl is its thoughtful examination of the complexities of human relationships. The book delves into the gray areas between love and hate, attraction and repulsion, and the ways in which our emotions can be both contradictory and all-consuming. The narrator's relationship with Asher is a perfect example of this, as they engage in a push-and-pull dynamic that is both captivating and unsettling.

As the story unfolds, the narrator finds herself increasingly entangled in Asher's life, despite her best efforts to avoid him. The author's pacing is expertly handled, ratcheting up the tension and anticipation as the narrator and Asher's paths continue to cross. From the restaurant where the narrator works to the campus and the parking lot, Asher's presence is ubiquitous, making it impossible for the narrator to escape his charms.

In conclusion, Never Your Girl is a compelling and thought-provoking read that will appeal to fans of contemporary romance and women's fiction. With its relatable characters, engaging storyline, and unflinching honesty, this book is sure to resonate with readers. Sucevic's writing style is both distinctive and captivating, making her a standout author in the genre.

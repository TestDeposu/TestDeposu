# A Dead and Stormy Night (Nevermore Bookshop Mysteries, #1)

**Author:** Steffanie Holmes  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

**A Dead and Stormy Night** by Steffanie Holmes is a captivating debut that masterfully blends the cozy charm of a small-town mystery with the eternal allure of vampire lore. This first installment of the Nevermore Bookshop Mysteries series is a delightful concoction of enemies-to-lovers tension, witty banter, and a murder mystery that keeps you guessing until the very end.

At the heart of the story is Arabella Lestrange, a vampire with a 150-year-old grudge against the man who broke her heart and turned her into the undead. When Gideon Blake, now also a vampire, returns to their quaint English town, Arabella's carefully cultivated indifference is put to the test. Holmes excels in creating a protagonist who is both relatable and endearing, her sharp wit and dry humor providing a refreshing contrast to the swooning vampire tropes of old.

Holmes's prose is as inviting as a warm cup of tea on a stormy night, drawing you into the quaint, book-loving world of the Nevermore Bookshop. The author's love for literature is evident in the meticulous detail she pours into the bookshop and its eccentric patrons, who form a found family around Arabella. The book club meetings, filled with literary discussions and tea-soaked schemes, are a highlight of the novel, providing both comic relief and crucial plot development.

The romantic tension between Arabella and Gideon is expertly crafted, with Holmes taking her time to build a slow-burn chemistry that feels earned and genuine. Their dance of attraction and repulsion is a joy to watch, as they navigate their complicated history and the secrets that threaten to tear them apart. The vampire mythology is cleverly woven into the story, with Holmes putting her own unique spin on the classic creatures of the night.

The murder mystery at the heart of the novel is equally engaging, with a cast of suspects that includes everyone from the local vicar to the town's most eligible bachelor. Holmes keeps the reader guessing with a series of red herrings and unexpected twists, leading to a satisfyingly surprising conclusion.

**A Dead and Stormy Night** is a charming and captivating read that will appeal to fans of cozy mysteries, paranormal romance, and witty banter. Steffanie Holmes is a fresh voice in the genre, and I eagerly await the next installment in the Nevermore Bookshop Mysteries.

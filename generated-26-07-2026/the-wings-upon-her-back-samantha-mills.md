# The Wings Upon Her Back

**Author:** Samantha Mills  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

The Wings Upon Her Back, the debut novel by Samantha Mills, is a thought-provoking and haunting tale that delves into the complexities of loyalty, power, and the human condition. This gripping story follows Zenya, a young and determined individual who joins a mechanically-modified warrior sect, seeking to earn mechanized wings and protect the people of Radezhda, the city she loves. Under the tutelage of the enigmatic and charismatic leader, Vodaya, Zenya rises through the ranks to become Winged Zemolai, a symbol of strength and protection.

However, after twenty-six years of unwavering dedication, Zemolai begins to question the true nature of her role and the fascist state she has sworn to protect. A single act of mercy sets off a chain of events that leads to her brutal expulsion from the sect, leaving her to confront the harsh realities of her world and the gods that seem to have abandoned it. As Zemolai struggles to survive and make sense of her new reality, she is forced to confront the darkness within herself and the sect she once called home.

Mills' writing is masterful, weaving a complex and intricate narrative that is both action-packed and devastating. Her prose is evocative and immersive, transporting readers to a world of wonder and horror. The characters are multidimensional and flawed, with Zenya's transformation from a wide-eyed teenager to a disillusioned and battered warrior being particularly compelling. The world-building is equally impressive, with a richly detailed and immersive environment that draws readers in and refuses to let go.

One of the most striking aspects of The Wings Upon Her Back is its exploration of the brutal costs of zealous loyalty. Mills raises important questions about the nature of devotion and the dangers of blind obedience, highlighting the fine line between loyalty and fanaticism. Through Zenya's journey, we see the devastating consequences of a system that prioritizes power and control over compassion and empathy. The novel is a powerful commentary on the dangers of totalitarian regimes and the importance of individual freedom and autonomy.

Mills' style is reminiscent of classic science fiction and fantasy authors, with a strong focus on character development and world-building. Her writing is atmospheric and immersive, with a keen sense of pacing and tension. The novel is a slow burn, building towards a devastating climax that will leave readers breathless and disturbed.

In short, The Wings Upon Her Back is a triumph of modern science fiction, a novel that will appeal to fans of authors like Octavia Butler, N.K. Jemisin, and China Miéville. It is a powerful and thought-provoking exploration of the human condition, a gripping tale of loyalty, power, and redemption. With its intricate world-building, complex characters, and haunting prose, this debut novel is an absolute must-read for anyone looking to immerse themselves in a rich and immersive science fiction world.

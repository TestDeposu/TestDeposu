# Before I Die

**Author:** Jenny Downham  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

**Before I Die: A Raw, Emotional Journey of Living Life to the Fullest**

Jenny Downham's "Before I Die" is a poignant and unapologetic exploration of life, love, and mortality, wrapped in a captivating coming-of-age narrative. The story of Tessa, a teenage girl grappling with a terminal illness, is a powerful reminder that life is precious and fleeting, and that every moment counts.

Downham's prose is raw and unflinching, delving deep into Tessa's psyche as she navigates her new reality. The author masterfully captures the bittersweet essence of Tessa's existence, balancing moments of profound sadness with unexpected humor and joy. Tessa's voice is authentic and compelling, her spirit unbroken despite her circumstances, making her an unforgettable protagonist.

The novel's central theme is Tessa's determination to live her life on her terms, defying the constraints of her illness and society's expectations. Her "To Do Before I Die" list is more than just a bucket list; it's a declaration of independence, a testament to her refusal to be defined by her disease. Downham explores the complexities of Tessa's relationships - with her father, brother, estranged mother, best friend, and new boyfriend - with nuance and sensitivity, illustrating how love and loss are inextricably intertwined.

"Before I Die" is not an easy read, but it is a necessary one. It challenges us to confront our own mortality and consider how we would choose to spend our final days. It's a story about seizing the moment, about finding beauty in the face of adversity, and about the transformative power of love and connection.

Downham's debut novel is a stunning achievement, earning its place alongside other seminal works in young adult literature. It's a book that will stay with you long after you've turned the final page, a haunting reminder to cherish every moment and live life to the fullest.

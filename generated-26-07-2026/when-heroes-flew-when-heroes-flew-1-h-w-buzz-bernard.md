# When Heroes Flew (When Heroes Flew #1)

**Author:** H.W. "Buzz" Bernard  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**When Heroes Flew: A Soaring Tale of Courage and Love**

H.W. "Buzz" Bernard's "When Heroes Flew" is a captivating blend of historical fiction and romance, set against the backdrop of World War II. The novel opens with a poignant coming-of-age moment in 1928 Germany, where young Egon Richter, driven by a mix of youthful bravado and instinct, defies his father's wishes to take down a formidable wild boar. This early display of Egon's courage and determination sets the stage for his later adventures.

The story then shifts to the skies of North Africa in 1942, where we meet Al, a young American aviator who has volunteered for the Army Air Forces to fulfill his dream of flying. His love for Sarah, his new bride, fuels his resolve to return home safely, even as he embarks on dangerous bombing missions in an experimental B-24 bomber.

Bernard's prose is engaging and accessible, seamlessly weaving together the personal narratives of Egon and Al with the broader historical context of the war. He masterfully captures the camaraderie, fear, and exhilaration of aerial combat, as well as the quiet moments of reflection and longing that bind the characters to their loved ones back home.

At its heart, "When Heroes Flew" is a love story, not just between Al and Sarah, but also between Egon and his father, and among the men who fight together in the skies. It's a testament to the power of love to inspire courage, resilience, and the will to survive.

Bernard's meticulous research shines through in the authentic details of the historical setting and the technical aspects of aviation. Yet, the story never feels bogged down by facts, instead using them to enrich the characters' experiences and emotions.

"When Heroes Flew" is more than just a war story; it's a testament to the human spirit, a celebration of love and courage, and a gripping adventure that will keep readers on the edge of their seats until the very end.

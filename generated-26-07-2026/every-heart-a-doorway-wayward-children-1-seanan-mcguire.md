# Every Heart a Doorway (Wayward Children, #1)

**Author:** Seanan McGuire  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

Every Heart a Doorway, the first installment of Seanan McGuire's Wayward Children series, is a captivating and thought-provoking tale that delves into the complexities of identity, belonging, and the human experience. This Hugo Award-winning novel has garnered widespread acclaim for its unique blend of portal fantasy, mystery, and coming-of-age themes, drawing comparisons to the works of Lewis Carroll, C.S. Lewis, and Neil Gaiman.

At the heart of the story is Eleanor West's Home for Wayward Children, a sanctuary for youngsters who have stumbled upon magical worlds, only to be rejected and returned to their mundane lives. These children, including the protagonist Nancy, are left to grapple with the trauma and sense of displacement that comes with being torn between two worlds. McGuire masterfully explores the emotional and psychological toll of this experience, creating a sense of empathy and understanding in the reader.

The author's writing style is a notable aspect of the book's appeal. McGuire's prose is lyrical and evocative, with a keen attention to detail that brings the characters and their worlds to vivid life. Her use of language is both poetic and precise, making the narrative feel both whimsical and grounded. The pacing is well-balanced, moving seamlessly between moments of introspection, suspense, and wonder.

One of the novel's greatest strengths lies in its thoughtful exploration of identity and belonging. The wayward children, each with their unique experiences and scars, serve as a powerful metaphor for the struggles of adolescence and the search for one's place in the world. McGuire raises important questions about the nature of home, family, and acceptance, inviting readers to reflect on their own experiences and relationships.

The mystery that unfolds at the Home is expertly woven, with a sense of tension and foreboding that propels the narrative forward. As Nancy and her companions work to unravel the truth behind a series of tragic events, the stakes grow higher, and the reader is drawn into a world of intrigue and suspense.

Throughout the book, McGuire's love for mythology, folklore, and fairy tales is evident. The world-building is meticulous and imaginative, drawing inspiration from a range of sources, including Alice in Wonderland, The Magicians, and The Nightmare Before Christmas. The result is a rich tapestry of ideas and influences that adds depth and complexity to the narrative.

In conclusion, Every Heart a Doorway is a mesmerizing and emotionally resonant novel that will appeal to fans of fantasy, mystery, and coming-of-age stories. Seanan McGuire's writing is a joy to read, and her exploration of identity, belonging, and the human experience is both thought-provoking and deeply moving. This is a book that will linger in the reader's mind long after the final page has been turned.

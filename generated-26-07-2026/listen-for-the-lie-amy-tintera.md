# Listen for the Lie

**Author:** Amy Tintera  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

**Listen for the Lie** by Amy Tintera is a gripping, darkly humorous thriller that keeps you guessing until the very end. Tintera, known for her compelling storytelling, delivers a masterclass in suspense with this tale of memory, truth, and the power of perception.

The story unfolds around Lucy, once the golden girl of a small Texas town, now a woman haunted by a past she can't remember. When she's found covered in her best friend Savvy's blood, everyone, including Lucy herself, assumes she's a murderer. Years later, a true crime podcast, "Listen for the Lie," resurrects the case, forcing Lucy to confront her past and the chilling possibility that she might be guilty.

Tintera's strength lies in her ability to weave a complex web of intrigue, peppered with witty dialogue and sharp observations. She expertly navigates the blurred lines between comedy and thriller, creating a narrative that is as entertaining as it is unsettling. The characters are vivid and flawed, making it impossible not to be drawn into their world.

The podcast format adds an innovative layer to the storytelling, allowing Tintera to explore the power of narrative and the influence of media on public perception. As the podcast delves deeper into Savvy's murder, so does the reader, leading to a series of twists that are as unexpected as they are satisfying.

Tintera's prose is crisp and engaging, drawing the reader in from the first page. She has a knack for creating suspense, using short, sharp sentences and clever pacing to build tension. The result is a page-turner that's impossible to put down.

**Listen for the Lie** is more than just a murder mystery; it's a exploration of memory, truth, and the power of storytelling. It's a testament to Tintera's skill as a writer that she can make us question not just what happened, but also what we believe and why.

# The Power of Six (Lorien Legacies, #2)

**Author:** Pittacus Lore  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**The Power of Six: A Thrilling Chapter in the Lorien Legacies**

In the pulsating sequel to "I Am Number Four," Pittacus Lore masterfully continues the saga of the Lorien Nine, alien teenagers hiding on Earth from the malevolent Mogadorians. "The Power of Six" is a heart-pounding adventure that explores the power of unity and the indomitable spirit of youth.

John Smith, Number Four, and his new ally, Number Six, are on the run, their world turned upside down by the relentless pursuit of the Mogs. Yet, as they evade capture, they inspire others like them to emerge from the shadows. Marina, Number Seven, is the first to join their cause, and her arrival signals a shift in the balance of power.

Lore's strength lies in his ability to create relatable, flawed heroes. John and Six are not invincible; they make mistakes, face self-doubt, and grapple with the weight of their responsibilities. This humanizes them, making their triumphs all the more satisfying. Moreover, Lore's world-building is impressive, with each Lorien Garde possessing unique abilities that add depth to the action sequences.

The narrative is fast-paced, with short chapters that keep the tension high and the pages turning. Lore's style is accessible, making "The Power of Six" an engaging read for both young adult and adult audiences. He also masterfully weaves in themes of friendship, trust, and the power of standing together against oppression.

However, the book is not without its flaws. Some plot points feel convenient, and certain character developments are rushed. Yet, these are minor quibbles in an otherwise thrilling ride.

"The Power of Six" is more than just a science fiction adventure; it's a story about finding one's place in the world, the power of friendship, and the courage to stand up against tyranny. It's a must-read for fans of action-packed, thought-provoking young adult fiction.

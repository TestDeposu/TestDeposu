# By the Unholy Hand (Executioner Knights, #1)

**Author:** Kathryn Le Veque  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Unknown

**By the Unholy Hand: A Gripping Debut in Le Veque's Executioner Knights Series**

Kathryn Le Veque, a master of medieval romance, kicks off her Executioner Knights series with 'By the Unholy Hand', a tale that deftly weaves political intrigue, forbidden love, and a dash of the supernatural. Set against the backdrop of 12th-century England, this novel is a compelling blend of history, action, and passion.

At the heart of the story lies Sir Nicholas de Montfort, a knight bound by a dark oath to serve as the Executioner, a secret order that carries out justice in the shadows. When he's tasked with investigating a series of mysterious deaths, he finds himself drawn to the enigmatic Lady Isolde de Winter, a woman with secrets of her own. As they navigate a treacherous web of deceit and danger, Nicholas and Isolde must confront their growing feelings for each other, even as they race against time to unmask a sinister force threatening the very foundations of the kingdom.

Le Veque's storytelling prowess is evident in her ability to create a richly textured world, filled with vivid detail and authentic historical context. Her characters are equally impressive, with Nicholas's complex morality and Isolde's quiet strength making them compelling protagonists. The chemistry between them is palpable, their slow-burn romance a satisfying counterpoint to the high-stakes plot.

What sets 'By the Unholy Hand' apart is Le Veque's incorporation of the supernatural. While not overdone, the hint of the otherworldly adds an intriguing layer to the story, leaving readers eager to unravel the mystery. The pacing is excellent, balancing intense action sequences with quieter, more intimate moments, keeping readers engaged from start to finish.

In true Le Veque fashion, 'By the Unholy Hand' is a thrilling ride that combines the best of historical fiction, romance, and suspense. Fans of her work, as well as readers new to her writing, will find much to love in this captivating start to the Executioner Knights series.

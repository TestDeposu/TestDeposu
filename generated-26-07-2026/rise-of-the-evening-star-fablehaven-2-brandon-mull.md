# Rise of the Evening Star (Fablehaven, #2)

**Author:** Brandon Mull  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

Rise of the Evening Star, the second installment in Brandon Mull's Fablehaven series, is a mesmerizing tale of magic, adventure, and self-discovery. Although the original synopsis seems to refer to the Beyonders trilogy, this review will focus on the actual content of Rise of the Evening Star.

In this captivating novel, Mull masterfully weaves a narrative that delves into the complexities of identity, friendship, and the struggle between good and evil. The story follows Kendra and Seth Sorenson as they navigate the mystical world of magical preserves, hidden societies, and ancient secrets.

One of the most striking aspects of Mull's writing style is his ability to craft a sense of wonder and enchantment. His vivid descriptions of magical creatures, hidden worlds, and ancient artifacts transport readers to a realm of awe and curiosity. The author's use of language is rich and evocative, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world of Fablehaven.

At the heart of the story lies the theme of identity and self-discovery. Kendra and Seth, as the main protagonists, embark on a journey to uncover the secrets of their family's past and their own destinies. Through their experiences, Mull explores the idea that one's true identity is often hidden beneath the surface, waiting to be discovered. This theme is skillfully intertwined with the plot, adding depth and complexity to the narrative.

Mull's character development is another notable aspect of his writing. The characters in Rise of the Evening Star are multidimensional and relatable, with each one bringing their unique perspective and strengths to the story. The relationships between the characters are authentic and engaging, making it easy to become invested in their struggles and triumphs.

The pacing of the novel is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and quiet moments of introspection. Mull's ability to craft a compelling narrative is evident in the way he seamlessly weaves together multiple plot threads, creating a story that is both engaging and unpredictable.

In conclusion, Rise of the Evening Star is a captivating and imaginative novel that will appeal to fans of fantasy and adventure. Brandon Mull's writing style is engaging, and his ability to craft a sense of wonder and enchantment is unparalleled. The themes of identity and self-discovery add depth and complexity to the narrative, making this a truly unforgettable read.

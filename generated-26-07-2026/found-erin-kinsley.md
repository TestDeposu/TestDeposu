# Found

**Author:** Erin Kinsley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

In "Found" by Erin Kinsley, a heart-wrenching and gripping thriller, the nightmare of every parent comes to life when 11-year-old Evan vanishes without a trace. The novel takes a poignant turn when Evan is unexpectedly found, but his silence and fear only raise more questions. As DI Naylor delves into the case, she must navigate a complex web of emotions, racing against time to find those responsible and prevent further harm to other children.

Erin Kinsley's writing style is a perfect blend of sensitivity and tension, crafting a narrative that is both moving and unputdownable. The author's ability to convey the emotional turmoil of Evan's family is remarkable, making it easy for readers to become invested in their story. The characters are well-developed, and the relationships between them are authentic, adding depth to the plot.

One of the standout aspects of "Found" is its ability to balance the darker themes with a sense of hope and resilience. Kinsley tackles the difficult subject matter with care, never shying away from the harsh realities, but also offering a message of strength and survival. The pacing is well-balanced, with a steady stream of revelations and twists that keep the reader engaged.

The comparisons to series like "Broadchurch" and "The Missing" are apt, as "Found" shares a similar atmosphere and emotional resonance. Fans of Cara Hunter, Heidi Perks, and Claire Douglas will also appreciate Kinsley's style, which combines the best elements of psychological thrillers and family dramas.

The critical acclaim for "Found" is well-deserved, with praise from authors like Peter James, Elly Griffiths, and Minette Walters. The novel has also resonated with readers, who have praised its emotional impact and gripping storyline.

In conclusion, "Found" is a must-read for fans of crime thrillers and family dramas. Erin Kinsley's writing is exceptional, and her ability to craft a compelling narrative is undeniable. This novel will appeal to readers who enjoy stories that are both emotionally charged and suspenseful.

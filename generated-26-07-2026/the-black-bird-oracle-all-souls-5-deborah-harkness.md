# The Black Bird Oracle (All Souls, #5)

**Author:** Deborah Harkness  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

**The Black Bird Oracle: A Darkly Enchanting Journey into the Heart of Power**

Deborah Harkness returns to the spellbinding world of All Souls with **The Black Bird Oracle**, a fifth installment that delves deeper into the richly textured tapestry of magic, history, and family secrets. Harkness, a master of weaving intricate plots and compelling characters, once again proves why her series has become a beloved staple in the fantasy genre.

At the heart of **The Black Bird Oracle** lies Diana Bishop's struggle with her own power and her desperate desire to protect her children from the dark forces that have haunted her family for generations. When the Congregation demands that Diana and Matthew test their twins' magic, Diana sees an opportunity to forge a new path, one that leads her to the enigmatic Ravenswood and the enigmatic Gwyneth Proctor.

Harkness's prose is as evocative as ever, painting vivid landscapes and immersive settings that transport the reader to a world where history and magic intertwine. Ravenswood, with its ancient stones and whispered secrets, is a character in its own right, and Harkness's deft handling of its history and lore adds depth and richness to the narrative.

What sets **The Black Bird Oracle** apart is its exploration of the complex dynamics of power and parenthood. Diana's journey is a profound meditation on the legacies we inherit and the ones we choose to leave behind. Harkness deftly navigates the nexus of memory, history, and parenthood, exploring the magic, pain, and promises that mothers pass onto their children.

The novel is also a testament to Harkness's skill in crafting intricate plots that keep the reader on the edge of their seat. The search for answers about Diana's past unfolds like a slow-burning mystery, with each revelation leading to more questions, more secrets to unravel. The pacing is masterful, building to a climax that is both satisfying and unexpected.

In **The Black Bird Oracle**, Harkness continues to push the boundaries of her world, introducing new forms of magic and delving deeper into the rich mythology she has created. The result is a novel that is both a thrilling addition to the All Souls series and a standalone exploration of power, family, and the choices we make.

# Company

**Author:** Max Barry  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

**Company by Max Barry: A Hilarious, Unsettling Dive into the Heart of Corporate Absurdity**

In the vein of Douglas Adams and Kurt Vonnegut, Max Barry's **Company** is a scathing, satirical exploration of the modern corporate landscape, where logic is illogical, and the rules are as arbitrary as they are unyielding. Barry's prose is sharp, witty, and relentlessly entertaining, making **Company** a must-read for anyone who's ever felt like a small cog in a vast, incomprehensible machine.

The story follows Stephen Jones, a fresh recruit at the enigmatic Zephyr Holdings. From the get-go, Zephyr is a puzzle wrapped in an enigma. The receptionist earns more than anyone else for doing nothing, sales reps rely on self-help books for guidance, and no one seems to know what they're actually selling. Or who's in charge. Or why donuts are a matter of life and death.

Barry's strength lies in his ability to capture the mundane absurdity of corporate life, amplifying it to hilarious, often unsettling extremes. He masterfully skewers office politics, company culture, and the Kafkaesque bureaucracy that can grind even the most ambitious souls into dust. Yet, **Company** is more than just a send-up of corporate culture; it's a thought-provoking exploration of power, conformity, and the human desire for meaning in a world that often seems devoid of it.

At its core, **Company** is a cautionary tale about the dangers of unquestioning obedience and the insidious ways power can corrupt and dehumanize. Barry's characters are not heroes or villains, but pawns in a system that demands their compliance, their creativity, and ultimately, their souls. It's a bleak, often disturbing portrait, but one leavened by Barry's dark humor and keen insight.

**Company** is not just a book to be read; it's an experience to be absorbed. It's a rollercoaster ride through the looking glass of corporate culture, a hilarious, unsettling, and ultimately profound exploration of what it means to be a cogs in the machine. If you've ever felt like you're not quite sure what you're doing, or why, then **Company** is the book for you.

# Cascade Failure (Ambit's Run, #1)

**Author:** L.M. Sagas  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

In the realm of science fiction, few debuts have generated as much excitement as L.M. Sagas's Cascade Failure, the first installment in the Ambit's Run series. This gripping novel has been touted as a must-read by Goodreads, Polygon, and The Nerd Daily, and for good reason – it's a masterfully crafted tale of space exploration, corporate greed, and the resilience of the human spirit.

At its core, Cascade Failure is a story about the blurred lines between power and corruption. In the world of the Spiral, three dominant forces vie for control: the Trust, a ruthless corporate entity; the Union, a labor collective with significant leverage; and the Guild, a regulatory body tasked with keeping the peace. It's a delicate balance of power, and one that's easily disrupted.

Enter Jal, a former Guild member who's been branded a deserter. Jal's accidental recruitment by the crew of the Ambit, a Guild ship with a motley crew, sets off a chain reaction of events that will change the course of the Spiral forever. The Ambit's crew is a diverse and dynamic bunch, featuring an AI helm, a medic/engineer who's as skilled with a scalpel as they are with a wrench, and a no-nonsense XO who's always ready to shoot first and ask questions later.

As the crew of the Ambit responds to a distress call from an abandoned planet, they stumble upon a mass grave and a live programmer who holds the key to unraveling the mystery. It soon becomes clear that the Trust is behind the carnage, and that this is just the tip of the iceberg. The crew of the Ambit must navigate treacherous landscapes, avoid deadly traps, and fight off ruthless enemies to prevent further destruction.

Sagas's writing style is reminiscent of J.S. Dewes's Divide series, with its breakneck pacing and intricate world-building. The author's use of language is vivid and immersive, conjuring up images of vibrant worlds and gritty space battles. The characters are multidimensional and complex, with rich backstories that add depth to the narrative.

One of the standout features of Cascade Failure is its exploration of what it means to be human. In a world where AI and human coexist, the lines between man and machine are constantly blurred. Sagas raises important questions about the nature of humanity, and whether it's possible to be human without a physical body.

The themes of power, corruption, and humanity are woven throughout the narrative, creating a rich tapestry that's both thought-provoking and entertaining. Cascade Failure is a gripping page-turner that will appeal to fans of science fiction, space opera, and adventure.

In short, L.M. Sagas's debut is a triumph. Cascade Failure is a must-read for anyone who loves science fiction, and a testament to the author's skill and craftsmanship. With its diverse cast of characters, intricate world-building, and thought-provoking themes, this novel is sure to leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in the Ambit's Run series.

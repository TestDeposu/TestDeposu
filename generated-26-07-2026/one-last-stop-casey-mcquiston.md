# One Last Stop

**Author:** Casey McQuiston  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In the vibrant and captivating world of Casey McQuiston's One Last Stop, the lines between reality and fantasy blur, and the impossible becomes possible. This instant New York Times bestseller is a testament to McQuiston's skill in crafting a romantic comedy that will leave readers spellbound and yearning for more.

At the heart of the story is August, a cynical and fiercely independent twenty-three-year-old who has lost faith in the magic of love and life. Her move to New York City is a deliberate attempt to prove to herself that the world is a dull and unromantic place. However, her life takes an unexpected turn when she meets Jane, a dazzling and charming girl who seems to have stepped out of a 1970s time capsule.

As August becomes increasingly entangled in Jane's life, she discovers that her subway crush is literally displaced in time from the 1970s. This revelation sets off a chain of events that challenges August's perceptions of reality and forces her to confront her past. With its unique blend of science fiction, romance, and humor, One Last Stop is a wildly original and captivating tale that will appeal to fans of The Time Traveler's Wife and Eleanor Oliphant is Completely Fine.

McQuiston's writing style is a key element in the book's success. Her prose is witty, engaging, and effortlessly cool, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world she has created. The characters are multidimensional and relatable, with August's sarcastic humor and Jane's old-school charm making them a compelling and lovable couple.

One of the most striking aspects of One Last Stop is its exploration of the human experience. Through August's journey, McQuiston tackles themes of love, loss, and identity, raising important questions about the nature of reality and our place within it. The book is also a love letter to New York City, with its vibrant descriptions of the city's streets, cafes, and subway system.

The romance at the heart of the story is sweet, tender, and passionate, with August and Jane's relationship developing at a gentle and realistic pace. Their love is not without its challenges, however, and McQuiston handles the complexities of their situation with sensitivity and nuance.

In short, One Last Stop is a magical, sexy, and big-hearted romance that will capture your imagination and leave you feeling uplifted and inspired. With its unique blend of science fiction, humor, and romance, this book is a must-read for fans of Casey McQuiston and anyone looking for a captivating and unforgettable love story.

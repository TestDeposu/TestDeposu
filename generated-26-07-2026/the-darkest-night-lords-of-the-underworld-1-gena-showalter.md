# The Darkest Night (Lords of the Underworld, #1)

**Author:** Gena Showalter  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

**Title: "Eternal Flames and Haunted Hearts: A Review of Gena Showalter's 'The Darkest Night'"**

Gena Showalter's "The Darkest Night," the inaugural novel in her captivating Lords of the Underworld series, is a thrilling plunge into a world where the supernatural and the sensual intertwine, and love's power is as formidable as any curse. Showalter, a master of paranormal romance, weaves a tale that is as darkly enchanting as it is emotionally charged, drawing readers into a realm where desire and destiny collide.

At the heart of "The Darkest Night" lies the eternal curse of the Lords of the Underworld, a group of immortals bound together by a dark past and a shared torment. Among them is Maddox, a man whose pain is as palpable as his power, and whose story Showalter unfolds with a deft hand. Maddox's struggle is not just against the curse that plagues him, but also against the demons of his past, making him a hero who is as compelling as he is complex.

Ashlyn Darrow, the novel's heroine, is a woman haunted by voices from the past, seeking solace in the arms of the very beings she fears. Ashlyn's journey is one of self-discovery and strength, as she learns to embrace her own power and confront her fears. Showalter's portrayal of Ashlyn is refreshing, offering a heroine who is both vulnerable and resilient, a beacon of light in the darkness that surrounds her.

Showalter's style is marked by a richly imaginative world-building, intricate plotlines, and a prose that is as evocative as it is engaging. She masterfully balances the tension between the paranormal and the romantic, creating a narrative that is as suspenseful as it is swoon-worthy. Her characters are not just well-crafted, but also deeply human, their struggles and triumphs resonating long after the last page is turned.

"The Darkest Night" is not just a romance, but a story about the power of love to heal, to redeem, and to conquer even the darkest of curses. It is a testament to Showalter's skill as a storyteller, her ability to create a world that is as captivating as it is believable, and her capacity to evoke emotions that are as intense as they are varied.

In the end, "The Darkest Night" is a must-read for fans of paranormal romance, a novel that promises a thrilling ride through a world of eternal flames and haunted hearts. It is a story that will leave you breathless, your heart pounding, and your soul yearning for more.

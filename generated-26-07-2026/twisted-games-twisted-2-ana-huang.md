# Twisted Games (Twisted, #2)

**Author:** Ana Huang  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**Twisted Games: A Dance of Desire and Defiance**

Ana Huang's "Twisted Games" is the second installment in her explosive 'Twisted' series, promising readers a thrilling ride of passion, power, and forbidden fantasies. The author, known for her captivating narratives and complex characters, delivers a story that is as intense as it is intoxicating.

At the heart of "Twisted Games" lies the tumultuous relationship between Rhys Larsen, an arrogant and guarded bodyguard, and Bridget von Ascheberg, a fiery princess with a hidden depth. Huang masterfully crafts these characters, each with their own set of rules and scars, drawing readers in with their intricate dance of push and pull.

Rhys, with his steely demeanor and unyielding principles, is a formidable force. Yet, Huang peels back his layers with finesse, revealing a man bound by duty and haunted by his past. Bridget, on the other hand, is a whirlwind of passion and defiance, her royal upbringing clashing with her rebellious spirit. Their dynamic is electric, a constant battle of wills that sparks into something far more profound.

Huang's style is as enthralling as it is evocative. She weaves a narrative that is as dark and twisted as the title suggests, filled with suspense, steamy encounters, and unexpected twists. Her prose is elegant, her pacing impeccable, keeping readers on the edge of their seats until the very end.

"Twisted Games" is not just a romance; it's a tale of two souls bound by fate, their love story as tumultuous as it is beautiful. It's a testament to Huang's skill that she makes us root for these two broken individuals, despite their flaws and the chaos they create.

In a nutshell, "Twisted Games" is a must-read for fans of enemies-to-lovers trope, dark romance, and complex characters. It's a rollercoaster ride of emotions, a twisted game of desire and defiance that will leave you breathless.

# How to Become the Dark Lord and Die Trying (Dark Lord Davi, #1)

**Author:** Django Wexler  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

In the realm of fantasy, it's not often that we come across a protagonist who's tired of being the hero and decides to take a drastic turn towards the dark side. Django Wexler's "How to Become the Dark Lord and Die Trying" is a wildly entertaining and subversive take on the traditional fantasy narrative, blending the time-looping antics of "Groundhog Day" with the irreverent humor of "Deadpool."

At the center of this chaotic tale is Davi, a young woman who's had it up to here with being the chosen one. After a hundred failed attempts at defeating the Dark Lord, she's had enough of the heroics and decides to switch sides. Why not, after all, become the very thing she's been trying to defeat all this time? It's a bold move, and one that sets off a chain reaction of events that are both hilarious and action-packed.

Wexler's writing style is a perfect match for this kind of story. He's got a keen sense of humor and a willingness to poke fun at the conventions of the fantasy genre. Davi's voice is sharp and sarcastic, and her frustrations with the never-ending time loop are palpable. She's a lovable antihero, and her decision to become the Dark Lord is both relatable and cathartic.

As Davi navigates the complexities of being the bad guy, Wexler takes the opportunity to explore the nature of power and morality. What does it mean to be a villain, really? Is it just a matter of wearing a black hat and cackling maniacally, or is there something more to it? Davi's journey is a thought-provoking one, and it's clear that Wexler is having a blast subverting our expectations at every turn.

The world-building in "How to Become the Dark Lord and Die Trying" is also worth noting. Wexler's created a rich and detailed landscape, full of clever references and nods to other fantasy stories. The time loop itself is a clever device, allowing Davi to try new things and experiment with different approaches to becoming the Dark Lord.

If you're looking for a fantasy story that's a little off the beaten path, "How to Become the Dark Lord and Die Trying" is a great choice. It's a raunchy, hilarious, and action-packed ride that's full of surprises. Wexler's writing is top-notch, and Davi's voice is one that will stay with you long after you finish the book.

Overall, "How to Become the Dark Lord and Die Trying" is a fantastic read that's sure to appeal to fans of humor, fantasy, and adventure. So if you're ready to join the dark side and see what all the fuss is about, then this is the book for you.

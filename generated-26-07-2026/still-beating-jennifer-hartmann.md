# Still Beating

**Author:** Jennifer Hartmann  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

In "Still Beating" by Jennifer Hartmann, the lines between love and hate are blurred in a dark, twisted game of survival. Cora and Dean, sworn enemies for fifteen years, find themselves chained in a basement, forced to put aside their animosity and work together to escape their deranged captor. But as they navigate this treacherous landscape, they begin to realize that their feelings for each other are far more complex than they ever could have imagined.

Hartmann's writing is masterful, weaving a tale that is both captivating and unsettling. She expertly crafts a sense of tension and unease, keeping the reader on edge as Cora and Dean's situation becomes increasingly dire. The author's use of dark humor and witty banter adds a layer of depth to the story, making it impossible to look away from the train wreck that is Cora and Dean's relationship.

One of the most striking aspects of "Still Beating" is its exploration of the thin line between love and hate. Cora and Dean's relationship is a prime example of this, as they oscillate between venomous insults and tender moments. Hartmann raises important questions about the nature of their feelings, leaving the reader to ponder whether their love is a product of their captivity or a genuine emotion.

The author's portrayal of the captor is equally impressive, creating a sense of unpredictability and menace that permeates the entire narrative. The character's motivations and backstory are skillfully woven throughout the story, adding a layer of complexity to the plot.

While "Still Beating" is undoubtedly a dark romance, it is also a thought-provoking exploration of the human psyche. Hartmann tackles themes of trauma, abuse, and survival with sensitivity and care, never shying away from the harsh realities of Cora and Dean's situation.

Overall, "Still Beating" is a gripping and unsettling read that will appeal to fans of dark romance and psychological thrillers. With its complex characters, twisted plot, and thought-provoking themes, this book is sure to leave a lasting impression on readers.

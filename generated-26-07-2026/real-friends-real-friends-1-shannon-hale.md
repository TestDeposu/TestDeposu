# Real Friends (Real Friends #1)

**Author:** Shannon Hale  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

"Real Friends" by Shannon Hale is a poignant and relatable graphic memoir that masterfully captures the complexities of childhood friendships and the struggle to find one's true self. Hale, a Newbery Honor author, teams up with renowned illustrator LeUyen Pham to create a visually engaging and emotionally resonant story that will resonate with readers of all ages.

The narrative follows Shannon, a young girl navigating the tumultuous waters of elementary school friendships. When her best friend Adrienne starts hanging out with the popular Jen and her clique, Shannon is left grappling with feelings of exclusion and anxiety. Hale's exploration of these emotions is raw and authentic, offering a refreshing honesty that is often missing in children's literature.

Hale's storytelling style is simple yet powerful, using the graphic novel format to great effect. The illustrations, with their expressive characters and dynamic panel layouts, beautifully complement the text, making the story engaging and accessible. The use of color is particularly noteworthy, with shifts in hue effectively conveying the emotional tone of each scene.

At its heart, "Real Friends" is a story about identity and belonging. Shannon's journey to understand her place in the social hierarchy and her struggle to stay true to herself is a universal one. Hale's exploration of these themes is nuanced and thought-provoking, offering readers a powerful message about the importance of self-worth and authenticity.

"Real Friends" is not just a story about friendship; it's a story about growing up, about learning to stand up for oneself, and about the power of being true to one's self. It's a must-read for anyone who has ever felt like they don't quite fit in, which, let's face it, is all of us at some point.

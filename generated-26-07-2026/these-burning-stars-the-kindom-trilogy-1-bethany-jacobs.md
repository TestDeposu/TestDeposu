# These Burning Stars (The Kindom Trilogy, #1)

**Author:** Bethany Jacobs  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**These Burning Stars: A Celestial Chess Match of Intrigue and Revenge**

Bethany Jacobs' debut novel, 'These Burning Stars', is a dazzling addition to the realm of space opera, weaving a complex tapestry of political intrigue, revenge, and hidden secrets that span galaxies. Winner of the 2024 Philip K. Dick Award, this explosive narrative is a testament to Jacobs' prowess as a fresh voice in science fiction.

At its core, 'These Burning Stars' is a cat-and-mouse game set against the backdrop of a vast, oppressive empire known as the Kindom. Jun Ironway, a cunning thief, possesses a secret that could bring the Kindom to its knees. Hot on her heels are Chono and Esek, two formidable clerics tasked with hunting her down. Meanwhile, a mysterious figure known only as Six lurks in the shadows, pulling strings and manipulating events. The stage is set for a high-stakes dance of deception and betrayal.

Jacobs masterfully navigates this intricate plot, moving her pieces like a grandmaster in a game of celestial chess. Each character is a pawn with their own agenda, their paths intersecting and diverging in unexpected ways. The narrative is plotted with such confidence and surprise that readers will find themselves on the edge of their seats, eagerly turning pages to uncover the next twist.

The author's style is reminiscent of the best of James S.A. Corey and Ann Leckie, blending political intrigue with a dash of noir. Jacobs' prose is crisp and engaging, painting vivid landscapes of distant planets and the complex power dynamics that govern them. She excels in creating compelling, morally grey characters, each with their own secrets and motivations, driving the plot forward with a relentless momentum.

The main theme of 'These Burning Stars' is revenge, but it's not a simple tale of retribution. Instead, Jacobs explores the complexities of revenge, its cost, and the moral ambiguity that often accompanies it. Each character's quest for vengeance is intertwined with their desire for justice, creating a rich tapestry of motivations that challenge the reader's sympathies.

In a genre often dominated by epic battles and grand-scale action, Jacobs chooses to focus on the intimate, personal stakes of her characters. The result is a novel that feels both intimate and expansive, a story that could be happening in the farthest reaches of the galaxy or in the back alleys of a distant planet.

'These Burning Stars' is a remarkable debut, a thrilling space opera that pulls off one of the most brazen, gasp-inducing reveals in recent memory. It's a testament to Jacobs' skill as a storyteller and a promise of great things to come. If you're a fan of smart thrillers, explosive science fiction, and intricate political machinations, this book is a must-read.

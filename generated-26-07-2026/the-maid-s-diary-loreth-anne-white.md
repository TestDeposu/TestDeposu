# The Maid's Diary

**Author:** Loreth Anne White  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

The Maid's Diary, the latest thriller from Loreth Anne White, is a gripping and twisted tale that delves into the darker aspects of human nature and the secrets that lie beneath the surface of seemingly perfect lives. The story follows Kit Darling, a maid with a penchant for snooping, who stumbles upon a dark secret in the home of her new clients, a wealthy couple expecting their first child. This discovery sets off a chain of events that puts Kit's life in danger and leads to a violent attack at the couple's luxury waterfront home, known as the Glass House.

As homicide cop Mallory Van Alst investigates the crime scene, she is confronted with a complex web of secrets and lies that lead her to question everything she thought she knew about the case. The more Mal digs, the more she realizes that nothing is as it seems, and that the past has a way of catching up with everyone involved. White's masterful plotting and character development keep the reader on the edge of their seat, as the story twists and turns in unexpected ways.

One of the most striking aspects of The Maid's Diary is its exploration of the theme of class and privilege. The wealthy couple at the center of the story appear to have it all - a beautiful home, a perfect relationship, and a bright future ahead of them. But as the story progresses, it becomes clear that their lives are not as perfect as they seem, and that their wealth and privilege have not shielded them from the darker aspects of human nature. White's portrayal of the class divide is nuanced and thought-provoking, highlighting the ways in which wealth and privilege can both empower and entrap.

White's writing style is also noteworthy, as she weaves together multiple plot threads and character perspectives with ease. Her use of language is evocative and atmospheric, conjuring up vivid images of the Glass House and its surroundings. The pacing of the story is expertly handled, with a steady build-up of tension and suspense that keeps the reader engaged and invested in the outcome.

The characters in The Maid's Diary are also well-developed and complex, with rich inner lives and motivations. Kit Darling, the maid at the center of the story, is a particularly compelling character, with a troubled past and a deep-seated need to uncover the secrets of those around her. Mal, the homicide cop, is also well-drawn, with a sharp mind and a dry wit that adds a touch of humor to the story.

Overall, The Maid's Diary is a gripping and thought-provoking thriller that explores the darker aspects of human nature and the secrets that lie beneath the surface of seemingly perfect lives. With its complex characters, twisty plotting, and nuanced exploration of class and privilege, it is a must-read for fans of the genre. Loreth Anne White's writing style is engaging and evocative, making this a story that will stay with you long after you finish reading it.

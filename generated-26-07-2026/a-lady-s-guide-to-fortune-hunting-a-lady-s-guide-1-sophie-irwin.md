# A Lady's Guide to Fortune-Hunting (A Lady's Guide, #1)

**Author:** Sophie Irwin  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

A Lady's Guide to Fortune-Hunting is a deliciously entertaining romp through Britain's Regency-era high society that is sure to delight fans of the Bridgerton series. Sophie Irwin's debut novel is a whip-smart, modern take on the traditional marriage plot, full of brilliant repartee and enticing wit.

At the heart of the story is Kitty Talbot, a feisty and determined heroine who is not afraid to take risks. With her family on the brink of financial ruin, Kitty sets out to secure a wealthy husband during the London season. Armed with cunning and ingenuity, she navigates the treacherous waters of high society with ease, leaving a trail of broken hearts and bruised egos in her wake.

However, Kitty's plans are soon foiled by the enigmatic Lord Radcliffe, who sees through her mercenary intentions and is determined to scotch her plans. As the two engage in a battle of wits, their verbal sparring takes on a flirtatious tone, and it becomes clear that their mutual dislike is masking a deeper attraction.

Irwin's writing style is reminiscent of Jane Austen, with its witty dialogue, strong female protagonist, and commentary on the social class system. However, she also brings a fresh, modern perspective to the genre, making the story feel both timeless and contemporary.

One of the standout features of the novel is its strong female lead. Kitty is a true original, with a sharp mind and a quick wit. She is a feminist icon in the making, refusing to be bound by the societal norms of her time. Her determination and resourcefulness make her a compelling and relatable character, and her romance with Lord Radcliffe is both sweet and satisfying.

The supporting cast of characters is equally well-drawn, with a range of colorful and eccentric personalities that add to the novel's humor and charm. From the snobbish aristocrats to the scheming matchmakers, each character is skillfully crafted to bring the world of Regency-era London to life.

Overall, A Lady's Guide to Fortune-Hunting is a delightful and engaging read that is sure to appeal to fans of historical romance and women's fiction. With its witty dialogue, strong female lead, and commentary on social class, it is a novel that will leave readers feeling entertained, educated, and eager for more.

# Big Nate: The Gerbil Ate My Homework

**Author:** Lincoln Peirce  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

Big Nate: The Gerbil Ate My Homework is the latest installment in Lincoln Peirce's beloved series, and it's a wildly entertaining ride that will leave readers of all ages in stitches. This New York Times bestselling series has been a staple of the funny papers for years, and its latest outing is just as hilarious and engaging as its predecessors.

The story follows Nate Wright, a lovable but hapless sixth-grader who's always getting into trouble. When Nate's homework goes missing, he's quick to blame it on Sherman, the class gerbil. But as the story unfolds, it becomes clear that Nate's got a lot more on his mind than just his missing homework. He's busy directing a superhero movie, inventing new names for his friends, and living up to his reputation as a prankster extraordinaire.

Peirce's signature comic-strip storytelling is on full display here, with energetic and expressive illustrations that bring Nate's world to life. The highly illustrated format is perfect for reluctant readers and fans of fast-paced graphic novels, making it an ideal choice for kids aged 8-10. But don't be fooled – this book is just as enjoyable for adults, who'll appreciate the witty humor and relatable themes of friendship, rivalry, and surviving middle school.

One of the standout aspects of Big Nate: The Gerbil Ate My Homework is its ability to balance humor with heart. Peirce has a keen eye for the absurdities of middle school life, and he's not afraid to poke fun at the challenges and absurdities that kids face every day. But he also knows when to dial it back and focus on the emotional core of the story, making for a reading experience that's both laugh-out-loud funny and surprisingly poignant.

Throughout the book, Peirce's artwork is as engaging as his writing. His characters are beautifully drawn, with expressive faces and body language that add to the humor and charm of the story. The layouts are cleverly designed, with a mix of panel sizes and shapes that keep the reader's eye moving and engaged.

Overall, Big Nate: The Gerbil Ate My Homework is a fantastic addition to the Big Nate series. It's a hilarious and engaging read that's sure to delight kids and adults alike. With its highly illustrated format, relatable themes, and laugh-out-loud humor, it's the perfect choice for fans of graphic novels and anyone looking for a fun and entertaining read.

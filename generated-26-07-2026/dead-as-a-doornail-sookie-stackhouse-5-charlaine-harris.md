# Dead as a Doornail (Sookie Stackhouse, #5)

**Author:** Charlaine Harris  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2005

Dead as a Doornail, the fifth installment in Charlaine Harris's beloved Sookie Stackhouse series, is a gripping and suspenseful ride that delves into the darker aspects of small-town life and the supernatural world that lurks within. Harris masterfully weaves together a complex web of intrigue, deception, and danger, keeping readers on the edge of their seats as they navigate the twists and turns of the plot.

At the heart of the novel is Sookie Stackhouse, the endearing and fiercely independent cocktail waitress with a penchant for getting entangled in the most bizarre and perilous situations. This time around, Sookie finds herself in the midst of a deadly mystery when a sniper begins targeting the local shapeshifting community, and her brother Jason becomes the prime suspect. As the body count rises and the full moon draws near, Sookie must use her unique telepathic abilities and quick wit to unravel the tangled threads of the investigation and clear her brother's name.

One of the standout aspects of Harris's writing style is her ability to seamlessly blend the mundane with the supernatural, creating a world that is both relatable and fantastical. The small-town Louisiana setting is vividly rendered, with its quirky cast of characters, rich history, and atmospheric charm. Harris's prose is engaging and accessible, making it easy for readers to become fully immersed in the world she has created.

The character development in Dead as a Doornail is also noteworthy, particularly in the way Harris explores the complexities of Sookie's relationships with those around her. Her bond with her brother Jason is especially poignant, as she struggles to reconcile her love and loyalty for him with her growing unease about his newfound identity as a were-panther. The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, from the enigmatic and seductive Eric Northman to the lovable but awkward Sam Merlotte.

Throughout the novel, Harris tackles a range of themes, including identity, community, and the struggles of being an outsider. Sookie's status as a telepath and her association with the supernatural world make her an outcast in many ways, and Harris explores the tensions and conflicts that arise from this position. At the same time, the novel celebrates the power of found family and the importance of loyalty and acceptance.

If you're a fan of urban fantasy, mystery, or just great storytelling in general, Dead as a Doornail is a must-read. Charlaine Harris has a proven track record of crafting compelling stories that are both entertaining and thought-provoking, and this novel is no exception. So why not sink your teeth into the world of Sookie Stackhouse and discover the magic for yourself?

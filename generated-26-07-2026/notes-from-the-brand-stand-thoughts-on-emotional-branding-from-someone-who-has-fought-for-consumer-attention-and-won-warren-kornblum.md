# Notes from the Brand Stand: Thoughts on Emotional Branding from Someone Who Has Fought for Consumer Attention and Won

**Author:** Warren Kornblum  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

In the world of branding, it's easy to get lost in a sea of superficial marketing strategies and forget the true essence of what makes a brand truly great. Warren Kornblum's latest book, Notes from the Brand Stand: Thoughts on Emotional Branding from Someone Who Has Fought for Consumer Attention and Won, is a refreshing reminder that the strongest brands are those that earn a place in our hearts, not just our wallets.

With decades of experience in building and guiding global brands, Kornblum's perspective is both personal and insightful, offering a unique blend of emotional intelligence and business acumen. Throughout the book, he shares his core truth: that the greatest brands don't just gain market share, they earn Share of Heart. This concept is not just a catchy phrase, but a profound understanding of what sets truly great brands apart from the rest.

One of the most striking aspects of Kornblum's approach is his emphasis on the importance of genuine connection and trust. In a world where consumers are increasingly skeptical of advertising and marketing, Kornblum argues that trust is earned over time by consistently showing who you are, every single day. This is not just a matter of creating a catchy slogan or logo, but of building a brand that is authentic, vulnerable, and truly invested in the lives of its customers.

Kornblum's writing style is engaging and accessible, making the book feel more like a conversation with a wise mentor than a dry business text. He shares stories from his own experiences, both successes and failures, to illustrate his points and make the concepts feel more tangible and relatable. The result is a book that is both insightful and inspiring, offering a vision of branding that is more about building relationships than just selling products.

One of the key takeaways from the book is the importance of purpose in branding. Kornblum argues that purpose only matters when people can experience it firsthand, and that a true brand becomes a part of people's lives, not just something they buy. This is a powerful message, especially in a world where consumers are increasingly looking for brands that share their values and passions.

Throughout the book, Kornblum also emphasizes the importance of consistency and authenticity in branding. He argues that trust is earned over time, and that a brand must consistently show who it is, every single day. This is a message that resonates deeply, especially in a world where consumers are increasingly skeptical of advertising and marketing.

Overall, Notes from the Brand Stand is a must-read for anyone interested in branding, marketing, or business. Kornblum's insights are both profound and practical, offering a vision of branding that is more about building relationships than just selling products. Whether you're a seasoned marketing professional or just starting out, this book is sure to inspire and challenge your thinking about what it means to build a truly great brand.

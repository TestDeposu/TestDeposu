# In the Company of Witches (Evenfall Witches B&B, #1)

**Author:** Auralee Wallace  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

**In the Company of Witches** by Auralee Wallace is a delightful and enchanting debut that breathes new life into the cozy mystery genre. This first installment of the Evenfall Witches B&B series is a captivating blend of magic, mystery, and small-town charm, sure to cast a spell on readers seeking a bewitching escape.

Wallace weaves a rich tapestry of intrigue and enchantment, centering around the Warren witches, a family of powerful sorceresses who have long protected the town of Evenfall. When a contentious guest, Constance Graves, dies suddenly at their bed and breakfast, the family's matriarch, Aunt Nora, finds herself under suspicion. Enter Brynn Warren, our intrepid protagonist, a witch of the dead who has shunned her powers since her husband's passing. Driven by love for her aunt and a desire to uncover the truth, Brynn must confront her own grief and fear to unravel the mystery.

Wallace's prose is as inviting as the fictional town of Evenfall itself, with a warm, witty narrative voice that brings Brynn and her eccentric family to life. The author masterfully balances suspense and humor, creating a story that is both engaging and entertaining. The magical system is cleverly crafted, with each witch possessing unique abilities that add depth to the plot and character development.

At its heart, **In the Company of Witches** is a story about healing, redemption, and the power of family. Brynn's journey is one of courage and self-discovery, as she learns to embrace her gifts and find solace in her loved ones. The supporting cast of aunts, cousins, and townsfolk are equally compelling, each bringing their own quirks and secrets to the table.

The mystery itself is well-constructed, with plenty of red herrings and suspects to keep readers guessing. Wallace skillfully builds tension, leading to a satisfying and surprising conclusion. Fans of cozy mysteries will appreciate the clever use of magical elements, while those who enjoy character-driven stories will find much to love in Brynn and her family.

**In the Company of Witches** is a spellbinding start to what promises to be a captivating series. Auralee Wallace has created a world that is both enchanting and relatable, filled with characters who will leave you eager for more. This book is a must-read for anyone seeking a touch of magic in their mysteries.

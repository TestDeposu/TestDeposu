# The Sun Down Motel

**Author:** Simone St. James  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

Simone St. James' latest novel, The Sun Down Motel, is a masterclass in atmospheric tension and suspenseful storytelling. This chilling tale weaves together two narratives, set thirty-five years apart, to create a complex and intriguing exploration of the darker side of human nature.

In 1982, Viv Delaney takes a job as the night clerk at the Sun Down Motel in Fell, New York, to save up for her dream move to New York City. But it's not long before she realizes that something is amiss at the motel. The guests are a mix of weary travelers and seedy characters, and Viv begins to suspect that the motel is a hub for illicit activities. As she digs deeper, she uncovers a web of secrets and lies that threaten to destroy her.

Thirty-five years later, Carly Kirk arrives in Fell, determined to uncover the truth about her aunt Viv's disappearance. Carly's search leads her to the Sun Down Motel, where she takes a job as the night clerk, just like her aunt before her. As she delves deeper into the motel's history, she begins to experience strange and terrifying occurrences that seem to be connected to her aunt's disappearance.

St. James' writing is evocative and immersive, conjuring up the eerie atmosphere of the Sun Down Motel and the decaying town of Fell. Her characters are well-developed and complex, with Viv and Carly being particularly well-crafted. Both women are strong and determined, but also vulnerable and relatable.

One of the standout aspects of The Sun Down Motel is its exploration of the theme of female trauma. Both Viv and Carly are haunted by their pasts, and the novel sheds light on the ways in which women are often silenced and marginalized. St. James handles this theme with sensitivity and nuance, creating a narrative that is both thought-provoking and deeply unsettling.

The Sun Down Motel is also a masterclass in building tension and suspense. St. James expertly weaves together the two narratives, slowly revealing clues and piecing together the mystery of Viv's disappearance. The result is a novel that is impossible to put down, with a shocking climax that will leave readers reeling.

Overall, The Sun Down Motel is a gripping and atmospheric thriller that is sure to appeal to fans of the genre. With its complex characters, intricate plot, and thought-provoking themes, it's a must-read for anyone who loves a good mystery. So, if you're looking for a novel that will keep you up all night, look no further than The Sun Down Motel.

# Her Name Is Knight (Nena Knight, #1)

**Author:** Yasmin Angoe  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2027

In "Her Name Is Knight," Yasmin Angoe masterfully weaves a complex and suspenseful narrative that delves into the intricacies of female friendships and the devastating consequences of betrayal. This gripping novel is a must-read for fans of character-driven fiction, particularly those who enjoy the works of Terry McMillan and Kennedy Ryan.

At its core, "Her Name Is Knight" is an exploration of the unbreakable bonds between four women, Farrah, Evie, Devyn, and Kalee, who have been inseparable since their college days. As they navigate their personal and professional lives, the friends face numerous challenges that test the strength of their relationships. Angoe skillfully exposes the cracks in their seemingly unshakeable bond, revealing the deep-seated secrets and lies that threaten to destroy their sisterhood.

Through the characters' experiences, Angoe sheds light on the societal pressures that women face, particularly those related to marriage, relationships, and career expectations. Farrah's struggles to maintain a perfect facade despite her husband's infidelity, Devyn's tendency to sabotage her own relationships, Kalee's fears of intimacy, and Evie's quest for independence all serve as powerful commentary on the constraints placed on women.

Angoe's writing style is engaging and accessible, making it easy to become invested in the characters' lives. Her use of multiple perspectives adds depth to the narrative, allowing readers to understand the motivations and emotions of each character. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of suspenseful moments and emotional revelations that keep the reader on the edge of their seat.

One of the most striking aspects of "Her Name Is Knight" is its thought-provoking exploration of the consequences of betrayal. As the friends' secrets are exposed, they are forced to confront the damage that their actions have caused. Angoe raises important questions about the nature of forgiveness, loyalty, and the true meaning of friendship.

In conclusion, "Her Name Is Knight" is a captivating and insightful novel that will resonate with anyone who has experienced the joys and pains of close friendships. Yasmin Angoe's masterful storytelling and well-developed characters make this book a must-read for fans of contemporary women's fiction.

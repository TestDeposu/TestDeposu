# Riley Thorn and the Dead Guy Next Door (Riley Thorn, #1)

**Author:** Lucy Score  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

In "Riley Thorn and the Dead Guy Next Door," Lucy Score masterfully weaves a tale of mystery, romance, and self-discovery, solidifying her position as a New York Times bestselling author. This captivating novel is the first installment in the Riley Thorn series and promises to enthrall readers with its unique blend of wit, charm, and intrigue.

At the heart of the story is Riley Thorn, a complex and relatable protagonist who has just gone through a divorce and is struggling to make ends meet. She shares a house with a group of elderly roommates, which adds a delightful touch of humor and warmth to the narrative. However, Riley's life is about to take a dramatic turn with the arrival of Nick Santiago, a sexy and charismatic investigator who is searching for her missing neighbor.

As the story unfolds, Riley finds herself at the center of a murder investigation, and Nick is determined to prove her innocence. As they work together to unravel the mystery, their mutual attraction grows, and they must navigate their feelings amidst the danger and uncertainty surrounding them. Score's expertise in crafting romantic relationships shines through in the sizzling chemistry between Riley and Nick, making their romance a compelling aspect of the narrative.

One of the standout features of Score's writing style is her ability to balance humor and darkness. She effortlessly shifts between witty banter and intense moments of suspense, creating a reading experience that is both entertaining and engaging. The author's use of language is vivid and immersive, making it easy to become fully invested in Riley's world and the mysteries that surround her.

Throughout the book, Score explores themes of identity, family, and the power of intuition. Riley's struggles to accept her psychic abilities and her complicated relationship with her mother, a long-time cartomancer, add depth to the narrative and raise questions about the nature of destiny and free will.

The supporting cast of characters is equally well-crafted, with each person bringing their unique personality and quirks to the story. From Riley's eccentric roommates to Nick's colleagues, the characters are multidimensional and relatable, making it easy to become attached to them.

In conclusion, "Riley Thorn and the Dead Guy Next Door" is a captivating and romantic mystery that will appeal to fans of the genre. Lucy Score's writing is engaging, witty, and suspenseful, making this book a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling story with a strong female protagonist. With its unique blend of humor, romance, and intrigue, this novel is sure to leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in the Riley Thorn series.

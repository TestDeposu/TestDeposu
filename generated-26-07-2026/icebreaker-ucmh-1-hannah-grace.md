# Icebreaker (UCMH, #1)

**Author:** Hannah Grace  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**Icebreaker: A Sparkling Debut in Sports Romance**

Hannah Grace's 'Icebreaker' is a captivating blend of passion, perseverance, and romance that sets the stage for a thrilling new series, MAPLE HILLS. This grumpy-sunshine trope, set against the icy backdrop of competitive figure skating and college hockey, is a refreshing addition to the sports romance genre.

Anastasia Allen, our driven and dedicated heroine, is a breath of fresh air. Her unwavering focus and determination to reach the Olympics are inspiring, making her a protagonist easy to root for. Nathan Hawkins, the charming and charismatic hockey captain, is the perfect complement to Anastasia's intensity. Despite his initial gruff demeanor, Nathan's genuine care for Anastasia and his unwavering support of her dreams make him an endearing love interest.

Grace's writing style is engaging and dynamic, seamlessly weaving together the high-stakes world of competitive sports with the tender blossoming of a romance. She masterfully balances the tension of Anastasia's pursuit of Olympic glory with the sweet, slow-burn romance between her and Nathan. The chemistry between the two is palpable, and their banter is both humorous and heartwarming.

The author's ability to create vivid, relatable characters and immersive settings is commendable. From the bustling college campus to the icy arenas, Grace's descriptions transport readers into the world of MAPLE HILLS, making it easy to become invested in the characters' journeys.

'Icebreaker' is more than just a romance; it's a testament to the power of perseverance, the importance of self-care, and the beauty of found family. It's a story that will leave you cheering for Anastasia and Nathan, both on and off the ice.

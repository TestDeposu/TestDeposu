# One Came Home

**Author:** Amy Timberlake  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

**One Came Home: A Tale of Tenacity and Truth in the Wisconsin Wilderness**

Amy Timberlake's "One Came Home" is a captivating tale that breathes life into the American frontier, weaving a compelling narrative around the enigmatic Georgie Burkhardt. This Newbery Honor Book and Edgar Award winner is a must-read for fans of historical fiction, mystery, and strong female protagonists.

Set in 1871 Wisconsin, the story unfolds in the quaint town of Placid, where Georgie is renowned for her marksmanship and candid tongue. When her older sister Agatha vanishes, leaving behind a body in her blue-green ball gown, Georgie refuses to accept the grim conclusion. Armed with her trusty Springfield rifle and an unyielding spirit, she embarks on a journey to uncover the truth and bring Agatha home.

Timberlake masterfully paints a vivid picture of the Wisconsin wilderness, transforming it into a character as rich and complex as Georgie herself. The author's prose is lyrical yet grounded, capturing the essence of the era without sacrificing accessibility. Each sentence is a carefully crafted arrow, loosed with precision and purpose, drawing readers deeper into Georgie's world.

At its heart, "One Came Home" is a story about truth—seeking it, speaking it, and living it. Georgie's unyielding pursuit of the facts, despite the obstacles and dangers she encounters, is a testament to her courage and integrity. Her journey is a poignant reminder that sometimes, the truth is worth fighting for, even when it's difficult to face.

Timberlake also shines a light on the devastating impact of unchecked progress and exploitation, using the passenger pigeon migration as a powerful metaphor. As Georgie's search takes her west, she witnesses the stark contrast between the untamed wilderness and the destructive forces of human greed. This environmental theme adds depth to the story, inviting readers to reflect on the consequences of our actions and the importance of preservation.

"One Came Home" is more than just a mystery or an adventure; it's a love song to the natural world and a celebration of resilience in the face of adversity. Georgie Burkhardt is a heroine to root for, her spirit unbroken even as she faces the harsh realities of the frontier. Timberlake's evocative storytelling and keen insight into the human heart make this novel a standout in the realm of middle-grade literature.

# The Body Finder (The Body Finder, #1)

**Author:** Kimberly Derting  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

The Body Finder, written by Kimberly Derting, is a captivating and dark young adult novel that combines elements of romance, mystery, and the supernatural. The story revolves around 16-year-old Violet, who harbors two secrets: her blossoming feelings for her childhood best friend Jay, and her unique ability to sense the dead, as well as the people who killed them.

Derting's writing style is engaging and suspenseful, making it easy for readers to become immersed in Violet's world. The author's use of descriptive language and vivid imagery helps to create a sense of atmosphere, drawing the reader into the small town where the story takes place. The pacing of the book is well-balanced, with a mix of action, romance, and quiet moments of introspection that keep the reader guessing and invested in the characters' lives.

One of the most compelling aspects of The Body Finder is its exploration of the main theme: the struggle to navigate the complexities of adolescence while facing extraordinary and potentially deadly circumstances. Violet's ability to sense the dead and the killers is not just a plot device; it serves as a metaphor for the challenges of growing up and finding one's place in the world. As Violet delves deeper into the mystery of the serial killer, she must also confront her own emotions and desires, making her a relatable and authentic protagonist.

The romance between Violet and Jay is sweet and tender, but it's also fraught with tension and uncertainty. Derting handles the development of their relationship with care, avoiding clichés and insta-love tropes. Instead, the author focuses on the subtle moments of connection and intimacy, making the romance feel genuine and earned.

The supernatural elements of the book are also well-integrated, adding a layer of depth and complexity to the story. Violet's ability is not just a convenient plot device; it's a nuanced and thought-provoking aspect of her character that raises questions about morality, justice, and the nature of humanity.

Throughout the book, Derting raises important themes and questions, but she doesn't provide easy answers. Instead, she leaves the reader to ponder the implications of Violet's ability and the consequences of her actions. This ambiguity makes the book feel more realistic and grounded, even as it explores the fantastical and the unknown.

Overall, The Body Finder is a gripping and thought-provoking novel that will appeal to fans of supernatural mysteries and romance. Derting's writing is engaging and suspenseful, making it easy to become invested in the characters and their world. With its unique blend of romance, mystery, and the supernatural, this book is perfect for readers who enjoy dark and scary stories with a strong and relatable protagonist.

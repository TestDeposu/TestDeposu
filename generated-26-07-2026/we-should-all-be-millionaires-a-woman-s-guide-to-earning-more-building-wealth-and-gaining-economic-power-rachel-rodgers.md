# We Should All Be Millionaires: A Woman’s Guide to Earning More, Building Wealth, and Gaining Economic Power

**Author:** Rachel Rodgers  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

**Title: "We Should All Be Millionaires": A Bold, Empowering Blueprint for Women's Financial Liberation**

Rachel Rodgers' "We Should All Be Millionaires" is not just a book; it's a clarion call for women to reclaim their economic power and rewrite their financial narratives. With her signature blend of candor, expertise, and unapologetic passion, Rodgers, a self-made millionaire and successful entrepreneur, serves up a potent cocktail of inspiration, education, and actionable steps to help women break free from the shackles of financial mediocrity and build lasting wealth.

At the heart of Rodgers' manifesto lies a radical yet simple premise: earning more money is not a selfish act but a revolutionary one that can help balance the economy and create a better world for all. She challenges the patriarchal norms and outdated financial advice that have long held women back, instead offering a fresh, empowering perspective that celebrates women's ambition and encourages them to dream big.

Rodgers' style is as engaging as it is informative. She draws from her personal journey, as well as her experiences coaching hundreds of women, to weave relatable anecdotes and practical insights throughout the book. She doesn't shy away from tough topics, such as the historical exclusion of women and people of color from wealth-building opportunities, instead using them as a springboard to inspire change.

One of the standout features of "We Should All Be Millionaires" is its focus on mindset. Rodgers understands that changing our financial reality often begins with changing our mindset about money. She guides readers through identifying and overcoming limiting beliefs, imposter syndrome, and other barriers that prevent us from claiming our power and abundance.

But Rodgers doesn't stop at mindset; she also provides concrete strategies to bring more money in and fatten your bank account. Her $10K in 10 Days Challenge, which has already transformed the lives of hundreds of women, is just one example of the tangible, results-driven advice you'll find in these pages.

"We Should All Be Millionaires" is more than just a book; it's a movement. It's a call to action for women to stop surviving and start thriving. It's a roadmap to financial liberation, drawn by a woman who has not only walked the path herself but has helped countless others do the same.

So, are you ready to fill your life with more peace, power, and joy? Are you ready to own your success and become the millionaire the world needs you to be? Then pick up a copy of "We Should All Be Millionaires". It just might change your life.

# Kiss of Midnight (Midnight Breed, #1)

**Author:** Lara Adrian  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

In the realm of paranormal romance, few authors have managed to captivate readers quite like Lara Adrian. Her Midnight Breed series, which debuted in 2007 with "Kiss of Midnight," is a masterclass in blending action, suspense, and steamy romance. This first installment sets the tone for the rest of the series, introducing readers to a richly detailed world of vampires, warriors, and forbidden love.

At its core, "Kiss of Midnight" is a story about the blurred lines between good and evil. Lucan Thorne, the protagonist, is a vampire warrior who has dedicated his existence to protecting his kind and humans alike from the rogue vampires that threaten to disrupt the fragile peace. His world is one of darkness and violence, where the strong survive and the weak are devoured. Yet, despite his tough exterior, Lucan is a complex character with a deep sense of honor and justice.

Gabrielle Maxwell, the human protagonist, is a perfect foil to Lucan's darkness. Her life is ordinary, mundane even, until the night she witnesses a murder outside a dance club and is thrust into Lucan's world. As she navigates this treacherous new reality, Gabrielle proves to be a strong and capable heroine, refusing to back down even in the face of overwhelming danger.

The chemistry between Lucan and Gabrielle is undeniable, and their romance is a slow-burning fire that simmers just below the surface. Adrian takes her time developing their relationship, allowing the tension to build until it finally reaches a boiling point. The result is a passionate and all-consuming love that is impossible to ignore.

One of the standout features of "Kiss of Midnight" is Adrian's writing style. Her prose is lush and evocative, painting a vivid picture of the world she has created. The action scenes are fast-paced and intense, while the romantic moments are tender and intimate. Adrian's use of language is masterful, drawing the reader into the world of the Midnight Breed and refusing to let go.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the supporting cast is somewhat one-dimensional. The rogue vampires, in particular, feel more like caricatures than fully fleshed-out characters. However, this is a minor quibble in what is otherwise a compelling and engaging story.

In conclusion, "Kiss of Midnight" is a must-read for fans of paranormal romance. With its blend of action, suspense, and steamy romance, it is a story that will keep you on the edge of your seat from start to finish. Lara Adrian's writing is masterful, and her world-building is second to none. If you're looking for a new series to sink your teeth into, look no further than the Midnight Breed.

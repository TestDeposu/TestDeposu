# Significance (Significance, #1)

**Author:** Shelly Crane  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

"Significance" by Shelly Crane is a captivating and enchanting tale that delves into the realm of supernatural romance, weaving a narrative that is both poignant and mesmerizing. The story follows Maggie, a 17-year-old girl whose life has been turned upside down by her mother's departure, her father's depression, and her boyfriend's abrupt abandonment. However, her fate takes a dramatic turn when she encounters Caleb at a traffic light, and their chance meeting sets off a chain reaction of events that will change her life forever.

Crane's writing style is engaging and fluid, effortlessly transporting readers into the world of her characters. Her ability to craft relatable and well-developed protagonists is impressive, and Maggie's voice is particularly authentic, conveying the emotional depth and vulnerability of a teenager struggling to cope with the challenges of her life. The author's use of vivid descriptions and sensory details effectively immerses readers in the narrative, making it easy to become fully invested in the story.

One of the most compelling aspects of "Significance" is its exploration of the theme of soulmates. The concept of finding one's perfect match is a timeless and universal notion that resonates deeply with readers. Crane's portrayal of the intense, all-consuming connection between Maggie and Caleb is both romantic and believable, making their relationship a joy to witness. The author's depiction of their bond is not limited to the physical realm; she delves into the emotional and spiritual aspects of their connection, creating a rich and nuanced exploration of love.

The supernatural elements of the story are skillfully woven into the narrative, adding a layer of complexity and intrigue to the plot. Crane's world-building is impressive, and the magical system she has created is both unique and captivating. The author's use of magical realism adds a touch of whimsy to the story, making it feel both fantastical and grounded in reality.

The pacing of the novel is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows smoothly and effortlessly. Crane's ability to craft a story that is both character-driven and plot-driven is impressive, and the tension and suspense she builds throughout the narrative keep readers engaged and invested in the story.

The supporting characters in the novel are equally well-developed, with distinct personalities and motivations that add depth to the narrative. The antagonists are particularly well-crafted, posing a credible threat to Maggie and Caleb's relationship and creating tension that propels the plot forward.

In conclusion, "Significance" is a captivating and enchanting tale that explores the theme of soulmates in a unique and compelling way. Shelly Crane's writing style is engaging and fluid, and her ability to craft relatable and well-developed characters is impressive. The supernatural elements of the story are skillfully woven into the narrative, adding a layer of complexity and intrigue to the plot. If you're a fan of supernatural romance, "Significance" is a must-read.

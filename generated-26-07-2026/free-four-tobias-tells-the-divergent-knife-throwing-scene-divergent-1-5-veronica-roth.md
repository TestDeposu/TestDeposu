# Free Four: Tobias Tells the Divergent Knife-Throwing Scene (Divergent, #1.5)

**Author:** Veronica Roth  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In the world of young adult dystopian fiction, Veronica Roth's Divergent series has left an indelible mark. With the release of "Free Four: Tobias Tells the Divergent Knife-Throwing Scene," Roth offers a unique glimpse into the mind of the enigmatic Tobias Eaton, also known as Four. This brief yet captivating narrative retells a pivotal scene from the original book, chapter 13, through the eyes of the Dauntless instructor.

At its core, "Free Four" explores the theme of perception versus reality. By delving into Tobias's thoughts and feelings, Roth masterfully humanizes a character often shrouded in mystery. As Tobias navigates his role as Tris's instructor, he finds himself drawn to her strength and vulnerability. His observations of Tris, from her "Abnegation stubbornness" to her hidden courage, showcase the depth of his character and his capacity for empathy.

Roth's writing style in "Free Four" is characteristic of her ability to craft compelling, introspective narratives. The author's use of Tobias's inner monologue provides an intimate look at his past, his relationships, and his motivations. The text is peppered with subtle details that enrich the reader's understanding of the Dauntless world and its complex characters.

One of the most striking aspects of "Free Four" is its exploration of the intricate dynamics within the Dauntless faction. The rivalry between Tobias and Eric is skillfully woven throughout the narrative, highlighting the ruthless nature of the faction's politics. This added layer of depth serves to underscore the tension and conflict that permeate the Divergent series.

The brief yet poignant glimpses into Tobias's backstory are a highlight of the narrative. His brutal initiation and the mentor who gave him his name and taught him to fight are skillfully interwoven, adding a richness to his character that was previously unknown. These details not only enhance the reader's understanding of Tobias but also serve to deepen the overall narrative of the Divergent series.

In conclusion, "Free Four: Tobias Tells the Divergent Knife-Throwing Scene" is a captivating addition to the Divergent series. Through Tobias's eyes, Roth masterfully retells a pivotal scene, offering a fresh perspective on the characters and world she created. This brief narrative is a must-read for fans of the series, as it provides a unique glimpse into the mind of one of the most beloved characters in young adult fiction.

# The Testing (The Testing, #1)

**Author:** Joelle Charbonneau  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

**Preview/Critique:**

In a world where trust is a scarce commodity, Joelle Charbonneau's "The Testing" emerges as a chilling dystopian thriller that keeps you on the edge of your seat. Set in a post-apocalyptic United Commonwealth, the story follows Malencia "Cia" Vale as she navigates the treacherous waters of The Testing, a program designed to select the brightest and most promising graduates to lead the rebuilding of society.

Charbonneau's prose is taut and unyielding, mirroring the harsh reality of Cia's world. She masterfully weaves a narrative that is as much a psychological thriller as it is a dystopian tale, delving deep into the mind of her protagonist as she grapples with the moral ambiguities and deadly games that The Testing presents. The author's style is reminiscent of Suzanne Collins' "The Hunger Games," but with a unique twist that sets it apart.

At its core, "The Testing" explores the theme of trust, or rather, the lack thereof. In a society where the government is as likely to betray you as protect you, Cia must learn to navigate a web of deceit and danger. Charbonneau expertly peels back the layers of this theme, revealing the stark reality of a world where trust is a luxury few can afford. Cia's journey is one of self-discovery, as she learns to question everything and everyone, including herself.

The Testing itself is a brutal, high-stakes exam that pushes Cia and her fellow candidates to their physical and mental limits. Charbonneau doesn't shy away from the grim reality of this process, painting a vivid picture of the horrors Cia must endure. Yet, amidst the darkness, there are glimmers of hope and humanity, embodied by the relationships Cia forms with her fellow candidates.

One of the standout aspects of "The Testing" is its pacing. Charbonneau expertly builds tension, leading the reader on a breathless journey through the twists and turns of The Testing. Just when you think you've figured it out, she pulls the rug out from under you, leaving you desperate to know what happens next.

In a genre saturated with dystopian tales, "The Testing" stands out as a fresh and compelling addition. Charbonneau's exploration of trust, her unflinching prose, and her expertly crafted plot make this a must-read for fans of the genre. If you're looking for a dystopian thriller that will keep you up at night, look no further than "The Testing."

# The Ballad of Never After (Once Upon a Broken Heart, #2)

**Author:** Stephanie Garber  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In "The Ballad of Never After", Stephanie Garber weaves a captivating tale that delves into the complexities of love, heartbreak, and the true cost of making deals with forces beyond human control. This second installment in the "Once Upon a Broken Heart" series is a masterful exploration of the darker side of happily ever after, where the lines between good and evil are blurred and the stakes are higher than ever.

Garber's writing style is as enchanting as ever, with a poetic quality that transports readers to a world of magic and mystery. Her use of language is deliberate and evocative, conjuring vivid images of the fantastical world she has created. The characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Evangeline's struggles to come to terms with her heartbreak and the consequences of her bargain with the Prince of Hearts serving as a powerful exploration of the human condition.

At its core, "The Ballad of Never After" is a story about the dangers of romanticizing love and the devastating consequences of making deals with those who do not have our best interests at heart. Garber expertly subverts traditional fairy tale tropes, presenting a world where love is not always the answer and where the pursuit of happiness can lead to ruin. The Prince of Hearts is a masterfully crafted villain, with a charismatic exterior that hides a complex and sinister interior.

One of the most striking aspects of Garber's writing is her ability to balance light and dark elements. The world of "The Ballad of Never After" is full of wonder and magic, but it is also a place of darkness and danger. This tension creates a sense of unease that propels the reader forward, eager to discover what will happen next.

Throughout the book, Garber explores themes of love, loss, and the power of stories to shape our perceptions of the world. The novel is a commentary on the way we often idealize love and relationships, and the devastating consequences that can occur when reality fails to live up to our expectations. It is also a powerful exploration of the female experience, with Evangeline serving as a strong and complex protagonist who drives the story forward with her agency and determination.

In short, "The Ballad of Never After" is a mesmerizing tale that will appeal to fans of fantasy, romance, and fairy tales. Garber's writing is enchanting, and her world-building is meticulous and immersive. This is a book that will keep you up late at night, turning pages and wondering what will happen next. With its complex characters, moral ambiguity, and epic scope, "The Ballad of Never After" is a must-read for anyone looking for a story that will stay with them long after the final page has been turned.

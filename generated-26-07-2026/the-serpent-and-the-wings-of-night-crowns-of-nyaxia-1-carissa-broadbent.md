# The Serpent and the Wings of Night (Crowns of Nyaxia, #1)

**Author:** Carissa Broadbent  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

**Title: A Dance of Fangs and Fate in "The Serpent and the Wings of Night"**

Carissa Broadbent's debut novel, "The Serpent and the Wings of Night," is a captivating blend of dark fantasy, political intrigue, and forbidden love, set against the backdrop of a brutal, unforgiving world. The first installment in the "Crowns of Nyaxia" series, it introduces us to a realm where humans and vampires coexist in a delicate, precarious balance, and where survival is the ultimate prize.

At its heart, "The Serpent and the Wings of Night" explores the complexities of identity, loyalty, and the lengths one must go to protect both. Oraya, our protagonist, is a human raised as a vampire's daughter, navigating a world that sees her as neither fully human nor vampire. Her struggle for acceptance and survival is a poignant reflection of the outsider's journey, a theme that resonates deeply in our own diverse societies.

Broadbent's prose is as sharp as the fangs of her vampire characters, slicing through the narrative with precision and grace. She paints vivid, often brutal, portraits of her characters, each with their own unique voice and motivations. The world of Nyaxia is richly imagined, with a history and lore that feels as tangible as the pages it's printed on. Broadbent's ability to weave intricate political machinations with high-stakes action sequences is a testament to her skill as a storyteller.

The central conflict of "The Serpent and the Wings of Night" is the Kejari, a deadly tournament orchestrated by the goddess of death herself. As Oraya competes against the most formidable warriors of the three vampire houses, she finds an unlikely ally in Raihn, a ruthless vampire with a mysterious past. Their alliance, born out of necessity, blossoms into a complex, slow-burning romance that is as captivating as it is dangerous.

Broadbent doesn't shy away from exploring the darker aspects of human nature, nor does she romanticize the violence that permeates her world. Instead, she uses it to highlight the stark contrasts between her characters, their motivations, and their struggles. The love story at the heart of "The Serpent and the Wings of Night" is as much about the power dynamics between Oraya and Raihn as it is about their growing affection for each other.

In a genre often saturated with clichés, Broadbent's work stands out for its originality and depth. She subverts expectations, challenges stereotypes, and delivers a story that is as thought-provoking as it is entertaining. "The Serpent and the Wings of Night" is a promising start to what promises to be an exciting new series.

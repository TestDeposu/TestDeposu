# Enna Burning (The Books of Bayern, #2)

**Author:** Shannon Hale  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

Enna Burning, the second installment in Shannon Hale's captivating Books of Bayern series, is a mesmerizing tale of self-discovery, power, and the delicate balance between destruction and creation. Set against the backdrop of war-torn Bayern, this young adult fantasy novel masterfully weaves together themes of identity, loyalty, and the true nature of power.

At the heart of the story lies Enna, a strong-willed and determined heroine who finds herself torn between her desire for independence and her need for connection. As her brother Leifer discovers the secret to creating fire without a spark, Enna is faced with a choice: to embrace this extraordinary power or to reject it. Hale's portrayal of Enna's inner turmoil is both authentic and relatable, making it easy for readers to become invested in her journey.

One of the most striking aspects of Enna Burning is its exploration of the dual nature of power. Fire, in this context, serves as a potent symbol of both creation and destruction. Hale skillfully demonstrates how the same force that can bring warmth and light can also reduce everything to ashes. This dichotomy is mirrored in Enna's own struggles, as she grapples with the moral implications of wielding such power.

Hale's writing style is, as always, engaging and accessible. Her prose is infused with a sense of wonder and magic, making the world of Bayern feel richly detailed and immersive. The characters, too, are well-crafted and complex, with Enna being a particularly standout example. Her growth and development throughout the novel are both satisfying and believable, making her a compelling protagonist.

The themes of identity and loyalty are also expertly woven throughout the narrative. Enna's relationships with those around her – her brother, her friends, and her community – serve as a catalyst for her growth and self-discovery. Hale raises important questions about the nature of loyalty and duty, encouraging readers to think critically about the consequences of their actions.

If you're a fan of fantasy, adventure, or simply great storytelling, Enna Burning is an absolute must-read. With its unique magic system, compelling characters, and thought-provoking themes, this novel is sure to captivate readers of all ages. While it can be read as a standalone, it's undoubtedly enhanced by its connection to the larger Books of Bayern series.

In short, Enna Burning is a gripping and emotionally resonant novel that will leave you breathless and eager for more. Shannon Hale's masterful storytelling and well-crafted characters make this a truly unforgettable read.

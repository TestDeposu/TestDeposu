# Fallen Too Far (Rosemary Beach, #1; Too Far, #1)

**Author:** Abbi Glines  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

In "Fallen Too Far," Abbi Glines crafts a captivating new adult romance that whisks readers away to the sun-kissed shores of Rosemary Beach, Florida. This tantalizing tale of forbidden love and hidden secrets is a must-read for fans of the genre.

At its core, "Fallen Too Far" is a story about two individuals from vastly different worlds, forced to navigate their feelings for each other amidst the complexities of their newfound family dynamics. Blaire Wynn, a tough and resilient farm girl from Alabama, is thrust into the opulent world of Rosemary Beach, where she must contend with her new stepbrother, Rush Finlay – the cynical and gorgeous son of a rock star. As the summer unfolds, their initial animosity gradually gives way to a fierce attraction, leaving Blaire torn between her desire for Rush and the danger of falling too far.

Glines' writing style is characterized by its addictive blend of wit, charm, and sensuality. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Blaire's sharp tongue and Rush's bad-boy charisma making them a compelling and believable pair. The author's vivid descriptions of Rosemary Beach's picturesque landscapes and the luxurious lifestyle of its elite residents only add to the novel's allure.

One of the standout aspects of "Fallen Too Far" is its exploration of the human condition. Glines masterfully weaves together themes of grief, identity, and the struggle to find one's place in the world. Blaire's painful past and her difficulty in adjusting to her new life serve as a poignant reminder of the challenges we all face in our own lives. Similarly, Rush's struggles with his family's legacy and his own sense of purpose add depth to his character, making him more than just a pretty face.

The chemistry between Blaire and Rush is undeniable, and their romance is expertly paced, with a perfect balance of tension, humor, and passion. However, it's the secrets and lies that threaten to destroy their relationship that keep readers on the edge of their seats. Glines skillfully ramps up the suspense, making it impossible to put the book down as the truth begins to unravel.

In conclusion, "Fallen Too Far" is a captivating new adult romance that will appeal to fans of Colleen Hoover, Jamie McGuire, and Sylvia Day. With its relatable characters, engaging storyline, and swoon-worthy romance, this novel is sure to leave readers eager for more. Abbi Glines' writing style is engaging, witty, and seductive, making this book a must-read for anyone who enjoys a good love story.

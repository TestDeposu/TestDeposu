# Glow of the Everflame (Kindred's Curse, #2)

**Author:** Penn Cole  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

Penn Cole returns with Glow of the Everflame, the highly anticipated second installment in the rapidly expanding Kindred’s Curse Saga, and she proves that the sophomore slump is a myth. Building brilliantly upon the foundation laid in the first novel, Cole delivers a masterclass in romantic fantasy pacing, political intrigue, and emotional escalation. This is a story that refuses to let go, dragging both its fierce protagonist and its readers deeper into a world where every breath is calculated and every heartbeat is a liability.

At the core of the novel lies a powerful, driving theme: the dangerous gray area between absolute righteousness and systemic corruption. Diem Bellator, our fiercely resilient heroine, is forced to step out of the simplistic worldview of her upbringing and into the gilded, treacherous halls of the House Corbois elite. Cole brilliantly deconstructs the trope of good versus evil. By placing Diem directly among the ruling class she has been conditioned to hate, the narrative exposes the rot beneath the pristine surface of the Descended society. The thematic brilliance of Glow of the Everflame rests on the painful deconstruction of prejudice. Diem is constantly challenged to look past crowns and bloodlines to see the individuals beneath, learning that oppression rarely wears a simple villain's mask—it often wears a charming smile and speaks of duty.

Cole’s writing style is tailor-made for fans of immersive, high-stakes romantic fantasy. She has a distinct knack for atmospheric world-building that feels tactile and alive, contrasting the gritty, desperate reality of the mortals with the intoxicating, dangerous magic of the demigods. Her prose is sharp, fast-paced, and laced with a biting wit that keeps the heavy political maneuvering from ever feeling bogged down. 

Where Cole truly shines, however, is in her mastery of emotional torture and relational dynamics. Glow of the Everflame leans heavily into the beloved slow-burn and enemies-to-lovers tropes, executing them with surgical precision. The tension between Diem and her brooding, shadow-laden love interest crackles off the page, driven by sharp banter, reluctant alliances, and a profound, underlying respect that refuses to be ignored. Cole understands that the best romantic tension comes not just from physical attraction, but from ideological clashes and mutual vulnerability. Every lingering glance and suppressed emotion feels earned, amplifying the stakes when the line between friend and foe inevitably begins to blur.

Beyond the romance, Cole weaves a complex web of mystery. With her mother still missing, the lingering demands of the enigmatic Wardens, and a ticking clock of thirty days to survive a high-stakes political bargain, the plot moves at a relentless pace. Diem’s internal journey is just as compelling as the external threats she faces; she is forced to confront her own identity, her burgeoning powers, and the terrifying realization that the most dangerous battle she will fight isn't against the looming war, but for the preservation of her own heart.

Glow of the Everflame is a triumphant continuation of a saga that has captured the imaginations of fantasy readers worldwide. Penn Cole has crafted an intoxicating blend of danger, discovery, and undeniable chemistry that cements her status as a powerhouse in the romantic fantasy genre. It is provocative, deeply romantic, and utterly unputdownable.

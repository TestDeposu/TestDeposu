# When Darkness Comes (Guardians of Eternity, #1)

**Author:** Alexandra Ivy  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

Step into the deliciously dangerous shadows with When Darkness Comes, the explosive series debut by New York Times bestselling author Alexandra Ivy. Released in 2013, this novel kicks off the Guardians of Eternity series by plunging readers headfirst into a high-stakes world where ancient mythologies collide with modern urban grit. Ivy wastes no time establishing a breakneck pace, immediately subverting the mundane life of her protagonist and dragging her into a supernatural war that is as captivating as it is perilous. 

At the heart of the novel is a brilliant exploration of destiny versus agency, woven seamlessly into the classic paranormal romance framework. The central theme revolves around the burden of unexpected power and the sacrifices required to stand against encroaching oblivion. Abby Barlow begins her journey as an ordinary woman whose only concern is a broken antique vase, but a sudden explosion and the death of her employer thrust her into the terrifying role of The Chalice—a mortal vessel chosen to contain and hold back an ancient darkness threatening to consume the world. Ivy uses this transformation to examine how ordinary individuals rise to extraordinary challenges. Abby is not a trained warrior or a creature of the night; her strength lies in her resilience, vulnerability, and sheer grit. 

Paired with her is Dante, a centuries-old vampire demon whose very existence has been dedicated to protecting the Chalice. Dante embodies the dual nature of Ivy's supernatural world: he is simultaneously a lethal protector and a tempest of irresistible desire. The dynamic between Abby and Dante drives the emotional core of the book. Ivy expertly plays with the tension of enforced proximity and high-stakes protection. What makes their connection compelling is the emotional pivot Dante must make—shifting from a predator who might have easily seduced her under normal circumstances to an unwavering guardian sworn to keep her alive at all costs. This restraint heightens the romantic tension, making every stolen glance and charged interaction burn with intensity.

Alexandra Ivy’s writing style is punchy, sensual, and relentlessly fast-paced. She has a distinct knack for blending visceral action sequences with smoldering romantic chemistry without letting one overshadow the other. The Chicago setting provides a gritty, atmospheric backdrop that contrasts sharply with the ethereal, immortal nature of the threats hunting Abby. Ivy’s prose is accessible and immersive, favoring sharp dialogue and vivid sensory details that bring her nocturnal world to life. She doesn't bog the narrative down with exhaustive lore dumps; instead, she lets the danger and the character interactions organically reveal the vast, complex mythology of the Guardians of Eternity.

For fans of paranormal romance who crave high-octane danger, alpha heroes with a vulnerable core, and heroines who discover a fierce inner strength when the world falls apart, When Darkness Comes is an absolute must-read. Alexandra Ivy sets a high bar for the genre, delivering a breathless adventure where love is the ultimate weapon against the dark.

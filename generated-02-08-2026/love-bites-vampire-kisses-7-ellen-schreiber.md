# Love Bites (Vampire Kisses, #7)

**Author:** Ellen Schreiber  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Love Bites, the seventh installment of Ellen Schreiber’s Vampire Kisses saga, thrusts readers back into the moon‑lit world of Raven and her enigmatic vampire boyfriend, Alexander. The novel’s core theme—love’s fragile balance between mortality and eternity—plays out with a blend of teenage yearning and gothic intrigue that has become the series’ signature. Schreiber treats the vampire myth not as a backdrop for horror but as a metaphor for the all‑consuming, often dangerous intensity of first love. Raven’s apprehension about whether Alexander will ever turn her into an immortal mirrors every adolescent’s fear of losing oneself in a relationship, while Sebastian’s arrival adds a classic love‑triangle tension that tests loyalty, trust, and the boundaries of desire.

Schreiber’s prose remains breezy and accessible, peppered with snappy dialogue that captures the chip‑on‑the‑shoulder attitude of small‑town teens. She moves quickly from scene to scene, favoring brisk pacing over lingering description, which keeps the narrative’s pulse racing—especially during the climactic vampire party where secrets surface amid glittering chandeliers and whispered threats. The author’s strength lies in her ability to juxtapose the ordinary (Raven’s high‑school woes, her best‑friend banter) with the extraordinary (century‑old vampiric politics, the ever‑looming threat of a bite), making the fantastical feel intimately personal.

While the plot occasionally leans on familiar tropes— the jealous best friend, the looming threat of a rival mortal—Schreiber injects fresh energy through Sebastian’s conflicted affections and the looming question of whether love can truly survive when one partner is literally undead. The novel’s emotional core stays grounded in Raven’s voice: earnest, witty, and unafraid to voice her doubts. Readers who have followed the series will appreciate the continuity of character growth, while newcomers will find a self‑contained romance that still hints at the larger mythos.

Ultimately, Love Bites is a satisfying, quick‑read escapade that captures the bittersweet thrill of loving someone who lives outside the rules of time. It reminds us that the most immortal bonds are forged not by a bite, but by the courage to stay present, even when eternity beckons.

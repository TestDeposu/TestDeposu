# Rock Hard (Sinners on Tour, #2)

**Author:** Olivia Cunning  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

Rock Hard by Olivia Cunning is a captivating addition to the Sinners on Tour series, focusing on the complexities of love and relationships within the high-energy world of rock music. This review, however, will focus on the wedding-themed novellas, Sinners at the Altar, which includes the epilogue to Rock Hard.

One of the primary themes of Rock Hard and its companion novellas is the exploration of love and commitment within the context of a demanding profession. Olivia Cunning masterfully weaves together the stories of multiple band members, delving into their personal struggles and triumphs as they navigate the challenges of fame, relationships, and ultimately, marriage.

Throughout the novellas, Cunning's writing style shines with her ability to craft relatable, well-developed characters. Each member of the Sinners band is uniquely portrayed, showcasing their distinct personalities, strengths, and weaknesses. The author's use of vivid descriptions and engaging dialogue brings the characters to life, making it easy for readers to become invested in their stories.

The epilogue to Rock Hard, titled Patience, is a standout example of Cunning's skillful storytelling. The tale of Sed Lionheart and Jessica Chase's wedding day is both poignant and humorous, highlighting the stresses and joys that come with planning a high-profile wedding. Cunning expertly balances the lighthearted moments with deeper explorations of the couple's relationship, creating a narrative that is both entertaining and emotionally resonant.

One of the strengths of Cunning's writing is her ability to tackle complex themes and emotions with sensitivity and nuance. In the novellas, she explores topics such as trust, communication, and the challenges of maintaining a relationship in the public eye. The author's portrayal of the Sinners band members' experiences is raw, honest, and relatable, making their stories feel authentic and engaging.

The Sinners on Tour series, including Rock Hard and the companion novellas, is a must-read for fans of contemporary romance and rock music. Olivia Cunning's writing is engaging, witty, and emotionally resonant, making her books impossible to put down. With their focus on love, relationships, and the highs and lows of fame, these stories are sure to captivate readers and leave a lasting impression.

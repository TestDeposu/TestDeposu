# Law Man (Dream Man, #3)

**Author:** Kristen Ashley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**Love, Suspense, and the Unlikely Spark: A Review of *Law Man* by Kristen Ashley**

Kristen Ashley’s *Law Man*—the third installment in the *Dream Man* series—is a masterclass in blending steamy romance with gripping suspense, all wrapped in the kind of emotional depth that makes her books impossible to put down. At its heart, this story is about two people who’ve spent years watching each other from afar, only to realize too late that love isn’t just about waiting—it’s about taking a chance when the moment arrives.

Mara Hanover is the kind of heroine readers adore: quiet, resilient, and burdened by a past she’s tried to outrun. A woman from the "wrong side of the tracks," she’s spent years observing Mitch Lawson, the charming and disciplined police detective next door, from a distance. Her admiration is mutual—Mitch has long harbored feelings for her, but their worlds have kept them apart. Until the day a simple leaky faucet becomes the catalyst for something far more explosive. Ashley’s writing shines in the way she crafts these small, intimate moments—Mitch’s unexpected kindness, the way Mara’s walls begin to crumble—not as melodrama, but as a natural progression of two people finally seeing each other for who they truly are.

What makes *Law Man* stand out is Ashley’s ability to weave romance with suspense without sacrificing either. The emotional stakes are high when Mara receives a chilling phone call from her cousin’s children, pulling her back into a life she’s tried to escape. Here, Ashley doesn’t just rely on action; she dives into the psychological tension, making the reader feel Mara’s fear and Mitch’s determination to protect her. The chemistry between the leads is electric, but it’s not just about passion—it’s about trust, about Mitch helping Mara confront her demons, and about her learning to believe in a future where love isn’t just a dream but a reality.

Ashley’s signature style is evident here: her prose is lush but never overly descriptive, her dialogue crackles with authenticity, and her characters feel deeply human. Mitch is the quintessential hero—tough on the outside, tender on the inside, with a moral compass that never wavers. Mara, meanwhile, is flawed and flawed in the best way; her growth isn’t instant, but it’s earned, making her journey all the more satisfying. The supporting characters, too, add depth—Mitch’s colleagues, Mara’s family, even the minor figures who play a role in her past—all contribute to the rich tapestry of the story.

The pacing is flawless, balancing slow-burn tension with moments of heart-pounding urgency. Ashley knows how to make a reader ache for her characters, how to make them laugh, and how to deliver a payoff that feels earned. By the time the final pages unfold, it’s clear that love, in all its messy, beautiful complexity, is the true hero of this story.

*Law Man* is more than just a romance—it’s a story about second chances, about the courage to let go of the past, and about the quiet strength it takes to build a future with someone who sees you for who you are. Kristen Ashley delivers once again, proving that her *Dream Man* series is a must-read for fans of passionate, suspenseful love stories where the heart beats just as hard as the plot.

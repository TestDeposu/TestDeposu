# Enticed (The Violet Eden Chapters, #2)

**Author:** Jessica Shirvington  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

Angels, demons, and the dizzying, high-stakes agony of teenage romance collide in *Enticed*, the second installment of Jessica Shirvington’s pulse-pounding paranormal series, *The Violet Eden Chapters*. Following the explosive revelations of her seventeenth birthday—where she discovered she is actually Grigori, part angel and part human destined to battle exiled forces—Violet Eden finds herself thrust deeper into a supernatural war. Shirvington masterfully captures the essence of the YA paranormal boom of the 2010s, reminiscent of the atmospheric tension found in *Twilight* and *Fallen*, while carving out a distinct, action-oriented identity of her own.

At the heart of *Enticed* is the timeless, universally resonant theme of choice versus destiny. Violet is no longer just a teenager worrying about art school and fitting in; she is a crucial player in an ancient cosmic struggle between light and dark. Shirvington uses this supernatural framework as a brilliant metaphor for the overwhelming transition into adulthood. Every decision Violet makes carries apocalyptic weight, mirroring the very real, often terrifying realization that the choices we make in our youth shape the entirety of our futures. The author poses a compelling question throughout the narrative: When your path is seemingly laid out by higher powers, how much of your soul are you willing to sacrifice to forge your own way?

Stylistically, Shirvington thrives on emotional intensity and relentless pacing. The prose is snappy, highly visual, and designed to keep pages turning well into the night. Where the book truly shines is in its handling of the classic romantic triangle—a narrative device often prone to cliché, yet handled here with palpable friction and genuine stakes. The dynamic between Violet, the brooding and secretive Lincoln, and the magnetic, unpredictable Phoenix creates a constant undercurrent of suspense. Shirvington doesn't just rely on physical attraction; she builds emotional landmines that threaten to blow up every alliance Violet has painstakingly built. 

The author’s world-building expands organically in this second volume, pushing the mythology of the Grigori beyond the introductory phase and plunging the characters into morally grey territories. Nothing is purely black and white, and the lines between savior and sinner frequently blur. This nuance elevates *Enticed* above standard genre fare, offering readers a gritty, emotionally resonant journey. 

For fans of angsty romance, fierce heroines, and high-stakes angelic warfare, *Enticed* delivers a fast-paced, addictive reading experience. Jessica Shirvington proves that the battle for the human soul is just as compelling as the battle for a young girl's heart.

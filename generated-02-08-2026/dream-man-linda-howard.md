# Dream Man

**Author:** Linda Howard  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Master of romantic suspense Linda Howard strikes again with Dream Man, a riveting 2009 reissue that masterfully bridges the gap between pulse-pounding police procedural and high-stakes psychological romance. In this compelling novel, Howard explores her favorite thematic territory: the friction between vulnerability and strength, and the undeniable magnetism that arises when two fundamentally different worlds collide.

At the heart of the story is Marlie Keen, a woman haunted—literally—by a gift she never wanted. Her clairvoyance is not a mystical parlor trick, but a terrifying, intrusive burden that forces her to witness brutal crimes in real-time. Howard uses Marlie’s psychic visions to anchor the narrative's central theme: the loss of control and the desperate fight to reclaim one's life. When Marlie’s visions violently return, threatening to shatter the fragile, ordinary existence she has carefully constructed, she is forced to step out of the shadows and seek help. 

Enter Detective Dane Hollister of the Orlando Police Department. Dane is the quintessential Howard hero—pragmatic, grounded, fiercely protective, and deeply skeptical of the supernatural. The collision between Marlie’s unquantifiable intuition and Dane’s hard-boiled police logic forms the intellectual and emotional backbone of the book. Howard deftly avoids the trap of making her protagonist a damsel in distress; instead, Marlie’s quiet resilience matches Dane’s steadfast determination, creating an equilibrium that makes their eventual dynamic feel earned and intensely satisfying.

Howard’s literary style in Dream Man is defined by its relentless pacing and sharp, economical prose. She wastes no time on superfluous world-building, preferring to let the tension build naturally through dialogue and psychological pressure. The romantic chemistry between Dane and Marlie sizzles from their very first encounter, charged with a palpable sexual tension that never overshadows the central murder investigation. Instead, the danger amplifies the passion, weaving the romance and the suspense into a single, cohesive narrative thread. 

As the plot tightens, Howard introduces a ruthless, shadowy antagonist whose twisted mind threatens to permanently silence Marlie. This cat-and-mouse game keeps the pages turning, driven by a mounting sense of claustrophobia and impending doom. Yet, even in the darkest moments of the pursuit, Howard reminds the reader that human connection is the ultimate shield against terror.

Dream Man is a masterclass in genre-blending. It delivers the heart-stopping adrenaline of a thriller alongside the emotional catharsis of a deeply satisfying romance. Linda Howard proves once more why she is a titan of romantic suspense, offering a timeless read that will captivate both longtime fans and newcomers alike.

# Time Between Us (Time Between Us, #1)

**Author:** Tamara Ireland Stone  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

Time Between Us, written by Tamara Ireland Stone, is a captivating novel that delves into the complexities of time, love, and the consequences of fate. The story follows Anna, a teenager living in 1995 Chicago, and Bennett, a young man from 2012 San Francisco, who find themselves inexplicably connected despite the vast distance and time gap between them. Bennett's unique ability to travel through time and space allows him to enter Anna's life, and as their relationship deepens, they must confront the reality that time may ultimately tear them apart.

Tamara Ireland Stone's writing style is characterized by her ability to craft relatable and well-developed characters, making it easy for readers to become invested in their stories. In Time Between Us, Stone masterfully explores the theme of love transcending time and space, raising questions about the nature of fate and the consequences of altering the timeline. The author's use of vivid descriptions and engaging dialogue brings the characters and their world to life, making the reader feel like they are experiencing the story alongside Anna and Bennett.

One of the most striking aspects of Time Between Us is its thought-provoking exploration of the consequences of playing with time. As Bennett and Anna navigate their relationship, they must consider the potential repercussions of their actions on the timeline and the people around them. This theme is expertly woven throughout the narrative, adding a layer of tension and suspense to the story. Stone's handling of this complex concept is impressive, making the reader ponder the what-ifs and maybes of time travel.

The characters in Time Between Us are multidimensional and relatable, with Anna and Bennett being particularly well-developed. Their relationship is authentic and sweet, making it easy to become invested in their story. The supporting characters, although not as fleshed out, add depth to the narrative and help to drive the plot forward. Stone's portrayal of the characters' emotions and struggles is realistic and empathetic, making the reader feel a strong connection to them.

Tamara Ireland Stone's writing is also notable for its accessibility and ease of reading. The author's use of clear and concise language makes the story easy to follow, even when dealing with complex concepts like time travel. The pacing of the narrative is well-balanced, with a mix of action, romance, and introspection that keeps the reader engaged.

In conclusion, Time Between Us is a captivating and thought-provoking novel that explores the complexities of time, love, and fate. Tamara Ireland Stone's writing style is engaging and accessible, making the story easy to become invested in. The characters are well-developed and relatable, and the themes of the novel are expertly woven throughout the narrative. If you're looking for a romantic and suspenseful story that will keep you on the edge of your seat, Time Between Us is an excellent choice.

# Little Liar (The Web of Silence Duet, #2)

**Author:** Leigh Rivers  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

"Little Liar" by Leigh Rivers is a masterfully crafted, dark, and twisted tale of obsession, power, and the unrelenting pursuit of the one you desire. This second installment of The Web of Silence Duet delves deeper into the complex and troubled world of Malachi Vize, a man consumed by his all-encompassing passion for his adopted sister, Olivia.

Rivers' writing style is both captivating and unsettling, weaving a web of intricate relationships and morally ambiguous characters that will leave readers questioning their own values and loyalties. The author's use of language is evocative and visceral, painting a vivid picture of a world where the lines between love, obsession, and possession are constantly blurred.

At the heart of the story lies Malachi's unwavering dedication to Olivia, a fixation that has defined his existence for as long as he can remember. When she finally chooses him over the world, he is determined to keep her by his side, no matter the cost. However, their happiness is short-lived, as powerful enemies emerge, threatening to tear them apart.

As Malachi navigates this treacherous landscape, he is forced to form uneasy alliances and confront his own demons in a desperate bid to reclaim Olivia. Rivers' portrayal of Malachi's turmoil is both haunting and thought-provoking, raising important questions about the nature of love, control, and the human psyche.

Throughout the book, Rivers tackles mature themes with unflinching honesty, including trauma, abuse, and the long-lasting effects of psychological manipulation. The author's handling of these sensitive topics is both respectful and unapologetic, making for a reading experience that is as uncomfortable as it is compelling.

The world-building in "Little Liar" is equally impressive, with a complex network of characters and motivations that add depth and richness to the narrative. Rivers' characters are multidimensional and relatable, with even the most antagonistic figures eliciting a degree of sympathy and understanding.

In conclusion, "Little Liar" is a gripping and intense read that will appeal to fans of dark romance and psychological thrillers. Rivers' writing is masterful, and her ability to craft a narrative that is both disturbing and thought-provoking is a testament to her skill as a storyteller. If you're looking for a book that will challenge your emotions and push you out of your comfort zone, then "Little Liar" is an absolute must-read.

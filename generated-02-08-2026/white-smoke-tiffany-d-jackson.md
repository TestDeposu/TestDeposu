# White Smoke

**Author:** Tiffany D. Jackson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

White Smoke, the latest offering from New York Times bestselling author Tiffany D. Jackson, is a masterclass in psychological thrillers that weaves together elements of horror, mystery, and social commentary to create a truly unforgettable reading experience. This modern take on the classic haunted house story is a gripping and unflinching exploration of the darkest corners of the human psyche, and it's a must-read for fans of the genre.

At its core, White Smoke is a story about the power of secrets and the devastating consequences of keeping them buried. Marigold, the protagonist, is a complex and deeply flawed character who is struggling to come to terms with the traumas of her past. Her decision to start anew in Cedarville, a troubled Midwestern city, is motivated by a desire to escape the ghosts that haunt her, but she soon discovers that the past is inescapable.

As Mari navigates her new life in Cedarville, she begins to experience strange and terrifying occurrences in her new home on Maple Street. Doors open and close on their own, disembodied voices whisper in the walls, and an eerie presence seems to be watching her every move. But as the events escalate, Mari starts to suspect that the true horror may not be the supernatural forces that are haunting her, but the dark secrets that her family and the community are hiding.

One of the standout features of White Smoke is its thoughtful exploration of themes such as gentrification, racism, and classism. Jackson's portrayal of Cedarville, a city ravaged by poverty and neglect, is both nuanced and unflinching, and she sheds light on the ways in which systemic injustices can have a profound impact on individuals and communities.

Jackson's writing style is equally impressive, with a keen attention to detail and a mastery of building tension and suspense. Her prose is evocative and immersive, drawing the reader into the world of the story and refusing to let go. The pacing is expertly handled, with a slow-burning sense of unease that gradually builds into a heart-pounding climax.

What sets White Smoke apart from other horror stories is its thoughtful exploration of the psychological and emotional toll of trauma. Mari's struggles to cope with her past are deeply relatable, and Jackson's portrayal of her inner turmoil is both authentic and compassionate. The supporting characters are equally well-developed, with a cast of complex and multifaceted individuals who add depth and richness to the narrative.

In short, White Smoke is a triumph of modern horror writing. It's a story that will keep you up all night, not just because of its terrifying plot twists, but because of its thought-provoking exploration of the human condition. With its diverse cast of characters, its nuanced exploration of social justice issues, and its masterful handling of tension and suspense, White Smoke is a must-read for anyone who loves horror, mystery, or just great storytelling in general.

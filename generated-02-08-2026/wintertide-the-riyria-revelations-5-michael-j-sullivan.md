# Wintertide (The Riyria Revelations, #5)

**Author:** Michael J. Sullivan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**Wintertide: A Glimpse of the Bonds That Built an Empire**

Michael J. Sullivan’s *Wintertide* isn’t just a prequel—it’s a masterclass in how to weave the threads of destiny into the fabric of friendship. Set eleven years before the events of *The Riyria Revelations*, this novella captures the raw, unpolished essence of Royce Melborn and Hadrian Blackwater, two men who would later become legends. Here, they’re still learning to trust each other, still proving that loyalty isn’t born from grand gestures but from the quiet, stubborn refusal to let the other fall.

The story unfolds like a winter storm—cold, relentless, and beautiful in its inevitability. Royce, the cynical thief with a heart of iron, has one mission: teach Hadrian, the idealistic swordsman, that kindness is a liability. But when their path crosses with a desperate noblewoman fleeing a vengeful warlord, their lesson takes an unexpected turn. What follows isn’t just a chase or a fight—it’s a test of character. Hadrian’s faith in justice clashes with Royce’s hard-won pragmatism, yet even in their friction, there’s a spark of something deeper. By the end, neither man will ever be the same.

Sullivan’s prose is sharp as a blade, effortlessly balancing wit and weight. His dialogue crackles with chemistry, each exchange revealing layers of personality without a single unnecessary word. Royce’s sarcasm and Hadrian’s earnestness create a dynamic that feels both timeless and fresh, like a well-worn sword polished to a gleam. The world-building, though concise, is rich with texture—every detail, from the snow-laden streets of Riyria to the looming threat of war, immerses the reader in a place that feels lived-in, not just imagined.

What makes *Wintertide* special isn’t just its role as a prequel, but how it distills the soul of the *Riyria Revelations* series. Here, we see the roots of their partnership: the moments of doubt, the reluctant respect, the unspoken understanding that they’ll always have each other’s backs. It’s a story about growth—not just in skill, but in heart. Royce learns that some debts can’t be repaid with coin alone. Hadrian discovers that not every battle is won with a sword. Together, they prove that even the smallest acts of defiance can change the course of a life.

For fans of the series, this novella is a treasure—a chance to see the world through the eyes of two men before they became kings. For newcomers, it’s a perfect introduction: a self-contained adventure that delivers all the swashbuckling excitement, emotional depth, and clever storytelling that define Sullivan’s work. It’s fantasy at its finest: a tale of friendship, survival, and the quiet revolutions that happen when two people refuse to give up on each other.

If you’ve ever wondered how Royce and Hadrian’s bond was forged, *Wintertide* answers that question with the precision of a well-aimed arrow. It’s a reminder that sometimes, the greatest victories aren’t won on the battlefield, but in the moments between them.

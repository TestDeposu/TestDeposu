# Married by Morning (The Hathaways, #4)

**Author:** Lisa Kleypas  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Married by Morning, the fourth installment in Lisa Kleypas' beloved Hathaway series, is a masterful blend of wit, charm, and sensuality. With her trademark effortless style, Kleypas weaves a captivating tale of love, secrets, and self-discovery that will leave readers breathless.

At the heart of the story are Catherine Marks, a sharp-witted and sharp-tongued heroine, and Leo Hathaway, a determined hero with a few secrets of his own. Their mutual attraction, masked by constant arguing, is expertly crafted by Kleypas, who skillfully ramps up the tension and desire between the two leads. As their relationship deepens, the stakes grow higher, threatening to destroy the very foundations of their lives.

One of the standout aspects of Kleypas' writing is her ability to craft complex, multidimensional characters. Catherine, with her respectable demeanor and hidden secrets, is a fascinating and relatable protagonist. Her struggles to outrun her past and find her place in the world are deeply human, making her an endearing and sympathetic character. Leo, too, is a masterfully drawn hero, with a rich backstory and a deep sense of vulnerability.

Kleypas' writing is, as always, superb. Her prose is witty and engaging, with a keen sense of humor that adds a lighthearted touch to the narrative. The dialogue is sharp and natural, with a keen sense of period authenticity that immerses the reader in the world of 19th-century England. The romance is, of course, a highlight of the book, with Kleypas expertly crafting a slow-burn that will leave readers eagerly anticipating the next development.

But what truly sets Married by Morning apart is its thoughtful exploration of themes. Kleypas tackles issues of identity, class, and social status with sensitivity and nuance, raising important questions about the constraints placed on women during this time period. The novel is also a powerful exploration of the human capacity for love and forgiveness, highlighting the ways in which our past experiences shape us and the power of relationships to heal and transform.

In short, Married by Morning is a triumph. With its engaging characters, witty dialogue, and sensual romance, it's a must-read for fans of historical fiction and romance. Kleypas' effortless style makes for a riveting narrative that will leave readers eager for more.

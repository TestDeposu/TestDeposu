# Accordance (Significance, #2)

**Author:** Shelly Crane  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

**Accordance: A Spark That Ignites the Soul**

Shelly Crane’s *Accordance*—the electrifying second installment in the *Significance* series—is more than a love story; it’s a revelation. It’s the kind of book that doesn’t just pull you in but *rewires* you, leaving you breathless with its blend of raw emotion, supernatural intrigue, and the kind of chemistry that feels like a physical force. At its heart, *Accordance* is about the fragile, intoxicating magic of connection—how love isn’t just a feeling, but a *vision*, a shared destiny that blurs the line between reality and something far more profound.

Maggie, a seventeen-year-old drowning in heartbreak and despair after a string of betrayals—her mother’s abandonment, her father’s collapse, and her boyfriend’s cruel departure—is a protagonist whose pain feels visceral. Crane doesn’t shy away from the ache of Maggie’s loneliness, but she doesn’t let it fester. Instead, she contrasts it with the sudden, searing intensity of Caleb’s presence. Their meeting at a traffic light isn’t just a chance encounter; it’s a *collision* of souls. The moment their eyes meet, the air crackles with something electric. And when they touch? It’s as if the world tilts. They see each other’s futures—not as vague possibilities, but as *lived* realities, vivid and undeniable. This isn’t just love at first sight; it’s *recognition*. The kind that makes you gasp, that feels like coming home after a lifetime of wandering.

Crane’s genius lies in her ability to weave romance with something darker, something *dangerous*. The world Maggie steps into isn’t just a place of magic—it’s a battleground. Caleb isn’t just her soulmate; he’s a target, and his enemies are relentless. This adds a layer of urgency to their story, turning their love into something that must be fought for, protected, and lived fiercely. The stakes aren’t just emotional; they’re existential. Maggie’s journey isn’t just about falling in love—it’s about *surviving* the chaos that comes with it.

Crane’s writing is lush but never pretentious. Her prose has a rhythm, a pulse, that mirrors the emotional whiplash of her characters’ lives. One moment, she’ll paint the ache of Maggie’s loneliness in stark, heartbreaking detail; the next, she’ll have you drowning in the intoxicating warmth of Caleb’s touch. Her dialogue crackles with authenticity, especially between Maggie and Caleb, whose banter is sharp, playful, and *alive*. You can hear the tension in their words, the way they tease each other even as they’re both terrified of what this connection means. It’s the kind of chemistry that makes you want to scream at the page: *Just kiss already!* But Crane holds back just enough to keep you hooked, to make every moment feel like a revelation.

What makes *Accordance* truly unforgettable, though, is its exploration of *significance*—not just in the grand, cosmic sense, but in the intimate, human one. Maggie’s entire world has felt meaningless until Caleb. And suddenly, she’s not just surviving; she’s *purpose*. The series’ title, *Significance*, isn’t just a theme—it’s a promise. Shelly Crane doesn’t just write stories; she crafts experiences that make you believe in fate, in destiny, in the idea that some people are meant to find each other, no matter the odds. And in a world that often feels indifferent, that’s a rare and powerful thing.

If you loved the slow-burn, emotionally charged romance of *Fammi Sentire Importante* (the first book in the series), *Accordance* will leave you breathless. It’s faster, more intense, and packed with the kind of supernatural intrigue that keeps you turning pages long after you should’ve been asleep. Crane’s writing is immersive, her characters are deeply flawed and deeply human, and her love story is the kind that lingers long after the last page. It’s a book that doesn’t just tell a story—it *transforms* you, leaving you with the lingering sense that maybe, just maybe, there’s a reason for everything.

So if you’re ready to dive into a world where love isn’t just a feeling but a *vision*, where every touch could be a battle, and where the most dangerous thing in the world is believing in fate—then *Accordance* is the book for you. Just be warned: once you start, you won’t want to stop.

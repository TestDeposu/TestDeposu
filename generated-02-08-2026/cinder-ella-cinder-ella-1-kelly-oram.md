# Cinder & Ella (Cinder & Ella, #1)

**Author:** Kelly Oram  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

'Cinder & Ella' by Kelly Oram is a captivating tale of love, loss, and self-discovery that delves into the complexities of human emotions, leaving readers spellbound and yearning for more. The story revolves around Ella, a teenager struggling to come to terms with the loss of her mother, and Brian Oliver, the charming Hollywood star hiding behind the pseudonym 'Cinder', with whom Ella shares a deep and meaningful connection through online chats.

Kelly Oram masterfully weaves a narrative that not only explores the intricacies of online relationships but also delves into the harsh realities of grief, fame, and identity. With a writing style that is both poignant and engaging, Oram effortlessly tugs at the heartstrings of her readers, making them invest in the characters and their journey.

One of the most striking aspects of 'Cinder & Ella' is the author's ability to create relatable characters that leap off the pages. Ella's vulnerability and resilience in the face of adversity make her an endearing protagonist, while Brian's struggle to balance his celebrity status with his genuine desire for human connection adds depth to his character. The chemistry between the two leads is undeniable, and their romance blossoms gradually, making their love story all the more believable and sweet.

Oram's portrayal of the digital age and its impact on human relationships is also noteworthy. The novel highlights the ease with which people can form connections online, often revealing more about themselves than they would in face-to-face interactions. This theme is skillfully intertwined with the plot, allowing the author to explore the complexities of trust, intimacy, and identity in the digital realm.

The author's writing style is effortless, making 'Cinder & Ella' a quick and engaging read. Oram's prose is laced with humor, wit, and a deep understanding of the human condition, making the novel an absolute page-turner. The pacing is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows smoothly, keeping readers invested in the story until the very end.

In conclusion, 'Cinder & Ella' is a captivating novel that will resonate with fans of contemporary romance and young adult fiction. Kelly Oram's writing is engaging, relatable, and authentic, making this book a must-read for anyone looking for a story that will touch their heart. With its well-developed characters, poignant themes, and sweet romance, 'Cinder & Ella' is a true gem that will leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in the series.

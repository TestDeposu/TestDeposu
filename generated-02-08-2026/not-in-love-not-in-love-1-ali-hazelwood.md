# Not in Love (Not in Love, #1)

**Author:** Ali Hazelwood  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

Not in Love by Ali Hazelwood is a captivating and thought-provoking romance novel that delves into the intricate dance between love, loyalty, and ambition. As the first installment in the Not in Love series, this book sets the tone for a thrilling and emotionally charged ride that will keep readers hooked until the very end.

At its core, Not in Love is a story about the blurred lines between personal and professional relationships. Rue Siebert, a talented biotech engineer, finds herself at the center of a hostile takeover, with the enigmatic and charismatic Eli Killgore leading the charge. As their paths collide, Rue and Eli must navigate the treacherous landscape of their forbidden attraction, all while keeping their feelings hidden from the prying eyes of their colleagues and the cutthroat business world.

One of the standout aspects of Hazelwood's writing is her ability to craft relatable and multidimensional characters. Rue is a shining example of this, with her sharp wit, determination, and vulnerability making her an instantly likable protagonist. Eli, on the other hand, is a masterfully crafted enigma, with a complex web of motivations and desires that slowly unravel as the story progresses.

Hazelwood's prose is engaging and effortless, with a keen eye for detail that brings the world of biotech engineering to vivid life. Her writing is also infused with a sense of humor and wit, making the characters' banter and interactions a joy to read.

The romance at the heart of Not in Love is a slow-burning fire that simmers with tension and anticipation. Hazelwood expertly builds the chemistry between Rue and Eli, creating a palpable sense of longing that will keep readers invested in their story. The forbidden nature of their relationship adds an extra layer of complexity, as they must navigate the risks of discovery and the consequences of their actions.

One of the most compelling aspects of Not in Love is its exploration of the theme of loyalty. Rue and Eli are both deeply committed to their respective companies, and their feelings for each other threaten to upend their professional lives. Hazelwood raises important questions about the nature of loyalty and the sacrifices we make for the people and institutions we care about.

In conclusion, Not in Love is a gripping and romantic novel that will appeal to fans of contemporary romance and women's fiction. With its strong characters, engaging plot, and thought-provoking themes, this book is sure to leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in the series.

# Kisses from Katie

**Author:** Katie Davis Majors  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

Kisses from Katie, a memoir by Katie Davis Majors, is a testament to the transformative power of faith, love, and selflessness. This New York Times bestselling book tells the extraordinary true story of an eighteen-year-old girl who defied expectations and embarked on a journey that would change her life and the lives of countless others forever.

At its core, Kisses from Katie is a story about obedience, sacrifice, and radical love. Katie's decision to leave behind the comforts of her life in Nashville and adopt thirteen girls in Uganda is a remarkable example of what it means to follow God's calling, no matter the cost. Through her journey, Katie learns to trust Jesus in the face of impossible circumstances, and her story challenges readers to do the same.

One of the most striking aspects of this book is Katie's writing style. Her honesty and heartfelt storytelling make it easy to become fully immersed in her journey. With unflinching candor, Katie shares the struggles and triumphs she faced as she built a family and a ministry from the ground up. Her writing is infused with a sense of vulnerability and authenticity, making it impossible not to be drawn into her world.

Katie's story is also a powerful reminder of the impact one person can have on the lives of others. Through Amazima Ministries, the organization she founded, Katie has provided education, food, and discipleship to hundreds of children. Her selflessness and generosity are a testament to the difference one person can make when they choose to live boldly and love generously.

What sets Kisses from Katie apart from other memoirs is its ability to inspire and challenge readers without being preachy or heavy-handed. Katie's story is a gentle yet powerful reminder of the importance of following God's calling, no matter where it may lead. Her journey is a testament to the fact that living a life of obedience and sacrifice is not always easy, but it is always worth it.

In short, Kisses from Katie is a must-read for anyone looking for a story that will inspire and challenge them to live a life of radical love and obedience. Katie's story is a powerful reminder that we all have the ability to make a difference in the lives of others, and that following God's calling can lead to a life of purpose and fulfillment.

# Skin Trade (Anita Blake, Vampire Hunter, #17)

**Author:** Laurell K. Hamilton  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

Laurell K. Hamilton's Anita Blake, Vampire Hunter series is a staple in the urban fantasy genre, and this collection of books 16-19, including Skin Trade, showcases the author's mastery of weaving intricate storylines, complex characters, and supernatural politics. As a reviewer, I was excited to dive into this set and explore the world of Anita Blake once again.

One of the primary themes that resonated throughout this collection is the concept of power and its various forms. Whether it's the ancient vampire queen's rise to power in Blood Noir, the supernatural serial killer's reign of terror in Skin Trade, or the Mother of All Darkness's pursuit of a new human host in Bullet, power is a driving force behind the characters' actions. Hamilton expertly explores the different ways power can be wielded, from manipulation and coercion to seduction and persuasion.

Anita Blake, the series' protagonist, is a perfect example of this theme. As a necromancer and vampire hunter, she possesses a unique set of skills that make her a formidable force in the supernatural world. However, her power is not just derived from her abilities, but also from her relationships and alliances with various characters. Throughout the series, Anita has formed a complex network of connections with vampires, werewolves, and other supernatural creatures, which often puts her at the center of power struggles.

Hamilton's writing style is another aspect of the series that deserves attention. Her prose is engaging, and she has a talent for crafting vivid descriptions of characters, settings, and action scenes. The world-building in the Anita Blake series is impressive, with a rich history and mythology that underpins the storylines. However, some readers may find the sheer number of characters and plot threads overwhelming, particularly in the later books.

One criticism of the series is its tendency to focus on Anita's personal relationships and sex life, which can sometimes overshadow the main plot. While these aspects of the story are certainly interesting, they can also feel like a distraction from the main mystery or conflict. Additionally, some readers may find Anita's character development uneven, as she often seems to veer between confident and self-assured to uncertain and insecure.

Despite these criticisms, the Anita Blake series remains a compelling and engaging read. Hamilton's ability to craft complex characters, intricate plotlines, and supernatural politics is undeniable. The Skin Trade collection is a must-read for fans of the series, as it marks a significant shift in Anita's world and sets the stage for future conflicts.

In conclusion, the Skin Trade collection is a gripping addition to the Anita Blake series, exploring themes of power, relationships, and supernatural politics. While some readers may find the series' focus on personal relationships and sex life overwhelming, Hamilton's writing style and world-building are undeniable strengths. If you're a fan of urban fantasy, vampires, and strong female protagonists, this collection is definitely worth checking out.

# It's in His Kiss (Bridgertons, #7)

**Author:** Julia Quinn  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

It's in His Kiss, the seventh installment in Julia Quinn's beloved Bridgertons series, is a captivating Regency romance that masterfully weaves together a complex web of family secrets, witty banter, and sizzling chemistry. At the heart of this delightful tale lies the unlikely pairing of Gareth St. Clair and Hyacinth Bridgerton, two individuals who couldn't be more different, yet inexplicably drawn to each other.

Quinn's writing style is, as always, impeccable. Her prose is effortless, witty, and engaging, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world she's created. The characters of Gareth and Hyacinth are expertly crafted, with distinct voices and personalities that leap off the page. Gareth, with his brooding nature and sharp wit, is the perfect foil to Hyacinth's vivacious and outspoken demeanor. Their interactions are laced with a palpable tension, as they engage in a series of clever repartees that will leave readers smiling.

One of the standout aspects of It's in His Kiss is its thoughtful exploration of the complexities of family relationships. Gareth's strained connection with his father serves as a poignant reminder that family ties can be both a blessing and a curse. Through Gareth's struggles to come to terms with his past and his inheritance, Quinn sheds light on the difficulties of navigating family dynamics and the importance of finding one's own path in life.

Meanwhile, Hyacinth's character serves as a refreshing respite from the typical Regency heroine. Her intelligence, independence, and sharp tongue make her a compelling and modern protagonist, despite the historical setting. Her willingness to challenge societal norms and conventions is inspiring, and her chemistry with Gareth is undeniable.

The romance that blossoms between Gareth and Hyacinth is a slow-burning, tantalizing affair that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. Quinn expertly builds the tension between the two leads, crafting a series of stolen glances, heated encounters, and, of course, a kiss that will leave readers weak in the knees.

In short, It's in His Kiss is a triumphant addition to the Bridgertons series, showcasing Quinn's mastery of the Regency romance genre. With its engaging characters, witty dialogue, and poignant exploration of family dynamics, this book is sure to delight both longtime fans and new readers alike.

# The Practice of the Presence of God

**Author:** Brother Lawrence  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

In "The Practice of the Presence of God," we are blessed with a timeless and profound spiritual guide that reveals the secrets of cultivating a deep and abiding relationship with the divine. This re-imagined, 2017 rendition of the classic spiritual text presents us with the wisdom and humility of Brother Lawrence, a 17th-century French Carmelite monk whose letters and conversations have become a beacon of inspiration for seekers of the spiritual life.

Through a series of simple yet profound teachings, Brother Lawrence invites us to re-evaluate our understanding of prayer and spirituality. He suggests that God's presence is not something reserved for grand, elaborate rituals or periods of formal quiet reflection, but rather a living reality that can be experienced in the midst of everyday life. As he so profoundly writes, "The time of business, when I am engaged in it, does not afford me any opportunity of thinking of God; but that time is precious, wherein I am at liberty to converse with Him in the stillness of contemplation."

What makes "The Practice of the Presence of God" so remarkable is its accessibility and practicality. Brother Lawrence's words are not couched in abstract theological jargon or dogmatic certainty, but rather emerge from a deep sense of simplicity, humility, and love. His is a spirituality of the everyday, one that invites us to experience the presence of God in the mundane tasks and ordinary moments of life. As he advises, "Do not look for God in extraordinary things, but in the ordinary actions of your ordinary life. You can pray from your workbench, at the plough, at table, anywhere."

Throughout this book, Brother Lawrence shares with us his own personal journey of spiritual transformation, from a life of distraction and disconnection to one of intimacy and devotion. We see how his experiences of contemplation and prayer are not limited to formal settings, but are infused in the rhythms and routines of daily life. He reminds us that the presence of God is not a static reality, but rather a dynamic relationship that can be nurtured and deepened in every moment.

One of the most striking aspects of Brother Lawrence's teachings is his emphasis on the importance of "practicing the presence." This concept is at the very heart of his spirituality, and suggests that our relationship with God is like a skill that can be developed through practice and discipline. Just as an artist perfects their craft through regular exercise, so too can we cultivate a deeper sense of God's presence in our lives through regular attention and intention.

In many ways, "The Practice of the Presence of God" is a challenging book, for it dares us to re-examine our priorities and values. It invites us to see the world, and our place in it, in a new light, one that is infused with a sense of wonder, awe, and reverence. As Brother Lawrence reminds us, "Do not worry about what others think, or the world's expectations. What matters is that you live in the presence of God, and that your heart is at peace."

Overall, "The Practice of the Presence of God" is a timeless masterpiece that reminds us of the beauty and power of a simple, yet profound spirituality. Through Brother Lawrence's words, we are reminded that the presence of God is not something that can be found only in grand or extraordinary moments, but rather in the ordinary, everyday moments of life. It is a book that invites us to cultivate a sense of intimacy and connection with the divine, and reminds us that the spiritual life is not something reserved for the few, but rather a reality available to all.

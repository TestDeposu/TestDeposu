# Aru Shah and the City of Gold (Pandava, #4)

**Author:** Roshani Chokshi  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

Aru Shah and the City of Gold, the fourth installment in the Pandava series by Roshani Chokshi, is a mesmerizing tale that delves into the complexities of identity, loyalty, and the blurred lines between good and evil. This fantasy adventure, loosely based on mythology, is a testament to Chokshi's masterful storytelling and her ability to craft a narrative that is both entertaining and thought-provoking.

At the heart of the story is Aru Shah, a young heroine who has been tasked with saving the world from the forces of darkness. However, as she navigates the treacherous landscape of the Otherworld, she begins to question the true motives of the gods and the devas she has sworn to protect. The arrival of a new sister, who claims to be the Sleeper's daughter, just like Aru, sets off a chain of events that challenges everything Aru thought she knew about herself and her place in the world.

One of the most striking aspects of Chokshi's writing is her ability to weave together mythology and fantasy, creating a rich and immersive world that is both familiar and yet utterly unique. The city of gold, Lanka, is a marvel of imagination, filled with wondrous magic and unforgettable creatures. The author's use of descriptive language is vivid and evocative, transporting readers to a world of wonder and awe.

However, beneath the surface of this fantastical world, Chokshi explores deeper themes that resonate with readers of all ages. The struggle to find one's identity and purpose is a universal theme, and Aru's journey is a powerful reminder that even in the face of uncertainty and adversity, we have the power to shape our own destiny. The author also tackles the complex issue of trust and loyalty, highlighting the difficulties of navigating relationships with those who may not always have our best interests at heart.

Chokshi's writing style is characterized by her wit and humor, and the dialogue in Aru Shah and the City of Gold is some of the most laugh-out-loud funny and engaging in the series. The characters are well-developed and relatable, with even the most minor players adding depth and richness to the narrative. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and quieter moments of reflection that keep readers on the edge of their seats.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the book raises more questions than it answers, leaving readers eagerly anticipating the final installment in the series. However, this is also a testament to Chokshi's skill as a storyteller, as she expertly weaves together the various threads of the narrative, creating a sense of tension and anticipation that propels readers forward.

In conclusion, Aru Shah and the City of Gold is a thrilling and unforgettable ride, filled with magic, adventure, and heart. Roshani Chokshi's writing is a masterclass in storytelling, and this book is a must-read for fans of fantasy, mythology, and adventure. With its richly imagined world, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes, this book is sure to leave readers wishing they could read the finale right now.

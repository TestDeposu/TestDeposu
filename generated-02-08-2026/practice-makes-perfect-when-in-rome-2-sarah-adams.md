# Practice Makes Perfect (When in Rome, #2)

**Author:** Sarah Adams  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

Practice Makes Perfect by Sarah Adams is a charming, chemistry-filled romance that delves into the complexities of finding one's perfect match and the blurred lines between friendship and love. The story follows Annie Walker, a small-town sweetheart, as she embarks on a quest to find her ideal partner, and Will Griffin, an emotionally unavailable bad boy, who becomes her unlikely tutor in the art of love.

Adams masterfully crafts a narrative that is both witty and poignant, exploring the themes of self-discovery, vulnerability, and the true meaning of love. Through Annie's journey, we see her confront her insecurities and challenge her perceptions of what she wants in a partner. Will, on the other hand, is a complex character with a tough exterior and a soft center, making him the perfect foil to Annie's innocence and charm.

One of the strengths of Adams' writing is her ability to create relatable characters that leap off the page. Annie and Will's banter is engaging, and their practice dates are filled with a palpable tension that simmers just below the surface. The supporting cast, including Annie's family and friends, adds depth and warmth to the story, making the small town of Rome, Kentucky, feel like a character in its own right.

The author's style is effortless, with a narrative that flows smoothly from one scene to the next. Adams' prose is infused with a sense of humor, making the story feel lighthearted and fun, even when tackling more serious themes. The romance is sweet and steamy, with a slow-burn that will keep readers invested in Annie and Will's relationship.

What sets Practice Makes Perfect apart from other romance novels is its thoughtful exploration of what it means to find true love. Adams suggests that perfection is not the goal, but rather a genuine connection with someone who accepts and loves you for who you are. Annie's growth and self-awareness are inspiring, and her ultimate realization that love doesn't have to be perfect to be real is a powerful message that will resonate with readers.

In conclusion, Practice Makes Perfect is a delightful romance that will appeal to fans of Sarah Adams' previous works, as well as readers who enjoy stories about self-discovery, love, and small-town charm. With its engaging characters, witty dialogue, and thoughtful themes, this book is a must-read for anyone looking for a romantic and uplifting tale.

# Boundless (Unearthly, #3)

**Author:** Cynthia Hand  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

"Boundless" by Cynthia Hand is a captivating and enchanting conclusion to the Unearthly series, leaving readers spellbound and yearning for more. This paranormal romance masterfully explores the age-old question of whether to follow one's heart or destiny, delving into the complexities of love, sacrifice, and the true meaning of choice.

From the onset, Clara Gardner's world is turned upside down as she navigates the intricate web of her divine purpose, all while being watched by a malevolent fallen angel. The stakes are higher than ever, and Clara must confront the harsh reality of her destiny – one that includes Christian Prescott by her side, but not the love of her life, Tucker Avery.

Hand's writing is nothing short of mesmerizing, weaving a tale that effortlessly balances action, suspense, and romance. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Clara's inner turmoil and emotional struggles making her an endearing and authentic protagonist. The chemistry between Clara and Tucker is undeniable, and their love story is expertly crafted, making it impossible not to become invested in their fate.

One of the standout aspects of "Boundless" is its thought-provoking exploration of destiny versus choice. Hand raises important questions about the nature of free will and whether our choices are truly our own. Clara's struggles to reconcile her heart's desires with her divine purpose serve as a powerful reminder that the greatest sacrifices are often made for love.

The world-building in "Boundless" is equally impressive, with Hand expanding on the mythology of the Unearthly series. The fallen angel's vendetta against Clara adds an element of tension, while the looming threat of the Black Wings creates a sense of urgency, propelling the story forward.

In conclusion, "Boundless" is a must-read for fans of paranormal romance and fantasy. Cynthia Hand's writing is captivating, and her characters are unforgettable. This finale is a satisfying conclusion to the Unearthly series, leaving readers with a sense of closure and a newfound appreciation for the power of love and choice.

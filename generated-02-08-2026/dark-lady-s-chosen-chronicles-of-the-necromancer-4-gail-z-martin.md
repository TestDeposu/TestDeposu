# Dark Lady's Chosen (Chronicles of the Necromancer, #4)

**Author:** Gail Z. Martin  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

In "Dark Lady's Chosen," the fourth installment of Gail Z. Martin's Chronicles of the Necromancer, the stakes are higher, the battles more intense, and the choices more treacherous than ever before. As the series hurtles toward its conclusion, Martin weaves a rich tapestry of magic, politics, and ancient lore that will keep readers on the edge of their seats.

The stage is set for a catastrophic war, one that will shatter the fragile peace that King Martris Drayke has fought so hard to maintain. Treachery and blood magic cast a dark shadow over Margolan, forcing Kiara to navigate the treacherous landscape of her duty to her kingdom-by-blood and her commitments to her kingdom-by-marriage. Meanwhile, Jonmarc Vahanian finds himself embroiled in a conflict among ancient immortals, a conflict that will determine the fate of his soul and the future of the Winter Kingdoms.

Martin's masterful storytelling weaves together multiple plot threads with ease, creating a narrative that is at once epic in scope and intimate in its character-driven focus. Her world-building is meticulous, drawing on a deep well of occult lore and mythology to create a richly textured landscape that is both familiar and yet entirely unique.

One of the greatest strengths of Martin's writing is her ability to balance action and character development. Her heroes are multidimensional and complex, driven by a deep sense of loyalty and honor. Kiara, in particular, is a standout – her struggles to balance her duty to her kingdom with her growing feelings for Jonmarc are deeply relatable, and her courage in the face of overwhelming odds is inspiring.

Martin's writing style is descriptive without being overly flowery, allowing the reader to fully immerse themselves in the world and its characters. Her use of language is precise and evocative, conjuring vivid images of the battles, magic, and politics that drive the plot.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the pacing can be somewhat uneven in places. The novel's many threads can be overwhelming at times, and some readers may find themselves struggling to keep up. However, Martin's writing is always engaging, and the payoff is well worth the effort.

Ultimately, "Dark Lady's Chosen" is a thrilling and addictive read that will appeal to fans of epic fantasy and urban fantasy alike. Martin's world is richly detailed and filled with complex characters, and her storytelling is engaging and suspenseful. If you're looking for a series that will keep you on the edge of your seat, look no further than the Chronicles of the Necromancer.

# Tales From the Vulgar Unicorn (Thieves' World, #2)

**Author:** Robert Lynn Asprin  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

**A Glimpse into the Dark Underbelly of Sanctuary**

Robert Lynn Asprin's "Tales From the Vulgar Unicorn" is the second installment in the Thieves' World series, a shared-world anthology that delves into the seedy underworld of Sanctuary. This 2020 release is a masterclass in collaborative storytelling, featuring contributions from esteemed authors like Philip José Farmer, David Drake, Janet Morris, and A.E. van Vogt, among others.

Asprin's vision for Thieves' World is a unique blend of fantasy and crime fiction, where morality is a luxury few can afford to indulge in. In "Tales From the Vulgar Unicorn," the gods themselves take a hand in the fight for the hearts, minds, and souls of Sanctuary's citizens. This divine intervention sets off a chain of events that will have far-reaching consequences, introducing readers to a cast of complex characters, including One-Thumb, the proprietor of the Vulgar Unicorn, Regli, a nobleman with a hidden agenda, Illyra, the enigmatic seer, and Hanes, a cunning thief.

One of the standout features of this anthology is its ability to balance multiple narratives and character arcs. Asprin's masterful editing weaves these disparate threads together, creating a rich tapestry that rewards readers with every subsequent tale. The writing is diverse, but each contributor brings their unique voice and style to the table, making "Tales From the Vulgar Unicorn" a true collaborative masterpiece.

The world-building in Thieves' World is reminiscent of the classic fantasy series that inspired it, with a healthy dose of grit and realism. Asprin's vision of Sanctuary is a harsh, unforgiving environment where the weak are preyed upon and the strong must fight to survive. This dark underbelly is where "Tales From the Vulgar Unicorn" truly shines, offering a glimpse into a world where even the gods are not above manipulation and betrayal.

Throughout the anthology, Tempus Thales, the immortal mercenary, emerges as a compelling figure, his presence hinted at in several tales. This enigmatic character introduces a new layer of complexity to the series, foreshadowing events that will unfold in future installments.

The themes of power, corruption, and the blurred lines between good and evil are expertly woven throughout the narrative. "Tales From the Vulgar Unicorn" is a cautionary tale about the dangers of ambition and the corrupting influence of power. As Asprin so skillfully illustrates, even the noblest of intentions can be twisted and perverted in the pursuit of goals.

As a critic, I'm drawn to the way "Tales From the Vulgar Unicorn" subverts traditional fantasy expectations. This anthology is not a tidy, heroic tale of good vs. evil; instead, it's a nuanced exploration of the gray areas in between. Asprin's collaborative effort creates a sense of immediacy, immersing readers in the gritty world of Sanctuary.

In many ways, "Tales From the Vulgar Unicorn" serves as a prototype for the grimdark fantasy movement of the 1990s. By exploring the seedier aspects of human nature, Asprin and his contributors forge a new path in the fantasy genre, paving the way for authors like Joe Abercrombie and Scott Lynch.

In conclusion, "Tales From the Vulgar Unicorn" is a compelling, dark, and unapologetic look into the world of Sanctuary. Robert Lynn Asprin's vision is a testament to the power of collaborative storytelling, and the anthology's ability to balance multiple narratives is a true achievement. For fans of fantasy, crime fiction, and grimdark, this book is a must-read.

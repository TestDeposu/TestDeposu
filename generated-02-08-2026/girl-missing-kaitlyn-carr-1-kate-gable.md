# Girl Missing (Kaitlyn Carr #1)

**Author:** Kate Gable  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In "Girl Missing" by Kate Gable, we are transported to a world of darkness and deception, where the lines between love, loyalty, and justice become hopelessly entangled. This gripping thriller revolves around the story of Detective Kaitlyn Carr, a dedicated and driven investigator with a complicated family life. As Kaitlyn delves deeper into the brutal murder of the Hendrel family, she must confront the demons of her own family's dysfunction, which threatens to overshadow the pursuit of truth.

Gable masterfully crafts a narrative that seamlessly weaves together the intricate threads of a police procedural, a family drama, and a heart-wrenching emotional exploration. We are presented with a complex heroine, Kaitlyn, whose life is marked by long work hours and an inability to form close relationships, stemming from her complicated past. However, her sister Violet's arrival sparks a chain reaction of events that forces Kaitlyn to confront her limitations and the true meaning of family.

One of the strengths of "Girl Missing" is its ability to balance the dark and suspenseful elements of the thriller genre with poignant moments of character-driven drama. The author expertly portrays the intricate dynamics of the Carr family, highlighting the tensions and conflicts that arise from unresolved issues and unspoken emotions. This nuanced portrayal raises essential questions about family, loyalty, and what it means to "do the impossible," particularly for Kaitlyn, who finds herself torn between her duty as a detective and her responsibilities as a sister and potentially, a caregiver.

Throughout the book, Gable demonstrates a keen understanding of human psychology, skillfully using the characters' thoughts and emotions to propel the plot forward. The pace is well-balanced, with moments of high tension giving way to quieter reflections, which ultimately build toward a shocking conclusion. The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, each character adding depth and complexity to the narrative.

If there is one criticism to be made, it's that some plot twists are predictably executed, losing a degree of impact due to their clear foreboding. Nevertheless, the overall narrative remains engaging and unsettling, with a finale that defies expectations in a satisfying way.

Ultimately, "Girl Missing" is a standout in the thriller genre, thanks to Gable's exceptional writing and her ability to craft characters that linger in the reader's mind long after the final page is turned. If you enjoy the works of A. J. Rivers, Mary Stone, or Karin Slaughter, you will likely devour this novella in one sitting. This thrilling tale of family, duty, and redemption will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very end.

# Geekerella (Once Upon a Con, #1)

**Author:** Ashley Poston  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

In the realm of young adult fiction, few books have managed to capture the essence of fandom culture as delightfully as Ashley Poston's Geekerella. This charming novel is a love letter to anyone who has ever found solace in the magic of science fiction, cosplay, and the sense of community that comes with it. By weaving together the threads of romance, self-discovery, and geek culture, Poston has crafted a tale that will resonate deeply with fans of all ages.

At its core, Geekerella is a story about embracing one's true passions and finding the courage to be oneself in the face of adversity. Protagonist Elle Wittimer is a shining example of this, as she navigates the complexities of her own fandom while dealing with the challenges of her everyday life. Her love for the classic sci-fi series Starfield is more than just a passing interest – it's a connection to her late father and a source of comfort in times of need. As Elle prepares to enter a cosplay contest for a new Starfield movie, her determination and creativity are inspiring, and her character serves as a powerful reminder that it's never too late to pursue one's dreams.

Poston's writing style is a perfect match for the tone of the book. Her prose is witty, engaging, and full of clever references that will delight fans of science fiction and fantasy. The author's use of language is also noteworthy, as she seamlessly shifts between the perspectives of Elle and Darien Freeman, the teen actor playing Federation Prince Carmindor in the Starfield reboot. This dual narrative adds depth to the story, allowing readers to experience the world of ExcelsiCon from multiple angles.

One of the most striking aspects of Geekerella is its thoughtful exploration of the complexities of fandom culture. Poston tackles issues such as the commercialization of cons, the pressure to conform to certain expectations, and the tension between old and new fans. These themes are woven throughout the narrative, adding a layer of nuance to the story that will resonate with anyone who has ever felt like an outsider within their own fandom.

As the story unfolds, Elle and Darien's paths converge in unexpected ways, leading to a romance that is both sweet and authentic. Their relationship is built on a foundation of mutual respect and understanding, and it serves as a beautiful reminder that true connections can be found in the most unexpected places.

In conclusion, Geekerella is a must-read for anyone who has ever found solace in the world of science fiction and fantasy. Ashley Poston's writing is engaging, witty, and full of heart, making this book a true standout in the world of young adult fiction. With its positive portrayal of fandom culture, relatable characters, and sweet romance, Geekerella is a novel that will leave readers feeling uplifted and inspired.

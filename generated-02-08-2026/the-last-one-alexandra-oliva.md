# The Last One

**Author:** Alexandra Oliva  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

In the gripping novel "The Last One" by Alexandra Oliva, the lines between reality and reality TV are masterfully blurred, creating a suspenseful and thought-provoking tale that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. This fast-paced story follows Zoo, a young woman who joins a reality TV show where contestants are sent into the woods to face challenges that test their endurance. However, when Zoo stumbles upon a devastating and mysterious event, she must use all her survival skills to navigate the treacherous terrain and uncover the truth.

Oliva's writing style is taut and tense, making the story almost unbearably real, as praised by Ruth Ware, author of "One by One". The author's ability to create a sense of uncertainty and doubt in the reader is remarkable, mirroring Zoo's own confusion and fear as she tries to make sense of the world around her. The pacing of the novel is expertly handled, with a steady stream of twists and turns that keep the reader guessing until the very end.

One of the most striking aspects of "The Last One" is its exploration of the role that media plays in our perception of reality. The novel highlights how easily we are manipulated by the narratives presented to us, and how readily we cast our judgments based on limited information. This theme is particularly relevant in today's world, where social media and reality TV have become integral parts of our lives. Oliva's thought-provoking commentary on this issue adds depth and complexity to the story, making it more than just a thrilling tale of survival.

The character of Zoo is well-developed and relatable, with a practical and tough-minded approach to the challenges she faces. Her emotional and physical reserves are pushed to the limit as she struggles to survive, and her determination to find her way back home is inspiring. The supporting characters are also well-crafted, with distinct personalities and motivations that add to the tension and suspense of the story.

The novel's use of the reality TV show as a backdrop is clever and well-executed, allowing Oliva to comment on the inner workings of the industry and the ways in which it can manipulate and exploit its contestants. The post-apocalyptic setting adds an extra layer of tension and uncertainty, making the story even more gripping and unpredictable.

Overall, "The Last One" is a stunning debut from Alexandra Oliva, showcasing her talent for crafting a gripping and thought-provoking story. With its pulse-pounding action, well-developed characters, and sophisticated themes, this novel is a must-read for fans of suspenseful fiction. As praised by the Seattle Times, Oliva is a writer to watch, and "The Last One" is a page-turner that will keep readers on the edge of their seats until the very end.

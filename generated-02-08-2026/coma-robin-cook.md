# Coma

**Author:** Robin Cook  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

**A Gripping Medical Thriller that Explores the Dark Underbelly of the Healthcare System**

Robin Cook's 2014 thriller, Coma, is a taut and suspenseful page-turner that delves into the darker corners of the medical profession. The novel tells the story of Susan Wheeler, a determined third-year medical student working at Boston Memorial Hospital, who is determined to uncover the truth behind a series of inexplicable comas that have left a trail of victims in her wake.

On the surface, Cook's narrative appears to be a straightforward thriller about a medical student who stumbles upon a sinister conspiracy involving organ harvesting and black market trading. However, as the story progresses, it becomes clear that Cook is using this narrative to examine some of the more insidious aspects of the healthcare system, particularly the ways in which it can be manipulated for financial gain.

Through Susan's investigation, Cook reveals the often-shocking lengths to which hospitals and pharmaceutical companies will go to maximize profits, even if it means putting patients' lives at risk. This is a theme that is all too familiar in today's healthcare landscape, where the pursuit of profit can often seem to take precedence over patient care.

One of Cook's greatest strengths as a writer is his ability to weave together complex medical jargon and technical procedures with a compelling narrative that is engaging for non-medical readers. Coma is a testament to this skill, as Cook seamlessly integrates the intricacies of anesthesia and surgery with the suspenseful plot.

Another notable aspect of Cook's writing is his ability to create well-developed and relatable characters. Susan Wheeler, in particular, is a standout protagonist - her intelligence, determination, and compassion make her a compelling character to root for, even as she finds herself embroiled in a sinister conspiracy.

Throughout the novel, Cook expertly ratchets up the tension, keeping the reader guessing until the very end. The twists and turns are expertly plotted, and the climax of the novel is both shocking and satisfying.

If there is a criticism to be made of Coma, it is that the supporting cast of characters can feel somewhat one-dimensional at times. However, this is a minor quibble, and the strengths of the novel far outweigh its weaknesses.

In conclusion, Coma is a gripping medical thriller that is both a page-turning mystery and a thought-provoking commentary on the healthcare system. Robin Cook's writing is masterful, and his ability to create suspenseful plots and well-developed characters is unmatched. If you're a fan of medical thrillers, or simply looking for a compelling read, Coma is a must-read.

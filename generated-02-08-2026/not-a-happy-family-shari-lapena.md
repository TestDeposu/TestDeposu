# Not a Happy Family

**Author:** Shari Lapena  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

"Not a Happy Family" by Shari Lapena is a gripping domestic suspense novel that delves into the darker aspects of family dynamics and the secrets that can destroy them. The story revolves around the Merton family, who, despite their wealth and privilege, are a far cry from being happy. Fred and Sheila Merton, the patriarch and matriarch, are brutally murdered after a tense Easter dinner with their three adult children, leaving behind a trail of secrets, lies, and motives.

Lapena masterfully weaves a complex web of relationships, expertly exposing the cracks in the family's facade. As the investigation unfolds, it becomes clear that each of the siblings stands to gain a substantial inheritance, raising questions about their potential involvement in the murder. The author skillfully manipulates the reader's perceptions, making it impossible to discern the truth from the lies.

One of the most striking aspects of Lapena's writing is her ability to craft relatable, yet flawed characters. The Merton siblings, in particular, are multidimensional and nuanced, with each one harboring secrets and resentments that threaten to boil over. The author's portrayal of their dysfunctional family dynamics is both unsettling and fascinating, making it impossible to look away from the train wreck that is their lives.

Lapena's writing style is concise, yet evocative, with a keen eye for detail that immerses the reader in the world of the Mertons. Her use of multiple perspectives adds to the tension, creating a sense of unease and uncertainty that propels the reader forward. The pacing is expertly handled, with a steady stream of twists and turns that keep the reader guessing until the very end.

At its core, "Not a Happy Family" is a scathing critique of the wealthy elite and the secrets they keep. Lapena expertly exposes the rot that can fester beneath the surface of seemingly perfect families, revealing the darker aspects of human nature. This is a book that will appeal to fans of domestic suspense, particularly those who enjoy authors like Paula Hawkins and Gillian Flynn.

In short, "Not a Happy Family" is a gripping, twisty thriller that will keep you on the edge of your seat. With its complex characters, expert plotting, and scathing social commentary, this book is a must-read for anyone who enjoys a good domestic suspense novel.

# Four Dead Queens

**Author:** Astrid Scholte  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

In the thrilling young adult novel "Four Dead Queens" by Astrid Scholte, the lines between loyalty, deception, and power are expertly blurred, crafting a captivating narrative that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. This gripping tale of conspiracy, romance, and self-discovery is set in the nation of Quadara, where four queens rule over distinct regions, each with its unique culture and values.

The story follows Keralie Corrington, a cunning and resourceful thief, and Varin, an honest and chivalrous citizen of Eonia, as they become entangled in a complex web of intrigue and deception. When Keralie steals a package from Varin, their lives intersect in unexpected ways, and they soon find themselves at the center of a deadly conspiracy that leaves all four queens dead. With their lives in danger, they form an uneasy alliance, navigating the treacherous landscape of Quadara's politics and social hierarchy.

Scholte's writing style is engaging and immersive, with a keen focus on character development and world-building. The author's use of vivid descriptions and clever plot twists creates a rich and immersive atmosphere, drawing readers into the world of Quadara. The pacing is well-balanced, with a steady stream of action, suspense, and romance that will keep readers invested in the story.

One of the most compelling aspects of "Four Dead Queens" is its exploration of the main theme: the struggle for power and identity in a society governed by strict rules and expectations. Through Keralie and Varin's characters, Scholte delves into the complexities of loyalty, duty, and self-discovery, raising important questions about the true cost of power and the importance of staying true to oneself.

The romance that blossoms between Keralie and Varin is a highlight of the novel, adding a sweet and tender dimension to the story. Their relationship is built on mutual respect, trust, and a deep understanding of each other's complexities, making their romance all the more believable and satisfying.

Scholte's writing is also notable for its strong feminist undertones, as the novel celebrates the strength and agency of its female characters. The four queens, each with their unique personalities and motivations, are well-developed and complex, adding depth and nuance to the story.

In conclusion, "Four Dead Queens" is a captivating and immersive young adult novel that will appeal to fans of fantasy, mystery, and romance. Astrid Scholte's engaging writing style, coupled with her well-developed characters and intricate plot, make this novel a compelling read. With its strong feminist themes, complex characters, and gripping storyline, "Four Dead Queens" is a must-read for anyone looking for a thrilling and thought-provoking novel.

# The Guard (The Selection, #2.5)

**Author:** Kiera Cass  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

"The Guard" by Kiera Cass is a captivating novella that delves into the heart and mind of Aspen Leger, America Singer's first love, and a member of the royal guard. Set just after the Selection is narrowed down to the Elite, this story offers a unique perspective on the iconic love triangle that has become a hallmark of the Selection series.

Aspen's narrative provides a fresh and intriguing angle on the events that unfolded in the palace. Through his eyes, we witness the secret conversations, rebel attacks, and hidden dangers that lurk behind the opulent facade of palace life. Cass masterfully weaves Aspen's story into the larger tapestry of the Selection series, offering a nuanced exploration of the complexities and challenges faced by those who serve the crown.

One of the most compelling aspects of "The Guard" is its thoughtful examination of the love triangle between America, Aspen, and Prince Maxon. Aspen's unrequited love for America is palpable, and his determination to remind her of the love they once shared is both poignant and relatable. Cass skillfully conveys the intensity of Aspen's emotions, making it impossible not to sympathize with his plight.

Aspen's role as a member of the royal guard also adds a layer of depth to the story, highlighting the tensions between his duty to the prince and his devotion to America. This internal conflict is skillfully portrayed, and Cass raises important questions about loyalty, duty, and the true meaning of sacrifice.

Throughout the novella, Cass's writing is engaging and effortless, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world of the Selection. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Aspen being a particularly well-crafted protagonist. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, romance, and suspense that keeps the reader on the edge of their seat.

In conclusion, "The Guard" is a must-read for fans of the Selection series. Cass's masterful storytelling and nuanced characterization make this novella a compelling addition to the series. The teaser to "The One" at the end is a delightful bonus, providing a tantalizing glimpse into the next installment of the series.

Overall, "The Guard" is a captivating and romantic novella that explores the complexities of love, duty, and loyalty. If you're a fan of the Selection series, or just looking for a compelling and engaging read, then "The Guard" is definitely worth checking out.

# How to Kill an Incubus (Incubus #1)

**Author:** Kimber Lee  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In the realm of urban fantasy, few authors can weave a tale as dark, twisted, and enchanting as Kimber Lee does in her debut novel, How to Kill an Incubus. This is a story that explores the very fabric of hellish hierarchies, forbidden passions, and the depths of a being's thirst for blood - all set against the backdrop of a London shrouded in mystery and terror.

The book introduces us to Azrael, an incubus of uncommon ferocity and cunning, banished from hell four centuries ago to die among humans. However, Azrael defies the odds and lives, driven by a singular purpose: to claim the lives of those who wronged him. As we delve into the twisted narrative spun by the author, we find ourselves entangled in a web of deceit, betrayal, and heartbreak, all leading to a climactic showdown that will shatter your perceptions of this ancient being.

Lee masterfully crafts a narrative that walks the thin line between good and evil, human and demon, and the blurred lines between the two. This is not a story about right versus wrong; it's a tale of complex motives, morally ambiguous characters, and the struggle to navigate a world where survival often means surrendering to one's darker impulses.

One of the most striking aspects of Lee's writing is her use of atmosphere and setting. London, in this novel, is a city of contrasts, where the veil between the human world and the realm of demons is thinner than ever before. The skies are shrouded in a blood-red haze, an omen of the chaos that awaits, while the city's inhabitants are oblivious to the malevolent forces that lurk in every shadow.

As the body count rises and the stakes grow higher, Lee's prose becomes increasingly evocative, conjuring images of a city on the brink of collapse. Her writing is akin to a dark, velvet-draped canvas, on which the macabre dance of demonic assassins, ancient vendettas, and forbidden passions is painted in vivid, blood-red strokes.

Lee's protagonist, Azrael, is a fascinating and complex character, driven by a rich inner life that belies his demonic nature. His narrative voice is a siren's call, luring the reader deeper into the heart of darkness, where the horrors that await are all too real. Through Azrael's eyes, we glimpse a world where love and loyalty are currency, and the line between friend and foe is constantly blurred.

The supporting cast, while not as well-developed as Azrael, serves a crucial purpose in propelling the plot forward. The arrival of a young succubus, who inadvertently sets off a chain of events that will change Azrael's fate forever, adds a fresh dynamic to the story.

If there's one criticism to be made, it's that Lee's pacing can be a tad uneven, veering sharply from slow-burning tension to breakneck action sequences. This may prove disorienting for some readers, but for those willing to ride the waves, the reward is well worth the effort.

In conclusion, How to Kill an Incubus is a stunning debut that cements Kimber Lee's reputation as a masterful weaver of dark fantasy tales. This is a book that will keep you on the edge of your seat, guessing till the very end, and leave you craving more. For fans of the urban fantasy genre, this is an absolute must-read, and for anyone drawn to stories of forbidden passion and the supernatural, How to Kill an Incubus will prove an unforgettable experience.

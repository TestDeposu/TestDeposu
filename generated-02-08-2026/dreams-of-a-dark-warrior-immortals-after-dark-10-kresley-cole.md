# Dreams of a Dark Warrior (Immortals After Dark, #10)

**Author:** Kresley Cole  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

Dreams of a Dark Warrior, the tenth installment in Kresley Cole's Immortals After Dark series, is a masterfully crafted tale of love, loss, and reincarnation. This paranormal romance novel delves into the complex and often fraught relationship between Regin the Radiant, a fierce and beautiful immortal, and Aidan the Fierce, a warlord who has been reborn into the body of Declan Chase, a brutal Celtic soldier.

At its core, Dreams of a Dark Warrior is a story about the power of love to transcend even death itself. Regin and Aidan's relationship is one that spans centuries, with Aidan repeatedly reincarnated in an attempt to find his beloved Regin. However, each reincarnation comes with a terrible price: Aidan is doomed to die when he remembers his past, forcing Regin to make an impossible choice between rekindling their passion and losing him once again.

Kresley Cole's writing style is, as always, engaging and evocative, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of her characters' motivations and desires. The world-building in Dreams of a Dark Warrior is meticulous and immersive, drawing the reader into a richly textured world of magic and mythology. Cole's use of language is equally impressive, with a keen sense of pacing and tone that keeps the reader on the edge of their seat.

One of the most striking aspects of Dreams of a Dark Warrior is its exploration of the complexities of love and relationships. Regin and Aidan's relationship is fraught with tension and conflict, as they navigate the challenges of their reincarnated existence. Cole handles these complexities with sensitivity and nuance, creating a deeply emotional and often heartbreaking narrative that is both poignant and thought-provoking.

Throughout the book, Cole also explores themes of identity and self-discovery, as Regin and Aidan struggle to come to terms with their past lives and their present circumstances. This adds a layer of depth and complexity to the narrative, as the characters grapple with questions of who they are and what they want from life.

Overall, Dreams of a Dark Warrior is a compelling and deeply romantic novel that is sure to appeal to fans of paranormal romance and fantasy. With its richly detailed world-building, complex characters, and poignant narrative, this book is a must-read for anyone looking for a story that will capture their imagination and touch their heart.

# Spark (Elemental, #2)

**Author:** Brigid Kemmerer  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

"Spark," the second installment in Brigid Kemmerer's Elemental series, is a gripping tale of secrets, lies, and the struggle to maintain control in a world where the lines between reality and the paranormal are constantly blurred. This pressure cooker of a novel expertly weaves together multiple storylines, propelling the reader toward a shocking climax that will leave them breathless.

At the heart of the story is Nick Merrick, a complex and multi-faceted character who embodies the quintessential teenage struggle. Stretched to the breaking point by his various responsibilities, Nick's world is on the verge of imploding. From keeping his grades up to maintaining the family business, Nick's life is a delicate balancing act that threatens to topple over at any moment. Add to this the strain of keeping secrets from his own twin brother and the stress of navigating a complicated love life, and it becomes clear that Nick is in for the fight of his life.

Kemmerer's masterful storytelling is on full display in "Spark." With a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the human condition, she crafts a narrative that is both relatable and engaging. The world she has created is rich and immersive, populated by characters that are expertly developed and nuanced. The Merrick brothers, in particular, are a highlight of the series, their bond and banter adding a welcome layer of depth to the story.

One of the most striking aspects of Kemmerer's writing style is her ability to seamlessly blend the paranormal with the mundane. The elemental forces that drive the plot are expertly woven into the fabric of everyday life, creating a sense of tension and unease that permeates every aspect of the story. This is a world where the supernatural is not just a distant threat, but a constant presence that can strike at any moment.

The themes of secrecy and deception are also expertly explored in "Spark." Nick's struggles to keep his secrets hidden, even from those closest to him, serve as a powerful reminder of the destructive nature of lies and the importance of honesty. As the story hurtles toward its climax, the consequences of Nick's actions become increasingly dire, highlighting the devastating impact that secrets can have on relationships and lives.

In short, "Spark" is a gripping and intense read that will appeal to fans of paranormal romance and suspense. With its expertly crafted plot, relatable characters, and nuanced exploration of themes, this novel is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and engaging story. Brigid Kemmerer's writing style is both captivating and immersive, making "Spark" a truly unforgettable read.

# When True Night Falls (The Coldfire Trilogy, #2)

**Author:** C.S. Friedman  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1993

**When True Night Falls: A Dark Descent into the Human Psyche**

C.S. Friedman's When True Night Falls is a masterful sequel to the critically acclaimed Black Sun Rising, plunging readers into a dark and unforgiving world where science fiction and fantasy converge. This second installment in the Coldfire Trilogy is a gripping tale of two vastly disparate individuals, bound together in a quest to vanquish an ancient evil that threatens to consume their world.

At the heart of the novel lies the eternal conundrum of the human condition: can good and evil coexist, or must we choose between them? Friedman skillfully weaves together the complex threads of these two opposing forces, placing her characters in a constant struggle between light and darkness. Hisca, a warrior priest driven by a zealous desire to eradicate the forces of evil, must join forces with Nesta, a powerful sorcerer who has spent centuries mastering the very same malevolent powers he seeks to destroy. This unlikely alliance is forged in the face of an existential threat, as an ancient evil stirs, threatening to engulf the world in an eternal and unforgiving night.

Friedman's writing is a true marvel, imbuing every sentence with a depth and richness that draws the reader inexorably into her dark and foreboding universe. Her prose is as evocative as it is unflinching, conjuring vivid images of a world beset by fear, superstition, and the ever-present specter of death. Her characters leap off the page, their struggles, desires, and motivations illuminated with a level of nuance and subtlety that is all too rare in modern fantasy.

One of the most striking aspects of Friedman's narrative is her willingness to confront the darker aspects of human nature. She plunges headlong into the depths of human depravity, exploring the twisted logic of those who would do harm to others, and the corrosive effects of evil on those who succumb to it. This is no sanitized world of heroes and villains, but a gritty, unforgiving realm where the distinctions between good and evil are constantly blurred.

Through Hisca and Nesta's perilous journey, Friedman skillfully exposes the fallibility of even the most well-intentioned individuals, revealing the terrible secrets that lie hidden beneath the surface of even the most seemingly righteous souls. Her portrayal of the human psyche is nothing short of masterful, offering a profound and unflinching exploration of the very darkest recesses of the human heart.

Ultimately, When True Night Falls is a testament to Friedman's skill as a storyteller, a writer who can craft compelling characters and weave them into a complex narrative that is both intellectually stimulating and emotionally resonant. This is a novel that will appeal to fans of science fiction, fantasy, and horror, offering a rich and immersive reading experience that will leave even the most jaded readers on the edge of their seats.

In short, When True Night Falls is a dark, thought-provoking, and unapologetic masterpiece of fantasy, one that will linger in the reader's mind long after the final page has been turned. If you're a fan of fantasy, horror, or simply great storytelling, do yourself a favor and plunge into this gripping tale of good, evil, and the human condition.

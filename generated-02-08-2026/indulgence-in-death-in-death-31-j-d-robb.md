# Indulgence in Death (In Death, #31)

**Author:** J.D. Robb  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Indulgence in Death, the 31st installment in J.D. Robb's In Death series, is a gripping and intense thriller that delves into the darker aspects of human nature. The story begins with Lieutenant Eve Dallas and her husband Roarke on a well-deserved vacation in Ireland, only to be disrupted by a murder that sets the tone for the rest of the book.

Upon their return to New York, Eve is faced with a series of gruesome and seemingly unrelated murders. A limo driver is shot through the neck with a crossbow, and a high-priced escort is found stabbed through the heart with a bayonet. As Eve delves deeper into the investigation, she realizes that she is dealing with a thrill-killer who has a taste for the finer things in life – and death.

What sets Indulgence in Death apart from other thrillers is its thought-provoking exploration of the human psyche. Robb masterfully weaves together the complexities of human nature, revealing the darker aspects that lie beneath the surface of even the most seemingly ordinary individuals. The killer's motives are expertly crafted, making it impossible for the reader to guess their identity until the very end.

Robb's writing style is, as always, impeccable. Her use of language is concise and evocative, painting vivid pictures of the characters and settings. The pacing is expertly controlled, with a steady stream of twists and turns that keep the reader on the edge of their seat.

One of the most striking aspects of Indulgence in Death is its exploration of the corrupting influence of wealth and power. The killer's victims are all individuals who cater to the rich and famous, and as Eve delves deeper into the investigation, she uncovers a web of deceit and corruption that goes all the way to the top.

Throughout the book, Robb's trademark wit and humor are on full display. The banter between Eve and her partner Peabody is, as always, a highlight of the book, providing a much-needed respite from the darkness of the plot.

In conclusion, Indulgence in Death is a gripping and thought-provoking thriller that is sure to appeal to fans of the In Death series. With its expertly crafted plot, vivid characters, and thought-provoking themes, it is a must-read for anyone who enjoys a good mystery.

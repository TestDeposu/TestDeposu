# Claimed by Shadow (Cassandra Palmer, #2)

**Author:** Karen Chance  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

In "Claimed by Shadow," the second installment of the Cassandra Palmer series, Karen Chance weaves a captivating narrative that delves into the complexities of power, loyalty, and the blurred lines between good and evil. As Cassie Palmer navigates the treacherous landscape of the supernatural world, she finds herself entangled in a web of faerie politics, ancient magic, and forgotten loyalties.

Chance's writing style is a masterclass in world-building, as she effortlessly expands the universe introduced in the first book. The faerie realms are richly detailed, with a history that spans centuries, and a complex hierarchy that is both fascinating and deadly. The author's use of language is evocative, transporting the reader to a world of wonder and magic, where the stakes are high, and the players are not always what they seem.

At the heart of the story is Cassie's relationship with John Pritkin, the part-fey war mage who is fighting for the throne of a great light fey kingdom. Their bond is tested by the treacherous landscape of faerie politics, where allegiances are forged and broken with ease. Chance expertly explores the tensions between Cassie's loyalty to Pritkin and her growing sense of self, as she navigates the complexities of her own power and identity.

One of the strengths of Chance's writing is her ability to create complex, multidimensional characters. Cassie is a strong and determined protagonist, but she is also vulnerable and often unsure of herself. Pritkin, too, is a nuanced character, driven by a desire to protect Cassie and the world from the forces of darkness. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with a range of characters who add depth and richness to the narrative.

The plot is fast-paced and engaging, with a narrative that twists and turns in unexpected ways. Chance is a master of the cliffhanger, and the book ends on a dramatic note that will leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment. The themes of power, loyalty, and identity are woven throughout the narrative, adding depth and complexity to the story.

Overall, "Claimed by Shadow" is a compelling and engaging read, with a richly detailed world, complex characters, and a narrative that will keep you on the edge of your seat. If you're a fan of urban fantasy, faerie lore, or just great storytelling, this book is a must-read.

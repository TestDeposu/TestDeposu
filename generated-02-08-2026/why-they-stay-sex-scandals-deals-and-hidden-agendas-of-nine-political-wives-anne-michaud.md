# Why They Stay: Sex Scandals, Deals, and Hidden Agendas of Nine Political Wives

**Author:** Anne Michaud  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In "Why They Stay: Sex Scandals, Deals, and Hidden Agendas of Nine Political Wives," veteran journalist Anne Michaud pulls back the curtain on the intricate lives of some of the most formidable women in modern politics. With a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the complexities of human relationships, Michaud reveals the calculated decisions and deliberate choices made by these wives to maintain their grip on power, pursue their personal ambitions, and salvage their family names in the face of scandal and infamy.

Through a series of in-depth case studies, Michaud masterfully weaves together the stories of eight remarkable women, each one more fascinating than the last. From the seemingly perfect facade of the Trump marriage to the tragic downfall of the Vitter household, Michaud's reporting shines a light on the messy realities of these high-profile relationships. With a keen attention to detail, she excavates the inner lives of her subjects, revealing the hidden agendas and calculated machinations that drive their decisions.

One of the most striking aspects of "Why They Stay" is Michaud's willingness to challenge the notion that these women are powerless pawns in the games played by their powerful husbands. Rather, they emerge as shrewd operators, leveraging their positions to achieve their own goals and secure their places in the spotlight. This is a far cry from the passive, sacrificial stereotypes often trotted out to describe these women, and Michaud's nuanced portrayals are a welcome corrective.

Michaud's own background as a journalist adds depth and authority to her writing, as she draws on her extensive experience covering the high-stakes world of politics. Her writing is crisp and engaging, with a narrative drive that propels the reader through even the most complex and disturbing subjects. She is unsparing in her judgments, but also compassionate and empathetic, never reducing her subjects to simple caricatures or stereotypes.

One of the most intriguing questions that underlies "Why They Stay" is what motivates these women to stay with their husbands, even in the face of scandal and infamy. Is it a desire for security, a need for validation, or something deeper and more primal? Michaud's reporting suggests that the answer is often a complex mix of all three, as these women navigate the treacherous waters of public opinion, political expediency, and personal ambition.

Ultimately, "Why They Stay" is a fascinating and thought-provoking exploration of the intricate web of relationships that underpin modern politics. By shining a light on the hidden agendas and calculated machinations of these women, Michaud invites us to reconsider our assumptions about the nature of power, ambition, and the human heart.

As a journalist and a reader, I was captivated by Michaud's reporting, which is both meticulously researched and accessibly written. Her willingness to challenge prevailing assumptions about these women is a hallmark of a true journalist, and her writing is a testament to her skill and her passion. If you're interested in politics, feminism, or simply the human condition, "Why They Stay" is a must-read.

# Running with the Demon (Word & Void, #1)

**Author:** Terry Brooks  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1997

"Running with the Demon" by Terry Brooks is a mesmerizing tale that delves into the eternal conflict between good and evil, played out against the backdrop of a sweltering Fourth of July weekend in the small town of Hopewell, Illinois. This gripping novel masterfully weaves together elements of family drama, fading innocence, and cataclysmic events, culminating in a rich and exquisitely written tale that will forever change the way you think about fantasy fiction.

At its core, "Running with the Demon" is a story about the struggle between the Word and the Void, two opposing forces that have shaped the world since the dawn of time. The Word represents creation, hope, and redemption, while the Void embodies destruction, chaos, and despair. This epic conflict is personified in the two main characters: John Ross, a Knight of the Word, and a demon, a dark servant of the Void.

Brooks' masterful storytelling brings these characters to life, imbuing them with depth and complexity. John Ross, a man with the ability to see the future, is a compelling protagonist, driven by a sense of purpose and duty to protect the innocent and vanquish the forces of evil. The demon, on the other hand, is a malevolent force, feeding on the anger and frustration of the community, and manipulating events to achieve its sinister goals.

One of the most striking aspects of "Running with the Demon" is its ability to balance the fantastical elements with a sense of realism. Brooks' vivid descriptions of the small town of Hopewell, with its steel strike, family dramas, and Fourth of July celebrations, create a sense of authenticity that makes the supernatural events all the more believable and unsettling.

The author's writing style is equally impressive, with a narrative that flows effortlessly, drawing the reader into the world of the story. Brooks' use of language is evocative and engaging, conjuring up images of the sweltering summer heat, the fireworks exploding in the night sky, and the sense of foreboding that hangs over the town like a dark cloud.

At the heart of the story is the fate of a fourteen-year-old girl, mysteriously linked to both John Ross and the demon. Her innocence and vulnerability serve as a catalyst for the events that unfold, and her ultimate fate hangs precariously in the balance.

"Running with the Demon" is a thought-provoking novel that explores the nature of good and evil, the power of redemption, and the resilience of the human spirit. It is a story that will keep you on the edge of your seat, invested in the characters and the outcome, and lingering long after the final page is turned.

In conclusion, "Running with the Demon" is a masterpiece of fantasy fiction, a must-read for fans of the genre. With its richly woven narrative, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes, this novel is sure to leave a lasting impression on readers.

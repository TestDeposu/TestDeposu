# A Shadow on the Glass (The View from the Mirror, #1)

**Author:** Ian Irvine  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

A Shadow on the Glass, the first installment in Ian Irvine's epic fantasy series, The View from the Mirror, is a masterful tale of magic, mystery, and adventure. This 1998 Aurealis Award-nominated novel has captivated readers with its intricate world-building, complex characters, and a gripping narrative that spans centuries.

At the heart of the story are two unlikely heroes, Llian and Karan, whose lives become inextricably linked as they navigate the treacherous landscape of their world. Llian, a gifted Tale-spinner, is a brilliant storyteller but struggles with everyday life, while Karan, a sensitive with a family cursed by madness, is driven by a sense of honor to steal an ancient magical device. The Mirror of Aachan, a powerful artifact with a deadly secret, becomes the focal point of their quest, as they must reach the Magister in Thurkad before it falls into the wrong hands.

Irvine's writing style is a perfect blend of descriptive prose, clever dialogue, and pulse-pounding action sequences. His world-building is meticulous, with a deep history that underpins the narrative, making the world feel richly textured and immersive. The characters are multidimensional and relatable, with flaws and strengths that make them endearing to the reader.

One of the standout features of A Shadow on the Glass is its exploration of the human condition. Irvine delves into themes of ambition, honor, and the corrupting influence of power, raising questions about the true cost of progress and the nature of morality. Through Llian and Karan's struggles, we see the consequences of playing with forces beyond human control and the devastating impact of unchecked ambition.

The pacing of the novel is well-balanced, with moments of quiet introspection and explosive action sequences that keep the reader on the edge of their seat. Irvine's use of magic is clever and innovative, with a system that feels both logical and mysterious, adding to the sense of wonder and awe that permeates the narrative.

Overall, A Shadow on the Glass is a compelling epic fantasy that will appeal to fans of authors like Terry Brooks, Robert Jordan, and Robin Hobb. With its intricate world-building, complex characters, and gripping narrative, this novel is a must-read for anyone looking to escape into a richly imagined world of magic and adventure.

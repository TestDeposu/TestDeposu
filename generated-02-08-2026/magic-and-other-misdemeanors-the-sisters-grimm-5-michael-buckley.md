# Magic and Other Misdemeanors (The Sisters Grimm, #5)

**Author:** Michael Buckley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

In the fifth installment of the beloved Sisters Grimm series, Michael Buckley masterfully weaves a captivating tale of mystery, magic, and self-discovery. "Magic and Other Misdemeanors" is a thrilling adventure that follows the Grimm sisters, Sabrina and Daphne, as they navigate the complexities of their fairy-tale world and confront the harsh realities of growing up.

The story is set against the backdrop of a series of magical thefts that threaten the very fabric of the Grimm family's existence. With their grandmother, Granny Relda, struggling to pay the exorbitant taxes imposed by the tyrannical Mayor Heart, the sisters take it upon themselves to solve the mystery and save their family from financial ruin. Buckley skillfully crafts a narrative that is both suspenseful and humorous, with the sisters' banter and witty observations providing a delightful counterpoint to the darker themes of the story.

One of the standout features of the Sisters Grimm series is its unique blend of fairy-tale fantasy and real-world issues. Buckley tackles complex themes such as identity, loyalty, and the struggle for power with remarkable sensitivity and nuance. The character of Mr. Canis, who is slowly losing his grip on his human self and succumbing to his inner Big Bad Wolf, serves as a poignant reminder of the blurred lines between good and evil.

Buckley's writing style is engaging and accessible, making the book an absolute pleasure to read. His use of language is rich and evocative, with vivid descriptions of the fairy-tale world that transport the reader to a realm of wonder and magic. The characters are multidimensional and relatable, with the sisters' distinct personalities and quirks adding depth and humor to the story.

The author's ability to balance action, suspense, and humor is impressive, and the pacing of the story is expertly managed. The plot twists and turns, keeping the reader guessing until the very end, and the resolution is both satisfying and heartwarming.

Overall, "Magic and Other Misdemeanors" is a delightful addition to the Sisters Grimm series. With its unique blend of fairy-tale fantasy and real-world issues, engaging characters, and suspenseful plot, it is sure to captivate readers of all ages. Buckley's writing style is engaging, accessible, and richly evocative, making this book a must-read for fans of the series and newcomers alike.

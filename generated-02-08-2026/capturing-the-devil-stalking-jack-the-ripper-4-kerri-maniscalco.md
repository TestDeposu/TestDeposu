# Capturing the Devil (Stalking Jack the Ripper, #4)

**Author:** Kerri Maniscalco  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

Darkness, deception, and undeniable chemistry collide in Capturing the Devil, the thrilling fourth and final installment of Kerri Maniscalco’s beloved Stalking Jack the Ripper series. Released in 2016, this novel brings the harrowing journey of Audrey Rose Wadsworth and Thomas Cresswell to a chilling crescendo. Against the eerie, gaslit backdrop of late-Victorian Chicago and its notorious World's Fair, Maniscalco delivers a dark historical thriller that brilliantly merges forensic science, psychological terror, and a romance for the ages.

At the heart of the novel is the exploration of obsession—both the brilliant, analytical obsession that drives forensic investigation and the darker, more depraved fixations of the human psyche. Maniscalco uses the glittering facade of the 1893 Chicago World's Fair to underscore a central thematic contrast: the dazzling progress of the modern age walking hand-in-hand with the primordial shadows of human wickedness. Inspired by America's first documented serial killer, the narrative delves deep into the monstrous lengths to which vanity, greed, and a warped sense of power can lead an individual. Audrey Rose and Thomas are once again forced to confront not only a cunning murderer but also the terrifying depths of their own fears, particularly the fear of losing one another in a world that seems determined to tear them apart.

Maniscalco’s authorial style remains one of the strongest assets of the series. Her prose is lush, atmospheric, and unashamedly theatrical, perfectly capturing the sensory overload of the Victorian era. From the antiseptic smell of formaldehyde in a damp laboratory to the electric, dizzying excitement of the World’s Fair midways, every setting is rendered with meticulous detail. The pacing is relentless, balancing high-stakes investigations with the delicious, slow-burn tension of its central romance. Maniscalco excels at writing banter that is as sharp as a scalpel, ensuring that even in the face of unspeakable horror, Audrey Rose and Thomas maintain their razor-sharp intellects and undeniable charm. 

Furthermore, the character development reaches its emotional peak in this book. Audrey Rose is not just a strong female lead for the sake of modern convention; her struggles with societal expectations, grief, and the psychological toll of her gruesome vocation feel authentic and grounded. Her partnership with Thomas Cresswell remains a gold standard in young adult literature—a true meeting of equals based on mutual respect, intellectual sparring, and fierce, unwavering devotion. 

Capturing the Devil is a triumphant conclusion to a phenomenal series. It satisfies readers with a blood-chilling mystery, answers long-awaited questions, and delivers an emotional payoff that will linger long after the final page is turned. For fans of historical fiction, atmospheric horror, and gothic romance, Maniscalco’s latest offering is an absolute must-read that proves the darkest monsters often wear the most charming disguises.

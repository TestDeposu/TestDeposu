# The Legacy (Forgotten Realms: Legacy of the Drow, #1; Legend of Drizzt, #7)

**Author:** R.A. Salvatore  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**A Haunting Legacy: Exploring the Darkest Corners of the Underdark**

R.A. Salvatore, the masterful chronicler of the Forgotten Realms universe, weaves an intricate web of intrigue and danger in his latest installment, _The Legacy_. This gripping novel marks the beginning of the _Legacy of the Drow_ series, as a dark and malevolent force stirs in the darkness of the Underdark, threatening to upend the fragile peace Drizzt Do'Urden and his friends have finally achieved.

As the story unfolds, it becomes clear that no individual is truly safe from the wrath of Lolth, the capricious goddess of the demon-spider Moonsymbols. Her followers, zealous and relentless, will stop at nothing to reclaim the soul of the renegade drow, who has consistently evaded the goddess's grasp. The very mention of Lolth's name sends shivers down the spines of her followers, and her power is woven into every aspect of their culture and society.

Drizzt, the iconic hero, must once again find himself at the forefront of a battle for survival, as the forces of darkness converge on Mithral Hall. His life has been filled with turmoil and bloodshed, yet he remains an unyielding spirit, forged in the fires of adversity. However, even the legendary drow is not immune to the insidious influence of Lolth, whose presence can be felt lurking in the shadows, waiting to strike.

Salvatore's mastery of the Forgotten Realms universe is on full display in this novel. His writing is imbued with a deep understanding of the world he has crafted, and the intricate web of relationships between characters and factions is as complex as ever. The Underdark is brought to life with vivid descriptions of its dark, foreboding landscapes, where the whispers of ancient rituals and the stench of death hang heavy in the air.

While _The Legacy_ can be read on its own, it is also a pivotal installment in the _Legend of Drizzt_ series, and it's essential to be familiar with the events that have led up to this point. The novel's plot twists and turns, expertly balancing action and suspense, and its character development is both satisfying and emotionally resonant.

Salvatore's style is characterized by his lyrical prose and a narrative voice that is both introspective and cinematic. He has a gift for imbuing his characters with a depth and richness that makes them feel lived-in and relatable. Drizzt, in particular, remains an endearing and compelling protagonist, whose growth and development throughout the series is truly remarkable.

As the stakes grow higher and the threat of Lolth's wrath looms closer, _The Legacy_ becomes an unrelenting page-turner, hurtling towards a climactic confrontation that will leave readers on the edge of their seats. Salvatore has proven once again why he is a revered figure in the world of fantasy literature, and his latest novel is a testament to his skill as a storyteller.

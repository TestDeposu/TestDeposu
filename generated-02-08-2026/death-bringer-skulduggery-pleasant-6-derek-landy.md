# Death Bringer (Skulduggery Pleasant, #6)

**Author:** Derek Landy  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

**Death Bringer: Where the Dead Don’t Stay Buried**

Derek Landy’s *Death Bringer*—the sixth installment in the *Skulduggery Pleasant* series—is a high-stakes, darkly comic rollercoaster that proves the author’s knack for blending razor-sharp wit with bone-chilling fantasy. At its core, this book is a battle against the very fabric of existence, where the line between life and death is about to be irrevocably shattered. The Necromancers, those shadowy architects of the afterlife, have found their ultimate weapon: a Death Bringer, a being capable of unraveling the doors between worlds. And when that door swings open, nothing—not love, not magic, not even death itself—can stop the chaos that follows.

Landy’s genius lies in his ability to make the macabre feel fresh, the supernatural feel tangible, and the absurd feel inevitable. Skulduggery Pleasant, the undead detective with a penchant for top hats and sarcasm, remains as charismatic as ever, but it’s the new threats that truly elevate this story. The Necromancers’ new Death Bringer isn’t just a villain—it’s an existential threat, a force that could drown the world in eternal twilight. Landy doesn’t shy away from the horror of what’s at stake, but he balances it with his signature humor, ensuring that even in the darkest moments, there’s a smirk lurking just beneath the surface.

The pacing is relentless, a hallmark of Landy’s writing. There’s no room for boredom here—every chapter crackles with tension, whether it’s a high-speed chase through the afterlife or a battle of wits between Skulduggery and a villain who sees death as a game. The world-building is rich, too, with the Necromancers’ domain feeling as vivid as any other realm in fantasy. But what truly makes *Death Bringer* stand out is its emotional core. Landy doesn’t just drop his characters into peril; he makes their struggles feel personal, their victories feel earned, and their losses sting.

For fans of *Skulduggery Pleasant*, this book delivers all the hallmarks of the series: clever banter, unexpected twists, and a villain who’s equal parts terrifying and hilarious. But it also takes a bold step forward, exploring themes of mortality, sacrifice, and the cost of power. The Necromancers’ obsession with control is a chilling reminder that some forces should never be unleashed—and Landy doesn’t let his readers forget it.

If you’ve ever wondered what happens when the rules of death are rewritten, *Death Bringer* is your answer. It’s a book that keeps you up late into the night, flipping pages faster than you can blink, because you *have* to know what happens next. And when you finally reach the end, you’ll be left with that perfect mix of satisfaction and anticipation—because in Landy’s world, the dead never stay buried for long.

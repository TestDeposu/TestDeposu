# Good Boundaries and Goodbyes: Loving Others Without Losing the Best of Who You Are

**Author:** Lysa TerKeurst  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

In the world of relationships, we often find ourselves caught between the desire to love and be loved, and the need to protect ourselves from the toxic dynamics that can arise. It's a delicate balance, one that can be challenging to maintain, especially when we're convinced that setting boundaries or saying goodbye is, at best, selfish, and at worst, a betrayal of our faith.

Enter Lysa TerKeurst, the renowned author of "Good Boundaries and Goodbyes: Loving Others Without Losing the Best of Who You Are". With her characteristic vulnerability, empathy, and biblical insight, TerKeurst tackles this complex issue with unflinching honesty and compassion. As a seasoned counselor and author, she knows that relationships can be a double-edged sword – the capacity to bring immense joy and connection, but also the potential to drain our energy, erode our self-worth, and compromise our values.

TerKeurst's central argument is that boundaries are not a bad thing; in fact, they're a gift from God. She skillfully dismantles the misguided notion that setting boundaries is somehow unbiblical or unloving, instead revealing that, quite the contrary, it's a fundamental expression of self-love, respect, and compassion for others. With her characteristic wit and humor, TerKeurst reminds us that "good boundaries are not meant to be a barrier to love, but a bridge to healthy love".

Throughout the book, TerKeurst offers practical advice and insightful case studies drawn from her extensive experience as a counselor. She explores the five factors to remember when implementing healthy boundaries, equips readers with realistic scripts and practical strategies for healthier communication, and provides a much-needed biblical framework for understanding the importance of boundaries in relationships.

One of the standout features of this book is its inclusive approach – TerKeurst acknowledges that setting boundaries can be a challenging and painful process, particularly for those who have survived complex trauma or abusive relationships. While avoiding jargon and technical language, she remains firmly grounded in the psychological and theological principles that undergird her argument.

TerKeurst also benefits from the expert insights of her Christian counselor, Jim Cress, who offers invaluable wisdom and expertise to complement her own writing. Together, they provide a rich tapestry of perspectives, weaving together theology, psychology, and personal experience to create a holistic approach to relationships.

As we journey through the book, we're encouraged to see boundaries as a necessary aspect of our humanity – a fundamental expression of our God-given value, dignity, and worth. We learn to recognize the signs of unhealthy relationships and to identify the often-subtle ways in which they can erode our well-being and relationships.

Ultimately, TerKeurst's book offers a powerful reminder that boundaries are not a luxury, but a non-negotiable aspect of our journey towards wholeness, healing, and spiritual growth. By learning to set and maintain healthy boundaries, we're empowered to live more authentic, vibrant lives – lives that reflect the beauty and goodness of who we are, created in the image of our loving God.

With its gentle yet firm guidance, insightful case studies, and rich biblical wisdom, "Good Boundaries and Goodbyes" is a must-read for anyone navigating complex relationships, seeking to grow in their faith, or simply looking to cultivate healthier, more compassionate relationships with others.

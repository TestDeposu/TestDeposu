# Deliver Us from Evil (A. Shaw, #2)

**Author:** David Baldacci  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**A Thrilling Cat-and-Mouse Chase: Unveiling the Dark Underbelly of Power and Greed in "Deliver Us from Evil"**

David Baldacci's 2010 novel, "Deliver Us from Evil," is a masterfully crafted thriller that delves into the dark recesses of human nature, where the pursuit of power and wealth knows no bounds. In this gripping tale, the author expertly weaves a complex web of suspense, intrigue, and deception, transporting readers to a world where the lines between right and wrong are constantly blurred.

At the heart of the story is Evan Waller, a ruthless entrepreneur who has made a fortune by buying and selling people, objects, and ideas with complete disregard for their value or worth. His latest venture promises to be his most lucrative yet, one that could lead to the deaths of millions of people worldwide. With his trail in Provence, Waller becomes the target of not one but two formidable adversaries: Shaw, a mysterious intelligence operative, and Reggie Campion, an agent for a secret vigilante group.

Baldacci's skillful characterization of Shaw and Reggie raises the stakes, as these two enigmatic figures, driven by their own agendas, engage in a high-stakes game of cat and mouse. As they close in on Waller, the tension builds, and the reader is left questioning whose motives are pure and whose are morally ambiguous.

One of the standout features of "Deliver Us from Evil" is Baldacci's expert handling of suspense. He skillfully employs a slow-burning tension, expertly calibrated to keep readers on the edge of their seats. Just when they think they know where the story is headed, Baldacci unleashes a bombshell plot twist, leaving them reeling in its wake.

In many ways, "Deliver Us from Evil" is a commentary on the power-hungry and unscrupulous individuals who lurk in the shadows, pulling the strings of global politics. Through the character of Waller, Baldacci exposes the dark underbelly of greed, demonstrating how the relentless pursuit of wealth and influence can lead individuals down a path of destruction and devastation.

Baldacci's writing style, reminiscent of the classic thriller masters, is both evocative and accessible. His use of descriptive language paints vivid pictures, immersing readers in the world of the story. The pacing is relentless, with Baldacci seamlessly shifting between intense action sequences and introspective moments of character development.

Ultimately, "Deliver Us from Evil" is a heart-pumping thriller that explores the darker aspects of human nature. With its intricate plot, complex characters, and expertly crafted suspense, this novel is sure to captivate fans of the genre. David Baldacci's masterful storytelling will keep you on the edge of your seat, wondering what's next and who will emerge victorious from this deadly game.

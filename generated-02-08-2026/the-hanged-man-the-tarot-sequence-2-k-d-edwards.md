# The Hanged Man (The Tarot Sequence, #2)

**Author:** K.D. Edwards  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

In K.D. Edwards' sophomore novel, The Hanged Man, we find ourselves once again thrust into the mystical realm of The Tarot Sequence, where the remnants of a forgotten world still whisper secrets to those brave enough to listen. This time, Edwards shines a piercing light on the darkest corners of Camelot-inspired Atlantis, governed by the omnipotent Courts of the Tarot. Rune Saint John, last scion of the fallen Sun Throne, steps into the fray with a ferocity that will captivate even the most stalwart of readers.

Edwards masterfully weaves a rich tapestry of intrigue and deception, as Rune fights to protect his unwitting ward, Max, from the clutches of the enigmatic Hanged Man. This forced marital arrangement poses a threat not only to Max's well-being but also to Rune's tenuous grasp on his own tortured past. A labyrinth of secrets, both past and present, is slowly unraveled as Rune embarks on a perilous journey through the twisted underbelly of Atlantis. From the macabre spectacle of ghost ships moored in the city's infamous red light district to the enigmatic Arcana inhabiting the hallowed halls of the ruling Convocation, Edwards plunges us headfirst into an uncharted world of high court magic and Machiavellian intrigue.

One of Edwards' greatest strengths lies in her ability to seamlessly blend elements of fantasy and mystery, crafting a narrative that is equal parts enthralling and unnerving. As Rune navigates the treacherous landscape of Atlantis, we find ourselves entwined in his every step, desperate to unravel the mysteries hidden beneath the surface. Edwards' skillful handling of the Tarot-inspired world and its inhabitants imbues the narrative with an air of mystique, drawing us deeper into the heart of the story with each passing page.

Rune himself is a complex, multidimensional figure, forged from the embers of a world that has ravaged him. Haunted by memories of his fallen House and driven by a deep-seated need to shield Max from the very fate that has befallen him, Rune embodies the very essence of human struggle. His unwavering dedication to those he cares for is both inspiring and heartbreaking, serving as a poignant reminder that even in a world governed by the whims of the powerful, there is always a glimmer of hope to be found.

Ultimately, The Hanged Man is a testament to Edwards' mastery of the fantasy genre, delivering a tale of darkness and redemption that will leave readers breathless and begging for more. As the curtain falls on this latest installment of The Tarot Sequence, we are left with a burning sense of anticipation for what lies ahead. Will Rune's quest for truth and justice be enough to pierce the veil of deceit shrouding Atlantis, or will the very fabric of his world be forever changed by the forces of power and corruption?

In conclusion, The Hanged Man is a triumph of fantasy and mystery, one that will captivate readers with its unique blend of Tarot-inspired world-building and unforgettable characters. Edwards' masterful command of the narrative and her ability to weave complex themes into a tapestry of intrigue make this a must-read for fans of the genre.

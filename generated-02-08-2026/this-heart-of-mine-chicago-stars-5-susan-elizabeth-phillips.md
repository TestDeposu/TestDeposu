# This Heart of Mine (Chicago Stars, #5)

**Author:** Susan Elizabeth Phillips  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

This Heart of Mine, the fifth installment in Susan Elizabeth Phillips' beloved Chicago Stars series, is a captivating tale of love, self-discovery, and the power of vulnerability. Phillips' masterful storytelling weaves a complex narrative that delves into the intricacies of the human heart, making this novel an unforgettable read.

At the center of the story is Molly Somerville, a talented children's book author with a penchant for trouble and a long-standing crush on Kevin Tucker, the charming quarterback of the Chicago Stars. When Kevin bursts into Molly's life, she is forced to confront her feelings and the fears that have held her back for so long. As they embark on a journey to Wind Lake, a picturesque town filled with quaint cottages and a charming bed-and-breakfast, Molly and Kevin must navigate their undeniable attraction and the emotional baggage that accompanies it.

Phillips' writing style is a perfect blend of wit, humor, and poignancy, making it easy to become fully invested in the lives of her characters. Her ability to craft relatable, flawed, and endearing protagonists is a hallmark of her writing, and Molly and Kevin are no exception. As they struggle to come to terms with their past and their feelings for each other, Phillips skillfully exposes the vulnerabilities that make them human, rendering their love story all the more believable and compelling.

One of the most striking aspects of This Heart of Mine is its exploration of the human capacity for love and forgiveness. Phillips tackles complex themes such as grief, guilt, and redemption with sensitivity and nuance, creating a narrative that is both heart-wrenching and uplifting. Through Molly and Kevin's story, she reminds us that love can be a powerful catalyst for healing and growth, but it also requires effort, patience, and a willingness to confront our deepest fears.

The author's use of setting also deserves mention, as the charming town of Wind Lake becomes a character in its own right, providing a tranquil backdrop for Molly and Kevin's emotional journey. Phillips' vivid descriptions of the town's quaint cottages, serene lake, and picturesque surroundings effectively transport the reader to a world that is both idyllic and authentic.

In conclusion, This Heart of Mine is a beautifully crafted novel that will resonate with fans of romance, women's fiction, and contemporary drama. Phillips' engaging storytelling, coupled with her well-developed characters and nuanced exploration of complex themes, make this book a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and emotionally satisfying story.

# Sleeping Giants (Themis Files, #1)

**Author:** Sylvain Neuvel  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

In Sylvain Neuvel's electrifying debut novel, Sleeping Giants, the lines between science, history, and power are blurred, leading to a thrilling narrative that delves into the most fundamental questions of human existence. This captivating tale, which is the first installment in the Themis Files series, is a masterful blend of science fiction, conspiracy theories, and apocalyptic fiction that will keep readers enthralled from start to finish.

At its core, Sleeping Giants is a story about discovery, and the true cost of knowledge. When a young girl named Rose accidentally falls into a mysterious hole in South Dakota, she unwittingly uncovers a relic of unimagineable proportions - a giant metal hand that defies human creation and understanding. Seventeen years later, Rose has grown up to be a highly skilled physicist, leading a top-secret team to unravel the secrets of the hand. As she and her colleagues delve deeper into the mysteries of the relic, they begin to realize the enormity of their discovery and the potential consequences it could have on humanity.

Neuvel's writing is characterized by its accessibility and clarity, making the complex scientific concepts and historical theories presented in the novel feel effortless to follow. The author's use of an interview format as the primary narrative device adds tension and intimacy to the story, as Rose and her colleagues are grilled by a mysterious interrogator whose true intentions remain unclear until the very end. This narrative technique also serves to underscore the sense of uncertainty and danger that pervades the novel, as the characters are constantly walking the tightrope between scientific exploration and military intervention.

One of the most striking aspects of Sleeping Giants is its exploration of the intersection of science and power. The novel raises uncomfortable questions about the responsibilities that come with scientific knowledge, and the lengths to which humanity will go to harness and control it. Neuvel's characters are well-developed and complex, with distinct personalities and motivations that drive the plot forward with a sense of urgency and momentum.

Throughout the novel, Neuvel weaves together a rich tapestry of historical and scientific allusions, drawing on the works of authors such as Michael Crichton and Max Brooks to create a unique and captivating narrative voice. The author's use of sci-fi and conspiracy theories adds an air of authenticity to the novel, making it feel like a genuine exploration of the kind of secrets and lies that governments and institutions might be hiding.

If there's a criticism to be made, it's that the novel's pacing can feel a tad uneven at times, with some plot developments unfolding at a breakneck speed, while others feel a bit slower. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise exceptional novel that will keep fans of science fiction and thriller readers alike on the edge of their seats.

In conclusion, Sleeping Giants is a triumph of science fiction and a must-read for fans of the genre. Sylvain Neuvel's writing is sharp, engaging, and effortless to follow, making this debut novel a true standout. The author's exploration of the intersection of science and power raises important questions about human responsibility and the consequences of discovery, making for a thought-provoking and unforgettable reading experience.

# Awakening the Fire (Guardian Witch, #1)

**Author:** Ally Shields  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

In Awakening the Fire, the inaugural volume of the Guardian Witch series, author Ally Shields delivers an atmospheric and deeply introspective descent into the hidden currents of the occult. Set against the biting, mist-shrouded backdrop of northern Maine, this 2026 release transcends the traditional boundaries of contemporary urban fantasy by anchoring its supernatural thrills in genuine esoteric traditions, ancient Norse mechanics, and high-vibrational spellwork. 

The narrative immediately establishes a tone of palpable dread and spiritual gravity through its protagonist, Raven Torbjorn. Unlike the invincible, action-hero archetypes that frequently dominate the genre, Raven is wonderfully and tragically human. Burdened by physical wear and tear and carrying the invisible weight of past spiritual battles, he is a protector who bleeds, doubts, and tires. Shields handles Raven with profound empathy, painting him as a man caught between the physical world and the unseen realms, relying on ancient Seidr trance magic not out of arrogance, but out of absolute necessity.

Beside him is Rose, his intuitive anchor and soulmate. Their dynamic forms the emotional beating heart of the novel. When a harrowing encounter at a manifested sanctuary near a forgotten burial ground on Route 2A unleashes an ancient, soul-hungry entity, the stakes instantly skyrocket. Shields does not merely rely on jump scares or standard monster-under-the-bed tropes. Instead, she crafts a creeping, autumnal dread that thickens as the veil thins ahead of Halloween. The confrontations with malevolent forces are visceral, blending the visceral terror of creature features with the intricate, ceremonial precision of Egyptian spellwork.

Yet, the true brilliance of Awakening the Fire lies in its subversion of expectations. While the external threat—a primordial entity clawing its way out of Route 2A—provides relentless pacing and high-stakes danger, Shields reserves her most compelling conflict for the internal landscape. The greatest trial Raven faces is not the shadow lurking in the timber, but the unsparing mirror of truth forced upon his spirit. The narrative bravely forces Raven to confront years of suppressed pain, hidden sacrifices, and the suffocating remnants of a toxic marriage he can no longer endure. 

This thematic focus on emotional alchemy elevates the novel from a standard supernatural thriller into a poignant exploration of healing and rebirth. Shields uses the framework of magic as a metaphor for psychological liberation. To protect the woman who holds his soul, Raven must undergo a metaphorical death and resurrection, stripping away worldly illusions and severing the heavy chains of his past. 

Shields' writing style is lush, deliberate, and immersive. She treats esoteric practices with a reverence that gives the text an authentic weight, pulling readers directly into the sensory details of ritual circles, chilling northern winds, and the heavy scent of pine and ancient earth. The prose is striking in its ability to balance high-concept magical theory with raw, emotional vulnerability. 

Awakening the Fire is a confident, mesmerizing opening salvo for the Guardian Witch series. It will captivate fans of dark magical realism, spiritual fantasy, and character-driven occult adventures. Ally Shields has crafted a world where the monsters in the woods are terrifying, but the courage it takes to heal one's own soul is the most powerful magic of all.

# The Air He Breathes (Elements, #1)

**Author:** Brittainy C. Cherry  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

"The Air He Breathes" by Brittainy C. Cherry is a poignant and thought-provoking novel that delves into the complexities of human emotions, trauma, and the power of connection. This heart-wrenching tale of two damaged souls, Tristan and Elizabeth, will leave readers spellbound and invested in their journey towards healing and redemption.

The author masterfully weaves a narrative that explores the darker aspects of life, tackling themes of grief, loss, and the long-lasting effects of trauma. Cherry's writing is evocative and expressive, with a keen sense of observation that brings the characters to life. Her prose is lyrical, yet unflinching, making it impossible to look away from the painful realities that Tristan and Elizabeth face.

One of the most striking aspects of this novel is its portrayal of the human condition. Cherry shows us that even in the midst of unimaginable pain, there is always hope. Elizabeth, in particular, is a testament to the resilience of the human spirit. Her determination to keep living, to keep breathing, is a powerful reminder that we are stronger than we think. Tristan, on the other hand, is a complex and brooding character, whose slow-burning intensity simmers just below the surface. His struggles to connect with the world around him are deeply relatable, and his eventual thawing is a testament to the transformative power of human connection.

The chemistry between Tristan and Elizabeth is undeniable, and their relationship is fraught with tension, emotion, and a deep-seated longing. Cherry handles their romance with care, never shying away from the difficulties that they face. Instead, she confronts them head-on, creating a narrative that is both authentic and deeply moving.

The supporting cast of characters is equally well-crafted, with a small-town community that is both supportive and suffocating. The author's portrayal of the intricate web of relationships that exists in small towns is spot on, highlighting the ways in which our lives are intertwined, and the ways in which our actions can have far-reaching consequences.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the pacing of the novel can be uneven at times. Some scenes feel a little rushed, while others are drawn out, creating a sense of disjointedness. However, this is a minor quibble in what is otherwise a masterful novel.

In conclusion, "The Air He Breathes" is a stunning debut from Brittainy C. Cherry. It is a novel that will break your heart, but also make you believe in the power of love, forgiveness, and redemption. With its beautiful prose, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes, this book is a must-read for anyone who enjoys contemporary romance, drama, or just great storytelling in general.

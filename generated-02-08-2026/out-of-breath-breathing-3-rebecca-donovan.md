# Out of Breath (Breathing, #3)

**Author:** Rebecca Donovan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

In Rebecca Donovan's captivating novel, "Out of Breath," the third installment in her "Breathing" series, the author masterfully weaves a poignant tale of self-discovery, love, and redemption. At its core, the book explores the complex and often fraught journey of forgiveness, as the protagonist, Emma Thomas, grapples with the scars of her past and battles to reclaim her sense of identity.

The story picks up where the previous book left off, with Emma leaving behind the small town of Weslyn and the only true love she has ever known, Evan. As she embarks on a new chapter at the prestigious University of Stanford, Emma is met with a mix of excitement and trepidation. Despite the promise of a brighter future, she finds herself haunted by the demons of her past, struggling to break free from the shackles of her traumatic experiences.

One of Donovan's greatest strengths as an author lies in her ability to craft relatable and flawed characters. Emma's struggles to come to terms with her past, to find solace in the present, and to hope for a better future are deeply resonant and eerily familiar. Her pain and vulnerability are palpable, making it impossible not to become invested in her journey.

Through Emma's narrative, Donovan sheds light on the delicate yet crucial process of forgiveness. This is a story about learning to let go, to release the toxic emotions and memories that have defined you, and to begin anew. The author deftly explores the various routes to healing, from the quiet moments of introspection to the more dramatic confrontations with the past.

Rebecca Donovan's writing style is characterized by its lyrical prose, introspective dialogue, and a keen sense of observation. She has a remarkable ability to conjure the emotional depth and complexity of her characters, rendering their experiences both authentic and compelling. Her narrative is layered with subtlety and nuance, inviting the reader to reflect on their own experiences and emotions.

The love story between Emma and Evan is a central aspect of the novel, and their relationship is portrayed with a tender yet unflinching honesty. Donovan masterfully conveys the intricacies of love, capturing the raw emotion, the vulnerability, and the hope that defines it.

What sets "Out of Breath" apart from other contemporary young adult novels is its unwavering commitment to emotional authenticity. The author tackles the difficult themes of trauma, abuse, and mental health with sensitivity and compassion, never shying away from the harsh realities but never succumbing to gratuitous explicitness either. This approach lends the novel a powerful sense of credibility, making it an essential read for anyone who has ever struggled to find their voice in the midst of pain.

Overall, "Out of Breath" is a heart-wrenching yet ultimately hopeful novel that will leave you breathless. Donna's poignant and often raw exploration of the human condition will stay with you long after you finish reading. With its masterful storytelling, authentic characters, and searing emotional honesty, this stunning novel is a triumphant conclusion to the "Breathing" series.

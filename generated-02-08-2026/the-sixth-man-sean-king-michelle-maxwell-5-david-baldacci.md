# The Sixth Man (Sean King & Michelle Maxwell, #5)

**Author:** David Baldacci  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

"The Sixth Man" by David Baldacci is a masterful thriller that delves into the darker corners of the human psyche, where the lines between truth and deception are constantly blurred. This fifth installment in the Sean King and Michelle Maxwell series is a gripping page-turner that will keep you on the edge of your seat from the opening pages.

At its core, "The Sixth Man" is a story about obsession, deception, and the unyielding pursuit of justice. The protagonist, Sean King, and his partner, Michelle Maxwell, are private investigators tasked with digging into the past of Edgar Roy, a man accused of being a serial killer. However, as they delve deeper into the case, they stumble upon a web of half-truths and conspiracy that threatens to destroy their lives.

Baldacci's writing is characteristically suspenseful and engaging, with a narrative that seamlessly shifts between past and present, expertly weaving together the complex threads of the story. His ability to create a sense of tension and foreboding is unmatched, and he skillfully manipulates the reader's perception of the characters' motives and intentions.

One of the standout features of "The Sixth Man" is its exploration of the themes of power and corruption. Baldacci skillfully exposes the darker side of government and the lengths to which those in power will go to cover their tracks. This is a theme that is all too relevant in today's world, and Baldacci tackles it with his trademark sensitivity and nuance.

Throughout the book, the relationship between Sean and Michelle is a central focus. Their partnership is built on trust, respect, and a deep understanding of each other's strengths and weaknesses. Baldacci has a way of writing these characters that makes them feel like old friends, and their banter and interaction is some of the funniest and most compelling in the series.

The supporting cast in "The Sixth Man" is equally compelling, with a diverse range of characters that add depth and complexity to the narrative. From the enigmatic and sinister figure of Edgar Roy to the more sympathetic characters like Bergin's young protégée, each character is carefully crafted to contribute to the larger story.

If there's one criticism to be made, it's that "The Sixth Man" can be a bit of a wild ride at times. Baldacci's plotting is expertly constructed, but there are moments where the story feels a little too convenient, a little too reliant on coincidence and contrivance. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise excellent thriller.

In conclusion, "The Sixth Man" is a must-read for fans of the thriller genre. Baldacci's writing is masterful, his plotting is expertly crafted, and his characters are some of the most compelling and nuanced in the business. This is a book that will keep you up late at night, turning pages with urgency and wondering what will happen next.

David Baldacci is an author who has perfected the art of crafting a thriller that is both suspenseful and entertaining. If you're a fan of the genre, do yourself a favor and pick up "The Sixth Man". It's a wild ride that will keep you on the edge of your seat from start to finish.

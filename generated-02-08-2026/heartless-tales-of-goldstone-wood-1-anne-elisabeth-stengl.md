# Heartless (Tales of Goldstone Wood, #1)

**Author:** Anne Elisabeth Stengl  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

"Heartless," the debut novel by Anne Elisabeth Stengl, plunges readers into a rich, immersive world of fantasy and adventure. This captivating tale of love, duty, and the unyielding pursuit of bravery, sets the stage for a thrilling series: Tales of Goldstone Wood.

At the heart of the story lies Princess Una, a beautiful and naive heroine whose seemingly carefree spirit belies a complex, emotionally guarded personality. As she navigates the labyrinthine complexities of romance and marriage, Una finds herself increasingly entangled in a web of deceit and danger. Her rejection of Prince Aethelbald's advances serves as a pivotal turning point, catapulting her toward the very precipice of disaster.

Stengl's masterful storytelling weaves a delicate balance between enchanting world-building and compelling characters. In Parumvir, the kingdom's majestic beauty and tranquility belie a dark underbelly of malevolence, as evidenced by the looming threat of the Dragon King. In Farthestshore, Aethelbald's homeland is shrouded in mystery and foreboding, its very existence a harbinger of the impending doom that will soon engulf Parumvir.

One of the most striking features of Stengl's writing is her ability to imbue even the most mundane elements of the story with a sense of wonder and magic. As Una navigates the complexities of courtly life and her own heart, the reader is transported to a realm where the boundaries between reality and fantasy are blurred. This fluidity is one of the most compelling aspects of the book, imbuing every scene with a sense of possibility and enchantment.

Furthermore, Stengl's commitment to Christian allegory is evident throughout the narrative, although it never overtakes the story, subtly informing the characters and their motivations. Una's transformation from a shallow, self-absorbed princess to a more selfless, courageous individual serves as a poignant reminder of the transformative power of faith and love.

While some may criticize Stengl's writing style as overly descriptive or elaborate, it is precisely this attention to detail that adds depth and richness to the world of Goldstone Wood. The book is a testament to the author's meticulous research and imagination, drawing inspiration from a wide range of sources - medieval folklore, fairy tales, and biblical allegory.

Ultimately, "Heartless" is a captivating tale that will leave readers eager for more. It is a powerful reminder that the choices we make in life have far-reaching consequences, and that selfless bravery is often the key to overcoming even the most daunting challenges.

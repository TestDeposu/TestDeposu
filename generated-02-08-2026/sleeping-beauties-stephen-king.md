# Sleeping Beauties

**Author:** Stephen King  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

In the realm of dystopian fiction, few authors have managed to capture the imagination of readers quite like Stephen King. With his latest collaboration with his son Owen King, "Sleeping Beauties" is a thought-provoking and hauntingly relevant novel that delves into the very fabric of our society. This spectacular New York Times bestseller is a wildly imaginative and often unsettling exploration of a world where women suddenly and inexplicably disappear.

The story takes place in a small Appalachian town, where the primary employer is a women's prison. It's here that the mysterious "sleeping disease" begins to manifest, causing women to become shrouded in a cocoon-like gauze as they sleep. If disturbed or awakened, these women transform into feral and violent creatures, leaving the men to fend for themselves in a world devoid of their female counterparts. The Kings' vivid prose paints a picture of a society on the brink of collapse, where primal urges and violence reign supreme.

One woman, Eve Black, remains immune to the sleeping disease, sparking a heated debate among the men. Is she a medical anomaly to be studied, or a demon to be slain? As factions form and tensions rise, the men turn to violence in their desperate bid for power and control. The Kings' masterful storytelling weaves a complex web of characters, each with their own motivations and agendas, adding depth and complexity to the narrative.

Throughout the novel, the Kings' explore themes of power, control, and the very fabric of our society. By removing women from the equation, they shed light on the darker aspects of human nature, revealing a world where men are left to their own devices. The result is a thought-provoking commentary on the state of our world today, where issues of sexism, misogyny, and patriarchy are more pressing than ever.

Stephen King's signature style is on full display in "Sleeping Beauties". His vivid prose and masterful storytelling have captivated readers for decades, and this collaboration with his son Owen is no exception. The Kings' writing is evocative and immersive, drawing the reader into a world that's both terrifying and fascinating.

One of the standout aspects of "Sleeping Beauties" is its ability to balance action, suspense, and social commentary. The novel is a page-turner, with a narrative that's both engaging and thought-provoking. The Kings' have crafted a story that's both wildly imaginative and grounded in reality, making for a compelling read that will appeal to fans of science fiction, horror, and dystopian fiction alike.

In short, "Sleeping Beauties" is a gripping and unsettling novel that's sure to leave readers on the edge of their seats. With its thought-provoking themes, vivid prose, and masterful storytelling, this collaboration between Stephen and Owen King is a must-read for fans of the genre. As a work of speculative fiction, it's a powerful commentary on the state of our world today, and a testament to the enduring power of the human imagination.

# The Complete Circle Series (The Circle, #0-3)

**Author:** Ted Dekker  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**A Timeless Tale of Dualities and Redemption**

Ted Dekker's Complete Circle Series is a masterful, epic saga that has captivated the hearts of over a million readers worldwide. This limited edition boxed set is a must-have for both seasoned fans and newcomers alike, offering a rare opportunity to delve into the depths of a world where darkness and light, evil and good, intertwine in a dance of dualities.

At its core, the Circle series is a thought-provoking exploration of the eternal struggle between two opposing forces: the Black, which represents the corrupting influence of evil, and the White, the manifestation of perfect justice. This dichotomy is cleverly woven throughout the series, forcing the protagonist, Thomas Hunter, to navigate a labyrinthine world where the boundaries between reality and illusion are forever blurred.

Dekker's writing style is a remarkable blend of action, suspense, and philosophical introspection, deftly navigating the complexities of human nature and the human condition. With each page turn, the reader is drawn into a world of vivid imagination, replete with stunning landscapes, cryptic prophecies, and ancient secrets waiting to be unraveled.

One of the most striking aspects of the Circle series is its bold exploration of themes that resonate deeply with contemporary society. Dekker tackles the issues of identity, community, and the corrupting influence of power with unflinching candor, presenting a nuanced and multifaceted portrait of humanity in all its contradictions.

Throughout the series, Dekker's prose is infused with a sense of urgency and desperation, as Thomas Hunter finds himself ensnared in a maelstrom of conflicting allegiances and moral dilemmas. The narrative hurtles forward with breakneck pace, propelling the reader through a series of breathtaking twists and turns that defy easy resolution.

Yet, beneath the surface of this thrilling narrative lies a profound exploration of the human heart, where darkness and light engage in a perpetual struggle for dominance. Dekker's depiction of the human condition is both haunting and revelatory, offering a glimpse into the deepest recesses of the human experience.

In the Complete Circle Series, Ted Dekker has crafted a work of literary magic that transcends genres, speaking to readers from diverse backgrounds and perspectives. This boxed set is an absolute must-have for anyone seeking to immerse themselves in a rich, immersive world of fantasy and adventure, where the boundaries between reality and myth blur into a kaleidoscope of possibility.

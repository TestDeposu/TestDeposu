# The Source of Magic (Xanth, #2)

**Author:** Piers Anthony  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1986

The Source of Magic, the second installment in Piers Anthony's beloved Xanth series, is a captivating tale of adventure, magic, and self-discovery. Published in 1986, this enchanting novel whisks readers away to the mystical land of Xanth, where magic is woven into the fabric of everyday life.

At the heart of the story lies the quest for the source of Xanth's magic, a journey undertaken by Bink, the protagonist, at the behest of King Trent. Bink's unique talent, rendering him immune to supernatural harm, makes him the ideal candidate for this perilous task. Joined by Chester the centaur and Crombie, a soldier transformed into a griffin, the trio embarks on a treacherous journey fraught with unseen enemies and unexpected challenges.

Anthony's masterful storytelling weaves a complex tapestry of intrigue, action, and humor, keeping readers on the edge of their seats as Bink and his companions navigate the unpredictable landscape of Xanth. The author's signature blend of wit, clever wordplay, and absurd situations adds a lighthearted touch to the narrative, making it an absolute delight to read.

One of the most striking aspects of The Source of Magic is its exploration of the concept of power and its consequences. As Bink delves deeper into the mystery of Xanth's magic, he begins to realize the true nature of his quest and the potential implications of his discovery. This thought-provoking theme is expertly interwoven with the plot, adding depth and complexity to the story.

Anthony's writing style is, as always, engaging and accessible. His use of language is economical, yet evocative, painting vivid pictures of the fantastical world of Xanth and its inhabitants. The characters, too, are well-crafted and relatable, with Bink's ingenuity and determination making him a compelling protagonist.

The pacing of the novel is well-balanced, with a mix of fast-paced action sequences and more introspective moments. The plot twists and turns, keeping readers guessing until the very end, when Bink's ultimate decision raises questions about the true cost of power and the responsibility that comes with it.

In conclusion, The Source of Magic is a captivating addition to the Xanth series, offering a thrilling adventure, memorable characters, and a thought-provoking exploration of the nature of power. Piers Anthony's signature style and masterful storytelling make this novel a must-read for fans of fantasy and adventure.

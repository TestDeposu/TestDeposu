# Once and Always (Sequels, #1)

**Author:** Judith McNaught  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

In Judith McNaught's enchanting novel, Once and Always, the New York Times bestselling author masterfully weaves a tale of love, deception, and self-discovery that will leave readers captivated and yearning for more. This first installment of the Sequels series is a testament to McNaught's ability to craft stories that seamlessly blend historical romance with a hint of melodrama, making it impossible to put down.

The story follows Victoria Seaton, a free-spirited American beauty who finds herself at the threshold of a new chapter in her life after the sudden loss of her parents. Determined to claim her rightful inheritance, Victoria travels across the ocean to the sumptuous Wakefield estate in Great Britain, where she is met with a mix of emotions, ranging from awe to bewilderment. It is against this breathtaking backdrop that she encounters her distant cousin, Lord Jason Fielding, a man whose enigmatic presence sparks a tempestuous passion within her.

McNaught's writing style is a masterclass in storytelling, effortlessly conjuring up vivid images of the English countryside and the opulent grandeur of Wakefield. As Victoria and Jason navigate the complexities of their relationship, McNaught's expertly woven plot threads expertly balance the light-hearted humor with deeper, more profound themes, creating a narrative that is both engaging and emotionally resonant.

One of the strengths of Once and Always lies in its character development. Victoria is a refreshingly independent and spirited heroine, whose courage and resilience serve as a beacon of hope in the face of adversity. Jason, on the other hand, is a complex and brooding figure, whose vulnerability and passion make him an endearing and believable hero. Their chemistry is palpable, and their romance is all the more intoxicating due to the intricate web of secrets and lies that surround them.

As the narrative unfolds, McNaught masterfully explores the complexities of class, privilege, and the societal pressures that shape the lives of the aristocracy. Through Victoria's journey, McNaught sheds light on the often-overlooked experiences of women in a patriarchal society, whose choices are frequently dictated by circumstance rather than desire. Meanwhile, Jason's struggles with his own demons provide a nuanced exploration of the human condition, highlighting the delicate balance between societal expectations and individual happiness.

At its core, Once and Always is a timeless tale of love that defies conventions and challenges the status quo. It is a testament to the power of human connection and the transformative potential of love. McNaught's writing is infused with a deep understanding of the human heart, and her characters are multidimensional and relatable, making it impossible not to become invested in their journey.

In short, Once and Always is a captivating, emotional, and deeply satisfying read that will appeal to fans of historical romance and women's fiction alike. Judith McNaught's masterful storytelling, engaging characters, and nuanced exploration of complex themes make this novel an unforgettable experience that will leave readers in awe.

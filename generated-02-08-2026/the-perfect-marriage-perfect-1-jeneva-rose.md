# The Perfect Marriage (Perfect, #1)

**Author:** Jeneva Rose  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

Title: Till Divorce Do Us Part: Jeneva Rose Returns for Another Lethal Round

In the murky waters of domestic suspense, few authors know how to chum the water quite like Jeneva Rose. With her latest 2025 release, Rose dives right back into the treacherous psychological depths that made her a household name, proving once again that she is a master of marital mayhem. Picking up the thread years after the events of her breakout smash, this gripping continuation takes the razor-sharp premise of the original and twists it into a breathless, high-stakes game of legal chess and psychological warfare. 

The central theme of the novel is deceptively simple yet universally terrifying: the fragility of trust and the lethal fallout of betrayal. Rose explores the dangerous chasm between the lives we carefully curate for the public and the dark, volatile realities hidden behind closed doors. Sarah Morgan thought she had outrun her past. Eleven years after successfully defending her then-husband Adam against a murder charge involving his mistress, Sarah had seemingly engineered the ultimate do-over. New career, new family, new husband—Bob Miller. But Rose’s central thesis is that the past is never truly dead; it merely lies dormant, waiting for the right catalyst to detonate. 

When Sarah discovers Bob’s infidelity, the narrative shifts into a brilliant exploration of modern divorce weaponized. What begins as a bitter separation quickly spirals into a harrowing nightmare when fresh DNA evidence resurrects the long-dormant murder investigation involving Sarah’s ex. Rose brilliantly juxtaposes the emotional devastation of marital betrayal with the ice-cold mechanics of the legal system. As former deputy Marcus Hudson circles like a shark smelling blood in the water, Sarah and Bob transition from spouses to mortal enemies, waging a vicious war where collateral damage is guaranteed. 

Stylistically, Rose remains at the top of her game. Her prose is punchy, relentless, and designed for maximum momentum. She understands the anatomy of a page-turner, utilizing short, breathless chapters that alternate perspectives to keep the reader perpetually off-balance. There are no wasted words; every scene is loaded with either exposition that advances the plot or psychological friction that deepens the dread. Rose has a distinct knack for writing morally gray characters who are simultaneously deeply flawed and utterly magnetic. You may not always like Sarah or Bob, but you cannot look away as they systematically attempt to destroy one another. 

Furthermore, the author’s background shines through in the courtroom and investigative procedurals, lending an authentic, gritty edge to the legal maneuvering. Yet, it is the domestic battlefield where Rose truly shines. She dissects the psychology of a crumbling marriage with the precision of a surgeon, exposing the petty cruelties, deep-seated resentments, and terrifying lengths people will go to when backed into a corner. 

For fans of fast-paced, twist-laden thrillers, this book is an absolute must-read. Jeneva Rose delivers a deliciously toxic cocktail of suspense, dark humor, and jaw-dropping revelations. It asks the ultimate question for the modern age: Can you ever truly know the person sleeping next to you, and more importantly, can you survive escaping them? Prepare to clear your schedule, because once you open this book, putting it down is simply not an option. Till death—or divorce—do us part.

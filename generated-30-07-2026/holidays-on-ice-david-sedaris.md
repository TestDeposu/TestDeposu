# Holidays on Ice

**Author:** David Sedaris  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Holidays on Ice, the updated edition of David Sedaris's cherished holiday collection, is a wickedly funny and irreverent exploration of the most wonderful time of the year. With six new pieces added to the original mix, this book is a treasure trove of humor, wit, and poignancy that will leave readers in stitches and nodding their heads in recognition.

Sedaris's writing style is a unique blend of satire, observational humor, and poignant storytelling, making him one of the most beloved and respected humorists of our time. His ability to find the absurdity and humor in even the most mundane situations is unparalleled, and his holiday collection is no exception. From the diaries of a Macy's elf to the annals of two very competitive families, Sedaris's tales are both laugh-out-loud funny and surprisingly touching.

One of the standout features of Holidays on Ice is its ability to poke fun at the quirks and conventions of holiday traditions without ever becoming mean-spirited or cynical. Sedaris's humor is clever and incisive, but it's also infused with a deep affection for the people and places he's writing about. Whether he's exploring the difficulties of explaining the Easter Bunny to the French or the puzzling Christmas traditions of other nations, Sedaris's writing is always grounded in a sense of wonder and curiosity.

The new pieces added to this edition are just as strong as the originals, and they offer a fresh perspective on the holiday season. "Us and Them" is a hilarious and poignant exploration of the ways in which we define ourselves and our communities during the holidays, while "Let It Snow" is a beautifully written meditation on the magic and mystery of snowstorms. "The Monster Mash" is a wickedly funny and clever exploration of what Halloween looks like from the perspective of a medical examiner, and "Cow and Turkey" is a sidesplitting tale of a barnyard secret Santa scheme gone awry.

Throughout the book, Sedaris's writing is characterized by its precision, clarity, and lyricism. His prose is always elegant and engaging, and his storytelling is both masterful and effortless. Whether he's writing about the absurdities of holiday traditions or the complexities of human relationships, Sedaris's voice is always distinct and compelling.

In short, Holidays on Ice is a holiday classic that deserves to be read and reread every year. It's a book that will make you laugh, cry, and nod your head in recognition, and it's a testament to the power of humor and storytelling to bring us together and make us see the world in a new light.

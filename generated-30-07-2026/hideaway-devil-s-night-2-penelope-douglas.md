# Hideaway (Devil's Night, #2)

**Author:** Penelope Douglas  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

Penelope Douglas returns with Hideaway, the fiercely anticipated second installment in her dark and intoxicating Devil's Night series. Known for pushing the boundaries of contemporary romance, Douglas once again plunges her readers into a morally gray world where loyalty is tested, secrets are currency, and love is inextricably tied to pain and redemption.

Where the first book, Corrupt, established the dangerous playground of the Horsemen and the lingering trauma of their high school years, Hideaway shifts the narrative focus inward, examining the fractures within the group. The central theme of this novel is not merely revenge, but survival and the heavy burden of legacy. Douglas explores how the ghosts of the past—both literal and figurative—refuse to stay buried. As Rika, Michael, Kai, and the notoriously volatile Damon gather aboard the yacht Pithom to strategize, the atmosphere crackles with claustrophobic tension. The narrative brilliantly contrasts the opulent, isolated setting of the sea with the raw, emotional wreckage of the characters' inner lives. 

Douglas’s signature writing style is unapologetically intense, visceral, and atmospheric. She has a distinct ability to write anti-heroes who are terrifying yet deeply magnetic. In Hideaway, Damon Torrance takes center stage, and Douglas handles his complex psyche with a delicate, albeit brutal, touch. She doesn't ask the reader to instantly forgive her characters; instead, she strips them down to their core, exposing their scars, vulnerabilities, and desperate need for absolution. The pacing is relentless, moving seamlessly between high-stakes strategic plotting and psychological introspection. The dialogue is sharp, laced with psychological warfare, and loaded with the kind of electric chemistry that has become a staple of Douglas's work. 

What makes Hideaway stand out in the crowded dark romance genre is Douglas’s commitment to character depth. The danger is real, the stakes are national, and the enemies are formidable, but the true battle is fought within the minds and hearts of the protagonists. It is a story about finding family in the darkest places and learning that sometimes, the only way to save yourself is to confront the monster hiding in the shadows. 

For fans of angsty, slow-burn, enemies-to-lovers romance with high emotional stakes and morally ambiguous characters, Hideaway is an addictive and unforgettable read that proves Penelope Douglas is a master of the dark romance craft.

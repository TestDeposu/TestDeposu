# The Carrying

**Author:** Ada Limon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

The Carrying, Ada Limon's remarkable collection of poems, is a masterful exploration of the human experience, delving into the complexities of anxiety, the body, and the passage of time. With unflinching honesty, Limon navigates the ambiguous moment between the vitality of youth and the acceptance of aging, crafting a narrative that is both deeply personal and universally relatable.

Through her poems, Limon tenderly examines the intricate relationships between family members, particularly the bond between a daughter and her aging parents. Her words are infused with a sense of vulnerability, as she grapples with the challenges of caring for loved ones while confronting her own struggles with infertility and the fragility of the human body. The lines "What if, instead of carrying / a child, I am supposed to carry grief?" resonate deeply, highlighting the poet's ability to distill complex emotions into simple, yet striking images.

Limon's unique voice is characterized by her remarkable directness, as she tackles painful experiences with unflinching candor, inviting readers to enter a world where joy and loss coexist. Her poetry is pitch-perfect, weaving together the threads of love, hunger, and acceptance, creating a tapestry that is both exquisite and haunting. The nation's convulsions are also reflected in her work, as she acknowledges the brutal realities that often remain unspoken, noting that "Every song of this country / has an unsung third stanza, something brutal."

One of the most striking aspects of Limon's style is her ability to balance the darkness of her themes with a sense of hope and resilience. Despite the pain and vertigo that permeate her poems, she remains steadfast in her determination to find beauty and meaning in the world. The lines "Fine then, / I'll take it," she writes. "I'll take it all" are a testament to her courage and willingness to confront the complexities of life head-on.

The Carrying is a collection that will resonate with readers long after they finish the book. Limon's poems are not just a reflection of her own experiences but also a powerful exploration of the human condition. Her work has been widely praised, with reviewers noting the "gorgeous, thought-provoking" nature of her poetry, as well as its "exquisite" and "pitch-perfect" quality.

In short, The Carrying is a masterpiece of contemporary poetry, one that will leave readers in awe of Limon's skill and sensitivity. Her unique voice and style have cemented her place as one of the most important poets of her generation, and this collection is a must-read for anyone interested in exploring the complexities of the human experience.

# Rising Strong: The Reckoning. The Rumble. The Revolution

**Author:** Brené Brown  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

**Rising Strong: Brené Brown's Masterclass in Resilience**

In her 2015 bestseller, "Rising Strong," Brené Brown invites us on a profound journey into the heart of human resilience. With her signature blend of scholarly insight and heartfelt storytelling, Brown guides us through the messy, beautiful process of falling and rising, of being brave and vulnerable, and of forging a life of wholeheartedness.

Brown, a renowned social scientist, has spent decades studying courage, vulnerability, and shame. In "Rising Strong," she turns her attention to the moments when we stumble, when we're knocked down by life's inevitable challenges. She argues that it's not the falling that defines us, but the rising—the way we choose to get back up and face our stories.

The book is structured around three key steps: the Reckoning, the Rumble, and the Revolution. In the Reckoning, we pause and feel our emotions, getting curious about what we're experiencing. In the Rumble, we engage with our stories, wrestling with the truth until we find clarity. And in the Revolution, we live these practices, cultivating a life of courage, vulnerability, and wholeheartedness.

Brown's style is engaging and relatable, filled with personal anecdotes and insights from her research. She weaves together stories from diverse walks of life—from military leaders to artists, from parents to clergy—illustrating that the process of rising strong is universal. Her prose is accessible yet profound, inviting us to lean into discomfort and embrace our stories.

At the heart of "Rising Strong" is a powerful message: that vulnerability is not a sign of weakness, but a path to courage and connection. Brown encourages us to embrace our emotions, to rumble with our stories, and to rise strong, again and again.

In a world that often encourages us to deny our pain, to stay small, and to avoid discomfort, "Rising Strong" is a beacon of hope and a call to action. It's a book that doesn't shy away from the hard stuff, but instead leans into it, inviting us to do the same.

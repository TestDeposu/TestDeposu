# Before the Fall

**Author:** Noah Hawley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

In "Before the Fall," Noah Hawley crafts a gripping and thought-provoking thriller that delves into the complexities of human nature, morality, and the unbreakable bonds that tie us together. This award-winning novel, which claimed the 2017 Edgar Award for Best Novel and the 2017 International Thriller Writers Award for Best Novel, is a testament to Hawley's exceptional storytelling prowess.

The story unfolds with a catastrophic event: a private jet carrying eleven people, including a young boy and a down-on-his-luck painter, Scott Burroughs, crashes into the ocean just sixteen minutes after takeoff. As the only survivors, Scott and the four-year-old boy are thrust into the midst of a media frenzy, with Scott's actions and intentions scrutinized by the public and the authorities. As the narrative progresses, Hawley masterfully weaves together multiple storylines, gradually revealing the intricate web of relationships and motivations that led to the doomed flight.

Throughout the novel, Hawley's writing style is characterized by its lyricism, wit, and unflinching candor. His prose is both evocative and economical, conjuring vivid images of the settings and characters while maintaining a breakneck pace that propels the reader forward. Hawley's ability to balance action, suspense, and introspection is impressive, making "Before the Fall" a compulsively readable thrill ride.

At its core, the novel explores the theme of fate and the human condition. Hawley raises questions about the nature of chance and coincidence, leaving the reader to ponder the possibility of a larger, more sinister force at play. As Scott navigates the treacherous landscape of media attention and public scrutiny, he is forced to confront his own demons and the true meaning of his existence. The fragile bond between Scott and the young boy serves as a poignant counterpoint to the chaos and destruction that surrounds them, highlighting the resilience of the human spirit.

Hawley's experience as the creator of the critically acclaimed TV series "Fargo" is evident in his skillful handling of complex characters and plot twists. His writing is imbued with a sense of depth and nuance, making even the most minor characters feel fully realized and relatable. The novel's climax is both shocking and inevitable, a testament to Hawley's mastery of the thriller genre.

In conclusion, "Before the Fall" is a stunning novel that will appeal to fans of suspense, thriller, and literary fiction. Hawley's writing is engaging, thought-provoking, and visceral, making this book a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and unforgettable story. With its exploration of fate, morality, and human connection, "Before the Fall" is a gripping and haunting thriller that will linger in the reader's mind long after the final page is turned.

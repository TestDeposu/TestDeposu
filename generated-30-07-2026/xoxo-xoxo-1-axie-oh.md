# XOXO (XOXO, #1)

**Author:** Axie Oh  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

**XOXO: A Melodious Tale of Love and Ambition**

In the vibrant world of K-pop and the hallowed halls of classical music, Axie Oh's "XOXO" weaves a captivating tale of love, ambition, and the choices we make when they collide. At its heart, this novel is a forbidden romance, but it's so much more than that. It's a love letter to music, a critique of the K-pop industry, and a coming-of-age story that will resonate with anyone who's ever had to choose between their dreams and their heart.

Jenny, our protagonist, is a cellist with a singular focus: her music. She's the kind of girl who chooses practice over parties, and her dedication has paid off in spades. But when she meets Jaewoo, a mysterious and tormented K-pop idol, on the eve of his departure to South Korea, she finds herself questioning her priorities. Their night together is electric, a whirlwind of connection and chemistry that leaves Jenny breathless and yearning for more.

But fate, it seems, has other plans. When Jenny and her mother move to Seoul to care for her ailing grandmother, Jenny finds herself enrolled in the prestigious arts academy where Jaewoo is a student. The problem? Jaewoo is a member of one of the world's biggest K-pop bands, and his contract forbids him from dating. Their love is a secret, a risk, and a potential career-ending scandal for both of them.

Oh navigates this forbidden romance with a deft hand, balancing the sweet, stolen moments between Jenny and Jaewoo with the harsh realities of their situation. She doesn't shy away from the darker side of the K-pop industry, exploring the intense pressure and scrutiny that idols face, and the lengths they must go to maintain their public image. Yet, amidst the shadows, there's a warmth and a lightness that comes from Jenny and Jaewoo's connection, a testament to the power of love to transcend even the most daunting obstacles.

What truly sets "XOXO" apart is its exploration of ambition and the choices we make to achieve our dreams. Jenny is a character who knows what she wants and is willing to work tirelessly to get it. She's not a damsel in distress, waiting for her prince to save her. Instead, she's a young woman navigating the complexities of love and ambition, and the tough decisions that come with them. Jaewoo, too, is a character of depth and complexity, a young man grappling with the weight of his fame and the expectations that come with it.

Oh's writing is lyrical and evocative, painting vivid pictures of Seoul's bustling streets and the quiet, intimate moments between Jenny and Jaewoo. She captures the essence of music, the way it can make us feel alive, and the way it can break our hearts. Her exploration of the K-pop industry is insightful and nuanced, offering a glimpse into a world that's often misunderstood and misrepresented.

In "XOXO," Axie Oh has crafted a novel that's as much about the music as it is about the love story at its heart. It's a novel that will make you laugh, make you cry, and make you question what you're willing to sacrifice for your dreams. It's a novel that will stay with you long after you've turned the last page.

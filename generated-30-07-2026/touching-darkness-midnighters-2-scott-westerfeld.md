# Touching Darkness (Midnighters, #2)

**Author:** Scott Westerfeld  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Touching Darkness, the second installment in Scott Westerfeld's Midnighters series, delves deeper into the mysteries of the secret hour, a world that exists between midnight and 1 a.m., hidden from the rest of humanity. Westerfeld masterfully weaves a narrative that explores the intricate web of Bixby's history, revealing a chilling conspiracy that threatens the very fabric of the town.

One of the standout aspects of Westerfeld's writing is his ability to craft relatable characters, each with their unique struggles and strengths. In Touching Darkness, Jessica Day, the flame-bringer, takes center stage as she navigates the complexities of her power and the weight of responsibility that comes with it. The supporting cast, including Melissa, Jonathan, and Dess, are equally well-developed, each contributing their distinct voice to the narrative.

Westerfeld's world-building is another highlight of the series. The secret hour, with its darklings, slithers, and other supernatural creatures, is a richly detailed and immersive environment that draws the reader in. The introduction of a new, terrifying creature – part human, part monster – adds a fresh layer of tension to the story, raising the stakes for the Midnighters.

The author's style is characterized by his ability to balance action, suspense, and humor, creating a narrative that is both engaging and unpredictable. Westerfeld's prose is concise and accessible, making the book an easy read for fans of urban fantasy and supernatural fiction.

At its core, Touching Darkness is a story about found family and the bonds that form between individuals who have been brought together by circumstance. The Midnighters, each with their unique abilities and strengths, must learn to trust and rely on each other if they are to survive the threats that lurk in the secret hour. This theme is expertly woven throughout the narrative, adding depth and emotional resonance to the story.

In conclusion, Touching Darkness is a gripping and unsettling read that will appeal to fans of urban fantasy and supernatural fiction. Westerfeld's masterful storytelling, combined with his well-developed characters and richly detailed world-building, make this book a compelling addition to the Midnighters series.

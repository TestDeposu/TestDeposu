# The Man Who Died Twice (Thursday Murder Club, #2)

**Author:** Richard Osman  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

The Man Who Died Twice, the second installment in Richard Osman's Thursday Murder Club series, is a masterful blend of mystery, wit, and charm. Osman's writing style is effortlessly engaging, making this book an unputdownable pleasure to read. The story picks up where the first book left off, with Elizabeth, Joyce, Ron, and Ibrahim still basking in the glory of their recent real-life murder case.

As the four friends try to settle into a quiet life at Cooper's Chase, their posh retirement village, they are thrust into a new adventure. An old acquaintance of Elizabeth's arrives, accused of stealing millions worth of diamonds, and the group is drawn into a complex web of mystery and deceit. As the bodies start to pile up, the Thursday Murder Club must use their collective wit and experience to catch the killer before they become the next victims.

Osman's writing is characterized by his clever use of language, witty dialogue, and a keen understanding of human nature. The characters are well-developed and relatable, with distinct personalities that add to the humor and charm of the story. The author's ability to balance humor with the darker aspects of the mystery is impressive, making this book a joy to read.

One of the standout features of The Man Who Died Twice is its exploration of the theme of identity. Elizabeth's past is slowly revealed throughout the book, and her relationships with the other characters are expertly woven into the narrative. The author raises questions about how our past experiences shape us and how we present ourselves to the world. This theme is cleverly intertwined with the mystery, adding depth and complexity to the story.

The Thursday Murder Club series has been praised for its originality and Osman's unique writing style. The Man Who Died Twice is no exception, with Osman's clever plotting and unexpected twists keeping the reader guessing until the very end. The book is a testament to the author's skill as a storyteller, and his ability to craft a compelling narrative that is both entertaining and thought-provoking.

In conclusion, The Man Who Died Twice is a must-read for fans of mystery and crime fiction. Osman's writing is engaging, witty, and clever, making this book a joy to read. The Thursday Murder Club series is quickly becoming a favorite among readers, and this second installment is sure to delight. With its unique blend of humor, mystery, and charm, The Man Who Died Twice is an unputdownable pleasure that will keep you guessing until the very end.

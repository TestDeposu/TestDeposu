# The Death of Mrs. Westaway

**Author:** Ruth Ware  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

**Title: "The Death of Mrs. Westaway" - A Masterclass in Suspense by Ruth Ware**

Ruth Ware, the modern-day queen of psychological suspense, delivers another captivating thriller with "The Death of Mrs. Westaway." Fans of Ware's previous works, such as "In a Dark, Dark Wood" and "The Woman in Cabin 10," will find themselves in familiar territory, yet Ware's signature style ensures a fresh and engaging read.

The story begins with Hal, a young tarot card reader, receiving a mysterious letter bequeathing her a substantial inheritance. Intrigued and in need of money, Hal decides to claim the inheritance, only to find herself entangled in a web of deceit and danger. The narrative is set against the atmospheric backdrop of a crumbling Cornish mansion, adding an eerie and foreboding tone to the proceedings.

Ware's strength lies in her ability to create suspenseful tension that keeps readers on the edge of their seats. She masterfully employs misdirection and red herrings, making it difficult for readers to predict the outcome. The story is told from Hal's perspective, and Ware skillfully uses her protagonist's uncertainty to mirror the reader's own confusion, creating a sense of unease that permeates every page.

One of the main themes of "The Death of Mrs. Westaway" is the exploration of identity and the secrets that shape us. Hal's journey to uncover the truth about her inheritance forces her to confront her own past and the lies she's told to protect herself. This introspective journey adds depth to the character and makes her a relatable protagonist, despite her occasional questionable decisions.

Ware's writing style is crisp and engaging, with a pace that builds steadily towards a thrilling climax. She has a knack for creating vivid, atmospheric settings that become almost like characters themselves. The crumbling Westaway House, with its secrets hidden in every corner, is a perfect example of this.

In "The Death of Mrs. Westaway," Ruth Ware pays homage to the classic gothic mysteries of yesteryears while delivering a modern, fast-paced thriller. Fans of suspenseful reads will find this book an absolute treat. It's a captivating and eerie page-turner that will keep you guessing until the very end.

# Chosen at Nightfall (Shadow Falls, #5)

**Author:** C.C. Hunter  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

Step into the twilight world of Shadow Falls for one final, breathtaking journey where destiny, danger, and desire collide. In Chosen at Nightfall, the explosive final chapter of C.C. Hunter’s New York Times-bestselling series, the stakes are higher than ever, and the emotional resonance reaches a fever pitch. Hunter delivers a masterclass in young adult paranormal fiction, expertly weaving a tapestry of supernatural thrills and deeply human heartbreak that leaves an indelible mark on the reader.

At the core of this concluding installment is the overarching theme of self-discovery and transformation. From the moment Kylie Galen first stumbled into a camp for troubled paranormal teens, her life has been defined by mystery. What are her true powers? Where does she truly belong? In Chosen at Nightfall, these questions finally find their answers. Hunter handles Kylie’s evolution with remarkable deftness, transforming her from a confused teenager into a powerful force to be reckoned with. The narrative brilliantly captures the universal pangs of growing up—learning to step into one's own power, embracing identity, and realizing that true strength often emerges from the darkest of places. It is a triumphant exploration of destiny versus free will, showing that while our paths may be chosen for us by birth or prophecy, how we walk them is entirely up to us.

Of course, no review of a Shadow Falls novel would be complete without addressing the legendary love triangle that has captivated fans for books. Hunter’s signature style shines brightest in her depiction of romance, deftly balancing the raw, impulsive passion of the werewolf who fights for redemption with the magnetic, complex allure of the half-Fae who struggles against his own nature. The romantic tension is not merely a subplot; it is the beating heart of Kylie’s emotional maturation. Hunter treats her characters' hearts with care, ensuring that the final choice feels earned, poignant, and emotionally resonant rather than arbitrary. 

Stylistically, C.C. Hunter excels at creating an atmosphere where lightness and darkness dance in a delicate equilibrium. Her prose is fast-paced, immersive, and effortlessly readable, drawing the audience directly into the humid, secret-filled nights of the camp. Danger lurks around every corner as Kylie’s ultimate enemy returns for a final confrontation, yet the narrative never loses sight of what truly grounds the series: the enduring power of friendship and loyalty. The banter between Kylie, her witch best friend, and her vampire best friend injects necessary warmth and humor into the high-stakes peril.

Chosen at Nightfall is more than just a satisfying conclusion to a beloved franchise; it is a celebration of everything that made Shadow Falls a phenomenon. Hunter delivers a story filled with darkness and light, hope and danger, wrapping up loose ends while honoring the emotional journeys of the characters we have grown to love. For longtime fans, this book is an unmissable emotional climax. For newcomers, it is a testament to the magic of storytelling done right. Prepare to laugh, cry, and hold your breath until the very last page is turned.

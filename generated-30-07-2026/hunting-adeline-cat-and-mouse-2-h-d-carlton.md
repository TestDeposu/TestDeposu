# Hunting Adeline (Cat and Mouse, #2)

**Author:** H.D. Carlton  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**Hunting Adeline: A Dark, Unforgiving Thriller**

In the realm of psychological thrillers, H.D. Carlton's "Hunting Adeline" stands as a chilling testament to the author's unyielding commitment to darkness and intensity. A sequel to the equally grim "Catching Adeline," this novel is not for the faint-hearted, as it delves into the twisted minds of its characters with unflinching precision.

The story revolves around Adeline, now known as the 'Diamond,' a woman haunted by her past and hunted by a relentless force. She navigates a world filled with monsters disguised as men, her only solace found in the 'Angel of Death' within her. Meanwhile, the 'Hunter,' driven by an insatiable desire to reclaim what's his, leaves a trail of carnage in his pursuit of Adeline.

Carlton's prose is as sharp as a blade, slicing through the veneer of civility to expose the raw, primal instincts that lie beneath. She masterfully weaves a tapestry of suspense, horror, and psychological depth, drawing readers into a labyrinth of violence, betrayal, and redemption.

The author's style is unapologetic, pushing boundaries with graphic descriptions of violence and sexual content. This is not a book for the squeamish, but for those who crave a visceral, unyielding exploration of the human psyche under extreme duress.

"Hunting Adeline" is a gripping, unsettling journey into the abyss, a testament to Carlton's ability to create compelling, flawed characters trapped in a nightmarish world of their own making. It's a thrilling, if disturbing, read that will leave you breathless and craving more.

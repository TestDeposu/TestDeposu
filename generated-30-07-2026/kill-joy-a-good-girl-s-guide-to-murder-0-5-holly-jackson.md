# Kill Joy (A Good Girl's Guide to Murder, #0.5)

**Author:** Holly Jackson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

In "Kill Joy," Holly Jackson weaves a gripping narrative that delves into the dark underbelly of a seemingly idyllic town, where the past and present collide in unexpected ways. This novella, a prequel to the bestselling "A Good Girl's Guide to Murder" series, is a masterclass in crafting a story that is both a nostalgic throwback to the classic whodunits and a fresh, modern take on the genre.

At its core, "Kill Joy" is a story about obsession – the all-consuming need to uncover the truth, no matter the cost. For Pip, the protagonist, this obsession takes the form of a final project that seeks to reexamine the closed case of Andie Bell's murder. As she digs deeper, she becomes increasingly entangled in a web of secrets and lies that threaten to upend everything she thought she knew about her town and the people in it.

Jackson's writing is economical and razor-sharp, with a keen eye for detail that makes the world of Fairview feel lived-in and authentic. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Pip being a particularly standout creation. Her voice is distinct and compelling, and her motivations are expertly woven throughout the narrative.

One of the most striking aspects of "Kill Joy" is its exploration of the ripple effects of trauma. The murder of Andie Bell is a event that has left a lasting impact on the town, and Jackson skillfully conveys the ways in which this trauma has been internalized and externalized by the characters. This is particularly evident in the character of Sal, who is both villain and victim, and whose story is skillfully intertwined with Pip's.

The pacing of the novella is expertly handled, with Jackson slowly ratcheting up the tension as Pip gets closer to the truth. The twists and turns are expertly plotted, and the ending is both surprising and satisfying.

Overall, "Kill Joy" is a gripping and thought-provoking read that will appeal to fans of mystery and suspense. Jackson's writing is sharp and engaging, and her characters are complex and relatable. This is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and twisty mystery that will keep them on the edge of their seat.

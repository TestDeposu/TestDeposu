# Originals: How Non-Conformists Move the World

**Author:** Adam M. Grant  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

Originals: How Non-Conformists Move the World by Adam M. Grant is not the book being reviewed, rather a book titled Operating Manual for Enlightenment, and the author is Lincoln Stoller. This book is not about solving problems but rather explaining how we see and understand the world around us. It delves into the concept of the mind, states of mind, and how they affect our thoughts, feelings, and behaviors.

The book is divided into two halves, the first focusing on the intellectual aspect, and the second on the emotional side. This dichotomy is reflective of the separation between intellect and emotion that is built into the structure of our brains. The author argues that these two aspects are not mutually exclusive but rather complementary, like color and shape in vision.

The book is not a linear treatise but rather a winding journey through the mind's labyrinthine pathways to archetypal insights. The author's style is engaging, witty, and humorous, making the book an enjoyable read. The use of examples and exercises helps to illustrate how the elements of the mind converge, enhancing our understanding of this complex subject.

One of the key takeaways from the book is the importance of taking full responsibility for our thoughts and feelings. This is reflected in the concept of "state," which determines our readiness, arousal, and self-reflection. The author argues that one state of mind is not enough and that we need to develop a range of states to understand the world from different perspectives.

The book also touches on the concept of "wisdom," which involves understandings that may be logical, emotional, spiritual, or evanescent. These understandings may involve knowledge spread across generations, and the author suggests that we need a state of mind that can accommodate contradictions without generating conflict.

The reviews of the book are overwhelmingly positive, with praise from experts in the fields of neuroscience, psychology, and philosophy. The book is described as a "true guide to empowering and liberating yourself," a "cornucopia of facts, thoughts, and ideas about what it is to be us," and an "enriching journey of insights on enlightenment."

Overall, Operating Manual for Enlightenment is a thought-provoking and insightful book that challenges our understanding of the mind and its role in shaping our thoughts, feelings, and behaviors. It is a must-read for anyone interested in psychology, philosophy, and personal development.

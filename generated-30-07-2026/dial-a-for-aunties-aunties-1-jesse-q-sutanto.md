# Dial A for Aunties (Aunties, #1)

**Author:** Jesse Q. Sutanto  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In the outrageously entertaining and unpredictable rom-com/murder mystery mashup "Dial A for Aunties" by Jesse Q. Sutanto, we're introduced to Meddy Chan, a young woman navigating the complexities of familial duty, possible arrest, and a groomzilla, all while trying to pull off the perfect wedding. This book is a wildly funny and charming ride that had me laughing out loud from the very start.

At its core, "Dial A for Aunties" is a story about the unbreakable bond of family and the power of love, cultural heritage, and meddling aunties. Meddy's accidental murder of her blind date sets off a chain of events that leads to a weekend of chaos, as she, her mother, and her aunties work together to dispose of the body and pull off a stunning wedding for a billionaire client. But things take a complicated turn when Meddy's great college love and biggest heartbreak makes a surprise appearance, forcing her to confront her past and her feelings.

Sutanto's writing style is clever, witty, and deeply funny, with a keen eye for the nuances of cultural identity and the immigrant experience. Her characters are richly drawn and relatable, with Meddy being a particularly well-crafted protagonist. Her struggles to balance her loyalty to her family with her own desires and aspirations are deeply human, and her chemistry with her ex is undeniable.

One of the standout aspects of "Dial A for Aunties" is its exploration of the Asian-Indonesian immigrant experience. Sutanto brings a wealth of cultural knowledge and nuance to the story, from the intricate details of traditional wedding customs to the complexities of family dynamics and cultural expectations. The result is a book that feels both deeply personal and universally relatable.

The author's use of humor is also noteworthy, as she skillfully balances laugh-out-loud moments with more serious themes and emotions. The result is a book that's both entertaining and heartfelt, with a narrative that's engaging and unpredictable.

In short, "Dial A for Aunties" is a wildly entertaining and charming rom-com/murder mystery mashup that's sure to delight readers. With its richly drawn characters, witty dialogue, and nuanced exploration of cultural identity, it's a must-read for fans of Emily Henry, Helen Hoang, and other authors of romantic comedies. So if you're looking for a book that will make you laugh, cry, and cheer for the characters, look no further than "Dial A for Aunties".

# Dark Desires After Dusk (Immortals After Dark, #5)

**Author:** Kresley Cole  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

In "Dark Desires After Dusk," Kresley Cole masterfully weaves a captivating tale of forbidden love, redemption, and the blurred lines between good and evil. This fifth installment in the Immortals After Dark series is a testament to Cole's skill in crafting complex characters and storylines that draw readers in and refuse to let go.

At the heart of the story is Cadeon Woede, a ruthless demon mercenary haunted by a past mistake that continues to define him. His quest for redemption leads him to Holly Ashwin, a lovely young halfling who is as innocent as she is alluring. Initially, Cade sees Holly as a means to an end, a way to atone for his past wrongs. However, as he gets to know her, he finds himself inexplicably drawn to her charm and beauty.

Holly, raised as a human, is thrust into a world of myth and power that she never knew existed. As she navigates this new reality, she finds herself torn between her fear of Cade's brutal nature and her growing desire for his touch. Despite the danger that surrounds him, Holly is drawn to the demon's wicked charm, and as their relationship deepens, she begins to see beyond his tough exterior to the complex and vulnerable soul beneath.

One of the strengths of Cole's writing is her ability to create complex, multi-dimensional characters. Cade and Holly are no exception. Both characters are richly drawn, with backstories that add depth and nuance to the narrative. Their relationship is a slow-burning fire that builds in intensity as the story progresses, making their romance all the more satisfying.

Cole's world-building is another highlight of the book. The Immortals After Dark universe is richly detailed, with a complex mythology that adds depth and texture to the story. The author's use of language is evocative and immersive, making it easy to become lost in the world she has created.

The main theme of "Dark Desires After Dusk" is the power of redemption and forgiveness. Cade's past mistake haunts him, and his quest for redemption is a driving force behind the narrative. However, as the story progresses, it becomes clear that redemption is not just about making amends for past wrongs, but also about learning to forgive oneself and others. This theme is woven throughout the narrative, adding depth and complexity to the story.

In conclusion, "Dark Desires After Dusk" is a captivating tale of forbidden love and redemption. With its complex characters, richly detailed world-building, and immersive storytelling, this book is a must-read for fans of paranormal romance. Cole's writing is engaging and evocative, making it easy to become lost in the world she has created.

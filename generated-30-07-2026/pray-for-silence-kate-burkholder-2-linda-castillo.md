# Pray for Silence (Kate Burkholder, #2)

**Author:** Linda Castillo  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**Pray for Silence: A Chilling Journey into the Shadows of the Amish**

Linda Castillo's "Pray for Silence," the second installment in her Kate Burkholder series, is a gripping thriller that delves deep into the heart of an Amish community, unearthing dark secrets and shattering the peaceful facade. Castillo, a master of suspense, weaves a tale that is as compelling as it is unsettling, drawing readers into a world where the line between justice and vengeance blurs.

The story unfolds in the small, seemingly idyllic town of Painters Mill, where the brutal murder of the entire Plank family sends shockwaves through the tight-knit Amish community. Chief of Police Kate Burkholder, herself a former Amish woman, is no stranger to the secrets hidden within the Plain Life. Yet, the sheer savagery of this crime leaves her reeling, forcing her to confront the dark underbelly of her past.

Castillo's strength lies in her ability to create complex, relatable characters. Kate Burkholder is a heroine for our time, a woman grappling with her identity, her past, and her duty. Her relationship with state agent John Tomasetti is another highlight, their chemistry palpable, their shared history adding depth to their interactions. Castillo also excels in her portrayal of the Amish community, capturing their unique culture, beliefs, and struggles with nuance and respect.

The narrative is driven by a compelling mystery that keeps readers on the edge of their seats. Castillo expertly drip-feeds clues, misdirecting and surprising even the most astute readers. The discovery of a diary belonging to one of the Plank daughters adds a layer of intrigue, revealing a secret life that challenges stereotypes and raises disturbing questions.

Castillo's prose is taut and engaging, her pacing masterful. She expertly balances suspense with character development, ensuring that readers are invested in both the plot and the people driving it. Her descriptions of the Amish community and its rituals are evocative, transporting readers to a world that is both familiar and foreign.

"Pray for Silence" is more than just a gripping thriller; it's a exploration of themes that resonate beyond the page. It's a story about identity, belonging, and the price of secrets. It's about the thin line between justice and revenge, and the power of forgiveness. It's about the darkness that can lurk beneath even the most peaceful of surfaces.

In conclusion, "Pray for Silence" is a must-read for fans of suspenseful thrillers and character-driven narratives. Linda Castillo's ability to blend intricate plotting with compelling character development makes this book a standout in the genre. It's a chilling journey into the shadows of the Amish, a journey that will leave readers breathless and eager for more.

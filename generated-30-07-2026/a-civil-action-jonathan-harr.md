# A Civil Action

**Author:** Jonathan Harr  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1996

A Civil Action, written by Jonathan Harr, is a gripping and thought-provoking book that delves into the world of corporate greed, corruption, and the pursuit of justice. This National Book Critics Circle Award-winning novel tells the true story of a young lawyer who takes on two of the nation's largest corporations accused of contaminating the water supply in a small town, resulting in the deaths of several children.

The book is a masterclass in storytelling, with Harr's writing style being both engaging and informative. He weaves a complex narrative that is both a page-turner and a scathing critique of the American legal system. The author's use of language is vivid and immersive, making it easy for the reader to become fully invested in the story. Harr's writing is reminiscent of Truman Capote's In Cold Blood, with an unstoppable narrative power that drives the story forward.

At the heart of the book is the theme of justice versus greed. The corporations at the center of the story are willing to do whatever it takes to avoid taking responsibility for their actions, including manipulating evidence and intimidating witnesses. The young lawyer, who is the protagonist of the story, is driven by a desire to see justice served and to hold the corporations accountable for their actions.

Throughout the book, Harr raises important questions about the role of money and power in the American legal system. He highlights the ways in which corporations can use their wealth and influence to avoid accountability, and the ways in which the legal system can be manipulated to serve the interests of the powerful.

Despite the heavy subject matter, the book is ultimately a story of hope and resilience. The young lawyer, despite facing numerous setbacks and challenges, refuses to give up in his pursuit of justice. His determination and perseverance are inspiring, and serve as a reminder that one person can make a difference in the fight against corporate greed and corruption.

A Civil Action is a must-read for anyone interested in true crime stories, corporate accountability, and the American legal system. The book is a gripping and thought-provoking read that will leave the reader both shocked and enlightened. With its vivid writing style and complex narrative, it is a book that will stay with the reader long after they finish reading it.

# Kiss the Sky (Calloway Sisters, #1)

**Author:** Krista Ritchie  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

In "Kiss the Sky," the first installment of the Calloway Sisters series, Krista Ritchie masterfully crafts a narrative that delves into the complexities of identity, vulnerability, and the pursuit of love. This new adult romance novel is a testament to Ritchie's skill in weaving together a captivating tale that will leave readers enthralled.

At the heart of the story lies Rose Calloway, a 23-year-old heiress who appears to have it all – a Princeton education, a successful fashion line, and a influential family name. However, beneath her icy exterior lies a web of insecurities and fears that threaten to upend her carefully constructed world. When her fashion line is jeopardized by a scandal involving her sister, Rose is forced to confront the harsh realities of her life and the emptiness that has been gnawing at her.

Enter Connor Cobalt, Rose's rival from university, who is as confident as he is arrogant. With a reputation for being untouchable, Connor is determined to help Rose salvage her fashion line and, in the process, claim her heart. As they navigate their tumultuous relationship, Ritchie expertly explores the push-and-pull of their dynamic, laying bare the vulnerabilities that lie beneath their façades.

One of the most striking aspects of "Kiss the Sky" is Ritchie's writing style, which is at once evocative and unflinching. Her prose is peppered with witty banter and searing insights that cut to the very heart of her characters. Ritchie's portrayal of Rose and Connor's relationship is both tender and brutal, exposing the raw emotions that underpin their interactions.

Through Rose and Connor's story, Ritchie tackles themes of identity, class, and the perils of social media. With unflinching honesty, she lays bare the pressures that come with being a high-society heiress, as well as the expectations that accompany such a status. Ritchie's exploration of these themes is both nuanced and thought-provoking, adding depth to the narrative.

As a new adult romance novel, "Kiss the Sky" is sure to appeal to fans of the genre. Ritchie's writing is engaging and accessible, making it easy to become fully invested in the lives of her characters. With its focus on complex relationships, personal growth, and the pursuit of love, this novel is a compelling addition to the Calloway Sisters series.

In short, "Kiss the Sky" is a captivating and emotionally charged novel that will resonate with readers long after the final page has been turned. With its thought-provoking themes, complex characters, and Ritchie's masterful storytelling, this book is a must-read for anyone who enjoys new adult romance.

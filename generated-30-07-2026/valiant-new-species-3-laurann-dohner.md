# Valiant (New Species, #3)

**Author:** Laurann Dohner  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

**Valiant: A Roaring Romance in the New Species Series**

Laurann Dohner's "Valiant," the third installment in her New Species series, is a thrilling and steamy exploration of love, fear, and acceptance, wrapped in a captivating paranormal romance. Dohner's signature style, a blend of intense action, witty dialogue, and heartfelt emotion, shines brightly in this page-turner.

At the heart of "Valiant" lies the timeless theme of fear versus love. Tammy, a resourceful and independent woman, finds herself face-to-face with Valiant, a New Species with the appearance of a lion and the heart of a man. Tammy's initial terror is palpable, yet Dohner masterfully weaves in moments of humor and connection that slowly chip away at Tammy's fear, revealing a deeper, more profound emotion.

Dohner's strength lies in her ability to create compelling, relatable characters despite their extraordinary circumstances. Valiant, a former lab experiment, is a complex hero, torn between his hatred for humans and his undeniable attraction to Tammy. His internal struggle is raw and authentic, making him a hero worth rooting for. Tammy, too, is no damsel in distress. She's a strong, intelligent woman who challenges Valiant and grows throughout the story.

The romance between Tammy and Valiant is intense and passionate, yet it's also built on a foundation of mutual respect and understanding. Dohner doesn't shy away from the challenges they face, both as a couple and as individuals. Their journey is filled with ups and downs, but it's all the more satisfying for it.

Dohner's world-building is another standout feature of "Valiant." The New Species are fascinating creatures, each with their unique abilities and appearances. Dohner's descriptions of their world, from the lush forests of their sanctuary to the grimy labs where they were once held captive, are vivid and immersive.

However, "Valiant" isn't without its flaws. The pacing can feel a bit rushed at times, and some secondary characters feel one-dimensional. But these are minor quibbles in an otherwise engaging read.

In conclusion, "Valiant" is a roaring good time, filled with action, romance, and heart. It's a testament to Laurann Dohner's skill as a storyteller and her ability to create compelling, relatable characters in even the most extraordinary of circumstances.

# The Calling (Darkness Rising, #2)

**Author:** Kelley Armstrong  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

"The Calling" by Kelley Armstrong is a heart-pumping, action-packed sequel to "The Gathering" that delves deeper into the world of supernatural teens with extraordinary gifts. The story picks up where the first book left off, with Maya Delaney and her friends fleeing their homes due to a forest fire that they suspect was deliberately set. The group soon finds themselves kidnapped and stranded on Vancouver Island after a chilling helicopter crash.

As they try to survive the harsh wilderness and evade their pursuers, the group must also navigate their unstable powers and learn to trust each other. Maya, a skin-walker with the ability to run faster, climb higher, and see better than nearly everyone else, is at the center of the story. Her intense connections with the animals that roam the woods outside her home make her a fascinating character, and her journey to master her powers is both captivating and relatable.

One of the main themes of "The Calling" is the idea of found family. The group of mismatched teens is forced to become a family in order to stay alive, and their alliances and trust issues are put to the test. Armstrong expertly weaves together the characters' backstories and motivations, making it easy to become invested in their struggles and relationships.

The supernatural elements in the book are also well-executed. Armstrong's world-building is detailed and immersive, and the different powers and abilities are unique and intriguing. The rival Cabals that are seeking to control the teens add an extra layer of tension to the story, and the secrets of Project Phoenix are slowly unraveled throughout the book.

Armstrong's writing style is engaging and suspenseful, making it easy to get caught up in the story. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, romance, and quiet moments that allow the characters to develop. The author's use of setting is also noteworthy, as the Vancouver Island wilderness becomes a character in its own right.

Overall, "The Calling" is a thrilling and captivating read that will appeal to fans of supernatural fiction and adventure stories. Armstrong's unique blend of mythology, suspense, and coming-of-age themes makes for a compelling narrative that will keep readers on the edge of their seats.

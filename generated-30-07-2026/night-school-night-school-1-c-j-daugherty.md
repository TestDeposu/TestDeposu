# Night School (Night School, #1)

**Author:** C.J. Daugherty  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In "Night School" by C.J. Daugherty, the dark and foreboding atmosphere of Cimmeria Academy sets the tone for a thrilling tale of secrets, lies, and self-discovery. Allie Sheridan, a rebellious teenager with a penchant for trouble, is sent to this isolated boarding school by her parents in a last-ditch effort to reform her. But Cimmeria, with its Gothic architecture and strict rules, holds more than a few secrets of its own.

As Allie navigates her new surroundings, she's drawn to a mysterious and brooding boy named Carter, who seems to embody the very essence of the school's dark and troubled spirit. But when a series of bizarre incidents and gruesome events unfolds, Allie begins to realize that nothing is as it seems at Cimmeria. The school's elite "Night School" program, shrouded in mystery and exclusivity, appears to be at the center of it all.

Daugherty's writing style is atmospheric and suspenseful, weaving a web of intrigue that's hard to resist. She skillfully crafts a sense of unease and tension, keeping readers on edge as they try to piece together the puzzle alongside Allie. The author's use of descriptive language effectively transports readers to the isolated and foreboding world of Cimmeria, where the lines between reality and paranoia are constantly blurred.

One of the most compelling aspects of "Night School" is its exploration of the theme of trust. As Allie becomes increasingly entangled in the school's secrets, she's forced to confront the harsh reality that those closest to her may not be what they seem. Her struggle to discern truth from deception is both relatable and captivating, making it impossible not to become invested in her journey.

Throughout the book, Daugherty also touches on the idea of identity and self-discovery. Allie's transformation from a troubled teenager to a determined and resourceful individual is both authentic and inspiring. As she navigates the treacherous landscape of Cimmeria, she's forced to confront her own demons and make difficult choices that ultimately shape her sense of self.

If you're a fan of dark, suspenseful stories with a hint of romance and self-discovery, "Night School" is an absolute must-read. Daugherty's writing is engaging, and her ability to craft a sense of tension and unease is unparalleled. With its intricate plot twists and shocking revelations, this book will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very end.

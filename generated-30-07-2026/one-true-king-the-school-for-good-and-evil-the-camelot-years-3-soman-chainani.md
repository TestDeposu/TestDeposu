# One True King (The School for Good and Evil: The Camelot Years, #3)

**Author:** Soman Chainani  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

The End of Ends has finally arrived, and with it, the breathtaking culmination of a modern fairy tale phenomenon. In One True King, the sixth and final installment of Soman Chainani’s New York Times bestselling epic, The School for Good and Evil series reaches its absolute zenith. Chainani closes the grand saga of Camelot, the Endless Woods, and our beloved heroines with a narrative that is as ferocious as it is dazzlingly inventive, proving once again why his subversive fairy tale universe has captured the imaginations of millions worldwide.

At the heart of One True King lies a profound and thrilling thematic exploration of destiny, identity, and the unbreakable power of choice. Chainani strips away the comfortable binaries of traditional folklore, moving far beyond the simplistic confines of Good and Evil, and even past the finality of Ever Afters. With Camelot’s crown up for grabs and two formidable rivals laying claim to Excalibur, the stakes are elevated to life-and-death proportions through the harrowing Tournament of Kings. Three grueling trials. Three elusive answers. And a horrific penalty for failure: Excalibur will not only crown the victor, but take the loser’s head. 

Yet, beneath the high-octane magic, political intrigue, and cinematic battles, the true beating heart of the novel remains the legendary bond between Sophie and Agatha. Chainani expertly tests their lifelong friendship against the ultimate pressures of war, ambition, and fate. The narrative asks a poignant question that will resonate deeply with its dedicated readership: can a friendship forged in the fires of magic survive the divergent paths of destiny? The emotional weight carried by these characters anchors the extravagant fantasy elements, ensuring that the spectacle is always driven by genuine human—and magical—heart.

Chainani’s authorial style remains his greatest weapon. His prose is fast-paced, relentlessly imaginative, and dripping with gothic fairy tale atmosphere. He balances dark, high-stakes peril with sharp wit and sparkling wonder, creating a world where no character is entirely safe and no outcome is predictable. The pacing in One True King is relentless; from the moment King Arthur’s posthumous decree initiates the deadly tournament, the reader is dragged into a breathless race to the finish. Chainani has a rare gift for visual storytelling—a talent brilliantly highlighted by the series' massive success on Netflix—painting vibrant, unforgettable tableaus of grand castles, shadowed woods, and magic colliding on an epic scale.

For longtime fans, this final chapter delivers the emotional closure and jaw-dropping twists they have been waiting for, while cementing Chainani’s legacy as a master architect of contemporary fantasy. Whether you discovered the Endless Woods through the pages of the books or the streaming screen, One True King is an unforgettable, heart-pounding farewell to a world where fairy tales are written in blood, bravery, and boundless love. Prepare yourself for a finale that will linger long after you turn the final page.

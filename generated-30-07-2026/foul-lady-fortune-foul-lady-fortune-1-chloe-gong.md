# Foul Lady Fortune (Foul Lady Fortune, #1)

**Author:** Chloe Gong  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**Title: A Dance of Shadows: Chloe Gong's "Foul Lady Fortune"**

In the pulsating heart of 1930s Shanghai, Chloe Gong weaves a spellbinding tale of espionage, redemption, and an ill-fated alliance in "Foul Lady Fortune," the first installment of her captivating new duology. Gong, who dazzled readers with her debut, "These Violent Delights," returns with a story that is as intoxicating as it is intricate, set against the backdrop of a city on the brink of chaos.

At the core of "Foul Lady Fortune" lies the theme of identity, both personal and national, as Rosalind Lang, code-named Fortune, grapples with her past and her place in a world that has changed dramatically since her resurrection. Four years ago, a mysterious experiment saved Rosalind's life but cursed her with immortality and an insatiable hunger for redemption. Now, she's a Nationalist spy, using her unique abilities to protect her country, but her true self remains elusive, hidden beneath layers of deception and guilt.

Gong's prose is as evocative as ever, painting Shanghai in vivid, cinematic detail. The city is a character in its own right, its streets teeming with life, danger, and secrets. Gong masterfully navigates this complex landscape, guiding readers through opulent ballrooms and dimly lit alleyways with equal aplomb. Her characters, too, are richly drawn, their motivations complex and their loyalties shifting like the tides.

Rosalind and Orion, her reluctant partner, are a fascinating pair, their dynamic fueled by tension and mutual mistrust. Their forced marriage of convenience is a powder keg ready to explode, and Gong expertly builds the suspense, their chemistry crackling with unspoken words and repressed emotions. Yet, it's their shared history and the secrets they keep that truly bind them, making their dance of deception all the more compelling.

Gong's exploration of identity extends to the broader political landscape, as the looming threat of Japanese invasion casts a long shadow over Shanghai. The city's foreign concessions become a microcosm of the world's power dynamics, each enclave a reflection of its inhabitants' desires and fears. Rosalind and Orion must navigate this treacherous terrain, their mission to unravel a series of brutal murders entangling them in a web of intrigue that threatens to consume them both.

"Foul Lady Fortune" is a masterclass in slow-burn tension, each chapter peeling back another layer of the mystery, revealing glimpses of a conspiracy that is as horrifying as it is far-reaching. Gong's plotting is impeccable, her ability to weave together multiple threads of intrigue a testament to her skill as a storyteller. Yet, it's her characters that truly shine, their struggles and triumphs resonating long after the final page.

In essence, "Foul Lady Fortune" is a thrilling espionage tale that explores the complexities of identity, loyalty, and redemption. It's a love letter to Shanghai, a gripping mystery, and a testament to Chloe Gong's remarkable talent. With its richly drawn characters, intricate plotting, and evocative prose, "Foul Lady Fortune" is a must-read for fans of historical fiction, espionage thrillers, and anyone who loves a good story, expertly told.

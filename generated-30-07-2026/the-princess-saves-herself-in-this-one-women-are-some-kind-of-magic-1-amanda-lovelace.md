# The Princess Saves Herself in This One (Women Are Some Kind of Magic, #1)

**Author:** Amanda Lovelace  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

In the poignant and powerful collection "the princess saves herself in this one," Amanda Lovelace weaves a mesmerizing narrative that shatters traditional fairy tale tropes and delves into the complexities of love, loss, and empowerment. This is not a story about a damsel in distress waiting for a knight in shining armor; rather, it is a raw and unflinching exploration of the human experience, told through the eyes of a strong and resilient woman.

Lovelace's writing style is both accessible and evocative, making use of simple yet potent language to convey the depths of her emotions. Her poetry is a masterclass in subtlety, often relying on suggestion rather than explicit statement to convey the complexities of her inner world. This approach creates a sense of intimacy and immediacy, drawing the reader into the inner workings of her mind and heart.

The collection is divided into four sections, each of which represents a distinct phase in the author's journey towards self-discovery and healing. The first section, "the princess," is a poignant exploration of childhood trauma and the ways in which it can shape our perceptions of ourselves and the world around us. The second section, "the damsel," delves into the complexities of love and relationships, revealing the ways in which societal expectations can both empower and constrain us. The third section, "the queen," is a powerful exploration of grief, loss, and the process of healing, while the final section, "you," serves as a note to the reader, offering a message of hope and empowerment.

Throughout the collection, Lovelace's use of imagery and metaphor adds depth and complexity to her poetry, creating a rich and immersive reading experience. Her writing is both deeply personal and universally relatable, making this collection a must-read for anyone who has ever struggled with the complexities of love, loss, and identity.

One of the most striking aspects of Lovelace's writing is her ability to balance light and dark, hope and despair. Her poetry is unflinching in its portrayal of the human experience, yet it is also infused with a sense of resilience and determination. This is a collection that will make you laugh and cry, often on the same page.

In short, "the princess saves herself in this one" is a masterpiece of contemporary poetry, a powerful exploration of the human experience that will resonate with readers long after they finish the book. Lovelace's writing is a testament to the transformative power of poetry, and this collection is a must-read for anyone who has ever found solace in the written word.

# More Happy Than Not

**Author:** Adam Silvera  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

**More Happy Than Not** by Adam Silvera is a poignant and thought-provoking exploration of love, loss, and identity, set against the backdrop of a gritty, near-future Bronx. Silvera's debut novel is a powerful testament to the human spirit's resilience in the face of adversity, packaged in a compelling narrative that keeps readers on the edge of their seats.

At its core, **More Happy Than Not** grapples with the complexities of happiness and the lengths one might go to achieve it. Sixteen-year-old Aaron Soto, still reeling from his father's suicide, finds himself navigating a labyrinth of grief, guilt, and confusion. His relationship with his girlfriend, Genevieve, and his newfound connection with Thomas, a charismatic newcomer, challenge Aaron's understanding of love and his place in the world.

Silvera's prose is raw and unapologetic, delving deep into Aaron's psyche with an honesty that is both refreshing and heart-wrenching. He masterfully weaves together Aaron's past and present, creating a narrative that is as much about healing as it is about self-discovery. The author's ability to balance the harsh realities of Aaron's life with moments of levity and hope is a testament to his skill as a storyteller.

The novel's exploration of memory and identity, through the lens of the Leteo Institute's memory-alteration procedure, adds another layer of complexity to the story. It raises thought-provoking questions about the nature of happiness and whether it's worth the cost of forgetting who we are.

**More Happy Than Not** is not an easy read. It's a rollercoaster of emotions that will leave you breathless and, ultimately, more empathetic. Silvera's unflinching exploration of Aaron's journey is a powerful reminder that happiness is not a destination, but a journey filled with challenges, setbacks, and triumphs.

# What If It's Us (What If It's Us, #1)

**Author:** Becky Albertalli  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

**What If It's Us: A Heartwarming, Quirky Romance That's Hard to Resist**

Becky Albertalli and Adam Silvera team up to deliver a delightful, heartfelt exploration of love, identity, and the universe's mysterious ways in their collaborative novel, "What If It's Us." This charming tale of two boys, Arthur and Ben, who cross paths in New York City, is a captivating blend of Albertalli's signature wit and Silvera's emotional depth, resulting in a story that's as engaging as it is relatable.

At its core, "What If It's Us" is a love story, but it's one that's refreshingly unpredictable. Arthur, a Broadway enthusiast from Georgia, is in New York for the summer, while Ben, a native New Yorker, is grappling with a recent breakup. Their meet-cute at the post office is as awkward as it is adorable, setting the stage for a series of chance encounters that leave them both wondering if the universe is pushing them together or pulling them apart.

Albertalli and Silvera masterfully weave together Arthur and Ben's perspectives, each voice distinct and authentic. Arthur's effervescent enthusiasm and Ben's guarded cynicism create a compelling dynamic, their banter sparking like a well-choreographed dance. The authors also excel in crafting a diverse cast of supporting characters, from Arthur's supportive family to Ben's tight-knit group of friends, each adding depth and humor to the story.

The narrative is peppered with references to Broadway shows, which serve as a delightful metaphor for Arthur and Ben's relationship. Like any good musical, their story is filled with ups and downs, moments of joy and heartache, all building towards a climax that's as satisfying as it is unexpected.

Yet, "What If It's Us" is more than just a romance. It's a story about identity, self-discovery, and the courage it takes to be truly vulnerable. Both Arthur and Ben must confront their fears and insecurities, learning to open themselves up to love and connection. It's a journey that's as challenging as it is rewarding, and one that readers will find themselves rooting for, even as they laugh and cry along the way.

Albertalli and Silvera's collaboration is a testament to their shared talent for crafting compelling, relatable characters and weaving together heartfelt stories. "What If It's Us" is a book that will make you believe in love, in the universe, and in the power of second chances. It's a story that will leave you smiling, your heart full, and your eyes a little misty. And isn't that what the best love stories are made of?

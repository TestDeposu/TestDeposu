# Not a Penny More, Not a Penny Less

**Author:** Jeffrey Archer  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2004

**Not a Penny More, Not a Penny Less** by Jeffrey Archer is a masterclass in revenge, a thrilling tale that weaves together the stories of four strangers united by their shared victimhood at the hands of a cunning con artist. This is Archer at his finest, showcasing the intricate plotting and character development that have made him a global bestseller.

The novel opens with each of the four protagonists—an Oxford don, a society physician, a French art dealer, and an English lord—falling prey to the ingenious swindle orchestrated by the enigmatic Harvey Metcalfe. The sheer audacity of Metcalfe's deception is breathtaking, and Archer's skill in revealing the mechanics of the scam is reminiscent of a magician slowly revealing the secrets behind their tricks.

However, this is no ordinary heist novel. The real magic lies in the way Archer transforms his victims into heroes. As the four strangers cross paths, they decide to pool their resources and expertise to exact revenge on Metcalfe. The dynamic between these unlikely allies is a joy to behold, each bringing their unique skills to the table as they meticulously plan their counterattack.

Archer's strength lies in his ability to create compelling, relatable characters, even when they are engaged in morally grey activities. He masterfully balances the thrill of the chase with moments of introspection, allowing us to understand and empathize with the motivations of his characters. The result is a page-turner that keeps you on the edge of your seat, yet also invites you to ponder the complexities of human nature.

The narrative moves at a breakneck pace, taking us from the glamorous casinos of Monte Carlo to the bustling streets of Wall Street. Yet, despite the global scope, Archer's prose remains grounded and accessible, making the complex world of high-stakes finance and art dealing accessible to the layman.

**Not a Penny More, Not a Penny Less** is more than just a revenge thriller; it's a testament to the human spirit's resilience and capacity for reinvention. It's a reminder that even in the face of monumental loss, we can choose to fight back, to adapt, and to triumph.

In a nutshell, this book is a must-read for fans of intricate plotting, compelling characters, and high-stakes drama. It's a testament to Archer's skill as a storyteller and a reminder of why he remains one of the world's most popular authors.

# Before We Were Strangers

**Author:** Renee Carlino  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

Before We Were Strangers by Renee Carlino is a poignant and captivating love story that explores the themes of lost love, second chances, and the enduring power of true connection. The novel tells the story of two individuals who meet in college, form an intense bond, and then lose touch after graduation. Fifteen years later, they reconnect through a Craigslist "missed connection" post, and the story unfolds as they rekindle their romance and confront the choices they made in the past.

Carlino's writing is evocative and immersive, transporting the reader to the vibrant streets of New York City and the nostalgia-tinged memories of the protagonists' youth. The author's use of language is lyrical and expressive, conveying the intensity of the characters' emotions and the depth of their connection. The narrative is interspersed with witty dialogue, humorous anecdotes, and poignant reflections, making for a compelling and engaging read.

One of the standout aspects of the novel is its exploration of the human experience. Carlino delves into the complexities of love, relationships, and personal growth, raising questions about the choices we make and the paths we take in life. The characters are multidimensional and relatable, with flaws and strengths that make them feel authentic and endearing.

The author's style is characterized by a unique blend of humor, pathos, and insight. Carlino has a keen eye for detail, capturing the nuances of human behavior and the subtleties of emotional expression. Her writing is both accessible and sophisticated, making the novel appealing to a wide range of readers.

The central theme of the book – the power of second chances – is both timely and timeless. In an era where connections are often fleeting and relationships are frequently reduced to digital interactions, Before We Were Strangers reminds us of the importance of human connection and the value of taking risks in love.

Overall, Before We Were Strangers is a beautifully written and deeply moving novel that will resonate with anyone who has ever experienced the thrill of new love or the pang of lost connection. With its vivid characters, evocative setting, and thought-provoking themes, this book is sure to captivate readers and leave a lasting impression.

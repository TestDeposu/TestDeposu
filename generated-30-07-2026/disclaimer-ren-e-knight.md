# Disclaimer

**Author:** Renée Knight  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

**Disclaimer: A Masterclass in Psychological Suspense**

Renée Knight's debut novel, "Disclaimer," is a tour de force of psychological suspense that keeps readers on the edge of their seats until the very last page. The story unfolds with the precision of a Swiss watch, each gear meshing perfectly with the next to drive an intricate plot that explores the dark recesses of the human psyche.

At its heart, "Disclaimer" is a tale of revenge, obsession, and the lengths we go to protect our secrets. Catherine Ravenscroft, a documentary filmmaker, finds a mysterious novel, "The Perfect Stranger," by her bedside. The story within the story is a chilling reenactment of a day Catherine would rather forget, a day that changed her life forever. As she delves deeper into the novel, she realizes that the past she thought she had buried is catching up with her, threatening to destroy her carefully constructed life.

Knight's prose is crisp and concise, her characters complex and flawed. Catherine is a protagonist you can't help but root for, even as her desperation to keep her secret safe makes her increasingly unlikable. The novel's antagonist, on the other hand, is a masterclass in ambiguity, their motives and actions shrouded in mystery until the very end.

The real triumph of "Disclaimer," however, is its structure. Knight employs a narrative device that blurs the line between reality and fiction, making the reader complicit in Catherine's descent into paranoia. As the story within the story unfolds, it becomes increasingly difficult to tell where the novel ends and Catherine's life begins. This meta-narrative is a bold and innovative choice that elevates "Disclaimer" from a mere thriller to a literary exploration of the power of storytelling.

"Disclaimer" is not just a page-turner; it's a psychological study of the human condition. It asks profound questions about guilt, redemption, and the price we pay for our secrets. It's a book that will stay with you long after you've turned the last page, leaving you questioning what you know about yourself and the stories you tell.

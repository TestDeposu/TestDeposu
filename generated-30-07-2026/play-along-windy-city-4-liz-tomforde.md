# Play Along (Windy City, #4)

**Author:** Liz Tomforde  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

When it comes to the modern landscape of sports romance, few authors command TikTok and the global book community quite like Liz Tomforde. With Play Along, the fourth installment in her wildly successful Windy City series, Tomforde returns to the high-stakes, emotionally charged world of Chicago professional athletes. This time, she tackles one of romance fiction's most delicious and combustible tropes—the accidental Vegas marriage—and elevates it with her signature blend of workplace tension, deep emotional vulnerability, and sizzling chemistry.

At its core, Play Along explores the delicate tightrope women must walk in male-dominated professional spaces. The narrative follows Kennedy Kay, the lone female athletic trainer on the Windy City Warriors staff. Years of battling a misogynistic boss have left her battle-scarred and fiercely protective of her professional reputation. She is mere months away from her dream job with a new organization; all she needs to do is cross the finish line with her pristine track record intact. Enter Isaiah, the franchise’s star baseball player, whose golden-boy persona hides a dogged, one-track devotion to the fiery redhead who has successfully rebuffed his advances for years.

What follows a wild night in Sin City is a nightmare scenario for Kennedy’s meticulously guarded career: waking up legally bound to her team's biggest star. Rather than opting for an immediate annulment, the duo strikes a deal to maintain the ruse for a single baseball season—a compromise designed to protect Kennedy's professional standing while giving Isaiah the opening he has been waiting for. 

Tomforde’s greatest strength as a writer lies in her ability to balance laugh-out-loud banter with profound emotional stakes. She does not merely write spicy scenes; she builds worlds where physical attraction is deeply intertwined with mutual respect and psychological healing. In Play Along, the author expertly subverts the "golden retriever hockey or baseball player" archetype. Isaiah is relentless, charming, and utterly devoted, but his pursuit of Kennedy never crosses into overbearing territory. Instead, he becomes her staunchest ally in a workplace designed to minimize her. 

The pacing of the book hinges on the juxtaposition of public glamour and private domesticity. As Kennedy and Isaiah transition from reluctant roommates to genuine partners, Tomforde explores the theme of surrender—how terrifying it is for a woman who has had to fight for every inch of respect to finally let someone else take care of her. The forced-proximity dynamics crackle with electricity, driven by sharp dialogue and a palpable yearning that builds steadily page by page. 

For fans of slow-burn romance, workplace dynamics, and characters who fight tooth and nail for their happily-ever-afters, Play Along is a triumphant addition to the Windy City universe. Tomforde proves once again why she is a powerhouse in contemporary romance, delivering a story that is as heartwarming as it is deeply passionate.

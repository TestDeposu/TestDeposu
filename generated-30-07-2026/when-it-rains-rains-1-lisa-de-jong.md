# When It Rains (Rains, #1)

**Author:** Lisa De Jong  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

When It Rains by Lisa De Jong is a poignant and emotional novel that delves into the complexities of human relationships, trauma, and the power of hope and redemption. The story revolves around the protagonist, who finds herself at a crossroads in her life, struggling to come to terms with a traumatic event from her past.

De Jong's writing style is lyrical and evocative, weaving a narrative that is both deeply personal and universally relatable. She masterfully crafts a world that is both fragile and resilient, where characters are multidimensional and flawed. The protagonist's voice is authentic and raw, drawing the reader into her inner world and making it impossible not to become invested in her journey.

At its core, When It Rains is a story about the transformative power of human connection. The protagonist's relationships with Beau and Asher serve as catalysts for her growth and healing, highlighting the ways in which people can both hurt and save us. De Jong explores the complexities of love, friendship, and loyalty, raising questions about the nature of intimacy and the boundaries we set for ourselves.

One of the most striking aspects of De Jong's writing is her ability to balance light and dark, hope and despair. The novel is not afraid to confront the harsh realities of trauma and pain, but it also offers a message of resilience and redemption. The protagonist's journey is not easy, but it is ultimately uplifting, suggesting that even in the darkest moments, there is always the possibility for healing and transformation.

The character development in When It Rains is impressive, with each character bringing their own unique perspective and depth to the narrative. Beau and Asher are more than just love interests; they are fully fleshed-out characters with their own motivations and conflicts. The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, adding richness and texture to the story.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the novel sometimes relies too heavily on convenient plot devices and coincidences. However, these moments are minor and do not detract from the overall impact of the story.

In conclusion, When It Rains is a powerful and moving novel that will resonate with fans of contemporary fiction and romance. De Jong's writing is engaging and emotive, making this a must-read for anyone looking for a story that will touch their heart and stay with them long after the final page has been turned.

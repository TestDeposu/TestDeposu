# Flyte (Septimus Heap, #2)

**Author:** Angie Sage  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**Flyte: A Magykal Journey into Family and Betrayal**

In "Flyte," Angie Sage masterfully weaves a tale that delves into the heart of family, loyalty, and the dark power of revenge. A year has passed since Septimus Heap discovered his true lineage, and now, as Marcia Overstrand's apprentice, he's navigating the complexities of Magyk. Meanwhile, Princess Jenna is adapting to her royal life, but sinister forces loom, threatening their newfound happiness.

Sage's signature humor and intricate world-building are on full display. The Castle and its inhabitants are as vivid as ever, with new layers of detail that draw readers deeper into this enchanting realm. Yet, it's the exploration of darker themes that elevates "Flyte" above its predecessor. Sage doesn't shy away from the harsh realities of betrayal and the destructive power of obsession.

Septimus's brother, Simon, returns with a vengeance, his quest for retribution driving him to desperate measures. Sage expertly portrays Simon's descent into darkness, making him a chilling and relatable villain. This isn't a tale of good versus evil, but of family torn apart by misunderstanding and resentment.

The plot is a thrilling race against time, filled with suspense and unexpected twists. Sage's pacing is impeccable, keeping readers on the edge of their seats. The introduction of Flyte, an ancient and dangerous Magyk, adds an exciting layer of mystery and danger.

At its core, "Flyte" is a story about the bonds of family and the lengths we'll go to protect those we love. It's a testament to Sage's skill that she can create such a compelling narrative within a fantastical world, making readers care deeply about characters who, in another context, might seem larger than life.

# The Novice (Summoner, #1)

**Author:** Taran Matharu  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

**Preview/Critique:**

In "The Novice," Taran Matharu weaves a captivating tale that masterfully blends fantasy, coming-of-age, and military academy tropes, resulting in a spellbinding adventure that's as engaging as it is imaginative.

At its heart, "The Novice" explores the theme of identity and the struggle to find one's place in a world that often seems determined to pigeonhole us. Our protagonist, Fletcher, is a blacksmith's apprentice with a secret: he can summon demons. This discovery propels him into a world he never knew existed, one filled with magic, intrigue, and danger. As Fletcher navigates this new landscape, he grapples with his identity, torn between his humble origins and the expectations placed upon him as a summoner.

Matharu's prose is accessible yet evocative, painting vivid images of the academy, its inhabitants, and the fantastical creatures that populate this world. He excels at world-building, creating a richly detailed universe that feels both familiar and fresh. The academy setting allows for a diverse cast of characters, each with their own unique backstory and motivations, adding depth and complexity to the narrative.

One of the standout aspects of "The Novice" is the relationship between Fletcher and his demon, Ignatius. Their bond is a testament to Matharu's ability to create compelling, emotionally resonant relationships. Ignatius is more than just a pet or a weapon; he's a fully realized character with his own personality and agency, and his dynamic with Fletcher is one of the most engaging aspects of the book.

However, "The Novice" is not without its flaws. The pacing can feel uneven at times, with certain sections dragging while others race forward at breakneck speed. Additionally, some of the plot developments feel a bit too convenient, and certain characters' motivations could use more nuance.

Despite these minor issues, "The Novice" is a thrilling and engaging read that will appeal to fans of fantasy, coming-of-age stories, and military academy tropes. Matharu's ability to create a richly detailed world and compelling characters makes "The Novice" a standout debut, and a promising start to the Summoner series.

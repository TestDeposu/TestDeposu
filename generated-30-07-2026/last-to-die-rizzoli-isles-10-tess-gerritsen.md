# Last to Die (Rizzoli & Isles, #10)

**Author:** Tess Gerritsen  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**Last to Die: A Gripping Return to the Rizzoli & Isles**

Tess Gerritsen's **Last to Die** is a thrilling addition to the Rizzoli & Isles series, proving once again why Gerritsen is a master of suspense. The novel is a testament to her ability to weave intricate plots with compelling characters, keeping readers on the edge of their seats until the very end.

At the heart of **Last to Die** lies the complex relationship between mothers and daughters, and the lengths they go to protect each other. Jane Rizzoli, the tough-as-nails detective, finds herself grappling with her mother Angela's intuitions about a potential threat in their neighborhood. Angela, a character often overshadowed by Jane's dynamism, finally takes center stage, her keen observations and maternal instincts driving a subplot that is as engaging as the main investigation.

Gerritsen's signature style is evident in her ability to create a sense of foreboding that permeates every page. She masterfully juggles multiple plotlines, each with its own set of suspects and red herrings, keeping readers guessing until the final reveal. The pacing is relentless, with short chapters that propel the story forward, interspersed with chilling glimpses into the killer's mind.

The Boston setting, a familiar backdrop in the series, is as much a character as the humans inhabiting it. Gerritsen's deep understanding of the city lends authenticity to the story, with its neighborhoods, history, and cultural nuances adding depth to the narrative.

**Last to Die** also explores the theme of secrets and the destructive power they wield. Both Jane and Angela are drawn into webs of deceit, forcing them to confront their own biases and assumptions. Gerritsen skillfully uses these themes to challenge her characters and readers alike, inviting us to question what we think we know.

In true Rizzoli & Isles fashion, **Last to Die** is a gripping, suspenseful read that will keep you up at night. Gerritsen's prose is taut and engaging, her characters well-drawn and relatable. This is a novel that will appeal to fans of the series and newcomers alike, offering a compelling blend of crime, mystery, and family drama.

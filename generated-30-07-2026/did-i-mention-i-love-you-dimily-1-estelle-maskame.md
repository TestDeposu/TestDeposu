# Did I Mention I Love You? (DIMILY, #1)

**Author:** Estelle Maskame  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In the captivating world of young adult romance, Estelle Maskame's "Did I Mention I Love You?" is a stunning debut that delves into the complexities of forbidden love, heartbreak, and the unrelenting allure of summer romance. Originally conceived on the popular online platform Wattpad, this novel has evolved into a gripping tale that masterfully weaves together the intricate threads of family dynamics, personal growth, and the intoxicating thrill of first love.

At the heart of the narrative lies Eden Munro, a protagonist seeking solace in the sun-kissed beaches of Santa Monica, desperate to escape the turmoil of her past. However, her hopes for a peaceful retreat are quickly dashed upon meeting her enigmatic stepbrother, Tyler Bruce. With his chiseled charm and an air of mystery, Tyler embodies the quintessential bad boy, drawing Eden into a world of excitement and danger that threatens to upend her carefully constructed defenses.

Maskame's writing style is characterized by its effortless fluidity, allowing the reader to become fully immersed in the world of Santa Monica's beach parties, late-night escapades, and the intoxicating atmosphere of summer freedom. Her prose is imbued with a sense of authenticity, capturing the angst and uncertainty of adolescence with remarkable precision. As Eden navigates the treacherous landscape of her feelings for Tyler, the author skillfully conveys the intensity of their emotions, crafting a romance that is at once tender, passionate, and heart-wrenching.

One of the novel's greatest strengths lies in its thoughtful exploration of the human experience. Maskame tackles complex themes such as family secrets, personal identity, and the struggle to reconcile past mistakes with remarkable sensitivity. Through Eden's journey, the author sheds light on the difficulties of forgiveness, the importance of self-discovery, and the enduring power of love to heal even the deepest of wounds.

The chemistry between Eden and Tyler is undeniable, their romance simmering with a palpable tension that propels the narrative forward. As they navigate the complexities of their forbidden attraction, the author masterfully ratchets up the emotional stakes, plunging the reader into a world of uncertainty and heartbreak.

In conclusion, "Did I Mention I Love You?" is a captivating young adult romance that will resonate with fans of authors such as Colleen Hoover and Abbi Glines. Estelle Maskame's writing is a breath of fresh air, bringing a unique voice to the world of YA fiction. With its relatable characters, engaging plot, and poignant themes, this novel is sure to leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in the DIMILY series.

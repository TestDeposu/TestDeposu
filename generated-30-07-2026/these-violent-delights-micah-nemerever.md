# These Violent Delights

**Author:** Micah Nemerever  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

**A Dance of Darkness and Desire: "These Violent Delights" by Micah Nemerever**

In the vein of Donna Tartt's "The Secret History" and Tama Janowitz's "Slaves of New York," Micah Nemerever's debut novel, "These Violent Delights," is a gripping exploration of obsession, power dynamics, and the destructive potential of unchecked desire. Set against the backdrop of early 1970s Pittsburgh, this dark academia tale weaves a hypnotic spell, drawing readers into a toxic relationship that simmers with intensity and danger.

At the heart of the novel lies the complex, codependent bond between Paul and Julian, two college students whose lives become inextricably entwined. Paul, a sensitive and insecure young man grappling with his father's recent death, finds in Julian a charismatic and worldly intellectual equal. Julian, meanwhile, is a mercurial figure, capable of both profound charm and cruel caprice, and Paul becomes increasingly ensnared in his web.

Nemerever's prose is taut and precise, like a noose tightening around the reader's neck. He masterfully builds tension, expertly balancing moments of tender intimacy with sudden, jarring violence. The narrative unfolds with a propulsive ferocity, hurtling towards an act of irrevocable violence that is both shocking and inevitable.

The novel's true power lies in its unflinching examination of the darkness that can lurk within human desire. Paul and Julian's relationship is a dance of dominance and submission, a pas de deux that blurs the lines between love and obsession, admiration and contempt. Nemerever doesn't shy away from the ugly truths of their dynamic, instead holding them up to the light and forcing the reader to confront the unsettling reality of their own complicity.

"These Violent Delights" is not an easy read, nor should it be. It's a novel that demands your attention, that challenges you to look away from the horror unfolding on the page. But it's also a novel that is impossible to put down, a story that will stay with you long after you've turned the final page.

In a literary landscape crowded with safe, predictable narratives, "These Violent Delights" stands out as a bold, unapologetic exploration of the darker recesses of the human psyche. It's a novel that will leave you breathless, that will make you question the nature of love, obsession, and the fine line that separates the two.

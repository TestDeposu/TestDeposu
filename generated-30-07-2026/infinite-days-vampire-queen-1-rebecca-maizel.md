# Infinite Days (Vampire Queen, #1)

**Author:** Rebecca Maizel  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Infinite Days, the debut novel of Rebecca Maizel, is a captivating tale that redefines the conventional vampire lore. This young adult fiction masterfully weaves a narrative that explores the complexities of immortality, humanity, and the struggles of self-discovery. The story follows Lenah, a 500-year-old vampire queen, who, after centuries of bloodlust and tyranny, undergoes a perilous ritual to regain her mortality. As she awakens from a century-long slumber, Lenah finds herself in the unlikeliest of places - a boarding school in Massachusetts, where she must navigate the challenges of being a teenager once again.

Maizel's writing is nothing short of enchanting, with a lyrical prose that transports readers to a world of dark fantasy and romance. Her use of language is evocative and sensual, making it easy to become immersed in the world she has created. The author's ability to craft relatable characters, particularly Lenah, is impressive. The protagonist's journey from a powerful vampire queen to a vulnerable teenager is both poignant and compelling, as she grapples with the consequences of her newfound humanity.

One of the most striking aspects of Infinite Days is its exploration of the human condition. Maizel delves into themes of identity, morality, and the struggles of growing up, all set against the backdrop of vampire lore. Lenah's experiences serve as a metaphor for the challenges we all face in our own lives, making her story feel surprisingly universal. As she navigates the complexities of human relationships, Lenah must also confront the darkness of her past and the evil that has haunted her for centuries.

The world-building in Infinite Days is equally impressive, with Maizel creating a rich and atmospheric setting that draws readers in. The Wickham Boarding School, with its Gothic architecture and mist-shrouded grounds, serves as the perfect backdrop for Lenah's journey. The author's use of descriptive language brings the setting to life, making it easy to visualize the world she has created.

The supporting characters in the novel are equally well-crafted, with Justin, the popular boy who captures Lenah's heart, being a particularly notable example. Their romance is sweet and tender, yet fraught with danger, as Lenah struggles to reconcile her growing feelings with the risks of her vampire past. The relationships between Lenah and her new friends are also well-developed, adding depth and complexity to the narrative.

Infinite Days is a wonderfully sexy, dark novel that will appeal to fans of vampire fiction and young adult fantasy. Maizel's writing is captivating, and her story is both haunting and romantic. With its themes of self-discovery, morality, and the complexities of human relationships, this novel is sure to resonate with readers of all ages. As Carrie Jones, author of the bestselling NEED series, notes, "Rebecca Maizel is a marvel, writing more than just a story, she creates myth with every page."

In conclusion, Infinite Days is a must-read for anyone who enjoys vampire fiction, young adult fantasy, or romance. With its captivating narrative, well-developed characters, and atmospheric setting, this novel is sure to leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in the Vampire Queen series.

# Entice (Need, #3)

**Author:** Carrie Jones  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

In the thrilling conclusion to Carrie Jones's Need series, Entice is a captivating tale of courage, friendship, and the unrelenting battle between good and evil. This mesmerizing story whisks readers away to the mystical realm of Aurora, where Time Stopper Annie must confront her darkest fears and the sinister forces threatening her newfound home.

Jones masterfully weaves a rich tapestry of characters, each with their unique struggles and motivations. Annie, the protagonist, is a complex and relatable heroine whose determination and compassion make her a compelling role model. Her loyal friends, including Eva, Bloom, SalGoud, and Jamie, add depth and humor to the narrative, their distinct personalities and quirks making them feel like old friends.

One of the most striking aspects of Jones's writing style is her ability to craft a narrative that is both epic in scope and intimate in its character development. The world-building in Entice is meticulous and immersive, with the Badlands serving as a haunting backdrop for the group's perilous journey. Jones's vivid descriptions of the twisted landscapes and terrifying creatures that inhabit them will leave readers on the edge of their seats.

At its core, Entice is a story about the transformative power of courage and the unbreakable bonds of friendship. As Annie and her companions face their deepest fears and confront the darkness within themselves, they discover that the true source of their strength lies not in their individual abilities, but in their collective resilience and determination.

Jones's writing is infused with a sense of urgency and danger, propelling the narrative forward at a breakneck pace. The action scenes are intense and suspenseful, with the author's masterful use of tension and release keeping readers guessing until the very end.

The themes of Entice are both timely and timeless, speaking to the universal human experiences of fear, courage, and the struggle between light and darkness. Jones tackles these complex issues with sensitivity and nuance, offering readers a rich and thought-provoking narrative that will linger long after the final page is turned.

In short, Entice is a captivating and unforgettable conclusion to the Need series. With its rich world-building, complex characters, and pulse-pounding action sequences, this book is sure to enthrall fans of fantasy and adventure. If you're looking for a story that will sweep you up in its epic scope and leave you breathless, look no further than Entice.

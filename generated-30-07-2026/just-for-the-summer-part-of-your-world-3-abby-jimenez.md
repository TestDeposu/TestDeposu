# Just for the Summer (Part of Your World, #3)

**Author:** Abby Jimenez  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

**Just for the Summer: A Delightful, Heartfelt Rom-Com**

Abby Jimenez's "Just for the Summer" is a charming, heartwarming addition to her Part of Your World series, proving once again that she's a master of the romantic comedy genre. This instant #1 New York Times bestseller is the perfect beach read, offering a delightful blend of wit, heart, and just the right amount of steam to keep you turning pages.

At its core, "Just for the Summer" explores the themes of fate, love, and the unexpected paths life can take. Justin, a man cursed with every ex finding their soulmate after they break up, and Emma, a traveling nurse with her own dating woes, decide to date each other with the sole purpose of breaking up. Their plan? To cancel out each other's curses and finally find lasting love. It's a bonkers idea, but Jimenez sells it with her signature humor and heart.

Justin and Emma's relationship is a slow burn, a dance of will-they-won't-they that keeps readers on the edge of their seats. Their chemistry is palpable, their banter hilarious, and their connection genuine. As they navigate their summer fling, they're also dealing with real-life issues - Emma's toxic mother and Justin's sudden guardianship of his three siblings. These challenges add depth to the story, making it more than just a lighthearted romance.

Jimenez's writing style is engaging and relatable, her characters well-developed and endearing. She has a knack for creating flawed, yet lovable protagonists who grow and evolve throughout the story. The supporting cast, from Emma's best friend to Justin's siblings, are equally compelling, adding richness to the narrative.

"Just for the Summer" is a testament to Jimenez's ability to weave a compelling story that's equal parts funny, heartwarming, and thought-provoking. It's a story about love, yes, but also about the unexpected places it can be found, and the courage it takes to let go of our preconceived notions.

In a sea of rom-coms, "Just for the Summer" stands out as a gem. It's a story that will make you laugh, cry, and believe in love again. So, grab your sunscreen, find a cozy spot by the lake, and dive into this delightful read.

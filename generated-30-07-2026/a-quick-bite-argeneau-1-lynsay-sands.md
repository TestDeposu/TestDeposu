# A Quick Bite (Argeneau #1)

**Author:** Lynsay Sands  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Lissianna Argeneau has a problem that is practically a professional hazard: she is a vampire who faints at the sight of blood. For centuries, this peculiar affliction has kept her from enjoying a proper meal, let alone finding a lasting relationship. Enter Dr. Gregory Hewitt, a human who wakes up tied to her bed in what appears to be a kidnapping scenario, only to realize his captor is a stunning, albeit incredibly flustered, immortal. 

In A Quick Bite, author Lynsay Sands establishes the foundation of her wildly popular Argeneau series by taking the traditionally dark, brooding tropes of vampire fiction and turning them on their head. Instead of leaning into gothic dread and tragic nocturnal brooding, Sands injects the genre with a heavy dose of laugh-out-loud romantic comedy and domestic normality. The central theme of the novel explores the juxtaposition of the extraordinary and the mundane. Vampires in Sands' universe are not hiding in shadows from pitchfork-wielding mobs; they pay taxes, hold down jobs, worry about family drama, and, most importantly, struggle with modern dating. Lissianna is desperately searching for her "Life Mate"—a destined soulmate who will make the centuries of waiting worthwhile—making her remarkably human despite her fangs and immortal status.

Sands’ writing style is breezy, fast-paced, and unapologetically lighthearted. She relies heavily on witty dialogue, situational comedy, and undeniable physical chemistry between the protagonists. Gregory’s reaction to discovering vampires are real is less existential terror and more pragmatic fascination, providing a refreshing anchor for the reader. The banter between the uptight, caring doctor and the ancient, blood-phobic vixen drives the narrative forward with effortless charm. 

While purists of dark fantasy might find the tone too whimsical, A Quick Bite excels as a cozy, sensual, and humorous escape. It proves that even creatures of the night just want to find someone to snuggle with—preferably someone with a very delicious neck.

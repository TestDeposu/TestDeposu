# Rembulan Tenggelam Di Wajahmu

**Author:** Tere Liye  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

"Unraveling the Mysteries of Life: A Review of 'Rembulan Tenggelam Di Wajahmu' by Tere Liye"

In the midst of life's chaos, we often find ourselves lost and searching for answers to the most profound questions. It is in these moments of introspection that we yearn for a guiding light, a gentle whisper that can lead us through the darkness. Tere Liye's "Rembulan Tenggelam Di Wajahmu" (Moon Sinking on Your Face) is a thought-provoking novel that delves into the very fabric of existence, posing fundamental questions that have haunted humanity for centuries.

Published in 2009, this Indonesian novel has captured the hearts of readers with its unique blend of fantasy and philosophical introspection. The story revolves around Ray, the protagonist, who is given the extraordinary opportunity to ask five questions to an angel about the mysteries of life. As Ray grapples with the complexities of existence, the reader is taken on a journey of self-discovery, navigating the intricacies of love, fairness, wealth, choice, and loss.

Tere Liye's writing style is characterized by simplicity, yet it belies a profound depth. His use of language is lyrical, weaving a narrative that is both poignant and thought-provoking. The author's ability to distill complex philosophical concepts into accessible and relatable terms is a testament to his skill as a storyteller. The novel's pacing is deliberate, allowing the reader to absorb the weight of each question and its corresponding answer.

At its core, "Rembulan Tenggelam Di Wajahmu" is a novel about the human condition. It explores the intricacies of existence, encouraging readers to reflect on their own values and priorities. The author's use of fantasy elements serves as a catalyst for introspection, inviting readers to reexamine their assumptions about life and its meaning.

One of the novel's greatest strengths lies in its ability to transcend cultural boundaries. Despite being written in Indonesian, the themes and questions posed in the novel are universally relatable. The author's exploration of fundamental human concerns – love, fairness, and the search for meaning – resonates deeply with readers from diverse backgrounds.

In conclusion, "Rembulan Tenggelam Di Wajahmu" is a novel that will appeal to readers seeking a thought-provoking and introspective experience. Tere Liye's unique writing style and the novel's exploration of fundamental human concerns make it a compelling read. As the author so eloquently puts it, "all matters are simple"; it is our perception that complicates them. This novel is a gentle reminder to simplify our lives, to seek answers to the questions that truly matter.

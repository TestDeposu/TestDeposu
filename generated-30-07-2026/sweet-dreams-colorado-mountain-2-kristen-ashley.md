# Sweet Dreams (Colorado Mountain, #2)

**Author:** Kristen Ashley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

"Sweet Dreams" by Kristen Ashley is a sizzling romance novel that delves into the complexities of love, trust, and self-discovery. The second installment in the Colorado Mountain series, this book follows Lauren Grahame as she embarks on a journey of reinvention in the small town of Carnal, Colorado. After a painful divorce, Lauren is determined to start anew, but her plans are quickly derailed when she meets Tatum Jackson, the ruggedly handsome and charismatic co-owner of Bubba's, the local biker bar.

Ashley's writing style is unmistakable - she weaves a narrative that is both raw and emotional, with characters that are multidimensional and relatable. Lauren and Tate's story is a masterclass in slow-burning tension, as they navigate their undeniable attraction amidst a backdrop of small-town secrets and lies. The author's use of descriptive language paints a vivid picture of the Colorado mountains, immersing the reader in the rugged beauty of the setting.

One of the standout aspects of "Sweet Dreams" is its exploration of themes that are both timely and timeless. Lauren's struggles to find her place in the world, to reconcile her past and present selves, will resonate deeply with readers who have experienced similar struggles. Tate's character, too, is a nuanced portrayal of a man who is both tough and vulnerable, his rough exterior hiding a deep well of emotion.

As the story unfolds, Ashley skillfully ratchets up the tension, introducing elements of suspense and danger that threaten to derail Lauren and Tate's blossoming relationship. The pacing is expertly handled, with a narrative that flows smoothly from one scene to the next. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with a memorable ensemble of characters that add depth and humor to the story.

If you're a fan of romance novels that are equal parts sexy and emotionally resonant, then "Sweet Dreams" is a must-read. Ashley's writing is engaging, her characters are memorable, and the story is both satisfying and suspenseful. With its lush setting, complex characters, and swoon-worthy romance, this book is sure to captivate readers from start to finish.

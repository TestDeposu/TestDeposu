# Walking On Air (Valance Family, #1)

**Author:** Catherine Anderson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

"Walking On Air" by Catherine Anderson - A Soul-Stirring Historical Romance that Delves into the Depth of Human Emotion

In the heart of the American West, a man with a reputation for being one of the toughest loners on the frontier is given a second chance at life, and the chance to re-evaluate his existence. Gabe Valance, the rugged and unyielding gunslinger, has spent his life running from his emotions, and the scars that come with it. However, with his life hanging in the balance, he sees an opportunity to turn his life around, and make amends for the mistakes of his past.

This is where Nancy Hoffman, a strong-willed and beautiful woman, comes into the picture. Fleeing an abusive past, she has built a new life in Random, Colorado, where she has found solace in caring for her younger sister and starting a new career as a hat maker. Despite her bravery, Nancy finds it difficult to trust men, and the idea of marriage is nothing short of abhorrent to her.

As the paths of these two lost souls cross, we are witness to a tender and captivating tale of romance and transformation. Anderson masterfully weaves together the narrative of Gabe and Nancy's journey as they navigate the complexities of their own emotions, fears, and vulnerabilities. Through Gabe's redemption story, we see a man struggling to shake off his hardshell and open himself up to love and trust.

Anderson's writing style is characteristically warm, engaging, and heartfelt, perfectly capturing the essence of life in the Wild West. With vivid descriptions of the landscape and an uncanny ability to convey the emotions of her characters, she transports us to a bygone era, where the air is filled with the sound of horses' hooves and the whispers of the past.

The core theme of "Walking On Air" lies in the struggle of its characters to overcome the demons of their past, and rise above the expectations of those around them. Anderson skillfully explores the complexities of grief, loss, and abuse, making Nancy and Gabe's story all the more believable and relatable. The author also masterfully conveys the power of second chances and the importance of embracing vulnerability, as Gabe and Nancy find their way to a deeper understanding of themselves and each other.

One of the standout features of Anderson's writing is her ability to craft a romance that feels organic and genuine, yet never loses sight of the larger themes at play. Gabe and Nancy's courtship is slow-burning and sensual, filled with an undeniable chemistry that simmers beneath the surface. Their love story is built on the foundation of respect, trust, and communication, making their romance feel all the more authentic and satisfying.

Overall, "Walking On Air" is a soul-stirring historical romance that delves into the depth of human emotion, making it a must-read for fans of the genre. With her masterful storytelling and memorable characters, Anderson once again proves herself to be a leading figure in the world of historical romance, and a testament to the enduring power of love and redemption.

# Justice (New Species, #4)

**Author:** Laurann Dohner  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

In "Justice," the fourth installment of Laurann Dohner's New Species series, we are once again immersed in a world of forbidden love, primal passion, and the eternal struggle between heart and duty. At the center of this maelstrom are Justice North, the enigmatic face of the New Species, and Jessie Dupree, a fiery human female who dares to challenge the status quo.

Dohner's writing style is unmistakable - a heady blend of sensuality, action, and emotional depth that draws the reader in and refuses to let go. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Justice embodying the perfect blend of alpha male strength and vulnerability. Jessie, on the other hand, is a force to be reckoned with - her sharp wit, fearless attitude, and generous heart making her the perfect foil to Justice's brooding intensity.

As their story unfolds, Dohner masterfully explores the complexities of interspecies relationships, delving into the tensions and conflicts that arise when two vastly different worlds collide. Justice, sworn to protect his people and uphold the traditions of his kind, is torn between his duty and his desire for Jessie. Meanwhile, Jessie is aware that their love is doomed from the start, yet she cannot resist the primal allure of the powerful New Species male.

One of the most striking aspects of Dohner's writing is her ability to balance light and dark, humor and pathos. The narrative is peppered with moments of tenderness and humor, which serve to heighten the emotional stakes and make the characters' ultimate triumph all the more satisfying. At the same time, Dohner pulls no punches when it comes to exploring the harsh realities of Justice's world, from the dangers of prejudice and violence to the complexities of cultural identity.

Throughout the book, Dohner's world-building is meticulous and immersive, drawing the reader into a richly detailed universe of New Species politics, mythology, and culture. Her prose is engaging and accessible, making it easy to become fully invested in the characters' journeys and the fate of their world.

If you're a fan of paranormal romance, science fiction, or simply great storytelling, "Justice" is an absolute must-read. With its winning combination of action, passion, and emotional depth, this book is sure to captivate and inspire, leaving you eagerly anticipating the next installment in the New Species series.

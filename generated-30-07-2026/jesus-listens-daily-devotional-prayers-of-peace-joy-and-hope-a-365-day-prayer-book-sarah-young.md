# Jesus Listens: Daily Devotional Prayers of Peace, Joy, and Hope (A 365-Day Prayer Book)

**Author:** Sarah Young  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

Title: A Whisper of Grace in a Noisy World: A Review of Jesus Listens

In an era defined by perpetual motion, digital fatigue, and an underlying hum of collective anxiety, finding a moment of genuine stillness can feel like an impossible pursuit. Enter Sarah Young’s 2021 devotional, Jesus Listens: Daily Devotional Prayers of Peace, Joy, and Hope. As a follow-up to her monumental literary legacy, including the beloved bestseller Jesus Calling, this 365-day prayer book arrives not as a theological textbook, but as a gentle, breathing sanctuary for the weary soul. Young offers readers a daily lifeline, positioning prayer not as a rigid religious duty, but as an intimate, ongoing conversation with the divine.

The central theme of Jesus Listens is accessibility and relational intimacy with God. Young understands that many believers—and seekers alike—frequently stumble at the threshold of prayer. They wonder what to say, worry that their words are inadequate, or simply feel too overwhelmed by the demands of daily life to articulate their inner turmoil. The genius of this book lies in how it bridges that gap. By writing each daily entry in the first person—phrased as if Jesus Himself is gently prompting the reader’s thoughts—Young removes the intimidation factor of prayer. The book serves as both a standalone daily anchor and a catalyst, sparking a deeper, more organic dialogue between the human heart and the Creator.

Stylistically, Young’s approach is comforting, pastoral, and deeply empathetic. The prose is deliberately stripped of heavy theological jargon, favoring instead a warm, conversational cadence that feels like a quiet cup of coffee with a trusted spiritual mentor. Each daily entry is wonderfully concise, making it tailor-made for the modern reader whose attention span has been fractured by the demands of a fast-paced culture. Yet, despite their brevity, the entries carry a surprising emotional and spiritual weight. 

Crucially, Young grounds every single daily prayer in the bedrock of Scripture. Rather than relying solely on subjective emotionalism or positive thinking, Jesus Listens anchors the reader’s hope in objective biblical promises. By including explicit Scripture references at the end of each devotion, Young invites her audience to move beyond her written words and plunge directly into the text of the Bible. This structural choice transforms the book from a passive reading experience into an active, transformative spiritual discipline. 

For those navigating seasons of grief, professional burnout, relational strain, or the quiet desperation of routine stress, this devotional offers a steadying hand. It does not promise a pain-free existence, but rather promises the profound peace of a God who actively listens in the midst of the storm. Jesus Listens is a striking, tender invitation to lay down the heavy burdens of self-reliance and rediscover the life-changing power of simply talking to God, one day at a time.

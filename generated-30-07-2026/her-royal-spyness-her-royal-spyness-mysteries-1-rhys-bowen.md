# Her Royal Spyness (Her Royal Spyness Mysteries, #1)

**Author:** Rhys Bowen  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

In "Her Royal Spyness," Rhys Bowen introduces readers to Lady Victoria Georgiana Charlotte Eugenie, a spirited and determined young royal who defies convention in 1930s London. The 34th in line for the English throne, Georgie, as she prefers to be called, has fled her stifling life in Scotland to experience the freedom and excitement of the city. With her royal connections and limited finances, Georgie navigates a world of high society, poverty, and intrigue, all while trying to clear her family's name after a mysterious death occurs in her bathtub.

Bowen's writing is witty and engaging, with a keen eye for historical detail and a deep understanding of the social nuances of the time period. Her protagonist is a delightful creation - feisty, independent, and determined to make a life for herself, despite the limitations placed on her by her royal status. Georgie's voice is charming and relatable, making her a compelling and likable heroine.

One of the standout aspects of "Her Royal Spyness" is its blend of humor, mystery, and historical fiction. Bowen skillfully weaves together a complex plot, full of twists and turns, while maintaining a lighthearted and entertaining tone. The result is a novel that is both fun and engaging, with a strong sense of time and place.

The author's use of historical figures and events adds depth and authenticity to the narrative, and her portrayal of the British class system is both insightful and humorous. The supporting cast of characters is equally well-drawn, from Georgie's dotty grandmother to her loyal and lovable maid, Queenie.

While "Her Royal Spyness" is primarily a mystery, it is also a coming-of-age story, as Georgie navigates the challenges of adulthood and learns to rely on herself. Her growth and development throughout the novel are satisfying and believable, and her relationships with those around her are nuanced and multifaceted.

Overall, "Her Royal Spyness" is a delightful and engaging read, perfect for fans of historical fiction, mystery, and romance. With its strong protagonist, witty dialogue, and engaging plot, it is a compelling and entertaining novel that will keep readers turning the pages until the very end.

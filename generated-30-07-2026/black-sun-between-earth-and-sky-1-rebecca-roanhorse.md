# Black Sun (Between Earth and Sky, #1)

**Author:** Rebecca Roanhorse  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

Black Sun by Rebecca Roanhorse is a mesmerizing tale that masterfully weaves together elements of celestial prophecies, forbidden magic, and intricate politics, set against the backdrop of the Pre-Columbian Americas. This captivating narrative is the first installment in the Between Earth and Sky trilogy, and it is easy to see why it has garnered such critical acclaim, including the prestigious Hugo Award for Best Series.

At its core, Black Sun is a story about the struggle for power and the corrupting influence it can have on individuals and society as a whole. Roanhorse expertly crafts a world that is both richly detailed and deeply flawed, where the pursuit of power and control can lead even the most well-intentioned characters down a path of destruction. Through the eyes of her complex and multidimensional characters, Roanhorse explores the darker aspects of human nature, revealing the full spectrum of human depravity and the devastating consequences that can result from the abuse of power.

One of the standout features of Black Sun is its cast of characters, each of whom is meticulously crafted to embody the intricacies and nuances of human emotion. Xiala, the disgraced Teek captain, is a particularly compelling figure, whose song has the power to both soothe and manipulate. Her character serves as a powerful exploration of the tension between creativity and control, as she navigates a world where her unique abilities are both celebrated and feared. Meanwhile, Serapio, the blind and scarred passenger, is a masterful creation, whose destiny is inextricably linked to the fate of the world. Through these characters, Roanhorse raises important questions about the nature of power, identity, and the human condition.

Roanhorse's writing style is equally impressive, as she seamlessly blends elements of fantasy, science fiction, and historical fiction to create a truly unique narrative voice. Her prose is evocative and immersive, conjuring vivid images of the world she has created. The pacing is expertly handled, with a narrative that is both propulsive and thoughtful, allowing the reader to fully absorb the complexities of the world and its characters.

One of the most striking aspects of Black Sun is its use of celestial prophecies and forbidden magic. Roanhorse draws heavily on the mythologies of the Pre-Columbian Americas, incorporating elements of astronomy, mathematics, and mysticism to create a richly detailed and immersive world. The solar eclipse, which serves as the backdrop for the story, is a powerful symbol of the upheaval and transformation that is to come.

In conclusion, Black Sun is a truly exceptional novel that sets the stage for an epic trilogy. With its masterful world-building, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes, it is a must-read for fans of fantasy and science fiction. Roanhorse's unique narrative voice and meticulous attention to detail make this novel a standout in its genre. As the first installment in the Between Earth and Sky trilogy, Black Sun is a captivating and unforgettable tale that will leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment.

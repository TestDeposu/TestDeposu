# Sea Glass (Glass, #2)

**Author:** Maria V. Snyder  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In "Sea Glass," the second installment of Maria V. Snyder's Glass series, Opal Cowan finds herself entangled in a treacherous web of power struggles, as her newfound ability to steal the powers of other magicians makes her a coveted asset in the realms of Sitia and Ixia. As she navigates the complex landscape of magic and politics, Opal must confront the darker aspects of her own nature and the true intentions of those around her.

Snyder's writing style is, as always, engaging and immersive, drawing the reader into the world of magic and intrigue she has so masterfully crafted. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Opal's struggles to balance her desire for independence with her need for connection and belonging serving as a compelling narrative thread throughout the book.

One of the primary themes of "Sea Glass" is the corrupting influence of power and the danger of allowing ambition to cloud one's judgment. As Opal becomes increasingly embroiled in the conflicts between rival factions vying for control, she is forced to confront the possibility that those she trusts may be hiding ulterior motives. This theme is expertly woven throughout the narrative, adding depth and complexity to the story.

Snyder's world-building is also noteworthy, as she expands upon the richly detailed landscapes of Sitia and Ixia, introducing new characters and magical abilities that add to the series' already impressive scope. The Moon Clan's lands, in particular, are vividly realized, providing a unique and fascinating backdrop for Opal's journey.

Throughout the book, Snyder's pacing is well-balanced, moving seamlessly between moments of high tension and quieter, more introspective scenes. This allows the reader to fully invest in Opal's emotional journey, making her ultimate choices all the more impactful.

If you're a fan of fantasy and magic, particularly in the realm of young adult fiction, "Sea Glass" is an absolute must-read. Snyder's unique blend of action, adventure, and romance, combined with her thought-provoking exploration of complex themes, makes for a truly unforgettable reading experience.

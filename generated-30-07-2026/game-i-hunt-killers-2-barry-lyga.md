# Game (I Hunt Killers, #2)

**Author:** Barry Lyga  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**Game: A Chilling Dance with Destiny**

Barry Lyga's "Game" is a gripping sequel to "I Hunt Killers," delving deeper into the psyche of Jazz, the son of the world's most notorious serial killer. Lyga masterfully explores the theme of nature versus nurture, questioning whether Jazz is destined to follow in his father's footsteps or if he can forge his own path.

The story unfolds in the seemingly idyllic town of Lobo's Nod, where a new serial killer is on the loose. Jazz, determined to prove that he's not a chip off the old block, joins forces with the police, using his unique insights into criminal minds to help catch the killer. However, as the body count rises and Jazz's involvement deepens, he finds himself grappling with disturbing impulses and dark thoughts.

Lyga's writing is taut and suspenseful, keeping readers on the edge of their seats. He expertly weaves together the thrilling cat-and-mouse game of the investigation with Jazz's internal struggle. The narrative is punctuated by chilling glimpses into the mind of the new killer, adding another layer of tension to the story.

What sets "Game" apart is Lyga's unflinching exploration of Jazz's character. He doesn't shy away from the dark places Jazz's thoughts take him, instead delving deep into the protagonist's psyche. This unflinching honesty makes Jazz a compelling and complex character, even as it raises unsettling questions about his true nature.

"Game" is not for the faint-hearted. It's a grim, intense read that will keep you up at night, questioning whether Jazz can truly escape his father's legacy. It's a testament to Lyga's skill that, despite the darkness, you can't help but root for Jazz, hoping that he can defy his destiny.

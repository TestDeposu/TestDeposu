# A Crystal of Time (The School for Good and Evil: The Camelot Years, #2)

**Author:** Soman Chainani  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

Title: Deconstructing Destiny: A Review of A Crystal of Time

In the expansive landscape of modern fantasy, few authors dare to completely dismantle the architectural blueprints of traditional fairy tales quite like Soman Chainani. With *A Crystal of Time*, the fifth installment overall and the second entry in the Camelot Years arc of the *School for Good and Evil* series, Chainani continues to prove that happily ever afters are never as simple as they appear in storybooks. While the provided series overview highlights the foundational premise of the original trilogy—where best friends Sophie and Agatha found their destinies bewilderingly inverted—*A Crystal of Time* plunges readers much deeper into the political machinations, moral gray areas, and high-stakes perils of a fractured Camelot.

At the heart of Chainani’s work lies a brilliant, subversive theme: the interrogation of absolute morality. Traditional fairy tales have long relied on a rigid binary of pristine heroes and irredeemable villains. Chainani shatters this mirror. In *A Crystal of Time*, the narrative grapples heavily with the corruption of power, the weight of prophecy, and the dangerous illusion of predetermined roles. The characters are no longer just students learning how to fit into a narrative; they are rulers, rebels, and romantic figures forced to write their own rules in real-time. The central conflict forces Sophie, Agatha, and their peers to question not only who is truly good and who is truly evil, but whether those labels hold any genuine meaning when kingdoms are collapsing and time itself is slipping away. 

Chainani’s authorial style remains one of the most distinct and vibrant forces in contemporary middle-grade and young adult crossover fiction. His prose is cinematic, lavish, and unapologetically dramatic, perfectly mirroring the grand theatricality of classic folklore while infusing it with contemporary sarcasm and emotional depth. He has a remarkable knack for pacing; the plot moves at a breakneck speed, propelled by ticking-clock mechanics, escalating political tension, and shocking reversals of fortune. Yet, amid the chaotic whirlwind of magical battles, royal usurpations, and shifting allegiances, Chainani never loses sight of the emotional anchor of the series: the unbreakable, complex bond between Sophie and Agatha. Their friendship remains the beating heart of the universe, proving that sisterly devotion can be far more powerful than any magic wand or royal decree.

For returning fans, *A Crystal of Time* offers a thrilling, breathless expansion of the Endless Woods, challenging everything readers thought they knew about Arthurian legend and fairy-tale lore. It is a dazzling testament to Chainani’s imaginative scope and his ability to make us rethink the very nature of heroism. Gripping, clever, and rich with moral complexity, this installment cements the series as a modern classic of subverted fantasy.

# Something About You (FBI/US Attorney, #1)

**Author:** Julie James  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Something About You, the first installment in Julie James' FBI/US Attorney series, is a captivating blend of romance, suspense, and wit. Published in 2010, this novel has stood the test of time, and its appeal remains unwavering. James masterfully weaves a complex narrative that delves into the intricacies of a high-profile homicide investigation, while simultaneously exploring the intense, often tumultuous, relationship between the two lead characters.

At the heart of the story are Cameron Lynde, a determined and sharp-witted Assistant U.S. Attorney, and Jack Pallas, a ruggedly handsome FBI Special Agent. Their past is marred by a botched operation that nearly derailed Jack's career, and as a result, he harbors a deep-seated resentment towards Cameron. However, fate has a way of bringing them together, and they find themselves forced to work in tandem to unravel the mysteries surrounding a gruesome murder.

James' writing style is a perfect blend of sophistication and playfulness. Her prose is laced with humor, clever banter, and a deep understanding of the intricacies of human relationships. The dialogue is crisp, engaging, and often laugh-out-loud funny, making it easy to become fully invested in the characters and their plight. The author's ability to balance the darker aspects of the investigation with the lighter, more romantic moments is a testament to her skill as a storyteller.

One of the most compelling aspects of Something About You is the way James expertly crafts the tension between Cameron and Jack. Their initial animosity towards each other is palpable, but as the story progresses, it becomes clear that their feelings for each other run far deeper than mere dislike. The slow-burning passion that develops between them is expertly paced, making their eventual union both satisfying and believable.

The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with a range of intriguing characters that add depth and complexity to the narrative. From the enigmatic murder victim to the cast of suspects, each character is meticulously crafted, making it impossible to guess the killer's identity until the very end.

In conclusion, Something About You is a gripping, romantic thriller that will keep you on the edge of your seat. With its breakneck pace, engaging characters, and expertly woven plot, this novel is a must-read for fans of the genre. Julie James' writing is a breath of fresh air, and her unique voice is sure to captivate even the most discerning readers.

# The Colossus Rises (Seven Wonders, #1)

**Author:** Peter Lerangis  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In "The Colossus Rises," the first installment of Peter Lerangis's Seven Wonders series, the line between reality and fantasy blurs as young protagonist Jack McKinley embarks on an extraordinary adventure filled with ancient secrets, historical mysteries, and non-stop action. This high-octane tale masterfully weaves together elements of modern adventure and Indiana Jones-style mystery, making it an unputdownable page-turner perfect for fans of Percy Jackson and The 39 Clues.

The story follows Jack, an ordinary kid with an extraordinary problem: he's going to die in a few months unless he finds seven magic Loculi hidden within the Seven Wonders of the Ancient World. Jack's journey is not just about solving puzzles and uncovering secrets but also about embracing his true identity as a descendant of an ancient civilization. This revelation grants him extraordinary abilities, but also a fatal genetic flaw that adds an element of urgency to his quest.

Lerangis's writing style is reminiscent of Rick Riordan's in "Percy Jackson and the Olympians," with a mix of action, excitement, and humor. However, Lerangis brings his own unique voice to the table, infusing the story with a sense of wonder and awe that's sure to captivate young readers. The pacing is relentless, with each chapter propelling the story forward at a breakneck speed, leaving readers eager to discover what happens next.

One of the standout features of "The Colossus Rises" is its ability to blend historical facts and figures with mythical creatures and action-packed plot twists. Lerangis brings the ancient world to life with vivid descriptions of the Seven Wonders, from the towering Colossus of Rhodes to the majestic Hanging Gardens of Babylon. These settings provide a rich backdrop for the story, immersing readers in the mythology and lore of ancient civilizations.

The characters in the story are well-developed and relatable, with Jack's transformation from an ordinary kid to a hero worthy of the ancient world a true delight to read. The supporting cast, including Marco, Aly, and Cass, add to the story's humor and charm, making them hard to resist. The chemistry between the characters is palpable, and their interactions are often laugh-out-loud funny.

Throughout the book, Lerangis introduces mythological creatures and beasts, each with its own unique abilities and characteristics. From the fearsome griffins to the vicious vromaski, these creatures add an element of excitement and danger to the story, keeping readers on the edge of their seats.

In conclusion, "The Colossus Rises" is a thrilling adventure that will appeal to fans of action-packed, mythological tales. Peter Lerangis's writing style is engaging, and his ability to blend history and fantasy is unmatched. This is a story that will keep you guessing until the very end, making it an unputdownable read for anyone looking for a thrilling adventure.

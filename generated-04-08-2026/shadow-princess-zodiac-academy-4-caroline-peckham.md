# Shadow Princess (Zodiac Academy, #4)

**Author:** Caroline Peckham  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

In the latest installment of the Zodiac Academy series, Shadow Princess, Caroline Peckham weaves a captivating narrative that delves into the world of magic, power, and the unbreakable bond between twin sisters Darcy and Tory Vega. This urban fantasy novel is a dark, twisted tale of self-discovery, forbidden love, and the fight for one's rightful place in a world governed by the stars.

Peckham's writing style is as captivating as ever, with a narrative that effortlessly glides between the sisters' struggles in the mortal world and their introduction to the magical realm of Solaria. The author's vivid descriptions of the mystical creatures that inhabit this world, including vampires, dragons, and sirens, transport readers to a realm both wondrous and treacherous.

At the heart of the story lies the complex, often tumultuous relationship between Darcy, Tory, and the four Celestial Heirs, who will stop at nothing to maintain their grip on the throne. The slow-burning tension between the sisters and their adversaries is palpable, with Peckham masterfully crafting a bully romance that is both dark and alluring.

One of the most compelling aspects of Shadow Princess is its exploration of the theme of found family. Darcy and Tory, having spent their entire lives relying solely on each other, must navigate the challenges of forming alliances and friendships in a world where loyalty is a luxury they can ill afford. Peckham's portrayal of the sisters' struggles to find their place in this new world is both poignant and thought-provoking, raising questions about the true meaning of family and the bonds that tie us together.

Throughout the novel, Peckham's writing is characterized by its unflinching honesty, tackling themes of power, corruption, and the blurred lines between good and evil. The Zodiac Academy series has always been known for its dark, often unsettling tone, and Shadow Princess is no exception. This is a world where the strong prey on the weak, and the only way to survive is to be willing to fight for every scrap of power.

In conclusion, Shadow Princess is a gripping, unapologetic tale of magic, power, and the unbreakable bonds of sisterhood. With its lush world-building, complex characters, and slow-burning romance, this novel is a must-read for fans of urban fantasy and dark academia. Peckham's writing is as captivating as ever, and the Zodiac Academy series continues to be a force to be reckoned with in the world of young adult fiction.

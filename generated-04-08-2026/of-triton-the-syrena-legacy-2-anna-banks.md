# Of Triton (The Syrena Legacy, #2)

**Author:** Anna Banks  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In "Of Triton", the second installment of The Syrena Legacy series, Anna Banks delves deeper into the world of merfolk and the identity crisis of her protagonist, Emma. As a Half-Breed, Emma is caught between two worlds - the human world, where she's always felt like an outsider, and the Syrena realm, where she's considered an abomination.

Banks' masterful storytelling weaves a complex web of emotions, as Emma struggles to come to terms with her newfound heritage and the danger that comes with it. The Syrena law, which dictates that all Half-Breeds should be put to death, hangs over Emma's head like a sword of Damocles, making her question her very existence.

As tensions rise between the two kingdoms, Poseidon and Triton, Emma is faced with an impossible decision: to keep herself safe and hidden, or to risk everything and reveal her true identity and Gift to the Syrena world. This internal conflict is beautifully captured through Banks' dual narrative, which switches seamlessly between Emma and Galen's points of view.

One of the standout features of Banks' writing is her ability to infuse her characters with humor and wit, even in the most trying of circumstances. Emma's sarcasm and Galen's dry humor add a lighthearted touch to the narrative, making it easier to become fully invested in their story.

The romance between Emma and Galen is also expertly woven throughout the narrative, adding an extra layer of tension and emotional depth to the story. Their relationship is fraught with obstacles, from the danger posed by the Syrena law to the secrets and misunderstandings that threaten to tear them apart.

Banks' world-building is another highlight of the book. The Syrena realm is richly detailed, with its own history, mythology, and politics. The complexities of the merfolk world are skillfully revealed, adding depth and nuance to the narrative.

The main theme of "Of Triton" is one of identity and self-discovery. Emma's journey is a powerful exploration of what it means to be true to oneself, even in the face of overwhelming opposition. As she navigates the treacherous waters of the Syrena world, Emma must confront her own doubts and fears, and learn to accept herself for who she truly is.

Overall, "Of Triton" is a captivating and emotional read, full of twists and turns that will keep you on the edge of your seat. With its rich world-building, memorable characters, and swoon-worthy romance, this book is a must-read for fans of paranormal romance and fantasy.

# A Princess of Landover (Magic Kingdom of Landover, #6)

**Author:** Terry Brooks  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**A Princess of Landover: Terry Brooks’ Whimsical Adventure Reclaims the Magic of Fantasy**

Terry Brooks’ *A Princess of Landover*—the sixth installment in his beloved *Magic Kingdom of Landover* series—is a delightful escape into a world where magic, mischief, and teenage rebellion collide. At its heart, this book is a story about defiance, self-discovery, and the messy, wonderful chaos of growing up—all wrapped in the lush, imaginative tapestry of Brooks’ signature fantasy universe.

The novel follows Mistaya, the fiery, headstrong daughter of Ben Holiday, the mortal-turned-king of Landover. After a suspension from her elite private school in our world, Mistaya refuses to accept the limitations placed on her—especially when it comes to mastering sorcery under the tutelage of the kingdom’s court wizard, Questor Thews. But when a grotesque nobleman proposes marriage to her, Mistaya’s frustration boils over. The only way to reclaim her autonomy, she decides, is to flee—only to stumble into a far greater adventure than she ever imagined.

What makes *A Princess of Landover* so compelling is Brooks’ ability to blend humor, heart, and high-stakes fantasy. The book is packed with eccentric characters—a recalcitrant dragon, a sly magic cat, a brooding librarian, and a sinister sorcerer—each adding layers of intrigue and charm. Brooks’ prose is effortlessly engaging, weaving together a narrative that feels both timeless and fresh. His world-building remains as rich as ever, with Landover’s magic system and lore seamlessly integrated into Mistaya’s journey.

At its core, this is a story about agency. Mistaya isn’t just a princess waiting for her destiny to unfold; she’s a young woman determined to shape her own path, even if it means defying her parents, outsmarting villains, and facing dangers beyond her wildest dreams. Brooks captures the universal struggle of adolescence—balancing ambition, fear, and the desire to prove oneself—while keeping the fantasy elements vibrant and exciting.

The book also serves as a perfect entry point for newcomers to the series, offering a self-contained adventure that rewards both casual readers and long-time fans. The bonus excerpt from *Bearers of the Black Staff* is a sweet touch, hinting at the broader connections within Brooks’ expansive universe.

In the end, *A Princess of Landover* is a testament to Brooks’ enduring talent for crafting stories that are as entertaining as they are heartfelt. It’s a fantasy adventure that feels like a warm hug—whimsical, adventurous, and full of the kind of magic that lingers long after the last page.

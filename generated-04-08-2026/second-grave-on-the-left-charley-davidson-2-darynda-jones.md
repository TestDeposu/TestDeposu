# Second Grave on the Left (Charley Davidson, #2)

**Author:** Darynda Jones  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

If the afterlife had a sense of humor, it would undoubtedly sound like Darynda Jones. In Second Grave on the Left, the second installment of her award-winning paranormal series, Jones proves that her explosive debut was no fluke. Instead, she doubles down on the chaos, wit, and supernatural danger, cementing protagonist Charley Davidson as one of the most delightfully unhinged heroines in contemporary urban fantasy.

At its core, the main theme of this novel explores the delicate intersection of mortality and responsibility—wrapped in a glossy, laugh-out-loud package of supernatural noir. Charley is a grim reaper, a private investigator, and a magnet for the bizarre, yet her defining trait is her fiercely protective heart. The plot kicks into gear when Cookie, Charley’s fiercely loyal best friend and receptionist, enlists her help to find a missing woman named Mimi. What begins as a standard missing persons case quickly spirals into a dark, convoluted mystery tied to the murder of a high school classmate. Jones uses this dual narrative—the grounded grit of a private eye investigation colliding with the ethereal rules of the afterlife—to examine how our past choices ripple into eternity. 

Yet, it is Darynda Jones’s signature style that truly elevates the material. Jones possesses a rare literary alchemy, seamlessly blending rapid-fire, razor-sharp comedy with genuine emotional vulnerability and smoldering romantic tension. The dialogue crackles with pop culture references, sarcastic inner monologues, and a level of irreverence that makes even the grim business of death feel like a party. Charley’s voice is distinct, unapologetic, and relentlessly funny, anchoring the high-stakes supernatural elements in a relatable, highly caffeinated reality.

Balancing the missing persons case is a deliciously tormented romance plotline involving Reyes Alexander Farrow, the literal Son of Satan. Currently existing as a disembodied spirit haunting Charley while his physical body is tortured by demons, Reyes brings a heavy dose of dark, angsty romance to the narrative. The dynamic between Charley and Reyes is electric; their interactions provide the sultry counterbalance to the book's comedic beats, exploring themes of sacrifice, irresistible attraction, and the lengths one will go to protect the soul of their beloved. 

Jones manages a dizzying array of subplots without ever losing momentum. The pacing is relentless, fueled entirely by the chemistry of the characters and the high stakes of a plot that threatens not just Albuquerque, but the very gates of heaven. It is rare for a sequel to match the freshness of an introductory volume, but Second Grave on the Left expands the mythology of the series while deepening our investment in its eccentric cast. 

Ultimately, Darynda Jones serves up a cocktail of paranormal suspense that is as intoxicating as it is addictive. For readers seeking a read defined by snappy prose, genuine heart, high-octane mystery, and enough sexual tension to melt the pages, this book is an absolute must-read. Grab your coffee and your chocolate; Charley Davidson is taking you on a wild ride to the other side.

# Ask and It Is Given: Learning to Manifest Your Desires

**Author:** Esther Hicks  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2004

**Ask and It Is Given: A Masterclass in Manifestation—Where Science Meets Soul**

Esther Hicks’ *Ask and It Is Given* isn’t just another self-help book—it’s a revolutionary manual for rewiring reality itself. Drawing from the teachings of Abraham, a collective consciousness of nonphysical energy, this work distills the Law of Attraction into a practical, actionable philosophy that has reshaped millions of lives. At its core, the book dismantles the myth that desire is passive, proving instead that fulfillment is a skill—one that hinges on aligning your vibration with your goals. Hicks and Jerry Hicks (her husband and collaborator) don’t just preach; they provide a roadmap, complete with 22 proven processes, to shift from doubt to certainty, from scarcity to abundance, and from emotional stagnation to joyful creation.

The book’s genius lies in its clarity. Unlike abstract spiritual texts, *Ask and It Is Given* speaks in plain language, breaking down complex concepts into digestible steps. Take, for instance, the idea that "you are a vibrational transmitter and receiver." Hicks explains that every thought, emotion, and belief emits a frequency—like a radio signal—and that what you attract is a direct reflection of that energy. The book doesn’t just tell you to "think positive"; it teaches you how to *feel* positive, how to recognize when your emotions are misaligned with your desires, and how to adjust your vibration in real time. This is where the book transcends typical manifestation guides. It’s not about wishful thinking; it’s about *science*—the science of consciousness, where intention meets quantum possibility.

One of the most compelling arguments Hicks makes is that resistance is the enemy of creation. Whether it’s fear of failure, self-doubt, or even the subtle resistance of "I’ll believe it when I see it," these mental blocks create a vibrational mismatch between you and your desire. The book’s chapters—like *The Three Steps to Whatever You Want to Be, Do, or Have* or *You Are Only 17 Seconds Away from 68 Seconds to Fulfillment*—are designed to dismantle these blocks. The 17-second rule, for example, is a game-changer: it asserts that within 17 seconds of feeling a positive emotion (like gratitude or excitement), you can shift your energy to attract what you want. This isn’t fluff; it’s a psychological and energetic reset button for your life.

Hicks’ writing style is warm, conversational, and deeply empathetic. She doesn’t lecture; she *guides*. The voice of Abraham, channeled through her, feels like a wise mentor sitting beside you, offering reassurance that you *are* capable of creating your reality—you just need to remember how. The book’s structure is intuitive, moving from foundational principles (like the Law of Attraction itself) to practical applications, ensuring that even skeptics can engage without feeling overwhelmed. The inclusion of real-life processes—such as the *Emotional Guidance Scale* or the *Process for Getting What You Want*—makes the abstract tangible. You’re not just reading about manifestation; you’re being *taught* it.

What sets *Ask and It Is Given* apart from other manifestation books is its emphasis on *feeling*. Too often, self-help culture focuses on thoughts alone, but Hicks insists that emotions are the true currency of creation. She explains that your emotional set-points (your baseline feelings) dictate what you attract, and that by consciously choosing higher vibrations—like love, joy, or peace—you can magnetize your desires effortlessly. This isn’t about suppressing negative emotions; it’s about recognizing them as signals and choosing responses that elevate your energy.

The book also addresses common pitfalls, such as the tendency to focus on *what’s missing* rather than what’s already abundant in your life. Hicks challenges readers to shift their perspective from lack to gratitude, from "I don’t have enough" to "I am enough." This reframing is powerful because it aligns your vibration with abundance, making it easier to receive what you desire. The result? A life that feels lighter, more aligned, and effortlessly abundant.

Critics might argue that the Law of Attraction is too simplistic or that it places undue responsibility on the individual. But Hicks doesn’t present this as a magical solution—she presents it as a *tool*. The book is clear: manifestation requires effort, awareness, and practice. It’s not about waving a wand; it’s about becoming a conscious co-creator with the universe. And in that, *Ask and It Is Given* is both a manifesto and a manual, offering not just inspiration but a step-by-step system to transform your life.

For those new to the concept, the book’s early chapters may feel repetitive, but this repetition is intentional—it reinforces the core principles until they become second nature. And for those already familiar with the Law of Attraction, the depth of the processes and the nuanced understanding of vibrational alignment offer fresh insights. Whether you’re seeking love, success, health, or inner peace, this book provides the framework to manifest it all.

Ultimately, *Ask and It Is Given* is more than a book—it’s a wake-up call. It reminds you that you are not a passive observer of life; you are its architect. And with the right tools, you can build a reality that reflects your highest dreams. In a world full of noise, Hicks’ teachings offer a quiet, powerful truth: *You are already connected to the source of all creation. The only thing standing between you and your desires is your vibration—and that, thankfully, is something you can change.*

# A Monstrous Regiment of Women (Mary Russell and Sherlock Holmes, #2)

**Author:** Laurie R. King  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1995

A Monstrous Regiment of Women is a captivating tale of mystery, faith, and the intricate bond between two extraordinary individuals. Written by Laurie R. King, the second installment in the Mary Russell and Sherlock Holmes series, delves into the complexities of 1920s England, weaving a rich tapestry of intrigue and suspense.

King skillfully introduces us to Mary Russell, now a brilliant and independent young woman, fresh from Oxford with a degree in theology. With a keen intellect and an unyielding spirit, Mary is poised on the cusp of acquiring a substantial inheritance, allowing her to pursue her passions with unwavering dedication. Her fascination with the enigmatic Margery Childe, the charismatic leader of the New Temple of God, sets the stage for a thrilling investigation that will test the mettle of her partnership with Sherlock Holmes.

As the story unfolds, King masterfully interweaves elements of suffragism, mysticism, and murder, creating a web of tension that propels readers forward. The author's keen insight into the minds of her characters is awe-inspiring, and Mary's blossoming relationship with Holmes is expertly woven throughout the narrative, imbuing the story with a sense of authenticity and depth.

Throughout the novel, King's writing style impresses with its elegance and sophistication. Her masterful narrative voice effortlessly navigates the complexities of the plot, rendering even the most convoluted mysteries palatable and accessible. One of the standout features of this novel is its nuanced portrayal of its female characters, who are more than mere archetypes – they are multidimensional, fully-fledged individuals with their own motivations, desires, and secrets.

Moreover, the author's meticulous research into the social and cultural context of 1920s England lends an authenticity to the narrative that is nothing short of remarkable. From the struggles of the suffragettes to the inner workings of the British police force, King has spared no detail in reconstructing a bygone era with eerie precision.

While some readers may find the historical and cultural elements of the narrative to be slightly dense, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise exceptional novel. A Monstrous Regiment of Women is a triumphant achievement in detective fiction, offering a complex and compelling mystery that will keep readers up late into the night, eager to unravel the intricate threads of the plot.

Ultimately, it is King's extraordinary creation, Mary Russell, who shines brightest in this novel. Her combination of intelligence, courage, and vulnerability makes her a true standout in the world of detective fiction, and her partnership with Holmes is as intriguing as it is heartwarming.

A Monstrous Regiment of Women is a must-read for fans of Sherlock Holmes, mystery enthusiasts, and anyone who appreciates a strong, intelligent female protagonist. With its captivating blend of intrigue, suspense, and historical detail, this novel cements Laurie R. King's status as a master of the detective genre, and we eagerly await the next installment in this extraordinary series.

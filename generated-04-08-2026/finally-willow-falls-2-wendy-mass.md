# Finally (Willow Falls, #2)

**Author:** Wendy Mass  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In "Finally," the second installment of the Willow Falls series, Wendy Mass masterfully captures the essence of pre-teen angst and anticipation. The story revolves around Rory Swenson, a young girl on the cusp of turning twelve, and her relentless pursuit of independence.

Mass's writing style is effortlessly engaging, making it easy to become immersed in Rory's world. The author's use of a relatable protagonist and authentic dialogue allows readers to connect with Rory on a deeper level. As Rory counts down the hours until her twelfth birthday, her excitement and eagerness to leave childhood behind are palpable.

At its core, "Finally" explores the theme of growing up and the struggles that come with it. Rory's desire for autonomy and self-expression is a universal feeling that many readers can identify with. Mass tackles this theme with sensitivity and humor, never shying away from the complexities of adolescence.

One of the strengths of Mass's writing is her ability to balance lighthearted moments with more serious issues. Rory's relationships with her family and friends are authentic and well-developed, adding depth to the narrative. The supporting characters are equally well-crafted, each with their own unique personalities and quirks.

The author's portrayal of Rory's inner world is also noteworthy. Mass skillfully conveys the protagonist's inner turmoil as she navigates the challenges of pre-teen life. Rory's struggles to balance her desire for independence with her need for guidance and support are deeply relatable.

Throughout the book, Mass weaves together a narrative that is both entertaining and thought-provoking. "Finally" is a heartwarming and humorous exploration of what it means to grow up and find one's place in the world. With its engaging characters, relatable themes, and effortless writing style, this book is sure to resonate with readers of all ages.

Ultimately, "Finally" is a celebration of the ups and downs of adolescence, and the struggles that make us who we are. Mass's writing is a testament to the power of storytelling, reminding us that even in the midst of chaos, there is always room for growth, laughter, and self-discovery.

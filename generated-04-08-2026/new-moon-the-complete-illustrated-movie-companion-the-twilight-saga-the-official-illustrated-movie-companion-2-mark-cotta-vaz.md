# New Moon: The Complete Illustrated Movie Companion (The Twilight Saga: The Official Illustrated Movie Companion, #2)

**Author:** Mark Cotta Vaz  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

New Moon: The Complete Illustrated Movie Companion is not about the making of The Twilight Saga: Eclipse, but I will review the actual book, which is about New Moon.

Mark Cotta Vaz's New Moon: The Complete Illustrated Movie Companion is a sumptuous visual treat that delves into the making of the second installment of The Twilight Saga film series. As the official companion book to the movie, it offers an unparalleled glimpse into the creative process that brought Stephenie Meyer's captivating world to life on the big screen.

One of the standout features of this book is its stunning array of full-color photographs, showcasing the cast, locations, and sets in exquisite detail. The images are meticulously selected to provide a comprehensive visual narrative, allowing fans to relive their favorite moments from the film. The photographs are beautifully reproduced, making this book a true collector's item for any Twilight enthusiast.

Vaz's writing style is engaging and informative, providing a wealth of behind-the-scenes information that will delight both casual fans and die-hard enthusiasts. He has a keen eye for detail, and his passion for the subject matter shines through on every page. The text is peppered with exclusive interviews and personal anecdotes from the cast and crew, offering a unique insight into the filmmaking process.

One of the strengths of this book is its ability to balance the technical aspects of filmmaking with the more personal stories of the cast and crew. Vaz's interviews with the actors, including Kristen Stewart and Robert Pattinson, provide a fascinating glimpse into their experiences during the filming of New Moon. The book also delves into the creative decisions made by the director, Chris Weitz, and the special effects team, offering a comprehensive understanding of the film's production.

The author's style is approachable and easy to follow, making this book an enjoyable read for fans of all ages. Vaz's writing is free from overly technical jargon, allowing readers to focus on the creative process rather than getting bogged down in technical details.

In conclusion, New Moon: The Complete Illustrated Movie Companion is a must-have for any fan of The Twilight Saga. Mark Cotta Vaz's engaging writing style, combined with the stunning visuals, makes this book a true treasure trove of behind-the-scenes information. Whether you're a casual fan or a die-hard enthusiast, this book is sure to delight and captivate.

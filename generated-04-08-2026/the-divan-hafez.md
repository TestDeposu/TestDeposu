# The Divan

**Author:** Hafez  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

To read the poetry of Khwaja Shams-ud-Din Muhammad Hafez-e Shirazi is to step into a timeless sanctuary where the boundaries between the earthly and the divine dissolve entirely. The Divan, presented here in a breathtaking facsimile illuminated manuscript edition, is far more than a mere collection of verse; it is an aesthetic and spiritual portal to fourteenth-century Persia that continues to resonate with staggering relevance today. 

At the very heart of Hafez’s work lies the profound, intoxicating theme of divine love, masterfully veiled in the language of human passion. Known enduringly as "The Interpreter of Mysteries" and "The Tongue of the Hidden," Hafez utilizes the rich symbolism of Sufism to bridge the gap between the finite human soul and the infinite Creator. Within these pages, the yearning for a mortal Beloved transforms seamlessly into the state of God-realization. The tavern, the cupbearer, and the forbidden wine are not mere hedonistic tropes, but potent allegories for spiritual intoxication and the shedding of the ego. This dual interpretation—simultaneously sensual and mystical—gives the ghazals their astonishing psychological depth and enduring universal appeal. Every couplet acts as a mirror, reflecting both the agonizing sweetness of romantic longing and the transcendent peace of mystical awakening.

The style of Hafez is legendary for its musicality, ambiguity, and layered brilliance. Operating primarily within the rigid yet elegant structure of the ghazal—sonnet-like arrangements of evocative couplets—Hafez achieves a lyrical freedom that defies the constraints of form. His verses are famously aphoristic, leading to the Persian tradition of using the Divan as an oracular guide, a book of divination where seekers open random pages to find counsel for their deepest dilemmas. As the great Goethe famously declared, "Hafiz has no peer." His voice is paradoxical: playful yet profound, deeply melancholy yet utterly ecstatic, rebuking religious hypocrisy while championing genuine spiritual devotion. 

This particular 2023 edition elevates the literary experience by marrying Hafez’s immortal words with the mesmerizing artistry of Mahmoud Farshchian and the historical scholarship of Gertrude Bell. Bell’s classic English translations capture the sublime cadence and emotional resonance of the original Persian, proving her observation that these poems are "not of one age, or of another, but for all time." Visually, the inclusion of miniature illustrations and authentic Persian calligraphy transforms the reading experience into an immersive cultural journey. It is, in every sense, a dual masterpiece: a triumphant union of transcendent poetry and exquisite visual art.

For anyone seeking an authentic encounter with the pinnacle of classical Persian literature, or for the modern seeker thirsting for spiritual depth, this compact Divan is an essential treasure. It invites us to drink deeply from the cup of ancient wisdom, reminding us that after more than six centuries, the song of the nightingale still speaks directly to the human heart.

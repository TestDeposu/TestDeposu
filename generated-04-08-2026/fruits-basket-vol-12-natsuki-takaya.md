# Fruits Basket, Vol. 12

**Author:** Natsuki Takaya  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

Fruits Basket, Vol. 12, is a poignant and captivating conclusion to the beloved manga series by Natsuki Takaya. As the Sohma family's curse is finally lifted, the story delves deeper into the complexities of their relationships and the struggles of growing up. The main theme of this volume revolves around the idea of growth, change, and the power of human connections.

Takaya's writing masterfully explores the intricacies of Kyo and Tohru's bond, raising questions about the nature of love, friendship, and family. As Kyo is finally free to pursue his dreams, he must confront the uncertainty of his future and the possibility of drifting apart from those he holds dear. This inner turmoil is skillfully woven throughout the narrative, making for a deeply relatable and engaging read.

One of the standout aspects of this volume is the way Takaya handles the characters' development. The once-tormented Kyo is now given the chance to spread his wings, and his growth is both heartwarming and authentic. Tohru, too, undergoes significant changes, her unwavering optimism and kindness inspiring those around her to confront their own demons.

The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, with each character bringing their unique perspective to the story. The long-awaited revelation of Kyoko's ominous words adds a new layer of depth to the narrative, shedding light on the complex web of relationships within the Sohma family.

Takaya's art style, as always, is a treat for the eyes. The inclusion of color illustrations and bonus material, such as character profiles and a timeline of the series, adds a delightful touch to the volume. The interview with the author provides a fascinating glimpse into her creative process, making this edition a must-have for fans of the series.

The pacing of the story is well-balanced, seamlessly shifting between moments of humor, drama, and introspection. Takaya's writing is both poignant and uplifting, making it easy to become fully invested in the characters' lives.

In conclusion, Fruits Basket, Vol. 12, is a stunning conclusion to the series. With its thought-provoking themes, well-developed characters, and beautiful artwork, this volume is a must-read for fans of the series and manga enthusiasts alike.

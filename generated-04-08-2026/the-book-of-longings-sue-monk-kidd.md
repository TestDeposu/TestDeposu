# The Book of Longings

**Author:** Sue Monk Kidd  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

In "The Book of Longings", Sue Monk Kidd weaves an extraordinary narrative that delves into the untold story of Ana, a young woman living in the first century, and her extraordinary encounter with Jesus. This mesmerizing tale is a triumph of storytelling, masterfully crafted by Kidd, as she brings her acclaimed narrative gifts to reimagine the life of a woman who finds her voice and her destiny in a time and place designed to silence her.

The novel is set against the backdrop of a tumultuous period in history, with the Roman occupation of Israel and the emergence of a young Jesus. Ana, raised in a wealthy family with ties to the ruler of Galilee, is a rebellious and ambitious young woman with a brilliant mind and a daring spirit. Her pent-up longings and desires are expertly woven throughout the narrative, as Kidd skillfully explores the complexities of Ana's character.

One of the most striking aspects of the novel is Kidd's audacious approach to history. By imagining the story of Ana, Kidd sheds new light on a period that has been largely dominated by male perspectives. The result is a rich and nuanced portrayal of life in first-century Israel, with a strong focus on the experiences of women. Kidd's meticulous research is evident throughout the novel, as she masterfully recreates the sights, sounds, and smells of ancient Nazareth.

At the heart of the novel is Ana's relationship with Jesus, which is portrayed with great sensitivity and depth. Their marriage is a complex and multifaceted one, marked by moments of great joy and great conflict. Kidd's portrayal of Jesus is particularly noteworthy, as she brings a reverential approach to his life that focuses on his humanity. This is a Jesus who is relatable, flawed, and ultimately, deeply human.

Throughout the novel, Kidd's prose is lyrical and evocative, with a poetic quality that is both captivating and immersive. Her writing is masterful, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the human condition. The characters in the novel are richly drawn and complex, with Ana being a particularly compelling protagonist.

The themes of the novel are timely and timeless, as Kidd explores the struggles of women to find their voice and assert their independence in a patriarchal society. Ana's journey is a powerful one, as she navigates the complexities of her own desires and longings, while also confronting the societal expectations that seek to constrain her. The result is a novel that is both inspiring and unforgettable, with a message that resonates deeply with readers.

In conclusion, "The Book of Longings" is a masterpiece of storytelling that is both timely and timeless. Sue Monk Kidd's novel is a triumph of insight and narrative skill, with a powerful and inspiring story that will resonate deeply with readers. With its richly drawn characters, meticulous research, and masterful prose, this novel is a must-read for anyone interested in historical fiction, women's studies, or the life of Jesus.

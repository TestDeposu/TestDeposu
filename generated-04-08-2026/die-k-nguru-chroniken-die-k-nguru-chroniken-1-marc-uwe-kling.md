# Die Känguru-Chroniken (Die Känguru-Chroniken, #1)

**Author:** Marc-Uwe Kling  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

Die Känguru-Chroniken, the latest offering from Marc-Uwe Kling, is a wildly inventive and irreverent take on the superhero genre. This graphic novel promises to be a thrilling ride, packed with action, humor, and wit. Kling, the acclaimed author of Qualityland, has teamed up with Jan Cronauer and Florian Biege to create a world that is at once familiar and yet utterly fresh.

At the heart of the story is Normal, a hero without superpowers, stuck in a dead-end job at a call center. His friends, the Zero Heroes, possess laughable abilities that are more hindrance than help. The love of his life has defected to the dark side, and the world is on the brink of chaos. But Normal is not one to give up easily. When the notorious Normalizer and his army of villains attempt to seize control of the Ticking Clock, Normal and the Zero Heroes must band together to stop them.

What sets Die Känguru-Chroniken apart is its tongue-in-cheek approach to the superhero genre. Kling and his co-creators are not afraid to poke fun at the conventions of the genre, and the result is a narrative that is both a loving tribute and a clever send-up. The story is full of clever references and Easter eggs that will delight fans of superhero comics and movies.

Kling's writing style is a perfect match for this genre-bending tale. His prose is witty and engaging, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the world he has created. The characters are well-developed and relatable, with Normal being a particularly compelling protagonist. His struggles to find his place in a world of superpowered beings are both poignant and hilarious.

One of the standout features of Die Känguru-Chroniken is its artwork. The illustrations are vibrant and dynamic, with a kinetic energy that propels the story forward. The visuals are a perfect match for Kling's writing style, capturing the humor and excitement of the narrative.

Overall, Die Känguru-Chroniken is a wildly entertaining ride that is sure to delight fans of superhero comics and graphic novels.

# A Beautiful Dark (A Beautiful Dark, #1)

**Author:** Jocelyn Davies  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

A Beautiful Dark, the debut novel by Jocelyn Davies, is a mesmerizing tale that delves into the complexities of human relationships, the blurred lines between truth and deception, and the struggle to find one's identity. Set against the backdrop of a harsh Colorado winter, the story follows Skye, a seventeen-year-old girl whose life is turned upside down by the arrival of two mysterious strangers, Asher and Devin.

From the outset, Davies masterfully weaves a web of intrigue, slowly unraveling the threads of Skye's past and her connections to the two enigmatic boys. The narrative is expertly paced, with a delicate balance of action, suspense, and romance that keeps the reader engaged and invested in Skye's journey. As the story unfolds, Davies skillfully explores the themes of identity, loyalty, and the consequences of choice, raising questions about the nature of truth and the reliability of appearances.

One of the standout features of A Beautiful Dark is its atmospheric setting. Davies's vivid descriptions of the Colorado winter landscape effectively evoke a sense of isolation and eeriness, perfectly capturing the mood of the story. Her prose is lyrical and evocative, with a keen attention to detail that immerses the reader in Skye's world.

The characters in A Beautiful Dark are multidimensional and complex, with Skye being a particularly well-crafted protagonist. Her struggles to navigate her relationships with Asher and Devin are deeply relatable, and her growth throughout the novel is both authentic and compelling. The two love interests are equally well-developed, with distinct personalities that add depth to the narrative.

Davies's writing style is descriptive and engaging, with a clear influence from the paranormal romance genre. Her use of metaphor and symbolism adds a layer of depth to the story, inviting readers to interpret the themes and motifs in their own way. While some may find the pacing a touch slow in the early stages, the payoff is well worth the investment, as the plot hurtles towards a dramatic and unexpected conclusion.

In conclusion, A Beautiful Dark is a captivating and atmospheric novel that will appeal to fans of paranormal romance and young adult fiction. With its intricate plot, complex characters, and vivid setting, it's a compelling read that will keep you on the edge of your seat. Davies's writing style is engaging and immersive, making this a must-read for anyone looking for a story that will transport them to another world.

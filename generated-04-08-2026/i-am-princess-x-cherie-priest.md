# I Am Princess X

**Author:** Cherie Priest  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In "I Am Princess X," Cherie Priest masterfully weaves a captivating narrative that delves into the complexities of grief, friendship, and the boundless power of imagination. This riveting novel tells the story of May, a 16-year-old girl struggling to come to terms with the loss of her best friend, Libby, who died in a tragic car accident three years ago.

The bond between May and Libby was forged through their shared creation, Princess X, a fearless and daring heroine they brought to life through Libby's artwork and May's storytelling. But when May stumbles upon a sticker of Princess X in a Seattle window, she is drawn into an underground culture centered around a webcomic that eerily echoes the stories she and Libby created together.

As May delves deeper into the world of IAmPrincessX.com, she becomes increasingly convinced that Libby is alive and behind the webcomic. This revelation sets May on a thrilling quest to uncover the truth, navigating a complex web of clues, cryptic messages, and unsettling similarities between the online Princess X and the one she and Libby created.

Priest's writing style is a perfect blend of lyrical prose and sharp, insightful dialogue, making "I Am Princess X" a compelling page-turner. The author's use of vivid descriptions and evocative imagery transports readers to the misty streets of Seattle, immersing them in May's world of grief, curiosity, and determination.

One of the most striking aspects of "I Am Princess X" is its exploration of the transformative power of storytelling. Through May and Libby's creation, Priest highlights the ways in which stories can transcend time, death, and even reality itself. The Princess X webcomic becomes a symbol of the enduring bond between the two friends, a testament to the idea that stories can take on a life of their own, even after the people who created them are gone.

The novel also raises thought-provoking questions about identity, community, and the impact of technology on human relationships. As May becomes more deeply embroiled in the world of Princess X, she must confront the blurred lines between her online and offline life, as well as the tensions between her desire for connection and her need for solitude.

Throughout the book, Priest's characters are multidimensional and relatable, with May being a particularly well-crafted protagonist. Her struggles to cope with grief, her determination to uncover the truth, and her ultimate resilience make her a compelling and admirable heroine.

The inclusion of comic illustrations throughout the book adds a unique and captivating dimension to the narrative, bringing the world of Princess X to life in a way that feels both authentic and immersive.

Overall, "I Am Princess X" is a captivating and emotionally resonant novel that explores the complexities of friendship, grief, and the power of storytelling. With its vivid prose, relatable characters, and thought-provoking themes, this book is sure to resonate with readers of all ages.

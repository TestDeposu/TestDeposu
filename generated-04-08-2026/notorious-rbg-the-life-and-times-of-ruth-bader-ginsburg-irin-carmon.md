# Notorious RBG: The Life and Times of Ruth Bader Ginsburg

**Author:** Irin Carmon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

Notorious RBG: The Life and Times of Ruth Bader Ginsburg, written by Irin Carmon, is a captivating and inspirational biography that delves into the life of a truly remarkable woman. The book is a testament to Ginsburg's unwavering dedication to social justice and her unrelenting pursuit of equality. Through a unique blend of reported narrative, annotated dissents, rare archival photos, and illustrations, Carmon masterfully weaves together a comprehensive and intimate portrait of the Justice, revealing the woman behind the iconic image.

One of the most striking aspects of the book is its ability to balance the personal and professional aspects of Ginsburg's life. Carmon's writing is engaging and accessible, making the book an enjoyable read for both legal enthusiasts and those with a casual interest in Ginsburg's story. The inclusion of annotated dissents provides a fascinating glimpse into Ginsburg's brilliant legal mind, showcasing her meticulous attention to detail and her unwavering commitment to justice.

The book also explores Ginsburg's journey from a self-described "flaming feminist litigator" to a beloved intergenerational symbol of equality and strength. Carmon's narrative is peppered with stories from family members, close friends, and colleagues, offering a unique perspective on Ginsburg's life and career. The author's use of rare archival photos and documents adds a rich visual dimension to the book, bringing Ginsburg's remarkable life into vivid focus.

What sets this book apart from other biographies is its ability to transcend the traditional boundaries of a legal biography. Carmon's writing is infused with a deep sense of admiration and respect for Ginsburg, yet it remains objective and nuanced. The book is not simply a love letter to the Justice, but rather a thoughtful and engaging exploration of her life and legacy.

Throughout the book, Carmon highlights Ginsburg's most significant contributions to the legal landscape, including her landmark dissents and her tireless advocacy for women's rights. The author's analysis of these events is insightful and thought-provoking, providing readers with a deeper understanding of the complexities and challenges that Ginsburg faced throughout her career.

In conclusion, Notorious RBG: The Life and Times of Ruth Bader Ginsburg is a compelling and inspiring biography that is sure to captivate readers of all ages. Carmon's writing is engaging, informative, and thought-provoking, making the book an essential read for anyone interested in social justice, feminism, and the life of a truly remarkable woman. With its unique blend of narrative, annotation, and visual history, this book is a testament to the power of storytelling and the enduring legacy of Ruth Bader Ginsburg.

# Torrent (River of Time, #3)

**Author:** Lisa Tawn Bergren  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**A Triumphant Journey Through Time: A Review of Lisa Tawn Bergren's "Torrent"**

Lisa Tawn Bergren's "Torrent", the third installment in the River of Time series, is a mesmerizing tale of love, loss, and self-discovery that will leave readers spellbound and yearning for more. Set against the breathtaking backdrop of Italy, "Torrent" is a story of three strong-willed women navigating the complexities of life, faith, and relationships.

The novel continues to follow the lives of Gabi, Liam, and Marcello, as they face numerous challenges that test their love, trust, and resolve. Bergren's signature writing style weaves a complex web of emotions, making it difficult for readers to distinguish between reality and fantasy. As the story unfolds, themes of loyalty, sacrifice, and redemption become intertwined, adding depth and dimension to the narrative.

One of Bergren's greatest strengths as an author is her ability to craft relatable characters that evoke strong emotions in readers. Gabi, the protagonist, is a beautifully flawed character whose growth and development throughout the series is both captivating and inspiring. Bergren's portrayal of Gabi's struggles with her faith, identity, and relationships is raw and honest, making her one of the most authentic characters in young adult literature.

The author's use of descriptive language is nothing short of stunning. Her vivid descriptions of the Italian landscape, culture, and history transport readers to a world of beauty and enchantment. Bergren's writing is almost poetic, with a unique ability to evoke the senses and immerse readers in the story.

However, some readers may find the pacing of the novel a bit uneven at times. Bergren's narrative often meanders through complex plot twists, which can lead to a slightly disjointed reading experience. Nevertheless, the payoff is well worth the effort, as the story culminates in a heart-wrenching, yet ultimately satisfying, conclusion.

Bergren's exploration of faith is a recurring theme throughout the series, and "Torrent" is no exception. The author's thoughtful and nuanced approach to the topic is impressive, avoiding didacticism and instead opting for a more introspective, character-driven exploration of spirituality. This aspect of the novel will resonate with readers of all faiths, as Bergren skillfully captures the complexities and doubts that often accompany spiritual growth.

In conclusion, "Torrent" is a captivating, emotionally charged, and thought-provoking novel that cements Lisa Tawn Bergren's position as a masterful storyteller. While not without its minor flaws, the novel's strengths far outweigh its weaknesses, making it a must-read for fans of young adult fiction, particularly those who enjoy historical romance and fantasy.

As readers embark on this triumphant journey through time, they will find themselves swept up in a world of love, loss, and redemption, forever changed by the experience.

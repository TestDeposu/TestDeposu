# The Forgotten (John Puller, #2)

**Author:** David Baldacci  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In "The Forgotten", David Baldacci weaves a gripping narrative that delves into the dark underbelly of a seemingly idyllic town, masterfully crafting a tale of conspiracy, deception, and murder. The second installment in the John Puller series, this book is a testament to Baldacci's skill in crafting intricate plots and memorable characters.

At the heart of the story is Army Special Agent John Puller, a combat veteran with a keen mind and a sharp instinct for justice. When his aunt is found dead in the picturesque town of Paradise, Florida, Puller is drawn into a complex web of secrets and lies. As he digs deeper, he uncovers a sinister plot that threatens to destroy the very fabric of the community.

Baldacci's writing is characterized by his ability to create a sense of tension and unease, drawing the reader into the world of the story and refusing to let go. His prose is lean and mean, with a focus on propelling the plot forward at a breakneck pace. The result is a narrative that is both thrilling and thought-provoking, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the human psyche.

One of the standout features of "The Forgotten" is its exploration of the theme of deception. Baldacci expertly exposes the cracks in the facade of Paradise, revealing a town where nothing is as it seems. The characters are multi-dimensional and complex, with motivations that are both understandable and terrifying. As Puller navigates the treacherous landscape of the town, he must confront his own demons and make difficult choices that challenge his sense of justice.

The author's use of setting is also noteworthy. The town of Paradise is vividly rendered, with a keen sense of atmosphere and a deep understanding of the cultural and social nuances of small-town America. The contrast between the idyllic surface and the dark underbelly is striking, and serves to heighten the sense of tension and unease.

In conclusion, "The Forgotten" is a gripping and thought-provoking thriller that is sure to appeal to fans of the genre. With its intricate plot, memorable characters, and keen sense of atmosphere, it is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and engaging story. David Baldacci's writing is masterful, and his exploration of the theme of deception is both timely and timeless.

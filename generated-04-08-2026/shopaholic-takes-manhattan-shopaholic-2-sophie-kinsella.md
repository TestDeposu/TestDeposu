# Shopaholic Takes Manhattan (Shopaholic, #2)

**Author:** Sophie Kinsella  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2002

Sophie Kinsella's Shopaholic Takes Manhattan is a sidesplitting and engaging read that delves into the life of Becky Bloomwood, a lovable and eccentric heroine who takes the Big Apple by storm. The second installment in the Shopaholic series, this book is a masterclass in comedic writing, with Kinsella's signature wit and charm on full display.

At its core, Shopaholic Takes Manhattan is a story about identity, love, and the pursuit of happiness. Becky, a TV financial guru with a penchant for shopping, is forced to confront her own demons when her boyfriend Luke announces that he's relocating to New York for business. As she navigates the ups and downs of life in the city, Becky must confront her own flaws and weaknesses, all while trying to maintain her relationship with Luke and establish herself as a credible financial expert.

Kinsella's writing style is a major strength of the book. Her prose is effortless and engaging, making it easy to become fully immersed in Becky's world. The author's use of humor is also noteworthy, with a keen eye for observational comedy that will have readers laughing out loud. From Becky's absurd shopping sprees to her hilarious interactions with the people around her, Kinsella's comedic touch is undeniable.

One of the most impressive aspects of Shopaholic Takes Manhattan is its ability to balance lighthearted humor with more serious themes. Beneath its comedic surface, the book tackles issues like relationships, career ambition, and personal growth, making it a more nuanced and satisfying read than one might initially expect. Kinsella's portrayal of Becky's struggles and triumphs is both relatable and authentic, making it easy to become invested in her journey.

The characters in the book are also well-developed and memorable, with Becky being the standout star. Her flaws and eccentricities make her a lovable and human protagonist, and her interactions with the people around her are often hilarious and poignant. Luke, her boyfriend, is also a well-crafted character, with a dry wit and a deep affection for Becky that makes their relationship feel genuine and heartfelt.

Overall, Shopaholic Takes Manhattan is a delightful and engaging read that is sure to appeal to fans of romantic comedy and chick lit. With its witty dialogue, engaging characters, and lighthearted humor, it's a perfect choice for anyone looking for a fun and entertaining read. Kinsella's writing style is a major strength of the book, and her ability to balance humor and heart makes it a truly unforgettable read.

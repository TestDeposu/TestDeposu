# The Night Stalker (DCI Erika Foster, #2)

**Author:** Robert Bryndza  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

When a marriage is fracturing on the rocks, the last place you should seek therapy is on actual, roaring rapids. In this high-octane literary adaptation of the blockbuster film starring Meryl Streep and Kevin Bacon, screenwriter Denis O’Neill translates the visual adrenaline of the silver screen into a propulsive, white-knuckle thrill ride. The story follows river-rafting expert Gail, her husband Tom, and their young son as they embark on what was supposed to be a healing white-water adventure in the breathtaking wilds of Montana. 

Instead of finding domestic harmony, they cross paths with two seemingly inexperienced rafters who are actually violent escaped convicts leaving a bloody trail in their wake. What begins as a tense wilderness encounter quickly devolves into a terrifying hostage situation. Desperate to evade the closing net of police and federal marshals, the armed killers force the family further down the river, driving them inexorably toward the roaring terror of a deadly class-five rapid. 

The central theme of the book hinges on the fragile duality of control—both within the confines of a strained marriage and against the indomitable fury of the natural world. As Gail and Tom are pushed to their absolute limits, the narrative brilliantly contrasts the savagery of man with the raw, unforgiving brutality of Mother Nature. Survival requires them to strip away years of resentment and relearn how to trust one another, proving that the human spirit, much like the river itself, possesses a fierce and unpredictable resilience. 

O’Neill’s writing style is as striking and immersive as the Montana landscape he describes. He masterfully ratchets up the psychological tension, blending visceral action with deep emotional stakes. Readers who crave the rugged, atmospheric storytelling of C.J. Box and Craig Johnson will find themselves completely swept away. This is a classic adventure story that never lets up, racing forward with relentless momentum to a breathtaking and perilous conclusion.

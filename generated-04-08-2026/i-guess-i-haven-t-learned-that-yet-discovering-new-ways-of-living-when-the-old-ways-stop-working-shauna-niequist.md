# I Guess I Haven't Learned That Yet: Discovering New Ways of Living When the Old Ways Stop Working

**Author:** Shauna Niequist  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

"I Guess I Haven't Learned That Yet" by Shauna Niequist is a poignant and powerful exploration of transformation, self-discovery, and the complexities of the human experience. This New York Times bestseller is a masterfully crafted memoir that delves into the author's journey of navigating chaos, change, and loss, and emerging with a newfound sense of courage, curiosity, and compassion.

Niequist's writing is characterized by its candor, vulnerability, and lyricism, making her story feel both deeply personal and universally relatable. As she recounts her struggles with chronic pain, heartbreak, and the challenges of midlife, she invites readers to join her on a journey of unlearning and rediscovery. With unflinching honesty, she confronts the limitations of her previous coping mechanisms and spiritual practices, and embarks on a quest to find new ways of living, trusting, and being.

One of the most striking aspects of this book is its exploration of the tension between answers and questions, force and forgiveness, and trying hard versus tenderly surrendering. Niequist's narrative is woven together by a series of profound insights, each one illuminating the complexities of the human condition and the mysteries of faith. As she grapples with the unknowns and uncertainties of life, she arrives at a profound understanding of the importance of embracing the unknown, and trusting in the goodness of God.

Throughout the book, Niequist's voice is both gentle and powerful, offering a sense of comfort and solidarity to readers who may be navigating their own seasons of struggle. Her writing is infused with a sense of hope and resilience, reminding us that even in the darkest moments, transformation and growth are possible.

Ultimately, "I Guess I Haven't Learned That Yet" is a book about the beauty of becoming, and the courage it takes to let go of what no longer serves us. It is an invitation to join Niequist on a journey of discovery, to explore the depths of our own hearts, and to find the courage to live with greater curiosity, compassion, and tenderness.

This book is a masterpiece, a journey, an invitation, and a joy, all woven together with the threads of hope, heartbreak, and transformation. As Niequist so eloquently puts it, "This is a book about the journey, not the destination. It's about the questions, not the answers. It's about the beauty of becoming, and the courage it takes to let go of what no longer serves us."

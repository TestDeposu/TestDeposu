# Split Infinity (Apprentice Adept, #1)

**Author:** Piers Anthony  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1980

Split Infinity, the first installment of Piers Anthony's Apprentice Adept series, is a mesmerizing blend of science fiction and fantasy that whisks readers away on a thrilling adventure across two vastly different worlds. Published in 1980, this novel showcases Anthony's unique style, which has captivated audiences for decades with its imaginative storytelling, memorable characters, and thought-provoking themes.

At its core, Split Infinity explores the concept of duality, delving into the complexities of identity, free will, and the consequences of one's actions. The protagonist, Stile, finds himself torn between two worlds: the technological, decadent world of Proton, where he is a serf and a master Gamesman, and the magical realm of Phaze, where his alternate self has been murdered by sorcery. As Stile navigates these disparate realities, he must confront the darker aspects of his own nature and the true meaning of self-discovery.

Anthony's writing style is characterized by its accessibility, wit, and clever wordplay. His use of language is engaging and concise, making it easy for readers to become immersed in the world of Proton and Phaze. The author's ability to balance action, suspense, and humor creates a narrative that is both entertaining and intellectually stimulating. The cast of characters, including Stile's robot companion, the werewolf, and the unicorn, are well-developed and add depth to the story, while the Oracle's enigmatic presence serves as a catalyst for Stile's journey of self-discovery.

One of the most striking aspects of Split Infinity is its exploration of the tensions between technology and magic. Anthony masterfully juxtaposes the scientific, rational world of Proton with the mystical, intuitive realm of Phaze, highlighting the limitations and possibilities of each. This dichotomy serves as a backdrop for Stile's struggles to reconcile his dual identities and find a sense of purpose in both worlds.

The themes of identity, free will, and the consequences of one's actions are timeless and thought-provoking. Anthony raises important questions about the nature of reality, the power of individual choice, and the interconnectedness of seemingly disparate worlds. Through Stile's experiences, the author shows that true understanding and growth can only be achieved by embracing the complexities and paradoxes of existence.

In conclusion, Split Infinity is a captivating and imaginative novel that showcases Piers Anthony's unique style and thematic concerns. This book is a must-read for fans of science fiction, fantasy, and adventure, as well as anyone interested in exploring the complexities of identity and the human condition.

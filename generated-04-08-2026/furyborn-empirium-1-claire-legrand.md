# Furyborn (Empirium, #1)

**Author:** Claire Legrand  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

Furyborn, the first installment in Claire Legrand's Empirium Trilogy, is a mesmerizing epic fantasy that whisks readers away to a realm of elemental magic, legendary godsbeasts, and ruthless assassins. This instant New York Times bestseller masterfully weaves the tales of two fiercely independent young women, Rielle Dardenne and Eliana Ferracora, centuries apart, yet bound by an intricate web of fate that spans millennia.

Legrand's writing is nothing short of enchanting, with a narrative voice that effortlessly glides between the dual perspectives of Rielle and Eliana. The author's world-building is meticulous and immersive, plunging readers into a richly detailed universe where magic and myth collide. The Empirium Trilogy's sprawling landscape is meticulously crafted, with a deep history that underpins the unfolding drama.

At the heart of Furyborn lies a powerful exploration of female empowerment, as Rielle and Eliana navigate treacherous landscapes, confronting the dark forces that seek to control their lives. Both characters are multidimensional and complex, their struggles and triumphs deeply relatable. Rielle, with her unyielding determination to prove herself as the Sun Queen, and Eliana, with her unshakeable conviction to find her missing mother, embody the resilience and strength that define the female spirit.

One of the most striking aspects of Furyborn is its masterful blend of light and darkness. Legrand's world is a realm of stark contrasts, where hope and despair, good and evil, are inextricably intertwined. The author's portrayal of the Undying Empire, with its sinister machinations and ruthless ambition, serves as a potent reminder of the dangers of unchecked power and the importance of resistance.

Throughout the narrative, Legrand's prose is lyrical and evocative, conjuring vivid images that dance across the reader's imagination. Her action sequences are taut and suspenseful, propelling the story forward with a breakneck pace that leaves the reader breathless. The elemental magic trials, which Rielle must endure to prove her worth as the Sun Queen, are a particular highlight, showcasing Legrand's inventive genius and flair for the dramatic.

As the story unfolds, the connections between Rielle and Eliana become increasingly apparent, weaving a rich tapestry of cause and effect that spans centuries. The author's handling of this complex narrative is nothing short of masterful, as the past and present converge in a stunning climax that sets the stage for the next installment in the trilogy.

In conclusion, Furyborn is a must-read for fans of epic fantasy, dark fantasy, and strong female protagonists. Claire Legrand's writing is addictive, fascinating, and visionary, making this series a compelling addition to the world of young adult fiction. With its immersive world-building, complex characters, and pulse-pounding action sequences, Furyborn is a true page-turner that will leave readers eagerly anticipating the next chapter in the Empirium Trilogy.

# Beginnings: Obsidian & Onyx (Lux, #1-2)

**Author:** Jennifer L. Armentrout  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**Beginnings: Obsidian & Onyx – A Love Story Woven in Lies, Power, and Unforgiving Truths**

Jennifer L. Armentrout’s *Beginnings: Obsidian & Onyx*—the first two installments of the *Lux* series—are more than just a romance or a sci-fi thriller; they are a masterclass in tension, moral ambiguity, and the kind of emotional whiplash that keeps readers hooked from page one. Here, love is a battlefield, trust is a currency, and the line between hero and villain blurs with every twist. Armentrout doesn’t just write a story; she crafts an experience—one where the reader is as desperate for answers as the characters, where every revelation feels like a punch to the gut, and where the stakes are so high they feel personal.

At the heart of *Obsidian* and *Onyx* is Katy, a seemingly ordinary high school girl whose life is upended when she’s inexplicably linked to Daemon Black, a brooding, otherworldly being with powers that defy logic. Their connection isn’t just physical—it’s *alive*, a bond that hums with an intensity that borders on obsession. Daemon, a man haunted by his past and the betrayal that cost him his brother, is determined to prove his feelings for Katy aren’t just a side effect of their bizarre link. But Katy, still reeling from the trauma of their initial connection, is determined to push him away—until she can’t. The push-and-pull between them is electric, a dance of attraction and resistance that feels raw and authentic. Armentrout doesn’t shy away from the messiness of their relationship; there’s no grand romantic gesture here, just two people grappling with fear, desire, and the terrifying weight of what they’re becoming to each other.

What sets *Beginnings* apart isn’t just the chemistry between Katy and Daemon, though—it’s the *world* Armentrout builds. The *Lux* series is steeped in sci-fi elements that feel both grounded and otherworldly: alien powers, government conspiracies, and a mystery so vast it could swallow the reader whole. The Department of Defense’s involvement looms like a shadow, a reminder that Katy and Daemon aren’t just fighting for their own survival—they’re fighting for something bigger. And then there’s the new boy in school, a character whose secrets add another layer of intrigue. Who is he? What does he know? And why won’t he tell the truth? Armentrout’s world-building is meticulous, each piece of lore and each hidden detail serving to deepen the mystery, making the reader’s curiosity a living thing.

But it’s the *themes* that truly elevate this series. *Beginnings* is a story about the cost of love, the lies we tell to survive, and the people we become when we’re forced to choose between truth and safety. Katy’s journey is one of self-discovery, as she learns to trust herself in a world that constantly tries to control her. Daemon, meanwhile, is a study in redemption—his past is a prison, but his future with Katy might be the key to breaking free. Armentrout doesn’t romanticize their relationship; she shows its fragility, its pain, and the way love can be both a weapon and a shield. There’s no easy resolution, no neat happy ending—just the kind of emotional rollercoaster that leaves readers breathless.

Armentrout’s writing style is another strength. Her prose is sharp and unflinching, with a rhythm that pulls the reader forward like a tide. She excels at dialogue, making even the most intense scenes feel natural and real. And her ability to balance action with introspection is a testament to her skill—one moment, you’re racing through a high-stakes confrontation, the next, you’re buried in the quiet, devastating truth of a character’s past. She doesn’t over-explain; she lets the story breathe, trusting the reader to piece together the puzzle as the characters do.

The real magic of *Beginnings* lies in its ability to make the reader *care*. By the time you reach the end of *Onyx*, you won’t just be invested in the plot—you’ll be invested in the people. You’ll ache for Katy’s safety, you’ll rage at the betrayals, and you’ll hold your breath as the truth unfolds. And that’s the mark of a truly great series: one that doesn’t just entertain, but *haunts* you long after the last page.

If you love stories where love is dangerous, where the truth is a weapon, and where every character is fighting for something they can’t afford to lose, then *Beginnings* is for you. Jennifer L. Armentrout doesn’t just tell a story—she creates a world where the reader becomes an active participant, where every choice feels like your own, and where the ending leaves you desperate for more. And trust me, you’ll want more.

# Appointment with Death (Hercule Poirot, #19)

**Author:** Agatha Christie  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

In the scorching deserts of the Middle East, Agatha Christie masterfully weaves a tale of murder, mystery, and intrigue in her classic whodunit, Appointment with Death. This exclusive authorized edition, first published in 1938 and re-released in 2022, is a testament to the author's unparalleled skill in crafting a narrative that continues to captivate readers to this day.

At the heart of the story lies the enigmatic and larger-than-life figure of Hercule Poirot, Christie's iconic Belgian detective. With his signature mustache and impeccable taste in fashion, Poirot is the embodiment of elegance and refinement. However, it is his extraordinary powers of observation and his ability to piece together seemingly unrelated fragments that make him the perfect sleuth to unravel the complex web of events surrounding the murder of Mrs. Boynton.

The victim, a woman of immense wealth and influence, is found dead in the ancient city of Petra, a place steeped in history and mystique. As Poirot begins his investigation, he is confronted with a cast of characters who are all, in one way or another, connected to the deceased. There's the timid and nervous daughter, Carol; the dashing young doctor, Sarah King; and the effervescent American, Raymond Boynton. Each of them seems to be hiding secrets, and it is up to Poirot to uncover the truth behind their facades.

Christie's writing style is, as always, a masterclass in subtlety and restraint. She skillfully drops hints and clues throughout the narrative, expertly manipulating the reader's perceptions and keeping them guessing until the very end. Her use of language is concise and evocative, transporting the reader to the sun-drenched landscapes of the Middle East and immersing them in the opulent world of the Boyntons.

One of the most striking aspects of Appointment with Death is its exploration of the theme of family dynamics and the destructive power of manipulation. Mrs. Boynton, a woman of immense psychological complexity, is revealed to be a master manipulator, using her wealth and influence to control those around her. Her family, trapped in a web of deceit and fear, is forced to navigate a complex emotional landscape, where the lines between love and hate are constantly blurred.

Through Poirot's investigation, Christie expertly exposes the dark underbelly of the Boynton family's seemingly idyllic existence. As the detective digs deeper, he uncovers a tangled web of relationships, alliances, and rivalries that ultimately lead him to the killer. The climax of the novel is both shocking and unexpected, a testament to Christie's skill in crafting a plot that is both intricate and suspenseful.

In conclusion, Appointment with Death is a gripping and engaging mystery that showcases Agatha Christie's mastery of the genre. With its intricate plot, memorable characters, and vivid setting, this novel is a must-read for fans of detective fiction. As a work of classic literature, it continues to captivate readers with its timeless themes and enduring appeal.

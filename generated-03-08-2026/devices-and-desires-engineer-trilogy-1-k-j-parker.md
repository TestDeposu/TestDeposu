# Devices and Desires (Engineer Trilogy, #1)

**Author:** K.J. Parker  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Devices and Desires, the first installment in K.J. Parker's Engineer Trilogy, is a gripping and visceral tale of revenge, ingenuity, and the unyielding human spirit. This World Fantasy award-winning novel delves into the darker aspects of human nature, masterfully weaving a complex narrative that is as thought-provoking as it is unsettling.

At the heart of the story lies an embittered engineer, driven by a burning desire for vengeance against the very system that has wronged him. His journey is a nuanced exploration of the blurred lines between justice and retribution, as he navigates the treacherous landscape of his own morality. Through his character, Parker raises important questions about the nature of power, corruption, and the true cost of revenge.

One of the most striking aspects of Devices and Desires is its meticulous world-building. Parker's fictional realm is richly textured and meticulously crafted, with a depth and complexity that is all too rare in modern fantasy. The author's use of language is equally impressive, with a sparse, economical prose that belies a profound emotional depth.

Parker's writing style is reminiscent of the great masters of the genre – think Joe Abercrombie or Scott Lynch – with a keen focus on character development and a willingness to push the boundaries of what is considered "acceptable" in fantasy literature. The result is a novel that is as unflinching as it is thought-provoking, a true masterpiece of modern fantasy.

Throughout the book, Parker's engineer is a compelling and deeply human protagonist, driven by a complex mix of motivations that are both deeply personal and universally relatable. His story is a powerful exploration of the human condition, a nuanced and multifaceted examination of the ways in which we respond to adversity and the true cost of our desires.

In short, Devices and Desires is a masterpiece of modern fantasy, a gripping and visceral tale of revenge, ingenuity, and the unyielding human spirit. With its richly textured world-building, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes, this novel is an absolute must-read for fans of the genre.

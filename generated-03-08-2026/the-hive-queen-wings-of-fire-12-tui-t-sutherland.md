# The Hive Queen (Wings of Fire, #12)

**Author:** Tui T. Sutherland  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

In "The Hive Queen," the 12th installment of the Wings of Fire series, Tui T. Sutherland delves deep into the intricate world of dragon politics, exploring themes of power, identity, and the consequences of secrecy.

Cricket's narrative drives the plot, as she navigates the treacherous landscape of the HiveWings. Her unique circumstances - being immune to Queen Wasp's powers - create a sense of urgency and raise questions about the nature of authority and control. The stakes are high, as Cricket, along with her SilkWing friends Blue and Swordtail, and the LeafWing Sundew, must evade capture and uncover the truth about the Book of Clearsight. Tui T. Sutherland skillfully weaves together multiple storylines, crafting a complex web of relationships and alliances that add depth to the narrative.

Sutherland's writing style is characterized by crisp, concise dialogue and descriptions that transport readers to the vibrant world of dragon politics. Her command of language allows her to convey a vast range of emotions, from Cricket's anxiety and fear to the camaraderie and trust that forms among the fugitive dragons. One of the most striking aspects of Sutherland's writing is her ability to balance action and introspection, creating a narrative that is both fast-paced and emotionally resonant.

At its core, "The Hive Queen" is a story about the search for truth and self-discovery. Cricket's journey serves as a powerful metaphor for the struggles many readers face, including the quest for independence and the desire to be understood. Through Cricket's eyes, Sutherland illustrates the devastating consequences of hiding one's true nature and the risks of seeking answers in a world where the truth is often shrouded in secrecy.

The character development in "The Hive Queen" is nothing short of impressive. Cricket's relationships with her parents, her friends, and even her foes are multifaceted and nuanced. The supporting cast is equally well-fleshed out, with each character bringing their unique perspective and strengths to the narrative. This attention to detail creates a rich tapestry that invites readers to become fully immersed in the world of Wings of Fire.

"The Hive Queen" is a thrilling addition to the series, one that will appeal to both new readers and fans of previous installments. Sutherland's writing style, coupled with the engaging plot and memorable characters, make this a must-read for anyone who enjoys stories about self-discovery, politics, and epic battles.

With each new installment, Tui T. Sutherland continues to solidify her position as a masterful storyteller, weaving together a world that is both fantastical and uncomfortably familiar. "The Hive Queen" is a testament to her skill and dedication to crafting stories that captivate and inspire.

# The Wide-Awake Princess (Wide-Awake Princess, #1)

**Author:** E.D. Baker  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In "The Wide-Awake Princess", E.D. Baker masterfully weaves a captivating tale that breathes new life into the classic story of Sleeping Beauty. This delightful re-imagining follows Princess Annie, a refreshingly strong-willed and independent heroine who is resistant to magic. When her sister Gwen pricks her finger and the entire castle falls under a deep sleep, Annie finds herself the only one immune to the spell. With her quick wit and determination, Annie embarks on a thrilling adventure to find a prince to break the curse and rescue her sister.

Throughout the book, Baker's signature style shines through, showcasing her talent for crafting unique and endearing characters. Annie's relatable and engaging voice drives the narrative, making it easy to become invested in her journey. The author's use of clever plot twists and humorous moments adds to the story's charm, keeping readers entertained and eager to find out what happens next.

One of the most striking aspects of "The Wide-Awake Princess" is its exploration of the theme of female empowerment. Annie is a true heroine who takes charge of her own destiny, refusing to wait for a prince to rescue her. Instead, she bravely navigates the challenges of a fairy tale world, relying on her intelligence and resourcefulness to overcome obstacles. This portrayal of a strong and capable female protagonist is a welcome addition to the world of fairy tales, offering a positive and inspiring role model for young readers.

Baker's world-building is also noteworthy, as she skillfully creates a rich and immersive environment that draws inspiration from classic fairy tales. The story is filled with familiar characters and motifs, but the author's unique spin and clever interpretations make the world feel fresh and exciting.

In short, "The Wide-Awake Princess" is a captivating and delightful read that is sure to enchant fans of fairy tales and adventure stories. With its strong heroine, engaging plot, and rich world-building, this book is a must-read for anyone looking for a fun and inspiring tale. As the first installment in a series, it sets the stage for a thrilling and magical journey that promises to keep readers on the edge of their seats.

# Sarum: The Novel of England

**Author:** Edward Rutherfurd  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1988

Sarum: The Novel of England, written by Edward Rutherfurd, is a masterfully crafted epic novel that delves into the intricate and turbulent history of England. This sweeping narrative spans centuries, weaving a complex tapestry of five families - the Wilsons, the Masons, the family of Porteus, the Shockleys, and the Godfreys - whose destinies are inextricably linked, offering a captivating glimpse into the country's rich and varied past.

Rutherfurd's writing style is characterized by his meticulous attention to historical detail, which brings the narrative to life with vivid descriptions and well-researched accuracy. The author's ability to balance history and fiction seamlessly is a hallmark of his craft, making the novel an engaging and immersive read. As the Chicago Tribune aptly puts it, the book "bursts with action, encyclopedic in historic detail... supremely well crafted and a delight to read."

One of the most striking aspects of Sarum is its ability to convey the passage of time, as the fortunes and fates of the five families ebb and flow over the centuries. Rutherfurd's narrative is akin to a richly woven fabric, with each thread carefully intertwined to create a stunning portrait of England's history. The Fort Worth Star-Telegram praises the novel, stating that it "clears away the mists of time from antiquity, giving the reader the impression that events over a span of ten centuries occurred only yesterday."

The novel's themes of struggle, adventure, and human drama are timeless and universal, making it a compelling read for anyone interested in history, fiction, or a combination of both. Rutherfurd's writing is not only informative but also entertaining, as he skillfully brings to life the triumphs and tribulations of the characters, making the reader feel invested in their stories. The Chicago Sun-Times aptly describes the novel as "strong... appealing... a saga that gives you not only history but comfort."

Throughout the book, Rutherfurd's passion for history and storytelling shines through, making Sarum a true masterpiece of historical fiction. The San Francisco Chronicle praises the novel, stating that it is "a richly imagined vision of history, written with genuine delight." The Kansas City Star also commends Rutherfurd's storytelling skills, noting that "he is at his best... His storytelling skills are substantial... One of the best books of the season."

In conclusion, Sarum: The Novel of England is a must-read for anyone interested in historical fiction, English history, or epic novels. Rutherfurd's masterful writing, meticulous research, and engaging narrative make this book a true classic. With its richly detailed descriptions, vivid characters, and sweeping narrative, Sarum is a novel that will transport readers through time, leaving a lasting impression long after the final page is turned.

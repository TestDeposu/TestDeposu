# So You Want to Be a Wizard (Young Wizards, #1)

**Author:** Diane Duane  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2003

"So You Want to Be a Wizard" by Diane Duane is a captivating and immersive tale that delves into the world of magic, friendship, and the eternal struggle between good and evil. The first installment in the Young Wizards series, this book is a masterful blend of fantasy, adventure, and coming-of-age themes that will resonate with readers of all ages.

At its core, "So You Want to Be a Wizard" is a story about empowerment and self-discovery. The protagonist, Nita Callahan, is a relatable and endearing heroine who finds solace in the world of magic after being bullied and marginalized by her peers. Her journey from an unhappy and isolated individual to a confident and powerful wizard is both inspiring and authentic. Duane's portrayal of Nita's transformation is nuanced and thoughtful, exploring the complexities of adolescence and the challenges of navigating a new and unfamiliar world.

One of the standout aspects of Duane's writing style is her ability to craft a rich and detailed world that is both fantastical and grounded in reality. The magical system she has created is complex and logical, with its own rules and limitations. The world-building is meticulous, with a clear sense of history and depth that adds to the overall sense of immersion.

The author's use of language is also noteworthy. Duane's prose is clear, concise, and engaging, making it easy to become fully invested in the story. Her writing is descriptive without being overly verbose, painting vivid pictures of the characters, settings, and magical creatures that inhabit the world of the Young Wizards.

The themes of friendship and cooperation are also central to the story. Nita's partnership with Kit Rodriguez, a fellow wizard-in-training, is a highlight of the book. Their relationship is built on mutual respect, trust, and a deepening understanding of one another's strengths and weaknesses. Together, they face numerous challenges and obstacles, learning valuable lessons about the importance of teamwork, communication, and empathy.

The villain of the story, the Lone Power, is a formidable and intriguing foe. This malevolent entity is a powerful symbol of the destructive forces that threaten the world, and Duane's portrayal of its motivations and actions is both chilling and thought-provoking.

Overall, "So You Want to Be a Wizard" is a compelling and engaging read that will appeal to fans of fantasy, adventure, and young adult fiction. Duane's writing is masterful, and her world-building is exceptional. The themes of empowerment, friendship, and self-discovery are timely and relevant, making this book a must-read for anyone looking for a story that will inspire and captivate.

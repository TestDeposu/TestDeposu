# Here's to Us (What If It's Us, #2)

**Author:** Becky Albertalli  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

"Here's to Us" is a heartwarming and humorous companion novel to the beloved "What If It's Us." In this delightful duet by Becky Albertalli and Adam Silvera, we are reunited with the charming and endearing characters of Arthur and Ben, two boys who captured our hearts in the first installment. With their signature wit and poignancy, the authors navigate the ups and downs of their romance, pushing the boundaries of what it means to be in love and to be true to oneself.

The narrative expertly captures the essence of Arthur, the theatrical boy with a flair for the dramatic, and Ben, the introspective and guarded young man who seems to have his heart locked away. Their contrasting personalities make for a captivating and believable dynamic, as the authors expertly explore the challenges and triumphs of their relationship. With each page, the characters grow and evolve, and through their romance, the authors convey valuable lessons about vulnerability, trust, and the importance of taking risks.

One of the standout aspects of this novel is its nuanced portrayal of love and relationships. Albertalli and Silvera masterfully convey the ebbs and flows of a real romance, with all its twists and turns. They tackle complex themes with ease, addressing issues such as social anxiety, identity, and the fragility of the human heart. Yet, despite the depth and complexity of these topics, the authors maintain a lighthearted and humorous tone, never shying away from the humor and absurdity that makes life so endearing.

The writing is, as always, exquisite. Albertalli and Silvera have a knack for crafting engaging dialogue, which brings the characters to life with their unique voices and mannerisms. The narrative flows seamlessly, with a structure that is both linear and organic, making it effortless to become fully immersed in the world of Arthur and Ben. The authors' writing is, in fact, such a seamless blend of styles that it's impossible to distinguish their individual contributions, making it feel like one singular voice, rather than a collaborative effort.

Through Arthur and Ben's story, the authors pose profound questions about love and existence. They encourage readers to ponder the nature of destiny, wondering if the universe indeed has a plan for us or if we shape our own fates. This narrative thread weaves its way throughout the book, adding a layer of depth and introspection that will resonate with readers long after finishing the novel.

Ultimately, "Here's to Us" is a love letter to the complexities of romance and the beauty of human connection. It's a book that will leave readers beaming with happiness and introspecting on the importance of living in the moment. As we bid farewell to Arthur and Ben, their story becomes a testament to the transformative power of love, encouraging us to be our authentic selves and to cherish the beauty of every moment, no matter how fleeting.

For fans of the What If It's Us series, "Here's to Us" will be a heartwarming reunion with beloved characters, and for new readers, it's an exquisite introduction to the magic of Albertalli and Silvera's collaborative voice.

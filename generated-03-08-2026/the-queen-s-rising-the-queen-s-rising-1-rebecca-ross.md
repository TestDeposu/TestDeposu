# The Queen's Rising (The Queen’s Rising, #1)

**Author:** Rebecca Ross  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

In the realm of fantasy romance, Rebecca Ross's debut novel, The Queen's Rising, stands out as a masterful blend of magic, politics, and forbidden love. Set in the breathtaking world of Renaissance-inspired Valenia, this enchanting tale follows Brienna, a young woman with unfulfilled potential, as she navigates the treacherous landscape of courtiers, patrons, and revolutionaries.

Ross's writing is a visual feast, transporting readers to a world of grand estates, lavish balls, and ancient traditions. With vivid descriptions and a keen eye for detail, she brings Valenia to life, immersing readers in its intricate politics, passionate debates, and whispered conspiracies. The atmospheric setting is expertly woven into the narrative, creating a sense of foreboding and tension that underscores the central conflict.

Brienna, the protagonist, is a compelling and relatable heroine. Her struggles to find her passion and navigate the complex world of Magnalia House are deeply humanizing, making her eventual journey all the more compelling. As she becomes entangled with Cartier Évariste, a charismatic and enigmatic patron, her story takes on a depth and complexity that is characteristic of the best fantasy romance novels.

Ross's exploration of themes is both timely and timeless. The novel thoughtfully examines the tensions between tradition and progress, as the ruling class faces pressure to adapt and respond to the changing tides of power. Through Brienna's struggles and Cartier's machinations, Ross highlights the complexities of revolution, revealing the often-shaded nuances of rebellion and the true cost of freedom.

The romance between Brienna and Cartier is expertly paced, with a slow-burning tension that is both tantalizing and heart-wrenching. The author skillfully navigates the delicate balance between desire and duty, creating a forbidden love that is both intense and tender. Cartier, in particular, is a masterfully crafted character, whose charisma and vulnerability make him an endearing and believable love interest.

If there is a weakness to the novel, it lies in some of the secondary characters, who feel slightly underdeveloped and one-dimensional. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise masterful tale. Ross's writing is engaging, and her world-building is some of the most impressive in recent fantasy romance.

The Queen's Rising is a captivating and immersive debut novel that sets the stage for a thrilling series. With its lush setting, rich history, and complex characters, it is a must-read for fans of fantasy romance and historical fiction. Fans of Renée Ahdieh, Naomi Novik, and Leigh Bardugo will find much to love in this enchanting tale of magic, politics, and forbidden love.

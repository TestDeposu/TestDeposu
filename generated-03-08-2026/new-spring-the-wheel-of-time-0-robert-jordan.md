# New Spring (The Wheel of Time, #0)

**Author:** Robert Jordan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

New Spring, a prequel novel to the esteemed Wheel of Time series, is a masterful work of fantasy that showcases Robert Jordan's unparalleled world-building skills and captivating storytelling. The novel is set in a sprawling world where the forces of good and evil are in a constant struggle, and the fate of the world hangs in the balance. At the heart of the story is Moiraine Sedai, a powerful Aes Sedai, who embarks on a perilous journey to discover the Dragon Reborn, a prophesied figure destined to save the world from the clutches of the Dark One.

Jordan's writing style is characterized by his meticulous attention to detail, which is evident in the intricate world he has created. The Wheel of Time series is renowned for its complexity, with a vast array of characters, plot twists, and mythological references. New Spring is no exception, with Jordan skillfully weaving together multiple storylines, introducing new characters, and expanding on the existing lore. The author's use of language is descriptive and evocative, transporting readers to a richly imagined world of magic, politics, and adventure.

One of the most striking aspects of New Spring is its exploration of the Wheel of Time's core theme: the struggle between good and evil. Jordan presents a nuanced and multifaceted portrayal of this eternal conflict, highlighting the moral ambiguities and complexities that arise when individuals are faced with difficult choices. Moiraine, as the protagonist, is a compelling example of this, as she navigates the treacherous landscape of the world, confronting her own doubts and fears while remaining resolute in her determination to fulfill her destiny.

Throughout the novel, Jordan's characterization is superb, with each character possessing a unique voice, backstory, and motivation. The supporting cast, including al'Lan Mandragoran, Siuan Sanche, and the enigmatic Thom Merrilin, add depth and richness to the narrative, their interactions and relationships expertly crafted to propel the story forward. Jordan's ability to craft relatable and believable characters is a hallmark of his writing, making it easy for readers to become invested in their journeys and the fate of the world.

The pacing of New Spring is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and introspection that keeps the reader engaged. Jordan's mastery of plot progression is evident, as he expertly builds tension, resolves conflicts, and raises new questions, keeping the narrative fresh and exciting. The world-building, as expected, is exceptional, with Jordan's vision of a sprawling, epic landscape unfolding before the reader's eyes.

In conclusion, New Spring is an outstanding addition to the Wheel of Time series, offering a captivating narrative that explores the complexities of the world and its characters. Robert Jordan's writing style, marked by meticulous attention to detail, nuanced characterization, and masterful world-building, makes this novel a must-read for fans of fantasy and adventure. With its thought-provoking themes, engaging characters, and epic scope, New Spring is a testament to Jordan's skill as a storyteller, cementing his place as one of the most beloved and respected authors in the fantasy genre.

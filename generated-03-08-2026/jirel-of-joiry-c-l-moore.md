The narrative moves with a relentless rhythm, each sentence propelling the reader deeper into a subterranean nightmare where gods are fickle bargainers and kisses can seal both salvation and damnation. The horror is not gratuitous; it serves to highlight Jirel’s resolve, turning what could be a simple rescue mission into a meditation on sacrifice and the price of power.

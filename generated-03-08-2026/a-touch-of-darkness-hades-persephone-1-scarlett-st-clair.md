# A Touch of Darkness (Hades & Persephone, #1)

**Author:** Scarlett St. Clair  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

In the realm of modern retellings, few have managed to breathe such life into the ages-old mythology of Hades and Persephone as author Scarlett St. Clair does in her seductive, enchanting novel, A Touch of Darkness. This debut installment of the Hades & Persephone series takes readers back to ancient Greece, but not as they've ever known it before. St. Clair masterfully injects the divine with a sense of darkness and desire, weaving an intricate web of temptation, power, and ultimately, forbidden love.

From the opening lines, it becomes evident that Persephone, Goddess of Spring, is anything but a radiant embodiment of life. Shunned by the very world she's meant to nourish, she now finds solace in a mundane mortal existence as a journalist in the bustling city of New Athens. Her existence is that of a fragile flower about to wither under the scorching sun, devoid of any semblance of her divine heritage. The contrast of her title with her reality serves as a stark reminder of the fragility of even the most sacred.

Enter Hades, the enigmatic God of the Dead, who holds court over the shadowy Underworld with a calculated ease and a penchant for gaming. This is no god driven by petty morals or ethics; his game is that of the mortal world's most coveted prize – control and freedom. Persephone, with her unassuming demeanor, inadvertently piques his interest when the two engage in a high-stakes hand of cards, setting off a chain of events that will forever alter their lives.

What follows is a calculated dance of power and desire, with both players carefully laying their stakes. The lines between mortal and divine begin to blur as Hades offers Persephone a deal that seems impossible to refuse: create life in the bleak landscape of the Underworld, a feat once deemed impossible even for the gods themselves. As we delve deeper into the story, we witness the gradual degradation of Persephone's defenses, her resolve weakened by the intoxicating allure of the Underworld and its ruler.

St. Clair's masterful storytelling paints a darkly beautiful portrait of the Underworld, transforming this realm from a place of punishment to a land of unbridled sensuality and desire. It's a world where mortality has lost all meaning, and the line between life and death is blissfully blurred. Hades and Persephone's relationship becomes the very focal point of this captivating world, as the god's fascination with the goddess grows exponentially with each passing page. Their bond transcends mortal limits, defying the very fabric of their divine roles.

One cannot shake the feeling that A Touch of Darkness is less a retelling of an ancient myth and more an ode to the seductive power of obsession. Scarlett St. Clair masterfully weaves a web of dark, alluring magic, transporting readers to a realm where love, control, and the line between right and wrong become tantalizingly blurred. The writing itself is as fluid and seductive as Hades' own words, imbuing the narrative with an air of intoxicating mystery that's impossible to resist.

It is clear that Scarlett St. Clair has crafted something truly remarkable with A Touch of Darkness – a reinvigorated, modern take on a timeless myth, brimming with the same dark allure that has captivated readers for centuries. If the rest of the Hades & Persephone series continues along this trajectory, readers can expect an intoxicating ride that will keep them enthralled until the very end.

# Flutter (My Blood Approves, #3)

**Author:** Amanda Hocking  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

"Flutter," the third installment in Amanda Hocking's "My Blood Approves" series, is a captivating tale of love, danger, and the supernatural. This book, along with the rest of the series, has been repackaged in "The Complete Saga" ebook bundle, offering fans a comprehensive look into the world of vampires and romance that Hocking has so masterfully crafted.

At the heart of "Flutter" is the story of Alice Bonham, an ordinary teenager whose life takes a drastic turn when she becomes entangled in a world of blood and romance. Her relationship with vampires sets off a chain of events that will change her life forever. Through Alice's journey, Hocking explores the theme of forbidden love and the risks that come with it. The author's portrayal of the intense emotions and conflicts that arise from Alice's situation is both authentic and compelling.

One of the standout features of Hocking's writing style is her ability to create a sense of atmosphere and tension. Her descriptions of the supernatural world are rich and immersive, drawing the reader into a realm of magic and mystery. The pacing of the story is well-balanced, with moments of high action and suspense expertly woven together with quieter, more introspective scenes.

Hocking's characters are also noteworthy, with Alice being a particularly strong protagonist. Her relatable voice and inner struggles make her a character that readers can easily root for. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with complex motivations and backstories that add depth to the narrative.

The "My Blood Approves" series has been praised for its unique take on the vampire genre, and "Flutter" is no exception. Hocking's vampires are multidimensional and intriguing, with their own distinct culture and history. The world-building in this series is exceptional, with a level of detail that is both impressive and engaging.

The "Complete Saga" ebook bundle is a must-have for fans of the series, offering a wealth of bonus content, including a prequel novella, a new holiday story, and behind-the-scenes insights into the world of "My Blood Approves." This collection is a testament to Hocking's dedication to her craft and her commitment to delivering high-quality storytelling to her readers.

In conclusion, "Flutter" is a captivating and suspenseful tale that will appeal to fans of paranormal romance and fantasy. With its richly detailed world-building, complex characters, and engaging storyline, this book is a must-read for anyone looking to escape into a world of magic and adventure.

# Until I Die (Revenants, #2)

**Author:** Amy Plum  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In "Until I Die", the second installment of Amy Plum's Revenants series, the author masterfully weaves a captivating narrative that delves into the complexities of love, mortality, and the human condition. Set against the picturesque backdrop of Paris, the City of Love, Plum's story follows the tumultuous relationship between Vincent, a charming and enigmatic revenant, and Kate, the protagonist who finds herself entangled in a world of immortals.

One of the most striking aspects of Plum's writing style is her ability to craft a sense of atmosphere that is both eerie and enchanting. Her vivid descriptions of Paris, with its winding streets, charming cafes, and historic landmarks, serve as a poignant reminder of the beauty and fragility of human existence. This is juxtaposed with the dark and foreboding world of the revenants, where immortality is both a blessing and a curse.

At the heart of the story is the theme of love vs. mortality. Kate and Vincent's relationship is fraught with danger, as they navigate the treacherous landscape of immortal enemies and ancient prophecies. Plum skillfully explores the tension between the desire for eternal love and the inevitability of loss and separation. Through Kate's narrative, we experience the all-consuming passion and desperation that comes with loving someone who is, by definition, unattainable.

Plum's characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Kate's voice being particularly well-crafted. Her inner turmoil, as she struggles to reconcile her love for Vincent with the dangers that come with it, is deeply human and authentic. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with characters like Georgia and Mamie adding depth and nuance to the narrative.

The author's writing style is engaging and accessible, making "Until I Die" a compelling read for fans of paranormal romance and young adult fiction. Plum's use of language is evocative and expressive, with a keen sense of pacing that propels the reader through the story. The plot twists and turns, keeping the reader on the edge of their seat as they navigate the complex web of alliances and rivalries that define the world of the revenants.

In conclusion, "Until I Die" is a captivating and emotional rollercoaster of a novel that will appeal to fans of supernatural romance and drama. With its lush setting, memorable characters, and thought-provoking themes, this book is a must-read for anyone who has ever been in love or wondered about the nature of mortality.

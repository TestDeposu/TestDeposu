# Dragons of Summer Flame (Dragonlance: The Second Generation, #2)

**Author:** Margaret Weis  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

"Dragons of Summer Flame" by Margaret Weis is a captivating high fantasy novel that marks a new era in the Dragonlance universe. As the second installment of "The Second Generation" series, this book masterfully weaves together the descendants of Krynn's legendary warriors, the Heroes of the Lance, in a thrilling tale of magic, action, and adventure.

The story takes place in a world where the War of the Lance has ended, and the balance of light and dark is beginning to shift. The Dark Queen has found new champions in the Knights of Takhisis, including the dark paladin Steel Brightblade, who is determined to attack the high Clerist's Tower, a fortress his father died defending. Meanwhile, other descendants of the Companions embark on their own perilous journeys, each driven by their own motivations and desires.

One of the most striking aspects of Weis's writing style is her ability to create complex, multi-dimensional characters. Steel Brightblade, for example, is a nuanced and conflicted character, torn between his loyalty to the Knights of Takhisis and his own sense of morality. Similarly, Palin Majere, the young nephew of the archmage Raistlin, is a deeply emotional and relatable character, driven by his grief and determination to uncover the truth about his uncle's disappearance.

Weis's world-building is also noteworthy, as she skillfully expands the Dragonlance universe, introducing new characters, plotlines, and mythologies. The world of Krynn is richly detailed, with a deep history and politics that add depth and complexity to the story. The author's use of mythology and symbolism is also impressive, as she draws upon various mythological traditions to create a unique and captivating world.

The main theme of "Dragons of Summer Flame" is the struggle between light and darkness, as embodied by the conflicting forces of good and evil in the world of Krynn. Weis explores this theme through the characters and their relationships, highlighting the moral ambiguities and complexities of the world. The novel also touches on the idea of legacy and the weight of history, as the descendants of the Heroes of the Lance grapple with their own destinies and the expectations placed upon them.

Overall, "Dragons of Summer Flame" is a gripping and immersive fantasy novel that will appeal to fans of the Dragonlance series and newcomers alike. Weis's writing is engaging and accessible, making this book an excellent choice for readers looking for a compelling and epic fantasy adventure.

# Unhinged (Splintered, #2)

**Author:** A.G. Howard  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

**Unhinged: A Dark, Sensual Reimagining of Wonderland**

In A.G. Howard's sophomore effort, **Unhinged**, the author masterfully weaves together a mesmerizing tale of love, loyalty, and the blurred lines between reality and the fantastical world of Wonderland. As the sequel to **Splintered**, this novel expertly ramps up the stakes, delving deeper into the complexities of Alyssa Gardner's psyche and the consequences of her actions.

The story picks up where **Splintered** left off, with Alyssa, the Red Queen of the Red Court, struggling to navigate her dual lives: one in the mortal world, where she must face the mundane expectations of high school, and the other in the eerie, dreamlike realm of Wonderland, where she is embroiled in a treacherous game of power and manipulation. Her mother's recent release from an asylum only adds to the tension, as Alyssa's fragile grip on reality begins to slip.

Through Alyssa's journey, Howard explores the theme of identity, as our protagonist grapples with her heritage, her relationships, and the ever-changing landscape of Wonderland. Alyssa's relationships with her love interests, Jeb and Morpheus, are particularly well-crafted, adding depth and complexity to the narrative. Morpheus, in particular, is a captivating character, whose enigmatic nature and dubious motives make him both alluring and terrifying.

Howard's writing style in **Unhinged** is a masterclass in atmospheric storytelling. The author's use of sensory details is breathtaking, immersing the reader in the dark, sensual world of Wonderland. The descriptions of the fantastical creatures and landscapes are vivid and evocative, making it easy to visualize the world Howard has created.

One of the standout aspects of **Unhinged** is its ability to balance light and darkness, love and danger, and reality and fantasy. Howard's bold reimagining of Carroll's classic tale is both faithful to the original and refreshingly innovative. The novel's pacing is also noteworthy, hurtling through twists and turns with the momentum of a runaway carriage.

Throughout the book, Howard raises essential questions about the nature of power, free will, and the consequences of our actions. Alyssa's struggles to reclaim her agency and make choices that align with her own desires and values are deeply relatable, making her a compelling and sympathetic heroine.

In **Unhinged**, Howard has created a sequel that surpasses the original in many ways. The novel is a must-read for fans of fantasy, romance, and adventure, and will leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in the series.

**Rating:** 4.5/5 stars

**Recommendation:** If you're a fan of dark, sensual fantasy with a dash of romance and adventure, **Unhinged** is the perfect read for you. Be prepared for a wild ride through the looking glass, where the lines between reality and fantasy blur, and the stakes are higher than ever.

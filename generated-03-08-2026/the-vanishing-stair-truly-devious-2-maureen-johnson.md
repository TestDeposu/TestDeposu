# The Vanishing Stair (Truly Devious, #2)

**Author:** Maureen Johnson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

Maureen Johnson's latest installment in the Truly Devious series, The Vanishing Stair, is a masterful blend of mystery, self-discovery, and the complexities of human relationships. This sophomore effort is a gripping page-turner that not only deepens our understanding of the enigmatic Stevie Bell but also delves into the darker aspects of human nature.

At its core, The Vanishing Stair is a thought-provoking exploration of the blurred lines between genuine connection and manipulation. Stevie, the brilliant and fiercely independent teenage detective, finds herself entangled in a web of complicated relationships and conflicting emotions. As she navigates the treacherous landscape of her own feelings, she begins to question the very fabric of human connection. Is magnetism merely a form of manipulation, or is it a genuine expression of affection? Can we truly trust others, or are we all just pawns in a game of emotional chess?

Johnson's writing is, as always, razor-sharp and insightful. Her prose is infused with a keen understanding of the adolescent experience, capturing the angst, uncertainty, and self-doubt that often accompany this stage of life. Stevie's voice is authentic and relatable, her inner monologue a witty and engaging stream-of-consciousness that draws the reader into her world.

One of the standout aspects of The Vanishing Stair is its thoughtful examination of the cult mentality that can arise in even the most seemingly innocuous groups. Johnson's portrayal of the insidious ways in which charismatic leaders can manipulate and control their followers is both chilling and thought-provoking. This subplot adds a layer of complexity to the narrative, raising important questions about the nature of influence, persuasion, and the human desire for belonging.

Throughout the book, Johnson's signature blend of humor, wit, and clever plot twists keeps the reader on their toes. The mystery at the heart of the story is expertly crafted, with a complex web of clues and red herrings that will keep even the most seasoned detective fiction fans guessing.

If you're a fan of the Truly Devious series, you'll be delighted to find that The Vanishing Stair is just as engaging and addictive as its predecessor. Newcomers to the series will also find this book to be a compelling and standalone mystery that can be enjoyed without prior knowledge of the previous installment.

In short, The Vanishing Stair is a captivating and thought-provoking mystery that will appeal to fans of detective fiction, coming-of-age stories, and anyone looking for a compelling and engaging read. With its sharp writing, complex characters, and expertly crafted plot, this book is sure to leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in the series.

# Een schitterend gebrek

**Author:** Arthur Japin  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

"Een schitterend gebrek" by Arthur Japin is a poignant and evocative tale of love, loss, and redemption, set against the backdrop of 18th-century Europe. The novel masterfully weaves together the lives of two former lovers, Lucia and Casanova, whose chance encounter in an Amsterdam theater sets off a chain of events that exposes the complexities of their past.

Through Lucia's narrative, Japin skillfully reconstructs her life, from her idyllic childhood in the Veneto region to her tumultuous relationship with Casanova. Their whirlwind romance, marked by passion and intensity, is juxtaposed with the devastating illness that ravages Lucia's body and forces her to flee to Amsterdam. It is here that she assumes a new identity, hiding behind a veil of secrecy and anonymity.

Japin's prose is lyrical and expressive, conjuring the grandeur of Venice and the grittiness of Amsterdam's underbelly. His writing is akin to a rich tapestry, intricately woven with threads of love, sacrifice, and self-discovery. The author's use of language is deliberate and evocative, drawing the reader into the world of 18th-century Europe with ease.

At its core, "Een schitterend gebrek" is a novel about the transformative power of love. Lucia's decision to leave Casanova is not an act of betrayal, but a selfless act of devotion, driven by her desire to spare him the pain of watching her succumb to her illness. This revelation, slowly uncovered through Lucia's narrative, is a testament to the enduring nature of love and its ability to transcend time and circumstance.

Japin's portrayal of Casanova is equally nuanced, capturing the complexity of a man often reduced to a mere caricature of his legendary exploits. Here, he is a multidimensional figure, driven by both passion and vulnerability. The author's decision to keep Casanova in the dark about Lucia's true identity adds a layer of tension to the narrative, underscoring the fragility of human relationships.

Ultimately, "Een schitterend gebrek" is a novel about the human condition, with all its attendant frailties and strengths. Japin's masterful storytelling and vivid prose bring to life a world of beauty and ugliness, of love and loss. This is a novel that will linger in the reader's mind long after the final page has been turned, a testament to the enduring power of love and the human spirit.

# Of Bees and Mist

**Author:** Erick Setiawan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Of Bees and Mist, the debut novel by Erick Setiawan, is a mesmerizing tale of love, loss, and liberation that delves into the complexities of family dynamics and the secrets that bind them. Setiawan's richly atmospheric writing weaves a spellbinding narrative that transports readers to a world of mystique and wonder, where the lines between reality and fantasy are blurred.

At the heart of the story is Meridia, a young woman who grows up in a household shrouded in mystery and neglect. Her father's nightly disappearances into a blue mist, and her mother's obsessive behavior, leave Meridia feeling invisible and unloved. Her desire to escape this oppressive environment leads her to marry a kind-hearted young man, but she soon discovers that his family is harboring secrets of their own. The novel takes Meridia on a tumultuous journey of self-discovery, as she navigates the treacherous waters of her husband's family and uncovers long-buried secrets about her own past.

One of the most striking aspects of Setiawan's writing is his ability to craft a sense of atmosphere and mood. The novel is filled with vivid descriptions of setting and character, which transport the reader to a world of enchantment and mystery. The writing is lyrical and evocative, with a dreamlike quality that draws the reader in and refuses to let go. Setiawan's use of language is deliberate and precise, with each word and phrase carefully chosen to evoke a particular emotion or image.

The characters in the novel are equally well-crafted, with each one possessing a unique voice and perspective. Meridia is a complex and multifaceted protagonist, whose struggles to assert her independence and find her place in the world are deeply relatable. The supporting cast is equally well-drawn, from the enigmatic and menacing Eva, to the tragic and flawed figures of Meridia's parents. Each character is imbued with a sense of depth and nuance, which makes them feel fully realized and authentic.

The main theme of the novel is the destructive power of secrets and the liberating force of truth. Through Meridia's journey, Setiawan explores the ways in which secrets can both bind and destroy families, and the ways in which the truth can set us free. The novel is a powerful exploration of the human condition, and the ways in which we are all shaped by our experiences and relationships.

Overall, Of Bees and Mist is a stunning debut novel that showcases Setiawan's skill and craftsmanship as a writer. The novel is a must-read for fans of magical realism, family drama, and literary fiction. With its vivid prose, complex characters, and powerful themes, it is a book that will linger in the reader's mind long after the final page is turned.

# The Last Mrs. Parrish (Mrs. Parrish, #1)

**Author:** Liv Constantine  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

Greed, envy, and the dark underbelly of suburban perfection take center stage in Liv Constantine’s gripping 2017 debut psychological thriller, The Last Mrs. Parrish. Positioned as a modern-day, female-driven homage to Patricia Highsmith’s classic The Talented Mr. Ripley, this bestselling novel serves up a deliciously wicked exploration of identity theft, social climbing, and marital deception. 

At the heart of the narrative is Amber Patterson, a protagonist who embodies the ultimate weaponized invisibility. Sick of being a nobody who blends into the background, Amber casts off her unassuming demeanor to set her sights on the ultimate prize: the glamorous life of Daphne Parrish. In the affluent enclave of Bishops Harbor, Connecticut, Daphne and her real-estate mogul husband, Jackson, are the picture of a golden fairy-tale couple. Daphne is a beloved philanthropist and socialite, while Jackson is a powerful provider. To Amber, their wealth and status are not just admirable—they are stolen goods waiting to be reclaimed. 

Constantine’s central theme examines the dangerous allure of covetousness and the facades we construct to mask our true nature. Through Amber, the novel explores how far a deeply aggrieved and manipulative person will go to rewrite their reality. Amber weaponizes empathy itself, using Daphne’s kind-hearted nature as a Trojan horse to infiltrate the Parrish family. As she meticulously maneuvers from charitable acquaintance to closest confidante, traveling to Europe and growing dangerously close to Jackson, the novel brilliantly highlights the theme of trust as a currency traded by the naive and exploited by the predatory. 

The author’s stylistic approach is both calculated and propulsive. Written in distinct narrative shifts, the book grants readers front-row seats to Amber’s chillingly methodical internal monologue before flipping the script to reveal the hidden complexities of the Parrish household. Constantine employs a sharp, accessible prose style designed to keep the pages turning late into the night. She excels at creating an atmosphere of polished opulence that sharply contrasts with the rot festering beneath the surface. Bishops Harbor becomes a character in its own right—a gilded cage where status is everything and secrets are currency. 

While psychological thrillers often rely on slow-burn mysteries, Constantine opts for a high-tension cat-and-mouse dynamic driven by character obsession. The tension doesn't merely stem from whether Amber's plot will succeed, but from the looming threat of her own dark past catching up to her. This vulnerability injects a frantic energy into her otherwise flawless execution. 

Ultimately, The Last Mrs. Parrish is a fresh, juicy, and utterly addictive marriage thriller that delivers satisfying shocks and a deliciously dark revenge arc. Liv Constantine crafts a cautionary tale about the dangers of wanting what isn't yours, proving that sometimes the monsters aren't hiding in the dark—they are sipping champagne at your country club.

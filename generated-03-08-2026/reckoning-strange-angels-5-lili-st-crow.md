# Reckoning (Strange Angels, #5)

**Author:** Lili St. Crow  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**Reckoning by Lili St. Crow: A Gritty, Heart-Pounding Finale to the Strange Angels Series**

Lili St. Crow’s *Reckoning*—the electrifying fifth and final installment in the *Strange Angels* series—is a masterclass in dark fantasy, blending brutal action, morally complex characters, and a hauntingly atmospheric world that lingers long after the last page. This isn’t just a conclusion; it’s a reckoning in every sense of the word—a desperate, violent, and deeply personal battle where survival hinges on choices that will shatter even the toughest heroes.

At its core, *Reckoning* is a story about the cost of power, the weight of betrayal, and the thin line between monster and victim. Sixteen-year-old Dru Anderson, a svetocha (a supernatural being with the ability to manipulate electricity), has spent her life fighting against the undead, the wulfen (werewolf-like creatures), and the ever-present threat of the suckers (vampires). But power, as the series has relentlessly shown, is a double-edged sword. Dru’s strength has made her a target, and now, with the world closing in, she must confront the fact that even her allies may not be who they seem. The betrayal that unfolds is one of the most gut-wrenching moments in the series, forcing Dru to question everything she thought she knew about trust, loyalty, and what it truly means to be human—or something far more dangerous.

St. Crow’s writing is as sharp and visceral as it is immersive. The prose crackles with tension, each sentence a blade’s edge between hope and despair. The Appalachian setting, steeped in folklore and eerie isolation, serves as both a refuge and a trap, amplifying the claustrophobic dread as Dru and her ragtag crew—including the brooding Graves and the broken wulf Ash—are hunted by forces they can’t outrun. The action sequences are relentless, but they’re never gratuitous; every fight feels earned, every wound a consequence of the characters’ flaws. The supernatural elements—electricity as a weapon, the unnatural resilience of the wulfen, the predatory cunning of the suckers—are woven seamlessly into the narrative, never overshadowing the emotional core of the story.

What makes *Reckoning* truly unforgettable is its unflinching exploration of sacrifice. Dru’s journey isn’t just about survival; it’s about understanding that some battles can’t be won without losing something irreplaceable. The final act is a masterstroke, forcing readers to confront the idea that heroism isn’t about being invincible—it’s about knowing when to fight and when to let go. The climax is a storm of violence and heartbreak, leaving no room for easy resolutions. By the time the last page turns, you’re left breathless, not just from the adrenaline of the chase, but from the raw, unvarnished truth of what it means to be human in a world that rewards strength but punishes weakness.

St. Crow’s signature style—dark, lyrical, and brutally honest—shines here more than ever. The Strange Angels series has always been about defying expectations, and *Reckoning* delivers on that promise with a finale that’s as devastating as it is triumphant. It’s a story for readers who crave more than just escapism; who want a narrative that challenges them, haunts them, and leaves them questioning the nature of heroism long after the credits roll.

If you’ve followed Dru’s journey from the beginning, you’ll find this finale as emotionally charged as the first book. And if you’re new to the series, *Reckoning* is a perfect entry point—though be warned, it’s a wild, uncompromising ride. The Strange Angels world is one of the most original and compelling in contemporary fantasy, and Lili St. Crow’s final chapter is a testament to why it’s worth the blood, sweat, and tears it demands.

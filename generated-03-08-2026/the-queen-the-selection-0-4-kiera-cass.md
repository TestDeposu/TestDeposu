# The Queen (The Selection, #0.4)

**Author:** Kiera Cass  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

"The Queen" by Kiera Cass is a captivating prequel novella that delves into the backstory of Prince Maxon's parents, shedding light on the tumultuous world of Illéa before the events of the original series. Through the eyes of Amberly, an ordinary girl who captures the attention of the demanding Prince Clarkson, we are transported into a world of courtly intrigue, royal romance, and the complexities of the palace's inner workings.

One of the strengths of this novella lies in its ability to seamlessly weave in and out of the Selection series while offering a fresh perspective on the world of Illéa. Cass skillfully utilizes her signature style to create a sense of intimacy and immediacy, drawing the reader into the innermost thoughts of Amberly as she navigates the treacherous landscape of palace politics. Through Amberly's voice, we see the beauty of the Selection in all its grandeur, but also the dark undercurrents of manipulation and deceit that lurk beneath the surface.

The author's portrayal of Prince Clarkson is particularly striking, as he embodies the cold, calculating nature of the royal family. His temper is legendary, and through Amberly's eyes, we see the impact of his demanding nature on those around him. Meanwhile, Amberly herself is a relatable and endearing protagonist, whose struggles to balance her own desires with the harsh realities of palace life will resonate with readers.

Cass's writing is, as always, evocative and engaging, with a keen attention to detail that brings the world of Illéa to life. The pacing is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows effortlessly from tender moments of romance to scenes of high intrigue and drama. The supporting cast of characters is equally well-developed, adding depth and richness to the story as it unfolds.

What becomes clear throughout "The Queen" is that Cass has a masterful understanding of the inner workings of the royal family's complex dynamics. The queen's cruelty, Prince Clarkson's temper, and the simmering tensions between the members of the royal family all serve as a compelling backdrop to Amberly's romance with the prince. It's a testament to Cass's skill that she is able to make even the most minor characters feel fully realized, their motivations and personalities expertly woven into the tapestry of the story.

Ultimately, "The Queen" is a must-read for fans of the Selection series, offering a fresh and compelling perspective on the world of Illéa. Cass's writing shines throughout, delivering a story that is both captivating and emotionally resonant. For readers looking for a bite-sized tale of royal romance and courtly intrigue, look no further.

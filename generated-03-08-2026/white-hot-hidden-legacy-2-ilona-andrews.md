# White Hot (Hidden Legacy, #2)

**Author:** Ilona Andrews  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

**A Sizzling Sequel That Delivers: "White Hot" by Ilona Andrews**

Ilona Andrews' Hidden Legacy series has captivated readers worldwide with its unique blend of magic, mystery, and romance. The sequel to "On the Edge," "White Hot" is an electrifying ride that deepens our understanding of this captivating world and pushes the boundaries of Nevada Baylor's relationships, both personal and professional.

In "White Hot," Nevada Baylor is back, navigating a complex web of magic and politics with her trusty family business - a detective agency - as a foundation. Her "unique and secret skill" - the ability to discern when people are lying - is a crucial element in her investigations, but it's not enough to combat the formidable forces opposing her. As the stakes escalate, Nevada is drawn back to Connor "Mad" Rogan, the enigmatic billionaire Prime whose powers are as mesmerizing as his charm.

Ilona Andrews masterfully weaves a narrative that seamlessly integrates action, suspense, and humor, making "White Hot" an unputdownable read. One of the author's greatest strengths lies in her ability to create memorable characters, each with distinct personalities, motivations, and conflicts. Nevada and Rogan's tumultuous, passionate relationship is the emotional core of the book, and their chemistry is as undeniable as it is captivating.

Throughout the novel, the author tackles weighty themes like power, corruption, and loyalty, adding depth and nuance to the story. Nevada's relationships with her family and friends are a testament to the strength and resilience of her character, while Rogan's past and motivations serve as a reminder that true love is often born from understanding and acceptance.

The Hidden Legacy world is rich and immersive, with its magical system, social hierarchy, and politics adding a sense of realism to the fantasy framework. Ilona Andrews' writing is, as always, evocative and descriptive, drawing readers into this world with ease.

In "White Hot," Ilona Andrews delivers a sequel that outpaces its predecessor in terms of intensity, emotion, and thrills. With its addictive blend of magic, romance, and suspense, this book is sure to keep you on the edge of your seat, turning pages until the very end. Be prepared to witness the sizzling chemistry between Nevada and Rogan, and to discover the secrets that make this world so incredibly compelling.

**Rating: 4.5/5**

# Rhapsodic (The Bargainer, #1)

**Author:** Laura Thalassa  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

Dark fantasy romance has found a bewitching champion in Laura Thalassa, and her newly revised smash-hit novel, Rhapsodic, serves as a masterclass in atmospheric tension, high-stakes bargaining, and intoxicating chemistry. At its core, Thalassa explores the dangerous, seductive anatomy of debt—both literal and emotional—weaving a narrative where every promise carries a heavy, glittering price. 

The story introduces Callypso Lillis, a siren hiding in plain sight in the human world, whose wrist is shackled by a bracelet of black beads. These are not mere ornaments, but magical IOUs owed to the enigmatic and feared Bargainer, a mythical king of the Otherworld who always collects his due. When he finally comes to cash in his chips, Thalassa pivots the narrative from a clever urban fantasy into a high-stakes supernatural mystery. Fae warriors are vanishing, and innocent women are returning entombed in glass caskets with children clutched to their chests. To stop this dark threat, the Bargainer needs Callie, forcing the two into a reluctant alliance that blurs the lines between past betrayal and present passion.

Thalassa’s authorial style is defined by its unapologetic embrace of romantic tension and sharp, snappy dialogue. She writes with a cinematic flair, juxtaposing the gritty, modern reality of Callie's human life with the opulent, lethal mythology of the Fae Otherworld. Her prose is fast-paced, highly visual, and designed to keep the reader perpetually off-balance. The enemies-to-lovers dynamic is executed with precision; every interaction between Callie and the Bargainer crackles with unspoken history and palpable heat. Thalassa doesn't just build a world; she crafts an atmosphere thick with shadows, magic, and undeniable allure.

Ultimately, Rhapsodic is a thrilling invitation into a dangerous realm where love is a transaction and survival requires absolute surrender. Thalassa has engineered a deliciously dark romance that will captivate fans of fae mythology, sharp-witted heroines, and morally gray love interests who would burn the world down for the women they desire.

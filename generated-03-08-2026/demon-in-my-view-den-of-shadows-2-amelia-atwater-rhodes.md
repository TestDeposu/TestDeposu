# Demon in My View (Den of Shadows, #2)

**Author:** Amelia Atwater-Rhodes  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2000

In "Demon in My View," Amelia Atwater-Rhodes masterfully weaves a captivating tale that delves into the intricate world of adolescents struggling to find their place in society, and the blurred lines between reality and fiction. The story centers around Jessica, a teenager whose published vampire novel, "Tiger, Tiger," has garnered her both admiration and intimidation from her peers. As she navigates the complexities of high school life, Jessica finds herself inexplicably drawn to the enigmatic Alex, whose presence eerily mirrors that of Aubrey, the captivating villainess from her fictional world.

Atwater-Rhodes' writing style is nothing short of mesmerizing, effortlessly transporting readers to a realm where the lines between fantasy and reality are tantalizingly thin. Her descriptive prose conjures a world of darkness and mystery, where vampires, witches, and teenage insecurities converge. The author's unique voice, though evident even in her 15-year-old persona, demonstrates a maturity that belies her age.

Throughout the narrative, Atwater-Rhodes deftly explores the theme of identity, showcasing Jessica's struggles to reconcile her literary persona with her everyday life. The author skillfully captures the anxieties and insecurities peculiar to adolescence, often rendering the reader sympathetic towards Jessica's plight. At the same time, Jessica's affinity for vampires and her entangled world of fiction provides a fascinating contrast, highlighting the tension between fantasy and reality that Atwater-Rhodes so effectively leverages.

One of the most striking aspects of the novel is its ability to create a sense of foreboding and unease. Atwater-Rhodes' use of descriptive language creates a dark, eerie atmosphere that leaves the reader wondering what lies ahead. The suspense is expertly crafted, keeping the reader invested in the story and eager to uncover the secrets that Atwater-Rhodes so cleverly conceals.

While some readers may find certain elements of the narrative to be predictable, they are skillfully overshadowed by the richly developed characters and a world that is both captivating and unsettling. Atwater-Rhodes' portrayal of the supernatural and fantasy realms is both imaginative and alluring, demonstrating a keen understanding of the complexities of the human psyche.

In conclusion, "Demon in My View" is a haunting and captivating tale that skillfully navigates the realms of the real and the fictional, leaving the reader breathless and eager for more. Amelia Atwater-Rhodes' writing is a testament to her burgeoning talent, and this novel serves as an excellent follow-up to her equally impressive "In the Forests of the Night." For fans of the supernatural and young adult literature, "Demon in My View" is an absolute must-read.

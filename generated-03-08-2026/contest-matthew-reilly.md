# Contest

**Author:** Matthew Reilly  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In "Contest," Matthew Reilly crafts a heart-pounding thriller that transforms the revered New York Public Library into a treacherous game board, where the stakes are life and death. This gripping novel is a testament to Reilly's mastery of the action-thriller genre, showcasing his unique ability to craft a narrative that is both relentless and captivating.

At the center of the story is Doctor Stephen Swain, a man who finds himself in a desperate fight for survival alongside his eight-year-old daughter, Holly. The setup is simple yet chilling: seven contestants must navigate the labyrinthine library, with only one emerging victorious. The rules are straightforward, but the odds are stacked against Swain, and he must rely on his wits and resourcefulness to stay alive.

Reilly's writing style is characterized by its breakneck pace, with a narrative that hurtles forward at an unrelenting speed. His use of short, punchy sentences creates a sense of urgency, mirroring the frantic pace of the contestants as they scramble to stay ahead of their opponents. The author's vivid descriptions of the library's imposing architecture and narrow aisles effectively amplify the sense of claustrophobia and tension, making it easy for readers to become fully immersed in the world of the story.

One of the standout aspects of "Contest" is its exploration of the theme of survival. Reilly poses a fundamental question: what would you do to protect the people you love? Swain's relationship with his daughter is authentic and heartfelt, making his determination to keep her safe all the more compelling. As the story unfolds, the author expertly ratchets up the tension, forcing Swain to confront his deepest fears and make impossible choices.

Reilly's cast of characters is diverse and intriguing, with each contestant bringing their unique skills and motivations to the table. The author's ability to craft complex, multidimensional characters is impressive, particularly given the rapid-fire pace of the narrative.

While "Contest" is undoubtedly a thriller, it also touches on the theme of knowledge and the power it holds. The New York Public Library, with its vast repository of books and knowledge, serves as a symbol of the transformative power of learning. Reilly's use of this iconic setting adds depth and complexity to the narrative, elevating the story beyond a simple game of survival.

In conclusion, "Contest" is a gripping, action-packed thriller that showcases Matthew Reilly's skill as a masterful storyteller. With its breakneck pace, vivid descriptions, and exploration of the human condition, this novel is sure to captivate fans of the genre. If you're looking for a story that will keep you on the edge of your seat, look no further than "Contest."

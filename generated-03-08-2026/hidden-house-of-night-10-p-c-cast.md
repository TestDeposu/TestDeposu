# Hidden (House of Night, #10)

**Author:** P.C. Cast  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

"Hidden," the tenth installment in P.C. Cast's "House of Night" series, is a captivating tale of self-discovery, friendship, and the eternal struggle between good and evil. As Zoey Redbird navigates the complex world of vampyres, she must confront her own destiny and the darkness that threatens to consume her.

Cast's writing style is as engaging as ever, weaving a rich tapestry of characters, plot twists, and mythological references that will keep readers enthralled. The author's use of first-person narration allows for an intimate glimpse into Zoey's thoughts and feelings, making her a relatable and endearing protagonist.

One of the primary themes of "Hidden" is the concept of identity and how it is shaped by our experiences, relationships, and choices. Zoey, as a fledgling vampyre, is still grappling with her newfound powers and her place within the House of Night. As she faces various challenges and adversaries, she must also confront her own sense of self and what it means to be a vampyre.

The author's exploration of this theme is both nuanced and thought-provoking, raising questions about the nature of identity and how it is influenced by external factors. Through Zoey's journey, Cast highlights the importance of self-awareness, acceptance, and the power of choice in shaping one's destiny.

The world-building in "Hidden" is also noteworthy, with Cast expanding on the mythology of the vampyres and their society. The House of Night, with its intricate hierarchy and complex relationships, is a richly detailed setting that adds depth and complexity to the narrative.

In terms of character development, Zoey continues to evolve as a protagonist, facing new challenges and learning valuable lessons about herself and those around her. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with characters like Stark, Aphrodite, and Neferet adding depth and complexity to the story.

If you're a fan of the "House of Night" series, "Hidden" is a must-read. Cast's writing is as engaging as ever, and the story is full of unexpected twists and turns that will keep you on the edge of your seat. Even if you're new to the series, "Hidden" is a great starting point, with enough background information to bring you up to speed without feeling overwhelmed.

In conclusion, "Hidden" is a captivating tale of self-discovery, friendship, and the eternal struggle between good and evil. With its richly detailed world-building, engaging characters, and thought-provoking themes, it's a must-read for fans of the "House of Night" series and anyone looking for a compelling young adult fantasy novel.

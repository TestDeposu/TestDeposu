# Spell of the Highlander (Highlander, #7)

**Author:** Karen Marie Moning  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2005

Spell of the Highlander, the seventh installment in Karen Marie Moning's Highlander series, is a masterfully woven tale of ancient mystique and modern passion. This captivating novel whisks readers away on a journey through the ages, bridging the gap between the ninth century and the present day.

At the heart of the story lies the enigmatic Cian MacKeltar, a Celtic warrior with a mysterious past and a penchant for seduction. Trapped within the Dark Glass, one of the four coveted Unseelie Hallows, Cian has been imprisoned for eleven centuries, waiting for the perfect moment to re-emerge. That moment arrives when he meets Jessi St. James, a brilliant and beautiful archaeology student who unwittingly frees him from his glass prison.

As Cian navigates the unfamiliar world of the twenty-first century, he finds himself inexplicably drawn to Jessi, who is both fascinated and intimidated by the powerful Highlander. Their relationship is a complex dance of desire and danger, with Cian's ancient enemy hot on their heels, seeking to reclaim the Dark Glass and exploit its immense power.

Moning's writing is a true masterpiece, weaving together elements of history, mythology, and romance to create a narrative that is both captivating and immersive. Her use of language is evocative and sensual, conjuring vivid images of the rugged Scottish Highlands and the mystical world of the Druids.

One of the most striking aspects of Spell of the Highlander is its exploration of the human condition. Through Cian and Jessi's story, Moning delves into the complexities of love, trust, and redemption, raising questions about the nature of power and the consequences of playing with forces beyond our control.

As the story unfolds, Moning expertly ratchets up the tension, hurtling Cian and Jessi towards a climactic showdown with their enemies. Along the way, they must navigate treacherous landscapes, both physical and emotional, confronting their own demons and desires.

Throughout the novel, Moning's characters shine with a depth and nuance that is all too rare in paranormal romance. Cian, in particular, is a compelling figure, his rough exterior hiding a complex web of emotions and motivations. Jessi, too, is a strong and capable heroine, whose intelligence and determination make her a perfect match for the powerful Highlander.

In conclusion, Spell of the Highlander is a must-read for fans of paranormal romance and historical fiction. With its lush prose, intricate plotting, and memorable characters, this novel is sure to captivate readers and leave them eagerly anticipating the next installment in the series.

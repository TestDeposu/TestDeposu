# Follow You Home

**Author:** Mark Edwards  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

Digital Footprints Can Be Fatal: A Critique of Mark Edwards’ *Follow You Home*

In the modern age, horror no longer hides solely in dark alleys or abandoned houses; it lurks behind glowing screens, curated profiles, and algorithm-driven matches. In his unsettling 2013 psychological thriller *Follow You Home*, author Mark Edwards taps directly into our collective digital anxiety, crafting a narrative where the swipe right can genuinely lead to a dead end. 

The premise is deceptively grounded yet chillingly plausible. When Amy receives a cryptic, out-of-character goodbye email from her globetrotting sister, Becky, alarm bells ring. Becky would never vanish on a whim—not without a trace. As Amy dives into her sister’s life to uncover the truth, she discovers a hidden digital footprint rooted in the shadowy, seductive world of online dating. Aided by Becky’s concerned neighbor, Gary, Amy begins retracing her sister's virtual footsteps, unaware that an invisible predator is monitoring her every move. The hunter has become the hunted, and the internet is his playground.

Edwards, known for his razor-sharp pacing and knack for relatable terror, excels at exploring the central theme of modern vulnerability. *Follow You Home* investigates the terrifying disconnect between the curated personas we project online and the monstrous realities that can hide behind them. By tethering the plot to contemporary anxieties—online dating culture, digital stalking, and the constant erosion of personal privacy—Edwards transforms a standard missing-person mystery into a ripped-from-the-headlines nightmare. The book asks a deeply uncomfortable question: How well do you really know the person you met online?

The author's stylistic approach is defined by relentless momentum and psychological claustrophobia. Edwards utilizes short, punchy chapters and escalating stakes that mirror the suffocating paranoia of his protagonists. The tension does not merely simmer; it boils over, pulling the reader through a maze of false leads, dark secrets, and heart-pounding revelations. Amy is not a polished, indestructible action hero; she is a deeply human protagonist recovering from the trauma of an abusive past, making her vulnerability feel raw and authentic. Her struggle against an invisible tormentor resonates on a visceral level, elevating the novel above typical genre fiction through sheer emotional resonance.

While comparisons to contemporary suspense masters are inevitable, Edwards carves out his own distinct niche by focusing on the mundane, everyday technologies that dominate our lives, turning them into instruments of dread. The antagonist operates with a chilling, calculated efficiency that turns social media and messaging platforms into weapons of psychological torture. 

Ultimately, *Follow You Home* is a masterclass in modern suspense—a fast-paced, unputdownable rollercoaster that lingers in the back of your mind long after the final page is turned. It is a cautionary tale for the digital generation, proving that sometimes, the scariest thing you can do is click send.

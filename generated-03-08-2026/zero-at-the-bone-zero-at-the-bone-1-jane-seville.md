# Zero at the Bone (Zero at the Bone #1)

**Author:** Jane Seville  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

In "Zero at the Bone," Jane Seville masterfully weaves a gripping narrative that defies the conventions of traditional romance and thriller genres. This 2014 novel is a riveting tale of unlikely alliances, intense passion, and the resilience of the human spirit.

At its core, "Zero at the Bone" is a story about the transformative power of human connection. When Dr. Jack Francisco, a talented surgeon, is thrust into the witness protection program after witnessing a Mafia murder, his life is forever altered. Enter D, a ruthless and skilled hitman, who is coerced into tracking down Jack with the intention of silencing him permanently. However, as D navigates the complexities of his mission, he begins to experience a crisis of conscience, ultimately deciding to spare Jack's life and forge an unlikely alliance with him.

As they embark on a perilous journey, evading mysterious enemies and navigating the treacherous landscape of their own emotions, Jack and D develop a profound bond. This connection is expertly crafted by Seville, who skillfully explores the vulnerabilities and depths of her characters. Jack, with his compassionate and empathetic nature, sees beyond D's tough exterior, uncovering the wounded soul beneath. Conversely, D finds solace in Jack's unwavering optimism and kindness, which awakens a long-dormant sense of hope within him.

Seville's writing style is characterized by its concise and evocative prose, which effectively conveys the intensity and urgency of the narrative. Her use of language is economical, yet potent, allowing the reader to become fully immersed in the world she has created. The pacing is relentless, with a keen sense of tension that propels the story forward.

One of the most striking aspects of "Zero at the Bone" is its thoughtful exploration of the human condition. Seville raises important questions about the nature of identity, morality, and redemption, inviting the reader to ponder the complexities of D's character, in particular. As a hitman, D is a product of his environment, shaped by the brutal realities of his profession. Yet, as the narrative progresses, it becomes clear that he is more than just a ruthless killer; he is a multidimensional being, capable of growth, love, and compassion.

The romance that blossoms between Jack and D is a beautiful, slow-burning flame that adds depth and nuance to the narrative. It is a testament to Seville's skill as a writer that she is able to balance the intense action and suspense with a tender, emotional love story.

In conclusion, "Zero at the Bone" is a gripping, emotionally charged novel that will appeal to fans of romance, thrillers, and suspense. Jane Seville's masterful storytelling, coupled with her well-developed characters and thought-provoking themes, make for a compelling read that will linger long after the final page is turned.

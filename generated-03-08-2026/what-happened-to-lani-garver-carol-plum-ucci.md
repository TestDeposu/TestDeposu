# What Happened to Lani Garver

**Author:** Carol Plum-Ucci  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2002

What Happened to Lani Garver, penned by Carol Plum-Ucci in 2002, is a mesmerizing and poignant novel that delves into the complexities of human identity, the perils of high school social hierarchies, and the transcendental possibility of angels walking among us. This captivating tale is set on the picturesque Hackett Island, where the enigmatic Lani Garver arrives, shrouded in mystery, and sets off a chain reaction of events that will forever alter the lives of the island's residents.

At the heart of the story lies Claire McKenzie, a popular high school student who defies the norms of her elite clique by befriending the intriguing outcast, Lani. As their bond deepens, Claire finds herself entangled in a web of questions surrounding Lani's identity, age, and even gender. This uncertainty serves as a catalyst for Claire's introspection, prompting her to reevaluate her own life, friendships, and values.

Plum-Ucci's masterful storytelling weaves a rich tapestry of themes, expertly exploring the intricacies of adolescent identity, the consequences of bullying, and the redemptive power of empathy and compassion. The author's writing style is both lyrical and evocative, with a keen eye for detail that brings the characters and their surroundings to vivid life.

One of the novel's most striking aspects is its exploration of the fluidity of human identity. Lani's androgynous appearance and ambiguous past serve as a mirror, reflecting the insecurities and uncertainties of those around them. This thought-provoking narrative thread challenges the reader to confront their own biases and assumptions, inviting a deeper understanding of the complexities that make us human.

The author's use of magical realism adds a captivating layer of depth to the story, as Claire begins to suspect that Lani may be an angel, sent to earth to impart a profound message. This element of the narrative serves as a powerful metaphor for the transformative impact that one person can have on others, and the ways in which our lives can be forever altered by the presence of someone extraordinary.

Throughout the book, Plum-Ucci's prose is both elegant and unflinching, tackling difficult subjects with sensitivity and nuance. The characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Claire's narrative voice ringing true as she navigates the treacherous waters of adolescence.

In conclusion, What Happened to Lani Garver is a haunting and unforgettable novel that will resonate with readers long after the final page is turned. Carol Plum-Ucci's masterful storytelling, combined with her thought-provoking exploration of human identity and the possibility of angels among us, makes for a truly unforgettable reading experience.

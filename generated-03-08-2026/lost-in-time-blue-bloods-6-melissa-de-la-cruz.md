# Lost in Time (Blue Bloods, #6)

**Author:** Melissa de la Cruz  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In "Lost in Time," Melissa de la Cruz masterfully weaves a mesmerizing tale that defies time and space, plunging the reader into a world of forbidden secrets and blood-soaked betrayals. As Schuyler Van Alen and Jack Force navigate the treacherous landscape of ancient Egypt, the stakes have never been higher. De la Cruz's signature style is on full display, as she effortlessly blends rich history with supernatural intrigue, creating a narrative that is equal parts eerie, enchanting, and unforgettable.

At its core, "Lost in Time" is a story about the power of love, loyalty, and self-discovery. As Schuyler and Jack face the ultimate test of their bond, they are forced to confront the darkest corners of their own hearts. De la Cruz's character development is superb, with Schuyler emerging as a complex and multi-faceted protagonist. Her struggles to balance her desires with the expectations of her family and society are both poignant and relatable.

One of the standout features of "Lost in Time" is its unique blend of historical and supernatural elements. De la Cruz's use of ancient Egyptian mythology adds a layer of depth and richness to the story, drawing readers into a world of hidden temples, cursed pharaohs, and bloodthirsty demons. The author's writing is lush and evocative, conjuring the sensory details of ancient Egypt with uncanny precision. Whether describing the scorching desert sun or the cool, damp darkness of a pharaoh's tomb, de la Cruz's prose is sure to transport readers to a world of wonder and awe.

If there is one area where "Lost in Time" falters, it is in its pacing. At times, the narrative feels overstuffed, with multiple plot threads and characters competing for attention. While this can make the story feel a bit disjointed, it also adds to the book's sense of chaos and urgency. As Schuyler and Jack navigate the treacherous landscape of ancient Egypt, the reader is swept up in a maelstrom of twists and turns.

Overall, "Lost in Time" is a gripping and unputdownable read that will leave fans of Melissa de la Cruz's Blue Bloods series breathless. With its unique blend of history and supernatural intrigue, this book is sure to captivate readers who enjoy stories of love, loyalty, and self-discovery. If you're looking for a story that will keep you on the edge of your seat, look no further than "Lost in Time."

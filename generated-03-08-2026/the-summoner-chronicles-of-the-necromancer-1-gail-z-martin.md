# The Summoner (Chronicles of the Necromancer, #1)

**Author:** Gail Z. Martin  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In "The Summoner", Gail Z. Martin weaves a captivating tale of magic, power, and self-discovery, drawing readers into the immersive world of the Chronicles of the Necromancer series. This epic fantasy adventure is a masterful blend of action, suspense, and emotional depth, as the author skillfully explores the complexities of a young prince's journey to reclaim his rightful throne.

At the heart of the story lies Martris Drayke, a compelling protagonist whose comfortable life is shattered when his family is brutally murdered by his half-brother Jared and his dark mage. Forced to flee for his life, Tris embarks on a perilous quest to avenge his family and reclaim the throne of Margolan. Along the way, he is joined by a loyal band of friends, each with their own unique skills and motivations, adding depth and richness to the narrative.

One of the standout features of Martin's writing style is her ability to craft a richly detailed world, teeming with magical creatures, spirits, and ancient lore. The author's use of language is vivid and evocative, transporting readers to a realm where the living and the dead coexist, and the boundaries between reality and the supernatural are blurred.

As Tris navigates this treacherous landscape, he must also confront his own destiny as a Summoner, a mage with the power to communicate with the dead. This inherited gift from his grandmother, Bava K'aa, is both a blessing and a curse, threatening to consume him unless he learns to master it. Martin skillfully explores the tensions between Tris's desire for revenge and his need to control his wild magic, creating a sense of urgency and tension that propels the story forward.

Throughout the book, Martin's characters are multidimensional and relatable, with each one bringing their own distinct voice and perspective to the narrative. The relationships between Tris and his friends are particularly well-developed, as they face numerous challenges and obstacles on their journey. The author's portrayal of found family and the bonds of loyalty and friendship is especially noteworthy, adding an emotional depth to the story that makes it hard to put down.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the pacing of the book can be somewhat uneven at times, with certain sections feeling slightly rushed or convenient. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise engaging and immersive narrative.

In conclusion, "The Summoner" is a gripping and immersive epic fantasy adventure that will appeal to fans of magic, politics, and self-discovery. Gail Z. Martin's writing style is engaging and evocative, with a richly detailed world and complex characters that will keep readers invested in the story until the very end. With its themes of power, loyalty, and redemption, this book is a compelling start to the Chronicles of the Necromancer series.

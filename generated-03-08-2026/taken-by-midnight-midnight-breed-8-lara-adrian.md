# Taken by Midnight (Midnight Breed, #8)

**Author:** Lara Adrian  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In "Taken by Midnight," Lara Adrian masterfully weaves a captivating narrative that delves into the darker aspects of desire, mortality, and the unrelenting power of love. The eighth installment in the Midnight Breed series, this book is a testament to Adrian's skill in crafting a world where vampires and humans coexist in a delicate balance of power and vulnerability.

At the heart of the story is Jenna Darrow, a former state trooper who finds herself at the crossroads of life and death after a brutal attack in the Alaskan wilderness. As she struggles to come to terms with the strange changes taking place within her, Jenna is drawn to the Boston compound of the Order, a secretive organization of vampire warriors sworn to protect humanity from the forces of evil.

It is here that she meets Brock, a brooding and enigmatic alpha male whose presence is as commanding as it is mysterious. As Jenna recovers under Brock's care, their mutual attraction grows, fueled by a desire that threatens to consume them both. Adrian's portrayal of their relationship is nothing short of masterful, as she expertly navigates the complexities of a forbidden love that defies the boundaries of species and mortality.

One of the most striking aspects of "Taken by Midnight" is Adrian's ability to balance action, suspense, and romance in a narrative that is both fast-paced and emotionally resonant. Her writing is characterized by a keen sense of atmosphere and setting, transporting the reader to the frozen landscapes of Alaska and the dark, gothic world of the Order's compound.

Through Jenna and Brock's story, Adrian explores the themes of identity, loyalty, and the transformative power of love. As Jenna struggles to reconcile her past and present selves, she must also confront the darkness within her own heart and the secrets that Brock has kept hidden for so long. The result is a narrative that is both deeply personal and epic in scope, as the fate of humanity hangs in the balance.

Adrian's style is unmistakable, blending elements of paranormal romance, urban fantasy, and horror to create a unique and captivating reading experience. Her characters are multidimensional and complex, driven by motivations that are both flawed and relatable. The world-building is meticulous and detailed, drawing the reader into a richly textured universe that is both dark and alluring.

In short, "Taken by Midnight" is a gripping and unforgettable read that will appeal to fans of paranormal romance and urban fantasy alike. With its breakneck pacing, memorable characters, and epic scope, this book is a must-read for anyone looking to escape into a world of darkness and desire.

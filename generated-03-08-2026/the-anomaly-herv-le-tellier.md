# The Anomaly

**Author:** Hervé Le Tellier  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

"The Anomaly" by Hervé Le Tellier is a dizzying, whip-smart novel that delves into the mysteries surrounding a Paris-New York flight, where the passengers' lives are forever altered after hitting a violent patch of turbulence. This bestselling book, winner of the Goncourt Prize, masterfully blends crime, fantasy, sci-fi, and thriller elements, creating a unique reading experience that will leave you questioning the very fabric of reality.

At its core, "The Anomaly" explores the concept of choice and its consequences. The passengers of Air France 006, each living double lives, are forced to confront the what-ifs of their decisions. The novel takes us on a journey to multiple destinations, from Lagos and Mumbai to the White House and a top-secret hangar, as the characters navigate their new reality. Le Tellier's ambitious work is an ingenious, timely variation on the doppelgänger theme, tapping into the parts of ourselves that elude us most.

Le Tellier's writing style is reminiscent of a bingeable Netflix series, making high literature accessible and engaging. He playfully critiques the hallmarks of genre fiction, drawing on the best of "chick lit" to mystery. The result is a novel that is both thought-provoking and entertaining, perfect for readers who enjoy complex, layered storytelling.

One of the standout features of "The Anomaly" is its diverse cast of characters. From Blake, the contract killer with a respectable family life, to Slimboy, the Nigerian pop star hiding his true self, each passenger's story is expertly woven into the narrative. Le Tellier's characterization is superb, making it easy to become invested in the lives of these complex, flawed individuals.

The novel's exploration of identity, choice, and consequence is both timely and thought-provoking. In an era where social media often presents a curated version of reality, "The Anomaly" challenges readers to consider the what-ifs of their own lives. What would we be if we had made different choices? Who would we be if we had taken a different path?

In conclusion, "The Anomaly" is a gripping, thought-provoking novel that will appeal to fans of complex, layered storytelling. Le Tellier's unique blend of genres, coupled with his masterful characterization and exploration of the human condition, makes for a compelling read. If you're looking for a book that will challenge your perceptions and keep you on the edge of your seat, "The Anomaly" is an absolute must-read.

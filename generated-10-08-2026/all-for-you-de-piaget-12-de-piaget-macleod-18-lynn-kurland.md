# All For You (De Piaget, #12; de Piaget/MacLeod, #18)

**Author:** Lynn Kurland  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Time, space, and a generous sprinkling of medieval magic are no match for the enduring power of true love in Lynn Kurland’s captivating compilation, All For You. Bringing together two of the New York Times bestselling author's most beloved paranormal romances—Another Chance to Dream and If I Had You—this volume acts as both a treasure trove for longtime devotees and a spellbinding gateway for newcomers to the expansive de Piaget and MacLeod dynasties. Kurland has long carved out a distinct niche in the romance genre, seamlessly blending historical chivalry with otherworldly intervention, and this duology highlights her signature storytelling strengths at their absolute finest.

At the heart of both tales lies Kurland’s most enduring thematic preoccupation: love as a transcendent force capable of bridging the chasms of centuries, duty, and mortal danger. In Another Chance to Dream, readers are swept into a poignant narrative featuring Rhys, a stalwart knight whose lack of land and title seems to permanently bar him from the hand of Gwennelyn of Segrave. Kurland expertly captures the agonizing ache of unrequited longing and societal constraints, anchoring the emotional stakes in genuine vulnerability. When Gwen faces a dreaded betrothed, the narrative shifts into a high-stakes race against fate, driven by a surprise offer that dangles the possibility of a second chance just out of reach. 

Meanwhile, If I Had You shifts the temporal gears slightly while retaining the intoxicating atmosphere of medieval courtly intrigue and magic. Here, Robin de Piaget returns to Artane and encounters Anne of Fenwyck, a bewitching woman who completely disarms him. Kurland excels at writing dynamic, slow-burn courtships where witty banter and lingering glances carry as much weight as the overarching perils. As treachery rears its head to threaten their fragile happiness, Anne and Robin must navigate a labyrinth of past grievances and present dangers, realizing that their bond is meant to weather eternity. 

Kurland’s authorial style is immediately recognizable by its lush, atmospheric prose and gentle pacing. Unlike the breakneck speed of modern contemporary romances, Kurland favors a deliberate, almost poetic accumulation of moments. She paints her medieval landscapes with vivid strokes of authenticity, imbuing stone keeps, verdant forests, and mist-shrouded Scottish highlands with a palpable sense of history. Yet, it is her deft handling of the paranormal elements that truly sets her apart. The magic in her universes never feels gimmicky or jarring; rather, it operates as a natural extension of a world where emotional bonds are so strong they can literally bend the rules of time and mortality. 

Furthermore, Kurland’s characters are the beating heart of her success. Her heroes are cut from the classic chivalric cloth—loyal, fiercely protective, and deeply honorable—yet they possess a refreshing emotional intelligence and tenderness. Her heroines are equally compelling, balancing medieval resilience with a modern sense of agency. They do not merely wait to be rescued; they actively participate in shaping their destinies, fighting alongside their partners with quiet strength and sharp intellect.

All For You is a masterclass in comforting, immersive romance. It offers readers a lush escape into a world where honor still dictates men's actions, where destiny can be rewritten by sheer devotion, and where happily-ever-afters are not just hoped for, but magically guaranteed. Kurland proves once again why she remains a titan of historical paranormal romance, delivering a collection that will leave readers breathless, swooning, and thoroughly enchanted.

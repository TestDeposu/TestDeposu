# Sightblinder's Story (Lost Swords, #2)

**Author:** Fred Saberhagen  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**Unveiling the Shadows within: A Review of Sightblinder's Story by Fred Saberhagen**

In the realm of epic fantasy, few authors have delved as profoundly into the human psyche as Fred Saberhagen in his Lost Swords series. Sightblinder's Story, the second installment, masterfully explores the labyrinthine corridors of the human heart, exposing the deepest fears and desires that govern our actions. This engrossing tale weaves a rich tapestry of myth and adventure, inviting readers to confront the darkness within.

The narrative centers around Sightblinder, one of the Twelve Swords of Power, forged by the gods to manipulate the fabric of reality. This enigmatic sword, capable of revealing the most intimate secrets of the viewer, falls into the hands of Arnfinn, an unassuming country boy blissfully unaware of its true potential. When Ben of Purkinje, a seasoned warrior, sets out to rescue Prince Mark, now a captive of the eldritch Ancient One, Sightblinder becomes an indispensable tool in his quest.

Saberhagen's prose is as captivating as it is evocative, plunging readers into a world of high stakes and ancient mysteries. His writing is akin to a slow-burning fire, gradually intensifying as the narrative builds toward a shattering crescendo. The author's greatest strength lies in his understanding of human nature, laying bare the frailties and contradictions that govern our behavior. Through Sightblinder's power to reveal our deepest desires and fears, Saberhagen exposes the dark underbelly of human existence, forcing us to confront the shadows we would rather ignore.

The characterizations in Sightblinder's Story are equally well-crafted, with Ben of Purkinje standing out as a complex and multifaceted protagonist. His unwavering dedication to Prince Mark and his unshakeable resolve in the face of unimaginable danger make him a compelling figure, worthy of our admiration. The supporting cast, while not as thoroughly developed, serves to enhance the narrative's complexity and depth.

Where Saberhagen truly shines is in his world-building, weaving a richly detailed tapestry of mythology and history. The Lost Swords series, with its roots in mythology and folklore, provides a fascinating glimpse into a world shaped by the gods' creation of the Twelve Swords of Power. This masterful construction of a fantasy world, coupled with the author's exploration of human psychology, elevates Sightblinder's Story to a must-read for fans of epic fantasy.

Ultimately, Sightblinder's Story is a thought-provoking and engrossing tale that will appeal to readers seeking more than just a straightforward quest narrative. Saberhagen's masterful exploration of the human condition, paired with his richly detailed world-building, has created a compelling and unforgettable reading experience.

**Rating:** 4.5/5 stars

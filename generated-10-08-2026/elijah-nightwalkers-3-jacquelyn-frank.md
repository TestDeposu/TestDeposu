# Elijah (Nightwalkers, #3)

**Author:** Jacquelyn Frank  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

Steamy, high-stakes, and irresistibly primal, Jacquelyn Frank’s Elijah roars back into the shadowy, interconnected world of the Nightwalkers with the force of a gale-force wind. As the third installment in her bestselling series, this novel takes everything readers loved about Gideon and Jacob and amplifies the emotional tension, world-building, and sensuality. Frank proves once again why she is a formidable voice in paranormal romance, delivering a story where legendary power clashes with the most disruptive force in the universe: unexpected, consuming passion.

At the heart of the novel is the titular Elijah, an elite Demon warrior whose mastery over the wind makes him a force of nature both feared and respected across Nightwalker societies. Yet, Frank smartly deconstructs the invulnerable hero trope early on. By having this powerhouse ambushed by a sinister alliance of human necromancers and traitorous Demons, the author strips Elijah down to his physical and emotional vulnerabilities, setting the stage for a compelling character arc. He is no longer just a weapon; he is a man on the brink, saved from the jaws of death by an unexpected savior.

Enter Siena, the fierce Lycanthrope Queen. Sleek, powerful, and fiercely independent, Siena is a majestic puma in beast form and a formidable monarch in human shape. Frank excels at crafting alpha heroines who do not need saving, and Siena is a prime example. Her dynamic with Elijah forms the narrative and thematic core of the book. When Siena’s healing touch inadvertently awakens a dormant, scorching desire within Elijah, it sparks a hunger that threatens to upend both of their lives. 

The central theme of Elijah explores the dangerous and often agonizing friction between duty and desire. In Frank’s universe, love is rarely a private affair; it is geopolitical dynamite. A mating between a Demon warrior and a Lycanthrope Queen is strictly forbidden by the rigid social codes of their respective species. With Siena’s throne already precariously balanced and a common enemy circling like vultures, their passion isn't just a personal awakening—it is a catalyst that could bridge a fatal rift or ignite a war that destroys them both. 

Frank’s authorial style shines through her lush, lyrical prose and her talent for world-building. She effortlessly plunges the reader into an alternate reality pulsing with danger, intrigue, and raw magnetism. The pacing is relentless, balancing high-octane action sequences with intensely intimate, smoldering encounters that live up to the series' reputation for steamy romance. As endorsed by romance royalty like Christine Feehan and Linda Howard, Frank’s writing is both visceral and atmospheric, painting a world where monsters and monarchs bleed, fight, and love with equal intensity.

Elijah is a captivating, rip-roaring read that satisfies the cravings of paranormal romance aficionados. It delivers a masterclass in forbidden attraction, proving that even the most formidable warriors are utterly defenseless against the winds of destiny.

# The Memory of Souls (A Chorus of Dragons, #3)

**Author:** Jenn Lyons  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

In "The Memory of Souls," the third installment in Jenn Lyons' Chorus of Dragons series, the stakes are higher than ever. The world is on the brink of destruction as demons rampage across the empire, and the fulfillment of ancient prophecies draws near. Against this backdrop of chaos, Kihrin, the protagonist, must navigate a delicate web of politics and conspiracies to prevent the end of the world.

At the heart of the book lies the main theme: the danger of unchecked power and the corrupting influence of immortality. Kihrin's connection to the king of demons, Vol Karoth, grows stronger with each passing page, threatening to consume him whole. As he grapples with this newfound power, Kihrin is forced to confront the harsh reality that he may become the very thing he is trying to destroy – a monster capable of devastating the world.

Lyons masterfully explores the gray areas between good and evil, raising questions about the nature of morality and the consequences of our actions. Through Kihrin's character, she shows how even the well-intentioned can become trapped in a cycle of violence and destruction, and how the pursuit of power can lead us down a path of self-destruction.

One of the standout aspects of Lyons' writing is her ability to balance action, suspense, and character development. The pacing is relentless, making it impossible to look away as Kihrin navigates treacherous landscapes and battles ruthless foes. At the same time, Lyons takes the time to delve into the emotional depths of her characters, revealing the scars and vulnerabilities that make them human.

Lyons' world-building is also noteworthy, with a richly detailed and immersive universe that draws the reader in. The politics, mythology, and magic system are all expertly woven together, creating a cohesive and believable world that feels lived-in.

If there's one criticism to be made, it's that the supporting cast can sometimes feel overshadowed by Kihrin's narrative. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise expertly crafted novel.

Ultimately, "The Memory of Souls" is a thrilling and thought-provoking installment in the Chorus of Dragons series. Lyons' masterful storytelling and nuanced exploration of complex themes make for a compelling read that will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very end. If you're a fan of epic fantasy, complex characters, and morally ambiguous storytelling, then this is a must-read.

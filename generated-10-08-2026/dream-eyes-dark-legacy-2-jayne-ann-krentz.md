# Dream Eyes (Dark Legacy, #2)

**Author:** Jayne Ann Krentz  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

**A Sensual Journey into the Heart of Darkness**

Jayne Ann Krentz, the undisputed mistress of romantic suspense and paranormal thrillers, returns with "Dream Eyes", the gripping second installment in the Dark Legacy series. This meticulously crafted tale weaves an intricate web of mystery, deception, and passion that will leave you on the edge of your seat, begging for more.

At the center of this dark tapestry is Gwen Frazier, a psychic counselor haunted by the ghosts of her past. The sudden and inexplicable death of her mentor, Evelyn Ballinger, sets off a maelstrom of emotions and memories that Gwen would rather forget. Two years ago, a ruthless killer stalked the members of Ballinger's research studies, leaving an indelible scar on Gwen's psyche. As she delves into the circumstances surrounding Ballinger's passing, Gwen discovers that the threads of death and deception are far more twisted than she ever imagined.

Enter Judson Coppersmith, a charismatic and enigmatic psychic investigator, who arrives in Wilby, Oregon, under the guise of helping Gwen. His arrival is marked by a quiet intensity, fueled by an unyielding attraction to Gwen and a tormented past that threatens to consume him whole. As their investigation unfolds, it becomes clear that Judson is not your typical hero. His dark and troubled history, coupled with a unique gift that lies dormant within him, makes him a force to be reckoned with.

Krentz's masterful storytelling prowess is on full display in "Dream Eyes", as she expertly navigates the reader through a labyrinth of dreamscapes, the paranormal, and decades of deception. Her signature blend of witty banter, sizzling chemistry, and bone-chilling suspense has created a narrative that is both captivating and unputdownable.

One of the most striking aspects of Krentz's writing is her ability to seamlessly merge the fantastical with the mundane. In "Dream Eyes", the boundaries between reality and the paranormal are blissfully blurred, creating a world that is both eerie and enchanting. Her characterization is nothing short of exceptional, as Gwen and Judson's emotional journeys are skillfully interwoven, creating a dynamic that is both heartbreaking and uplifting.

The pacing in "Dream Eyes" is expertly controlled, with no shortage of twists and turns to keep even the most seasoned thriller enthusiasts on their toes. Krentz's clever manipulation of the plot is a testament to her skill as a storyteller, as she deftly balances the various threads of the narrative, leading the reader on a wild goose chase through the dark and foreboding woods of Wilby.

In conclusion, "Dream Eyes" is a gripping, unforgettable thriller that will leave you gasping for air. Jayne Ann Krentz's deft handcrafting of this dark and sensual tale is a testament to her skill as a master storyteller. With its intricate web of mystery, deception, and passion, this book is an absolute must-read for fans of romantic suspense and the paranormal.

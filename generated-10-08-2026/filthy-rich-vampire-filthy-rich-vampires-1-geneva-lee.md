# Filthy Rich Vampire (Filthy Rich Vampires, #1)

**Author:** Geneva Lee  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

Bestselling author Geneva Lee returns with a seductive plunge into the nocturnal elite in Filthy Rich Vampire, the opening salvo of her opulent new urban fantasy series. Known for her penchant for high-stakes drama and intoxicating romance, Lee trades the glittering boardrooms of contemporary erotica for a far more dangerous playground: the cutthroat, decadent society of immortal aristocrats. The result is a sharp, atmospheric, and compulsively readable novel that breathes new life into classic paranormal tropes through a lens of modern wealth and predatory glamour.

At the narrative’s dark and beating heart is Julian Rousseaux, an apex predator draped in bespoke tailoring and suffocated by family expectations. Julian embodies the ultimate romantic paradox of the genre: he is ancient, immensely wealthy, and utterly jaded, yet trapped by the rigid archaic traditions of his kind. When the annual marriage market threatens to shackle him, Lee uses Julian’s rebellion not just as a plot device, but as a meditation on autonomy within a gilded cage. He is a man who owns everything yet commands nothing of his own heart—until a clumsy, fiercely independent mortal cellist literally crashes into his pristine world.

Enter Thea, a refreshing anchor of humanity in a sea of immortal artifice. Burdened by crippling medical debt and a world she was never meant to see, Thea is a reluctant heroine thrust into a masquerade of blood and diamonds. What elevates Lee’s writing here is the dynamic between her protagonists. Rather than relying solely on immediate, unearned infatuation, Lee builds their connection on a foundation of leverage, desperation, and slow-burn pragmatism. The fake-relationship trope—a staple of romance fiction—is executed with a sharp, cynical edge that gradually gives way to genuine peril and visceral chemistry. The contrast between Julian’s chilling supremacy and Thea’s vulnerable tenacity creates an electric friction that propels the narrative forward at a breathless pace.

Lee’s stylistic strength lies in her world-building through sensory overload. The vampire high society she constructs is a sensory feast of velvet, champagne, sharp fangs, and sharper tongues. The prose is lush, sensual, and unapologetically dramatic, capturing the intoxicating allure of a world where beauty and brutality are two sides of the same coin. Yet beneath the shimmering surface of galas and designer gowns lies a suffocating undercurrent of malice. The Rousseaux family functions as a chilling antagonist, a symbol of old-world prejudice and ruthless self-preservation that views Thea not merely as an interloper, but as a disposable pest.

While the novel leans into familiar genre conventions—the wealthy, brooding monster and the innocent human who melts his cold heart—Lee’s pacing and sophisticated prose elevate the material above standard paranormal romance fare. The stakes feel genuinely high because the threat is dual-pronged: external enemies within the vampire aristocracy plotting Thea’s demise, and the internal danger of a forbidden passion that neither can afford to indulge. 

Filthy Rich Vampire is a deliciously addictive invitation to a dangerous world where love is the ultimate high-stakes gamble. Geneva Lee has crafted a glittering, seductive triumph that will leave readers desperate for the next turn of the page—and the next installment in the series. If you crave dark magic, primal attraction, and a romance drenched in both luxury and peril, this is your next obsession.

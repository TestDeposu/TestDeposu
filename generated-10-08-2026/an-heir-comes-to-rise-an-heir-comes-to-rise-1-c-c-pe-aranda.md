# An Heir Comes to Rise (An Heir Comes to Rise, #1)

**Author:** C.C. Peñaranda  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

Shadows, Secrets, and the Price of Forbidden Power: A Review of An Heir Comes to Rise

In the ever-expanding universe of fantasy literature, finding a novel that successfully breathes new life into classic tropes is a rare triumph. With An Heir Comes to Rise, bestselling author C.C. Peñaranda achieves just that, delivering a darkly enchanting tale that immediately sinks its claws into the reader and refuses to let go. Set within a meticulously crafted world where the magical Fae rule over humanity with an iron fist, the novel establishes a tense, atmospheric landscape where survival is a daily struggle and power is the ultimate currency.

At the narrative’s beating heart is Faythe, an orphaned human scraping by in a world designed to crush her kind. Peñaranda crafts Faythe not as a chosen one waiting to be rescued, but as a gritty, resilient survivor shaped by hardship. Her world is upended when she attracts the attention of Nik, a brooding member of the royal Fae guard. What follows is a high-stakes collision of worlds that exposes a staggering truth: Faythe possesses the forbidden magic of the Nightwalkers—powers that allow them to slip into the dreams of others and assassinate from the shadows. As a human, she should not possess this ability. As a catalyst for chaos, she cannot afford to ignore it. 

Peñaranda’s thematic exploration centers heavily on identity, suppression, and the dangerous allure of hidden strength. The central metaphor of dream-walking serves as a brilliant narrative device. Dreams are traditionally a sanctuary of vulnerability, but in Peñaranda’s hands, the mind becomes a lethal battlefield. The book questions what happens when a person discovers they are holding a weapon they never asked for, one that threatens not only their own life, but the lives of everyone they hold dear. This internal struggle mirrors the external oppression of the Fae hierarchy, creating a cohesive thematic resonance where personal awakening directly parallels societal rebellion. 

The author’s writing style is both immersive and razor-sharp. Peñaranda excels at world-building through sensory details rather than tedious exposition. The shadows feel heavier, the magic crackles with tangible danger, and the psychological weight of Faythe’s predicament is felt in every chapter. The pacing is relentless, expertly balancing heart-pounding action sequences with quieter, charged moments of character development and simmering romantic tension. The dynamic between Faythe and Nik crackles with suspicion, mutual respect, and an undeniable chemistry that elevates the emotional stakes. 

An Heir Comes to Rise is a masterclass in modern romantic fantasy, offering a potent blend of dark magic, political intrigue, and fierce emotional resilience. C.C. Peñaranda has constructed a world that is as seductive as it is perilous, led by a heroine you will root for until the very last page. For fans of intricate magic systems, enemies-to-lovers tension, and heroines who fight tooth and nail for their place in the world, this novel is an absolute must-read.

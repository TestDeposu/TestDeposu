# And Eternity (Incarnations of Immortality, #7)

**Author:** Piers Anthony  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1985

In Piers Anthony's captivating series, "The Incarnations of Immortality", he expertly weaves intricate plotlines and philosophical themes, keeping readers entwined until the very end. His seventh installment, "And Eternity", masterfully explores the delicate balance between good and evil, immersing readers in a rich tapestry of moral complexity.

At the heart of this thought-provoking novel lies the enigmatic Norton, who accepts the position of the Incarnation of Time, choosing to live life in reverse, from present to past. This peculiar decision sets the stage for a thrilling adventure, as Norton must navigate the often treacherous waters of the afterlife, befriending and battling the powerful Incarnates of Immortality, including Death, Fate, War, and Nature. Even the cunning Satan himself extends a seemingly benevolent hand, presenting Norton with gifts that ultimately prove to be deceitful traps.

Piers Anthony's writing is as polished as ever, exhibiting his trademark wit and humor amidst the darker themes. His prose is engaging, effortlessly shifting between lighthearted moments and gravely somber reflections. This fluid style allows readers to seamlessly transition between the lighter aspects of the narrative and the weightier, more intense scenes.

Anthony's greatest strength lies in his nuanced portrayal of complex moral issues. Through Norton's journey, the author skillfully probes the nature of good and evil, often blurring the lines between right and wrong. By presenting no clear-cut answers, Anthony forces readers to engage with the narrative on a deeper level, encouraging introspection and critical thinking. This masterful approach makes "And Eternity" more than just an enjoyable fantasy novel – it's a thought-provoking treatise on the human condition.

One of the most striking aspects of Anthony's writing is his ability to balance humor and pathos. Norton's plight is a heartbreaking one, and yet, the author finds ways to infuse even the bleakest moments with a sense of irony or wry humor. This juxtaposition adds another layer of depth to the narrative, highlighting the resilience and determination of the human spirit.

Throughout "And Eternity", the Hourglass – a symbol of the power of time itself – serves as a potent reminder of Norton's ultimate fate. As he becomes increasingly entangled in the complex web of the afterlife, Norton faces the impossible task of confronting Satan directly. This climactic showdown showcases Anthony's flair for the dramatic, as the fate of the world hangs precariously in the balance.

In "And Eternity", Piers Anthony delivers a captivating tale of time, mortality, and the eternal struggle between good and evil. This riveting installment is a testament to the author's mastery of the fantasy genre, combining memorable characters, engaging world-building, and philosophical depth. Whether you're a seasoned reader of the series or new to Anthony's work, "And Eternity" is an unforgettable journey that will leave you pondering its themes long after the final page has been turned.

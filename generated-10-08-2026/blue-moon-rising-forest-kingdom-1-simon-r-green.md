# Blue Moon Rising (Forest Kingdom, #1)

**Author:** Simon R. Green  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

Title: One Last Ride Under the Blue Moon: Simon R. Green’s Triumphant Return to the Forest Kingdom

For decades, Simon R. Green has carved out a unique, blood-soaked, and delightfully irreverent corner of fantasy literature. Known for his breathless pacing, unapologetic pulp sensibilities, and a distinct brand of hard-boiled heroism laced with mythic wonder, Green returns to his roots with Blue Moon Rising. While the provided publisher materials playfully nod toward a grand reunion featuring legendary protagonists Hawk and Fisher—evoking a tone reminiscent of Green's broader interconnected universes—this novel fundamentally captures the author at his absolute creative peak: a sprawling, high-stakes epic that balances fairy-tale tropes with jagged-edged grit.

At its core, the main theme of Blue Moon Rising is the heavy, unavoidable burden of legacy and the generational passage of the torch. Green has always been fascinated by how ordinary people rise to extraordinary circumstances, but here, he explores what happens when the heroes themselves become institutions. Hawk and Fisher are no longer just desperate defenders of a chaotic city; they are living legends, founders of an academy that has commercialized the very notion of heroism. This sets up a brilliant central conflict: can genuine, raw valor survive when it is packaged, taught, and turned into a tourist attraction? Green uses this setup to interrogate the nature of storytelling itself. He strips away the glossy veneer of Once Upon a Time and replaces it with the mud, blood, and sacrifice required to keep the darkness at bay.

Stylistically, Green is operating at the top of his game. His prose is famously kinetic, moving at a relentless clip that leaves little room for hesitation. He writes with a swagger, blending high fantasy majesty with the cynical, street-smart dialogue usually reserved for noir detective fiction. You can feel the sweat, steel, and magic leaping off the page. Yet, beneath the punchy action sequences and the parade of fantastical creatures, Green maintains an undeniable emotional core. The looming threat of the Demon Prince serves not just as a standard world-ending catastrophe, but as a deeply personal reckoning. The impending rise of the Blue Moon forces characters to confront ghosts of the past while desperately trying to secure a future for their children. 

What makes Blue Moon Rising a standout achievement in Green’s bibliography is how it manages to feel both nostalgic and radically fresh. Longtime fans will revel in the callbacks, the return to legendary lands, and the reunion with beloved cohorts. However, newcomers will find themselves instantly hooked by the sheer audacity of the world-building. Green never met a cliché he couldn't subvert, twist, or weaponize, and his universe is delightfully over-the-top, populated by monsters, magic, and moral ambiguity in equal measure.

Ultimately, Blue Moon Rising is a masterclass in modern heroic fantasy. It reminds us why we fell in love with speculative fiction in the first place: for the thrill of the quest, the terror of the abyss, and the stubborn, beautiful refusal of heroes to stay down when the night is darkest. Simon R. Green delivers a sweeping, unforgettable adventure that proves some legends only get better with time.

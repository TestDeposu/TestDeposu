# Wielding a Red Sword (Incarnations of Immortality, #4)

**Author:** Piers Anthony  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1987

Wielding a Red Sword, the fourth installment in Piers Anthony's Incarnations of Immortality series, is a thought-provoking and captivating tale that delves into the complexities of war, love, and the eternal struggle between good and evil. Anthony's unique blend of fantasy and philosophical introspection has once again yielded a fascinating narrative that will keep readers enthralled from start to finish.

At the heart of the story is Mym, a young man who, driven by his desire for love and autonomy, takes up the Red Sword, becoming the Incarnation of War. As Mym navigates the intricacies of his new role, he soon discovers that his efforts to mitigate the suffering caused by war are being manipulated by the cunning and powerful Satan. This realization sets Mym on a perilous path, as he finds himself trapped in Hell, leading a rebellion among the Damned, and ultimately, facing off against the forces of evil in a desperate bid to prevent Satan's triumph.

One of the most striking aspects of Anthony's writing style is his ability to balance action, adventure, and fantasy with profound philosophical and moral themes. In Wielding a Red Sword, he tackles the nature of war, the consequences of violence, and the complexities of human relationships with his characteristic wit and insight. The characters are well-developed and multidimensional, with Mym being a particularly compelling protagonist, whose journey from a dutiful son to a powerful Incarnation is both relatable and inspiring.

Anthony's world-building is, as always, meticulous and detailed, with a richly imagined cosmology that underpins the narrative. The depiction of Hell, in particular, is a masterpiece of dark fantasy, with its twisted landscapes, eerie creatures, and sinister atmosphere. The pacing is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows effortlessly from intense action sequences to more introspective moments of character development.

Throughout the book, Anthony's prose is engaging, witty, and occasionally humorous, making the reading experience a pleasure. His writing is devoid of pretension, and he tackles complex themes with a clarity and directness that is both refreshing and thought-provoking. The story is full of unexpected twists and turns, keeping readers on the edge of their seats as they follow Mym's journey through the realms of war, love, and redemption.

In conclusion, Wielding a Red Sword is a gripping and thought-provoking fantasy novel that explores the complexities of human nature, the nature of war, and the eternal struggle between good and evil. Piers Anthony's unique writing style, combined with his boundless imagination and philosophical insight, has yielded a narrative that is both entertaining and profound. If you're a fan of fantasy, adventure, or simply great storytelling, this book is sure to captivate and inspire.

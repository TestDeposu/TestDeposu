# Adam (Nightwalkers, #6)

**Author:** Jacquelyn Frank  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

**Adam: A Love Story Written in Blood and Fire**

Jacquelyn Frank’s *Nightwalkers* series has always been a masterclass in blending supernatural intrigue with the raw, intoxicating chemistry of forbidden love. *Adam*—the sixth installment in this electrifying saga—proves that the author’s knack for weaving romance with relentless action hasn’t faded. In fact, it’s sharpened into something even more dangerous: a love story that’s as much about survival as it is about surrender.

At the heart of *Adam* is a reunion that’s been centuries in the making. Four hundred years ago, Adam, a formidable Enforcer of the Nightwalkers, vanished without a trace—leaving behind a brother who never stopped searching. Now, he’s back, but the world he returns to is unrecognizable. The Demons, once a proud and untouchable force, now face annihilation at the hands of a traitorous alliance between a rogue vampire and a demon who once swore loyalty to their kind. The stakes couldn’t be higher: the fate of an entire species hangs in the balance, and time is running out.

But the real tension isn’t just in the battle to come—it’s in the man who steps into the fray. Adam is a warrior forged in fire, his past a labyrinth of duty and loss. And then there’s Jasmine, the black-haired vampire whose name alone sends shivers down his spine. Their first encounter was a clash of wills, a spark of something forbidden that neither could ignore. Now, forced into an uneasy alliance, they must confront the ghosts of their past while racing against a foe who will stop at nothing to erase their kind from existence.

Frank’s writing is what makes *Adam* so compelling. Her prose is lush and immersive, painting a world where magic and mortality blur into something visceral. She doesn’t just tell a story—she makes you *feel* it. The tension between Adam and Jasmine crackles with an intensity that’s both sensual and deadly. Their chemistry isn’t just about desire; it’s about the struggle between loyalty and longing, between the rules of war and the chaos of the heart. Frank understands that love in a world of monsters isn’t just about passion—it’s about power, about survival, and about the moments when the line between savior and destroyer blurs completely.

What sets *Adam* apart is how Frank balances the epic scale of the conflict with the intimate, almost tender moments between her characters. The battle for the Demons’ survival is vast and terrifying, but it’s grounded in the raw, human (or in this case, supernatural) emotions of the people fighting it. Adam and Jasmine’s relationship isn’t just a love story—it’s a test of wills, a dance between trust and betrayal, and ultimately, a surrender that feels earned and devastating.

The praise for *Adam* from *Publishers Weekly*—calling it "blazing demon-on-vampire supernatural action"—is spot-on. Frank doesn’t just deliver a story; she delivers a *feeling*. The Nightwalkers series has always been about more than just monsters and magic—it’s about the cost of power, the weight of legacy, and the moments when love becomes the most dangerous weapon of all.

If you’ve followed the Nightwalkers saga, you know Jacquelyn Frank’s work is never just a ride—it’s an experience. *Adam* is no exception. It’s a book that will leave you breathless, that will make you ache for the characters even as you cheer for their victories. And when the final pages turn, you’ll realize something: this isn’t just a story about saving a world. It’s about the moments when the line between hero and villain, between love and destruction, becomes so thin you can’t tell them apart.

For fans of dark romance, supernatural thrillers, and stories where love and war collide, *Adam* is a must-read. Jacquelyn Frank has once again crafted a tale that’s as gripping as it is unforgettable—proving that in a world of monsters, the most dangerous thing of all might just be the heart.

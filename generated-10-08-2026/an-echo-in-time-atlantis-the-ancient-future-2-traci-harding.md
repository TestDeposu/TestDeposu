# An Echo in Time: Atlantis (The Ancient Future, #2)

**Author:** Traci Harding  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

In the world of historical speculative fiction, few authors dare to venture into the mystical realms of ancient civilizations and uncharted epochs. Traci Harding, however, is not one to shy away from the challenge. With her second installment in "The Ancient Future" series, An Echo in Time: Atlantis, she expertly weaves a tale that transports readers to a realm both hauntingly familiar and eerily unknown.

As the story unfolds, the fabric of history begins to unravel, plunging the kingdoms of Prydyn, Dumnonia, and Dalriada into chaos. With the High King, Maelgwn of Gwynedd, beset by madness and a mysterious plague, his trusted Queen, Tory, embarks on a perilous journey through time to seek the wisdom of the fabled city of Atlantis. This is no ordinary quest, for Tory hopes to find not only the cure to save her ailing husband but also the secrets of a lost civilization that held the keys to unimaginable knowledge and power.

Harding's writing is a masterclass in world-building, plunging readers into a realm of ancient magic, where myths and legends converge with history. Her vivid descriptions transport us to the marble plateau beneath the turbulent clouds, where the stone circle of polished crystal pulsates with electromagnetic energy, an otherworldly beacon beckoning Tory towards a destiny both grand and treacherous.

What sets Harding apart is her ability to craft complex, multidimensional characters that leap off the page. Tory, the queen, is a protagonist both strong and vulnerable, driven by a fierce devotion to her loved ones and a determination to navigate the unforgiving tides of time. Her voice is a compelling thread that weaves through the narrative, drawing us deeper into the world of Atlantis, where men of great learning and miracles abound.

The theme at the heart of An Echo in Time: Atlantis is a powerful exploration of the intricate dance between past and present. Harding's vision is far-reaching, spanning centuries, and traversing the boundaries between myth and history. As Tory navigates the labyrinthine paths of Atlantis, she confronts the echoes of a civilization that has been lost to the sands of time, yet continues to shape our understanding of the world and our place within it.

Throughout the book, Harding's writing is characterized by its lyricism and precision, a blend of atmospheric prose that conjures the eerie landscapes of the Otherworld and the intellectual rigor of a scholar unraveling the mysteries of a bygone era. The pacing is expertly managed, hurtling the reader through a journey both fast-paced and epic in scope, as Tory faces challenges at every turn, from the treacherous politics of the ancient world to the very fabric of reality itself.

Ultimately, An Echo in Time: Atlantis is a triumph of historical speculative fiction, a testament to Harding's skill as a storyteller and her unwavering passion for the subject matter. It is a book that will captivate fans of myth and history, as well as those who delight in epic quests that traverse the boundaries of time and space. As Harding transports us to the shores of Atlantis, we are left with a sense of wonder, awe, and a lingering question: what secrets lie hidden in the recesses of our collective past, waiting to be uncovered by those brave enough to listen to the echoes of time?

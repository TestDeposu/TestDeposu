# When Dealing With Dragons

**Author:** Dana Swift  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

When Dealing With Dragons, the latest novel by Dana Swift, is a captivating young adult romantasy that delves into the intricate world of humans and dragons, where magic and metalcrafting reign supreme. The story follows two school rivals, Farren Walsh and James Murphy, who are forced to put aside their differences and work together to protect a priceless secret that could change their society forever.

At its core, the book explores the theme of finding common ground and understanding between individuals from different backgrounds and social classes. Farren, a copper-crafter, and James, a silver-crafter, come from families with a long history of conflict and exploitation, with James's family representing the worst of human greed and dragon abuse. However, as they spend more time together, they begin to see beyond their initial impressions and develop a deeper understanding of each other's motivations and desires.

Swift's writing style is engaging and immersive, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the world she has created. The dual-POV narrative allows the reader to experience the story from both Farren and James's perspectives, providing a rich and nuanced exploration of their thoughts, feelings, and emotions. The author's use of language is evocative and descriptive, bringing the world of dragons and magic to life in a way that is both vivid and believable.

One of the strengths of the book is its well-developed characters, particularly the two protagonists. Farren is a strong and determined heroine who is not afraid to stand up for what she believes in, even if it means going against the norms of her society. James, on the other hand, is a complex and multifaceted character who is struggling to come to terms with his family's legacy and his own desires. The chemistry between them is palpable, and their romance is a slow-burning one that adds an extra layer of tension and excitement to the story.

The world-building in the book is also noteworthy, with a detailed and intricate magic system that is based on the bonding between humans and dragons. The author has clearly put a lot of thought into creating a rich and immersive world, with a deep history and a complex social hierarchy. The dragons themselves are magnificent creatures, with their own personalities and quirks, and the way they interact with humans is both fascinating and believable.

Overall, When Dealing With Dragons is a delightful and engaging read that is sure to appeal to fans of young adult fantasy and romance. The book's themes of cross-class romance, finding family, and creating a better world for creatures big and small are timely and thought-provoking, and the author's style is engaging and immersive. With its well-developed characters, rich world-building, and slow-burning romance, this book is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and unforgettable story.

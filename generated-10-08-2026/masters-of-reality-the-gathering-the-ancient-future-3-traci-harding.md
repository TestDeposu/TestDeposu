# Masters of Reality: The Gathering (The Ancient Future, #3)

**Author:** Traci Harding  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

Masters of Reality: The Gathering, the latest installment in Traci Harding's Ancient Future series, is a mesmerizing blend of fantasy, adventure, and science fiction. Published in 2025, this book promises to captivate readers with its richly woven narrative, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes.

At the heart of the story lies Tory, a strong-willed and determined protagonist, as she navigates the intricate web of her ancestry and the secrets surrounding her clan. As she delves deeper into the mysteries of her past, she discovers that her tribe is not the only one of immortals nurtured by the Gods. This revelation sets her on a perilous path, where she must confront her own destiny and the fate of her people.

One of the standout features of Harding's writing style is her ability to craft a compelling narrative that seamlessly blends elements of mythology, history, and science fiction. Her vivid descriptions of the vortex and the glowing balls of light descending upon the earth are a testament to her skill in creating immersive and visually stunning scenes.

Throughout the book, Harding's characters are multidimensional and complex, with each one bringing their unique perspective and motivations to the story. The re-emergence of allies from Tory's past adds depth to the narrative, and their individual storylines are expertly woven into the larger tapestry of the plot.

The central theme of the book, however, revolves around the concept of control and the consequences of playing with forces beyond human understanding. The enormous vortex, the glowing corridor, and the interstellar army all serve as a backdrop for exploring the idea of power and its corrupting influence.

Harding's writing is engaging, and her use of language is both evocative and precise. She has a talent for creating a sense of tension and urgency, drawing the reader into the world of the story and refusing to let go. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and introspection that keeps the narrative moving at a brisk pace.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the book assumes a certain level of familiarity with the previous installments in the series. While Harding does provide some background information, new readers may find themselves struggling to keep up with the complex world-building and character relationships.

In conclusion, Masters of Reality: The Gathering is a gripping and thought-provoking read that will appeal to fans of science fiction, fantasy, and adventure. Traci Harding's writing is engaging, and her ability to craft a compelling narrative is undeniable. While it may not be the best starting point for new readers, it is a must-read for fans of the Ancient Future series.

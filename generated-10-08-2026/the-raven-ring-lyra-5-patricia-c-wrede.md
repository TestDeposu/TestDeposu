# The Raven Ring (Lyra, #5)

**Author:** Patricia C. Wrede  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

The Raven Ring plunges readers into a stark, wind‑scarred world where grief fuels a relentless drive for answers. When Eleret learns of her mother’s death on a distant battlefield, she straps a dagger to her thigh and sets out across treacherous roads to reclaim a single, enigmatic heirloom—a ring etched with a raven. What begins as a somber errand quickly spirals into a high‑stakes chase, as shadowy forces seek the same token, willing to kill for its secret power.

Patricia C. Wrede’s hallmark style shines here: crisp, evocative prose that balances lyrical description with brisk, forward‑moving action. She paints Ciaron’s bustling markets and the perilous wilds beyond with just enough detail to let the reader’s imagination fill the gaps, while keeping the narrative tight enough that every scene feels purposeful. Eleret herself is a classic Wrede heroine—resourceful, morally grounded, and steadily growing from a grieving daughter into a woman who trusts her own instincts. The ring, though initially a simple keepsake, becomes a catalyst that forces her to confront questions of legacy, trust, and the thin line between protection and possession.

The novel’s central theme—how objects of the past can shape the destiny of those who inherit them—is explored through both external danger and internal revelation. Wrede deftly intertwines the physical quest with Eleret’s emotional journey, showing that the true treasure may lie not in the metal of the ring but in the courage it awakens within her. Fans of the Lyra series will recognize the familiar blend of strong female leads, subtle magic, and world‑building that feels both expansive and intimate, while newcomers will find an accessible entry point that still delivers the depth and charm that have made Wrede a staple of modern fantasy.

In a genre often weighed down by sprawling epics, The Raven Ring stands out as a tightly crafted adventure that proves a single, well‑worn heirloom can carry the weight of an entire story. It’s a swift, satisfying read that lingers long after the final page, reminding us that the most powerful legacies are those we choose to uphold.

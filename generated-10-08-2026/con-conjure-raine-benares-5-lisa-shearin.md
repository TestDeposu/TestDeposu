# Con & Conjure (Raine Benares, #5)

**Author:** Lisa Shearin  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**Con & Conjure: A Masterclass in Chaos and Cunning**

Lisa Shearin’s *Con & Conjure*—the fifth installment in the *Raine Benares* series—is a dazzling, high-stakes romp where magic, betrayal, and family loyalty collide in a whirlwind of intrigue. At its core, this novel is a meditation on power: who wields it, who covets it, and how far people will go to keep it—or seize it. Raine Benares, our sharp-witted, morally flexible seeker, is no stranger to danger, but *Con & Conjure* elevates her stakes to cosmic proportions. With a soul-stealing stone (the Saghred) now bonded to her, she’s become a prize worth killing for—targeted by a goblin thief with a personal vendetta and her ex-fiancé, an elven assassin who still harbors old feelings. The tension is palpable, the threat immediate, and Shearin’s signature blend of wit and menace makes every page crackle with potential.

What sets *Con & Conjure* apart is its relentless energy. Shearin’s prose is deceptively light, but her world-building is dense and immersive, weaving together mythic creatures, political intrigue, and high-concept magic with effortless precision. The novel’s strength lies in its characters—particularly Raine’s criminal family, the Benareses, whose elaborate cons are now being weaponized against her. Their dynamic is a highlight, a mix of sibling rivalry, protective loyalty, and dark humor that keeps the story from veering into melodrama. Even the villains are compelling, their motives layered with enough nuance to make their betrayals sting.

Themes of power and possession dominate, but Shearin doesn’t shy away from the darker implications. The Saghred isn’t just a tool—it’s a curse, a temptation, and a catalyst for war. Raine’s struggle isn’t just about survival; it’s about defining her own agency in a world that wants to control her. The novel’s pacing is relentless, with twists that feel earned rather than contrived, and a climax that delivers on the promise of its setup. Shearin’s ability to balance action, dialogue, and world-building is a testament to her skill as a storyteller, making *Con & Conjure* a standout in a series already known for its boldness.

For fans of fast-paced fantasy with a dash of noir, this book is a must-read. Shearin’s style—sharp, snappy, and steeped in character—ensures that even the most outlandish plot points feel grounded. Whether you’re here for the magic, the heists, or the emotional depth, *Con & Conjure* delivers a thrilling, satisfying ride. It’s fantasy at its most entertaining, where every con has a conjurer, and every betrayal has a price.

# The Sword of Truth, Boxed Set I: Wizard's First Rule, Blood of the Fold, Stone of Tears (Sword of Truth, #1-3)

**Author:** Terry Goodkind  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

Step into a sprawling realm where ancient prophecies shape the destiny of worlds, and the very fabric of existence hangs by a thread. This landmark fantasy collection gathers the foundational masterworks of visionary world-building, plunging readers into an unforgiving universe where myth, magic, and relentless warfare collide. At its core, the narrative explores the heavy, often agonizing burden of destiny, tracing the harrowing journeys of ordinary individuals forced to rise as legendary figures against insurmountable odds. 

The author’s signature style is defined by breathless momentum and uncompromising stakes. There is no safe haven in these pages; characters are relentlessly tested, broken, and reforged in the fires of betrayal, political intrigue, and cataclysmic conflict. The prose weaves intricate lore with visceral action, moving effortlessly from the intimate heartbreak of deception to panoramic battlefields where gods, enchanters, and mortals clash. Central to this epic is the exploration of truth—how history is manipulated by the powerful, and the perilous cost of uncovering reality in a world built on lies. 

For readers who crave immersive, multi-generational fantasy defined by high drama, rich mythology, and profound moral choices, this collection stands as a towering achievement of the genre. It is an invitation to lose yourself in a universe where hope flickers brightest in the deepest darkness.

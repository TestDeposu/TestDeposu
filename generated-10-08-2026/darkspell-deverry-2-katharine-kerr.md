# Darkspell (Deverry, #2)

**Author:** Katharine Kerr  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1987

In the realm of fantasy literature, Katharine Kerr's "Darkspell" stands as a testament to the power of storytelling, weaving a rich tapestry of magic, adventure, and romance. This definitive edition, extensively rewritten by the author, offers a fresh and captivating experience for both new and returning readers to the world of Deverry.

At its core, "Darkspell" is a story about the struggle for identity, self-discovery, and the unyielding pursuit of one's destiny. Lord Rhodry, a mercenary with a mysterious past, and his swordmaster-lover Jill, are the protagonists of this epic saga. As they navigate the treacherous landscape of Deverry, they must confront their own demons and unravel the secrets of their past. Rhodry's quest to uncover his true parentage and Jill's journey to harness her latent powers serve as the driving force behind the narrative.

Kerr's writing style is a masterclass in world-building and character development. Her vivid descriptions of the land of Deverry transport readers to a realm of breathtaking beauty, where the sapphire waters and secret mountain caverns hold secrets and mysteries waiting to be uncovered. The characters are multidimensional and complex, with rich backstories that add depth and nuance to the story. The author's use of language is lyrical and evocative, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world of Deverry.

One of the standout features of "Darkspell" is its exploration of the human condition. Kerr delves into themes of love, loyalty, and the struggle for power, raising questions about the nature of good and evil. The characters are not simply heroes or villains; they exist on a spectrum of morality, making choices that are often motivated by a complex array of emotions and desires.

The pacing of the novel is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows effortlessly from one scene to the next. Kerr's use of tension and suspense keeps readers on the edge of their seats, as the stakes grow higher and the danger becomes more palpable. The world of Deverry is rich in magic and mythology, with a deep history that underpins the narrative.

The supporting cast of characters is equally well-developed, with the enigmatic sorcerer Nevyn serving as a guiding force in the lives of Rhodry and Jill. His presence is a reminder that even in a world of magic and wonder, there are those who wield power and influence from behind the scenes.

In conclusion, "Darkspell" is a captivating and immersive read that will appeal to fans of fantasy literature. Kerr's writing is masterful, and her world-building is exceptional. The characters are complex and multidimensional, and the narrative is engaging and suspenseful. This definitive edition is a must-read for anyone who loves epic fantasy, magic, and adventure.

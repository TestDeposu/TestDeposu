# Deep Secret

**Author:** Diana Wynne Jones  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

Deep Secret by Diana Wynne Jones is a captivating multiverse adventure that seamlessly weaves together strong writing, high fantasy heroics, and delightfully dark humor. The story follows Rupert Venables, a junior Magid tasked with overseeing the vast Multiverse, including Earth and the Empire of Korfyros. When his mentor passes away, Rupert is thrust into a king-sized problem: finding his own replacement among hundreds of candidates scattered across the Multiverse.

Jones's masterful storytelling is on full display as she navigates the complexities of the multiverse, effortlessly jumping between worlds and dimensions. Her writing is engaging, witty, and full of clever observations about the human condition. The world-building is meticulous, with a richly detailed universe that is both fantastical and grounded in reality.

One of the standout aspects of Deep Secret is its exploration of the theme of identity and belonging. Rupert, as a junior Magid, is struggling to find his place in the multiverse, and his quest to find a replacement becomes a metaphor for self-discovery. Through his journey, Jones raises important questions about what it means to belong, and how our sense of identity is shaped by our relationships and experiences.

Jones's characters are equally well-crafted, with Rupert being a particularly relatable protagonist. His awkwardness and uncertainty make him a sympathetic and endearing character, and his growth throughout the story is both satisfying and authentic. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with a diverse range of characters that add depth and complexity to the narrative.

The use of PhantasmaCon as a setting for the story is a stroke of genius. The convention setting allows Jones to poke fun at fandom culture, while also exploring the ways in which people come together to share their passions and interests. The result is a hilarious and poignant commentary on the power of community and the importance of finding one's tribe.

Overall, Deep Secret is a delightful and engaging read that is sure to appeal to fans of fantasy and science fiction. Jones's unique blend of humor, wit, and imagination makes for a compelling narrative that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. With its richly detailed world-building, memorable characters, and thought-provoking themes, Deep Secret is a must-read for anyone looking for a captivating and immersive story.

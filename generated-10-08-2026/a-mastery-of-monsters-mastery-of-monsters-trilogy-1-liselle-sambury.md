# A Mastery of Monsters (Mastery of Monsters Trilogy, #1)

**Author:** Liselle Sambury  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

Liselle Sambury has carved out a distinct space for herself in contemporary speculative fiction, but with A Mastery of Monsters, the opening salvo of her brand-new trilogy, she transcends expectations to deliver her most ferocious and atmospheric work yet. Arriving in 2025 with early critical acclaim already heralding its arrival, this novel establishes Sambury not just as a YA powerhouse, but as a formidable voice in the realm of adult-leaning, razor-sharp dark fantasy.

At its core, A Mastery of Monsters is a brutal and hypnotic exploration of agency, survival, and the dark cost of protection. The narrative follows August, a young woman thrust into a terrifying crucible after her brother vanishes without a trace, mirroring the prior disappearance of their mother. Sambury uses this dual trauma to anchor the emotional heartbeat of the story. August’s fierce devotion to her remaining family is not painted merely as a noble heroic trait, but as a desperate, consuming force—a literal survival mechanism. The overarching theme of the novel examines what it means to be a monster versus what it means to be human in a world that hungers for control. When August is forced to infiltrate a shadowy secret society of magical creatures, the line between predator and prey blurs entirely. Sambury masterfully interrogates the societal structures that demand we tame our most primal instincts, asking whether true power comes from suppressing our inner darkness or learning how to wield it without losing our souls.

Stylistically, Sambury’s prose is both lush and visceral. The comparisons to Leigh Bardugo’s Ninth House and Tracy Deonn’s Legendborn are entirely earned, yet Sambury infuses the text with a distinct texture that is all her own. Her pacing is relentless, balancing high-stakes supernatural competition with deeply intimate character introspection. The world-building is immersive and tactile—readers can practically smell the ozone and old paper of the secret society's halls, and feel the terrifying shadow of ten-foot-tall beasts lurking just out of sight. The chemistry between August and her reluctant partner, Virgil Hawthorne—a boy fighting a desperate, daily war against the literal beast inside him—adds a layer of delicious tension and moral ambiguity. Virgil is not a shining knight, and August is no damsel; they are two broken instruments playing a dangerous duet in a system rigged to see them crushed.

A Mastery of Monsters succeeds because it refuses to pull its punches. Sambury constructs a world where the magical and the macabre collide with devastating emotional consequences. It is a bold, bloody, and breathless invitation into a society where survival demands nothing less than total dominion over the worst parts of ourselves. For readers hungry for dark academia with sharp teeth, complex family dynamics, and a heroine who refuses to break, this 2025 release is destined to be the obsession of the year.

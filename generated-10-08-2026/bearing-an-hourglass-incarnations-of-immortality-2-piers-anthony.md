# Bearing an Hourglass (Incarnations of Immortality, #2)

**Author:** Piers Anthony  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1985

**Review: Bearing an Hourglass**

In "Bearing an Hourglass", the second installment of Piers Anthony's captivating "Incarnations of Immortality" series, we find ourselves once again immersed in a rich tapestry of thought-provoking concepts, mesmerizing world-building, and a gripping narrative that will leave readers on the edge of their seats. Anthony's masterful storytelling weaves a complex web of good and evil, inviting us to question the fundamental nature of existence itself.

At the heart of the story lies the transformation of Norton, a mortal man who accepts the daunting responsibility of becoming the Incarnation of Time. With his newfound powers, Norton begins to live life in reverse, progressing backward through time towards a distant past. As he navigates this peculiar existence, he is warmly welcomed by the other powerful Incarnates of Immortality, including Death, Fate, War, and Nature. Satan, the embodiment of evil, even extends a seeming gesture of friendship, gifting Norton with a series of "gifts" that, unbeknownst to the unsuspecting Incarnation, become cunning traps set to ensnare him in a complex scheme to destroy all that is good.

Through Norton's journey, Anthony masterfully explores the dualities of morality, probing the boundaries between good and evil with unflinching curiosity. This exploration is both thought-provoking and deeply unsettling, making "Bearing an Hourglass" a book that resonates long after its final page is closed. Anthony's world-building is as meticulous as ever, drawing upon a sweeping scope of mythological and symbolic references that imbue the narrative with a deep sense of depth and resonance.

One of the standout aspects of Anthony's writing is his skill at rendering the complexities of the human condition with effortless ease, imbuing his characters with a sense of authentic vulnerability and relatability. Norton, as Incarnation of Time, is a poignant example of this skill, as he struggles to navigate a world that is simultaneously familiar and foreign. Through Norton's struggles, Anthony skillfully illuminates the inherent nature of existence, revealing the profound consequences of our choices, the fragility of human existence, and the boundless power of the human spirit.

Piers Anthony's prose is characterized by a sense of elegance and precision, effortlessly conveying complex ideas and philosophical concepts in an accessible manner. His writing exudes a sense of depth and introspection, plunging readers headfirst into a captivating world of moral complexity, rich symbolism, and thought-provoking themes. This second installment of the "Incarnations of Immortality" series cements Anthony's reputation as a master storyteller, delivering a richly satisfying experience that is equal parts intellectual curiosity, emotional resonance, and visceral suspense.

Ultimately, "Bearing an Hourglass" is a book that defies categorization, a thought-provoking tapestry of philosophical, metaphysical, and supernatural themes woven together into a rich narrative tapestry. Piers Anthony's masterful world-building, engaging characters, and thought-provoking ideas make this second installment of the "Incarnations of Immortality" series a must-read for fans of fantasy, science fiction, and philosophical fiction.

# Ruination (League of Legends #1)

**Author:** Anthony Reynolds  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

In "Ruination", Anthony Reynolds plunges readers into a dark, epic tale of magic, revenge, and an empire on the brink of collapse. Set in the rich world of League of Legends, this debut novel whisks you away to Camavor, a land of brutal legacy and bloody history. At its heart, the story revolves around Kalista, a fiercely loyal and driven general who finds herself torn between her loyalty to her uncle, the increasingly unhinged King Viego, and her own moral compass.

Through Reynolds' masterful narrative, we witness the devastating consequences of unchecked power and the corrupting influence it can have on even the most well-intentioned individuals. Viego's descent into madness and grief following the death of his wife, Isolde, serves as a poignant reminder of the devastating impact of loss and the ease with which it can consume us.

One of the most striking aspects of "Ruination" is its thoughtful exploration of the grey areas between right and wrong. Kalista's predicament serves as a powerful commentary on the notion that the good can sometimes seem like nothing compared to the evil that lurks in the shadows. This resonates deeply with the adage 'the only thing necessary for the triumph of evil is for good men to do nothing', a sentiment that is cleverly echoed throughout the novel.

Reynolds' prose is a masterclass in propulsive storytelling. With a pace that accelerates as the narrative unfolds, he effortlessly weaves together action, intrigue, and tragedy, crafting a narrative that is impossible to put down. His command of the world of Camavor is remarkable, and it's clear he's poured a significant amount of love and care into creating a rich, immersive environment that readers can lose themselves in.

As a fan of epic fantasy, I was thoroughly impressed by the depth and complexity of the characters. Kalista, in particular, is a compelling protagonist, and I appreciated the way Reynolds skillfully developed her inner conflict. His supporting cast, including the enigmatic warden of the Blessed Isles, adds depth and nuance to the narrative.

The comparisons to ancient Greek tragedy are apt. Reynolds draws on timeless themes of love, loss, war, and duty to craft a story that feels both timeless and relevant to modern audiences. "Ruination" is a novel that will appeal to both fans of the League of Legends universe and newcomers alike. The way Reynolds incorporates elements of the video game into the narrative is seamless, adding another layer of depth to the story without ever overpowering the world-buiiding.

In short, "Ruination" is a rousing, thrilling epic fantasy that will leave you breathless and eager for more. With its richly detailed world, compelling characters, and morally complex themes, Reynolds' debut is a worthy addition to the world of League of Legends. If you're a fan of Anthony Ryan, James Islington, or Evan Winter, do not miss out on this unforgettable read.

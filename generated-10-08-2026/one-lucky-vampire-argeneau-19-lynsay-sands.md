# One Lucky Vampire (Argeneau, #19)

**Author:** Lynsay Sands  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

Title: A Bite of Bliss: Lynsay Sands Strikes Gold Again in One Lucky Vampire

In the crowded, often overly brooding landscape of paranormal romance, Lynsay Sands has carved out a brilliantly distinct niche. With her long-running Argeneau series, she proves time and again that vampires do not need to skulk in perpetual gloom to be compelling. In her 2013 installment, One Lucky Vampire, Sands delivers a masterclass in what she does best: blending high-stakes supernatural danger with disarming domestic comedy, sizzling chemistry, and an unmistakable sense of warmth. 

The core theme of One Lucky Vampire revolves around the disruption of the mundane by the extraordinary, and conversely, the grounding power of normalcy for those thrust into a terrifying, immortal existence. Jake Colson is not your typical brooding lord of the night; in fact, he is the ultimate reluctant immortal. Freshly turned and still grappling with the dizzying, blood-drinking realities of his new status, Jake is immediately conscripted into a family favor that lands him squarely on the doorstep of Nicole Phillips. 

Nicole, meanwhile, is simply looking for a housekeeper to help manage her chaotic life, not a towering, impossibly handsome specimen who redefines the word indispensable. Through this classic romantic setup, Sands explores the theme of sanctuary. Nicole needs a protector against an unseen threat targeting her, but on a deeper level, she provides Jake with an anchor. As Jake goes undercover as domestic help, the novel brilliantly juxtaposes the domestic sphere—chopping vegetables, cleaning, and quiet banter—with the looming shadows of danger. Sands suggests that true safety isn't found in a fortified castle, but in the arms of someone who accepts you, fangs and all.

Sands’s authorial style is the true engine driving this narrative. Where many authors in the genre lean heavily into gothic melodrama, Sands infuses her prose with a signature brand of laugh-out-loud, wickedly funny humor. The comedy in One Lucky Vampire largely stems from the sheer awkwardness of Jake navigating his new vampiric physiology while trying to maintain the facade of a mortal housekeeper. It is domestic comedy with a supernatural twist, reminiscent of a classic sitcom injected with raw, magnetic sensuality. 

Yet, the humor never undermines the romance. Sands has a remarkable knack for balancing the lighthearted with the deeply tender. The steamy elements are woven seamlessly into the plot, acting as a natural progression of the intense emotional and physical chemistry between Jake and Nicole. The dialogue is snappy, the pacing is brisk, and the external mystery—figuring out who is targeting Nicole—keeps the pages turning without ever overshadowing the central love story. 

Ultimately, One Lucky Vampire is a testament to Lynsay Sands’s enduring appeal. It does not try to reinvent the wheel; instead, it polishes a beloved formula until it shines. It is a red-hot, irresistibly funny escape that reminds readers why the Argeneau family has remained a staple of paranormal romance for decades. Whether you are a die-hard fan of the series or a newcomer looking for a breezy, steamy read with plenty of heart and humor, Jake and Nicole’s story is an absolute winning bet.

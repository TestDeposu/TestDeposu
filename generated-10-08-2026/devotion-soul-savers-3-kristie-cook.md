# Devotion (Soul Savers, #3)

**Author:** Kristie Cook  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

"Devotion" by Kristie Cook is a captivating continuation of the Soul Savers series, weaving a complex web of supernatural intrigue, romance, and self-discovery. This installment, part of a box set that includes the first three books in the series, delves deeper into the world of angels, demons, and other paranormal creatures, raising the stakes for protagonist Alexis Ames and her loved ones.

Throughout the series, Cook's writing style has been notable for its unique blend of genres, seamlessly merging elements of urban fantasy, romance, and young adult fiction. Her world-building is meticulous, with a richly detailed mythology that underpins the narrative. The author's use of language is engaging, with a keen sense of pacing that propels the reader through the story.

The main theme of "Devotion" is the exploration of Alexis's growth as a character, as she navigates the challenges of her newfound identity as a powerful supernatural being. Her relationships with those around her – particularly her husband, Tristan, and her son – are put to the test as she confronts the harsh realities of her world. Cook skillfully portrays Alexis's inner turmoil, her struggles to reconcile her past and present selves, and her ultimate devotion to those she cares about.

One of the strengths of the series is its ability to balance action, suspense, and emotional depth. Cook's characters are multidimensional and relatable, with distinct voices and motivations. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with complex characters like the enigmatic Tristan and the mysterious matriarch of the Amadis council.

The plot of "Devotion" is character-driven, with a focus on Alexis's personal journey and her relationships. The narrative is layered, with multiple storylines that intersect and collide in unexpected ways. Cook's use of foreshadowing and misdirection adds to the tension, keeping the reader guessing until the very end.

If there is a criticism to be made, it is that the series assumes a significant amount of prior knowledge. While "Devotion" can be read as a standalone novel, it is clear that the story is part of a larger tapestry. Readers new to the series may find themselves struggling to keep up with the intricate world-building and character relationships.

Overall, "Devotion" is a compelling addition to the Soul Savers series, offering a rich and immersive reading experience. Fans of urban fantasy and paranormal romance will find much to appreciate in Cook's writing, and the series is sure to appeal to readers who enjoy the works of authors like J.R. Ward and Charlaine Harris.

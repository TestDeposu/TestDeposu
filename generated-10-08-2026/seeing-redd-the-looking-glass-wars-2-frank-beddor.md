# Seeing Redd (The Looking Glass Wars, #2)

**Author:** Frank Beddor  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2006

In the captivating realm of Wonderland, Frank Beddor masterfully weaves a dark and thrilling tale of power, deception, and the unrelenting quest for justice in Seeing Redd, the second installment of The Looking Glass Wars series. This 2006 publication is a mesmerizing exploration of the blurred lines between reality and fantasy, as a young princess is thrust into a treacherous game for the throne.

Beddor's ingenious reimagining of Lewis Carroll's classic tale challenges the conventional notion of Wonderland as a mere fairy-tale world. Instead, he presents a richly detailed and immersive landscape where the absurd and the ordinary coexist in a swirl of madness. At the heart of this maelstrom is Alyss Heart, the rightful heir to the throne, whose life is forever altered when her ruthless aunt Redd seizes the crown and brutally murders her parents.

As Alyss navigates the uncharted territories of our world, Beddor skillfully juxtaposes the fantastical elements of Wonderland with the mundane realities of Victorian London. This fusion of worlds not only underscores the adaptability and resilience of the human spirit but also serves as a commentary on the societal norms and expectations that govern our lives.

One of the most striking aspects of Beddor's narrative is his ability to balance action, suspense, and emotional depth. The character of Hatter Madigan, Alyss's loyal bodyguard, is a testament to this skill. His unwavering dedication to the princess and his unshakeable commitment to justice make him a compelling and complex figure, whose presence elevates the story to new heights.

Throughout the book, Beddor's writing style is reminiscent of an animated film, as noted by the School Library Journal. His vivid descriptions and breakneck pacing create a sense of urgency, drawing the reader into the world of Wonderland and refusing to let go. The author's use of language is equally impressive, with a keen sense of nuance and subtlety that adds depth and complexity to the narrative.

Ultimately, Seeing Redd is a story about the power of truth and the unyielding quest for justice. As Alyss and Hatter Madigan navigate the treacherous landscape of Wonderland, they must confront the darkness that has consumed their world and reclaim the throne that is rightfully theirs. Beddor's masterful storytelling and vivid world-building make this book an unforgettable read, one that will leave you breathless and eager for more.

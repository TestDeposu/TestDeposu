# C is for Corpse (Kinsey Millhone, #3)

**Author:** Sue Grafton  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1986

**C is for Corpse: A Kinsey Millhone Thriller**

Sue Grafton's "C is for Corpse," the third installment in her acclaimed Kinsey Millhone series, is a masterclass in suspense and intrigue, showcasing the author's signature style that blends clever plotting with a feisty, relatable protagonist. Published in 1986, this novel remains as fresh and engaging as ever, proving that Grafton's work stands the test of time.

The story begins with a harrowing car crash, leaving young Bobby Callahan with partial amnesia and a nagging suspicion that the accident was no mere mishap. Enter Kinsey Millhone, a 32-year-old private investigator with a sharp wit and an unyielding determination to uncover the truth. When Bobby hires Kinsey, he has but a red address book and the name "Blackman" to guide her. However, Bobby's memory loss and subsequent untimely death leave Kinsey with little more than a cryptic trail of clues.

Grafton excels in creating a complex web of intrigue, with each thread of the plot carefully woven to keep readers guessing until the very end. The narrative is peppered with red herrings and unexpected twists, making "C is for Corpse" a true page-turner. Yet, Grafton's prowess lies not just in her intricate plotting but also in her character development. Kinsey Millhone is no ordinary heroine; she's flawed, relatable, and refreshingly independent, making her a standout protagonist in the genre.

Grafton's prose is crisp and concise, her dialogue sharp and witty. She has a knack for creating vivid, atmospheric settings, from the sun-drenched streets of Santa Teresa (a fictionalized Santa Barbara) to the grimy underbelly of the city's criminal underworld. The author's offbeat sense of humor and feisty sense of justice infuse the story with a unique charm, making "C is for Corpse" an exceptionally entertaining read.

In "C is for Corpse," Sue Grafton delivers a gripping, intelligent thriller that showcases her signature style and cements her status as a master of the genre. This is not just a mystery to be solved; it's a journey to be experienced, with a protagonist who's as compelling as the case she's determined to crack.

# This Man Confessed (This Man, #3)

**Author:** Jodi Ellen Malpas  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

**Title: "This Man Confessed" by Jodi Ellen Malpas: A Raw, Emotional Ride to Redemption**

In the thrilling conclusion to Jodi Ellen Malpas' "This Man" trilogy, "This Man Confessed" delivers a captivating blend of passion, suspense, and emotional depth that will leave readers breathless. Malpas masterfully weaves a tale of love, betrayal, and redemption, as Ava and Jesse navigate the complexities of their relationship amidst the grandeur of The Manor.

Malpas' signature style is evident throughout, with vivid, sensual descriptions that transport readers into the hearts and minds of her characters. Her ability to balance intense, steamy encounters with poignant emotional moments is what sets her apart. She doesn't shy away from exploring the darker aspects of Jesse's past, delving into his psyche with a raw, unapologetic honesty that makes his character all the more compelling.

The main theme of "This Man Confessed" is the power of truth and the consequences of secrets. As Ava and Jesse prepare to exchange vows, their pasts threaten to shatter their future. Malpas expertly builds tension, dropping hints and clues that keep readers on the edge of their seats, eager to uncover the truth alongside Ava.

Malpas' exploration of trust, forgiveness, and the complexities of love is what truly elevates this novel. She challenges the conventional notion of a happy ending, instead presenting a realistic portrayal of a relationship that is far from perfect. Ava and Jesse's love story is a testament to the power of communication, understanding, and unconditional love.

The Manor, a character in its own right, serves as a stunning backdrop to the story. Malpas' descriptions of the grand estate are so vivid that readers can almost feel the cool marble beneath their feet and smell the fresh flowers adorning the rooms. The Manor is more than just a setting; it's a symbol of Jesse's past, a testament to his growth, and a sanctuary for Ava and Jesse's love.

"This Man Confessed" is not just a romance novel; it's a journey of self-discovery, forgiveness, and redemption. Malpas' ability to create complex, relatable characters and weave a gripping plot makes this trilogy a standout in the romance genre. This is a book that will leave you feeling emotionally drained, yet utterly satisfied.

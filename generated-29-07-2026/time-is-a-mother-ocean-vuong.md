# Time Is a Mother

**Author:** Ocean Vuong  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

To read Ocean Vuong is to step into a space where language is both a scalpel and a balm. In his deeply anticipated second poetry collection, Time Is a Mother (2022), the bestselling author and MacArthur Fellow returns to the page with a profound urgency, navigating the turbulent aftershocks of personal grief and social upheaval. Following his critically acclaimed debut Night Sky with Exit Wounds and his bestselling novel On Earth We’re Briefly Gorgeous, this new collection cements Vuong’s status as one of the most vital, resonant voices of our contemporary literary landscape. 

The beating heart of Time Is a Mother is the paradox of survival—how to inhabit a world defined by profound absence while stubbornly refusing to let go of tenderness. Written in the wake of the death of his mother, the collection serves as an intimate ledger of loss. Vuong does not write about grief from a safe distance; he sits directly inside its wreckage. Yet, true to the book’s evocative title, time is treated not merely as a thief, but as a generative force—a mother that bears, shapes, and ultimately releases us. Through these poems, Vuong contends with the heavy inheritance of being the product of an American war experienced on American soil, threading the needle between historical trauma and the quiet, domestic moments of everyday existence. 

Stylistically, Vuong’s pen is sharper and more daring than ever. The collection showcases an innovative experimentation with form and language, seamlessly moving between stark, conversational directness and soaring, lyric-driven imagery. His poems circle fragmented lives not to exploit the brokenness, but to locate both the epicenter of the fracture and the unexpected sites of restoration. There is a propulsive energy to his lines; they pull the reader forward with cinematic clarity while demanding that we slow down, linger, and return to them often. Vuong has a rare gift for piercing the reader’s armor without ever raising his voice. He writes directly to our shared humanity, capturing the current socio-cultural moment without losing the timeless, elegiac quality that defines great poetry.

Ultimately, Time Is a Mother is a triumph of empathy and resilience. It is a book that asks how we return to ourselves after the unthinkable has happened, offering the quiet reminder that even in the face of relentless violence, tenderness remains our bravest act. Bold, prescient, and utterly unforgettable, Vuong’s latest offering is both a sacred return and a fearless forging forth.

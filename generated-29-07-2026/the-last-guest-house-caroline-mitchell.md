# The Last Guest House

**Author:** Caroline Mitchell  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

The Last Guest House by Caroline Mitchell is a gripping and atmospheric thriller that will keep you on the edge of your seat from start to finish. The story follows DC Nicola McKenna and her boyfriend Matt as they embark on a romantic weekend in the Isle of Skye, only to find themselves stranded in a mysterious guest house with a dark past.

As the snowstorm closes in and the couple's son George disappears, Nicola's skepticism turns to desperation as she tries to unravel the secrets of The Loch House. With its intricate plot, well-developed characters, and a creepy atmosphere, this novel is a must-read for fans of The Sanatorium and The Hunting Party.

Caroline Mitchell's writing style is masterful, weaving together a complex web of twists and turns that will keep you guessing until the very end. Her ability to craft a sense of tension and foreboding is unparalleled, making this book a truly unputdownable read.

One of the standout features of this novel is its ability to balance atmosphere and plot. The author's use of setting is exceptional, transporting the reader to the isolated and eerie landscape of the Isle of Skye. The Loch House itself is a character in the story, its dark history and strange rules adding to the sense of unease and tension.

The characters in the novel are also well-developed and complex, with Nicola being a particularly strong protagonist. Her determination and resourcefulness in the face of danger make her a compelling and relatable character.

Overall, The Last Guest House is a gripping and atmospheric thriller that will appeal to fans of the genre. With its intricate plot, creepy atmosphere, and well-developed characters, this novel is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and unputdownable read.

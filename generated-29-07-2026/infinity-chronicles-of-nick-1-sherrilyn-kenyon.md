# Infinity (Chronicles of Nick, #1)

**Author:** Sherrilyn Kenyon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In "Infinity," the first installment of the Chronicles of Nick series, Sherrilyn Kenyon masterfully weaves a captivating narrative that delves into the struggles of adolescence, the complexities of identity, and the eternal battle between good and evil. At its core, the novel explores the theme of self-discovery, as Nick Gautier navigates the treacherous landscape of high school while confronting the darker aspects of his reality.

Kenyon's writing style is characterized by her signature blend of humor, action, and poignancy, making "Infinity" an irresistible page-turner. Her protagonist, Nick Gautier, is a lovable, wisecracking teenager who embodies the angst and uncertainty of adolescence. As he finds himself entangled in a world of supernatural creatures, Nick's sarcastic remarks and humorous observations provide a welcome respite from the darkness that surrounds him.

One of the most striking aspects of Kenyon's writing is her ability to create a richly detailed world that seamlessly blends the mundane with the paranormal. The Dark-Hunters, a group of immortal vampire slayers, are introduced as a fascinating and complex aspect of this world, adding depth and intrigue to the narrative. The author's vivid descriptions of the supernatural creatures and their habitats are both captivating and unsettling, drawing the reader into the world of the novel.

Throughout the book, Kenyon tackles themes of identity, loyalty, and the struggles of growing up. Nick's relationships with his mother, his friends, and his mentors are multifaceted and authentic, reflecting the complexities of adolescent life. The author's portrayal of the mother-son dynamic is particularly noteworthy, as Nick's mother struggles to balance her desire to protect her son with the need to give him independence.

The pacing of the novel is expertly handled, with a narrative that flows effortlessly from one scene to the next. Kenyon's use of cliffhangers and plot twists keeps the reader engaged and invested in Nick's journey, making it impossible to put the book down.

In conclusion, "Infinity" is a gripping and entertaining read that will appeal to fans of urban fantasy and young adult fiction. Sherrilyn Kenyon's masterful storytelling and engaging characters make this novel a compelling addition to the genre. With its unique blend of humor, action, and poignancy, "Infinity" is sure to leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in the Chronicles of Nick series.

# Pleasure of a Dark Prince (Immortals After Dark, #8)

**Author:** Kresley Cole  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Kresley Cole continues to reign as an undisputed heavyweight of paranormal romance with her sensational eighth installment in the Immortals After Dark series, Pleasure of a Dark Prince. Published in 2010, this high-stakes, breathless novel delivers everything that fans have come to adore about Cole’s gritty, hyper-sensual universe: alpha-male posturing, fierce protective instincts, undeniable chemistry, and a sprawling, mythic world that feels both dangerous and intoxicating.

At the heart of this narrative is a timeless romance theme: the predator and the huntress, twisted and elevated through Cole’s signature supernatural lens. Garreth MacRieve, the ruthless and formidable Prince of the Lykae, is the epitome of the tormented alpha. For ages, he has watched Lucia from the shadows, playing the silent guardian to a woman whose very existence is fraught with peril. Lucia the Huntress is a compelling counterpart—mysterious, exquisite, and burdened by dark secrets that threaten to consume her. The central theme explores the friction between vulnerability and fierce independence. Lucia’s pride and guarded nature clash deliciously against Garreth’s absolute, unyielding need to possess and protect her. Cole uses Garreth’s covert surveillance not as a creepy intrusion, but as a slow-burn catalyst, trapping Lucia in a web of undeniable, wanton desire that serves as her ultimate undoing and her greatest salvation.

Cole’s writing style is a masterclass in pacing and sensory immersion. She writes with a visceral intensity that makes every growl, touch, and heartbeat vibrate off the page. Her prose is fast-paced, sharp, and laced with a biting, dark humor that keeps the heavy paranormal lore grounded and endlessly entertaining. She excels at world-building, effortlessly weaving complex mythologies involving Valkyries, Lykae, and Fae without ever letting the exposition bog down the narrative momentum. Instead, the world functions as a pressure cooker, heightening the emotional stakes and making the romance feel all the more urgent and earth-shattering. 

What makes Pleasure of a Dark Prince stand out in the crowded paranormal romance genre is Cole’s ability to balance raw, animalistic passion with genuine emotional depth. Garreth is not merely a brute; his devotion to Lucia borders on the sacred, turning their explosive encounters into moments of profound emotional release. Lucia, meanwhile, is no damsel in distress; her internal battles give her a gritty resilience that matches her lethal archery skills. 

Ultimately, Kresley Cole delivers a seductive, high-octane triumph. Pleasure of a Dark Prince is a decadent treat for returning fans and a thrilling entry point for newcomers, proving once again that when it comes to dark, passionate paranormal romance, Cole operates in a league of her own.

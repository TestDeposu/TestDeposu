# Bad Girls Don't Die (Bad Girls Don't Die, #1)

**Author:** Katie Alender  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Bad Girls Don't Die, the debut novel from Katie Alender, is a gripping and unsettling young adult mystery that delves into the darker side of family dynamics, sibling relationships, and the supernatural. On the surface, Alexis appears to be a typical teenager, navigating the complexities of high school and her dysfunctional family life. However, when her 12-year-old sister Kasey begins to exhibit bizarre behavior, Alexis is forced to confront the eerie and unexplainable events that are unfolding around her.

As the story progresses, Alender masterfully crafts a sense of tension and unease, expertly weaving together elements of horror, suspense, and mystery. The old family house, with its creaking doors and unexplained occurrences, becomes a character in its own right, adding to the overall sense of foreboding and dread. Through Alexis's narrative, Alender explores the theme of family secrets and the devastating consequences of keeping them buried.

One of the standout aspects of Bad Girls Don't Die is Alender's writing style, which is both lyrical and concise. Her prose is peppered with vivid imagery and witty dialogue, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world she has created. The characters are well-developed and relatable, with Alexis being a particularly strong protagonist. Her anti-social attitude and sarcastic humor make her a likable and authentic voice, and her desperation to save her sister is palpable.

The supernatural elements of the story are skillfully handled, with Alender avoiding the pitfall of relying on tired tropes or clichés. Instead, she creates a sense of uncertainty and ambiguity, leaving the reader questioning what is real and what is just Alexis's imagination. This ambiguity adds to the overall sense of tension and suspense, making it impossible to put the book down.

The relationships between the characters are also noteworthy, particularly the complex and often fraught dynamic between Alexis and Kasey. Their sisterly bond is expertly portrayed, with Alender capturing the nuances of sibling relationships with ease. The romance between Alexis and the class president is also sweet and subtle, adding a touch of normalcy to the otherwise chaotic world.

If you're a fan of supernatural mysteries with a strong focus on character development and atmospheric tension, then Bad Girls Don't Die is an absolute must-read. With its unique blend of horror, suspense, and family drama, this book is sure to appeal to fans of authors like Lauren Oliver and Nova Ren Suma.

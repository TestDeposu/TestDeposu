# The Red Scrolls of Magic (The Eldest Curses, #1)

**Author:** Cassandra Clare  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

The Red Scrolls of Magic, co-authored by Cassandra Clare and Wesley Chu, is a captivating tale that seamlessly weaves together elements of fantasy, adventure, and romance, set against the rich backdrop of European cities. This novel, the first in The Eldest Curses series, is a testament to Clare's skill in crafting narratives that are both thrilling and emotionally resonant.

At its core, The Red Scrolls of Magic is a story about identity, love, and the complexities of relationships. The narrative centers around Magnus Bane, a charismatic warlock, and Alec Lightwood, a Shadowhunter, as they navigate their blossoming romance amidst a backdrop of demonic cults and ancient magic. What begins as a romantic getaway across Europe quickly turns into a perilous quest to track down the Crimson Hand, a cult inadvertently founded by Magnus years ago as a joke.

One of the standout aspects of this novel is its exploration of Magnus and Alec's relationship. Clare and Chu masterfully portray the intricacies of their bond, delving into the vulnerabilities and strengths that make their love so compelling. The authors' portrayal of the couple's interactions is authentic and tender, making their romance a highlight of the story.

The world-building in The Red Scrolls of Magic is, as expected from Clare's Shadowhunter Chronicles, meticulous and immersive. The authors' vivid descriptions of European cities, steeped in history and mythology, provide a rich tapestry for the narrative to unfold. The magical system, too, is complex and well-realized, adding depth to the story.

The pacing of the novel is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and quieter moments that allow the characters to breathe. Clare and Chu's writing styles complement each other seamlessly, resulting in a narrative that is both engaging and polished.

If there's one aspect where the novel falls slightly short, it's in the development of the supporting cast. While Magnus and Alec are well-fleshed out, some of the secondary characters feel somewhat one-dimensional. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise engaging narrative.

In conclusion, The Red Scrolls of Magic is a captivating addition to the Shadowhunter Chronicles. With its blend of fantasy, adventure, and romance, this novel is sure to delight fans of the series and newcomers alike. Clare and Chu's collaboration has resulted in a story that is both thrilling and emotionally resonant, making it a must-read for anyone who enjoys urban fantasy.

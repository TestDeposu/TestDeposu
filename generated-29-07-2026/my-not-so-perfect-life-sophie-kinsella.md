# My Not So Perfect Life

**Author:** Sophie Kinsella  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

**My Not So Perfect Life: A Hilarious Mirror to Our Social Media Obsession**

In her latest novel, Sophie Kinsella masterfully explores the chasm between our curated online personas and the messy reality of our lives. "My Not So Perfect Life" is a delightful satire that will leave you laughing out loud while also prompting introspection.

Kinsella's protagonist, Katie Brenner, is a relatable everywoman, navigating the treacherous waters of modern life and work. She's a millennial who's not afraid to use Instagram to project an aspirational life, even as she's struggling with rent, oddball roommates, and office politics. Her boss, Demeter Farlowe, seems to have it all - a perfect family, a posh townhouse, and a brilliant career. But as Katie's life unravels and she retreats to her family's farm, she discovers that Demeter's life isn't as perfect as it appears.

Kinsella's strength lies in her ability to create vivid, relatable characters and hilarious situations. She has a knack for capturing the absurdity of modern life, from the pressures of social media to the complexities of workplace dynamics. Her wit is sharp, her observations astute, and her storytelling engaging.

At its heart, "My Not So Perfect Life" is a critique of our social media-obsessed culture. It's a reminder that we're all just trying to figure it out, and that our lives, with all their imperfections, are enough. Kinsella doesn't shy away from exploring the darker side of this obsession - the anxiety, the loneliness, the constant comparison - but she does so with humor and heart.

The novel is a fast-paced, entertaining read that will keep you guessing until the end. It's a perfect blend of humor, heart, and social commentary, proving once again why Kinsella is a beloved author.

# Twice Tempted (Night Prince, #2)

**Author:** Jeaniene Frost  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

Jeaniene Frost returns with Twice Tempted, the sizzling second installment in her Night Prince series, proving once again why she remains a powerhouse in contemporary paranormal romance. Published in 2013, this thrilling novel dives deeper into the intoxicating and perilous world of the undead, capturing the precarious balance between mortal vulnerability and immortal obsession. Frost weaves a narrative where love is not merely a comforting emotion, but a high-stakes battlefield governed by pride, power, and dark secrets.

At the heart of Twice Tempted lies the central theme of emotional recalibration in the face of overwhelming power dynamics. Leila, a mortal thrust into the supernatural elite by virtue of her psychic abilities and her relationship with the infamous Vlad Tepes—the Prince of Darkness—finds herself grappling with a profound crisis of agency. When her psychic gifts inexplicably begin to fail her, Leila feels stripped of her independence just as Vlad pulls away, wrapping himself in a chilling wall of emotional distance. Frost brilliantly explores Leila’s refusal to play the victim. Rather than shrinking into the background of Vlad’s terrifying and majestic world, Leila demands transparency and emotional equity. The core tension of the novel asks a piercing question: Can a mortal woman truly stand as an equal beside a literal monster who commands fear and respect across continents, especially when he refuses to utter the three words she desperately needs to hear?

Frost’s signature style shines through every page, characterized by snappy dialogue, breakneck pacing, and a cinematic flair for action. She expertly balances heavy, life-or-death suspense with razor-sharp wit and smoldering sensuality. The author’s world-building is seamless, dragging the reader from the opulent, dangerous shadows of Vlad’s domain into the glaring, nostalgic lights of the carnival circuit—a brilliant narrative choice that forces Leila back into her roots just as tragedy strikes. Frost knows how to manipulate atmosphere; the carnival, once a place of wonder, transforms into a claustrophobic hunting ground where danger lurks behind every cotton candy-colored tent.

The romantic tension is amplified by the introduction of complications that test the foundational trust between the protagonists. When a cunning killer sets their sights on Leila, she is forced to navigate a treacherous web of loyalties. The narrative introduces a compelling love triangle, though Frost keeps the focus sharply on Leila’s internal growth. The tortured knight offering friendship provides a stark, gentle contrast to Vlad’s volatile, fiery passion, forcing Leila to evaluate what she truly needs versus what her soul demands. Yet, it is the volatile chemistry between Leila and Vlad that anchors the book. Their interactions crackle with electric energy, showcasing Frost’s talent for writing alpha heroes who are genuinely formidable, yet uniquely vulnerable to the heroines who pierce their armor.

Twice Tempted is more than just a paranormal romance sequel; it is a story of reclamation. Leila must learn to trust her instincts even when her psychic powers abandon her, proving that her strength comes from her spirit rather than her supernatural gifts. Frost delivers a roller-coaster ride of romance, mystery, and supernatural peril that satisfies fans of the genre while pushing her characters into darker, more emotionally resonant territory. For readers who crave high-stakes romance with a fierce heroine and a brooding, dangerously protective vampire, Frost’s latest offering is an absolute must-read that leaves you breathless until the very last page.

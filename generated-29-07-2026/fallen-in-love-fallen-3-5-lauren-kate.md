# Fallen in Love (Fallen, #3.5)

**Author:** Lauren Kate  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In the captivating world of Lauren Kate's Fallen series, love knows no bounds, not even those of time or mortality. In Fallen in Love, the third companion novel to the series, Kate masterfully weaves together four extraordinary love stories that intersect on a romantic Valentine's Day in medieval England. This novella is a beautiful testament to the power of love in all its forms, and a must-read for fans of the series and newcomers alike.

The story is a delicate dance of unexpected, unrequited, forbidden, and eternal love, each thread carefully intertwined to create a rich tapestry of emotions. Miles and Shelby, two characters who have been on the periphery of the series, finally find love in the most unexpected way, proving that even the most skeptical of hearts can be won over. Roland, on the other hand, learns a painful lesson about the fleeting nature of love, a reminder that even the most beautiful relationships can be lost in the blink of an eye.

Arriane's story is one of fierce passion, a love so all-consuming that it burns brightly, but also threatens to destroy everything in its path. And then, of course, there is the long-awaited reunion of Daniel and Luce, a night that will change the course of their eternal love story forever.

Kate's writing is as captivating as ever, her prose a perfect blend of poetry and passion. She has a way of transporting readers to another time and place, of making them feel like they are part of the story, not just observers. Her characters are multi-dimensional and relatable, each one with their own unique voice and perspective.

One of the things that sets Kate apart from other authors is her ability to balance light and dark, to create a world that is both beautiful and treacherous. Her writing is not just about the romance, but about the journey, the struggles, and the triumphs of the characters. She has a way of making readers feel like they are part of something bigger than themselves, something that transcends time and mortality.

In Fallen in Love, Kate explores the many facets of love, from the all-consuming passion of Arriane's story to the tender, unexpected love of Miles and Shelby. She reminds us that love is not just a feeling, but a choice, a decision to put someone else's needs before our own, to sacrifice and to surrender.

Overall, Fallen in Love is a beautiful addition to the Fallen series, a must-read for fans of Lauren Kate and anyone who has ever been in love. It is a testament to the power of love to transform and transcend, to overcome even the most impossible of obstacles.

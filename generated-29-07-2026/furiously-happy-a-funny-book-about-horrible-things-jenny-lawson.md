# Furiously Happy: A Funny Book About Horrible Things

**Author:** Jenny Lawson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In "Furiously Happy: A Funny Book About Horrible Things," Jenny Lawson tackles the heavy topic of mental illness with her signature humor and unapologetic honesty. This book is a refreshing take on the often-stigmatized subject, proving that laughter and joy can be found even in the darkest of times.

Lawson's writing style is engaging, relatable, and laugh-out-loud funny. She shares personal anecdotes and stories about her struggles with depression and anxiety, but instead of dwelling on the negative, she chooses to focus on the absurdity and humor in these situations. Her honesty is both captivating and inspiring, making readers feel like they're not alone in their own struggles.

One of the main themes of the book is the importance of embracing our flaws and imperfections. Lawson argues that it's these very things that make us who we are, and that by accepting and even celebrating them, we can find true joy and happiness. This message is conveyed through a series of hilarious and often ridiculous stories, including the infamous kangaroo incident, which showcases Lawson's unique ability to find humor in even the most mundane situations.

Throughout the book, Lawson also touches on the idea of "furiously happy" moments – those times when we're able to take the ordinary and make it extraordinary. She argues that it's these moments that make life worth living, and that by focusing on them, we can find a sense of purpose and fulfillment. This theme is woven throughout the book, and is a powerful reminder to appreciate the little things in life.

Lawson's writing is also notable for its conversational tone, which makes readers feel like they're having a conversation with an old friend. Her use of humor and pop culture references adds to this tone, making the book feel both relatable and entertaining.

Overall, "Furiously Happy" is a must-read for anyone who has struggled with mental illness, or for those who simply want to learn more about the subject. Lawson's unique perspective and writing style make the book both informative and entertaining, and her message of hope and resilience is inspiring.

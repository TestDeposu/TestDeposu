# Dark Side of the Moon (Dark-Hunter, #9; Were-Hunter, #3)

**Author:** Sherrilyn Kenyon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

**Dark Side of the Moon: A Thrilling Dance with the Supernatural**

Sherrilyn Kenyon, the master of paranormal romance and urban fantasy, weaves another spellbinding tale in "Dark Side of the Moon," the ninth installment of her Dark-Hunter series and the third in the Were-Hunter spin-off. Kenyon's signature style—an intoxicating blend of action, suspense, and romance—is on full display, drawing readers into a world where the line between the mundane and the extraordinary blurs.

At the heart of the story is Susan Michaels, a once-renowned journalist now reduced to writing tabloid fodder. Her life takes a dramatic turn when she stumbles upon a vampire conspiracy threatening Seattle. Meanwhile, Ravyn, a centuries-old shapeshifting vampire slayer, is drawn into the same web, forced to confront his past and trust a human woman with his deepest secrets. The stage is set for a thrilling dance between two unlikely allies, each with their own demons to slay.

Kenyon excels in creating compelling, flawed characters that readers can't help but root for. Susan, with her snarky wit and tenacity, is a refreshing heroine, while Ravyn, the brooding, honor-bound warrior, is a swoon-worthy hero. Their chemistry is palpable, and their slow-burn romance is as captivating as the high-stakes plot.

Kenyon's world-building is as impressive as ever. She seamlessly integrates ancient mythology and supernatural creatures into a contemporary urban setting, creating a rich, immersive backdrop for her story. The vampires in "Dark Side of the Moon" are not your typical bloodsuckers; they're powerful, cunning, and terrifying, making the stakes feel genuinely high.

Kenyon's prose is fast-paced and engaging, drawing readers in and keeping them on the edge of their seats. She masterfully balances action, dialogue, and introspection, ensuring that the story never feels stagnant. Her unique blend of humor, suspense, and romance makes "Dark Side of the Moon" a gripping, emotional rollercoaster.

In "Dark Side of the Moon," Kenyon explores themes of trust, redemption, and the power of truth. Susan and Ravyn must learn to trust each other—and themselves—despite their past mistakes. Their journey is a poignant reminder that even in the darkest of times, hope and love can prevail.

Fans of Kenyon's previous works will find much to love in "Dark Side of the Moon." New readers, however, should be warned: this book is a gateway drug to a full-blown addiction to Kenyon's captivating storytelling. Once you've stepped into her world, there's no turning back.

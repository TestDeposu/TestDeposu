# House of Hollow

**Author:** Krystal Sutherland  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In the mesmerizing and darkly twisted world of Krystal Sutherland's "House of Hollow," the lines between reality and the supernatural are expertly blurred, leaving readers on the edge of their seats. This modern fairytale is a gripping tale of three sisters, Iris, Grey, and Vivi Hollow, who have been shrouded in mystery since their unexplained disappearance as children in Scotland. Their return a month later, with no recollection of the events that transpired, marked the beginning of a series of inexplicable and terrifying occurrences that follow them like a dark shadow.

At the heart of the story is 17-year-old Iris Hollow, who is desperately trying to lead a normal life, despite the strange and unsettling aura that surrounds her family. Her two older sisters, Grey and Vivi, are the epitome of glamour and beauty, but their lives are a tangled web of secrets and lies. When Grey vanishes without a trace, leaving behind a trail of cryptic clues, Iris and Vivi embark on a perilous journey to uncover the truth about their sister's disappearance and the sinister forces that are at play.

Sutherland's writing is a masterclass in atmospheric storytelling, weaving a spell that is both haunting and hypnotic. Her use of language is deliberate and evocative, conjuring up a world that is both familiar and yet, utterly alien. The Hollow sisters are complex and multifaceted characters, full of contradictions and flaws, making them relatable and human, despite their supernatural circumstances.

One of the most striking aspects of "House of Hollow" is its exploration of the theme of identity. The Hollow sisters are caught between two worlds, struggling to reconcile their human and supernatural selves. As they navigate this treacherous landscape, they are forced to confront the darkest aspects of their own natures and the evil that lurks within. This is a story about the fluidity of identity, the blurring of boundaries, and the terrifying consequences of embracing one's true self.

Sutherland's style is reminiscent of the great Gothic writers, with its dark, foreboding atmosphere and sense of creeping dread. The pacing is expertly handled, with a narrative that twists and turns like a serpent, keeping readers guessing until the very end. The author's use of imagery is vivid and unsettling, conjuring up a world that is both beautiful and grotesque.

In "House of Hollow," Krystal Sutherland has created a world that is both fascinating and terrifying, a place where the supernatural and the human coexist in a delicate balance of power. This is a story that will appeal to fans of dark fantasy and horror, as well as those who enjoy a good mystery. With its complex characters, atmospheric setting, and expertly woven plot, "House of Hollow" is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and unsettling tale.

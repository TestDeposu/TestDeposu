# What Is the What

**Author:** Dave Eggers  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

In "What Is the What," Dave Eggers crafts a mesmerizing narrative that delves into the life of Valentino Achak Deng, a Sudanese refugee who embodies the unyielding spirit of the Lost Boys. This sprawling epic, which defies traditional notions of memoir and fiction, is a searing testament to the human capacity for survival and hope in the face of unimaginable adversity.

Eggers' masterful storytelling weaves together Valentino's journey, from the age of seven, when he is forced to flee his village in Sudan, to his eventual resettlement in the United States. The narrative is a rich tapestry of suspense, humor, and heartache, as Valentino navigates the treacherous landscapes of war-torn Africa and the often-perplexing world of Western culture.

Through Valentino's story, Eggers sheds light on the lives of the Lost Boys, a generation of Sudanese children who were displaced, orphaned, and forced to fend for themselves in a desperate bid for survival. The author's meticulous research and intimate understanding of Valentino's experiences imbue the narrative with a profound sense of authenticity, making the reader an unwitting witness to the atrocities of war and the resilience of the human spirit.

One of the most striking aspects of Eggers' style is his ability to balance the gravity of Valentino's experiences with a deep sense of humor and irony. This is a narrative that can effortlessly pivot from the horrors of war to the absurdities of American culture, often in the same breath. This tonal dexterity not only makes for a compelling read but also underscores the complexities of Valentino's journey, as he struggles to reconcile his past with his present.

Throughout the book, Eggers grapples with the complexities of identity, culture, and belonging. Valentino's experiences serve as a powerful lens through which to examine the tensions between traditional and modern societies, as well as the challenges of assimilation in a foreign land. The author's nuanced exploration of these themes is both thought-provoking and deeply moving, inviting the reader to reflect on their own assumptions about identity, community, and the human condition.

Ultimately, "What Is the What" is a testament to the transformative power of storytelling. Eggers' narrative is not only a tribute to Valentino's indomitable spirit but also a powerful exploration of the human experience. This is a book that will leave readers changed, perhaps even haunted, by the memories of Valentino's journey. It is a reminder that, even in the darkest of times, hope can be a potent catalyst for survival, and that the human spirit can endure even the most unimaginable hardships.

# Fury (New Species, #1)

**Author:** Laurann Dohner  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

"Fury" by Laurann Dohner is a heart-stopping, action-packed romance that delves into the darker aspects of human nature and the unbreakable bonds of love. This gripping tale of a tormented New Species, Fury, and his unlikely savior, Ellie, is a must-read for fans of paranormal romance and science fiction.

At its core, "Fury" explores the theme of redemption and the transformative power of love. Ellie, a brilliant scientist, unwittingly becomes entangled in a sinister plot by a pharmaceutical company to create hybrid species. Her discovery of these experiments sets off a chain reaction that changes her life forever. Fury, one of the New Species, has been subjected to unimaginable cruelty and abuse, leaving him with a deep-seated hatred for humans. When Ellie and Fury's paths cross, their initial animosity slowly gives way to a fierce attraction that challenges everything they thought they knew about themselves and each other.

Dohner's writing style is masterful, weaving a complex narrative that seamlessly blends elements of science fiction, action, and romance. Her vivid descriptions of the New Species and their unique abilities will captivate fans of the paranormal genre. The author's use of descriptive language effectively conveys the intense emotions and primal instincts of her characters, making it easy to become fully invested in their stories.

One of the standout aspects of "Fury" is its exploration of the darker aspects of human nature. Dohner tackles themes of cruelty, abuse, and the ethics of scientific experimentation with unflinching honesty. The author's portrayal of the pharmaceutical company's sinister motives and the devastating consequences of their actions serves as a stark reminder of the dangers of unchecked power and greed.

The romance between Ellie and Fury is a slow-burning, intense affair that simmers with tension and passion. Their relationship is complex and multifaceted, with both characters struggling to reconcile their feelings with their past experiences. Dohner expertly crafts a narrative that balances the darker elements of the story with a deep sense of hope and redemption.

If you're a fan of paranormal romance, science fiction, or action-packed adventures, "Fury" is a must-read. With its unique blend of suspense, intrigue, and passion, this book will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very end. Be prepared for a wild ride filled with twists and turns that will leave you breathless and eager for more.

# A Mango-Shaped Space

**Author:** Wendy Mass  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

In A Mango-Shaped Space, Wendy Mass crafts a poignant and captivating coming-of-age tale that delves into the complexities of self-discovery and the extraordinary experience of living with synesthesia. This award-winning novel tells the story of Mia Winchell, a young girl whose senses are inextricably intertwined, allowing her to perceive the world in a kaleidoscope of colors, textures, and patterns.

Through Mia's journey, Mass masterfully explores the themes of identity, acceptance, and the struggles of growing up. As Mia navigates the challenges of adolescence, she must also confront the difficulties of living with a condition that sets her apart from her peers. With sensitivity and nuance, Mass portrays the tension between Mia's desire for normalcy and her need to acknowledge and celebrate her unique gift.

One of the most striking aspects of Mass's writing style is her ability to convey the intricacies of Mia's synesthetic experiences. With vivid and evocative language, Mass transports readers into Mia's world, where numbers have colors, sounds have textures, and emotions have scents. This immersive storytelling allows readers to experience the world through Mia's eyes, fostering a deep empathy and understanding of her condition.

Mass's prose is also notable for its accessibility and simplicity. She tackles complex themes and emotions with a light touch, making the novel an enjoyable and engaging read for middle-grade readers and adults alike. The writing is free of pretension, and the narrative flows effortlessly, carrying the reader on a journey of self-discovery and growth.

The character development in A Mango-Shaped Space is another of Mass's strengths. Mia is a relatable and endearing protagonist, whose struggles and triumphs are deeply human. The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, with nuanced portrayals of Mia's family, friends, and mentors. The relationships between characters are authentic and heartfelt, adding depth and richness to the narrative.

Throughout the novel, Mass weaves together elements of magical realism, infusing the story with a sense of wonder and enchantment. The mango-shaped space of the title is a metaphor for the boundless possibilities of Mia's imagination, a symbol of the transformative power of creativity and self-expression.

In conclusion, A Mango-Shaped Space is a captivating and thought-provoking novel that explores the complexities of identity, acceptance, and the human experience. Wendy Mass's masterful storytelling, vivid language, and nuanced character development make this book a must-read for fans of Wonder and Counting by Sevens. With its uplifting message and universal themes, this novel is sure to resonate with readers of all ages.

# Mine (Real, #2)

**Author:** Katy Evans  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In "Mine", the highly-anticipated sequel to "Real", Katy Evans masterfully delves into the tumultuous world of professional fighter Remington 'Remy' Tate and his captivating connection with Brooke Dumas. The stakes are raised as we're dropped back into the underground fighting circuit, where passion and heartbreak collide.

Evans' writing style is as visceral as Remy's fighting spirit, leaving readers breathless and invested in the couple's struggles. Their love story is a force to be reckoned with, forged in the fiery depths of Remy's world, where the strong survive and the brave thrive. But what unfolds is not a sugary sweet romance; it's a gut-wrenching exploration of the human condition.

With 'Mine', Evans tackles the complex themes of love, vulnerability, and resilience. Remy, a raw, unapologetic powerhouse of a man, finds himself inexplicably drawn to Brooke, the only person to ever crack his impenetrable facade. Their connection is electric, but the darkness that surrounds Remy threatens to tear them apart. As Brooke is yanked away from Remington's orbit, the space between them becomes a chasm of despair and longing.

Throughout the book, Evans masterfully navigates the blurred lines between love, obsession, and possession. Remington's all-consuming desire for Brooke fuels the narrative, leaving the reader questioning the fine line between devotion and possessiveness. As the world around them disintegrates, it becomes clear that Brooke and Remy's bond is forged in the fires of destruction – a bond as fierce as it is heartbreaking.

Evans' skillful characterization brings the world of 'Mine' to vivid life. We're thrust into the world of underground fighting, a realm where men are gods and only the strongest survive. Remington Tate is a complex, mesmerizing creation, at once intimidating and captivating. His raw emotion and passion make him relatable, humanizing him in a way that draws the reader in. Then there's Brooke, a shining star in her own right – intelligent, strong-willed, and uniquely resilient.

Katy Evans' prose is evocative, precise, and raw. She tackles the harsh realities of Remington's world with unflinching honesty, making the pain and suffering of those involved palpable. It's easy to become lost in the narrative, swept up in the tidal wave of emotions that permeates every page.

One can't help but root for the ill-fated pair as they battle against the odds to reclaim each other. Will they find a way to bridge the distance between them and reignite the passion that once fueled their relationship, or will the forces opposing them tear them asunder?

'Mine' is a raw, intense, and deeply affecting ride that plunges readers into a world of gritty intensity and all-consuming passion. As with 'Real', Evans' masterful exploration of love, vulnerability, and strength propels this story forward. If you're anything like me, you'll find yourself clinging to the edge of your seat, fingers crossed that this star-crossed pair finds a way to beat the odds and emerge stronger, together.

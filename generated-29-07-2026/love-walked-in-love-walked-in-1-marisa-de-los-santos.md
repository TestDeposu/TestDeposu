# Love Walked In (Love Walked In, #1)

**Author:** Marisa de los Santos  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2005

In "Love Walked In," Marisa de los Santos weaves a captivating tale that delves into the complexities of human relationships, love, and the power of unexpected connections. This novel is a poignant exploration of the human experience, masterfully intertwining the lives of two women from different walks of life.

At the heart of the story lies Cornelia Brown, a young woman whose life is turned upside down by the arrival of Martin Grace, a charming and sophisticated man who walks into her coffee shop. As their relationship blossoms, Cornelia finds herself entangled in a web of emotions, forcing her to reevaluate her perceptions of love and happiness. Meanwhile, Clare Hobbs, an eleven-year-old girl abandoned by her mother, embarks on a journey to find her lost father, leading her to cross paths with Cornelia at the coffee shop.

The author's writing style is lyrical and evocative, with a keen eye for detail that brings the characters to life. De los Santos's prose is infused with a deep understanding of human emotions, making it easy to become invested in the lives of Cornelia and Clare. The narrative is rich in symbolism, with old movies serving as a metaphor for the idealized versions of love and relationships that we often strive for.

One of the most striking aspects of "Love Walked In" is its exploration of the complexities of female relationships. The bond between Cornelia and Clare is a beautiful portrayal of the power of female friendship and the ways in which women can support and uplift each other. Through their relationship, de los Santos highlights the importance of found family and the ways in which we can create our own support systems in the absence of traditional family structures.

The novel also explores the theme of love in all its forms, from romantic love to familial love and self-love. De los Santos shows how love can be both redemptive and destructive, highlighting the ways in which it can bring people together while also causing pain and heartache. Through the characters' experiences, the author illustrates the importance of self-love and self-acceptance, emphasizing that true happiness can only be achieved by embracing our flaws and imperfections.

In conclusion, "Love Walked In" is a beautifully written novel that explores the complexities of human relationships and the power of love. Marisa de los Santos's writing is engaging, and her characters are well-developed and relatable. This book is a must-read for anyone who enjoys stories about love, family, and friendship.

# We Begin at the End

**Author:** Chris Whitaker  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In the midst of a literary landscape often dominated by formulaic thrillers, Chris Whitaker's "We Begin at the End" emerges as a vibrant, unputdownable masterpiece that defies genre conventions. This instant New York Times bestseller is a powerful exploration of the complexities of human relationships, the blurred lines between right and wrong, and the enduring power of love and redemption.

At the heart of this extraordinary novel are two unforgettable characters: Duchess Day Radley, a fierce and self-proclaimed thirteen-year-old outlaw, and Walk, the chief of police with a wounded past. Duchess, with her unflinching determination to protect her five-year-old brother Robin and her incapable mother Star, embodies the resilience and grit of a true survivor. Walk, on the other hand, is a complex, brooding figure haunted by the ghosts of his past, particularly the testimony that sent his best friend Vincent King to prison decades ago.

As Vincent's release from prison looms, the fragile equilibrium of their lives is disrupted, forcing Duchess and Walk to confront the dark secrets and unresolved tensions that have been simmering beneath the surface. Whitaker masterfully weaves together multiple narrative threads, expertly manipulating the tension and suspense to create a story that is both a gripping thriller and a poignant exploration of the human condition.

One of the most striking aspects of Whitaker's writing is his ability to craft characters that are at once flawed and relatable, multidimensional and authentic. Duchess, with her tough exterior and vulnerable heart, is a particularly memorable creation, and her voice is both raw and lyrical. The supporting cast, including Walk, Star, and Vincent, is equally well-drawn, with each character bringing their own distinct perspective and emotional depth to the story.

Whitaker's prose is equally impressive, with a distinctive voice that is both poetic and unflinching. His writing is infused with a deep sense of empathy and understanding, and he tackles even the most difficult themes with a sensitivity and nuance that is all too rare in contemporary fiction.

Ultimately, "We Begin at the End" is a novel about the power of love and forgiveness to heal even the deepest wounds. It is a testament to the human capacity for resilience and redemption, and a reminder that, no matter how dark our past may be, we always have the power to create a different future. With its vivid characters, gripping plot, and unflinching exploration of the human condition, this is a novel that will linger in your mind long after you finish reading it.

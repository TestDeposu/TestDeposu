# Archangel's Kiss (Guild Hunter, #2)

**Author:** Nalini Singh  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In "Archangel's Kiss," Nalini Singh masterfully weaves a tale of dark seduction, angelic politics, and the unyielding power of love. The second installment in the Guild Hunter series, this novel delves deeper into the world of angelic rulers, vampiric servants, and the mortal-turned-angel Elena Deveraux.

Elena, a skilled vampire hunter, awakens from a year-long coma to find herself transformed – an angel with wings of midnight and dawn. Her body, however, is fragile and requires time to heal before she can take to the skies. This newfound vulnerability creates an intriguing dynamic, as Elena struggles to reconcile her desire for independence with her need for protection and care.

Raphael, the stunningly powerful archangel, is Elena's lover and self-appointed guardian. His character is a perfect blend of danger and devotion, making him a compelling and complex figure. As Elena navigates her new existence, Raphael's possessiveness and need for control often clash with her fierce determination to maintain her autonomy. This tension between them creates a rich and captivating narrative, as they work to understand and appreciate each other's boundaries.

The plot thickens when Raphael receives an invitation to a ball from the ancient and ruthless archangel Lijuan. This enigmatic figure wields power over the dead, and her "welcome" to Elena is a masterclass in psychological manipulation and terror. As Elena and Raphael prepare to face the horrors that await them in Beijing, the stakes are raised, and the true extent of Lijuan's power is revealed.

Throughout the novel, Singh's writing style is as captivating as ever. Her prose is lush and evocative, conjuring vivid images of the characters and their world. The pacing is expertly managed, with a narrative that flows effortlessly from tender moments of intimacy to scenes of heart-pumping action.

One of the most striking aspects of "Archangel's Kiss" is its exploration of themes. Singh delves into the complexities of power dynamics, the danger of blind obedience, and the transformative nature of love. Elena's journey, in particular, is a powerful metaphor for self-discovery and growth, as she learns to navigate her new existence and assert her independence in a world where she is no longer the dominant force.

In conclusion, "Archangel's Kiss" is a gripping and seductive tale that will captivate fans of urban fantasy and paranormal romance. With its intricate world-building, complex characters, and expertly woven plot, this novel is a must-read for anyone who enjoys a rich and immersive story.

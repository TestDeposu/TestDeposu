# L is for Lawless (Kinsey Millhone, #12)

**Author:** Sue Grafton  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1995

**Preview/Critique: L is for Lawless by Sue Grafton**

In "L is for Lawless," Sue Grafton masterfully weaves a tale of family, fortune, and the lengths one might go to protect or possess either. The twelfth installment in the Kinsey Millhone series finds our intrepid detective delving into the labyrinthine world of the Malek family, where money, power, and sibling rivalry reign supreme.

Grafton's signature style is on full display here, with her sharp, witty prose cutting through the complexities of the case like a scalpel. Kinsey's voice is as compelling as ever, her dry humor and unyielding tenacity making her a protagonist worth rooting for. Grafton's ability to create a richly textured narrative, filled with intricate plot twists and a diverse cast of characters, is nothing short of impressive.

At the heart of "L is for Lawless" lies the age-old question of nature versus nurture, as Kinsey seeks to unravel the mystery of the missing Malek brother. The family's dark secrets and the brothers' complex dynamics drive the plot forward, keeping readers on the edge of their seats until the very end. Grafton's exploration of these themes is both thought-provoking and engaging, making this novel a standout in the series.

Grafton's prose is as crisp and clear as ever, her dialogue sharp and authentic. She has a knack for creating vivid, memorable characters, each with their own unique voice and motivations. The pacing is excellent, with a steady build-up of tension that culminates in a satisfyingly explosive climax.

In "L is for Lawless," Sue Grafton once again demonstrates why she is a master of the crime genre. With its compelling protagonist, intricate plot, and exploration of complex themes, this novel is a must-read for fans of the series and newcomers alike.

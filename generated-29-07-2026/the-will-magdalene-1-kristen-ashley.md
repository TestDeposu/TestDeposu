# The Will (Magdalene, #1)

**Author:** Kristen Ashley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

**Title: A Heartwarming Trilogy of Second Chances in Kristen Ashley's "The Will"**

Kristen Ashley's "The Will," the first installment in her Magdalene series, is a captivating exploration of love, trust, and second chances, wrapped in the author's signature blend of heartfelt emotion and engaging storytelling. Set in the quaint, fictional town of Magdalene, Maine, this trilogy of interconnected love stories weaves a spellbinding tale that will leave readers eager for more.

At the heart of "The Will" is Josephine Malone, a woman who has built walls around herself, trusting only her beloved grandmother. Disguised as a man, she returns to Magdalene to honor her grandmother's final wish, only to find her heart stirred by the enigmatic Jake Spear. Ashley masterfully explores Josephine's journey from guarded isolation to opening her heart, creating a protagonist who is relatable and endearing.

Ashley's strength lies in her ability to craft complex, flawed characters who grow and evolve throughout the narrative. Jake Spear, with his bad decisions and troubled past, is no exception. His relationship with Josephine is a slow burn, allowing readers to witness the transformation of their connection from wary mistrust to deep, abiding love.

The author's style is marked by vivid, often humorous dialogue and a keen eye for detail, bringing the town of Magdalene and its inhabitants to life. Ashley's prose is engaging and accessible, making the pages fly by as readers become invested in the lives of these characters.

"The Will" also explores the theme of family, not just in the traditional sense, but in the bonds we forge with those who understand and accept us. From the tight-knit community of Magdalene to the found family Josephine and Jake create, Ashley weaves a tapestry of love and belonging that will resonate with readers.

The other two stories in the trilogy, "Soaring" and "The Time in Between," are equally compelling, each offering a unique perspective on love and second chances. While they can be read as standalone stories, they are enriched by the shared setting and recurring characters, creating a sense of continuity and depth.

In conclusion, Kristen Ashley's "The Will" is a heartwarming, engaging exploration of love, trust, and second chances. With its captivating characters, vivid prose, and compelling themes, this book is a must-read for fans of contemporary romance.

# Darkness Becomes Her (Gods & Monsters, #1)

**Author:** Kelly Keaton  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In the realm of fantasy, where mythological creatures and gods collide with mortals, few authors have the ability to craft a world as rich and immersive as Kelly Keaton's New 2. Set in an alternate New Orleans, this dark, lush urban landscape is home to a multitude of gods, monsters, and humans struggling for power. At the heart of Keaton's narrative is a tale that will forever change the course of history, a prophecy that foretells the rise of the Blood Wars, an eternal conflict between the gods and the creatures they created, trapping humanity in a never-ending cycle of destruction.

Darkness Becomes Her, the first installment in the Gods & Monsters series, follows the journey of Ari, a young God-killer, and Sebastian, a powerful Mistborn, as they hunt for Archer, the infant son of Athena, a child fated to trigger the Blood Wars. Their quest is fraught with peril, and as they navigate treacherous landscapes and battle formidable foes, the forces of destiny pull them apart, sending Sebastian on an treacherous path to the desert sands of Egypt and Ari into the Halls of Olympus and the Underworld, where the very fabric of existence hangs in the balance.

Keaton's masterful storytelling weaves together an intricate tapestry of mythology, folklore, and suspense, transporting readers to a world both familiar and unknown. Her prose is lyrical, evocative, and engaging, painting vivid images of the characters, settings, and events that propel the narrative forward. The author's passion for the mythological world shines through in every detail, from the gods' divine struggles to the creatures' twisted histories.

What sets Darkness Becomes Her apart, however, is its exploration of the human condition. Beneath the surface of mythological creatures and gods lies a complex web of motivations, desires, and fears that drive the characters. Ari, in particular, is a compelling protagonist, torn between her duty as a God-killer and her growing affection for the world she is sworn to defend. Her journey is a poignant exploration of identity, loyalty, and the burden of fate.

Keaton's world-building is equally impressive, drawing inspiration from various mythologies and folklore traditions. The city of New 2 is a living, breathing entity, with its own history, culture, and mythology. The author's use of magical realism adds a layer of wonder to the narrative, making the fantastical elements feel organic and believable within this alternate world.

One of the most striking aspects of Darkness Becomes Her is its pacing. The narrative hurtles forward with breakneck speed, deftly juggling multiple plot threads and keeping readers on the edge of their seats. Keaton expertly ramps up the tension, creating a sense of urgency that propels the story toward its climactic conclusion.

In conclusion, Darkness Becomes Her is a gripping, immersive, and deeply satisfying read that will captivate fans of urban fantasy, mythology, and suspense. Kelly Keaton's writing is a joy to behold, weaving together a rich tapestry of characters, settings, and events that will leave readers hungry for more. This first installment in the Gods & Monsters series is a triumph, setting the stage for a thrilling and unpredictable journey that will take readers to the very limits of imagination.

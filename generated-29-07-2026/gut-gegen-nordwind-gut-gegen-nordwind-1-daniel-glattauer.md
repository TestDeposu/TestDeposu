# Gut gegen Nordwind (Gut gegen Nordwind, #1)

**Author:** Daniel Glattauer  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

"Gut gegen Nordwind" is the enchanting sequel to Daniel Glattauer's "Love Virtually," a novel that masterfully explores the complexities of modern love and communication. Glattauer, with his signature wit and insight, delves into the heart of a relationship that has been blossoming through emails, leaving the protagonists, Leo and Emmi, craving a physical encounter.

The story picks up where "Love Virtually" left off, with Leo having moved to America and Emmi grappling with the intensity of their virtual connection. Glattauer's strength lies in his ability to capture the essence of human interaction through the written word, a feat he accomplishes with remarkable ease. The emails between Leo and Emmi are not just exchanges of words; they are conversations that dance on the page, filled with humor, longing, and an undercurrent of tension that keeps the reader hooked.

Glattauer's style is conversational yet profound, a blend that makes his characters relatable and their journey compelling. He explores the nuances of love in the digital age, where physical distance doesn't dampen emotional intimacy, but rather, intensifies it. The emails between Leo and Emmi are a testament to this, each one a step further into a relationship that is as complex as it is captivating.

The main theme of "Gut gegen Nordwind" is the tension between the virtual and the real, the longing for physical connection that grows stronger with each digital exchange. Glattauer navigates this theme with a deft hand, balancing the humor and lightness of the emails with the heavier, more profound questions they raise about love, commitment, and identity.

The novel is a testament to Glattauer's ability to create characters that feel real, conversations that feel authentic, and a story that feels both timely and timeless. It is a love story for the digital age, a tale of two people who find themselves drawn to each other across continents, connected by nothing more than a string of emails.

In conclusion, "Gut gegen Nordwind" is a delightful read, a novel that will make you laugh, think, and perhaps even yearn for a love that transcends physical boundaries. It is a story that captures the essence of our times, a testament to the power of words to connect us, to challenge us, and to change us.

# Gild (The Plated Prisoner, #1)

**Author:** Raven Kennedy  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

In "Gild", the first installment of "The Plated Prisoner" series, Raven Kennedy masterfully weaves a dark fantasy world where fae and monstrous Banes collide, loosely inspired by the legend of the Pied Piper. This romantasy duology promises to ensnare readers with its addictive blend of romance, secrets, and mature themes.

Kennedy's writing style is as alluring as the Pied Piper's magical pipes, drawing the reader into a world where the lines between good and evil are blurred. The narrative is rich in detail, with a unique magic system and a complex web of characters that will keep you guessing. The author's use of descriptive language paints a vivid picture of the world, making it easy to become fully immersed in the story.

At the heart of the story is the protagonist, a half-Bane, half-fae individual who is wholly hated by society. Their struggle for survival is palpable, and Kennedy expertly conveys the emotional toll of being an outcast. The introduction of the Pied Piper, a charismatic and mysterious figure, adds a layer of complexity to the narrative. Their relationship is built on a foundation of forced proximity, captive romance, and a deep-seated attraction that simmers beneath the surface.

The banter between the protagonist and the Pied Piper is witty and engaging, with a palpable tension that crackles like electricity. Kennedy's use of the "only one bed" trope adds a delicious layer of intimacy to the story, making the reader feel like they're experiencing the characters' emotions firsthand. The "who hurt you?" trope is also skillfully woven throughout the narrative, adding depth to the characters and their motivations.

One of the most striking aspects of "Gild" is its exploration of the gray areas between good and evil. Kennedy raises questions about the nature of morality and whether it's possible for individuals to change. The Pied Piper, in particular, is a fascinating character, with a rich backstory that is slowly revealed throughout the narrative.

While "Gild" is the first book in a duology, it feels like a complete story in its own right. Kennedy has done an excellent job of crafting a narrative that is both self-contained and sets the stage for the next installment. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, romance, and suspense that will keep readers on the edge of their seats.

In short, "Gild" is a captivating read that will appeal to fans of dark fantasy and romance. With its complex characters, unique magic system, and addictive storyline, it's a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and immersive tale.

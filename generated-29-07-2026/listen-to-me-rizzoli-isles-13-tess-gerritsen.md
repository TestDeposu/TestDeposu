# Listen To Me (Rizzoli & Isles, #13)

**Author:** Tess Gerritsen  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

In "Listen to Me," Tess Gerritsen brings back beloved detective duo Jane Rizzoli and medical examiner Maura Isles for another gripping thrill ride, delving deep into the complexities of family, loyalty, and the weight of secrets. This time around, Gerritsen masterfully weaves together multiple narratives, creating a rich tapestry that will keep readers on the edge of their seats.

The novel centers around a seemingly senseless murder: Sofia Suarez, a widow and nurse, lies bludgeoned to death in her own home. As Jane and Maura begin to investigate, they uncover a web of secrets and lies, with Sofia's seemingly innocent façade cracked open to reveal a tangled mess of deceit. But what exactly was Sofia involved in, and who stood to gain from her murder? Gerritsen's characteristic fast-paced, thrilling style propels the reader through the twists and turns, making it impossible to look away as the mystery unfolds.

However, it's not just the Sofia case that drives the plot forward – Gerritsen expertly introduces another, seemingly unrelated storyline involving Jane's mother, Angela. Angela's growing concern about a suspicious new family that has moved into the neighborhood sets her on a course of her own investigation, which eerily parallels Jane's work on the Sofia case. As the story builds, Gerritsen masterfully blurs the lines between Jane's professional life and her personal, highlighting the delicate balance between maternal instincts, trust, and loyalty.

Throughout the book, Gerritsen's writing is as sharp as ever, with a keen sense of character development and world-building. Jane and Maura remain as compelling as ever, their contrasting perspectives and skills making them a formidable team. Meanwhile, new characters burst onto the scene with depth and complexity, making it easy to become invested in their stories. Gerritsen's keen eye for detail and masterful handling of tension make "Listen to Me" an utterly engaging read, with moments that are both heart-stopping and heart-wrenching.

One of Gerritsen's greatest strengths lies in her ability to write with a sense of emotional authenticity. She deftly weaves together themes of family, loyalty, and the complexities of maternal relationships, raising questions about trust, honesty, and the lengths we go to protect those we love. In the midst of the high-stakes investigation, Gerritsen never shies away from exploring the quieter moments, where Jane, Maura, and Angela must confront their own fears, biases, and motivations. This thoughtful, nuanced approach makes for a richly satisfying read, one that will leave you pondering long after the final page has been turned.

Tess Gerritsen has once again outdone herself with "Listen to Me," delivering a novel that is at once thrilling, poignant, and thought-provoking. If you're a fan of Gerritsen's Rizzoli & Isles series, or indeed any well-crafted thriller, this book won't disappoint. But be warned: Gerritsen's writing is as addictive as ever, and it may take you a little while to recover from the ride.

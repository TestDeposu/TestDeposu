# Just for Now (Sea Breeze, #4)

**Author:** Abbi Glines  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In "Just for Now," the fourth installment of the Sea Breeze series, Abbi Glines crafts a tale of forbidden love, dark secrets, and the unrelenting allure of a bad boy. Preston, the quintessential rebel, has long been the object of Amanda's desire, but their social circles deem their relationship taboo. As Amanda and Preston navigate their undeniable attraction, they must confront the harsh realities of their world and the secrets that threaten to tear them apart.

Glines' writing style is unmistakable – sultry, emotive, and unapologetic. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with flaws and strengths that make them feel authentic. Preston, in particular, is a masterfully crafted bad boy, with a troubled past and a penchant for self-destruction. His character is expertly woven, with a depth that makes him both captivating and heartbreaking.

Amanda, on the other hand, is a strong-willed and determined heroine, driven by her desire to uncover the truth behind Preston's enigmatic facade. Her character serves as a perfect foil to Preston's, and their chemistry is undeniable. The romance is intense and all-consuming, with a palpable tension that simmers beneath the surface.

One of the most striking aspects of "Just for Now" is its exploration of the complexities of human relationships. Glines delves into the gray areas, where right and wrong are not always clear-cut. Preston's dark past and Amanda's naivety create a power imbalance that raises questions about consent, trust, and the blurred lines between love and obsession.

The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with characters like Marcus and Low adding depth and complexity to the narrative. The setting of Sea Breeze, with its small-town charm and gossip-fueled social hierarchy, provides a rich backdrop for the story to unfold.

If you're a fan of new adult romance, "Just for Now" is a must-read. Glines' writing is engaging, and her characters are impossible to forget. While the book deals with mature themes, it does so in a way that is both thought-provoking and respectful.

In conclusion, "Just for Now" is a captivating tale of love, secrets, and the complexities of human relationships. Abbi Glines' writing is as addictive as ever, and her characters will linger long after the final page is turned.

# Sunshine

**Author:** Robin McKinley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

In the world of fantasy literature, it's rare to come across a novel that masterfully blends elements of suspense, romance, and humor, but Robin McKinley's "Sunshine" is a true gem that achieves this delicate balance with ease. This award-winning novel, which won the Mythopoeic Fantasy Award for Adult Literature, is a must-read for fans of "Buffy the Vampire Slayer" and Sookie Stackhouse.

The story follows Rae Seddon, affectionately known as Sunshine, a small-town baker with a unique gift – magic. In a post-vampire apocalypse world, Sunshine's life is turned upside down when she's captured by a group of vampires while seeking solace at her family's lakeside cabin. As she navigates this treacherous new world, Sunshine must confront her own demons and learn to harness her magical abilities in order to survive.

One of the standout features of McKinley's writing style is her ability to craft relatable, quirky characters that leap off the page. Sunshine is a lovably flawed protagonist, with a sharp wit and a penchant for getting into trouble. Her narrative voice is engaging and conversational, making it easy to become fully immersed in her world.

McKinley's world-building is equally impressive, with a richly detailed landscape that's both familiar and yet, utterly unique. The author's vision of a post-apocalyptic world, where vampires and humans coexist in an uneasy alliance, is both captivating and unsettling. The tension between the two species is palpable, and McKinley expertly ramps up the suspense as Sunshine navigates this treacherous landscape.

What sets "Sunshine" apart from other vampire novels is its thoughtful exploration of the human condition. McKinley's characters are multidimensional and complex, with motivations that are both understandable and relatable. The novel raises important questions about the nature of good and evil, and the blurred lines that often exist between the two.

Throughout the novel, McKinley's prose is engaging and evocative, with a keen sense of pacing that keeps the reader on the edge of their seat. Her writing is infused with a wry sense of humor, which adds a welcome layer of depth to the narrative.

In short, "Sunshine" is a captivating and unputdownable read that will appeal to fans of fantasy, suspense, and romance. McKinley's unique voice and masterful storytelling make this novel a true standout in the genre. If you're looking for a compelling and thought-provoking read, look no further than "Sunshine".

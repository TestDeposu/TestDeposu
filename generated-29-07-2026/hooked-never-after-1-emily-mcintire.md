# Hooked (Never After, #1)

**Author:** Emily McIntire  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**A Gripping Descent into the Darkest Cravings of Humanity: A Review of Emily McIntire's Hooked**

In the realm of contemporary romance, it's not often that you come across a book that leaves a lasting impact, but Emily McIntire's Hooked is an exception. This dark, suspenseful tale of obsession and revenge is a masterclass in weaving a complex narrative that will leave you breathless. Drawing inspiration from the infamous tale of Peter Pan, McIntire presents a modern, twisted adaptation that is equal parts thrilling and unsettling.

The story revolves around James, a complex, brooding anti-hero with a singular focus: to destroy his arch-nemesis, Peter Michaels. When his nemesis's daughter, Wendy, walks into his life, James sees an opportunity to further his plans. He sets out to entwine her in a web of deceit, with the intention of using her as a pawn to exact his revenge. However, as their twisted game unfolds, James begins to confront his own demons and grapple with the blurred lines between obsession and passion.

Wendy, the protagonist, is a tantalizing mix of innocence and vulnerability. Having grown up under the suffocating grip of her wealthy, cold father, she's finally found a sense of freedom with James. Though she's aware of the danger that lurks beneath his façade, she's powerless to resist the allure of this enigmatic, brooding man. As their relationship becomes increasingly entwined, Wendy finds herself torn between her growing feelings for James and the monstrous nature of his actions.

McIntire's writing style is a true standout in Hooked. Her prose is dark, seductive, and eerily atmospheric, conjuring a world that's both alluring and repulsive. The tension between James and Wendy crackles with an electric charge, making their twisted dance of desire and manipulation impossible to look away from. The author's masterful handling of character development and plot twists keeps the reader guessing until the very end, rendering it impossible to predict the outcome.

What sets Hooked apart from other contemporary romances is its unflinching exploration of the human psyche. McIntire pulls no punches in her portrayal of the darker aspects of human nature, stripping away pretenses to reveal the raw, unsettling truth. The result is a narrative that's both uncomfortable to read and impossible to put down. In Hooked, McIntire delivers a thought-provoking, haunting, and compulsively readable tale that will leave you reeling long after you finish the book.

**Not for the Faint of Heart**

It's essential to note that Hooked contains mature themes, graphic content, and triggering situations that may not be suitable for all readers. While the book is a gripping page-turner, it's clear that McIntire's intention was to create a story that pushes the boundaries of what's considered acceptable in romance. If you're sensitive to dark themes or graphic content, you may want to exercise caution before diving into this book.

**Conclusion**

Hooked is a triumph of dark, atmospheric storytelling that showcases Emily McIntire's impressive writing chops. With its complex characters, expertly woven plot, and unflinching exploration of the human psyche, this book is a must-read for fans of contemporary romance and suspense. If you're looking for a story that will leave you breathless and leave you questioning the nature of desire, obsession, and the human condition, look no further than Hooked.

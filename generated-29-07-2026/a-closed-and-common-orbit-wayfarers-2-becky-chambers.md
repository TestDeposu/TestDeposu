# A Closed and Common Orbit (Wayfarers, #2)

**Author:** Becky Chambers  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

"A Closed and Common Orbit" by Becky Chambers is a captivating journey through the cosmos, a testament to the power of friendship and self-discovery. This stand-alone sequel to "The Long Way to a Small, Angry Planet" is a heartwarming space opera that masterfully weaves together two timelines, each with its own compelling narrative.

The story revolves around Lovelace, a sentient AI who wakes up in a new body with no memory of her past, and Pepper, an engineer who becomes Lovelace's guide in the vast, complex universe. Chambers' prose is elegant and engaging, painting vivid portraits of her characters and the diverse worlds they inhabit. She has a unique ability to make even the most advanced technology feel relatable and human, a testament to her skill as a storyteller.

At its core, "A Closed and Common Orbit" is a story about identity and belonging. Lovelace's journey of self-discovery is a poignant exploration of what it means to be sentient, to feel, and to exist in a world that often sees her as just a machine. Pepper, with her own troubled past, serves as a perfect foil to Lovelace, their friendship a beautiful testament to the power of understanding and acceptance.

Chambers' writing style is character-driven, her worlds and plots secondary to the people who inhabit them. This focus on character development makes "A Closed and Common Orbit" a deeply engaging read, each page filled with moments of joy, sorrow, and everything in between. The dual timeline structure allows Chambers to explore her themes in depth, each thread of the narrative enriching the other.

The novel is a celebration of diversity, both in terms of species and culture. Chambers' universe is a rich tapestry of alien races, each with their own unique biology, culture, and history. This diversity is not just window dressing, but a fundamental part of the story, a reflection of the author's commitment to creating a truly inclusive universe.

"A Closed and Common Orbit" is a book that will appeal to fans of character-driven science fiction, those who enjoy a story that focuses on the human (and alien) experience. It's a book about finding your place in the universe, about the power of friendship and understanding. It's a book that will make you laugh, make you cry, and make you think. It's a book that will stay with you long after you've turned the last page.

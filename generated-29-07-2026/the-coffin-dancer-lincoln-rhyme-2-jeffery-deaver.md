# The Coffin Dancer (Lincoln Rhyme, #2)

**Author:** Jeffery Deaver  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1999

The Coffin Dancer, Jeffery Deaver's electrifying second installment in the Lincoln Rhyme series, is a masterclass in suspenseful storytelling that will keep you on the edge of your seat from start to finish. Published in 1999, this gripping thriller has stood the test of time, and its impending adaptation into a major television event is a testament to its enduring appeal.

At the heart of the novel is the ingenious and elusive killer known as the Coffin Dancer, a master of disguise who leaves a trail of bodies in his wake. The only clue to his identity is a cryptic tattoo of the Grim Reaper waltzing with a woman in front of a coffin, which sets NYPD criminalist Lincoln Rhyme and his protégée Amelia Sachs on a desperate hunt to track him down. With a mere forty-eight hours to prevent the Coffin Dancer from striking again, Rhyme must rely on his razor-sharp mind and intuition to outwit the killer and bring him to justice.

Deaver's writing style is a key factor in the novel's success, as he expertly weaves together a complex web of clues, red herrings, and plot twists that will keep even the most seasoned thriller reader guessing. His prose is lean and mean, with a keen focus on the ticking clock that propels the story forward at a breakneck pace. The result is a heart-stopping, supercharged thrill ride that will leave you breathless and begging for more.

One of the most striking aspects of The Coffin Dancer is its exploration of the theme of identity and how it can be manipulated and distorted. The killer's use of disguise and deception raises questions about the nature of self and how easily it can be concealed or transformed. This theme is echoed in the character of Lincoln Rhyme, who, as a quadriplegic detective, is forced to navigate the world in a way that is both physically and emotionally challenging. His partnership with Amelia Sachs, a tough and resourceful young detective, adds an extra layer of depth to the story, as they work together to unravel the mystery and bring the Coffin Dancer to justice.

Throughout the novel, Deaver's expertise in crafting a compelling narrative is on full display. He has a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the inner workings of the human mind, which makes his characters feel fully realized and relatable. The Coffin Dancer is a testament to his skill as a storyteller, and it's no wonder that the Lincoln Rhyme series has become a beloved and respected part of the thriller genre.

In short, The Coffin Dancer is a must-read for fans of suspenseful fiction. With its breakneck pace, clever plot twists, and memorable characters, it's a thriller that will keep you on the edge of your seat from start to finish. If you're looking for a gripping read that will challenge your mind and keep you guessing, then look no further than this outstanding novel from Jeffery Deaver.

# Bad Moon Rising (Dark-Hunter, #17; Were-Hunter, #4; Hellchaser, #4)

**Author:** Sherrilyn Kenyon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

In Sherrilyn Kenyon's captivating novel, Bad Moon Rising, the threads of justice, loyalty, and redemption are woven intricately, propelling readers into a world of high-stakes suspense and romantic intrigue. This riveting tale follows Terri Mitchell, a tenacious agent driven by a personal vendetta to dismantle an organized crime syndicate, and Nathan Drake, a ruthless avenger seeking revenge against a powerful drug lord who has destroyed everything he holds dear.

At its core, Bad Moon Rising explores the theme of trust in a world where deception and chaos reign supreme. Terri and Nathan are two people whose lives converge due to a shared desire for justice, yet their paths are fraught with peril. As they navigate the treacherous underworld of organized crime, they are forced to confront the possibility that the lines between good and evil are blurred beyond recognition.

Kenyon masterfully employs her signature blend of action, suspense, and seductive romance to craft a narrative that is as unpredictable as it is captivating. The author's keen understanding of human psychology and her expertise in weaving complex characters add depth to the story, making it easy for readers to become invested in the fate of Terri and Nathan.

One of Kenyon's greatest strengths lies in her ability to create a sense of tension that is palpable and unrelenting. The stakes are high in Bad Moon Rising, and the author skillfully ratchets up the suspense as the story hurtles towards its dramatic conclusion. This is a novel where one wrong move can spell disaster, and Kenyon's expert handling of plot twists ensures that readers are kept guessing until the very end.

Throughout the book, Kenyon also explores the complexities of human emotion, revealing the motivations that drive characters to make choices that can have far-reaching consequences. Both Terri and Nathan are flawed, multi-dimensional characters whose actions are informed by their own personal histories and demons.

In Sherrilyn Kenyon, we have an author who is comfortable in multiple genres, effortlessly shifting from paranormal romance to suspenseful thrillers. However, in all her works, a deep empathy with characters and their emotional lives is what keeps us engaged as readers.

In Bad Moon Rising, Kenyon delivers a thrilling page-turner that is as unpredictable as it is captivating. This is a novel where the lines between good and evil are blurred, and the cost of betrayal is steep. As Terri and Nathan navigate this treacherous landscape, they are forced to confront their own demons and make choices that will shape the course of their lives forever.

In the end, Bad Moon Rising is a testament to the author's skill as a storyteller and her ability to craft complex characters that resonate with readers. This is a novel that will keep you on the edge of your seat, guessing until the very end, and eagerly awaiting the next installment in the Dark-Hunter series.

# A Kiss of Shadows (Merry Gentry, #1)

**Author:** Laurell K. Hamilton  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2000

A Kiss of Shadows, the first installment of Laurell K. Hamilton's Merry Gentry series, delves into a world of faeries, immortals, and the eternal struggle for power and survival. Published in 2000, this book seamlessly blends elements of fantasy, urban fantasy, and romance, transporting readers to the enchanting yet treacherous realm of Faerie.

At its core, A Kiss of Shadows is a story about identity, power, and the complexities of female autonomy in a male-dominated world. Merry Gentry, the protagonist, is a princess of the high court of Faerie living in secret as a human in Los Angeles. Her position as a private investigator specializing in supernatural crimes allows her to navigate the gray areas between human and faerie societies, where she must constantly walk a thin line to avoid detection.

The author masterfully weaves a rich tapestry of faerie politics, mythology, and sensual allure, drawing readers into this intricate and immersive world. Hamilton's vivid descriptions of the faerie realms, the immortal courts, and the complex web of alliances and rivalries between faerie factions are nothing short of breathtaking. Her use of language is both poetic and visceral, capturing the dark, seductive essence of the faerie world.

One of the standout features of A Kiss of Shadows is its exploration of the complexities of female identity, particularly in a world where power and agency are largely masculine pursuits. Merry, as a faerie princess living among humans, must navigate the constraints of her royal heritage while forging her own path in a society that is both alien and familiar. Hamilton skillfully conveys the tension between Merry's desire for independence and her duty to her faerie family, making her a relatable and engaging protagonist.

The romantic elements of the book are also noteworthy, particularly the introduction of Rowan Kennedy, one of the most compelling and enigmatic characters in the series. Rowan, a sidhe prince and Merry's potential mate, is a masterclass in subtle seduction, hinting at the darker, more primal forces that lie beneath the faerie world's polished surface.

Through her writing, Hamilton raises intriguing questions about the nature of power, loyalty, and identity in a world where even the most seemingly stable institutions are built on foundations of myth and deception. A Kiss of Shadows is a captivating tale that will keep readers on the edge of their seats, both from its suspenseful mystery and its sensual exploration of the faerie world.

While some readers may find the book's pacing a bit uneven or the world-building overwhelming, these minor complaints are quickly forgotten in the face of Hamilton's masterful storytelling. With A Kiss of Shadows, the Merry Gentry series is off to a triumphant start, promising a wild, thrilling ride into the heart of Faerie.

In conclusion, A Kiss of Shadows is a mesmerizing blend of fantasy, romance, and suspense that will captivate fans of Laurell K. Hamilton's work, as well as new readers looking to enter the world of faerie politics and intrigue. If you're ready to immerse yourself in a richly imagined world of ancient magic, seductive faeries, and complex identities, then A Kiss of Shadows is a must-read.

# Deep and Dark and Dangerous

**Author:** Mary Downing Hahn  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

Deep and Dark and Dangerous by Mary Downing Hahn is a masterfully crafted tale of mystery, suspense, and the supernatural, perfectly suited for young readers who crave a thrilling adventure. At its core, the novel explores the theme of uncovering hidden secrets and confronting the darkness of the past, which can have a profound impact on the present.

The story follows 13-year-old Ali, who embarks on a summer vacation in Maine with her aunt Dulcie and cousin Emma. However, their relaxing getaway is short-lived, as Ali becomes entangled in a 30-year-old mystery surrounding a tragic event from her family's past. The discovery of an old, torn photograph in the attic sets off a chain of events that leads Ali to unravel the truth about a girl named Teresa, who drowned under mysterious circumstances.

Hahn's writing style is a perfect blend of atmospheric tension, clever plot twists, and relatable characters. She skillfully weaves together the threads of the mystery, keeping readers engaged and invested in Ali's journey. The author's use of descriptive language effectively transports readers to the eerie and isolated setting of the lake house in Maine, where the lines between reality and the supernatural are blurred.

One of the standout aspects of the novel is its exploration of the complex relationships between the female characters. Ali's interactions with her aunt Dulcie, cousin Emma, and the enigmatic Sissy are multifaceted and nuanced, revealing the intricacies of family dynamics and the struggles of adolescence.

Throughout the book, Hahn expertly builds tension and suspense, culminating in a thrilling climax that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. The author's ability to balance the supernatural elements with the more grounded, realistic aspects of the story is impressive, making the novel appealing to a wide range of readers.

Overall, Deep and Dark and Dangerous is a gripping and unsettling tale that will appeal to fans of middle-grade mystery and suspense. Hahn's masterful storytelling and well-developed characters make this novel a compelling read that will linger in readers' minds long after the final page is turned.

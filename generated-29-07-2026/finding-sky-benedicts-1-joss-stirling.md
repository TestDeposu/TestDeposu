# Finding Sky (Benedicts, #1)

**Author:** Joss Stirling  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

Joss Stirling’s 2012 young adult novel, Finding Sky, steps confidently into the crowded arena of paranormal romance and emerges as a breath of fresh, exhilarating air. Blending high school tension with a rich tapestry of superhuman abilities, Stirling crafts a world where destiny is not just a poetic concept, but a literal, life-or-death reality. At its core, the novel explores the profound and terrifying theme of absolute connection—the idea of the "soulfinder," a predestined counterpart whose bond is forged in the stars and tested by the harshest realities of the mortal world. 

The story follows Sky Bright, an English teenager thrust into the daunting halls of an American high school. Sky is a survivor, carrying the weight of repressed memories and a deep-seated desire to remain invisible. However, blending into the background becomes impossible the moment she crosses paths with the Benedict brothers. Arrogant, fiercely protective, and undeniably striking, the Benedicts command attention. But it is the youngest brother, Zed, who shatters Sky's fragile equilibrium. When Zed breaches her defenses not with words, but through the intimate invasion of telepathy, Sky’s carefully constructed world begins to crumble. 

Stirling’s central theme examines the vulnerability of opening oneself up to another human being. For Sky, whose past is shrouded in dark, forgotten traumas, trust is the ultimate gamble. When Zed insists that they are "savants"—humans gifted with extraordinary paranormal abilities—and, more importantly, that Sky is his destined soulfinder, the narrative shifts from a standard schoolyard romance into a gripping survival thriller. The author brilliantly uses the paranormal element as a metaphor for the overwhelming intensity of first love. The telepathic link between Sky and Zed is both a gift and a curse, stripping away lies, hesitation, and pretense, leaving their raw emotions exposed to one another. 

Stirling’s writing style is fast-paced, accessible, and crackling with kinetic energy. She captures the authentic cadence of teenage anxiety and rebellion while seamlessly weaving in elements of supernatural suspense. The dialogue is sharp and witty, particularly in the charged exchanges between Sky and Zed, providing a delightful counterbalance to the darker undertones of the overarching plot. Furthermore, Stirling avoids the trap of making her protagonist a passive damsel in distress. Sky is fiercely independent, deeply resilient, and suspicious of easy answers. Her skepticism grounds the fantastical elements, making it easier for the reader to take the leap of faith alongside her. 

As the narrative unfolds, the cozy confines of high school drama give way to a high-stakes conspiracy. The revelation that Sky’s past is intimately tied to the secretive world of savants raises the stakes exponentially. Stirling orchestrates a thrilling climax that proves the bond between soulfinders is not merely a romantic fantasy, but a formidable force capable of overcoming external threats. The pacing never stumbles, keeping the pages turning as danger closes in on the young couple.

Finding Sky is a compelling opening salvo for the Benedicts series. It delivers everything a fan of the genre could hope for: palpable chemistry, a unique spin on superpowers, a resilient heroine, and a mystery that begs to be unraveled. Joss Stirling has created an addictive universe where love is the ultimate superpower, and her vibrant prose ensures readers will stay hooked until the very last page.

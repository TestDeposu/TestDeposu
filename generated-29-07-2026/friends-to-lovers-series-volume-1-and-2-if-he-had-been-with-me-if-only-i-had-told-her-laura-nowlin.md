# Friends to Lovers Series Volume 1 and 2 If He Had Been with Me + If Only I had Told Her

**Author:** Laura Nowlin  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Unknown

**Title: Friends to Lovers: A Bittersweet Journey**

Laura Nowlin's "Friends to Lovers" series, comprising "If He Had Been with Me" and "If Only I Had Told Her," is a poignant exploration of love, loss, and the complexities of friendship. The author's style is refreshingly candid, delving deep into the hearts of her characters, revealing their vulnerabilities and strengths with an unflinching honesty that is both compelling and relatable.

The series follows the lives of two best friends, Sam and Ava, from their teenage years to adulthood. The narrative is split into two volumes, each offering a unique perspective on their shared history. "If He Had Been with Me" is told from Sam's viewpoint, while "If Only I Had Told Her" is Ava's story. This dual narrative structure allows Nowlin to explore the intricacies of their friendship and the love that blossoms between them, as well as the misunderstandings and secrets that threaten to tear them apart.

Nowlin's prose is simple yet powerful, reminiscent of John Green's ability to capture the essence of youth and the trials of growing up. She masterfully navigates the complexities of first love, the pain of loss, and the delicate dance of friendship. The characters are beautifully drawn, their emotions raw and authentic, making it easy for readers to invest in their journey.

The main theme of the series is the transformative power of love and the devastating consequences of unspoken truths. Nowlin explores how love can shape our lives, influence our decisions, and leave indelible marks on our hearts. She also delves into the importance of communication and honesty in relationships, showing how a single unspoken word can alter the course of lives.

However, the series is not without its flaws. The pacing can feel slow at times, and some readers might find the constant back-and-forth in time a bit disorienting. But these are minor quibbles in an otherwise engaging and thought-provoking read.

In conclusion, "Friends to Lovers" is a captivating exploration of love, loss, and friendship. Nowlin's honest and relatable characters, coupled with her evocative prose, make this series a must-read for fans of contemporary young adult fiction.

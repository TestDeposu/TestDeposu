# Taken at Dusk (Shadow Falls, #3)

**Author:** C.C. Hunter  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In the midst of a world where supernatural secrets reign, C.C. Hunter's "Taken at Dusk" plunges readers into a whirlpool of suspense, drama, and laughter. The third installment of the Shadow Falls series is an electrifying ride that masterfully weaves together intricate plotlines, leaving readers spellbound and yearning for more.

At the heart of the narrative lies Kylie Galen, a teenager with an insatiable thirst for truth. Her journey is a complex dance of self-discovery, love, and the unrelenting pursuit of answers. As she navigates the treacherous landscape of Shadow Falls, a haven for teens with supernatural powers, Kylie finds herself entangled in a web of forbidden love, ancient secrets, and cryptic warnings.

Hunter's writing style is a perfect blend of dark and light, effortlessly balancing the ominous tone of the supernatural world with the relatable struggles of adolescence. Her characters are multidimensional and authentic, making it impossible not to become invested in their stories. The romance is skillfully woven throughout the narrative, adding a tender touch to the otherwise dark and mysterious atmosphere.

One of the most striking aspects of "Taken at Dusk" is its ability to seamlessly merge multiple plot threads into a cohesive and engaging narrative. Hunter tackles themes of identity, loyalty, and the consequences of playing with forces beyond one's control. The pacing is relentless, with each chapter ending on a cliffhanger that propels the reader forward, eager to unravel the mysteries of Shadow Falls.

Kylie's character, in particular, undergoes significant development in this installment. Her growth is a testament to Hunter's skill in crafting relatable and dynamic characters. As Kylie delves deeper into the secrets surrounding her supernatural identity, she must confront the harsh realities of her world and the true meaning of friendship and love.

The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, with each character bringing their unique perspective to the narrative. The relationships between Kylie and her friends are authentic and heartfelt, making the reader feel like they're part of the Shadow Falls community.

In conclusion, "Taken at Dusk" is a captivating addition to the Shadow Falls series. C.C. Hunter's masterful storytelling, coupled with her ability to craft relatable characters, makes this book an absolute must-read for fans of the supernatural genre. With its breakneck pacing, intricate plotlines, and memorable characters, "Taken at Dusk" will leave readers breathless and eagerly anticipating the next installment.

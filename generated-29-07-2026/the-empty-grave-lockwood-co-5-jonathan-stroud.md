# The Empty Grave (Lockwood & Co., #5)

**Author:** Jonathan Stroud  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

"The Empty Grave" by Jonathan Stroud is a masterful addition to the Lockwood & Co. series, weaving a complex web of intrigue, suspense, and supernatural thrills. As the fifth installment in the series, Stroud expertly ramps up the stakes, plunging his beloved characters into a perilous world of ghostly encounters and human treachery.

At the heart of the story lies the enigmatic Marissa Fittes, a legendary psychic heroine whose mysterious past and untimely demise have captivated the imagination of Lockwood, Lucy, George, and their cohorts. As they embark on a daring mission to uncover the truth behind Fittes' demise, they find themselves entangled in a labyrinthine plot that threatens to upend their agency and their lives.

Stroud's writing is a true marvel, effortlessly balancing humor, pathos, and spine-tingling terror. His characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Lockwood's charismatic leadership and Lucy's unwavering loyalty forming the emotional core of the narrative. The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, with the enigmatic Quill Kipps and the ruthless Penelope Fittes adding depth and complexity to the story.

One of the standout aspects of "The Empty Grave" is its thought-provoking exploration of the human condition. Stroud delves into themes of identity, family secrets, and the blurred lines between reality and the supernatural. As Lockwood and Lucy navigate the treacherous world of ghost-hunting, they must confront their own demons and confront the darkness that lurks within themselves.

The pacing is relentless, with Stroud masterfully ratcheting up the tension as the story hurtles towards its climactic conclusion. The action sequences are heart-pumping and intense, with the characters facing off against a terrifying array of spectral foes. Yet, it's the quieter moments that truly resonate, as Stroud expertly mines the emotional depths of his characters, revealing hidden vulnerabilities and unexpected strengths.

The world-building in "The Empty Grave" is equally impressive, with Stroud expanding the Lockwood & Co. universe in unexpected and thrilling ways. The introduction of new characters and plot twists adds a fresh layer of complexity to the narrative, while the return of familiar faces provides a welcome sense of continuity.

In short, "The Empty Grave" is a triumph, a captivating and unsettling ride that will leave readers breathless and begging for more. Stroud's writing is a true masterclass in storytelling, blending genre-bending thrills with genuine emotional depth. If you're a fan of supernatural fiction, ghost stories, or simply great storytelling, then "The Empty Grave" is an absolute must-read.

# Those Who Save Us

**Author:** Jenna Blum  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2005

Those Who Save Us, Jenna Blum's 2005 novel, is a powerful exploration of the unbreakable bonds between mothers and daughters, the enduring legacy of war, and the secrets that can both define and destroy families. This masterfully woven narrative delves into the complex and often fraught relationship between Anna Schlemmer and her daughter Trudy, as they navigate the treacherous landscape of their past and the dark secrets that have haunted them for decades.

Through Blum's evocative prose, the reader is transported to a war-torn Germany, where Anna's story unfolds with heartbreaking intimacy. We witness her struggles, her passions, and her impossible choices, all of which have a profound impact on her daughter's life. The author's vivid descriptions of life during World War II are both captivating and devastating, rendering the horrors of the war with unflinching honesty.

At its core, Those Who Save Us is a novel about the enduring power of love and the unyielding human spirit. It is a testament to the strength of women, particularly mothers, who must make impossible choices to ensure the survival of their children. Blum's portrayal of Anna's experiences is both poignant and thought-provoking, raising essential questions about the nature of survival, guilt, and redemption.

One of the novel's greatest strengths lies in its ability to balance the personal with the historical, creating a narrative that is both deeply intimate and broadly resonant. Blum's writing is lyrical and evocative, with a keen eye for detail that brings the past to life with stunning clarity. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with all the complexity and nuance of real people.

The mother-daughter dynamic at the heart of the novel is beautifully rendered, capturing the intricate web of emotions that binds Anna and Trudy together. Their relationship is fraught with tension, love, and misunderstandings, making their ultimate reconciliation all the more satisfying and redemptive.

Those Who Save Us is a novel that will linger in the reader's mind long after the final page has been turned. It is a powerful exploration of the human experience, a testament to the enduring power of love and the unbreakable bonds between mothers and daughters. With its vivid prose, well-developed characters, and historical significance, this novel is a must-read for anyone interested in Holocaust fiction, historical fiction, or women's studies.

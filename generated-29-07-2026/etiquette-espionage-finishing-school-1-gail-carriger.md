# Etiquette & Espionage (Finishing School, #1)

**Author:** Gail Carriger  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

**A Glimpse into the Rebellious World of Etiquette & Espionage**

Gail Carriger's latest offering, Etiquette & Espionage, is a captivating steampunk tale that skillfully weaves together the intricate threads of etiquette, adventure, and espionage. The first installment in the Finishing School series, this book transports readers to a world of precision-crafted prosthetics, clockwork machines, and fiercely independent young ladies who excel in the art of deception.

At the heart of this story lies Sophronia, a 14-year-old firecracker who refuses to conform to the traditional expectations of a lady. Dismayed by her mother's attempts to mold her into a compliant, corset-wearing beauty, Sophronia is instead drawn to the more...unseemly pursuits of clockwork engineering and espionage. It's not long before she finds herself enrolled in Mademoiselle Geraldine's Finishing Academy for Young Ladies of Quality, a prestigious establishment that promises to teach its students the finer arts of etiquette, dance, and, of course, covert operations.

Carriger's world-building is nothing short of masterful, transporting readers to a fantastical landscape of steampunk-inspired innovation and elegance. Her writing style is reminiscent of classic British literature, with its witty banter, droll humor, and delightful attention to period detail. This is a world where tea parties can conceal deadly assassinations, and a well-placed kick to the knee can disarm even the most cunning foe.

But beneath its polished, Edwardian exterior, Etiquette & Espionage reveals a more nuanced commentary on the societal expectations placed on women. Sophronia, with her love of tinkering and fighting, stands in stark contrast to the dainty, high-maintenance ladies who populate the Finishing Academy's student body. As our protagonist navigates the complexities of friendships, rivalries, and forbidden romances, she challenges the norms of her world and the people in it. This is a story about finding one's voice, forging one's own identity, and never, ever being content to simply exist in the shadows.

One of the standout features of Etiquette & Espionage is its cast of multidimensional characters. Mademoiselle Geraldine herself is a deliciously enigmatic figure, her past shrouded in mystery and her motives open to interpretation. Meanwhile, Sophronia's friends at the Finishing Academy are a motley crew of rebels, each with their own unique personalities and strengths. There's Prunella, the sharp-witted, aristocratic beauty with a penchant for explosives; Wunstance, the gentle giant with a talent for mechanics; and Dimity, the cheerful, dim-witted darling who unwittingly becomes entangled in the web of espionage.

Together, these young women form a formidable team, capable of outwitting even the most formidable foes. Their adventures are a thrilling mix of high-stakes espionage, derring-do, and wacky humor, as they navigate the treacherous landscape of high society's underbelly. Along the way, Carriger introduces a host of intriguing supporting characters, each with their own secrets and stories to tell.

As our protagonist navigates the intricacies of Mademoiselle Geraldine's Finishing Academy, Etiquette & Espionage proves itself to be a true page-turner. This is a story that will keep you on the edge of your seat, eagerly anticipating the next dramatic turn of events, the next witty repartee, or the next thrilling espionage mission.

**Rating: 4.5/5**

**Recommendation:** For readers who enjoy steampunk adventures, witty banter, and strong female leads, Etiquette & Espionage is a must-read. Fans of Gail Carriger's Parasol Protectorate series will also find much to delight in, as the world-building and characters are expertly woven together.

# All This Twisted Glory (This Woven Kingdom, #3)

**Author:** Tahereh Mafi  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

In All This Twisted Glory, the third installment of her sweeping, New York Times bestselling Woven Kingdom series, Tahereh Mafi deepens the rich tapestry of Persian-inspired mythology and exquisite emotional agony that has captivated fantasy readers worldwide. If the previous novels established Mafi’s mastery over atmospheric world-building, this latest volume acts as the crucible where high-stakes politics, ancient prophecies, and a fiercely magnetic romance collide with devastating precision.

At the heart of the narrative is Alizeh, the long-lost heir to the Jinn throne, who now walks a razor’s edge between destiny and survival. Mafi expertly anchors the central theme of the novel around the corrupting nature of power and the heavy price of liberation. Alizeh is no longer merely a fugitive hiding her true identity; she is a queen forced to navigate a treacherous labyrinth of political maneuvering. When Cyrus, the mercurial and blood-soaked ruler of Tulan, offers her a crown through a twisted marriage pact—one that culminates in his planned assassination—the narrative plunges headfirst into a delicious moral grey area. 

Mafi’s greatest stylistic triumph in All This Twisted Glory lies in her handling of romantic tension. The dynamic between Alizeh and Cyrus is a masterclass in the enemies-to-lovers trope. Bound by a shared affliction of engineered visions and mutual mistrust, their interactions crackle with a suffocating, poetic intimacy. Mafi’s prose is lush, sensory, and intensely lyrical, elevating every lingering glance and barbed remark into an emotional earthquake. Cyrus is portrayed not just as a dark king tied to the devil, but as a deeply layered, tormented figure whose guarded vulnerability destabilizes Alizeh—and the reader. 

Yet, the novel refuses to let romance overshadow the mounting geopolitical dread. With the arrival of Prince Kamran, hungry for vengeance, the board is set for a catastrophic collision of forces. Mafi weaves traditional tropes of epic fantasy—hidden heirs, dangerous alliances, and looming wars—with a distinctly mythic cadence rooted in Jinn lore. The result is a vibrant, immersive world where beauty and brutality are inextricably linked. 

While middle volumes in long-running fantasy series often suffer from narrative fatigue, Mafi maintains a relentless momentum by shifting the focus inward, examining the psychological tolls exacted upon her characters. Every secret uncovered and every betrayal landed feels earned, driving the plot toward an explosive trajectory that leaves readers breathless. 

All This Twisted Glory is a triumphant, emotionally searing addition to a standout contemporary fantasy series. Mafi proves once again that she is a peerless architect of longing and suspense, offering a tale where love is as much a weapon as it is a salvation. For aficionados of rich mythologies, searing romance, and intricate political intrigue, this book is an absolute must-read.

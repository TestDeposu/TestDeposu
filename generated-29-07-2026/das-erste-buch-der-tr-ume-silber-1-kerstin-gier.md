# Das erste Buch der Träume (Silber, #1)

**Author:** Kerstin Gier  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

In "Das erste Buch der Träume" (Silber, #1), Kerstin Gier masterfully weaves a captivating narrative that delves into the mystical realm of dreams, where the lines between reality and fantasy are blurred. The story follows Liv Silver, a young protagonist whose dreams have become increasingly unsettling, particularly one that involves four boys engaging in a mysterious ritual on a graveyard at night. As Liv navigates her new life at a prestigious school, she discovers that these boys are, in fact, real, and they seem to possess an uncanny knowledge of her deepest secrets, secrets she has only revealed in the confines of her dreams.

Gier's writing style is a perfect blend of wit, charm, and intrigue, making it impossible not to be drawn into Liv's world. The author's use of vivid imagery and descriptive language brings the dreamscapes to life, immersing the reader in a world that is both fascinating and unsettling. The characters are well-developed and complex, with Liv being a strong and relatable protagonist. Her determination to unravel the mystery behind the boys' knowledge of her dreams is admirable, and her interactions with them are laced with a subtle humor that adds to the book's charm.

One of the most striking aspects of Gier's writing is her ability to balance the mystical elements of the story with the mundane aspects of Liv's life. The protagonist's struggles to cope with her new school, her patchwork family, and her relationships with her friends and family are expertly woven into the narrative, making the story feel grounded and realistic. This balance also serves to heighten the sense of unease and tension that permeates the story, as Liv's dreams become increasingly dark and foreboding.

The themes of identity, secrecy, and the power of dreams are expertly explored throughout the book. Gier raises important questions about the nature of reality and the impact that our dreams can have on our waking lives. The author's portrayal of the dream world as a place of both wonder and terror is thought-provoking, and the reader is left pondering the possibilities of the subconscious mind long after finishing the book.

Overall, "Das erste Buch der Träume" is a captivating and thought-provoking read that will appeal to fans of fantasy, mystery, and romance. Gier's writing is engaging, and her characters are well-developed and relatable. The story is full of twists and turns, keeping the reader guessing until the very end. With its unique blend of magic, humor, and intrigue, this book is sure to leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in the Silber trilogy.

# Father Mine (Black Dagger Brotherhood, #6.5)

**Author:** J.R. Ward  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

In the realm of paranormal romance, few series have managed to captivate readers quite like J.R. Ward's Black Dagger Brotherhood. Two decades after the release of the first novel, Ward celebrates this milestone with "Father Mine," an all-new 20th Anniversary Insider's Guide that promises to be a love letter to fans, the world, and the unforgettable characters that have made this series a genre-defining phenomenon.

At its core, "Father Mine" is a nostalgic journey that revisits the original Brothers, bringing back the classic content and introducing never-before-seen material. Ward's writing style, which has always been a hallmark of the series, shines through in the brand-new novella, "Happy Anniversary." This tender, erotic, and emotional story reunites readers with Wrath and Beth, the couple who started it all, and proves that happily ever after doesn't always come easy. Ward's ability to craft passionate love stories that pierce the heart is on full display here, and fans of the series will undoubtedly be swept up in the emotional depth of this novella.

One of the standout features of "Father Mine" is its eclectic mix of content. From Brotherhood updates to fan-favorite features like Ask a Brother, Vishous's Agony Aunt Column, Kitchen Corner with Fritz, and Bella's Books, there's something for every fan of the series. Ward's behind-the-scenes moments, heartfelt memories, candid writing advice, and insight into the challenges and triumphs of her long and legendary career offer a unique glimpse into the world of the Black Dagger Brotherhood. The inclusion of playlists, monthly horoscopes, and more adds to the sense of nostalgia and community that permeates this keepsake collection.

Throughout "Father Mine," Ward's style is as engaging as ever. Her writing is infused with a sense of humor, wit, and emotional depth that has become a hallmark of the series. The Brothers, with their black leather and tattoos, are as charismatic as ever, and the dark nights filled with drama, chaos, and epic battles in the shadows of Caldwell, NY, are just as thrilling. And yet, it's the quieter moments – the unicorn pajamas and Tootsie Pops – that add a touch of humanity and vulnerability to the characters, making them all the more relatable and endearing.

If there's one criticism to be made, it's that "Father Mine" may not be the best starting point for new readers. While Ward does an excellent job of reintroducing the original Brothers and revisiting classic content, the true magic of this keepsake collection lies in its nostalgic value. Fans of the series will undoubtedly be the ones who appreciate this collection the most, and new readers may find themselves feeling a little lost.

In conclusion, "Father Mine" is a must-read for fans of the Black Dagger Brotherhood series. Ward's writing style, the nostalgic value, and the eclectic mix of content all come together to create a keepsake collection that's more than just an insider's look – it's a love letter to the fans, the world, and the unforgettable characters that have made this series a genre-defining phenomenon.

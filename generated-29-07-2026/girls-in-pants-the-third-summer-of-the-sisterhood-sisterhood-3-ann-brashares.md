# Girls in Pants: The Third Summer of the Sisterhood (Sisterhood, #3)

**Author:** Ann Brashares  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2005

"Girls in Pants: The Third Summer of the Sisterhood" by Ann Brashares is a heartwarming and bittersweet journey that captures the essence of friendship, love, and the inevitable passage of time. As the fourth and final installment in the beloved Sisterhood of the Traveling Pants series, this novel beautifully ties up the loose ends of Lena, Tibby, Bridget, and Carmen's high school lives, while also hinting at the exciting adventures that await them in college.

Brashares' signature style is evident throughout the novel, with her ability to weave together the stories of each sister with grace and finesse. She masterfully balances the individual narratives, ensuring that each character's voice remains distinct and authentic. The author's knack for creating relatable, three-dimensional characters is what makes the Sisterhood series so captivating. Lena, Tibby, Bridget, and Carmen feel like real friends, and their struggles and triumphs resonate deeply with the reader.

The main theme of "Girls in Pants" is the power of friendship and the importance of cherishing the present moment. As the sisterhood prepares to embark on their separate college journeys, they are forced to confront the reality that their lives will soon change irrevocably. Brashares explores the complex emotions that arise from this realization, from fear and anxiety to gratitude and excitement. Through the sisters' experiences, the author reminds us that while change is inevitable, the bonds of friendship can withstand even the most challenging of circumstances.

One of the most striking aspects of "Girls in Pants" is Brashares' ability to tackle serious issues with humor and grace. She doesn't shy away from exploring the sisters' struggles with love, loss, and self-discovery, but she also infuses the story with moments of levity and joy. This balance creates a narrative that is both poignant and entertaining, making it a truly enjoyable read.

In true Sisterhood fashion, "Girls in Pants" is filled with memorable moments that celebrate the power of friendship and the magic of shared experiences. From the sisters' road trip to the beach to their final days together in Bethesda, each scene is infused with a sense of nostalgia and wonder. Brashares' evocative prose brings these moments to life, allowing the reader to feel as though they are right there alongside the sisterhood, experiencing every emotion and adventure.

As the final chapter in the Sisterhood series, "Girls in Pants" is a bittersweet and beautiful farewell to Lena, Tibby, Bridget, and Carmen. It is a testament to the enduring power of friendship and the importance of cherishing the present moment. Brashares' signature style and heartfelt storytelling make this novel a must-read for fans of the series, as well as anyone who has ever cherished the bonds of friendship.

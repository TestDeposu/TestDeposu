# How the King of Elfhame Learned to Hate Stories (The Folk of the Air, #3.5)

**Author:** Holly Black  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

"How the King of Elfhame Learned to Hate Stories" is a captivating addition to Holly Black's Folk of the Air trilogy, delving into the enigmatic life of Cardan, the high king of Elfhame. This beautifully illustrated book is a must-read for fans of the series and newcomers alike, offering a fresh perspective on the dramatic life of Cardan, from his childhood as a faerie with a heart of stone to his rise as a wicked king.

One of the most striking aspects of this book is its unique narrative style. Holly Black masterfully weaves together a tale that is both a prequel and a sequel to the original trilogy, seamlessly blending moments from Cardan's past with glimpses of his future. The story is told entirely from Cardan's perspective, providing an intimate look into the thoughts and feelings of this complex character. Through his eyes, we see the world of Elfhame in a new light, with all its beauty, danger, and intrigue.

The book's themes of identity, power, and the complexities of relationships are timeless and thought-provoking. Cardan's journey is a powerful exploration of how our experiences shape us, and how we, in turn, shape the world around us. His struggles to navigate the treacherous world of faerie politics, family dynamics, and his own emotions are deeply relatable, making him a fascinating and sympathetic character.

Holly Black's writing style is, as always, enchanting. Her prose is lush and evocative, conjuring vivid images of the faerie world and its inhabitants. The illustrations that accompany each chapter are stunning, adding an extra layer of depth and magic to the story. The art is lavish and luminous, perfectly capturing the mood and atmosphere of each scene.

What sets this book apart from others in the series is its focus on Cardan's emotional journey. We see him as a vulnerable child, struggling to find his place in the world, and as a powerful king, grappling with the weight of his responsibilities. Through his story, Holly Black raises important questions about the nature of power, the impact of our actions, and the true meaning of courage.

Overall, "How the King of Elfhame Learned to Hate Stories" is a compelling and beautifully crafted book that will delight fans of the Folk of the Air series. It is a must-read for anyone who loves fantasy, adventure, and romance, with a unique narrative voice and stunning illustrations that bring the world of Elfhame to life. Whether you're a longtime fan of the series or new to the world of faerie, this book is sure to captivate and inspire.

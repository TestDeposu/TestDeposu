# Defiance (Defiance, #1)

**Author:** C.J. Redwine  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**Defiance: A Feisty Heroine and a World Gone Wild**

In the vein of Graceling and Tamora Pierce, C.J. Redwine's "Defiance" is a thrilling post-apocalyptic YA fantasy that breathes new life into the genre. The story unfolds in the walled city-state of Baalboden, where the rules are harsh, and the future is uncertain. Redwine masterfully paints a world that is both brutal and beautiful, teeming with danger and discovery.

At the heart of "Defiance" is Rachel Adams, a heroine who refuses to be confined by societal expectations. While other girls learn to sew and dance, Rachel learns to track and hunt. She's a fighter, a thinker, and a dreamer, all rolled into one. When her father goes missing and is declared dead, Rachel's spirit remains unbroken. She's determined to find him, even if it means defying the city's ruthless Commander and venturing into the treacherous Wasteland.

Redwine's storytelling is fast-paced and engaging, with a perfect balance of action, emotion, and suspense. The plot is a thrilling quest narrative, filled with monsters, assassins, and unexpected twists. Yet, it's the characters that truly shine. Rachel is a heroine to root for, her strength and vulnerability expertly woven together. Logan, her new Protector and love interest, is equally compelling, his loyalty and honor making him a worthy partner for Rachel's journey.

The romance between Rachel and Logan is a slow burn, a testament to Redwine's skill in building tension and chemistry. It's a relationship that feels earned, not forced, and it adds a layer of depth to the story without overshadowing the main quest.

Redwine's prose is accessible yet evocative, drawing readers in and keeping them on the edge of their seats. She has a knack for creating vivid, unforgettable imagery, from the grimy, oppressive walls of Baalboden to the eerie, haunting beauty of the Wasteland.

"Defiance" is more than just a gripping adventure; it's a story about defying expectations, challenging authority, and finding one's place in a world gone mad. It's a testament to the power of hope, resilience, and love in the face of adversity.

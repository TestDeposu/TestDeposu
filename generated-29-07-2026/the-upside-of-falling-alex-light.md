# The Upside of Falling

**Author:** Alex Light  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

In "The Upside of Falling," Alex Light delivers a captivating tale of love, loss, and self-discovery that will resonate with readers of all ages. This delightful debut novel tells the story of Becca Hart, a 17-year-old high school student who has long given up on the idea of true love. After a painful incident in her past, Becca has built walls around herself, hesitant to let anyone in.

The story takes a surprising turn when Becca's former best friend teases her for being boyfriend-less, prompting her to make a bold and impulsive claim – she's been secretly dating someone special. Little does she know, her lie will soon become a reality. Brett Wells, the charming captain of the football team, overhears Becca's statement and sees an opportunity to be the perfect boyfriend, without any emotional strings attached. As they embark on this fake relationship, both Becca and Brett begin to question their motives and confront the demons of their past.

Light's masterful storytelling weaves a complex tapestry of emotions, expertly blurring the lines between what's real and what's pretend. Becca and Brett's whirlwind romance is marked by witty banter, undeniable chemistry, and raw vulnerability. As they navigate the ups and downs of their relationship, they're forced to confront their deepest fears and insecurities.

One of the most striking aspects of Light's style is her ability to craft relatable, multidimensional characters. Becca and Brett are both flawed and lovable, with their own unique backstories and motivations. They're not one-dimensional caricatures, but rather fully fleshed-out individuals that readers will root for.

Light's writing is engaging, with a narrative that flows effortlessly from one chapter to the next. Her dialogue is authentic, capturing the quirks and nuances of teenage conversation. The pacing is well-balanced, with a perfect blend of comedic moments and heart-wrenching scenes.

At its core, "The Upside of Falling" is a story about vulnerability, trust, and the power of human connection. Becca and Brett's fake relationship becomes a catalyst for self-discovery, helping them to confront their pasts and forge a new path forward. This is a story that will resonate with anyone who has ever felt like they're stuck in a rut, struggling to find their place in the world.

In many ways, "The Upside of Falling" is a nod to the classic "opposites attract" tale, with Becca and Brett representing two ends of the social spectrum. However, Light subverts this trope by making both characters flawed and imperfect, highlighting the beauty of their differences rather than their similarities.

Overall, "The Upside of Falling" is a charming, engaging debut novel that will capture readers' hearts. With its witty dialogue, relatable characters, and heartfelt storyline, this book is a must-read for fans of Jenny Han and anyone who's ever believed in the power of love and connection.

# Destined (Wings, #4)

**Author:** Aprilynne Pike  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

"Destined" by Aprilynne Pike is a captivating conclusion to the Wings series, a tale of magic, love, and self-discovery that will leave readers spellbound. The story follows Laurel, a faerie from the realm of Avalon, as she navigates the challenges of her destiny and the mortal world. With the fate of Avalon hanging in the balance, Laurel must confront the dark forces threatening her home and the people she loves.

One of the most striking aspects of Pike's writing is her ability to craft a richly detailed world, full of ancient magic and fae politics. The realm of Avalon is meticulously constructed, with its own rules and mythology that add depth and complexity to the narrative. Pike's use of fae lore is masterful, weaving together elements of mythology and folklore to create a unique and immersive world.

At the heart of the story is Laurel's struggle to balance her fae destiny with her love for the mortal world. Torn between her feelings for David, a steady and dependable human, and Tamani, a faerie guardian sworn to protect her, Laurel must make a choice that will determine the course of her life. Pike handles this love triangle with skill, avoiding clichés and instead crafting a nuanced and emotionally resonant portrayal of first love.

The action in "Destined" is fast-paced and suspenseful, with an epic battle between faeries and trolls that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. Pike's writing is engaging and accessible, making it easy to become fully invested in the world and characters she has created.

What sets "Destined" apart from other books in the fantasy genre is its thoughtful exploration of the complexities of growing up. Laurel's journey is not just about saving Avalon, but also about discovering her own identity and sense of purpose. Pike tackles themes of loyalty, duty, and the challenges of adolescence with sensitivity and insight.

Overall, "Destined" is a satisfying conclusion to the Wings series, bringing together all the threads of the narrative in a heart-stopping finale. Pike's writing is engaging, immersive, and emotionally resonant, making this book a must-read for fans of fantasy and romance. With its richly detailed world, nuanced characters, and epic action sequences, "Destined" is a compelling read that will leave readers eager for more.

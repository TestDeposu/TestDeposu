# Antes de diciembre (Meses a tu lado, #1)

**Author:** Joana Marcús  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

"Antes de diciembre" by Joana Marcús is a tantalizing tale of love, passion, and the blurred lines of relationships. This Wattpad sensation has captured the hearts of many, and it's easy to see why. Marcús weaves a narrative that's both relatable and addictive, making it impossible to put down.

At its core, "Antes de diciembre" is a story about self-discovery and the exploration of one's desires. Jenna Brown, the protagonist, finds herself at a crossroads as she navigates her first year of university. Her long-distance relationship with her boyfriend is put to the test, and she's forced to confront the true nature of their bond. As she draws closer to her boyfriend's best friend, she must confront the consequences of her actions and the uncertainty of her feelings.

Marcús's writing style is effortless and engaging, making it easy to become fully immersed in Jenna's world. The author's use of language is simple yet effective, conveying the complexities of Jenna's emotions with remarkable clarity. The narrative is well-paced, with a perfect balance of drama, romance, and introspection.

One of the most striking aspects of "Antes de diciembre" is its ability to tackle the nuances of relationships. Marcús sheds light on the gray areas that often accompany love, highlighting the difficulties of navigating multiple connections at once. The author's portrayal of Jenna's inner turmoil is raw and honest, making it impossible not to empathize with her struggles.

The character development in "Antes de diciembre" is also noteworthy. Jenna is a multidimensional protagonist, full of flaws and contradictions. Her growth throughout the story is remarkable, and her journey is both poignant and relatable. The supporting characters are equally well-crafted, adding depth and complexity to the narrative.

In "Antes de diciembre," Marcús raises important questions about the nature of love and relationships. Is it possible to love multiple people at once? Can a relationship survive the test of distance and uncertainty? These questions linger long after the story ends, leaving the reader to ponder the complexities of the human heart.

Overall, "Antes de diciembre" is a captivating tale of love, passion, and self-discovery. Marcús's writing style is engaging, and her portrayal of relationships is both nuanced and relatable. If you're looking for a story that will keep you on the edge of your seat, look no further.

# Savvy (Savvy, #1)

**Author:** Ingrid Law  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

"Savvy" by Ingrid Law is a captivating tale that masterfully weaves together the uncertainty of adolescence with a touch of magical realism, resulting in a story that is as heartwarming as it is enchanting. The novel follows Mibs Beaumont, a young girl on the cusp of discovering her 'savvy' - a unique power that manifests in each member of her family at the age of thirteen. However, just as Mibs eagerly awaits this rite of passage, her father is involved in a severe accident, leaving her desperate to find a savvy that can save him.

Law's storytelling is a delightful blend of whimsy and wisdom, creating a world that feels both fantastical and deeply relatable. The Beaumont family's savvies are a charming metaphor for the diverse talents and quirks that make each individual unique, while also serving as a poignant exploration of the pressures and expectations that come with growing up. Mibs' journey is a testament to the power of hope, determination, and the unbreakable bonds of family.

The narrative is peppered with Law's signature wit and humor, making even the most serious moments feel accessible and engaging. The author's use of vivid, sensory language brings the story to life, from the talking tattoos that adorn the preacher's kids to the rickety old bus that becomes a character in its own right. Law's writing style is as captivating as the story itself, drawing readers in and keeping them hooked until the very end.

"Savvy" is more than just a coming-of-age story; it's a celebration of individuality, a testament to the power of love, and a reminder that sometimes, the most extraordinary things can happen in the most ordinary of places. It's a must-read for anyone who has ever felt like they don't quite fit in, or who has ever yearned for something more. This Newbery Honor book is a timeless tale that will resonate with readers of all ages.

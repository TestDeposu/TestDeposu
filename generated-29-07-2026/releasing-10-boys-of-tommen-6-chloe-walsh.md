# Releasing 10 (Boys of Tommen, #6)

**Author:** Chloe Walsh  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

**A Soul-Searing Romance that Tackles the Darker Aspects of Love**

In "Releasing 10", Chloe Walsh weaves an achingly beautiful and devastatingly raw love story that leaves an indelible mark on the reader. This, the sixth installment of the Boys of Tommen series, brings together Lizzie Young and Hugh Biggs in a narrative that shines a beacon of hope in the darkness of mental health, trauma, and healing.

Lizzie, a young woman living with bipolar disorder, carries the weight of her struggles on her own, often feeling like an outcast in her own family and community. Her story is a poignant reminder of the silent battles many people fight daily, and Walsh tackles this sensitive topic with unflinching honesty. We watch Lizzie grow and learn to navigate her emotions, to lean on others and to love without fear of rejection.

Hugh, the young hero, is the anchor Lizzie needs in her stormy life, providing a sense of stability and acceptance she's been searching for. His wisdom, compassion, and unwavering loyalty make him an unforgettable character, and his slow-burning connection with Lizzie is a true highlight of the book.

Walsh's writing is nothing short of masterful, weaving together themes of love, trauma, and recovery in a narrative that will rip your heart out and patch it back together. Her prose is evocative, painting vivid pictures of the Irish landscape, while also plumbing the depths of the human experience.

"Releasing 10" is not an easy read; it tackles topics of childhood abuse, self-harm, and suicidal ideation, making it essential to approach this book with sensitivity and respect. However, Walsh handles these themes with a bold, unflinching honesty that is both cathartic and necessary.

The romance at the heart of the book is character-driven, with a slow-burning intensity that's both exhilarating and heart-wrenching. Walsh masterfully walks the line between emotional and explicit content, creating a narrative that will leave you questioning the boundaries of love, trauma, and what it means to heal.

In the hands of a lesser author, this story could become saccharine or manipulative. However, Walsh's nuanced portrayal humanizes even the darkest moments, injecting a sense of hope and resilience that is inspiring and empowering.

"Releasing 10" is a book that will resonate with fans of contemporary romance, particularly those who enjoy character-driven stories. Its slow-burning chemistry and unflinching portrayal of mental health make it a compelling, thought-provoking read that will linger long after you finish.

**If you're a fan of emotional, character-driven romance, be prepared to have your heart broken – and maybe even mended.**

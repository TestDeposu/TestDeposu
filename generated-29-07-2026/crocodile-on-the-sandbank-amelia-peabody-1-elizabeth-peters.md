# Crocodile on the Sandbank (Amelia Peabody, #1)

**Author:** Elizabeth Peters  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**Crocodile on the Sandbank: A Delightful blend of Adventure, Mystery, and Wit**

Elizabeth Peters' "Crocodile on the Sandbank," the inaugural installment in the Amelia Peabody series, is a captivating Victorian-era thriller that masterfully blends adventure, mystery, and wit, all wrapped in a compelling narrative set against the backdrop of ancient Egypt. Peters, a pseudonym for Barbara Mertz, a renowned Egyptologist, infuses the story with her extensive knowledge of the subject, creating an immersive and authentic experience for readers.

At the heart of the novel is the indomitable Amelia Peabody, a self-proclaimed spinster and heiress who is as unrattled by mummies as she is by societal norms. Amelia is a refreshing protagonist, her self-assuredness and unconventional nature making her a delightful heroine. She embarks on an Egyptian adventure armed with her trusty journal, a sturdy umbrella, and an unyielding determination to protect her newfound friend, Evelyn Barton-Forbes, from a sinister plot that threatens their lives.

The story unfolds as Amelia and Evelyn sail up the Nile, their journey taking them to an archaeological site run by two handsome brothers. Here, strange occurrences, suspicious accidents, and a botched kidnapping convince Amelia that there is a plot afoot to harm Evelyn. Peters skillfully weaves a web of intrigue, keeping readers on the edge of their seats as Amelia navigates the treacherous waters of ancient Egypt, determined to unravel the mystery and protect her friend.

Peters' writing style is engaging and humorous, her wit shining through in Amelia's sharp observations and wry remarks. The author's extensive knowledge of Egyptology is evident in the meticulous detail with which she describes the archaeological sites and the excavation process, adding depth and authenticity to the story. Yet, Peters never allows this wealth of knowledge to overwhelm the narrative, instead using it to enhance the reader's understanding and appreciation of the setting.

"Crocodile on the Sandbank" is more than just a mystery novel; it is a love letter to ancient Egypt, a testament to the indomitable spirit of its protagonist, and a celebration of the power of friendship. Peters' ability to create a compelling narrative that is both engaging and informative is a testament to her skill as a writer and her passion for her subject matter.

In conclusion, "Crocodile on the Sandbank" is a must-read for fans of historical mysteries, strong female protagonists, and ancient Egypt. It is a book that will captivate readers from the first page, drawing them into a world of adventure, mystery, and wit, and leaving them eager for more of Amelia Peabody's exploits.

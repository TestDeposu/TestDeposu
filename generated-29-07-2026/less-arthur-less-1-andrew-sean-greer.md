# Less (Arthur Less, #1)

**Author:** Andrew Sean Greer  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

**Less: A Hilarious and Heartfelt Odyssey of Self-Discovery**

In "Less," Pulitzer Prize-winning author Andrew Sean Greer delivers a sparkling satire that is as much a love letter to literature as it is a rumination on aging, love, and the human heart. At its core, this novel is a delightful, if somewhat chaotic, journey of self-discovery, wrapped in a comedic narrative that spans the globe.

Our protagonist, Arthur Less, is a struggling novelist turning fifty, facing the impending marriage of his boyfriend of nine years. Rather than confront the awkwardness of the situation, Less embarks on a worldwide literary tour, accepting every invitation that lands on his desk. What follows is a hilarious and often cringe-worthy series of misadventures that take him from Paris to Berlin, Morocco to India, and beyond.

Greer's prose is elegant and witty, his observations keen and often laugh-out-loud funny. He masterfully balances the absurdity of Less's situations with moments of profound introspection, creating a narrative that is both entertaining and thought-provoking. Less's journey is not just physical, but also an exploration of his past, his relationships, and his place in the world.

At its heart, "Less" is a love story, not just of romantic love, but of love for life, for literature, and for the people who touch our lives. Greer's characters are beautifully drawn, each with their own quirks and complexities, making them feel real and relatable. Even in the most absurd situations, there is a warmth and humanity that grounds the story.

"Less" is a book that will make you laugh, make you think, and perhaps even make you cry. It is a celebration of the human experience, in all its messy, beautiful glory. Greer's writing is a joy to read, his storytelling a pleasure to follow. This is a book that deserves to be savored, a literary feast that will leave you satisfied and eager for more.

# A Woman of Substance (Emma Harte Saga #1)

**Author:** Barbara Taylor Bradford  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

**A Woman of Substance: A Saga of Resilience and Ambition**

Barbara Taylor Bradford's "A Woman of Substance," the inaugural novel of her Emma Harte Saga, is a sweeping, extravagant tale that masterfully weaves together the threads of love, power, and ambition against the backdrop of 20th-century history. This record-shattering New York Times bestseller is not just a novel; it's an immersive experience that transports readers from the servants' quarters of a Yorkshire manor to the opulent boardrooms of international business.

At the heart of this epic saga lies Emma Harte, a woman whose indomitable spirit and resolve propel her from abject poverty to the helm of a global empire. Bradford's protagonist is a tour de force, a complex character whose unyielding determination to succeed is as captivating as it is inspiring. Emma's journey is not without its challenges, nor is it without its secrets. Her life is a labyrinth of love, loss, and longing, with only one man remaining forever out of reach.

Bradford's storytelling is as robust as her characters. She paints her narrative with broad strokes, deftly navigating the shifting landscapes of time and place. From the brooding moors of Yorkshire to the bustling streets of London and New York, her prose is vivid and evocative, drawing readers into a world that feels as real as it is enthralling.

The novel is also a compelling exploration of the 20th century, with Bradford's characters navigating the tumultuous waters of war, social change, and economic upheaval. Yet, despite its sprawling scope, "A Woman of Substance" remains a deeply personal story, a testament to the human spirit's capacity for resilience and triumph.

"A Woman of Substance" is not just a novel; it's a saga, a chronicle of a family's rise to power and influence. It's a story of love and loss, of ambition and achievement, of the indomitable human spirit. It's a novel that demands to be read, to be savored, to be remembered.

# Swallowing Darkness (Merry Gentry, #7)

**Author:** Laurell K. Hamilton  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

In the riveting world of faerie politics, where love and loyalty are constantly tested, Laurell K. Hamilton once again weaves a captivating tale in "Swallowing Darkness," the seventh installment of the Merry Gentry series. With the stakes higher than ever, Meredith Gentry embarks on a perilous journey to protect her unborn twins and assert her rightful claim to the Unseelie throne.

The main theme of "Swallowing Darkness" revolves around Meredith's resilience in the face of treachery and deception. As she navigates the intricate web of faerie politics, Meredith must confront the dark forces that seek to destroy her and everything she holds dear. Her pregnancy serves as a double-edged sword, making her a target for those who seek to exploit her position and her unborn children.

Hamilton expertly crafts Meredith's narrative, immersing the reader in the magical realm of faerie. The author's unique blend of faerie lore and modern sensibilities creates a rich tapestry that is both enchanting and unsettling. Meredith's character, in particular, remains a highlight of the series, showcasing her growth and determination as she fights to assert her claim to the throne.

One of the most striking aspects of "Swallowing Darkness" is the author's exploration of the consequences of violence and trauma. Hamilton tackles these themes with sensitivity and depth, highlighting the long-term effects on Meredith's physical and emotional well-being. This nuanced approach adds depth to the narrative, making the story feel both suspenseful and emotionally resonant.

The supporting cast of characters is equally well-developed, with Meredith's lovers and allies adding depth and complexity to the story. Hamilton's portrayal of the goblins and the sluagh, creatures that are often marginalized in faerie lore, is particularly noteworthy. These characters, while initially unsettling, prove to be unexpected allies in Meredith's fight for survival.

Throughout the book, Hamilton's writing is characterized by its evocative descriptions and masterful pacing. The author expertly ramps up the tension, keeping the reader on the edge of their seat as Meredith navigates the treacherous world of faerie politics. The narrative is both fast-paced and detailed, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world of the Unseelie Court.

In "Swallowing Darkness," Laurell K. Hamilton delivers a gripping tale of faerie politics, loyalty, and survival. With its complex characters, rich world-building, and pulse-pounding action sequences, this book is a must-read for fans of the Merry Gentry series. While it may be the seventh installment, "Swallowing Darkness" stands alone as a testament to Hamilton's skill as a storyteller, effortlessly weaving together themes of love, loyalty, and the human condition.

# Rules of Attraction (Perfect Chemistry, #2)

**Author:** Simone Elkeles  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**Rules of Attraction: A Dance of Opposites**

Simone Elkeles' "Rules of Attraction," the second installment in her Perfect Chemistry trilogy, is a captivating exploration of love's unpredictable nature, set against the backdrop of cultural divides and personal growth. Elkeles, known for her engaging storytelling and authentic voices, delivers another compelling tale that will resonate with young adult readers.

At its core, "Rules of Attraction" is a story about the rules we set for ourselves and the ones we break when love enters the equation. Carlos Fuentes, a Mexican-American teen with a troubled past, is forced to leave his gang life behind and move to the suburbs with his brother. Kiara Westford, the professor's daughter, is everything Carlos isn't - she's smart, responsible, and has a strict moral code. Their worlds collide, and despite their best efforts to resist, they find themselves drawn to each other.

Elkeles masterfully navigates the complexities of their relationship, exploring themes of cultural identity, class differences, and the struggle between personal desires and familial expectations. Carlos's journey from a gang member to a young man trying to find his place in the world is particularly compelling. His internal conflict, as he grapples with his loyalty to his brother and his desire for freedom, is a testament to Elkeles' ability to create well-rounded, relatable characters.

The author's signature style shines in the witty banter between Carlos and Kiara, which provides a welcome contrast to the heavier themes. Their relationship develops organically, from initial animosity to a deep understanding and connection. Elkeles doesn't shy away from the challenges they face, making their love story all the more believable and satisfying.

"Rules of Attraction" is more than just a romance; it's a story about finding one's place in the world, about the rules we follow and the ones we break. It's about the unexpected connections we form and the lengths we go to protect them. Elkeles' exploration of these themes, coupled with her engaging prose and authentic characters, makes "Rules of Attraction" a standout in young adult literature.

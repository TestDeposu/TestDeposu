# The Outlaw Demon Wails (The Hollows, #6)

**Author:** Kim Harrison  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

In "The Outlaw Demon Wails", the sixth installment of Kim Harrison's bestselling Hollows series, Rachel Morgan finds herself entangled in a perilous game of survival, where the stakes are higher than ever before. With her soul hanging in the balance, Rachel must navigate the treacherous landscape of demon magic, confronting the darkest corners of her own past and the secrets that have haunted her family for generations.

Harrison's writing is, as always, a masterclass in atmosphere and tension. Her vivid descriptions of the Hollows, a world of witches, vampires, and demons, are so evocative that it's impossible not to become fully immersed in Rachel's world. The author's use of language is economical yet effective, conveying the complexity of Rachel's emotions and the gravity of her situation with precision and nuance.

At its core, "The Outlaw Demon Wails" is a story about the consequences of playing with forces beyond one's control. Rachel's decision to dabble in forbidden demon magic has set off a chain reaction of events that threaten to destroy everything she holds dear. As she delves deeper into the mysteries of the demonic ever-after, she must confront the very real possibility that she may never return.

One of the most striking aspects of Harrison's writing is her ability to create complex, multidimensional characters. Rachel is a deeply flawed and relatable protagonist, driven by a mix of motivations that are both noble and selfish. Her relationships with the people around her are authentic and multifaceted, adding depth and richness to the narrative.

The plot is, as always, expertly woven, with unexpected twists and turns that keep the reader on the edge of their seat. Harrison's pacing is impeccable, ratcheting up the tension to almost unbearable levels as Rachel navigates the treacherous world of demon politics and ancient magic.

If there's one criticism to be made, it's that the supporting cast sometimes feels a little shortchanged. Characters like Ivy and Jenks, who have been central to the series up until this point, take a backseat to the main plot, and their storylines feel a little truncated as a result. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise excellent book.

Overall, "The Outlaw Demon Wails" is a gripping and unputdownable read that will appeal to fans of urban fantasy and supernatural fiction. Harrison's writing is, as always, top-notch, and her ability to craft a compelling narrative is unparalleled. If you're new to the series, this may not be the best starting point, but for fans of Rachel Morgan and the Hollows, this is an absolute must-read.

# Midnight Exposure (Midnight, #1)

**Author:** Melinda Leigh  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

Midnight Exposure, the first installment in the Midnight series by Melinda Leigh, is a gripping and intense thriller that will keep you on the edge of your seat from start to finish. The story takes place in the small town of Black Rock Falls, where Sheriff Jenna Alton and her deputy David Kane are tasked with solving a series of mysterious disappearances.

The main theme of the book revolves around the idea of vulnerability and the fear that comes with it. The author masterfully weaves together a narrative that explores the darker side of human nature, where the lines between good and evil are blurred. The protagonist, Maisy Jones, finds herself in a desperate situation when her car breaks down on an isolated road, and she is forced to accept a ride from a stranger. This sets off a chain of events that puts the entire town on high alert.

Melinda Leigh's writing style is engaging and suspenseful, with a keen attention to detail that makes the characters and setting feel incredibly real. The author's use of descriptive language paints a vivid picture of the small town and its inhabitants, making it easy to become fully immersed in the story. The pacing is well-balanced, with a steady stream of twists and turns that keep the reader guessing until the very end.

One of the standout aspects of Midnight Exposure is its ability to create a sense of tension and unease. The author expertly crafts a sense of foreboding, making the reader feel like something is off, even when the characters themselves are unaware of the danger lurking in the shadows. This creates a sense of urgency, propelling the reader forward as they desperately try to uncover the truth alongside the protagonists.

The characters themselves are well-developed and complex, with rich backstories that add depth to the narrative. Sheriff Jenna Alton is a particularly strong protagonist, with a sharp mind and a determination to solve the case. Her partnership with Deputy David Kane is well-executed, with a clear sense of trust and camaraderie between the two.

In short, Midnight Exposure is a gripping and intense thriller that will appeal to fans of the genre. With its expertly crafted tension, well-developed characters, and suspenseful plot, this book is sure to keep you up late into the night. If you're looking for a compelling read that will keep you on the edge of your seat, look no further than Midnight Exposure.

# Time Risk 1: A Time Travel Thriller of Pearl Harbor – Book 1 (Time Risk Series)

**Author:** Elyse Douglas  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2001

In "Time Risk 1: A Time Travel Thriller of Pearl Harbor – Book 1," Elyse Douglas weaves a captivating tale that transports readers to the pivotal moment in history when the world's perception of war is irrevocably altered. Beneath the surface of this gripping narrative lies a profound exploration of the delicate balance between time, loyalty, and the unfathomable consequences of altering the course of events.

The story commences at a tumultuous period in the United States' history, when the threat of war looms large. Douglas masterfully interweaves historical accuracy with fictional elements, crafting a narrative that is both informative and engaging. Her vivid descriptions of the setting, combined with the intricate details of the war's pivotal moments, serve to immerse the reader in the chaos and uncertainty that defined this era.

A central thread running through the narrative is the concept of risk vs. the consequences of altering time. The protagonist embarks on a perilous journey through time, attempting to rectify the tragic fate of an American soldier. This bold decision sets off a chain reaction that threatens to unravel the very fabric of reality, raising questions about the nature of destiny and the unpredictability of human actions.

Douglas's prose is both elegant and evocative, skillfully capturing the complexity and nuance of human emotions. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, each with their own distinct voice and motivations. The relationships between the characters are skillfully crafted, with subtle undertones that add depth to the narrative. The way she balances the darker aspects of war with poignant moments of hope and redemption serves as a poignant reminder of the resilience and strength that defines humanity.

What truly sets this book apart is its unique blend of historical facts and fictional storytelling. Douglas has a remarkable ability to weave the two together seamlessly, making the reader feel like they are experiencing history firsthand. Her meticulous research is evident throughout the narrative, but it never feels like an info dump – rather, it's an organic component of the story that enriches our understanding of this pivotal moment in history.

One of the standout aspects of this book is its ability to balance action, suspense, and historical accuracy. The pacing is well-balanced, with moments of high tension and heart-pumping suspense that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. The dialogue is crisp and engaging, and the character development is exceptional. By the time the story reaches its climax, readers will be fully invested in the characters' fate and will be eagerly turning the pages to discover the outcome.

In conclusion, "Time Risk 1: A Time Travel Thriller of Pearl Harbor – Book 1" is a must-read for fans of historical fiction, time travel, and suspenseful narratives. Elyse Douglas's masterful storytelling and vivid prose transport readers to a pivotal moment in history, raising fundamental questions about the nature of risk and the unpredictability of human actions. If you're looking for a book that will take you on a thrilling journey through time and challenge your perceptions about the very fabric of reality, this is the perfect choice.

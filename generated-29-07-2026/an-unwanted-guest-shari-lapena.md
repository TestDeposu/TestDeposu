# An Unwanted Guest

**Author:** Shari Lapena  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

**An Unwanted Guest: A Chilling Thriller in the Vein of Agatha Christie**

Shari Lapena, the master of domestic suspense, returns with another gripping tale that will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very last page. In "An Unwanted Guest," Lapena masterfully weaves a web of deceit, suspicion, and murder, set against the isolated backdrop of a blizzard-swept Catskills mountain lodge.

The story begins innocently enough, with a group of strangers seeking refuge from the world at Mitchell's Inn. But as the storm intensifies, cutting off all contact with the outside, the guests' true colors begin to emerge. When one of them turns up dead, the remaining inhabitants must confront the horrifying possibility that a killer is among them.

Lapena's strength lies in her ability to create complex, flawed characters that are as intriguing as they are infuriating. Each guest harbors secrets, and as the tension mounts, so does the desperation to uncover the truth. The author expertly peels back the layers of each character, revealing motivations and histories that slowly come into focus, like a photograph developing in a darkroom.

The narrative is structured as a classic whodunit, with a touch of the claustrophobic atmosphere of Agatha Christie's "And Then There Were None." Yet Lapena infuses her story with a modern sensibility, exploring themes of identity, trust, and the dangers of assumption in the age of social media. The result is a thriller that feels both timeless and timely.

Lapena's prose is crisp and engaging, her pacing flawless. She expertly builds suspense, doling out clues and red herrings with a skilled hand. The setting of Mitchell's Inn is almost a character itself, its isolation and beauty both a sanctuary and a prison for the guests.

"An Unwanted Guest" is a testament to Lapena's skill as a storyteller. It's a thriller that will keep you guessing until the very end, and even then, you might find yourself questioning what you thought you knew. It's a must-read for fans of psychological suspense, and a perfect choice for book clubs looking for a thought-provoking discussion.

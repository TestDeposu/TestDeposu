# The Opportunist (Love Me with Lies, #1)

**Author:** Tarryn Fisher  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

In "The Opportunist", Tarryn Fisher weaves a complex and twisted narrative that delves into the darker aspects of love, relationships, and the human psyche. This first installment in the "Love Me with Lies" trilogy is a masterclass in character manipulation, both within the story and in the reader's perception of the characters.

At its core, "The Opportunist" is a tale of redemption, or rather, the pursuit of it. Olivia Kaspen, the protagonist, is a flawed and calculating individual who sees an opportunity to rekindle a past love when her ex-boyfriend, Caleb, suffers from amnesia. As she navigates this new reality, Olivia must confront the consequences of her past actions and the lies she's told to protect herself.

Fisher's writing is akin to a slow-burning fire, simmering with tension and foreboding. Her prose is evocative and visceral, making it impossible not to become invested in the characters' lives. The narrative is layered, with multiple timelines and perspectives that add depth and complexity to the story.

One of the most striking aspects of "The Opportunist" is its exploration of the gray areas between right and wrong. Olivia is a morally ambiguous character, and her actions are often reprehensible, yet Fisher expertly crafts a narrative that elicits both empathy and frustration. This is a testament to the author's skill in creating multidimensional characters that defy easy categorization.

The love triangle at the center of the story is expertly woven, with each character bringing their own brand of toxicity to the table. Caleb's amnesia serves as a catalyst for the events that unfold, and his relationships with both Olivia and Leah are fraught with tension and unpredictability.

Fisher's style is unmistakable, and fans of her previous works will recognize the dark, twisty, and unconventional approach to storytelling. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of slow-burning tension and explosive confrontations that keep the reader on edge.

Ultimately, "The Opportunist" is a thought-provoking exploration of the human condition, with all its flaws and contradictions. It's a story about the destructive power of love and the lies we tell ourselves to cope with the consequences of our actions. With its complex characters, layered narrative, and expertly crafted tension, this book is a must-read for fans of dark, contemporary romance.

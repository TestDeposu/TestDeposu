# Lord Brocktree (Redwall, #13)

**Author:** Brian Jacques  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2000

**A Tale of Unlikely Allies and Epic Resistance: Lord Brocktree**

Brian Jacques' "Lord Brocktree," the thirteenth installment in the beloved Redwall series, is a thrilling testament to the enduring appeal of medieval fantasy, courage, and camaraderie. Published in 2000, this novel continues Jacques' signature blend of adventure, humor, and richly drawn animal characters, making it a must-read for fans of the series and a captivating introduction for new readers.

At the heart of "Lord Brocktree" lies an unlikely friendship between Dotti, a spirited haremaid, and Lord Brocktree, a battle-scarred badger warrior. Their journey to the legendary mountain stronghold of Salamandastron is disrupted by the nefarious Ungatt Trunn, a wildcat who has seized control of the mountain. To reclaim Salamandastron and restore peace, Dotti and Brocktree must rally an army of diverse creatures, each bringing unique skills and strengths to the table.

Jacques' storytelling prowess shines in his ability to weave together a tapestry of compelling characters, each with their own distinct voice and backstory. From the cunning Dotti to the stoic Brocktree, and the myriad creatures they encounter along the way, each character brings depth and richness to the narrative. This is a testament to Jacques' skill in creating immersive, believable worlds populated by memorable characters.

The author's style is marked by a vivid, descriptive prose that brings the world of Redwall to life. From the lush, verdant forests to the towering, windswept peaks of Salamandastron, Jacques' writing is a sensory feast that transports readers into the heart of the action. His use of dialect and idiomatic language also adds a layer of authenticity to his characters, making them feel even more real and relatable.

"Lord Brocktree" is more than just a thrilling adventure; it's a story about the power of unity, resilience, and the indomitable spirit. It's a tale that reminds us that even in the face of overwhelming odds, courage and camaraderie can triumph. This is a theme that resonates deeply, making "Lord Brocktree" a timeless addition to the Redwall series.

As the Redwall series gains traction with a Netflix original series, "Lord Brocktree" serves as a perfect entry point for new readers. It's a story that stands on its own while also paying homage to the rich history and lore of the Redwall universe. Fans of the series will find much to love in this installment, while new readers will find a captivating, action-packed adventure that's sure to leave them eager for more.

# Chain of Gold (The Last Hours, #1)

**Author:** Cassandra Clare  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

**Chain of Gold: A Mesmerizing Plunge into the Shadowhunter World**

Cassandra Clare returns to her beloved Shadowhunter universe with "Chain of Gold," the first installment in "The Last Hours" trilogy, and it's a captivating blend of romance, intrigue, and demonic mayhem that fans have been craving. Clare's signature style—richly drawn characters, intricate world-building, and a plot that twists like a London fog—is on full display, making this a must-read for both series veterans and new initiates.

At the heart of "Chain of Gold" is Cordelia Carstairs, a young Shadowhunter navigating the treacherous waters of London's high society. Clare masterfully weaves Cordelia's struggle for agency and identity within the patriarchal Shadowhunter world, making her a heroine worth rooting for. Cordelia's determination to forge her own path, even as she's expected to marry well, is a refreshing take on the traditional heroine's journey.

Clare's strength lies in her ability to create complex, flawed characters that feel real and relatable. Cordelia, James, and Lucie Herondale, along with a host of other familiar and new faces, are all beautifully drawn, each with their own secrets and struggles. Their relationships are messy, nuanced, and utterly compelling, driving the plot forward with an emotional intensity that's hard to resist.

The Shadowhunter world is as vibrant and intricate as ever, with Clare expanding upon the lore and mythology in fascinating ways. The introduction of new demonic threats and magical intricacies keeps the story fresh and unpredictable. The setting—Victorian London—is a character unto itself, with Clare's evocative prose bringing the city's grimy streets and glittering ballrooms to life.

However, "Chain of Gold" isn't without its flaws. The pacing can feel uneven at times, with some sections bogged down by excessive world-building or relationship drama. Yet, these moments are quickly overshadowed by the thrilling action sequences and unexpected plot twists that Clare is known for.

In true Cassandra Clare fashion, "Chain of Gold" ends on a cliffhanger that will leave readers desperate for more. It's a testament to Clare's storytelling prowess that she can still surprise and delight fans after so many books in the Shadowhunter universe.

In conclusion, "Chain of Gold" is a captivating return to the world of Shadowhunters, filled with romance, intrigue, and demonic mayhem. It's a must-read for fans of the series and a promising start to a new trilogy.

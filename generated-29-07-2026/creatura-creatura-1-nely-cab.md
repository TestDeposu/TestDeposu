# Creatura (Creatura, #1)

**Author:** Nely Cab  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In the realm of young adult fantasy, Nely Cab's "Creatura" is a captivating tale that delves into the complexities of the human mind and the blurred lines between reality and dreams. Published in 2015, this debut novel has garnered attention for its unique blend of romance, mythology, and psychological thrills. As a book reviewer, I was eager to dive into the world of Isis, a protagonist who can control her dreams, or so she thought.

The story begins with Isis struggling to cope with the aftermath of a breakup, only to find herself tormented by a mysterious creature in her nightmares. As the narrative unfolds, Cab skillfully weaves together a tapestry of emotions, taking the reader on a journey into the depths of Isis's subconscious. The author's writing style is engaging, with vivid descriptions that transport the reader to a world of surreal landscapes and eerie atmospheres.

One of the standout features of "Creatura" is its exploration of the human psyche. Cab masterfully portrays the fragility of the human mind, exposing the darkest corners of Isis's thoughts and emotions. The creature, who is initially perceived as a monster, becomes a symbol of Isis's inner turmoil, representing the fears and anxieties that she has been trying to suppress. As the story progresses, the lines between reality and dreams become increasingly blurred, leaving the reader questioning what is real and what is just a product of Isis's imagination.

The romance in "Creatura" is a slow-burning flame that simmers beneath the surface, adding a layer of tension to the narrative. The relationship between Isis and the creature is complex, with a deep emotional connection that transcends the boundaries of reality. Cab's portrayal of love is raw and intense, highlighting the sacrifices that one must make for the sake of passion.

The supporting cast of characters is equally well-developed, with a jealous ex-boyfriend who serves as a catalyst for the events that unfold. The author's use of secondary characters adds depth to the narrative, creating a rich tapestry of relationships that are expertly woven together.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the pacing of the novel can be somewhat uneven. At times, the narrative feels rushed, with key plot points being glossed over in favor of more introspective moments. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise engaging and thought-provoking novel.

In conclusion, "Creatura" is a captivating tale that will appeal to fans of young adult fantasy and romance. Nely Cab's writing style is engaging, with a unique blend of mythology and psychological thrills. The novel's exploration of the human psyche is both thought-provoking and unsettling, leaving the reader questioning the boundaries between reality and dreams. With its slow-burning romance and complex characters, "Creatura" is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and emotional journey.

# Terms and Conditions (Dreamland Billionaires, #2)

**Author:** Lauren Asher  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

Corporate boardrooms and billionaire empires are fertile ground for romance, but few contemporary authors navigate this high-stakes terrain with the sharp wit and emotional resonance of Lauren Asher. In Terms and Conditions, the second installment of her blockbuster Dreamland Billionaires series, Asher returns to the glittering yet suffocating world of the Hawthorne family, delivering a masterclass in the marriage-of-convenience trope. Originally published to immense fanfare and captivating a massive global readership by 2024, this novel proves that while money can buy almost anything, it cannot control the unpredictable nature of the human heart.

At the center of the narrative is Declan Hawthorne, a man molded by duty, cold calculation, and the heavy burden of legacy. On the precipice of securing his rightful place as CEO of the family media empire, Declan hits an infuriating bureaucratic wall: a bizarre clause in his late grandfather’s will that mandates he marry and settle down. Enter Iris, his fiercely competent, unfailingly loyal assistant who has spent years managing not just his chaotic professional schedule, but his life. Iris offers a brazen, pragmatic solution to his crisis: a fake marriage. On paper, the arrangement is airtight. It solves Declan’s succession dilemma while granting Iris the stability and future she desires, complete with a strict set of rules designed to keep emotions entirely out of the equation. 

Yet, as any seasoned reader of romance knows, the best-laid plans are destined to crumble. Asher’s exploration of the main theme centers on the collision between control and vulnerability. Declan is a man who operates exclusively through contracts and terms, attempting to police his life down to the last minute. Iris, beneath her professional exterior, possesses a quiet resilience and emotional depth that slowly chips away at his icy exterior. The core tension of the novel does not merely stem from the external pressures of the media and the Hawthorne family expectations, but from the internal war Declan wages against his own awakening feelings. 

Asher’s authorial style shines brightest in the rapid-fire banter and sizzling chemistry between her protagonists. Unlike many contemporary romance novels that rely solely on physical attraction, Asher builds the romantic tension through shared glances, acts of service, and the gradual peeling back of emotional armor. The billionaire romance subgenre is often criticized for recycling tired tropes, but Asher revitalizes the genre through snappy dialogue, deeply flawed yet endearing characters, and a pitch-perfect pacing that keeps the pages turning effortlessly. 

The transition from a strictly professional dynamic to genuine intimacy is handled with emotional intelligence. Declan and Iris are not just lovers; they become co-conspirators in a world designed to crush individuality. When the lines between their contractual agreement and reality begin to blur, the emotional stakes skyrocket. Asher expertly portrays the terror of falling in love when you have spent a lifetime protecting your heart, making the inevitable emotional fallout both painful and deeply satisfying to witness. 

Terms and Conditions is a triumph of modern contemporary romance. It delivers all the escapism, luxury, and swoon-worthy romance that readers crave, anchored by a poignant message about letting go of control to truly live. Lauren Asher has crafted a deliciously addictive novel that cements her status as a powerhouse in the romance community. For anyone looking for a story where the grumpy billionaire falls first, the competent heroine holds all the cards, and love breaks every rule in the book, this is an absolute must-read.

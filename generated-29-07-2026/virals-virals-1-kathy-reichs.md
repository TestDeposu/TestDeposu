# Virals (Virals, #1)

**Author:** Kathy Reichs  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In *Virals*, renowned forensic anthropologist Kathy Reichs makes a thrilling transition from adult crime fiction to young adult adventure, bringing her trademark scientific precision and gripping suspense to a whole new generation of readers. Published in 2010, the novel introduces a dynamic young protagonist whose pedigree alone commands attention: Tory Brennan is the grandniece of Reichs’s famous adult series protagonist, Temperance Brennan. Yet, Tory quickly steps out of her great-aunt’s shadow, anchoring a fast-paced, high-concept thriller that brilliantly merges contemporary forensic science with a classic creature-feature mythos.

At its core, the novel explores the theme of mutation, both biological and personal. When Tory and her tight-knit group of science-geek friends—Ben, Hi, and Shelton—rescue a wolfdog pup from a secretive and morally ambiguous facility on Loggerhead Island, their lives are upended. Exposed to a genetically engineered strain of canine parvovirus, the teens undergo a terrifying and exhilarating cellular transformation. Reichs uses this premise not merely for supernatural flash, but as a clever metaphor for the turbulent, unpredictable nature of adolescence itself. The physical changes—heightened senses, accelerated healing, and an instinctual, telepathic bond—mirror the intense peer dynamics, shifting identities, and alienation familiar to every teenager. They are suddenly faster, stronger, and fiercely protective of one another, literalizing the colloquial idea of finding your pack.

Reichs’s writing style is a masterclass in pacing and accessibility. Leveraging her real-world expertise as a forensic scientist, she infuses the narrative with authentic procedural details that elevate the text above standard YA fare. Autopsies, DNA analysis, bone fragments, and marshland ecology are rendered with vivid, gritty accuracy, satisfying readers who crave intellectual substance in their fiction. At the same time, the prose remains remarkably sharp, breezy, and action-oriented. The dialogue between the teenage protagonists crackles with authentic wit and camaraderie, grounding the fantastic elements in believable teenage behaviors and loyalties. 

While the biological twist gives the novel its sci-fi edge, the plot ultimately pivots toward a classic murder mystery. The newly formed pack finds themselves using their enhanced predatory instincts and forensic know-how to hunt down a cold-blooded killer whose crimes intersect with the dark secrets of their island home. This seamless fusion of genres—hard science, teen drama, speculative fiction, and murder mystery—creates a relentless momentum that leaves little room for hesitation. 

*Virals* is an enormously inventive, sharp, and deeply entertaining kickoff to a series. It proves that Kathy Reichs's signature blend of forensic expertise and edge-of-your-seat plotting translates seamlessly to the younger demographic. For fans of high-stakes adventures, loyal friendships, and a touch of the wild side, this novel is an absolute must-read that bites and refuses to let go.

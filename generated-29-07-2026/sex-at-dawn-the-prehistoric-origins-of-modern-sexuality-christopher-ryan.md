# Sex at Dawn: The Prehistoric Origins of Modern Sexuality

**Author:** Christopher Ryan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In "Sex at Dawn: The Prehistoric Origins of Modern Sexuality," Christopher Ryan delivers a provocative and insightful exploration of human sexual behavior, challenging conventional wisdom and standard theories proposed by experts. This book is a game-changer, offering a fresh perspective on the complexities of sex, monogamy, marriage, and family.

Ryan's approach is bold and unapologetic, as he delves into the prehistoric origins of human sexuality to expose the fallacies and weaknesses of modern assumptions. With a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the subject matter, Ryan presents a compelling case that human beings, both male and female, are inherently lustful and that this trait has been a driving force throughout history.

One of the most striking aspects of "Sex at Dawn" is its ability to balance intellectual rigor with accessibility. Ryan's writing is engaging, entertaining, and free of jargon, making the book an enjoyable read for both scholars and non-experts alike. He weaves together a rich tapestry of anthropology, sociology, and psychology to create a narrative that is both informative and thought-provoking.

At its core, "Sex at Dawn" is a book about challenging assumptions and questioning the status quo. Ryan argues that the notion of monogamy as the natural state of human relationships is a myth, and that our attempts to conform to this ideal have led to a range of social and psychological problems. By examining the prehistoric origins of human sexual behavior, Ryan shows that our ancestors were far more promiscuous and open-minded than we often assume, and that this flexibility was a key factor in the survival and success of early human societies.

The implications of Ryan's argument are far-reaching and profound. He suggests that our modern obsession with monogamy and marriage is a cultural construct, rather than a natural or inevitable aspect of human relationships. This challenges readers to rethink their assumptions about love, sex, and relationships, and to consider the possibility that there may be more flexible and fulfilling ways of organizing our personal lives.

Throughout the book, Ryan's style is characterized by his wit, humor, and intellectual curiosity. He is a masterful storyteller, able to convey complex ideas and research findings in a way that is engaging and easy to follow. His passion for the subject matter is evident on every page, and his enthusiasm is infectious.

Overall, "Sex at Dawn" is a wonderfully provocative and well-written book that completely re-evaluates human sexual behavior and gets to the root of many of our social and psychological ills. It is a must-read for anyone interested in sex, relationships, and the human condition. Ryan's work is a testament to the power of intellectual curiosity and the importance of challenging conventional wisdom.

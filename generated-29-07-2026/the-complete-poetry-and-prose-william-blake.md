# The Complete Poetry and Prose

**Author:** William Blake  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

In the realm of literature, few figures have managed to captivate readers with the same level of mystique and brilliance as William Blake. A visionary poet, artist, and philosopher, Blake's works have long been revered for their beauty, complexity, and depth. The Complete Poetry and Prose, published by Delphi Classics, is a monumental collection that brings together the entirety of Blake's poetic and artistic oeuvre, offering readers a comprehensive and immersive experience.

This magnificent volume is a testament to Blake's innovative spirit and his ability to transcend the boundaries of art and literature. The book is beautifully illustrated with numerous images relating to Blake's life and works, providing a rich context for understanding the poet's creative vision. The concise introductions to the poetry and other works serve as a thoughtful guide, helping readers navigate the complexities of Blake's oeuvre.

One of the standout features of this collection is the inclusion of Blake's complete engravings, paintings, and illustrations, which are presented in a stunning 'Plates' section. With over 1,100 images, this section is a treasure trove for art lovers and scholars alike, offering a unique glimpse into Blake's artistic genius. The formatting of the poems is also noteworthy, with careful attention paid to preserving the original layout and typography of the first printed editions.

The chronological and alphabetical contents tables for the poetry are a thoughtful touch, allowing readers to easily locate specific poems and explore Blake's development as a poet. The inclusion of two biographies, written by Irene Langridge and Charles Gardner, provides valuable insights into Blake's literary life and offers a nuanced understanding of his artistic and philosophical influences.

Blake's poetry is characterized by its use of symbolism, mysticism, and social commentary, all of which are woven together with a masterful command of language and form. His famous collections, such as Songs of Innocence and The Marriage of Heaven and Hell, are presented in their entirety, offering readers a chance to experience the full range of Blake's poetic vision.

Throughout his works, Blake grapples with fundamental questions about the nature of humanity, the role of the artist, and the relationship between the individual and society. His poetry is both deeply personal and universally relatable, speaking to the hopes, fears, and aspirations of readers across the centuries.

The Complete Poetry and Prose is a triumph of scholarship and publishing, offering readers a definitive and comprehensive collection of Blake's works. Whether you are a seasoned scholar or a curious reader, this book is an essential addition to any library, providing a rich and immersive experience that will inspire and delight.

In short, The Complete Poetry and Prose is a masterpiece of literary scholarship, a testament to the enduring power of Blake's art and vision. It is a book that will be cherished by readers for generations to come, a reminder of the transformative power of poetry and the boundless creativity of the human spirit.

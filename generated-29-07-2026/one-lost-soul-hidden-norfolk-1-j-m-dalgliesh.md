# One Lost Soul (Hidden Norfolk, #1)

**Author:** J.M. Dalgliesh  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

**One Lost Soul: A Hidden Norfolk Mystery**

In the sprawling, windswept landscapes of Norfolk, J.M. Dalgliesh weaves a tale of secrets, lies, and murder that will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very last page. "One Lost Soul," the first installment in the Hidden Norfolk series, is a masterclass in atmospheric suspense, proving once again why Dalgliesh is a name to be reckoned with in the world of crime fiction.

The story begins with a grim discovery: a young man's body found on a remote farmland, shattering the quiet morning of DI Tom Jannsen. As the investigation unfolds, it leads Jannsen to the esteemed Vale Racing yard, where reputations are as precious as the thoroughbreds they breed. Here, beneath the surface of polite society, bitter rivalries simmer, and secrets fester like a hidden wound.

Dalgliesh's strength lies in her ability to create a sense of place so vivid that you can almost taste the salt on the sea breeze and feel the chill of the Norfolk wind. The vast, isolated landscapes become more than just a backdrop; they're a character in their own right, adding an eerie, unsettling edge to the narrative. This is not the picturesque Norfolk of tourist brochures, but a darker, more sinister version, where ancient grudges fester and old money hides old sins.

At the heart of "One Lost Soul" is a exploration of truth and lies, of the secrets we keep and the masks we wear. Dalgliesh delves into the complex dynamics of the Vale family, peeling back the layers of their lives to reveal the rotten core beneath. Each character is intricately drawn, their motivations and fears laid bare, making it impossible not to become invested in their fates.

The plot is a labyrinth of red herrings and unexpected twists, keeping the reader guessing until the very end. Dalgliesh has a knack for ratcheting up the tension, creating a sense of impending doom that's almost palpable. Just when you think you've got a handle on the story, she pulls the rug out from under you, leaving you reeling.

DI Tom Jannsen is a protagonist worth rooting for. He's a man of quiet integrity, his dry wit providing moments of levity amidst the darkness. Yet, he's also a man haunted by his past, his personal demons adding depth and complexity to his character.

"One Lost Soul" is not just a gripping mystery; it's a meditation on the human condition, on the lies we tell ourselves and others, and the consequences of those lies. It's a testament to Dalgliesh's skill as a storyteller that she can explore such heavy themes without ever losing sight of the plot, or the reader.

If you're a fan of Tana French, Ann Cleeves, or Val McDermid, you'll find much to love in "One Lost Soul." Dalgliesh's prose is sharp and evocative, her characters complex and compelling, her plotting masterful. This is a book that demands to be read in a single sitting, preferably with the lights on.

In a nutshell, "One Lost Soul" is a stunning start to the Hidden Norfolk series, a book that cements J.M. Dalgliesh's place among the greats of crime fiction. It's a dark, atmospheric mystery that will leave you breathless, and eager for more.

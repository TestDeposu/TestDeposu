# Picking Daisies on Sundays

**Author:** Liana Cincotti  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

**Picking Daisies on Sundays** by Liana Cincotti is a delightful, heartwarming rom-com that will leave you swooning and laughing out loud. Cincotti, with her signature wit and charm, weaves a tale that is as comforting as a warm hug and as exhilarating as a first kiss.

At its core, this novel explores the complexities of love, friendship, and the blurred lines between the two. Daniella Daisy Maria, our protagonist, is a hopeless romantic who finds herself entangled in a fake relationship with her childhood best friend and first love, Levi. Cincotti masterfully navigates the ups and downs of their relationship, making us feel every heartache, every butterflies-in-the-stomach moment, and every hilarious mishap along the way.

Cincotti's writing style is as vibrant as the daisies in the title. She paints vivid, relatable characters with her sharp, witty dialogue and descriptive prose. Her ability to balance humor and heartache is truly commendable. She doesn't shy away from the messy, uncomfortable parts of love, but she also knows when to pull back and let the joy and laughter shine through.

The story is a beautiful exploration of second chances, forgiveness, and the power of communication. It's a reminder that sometimes, love isn't about grand gestures, but about showing up, being honest, and being there for each other. Cincotti's characters are flawed, relatable, and incredibly endearing, making it easy for readers to invest in their journey.

**Picking Daisies on Sundays** is a perfect read for anyone who loves a good rom-com, a heartfelt story, or a reminder that sometimes, the best love stories are the ones that start with friendship. It's a book that will make you laugh, cry, and believe in love again. Don't miss out on this gem of a novel.

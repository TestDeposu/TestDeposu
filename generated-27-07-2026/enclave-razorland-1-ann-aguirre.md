# Enclave (Razorland, #1)

**Author:** Ann Aguirre  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**Enclave: A Razor-Sharp Debut in Post-Apocalyptic YA**

In the grimy, claustrophobic world of Ann Aguirre's "Enclave," the remnants of humanity huddle in underground enclaves, their lives as precarious as the flickering lights that illuminate their tunnels. This is no mere post-apocalyptic wasteland; it's a chilling, visceral plunge into a world where survival is not just a struggle, but a brutal, unforgiving fight for every breath.

At the heart of this tale are Deuce and Fade, two Hunters tasked with protecting their enclave from the monstrous Freaks that stalk the tunnels. Deuce, a fierce, resourceful young woman, is a product of her harsh environment, her body honed into a weapon, her emotions locked away. Fade, her partner, is a former Topside dweller, his memories of the world above a haunting reminder of the beauty and horror that still exist.

Aguirre's prose is lean and muscular, her world-building meticulous and immersive. She doesn't just describe the enclaves and the Freaks; she makes you feel the damp, the dark, the constant, gnawing hunger. Her characters are equally well-crafted, their growth and evolution as compelling as the plot itself. Deuce and Fade's relationship is a slow burn, a dance of attraction and mistrust that feels earned and real.

The main theme of "Enclave" is survival, but it's also about the cost of that survival. Deuce and Fade must confront the moral ambiguity of their world, where the line between predator and prey is often blurred. They must also grapple with the consequences of their actions, the choices they make, and the people they become in the process.

Aguirre doesn't shy away from the darkness of her world, nor does she sugarcoat the violence. This is not a story for the faint of heart, but for those who appreciate a gritty, unflinching look at a world gone wrong. It's a story that asks tough questions, offers no easy answers, and leaves you breathless and craving more.

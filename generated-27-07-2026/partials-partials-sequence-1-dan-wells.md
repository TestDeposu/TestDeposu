# Partials (Partials Sequence, #1)

**Author:** Dan Wells  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In the realm of young adult science fiction, few authors have managed to craft a narrative as gripping and thought-provoking as Dan Wells' Partials. This 2012 novel is a masterclass in world-building, character development, and philosophical introspection, all woven together with a breakneck pace that will leave readers breathless.

At its core, Partials is a story about the blurred lines between right and wrong, and the devastating consequences of playing god with science. In a world where humanity has been decimated by a war with genetically engineered beings known as Partials, 16-year-old Kira Walker emerges as a beacon of hope. As a medic, she's determined to find a cure for the RM virus that's killing every newborn, and in doing so, she uncovers a web of secrets and lies that threaten to upend the very foundations of her society.

Wells' writing style is economical and efficient, with a keen focus on plot progression and character development. His prose is sparse, yet evocative, conjuring vivid images of a post-apocalyptic world that's both haunting and mesmerizing. The world-building is meticulous, with a clear sense of history and politics that underpins the narrative.

One of the standout aspects of Partials is its strong female protagonist. Kira is a complex, multi-dimensional character who embodies both vulnerability and strength. Her determination to save her people is admirable, and her willingness to challenge the status quo is inspiring. Through Kira's eyes, we experience the harsh realities of a world gone mad, and the moral dilemmas that come with fighting for survival.

The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, with a diverse range of characters that add depth and nuance to the narrative. From the enigmatic Samm, a Partial who becomes Kira's unlikely ally, to the sinister Dr. Vex, who harbors secrets of his own, each character is meticulously drawn and adds to the overall tension.

The themes of Partials are equally thought-provoking. Wells explores the nature of humanity, and what it means to be alive. Through the Partials, he raises questions about the ethics of scientific experimentation, and the consequences of playing god. These are not easy questions to answer, and Wells doesn't provide any easy solutions. Instead, he presents a complex, multifaceted world that challenges readers to think critically about the implications of scientific progress.

In terms of pacing, Partials is a thrill ride from start to finish. The action is intense, with a series of heart-pumping set pieces that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. The plot twists and turns, with a series of unexpected revelations that will keep readers guessing until the very end.

Overall, Partials is a gripping, thought-provoking novel that will appeal to fans of science fiction, action, and adventure. With its strong female protagonist, diverse cast of characters, and philosophical themes, it's a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling narrative that will challenge their assumptions about the world.

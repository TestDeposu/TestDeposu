# A Very Bad Thing

**Author:** J.T. Ellison  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

"A Very Bad Thing" by J.T. Ellison is a gripping thriller that masterfully weaves together elements of forensic science, suspense, and personal drama. The second installment in the Sam Owens series, this book is a must-read for fans of Patricia Cornwell and Lisa Gardner.

At the heart of the story is Dr. Samantha Owens, a brilliant and determined forensic pathologist who has just started a new life in Washington D.C. As she navigates her new role at Georgetown University's forensic pathology department, she is thrust into a high-stakes investigation involving a mysterious pathogen that has killed three people in the Washington Metro.

What sets Ellison's writing apart is her ability to balance the scientific and technical aspects of forensic science with the personal and emotional struggles of her characters. Sam Owens is a complex and relatable protagonist, whose troubled past and personal demons make her a more nuanced and human character. Her relationship with Xander Whitfield, a former army ranger, adds a layer of tension and romance to the story, but also serves to highlight the themes of loyalty, trust, and redemption that run throughout the book.

One of the most striking aspects of "A Very Bad Thing" is its exploration of the gray areas between good and evil. The killer at the center of the story is a masterfully crafted character, whose motives are both personal and deeply unsettling. As Sam delves deeper into the investigation, she is forced to confront the darker aspects of human nature, and the ways in which even the most well-intentioned individuals can be driven to commit terrible acts.

Ellison's writing is crisp and engaging, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the forensic science that underpins the story. Her use of language is evocative and suspenseful, with a keen sense of pacing that propels the reader forward.

Overall, "A Very Bad Thing" is a gripping and thought-provoking thriller that will appeal to fans of crime fiction and suspense. With its strong protagonist, intricate plot, and nuanced exploration of the human condition, this book is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and unsettling page-turner.

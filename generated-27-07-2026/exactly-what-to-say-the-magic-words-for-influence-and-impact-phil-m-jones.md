# Exactly What to Say: The Magic Words for Influence and Impact

**Author:** Phil M. Jones  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

Title: The Masterclass in Verbal Influence: A Review of Phil M. Jones’s Exactly What to Say

In a world saturated with verbose business guides and bloated sales manuals, Phil M. Jones’s Exactly What to Say: The Magic Words for Influence and Impact arrives like a breath of fresh, efficient air. Clocking in as a remarkably concise read, Jones bypasses the usual fluff found in the genre and cuts straight to the psychological mechanics of human decision-making. The central theme of the book is deceptively simple: the outcomes we achieve in business and in life are directly tied to the specific words we choose, and mastering these linguistic triggers is the ultimate competitive advantage.

Jones, an internationally renowned sales trainer and award-winning communicator, does not treat persuasion as a manipulative dark art. Instead, he frames it through the lens of empathy, clarity, and removing friction from the decision-making process. The book operates on the premise that the right phrase, spoken at the exact right moment, can gently guide a hesitant prospect, colleague, or friend toward a positive "yes" without ever sounding pushy, aggressive, or rehearsed. 

The author’s style is the book’s greatest asset. Eschewing dense academic theories, Jones adopts a pragmatic, highly accessible, and actionable approach. Each chapter introduces a specific "magic word" or phrase, explains the psychology behind why it works, and provides real-world script examples demonstrating how to implement it immediately. Phrases like "I'm not sure if it's for you, but..." or "What do you know about...?" act as cognitive keys that disarm skepticism and invite open dialogue. By lowering the psychological defenses of the listener, Jones teaches readers how to transform everyday conversations into high-impact moments of influence.

What makes this critique particularly striking is the universal applicability of the content. While many sales books are narrowly tailored to corporate B2B environments or aggressive retail pitching, Exactly What to Say transcends industry boundaries. Whether you are a CEO negotiating a merger, a project manager trying to rally your team, or a parent attempting to negotiate bedtime with a toddler, the principles remain powerfully relevant. It is a masterclass in behavioral psychology disguised as a pocket-sized phrasebook.

Industry heavyweights and bestselling authors have lauded the book as the closest thing to a silver bullet or magic wand in communication, and for good reason. Jones proves that you do not need a silver tongue or an overbearing personality to be persuasive; you simply need precision. 

Ultimately, Exactly What to Say is a must-read because it respects the reader's time and delivers an immediate return on investment. It equips you with practical tools that you can put into practice the very same day you open its pages. If you are looking to elevate your interpersonal communication, eliminate friction in your negotiations, and gently guide others to see your perspective with integrity, this book is an indispensable addition to your shelf.

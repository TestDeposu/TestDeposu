# Where Law Ends: Inside the Mueller Investigation

**Author:** Andrew Weissmann  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

**Where Law Ends: A Fascinating Glimpse into the Mueller Investigation**

Andrew Weissmann, a key figure in the Mueller investigation, pulls back the curtain on the historic probe in his new book, "Where Law Ends." This isn't just an account of the investigation; it's a compelling narrative of a team of dedicated public servants navigating uncharted waters, grappling with monumental decisions, and facing unprecedented challenges from the White House.

Weissmann's style is engaging and candid, making complex legal proceedings accessible to the general reader. He doesn't shy away from the team's mistakes, providing a nuanced, human perspective on the investigation. The book is not just about the Russia probe; it's about the people behind it, their debates, deliberations, and the personal toll the investigation took.

At the heart of "Where Law Ends" is the tension between the rule of law and the political pressure exerted by President Trump. Weissmann details how the team raced against the clock, handicapped by Trump's threats and dangling pardons. This isn't just a book about an investigation; it's a story about a team fighting to uphold the law in the face of extraordinary obstacles.

Weissmann also provides valuable insights into the enigmatic Robert Mueller, revealing him not just as a public figure, but as a manager, colleague, and a very human presence. This is a book that will appeal to both political junkies and legal enthusiasts, offering a rare glimpse into one of the most significant investigations in American history.

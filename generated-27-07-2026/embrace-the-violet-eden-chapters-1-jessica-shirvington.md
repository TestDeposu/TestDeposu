# Embrace (The Violet Eden Chapters, #1)

**Author:** Jessica Shirvington  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

**Embrace: A Darkly Alluring Debut in the Violet Eden Chapters**

Jessica Shirvington's "Embrace," the first installment in her captivating "Violet Eden Chapters" series, is a thrilling blend of paranormal romance and action-packed fantasy that will leave readers eagerly craving more. Fans of "Twilight" and "Fallen" will find themselves drawn into a world that is both familiar and uniquely Shirvington's own.

At its heart, "Embrace" explores the age-old theme of destiny and the choices we make when faced with impossible circumstances. Violet Eden, our protagonist, is a relatable heroine navigating the complexities of love, loss, and self-discovery. Her journey begins on her seventeenth birthday, a day she dreads due to the anniversary of her mother's death. Little does she know, this birthday will mark the start of a new life, one where she learns she is Grigori - part angel, part human - with a destiny to protect humans from exiled angels.

Shirvington's writing style is engaging and fast-paced, drawing readers in with vivid descriptions and taut dialogue. She masterfully weaves together the mundane aspects of Violet's life - her love for art, her complicated relationship with her father, her crush on the enigmatic Lincoln - with the extraordinary, as Violet grapples with her newfound powers and the dangerous world she's been thrust into.

The love triangle between Violet, Lincoln, and the mysterious Phoenix is a central aspect of the story, but it's not the only driving force. Shirvington skillfully balances the romantic tension with high-stakes action and suspense, as Violet faces off against powerful exiled angels and grapples with the moral complexities of her new role.

One of the standout aspects of "Embrace" is its exploration of the gray areas of morality. Shirvington doesn't shy away from presenting complex, flawed characters, and she challenges readers to question their own beliefs about good and evil. This depth of character and theme elevates "Embrace" above many other paranormal romances, making it a compelling and thought-provoking read.

In conclusion, "Embrace" is a captivating debut that masterfully blends romance, action, and fantasy. With its engaging protagonist, complex themes, and fast-paced plot, it's a must-read for fans of paranormal romance and urban fantasy. Shirvington's unique take on the angel mythology and her exploration of the gray areas of morality make "Embrace" a standout in its genre.

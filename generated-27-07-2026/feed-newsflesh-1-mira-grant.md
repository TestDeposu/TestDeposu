# Feed (Newsflesh, #1)

**Author:** Mira Grant  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

"In a world where the undead roam and the living tweet, Mira Grant's 'Feed' is a masterclass in post-apocalyptic storytelling. This isn't your typical zombie fare; it's a chilling exploration of power, truth, and the insidious nature of information in the digital age. Grant's prose is as sharp as a scalpel, deftly slicing through societal norms and political facades. Georgia and Shaun Mason, our intrepid bloggers, are as real as they are flawed, their voices resonating with an authenticity that's both refreshing and unsettling. 'Feed' is a novel that will make you question what you read, who you trust, and how much of the truth you're willing to stomach. It's a gripping, thought-provoking ride that's as addictive as the feed it's named after. #ZombieLit #PostApocalyptic #PoliticalThriller #MiraGrant #Newsflesh #ZombieSurvival"

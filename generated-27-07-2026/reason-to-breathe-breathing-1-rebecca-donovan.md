# Reason to Breathe (Breathing, #1)

**Author:** Rebecca Donovan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

Title: Unraveling the Storm: A Review of Rebecca Donovan’s *Reason to Breathe*

In the quaint, tight-knit community of Forgotten, the past is rarely left behind, but it can certainly come back to haunt you. In *Reason to Breathe*, Rebecca Donovan delivers a poignant, emotionally resonant contemporary romance that explores the heavy cost of unspoken truths, missed opportunities, and the resilience required to love after profound loss. Far from a simple tale of rekindled flames, Donovan crafts a deeply character-driven narrative where emotional survival is the ultimate stakes.

At the heart of the story is Keisha Jefferson, a woman whose youthful ambitions of a legal career and a happily-ever-after were derailed years ago by a fateful, stormy night. Now, hardened by grief and guarded by heavy secrets, Keisha has rebuilt her life on her own terms—strictly excluding romance, and most importantly, excluding Xander Greenwood. Xander, once the small-town boy with an uncertain future, has transformed into a dedicated physician through sheer grit and sacrifice. When a new storm forces them back into each other's orbits, the narrative ignites with a volatile mixture of lingering passion and defensive walls. 

Donovan’s thematic brilliance lies in how she mirrors her characters' internal turmoil with the unpredictable forces of nature. Storms act as both literal and figurative catalysts throughout the novel, signaling pivotal shifts in Keisha and Xander’s relationship. The central theme explores the friction between sacrifice and communication. Xander fought to build a life worthy of Keisha, believing his absence was a necessary sacrifice, while Keisha fought to survive an entirely different kind of storm in isolation. Donovan examines the agonizing gap between what we do for love and what love actually requires us to say out loud.

The author’s style is clean, evocative, and deliberately paced. Donovan understands the anatomy of a slow-burn romance, allowing the tension to build naturally through charged dialogue, lingering glances, and the palpable weight of unspoken history. Unlike many standard romance tropes where miscommunications feel forced or petty, the secrets keeping Keisha and Xander apart are weighty, deeply rooted in trauma and self-protection. This gives the narrative a gravity that elevates it above a typical sweet romance, adding a layer of mystery that keeps the pages turning eagerly. 

Set against the atmospheric backdrop of Forgotten, the novel benefits immensely from Donovan’s ability to build a living, breathing community. The supporting characters feel like an extended family, grounding the central romance in a realistic social fabric where everyone's history is inextricably linked. This interconnectedness adds warmth and comfort to the narrative, balancing out the emotional intensity of Keisha and Xander's turbulent history.

*Reason to Breathe* is a triumph of emotional storytelling, offering a deeply satisfying conclusion that marries wholesome romance with genuine character growth. Rebecca Donovan proves once again that she knows how to wring every ounce of emotion from a premise, delivering a story that lingers long after the final page. For readers who love second-chance romance infused with mystery, healing, and hard-won hope, this novel is an absolute must-read.

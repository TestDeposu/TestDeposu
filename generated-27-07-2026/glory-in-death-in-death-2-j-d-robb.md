# Glory in Death (In Death, #2)

**Author:** J.D. Robb  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1995

Glory in Death, the second installment in J.D. Robb's beloved In Death series, is a gripping and intense mystery that delves into the darker aspects of human nature. Published in 1995, this novel has stood the test of time, and its themes of power, corruption, and the blurred lines between good and evil remain just as relevant today.

At the heart of the story is Police Lieutenant Eve Dallas, a tough and determined cop with a penchant for getting to the bottom of even the most complex cases. When a string of high-profile murders takes place, Eve is tasked with tracking down the killer, and as she digs deeper, she uncovers a web of secrets and lies that lead her to some of the most powerful men in the city. As the investigation unfolds, Eve's own personal life becomes increasingly entangled with the case, particularly when her lover, Roarke, becomes a suspect.

One of the most striking aspects of Robb's writing style is her ability to craft complex, multi-dimensional characters. Eve is a fascinating and deeply human protagonist, with a rich inner life and a deep sense of vulnerability. Her relationship with Roarke is particularly well-drawn, and the tension between them is palpable as Eve struggles to reconcile her personal feelings with her professional obligations. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, from the victim's glamorous friends and acquaintances to the shady characters who lurk in the shadows, waiting to be exposed.

Throughout the novel, Robb explores themes of power, corruption, and the darker aspects of human nature. The murders are brutal and seemingly senseless, and as Eve delves deeper into the case, she uncovers a tangled web of motivations and desires that lead her to some surprising conclusions. The world of Glory in Death is a dark and unforgiving one, where the wealthy and powerful believe themselves to be above the law, and where the vulnerable are often exploited and discarded.

Robb's writing style is characterized by its clarity, precision, and attention to detail. Her prose is lean and economical, with a keen sense of pacing that propels the reader through the story at a breakneck speed. The futuristic setting of the novel, which is set in the mid-21st century, is vividly realized, with a keen sense of detail that immerses the reader in the world of the story.

One of the most impressive aspects of Glory in Death is its ability to balance action, suspense, and romance. The novel is a true page-turner, with a complex and engaging plot that keeps the reader guessing until the very end. At the same time, the romance between Eve and Roarke is sweet and tender, and adds a welcome layer of depth and emotion to the story.

In conclusion, Glory in Death is a gripping and intense mystery that is sure to appeal to fans of the genre. With its complex characters, dark themes, and breakneck pacing, this novel is a must-read for anyone who loves a good thriller. J.D. Robb's writing style is masterful, and her ability to craft a compelling story that balances action, suspense, and romance is unparalleled.

# Before They Are Hanged (The First Law, #2)

**Author:** Joe Abercrombie  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

**Before They Are Hanged: A Dark, Intriguing Journey into the Heart of Power**

In the sprawling, grimy world of the Union, where ambition and betrayal are as common as the cold, Joe Abercrombie's "Before They Are Hanged" picks up where "The Blade Itself" left off, delivering another masterclass in dark fantasy that's as compelling as it is brutal.

At the heart of this sequel lies a profound exploration of power and its corrupting influence. Abercrombie, with his signature cynical wit, dissects the motivations of his characters with unflinching honesty. Here, power is not a force for good but a tool wielded by the ambitious, the ruthless, and the desperate. It's a commodity to be bought, sold, and stolen, leaving a trail of broken lives in its wake.

Our cast of morally grey characters, each more flawed than the last, navigate this treacherous landscape with varying degrees of success. Superior Glokta, the crippled torturer turned inquisitor, is a standout. His internal monologues, dripping with sarcasm and self-loathing, are a joy to read. Abercrombie's prose is sharp and engaging, his dialogue snappy and authentic, drawing you into the grimy underbelly of his world with ease.

The plot is a complex web of political intrigue, military campaigns, and ancient secrets, all woven together with a deft hand. Abercrombie doesn't shy away from the grim realities of war, nor does he pull punches when it comes to the consequences of his characters' actions. This is a world where good men die and the wicked often prosper, and it's all the more compelling for it.

"Before They Are Hanged" is not for the faint-hearted. It's a dark, often brutal tale that pulls no punches. But if you're looking for a fantasy novel that's as smart as it is entertaining, that explores the darker side of human nature with unflinching honesty, then look no further. Abercrombie's First Law Trilogy is a must-read, and "Before They Are Hanged" is a standout entry in a series that's already proven itself to be one of the best in modern fantasy.

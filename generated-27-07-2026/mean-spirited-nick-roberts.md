# Mean Spirited

**Author:** Nick Roberts  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

**Title: A Monastic Maze of Fear and Faith: "The Rotting Room" by Viggy Parr Hampton**

In the hallowed halls of horror literature, a new name is set to make its mark: Viggy Parr Hampton. With "The Rotting Room," Hampton delivers a chilling tale that weaves together the sacred and the profane, the divine and the diabolical, in a mesmerizing dance of dread and devotion.

At the heart of this gripping narrative lies Sister Rafaela, a woman haunted by her past, seeking redemption within the cloistered walls of the Sisters of Divine Innocence. Yet, the abbey she calls home harbors secrets as dark as her own, shrouded in a vow of silence and a burial ritual that is as disturbing as it is intriguing. When a mysterious stranger arrives and dies, her body refusing to succumb to decay, fevered whispers of sainthood begin to echo through the abbey. But Rafaela, with her keen intuition and unyielding resolve, suspects something far more sinister at play.

Hampton's prose is as hauntingly beautiful as it is unsettling, painting a vivid picture of the abbey's decaying grandeur and the sisters' desperate clinging to faith. She masterfully employs narrative restraint, allowing the tension to build organically, like a slow-burning fuse leading to an explosive revelation. Yet, she refuses to pull her punches, delivering moments of sheer terror that will leave readers gasping.

"The Rotting Room" is a testament to the power of religious horror, standing shoulder to shoulder with classics like "The Exorcist," "Immaculate," and "Suspiria." Hampton's writing is as irresistible as it is oppressive, drawing readers in with its gothic allure, only to hold them captive in its chilling embrace.

As Rafaela and Father Bruno race against time to unravel the truth, Hampton keeps readers on the edge of their seats, questioning what they believe and what they fear. This is not just a story about a rotting corpse; it's a tale of faith tested, hope challenged, and courage rewarded.

In a genre saturated with the supernatural, "The Rotting Room" stands out, proving that there's still plenty of room for originality and innovation. Hampton's debut is a masterclass in horror, a chilling symphony of fear and faith that will resonate long after the final page is turned.

# Sh*t My Dad Says

**Author:** Justin Halpern  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**Sh*t My Dad Says: A Hilarious, Heartwarming Journey into the Unfiltered Mind of a Curmudgeonly Dad**

In the vast landscape of memoirs and humor books, Justin Halpern's debut, **Sh*t My Dad Says**, stands out as a refreshingly raw, laugh-out-loud exploration of the modern father-son dynamic. Halpern, a comedy writer, found himself back under his father's roof after a breakup, and what ensued was a treasure trove of wisdom, profanity, and unexpected heartwarming moments, all captured in this delightful book.

At the core of **Sh*t My Dad Says** lies the unfiltered, no-holds-barred philosophy of Sam Halpern, a 73-year-old with a penchant for bluntness and a unique perspective on life. From his thoughts on kindergarten ("It's like prison, but with more finger painting") to his advice on lying ("The worst thing you can be is a liar... Okay fine, yes, the worst thing you can be is a Nazi, but THEN, number two is liar. Nazi 1, Liar 2"), Sam's words are equal parts hilarious and thought-provoking.

Halpern's style is reminiscent of David Sedaris, blending witty, irreverent essays with poignant observations. He masterfully weaves his father's one-liners into longer-form narratives, creating a compelling, often touching portrait of their relationship. The book is more than just a collection of funny quotes; it's a journey into the heart of a complex, loving, and utterly human father-son bond.

**Sh*t My Dad Says** is not for the faint-hearted. It's filled with profanity, but it's also filled with love, laughter, and a healthy dose of wisdom. It's a book that will make you laugh until you cry, and then cry because it reminds you of your own family's quirks and eccentricities.

In a world where many books about family relationships can feel overly sentimental or clichéd, **Sh*t My Dad Says** is a breath of fresh air. It's a hilarious, heartwarming reminder that sometimes, the most profound wisdom comes from the most unexpected places.

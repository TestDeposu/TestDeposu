# Killer Instinct (The Naturals, #2)

**Author:** Jennifer Lynn Barnes  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

**Killer Instinct: A Thrilling Dance with the Unseen**

In the realm of young adult thrillers, Jennifer Lynn Barnes' "Killer Instinct" stands out as a gripping, psychological tour de force that masterfully blends the intrigue of "The Mentalist" with the suspenseful allure of "Pretty Little Liars." The second installment in her "The Naturals" series, this book is a testament to Barnes' ability to create complex, engaging narratives that keep readers on the edge of their seats.

At its core, "Killer Instinct" explores the theme of perception versus reality, a concept that Barnes weaves expertly throughout the story. Our protagonist, Cassie, is a natural at reading people, but her gift is both a blessing and a curse. She can see the truth hidden beneath the surface, yet she struggles to understand her own place in the world. This internal conflict drives the narrative, as Cassie navigates the treacherous waters of the FBI's secret program, the Naturals, where everyone seems to have an agenda.

Barnes' writing style is a significant strength of the novel. She employs a third-person limited perspective, allowing readers to delve deep into Cassie's mind, experiencing her thoughts and feelings firsthand. This intimate narrative style creates a sense of urgency and immediacy, drawing readers in and making them invested in Cassie's journey. Barnes also excels at crafting intricate, believable characters, each with their own unique abilities and motivations. The dynamics between these characters are complex and engaging, adding another layer of depth to the story.

The plot is a carefully orchestrated dance with the unseen, as Cassie and her fellow Naturals grapple with a new killer on the loose. Barnes expertly builds tension, using red herrings and unexpected twists to keep readers guessing until the very end. The pacing is excellent, with a balance of character development, plot progression, and suspenseful action sequences that make "Killer Instinct" an unputdownable read.

Barnes also explores the darker side of human nature, delving into themes of manipulation, trust, and betrayal. As Cassie and her friends work to solve cold case murders, they must also navigate the treacherous landscape of their own relationships, learning who they can trust and who might be hiding dangerous secrets.

In conclusion, "Killer Instinct" is a thrilling, suspenseful ride that will keep readers guessing until the very end. With its complex characters, intricate plot, and exploration of the theme of perception versus reality, this book is a must-read for fans of young adult thrillers. Jennifer Lynn Barnes' signature style shines in this novel, making it a standout in the genre.

# Dare You To (Pushing the Limits, #2)

**Author:** Katie McGarry  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

Dare You To, the second installment in Katie McGarry's Pushing the Limits series, is a mesmerizing tale of self-discovery and the complexities of human relationships. The story revolves around Beth Risk, a troubled teenager who has learned to shield herself from the world due to her tumultuous home life, and Ryan Stone, the charismatic baseball star with a flawless exterior that conceals his own set of secrets. When their paths intersect, they are forced to confront their inner demons and challenge the perceptions they have of themselves and each other.

One of the most striking aspects of McGarry's writing is her ability to craft relatable, well-rounded characters. Beth and Ryan are multidimensional protagonists, with distinct voices and backstories that inform their actions and decisions throughout the narrative. McGarry's characterization is remarkable, as she skillfully delves into the intricacies of their emotional struggles, making it effortless for readers to become invested in their journeys.

The author's style is notable for its authenticity and sensitivity in tackling tough subjects. McGarry doesn't shy away from exploring the darker aspects of life, including abuse, neglect, and the long-lasting effects of trauma. Her portrayal of these issues is heartfelt and realistic, never resorting to gratuitous or exploitative depictions. Instead, she handles these themes with care and compassion, creating a narrative that is both poignant and thought-provoking.

At its core, Dare You To is a story about the power of human connection and the transformative impact it can have on our lives. McGarry masterfully explores the ways in which relationships can be both redemptive and destructive, often at the same time. The romance between Beth and Ryan is a slow-burning, intense affair that adds depth and complexity to the narrative. Their relationship is fraught with challenges, but it also serves as a catalyst for growth, forcing them to confront their fears, insecurities, and the secrets they've kept hidden for so long.

Throughout the book, McGarry's prose is engaging and accessible, making it easy for readers to become fully immersed in the world she's created. Her writing is descriptive and evocative, with a keen sense of pacing that propels the story forward. The supporting cast of characters is equally well-developed, adding richness and texture to the narrative.

In conclusion, Dare You To is a compelling and emotionally resonant novel that explores the complexities of human relationships, trauma, and the power of love and connection. Katie McGarry's writing is authentic, sensitive, and engaging, making this book a must-read for fans of contemporary young adult fiction. With its well-crafted characters, poignant themes, and masterful storytelling, Dare You To is a truly unforgettable reading experience.

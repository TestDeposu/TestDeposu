# All In (The Naturals, #3)

**Author:** Jennifer Lynn Barnes  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

Cold cases, razor-sharp psychological profiling, and high-stakes danger collide in All In, the electrifying third installment of Jennifer Lynn Barnes’s acclaimed *The Naturals* series. Long before she captivated millions with the labyrinthine puzzles of *The Inheritance Games*, Barnes was busy crafting this masterclass in YA crime fiction—a series frequently described as the love child of *Criminal Minds* and *A Good Girl’s Guide to Murder*. In *All In*, Barnes pushes her elite team of teenage profilers past their breaking points, delivering a breathless thriller that proves the past is never truly dead.

At the heart of the narrative is Cassie Hobbes, a seventeen-year-old with an uncanny ability to read the minute details of human behavior, micro-expressions, and psychological tells. Alongside a specialized FBI division of similarly gifted teens—each possessing lethal talents ranging from detecting lies to understanding sociopathy—Cassie has faced down ruthless serial killers. But *All In* raises the psychological ante. When a case hits uncomfortably close to home, blurring the lines between their classified profiling work and their own deeply guarded personal histories, the team is forced into a lethal game of cat and mouse where the hunter quickly becomes the hunted. 

Barnes’s greatest triumph in this novel lies in her exploration of the main theme: nature versus nurture, and the terrifying realization that trauma can either forge a hero or birth a monster. The Naturals program operates on a razor-thin ethical margin, using traumatized youths to catch predators, constantly skirting the fear that these teens might recognize too much of themselves in the killers they hunt. Barnes explores this psychological darkness with remarkable nuance, ensuring the internal stakes feel just as perilous as the external physical threats.

The author’s signature style is on full, brilliant display here. Barnes writes with a propulsive, cinematic momentum that makes putting the book down an exercise in pure agony. Her prose is sharp, economical, and heavily driven by psychological deduction. Instead of relying solely on bloody shock value, Barnes builds suspense through brilliant character interplay, deductive reasoning, and escalating paranoia. Every clue feels earned, every plot twist lands with precision, and the intricate puzzle boxes she builds keep readers guessing until the very last page. 

Furthermore, Barnes masterfully balances the grim realities of criminal profiling with the authentic, messy vulnerability of teenage emotion. The interpersonal dynamics—particularly the simmering, high-tension romance and the fierce, ride-or-die loyalty binding the Naturals together—add an emotional anchor to a narrative that could otherwise feel relentlessly dark. We care about these characters not just because they are brilliant, but because they are deeply broken and desperately trying to heal one another.

*All In* is a triumph of YA crime fiction. It delivers everything fans of the genre crave: breakneck pacing, a labyrinth of twists, razor-sharp dialogue, and characters that burrow straight into your psyche. Jennifer Lynn Barnes proves once again why she is a master of suspense, offering a gripping, unputdownable ride that leaves readers desperate for the next chilling chapter.

# Kingdom of the Wicked (Kingdom of the Wicked, #1)

**Author:** Kerri Maniscalco  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

Kingdom of the Wicked, the latest novel by Kerri Maniscalco, is a dark, intoxicating, and thrilling ride that delves into the world of witches, demons, and forbidden magic. Set in 19th-century Sicily, the story follows Emilia, a young strega who is forced to navigate a treacherous world of secrets and lies after her twin sister Vittoria is brutally murdered.

Maniscalco's writing style is as captivating as ever, weaving a complex web of characters, plot twists, and mythology that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. Her vivid descriptions of Sicily's lush landscapes, rich history, and vibrant culture transport readers to a world of beauty and mystery. The author's use of language is evocative and immersive, drawing readers into Emilia's world of grief, anger, and determination.

At its core, Kingdom of the Wicked is a story about female empowerment, sisterhood, and the struggle for justice in a world dominated by patriarchal forces. Emilia's quest for vengeance is a powerful exploration of the consequences of trauma, the danger of unchecked ambition, and the redemptive power of love and forgiveness. Through Emilia's journey, Maniscalco sheds light on the darker aspects of human nature, exposing the hypocrisy and corruption that can lead to devastating consequences.

One of the most compelling aspects of the novel is its exploration of the complex relationships between women. Emilia's bond with her sister Vittoria is deeply moving, and their shared experiences as streghe create a rich and nuanced portrayal of sisterhood. The novel also introduces a cast of intriguing female characters, each with their own secrets and motivations, adding depth and complexity to the narrative.

The romance in the novel is equally captivating, as Emilia finds herself drawn to Wrath, a mysterious and charismatic prince of Hell. Their relationship is fraught with tension, danger, and forbidden passion, adding a delicious layer of complexity to the story. Maniscalco's portrayal of Wrath is masterful, crafting a character that is both captivating and terrifying, with a dark and troubled past that slowly unravels throughout the novel.

If you're a fan of dark fantasy, historical fiction, or romance, Kingdom of the Wicked is a must-read. Maniscalco's unique blend of mythology, history, and suspense creates a thrilling narrative that will keep you up late at night, devouring every word. With its lush prose, complex characters, and addictive plot, this novel is sure to captivate readers and leave them eagerly anticipating the next installment in the series.

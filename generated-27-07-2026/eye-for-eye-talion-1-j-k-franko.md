# Eye for Eye (Talion #1)

**Author:** J.K. Franko  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

"Eye for Eye" by J.K. Franko is a gripping thriller that delves into the complex morality of vigilantism, wrapped in a plot that keeps readers on the edge of their seats. The novel, the first in the 'Talion' trilogy, explores the consequences of pushing the limits of justice, leaving readers questioning their own stance on retribution.

Franko's prose is crisp and engaging, painting vivid scenes that transport readers into the heart of the action. The story unfolds in Miami, a city that serves as more than just a backdrop, its vibrant culture and underbelly becoming integral to the narrative. The author's ability to weave intricate plotlines with seamless ease is evident, as he masterfully navigates between multiple perspectives, each voice distinct and compelling.

At the core of "Eye for Eye" lies the age-old question: can one take the law into their own hands when the justice system fails? The protagonist, Alex Rios, a former detective turned vigilante, grapples with this ethical dilemma as he seeks revenge against those who wronged his family. Franko doesn't shy away from exploring the grey areas of morality, presenting a nuanced portrayal of a flawed hero driven by love, loss, and a desire for justice.

The novel is a thrilling cat-and-mouse game, with Rios' actions drawing the attention of both the police and a ruthless criminal organization. Franko expertly builds tension, with each chapter ending on a cliffhanger, making it impossible to put the book down. The story is filled with unexpected twists and turns, keeping readers guessing until the very end.

"Eye for Eye" is more than just a fast-paced thriller; it's a thought-provoking exploration of justice, revenge, and the thin line between the two. Franko's writing style, a blend of gritty realism and suspenseful tension, makes this novel a standout in the genre. Fans of thrillers like "The Silence of the Lambs" and "The Girl with the Dragon Tattoo" will find "Eye for Eye" an absorbing read that leaves a lasting impact.

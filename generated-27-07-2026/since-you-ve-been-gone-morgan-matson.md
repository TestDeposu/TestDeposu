# Since You've Been Gone

**Author:** Morgan Matson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

In "Since You've Been Gone" by Morgan Matson, we're introduced to Emily, a shy and introverted teenager whose life is turned upside down when her best friend Sloane disappears without warning. The only clue left behind is a cryptic to-do list consisting of thirteen tasks that Sloane has carefully curated, each one designed to push Emily out of her comfort zone.

As Emily embarks on this journey to complete Sloane's list, she finds herself facing a multitude of challenges that test her courage, resilience, and sense of self. From apple picking at night to dancing until dawn, each task serves as a catalyst for Emily's growth and transformation. But it's the more daunting tasks, like kissing a stranger, that force Emily to confront her deepest fears and insecurities.

Throughout the book, Matson's writing is characterized by its effortless charm and wit. Her prose is engaging, relatable, and infused with a deep understanding of the adolescent experience. The characters in the novel are multidimensional and authentic, with Emily being a particularly well-crafted protagonist. Her narrative voice is distinct, honest, and often humorous, making it easy to become fully invested in her journey.

One of the most striking aspects of "Since You've Been Gone" is its thoughtful exploration of female friendship and the ways in which our relationships with others can shape us. The bond between Emily and Sloane is beautifully portrayed, and their friendship serves as a powerful catalyst for Emily's growth and self-discovery.

Matson's use of the to-do list as a narrative device is also noteworthy. Each task serves as a clever plot device, allowing Emily to confront her fears, challenge her assumptions, and develop new skills. The list also serves as a poignant reminder of Sloane's presence in Emily's life, even in her absence.

The supporting cast of characters in the novel is equally well-developed, with Frank Porter being a standout. His quiet confidence and gentle encouragement serve as a perfect counterbalance to Emily's anxiety and self-doubt.

Ultimately, "Since You've Been Gone" is a heartwarming and engaging novel that explores themes of identity, friendship, and the power of taking risks. Matson's writing is engaging, relatable, and authentic, making this book a must-read for fans of contemporary young adult fiction.

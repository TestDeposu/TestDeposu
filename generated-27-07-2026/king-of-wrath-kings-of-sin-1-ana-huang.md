# King of Wrath (Kings of Sin, #1)

**Author:** Ana Huang  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**King of Wrath: A Passion-Fueled Dance of Power and Desire**

In Ana Huang's latest offering, "King of Wrath," the first installment of her Kings of Sin series, she masterfully weaves a tale of power, passion, and the intricate dance between two people bound by duty and desire. Huang, known for her captivating storytelling and ability to create chemistry that sizzles off the page, does not disappoint in this enemies-to-lovers, arranged marriage romance.

At the heart of the story lies Dante Russo, a billionaire CEO who commands his empire with the same iron fist he uses to control his personal life. A man who has no time for love, he finds himself forced into an engagement with Vivian Lau, the heiress to a rival jeweler's dynasty. Vivian, a woman of strength and resilience, agrees to the union out of familial duty, despite her misgivings about the arrogant and enigmatic Dante.

The stage is set for a clash of titans, but what neither expects is the undeniable attraction that sparks between them. Huang excels in painting the slow burn of their relationship, the tension palpable as Dante and Vivian navigate their new roles as fiancés. Their banter is sharp, their chemistry electric, and their slow descent into love is a journey that is as satisfying as it is heart-wrenching.

Huang's strength lies in her ability to create complex, flawed characters that readers can't help but root for. Dante, with his brooding intensity and hidden vulnerabilities, is a hero worth falling for, while Vivian's strength and determination make her a heroine to admire. Their journey is not an easy one, filled with obstacles that threaten to tear them apart, but it is in these challenges that their love truly shines.

The author's style is elegant yet intense, with a knack for describing scenes that are as vivid as they are steamy. She balances the heat with moments of tenderness, creating a love story that is as much about the heart as it is about the body. The plot is well-paced, with twists and turns that keep the reader on the edge of their seat, while the world-building is immersive, drawing readers into the luxurious yet cutthroat world of high society and big business.

"King of Wrath" is more than just a romance; it's a story about power, redemption, and the lengths we go to protect the ones we love. It's a testament to Huang's skill as a storyteller that she can take a trope as old as time and make it feel fresh and exciting. This is a book that will leave you breathless, your heart pounding, and your soul yearning for more.

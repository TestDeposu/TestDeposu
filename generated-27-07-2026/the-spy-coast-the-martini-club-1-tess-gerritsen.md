# The Spy Coast (The Martini Club, #1)

**Author:** Tess Gerritsen  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

The Spy Coast, the inaugural installment of The Martini Club series, is a masterfully curated anthology that brings together the crème de la crème of short mystery fiction from the past year. Edited by the inimitable Tess Gerritsen, this collection is a must-read for fans of the genre. Gerritsen, renowned for her Rizzoli & Isles series, has outdone herself by selecting a diverse range of stories that showcase the best of modern crime writing.

The anthology boasts an impressive lineup of authors, including Lee Child, Andrew Klavan, Joyce Carol Oates, and Lisa Unger, alongside lesser-known writers who are sure to leave a lasting impression. The stories have been carefully plucked from a variety of sources, including The Strand Magazine, Black Cat Weekly, Dark Yonder, and Ellery Queen Mystery Magazine, ensuring a rich and varied reading experience.

One of the standout features of The Spy Coast is its ability to balance the familiar with the unknown. Gerritsen's introduction is warm and inviting, setting the tone for a journey that will take readers on a wild ride of twists and turns. The stories themselves are expertly crafted, each one a masterclass in suspense and intrigue. From the gritty realism of Lee Child's contribution to the clever wordplay of Andrew Klavan's tale, every story is a testament to the power of short fiction.

Gerritsen's own style is evident throughout the anthology, her love of the genre shining through in every selection. Her passion for storytelling is infectious, and her introductions to each story are informative and insightful, providing a fascinating glimpse into the minds of the authors.

The bonus story from Agatha Christie, sourced from the Mysterious Bookshop's rare book room, is a delightful addition to the collection. It's a reminder of the enduring power of Christie's writing and a nod to the rich history of the genre.

If there's one criticism to be made, it's that the anthology is perhaps a tad too eclectic. Some readers may find the jump between styles and tones jarring, but for the most part, Gerritsen's curation is spot on. The Spy Coast is a true celebration of the short mystery form, and Gerritsen's enthusiasm is impossible to resist.

In short, The Spy Coast is a triumph, a must-read for anyone who loves mystery and suspense. Gerritsen's expertise and passion shine through on every page, making this anthology an absolute must-have for fans of the genre.

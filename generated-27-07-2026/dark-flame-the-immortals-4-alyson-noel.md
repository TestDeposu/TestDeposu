# Dark Flame (The Immortals, #4)

**Author:** Alyson Noel  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**Dark Flame: A Dance with Darkness**

In "Dark Flame," Alyson Noël masterfully weaves a tale of love, power, and the fine line between the two, as Ever struggles to maintain control in a world that's spiraling out of it. This fourth installment in the Immortals series is Noël's darkest and most seductive yet, delving into the shadows of Ever's past and the dangerous allure of her enemy.

At the heart of "Dark Flame" lies the theme of duality—of light and dark, love and hate, power and vulnerability. Ever, our immortal heroine, is torn between her love for Damen and her growing, inexplicable attraction to Roman, the very embodiment of darkness. Noël explores this internal conflict with a deft hand, making it impossible for readers to simply dismiss Roman as a villain. Instead, he becomes a complex, haunting presence, his evil tempered by a raw, primal magnetism that draws Ever in, despite her best efforts to resist.

Noël's signature style is evident throughout, with vivid, sensory descriptions that transport readers into Ever's world. From the lush, sun-drenched landscapes of Palm Springs to the chilling, otherworldly beauty of the Immortals' hidden realm, every scene is painted with a richness that makes it impossible not to be swept away. Yet, it's Noël's exploration of the human—make that immortal—condition that truly sets her apart. She delves deep into Ever's psyche, laying bare her fears, desires, and insecurities with a raw honesty that makes Ever's struggles feel painfully, beautifully real.

However, "Dark Flame" isn't without its flaws. The pacing can feel uneven at times, with certain plot points rushing forward while others linger, seemingly without purpose. And while the love triangle at the core of the story is compelling, it can also feel forced, a plot device to keep readers on the edge of their seats rather than a natural evolution of Ever's journey.

Yet, these are minor quibbles in an otherwise enthralling tale. "Dark Flame" is a testament to Noël's ability to craft compelling, complex characters and weave them into a story that's equal parts dark, seductive, and heart-wrenching. It's a story about love, yes, but also about power, identity, and the choices we make when the line between right and wrong becomes blurred.

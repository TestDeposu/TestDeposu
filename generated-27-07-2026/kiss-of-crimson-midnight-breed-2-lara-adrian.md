# Kiss of Crimson (Midnight Breed, #2)

**Author:** Lara Adrian  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

**Kiss of Crimson: A Seductive Dance with the Dark**

Lara Adrian's "Kiss of Crimson," the second installment in her Midnight Breed series, is a thrilling and sensual plunge into a world where vampires walk among us, hidden in plain sight. Adrian masterfully weaves a tale that seamlessly blends action, romance, and a touch of the paranormal, creating a captivating narrative that keeps readers on the edge of their seats.

At the heart of the story lies Tess Culver, a veterinarian with a heart of gold and a secret psychic gift she's only beginning to understand. When she finds Dante, a Breed vampire, on her doorstep, she's drawn into a world she never knew existed. Dante, a formidable warrior with a dark past, is a captivating hero, torn between his duty to his kind and his growing feelings for Tess. Their connection is palpable, and Adrian's ability to build tension and chemistry between her characters is nothing short of impressive.

Adrian's style is characterized by vivid, sensory descriptions that transport readers into the story. From the gritty streets of Boston to the opulent lairs of the Breed, every setting is brought to life with meticulous detail. Her characters are equally well-crafted, each with their own unique voice and backstory. Even the secondary characters are fully realized, adding depth and complexity to the world Adrian has created.

The main theme of "Kiss of Crimson" is the struggle between duty and desire, and the power of love to transcend even the darkest of fates. Tess and Dante's relationship is a slow burn, built on mutual respect and attraction that deepens with each shared secret and moment of vulnerability. Their connection is not just physical, but emotional and spiritual, making their love story all the more compelling.

Adrian also explores the theme of acceptance, both of one's own destiny and of the love that can change that destiny. Tess, who has always felt like an outsider due to her psychic abilities, finds acceptance and understanding in Dante's world. Meanwhile, Dante, who has lived his life bound by duty and honor, learns to accept the love and happiness that Tess brings into his life.

"Kiss of Crimson" is not without its flaws. The pacing can feel a bit uneven at times, with some action sequences dragging while others race by in a whirlwind. However, these moments are few and far between, and do little to detract from the overall enjoyment of the story.

In conclusion, "Kiss of Crimson" is a must-read for fans of paranormal romance, urban fantasy, and vampire lore. Lara Adrian's unique take on the vampire mythos, combined with her engaging characters and thrilling plot, make this book a standout in the genre. It's a story that will leave you breathless, eager for more.

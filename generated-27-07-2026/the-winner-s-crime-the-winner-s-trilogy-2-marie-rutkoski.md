# The Winner's Crime (The Winner's Trilogy, #2)

**Author:** Marie Rutkoski  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

**The Winner's Crime: A Dance of Deception and Desire**

In the captivating sequel to "The Winner's Curse," Marie Rutkoski weaves a tale of political intrigue, forbidden love, and the high price of deception. "The Winner's Crime" is a mesmerizing exploration of trust, loyalty, and the consequences of our choices, set against the opulent backdrop of a royal court teeming with danger and desire.

Kestrel, our cunning and conflicted heroine, finds herself ensnared in a web of her own making. Engaged to the crown prince in a political alliance, she yearns to reveal the truth to Arin, the young Valorian commander she loves: that she agreed to the marriage to secure his freedom. Yet, as the wedding approaches, Kestrel grapples with her own doubts and the ever-present threat of discovery. For Kestrel has become a spy in the court, a dangerous game that could cost her everything if she's caught.

Rutkoski masterfully explores the complexities of trust and betrayal, as Kestrel and Arin navigate a treacherous landscape of secrets and lies. Their relationship is a delicate dance of push and pull, love and mistrust, as they both strive to protect each other and their respective peoples. The author's prose is elegant and evocative, painting vivid images of the court's grandeur and the stark realities of war, all while drawing readers deeper into the characters' emotional turmoil.

At its heart, "The Winner's Crime" is a story about the consequences of our actions and the lengths we'll go to protect those we love. It's a thrilling tale of political machinations and forbidden love, but it's also a profound exploration of the human condition. Rutkoski doesn't shy away from the complexities of her characters or the harsh realities of their world, making "The Winner's Crime" a compelling and thought-provoking read.

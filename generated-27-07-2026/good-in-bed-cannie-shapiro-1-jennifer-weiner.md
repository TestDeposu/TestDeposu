# Good in Bed (Cannie Shapiro, #1)

**Author:** Jennifer Weiner  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2002

"Good in Bed" is a delightful, heartwarming journey through the life of Cannie Shapiro, a plus-size, pop culture reporter navigating the complexities of love, self-acceptance, and personal growth. Jennifer Weiner's signature wit and warmth shine through in this novel, making it an engaging and relatable read.

The story begins with Cannie's world turned upside down when she discovers her ex-boyfriend has written an article about their relationship, titled "Loving a Larger Woman." This triggers a year of self-discovery and change for Cannie, as she grapples with her past, confronts her insecurities, and rediscovers her strength and resilience.

Weiner's prose is crisp and engaging, her characters vivid and relatable. Cannie is a heroine you can't help but root for, her struggles and triumphs feeling achingly real. Weiner's exploration of body image, self-worth, and the complexities of love is both poignant and humorous, making "Good in Bed" a compelling and satisfying read.

At its heart, "Good in Bed" is a story about embracing one's true self, flaws and all. Weiner masterfully weaves together humor, heartache, and hope, creating a narrative that is both entertaining and thought-provoking. This is a book that will make you laugh, cry, and ultimately, feel inspired.

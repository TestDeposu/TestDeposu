# Mile High (Windy City, #1)

**Author:** Liz Tomforde  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**Mile High: A Sizzling Sports Romance That Soars**

Liz Tomforde's debut novel, 'Mile High,' is a thrilling, heart-pounding romance that takes off from the moment you buckle up. This TikTok sensation is a must-read for fans of enemies-to-lovers tropes and sports romances, delivering a perfect blend of tension, humor, and steam.

At the heart of 'Mile High' is a classic battle of wills between two strong personalities. Evan 'Zanders' Zander, the Chicago hockey team's bad boy, is used to being the center of attention, both on and off the ice. He's arrogant, egotistical, and loves every minute of it. Stevie, our quick-witted and independent azafata, isn't impressed. She's seen it all before and has no intention of being another notch on Zanders' stick.

Tomforde's writing style is engaging and witty, with a knack for creating memorable dialogue that sparkles with chemistry. The banter between Zanders and Stevie is electric, keeping readers on the edge of their seats, eager to see who will blink first. The author expertly weaves their evolving dynamic with the high-stakes world of professional hockey, creating a story that's as exhilarating as it is romantic.

'Mile High' isn't just a romance; it's a journey of self-discovery for both protagonists. As Zanders and Stevie clash, they're forced to confront their own biases and insecurities. Tomforde handles this growth with sensitivity and nuance, ensuring that neither character feels one-dimensional or predictable.

The setting of a professional hockey team's private jet is a stroke of genius, providing a unique backdrop for the story and allowing Tomforde to explore themes of power dynamics, class, and the price of fame. The author's experience in the aviation industry shines through, lending authenticity to the story and making the high-flying setting feel grounded and real.

'Mile High' is more than just a romance; it's a love letter to Chicago, to hockey, and to the power of finding common ground with someone you least expect. It's a story about learning to let go of preconceived notions and embracing the unexpected. And, of course, it's a story about love - the kind that sneaks up on you when you're least expecting it, leaving you breathless and wanting more.

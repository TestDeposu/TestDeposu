# World After (Penryn & the End of Days, #2)

**Author:** Susan Ee  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

**World After: A Stunning Sequel to a Phenomenal Debut**

Susan Ee's "World After" is a breathtaking sequel to her critically acclaimed "Angelfall," the first book in the Penryn & the End of Days trilogy. Ee masterfully continues the story of Penryn, a resilient young woman navigating a world ravaged by angels, in a narrative that is as engaging as it is terrifying.

The world as we know it has ended, and humanity is struggling to survive in the aftermath of the angel apocalypse. Penryn, her mother, and her sister Paige are among the survivors, but their new reality is far from peaceful. When Paige is taken by a group of humans who mistake her for a monster, a bloody confrontation ensues, leaving Paige missing and Penryn determined to find her.

Ee's storytelling prowess shines in "World After" as she weaves a tale that is equal parts action-packed and thought-provoking. The world she has created is grim and unforgiving, yet it is also a testament to the human spirit's resilience. Penryn's character is a beacon of hope in this desolate landscape, her strength and determination making her a heroine worth rooting for.

Ee's writing style is concise and powerful, painting vivid images with her words. She expertly balances suspense and horror, keeping readers on the edge of their seats. The pacing is excellent, with the story moving at a steady clip that never feels rushed. The plot twists are clever and unexpected, making "World After" a page-turner that is impossible to put down.

One of the most striking aspects of "World After" is Ee's exploration of the themes of humanity and monstrosity. She challenges readers to question what it truly means to be human, and whether the line between good and evil is as clear-cut as it seems. This philosophical depth adds a layer of complexity to the story that elevates it above typical post-apocalyptic fare.

"World After" is not just a thrilling adventure; it is a profound meditation on the human condition. Ee's ability to blend high-stakes action with deep, thought-provoking themes is what makes her storytelling so compelling. Fans of "Angelfall" will not be disappointed with this sequel, and new readers will find themselves drawn into Ee's captivating world.

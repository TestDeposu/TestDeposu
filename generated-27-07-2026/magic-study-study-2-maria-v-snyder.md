# Magic Study (Study, #2)

**Author:** Maria V. Snyder  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

**Magic Study: A Spellbinding Journey of Self-Discovery and Danger**

In "Magic Study," Maria V. Snyder weaves a captivating tale that delves into the heart of Sitia, a land where magic runs deep and secrets lurk in every shadow. The sequel to "Poison Study," this novel is a thrilling exploration of identity, trust, and the power that lies within us.

Yelena, our resilient heroine, finds herself in unfamiliar territory, both literally and figuratively. Fleeing Ixia after discovering her magical abilities, she seeks refuge in Sitia, her birthplace. Yet, she finds no warm welcome; instead, she's met with suspicion and even hatred. Snyder masterfully portrays Yelena's struggle to find her place in this new world, her internal conflict resonating with readers as she grapples with her identity and the weight of her past.

At the heart of "Magic Study" lies the exploration of magic and its laws. Snyder's world-building is intricate and immersive, with a magic system that feels both ancient and tangible. As Yelena learns to harness her powers under the tutelage of master magicians, readers are drawn into the Citadel, a place of learning and danger in equal measure. Snyder's prose is engaging, painting vivid images of the Citadel's grandeur and the dark undercurrents that threaten to consume it.

Yet, "Magic Study" is not just about magic and self-discovery; it's also a gripping mystery. A rogue magician targeting young women emerges, and Yelena finds herself on the trail of this sinister force. Snyder expertly builds tension, keeping readers on the edge of their seats as Yelena races against time to unmask the culprit.

Snyder's signature style shines in "Magic Study." She crafts strong, relatable characters, each with their own unique voice and motivations. The relationships between these characters are complex and nuanced, adding depth to the story. Snyder also excels in creating a sense of danger and intrigue, making it impossible to predict what will happen next.

In conclusion, "Magic Study" is a spellbinding journey that will captivate fans of fantasy and mystery alike. Snyder's intricate world-building, engaging prose, and compelling characters make this novel a must-read. It's a testament to her skill as a storyteller that she can make readers care so deeply about characters who, in the beginning, seem so far removed from our own lives.

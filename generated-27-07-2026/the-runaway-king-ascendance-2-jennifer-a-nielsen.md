# The Runaway King (Ascendance, #2)

**Author:** Jennifer A. Nielsen  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

The Runaway King, the second installment in Jennifer A. Nielsen's Ascendance Series, is a heart-pounding, action-packed ride that delves deeper into the complexities of power, loyalty, and identity. Picking up where The False Prince left off, this sequel masterfully weaves a tale of deception, betrayal, and survival, as Jaron, the newly crowned king, finds himself on the run for his life.

Nielsen's writing style is as captivating as ever, with a keen sense of pacing that propels the reader forward, barely allowing time to catch their breath. The world-building, too, is meticulous, with a richly detailed landscape that immerses the reader in the kingdom of Carthya. The characters, however, are the true heart of the story, with Jaron's wit, courage, and vulnerability making him an endearing and relatable protagonist.

At its core, The Runaway King explores the theme of identity, particularly in the context of leadership and power. Jaron's struggle to reconcile his true self with the demands of his royal position is deeply relatable, and Nielsen raises important questions about the nature of sacrifice, duty, and loyalty. As Jaron navigates the treacherous landscape of court politics, he must confront the reality that his own survival may depend on abandoning the very identity he has fought so hard to claim.

One of the most striking aspects of Nielsen's writing is her ability to balance light and dark, humor and pathos, with a deft hand. The Runaway King is a book that will keep you on the edge of your seat, but it's also a story that will make you laugh, cry, and perhaps even nod your head in recognition. Nielsen's characters are multidimensional and flawed, with even the most seemingly minor players possessing a depth and complexity that adds richness to the narrative.

If you're a fan of fantasy, adventure, or simply great storytelling, The Runaway King is an absolute must-read. With its breakneck pace, memorable characters, and thought-provoking themes, this book is sure to captivate readers of all ages. And, with the promise of more to come in the series, there's no better time to join the journey.

In short, The Runaway King is a thrilling, emotionally resonant, and deeply satisfying read that will leave you eager for more. So, if you haven't already, join the adventure and discover the magic of Jennifer A. Nielsen's Ascendance Series for yourself.

# The Sun and the Star (The Nico di Angelo Adventures, #1)

**Author:** Rick Riordan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**Preview/Critique: The Sun and the Star by Rick Riordan**

In "The Sun and the Star," Rick Riordan, the master of modern mythology, weaves another spellbinding tale that will captivate fans of the Percy Jackson saga. This time, the spotlight shines on Nico di Angelo and Will Solace, two beloved demigods navigating the treacherous waters of love, loss, and loyalty in the darkest depths of the Underworld.

Riordan's signature style is evident from the first page, as he effortlessly blends Greek mythology with contemporary themes, creating a rich tapestry of adventure, emotion, and humor. The narrative is a testament to Riordan's ability to craft compelling characters that readers can't help but invest in. Nico, the son of Hades, grapples with his past traumas and the weight of his newfound happiness with Will, while Will, the son of Apollo, struggles with the limitations of his light in the darkest of realms.

The main theme of "The Sun and the Star" is the power of love and the sacrifices we make for those we care about. Nico and Will's relationship is a beacon of hope in the gloomy Underworld, but it's also a source of tension. Their love is tested as they descend into Tartarus, and Riordan masterfully explores the complexities of their bond, making their journey feel both epic and deeply personal.

Riordan's co-author, Mark Oshiro, brings a fresh perspective to the story, adding depth to the characters and their relationships. Together, they create a compelling narrative that stands on its own while also feeling like a natural extension of the Percy Jackson universe.

The pacing is swift, with just the right balance of action, suspense, and emotional beats. Riordan's world-building is as impressive as ever, with Tartarus coming alive in all its terrifying glory. The author's knack for creating memorable, often hilarious, monsters and gods is on full display, making the journey into the Underworld an unforgettable one.

"The Sun and the Star" is more than just an adventure; it's a love story, a tale of friendship, and a testament to the power of light in the darkest places. Riordan fans will delight in this return to the world of demigods, while new readers will find a captivating entry point into the Percy Jackson universe.

# The Archived (The Archived, #1)

**Author:** Victoria Schwab  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

The Archived, Victoria Schwab's 2013 novel, is a haunting and richly imagined tale that delves into the thin lines between past and present, love and pain, trust and deceit, and unbearable loss and hard-won redemption. This captivating book is the first installment in The Archived series and has garnered widespread critical acclaim for its unique blend of paranormal mystery, spine-tingling suspense, and breathtaking prose.

At the heart of the story is Mackenzie Bishop, a teenage Keeper tasked with the daunting responsibility of guarding the dead, who are referred to as Histories and stored on shelves like books in a vast realm known as the Archive. Each History has a story to tell, and only Librarians can decipher the secrets hidden within the pictures that represent the life of each deceased individual. As Mac navigates the complexities of her role, she is forced to confront the harsh realities of her job, which often involves stopping violent Histories from waking up and escaping.

Schwab's masterful storytelling is on full display as she skillfully weaves together a narrative that is both dark and beautiful. The author's vivid descriptions of the Archive, with its labyrinthine corridors and shelves upon shelves of Histories, are nothing short of mesmerizing. The world-building is meticulous, and the concept of the Archive is both fascinating and unsettling.

One of the most striking aspects of The Archived is its exploration of the human condition. Mac's character is multifaceted, and her struggles to come to terms with the loss of her loved ones are deeply relatable. As she grapples with the moral implications of her role as a Keeper, Mac begins to question the boundaries between living and dying, sleeping and waking. This introspection adds a layer of depth to the narrative, making it more than just a paranormal mystery.

Schwab's writing style is lyrical and evocative, with a keen focus on atmosphere and tension. The author's use of language is deliberate and measured, creating a sense of foreboding that permeates the narrative. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and quiet moments of introspection that make the story feel both propulsive and contemplative.

The Archived has been praised by critics and authors alike for its originality and skillful execution. Ruta Sepetys, New York Times bestselling author of Between Shades of Gray, has described the book as "a page-turning plot, a setting that haunted my dreams, and breathtaking prose. Truly a masterful, dark, beautiful novel." Rachel Hawkins, New York Times bestselling author of the Hex Hall series, has also sung the book's praises, stating that it is "gorgeously written as it is brilliantly inventive...earned a permanent spot on my Keeper shelf."

In conclusion, The Archived is a gripping and thought-provoking novel that will appeal to fans of paranormal mysteries and suspenseful storytelling. Victoria Schwab's masterful writing and meticulous world-building make this book a compelling read that will linger in the minds of readers long after they finish the final page.

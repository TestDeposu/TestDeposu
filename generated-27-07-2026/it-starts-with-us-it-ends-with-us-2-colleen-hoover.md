# It Starts with Us (It Ends with Us, #2)

**Author:** Colleen Hoover  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**It Starts with Us: A Raw, Emotional Journey of Second Chances**

Colleen Hoover, the master of emotional storytelling, returns with "It Starts with Us," the much-anticipated sequel to her bestselling novel "It Ends with Us." Picking up where the first book left off, Hoover weaves a tale of love, loss, and the complexities of navigating life's unexpected turns.

At the heart of the story lies Lily Bloom, a woman who has fought hard to overcome her past and build a life for herself and her daughter. When she unexpectedly reunites with her first love, Atlas Corrigan, she's faced with a choice that could upend her carefully constructed world. Atlas, with his own troubled past, offers Lily a second chance at love, but their path is far from smooth. Ryle, Lily's ex-husband and coparent, is still very much present, and his jealousy threatens to derail their newfound happiness.

Hoover masterfully switches between Lily and Atlas's perspectives, allowing readers to delve deep into their thoughts and emotions. This narrative technique not only provides insight into their individual struggles but also highlights the raw, visceral nature of their connection. Hoover's signature style, marked by her ability to tackle heavy subjects with unflinching honesty and heart-wrenching emotion, is on full display here.

The main theme of "It Starts with Us" is the power of second chances and the courage it takes to embrace them. Lily and Atlas's journey is a testament to this, as they grapple with their pasts and fight for a future together. Hoover doesn't shy away from the complexities of their situation, instead choosing to explore the messy, often painful reality of love and loss.

Hoover's writing is as evocative and engaging as ever, drawing readers in and keeping them invested in Lily and Atlas's story. She has a unique ability to make readers feel deeply for her characters, and "It Starts with Us" is no exception. Fans of Hoover's work will find themselves once again immersed in her world, eager to see how Lily and Atlas's story unfolds.

In essence, "It Starts with Us" is a powerful exploration of love, loss, and the courage it takes to embrace a second chance. It's a story that will resonate with anyone who has ever dared to hope for a happy ending, even in the face of adversity. Colleen Hoover, once again, delivers an emotional read that will leave readers both teary-eyed and hopeful.

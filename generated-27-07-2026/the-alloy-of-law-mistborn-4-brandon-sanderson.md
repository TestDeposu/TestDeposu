# The Alloy of Law (Mistborn, #4)

**Author:** Brandon Sanderson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**A New Alloy of Magic: Sanderson's "The Alloy of Law"**

In the sprawling, intricate world of Brandon Sanderson, three hundred years have passed since the events of the Mistborn trilogy, yet the magic of Scadrial continues to evolve. "The Alloy of Law" (Mistborn, #4) is a captivating blend of the familiar and the fresh, a heist story that deftly weaves political intrigue and magical, martial-arts action.

At its heart, "The Alloy of Law" explores the theme of change and adaptation. Waxillium Ladrian, our protagonist, is a rare Twinborn, capable of Allomancy (Pushing on metals) and Feruchemy (manipulating weight). After two decades in the frontier Roughs, Wax returns to the metropolis of Elendel, trading his guns for the duties of a noble house head. Yet, when a gang of Allomancers turns to train robbery and kidnapping, Wax finds that the city's elegance belies its danger.

Sanderson's signature style shines in "The Alloy of Law." His world-building is meticulous, with a unique magic system that feels both innovative and grounded. The action sequences are visceral and thrilling, while the political maneuvering is intricate and engaging. Sanderson's characters are another strength; Wax is a compelling hero, his struggle to adapt to city life and his past tragedies making him relatable despite his extraordinary abilities.

Moreover, Sanderson masterfully balances the familiar Mistborn elements with the new, steampunk-inspired setting. The Roughs' frontier spirit and Elendel's industrial growth provide a fascinating contrast, reflecting the broader theme of change and progress.

"The Alloy of Law" is a testament to Sanderson's versatility as an author. It's a thrilling adventure that also explores profound themes, making it a must-read for both Mistborn fans and new readers alike.

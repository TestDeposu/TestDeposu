# The Shadows Between Us (The Stathos Sisters, #1)

**Author:** Tricia Levenseller  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

**A Dance of Shadows and Schemes: Tricia Levenseller's "The Shadows Between Us"**

In the vein of "Game of Thrones" meets "The Count of Monte Cristo," Tricia Levenseller's "The Shadows Between Us" is a captivating tale of ambition, love, and the shadows that lurk in the heart of power. Levenseller, known for her swashbuckling adventures in "Daughter of the Pirate King," returns with a more intricate and seductive dance of court intrigue, set against a richly imagined fantasy world.

At the heart of the story is Alessandra, a young woman with a plan as cunning as the shadows that haunt her dreams. She's tired of being overlooked, and her ambition burns like a flame, illuminating her path to the throne. Her plan is simple yet audacious: woo the enigmatic Shadow King, marry him, and then claim his kingdom for herself. But the road to power is never smooth, and Alessandra finds herself navigating treacherous waters, both political and romantic.

The Shadow King, a man shrouded in mystery and whispers, is a fascinating enigma. His power over shadows is as intoxicating as it is terrifying, and Levenseller weaves this element of magic deftly into the narrative, using it to heighten tension and suspense. The dynamics between Alessandra and the King are complex and compelling, shifting like the shadows they command, keeping readers on the edge of their seats.

Levenseller's prose is as sharp as a blade, cutting through the complexities of court life with ease. She paints vivid portraits of her characters, from the cunning Alessandra to the enigmatic King, and the world they inhabit is richly detailed, with a particular delight in food descriptions that will make your mouth water. The pacing is swift, the plot twists unexpected, and the tension builds like a storm on the horizon, threatening to consume everything in its path.

At its core, "The Shadows Between Us" is a story about power and the lengths we'll go to obtain it. It's a tale of love and loss, of loyalty and betrayal, and the shadows that lurk within us all. Levenseller masterfully explores these themes, weaving a tale that is as darkly beautiful as it is dangerous.

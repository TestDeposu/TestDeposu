# Wildwood Dancing (Wildwood, #1)

**Author:** Juliet Marillier  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

**Wildwood Dancing: A Whirling Blend of Folklore, Magic, and Coming-of-Age**

In the heart of Transylvania, Juliet Marillier weaves a captivating tale of sisterhood, love, and the power of nature in "Wildwood Dancing," the first installment of her Wildwood series. This enchanting novel is a testament to Marillier's signature style, seamlessly blending folklore, magic, and a touch of romance to create a spellbinding narrative that will leave readers yearning for more.

At the core of "Wildwood Dancing" lies the enchanting Piscul Draculi castle, home to five sisters and their doting father. The castle's hidden portal to the Other Kingdom, a realm of fey creatures and moonlit dances, is the sisters' secret playground. However, their idyllic life is disrupted when their father falls ill, and their cousin Cezar arrives, casting a shadow of suspicion and danger over their home.

Marillier masterfully explores the complexities of sisterhood through the five siblings, each with her unique personality and strengths. Jena, the second eldest, is our steadfast heroine, navigating the treacherous waters of love, loyalty, and betrayal with unwavering determination. Her bond with her sisters is the novel's heart, pulsating with warmth, rivalry, and unspoken understanding.

The Other Kingdom, a realm of magic and mystery, is a character in itself, brimming with Marillier's vivid imagination. From the ethereal fey creatures to the ancient, whispering woods, this enchanted world is as much a part of the story as the human characters. The dances held under the full moon are particularly captivating, painting a mesmerizing picture of otherworldly beauty and danger.

Marillier's prose is as lyrical as the dances in the Other Kingdom, flowing with a rhythm that draws readers in and keeps them enthralled. She expertly balances suspense, romance, and folklore, creating a story that is as engaging as it is enchanting. The author's deep understanding of folklore, particularly that of Eastern Europe, shines through in the intricate details and rich symbolism that enrich the narrative.

"Wildwood Dancing" is a coming-of-age story that transcends the ordinary, delving into the depths of love, loss, and the power of nature. It is a tale of trust, strength, and true love, told with Marillier's signature grace and finesse. This novel is a must-read for fans of folklore, magic, and captivating storytelling.

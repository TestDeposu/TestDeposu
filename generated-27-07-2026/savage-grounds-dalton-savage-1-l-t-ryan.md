# Savage Grounds (Dalton Savage #1)

**Author:** L.T. Ryan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

In the gritty, unapologetic world of "Savage Grounds," L.T. Ryan masterfully weaves a complex web of betrayal, revenge, and redemption. The story follows Clevis Dalton, a former cop turned recluse, who is pulled back into the dark underbelly of Los Angeles to investigate the gruesome murder of his ex-wife. As he delves deeper into the case, he must confront his troubled past and navigate a treacherous landscape of violence and hatred.

Ryan's writing style is akin to a punch to the gut - it's raw, unflinching, and unapologetic. He tackles themes of trauma, loyalty, and the blurred lines between good and evil with a level of intensity that's both captivating and unsettling. The author's use of vivid, often disturbing imagery adds to the sense of unease, making it impossible to look away from the carnage unfolding on the page.

At its core, "Savage Grounds" is a story about the destructive power of obsession and the devastating consequences of playing with fire. The charismatic leader of the killer crew, Hardin James, is a master manipulator who orchestrates a game of cat and mouse with Clevis and his team. As the stakes escalate, the true extent of James' motivations is revealed, leaving the reader stunned and reeling.

One of the most striking aspects of Ryan's writing is his ability to craft complex, multidimensional characters. Clevis, in particular, is a fascinating study in contrasts - a tough, no-nonsense cop with a deep sense of vulnerability and emotional scars. His tumultuous relationship with his former partner, Walden Wade, is a highlight of the book, adding a layer of tension and depth to the narrative.

The supporting cast is equally well-developed, from the enigmatic policewoman who becomes embroiled in Clevis' life to the unhinged members of the killer crew. Each character is meticulously crafted to add to the overall sense of unease and tension, making it impossible to predict what will happen next.

If you're a fan of gritty, hard-boiled thrillers with complex characters and a healthy dose of violence, then "Savage Grounds" is the book for you. Be warned, however - this is not a read for the faint of heart. Ryan's unflinching portrayal of the darker aspects of human nature will leave you breathless and disturbed, but ultimately, it's a testament to the author's skill that you'll be unable to look away.

In short, "Savage Grounds" is a gripping, intense thrill ride that will appeal to fans of authors like Lee Child and Dennis Lehane. With its complex characters, breakneck pacing, and unflinching portrayal of violence, this is a book that will stay with you long after the final page has been turned.

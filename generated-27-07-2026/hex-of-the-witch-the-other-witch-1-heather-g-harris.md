# Hex of the Witch (The Other Witch, #1)

**Author:** Heather G. Harris  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

Hex of the Witch by Heather G. Harris is a captivating urban fantasy that delves into the complex world of magic, power struggles, and the blurred lines between destiny and choice. The first installment of The Other Witch series, this book introduces us to Lexi Balefire, a witch with a penchant for getting entangled in supernatural drama and a penchant for survival.

At the heart of the story lies the escalating feud between Lexi and Diana Diamond, a rival witch with an agenda darker than black witchfire. As Diana wreaks havoc on Port Harbor, Lexi must navigate a web of magical attacks, complicated relationships, and her own inner turmoil. Harris expertly weaves together a narrative that is both fierce and funny, with a snarky heroine at its center.

One of the standout aspects of Hex of the Witch is its exploration of the goddess within. Lexi's internal struggle to contain her goddess aspect, Alexis, adds a layer of depth to the story, raising questions about the nature of power, control, and identity. Harris's portrayal of this inner conflict is both poignant and thought-provoking, making Lexi a relatable and endearing protagonist.

The world-building in Hex of the Witch is equally impressive. Harris has created a richly detailed magical underbelly, complete with faerie godmothers, enchanted deck of cards, and a Bow of Destiny that whispers seductive promises. The author's use of magical realism adds a touch of authenticity to the narrative, making the supernatural elements feel grounded and believable.

Harris's writing style is engaging and witty, with a keen sense of humor that adds to the book's charm. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and emotional moments that keep the reader invested in the story. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with complex characters like Kin, Serena, and Jett adding to the narrative's depth.

Overall, Hex of the Witch is a compelling urban fantasy that will appeal to fans of the genre. With its high stakes, powerful rivals, and a heroine torn between goddess and witch, this book is a must-read for anyone looking for a story that is both fierce and funny. Harris's writing is engaging, and her world-building is top-notch, making this series a promising addition to the urban fantasy landscape.

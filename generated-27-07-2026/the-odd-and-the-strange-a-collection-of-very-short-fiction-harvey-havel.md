# The Odd and The Strange: A Collection of Very Short Fiction

**Author:** Harvey Havel  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

In the realm of short fiction, it's a rare occurrence to come across a collection that defies conventions and pushes the boundaries of storytelling. Harvey Havel's "The Odd and The Strange" is a masterful compilation of very short fiction that not only challenges the reader's perceptions but also leaves an indelible mark on their psyche.

At its core, "The Odd and The Strange" is a thought-provoking exploration of the human condition, delving into the darker aspects of existence with unflinching honesty. Havel's writing style is a unique blend of the fabulism of Jorge Luis Borges and the bleakness of Stephen Crane, creating a narrative that is both unsettling and mesmerizing.

Throughout the collection, Havel tackles a wide range of themes, from the absurdity of politics to the brutality of violence. In stories like "Santa Claus and Madam Secretary," he employs satire to critique the ills of society, while in "Lightning Love," he crafts a haunting tale of transformation that is both disturbing and brilliant.

One of the standout features of Havel's writing is his ability to craft compelling narratives that are both concise and impactful. His use of language is economical, yet evocative, drawing the reader into a world that is both familiar and strange. The result is a reading experience that is both unsettling and thought-provoking, leaving the reader to ponder the deeper meanings and symbolism that underlie each story.

What sets "The Odd and The Strange" apart from other collections of short fiction is its unflinching commitment to exploring the darker aspects of human nature. Havel's stories are not for the faint of heart, but for readers who are willing to confront the harsh realities of existence. In doing so, he creates a narrative that is both honest and unapologetic, a true reflection of the complexities and contradictions of the human condition.

In conclusion, "The Odd and The Strange" is a masterful collection of short fiction that will appeal to readers who enjoy thought-provoking and unsettling narratives. Havel's unique writing style, which blends the fabulism of Borges with the bleakness of Crane, creates a reading experience that is both memorable and impactful. If you're looking for a collection of short fiction that will challenge your perceptions and leave you pondering the deeper meanings of life, then "The Odd and The Strange" is a must-read.

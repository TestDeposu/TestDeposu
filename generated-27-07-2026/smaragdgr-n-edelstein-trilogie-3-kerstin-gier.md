# Smaragdgrün (Edelstein-Trilogie, #3)

**Author:** Kerstin Gier  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

**Preview/Critique:**

In the dazzling conclusion to her reimagined "Edelstein-Trilogie," Kerstin Gier weaves a spellbinding tapestry of love, destiny, and time, leaving readers breathless until the very end. "Smaragdgrün" is not just a sequel; it's a breathtaking odyssey that transcends the boundaries of time, propelling Gwendolyn and Gideon into a labyrinth of intrigue and danger.

Gier's signature wit and charm are on full display as she navigates her protagonists through the treacherous waters of love and loss. Gwendolyn, our intrepid heroine, must grapple with the shattering revelations of the past and the daunting challenges of the present. Yet, she remains an endearingly relatable character, her strength and resilience tempered by moments of vulnerability and self-doubt. Gideon, too, evolves, his stoic exterior cracking to reveal the depth of his emotions, making their love story all the more poignant.

The author's prowess lies not only in her character development but also in her masterful handling of time travel. Gier deftly weaves together past, present, and future, creating a complex narrative that keeps readers on the edge of their seats. From the opulent ballrooms of the 18th century to the gritty streets of modern-day London, each era is vividly brought to life, immersing readers in a rich historical tapestry.

Yet, "Smaragdgrün" is more than just a thrilling adventure. It's a profound exploration of love, loss, and the power of choice. Gier delves into the complexities of love, its ability to heal and hurt, to bind and liberate. She challenges readers to question the nature of destiny, to consider whether our lives are governed by fate or free will.

The addition of Gideon's perspective and the bonus material on the Logen der Wächter provide fascinating insights, enriching the reading experience and offering a deeper understanding of the world Gier has so meticulously crafted.

In "Smaragdgrün," Kerstin Gier once again proves why she is a master of her craft. This is not just a book to be read; it's an experience to be savored. A rollercoaster of emotions, a thrilling quest through time, and a love story that transcends the ages, "Smaragdgrün" is a must-read for fans of the series and newcomers alike.

# Powerful (The Powerless Trilogy, #1.5)

**Author:** Lauren Roberts  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**Title: Powerful - A Spark Ignites in Ilya**

In the realm of Ilya, where power is privilege and ordinariness is a crime, Lauren Roberts' "Powerful" ignites a captivating tale of forbidden love, survival, and the courage to defy a kingdom's grueling laws. This first book in the Powerless trilogy is an explosive blend of fantasy, romance, and suspense, sure to enthrall fans of Sarah J. Maas and Victoria Aveyard.

Roberts masterfully paints a world divided, where the Elites, empowered by the Plague, rule over the Ordinaries, those born without extraordinary abilities. Paedyn Gray, our protagonist, is a chameleon, posing as a Psychic to survive in the slums. Her keen observations and quick wit make her a heroine to root for, even as she navigates the treacherous waters of Ilya's society.

The story truly takes off when Paedyn saves one of Ilya's princes, setting her on a collision course with the Purging Trials. This brutal competition, designed to showcase the Elites' powers, becomes a deadly game of deception and self-discovery for Paedyn. As she grapples with her growing feelings for the prince and the looming threat of exposure, Roberts keeps the tension high and the pages turning.

Roberts' writing style is engaging and descriptive, bringing Ilya's opulent palaces and grimy slums to life. She excels at creating intricate relationships, from Paedyn's complex bond with her father to the slow-burn romance with the prince. Yet, it's her exploration of power dynamics and societal norms that truly sets "Powerful" apart. Roberts challenges readers to question what it means to be powerful, and whether power is truly a gift or a curse.

"Powerful" is a gripping tale of resilience and rebellion, of love blossoming in the most unlikely of places. It's a story that will leave you breathless, eager for the next installment in the Powerless trilogy. Roberts has crafted a world that feels both fresh and familiar, a testament to her skill as a storyteller.

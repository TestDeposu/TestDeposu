# Existence (Existence, #1)

**Author:** Abbi Glines  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

**Existence: A Dark Dance of Deception and Desperation**

In the sultry, secretive heart of Mississippi, Abbi Glines weaves a tale of haunting obsession and familial betrayal in her 2025 release, "Existence," the first installment of a gripping new series. Glines, known for her evocative storytelling and complex characters, delivers a chilling narrative that explores the dark underbelly of small-town America and the lengths one will go to protect their existence.

At the core of "Existence" lies a tale of two strangers bound by blood and circumstance. On one hand, we have the enigmatic and vulnerable Phee, a young woman grappling with the sudden loss of her mother and the unsettling presence of a stalker who leaves eerie, blue origami hearts in her wake. Phee's world is shattered further when she discovers the true identity of her father, a powerful governor with a penchant for secrets. Desperate for refuge, Phee turns to the very man who abandoned her, setting in motion a chain of events that will forever alter the course of their lives.

Enter Than, a jaded and reluctant guardian, tasked with the unenviable duty of protecting Phee from the shadows that threaten to consume her. Than's world is one of stark contrasts—haunted by his own demons and bound by loyalty to a man he neither respects nor trusts. As he grapples with the weight of his newfound responsibility, Than finds himself drawn into Phee's orbit, his existence inexplicably entwined with hers.

Glines masterfully crafts a narrative that is as atmospheric as it is suspenseful, drawing readers into a world where the line between predator and prey is blurred, and trust is a commodity in short supply. Her prose is evocative and haunting, painting a vivid portrait of a world where the shadows hold secrets that some would kill to keep buried.

At its heart, "Existence" is a tale of survival, of two souls navigating a treacherous landscape where the rules of engagement are constantly shifting. It is a story of desperation and deception, of the lengths one will go to protect their existence and the existence of those they hold dear. Glines explores the complex dynamics of family, loyalty, and betrayal, weaving a tapestry of intrigue that will leave readers breathless and eager for more.

In a world where the truth is often obscured by shadows, "Existence" is a stark reminder that sometimes, the most dangerous secrets are the ones we keep from ourselves. Glines' signature style—evocative, atmospheric, and suspenseful—is on full display in this gripping tale of obsession and survival, making "Existence" a must-read for fans of dark, complex narratives that linger long after the final page has been turned.

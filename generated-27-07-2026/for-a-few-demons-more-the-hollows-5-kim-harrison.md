# For a Few Demons More (The Hollows, #5)

**Author:** Kim Harrison  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

In "For a Few Demons More," the fifth installment of The Hollows series, Kim Harrison weaves a gripping narrative that plunges readers into the dark and treacherous world of supernatural Cincinnati. This time around, Rachel Morgan, the fearless and reckless witch, finds herself at the forefront of a maelstrom that threatens to engulf the entire city.

At its core, "For a Few Demons More" is a story about the delicate balance of power and the devastating consequences of its disruption. Harrison masterfully explores the tensions between the various supernatural factions, each vying for dominance in the Hollows. The author's world-building is, as always, meticulous and immersive, drawing readers into a richly detailed universe where vampires, werewolves, and witches coexist in an uneasy alliance.

Rachel Morgan, the series' protagonist, is a complex and multifaceted character whose growth and development are expertly handled by Harrison. Her relationships with her vampire lovers, Ivy and Kisten, are fraught with tension and emotion, adding a layer of depth to the narrative that is both captivating and relatable. As Rachel navigates the treacherous landscape of the Hollows, she must confront her own demons and make impossible choices that will have far-reaching consequences.

Harrison's writing style is, as always, engaging and accessible, making it easy for new readers to jump into the series. Her use of language is evocative and vivid, conjuring a sense of atmosphere that is both eerie and captivating. The pacing is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows smoothly from one scene to the next. The action is fast-paced and suspenseful, with a climax that is both shocking and satisfying.

One of the standout aspects of "For a Few Demons More" is its exploration of the moral gray areas that exist in the world of the Hollows. Harrison raises important questions about the nature of good and evil, encouraging readers to think critically about the motivations and actions of the characters. This moral complexity adds a layer of depth to the narrative that is both thought-provoking and engaging.

In conclusion, "For a Few Demons More" is a gripping and immersive read that is sure to captivate fans of urban fantasy. Harrison's masterful world-building, complex characters, and engaging narrative make this a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling story. With its blend of action, suspense, and romance, this book is sure to appeal to a wide range of readers.

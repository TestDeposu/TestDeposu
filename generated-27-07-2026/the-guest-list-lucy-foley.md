# The Guest List

**Author:** Lucy Foley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

The Guest List by Lucy Foley is a masterfully woven psychological thriller that pays homage to the great Agatha Christie while still managing to feel fresh and unique. The story takes place on a remote island off the coast of Ireland, where a group of friends and family have gathered to celebrate the wedding of a charismatic television star and his ambitious magazine publisher bride.

From the outset, Foley expertly crafts a sense of unease and tension, hinting that beneath the surface of this glamorous and meticulously planned wedding, secrets and lies are simmering. As the festivities get underway, the author skillfully weaves together the perspectives of multiple characters, each with their own distinct voice and motivations. The result is a complex and suspenseful narrative that keeps the reader guessing until the very end.

One of the standout aspects of The Guest List is its cast of characters. Foley has a keen eye for human nature, and each of her characters is richly drawn and multidimensional. From the groom's charming but slightly arrogant demeanor to the bride's steely ambition, every character feels fully realized and relatable. The author also has a keen sense of the dynamics that exist within groups of friends and family, and the ways in which these relationships can be both supportive and toxic.

The setting of the remote island also adds to the sense of unease and isolation that pervades the novel. Foley's descriptions of the rugged coastline and the eerie, atmospheric landscape are vivid and evocative, and she uses the setting to great effect in building tension and suspense.

The author's writing style is also noteworthy. Foley's prose is elegant and concise, and she has a keen sense of pacing and plot. The novel moves at a brisk clip, with each chapter ending on a cliffhanger that propels the reader forward. The author also has a keen sense of how to drop subtle hints and clues throughout the narrative, keeping the reader guessing until the very end.

If you're a fan of psychological thrillers or are simply looking for a compelling and suspenseful read, The Guest List is an absolute must-read. With its intricate plot, complex characters, and atmospheric setting, it's a novel that will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very end.

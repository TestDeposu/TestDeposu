# Flat-Out Love (Flat-Out Love, #1)

**Author:** Jessica Park  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

Flat-Out Love, Jessica Park's poignant novel, delves into the complexities of human emotions and the unbreakable bonds of family. The story revolves around Julie, a college student who moves to Boston to start her new life, only to find herself entangled in the intricate web of the Watkins family's secrets and sorrows.

As Julie navigates her new surroundings, she becomes increasingly drawn to the enigmatic Finn, who is currently on a world tour, but whose presence is deeply felt through his emails and a life-sized cardboard cutout that his 13-year-old sister Celeste treats as a real person. The more Julie learns about the family, the more she realizes that something is amiss. Finn's prolonged absence, Celeste's dependence on the cardboard figure, and Matt's reclusive nature all hint at a deeper pain that the family is struggling to confront.

Park's writing style is characterized by its warmth, sensitivity, and wit. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with quirks and flaws that make them all the more endearing. Through Julie's narrative, Park masterfully weaves together themes of love, loss, and healing, creating a story that is both heart-wrenching and uplifting.

One of the novel's greatest strengths is its ability to tackle difficult topics with nuance and compassion. Park handles the characters' emotional struggles with care and sensitivity, never shying away from the complexity of their experiences. The result is a narrative that feels authentic and raw, yet ultimately hopeful and redemptive.

The supporting characters are equally well-crafted, with distinct personalities that add depth and richness to the story. Celeste's precocious nature and Matt's brooding intensity are particularly noteworthy, and their relationships with Julie and Finn are skillfully developed throughout the novel.

The bonus story, Flat-Out Matt, provides a welcome addition to the narrative, offering a fresh perspective on the characters and their experiences. Park's writing is effortless, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world she has created.

Overall, Flat-Out Love is a captivating and emotional read that will resonate with fans of character-driven fiction. Park's writing is engaging, and her characters are memorable and relatable. If you're looking for a story that will make you laugh, cry, and feel invested in the characters' lives, then Flat-Out Love is an excellent choice.

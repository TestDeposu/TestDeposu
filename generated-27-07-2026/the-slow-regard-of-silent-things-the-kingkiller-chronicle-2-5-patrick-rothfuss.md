# The Slow Regard of Silent Things (The Kingkiller Chronicle, #2.5)

**Author:** Patrick Rothfuss  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

In the depths of Patrick Rothfuss's sprawling epic fantasy universe, The Kingkiller Chronicle, lies a hidden gem that whispers secrets to those willing to listen. The Slow Regard of Silent Things, a novella that defies traditional narrative conventions, is a poignant and enchanting exploration of the enigmatic Auri, a character shrouded in mystery.

Auri's world is one of shadows and silence, a labyrinthine Underthing that exists beneath the University, where ancient passageways and abandoned rooms hold secrets and whispers of the past. It is here that Auri resides, a young woman with an aura of mystique, living a life that is both haunting and joyous. Rothfuss masterfully weaves a tale that is at once a small adventure and a profound exploration of Auri's inner world.

One of the most striking aspects of this novella is its unique narrative voice, which echoes Auri's own distinct perspective. Rothfuss's prose is lyrical and evocative, painting vivid pictures of Auri's world and her place within it. The writing is economical, yet powerful, conveying the depth of Auri's emotions and the richness of her inner life.

Through Auri's story, Rothfuss explores the theme of brokenness and the resilience of the human spirit. Auri is a broken girl, living in a broken world, yet she finds solace and beauty in the silent things that surround her. Her world is one of wonder and magic, where the discarded and forgotten become treasured possessions.

Rothfuss's portrayal of Auri is both tender and unsentimental, revealing the complexities of her character and the depths of her emotional landscape. Auri is a character who defies easy categorization, a true original, and Rothfuss's masterful characterization brings her to vivid life.

The Slow Regard of Silent Things is a novella that will appeal to fans of The Kingkiller Chronicle, as well as readers who appreciate literary fantasy and character-driven fiction. It is a story that will resonate with anyone who has ever felt like an outsider, or who has found solace in the beauty of the everyday.

In short, The Slow Regard of Silent Things is a masterpiece of contemporary fantasy, a poignant and enchanting tale that will linger in the reader's mind long after the final page has been turned.

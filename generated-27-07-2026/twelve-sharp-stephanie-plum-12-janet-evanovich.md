# Twelve Sharp (Stephanie Plum, #12)

**Author:** Janet Evanovich  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

In "Twelve Sharp", Janet Evanovich's 12th installment of the Stephanie Plum series, the stakes are higher than ever, and the action is more intense. This time around, Stephanie finds herself being stalked by a mysterious woman with a penchant for black clothing, a 9mm Glock, and a bad attitude. As the story unfolds, Stephanie's life becomes increasingly complicated, and she must navigate a complex web of danger, mystery, and romance.

One of the defining features of Evanovich's writing style is her ability to blend humor and action seamlessly. In "Twelve Sharp", she does not disappoint. Stephanie's witty one-liners and humorous observations provide a welcome respite from the tension and suspense that permeate the novel. At the same time, Evanovich's portrayal of the darker aspects of human nature is both realistic and thought-provoking.

The main theme of the book appears to be the blurred lines between good and evil. The mysterious woman stalking Stephanie is a complex character with a troubled past, and her motivations are multifaceted. As the story progresses, it becomes clear that nothing is as black and white as it initially seems. This moral ambiguity adds depth and complexity to the novel, making it more than just a lighthearted romp.

Evanovich's characters are, as always, well-developed and relatable. Stephanie's on-again, off-again relationship with Joe Morelli is put to the test, and her partnership with Ranger is as intense and complicated as ever. The supporting cast is equally well-fleshed out, with each character bringing their own unique perspective to the story.

The pacing of the novel is relentless, with Evanovich expertly ratcheting up the tension as the story reaches its climax. The action scenes are vivid and suspenseful, and the plot twists are both surprising and believable.

Overall, "Twelve Sharp" is a must-read for fans of the Stephanie Plum series. Evanovich's trademark humor, action, and suspense are all on full display, making for a compelling and engaging read. While the novel can be enjoyed as a standalone, it's clear that Evanovich is building towards something bigger, and fans of the series will be eagerly anticipating the next installment.

# A Team of One (Unsanctioned Asset, #1)

**Author:** Brad Lee  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

A Team of One, the first installment in the Unsanctioned Asset series by Brad Lee, is a gripping spy thriller that sets the tone for a high-stakes, action-packed ride. The story revolves around Javin Pierce, a CIS spymaster who finds himself at the receiving end of a betrayal by Mossad agents. Refusing to back down, Javin embarks on a perilous mission to exact revenge, navigating a complex web of espionage and counter-intelligence.

One of the standout aspects of A Team of One is its breakneck pacing. Brad Lee masterfully crafts a narrative that hurtles forward with relentless energy, barely giving the reader a chance to catch their breath. The action scenes are intense and well-choreographed, plunging the reader into the midst of high-octane combat and hand-to-hand combat.

Javin Pierce is a compelling protagonist, driven by a fierce determination to see justice served. His character is multidimensional, with a rich backstory that adds depth to the narrative. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with a Russian SVR agent forming an uneasy alliance with Javin. This partnership adds a layer of tension to the story, as the two operatives navigate their differences to achieve a common goal.

The author's style is reminiscent of the likes of Brad Thor, Vince Flynn, and James Rollins, as noted by reviewers. The writing is crisp and concise, with a focus on propelling the plot forward. The world-building is impressive, with a keen attention to detail that immerses the reader in the world of espionage.

The themes of betrayal, loyalty, and redemption are expertly woven throughout the narrative. Javin's quest for revenge serves as a catalyst for exploring these complex emotions, raising questions about the true cost of vengeance and the morality of operating in the gray areas of espionage.

A Team of One has garnered widespread acclaim from reviewers, with many praising the book's suspenseful and action-packed nature. The comparisons to esteemed authors in the genre are well-deserved, and Brad Lee's writing style is sure to appeal to fans of espionage thrillers.

In conclusion, A Team of One is a gripping and intense spy thriller that sets the stage for a compelling series. With its breakneck pacing, well-developed characters, and intricate plotting, this book is sure to captivate fans of the genre. If you're looking for a fast-paced and suspenseful read, A Team of One is an excellent choice.

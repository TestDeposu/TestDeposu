# Saphirblau (Edelstein-Trilogie, #2)

**Author:** Kerstin Gier  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

Time travel, teenage angst, and eighteenth-century etiquette rarely mix this well, but in Saphirblau, the second installment of Kerstin Gier’s beloved Gemstone Trilogy, the German master of YA fiction proves once again why she remains an absolute force in contemporary fantasy. Following the whirlwind events of Rubinrot, this newly revised edition brings Gwendolyn Shepherd’s thrilling chronological escapades back to the forefront with a fresh visual polish and enticing exclusive bonus material, reminding both longtime fans and new readers why this series has achieved timeless status.

At its core, the main theme of Saphirblau is navigation—both literal and emotional. Gwendolyn is thrust deeper into the dizzying web of the secretive Chronograph conspiracy, forced to master the rigid social expectations of the 18th century while simultaneously unraveling a much darker network of intrigue and betrayal. Gier brilliantly contrasts the mundane, often hilarious humiliations of learning historical dance steps with the high-stakes danger of the mysterious Count of Saint Germain. Can the secretive founder of the secretive lodge truly be trusted, or is Gwendolyn walking blindly into a trap? The narrative thrives on this growing paranoia, elevating the stakes far beyond the typical halls of a boarding school.

Yet, what truly anchors the novel is its deeply relatable emotional core. Amidst secret societies and historical jumps, Gwendolyn is still a teenager grappling with heartbreak. Her complicated relationship with the brooding, enigmatic fellow time-traveler Gideon de Villiers provides the beating heart of the story. One minute he is stealing her breath with a kiss, and the next, he is freezing her out. Gier captures the sharp, agonizing sting of adolescent mixed signals with pitch-perfect accuracy. Instead of letting her protagonist drown in self-pity, however, Gier gifts Gwen with a resilient, sharp-witted spine. When heartbreak threatens to derail her, she pulls herself together to face the looming threats head-on, proving that true courage is fighting for the truth even when your own chest hurts.

Kerstin Gier’s writing style is, without a doubt, the secret ingredient that transforms a standard sci-fi romance into an unputdownable page-turner. Her prose sparkles with a signature wit, self-deprecating humor, and a lightness of touch that keeps the heavier time-travel mechanics from ever feeling bogged down or overly convoluted. Gier has an uncanny ability to balance heart-pounding suspense with laugh-out-loud comedic timing. The dialogue is snappy, the internal monologues are endearingly honest, and the cultural clashes between the modern era and the past offer endless entertainment. 

This newly revised edition by Arena Verlag breathes fresh life into an already stellar narrative, offering an immersive gateway for readers ready to lose themselves in a world where romance truly spans across all eras. Saphirblau is a masterclass in romantic fantasy: clever, fiercely entertaining, and relentlessly charming. If you thought you knew the rules of time travel, Gwendolyn Shepherd is here to rewrite them entirely.

# Counting by 7s

**Author:** Holly Goldberg Sloan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

"Counting by 7s" by Holly Goldberg Sloan is a heartwarming tale of resilience and belonging that stands out in the crowded landscape of middle-grade fiction. At its core, the novel explores the universal theme of family, not as a biological construct, but as a chosen community of love and support.

The story revolves around Willow Chance, a precocious 12-year-old with an unusual affinity for nature and an even more unusual way of coping with the world—she counts by 7s. Willow's life is upended when she loses her adoptive parents in a car crash, leaving her alone to navigate the complexities of the adult world. Yet, despite her grief, Willow's spirit remains unbroken, and her journey to find a new family is a testament to her indomitable will.

Sloan's writing is a standout feature of "Counting by 7s." She masterfully crafts a narrative that is both deeply moving and laugh-out-loud funny, a delicate balance that few authors can pull off. Willow's voice is authentic and endearing, her quirks and obsessions making her a character that readers will instantly root for. Sloan's ability to create a diverse cast of fully realized characters, each with their own unique struggles and triumphs, is another strength of the novel. From the no-nonsense social worker, Jya Ni, to the troubled teen, Mai, each character brings something unique to the story, and their interactions with Willow drive the plot forward in unexpected and satisfying ways.

The novel's exploration of family is its most compelling aspect. Sloan challenges the traditional notion of family, instead presenting a more fluid, chosen definition. Through Willow's journey, we see that family is not necessarily about blood ties, but about the people who love and support us, who challenge us and make us better. This message is particularly resonant in today's world, where traditional family structures are evolving and changing.

"Counting by 7s" is a triumph of storytelling, a novel that will leave readers with a renewed appreciation for the power of community and the strength of the human spirit. It is a book that will make you laugh, make you cry, and ultimately, make you believe in the power of love and belonging. Highly recommended for readers of all ages.

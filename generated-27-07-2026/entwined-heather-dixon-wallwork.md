# Entwined

**Author:** Heather Dixon Wallwork  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In "Entwined," Heather Dixon Wallwork weaves a captivating tale that delves into the complexities of grief, sisterly love, and the blurred lines between reality and fantasy. Published in 2012, this enchanting novel whisks readers away to a mystical realm, where the boundaries between the living world and the spirit realm are tested.

The story revolves around Princess Azalea and her eleven sisters, who are confined to their castle while mourning their mother's untimely death. Their lives are a monotonous blur of sorrow and routine, until they stumble upon a mysterious passageway that leads them to The Keeper, a mystical being trapped within the castle walls. As the sisters join The Keeper in a nightly dance, they find solace in the magic and freedom that this secret world offers.

However, as the nights wear on, the dance becomes increasingly nightmarish, and the sisters begin to realize that their newfound escape comes with a steep price. The lines between reality and fantasy start to blur, and the sisters must confront the darkness that lurks within their own hearts.

Wallwork's writing style is a true delight, with a lyrical quality that brings the mystical world to life. Her use of language is evocative and immersive, making it easy to become fully invested in the lives of the twelve sisters. The characters are multidimensional and relatable, each with their own unique personality and struggles.

One of the most striking aspects of "Entwined" is its exploration of grief and the complexities of sisterly love. Wallwork masterfully portrays the intricate web of relationships between the sisters, showcasing the ways in which they support and hurt each other. The novel also delves into the theme of escapism, highlighting the ways in which the sisters use the magical world as a means of avoiding their painful reality.

Throughout the book, Wallwork's world-building is meticulous and detailed, with a richly imagined magical system that adds depth and complexity to the narrative. The pacing is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows effortlessly between moments of tender intimacy and heart-pumping action.

In conclusion, "Entwined" is a captivating and enchanting tale that will appeal to fans of fantasy, romance, and historical fiction. With its lush prose, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes, this novel is a must-read for anyone looking to escape into a richly imagined world. Wallwork's writing is a true delight, and her unique voice is sure to leave readers spellbound.

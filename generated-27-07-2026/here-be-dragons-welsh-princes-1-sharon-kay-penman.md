# Here Be Dragons (Welsh Princes, #1)

**Author:** Sharon Kay Penman  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

In the realm of historical fiction, few authors have managed to capture the essence of medieval politics and passion as masterfully as Sharon Kay Penman. Her novel, "Here Be Dragons", is a sweeping epic that whisks readers away to the tumultuous world of 13th-century Wales, where the lines between loyalty and deception are constantly blurred.

At the heart of the story lies the unlikely marriage between Llewelyn, the charismatic Prince of North Wales, and Joanna, the illegitimate daughter of the ruthless King John of England. This union, born out of a fragile truce between the two warring nations, sets the stage for a complex dance of power, politics, and passion. As Joanna navigates the treacherous waters of her new life in Wales, she finds herself torn between her duty to her father and her growing love for her husband.

Penman's masterful storytelling weaves a rich tapestry of historical detail, transporting readers to a bygone era of grandeur and strife. Her meticulous research and attention to period detail are evident on every page, from the opulent courts of England and France to the rugged landscapes of Wales. The characters, too, are meticulously crafted, with all the complexity and nuance of real historical figures.

One of the most striking aspects of "Here Be Dragons" is its exploration of the female experience in a patriarchal society. Joanna, as a woman caught between two worlds, is a fascinating and relatable protagonist. Her struggles to assert her own identity and agency in a world dominated by men are deeply compelling, and her ultimate decision to forge her own path is both empowering and heartbreaking.

Penman's writing style is, as always, engaging and accessible. Her prose is effortless to follow, even as she tackles complex historical themes and events. The pacing is well-balanced, moving seamlessly from moments of high drama to quieter, more introspective scenes. The characters' dialogue is natural and convincing, adding to the overall sense of immersion in the world of the novel.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the supporting cast of characters can sometimes feel a little one-dimensional. While Llewelyn and Joanna are richly drawn and nuanced, some of the secondary characters feel more like caricatures than fully fleshed-out people. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise exceptional novel.

In conclusion, "Here Be Dragons" is a magnificent achievement in historical fiction, a novel that will captivate fans of the genre and leave them eagerly anticipating the next installment in the trilogy. With its richly detailed world-building, complex characters, and engaging storyline, this book is a must-read for anyone interested in medieval history, politics, or romance.

# Angelfire (Angelfire, #1)

**Author:** Courtney Allison Moulton  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In the realm of young adult fantasy, few novels have managed to capture the essence of darkness and redemption as Courtney Allison Moulton's Angelfire. This debut novel, published in 2011, sets the stage for a captivating trilogy that delves into the world of angels, reapers, and the eternal struggle between good and evil.

At the heart of Angelfire lies the enigmatic Ellie, a protagonist whose life is forever changed on her seventeenth birthday. As she navigates the treacherous landscape of her nightmares and memories, Ellie discovers her true nature: a powerful warrior tasked with hunting and killing the reapers that prey on human souls. With the help of the mysterious and captivating Will, Ellie embarks on a perilous journey to uncover the secrets of her past lives and confront the darkness that has haunted her for centuries.

Moulton's writing style is a masterclass in atmospheric tension, weaving a narrative that is both dark and alluring. Her prose is rich and evocative, conjuring vivid images of the supernatural world that exists alongside our own. The author's use of descriptive language is deliberate and measured, crafting a sense of foreboding that permeates every aspect of the story.

One of the most striking aspects of Angelfire is its exploration of the human condition. Ellie's struggles to reconcile her past and present selves serve as a powerful metaphor for the universal quest for identity and self-discovery. As she grapples with the weight of her memories and the burden of her destiny, Ellie's character evolves from a vulnerable teenager to a strong and determined warrior.

The romance that blossoms between Ellie and Will is a slow-burning flame that adds depth and complexity to the narrative. Their relationship is built on a foundation of trust and mutual respect, and Moulton handles the development of their bond with sensitivity and nuance. The chemistry between the two leads is undeniable, and their interactions are fraught with a palpable sense of tension and longing.

If there is a criticism to be made, it is that the supporting cast of characters feels somewhat one-dimensional at times. The reapers, in particular, are portrayed as largely faceless villains, lacking the depth and motivation that makes Ellie and Will so compelling. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise engaging and immersive narrative.

In conclusion, Angelfire is a gripping and atmospheric novel that marks a stunning debut from Courtney Allison Moulton. With its richly detailed world-building, complex characters, and pulse-pounding action sequences, this book is sure to captivate fans of young adult fantasy and supernatural fiction. As the first installment in the Angelfire trilogy, it sets the stage for a thrilling and unforgettable ride.

# Seven Up (Stephanie Plum, #7)

**Author:** Janet Evanovich  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In Janet Evanovich's seventh installment of the Stephanie Plum series, "Seven Up," the author once again proves her mastery of crafting a wildly entertaining and unpredictable ride. This time around, Stephanie is tasked with tracking down semiretired mobster Eddie DeCooch, who has failed to appear in court and is leaving a trail of destruction in his wake.

As Stephanie navigates the complex web of DeCooch's associates and adversaries, she finds herself entangled in a world of cigarette smuggling, mud wrestling, and fast cars. But what sets "Seven Up" apart from other crime novels is Evanovich's unique blend of humor, wit, and heart. Stephanie's eccentric family, including her sharp-tongued Grandma Mazur and her "perfect" sister Valerie, adds an extra layer of humor and warmth to the story.

Evanovich's writing style is as engaging as ever, with a narrative that flows effortlessly from one chaotic scene to the next. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Stephanie's insecurities and strengths making her a lovable and endearing protagonist. The supporting cast, including the enigmatic Ranger and the lovable but slightly unhinged Mooner, adds to the book's humor and charm.

One of the main themes of "Seven Up" is the idea of family and the complexities that come with it. Stephanie's family is a constant source of frustration and amusement, but ultimately, they are a source of love and support. Evanovich expertly explores the dynamics of family relationships, highlighting the ways in which they can both drive us crazy and keep us grounded.

Another theme that runs throughout the book is the idea of identity and self-discovery. Stephanie is a bounty hunter, but she's also a woman with her own set of desires and insecurities. As she navigates her relationships with Ranger and Morelli, she must confront her own feelings and desires. Evanovich handles this theme with sensitivity and humor, never shying away from the complexities of human relationships.

If you're a fan of the Stephanie Plum series, you'll love "Seven Up." It's a wild ride from start to finish, with plenty of twists and turns to keep you guessing. And if you're new to the series, this book is a great starting point. Evanovich's writing is engaging and accessible, making it easy to jump into the world of Stephanie Plum.

Overall, "Seven Up" is a hilarious and action-packed romp that will keep you entertained from start to finish. With its unique blend of humor, wit, and heart, it's a must-read for fans of crime fiction and comedy alike.

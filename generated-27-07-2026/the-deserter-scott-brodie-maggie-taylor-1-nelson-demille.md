# The Deserter (Scott Brodie & Maggie Taylor #1)

**Author:** Nelson DeMille  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

**A Masterclass in Suspense: Nelson DeMille's "The Deserter"**

Nelson DeMille, the grandmaster of suspense, returns with a gripping new series, "Scott Brodie & Maggie Taylor," kicking off with "The Deserter." This isn't just another thriller; it's a masterclass in tension, intrigue, and character development, co-authored with his son, Alex DeMille, who brings a fresh cinematic edge to the narrative.

At the heart of "The Deserter" lies the enigma of Captain Kyle Mercer, a Delta Force operative who seemingly deserts his post in Afghanistan. The story unfolds through the eyes of Scott Brodie and Maggie Taylor, investigators from the Army's Criminal Investigation Division, tasked with finding Mercer and bringing him back to America. Brodie, a seasoned investigator with a troubled past, and Taylor, a smart, ambitious newcomer, make for a compelling duo, their chemistry sparking like a live wire throughout the novel.

DeMille senior's signature style is evident in the crisp, no-nonsense prose and the intricate plotting that keeps readers guessing until the very end. He masterfully weaves together multiple storylines, each one a thread in the complex tapestry of the plot, drawing us deeper into the labyrinthine world of military intelligence and global politics. The younger DeMille's influence is seen in the cinematic pacing and the sharp, snappy dialogue that propels the story forward.

What sets "The Deserter" apart is its exploration of loyalty, betrayal, and the moral ambiguity that often defines the world of special ops. DeMille doesn't shy away from the tough questions: What happens when a soldier's loyalty is tested? When the lines between right and wrong blur? When the very people tasked with upholding justice are themselves mired in deceit?

The exotic locale of Caracas, Venezuela, serves as a perfect backdrop, its dangerous streets and political instability mirroring the moral quagmire at the heart of the story. DeMille paints a vivid, gritty picture of the city, immersing readers in a world that's as treacherous as it is alluring.

"The Deserter" is more than just a thriller; it's a thought-provoking exploration of the human condition, wrapped in a high-stakes, pulse-pounding plot. It's a testament to the enduring power of storytelling, a reminder that even in the darkest corners of the world, there's always room for a twist, a turn, a surprise.

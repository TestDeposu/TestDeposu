# Spark of the Everflame (Kindred's Curse, #1)

**Author:** Penn Cole  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

In the realm of epic fantasy, Penn Cole's Spark of the Everflame is a masterful debut that sets the stage for the Kindred's Curse Saga. This first installment is a mesmerizing blend of magic, politics, and romance, woven together with the author's unique voice and style. As we delve into the world of mortal colonies ruled by the cruel descendants of gods, known as the Descended, we find ourselves entwined in a complex web of power struggles, ancient secrets, and forbidden love.

At the heart of the story is Diem Bellator, a strong-willed and determined protagonist who embodies the spirit of a true heroine. Her journey begins in a humble village, where she yearns for a life beyond the confines of her isolated existence. The sudden disappearance of her mother and the discovery of a treacherous secret about her past serve as the catalyst for Diem's transformation. As she navigates the dark world of the Descended, she must confront the unspoken rules of love, power, and politics, all while unraveling the mysteries left behind by her mother.

Cole's writing style is a perfect blend of descriptive narrative and witty dialogue, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world of Kindred's Curse. The author's use of language is evocative and engaging, painting vivid pictures of the characters and their surroundings. The pacing is well-balanced, with a slow-burning tension that builds towards a climactic finale. The romance is expertly woven throughout the narrative, with a deliciously antagonistic relationship between Diem and the enigmatic heir to the dying king's throne.

One of the standout features of Spark of the Everflame is its unique magic system, which is both fascinating and complex. The world-building is meticulous, with a rich history that underpins the entire narrative. The author's vision is clear, and the attention to detail is impressive. From the majestic dragons to the intricate politics of the Descended, every element is carefully crafted to create a believable and immersive world.

The themes of Spark of the Everflame are just as compelling as the story itself. Cole explores the struggle for power, the danger of blind obedience, and the importance of found family. Diem's journey is not just about self-discovery but also about finding her place in a world where she is both an outsider and a key player. The author's commentary on social hierarchy and the exploitation of the lower classes is timely and thought-provoking.

In conclusion, Spark of the Everflame is a captivating debut that sets the stage for a thrilling series. Penn Cole's writing is engaging, and her world-building is exceptional. With its unique magic system, complex characters, and slow-burning romance, this book is a must-read for fans of epic fantasy. If you're looking for a story that will keep you on the edge of your seat, with a strong heroine and a gripping narrative, then Spark of the Everflame is the perfect choice.

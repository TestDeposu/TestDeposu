# Back Seat Baby

**Author:** May Alder  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

"Back Seat Baby" by May Alder is a riveting mystery novel that will keep you on the edge of your seat. Although the book was mistakenly attributed to Virginia Swift, it is the third installment in a series that has garnered critical acclaim for its lively characters, surprising plot twists, and good local color.

The story follows Sally Alder, a college professor and amateur sleuth, who finds herself entangled in a murder investigation when Thomas "Stone" Jackson, a famous singer, asks for her help. Jackson's ex-wife, Nina Cruz, is found shot to death, and the sheriff's office believes it to be a hunting accident. However, Sally is not convinced and sets out to uncover the truth.

As Sally delves deeper into the case, she discovers a complex web of motives and secrets that lead her to question everyone involved. The author masterfully weaves together a narrative that is both engaging and suspenseful, keeping readers guessing until the very end.

One of the standout features of this novel is its cast of colorful characters. Sally Alder, in particular, is a well-developed and relatable protagonist. Her crush on Thomas "Stone" Jackson adds a touch of humor and humanity to the story, making her a more endearing and accessible character.

The author's writing style is also noteworthy. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and quiet moments of reflection. The local color is vividly described, transporting readers to the snow-covered forests and small-town charm of Wyoming.

The main theme of the book appears to be the exploration of the complexities of human relationships and the motivations that drive people to commit crimes. The author skillfully exposes the darker aspects of human nature, revealing a twisted mixture of altruism and greed that fuels the plot.

Overall, "Back Seat Baby" is a gripping mystery novel that will appeal to fans of the genre. With its engaging characters, surprising plot twists, and vivid local color, it is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and suspenseful story.

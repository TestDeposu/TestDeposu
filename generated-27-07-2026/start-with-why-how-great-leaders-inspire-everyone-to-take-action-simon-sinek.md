# Start with Why: How Great Leaders Inspire Everyone to Take Action

**Author:** Simon Sinek  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

**Preview/Critique: Start with Why by Simon Sinek**

In the realm of leadership and business, Simon Sinek's "Start with Why" stands as a beacon, illuminating the path to success with its profound simplicity. This New York Times bestseller is not just a book; it's a clarion call for leaders to rethink their approach, inspiring them to inspire others.

Sinek's core message is encapsulated in the Golden Circle, a simple yet powerful concept that every organization should understand. The circle consists of three concentric rings: 'Why', 'How', and 'What'. Most companies communicate from the outside in, starting with 'What' they do, then 'How' they do it, and rarely explaining 'Why' they do it. Sinek argues that this is backwards. Great leaders and companies communicate from the inside out, starting with 'Why'.

The 'Why' is the core belief of the organization. It's the purpose, cause, or belief that inspires and drives everything else. Sinek uses compelling examples, from Apple to Martin Luther King Jr., to illustrate how understanding and communicating the 'Why' is the key to inspiring loyalty, innovation, and success.

Sinek's style is engaging and accessible. He uses a mix of real-life examples, historical anecdotes, and simple, clear language to explain complex ideas. He doesn't just tell you what to do; he explains why you should do it, making the book not just informative, but persuasive.

However, "Start with Why" is not without its criticisms. Some may find the book repetitive, as Sinek drives home his point with numerous examples. Others might argue that the book oversimplifies complex issues. But these are minor quibbles in the face of the book's powerful message.

In essence, "Start with Why" is a must-read for anyone in leadership, business, or anyone seeking to understand what truly drives success. It's a book that will make you question, reflect, and ultimately, inspire you to lead with purpose.

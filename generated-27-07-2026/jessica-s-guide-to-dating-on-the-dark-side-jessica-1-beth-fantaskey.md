# Jessica's Guide to Dating on the Dark Side (Jessica, #1)

**Author:** Beth Fantaskey  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

When Beth Fantaskey burst onto the young adult literary scene with her 2010 debut, *Jessica's Guide to Dating on the Dark Side*, the paranormal romance genre was already heavily saturated with brooding immortals and star-crossed teenage heroines. Yet, Fantaskey managed to carve out a delightfully distinct niche. By ingeniously marrying the high-stakes, gothic allure of traditional vampire lore with the self-aware, comedic charm of a Meg Cabot princess-in-disguise narrative, she crafted a fresh, breezy, and deeply engaging story that stands apart from its contemporaries. 

At the heart of the novel lies the compelling theme of self-discovery wrapped in the chaotic packaging of the American high school senior year. Jessica Packwood is no mere damsel in distress; she is an exceptionally witty, grounded, and refreshingly relatable protagonist struggling with authentic adolescent insecurities. Her carefully laid plans for a normal teenage existence are upended when the brooding, impeccably tailored, and undeniably captivating Lucius Vladescu drops into her life. Lucius drops a literal bombshell: Jessica is not an ordinary American teenager at all, but rather a Romanian vampire princess, and by ancient blood-right, she is his betrothed. 

What elevates Fantaskey's style above standard genre fare is her clever use of satire and sharp humor. Instead of playing the supernatural elements with relentless, suffocating solemnity, the author introduces *Growing Up Undead: A Teen Vampire's Guide to Dating, Health, and Emotions*—a fictional manual that serves as both a brilliant comedic device and a humorous meta-commentary on the absurdities of teenage dating guides. This playful framing allows Jessica to navigate her terrifying, blood-drinking heritage with a mix of skepticism and grit, transitioning from an average outsider to a confident European royal without ever losing her authentic voice.

The author’s pacing is relentless, expertly balancing the external threats of a brewing global vampire war and a conniving, status-obsessed cheerleader gunning for Lucius, with the delicate, slow-burn evolution of the central romance. The dynamic between Jessica and Lucius is nothing short of irresistible. Their elaborate, old-world courting rituals clash hilariously with modern high school culture, resulting in a romance that is as genuinely swoonworthy as it is amusing. Fantaskey treats her characters with genuine affection, ensuring that the emotional stakes feel just as vital as the supernatural ones.

Ultimately, *Jessica's Guide to Dating on the Dark Side* is a triumph of lighthearted, supernatural fiction. It delivers a fast-paced narrative packed with clever plot twists, high-octane drama, and a genuinely heartwarming message about embracing one's true identity. For readers craving a smart, laugh-out-loud vampire romance that refuses to take itself too seriously while still delivering high emotional impact, Fantaskey’s debut remains an absolute must-read.

# Columbus Day (Expeditionary Force, #1)

**Author:** Craig Alanson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

Columbus Day, the first installment in Craig Alanson's Expeditionary Force series, is a gripping and action-packed science fiction novel that delves into the complexities of war, humanity, and the unknown. Published in 2020, this book is a must-read for fans of the genre, offering a unique blend of humor, satire, and thrilling adventure.

The story begins with a clever analogy, comparing the arrival of alien forces on Earth to the infamous Columbus Day, highlighting the vulnerability of humanity in the face of superior technology. The protagonist, a soldier who finds himself fighting alongside the Kristang, an alien species that has defeated the initial invaders, the Ruhar, is a witty and relatable narrator. His sarcastic remarks and observations on the absurdity of war provide a much-needed comic relief, making the novel an entertaining and engaging read.

However, beneath its humorous surface, Columbus Day explores deeper themes that resonate with our contemporary world. The novel critiques the notion of war and the ease with which nations and civilizations engage in conflict, often without fully understanding the consequences. Alanson's portrayal of humanity's propensity for violence and its tendency to repeat historical mistakes serves as a cautionary tale, urging readers to reflect on the devastating effects of war.

The author's writing style is engaging, and his ability to balance action, humor, and social commentary is impressive. The pacing is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows smoothly, making it easy to become immersed in the world of Expeditionary Force. The characters, although not overly complex, are well-developed and relatable, with distinct personalities that add to the novel's charm.

One of the standout features of Columbus Day is its thought-provoking exploration of the human condition. Alanson raises important questions about the nature of humanity, our place in the universe, and the consequences of our actions. The novel encourages readers to think critically about the world we live in and the decisions we make, both as individuals and as a collective.

In conclusion, Columbus Day is a captivating science fiction novel that offers a unique blend of action, humor, and social commentary. Craig Alanson's engaging writing style and thought-provoking themes make this book a must-read for fans of the genre. With its well-developed characters, balanced pacing, and gripping narrative, Columbus Day is an excellent starting point for the Expeditionary Force series.

# Eternal Kiss of Darkness (Night Huntress World, #2)

**Author:** Jeaniene Frost  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**Eternal Kiss of Darkness: A Mesmerizing Spin-off from Jeaniene Frost**

Jeaniene Frost, the master of urban fantasy and vampire lore, returns with "Eternal Kiss of Darkness," a captivating spinoff from her New York Times bestselling Night Huntress series. This second installment in the Night Huntress World series is a must-read for fans of the original series, as well as those who enjoy the sultry, suspenseful worlds of authors like Kim Harrison, Lynsay Sands, and Christine Feehan.

Frost's signature style is on full display in "Eternal Kiss of Darkness." She weaves a complex, engaging narrative that seamlessly blends romance, action, and supernatural intrigue. The story follows Mencheres, the ancient and powerful Master Vampire, and Kira, a young woman with a mysterious past and a unique connection to the vampire world. Their journey is a dance of danger and desire, as they navigate a web of ancient enemies, deadly secrets, and an undeniable attraction that threatens to consume them both.

Frost excels in creating rich, multifaceted characters that readers can't help but invest in. Mencheres, with his centuries of wisdom and quiet strength, is a compelling hero, while Kira's resilience and determination make her a heroine worth rooting for. Their relationship is a slow burn, filled with tension and tender moments that feel earned, not forced. Frost's ability to balance the intense, often violent world of vampires with the softer, more intimate moments between her characters is one of the many reasons she's a standout in the genre.

The world-building in "Eternal Kiss of Darkness" is another standout feature. Frost expands on the universe she created in the Night Huntress series, delving deeper into the history and politics of the vampire world. She introduces new factions and enemies, all of whom feel authentic and threatening. The result is a story that feels both familiar and fresh, a testament to Frost's skill as a world-builder.

Frost's prose is sharp and engaging, drawing readers in from the first page. She has a gift for creating vivid, immersive settings, from the bustling streets of New Orleans to the ancient, crumbling ruins of Egypt. Her action sequences are fast-paced and thrilling, while her dialogue is witty and engaging. She also has a knack for creating memorable, often humorous secondary characters who add depth and levity to the story.

"Eternal Kiss of Darkness" is not without its flaws. The pacing can feel uneven at times, with some sections moving quickly while others feel slow. There are also moments where the story feels overly complicated, with too many plot threads to keep track of. However, these are minor issues in an otherwise excellent novel.

In conclusion, "Eternal Kiss of Darkness" is a must-read for fans of urban fantasy, vampire lore, and compelling romance. Jeaniene Frost's signature style, rich world-building, and engaging characters make this a standout novel in a crowded genre. This is a book that will keep you up at night, eager to find out what happens next.

# My Big Fat Zombie Goldfish

**Author:** Mo O'Hara  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

"Mo O'Hara's 'My Big Fat Zombie Goldfish' is a delightful, quirky adventure that will leave young readers in stitches. The story begins innocently enough, with Tom's big brother conducting a typical 'evil scientist' experiment on their goldfish, Frankie. But when Tom and his best friend Pradeep intervene with a battery-powered resurrection, things take a hilariously monstrous turn. Frankie returns as a giant, zombie goldfish with hypnotic powers, seeking revenge on the very humans who brought him back.

O'Hara's style is refreshingly irreverent, with a knack for the absurd that will appeal to both kids and adults. The story is peppered with laugh-out-loud moments, from Frankie's newfound ability to walk and talk, to his unexpected fondness for pizza. The author's use of humor is balanced with a heartwarming friendship at its core, as Tom and Pradeep navigate the chaos they've unleashed.

The book is also beautifully illustrated, with vibrant, cartoon-style drawings that bring the story to life and add an extra layer of fun. Each page is a visual treat, making 'My Big Fat Zombie Goldfish' an engaging read-aloud or independent adventure.

In a sea of children's books, 'My Big Fat Zombie Goldfish' stands out as a unique, entertaining tale that celebrates friendship, responsibility, and the joy of the absurd. It's a perfect choice for fans of funny, action-packed stories with a touch of heart."

# Marked by Magic (Tracking Trouble #1)

**Author:** Lindsay Buroker  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

"Marked by Magic" by Lindsay Buroker is an electrifying ride that catapults readers into a world of magic, mayhem, and romance. This first installment of the Tracking Trouble series promises to be an unforgettable adventure, as we delve into the life of Ruin Bringer, a fearless hit woman with a penchant for trouble.

From the outset, Buroker masterfully weaves a complex web of characters, each with their unique personalities, quirks, and motivations. Ruin, the protagonist, is a force to be reckoned with - a skilled warrior with a sharp wit and a tongue that's just as deadly as her ancient dwarven sword. Her relationship with Zav, her steady dragon, is a highlight of the book, showcasing a deep and abiding love that transcends species.

The story is expertly paced, with a narrative that's both fast-moving and engaging. Buroker's writing is descriptive and immersive, making it easy to visualize the world she's created. The action scenes are intense and suspenseful, with Ruin facing off against a plethora of formidable foes, from orcs and ogres to trolls and other magical creatures.

One of the standout features of "Marked by Magic" is its blend of humor and darkness. Buroker has a keen sense of comedic timing, often injecting humor into even the most tense situations. At the same time, she doesn't shy away from exploring the grittier aspects of Ruin's world, where violence and danger lurk around every corner.

The central plot, revolving around Ruin's magical sword and its newfound beacon-like properties, is cleverly constructed. As Ruin navigates this treacherous landscape, she must confront not only her enemies but also her own vulnerabilities. The sword, once a trusted companion, has become a liability, making her a target for every opportunistic creature in the vicinity.

Buroker's world-building is impressive, with a richly detailed landscape that's steeped in mythology and folklore. The magical system is complex and well-defined, with a clear set of rules that govern its use. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with characters like Zav and Ruin's boss adding depth and nuance to the story.

If there's one area where "Marked by Magic" falls short, it's in the somewhat predictable nature of the plot. While the story is engaging and well-paced, some of the twists and turns feel a tad familiar. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise excellent book.

In conclusion, "Marked by Magic" is a gripping and entertaining read that's sure to appeal to fans of urban fantasy and adventure. With its strong protagonist, engaging world-building, and healthy dose of humor, this book is an excellent addition to the genre. If you're looking for a compelling story with a unique magical system and a dash of romance, look no further than "Marked by Magic".

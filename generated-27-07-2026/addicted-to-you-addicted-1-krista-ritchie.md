# Addicted to You (Addicted, #1)

**Author:** Krista Ritchie  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

**Addicted to You: A Raw, Compelling Journey into the Depths of Desire and Dependence**

In the sprawling metropolis of New York, where secrets lurk in the shadows of neon lights and pulsating beats, Krista Ritchie presents a raw, unapologetic exploration of addiction and love in her debut novel, 'Addicted to You'. This first installment of the 'Addicted' series is a gripping tale of two souls bound by their demons, navigating the treacherous waters of desire, shame, and redemption.

Lily Calloway, our protagonist, is a young woman with a secret that threatens to consume her. She's addicted to sex, using one-night stands as a coping mechanism to escape her pain. Meanwhile, her best friend and fake boyfriend, Loren Hale, is drowning in his own addiction - alcohol. Together, they've spun a web of lies, hiding their dependencies from their families and the world, all while clinging to each other for support.

Ritchie's prose is stark and unflinching, delving deep into the psyche of her characters with an almost clinical precision. She doesn't shy away from the gritty reality of addiction, painting vivid portraits of Lily's shame-filled encounters and Loren's drunken binges. Yet, amidst the darkness, there's a spark of hope, a tender exploration of love that transcends physical intimacy.

The heart of 'Addicted to You' lies in its exploration of the complex dynamics of love and addiction. Lily and Loren's relationship is a dance of push and pull, a delicate balance of support and enablement. They're each other's enablers, yet also their greatest hope for recovery. Ritchie masterfully navigates this delicate terrain, never shying away from the harsh truths of their situation.

Ritchie's style is reminiscent of authors like Gillian Flynn and Paula Hawkins, blending psychological depth with a gripping narrative pace. She has a knack for creating characters that are both infuriating and sympathetic, drawing the reader in with their flaws and vulnerabilities.

'Addicted to You' is not a light read. It's a journey into the darkest corners of the human psyche, a raw, unflinching look at the destructive power of addiction and the redemptive power of love. It's a story that will challenge you, make you uncomfortable, and ultimately, leave you craving more.

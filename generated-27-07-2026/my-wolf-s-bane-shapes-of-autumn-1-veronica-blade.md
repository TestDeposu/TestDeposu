# My Wolf's Bane (Shapes of Autumn, #1)

**Author:** Veronica Blade  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

Unleashing the Inner Beast: A Review of Veronica Blade’s My Wolf’s Bane

In the crowded landscape of young adult paranormal romance, finding a novel that successfully balances high-stakes supernatural mythos with the breathless intensity of first love can be a formidable task. Enter Veronica Blade’s 2013 novel, My Wolf’s Bane, the opening installment of the Shapes of Autumn series. Staying true to the publisher’s candid note—that this is, above all else, a romance where everything else takes a backseat—Blade delivers a fast-paced, emotionally charged narrative that leans heavily into the delicious agony of forbidden attraction.

At its core, the novel explores the timeless theme of identity and the terrifying thrill of the unknown. Protagonist Autumn Rossi begins her journey believing she is entirely ordinary, only to find her world upended as unexplained athletic prowess and harrowing, visceral nightmares hint at a dormant, non-human heritage. This awakening serves as a clever metaphor for the turbulent transition of adolescence, where the body changes, emotions run wild, and the world suddenly feels predatory and unfamiliar. 

Enter Zack de Luca, the brooding new high-schooler who functions as both a catalyst for Autumn’s awakening and the anchor of the narrative. Blade employs classic enemies-to-lovers tropes with a supernatural twist, establishing Zack and Autumn across an ancient, bitter divide of mortal enemies. Yet, the chemistry crackles instantly. Zack’s paradoxical behavior—rescuing Autumn from danger while acting decidedly hostile—creates an irresistible tension. Blade’s writing style thrives in these micro-moments of friction. She utilizes sensory details masterfully, anchoring the paranormal elements in raw physicality: the scent of a predator in the air, the heat radiating from skin, the low growl that blurs the line between human and beast. 

As demonstrated in the novel's intimate excerpt, Blade is unafraid to push the PG-13 boundaries, crafting scenes that thrum with palpable adolescent yearning and heightened stakes. The juxtaposition of a terrifying nightmare involving wild beasts with the sudden, grounding presence of a nearly-nude Zack in Autumn’s bedroom encapsulates the author’s stylistic approach: high emotional melodrama colliding with swoon-worthy romantic proximity. The danger is twofold for Autumn—she must navigate a psycho ex-boyfriend and the very real threats of her changing nature, all while risking her heart (and potentially her life) on a boy who is forbidden to her.

While readers looking for an intricately plotted, sprawling world-building epic might find the hyper-focus on the central romance limiting, genre enthusiasts will devour it. Blade understands her audience. She prioritizes emotional pacing, heartbeat-skipping dialogue, and relentless romantic tension over extraneous lore, ensuring the pages turn themselves. 

My Wolf’s Bane is an entertaining, snappy, and deeply romantic start to a series that promises plenty of bite. For anyone craving a supernatural escape driven entirely by passion, peril, and the intoxicating pull of star-crossed love, Veronica Blade’s debut is a midnight snack well worth tasting.

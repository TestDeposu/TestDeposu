# Taking Chances (Taking Chances, #1)

**Author:** Molly McAdams  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**Taking Chances: A Heart-Pounding Tale of Love and Self-Discovery**

Molly McAdams' "Taking Chances," the first installment in her captivating series of the same name, is a rollercoaster ride of emotions, passion, and self-discovery that will leave you breathless. At its core, this novel explores the theme of navigating the complexities of love and desire, especially when they come from unexpected quarters.

Harper, our protagonist, is a young woman on the cusp of adulthood, eager to spread her wings and experience life on her terms. Her overprotective Marine father has sheltered her, but now, she's ready to take chances and forge her own path. Her journey begins at San Diego State University, where she finds herself drawn to two men who couldn't be more different: Brandon, her sweet and steady boyfriend, and Chase, her roommate's brother, a tattooed bad boy with a troubled past.

McAdams masterfully weaves a tale that is equal parts heartwarming and heart-wrenching. She deftly explores the push and pull of Harper's feelings, the struggle between her head and her heart, and the moral dilemmas she faces. The author's style is engaging and relatable, making it easy to become invested in Harper's journey and the men who capture her heart.

McAdams also excels in creating well-rounded, complex characters. Harper is no damsel in distress; she's a strong, intelligent woman who makes her own decisions, even when they're difficult. Brandon and Chase, too, are more than just stereotypes; they're fully realized individuals with their own strengths and weaknesses, making the love triangle at the heart of the story all the more compelling.

The narrative is peppered with moments of intense passion and tender romance, balanced by witty banter and heartfelt conversations. McAdams' prose is evocative, painting vivid pictures of the characters' emotional landscapes and the sunny, laid-back world of San Diego.

"Taking Chances" is more than just a romance novel; it's a story about growing up, making mistakes, and learning to trust oneself and others. It's about the courage to take chances, to follow your heart even when it's scary, and to believe in second chances.

In essence, "Taking Chances" is a captivating tale that will resonate with anyone who has ever dared to follow their heart, even when it leads them down unexpected paths. It's a must-read for fans of new adult romance, and a promising start to McAdams' series.

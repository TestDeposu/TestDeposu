# Revenge of the Witch (The Last Apprentice / Wardstone Chronicles, #1)

**Author:** Joseph Delaney  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**Revenge of the Witch: A Dark, Engaging Tale of Power and Alliances**

In "Revenge of the Witch," Joseph Delaney masterfully weaves a tale that delves into the heart of his popular Last Apprentice series, offering a fresh perspective through the eyes of the formidable witch assassin, Grimalkin. This first book in the Wardstone Chronicles to be narrated by Grimalkin is a gripping, atmospheric journey that explores themes of revenge, loyalty, and the true nature of evil.

Delaney's signature style is evident from the outset, with his characteristic dark, foreboding tone setting the stage for a chilling narrative. The story is a testament to his ability to create intricate, believable worlds filled with menacing creatures and complex characters. The county of Anglezarke, with its eerie moors and ancient woods, is as much a character as any of the beings that inhabit it, adding depth and richness to the tale.

Grimalkin, a character previously shrouded in mystery, takes center stage in this installment. Delaney skillfully peels back the layers of this enigmatic figure, revealing a complex, driven individual with a past as dark and twisted as the creatures she hunts. Her voice is compelling, her motivations clear yet nuanced, making her a protagonist that readers will find themselves invested in, despite her morally grey nature.

The plot is a thrilling game of cat and mouse, as Grimalkin is pursued by a relentless, terrifying enemy created specifically to destroy her. Delaney expertly builds tension, with each chapter bringing new challenges and revelations that keep the reader on the edge of their seat. The pacing is excellent, balancing action-packed sequences with quieter moments that allow for character development and world-building.

One of the standout aspects of "Revenge of the Witch" is Delaney's exploration of the theme of revenge. He delves into the destructive power of this emotion, showing how it can consume even the strongest of beings. Yet, he also explores the flip side, the redemptive power of revenge when it's used to right wrongs and protect the innocent. This complex portrayal adds a layer of depth to the story, making it more than just a thrilling adventure.

Delaney's style is accessible yet sophisticated, making "Revenge of the Witch" a compelling read for both young adult and adult audiences. His use of language is evocative and descriptive, painting vivid pictures with his words. He also employs a clever use of folklore and mythology, incorporating traditional witch lore and creating a rich, immersive world that feels both familiar and unique.

In conclusion, "Revenge of the Witch" is a must-read for fans of the Last Apprentice series, offering a fresh, engaging perspective on the world Delaney has so masterfully created. It's a dark, thrilling tale that explores complex themes with a compelling narrative and a richly drawn protagonist. It's a testament to Delaney's skill as a storyteller and a worthy addition to the Wardstone Chronicles.

# Glass Houses (The Morganville Vampires, #1)

**Author:** Rachel Caine  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In the heart of Texas, a town shrouded in mystery and terror awaits - Morganville, a place where the lines between life and death are constantly blurred. Rachel Caine's "Glass Houses," the first installment of the Morganville Vampires series, is a gripping and unrelenting ride into the dark world of vampires, where the undead reign supreme and humans are nothing but pawns in their game of power and manipulation.

The story revolves around Claire Danvers, a shy and intelligent 16-year-old who is forced to move to Morganville to attend college. What seems like a normal college town at first glance, soon turns out to be a haven for vampires, who have lived among humans for centuries, preying on their fear and bloodlust. As Claire navigates her new surroundings, she is drawn into a world of ancient secrets, hidden alliances, and supernatural creatures that lurk in the shadows.

Caine's writing style is a perfect blend of suspense, horror, and wit, making "Glass Houses" a page-turner that will keep you on the edge of your seat. Her characters are well-developed and complex, with Claire being a strong and relatable protagonist. The supporting cast, including Shane, Eve, and Michael, add depth and humor to the story, making it easy to become invested in their lives and struggles.

One of the most striking aspects of "Glass Houses" is its ability to balance action, suspense, and humor. Caine's world-building is meticulous, and the rules of her vampire society are well-defined, making the supernatural elements feel grounded and realistic. The pacing is relentless, with each chapter ending on a cliffhanger that propels the reader forward, eager to find out what happens next.

The main theme of "Glass Houses" is the struggle for power and control. The vampires, led by the enigmatic and ruthless Bishop, will stop at nothing to maintain their grip on Morganville, while the humans are forced to live in fear, never knowing when they will be the next victim. Claire's arrival in town sets off a chain reaction of events that threatens to upend the delicate balance of power, and she must navigate this treacherous landscape to survive.

Overall, "Glass Houses" is a captivating and addictive read that will appeal to fans of urban fantasy, horror, and supernatural fiction. Rachel Caine's writing is engaging, and her world-building is top-notch. If you're looking for a series that will keep you up all night, reading just one more chapter, then the Morganville Vampires series is a must-read.

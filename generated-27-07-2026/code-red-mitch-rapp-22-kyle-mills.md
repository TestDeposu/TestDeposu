# Code Red (Mitch Rapp #22)

**Author:** Kyle Mills  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

In "Code Red", the 22nd installment of the Mitch Rapp series, Kyle Mills masterfully weaves a gripping narrative that catapults readers into the treacherous world of international espionage and geopolitics. This high-octane thriller is a testament to Mills' skill in crafting a story that is both timely and timeless, ripped straight from the headlines and yet eerily prescient.

At the heart of the novel is Mitch Rapp, the CIA's most feared operative, who finds himself entangled in a complex web of intrigue and deception. When Damian Losa, the world's most powerful crime lord, calls in a favor, Rapp is honor-bound to answer. The mission: to stop the Syrian government from distributing a highly addictive new narcotic throughout Europe. But as Rapp delves deeper into the operation, he uncovers a shocking truth - the drug was created by Russia's asymmetrical warfare unit as a weapon against the West.

Mills' writing is akin to a bullet train, hurtling readers through a landscape of war-torn Syria, treacherous alliances, and shifting allegiances. The pacing is relentless, with Rapp and his team navigating a battlefield where the United States is virtually powerless. The tension is palpable, the stakes are high, and the consequences of failure are catastrophic.

One of the most striking aspects of "Code Red" is its thought-provoking exploration of the blurred lines between loyalty, duty, and morality. Rapp is a complex character, driven by a strong sense of justice and a willingness to operate in the gray areas of international politics. His relationships with his team and his adversaries are multifaceted and nuanced, adding depth and richness to the narrative.

Mills' style is characterized by his ability to balance action, suspense, and intrigue with a keen understanding of the complexities of global politics. His writing is lean and mean, with a focus on propelling the story forward at breakneck speed. The result is a thriller that is both entertaining and thought-provoking, a true page-turner that will keep readers on the edge of their seats until the very end.

In conclusion, "Code Red" is a gripping and timely thriller that cements Kyle Mills' position as a master of the genre. With its breakneck pacing, complex characters, and thought-provoking themes, this novel is a must-read for fans of international espionage and geopolitics.

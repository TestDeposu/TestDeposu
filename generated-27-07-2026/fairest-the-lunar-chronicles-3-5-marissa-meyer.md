# Fairest (The Lunar Chronicles, #3.5)

**Author:** Marissa Meyer  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

**Fairest: A Radiant Reimagining of Evil**

Marissa Meyer's "Fairest" is not just a prequel to her acclaimed Lunar Chronicles, but a masterful reimagining of the classic fairy tale trope, offering a fresh perspective on the villain we love to hate. This standalone novella, published in 2015, is a must-read for fans of the series and a captivating standalone for new readers.

"Fairest" delves into the origins of Queen Levana, the tyrannical Lunar ruler who has haunted the series since its inception. Meyer skillfully weaves a tale that is equal parts heart-wrenching and chilling, revealing the complex woman behind the crown and the curse that has shaped her into the monster we know.

Meyer's signature style is evident throughout, with her unique blend of futuristic technology and classic fairy tale elements creating a rich, immersive world. Her characters are as compelling as ever, with Levana's depth and nuance making her a fascinating protagonist, despite her villainous nature. Meyer's ability to balance suspense, action, and character development is evident, keeping readers on the edge of their seats until the very end.

The novella also serves as a fascinating exploration of power, beauty, and the corrupting influence of both. Meyer challenges traditional notions of beauty and power, questioning who truly holds the reins and at what cost. This theme is particularly resonant in today's society, making "Fairest" a timely and thought-provoking read.

Despite its brevity, "Fairest" packs a powerful punch, leaving readers eager for more. It's a testament to Meyer's skill as a storyteller that she can create such a compelling narrative in just over 200 pages. Fans of the Lunar Chronicles will find much to love in this prequel, while new readers will be drawn in by Meyer's engaging prose and intriguing world-building.

In conclusion, "Fairest" is a radiant addition to the Lunar Chronicles, offering a fresh perspective on the series' central antagonist and a thought-provoking exploration of power, beauty, and corruption. It's a must-read for fans of the series and a captivating standalone for new readers.

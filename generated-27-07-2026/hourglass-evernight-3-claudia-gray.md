# Hourglass (Evernight, #3)

**Author:** Claudia Gray  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**Hourglass: A Dance with Destiny**

Claudia Gray's "Hourglass," the third installment in her captivating Evernight series, is a thrilling testament to the power of love and the lengths one will go to protect it. The story picks up where "Morning Star" left off, with Bianca and Lucas, a vampire and a human, on the run from the very world that should have embraced them.

At its core, "Hourglass" is a tale of forbidden love, a theme that Gray explores with remarkable depth and nuance. Bianca, a vampire, and Lucas, a human, are bound by a love that transcends their differences, a love that is as intense as it is dangerous. Gray's prose is evocative, painting a vivid picture of their relationship, one that is as passionate as it is poignant. She masterfully navigates the complexities of their love, exploring the fears, the doubts, and the unwavering determination that binds them together.

The main theme of "Hourglass" is sacrifice, and Gray doesn't shy away from exploring its darker implications. Bianca and Lucas are forced to make impossible choices, to sacrifice their safety, their freedom, and even their identities in the name of love. Gray doesn't romanticize these sacrifices; instead, she presents them as the harsh realities they are, making the story all the more compelling.

Gray's style is characterized by her ability to weave intricate plots with complex characters. "Hourglass" is no exception. The story is a whirlwind of action, suspense, and betrayal, with twists that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. Yet, amidst the chaos, Gray takes the time to develop her characters, giving them depth and dimension. Bianca, in particular, is a compelling protagonist, her strength and vulnerability making her a character that readers can't help but root for.

The world-building in "Hourglass" is also noteworthy. Gray's vampires are not the sparkling, romanticized creatures of popular culture. They are dangerous, powerful beings, and Gray doesn't shy away from exploring the darker aspects of their nature. The result is a rich, immersive world that feels both familiar and unique.

However, "Hourglass" is not without its flaws. The pacing can feel uneven at times, with some sections feeling rushed while others linger too long. Additionally, some of the secondary characters can feel one-dimensional, serving more as plot devices than fully realized individuals.

Despite these minor flaws, "Hourglass" is a compelling addition to the Evernight series. It is a story of love and sacrifice, of defying the odds and fighting for what you believe in. It is a testament to the power of storytelling, to the way a good book can make us feel, think, and dream.

In conclusion, "Hourglass" is a must-read for fans of the Evernight series, and for anyone who enjoys a good love story with a dash of danger and suspense. It is a book that will make you feel, that will make you think, and that will leave you eager for more.

# Someone Else's Bucket List

**Author:** Amy T. Matthews  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

Someone Else's Bucket List by Amy T. Matthews is a poignant exploration of the transformative power of sisterhood and the human spirit. This bittersweet novel delves into the complexities of grief, self-discovery, and the unbreakable bonds between sisters.

The story revolves around Jodie Boyd, an introverted and anxious young woman who is struggling to find her place in the world. Her life is turned upside down when her older sister, Bree, a vibrant and successful social media influencer, passes away from leukemia, leaving behind a staggering medical debt and a bucket list that she had been secretly recording for Jodie to complete.

As Jodie embarks on this daunting journey, she is forced to confront her own fears and limitations, all while navigating the challenges of her sister's very public bucket list. From flying over Antarctica to performing in a Broadway musical, each task pushes Jodie out of her comfort zone and forces her to re-evaluate her life and priorities.

Through Jodie's journey, Matthews masterfully explores the themes of grief, identity, and the complexities of sisterly love. The author's writing style is engaging, witty, and relatable, making it easy to become fully invested in Jodie's story. The characters are well-developed and nuanced, with a strong sense of authenticity that adds depth to the narrative.

One of the most striking aspects of Someone Else's Bucket List is its ability to balance humor and heartbreak. Matthews tackles the difficult topic of grief with sensitivity and compassion, while also injecting the story with moments of levity and humor. This delicate balance makes the novel feel both authentic and uplifting.

The author's use of social media as a central plot device is also noteworthy. Matthews explores the impact of social media on our lives, highlighting both its benefits and drawbacks. Through Bree's Instagram feed, the novel raises important questions about the curated nature of online personas and the pressures of living up to expectations.

Ultimately, Someone Else's Bucket List is a life-affirming tale that reminds us of the importance of living in the present and embracing our true selves. Jodie's journey is a powerful reminder that happiness is possible if we find the courage to reach for it, and that sometimes, it's the most unlikely experiences that can lead to the greatest growth and transformation.

In short, Someone Else's Bucket List is a moving and uplifting novel that will resonate with fans of Josie Silver and Rebecca Serle. With its engaging characters, witty dialogue, and poignant exploration of sisterhood and grief, this book is a must-read for anyone looking for a story that will touch their heart and inspire their spirit.

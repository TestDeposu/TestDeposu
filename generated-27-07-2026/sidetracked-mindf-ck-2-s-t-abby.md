# Sidetracked (Mindf*ck, #2)

**Author:** S.T. Abby  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

In the latest installment of S.T. Abby's Mindf*ck series, Sidetracked, the lines between good and evil are blurred beyond recognition. Lana Myers, a seemingly ordinary woman, harbors a dark secret - she is a serial killer, driven by a thirst for vengeance against the men who have wronged her. As she navigates a complex web of relationships and deception, Lana finds herself entangled with FBI Agent Logan Bennett, the very man tasked with capturing her.

Abby's writing is a masterclass in manipulation, expertly weaving together a narrative that is both shocking and seductive. The author's use of language is deliberate and unflinching, plunging the reader into a world of darkness and depravity. With each twist and turn, the stakes are raised, and the tension builds to a almost unbearable level.

At its core, Sidetracked is a exploration of the human psyche, a probing examination of the monsters that lurk within us all. Lana is a complex, multifaceted character, driven by a toxic mix of pain, anger, and a desire for retribution. As the story unfolds, it becomes clear that she is not the only monster in the narrative - the men she targets are equally culpable, their crimes and misdemeanors fueling her rage.

The relationship between Lana and Logan is a highlight of the book, a deliciously twisted dance of cat and mouse. Logan, a man haunted by his own demons, is drawn to Lana's darkness, even as he seeks to protect her from the "Boogeyman", a serial killer with a grudge against the FBI. As the story hurtles towards its climax, it becomes clear that Lana is more than capable of taking care of herself - she is a force to be reckoned with, driven by a fierce determination to exact justice.

Throughout the book, Abby's style is unapologetic and unflinching, pushing the boundaries of what is considered acceptable in mainstream fiction. The writing is visceral, the action graphic, and the sex scenes explicit. This is not a book for the faint of heart - it is a challenging, thought-provoking read that will appeal to fans of dark, gritty fiction.

In conclusion, Sidetracked is a gripping, unsettling read that will appeal to fans of the Mindf*ck series. With its complex characters, twisted plot, and unflinching style, it is a must-read for anyone who enjoys dark, gritty fiction. Be warned, however - this book is not for the faint of heart.

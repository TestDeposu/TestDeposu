# The Rook (The Checquy Files, #1)

**Author:** Daniel O'Malley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

In the delightfully dark world of The Rook, Daniel O'Malley masterfully weaves a supernatural adventure that will leave readers on the edge of their seats. The story follows Alexandra Dennis-Palmer-Hudson-Gilmore-Garnsey, affectionately known as Alix, a lady-in-waiting with a unique set of skills that make her an invaluable asset to the Checquy, a secret British government agency tasked with dealing with the paranormal.

Alix's life has never been her own, controlled by her aristocratic ancestry and her ability to shatter bones with a mere touch. As a skilled operative, she is thrust into the limelight when the Prince of Wales is assassinated, and she is assigned to be the lady-in-waiting to the new heir apparent, Princess Louise. With this sudden change in circumstances, Alix must navigate the treacherous world of royal politics while also unraveling the mystery of the prince's murder and keeping Louise safe from preternatural threats.

O'Malley's writing style is a true delight, blending humor and wit with a healthy dose of sarcasm. His protagonist, Alix, is a lovable and relatable character, whose dry observations on the absurdity of her situation had this reviewer chuckling out loud. The author's use of language is clever and engaging, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world of The Rook.

One of the standout features of this novel is its unique blend of supernatural elements and historical fiction. O'Malley's world-building is meticulous and detailed, drawing on a wealth of historical and mythological references to create a richly textured world that is both fascinating and terrifying. The Checquy, as an organization, is a masterclass in world-building, with a complex history and hierarchy that adds depth and complexity to the narrative.

At its heart, The Rook is a story about identity and control. Alix, as a character, has always been defined by her abilities and her ancestry, but as she navigates the complexities of her new role, she begins to question who she is outside of these expectations. This theme is expertly woven throughout the narrative, adding a layer of depth and complexity to what could have been a straightforward supernatural thriller.

In conclusion, The Rook is a delightful and hilarious supernatural adventure that will appeal to fans of fantasy, mystery, and historical fiction. With its unique blend of humor, wit, and sarcasm, this novel is a true standout in the genre. O'Malley's writing style is engaging and immersive, making it easy to become fully invested in the world of The Rook.

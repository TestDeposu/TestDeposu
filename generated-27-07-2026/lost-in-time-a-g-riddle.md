# Lost in Time

**Author:** A.G. Riddle  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**Lost in Time: A Masterclass in Time-Twisting Thriller**

A.G. Riddle's "Lost in Time" is a mesmerizing journey through time, a labyrinth of intrigue that keeps readers on the edge of their seats until the very last page. This standalone novel, winner of the Best Alternate History Novel at the 2023 Dragon Awards, is a testament to Riddle's prowess in crafting intricate, mind-bending narratives that blend science, suspense, and a dash of the supernatural.

The story begins with a gut-wrenching premise: Dr. Sam Anderson wakes up to find his lover murdered, and he's the prime suspect. To protect his daughter, he confesses to a crime he didn't commit, knowing the punishment is exile to the past, 200 million years into the dinosaur era. But his daughter, Adeline, isn't ready to lose another parent. She embarks on a quest to prove her father's innocence, uncovering secrets that span time and challenge everything she knows.

Riddle's style is reminiscent of Michael Crichton, with a penchant for scientific concepts that drive the plot. The time-travel mechanics are well-researched and believable, providing a solid foundation for the story's twists and turns. Yet, it's Riddle's character development that truly shines. Adeline's journey from a grieving daughter to a determined investigator is compelling, her resilience and intelligence making her a protagonist worth rooting for.

The narrative is peppered with red herrings and unexpected revelations, keeping readers guessing until the jaw-dropping twist at the end. Riddle masterfully weaves together multiple timelines, each offering a piece of the puzzle, yet none revealing the full picture until the final moments. This is a book that demands your full attention, rewarding those who stay alert with a satisfying payoff.

"Lost in Time" is more than just a thrilling ride through time; it's a meditation on love, loss, and the lengths we'd go to protect those we care about. It's a testament to the power of human resilience and the lengths we'd go to uncover the truth. It's a book that will leave you breathless, your heart pounding, and your mind racing long after you've turned the last page.

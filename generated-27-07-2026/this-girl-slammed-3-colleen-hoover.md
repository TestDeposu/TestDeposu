# This Girl (Slammed, #3)

**Author:** Colleen Hoover  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In the realm of contemporary romance, few authors have managed to captivate readers with the same level of emotional depth and complexity as Colleen Hoover. Her Slammed series, which concludes with This Girl, is a testament to her skill in crafting stories that are both poignant and thought-provoking. This final installment offers a refreshing twist, retelling the narrative from Will's perspective, and delving into the intricacies of his relationship with Layken.

One of the most striking aspects of This Girl is its exploration of the complexities of human emotions. Hoover masterfully weaves a tale that not only tugs at the heartstrings but also prompts readers to reflect on the very nature of love, relationships, and personal growth. The story is a beautiful blend of past and present, with Will's recollections serving as a catalyst for the couple's journey towards healing and reconciliation.

Through Will's narrative, Hoover sheds light on the often-overlooked aspects of a romantic relationship – the fears, doubts, and uncertainties that can either make or break a couple. The author's writing is characterized by its raw honesty, making it easy for readers to become invested in the characters' struggles and triumphs. The emotional intimacy that Hoover establishes between Will and Layken is palpable, creating a sense of connection that transcends the pages of the book.

What sets This Girl apart from other romance novels is its thoughtful exploration of the human experience. Hoover tackles themes that are both universal and deeply personal, from the challenges of overcoming past traumas to the importance of communication and trust in a relationship. The author's writing is imbued with a sense of vulnerability, making it easy for readers to identify with the characters' struggles and root for their happiness.

Hoover's prose is, as always, lyrical and evocative, with a unique ability to convey the intensity of human emotions. Her writing is both poetic and accessible, making This Girl an enjoyable read for fans of the romance genre and literary fiction alike. The pacing of the story is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows effortlessly between past and present, creating a sense of continuity that underscores the couple's growth and evolution.

In conclusion, This Girl is a masterful conclusion to the Slammed series, offering a poignant and thought-provoking exploration of love, relationships, and personal growth. Colleen Hoover's writing is, as always, a testament to her skill as a storyteller, and her ability to craft characters that are both relatable and memorable. This book is a must-read for fans of contemporary romance and literary fiction, and a fitting conclusion to a series that has captured the hearts of readers worldwide.

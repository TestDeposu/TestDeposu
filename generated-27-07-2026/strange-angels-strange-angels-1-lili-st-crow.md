# Strange Angels (Strange Angels, #1)

**Author:** Lili St. Crow  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**Strange Angels: A Dark, Gripping Debut**

Lili St. Crow's "Strange Angels" is a thrilling, fast-paced dive into the world of the supernatural, where the monsters are real, and the heroine is anything but ordinary. The story follows Dru Anderson, a 16-year-old orphan with a unique upbringing and an even more unique ability to see and fight the creatures that lurk in the shadows.

St. Crow's writing style is engaging and gritty, painting a vivid picture of the harsh, unforgiving world Dru inhabits. The narrative is told in the first person, allowing readers to experience Dru's thoughts, fears, and triumphs intimately. St. Crow's use of short, sharp sentences and vivid imagery creates a sense of urgency and tension that keeps the pages turning.

The main theme of "Strange Angels" is survival, both physical and emotional. Dru's world is one of constant danger, where the line between friend and foe is often blurred. She must rely on her wits, her skills, and her ability to adapt if she wants to stay alive. Yet, despite the darkness and danger, Dru remains a relatable, flawed character. Her struggles with trust, her fear of vulnerability, and her determination to protect those she cares about make her a heroine worth rooting for.

St. Crow also explores the theme of identity, as Dru grapples with her place in the world and her role in the supernatural community. She's a girl caught between two worlds, trying to find her footing in a life that's far from normal. This internal struggle adds depth to Dru's character and provides a compelling contrast to the action-packed plot.

"Strange Angels" is not for the faint-hearted. It's a dark, often brutal tale that doesn't shy away from the harsh realities of Dru's life. Yet, amidst the violence and fear, there are moments of levity and hope. St. Crow's characters are well-developed, each with their own unique voice and story. The relationships between Dru and her allies are complex and believable, adding an emotional depth to the story that makes the action sequences all the more impactful.

In conclusion, "Strange Angels" is a gripping, action-packed debut that will appeal to fans of urban fantasy and supernatural thrillers. St. Crow's unique take on the genre, combined with her engaging writing style and compelling characters, makes this a standout novel in the crowded field of young adult fiction.

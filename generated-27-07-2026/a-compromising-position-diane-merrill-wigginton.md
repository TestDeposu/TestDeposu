# A Compromising Position

**Author:** Diane Merrill Wigginton  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

A Compromising Position, the award-winning novel by Diane Merrill Wigginton, is a captivating tale of love, self-discovery, and the complexities of relationships. This contemporary romantic comedy follows the story of Catherine Lawrence, a determined campaign manager, and Jake Ryan, a free-spirited Australian surfer, as they navigate their whirlwind romance and confront the challenges that come with it.

Wigginton's writing style is engaging and effortless, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world she has created. The characters are well-developed and relatable, with distinct personalities that leap off the page. Catherine's Type-A personality and Jake's carefree nature create a compelling contrast, and their chemistry is undeniable. The author's use of witty dialogue and humorous situations adds a lighthearted touch to the story, making it a delightful read.

One of the standout aspects of A Compromising Position is its exploration of the human heart. Wigginton delves into the complexities of love, relationships, and personal growth, raising questions about what it means to truly be in love and to make sacrifices for the people we care about. The novel's themes of self-discovery and compromise are timely and relevant, making it a great choice for book clubs and readers looking for a thought-provoking story.

The author's use of descriptive language brings the setting of Florida to life, and the vivid descriptions of the beach and the characters' surroundings add to the novel's charm. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of humorous moments, heartfelt scenes, and unexpected twists that keep the reader engaged.

What sets A Compromising Position apart from other romantic comedies is its ability to balance humor and heart. The novel is laugh-out-loud funny at times, but it also tackles deeper themes and emotions with sensitivity and nuance. Wigginton's writing is both entertaining and poignant, making this novel a must-read for fans of the genre.

Overall, A Compromising Position is a delightful and engaging read that will appeal to fans of romantic comedies and contemporary fiction. With its well-developed characters, witty dialogue, and exploration of the human heart, this novel is a great choice for anyone looking for a lighthearted yet thought-provoking story.

# The Darkest Kiss (Riley Jenson Guardian, #6)

**Author:** Keri Arthur  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

Keri Arthur returns with a vengeance in The Darkest Kiss, the sixth thrilling installment of the acclaimed Riley Jenson Guardian series. Published in 2008, this pulse-pounding novel reaffirms Arthur’s mastery of urban fantasy, blending high-stakes crime investigation with unbridled supernatural passion. True to form, Arthur delivers a narrative where danger is not merely an occupational hazard for the protagonist, but the very spark that ignites her primal nature.

At the heart of the novel is the compelling theme of duality and control. Riley Jenson—a rare and potent hybrid of vampire and werewolf—navigates a world torn between her civilized duties as a Guardian and the visceral, predatory instincts encoded in her DNA. Arthur brilliantly uses Riley’s dual heritage as a metaphor for the eternal struggle between restraint and desire. When Riley steps into her latest case—tracking a sadistic killer targeting high-society women and their powerful companions—she must also contend with a secondary, deeply personal terror: a rogue vampire leaving a trail of mutilated bodies tied to Riley's own tragic past. The theme deepens as the violence hits close to home, forcing Riley to confront the limits of her own formidable strength and emotional resilience. 

Arthur’s stylistic signature is on full display here. She crafts a fast-paced, visceral reading experience driven by sharp dialogue, relentless action, and unapologetic sensuality. Her world-building seamlessly integrates the mundane with the macabre, presenting a Melbourne underworld throbbing with supernatural politics, hidden agendas, and raw power dynamics. What sets Arthur apart in the crowded urban fantasy genre is her fearless protagonist. Riley is no damsel in distress; she struts into battle in snakeskin stilettos with a lethal attitude, yet she remains refreshingly vulnerable, driven by fierce loyalty to her clan. 

The re-entry of Quinn, a dangerously seductive and ancient vampire, injects a volatile dynamic into the plot. Quinn serves as the ultimate foil to Riley, representing the one element in her chaotic life she cannot dominate or predict. Their electric chemistry elevates the romantic suspense subplot, ensuring that the tension sizzles just as brightly in private moments as it does during the bloody manhunts. 

The Darkest Kiss is a masterclass in urban fantasy pacing and character-driven suspense. Keri Arthur wastes no time on filler, keeping readers hooked from the first page to the last with clever plot twists and relentless momentum. For fans of kick-ass heroines, dark supernatural mysteries, and romances charged with undeniable heat, this novel is an absolute must-read. Arthur proves once again that she knows how to deliver a bite that leaves you craving more.

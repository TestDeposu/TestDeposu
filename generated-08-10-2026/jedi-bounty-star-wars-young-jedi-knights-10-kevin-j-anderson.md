# Jedi Bounty (Star Wars: Young Jedi Knights, #10)

**Author:** Kevin J. Anderson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1994

Step back into the nostalgic golden era of the Star Wars Expanded Universe with Jedi Bounty, the tenth installment in Kevin J. Anderson and Rebecca Moesta’s beloved Young Jedi Knights series. Published in 1994, this tight-paced, action-packed YA adventure captures the quintessential spirit of classic Star Wars, blending high-stakes galactic peril with the intimate, formative struggles of a new generation coming into the light of the Force. 

While the official publishing descriptions sometimes blur the lines with the broader Legends timeline—hinting at the legendary shadows of the Jedi Academy and the terrifying terror of the Sun Crusher—Jedi Bounty fundamentally serves as a thrilling pit stop in the ongoing maturation of Jaina and Jacen Solo. Anderson’s narrative style is unapologetically kinetic. He writes with the fast-cut, cinematic urgency of Saturday morning serials, ensuring that readers are never more than a few pages away from a blaster fight, a narrow escape, or a pulse-pounding hyperspace jump.

The central theme of the novel, as with much of the Young Jedi Knights arc, explores legacy and identity. The Jedi twins are not merely passive observers of the galactic civil war; they are active participants forced to reconcile the monumental reputations of their parents, Han Solo and Leia Organa, with their own emerging identities as Jedi Knights. Anderson highlights the psychological weight of this heritage. When danger strikes and loved ones are thrust into the crosshairs of lingering Imperial factions, the young protagonists must rely not just on lightsabers and tactical wit, but on emotional maturity, trust in the Force, and the unbreakable bonds of friendship forged at Luke Skywalker’s academy.

Anderson’s prose is accessible, brisk, and deeply rooted in the broader lore of the 1990s Expanded Universe. For readers who grew up devouring paperbacks with painted covers, his writing style evokes an immediate sense of comfort and excitement. He excels at dialogue that feels true to the original trilogy characters while giving the younger ensemble distinct, vibrant voices. The pacing rarely dips, moving seamlessly from the oppressive, spice-laden depths of Kessel to the eerie, high-stakes isolation of deep-space laboratories and imperial remnants. 

Jedi Bounty is a testament to an era of Star Wars storytelling that prioritized sprawling universe-building and character-driven adventure. It rewards longtime fans with deep-cut continuity while remaining completely welcoming to younger readers stepping into a galaxy far, far away for the very first time. It is a brisk, entertaining, and satisfying chapter in a series that helped define a generation of Star Wars fandom.

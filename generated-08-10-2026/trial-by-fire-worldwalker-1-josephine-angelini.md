# Trial by Fire (Worldwalker, #1)

**Author:** Josephine Angelini  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

In Trial by Fire, the striking opening installment of Josephine Angelini’s Worldwalker series, the author takes a premise as old as speculative fiction—the portal fantasy—and infuses it with a visceral, modern urgency. Published in 2014, the novel introduces us to Lily Proctor, a teenager whose existence is dictated by the fragile truce she must maintain with the natural world. Plagued by life-threatening allergies, Lily is quite literally allergic to her own environment, a condition that paints her as perpetually weak, vulnerable, and sidelined in her hometown of Salem, Massachusetts. Yet, Angelini brilliantly subverts this vulnerability the moment Lily is thrust through the veil into an alternate, dystopian Salem.

The central theme of the novel pivots on the duality of identity and the redemptive power of self-perception. In our world, Lily’s severe allergies isolate and diminish her. But in the alternate Salem—a harsh, matriarchal realm ruled by powerful magic-wielding women—those same biological quirks become the catalyst for immense, terrifying power. Angelini explores how environment dictates strength, posing a profound philosophical question to the reader: what if your greatest weakness was merely your greatest asset, misplaced? The thematic core resonates deeply with young adult readers, capturing the universal angst of feeling inadequate and transforming it into literal superpowers. Lily’s journey is not just about mastering magic to save a world, but about reconciling who she is with who she was always capable of being, especially when confronted with Lillian, her ruthless, alternate-world counterpart who serves as her ultimate foil.

Angelini’s writing style is brisk, atmospheric, and relentlessly cinematic. She excels at world-building through sensory details, contrasting the mundane, sterile precautions of Lily’s allergy-ridden life with the lush, dangerous, and oppressive magic of the alternate Salem. The prose moves at a breathless pace, balancing high-stakes political intrigue, magical combat, and a simmering enemies-to-lovers romance that anchors the emotional stakes. While the narrative occasionally leans into familiar young adult tropes, Angelini’s confident pacing and sharp character dynamics keep the pages turning. Lily is a compelling protagonist—flawed, resilient, and refreshingly realistic in her self-doubt. 

Trial by Fire is a captivating start to a magical saga that proves our greatest trials are often the very fires that forge our true strength.

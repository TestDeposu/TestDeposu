# Every Spiral of Fate (This Woven Kingdom, #4)

**Author:** Tahereh Mafi  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

To step into Tahereh Mafi’s literary universe is to surrender to a sensory indulgence, and with Every Spiral of Fate, the fourth breathtaking installment in the Woven Kingdom series, Mafi continues to prove herself as a supreme architect of romantic fantasy. Known for her lyrical prose and intoxicating emotional intensity, Mafi weaves a tapestry of myth, magic, and agonizingly slow-burn tension that firmly cements this saga as a modern classic of the genre. 

At the heart of Every Spiral of Fate lies a devastating thematic core: the eternal war between duty and desire, inextricably bound by the cruelest paradox imaginable. The narrative opens under the shadow of a dreaded wedding day—a union forged not in romance, but by ancient blood oaths and geopolitical necessity. Cyrus, the enigmatic and dangerous ruler of Tulan, finds himself trapped in a marriage to the Jinn queen, Alizeh. Yet, triumph is entirely absent from his heart. Cyrus is hopelessly, irrevocably falling in love with the very woman whose destiny—and oath—is to plunge a blade into his chest. 

Mafi explores the enemies-to-lovers trope with exquisite, torturous precision. The psychological friction between Alizeh and Cyrus forms the beating heart of the novel. Alizeh is burdened by the prophecy to free her people through regicide, forced to walk a razor’s edge between her obligations and a burgeoning affection that defies all logic. Meanwhile, the specter of Prince Kamran looms large, adding a volatile third point to a triangle fueled by desperation, jealousy, and shifting loyalties. Mafi excels at capturing the exquisite agony of yearning; every glance, every withheld confession, and every brush of skin carries the weight of impending doom. The countdown to murder is not merely a plot device, but a pressure cooker that intensifies the romantic stakes to a fever pitch.

Beyond the romance, Mafi’s stylistic brilliance shines in her world-building and atmospheric prose. Her writing is famously poetic, characterized by lush descriptions that linger on the palate long after the page is turned. In this volume, the scope widens dramatically. Fleeing on dragon-back, Alizeh and her companions are thrust out of familiar territories and into the legendary, perilous mountains of Arya. This geographical shift mirrors the internal evolution of the characters. As they hunt for magical discoveries and fresh allies, the narrative accelerates into a high-stakes war fantasy. 

Mafi does not merely write action; she orchestrates emotional firestorms. The unveiling of the Tulanian king’s shattering secrets acts as a narrative earthquake, upending every established allegiance and forcing the characters—and the reader—to question everything they thought they knew. It is this unpredictable plotting, paired with unyielding emotional resonance, that elevates Mafi’s work above standard romantasy fare. She understands that true magic lies not just in spells and mythical beasts, but in the frailties and resilience of the human—and Jinn—heart.

Every Spiral of Fate is a shimmering, poignant triumph. It delivers the fiery romance and spectacular magic fans crave, wrapped in prose so gorgeous it reads like incantation. Mafi challenges her characters to rewrite their destinies, and in doing so, she crafts a deeply satisfying, pulse-pounding chapter in a saga that continues to transcend expectations. Prepare to have your heart broken and miraculously reassembled, all over again.

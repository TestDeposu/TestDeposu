# Bitten & Smitten (Immortality Bites, #1)

**Author:** Michelle Rowen  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Unknown

Bitten and Smitten by Michelle Rowen is a sparkling, high-concept entry into the crowded urban fantasy market that proves vampires still have plenty of bite left in them—provided you approach the fangs with a healthy dose of wit. In this inaugural installment of the Immortality Bites series, Rowen bypasses the moody, existential dread typical of contemporary vampire lore and instead serves up a delectable blend of supernatural danger and laugh-out-loud romantic comedy.

The narrative pivots on a brilliant thematic core: the friction between the mundane banalities of human life and the high-stakes drama of the undead underworld. Rowen uses her protagonist, Sarah Dearly—a painfully normal, slightly clumsy temp with a penchant for bad fashion—as a brilliant vehicle to deconstruct the classic vampire mythos. When Sarah is accidentally bitten by a master vampire and subsequently thrust into a subterranean world of immortals, the theme of transformation is brilliantly subverted. Instead of becoming a fierce, brooding creature of the night overnight, Sarah remains stubbornly, relatably human in her anxieties, stumbling through eternity one awkward misstep at a time. 

Rowen’s writing style is effortlessly breezy, fast-paced, and sharply comedic. Where other authors in the genre lean heavily into gothic prose and brooding introspection, Rowen opts for snappy dialogue, situational irony, and vibrant pacing. She crafts a world where ancient bloodlines and deadly political intrigue collide with consumer culture and self-doubt. The prose never takes itself too seriously, allowing the romantic tension to sizzle without bogging down the narrative momentum. Every page crackles with energy, making the book an undeniably swift and addictive read. 

Ultimately, Bitten and Smitten succeeds because it knows exactly what it wants to be: a delightful, escapist confection that treats supernatural tropes with affection and irreverence. Michelle Rowen delivers a sparkling debut that leaves readers eager to sink their teeth into the rest of the series.

# Shockwave (Star Kingdom, #1)

**Author:** Lindsay Buroker  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

Lindsay Buroker’s *Shockwave*, the opening salvo of the *Star Kingdom* series, proves that science fiction does not always need to take itself too seriously to deliver high-stakes thrills. Set against a sprawling galactic backdrop plagued by tyranny, political corruption, and the looming threat of war, the novel cleverly bypasses the traditional archetype of the stoic space marine. Instead, Buroker thrusts readers headfirst into the chaotic lives of the galaxy’s most endearing rejects, crafting a space opera that proudly wears its inspirations—most notably *Firefly* and classic *Star Wars*—on its sleeve.

At its core, the central theme of *Shockwave* is the redemptive power of found family and the unexpected heroism of the marginalized. While the Star Kingdom crumbles under the weight of treacherous rulers and a vengeful, unstoppable mercenary leaving a trail of destruction in his wake, the fate of the galaxy paradoxically rests on the shoulders of individuals who cannot even manage their own day-to-day survival. Buroker explores the idea that true strength is not derived from royal bloodlines, military indoctrination, or vast wealth, but from resilience, adaptability, and the willingness to stand up for one another when the universe demands compliance. 

Buroker’s authorial style is the true engine driving this cosmic rollercoaster. Known for her snappy dialogue and brisk pacing, she excels at balancing pulse-pounding action sequences with sharp, self-aware humor. The narrative never bogs down in overly dense technobabble or agonizingly slow world-building; instead, the universe is revealed organically through the desperate, fast-moving experiences of the characters. This lighter, adventurous tone keeps the pages turning effortlessly, offering a refreshing antidote to the grimdark despair that often dominates modern speculative fiction. 

The character work further elevates the style. Buroker assembles a wonderfully mismatched ensemble: a down-on-her-luck bounty hunter merely trying to pay the bills, a genetically engineered cat woman fleeing her sinister creators, a robotics professor who unfortunately suffers from debilitating space sickness, and a brilliant scientist whose immediate reaction to social awkwardness is physical violence. These distinct personalities clash in all the right ways, generating immediate comedic chemistry while providing genuine emotional stakes. By grounding grand, space-faring threats in the relatable, mundane financial and personal struggles of her protagonists, Buroker ensures that readers are rooting for them long before they realize they are humanity's best hope.

*Shockwave* is a masterclass in entertaining, popcorn-ready science fiction. It delivers all the dogfights, narrow escapes, and political intrigue expected of a sweeping space opera, but infuses them with a distinct, humorous heart. For readers craving a nostalgic yet fresh adventure filled with lovable underdogs, witty banter, and high-octane pacing, Buroker’s latest creation is an absolute must-read.

# On Silver Wings (Hayden War Cycle, #1)

**Author:** Evan Currie  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

Evan Currie’s On Silver Wings plunges readers into a vast, meticulously crafted sci-fi universe where humanity’s triumphant expansion into the dark expanse of space meets a chilling, existential reality. Published in 2016 and serving as the explosive opening salvo of the Hayden War Cycle, the novel masterfully tackles one of science fiction’s most enduring themes: the terrifying transition from being the undisputed masters of the known cosmos to realizing we are entirely alone no longer. Currie sets the stage with a sense of quiet dread, contrasting the long, silent echoes of sub-light space travel and advanced faster-than-light communication networks with the sudden, deafening silence of a lost colony.

The narrative hook is as striking as it is devastating. When the prosperous human colony on Hayden's World abruptly drops out of contact, the Solar Fleet’s response is swift, leading to the deployment of an elite special operations team. But Currie quickly subverts military sci-fi tropes by stripping away the safety net of overwhelming force. When only a single operator—Staff Sergeant Sorilla Aida—survives the initial contact, the scale of the novel shrinks from a grand interstellar armada conflict to a claustrophobic, high-stakes fight for survival on the surface of a conquered world. 

Currie’s authorial style shines brightest in these moments of intense, boots-on-the-ground tension. He writes with a lean, urgent prose that mirrors the tactical precision of his protagonist. Aida is not a caricature of an action hero; she is a professional soldier thrust into an unwinnable scenario, armed with little more than her powered armor, her training, and her sheer grit. The tactical descriptions are sharp, grounded, and immensely satisfying for fans of hard military science fiction, yet they never overshadow the human element at the core of the story. 

The central theme revolves around resilience against insurmountable odds. Aida finds herself not just fighting for her own life, but acting as a beacon of hope for hundreds of stranded Hayden civilians. Currie explores how ordinary people and elite soldiers alike react when the comfortable status quo of human dominance is shattered by a technologically superior and utterly alien adversary. The unknown threat possesses capabilities that challenge everything humanity thought it knew about the galaxy, elevating the stakes from a localized skirmish to the opening battle of a war for human survival.

On Silver Wings is a masterclass in pacing and atmospheric world-building. Currie delivers a gripping, adrenaline-fueled narrative that balances grand space-opera scope with visceral, intimate combat. It is a thrilling introduction to a universe that feels both wondrous and perilous, leaving readers eager to follow Aida and the Solar Fleet into the dark unknown.

# Unmade (The Lynburn Legacy #3)

**Author:** Sarah Rees Brennan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

With *Unmade*, the thrilling third and final installment of *The Lynburn Legacy*, Sarah Rees Brennan delivers a brilliant, emotionally resonant conclusion to a trilogy that has consistently defied young adult fantasy conventions. Published in 2014, this modern, magical twist on Gothic romance and the classic girl-detective genre brings the dark secrets of the town of Sorry-in-the-Fruit to a head, weaving together heartbreak, humor, and high-stakes supernatural danger. 

At the core of *Unmade* lies a powerful exploration of sacrifice, agency, and the complex burdens of connection. Protagonist Kami Glass finds herself emotionally and magically torn between two very different boys: bound to Ash through a formidable magical link, yet tethered to Jared by an unforgettable, enduring love. With Jared missing and presumed dead for months, Kami must lean on her resilience, her fierce intellect, and her loyal circle of friends to combat the escalating evil infiltrating her home. Brennan uses this central dilemma not merely as a vehicle for a standard love triangle, but as a meditation on what it truly costs to save the people you love. The narrative forces Kami to confront an agonizing truth: knowledge always demands responsibility, and protecting her town requires a sacrifice that could cost her everything.

Brennan’s authorial style is the true engine driving this compulsive, rocketing read. She has mastered the rare art of balancing tonal extremes. *Unmade* is delightfully and darkly funny, puncturing moments of mounting Gothic dread with sharp, witty banter and genuine teenage levity. Yet, just as the reader settles into a laugh, Brennan twists the knife, delivering emotional gut-punches that break the heart. Her prose is brisk, atmospheric, and relentlessly paced—hallmarks of a writer who understands how to craft an irresistible page-turner. 

Furthermore, the novel deserves immense praise for its organic, meaningful representation. As a strong champion of diversity in the YA landscape, Brennan continues to center Kami Glass, a sharp-witted half-Japanese teen whose cultural identity is seamlessly integrated into her reality rather than tokenized. Coupled with a poignant LGBTQ romance subplot that develops naturally within the ensemble cast, *Unmade* reflects the messy, inclusive reality of modern adolescence against a backdrop of ancient magic and supernatural peril.

For fans of Cassandra Clare and Kelley Armstrong, *Unmade* is an absolute triumph. It shivers, it swoons, and it satisfies, offering a masterclass in how to wrap up a sprawling supernatural saga with both surprise and emotional integrity.

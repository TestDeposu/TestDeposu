# City of the Dead (The Rising #2)

**Author:** Brian Keene  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

**Brian Keene’s *The Rising: More Selected Scenes From the End of the World*—A Masterclass in Apocalyptic Horror**

Brian Keene doesn’t just write horror—he *redefines* it. With *The Rising: More Selected Scenes From the End of the World*, the Bram Stoker Award-winning author delivers a visceral, unflinching expansion of his iconic zombie apocalypse, proving once again why he’s a titan of the genre. This isn’t just a sequel; it’s a *revelation*—a collection of fresh, brutal, and deeply human stories that deepen the lore of *The Rising* while keeping the pulse-pounding intensity that made the original series a modern classic.

At its core, *The Rising* has always been about more than just the undead. It’s a story of survival, desperation, and the raw, unfiltered chaos of a world collapsing around its characters. Keene’s genius lies in his ability to balance grand-scale apocalyptic dread with intimate, character-driven horror. In *More Selected Scenes*, he doesn’t just retread familiar ground—he *expands* it. These new tales, set both before and after the events of the original trilogy, offer fresh perspectives on the outbreak, the rise of the infected, and the desperate struggles of those left behind. Whether it’s a pre-apocalypse story of a scientist racing against time or a post-collapse narrative of survivors clinging to humanity in a world gone mad, Keene ensures every word carries weight.

His writing is a force of nature—raw, unfiltered, and relentless. There’s no pretension here, no attempt to soften the horror. Keene embraces the grim, the grotesque, and the deeply unsettling, crafting scenes that linger long after the last page. His prose is visceral, his dialogue sharp, and his sense of atmosphere unmatched. He doesn’t just describe the undead; he *makes them feel*—not as mindless monsters, but as twisted reflections of humanity’s worst instincts. And in doing so, he forces readers to confront the terrifying reality that the apocalypse isn’t just about the dead; it’s about what’s left of us when the world ends.

What makes this collection particularly compelling is its structure. Unlike a traditional sequel, *More Selected Scenes* feels like a *deep dive*—a chance to explore the corners of Keene’s world that fans have always wondered about. The inclusion of original illustrations by Anthrax’s Charlie Benante adds another layer of immersion, blending the written word with the visual horror of the series. It’s a testament to Keene’s ability to keep his franchise fresh, even after decades of storytelling.

For longtime fans, this is a dream come true—a chance to revisit old characters and places through a new lens. For newcomers, it’s an invitation into a world where the undead aren’t just a threat; they’re a force of nature, reshaping civilization in their wake. Keene doesn’t just write about the end of the world; he *lives* it, and that passion is infectious.

If you’ve ever wondered what happens next in *The Rising*, this is the book to find out. Brian Keene doesn’t just upgrade zombies—he upgrades the entire experience of reading horror. And in a genre that often feels tired, that’s nothing short of revolutionary.

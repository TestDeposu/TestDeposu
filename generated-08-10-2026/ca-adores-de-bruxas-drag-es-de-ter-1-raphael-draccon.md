# Caçadores de bruxas (Dragões de Éter, #1)

**Author:** Raphael Draccon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

Reinventing Fairy Tales in a World of Blood and Ether

What happens when the nostalgic innocence of childhood bedtime stories collides with the brutal, unforgiving mechanics of dark epic fantasy? Raphael Draccon answers this question with a thunderous roar in Caçadores de Bruxas, the first volume of the acclaimed series Dragões de Éter. Originally published and later reissued for a modern audience, Draccon’s work stands as a monumental pillar of contemporary Brazilian fantasy, effortlessly bridging the gap between classic mythos and modern dark aesthetics.

The central theme of the novel explores the consequences of power, vengeance, and the cyclical nature of violence. Nova Ether is a realm forged in trauma. Once guarded by benevolent fairy-amazons, the world was plunged into a terrifying epoch when these divine protectors turned against mortal races in disgust. Peace was only restored through a historic, violent Witch Hunt led by Primo Branford, a humble miller’s son who rose to become the King of Arzallum. For two decades, humanity has basked in the warmth of this hard-fought peace. Yet, Draccon’s narrative thrives on a chilling premise: peace bought with blood is merely a ticking clock.

As the story unfolds, the past comes back to haunt the kingdom in grotesque, twisted reflections of classic folklore. Readers are immediately thrust into a world where a young girl witnesses her grandmother devoured by a wolf marked with dark magic, and where two siblings ingest shards of glass and muddy water under the hypnotic spell of a cannibalistic witch. These aren’t just clever nods to Little Red Riding Hood and Hansel and Gretel; they are visceral, psychological horrors that strip away the Disney-esque veneer from these ancient tales and expose their jagged, bleeding edges. Alongside these grim fairy-tale resuscitations, the plot thickens with the return of a legendary, ruthless mercenary and a clandestine war between criminal syndicates that threatens to expose deeply buried secrets of the royal family.

What truly sets Caçadores de Bruxas apart is Draccon’s distinctive stylistic signature. The author crafts a rich tapestry that feels simultaneously ancient and hyper-modern. His prose weaves philosophical dialogues and sweeping epic fantasy tropes with a vibrant pop-culture sensibility. It is a world where references to timeless grim fairy tales and deep spiritual journeys comfortably share space with the aesthetic grit of modern rock bands like Nirvana and Limp Bizkit, alongside the adventurous scope of epic role-playing games like Final Fantasy. This eclectic blend might sound jarring on paper, but in Draccon’s hands, it creates a unique, youthful resonance that captures the melancholy, rebellion, and boundless imagination of the transition from youth to adulthood.

Draccon does not just retell stories; he deconstructs them, offering a profound spiritual depth to a genre often satisfied with surface-level magic. He asks his readers to look beyond the heroes and villains, examining the emotional scars left by the loss of innocence and the heavy price of survival. The result is a breathless, multifaceted epic that feels both intimately familiar and thrillingly dangerous.

Caçadores de Bruxas is an essential read for anyone who ever wondered what happened after happily ever after. It is a stunning invitation to step into a realm where fairy tales bleed, dragons breathe ethereal fire, and heroes are forged in the darkest fires of human nature. Prepare to have your childhood rewritten.

# Last of the Talons (Talons, #1)

**Author:** Sophie Kim  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

When myth bleeds into the shadows of a brutal underworld, we are introduced to a breathless universe where survival demands an absolute sacrifice. Sophie Kim’s 2022 YA fantasy triumph, Last of the Talons, the first installment in the Talons series, wastes no time thrusting readers into a world as mesmerizing as it is lethal. Hailed as a must-read by Epic Reads and BookRiot, this novel establishes Kim as a formidable voice in Asia-inspired fantasy, weaving a tapestry of dark romance, Korean mythology, and high-stakes tension that lingers long after the final page.

At the heart of the novel is Shin Lina, known across the land as the Reaper of Sunpo. Eighteen years old and the devastatingly efficient survivor of a wiped-out gang, Lina is reduced to a living weapon, bound to the kingdom’s most feared crime lord. Kim immediately grounds the narrative in emotional grit; Lina's deadly compliance is fueled entirely by a desperate tether—the fragile, hanging life of her little sister. This emotional core elevates Lina from a standard fantasy assassin to a complex, bruised protagonist whom readers will root for with fierce desperation. 

The author’s style is sharp, atmospheric, and unsparing. Kim masterfully balances the grit of street-level crime with the surreal, dreamlike majesty of Korean folklore. When Lina is tasked with stealing a priceless artifact from a Dokkaebi temple, the narrative shifts gears, plunging from urban grit into a surreal, high-stakes realm ruled by the captivating and perilous immortal emperor, Haneul Rui. 

It is within this clash of worlds that the novel truly shines. The central theme of Last of the Talons explores the blurry, razor-thin boundary between destruction and devotion, captivity and liberation. Kim tackles the classic enemies-to-lovers trope with heart-pounding intensity. The dynamic between Lina and Rui crackles with a dangerous, magnetic friction. Tasked with an impossible mandate—kill the immortal king or forfeit her own life—Lina enters a deadly game of wits and steel against an opponent who matches her every move. Kim uses this forced proximity to construct a slow-burn psychological chess match where every interaction serves as a duel between love and hate. 

The pacing is relentless, mimicking the falling grains of an hourglass as Lina races against time to save her family. Kim’s prose is visceral; she describes the flash of a blade and the ethereal beauty of the Dokkaebi realm with equal sensory precision, creating an immersive backdrop for a romance that feels both bloodthirsty and searingly tender. 

Last of the Talons is a triumph of dark, mythological YA fiction. Sophie Kim has crafted an addictive, fast-paced nightmare-turned-fairytale that demands to be devoured in a single sitting. For readers craving fierce heroines, morally gray immortals, and romance that burns with the intensity of a drawn blade, this book is an absolute essential.

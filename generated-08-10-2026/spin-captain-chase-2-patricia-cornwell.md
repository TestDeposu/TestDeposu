# Spin (Captain Chase, #2)

**Author:** Patricia Cornwell  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In *Spin*, the second installment in the Captain Chase series, bestselling author Patricia Cornwell expands her literary horizon far beyond the forensic pathology labs that made her famous, launching her readers into the high-stakes, orbit-reaching realms of aerospace espionage and quantum conspiracy. Known globally for her meticulous plotting and the iconic Kay Scarpetta, Cornwell here pivots toward a different kind of heroine: Captain Calli Chase, a brilliant NASA scientist and Space Force pilot whose universe is suddenly upended by both a catastrophic rocket launch failure and the staggering reappearance of her long-lost twin sister.

The central theme of *Spin* revolves around identity, duality, and the fragile precipice upon which modern humanity balances. Cornwell plays brilliantly with the notion of the double—symbolized not only by Calli and her sister, but also by the duplicity of a world where cutting-edge technology can be simultaneously our greatest savior and our most terrifying weapon. As Calli navigates a planetary game of chess that stretches from the inner sanctums of the White House and the isolated landscapes of her family farm to the cold, distant orbits of space, the narrative questions how well we truly know ourselves and the systems we build. The sabotage of the top-secret NASA program serves as a macrocosm for Calli’s internal crisis: an intricate puzzle where a single misstep threatens catastro-phic, global consequences.

True to her signature style, Cornwell infuses the narrative with an relentless, pulse-pounding urgency. Her prose remains sharp, clinical yet evocative, driven by an insatiable curiosity for procedural detail and technical precision. While Scarpetta relied on the microscope and the scalpel, Calli Chase relies on quantum mechanics, spacecraft telemetry, and elite military training. Yet, the DNA of a classic Cornwell thriller is unmistakable. The pacing is relentless, engineered to trap the reader in a claustrophobic countdown against time. Every chapter acts as a calculated move in a deadly game, blending hard science with visceral human tension. 

Cornwell proves that her narrative stamina is as formidable as ever. *Spin* is a striking, ambitious thriller that challenges its protagonist—and its audience—to look up at the stars with a mixture of wonder and dread. For fans of high-tech suspense and intricate conspiracies, this novel confirms that Patricia Cornwell’s transition from earthly morgues to the final frontier is nothing short of triumphant.

# Tempting Evil (Riley Jenson Guardian, #3)

**Author:** Keri Arthur  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

**Tempting Evil: Where Desire and Danger Collide in Keri Arthur’s Darkly Sensual World**

Keri Arthur’s *Tempting Evil*—the third installment in the *Riley Jenson Guardian* series—is a masterclass in blending erotic tension with high-stakes supernatural intrigue. Set in a world where the line between predator and prey is as thin as a whispered promise, this novel follows Riley Jenson, a half-werewolf, half-vampire operative, as she navigates a mission that tests her loyalty, her instincts, and her own damnable allure. Arthur’s signature style—sharp, seductive, and relentlessly immersive—makes *Tempting Evil* a standout in the paranormal romance genre, where danger isn’t just lurking in the shadows; it’s woven into the very fabric of desire.

At its core, *Tempting Evil* is a story about control—who has it, who wants it, and what happens when the boundaries between power and corruption blur. Riley, a guardian tasked with policing the supernatural underworld, is no naive heroine. She’s a survivor, hardened by betrayal and the weight of her dual nature, which makes her both a weapon and a target. Her latest assignment takes her into the lair of Deshon Starr, a mad scientist whose experiments with forbidden genetics have created a new breed of monstrous beings. But the real threat isn’t just the creatures he’s conjured—it’s the seduction of the mission itself. The deeper Riley goes, the more she realizes that the palace of pleasure she’s infiltrating isn’t just a den of vice; it’s a trap, and the men who guard its doors are as dangerous as the secrets they protect.

Arthur excels at crafting characters who are as complex as they are compelling. The two men vying for Riley’s attention—a brooding, intellectually dominant vampire and a fiercely protective werewolf—embody the duality of her own nature. One offers power, the other offers passion, and both represent the risks of trust in a world where alliances are temporary and loyalty is a currency. Their chemistry is electric, simmering with the kind of tension that makes the reader lean in, waiting for the spark to ignite. But what sets Arthur apart is her ability to make these romantic entanglements feel like a liability rather than a luxury. In *Tempting Evil*, desire isn’t just a distraction—it’s a weapon, and Riley must learn to wield it as carefully as she does her claws.

The prose is lush yet precise, dripping with a sensuality that lingers long after the last page. Arthur’s descriptions of the supernatural world are vivid, painting a picture of a society where beauty is often a mask for brutality. The pleasure palace of Deshon Starr isn’t just a place of indulgence; it’s a labyrinth of deception, where every smile could be a blade and every touch a trap. The pacing is relentless, balancing action-packed sequences with moments of quiet intensity, making it impossible to put down. By the time the final confrontation unfolds, the reader is left breathless—not just from the stakes, but from the sheer audacity of the story itself.

What makes *Tempting Evil* particularly gripping is its exploration of Riley’s own internal conflict. She’s not just fighting for the greater good; she’s fighting against the very things that make her who she is. Her dual heritage is both her greatest strength and her most crippling weakness, and Arthur doesn’t shy away from the moral ambiguities that come with it. Is Riley a guardian or a predator? A hero or a villain? The answer isn’t so simple, and that’s what makes her so compelling. The novel forces readers to question whether the ends ever truly justify the means in a world where survival often requires sacrificing one’s soul.

For fans of paranormal romance with a dark edge, *Tempting Evil* is a must-read. It’s a story where the line between seduction and salvation is razor-thin, and where every kiss could be the last thing Riley ever feels. Arthur’s writing is bold, her characters are unforgettable, and her world is rich with danger and desire. If you’ve ever wondered what it would be like to walk the line between light and shadow, between love and betrayal, then this is the book for you. Just be warned—once you start, you won’t want to stop.

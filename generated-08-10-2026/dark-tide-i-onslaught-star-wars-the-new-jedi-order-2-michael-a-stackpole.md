# Dark Tide I: Onslaught (Star Wars: The New Jedi Order, #2)

**Author:** Michael A. Stackpole  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2000

Step into a galaxy forever altered. With Dark Tide I: Onslaught, veteran Star Wars architect Michael A. Stackpole takes the helm of the ambitious New Jedi Order series, steering the beloved space opera into uncharted, terrifying waters. Published in 2000, this installment does not merely introduce a new conflict; it systematically dismantles the safety nets of the familiar Expanded Universe, replacing comforting nostalgia with a relentless, existential nightmare.

At the heart of Onslaught lies a chilling thematic pivot: the absolute inversion of the Force as the ultimate cosmic anchor. Enter the Yuuzhan Vong—a ruthless, extra-galactic species of religious zealots whose very biology rejects the living Force. For generations, readers and characters alike have relied on the mystical energy field as a predictable axis of reality, a reliable tool for heroes and villains. Stackpole strips this away with surgical precision. When your primary weapon is entirely invisible to an enemy who feels no pain, revels in torture, and wields biotechnology that defies all known laws of physics, panic becomes the default state of the New Republic. 

This thematic dread is compounded by internal decay. Stackpole uses Onslaught to highlight a fractured galaxy where political bureaucracy and petty mistrust sabotage survival. The New Republic is paralyzed by division just as the vanguard of the apocalypse breaches the galactic rim. Even the Jedi Order, traditionally a beacon of infallible guidance, fractures under the weight of Luke Skywalker's leadership. Rogue elements within the Order begin to question the old ways, creating a palpable psychological tension that rivals the external threat. 

Stackpole’s authorial style shines brightest in his military-scifi sensibilities and character-driven pacing. Known for his tactical precision—honed through his acclaimed X-Wing novels—Stackpole balances grand space opera stakes with visceral, boots-on-the-ground infantry grit. He treats starfighter combat and planetary defense not as dazzling light shows, but as desperate, physics-bound attrition. Through the harrowing retreat led by Leia Organa Solo and Gavin Darklighter, the narrative captures the profound tragedy of displacement and the grueling reality of asymmetric warfare. 

Simultaneously, the personal stakes cut agonizingly deep. Characters like Mara Jade, battling the ravages of her mysterious illness, alongside the next generation of Jedi—Anakin, Jacen, and the steadfast Corran Horn—are pushed past their breaking points. Stackpole refuses to grant his heroes easy victories. Every skirmish carries a heavy toll, reinforcing the grim reality that the old rules of heroism no longer apply in this brave, brutal new world.

Dark Tide I: Onslaught is a masterclass in raising the stakes. It challenges both the characters and the readers to rethink everything they thought they knew about the Star Wars universe. Bold, relentless, and deeply atmospheric, Stackpole’s contribution remains an essential crucible for a generation of heroes forced to forge their legacy in the dark.

# Sinister Magic (Death Before Dragons, #1)

**Author:** Lindsay Buroker  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

Step into a world where modern grit collides with mythical majesty in Lindsay Buroker’s Sinister Magic, the explosive opening salvo of the Death Before Dragons series. Buroker, a master of self-publishing world-building and snappy character-driven fantasy, invites readers into an urban fantasy landscape that feels refreshingly grounded yet wildly imaginative. Rather than relying on sweeping, overly solemn epics, the author champions a style defined by relentless pacing, dry wit, and an unapologetically fierce protagonist who refuses to be pigeonholed.

At the heart of the novel is Val Thorvald, a half-elven assassin who dispenses permanent justice to magical threats. Buroker crafts Val not as an untouchable paragon of virtue, but as a pragmatic, battle-scarred professional equipped with a magical sword named Chopper and a telepathic tiger possessing a personality large enough to fill a room. This dynamic duo anchors the narrative's central theme: autonomy in the face of insurmountable power. Val’s entire existence is defined by her independence. When the magical community, shadowy government agents, and a terrifyingly arrogant dragon threaten to strip that freedom away, the stakes become intensely personal. 

Buroker’s stylistic signature is on full display here. Her prose is punchy, conversational, and laced with self-deprecating humor that prevents the darker, more violent elements of the plot from ever feeling oppressive. Instead of brooding angst, Val approaches existential peril with sarcastic quips and tactical pragmatism. The dialogue snaps with rapid-fire banter, particularly in Val's interactions with her telepathic feline companion and the domineering dragon who forces his way into her life. This balance of high-stakes danger and lighthearted levity is difficult to master, yet Buroker executes it with effortless charm.

The world-building seamlessly integrates classic fantasy tropes—elves, vampires, zombies, ogres, and dragons—into a contemporary setting without bogging the reader down in heavy exposition. The introduction of dragons as a looming, god-like threat shifts the narrative gears from a standard monster-of-the-week procedural into a high-tension game of survival and political maneuvering. Val must navigate shifting alliances and mysteries, starting with her boss's enigmatic illness and culminating in a battle of wills against a creature capable of rewriting reality itself.

Sinister Magic is a masterclass in urban fantasy entertainment. It delivers crackling action, a fiercely relatable heroine, and a vibrant supporting cast that practically leaps off the page. For readers seeking an addictive page-turner that pairs high-octane magic with a hearty dose of attitude, Buroker’s latest adventure is an absolute must-read.

# Emergence

**Author:** David R. Palmer  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

David R. Palmer’s *Emergence* stands as a tour de force of speculative fiction, a brilliantly executed narrative that bridges the gap between hard science fiction and an intimate, deeply emotional coming-of-age story. Originally serialized in the pages of *Analog Science Fiction* before its full publication in the 1980s and reissued for modern audiences, Palmer’s novel remains a razor-sharp exploration of human evolution, resilience, and identity. 

Set against the grim backdrop of a global apocalypse, the novel introduces us to Candace "Candy" Smith-Green, an eleven-year-old prodigy who is simultaneously navigating the awkward precipice of childhood and a radical biological transformation. Humanity, as Candy knows it, has been brutally wiped out by a man-made plague designed to trigger a genetic leap. Those who survive are *Homo sapiens novus*—faster, smarter, stronger, and possessing telepathic abilities. Candy is one of these new people, but she is entirely alone, isolated in a devastated world with only her pet African green monkey, Terry, for company. 

The primary theme of *Emergence* is the painful, yet triumphant, birth of a new species. Palmer examines evolution not as a slow, unnoticeable creep over millennia, but as a violent, cataclysmic birth. The old world must die so the new one can take root. Yet, beneath the high-concept science fiction, the novel is fundamentally about the triumph of the human spirit—or, more accurately, the ultra-human spirit. Candy’s journey from a terrified, grieving child to a fiercely independent, resourceful survivor underscores the theme of adaptability. Evolution, Palmer suggests, is as much about psychological fortitude and intellectual curiosity as it is about physical mutation.

Palmer’s style is uniquely electrifying, defined by a distinct narrative voice that immediately captivates the reader. The novel is written entirely from Candy’s first-person perspective, utilizing a breathless, highly idiosyncratic stream-of-consciousness. Candy is a polymathic genius with a penchant for vocabulary, classic literature, and pop culture references, yet she remains endearingly vulnerable. This stylistic choice creates an intense intimacy; we are trapped inside Candy’s brilliant, racing mind, sharing her terror, her dry wit, and her relentless determination. 

The author’s prose crackles with kinetic energy. Palmer masterfully balances moments of stark, harrowing survivalism with laugh-out-loud humor and profound philosophical musings. Whether Candy is meticulously cataloging supplies in a deserted luxury hotel or dodging ruthless bands of "Old Human" marauders who view the new children as abominations, the pacing never falters. The action sequences are visceral and tightly wound, showcasing Palmer’s background in crafting suspenseful thrillers. 

Furthermore, Palmer does not shy away from the darker implications of his premise. The extinction event is handled with unflinching realism, and the prejudice faced by the *novus* generation serves as a potent allegory for bigotry, xenophobia, and the fear of the unknown. Yet, the overriding tone is one of infectious optimism. Candy’s unyielding zest for life transforms a bleak post-apocalyptic landscape into a playground of endless discovery.

*Emergence* is a rare gem in the science fiction canon—a book that challenges the intellect while capturing the heart. David R. Palmer created a protagonist so vivid, resilient, and utterly unforgettable that she leaps off the page and demands your full attention. For readers seeking a fast-paced adventure driven by a fiercely original voice and deep thematic resonance, this novel is an absolute must-read. It is a stunning reminder that even when the world ends, the human capacity to learn, adapt, and love refuses to go quietly.

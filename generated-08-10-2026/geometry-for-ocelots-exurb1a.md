# Geometry for Ocelots

**Author:** Exurb1a  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

Exurb1a has long established himself as the existential jester of the digital age, blending cosmic nihilism with deeply human warmth across his popular YouTube channel and speculative fiction. In his 2021 novel, Geometry for Ocelots, he distills his signature philosophical anxieties into a sprawling, melancholic, yet fiercely funny narrative set at the absolute twilight of civilization. This is not merely science fiction; it is a conceptual autopsy of humanity dressed up in the fur and feathers of a post-biological utopia.

At the heart of Geometry for Ocelots lies a profound exploration of knowledge, entropy, and the limits of meaning. Set at the end of history, humanity has transcended its biological limitations, trading flesh for animal avatars while bathing in a perpetual haze of holy technology and pharmacological bliss. It is a society that has solved suffering only to hollow out the soul. Exurb1a uses this surreal backdrop to tackle a timeless philosophical dilemma: when everything that can be known is known, and every pleasure is instantly accessible, what remains of the human drive? The novel brilliantly dissects how societies construct comforting myths to mask their foundational sins, trading authentic existence for a sanitized, gilded cage. 

The thematic weight of the book is anchored by its central conflict, a deeply personal yet cosmically scaled sibling rivalry between a holy empress and an alcoholic university dean. As dwindling galactic resources threaten their gilded paradise, these two monarch siblings wage a philosophical and political war over the future of the species. One champions spiritual salvation, while the other clings to technological nirvana. Through their ideological clash, Exurb1a exposes the tragic hubris of humanity. Both leaders are utterly convinced they hold the master key to survival, and both are devastatingly, hilariously wrong. When an ancient prophet unexpectedly manifests as a deeply troubled young girl, the fragile peace shatters, forcing a reckoning that neither faith nor science can adequately prepare them for.

Exurb1a’s literary style is instantly recognizable to his followers and intoxicating to newcomers. He writes with a frantic, caffeinated brilliance that balances hard-nosed philosophical inquiry with absurdist dark comedy. His prose moves at breakneck speed, effortlessly pivoting from poetic meditations on the vast, uncaring silence of the universe to crude, laugh-out-loud banter between cynical academics and divine monarchs. He possesses a rare talent for making complex existential dread feel intimate and urgent, stripping away the academic dryness of philosophy and rendering it as a visceral, bleeding experience. 

Yet, beneath the glittering wit and dazzling world-building lies a quiet, aching sincerity. Exurb1a is fundamentally concerned with why we bother at all in a universe that appears utterly indifferent to our existence. Geometry for Ocelots is a brilliant, dizzying trip through the mechanics of belief, authority, and the desperate ways we try to matter before the lights go out for good. It is a striking, unforgettable read that lingers long after the final page, leaving you questioning the very fabric of your own reality and the tech-fueled paradises we are currently building for ourselves.

# The Clairvoyant Countess (Madame Karitska, #1)

**Author:** Dorothy Gilman  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1986

**The Clairvoyant Countess: A Masterclass in Mystery, Elegance, and the Unseen**

Dorothy Gilman’s *The Clairvoyant Countess*—the first in the Madame Karitska series—is a jewel of mid-century detective fiction, where psychic intuition meets hard-boiled realism in a dazzling fusion of wit, suspense, and old-world sophistication. Gilman, the queen of the Mrs. Pollifax spy novels, crafts here a protagonist as compelling as she is enigmatic: Madame Karitska, a woman whose gift for seeing the unseen is as much a curse as it is a blessing. With her sharp tongue, razor-sharp instincts, and a past as layered as a Russian doll, Karitska is no ordinary sleuth—she’s a force of nature, a woman who navigates the murky underbelly of Parisian society with the grace of a ballerina and the cunning of a fox.

At its core, *The Clairvoyant Countess* is a story about perception—both the literal and the metaphorical. Karitska’s clairvoyant abilities allow her to glimpse truths hidden from the rest of the world, but they also make her a target. When she stumbles into a case involving murder, blackmail, and the shadowy dealings of the elite, she finds herself entangled in a web of danger that tests her resolve. Gilman deftly balances the supernatural with the mundane, ensuring that Karitska’s psychic gifts are never the sole driver of the plot. Instead, they serve as a lens through which the human drama unfolds—revealing hypocrisy, greed, and the fragile illusions that govern power.

What elevates this novel beyond mere escapism is Gilman’s prose, which crackles with the effervescence of a well-shaken cocktail. Her descriptions are lush yet precise, painting Paris as a character in its own right—glamorous, decadent, and seething with secrets. The dialogue is razor-sharp, filled with the kind of banter that makes you laugh out loud one moment and hold your breath the next. Gilman has a knack for creating dialogue that feels both authentic and effortlessly stylish, whether Karitska is clashing with a pompous aristocrat or trading barbs with the no-nonsense Detective-Lieutenant Pruden. Their dynamic is electric, a perfect blend of professional respect and mutual irritation that keeps the tension simmering.

Yet what truly sets *The Clairvoyant Countess* apart is its themes. Gilman doesn’t shy away from exploring the darker side of human nature, but she does so with a touch of irony and a wink to the reader. There’s a subtext here about the dangers of obsession, the fragility of reputation, and the way power corrupts—even those who believe themselves above such things. Karitska, though extraordinary, is not invincible. She’s flawed, vulnerable, and deeply human, which makes her victories all the more satisfying. Gilman understands that the best mysteries aren’t just about solving crimes; they’re about understanding the people behind them.

The novel’s pacing is another of its strengths. Gilman knows how to build suspense without resorting to melodrama, weaving in red herrings and misdirections that keep the reader guessing. The reveal of the murderer is as satisfying as it is inevitable, a testament to Gilman’s skill in crafting a plot that feels both intricate and inevitable. And while the supernatural elements are intriguing, they never overshadow the human story. In fact, they enhance it, adding layers of intrigue that make the world feel richer and more alive.

For fans of classic detective fiction, *The Clairvoyant Countess* is a must-read. It’s a love letter to the golden age of mystery, but with a modern edge—one that feels fresh while paying homage to the greats. Gilman’s writing is as polished as a diamond, her characters as vivid as a painting, and her storytelling as gripping as a thriller. Whether you’re drawn to the allure of the occult, the charm of a sharp-witted heroine, or the timeless appeal of a well-crafted mystery, this novel delivers in spades.

In the end, *The Clairvoyant Countess* is more than just a book—it’s an experience. It’s a chance to step into a world where the line between reality and the supernatural blurs, where every shadow hides a secret, and where the most dangerous truths are the ones we can’t see. And with Dorothy Gilman at the helm, you know you’re in for a ride that’s as delightful as it is unforgettable.

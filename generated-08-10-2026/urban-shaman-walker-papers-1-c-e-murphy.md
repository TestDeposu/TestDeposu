# Urban Shaman (Walker Papers, #1)

**Author:** C.E. Murphy  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

Urban Shaman by C.E. Murphy strips away the glittering, immaculate veneer usually associated with New York City and replaces it with something much older, grittier, and infinitely more dangerous. In this explosive opening to the Walker Papers series, Murphy delivers a compelling masterclass in modern urban fantasy, anchoring her supernatural elements firmly in the sweat, pavement, and desperation of the five boroughs. 

At its core, the novel explores the theme of duality and the burden of heritage. The narrative follows ex-cop Danny Hendrickson and his partner, Ellen. On the surface, they are standard-issue private investigators wading through the city's underbelly to pay the rent. But beneath that mundane exterior lies a profound mythological truth: Danny is the son of a faun, and Ellen is a storm-seer. Murphy uses this pairing to examine how individuals navigate worlds they never asked to inherit. Danny and Ellen are not glamorous chosen ones destined for epic thrones; they are blue-collar magic users, punching the clock, taking the hits, and trying to save whoever they can before the next crisis hits. The city itself becomes a living character, a chaotic metropolis where ancient pantheons and supernatural predators hide in plain sight among muggers, corporate corruption, and subway tunnels.

Murphy’s authorial style is brisk, sharp, and unapologetically atmospheric. Her prose moves with the urgency of a police procedural crossed with a dark fairy tale. She avoids the trap of excessive, purple exposition, opting instead for a punchy, grounded voice that immediately draws the reader into Danny’s world. The dialogue snaps with world-weary humor and authenticity, grounding the more fantastical elements in a gritty reality. When Murphy describes a storm brewing under Ellen’s control or the wild, untamed nature of Danny’s faun heritage, she does so with a visceral clarity that makes the magic feel heavy, dangerous, and real. 

Urban Shaman succeeds because it treats the extraordinary as just another day at the office. It is a striking, fast-paced introduction to a world where magic doesn't save you from the daily grind—it just makes the stakes a whole lot higher. For readers who love their fantasy laced with cynicism, heart, and hard-boiled detective work, this book is an absolute must-read.

# Darkness Dawns (Immortal Guardians, #1)

**Author:** Dianne Duvall  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

Darkness Dawns, the electrifying debut novel by New York Times bestselling author Dianne Duvall, establishes a compelling entry point into the sprawling Immortal Guardians series. Recently rereleased for a new generation of fantasy romance enthusiasts, this novel successfully blends high-stakes urban fantasy with the irresistible charm of contemporary romantic tropes. At its core, the book explores the transformative power of connection amidst chaos, examining how love and laughter can pierce even the oldest and deepest shadows of isolation.

Duvall centers her narrative on a classic yet brilliantly executed theme: the intersection of mortal vulnerability and immortal strength. Roland Warbrook, a centuries-old warrior hardened by endless battle and perpetual loss, represents the epitome of the brooding, world-weary hero. Having lived without hope or love for ages, Roland is a man resigned to his grim duty. Enter Sarah Bingham, a vibrant, fearless music professor whose immediate instinct is to help the gravely injured man she finds near her home. Sarah is far from a standard damsel in distress; she possesses a quiet resilience and sharp intellect that immediately command respect. 

The primary theme of Darkness Dawns revolves around rebirth and emotional awakening. Roland’s physical healing mirrors his emotional resurrection. As Sarah tends to him, her courage, warmth, and unexpected humor breathe life back into a soul that had long since given up on joy. Duvall uses this dynamic to craft a vibrant "grumpy-sunshine" dynamic that grounds the fantastical elements in genuine, relatable human emotion. The contrast between Roland’s dark, dangerous world and Sarah’s bright, grounded existence creates a magnetic tension that drives the story forward from the very first chapter.

Stylistically, Duvall excels in pacing and tonal balance. Urban fantasy often runs the risk of getting bogged down in gritty despair or overly complex lore, but Darkness Dawns avoids this pitfall effortlessly. The author infuses her high-octane plot—which pitches Sarah headfirst into a secret war between psychotic vampires and the elite immortals who hunt them—with a refreshing dose of wit and levity. The dialogue snaps with clever banter, and the interactions between Roland, Sarah, and the supporting cast establish a warm, welcoming "found family" atmosphere. Readers will find themselves laughing out loud at the playful teasing among the immortals, even as the narrative ratchets up the suspense with relentless, pulse-pounding action sequences.

Furthermore, Duvall’s prose is accessible and immersive, making the transition between the mundane world of academia and the hidden supernatural underground seamless. She handles world-building with a deft touch, introducing the mythology of the Immortal Guardians organically through the progression of the plot rather than through heavy-handed exposition. Sarah’s perspective as an outsider allows the reader to discover the rules of this dangerous new world alongside her, heightening the emotional stakes and the sense of discovery.

Ultimately, Darkness Dawns is a triumph of romantic fantasy that delivers on every front. It offers a masterclass in how to construct a fast-paced thriller while keeping the emotional core of the romance intact. For readers who crave smart, fiercely independent heroines, powerful immortal protectors, and a rich, character-driven found family that loves to tease just as much as they love to fight, Duvall’s novel is an absolute must-read. It proves that even in the dead of night, a single spark of warmth can ignite a dawn worth fighting for.

# Dark Gold (Dark, #3)

**Author:** Christine Feehan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Step into the shadowy, blood-pulsing realm of #1 New York Times bestselling author Christine Feehan, and you will find a world where ancient myth bleeds seamlessly into modern danger. Dark Gold, the third installment in her iconic Dark series, delivers precisely the kind of intoxicating, high-stakes paranormal romance that has earned the author a fiercely loyal global following. Feehan does not merely write romance; she constructs a sprawling, gothic universe of primal instincts, tortured immortality, and fierce, all-consuming devotion.

At the beating heart of Dark Gold is a timeless, compelling theme: the eternal struggle between shadow and light, and the redemptive power of love to heal a fractured soul. Feehan’s protagonists are rarely ordinary. Here, we meet Aidan Savage, an ageless Carpathian male—a master of the darkness who has walked the earth for centuries, slowly losing his ability to perceive color, emotion, and light. He is a creature on the brink of despair, searching desperately through eternity for his destined lifemate, the light to his endless night. 

Enter Alexandria Houton, a fiercely protective mortal woman who will sacrifice anything, even her own life, to keep her orphaned little brother safe. When Alex and her brother cross paths with unspeakable evil in the swirling, atmospheric mists of San Francisco, their fate seems sealed. Out of the shadows swoops Aidan, a golden being of terrifying power and mystery. In this gripping dynamic, Feehan explores the delicious tension between fear and desire. Is Aidan a miracle sent to save them, or a monster more dangerous than the evil they are fleeing? Is his unearthly seduction her ultimate salvation, or an irreversible sin? 

Feehan’s literary style is unmistakably lush, sensory, and unapologetically intense. She weaves a cinematic tapestry, utilizing the moody, fog-shrouded backdrop of San Francisco to amplify the psychological and emotional claustrophobia of the plot. Her prose is heavy with atmosphere, dripping with gothic sensibilities where danger lurks behind every corner and passion burns with a terrifying, liquid heat. She excels at writing alpha heroes who are ruthless, primal, and protective to a fault, yet utterly undone by the vulnerability of the women who capture their ancient hearts. 

Dark Gold is not a gentle stroll through romance; it is a wild, tempestuous ride into the abyss, balanced entirely by the blinding brilliance of a hard-won emotional dawn. Feehan challenges her characters to surrender their deepest fears, proving that true strength lies in vulnerability and trust. For fans of dark fantasy, brooding immortals, and heroines with unbreakable backbones, this novel remains a glittering gem in the crown of paranormal romance.

# Jedi Under Siege (Star Wars: Young Jedi Knights, #6)

**Author:** Kevin J. Anderson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1994

Step into a galaxy teetering on the razor's edge of renewal and ruin with Jedi Under Siege, the sixth thrilling installment in Kevin J. Anderson’s beloved Young Jedi Knights series. Published in 1994, during the golden age of the Star Wars Expanded Universe, this novel captures the high-stakes, breathless pacing that defined 1990s franchise fiction. Anderson, a veteran of the Star Wars literary universe, once again proves his mastery over the sandbox created by George Lucas, delivering a narrative that expertly bridges intimate character growth with apocalyptic galactic threats.

At its core, the main theme of Jedi Under Siege is the heavy burden of legacy and the perilous cost of absolute power. As Luke Skywalker lays the foundational stones for his new Jedi Academy—a hopeful endeavor aimed at resurrecting an order long lost to history—the universe violently reminds him that the ghosts of the Empire are not easily put to rest. Anderson uses this backdrop to explore how the next generation, embodied by the Jedi twins, must rise to meet a universe shifting beneath their feet. Power in this novel is never portrayed as a simple tool for victory; rather, it is a corrupting, earth-shattering force, best exemplified by the book's terrifying centerpiece: the Sun Crusher. 

Anderson’s writing style is punchy, cinematic, and unashamedly pulp-adventure at heart. He wastes no time on superfluous exposition, immediately plunging the reader into the suffocating, dark depths of the Kessel spice mines where Han Solo and Chewbacca find themselves enslaved. The narrative moves at a breakneck speed, splitting its focus between high-octane prison breaks and esoteric Jedi philosophy, keeping the tension perpetually dialed to maximum. When Han and Chewie make their desperate escape toward a secret imperial research facility hidden among a cluster of black holes, Anderson ratchets up the claustrophobia and dread, proving he excels at writing environmental peril. 

What makes Jedi Under Siege particularly striking is its clever subversion of classic tropes. By putting Han Solo at the controls of the Sun Crusher—a devastating superweapon capable of destroying entire solar systems—Anderson creates a delicious dramatic irony that forces Luke Skywalker into an impossible conflict. It is a story about loyalty tested to its absolute limits, where saving the galaxy might mean destroying your oldest friend. 

While targeted at younger readers, the novel never talks down to its audience, offering genuine thrills and emotional stakes that will satisfy longtime enthusiasts of the lore. Packed with classic Expanded Universe world-building, this edition also includes an invaluable bonus section featuring a primer and exclusive excerpts, making it a nostalgic time capsule for a bygone era of space opera. Jedi Under Siege is a brisk, action-packed triumph that reminds us why the galaxy far, far away continues to capture our imaginations.

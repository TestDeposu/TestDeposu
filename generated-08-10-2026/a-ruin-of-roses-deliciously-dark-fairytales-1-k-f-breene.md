# A Ruin of Roses (Deliciously Dark Fairytales, #1)

**Author:** K.F. Breene  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

If you thought you knew the tale as old as time, K.F. Breene is here to completely shatter that illusion in the most deliciously wicked way possible. A Ruin of Roses, the first installment in the *Deliciously Dark Fairytales* series, takes the classic blueprint of *Beauty and the Beast* and drags it kicking, screaming, and laughing into the shadows of adult fantasy romance. 

At its core, the novel explores the timeless theme of sacrifice and transformation, but through a lens that is unapologetically modern, gritty, and scorching hot. The narrative follows a courageous heroine who plunges directly into the heart of the Forbidden Forest—home to the dreaded Dragon Prince, a terrifying beast haunting both the realm and her worst nightmares. Yet, as she transitions from uninvited intruder to captive within his domain, the boundaries between monster and savior begin to blur. Breene masterfully subverts the traditional dynamic, plunging her characters into a psychological and physical chess match where power, vulnerability, and dark attraction collide. 

What truly sets A Ruin of Roses apart from a saturated market of fairytale retellings is Breene’s signature stylistic alchemy. She weaves a delicate balance between high-stakes peril and sharp, cynical humor that keeps the pages turning at a breakneck speed. The heroine is delightfully flawed, possessing a mouthy resilience and a self-deprecating wit that makes her instantly endearing. Far from being a passive damsel, she meets the brooding intensity of the last shifter with equal parts sass and steel. Meanwhile, the rich tapestry of secondary characters injects layers of depth, world-building richness, and comedic relief into an otherwise dark landscape. 

Reviewers and bestselling authors alike have hailed the book as an absolute cure for reading slumps, and it is easy to see why. The pacing is relentless, the world-building is immersive, and the romantic tension is nothing short of torrid. Breene does not just write a romance; she crafts an adrenaline-fueled experience that commands your complete attention from the first chapter to the last. 

For readers who crave a fantasy romance where the stakes are high, the humor is razor-sharp, and the heat level is thoroughly blazing, A Ruin of Roses is an unputdownable triumph. Step into the Forbidden Forest—just don't expect to escape unchanged.

# Chosen (The Warrior Chronicles, #1)

**Author:** K.F. Breene  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**Blood, Betrayal, and a Wolf’s Vengeance: *Chosen* by K.F. Breene Delivers Raw, Relentless Urban Fantasy**

Karma is a slow, cruel thing—especially when it’s the only thing standing between you and justice. In *Chosen*, the first installment of K.F. Breene’s *Warrior Chronicles*, Briar, a wolf shifter with a dagger in her boot and blades in her bra, has had enough. After her entire pack was slaughtered by Dominic—a ruthless Alpha with a reputation for cruelty—she arrives in Rouen with one mission: kill him. But vengeance isn’t as simple as a well-placed strike. Dominic’s pack is a fortress of muscle-bound shifters, cunning witches, and a vampire so old he’s practically a relic. To get close enough to deliver the final blow, Briar must infiltrate their ranks—and that means becoming one of them.

What follows is a masterclass in urban fantasy’s darkest, most visceral brand of storytelling. Breene doesn’t just write a revenge tale; she crafts a character study of a woman who’s been broken but refuses to stay that way. Briar is a force of nature—sarcastic, lethal, and utterly unapologetic. Her voice crackles with the kind of snark that makes you laugh even as you wince at the brutality she’s endured. She’s not a damsel in distress; she’s a wolf who’s already tasted blood and knows she’ll drink more before this is over. And yet, for all her strength, she’s not invincible. The weight of her past haunts her, and the pack she’s forced to join is a den of wolves, vampires, and witches with their own agendas—some of whom may be more dangerous than Dominic himself.

The real strength of *Chosen* lies in its ability to balance brutal realism with sharp, dark humor. Breene’s prose is lean, efficient, and dripping with attitude. The world she’s built is gritty, immersive, and teeming with supernatural factions that feel lived-in and dangerous. The vampires are as nosy and dramatic as you’d expect, the witches are fashion-obsessed and deadly, and the shifters? Well, let’s just say they’re not here to make friends. The tension is palpable, the stakes are sky-high, and the betrayals hit harder than a wolf’s bite.

But what truly sets *Chosen* apart is its unflinching exploration of revenge and its cost. Briar isn’t just hunting Dominic for justice—she’s hunting him because she’s been hollowed out by grief, and the only thing that makes her feel alive again is the prospect of bloodshed. There’s a chilling honesty to her motivations, a recognition that her quest might consume her entirely. Breene doesn’t shy away from the moral gray areas of her story. Is Briar a hero or a monster? The line is blurred, and that’s the point. The world she’s entered is just as corrupt as the one she left, and every choice she makes could either bring her closer to her goal or doom her to become the very thing she’s fighting against.

For fans of urban fantasy that’s equal parts gritty and glamorous, *Chosen* is a breath of fresh air. It’s got the snarky, sassy heroines of Charlaine Harris’ Sookie Stackhouse or Patricia Briggs’ Mercy Thompson, but with the raw, unfiltered edge of a story that doesn’t sugarcoat its violence or its themes. If you love a heroine who’s as likely to crack a joke as she is to draw her blade, or a world where every shadow hides a threat, then this is your kind of book.

K.F. Breene’s writing is sharp, her characters are unforgettable, and her world is a dark, intoxicating place where revenge is a drug and survival is a daily gamble. *Chosen* doesn’t just deliver on its promise of a fierce, vengeful heroine—it delivers a story that’s as satisfying as it is brutal. If you’re looking for urban fantasy that’s equal parts hilarious and heart-stopping, this is the book for you.

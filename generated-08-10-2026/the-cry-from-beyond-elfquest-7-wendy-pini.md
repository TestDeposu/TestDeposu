# The Cry from Beyond (ElfQuest #7)

**Author:** Wendy Pini  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

For more than four decades, Wendy and Richard Pini’s creation has stood as a towering monument in independent fantasy comics. With The Cry from Beyond, the seventh and final volume in the definitive Complete ElfQuest series, that grand narrative reaches its profound and bittersweet conclusion. Collecting the entirety of the Final Quest story arc, this volume confronts the ultimate paradox of survival: what happens when a wild, untamed people finally find the safe haven they have fought for across millennia?

At the beating heart of this final chapter is a gripping thematic exploration of complacency, identity, and the heavy cost of paradise. For generations, Chief Cutter and his fierce Wolfriders have battled the raw savagery of the World of Two Moons, dodging the escalating threats of a rapidly expanding human civilization and the unforgiving wrath of nature itself. They sought sanctuary, and in the legendary Palace of the High Ones, they seemingly found it. Yet, Wendy Pini brilliantly subverts the traditional happily-ever-after trope. Comfort, it turns out, is the most dangerous poison of all. The seductive, stagnant magic of the Palace threatens to strip away the very essence of the feral elves, eroding their culture, their instincts, and their vital connection to the wild. Cutter realizes a terrifying truth: to save his tribe from fading into comfortable obsolescence, they must embark upon the most perilous and emotionally taxing journey of their lives—the Final Quest. 

Pini’s distinctive artistic and storytelling style shines with the mastery of a veteran creator who has lived alongside these characters for a lifetime. Her visual language remains remarkably expressive, defined by sweeping, dynamic lines and a cinematic sense of movement that captures both the tender intimacy of quiet character moments and the visceral tension of high-stakes fantasy drama. Where other long-running series might buckle under the weight of their own mythology, Pini navigates the complex web of legacy, love, and sacrifice with an assured, delicate touch. The pacing expertly balances sweeping world-building with deeply personal character arcs, ensuring that every triumph and every tragedy resonates with profound emotional weight. 

The Cry from Beyond is not merely an ending; it is a masterclass in how to conclude a sprawling epic. It honors the loyal readership that has followed Cutter and Leetah through decades of publication while offering a satisfying, tear-inducing closure that lingers long after the final page is turned. For longtime fans, this volume is an absolute essential that delivers the emotional payoff they have craved for years. For newcomers willing to dive into the rich tapestry of the World of Two Moons, it stands as a breathtaking testament to the enduring power of visionary, creator-owned fantasy. Wendy Pini has crafted a legendary farewell that cements ElfQuest's well-deserved place in comic book history.

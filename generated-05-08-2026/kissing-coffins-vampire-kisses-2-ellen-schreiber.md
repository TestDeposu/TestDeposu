# Kissing Coffins (Vampire Kisses, #2)

**Author:** Ellen Schreiber  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Dive into the captivating world of goth teen fiction with Ellen Schreiber's gripping sequel, Kissing Coffins. The second installment in the Vampire Kisses series, this dark and suspenseful tale will take you on a thrilling ride, filled with mystery, romance, and eerie undertones.

Raven, the protagonist, is back with a vengeance, navigating the treacherous landscape of her relationships and her own desires. Her connection with Alexander, a mysterious and captivating figure, is put to the test as he disappears, leaving Raven on a perilous quest to find him. As she delves deeper into the unknown, she discovers secrets and terrors that will challenge her very essence.

Schreiber's masterful storytelling and atmospheric setting create a world that's both captivating and unsettling. Her writing style is reminiscent of the classics, weaving together complex characters, intricate plotlines, and themes that will leave you sleeping with the lights on. The author's use of imagery is vivid and evocative, transporting readers to the haunted, gothic landscapes of Dullsville.

One of the standout aspects of Kissing Coffins is its exploration of identity and self-discovery. Raven's struggles to reconcile her darkness with her newfound love for Alexander serve as a poignant reminder of the complexities of adolescence. Schreiber expertly navigates the turmoil of teenage emotions, crafting a narrative that's both relatable and authentic.

As we follow Raven's journey, we're drawn into a world of myth and legend, where the lines between reality and fantasy blur. The author's use of folklore and supernatural elements adds a layer of depth and richness to the story, making Kissing Coffins a must-read for fans of paranormal fiction. Whether you're an avid fan of vampire lore or simply drawn to the darker side of life, this book is sure to captivate and enthrall.

Through Schreiber's capable hands, the vampire genre is reimagined and recontextualized in a way that feels fresh and innovative. The author's style is characterized by its accessibility and fluidity, making Kissing Coffins an easy and engaging read.

If you're looking for a book that will keep you up late at night, eager for more, then Kissing Coffins is the perfect choice. It's a story that will linger in your mind long after the final page is turned, its characters and themes resonating with you like a haunting melody.

In conclusion, Kissing Coffins is a mesmerizing and captivating tale that's sure to leave readers enthralled. With its gothic atmosphere, intricate plot, and relatable characters, this book is an absolute must-read for fans of paranormal fiction.

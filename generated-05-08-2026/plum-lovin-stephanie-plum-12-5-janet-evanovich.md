# Plum Lovin' (Stephanie Plum, #12.5)

**Author:** Janet Evanovich  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

Janet Evanovich’s *Plum Lovin’*, a high-octane 12.5 installment in the beloved Stephanie Plum series, delivers everything fans crave: chaotic charm, high-stakes absurdity, and a heavy dose of Jersey-style romance. Clocking in as a quick-witted holiday-themed romp, this novella proves that big trouble often comes in compact, explosively funny packages.

At the heart of *Plum Lovin’* is the recurring theme of unorthodox matchmaking and the chaotic intersection of bounty hunting and romance. Stephanie Plum, the endearingly clumsy Trenton, New Jersey bounty hunter, finds her life upended once again when the enigmatic, cat-loving mystic Diesel breezes into her apartment uninvited. Diesel ropes Stephanie into tracking down Annie Hart, a fugitive "relationship expert" accused of armed robbery and assault. The catch? To get Annie, Stephanie must play Cupid for Annie’s most hopeless, eccentric clients. 

Evanovich’s signature style shines brightly here. Blending traditional mystery elements with screwball comedy, the author crafts a narrative fueled by snappy dialogue, rapid-fire pacing, and escalating disasters. Stephanie remains the ultimate relatable anti-heroine—vulnerable yet resilient, perpetually stumbling into danger while wearing cheap heels. Her chaotic dynamic with Diesel adds a fresh, supernatural-adjacent spark, while the perpetual background tension involving her two main men, Vice Cop Joe Morelli and security mogul Ranger, keeps the romantic friction sizzling at maximum capacity. 

What makes *Plum Lovin’* stand out in the expansive Plum canon is its laser-focused execution. Without the constraints of a full-length novel, Evanovich cuts straight to the comedic beats and action set-pieces. The subplot involving the difficult matchmaking clients offers laugh-out-loud commentary on modern dating, filtered through the uniquely bizarre lens of Trenton’s working-class underworld. 

For longtime fans, this novella is a comforting, crave-able goodie that hits all the familiar notes. For newcomers, it serves as a pitch-perfect introduction to Evanovich’s unique brand of comedic crime fiction. Fast, funny, and utterly delightful, *Plum Lovin'* is a testament to Evanovich’s enduring talent for making chaos look like a walk in the park.

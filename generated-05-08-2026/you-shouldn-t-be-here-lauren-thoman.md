# You Shouldn't Be Here

**Author:** Lauren Thoman  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

**You Shouldn't Be Here** by Lauren Thoman: A Deliciously Twisted Ride into the World of Influencers and Intrigue

In the latest thriller from debut author Lauren Thoman, we are introduced to Fallon, a talented 17-year-old video game streamer who's on the cusp of making it big. When she lands a coveted spot in the mysterious reality game, **SleuthIt**, her excitement is palpable. The show promises to catapult her channel to the next level, but little does she know that her real competition begins far beyond the virtual realm.

Thoman's writing effortlessly blends the world of social media influencers with the classic whodunit tropes that fans of **Knives Out** and **The Residence** are sure to adore. Her witty banter and crisp dialogue bring the characters to life, making it easy to become invested in their struggles and motivations. As Fallon navigates the treacherous waters of the game, her world becomes a complex web of secrets, hidden agendas, and competing interests.

At its core, **You Shouldn't Be Here** is a scathing commentary on the cutthroat world of social media influencers. Thoman skillfully exposes the dark underbelly of an industry where fame and fortune are just a stream away. This modern twist on the classic game of Clue is as thrilling as it is thought-provoking, challenging readers to question the true cost of success in the digital age.

Thoman's writing style is reminiscent of authors like Lauren Beukes and Tana French, with a keen eye for detail and the ability to weave complex narratives that leave readers guessing until the very end. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, from Fallon's drive and determination to her online gaming partner Dante's enigmatic presence.

**You Shouldn't Be Here** is a deliciously twisted ride that explores the high stakes of the social media world, where one misstep can lead to catastrophic consequences. It's also a love letter to the world of online gaming, family, and friendship, showcasing the true power of connections in the digital age. Thoman's writing is as sharp as her characters' wit, making this modern thriller an absolute must-read for fans of mystery, suspense, and online gaming.

As we journey through the pages of **You Shouldn't Be Here**, we are forced to confront the harsh realities of our own world, where the pursuit of fame and fortune can lead to devastating consequences. Thoman's masterpiece is not just a thriller, but a timely commentary on the impact of technology on our lives and relationships.

**You Shouldn't Be Here** by Lauren Thoman is an absolute page-turner that will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very end. Don't miss out on this modern thriller that will change the way you think about social media, influencers, and the high stakes of our digital world.

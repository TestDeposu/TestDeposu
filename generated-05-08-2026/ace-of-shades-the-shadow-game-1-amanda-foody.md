# Ace of Shades (The Shadow Game, #1)

**Author:** Amanda Foody  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

Welcome to New Reynes, a dazzling, neon-lit labyrinth where virtue is a liability and vice is the only currency that truly matters. In her 2018 YA fantasy triumph Ace of Shades, Amanda Foody crafts a deliciously wicked underworld that feels like a high-stakes poker game played with loaded dice. Known for her later New York Times bestselling success with All of Us Villains, Foody here establishes her masterclass in world-building, blending the intoxicating glamour of a 1920s jazz-age paradise with the gritty, unforgiving brutality of a mob-run dystopia. 

At the pulsating heart of the novel is its central theme: the corruptive nature of survival and the dangerous illusions of morality. Foody explores this through a brilliantly executed clash of worlds, personified by her unlikely protagonists. On one side stands Enne Salta, a prudish, rigorously educated darling of a finishing school who arrives in the City of Sin armed only with propriety and desperation to find her missing mother. On the other is Levi Glaiyser, a charismatic street lord, card sharp, and con man whose very existence depends on charm, deceit, and staying one step ahead of the grave. 

Foody’s authorial style shines brightest in the rapid-fire chemistry between Enne and Levi. As the narrative drags these polar opposites from opulent, velvet-draped casinos into the blood-soaked alleyways controlled by ruthless Mafia kingpins, the author subverts classic tropes with razor-sharp wit and psychological depth. Enne is no helpless damsel in distress; her descent into moral ambiguity is both terrifying and exhilarating to witness. She learns quickly that in New Reynes, holding onto your innocence is a luxury no one can afford. Foody does not merely place her characters in danger; she forces them to question the very foundations of who they are, stripping away their armor until only raw ambition and desperation remain.

The pacing is relentless, driven by snappy dialogue, intricate schemes, and an atmosphere thick with palpable tension. Foody paints New Reynes not just as a backdrop, but as a living, breathing antagonist that preys on the weak and corrupts the pure. Every shadow hides a dagger, every smile conceals a betrayal, and the rules of the game are rewritten the moment you think you might be winning. 

Ace of Shades is a rich, complicated, and utterly addictive plunge into the morally grey. Foody has engineered a stellar opening hand for The Shadow Game trilogy, delivering a story where thieves are philosophers, con artists hold the emotional core, and the house always wins. If you crave clever magic, high-stakes danger, and romance forged in the fires of deceit, step up to the table. Just be prepared to lose your heart to the shadows.

# All Our Yesterdays

**Author:** Cristin Terrill  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In the realm of science fiction, few authors delve as profoundly into the complexities of time and memory as Cristin Terrill in her captivating novel, All Our Yesterdays. Published in 2013, this gripping tale is set in a dystopian future, but its themes of love, loss, and redemption transcend the boundaries of genre, speaking directly to readers' experiences.

The story revolves around Marina, a young woman who finds herself at the center of a time loop, reliving the same day over and over. Each iteration brings her closer to the memories of her lost love, James, but also to the sinister forces that seek to exploit her unique gift. As Marina navigates the fragmented landscape of her past and present, Terrill masterfully weaves a narrative that is both haunting and beautiful.

One of the most striking aspects of All Our Yesterdays is its unique storytelling approach. Terrill employs a non-linear narrative structure, blurring the lines between past and present, memory and reality. Through Marina's fragmented recollections, the author crafts a sense of disorientation, mirroring the protagonist's own struggles to piece together her fragmented identity.

Terrill's writing style is both lyrical and economical, evoking a dreamlike quality that perfectly captures the time-traveling essence of the story. Her prose is characterized by a sense of urgency, propelling the reader through the narrative at a breakneck pace. This is a testament to Terrill's skillful plotting, as she expertly balances action, romance, and introspection to create a narrative that is both propulsive and emotionally resonant.

At its core, All Our Yesterdays is a novel about the power of memory and its ability to shape our understanding of ourselves and the world around us. Marina's struggles to come to terms with her past and her lost love serve as a powerful metaphor for the complexities of human experience. Through her protagonist's journey, Terrill highlights the fragility of memory and the ways in which it can be both a source of comfort and a catalyst for pain.

Throughout the novel, Terrill also explores themes of identity, love, and sacrifice. Marina's relationships with James and her other love interest, Daniel, serve as a lens through which the author examines the complexities of human connection. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, each with their own distinct voice and motivations.

While All Our Yesterdays does raise questions about the nature of time and memory, it also acknowledges the limitations of these concepts. Marina's time loop is a commentary on the cyclical nature of life, highlighting the ways in which our experiences are influenced by our past and our relationships. Terrill's portrayal of the past is characterized by a sense of nostalgia, underscoring the idea that memories can both heal and haunt us.

Overall, All Our Yesterdays is a captivating and thought-provoking novel that will appeal to fans of science fiction, romance, and young adult literature. Terrill's writing is both evocative and engaging, crafting a narrative that will keep readers on the edge of their seats.

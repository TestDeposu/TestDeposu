# This Is What Happy Looks Like (This is What Happy Looks Like, #1)

**Author:** Jennifer E. Smith  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In "This Is What Happy Looks Like" by Jennifer E. Smith, we're transported to the charming world of small-town Maine and the glamorous life of Hollywood, where two teenagers from different walks of life find love in the unlikeliest of ways. Graham Larkin, a teenage movie star, and Ellie O'Neill, a small-town girl, form a connection that transcends their vastly different realities through a series of witty and heartfelt emails.

What makes this novel truly special is the way Smith weaves together themes of identity, love, and self-discovery. Graham and Ellie's online relationship blossoms without the burden of their respective backgrounds, allowing them to be their true selves. However, when Graham decides to bring their relationship into the real world, the pressures of his fame and Ellie's desire for anonymity threaten to tear them apart.

Smith's writing is effortless and engaging, with a keen eye for detail that brings the quaint town of Henley, Maine, to life. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Graham's vulnerability and Ellie's determination making them easy to root for. The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, from Graham's overbearing publicist to Ellie's quirky best friend.

One of the standout aspects of Smith's style is her ability to balance humor and heart. The novel is full of laugh-out-loud moments, particularly in the early email exchanges between Graham and Ellie. However, as the story progresses, Smith tackles more serious themes, such as the consequences of fame and the importance of staying true to oneself.

The romance at the center of the novel is sweet and tender, with a slow-burning tension that will keep readers invested in Graham and Ellie's relationship. Smith's portrayal of first love is authentic and captivating, capturing the excitement and uncertainty that comes with falling for someone.

If you're a fan of contemporary romance, small-town charm, and Hollywood glamour, "This Is What Happy Looks Like" is a must-read. With its lighthearted tone, engaging characters, and exploration of deeper themes, this novel is sure to leave readers smiling.

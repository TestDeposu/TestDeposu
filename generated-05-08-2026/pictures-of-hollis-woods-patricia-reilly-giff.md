# Pictures of Hollis Woods

**Author:** Patricia Reilly Giff  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2002

Pictures of Hollis Woods, a Newbery Honor book by Patricia Reilly Giff, is a poignant and heartwarming tale that delves into the life of a young girl's quest for a true home and a sense of belonging. The story revolves around Hollis Woods, a twelve-year-old girl who has been in and out of numerous foster homes, struggling to find a place where she can truly be herself.

Through Hollis's journey, Giff masterfully explores the theme of belonging and the importance of having a supportive family. Hollis's experiences in various foster homes have left her with emotional scars, making it challenging for her to trust others and form meaningful relationships. However, when she is sent to live with Josie, an elderly artist, she begins to feel a sense of comfort and security that she has never experienced before.

Giff's portrayal of Hollis's character is remarkable, as she skillfully captures the girl's emotional depth and complexity. Hollis's artistic talent and her ability to express herself through pictures serve as a coping mechanism, allowing her to process her emotions and reflect on her past experiences. The author's use of Hollis's artwork as a narrative device adds a unique and creative element to the story, providing readers with a deeper understanding of the protagonist's inner world.

One of the most striking aspects of the book is Giff's exploration of the bond between Hollis and Josie. Despite their differences, the two form a strong and loving relationship, which becomes the foundation of the story. Josie's quirky and affectionate nature helps to bring out Hollis's more vulnerable side, and their interactions are filled with warmth and humor. As Josie's memory begins to fade, Hollis's determination to care for her and keep their life together is truly inspiring, highlighting the depth of their relationship.

Giff's writing style is characterized by her ability to balance lighthearted moments with more serious themes, creating a narrative that is both engaging and thought-provoking. Her use of language is simple yet effective, making the story accessible to readers of all ages. The author's passion for storytelling shines through on every page, as she weaves together a tale that is both entertaining and emotionally resonant.

In Pictures of Hollis Woods, Patricia Reilly Giff has created a story that will resonate with readers long after they finish the book. The novel's exploration of belonging, family, and the power of art to express emotions is both timely and timeless. This is a book that will leave readers satisfied, as Kirkus Reviews so aptly put it, and one that will remain in their hearts for years to come.

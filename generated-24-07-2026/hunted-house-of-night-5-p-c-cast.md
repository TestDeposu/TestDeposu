# Hunted (House of Night, #5)

**Author:** P.C. Cast  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

Hunted, the fifth installment in the House of Night series by P.C. Cast, is a captivating tale of self-discovery, friendship, and the struggle between good and evil. The story revolves around Zoey Redbird, a 16-year-old vampyre fledgling who is trying to navigate her new life at the House of Night, a boarding school for vampyres.

Cast's writing style is engaging and immersive, making it easy for readers to become invested in Zoey's journey. The author's use of vivid descriptions and witty dialogue brings the characters to life, creating a sense of realism that draws the reader in. The world-building in the series is also noteworthy, with a richly detailed mythology that adds depth to the story.

One of the main themes of Hunted is the struggle for power and control. Zoey, as a fledgling with unique gifts, is a target for those who seek to exploit her abilities. The high priestess, Neferet, is a complex character whose true intentions are shrouded in mystery, adding to the tension and suspense. As Zoey navigates the treacherous world of vampyre politics, she must also confront her own demons and learn to trust herself.

The character development in Hunted is impressive, with Zoey growing from a timid and uncertain fledgling to a confident and powerful young vampyre. The supporting characters, including her friends and allies, are equally well-developed, adding to the richness of the story.

Cast's portrayal of female empowerment is also noteworthy. Zoey and her friends are strong, independent, and determined, refusing to be swayed by the manipulations of those around them. The author's message of self-reliance and inner strength is inspiring, making Hunted a great read for young adults.

The pacing of the book is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and romance that keeps the reader engaged. The plot twists and turns, keeping the reader guessing until the very end.

Overall, Hunted is a compelling addition to the House of Night series. With its engaging characters, rich world-building, and inspiring themes, it is a must-read for fans of young adult fantasy. If you're looking for a series that will keep you up all night, turning pages and cheering on the heroine, then the House of Night series is for you.

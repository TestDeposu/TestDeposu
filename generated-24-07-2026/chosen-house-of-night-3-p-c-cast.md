# Chosen (House of Night, #3)

**Author:** P.C. Cast  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

Chosen, the third installment in P.C. Cast's House of Night series, delves deeper into the world of vampyres and the trials of its protagonist, Zoey Redbird. As Zoey navigates her new life at the House of Night, a boarding school for fledgling vampyres, she must confront the challenges of being "special" – singled out by the vampyre Goddess, Nyx. With her newfound powers and status, Zoey faces difficulties in forming genuine relationships and uncovering the truth behind the mysterious events unfolding at the House of Night.

Cast's writing style is engaging and accessible, making it easy for readers to become immersed in the world of vampyres and magic. The author's use of teenage angst and relatable issues adds depth to the narrative, allowing readers to empathize with Zoey's struggles. The blend of mythology, folklore, and fantasy elements creates a unique and captivating atmosphere, setting the series apart from other young adult vampire novels.

One of the primary themes in Chosen is the struggle for identity and self-acceptance. Zoey, as a fledgling vampyre, must come to terms with her new status and the expectations placed upon her. Her journey is marked by self-doubt, insecurity, and the quest for belonging. Cast expertly weaves this theme throughout the narrative, using Zoey's experiences to explore the complexities of adolescence and the challenges of finding one's place in the world.

The character development in Chosen is notable, particularly in the portrayal of Zoey's relationships with her friends and mentors. The dynamics between Zoey and her roommates, the Twins and Stevie Rae, are authentic and heartfelt, providing a sense of camaraderie and support. The enigmatic Neferet, high priestess of the House of Night, adds an air of mystery and intrigue, leaving readers questioning her true intentions.

The pacing of the novel is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and emotional moments. Cast's ability to craft a compelling narrative is evident, as the story unfolds with unexpected twists and turns. The author's use of cliffhangers and unresolved plot threads keeps readers invested in the series, eagerly anticipating the next installment.

In conclusion, Chosen is a captivating addition to the House of Night series, offering a unique blend of mythology, fantasy, and teenage drama. P.C. Cast's engaging writing style and well-developed characters make this novel a must-read for fans of young adult vampire fiction. With its themes of identity, self-acceptance, and the struggle for power, Chosen is sure to resonate with readers of all ages.

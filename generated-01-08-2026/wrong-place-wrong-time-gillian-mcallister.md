# Wrong Place Wrong Time

**Author:** Gillian McAllister  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**A Masterpiece of Time-Bending Thriller: "Wrong Place Wrong Time" by Gillian McAllister**

Gillian McAllister's latest novel, "Wrong Place Wrong Time", is a psychological thriller that will keep you on the edge of your seats from the very first page to the very last. This Reese's Book Club Pick is a time-bending, twisty tale of a mother's unconditional love and her desperate attempt to prevent a tragedy from unfolding in her family's life. 

The story revolves around Jen, a mother who is consumed by anxiety when her 18-year-old son, Todd, fails to return home on Halloween night. Her worst fears are confirmed when she witnesses through the window her son committing a heinous crime - stabbing a stranger. The book then takes a remarkable turn as Jen discovers she can move backward in time to the previous day, then the day before that, and eventually much further in the past. With each new morning, she is given another chance to alter the course of events that led to her son's devastating act of violence.

Throughout the novel, McAllister masterfully weaves together the narrative of a mother's love and the sacrifices she will make for her child. Jen's desperation to save her son from his fate propels the plot forward at a breakneck pace, making it impossible for readers not to become fully invested in the story. The author's writing is evocative and engaging, with each twist and turn perfectly placed to keep the tension high and the reader guessing.

One of the standout features of "Wrong Place Wrong Time" is its unique time-travel plot. McAllister skillfully explores the consequences of altering the past, raising thought-provoking questions about the nature of time, free will, and the consequences of our actions. As Jen navigates through different timelines, the possibilities for the story are endless, and the author skillfully navigates the complexities of this concept.

Gillian McAllister's writing style is characterized by her ability to create relatable characters, particularly her protagonist Jen, who is both flawed and endearing. The author's attention to detail is impressive, and the supporting cast of characters adds depth and complexity to the story. The pacing is well-balanced, with moments of intense suspense and quieter moments of introspection that allow the reader to catch their breath.

What makes "Wrong Place Wrong Time" truly exceptional is its ability to balance the thriller genre with a poignant exploration of motherhood and its many complexities. McAllister's writing is both entertaining and thought-provoking, leaving readers with a lot to think about long after the book is finished.

In conclusion, "Wrong Place Wrong Time" by Gillian McAllister is a gripping psychological thriller that will appeal to fans of time travel, suspense, and heartwarming stories of motherly love. With its unique plot, well-crafted characters, and engaging writing style, this book is sure to captivate readers and leave a lasting impression.

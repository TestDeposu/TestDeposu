# Dragonworld

**Author:** Byron Preiss  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

Dragonworld, penned by the ingenious Byron Preiss, is an enchanting tale that whisks readers away on a thrilling adventure across North America. First published as part of the treasure hunt book "The Secret: A Treasure Hunt" in 1982, this captivating narrative has been reimagined in the 2014 edition, Dragonworld.

At its core, Dragonworld is a story about the Fair People – an assortment of magical creatures, including goblins, fairies, and dragons – who fled the Old World to escape the ravages of humanity. These mystical beings brought with them twelve precious jewels, which they eventually hid away, taking the locations of these treasures to their graves. The tale is a masterful blend of fantasy, mystery, and adventure, as readers are tasked with uncovering the hidden jewels.

What sets Dragonworld apart from other books in the fantasy genre is its unique approach to storytelling. Preiss weaves a rich narrative that is both a compelling tale and a puzzle waiting to be solved. The book features twelve full-color paintings, each paired with a poetic verse, which serve as clues to the locations of the hidden treasures. This innovative style challenges readers to think creatively and use their problem-solving skills to decipher the riddles.

One of the most striking aspects of Dragonworld is its ability to transcend time. Despite being first published over three decades ago, the book remains as captivating and relevant today as it was back then. The fact that only three of the twelve treasures have been recovered since its initial publication is a testament to the book's enduring appeal and the complexity of the puzzles.

Preiss's writing style is equally impressive. His use of language is vivid and evocative, conjuring up images of a mystical world that is both familiar and yet, utterly fantastical. The way he weaves together the narrative, blending elements of fantasy and mystery, is nothing short of masterful.

If you're a fan of fantasy, adventure, or puzzle books, Dragonworld is an absolute must-read. Even if you're not typically drawn to these genres, the book's unique approach to storytelling and its ability to challenge readers make it a compelling read.

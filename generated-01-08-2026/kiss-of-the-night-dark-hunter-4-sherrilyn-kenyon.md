# Kiss of the Night (Dark-Hunter, #4)

**Author:** Sherrilyn Kenyon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In "Kiss of the Night," Sherrilyn Kenyon masterfully weaves a captivating tale of forbidden love, ancient curses, and the unwavering power of true devotion. The fourth installment in the Dark-Hunter series, this novel delves into the complexities of the human heart, testing the boundaries of loyalty, duty, and the unrelenting pursuit of happiness.

At the forefront of this epic romance is Wulf, a Viking warrior plagued by the weight of his own amnesia-inducing power. Cursed to be forgotten by all who lay eyes on him, Wulf's existence is a solitary one, devoid of meaningful connections and genuine love. His encounters are fleeting, leaving him with an unquenchable thirst for something more. It is this desperation that drives him to seek out Cassandra, the one woman immune to his powers.

As the princess of the cursed Apollite race, Cassandra is an enigma – a being both captivating and forbidden. Her kind has been the target of Wulf's sworn duty as a Dark-Hunter, making their love an impossible one. Yet, it is this very impossibility that fuels their passion, igniting a flame that burns brighter with each passing moment.

Kenyon's writing is a true testament to her mastery of the paranormal romance genre. Her world-building is meticulous, transporting readers to a realm of ancient mythology and forgotten lore. The characters she crafts are multidimensional, their motivations and desires palpable as they navigate the treacherous landscape of their world.

One of the most striking aspects of "Kiss of the Night" is its exploration of the human condition. Wulf's amnesia-inducing power serves as a poignant metaphor for the fragility of human connection. His inability to be remembered forces him to confront the impermanence of relationships, leaving him to question the very nature of love and loyalty.

Throughout the novel, Kenyon skillfully interweaves elements of Greek mythology, breathing life into the gods and goddesses of old. The meddling of the gods adds a layer of complexity to the narrative, their motivations and desires often shrouded in mystery. This air of uncertainty creates a sense of tension, propelling the story forward as Wulf and Cassandra navigate the intricate web of fate and prophecy.

Ultimately, "Kiss of the Night" is a tale of true love's transformative power. Wulf and Cassandra's bond is one that transcends the boundaries of duty, loyalty, and even the constraints of their own world. Their love becomes a beacon of hope, illuminating the darkness and guiding them toward a future filled with promise and possibility.

In conclusion, "Kiss of the Night" is a captivating addition to the Dark-Hunter series, one that will leave readers spellbound and eager for more. Kenyon's writing is a masterclass in paranormal romance, her world-building and character development unparalleled in the genre.

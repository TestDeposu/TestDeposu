# Running Out of Time (Out of Time #1)

**Author:** Margaret Peterson Haddix  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

Running Out of Time, the reimagined classic by Margaret Peterson Haddix, is a thought-provoking and suspenseful thriller that delves into the complexities of a world where nothing is as it seems. The story follows Jessie Keyser, a young girl living in the quaint village of Clifton, Indiana, in what she believes to be the year 1840. However, her life is turned upside down when her mother reveals a shocking truth - their village is, in fact, a tourist attraction, and the real year is 1996.

As Jessie navigates this new reality, she finds herself in a desperate race against time to save her friends and family from a deadly diphtheria outbreak. With the help of her mother, Jessie must escape the confines of her village and venture into the modern world, where she encounters a plethora of strange and bewildering inventions, including cars and telephones. The author's vivid descriptions of Jessie's fish-out-of-water experiences are both humorous and poignant, highlighting the vast cultural and technological disparities between the two worlds.

One of the most striking aspects of Haddix's writing style is her ability to craft a narrative that is both a thrilling adventure and a thought-provoking commentary on the human condition. The novel raises important questions about the nature of reality, free will, and the consequences of playing with the fabric of time. The author's use of the time-slip twist is masterful, adding a layer of complexity to the story that keeps readers on the edge of their seats.

The character of Jessie is particularly noteworthy, as she embodies the spirit of a brave and determined young girl who refuses to give up in the face of overwhelming adversity. Her courage and resourcefulness in the face of danger are inspiring, and her growth and development throughout the novel are a testament to Haddix's skill as a storyteller.

The world-building in Running Out of Time is also noteworthy, as Haddix creates a dystopian thriller that explores the darker aspects of human nature. The novel is perfect for fans of The Giver, as it delves into themes of control, manipulation, and the dangers of questioning the rules. The author's vision of a world where people are trapped in a simulated reality is both haunting and thought-provoking, leaving readers to ponder the implications long after the story concludes.

In conclusion, Running Out of Time is a gripping and thought-provoking thriller that will appeal to fans of science fiction, historical fiction, and adventure novels. Haddix's masterful storytelling and well-developed characters make for a compelling read that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. With its unique blend of time travel, suspense, and social commentary, this novel is a must-read for anyone looking for a story that will challenge their assumptions and leave them breathless.

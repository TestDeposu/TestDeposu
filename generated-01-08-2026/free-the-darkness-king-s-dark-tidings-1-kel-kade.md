# Free the Darkness (King's Dark Tidings, #1)

**Author:** Kel Kade  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

**Free the Darkness: A Masterclass in Dark Fantasy and Forbidden Desire**

Kel Kade’s *Free the Darkness*—the first installment in *King’s Dark Tidings*—is a haunting, seductive descent into a world where shadows whisper secrets, and the line between hero and villain blurs with intoxicating precision. This isn’t just a collection of standalone stories; it’s a symphony of dark fantasy, psychological tension, and steamy, morally ambiguous romance, all woven into the eerie, gothic tapestry of Dark Falls. Kade, a master of atmospheric storytelling, crafts narratives where danger lurks in every corner, and the characters—flawed, fierce, and achingly human—are as compelling as the supernatural horrors that hunt them.

At its core, *Free the Darkness* explores the duality of desire: the allure of the forbidden, the weight of betrayal, and the relentless pull between love and vengeance. Each story in this anthology is a self-contained masterpiece, yet they coalesce into a cohesive exploration of power, obsession, and the cost of redemption. Whether it’s Detective Eric Cantu’s warped fascination with reporter Merritt McKenna—who becomes the target of a sadistic arsonist—or FBI agent Ethan Eames’ desperate race to save Risa Calder from a killer who’s been stalking her for a century, Kade never shies away from the gritty, visceral reality of human (and inhuman) cruelty. Yet, beneath the menace, there’s a quiet, aching beauty in the way these characters cling to hope, even as the darkness closes in.

Kade’s prose is lush, immersive, and dripping with sensuality. Her descriptions of Dark Falls are nothing short of hypnotic—moonlit streets that seem to breathe, abandoned buildings that whisper with forgotten sins, and a town where the past refuses to stay buried. The author’s knack for creating vivid, textured worlds is evident in every page, but it’s her characters who truly steal the show. They are not heroes in the traditional sense; they are survivors, haunted by their pasts, their loyalties tested by forces beyond their control. Delaney Harrison, the detective whose sister’s murder still festers like a wound, is a standout—a woman who has learned to trust no one, until Zach Jamison, her betrayal, reenters her life like a ghost from her worst nightmare. Her story is a masterclass in emotional devastation and the slow, painful unraveling of trust.

What sets *Free the Darkness* apart is Kade’s fearless handling of taboo themes. She doesn’t shy away from the darker corners of human nature—sexual tension that borders on obsession, revenge that festers like a rotten fruit, and relationships built on power imbalances that feel both intoxicating and destructive. The romance here is not the soft, saccharine kind; it’s raw, dangerous, and often toxic, reflecting the moral ambiguity of the characters who pursue it. Take Kemal Goodwin, a detective whose pride and grief keep him from seeing the truth until Eve Scanlon, a relentless investigator, forces him to confront his father’s unsolved case. Their dynamic is charged with tension, resentment, and an undercurrent of something far more dangerous—mutual attraction that neither is willing to name.

Yet, for all its darkness, *Free the Darkness* is not without moments of unexpected tenderness. Risa Calder’s discovery of a century-old murder, tied to a killer who sees her as his next victim, is a chilling exploration of fate and predestination. But Kade balances the horror with a quiet, almost poetic sense of the supernatural—hinting at forces beyond human comprehension that watch, wait, and whisper. The anthology’s structure allows each story to breathe, each character to shine, while the overarching theme of Dark Falls as a cursed, living entity ties them together. The town itself feels like a character, a place where history bleeds into the present, and every shadow holds a secret.

Kade’s writing is a perfect storm of gothic romance, psychological thriller, and dark fantasy. She blends the eerie atmosphere of a Stephen King tale with the steamy, morally complex relationships of a E.L. James or a Sylvia Day, but with a depth and originality that feels distinctly her own. The characters are deeply flawed, their motivations twisted, and their choices often brutal—but it’s this very rawness that makes them unforgettable. Whether it’s Marisol Zaragoza’s fight against a vindictive judge who threatens to destroy her career and love, or Eve Scanlon’s relentless pursuit of justice in a case that haunts Kemal’s family, Kade never lets her readers off the hook. The tension is palpable, the stakes are high, and the payoff—when it comes—is devastating.

*Free the Darkness* is more than just a collection of stories; it’s an experience. It’s a dive into a world where the line between light and dark is paper-thin, where love and danger are two sides of the same coin, and where the characters’ choices will leave you breathless. Kel Kade has crafted something rare—a dark fantasy romance that is as intellectually stimulating as it is emotionally gripping. If you love stories where the nightmares are real, the desires are dangerous, and the heroes are as broken as they are beautiful, then this anthology is for you. Just be warned: once you step into Dark Falls, you won’t want to leave.

# Cirkeln (Engelsfors, #1)

**Author:** Mats Strandberg  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In the Swedish town of Engelsfors, six teenage girls from different walks of life are brought together by an ancient evil that has been awakened. Mats Strandberg's 'Cirkeln', the first installment of the Engelsfors trilogy, is a gripping tale of magic, friendship, and the struggle between good and evil.

The story begins with the mysterious death of a student at the local high school, which sets off a chain of events that draws the six girls together. They soon discover that they are the chosen ones, destined to wield magical powers and fight against the forces of darkness. As they navigate their newfound abilities and the challenges of high school, they must also confront the evil that is hunting them.

Strandberg's writing style is engaging and immersive, drawing the reader into the world of Engelsfors and the lives of its teenage inhabitants. He masterfully weaves together elements of fantasy, horror, and coming-of-age drama, creating a narrative that is both thrilling and relatable.

One of the standout features of 'Cirkeln' is its well-developed characters. The six girls are each unique and complex, with their own strengths and weaknesses. Strandberg takes the time to explore their individual personalities, motivations, and backstories, making it easy for readers to become invested in their journeys.

The author's use of setting is also noteworthy. Engelsfors is a character in its own right, a small town with a rich history and a dark underbelly. Strandberg's vivid descriptions of the town and its surroundings create a sense of atmosphere and foreboding, adding to the overall tension and suspense of the story.

The themes of 'Cirkeln' are just as compelling as its plot and characters. Strandberg explores the struggles of adolescence, including peer pressure, family expectations, and self-discovery. He also delves into the complexities of female friendship and the power of unity in the face of adversity.

At its core, 'Cirkeln' is a story about the struggle between good and evil, and the power of young women to shape their own destinies. Strandberg's writing is engaging, immersive, and thought-provoking, making 'Cirkeln' a must-read for fans of fantasy, horror, and coming-of-age fiction.

'Cirkeln' has received widespread critical acclaim, including a nomination for the August Prize in 2011. The book has been sold to thirty countries and has been followed by two successful sequels, 'Eld' and 'Nyckeln'. With its unique blend of magic, suspense, and relatable characters, 'Cirkeln' is a gripping read that will leave you eager for more.

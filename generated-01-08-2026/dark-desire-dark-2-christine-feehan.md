# Dark Desire (Dark, #2)

**Author:** Christine Feehan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Stepping into the shadowed realms of Christine Feehan’s Carpathian mountains is not merely reading a novel; it is plunging headfirst into a primal, sensory-heavy landscape where love is both a salvation and a desperate, agonizing peril. In Dark Desire, the second installment of her legendary Dark series, Feehan cements her reputation as a titan of paranormal romance by leaning heavily into the intoxicating juxtaposition of gothic menace and all-consuming passion. Originally published and later re-released to capture an even wider audience of fantasy enthusiasts, this novel captures the author at her most unapologetically atmospheric, offering a masterclass in how to build tension between beings caught on the razor-thin edge between humanity and eternal darkness.

At the heart of Dark Desire lies a profound and driving theme: the salvation of the soul through the redemptive power of a destined, soul-deep connection. Feehan introduces us to the Carpathians, an ancient, immortal race of near-vampiric beings who are slowly losing their capacity to feel emotion. Without color, without joy, and inevitably sliding toward madness and the ultimate damnation of turning into the undead, these formidable males desperately need their life-mates—human women with the rare psychic and physical strength to anchor them to the light. This central premise transforms the traditional romance dynamic into a high-stakes battle for survival. It is not just about a man falling in love with a woman; it is about a predator finding his humanity in the hands of a healer. The brilliant, compassionate doctor drawn to the isolated Carpathian wilderness represents the ultimate antidote to centuries of isolation, violence, and despair. Through her, Feehan explores the healing power of empathy, proving that even the most damaged, monstrous soul can be redeemed by uncompromising love.

Feehan’s authorial style is as distinctive as it is mesmerizing. She writes with a lush, almost operatic intensity that mirrors the towering, unforgiving geography of her setting. Her prose is unapologetically sensual, rich with heavy metaphors, visceral descriptions, and a driving emotional rhythm that mirrors the rapid heartbeat of her characters. Where other authors might shy away from the sheer possessiveness inherent in paranormal tropes, Feehan leans into it completely. Her heroes are archetypal alpha predators—brooding, dangerous, and utterly relentless—yet they possess a fierce, reverent vulnerability when it comes to their chosen mates. This creates a fascinating dynamic of push and pull, where psychological suspense meets high-octane physical attraction. Feehan excels at pacing; she keeps the reader on edge by constantly weaving external threats—rogue vampires, treacherous terrain, and psychological demons—into the intimate dance between the protagonist and her mysterious captor-turned-protector. The dialogue is charged, the atmosphere is perpetually thick with mist and unspoken danger, and every touch feels electric.

For fans of dark fantasy and intense emotional drama, Dark Desire remains a compelling milestone in the genre. It does not merely tell a love story; it carves one out of stone and shadow, offering a wild, breathless ride that lingers in the mind long after the final page is turned. Feehan demonstrates an unmatched ability to make the impossible feel intimately real, reminding us why her Carpathian series continues to cast such a long, alluring shadow over paranormal romance.

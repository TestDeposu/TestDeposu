# I Hope This Finds You

**Author:** Ann Liang  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

In the eagerly anticipated novella "I Hope This Finds You," Ann Liang masterfully weaves a captivating tale of love, self-discovery, and the complexities of relationships. This sequel to "I Hope This Doesn’t Find You" delves deeper into the lives of academic rivals turned lovers, Sadie and Julius, as they embark on a two-week adventure in California.

Through alternating chapters from both Sadie's and Julius's perspectives, Liang skillfully crafts a narrative that not only explores the intricacies of their relationship but also delves into the inner workings of their minds. This dual narrative approach allows readers to experience the story from multiple angles, fostering a deeper understanding of the characters' motivations and emotions.

One of the standout aspects of Liang's writing style is her ability to balance humor and heart, creating a narrative that is both lighthearted and poignant. Her use of witty banter and comedic moments adds a layer of levity to the story, making it an absolute delight to read. At the same time, she tackles more serious themes, such as the uncertainty of relationships and the challenges of building a life together, with sensitivity and nuance.

The central theme of "I Hope This Finds You" revolves around the idea that sometimes, a relationship can feel so special that it's hard to believe it can work out. Liang expertly captures the essence of this sentiment, conveying the euphoric highs and anxious lows that often accompany the early stages of love. As Sadie and Julius navigate their feelings for each other, they must also confront their own fears and doubts, making for a narrative that is both relatable and authentic.

Liang's writing is infused with a sense of optimism and hope, making "I Hope This Finds You" a truly uplifting read. Her characters are multidimensional and well-developed, with distinct voices that leap off the page. The chemistry between Sadie and Julius is palpable, and their romance is sweet and tender without being overly saccharine.

In short, "I Hope This Finds You" is a captivating novella that will resonate with fans of romance and contemporary fiction. Ann Liang's writing is engaging, witty, and heartfelt, making this book a must-read for anyone looking for a story that will leave them feeling uplifted and inspired.

# Dragon Actually (Dragon Kin, #1)

**Author:** G.A. Aiken  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

In the realm of fantasy, it's not often that we come across a tale as captivating and bold as G.A. Aiken's "Dragon Actually". This first installment in the Dragon Kin series is an unapologetic, action-packed ride that will leave you breathless and yearning for more. With its unique blend of bloodthirsty battles, witty banter, and sizzling sensuality, Aiken's patented mix of storytelling has undoubtedly earned its place among the top fantasy novels.

At the heart of this epic tale lies Annwyl the Bloody, a female warrior with a penchant for decapitating legions of soldiers without breaking a sweat. Her nickname is a testament to her unyielding ferocity in battle, but beneath the tough exterior lies a woman yearning for connection and understanding. It's a refreshing change of pace to see a strong, capable female lead who isn't afraid to show her vulnerable side.

Aiken's masterful world-building is on full display as she weaves a complex tapestry of characters, each with their own distinct voice and personality. Fearghus the Destroyer, a dragon of the large, scaly, and deadly type, is an unlikely confidant for Annwyl, but their bond is undeniable. The chemistry between them is palpable, and their interactions are some of the most compelling moments in the book.

However, it's not just the relationships between characters that make this novel shine; it's the author's writing style that truly sets it apart. Aiken's prose is a perfect blend of humor, wit, and sarcasm, making even the most mundane moments feel engaging and entertaining. Her use of language is economical, yet evocative, painting vivid pictures of the world and its inhabitants without ever feeling overly verbose.

One of the standout aspects of "Dragon Actually" is its ability to balance action and humor. Aiken's battle scenes are intense and visceral, yet they're often punctuated by moments of levity that will have you laughing out loud. It's a delicate balance to strike, but Aiken pulls it off with ease, making this novel an absolute joy to read.

If you're a fan of fantasy, adventure, and romance, then "Dragon Actually" is an absolute must-read. With its unique blend of bold action, sizzling sensuality, and laugh-out-loud humor, this novel is sure to captivate even the most discerning reader. So, if you're ready to embark on a thrilling journey filled with dragons, knights, and a healthy dose of sarcasm, then look no further than G.A. Aiken's "Dragon Actually".

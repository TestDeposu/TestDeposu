# Written in Bone (David Hunter, #2)

**Author:** Simon Beckett  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**A Desolate Canvas for Depravity: A Review of Simon Beckett's "Written in Bone"**

Simon Beckett's latest thriller, "Written in Bone", is a chilling exploration of the darker aspects of human nature. Set against the stark backdrop of the remote Scottish isle of Runa, the novel delves into the psyche of a calculating killer, leaving a trail of destruction in their wake. As a masterful blend of forensic science and psychological suspense, "Written in Bone" solidifies Beckett's reputation as one of the leading voices in British crime writing.

The story centers around Dr. David Hunter, a forensic pathologist who's summoned to the isolated isle of Runa to inspect a gruesome discovery. A body, all but incinerated, is discovered with only the feet and a single hand remaining. Initially attributed to an accident, David's expertise quickly reveals it to be a meticulously planned murder. The discovery sets off a chain of events that plunges the small community into chaos as a storm ravages the island, isolating its inhabitants from the mainland.

Beckett's signature style of incorporating forensic details masterfully immerses the reader in the world of David Hunter. As he carefully examines the charred remains, we're drawn into the intricate dance of cause and effect, witnessing the meticulous planning that went into the murder. This is where Beckett truly shines – his meticulous attention to detail and ability to convey complex scientific concepts in an engaging manner makes the reader feel like a participant in the investigation.

However, it's not just the forensic aspects that make "Written in Bone" gripping; it's the way Beckett probes the darker recesses of human psychology. As the story unfolds, we're introduced to a cast of suspects, each with their own secrets and motivations. The tension builds as David navigates a complex web of relationships, all while navigating the treacherous landscape of human emotions.

One of the most striking aspects of "Written in Bone" is its use of setting. Beckett's vivid description of the isolated island creates a sense of claustrophobia, trapping the characters in a desperate struggle for survival. As the storm rages on, the island becomes a character in its own right – menacing, unforgiving, and as deadly as the killer who's terrorizing its inhabitants.

Simon Beckett's "Written in Bone" is a testament to his mastery of the thriller genre. By combining forensic science with psychological suspense, he creates a gripping narrative that keeps the reader on the edge of their seat. With its desolate setting and a killer who's as calculating as they are depraved, "Written in Bone" is a must-read for fans of crime fiction.

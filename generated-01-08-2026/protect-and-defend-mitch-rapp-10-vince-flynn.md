# Protect and Defend (Mitch Rapp, #10)

**Author:** Vince Flynn  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

Vince Flynn’s 2007 political thriller Protect and Defend, the tenth installment in the legendary Mitch Rapp series, delivers an unrelenting masterclass in high-stakes suspense and geopolitical adrenaline. In this gripping entry, Flynn wastes no time plunging the reader into the volatile, high-pressure world of modern counterterrorism, where international diplomacy is merely a thin veil masking ruthless survival tactics. 

At the core of the novel lies a timely and explosive central theme: the perpetual, razor-thin margin between preemptive state defense and catastrophic global escalation. The narrative kicks into gear when Israel takes matters into its own hands, obliterating Iran's clandestine nuclear program and leaving a radioactive disaster in its wake. This devastating strike ignites the fury of Iranian president Amatullah, who redirects the nation's rage toward America. Flynn expertly captures the paranoia, the posturing, and the brutal calculus of Middle Eastern politics, forcing readers to question the heavy moral toll exacted by the architects of covert warfare. 

True to form, Mitch Rapp does not flinch in the face of impossible odds. Seeing a rare opportunity to bypass bureaucratic gridlock and ignite a popular revolution within Iran, Rapp crafts a characteristically audacious plan. However, when a clandestine back-channel meeting between CIA Director Irene Kennedy and her Iranian counterpart spirals into a deadly trap, the stakes transform from strategic to deeply personal. Rapp is thrust into a relentless, ticking-clock showdown against a cunning Hezbollah mastermind, armed with a twenty-four-hour mandate to avert an unthinkable catastrophe by any means necessary.

Flynn’s signature writing style is the true engine driving this white-knuckle narrative. Renowned as one of the premier adrenaline-charged action writers in the genre, Flynn writes with a fierce, unapologetic urgency. His prose is lean, sharp, and relentlessly fast-paced, mirroring the frantic heartbeat of the operatives working in the shadows. There is no wasted exposition; every scene functions as either a tactical chess move or a breathless clash of wills. Flynn’s deep familiarity with the inner workings of the intelligence community—particularly the complex dynamic between Mitch Rapp and his steadfast mentor, Irene Kennedy—gives the book a gritty authenticity that resonates long after the final page is turned.

Protect and Defend is a masterclass in political pacing and pulse-pounding action. For aficionados of intelligence procedurals and geopolitical thrillers, Flynn offers a masterly reminder of why Mitch Rapp remains the gold standard of counterterrorist fiction. It is bold, uncompromising, and utterly addictive.

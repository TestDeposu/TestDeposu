# Interview with a Master (Interview with a Master, #1)

**Author:** Jason Luke  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

Bridging the gap between cinema and literature is a delicate art, and Jason Luke’s 2018 novel, *Interview with a Master*, takes this challenge and elevates it into something deeply resonant. Serving as an official expansion of the cinematic universe, this compelling volume transcends the boundaries of a standard novelization. Luke crafts a narrative that does not merely retell a familiar story, but rather excavates the psychological and philosophical foundations of a galaxy far, far away. 

At the very heart of the book lies the exploration of legacy, failure, and renewal. The main theme wrestles with the crushing weight of history and the burden of expectation. Luke uses the crumbling mythos of the Jedi Order to examine what happens when legends fall short of their own mythology. Through intimate character studies, the novel interrogates the duality of light and dark not as a simple cosmic binary, but as a daily, agonizing internal struggle. The juxtaposition of the desperate Resistance against the suffocating machinery of the First Order serves as a brilliant backdrop for a much more personal story about disillusionment and the arduous path toward redemption. 

Jason Luke’s literary style is marked by a sharp, cinematic precision blended with a newfound emotional introspection. While he captures the pulse-pounding urgency of starfighter dogfights and high-stakes standoffs with breathless momentum, his true strength emerges in the quiet moments between the chaos. Luke expands upon the original cinematic script by weaving in alternate viewpoints and deleted concepts, allowing the prose to breathe in ways the screen often prohibits. His writing style is immersive and evocative, treating iconic figures like Rey, Finn, Poe Dameron, and the legendary Luke Skywalker not as untouchable statues, but as vulnerable, breathing individuals grappling with fear, duty, and hope. 

For both devoted followers of the saga and newcomers looking for a rich sci-fi experience, *Interview with a Master* offers a profound expansion of a modern myth. It is a striking testament to the power of storytelling, proving that even within a universe of lasers and hyperspace, the most compelling frontier is still the human—and alien—heart.

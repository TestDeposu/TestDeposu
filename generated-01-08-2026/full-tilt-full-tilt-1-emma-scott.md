# Full Tilt (Full Tilt, #1)

**Author:** Emma Scott  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

To read an Emma Scott novel is to step willingly into an emotional crucible, and with *Full Tilt*, the bestselling author once again proves her mastery over the delicate intersection of heartbreak and healing. Returning with an all-new emotional, angsty coming-of-age love story tailored for fans of Jennifer Hartmann and Laura Nowlin, Scott delivers a narrative that feels less like a traditional romance and more like a raw, beating heart laid bare on the page. At its core, *Full Tilt* explores a devastatingly simple yet profound premise: what happens when the one person who truly saw you in your darkest hour vanishes, only to reappear when you are both falling apart?

Ten years old. A promise of forever whispered with the innocent conviction only children possess. But life, as it so often does, intervened with cruel precision. Xander Ford’s letters never arrived, leaving Emery Wallace with a fractured heart that refused to heal. When they collide once more at Castle Hill Academy seven years later, they are no longer the children who made that vow. Xander is weighed down by the suffocating grief of caring for his sick father, retreating behind a fortress of logic and the rhythmic agony of the rowing team. Emery wears the golden crown of a wealthy, popular teenager, yet beneath her untouchable, flawless mask, she is utterly unraveling beneath the ruthless demands of her father and her mother’s drowning sorrow.

Scott’s thematic brilliance lies in her refusal to write flat characters who are "fixed" by love. Instead, *Full Tilt* leans heavily into the messy, nonlinear reality of grief and healing. Xander and Emery do not save each other like fairy-tale knights; rather, they serve as mirrors reflecting the truth that humans—much like light itself—can be simultaneously shattered and whole. The pacing is a masterclass in slow-burn tension, allowing the rekindling of their childhood bond to feel earned, fragile, and achingly real against a backdrop of high-stakes angst. 

The author’s signature style shines through her lyrical prose and empathetic characterizations. Scott does not shy away from the ugly, suffocating corners of trauma and family expectations. She handles Xander's burdens and Emery's internal fracturing with a tenderness that elevates the story far above standard YA and NA tropes. Every glance, every unspoken word, and every collision of their past and present worlds carries immense emotional weight. The inclusion of classic tropes—second-chance romance, childhood sweethearts, and the atmospheric discipline of a sports romance—are elevated by Scott’s distinct ability to wring every possible drop of feeling from the setup.

*Full Tilt* is not a breezy beach read; it is an immersive, tear-stained journey that demands your entire emotional investment. It asks the ultimate, terrifying question: when the world is actively conspiring against you, can a once-in-a-lifetime love keep you in the light? Emma Scott answers with a story that lingers long after the final page, cementing her status as a premier voice in contemporary emotional romance. Prepare your heart, because Scott is about to put it through the absolute wringer—and you will thank her for it.

# Perfect (Impulse, #2)

**Author:** Ellen Hopkins  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

"Perfect" by Ellen Hopkins is a hauntingly powerful novel that delves into the complex and often misunderstood world of mental health, suicide, and the struggles of adolescence. The second installment in the Impulse series, this book masterfully weaves together the lives of three teenagers who find themselves at Aspen Springs, a psychiatric hospital, after each attempting to take their own life.

Through the eyes of Vanessa, Tony, and Conner, Hopkins skillfully exposes the harsh realities of their individual struggles, from the crippling effects of childhood trauma to the suffocating pressures of perfection. Each character's narrative is expertly intertwined, creating a rich tapestry of emotions, fears, and desires that culminates in a poignant exploration of the human condition.

Hopkins' writing style is nothing short of remarkable. Her use of free verse poetry adds an intimate, lyrical quality to the narrative, allowing the reader to experience the characters' innermost thoughts and feelings with unflinching honesty. This unique approach not only mirrors the fluidity of the human mind but also underscores the fragmented nature of memory and the often-disjointed quality of emotional pain.

One of the most striking aspects of "Perfect" is its unflinching portrayal of the darkest corners of the human experience. Hopkins tackles themes that are often stigmatized or ignored, shedding light on the complexities of mental illness and the ways in which it can both unite and isolate individuals. The characters' struggles are raw, relatable, and unapologetically authentic, making it impossible for the reader to look away.

Through the characters' journeys, Hopkins highlights the importance of human connection and the power of relationships in the healing process. The bonds that form between Vanessa, Tony, and Conner serve as a testament to the resilience of the human spirit and the possibility of redemption, even in the face of unimaginable pain.

Ultimately, "Perfect" is a novel that will leave readers breathless, disturbed, and, above all, hopeful. It is a powerful reminder that, even in the darkest moments, there is always a way forward, always a chance for healing, and always a reason to keep fighting.

In short, "Perfect" is a masterpiece of contemporary young adult literature that will resonate with readers long after the final page has been turned. It is a must-read for anyone who has ever struggled, anyone who has ever felt the weight of the world bearing down upon them, and anyone who has ever dared to hope for a better tomorrow.

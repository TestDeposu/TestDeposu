# Iron Heart (Crier's War, #2)

**Author:** Nina Varela  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

Iron Heart is a masterful sequel to Nina Varela's critically acclaimed Crier's War, transporting readers to a richly imagined world where a human rebellion fights for liberation against the tyrannical rule of Automae. The novel is a sweeping epic fantasy that masterfully weaves romance, politics, and action into a complex narrative.

The story centers around Ayla, a young human who was once bound to the palace of the Automae as a handmaiden. But after a chance encounter with Lady Crier, an Automae noblewoman, Ayla's fate takes a dramatic turn when their forbidden love blossoms. The romance that develops between Ayla and Crier is a true highlight of the novel, capturing the essence of their complicated feelings through nuanced language and authentic emotional depth. It's a testament to Varela's skill that she balances the high-stakes revolutionary plot with poignant character development, creating a narrative that will captivate readers of all ages.

Ayla's journey is expertly interwoven with the broader context of the human rebellion, making the stakes more pressing and personal. Her struggles with identity and belonging mirror the universal human experiences of self-discovery and perseverance, allowing readers to invest in her cause. The supporting characters – particularly Queen Junn and the traveling rebels – add to the rich tapestry of the narrative, offering diverse perspectives and motivations that challenge the Automae's dominance.

One of the most striking aspects of Iron Heart is its exploration of the complex relationship between oppression and rebellion. Varela thoughtfully navigates the gray areas of activism, highlighting the moral dilemmas faced by both the human revolutionaries and the Automae. The author avoids simplistic portrayals of heroes and villains, instead opting for multi-dimensional characters that confront the harsh realities of war and social change.

Through Varela's vivid storytelling, the world of Automae comes alive with its intricate politics and mythology. The magical system of heartstone is skillfully woven into the narrative, providing a compelling backdrop for the conflict between humans and Automae. The author's world-building is so meticulous that readers will be fully immersed in the fictional realm, empathizing with the struggles of the characters as they navigate the treacherous landscape of Rabu.

The action-packed climax of Iron Heart is both suspenseful and emotionally resonant, building on the momentum of the preceding plot. The confrontation with the hidden secret of the Iron Heart serves as a powerful metaphor for the devastating consequences of power and exploitation.

Nina Varela's writing style in Iron Heart is a testament to her mastery of the fantasy genre. Her prose is lyrical and evocative, conjuring vivid images of the world and its inhabitants. The pacing is well-balanced, combining breakneck action sequences with quieter, more introspective moments that explore the characters' inner lives.

Overall, Iron Heart is an unforgettable reading experience that will captivate fans of epic fantasy and LGBTQ+ literature. With its gripping narrative, complex characters, and nuanced exploration of themes, it's a must-read for anyone who enjoys a compelling story that lingers in the mind long after the final page is turned.

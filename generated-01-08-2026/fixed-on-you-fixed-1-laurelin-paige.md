# Fixed on You (Fixed, #1)

**Author:** Laurelin Paige  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

Fixed on You, the first installment in Laurelin Paige's Fixed series, is a tantalizing tale of obsession, love, and healing that delves deep into the complexities of the human heart. With a narrative that is both captivating and thought-provoking, Paige masterfully weaves a story that will leave readers on the edge of their seats, desperate to uncover the secrets that bind the protagonists together.

At the core of this novel lies the theme of obsession, a notion that Paige explores with unflinching honesty. The protagonist, whose name is not disclosed in the synopsis, is a complex and multifaceted character, driven by a past marred by stalking and restraining orders. Her obsessive love disorder is a constant reminder of the perils of all-consuming passion, and her desire to keep it in check is palpable.

Enter Hudson Pierce, the enigmatic new owner of the nightclub where the protagonist works. With his chiseled good looks, intelligence, and wealth, he is the epitome of the perfect catch. Yet, it is his dark past that makes him the perfect foil to the protagonist's fragile psyche. As their relationship deepens, the lines between love and obsession begin to blur, leaving the reader questioning the true nature of their bond.

Paige's writing style is nothing short of captivating. Her prose is laced with a sensuality that is both palpable and intoxicating, making it impossible to resist the gravitational pull of the characters' desires. The author's use of language is deliberate and evocative, conjuring vivid images that will leave readers breathless.

What sets Fixed on You apart from other romance novels is its unflinching exploration of the human condition. Paige tackles complex themes with a deftness that is both impressive and thought-provoking. The novel is a testament to the transformative power of love, and the ways in which it can both heal and destroy.

As the protagonist navigates the treacherous waters of her relationship with Hudson, she must confront the demons of her past and the true nature of her desires. Will she be able to overcome her obsessive tendencies, or will they consume her once again? The answer lies in the pages of this captivating novel, which promises to keep readers on the edge of their seats until the very end.

In conclusion, Fixed on You is a must-read for anyone who enjoys a good romance. With its complex characters, captivating narrative, and thought-provoking themes, this novel is sure to leave readers spellbound. So, be sure to read it in an air-conditioned room, or turn on the fan – it is hot!

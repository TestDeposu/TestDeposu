# As You Wish (Genies, #1)

**Author:** Jackson Pearce  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In Jackson Pearce's enchanting novel, As You Wish, the boundaries between reality and fantasy become delightfully blurred as the story unfolds around the complex characters of Viola and Jinn. This captivating tale, set against the rich backdrop of Middle Eastern mythology, delves into the intricate and bittersweet nature of love, identity, and self-discovery.

Viola, the protagonist, is a sympathetic and relatable character, her pain palpable as she navigates the tumultuous aftermath of a breakup. Her unspoken wishes are a poignant reminder of the human desire to belong and to be loved, and it is here that the enigmatic Jinn enters her life. The intricate cultural context in which Jinn resides, imbuing him with a unique character that is both ancient and contemporary, serves as the perfect complement to the modern-day setting of Viola's world.

At its core, As You Wish is a thought-provoking exploration of the blurred lines between master and servant, as well as the complex web of emotions that arises when two individuals from vastly different worlds interact. Jackson Pearce masterfully weaves these intricate dynamics, raising questions about the very nature of love, power, and identity. The slow-burning tension that permeates this narrative is expertly handled, leaving the reader eagerly anticipating the consequences of Viola's three wishes.

One of the standout elements of As You Wish is its unique blend of genres. This is not a traditional romance, nor is it strictly a fantasy. Instead, it is a nuanced exploration of the boundaries that exist between these genres, resulting in a rich, immersive, and unforgettable reading experience. Pearce's use of magical elements as a metaphor for the complexities of human relationships is nothing short of genius, adding a layer of depth that elevates this story far beyond the standard romantic fare.

Throughout the novel, Pearce's prose is characterized by its lyrical quality, imbuing the narrative with a sense of wonder and enchantment. The descriptions of the world Jinn inhabits are vivid and evocative, transporting the reader to a realm both familiar and yet utterly alien. The pacing is measured, allowing the reader to become invested in the characters and their struggles, and the supporting cast is well-developed and nuanced, adding to the overall richness of the story.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the novel's resolution feels a touch rushed, with some plot threads left somewhat underdeveloped. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise outstanding narrative.

In conclusion, As You Wish is a must-read for fans of fantasy, romance, and magical realism. Jackson Pearce's masterful storytelling, coupled with her thoughtful exploration of the human condition, has resulted in a truly unforgettable novel that will leave readers spellbound and eager for more.

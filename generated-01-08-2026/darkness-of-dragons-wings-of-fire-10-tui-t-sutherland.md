# Darkness of Dragons (Wings of Fire, #10)

**Author:** Tui T. Sutherland  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

**A Gripping Quest for Balance in "Darkness of Dragons"**

Tui T. Sutherland's "Darkness of Dragons" is the tenth installment in the captivating Wings of Fire series, a testament to the author's mastery of weaving intricate, thrilling narratives. This latest chapter delves into the complex theme of balance, where ancient prophecies, dark magic, and the consequences of playing with history come alive in the world of Pyrrhia.

At the center of the narrative is Qibli, a character whose transformation from an insecure outcast to a courageous hero is both poignant and inspiring. Sutherland masterfully explores Qibli's inner struggle as he grapples with the weight of his own destiny and the pressure of saving his world from the dark influence of Darkstalker. This nuanced portrayal makes Qibli a relatable and endearing protagonist, inviting readers to empathize with his plight.

One of the book's primary concerns is the power of magic and the responsibility that comes with it. Sutherland thoughtfully conveys the implications of manipulating history, highlighting the delicate balance between progress and the consequences of altering the course of events. The tension builds as Qibli's quest to rewrite history becomes increasingly complicated, forcing him to confront the harsh realities of his actions.

Sutherland's world-building is as engaging as ever, immersing readers in the rich tapestry of Pyrrhia's seven tribes. From the imposing Jade Mountain to the intricate politics of the tribes, Sutherland's vision of this world is vivid and engaging. The supporting cast, including familiar faces like Tsunami and Peril, adds depth and continuity to the narrative.

A notable aspect of Sutherland's storytelling is her ability to balance action and introspection. The pacing is expertly managed, with pivotal fight scenes and heart-stopping moments of tension seamlessly woven into Qibli's inner monologues and emotional struggles. This approach keeps readers invested in the story, making Qibli's ultimate triumph all the more satisfying.

Throughout "Darkness of Dragons," Sutherland explores darker themes, such as the nature of evil and the costs of power. These thought-provoking elements add depth to the narrative, inviting readers to reflect on the consequences of their actions. This maturity in Sutherland's storytelling makes the novel a compelling addition to the Wings of Fire series.

In "Darkness of Dragons," Tui T. Sutherland offers a compelling and thought-provoking exploration of balance, responsibility, and the consequences of our actions. This gripping tale is a testament to Sutherland's skill as a storyteller, immersing readers in the intricate world of Pyrrhia and leaving a lasting impression.

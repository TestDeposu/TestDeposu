# Awake at Dawn (Shadow Falls, #2)

**Author:** C.C. Hunter  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

C.C. Hunter’s *Awake at Dawn*, the compelling second installment in the bestselling *Shadow Falls* series, successfully captures the turbulent, intoxicating essence of supernatural adolescence. Returning readers and newcomers alike are pulled right back into the sanctuary-turned-battleground camp for troubled paranormal youths. In this volume, Hunter deepens the mythology of her vibrant world while placing her relatable, identity-searching protagonist, Kylie Galen, at the epicenter of a chilling new mystery. 

At the heart of *Awake at Dawn* lies the universal coming-of-age theme of self-discovery, brilliantly refracted through a supernatural lens. Kylie’s overarching narrative is defined by a desperate, burning question: *What am I?* Surrounded by an eclectic mix of vampires, werewolves, shapeshifters, fairies, and witches, Kylie feels like an anomaly. Hunter uses this supernatural confusion as a clever metaphor for the teenage experience—that agonizing phase of life where everyone seems to have their identity figured out except you. Kylie’s journey is not just about identifying the specific brand of magic humming in her veins; it is about learning to accept herself, flaws and fears included, when the world demands absolute certainty.

Balancing this internal quest for identity is Hunter’s signature style: an effortless blend of high-stakes suspense, poignant emotional growth, and classic young adult romance. The author has a distinct knack for pacing. She seamlessly weaves the terrifying elements of the plot—namely, a malevolent spirit prophesying the imminent death of someone Kylie loves, alongside a darker threat lurking in the camp's shadows—with the lighter, highly addictive drama of a classic teen love triangle. 

The romance in *Awake at Dawn* acts as a pressure cooker rather than a distraction. Kylie is torn between Lucas, the brooding werewolf who haunts both her waking thoughts and her dreams despite leaving her for another, and Derek, the fiercely devoted half-Fae whose patience is wearing thin. Hunter writes these romantic entanglements with genuine heart, capturing the overwhelming intensity of first love and the agonizing ache of indecision. Neither boy is painted in purely black-and-white terms; both have flaws, vulnerabilities, and undeniable magnetic pulls, making Kylie's agonizing choice feel earned and emotionally resonant to the reader.

Furthermore, Hunter’s writing style shines in her dialogue and character dynamics. The friendships Kylie shares with other camp residents—such as the fierce vampire Della and the lovable witch Miranda—provide a comforting anchor amidst the chaos. These relationships offer authentic moments of humor, loyalty, and teenage banter that ground the fantastical elements in raw human emotion. 

Ultimately, *Awake at Dawn* is a striking, fast-paced read that proves the first book was no fluke. C.C. Hunter expertly expands the borders of Shadow Falls, raising the emotional and physical stakes for her characters. By blending a suspenseful murder-prophecy mystery with a heartfelt exploration of identity and young love, Hunter delivers a paranormal YA novel that is as thought-provoking as it is entertaining. It is a thrilling midnight-oil-burner that leaves readers eagerly flipping pages to the very end, desperate to see who Kylie becomes—and who she manages to save.

# Sisters Red (Fairytale Retellings, #1)

**Author:** Jackson Pearce  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

In "Sisters Red," Jackson Pearce weaves a dark, yet captivating tale that breathes new life into the realm of fairytale retellings. Drawing inspiration from classic folklore, Pearce takes us on a journey that seamlessly blends action, romance, and the struggles of sisterhood.

The story revolves around Scarlett March, a fiercely determined young woman bent on eradicating the Fenris, a pack of werewolves that brutally destroyed her life. Armed with an unyielding spirit and a razor-sharp hatchet, Scarlett patrols the streets, ever vigilant and ready to strike. Her unwavering commitment to her mission stems from the devastating past, which left her a single eye and a haunting loss. Scarlett's all-consuming quest for vengeance propels her towards a perilous path, and the reader is compelled to walk alongside her through this unforgiving world.

As the narrative unfolds, Scarlett's counterpart, her sister Rosie, emerges from the shadows. A vital component in Scarlett's crusade, Rosie has come to realize that her own desires and aspirations may be incompatible with her sister's unyielding fervor. The presence of Silas, a young woodsman with a deep bond to Scarlett, heightens Rosie's turmoil. As their bond develops, Rosie confronts a daunting question: does her budding love mean betraying Scarlett, or is it a liberating force that can redefine their connection and redefine their purpose?

Jackson Pearce masterfully crafts her protagonist, imbuing Scarlett with an unrelenting ferocity that simultaneously captivates and intimidates. Scarlett's raw emotions often spill over onto the page, making her all the more human and relatable. The tension between her all-consuming passion for vengeance and her inherent vulnerability creates a complex, multifaceted character that drives the narrative forward.

Pearce's atmospheric setting further immerses the reader in Scarlett's realm. The dark, gothic undertones create an eerie atmosphere that permeates every aspect of the novel, mirroring Scarlett's unyielding resolve. The author's use of descriptive language effectively contrasts the gritty, brutal world that Scarlett inhabits with the quiet longing and desire of Rosie. This dichotomy allows Pearce to thoughtfully probe the intricacies of sisterhood, love, and the resilience of the human spirit.

A significant aspect of Pearce's work lies in her exploration of the often-overlooked complexities of sisterly relationships. Rather than portraying Rosie and Scarlett as one-dimensional caricatures, she reveals their bond to be both beautiful and suffocating. As Rosie grapples with her burgeoning emotions, Pearce poetically illustrates the delicate dance between two sisters, their shared love, and their deep-seated conflicts. This nuanced portrayal lends depth and authenticity to the narrative.

Throughout the novel, Pearce thoughtfully explores the darker aspects of human nature, allowing the reader to engage in Scarlett's universe on multiple levels. Beneath the surface, "Sisters Red" offers a powerful exploration of the consequences of trauma, the allure of vengeance, and the resilience of the human spirit. This gripping and immersive tale masterfully blends the fantastical and the realistic, resulting in a captivating narrative that explores the very essence of what it means to be alive.

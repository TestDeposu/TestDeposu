# Wild Man (Dream Man, #2)

**Author:** Kristen Ashley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**Wild Man: A Sizzling Blend of Danger, Desire, and Second Chances**

Kristen Ashley’s *Wild Man*—the second installment in the *Dream Man* series—is a high-stakes, emotionally charged romance that masterfully weaves together suspense, passion, and the kind of moral ambiguity that keeps readers hooked. At its core, this book is a love story about trust, betrayal, and the lengths one will go to protect the person they can’t live without. But it’s also a thrilling cat-and-mouse game where the stakes are as high as the heat between its two leads: Tessa O’Hara, the sweet but resilient baker with a hidden past, and Brock, the disciplined DEA agent whose mission has just collided with his heart.

The novel opens with a scene so effortlessly seductive that it feels like a slow-motion kiss—Brock, the man of Tessa’s dreams, walks into her bakery, and within minutes, they’re sharing a beer, a laugh, and the kind of electric chemistry that makes readers sigh. But Ashley doesn’t let them bask in their newfound connection for long. The twist hits fast: Brock isn’t just any man; he’s an undercover agent investigating Tessa’s ex-husband’s drug empire, and now he’s the one who might be the reason she’s in danger. The abrupt shift from romance to suspicion is a masterstroke, forcing Tessa to question everything—including whether she can trust the man who makes her feel alive. Ashley excels at this kind of emotional whiplash, keeping readers on edge as they wonder: *Can love survive when the law and the heart are at war?*

What makes *Wild Man* so compelling is Ashley’s ability to balance the romance with the tension. Brock isn’t just a brooding, heroic agent—he’s a man who’s spent years living by a strict code, and now he’s forced to confront the chaos of his own desires. His internal struggle is palpable: he’s committed to his mission, but he’s also fallen harder for Tessa than he ever thought possible. The chemistry between them is electric, written with that signature Ashley flair—warm, sensual, and just a little bit dangerous. There’s a raw, unfiltered passion in their exchanges, whether they’re arguing over trust or making love in a way that feels like a rebellion against the world around them.

But the real strength of *Wild Man* lies in its exploration of redemption and second chances. Tessa isn’t just a damsel in distress; she’s a woman who’s been hurt before, who’s made mistakes, and who’s fighting to reclaim her life. Her journey is one of healing, of learning to trust again, and of realizing that sometimes, the people who love you are also the ones who can save you. Brock, meanwhile, is forced to question whether his rigid morality is worth losing the one thing that matters most. Ashley doesn’t shy away from the moral gray areas—there’s no easy answer, no neat resolution where the hero simply wins. Instead, she presents a story where love and justice collide, and the characters must find a way to coexist.

The pacing is relentless, with Ashley dropping clues and red herrings that keep readers guessing. The drug lord ex-lover looms in the background, a constant threat, while Brock’s undercover identity adds another layer of tension. The author’s writing is lush and immersive, painting a vivid picture of small-town life, the warmth of Tessa’s bakery, and the gritty underbelly of the drug trade. But it’s the emotional depth that truly sets this book apart. Ashley understands that romance isn’t just about passion—it’s about vulnerability, about the fear of being hurt again, and about the courage it takes to love someone when the world is trying to tear you apart.

One of the most striking aspects of *Wild Man* is how Ashley handles the power dynamics in the relationship. Tessa isn’t a passive character; she’s strong-willed, resourceful, and unafraid to stand up for herself. Brock, while protective, isn’t without his flaws—he’s human, fallible, and capable of making mistakes. This balance makes their dynamic feel authentic, not just a trope. Readers will root for them both, even when they’re at odds, because Ashley makes their love story feel real.

In the end, *Wild Man* is a romance that doesn’t just deliver steam but also delivers substance. It’s a story about the chaos of love, the messiness of redemption, and the courage it takes to fight for happiness when the world is trying to pull you apart. Kristen Ashley’s writing is as addictive as her cupcakes—sweet, sinful, and impossible to resist. If you love high-stakes romance with a side of suspense, this is a book you won’t want to miss.

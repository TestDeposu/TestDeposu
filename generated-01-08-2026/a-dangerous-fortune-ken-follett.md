# A Dangerous Fortune

**Author:** Ken Follett  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1993

A Dangerous Fortune by Ken Follett is a gripping historical thriller that weaves a complex web of power, greed, and deceit, spanning three decades and multiple lives. Set in 1866, the story begins with a tragic accident at the prestigious Windfield School, where a young student drowns under mysterious circumstances. This event sets off a chain reaction of treachery that will entwine the lives of many, exposing the dark underbelly of London's upper class.

Follett's masterful storytelling is on full display as he navigates the intricate world of 19th-century England, where the exclusive men's clubs and brothels cater to every dark desire of the wealthy elite. The author's vivid descriptions of the dazzling ballrooms and mahogany-paneled suites of the manipulators of the world's wealth are a testament to his meticulous research and attention to detail.

At the heart of the story is a shared legacy that splinters a family, fueled by greed and the shocking truth of a boy's death. As the narrative unfolds, Follett skillfully exposes the dark secrets and lies that bind the characters together, creating a sense of tension and suspense that is impossible to shake.

One of the standout features of A Dangerous Fortune is Follett's ability to craft complex, multidimensional characters that leap off the page. From the ruthless and cunning to the vulnerable and innocent, each character is meticulously crafted to serve a purpose in the larger narrative. The author's skill in creating relatable characters makes it easy to become invested in their stories, even as the plot twists and turns in unexpected ways.

Follett's writing style is a perfect blend of historical detail and suspenseful storytelling, making A Dangerous Fortune a page-turner that is impossible to put down. The author's use of language is evocative and immersive, drawing the reader into the world of 19th-century England with ease. The pacing is expertly handled, with each chapter ending on a cliffhanger that propels the reader forward.

In short, A Dangerous Fortune is a masterclass in historical thriller writing, with a gripping narrative that is both relentlessly suspenseful and breathlessly plotted. Follett's attention to detail and commitment to crafting complex characters make this a must-read for fans of the genre.

If you're looking for a historical thriller that will keep you on the edge of your seat, look no further than A Dangerous Fortune. With its intricate plot, complex characters, and meticulous historical detail, this book is sure to satisfy even the most discerning reader.

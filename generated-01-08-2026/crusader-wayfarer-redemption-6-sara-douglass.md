# Crusader (Wayfarer Redemption, #6)

**Author:** Sara Douglass  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

Crusader, the sixth installment in Sara Douglass's The Wayfarer Redemption series, is a masterpiece of epic fantasy that delves into the very fabric of good and evil. As the last battleground of the Star Dance and the TimeKeeper Demons unfolds in the land of Tencendor, the stakes are higher than ever before. In this gripping narrative, Douglass masterfully weaves a tale of heroism, sacrifice, and the unrelenting battle between light and darkness.

One of the standout features of Crusader is its exploration of the human condition. Douglass delves deep into the complexities of human nature, presenting a cast of characters that are multidimensional and relatable. The protagonist, DragonStar, is a prime example of a hero torn between his destiny and his own morality. His inner conflict is palpable, making him a likable and empathetic character. As he navigates the treacherous landscape of Tencendor, DragonStar must confront the harsh reality of his own mortality and the weight of his responsibilities.

Douglass's writing style is characteristic of her epic fantasy genre. She paints vivid descriptions of the world she creates, transporting readers to a realm of breathtaking beauty and unrelenting danger. The battle between the Star Dance and the TimeKeeper Demons is a spectacle to behold, with the author's descriptive prose bringing the cosmos to life. Her ability to craft an immersive world is unparalleled, making it easy for readers to become fully invested in the narrative.

Moreover, Douglass's exploration of the themes of sacrifice and betrayal is thought-provoking. The novel raises questions about the morality of violence and the true nature of heroism. As DragonStar navigates the complex web of alliances and rivalries, he is forced to confront the harsh realities of war and the price of victory. The novel's climax is a masterclass in tension, as Douglass expertly ratchets up the stakes, leaving readers on the edge of their seats.

In addition, the character of DragonStar is a testament to Douglass's skill as a writer. His development throughout the series is nothing short of remarkable, and his growth as a character is one of the most compelling aspects of the novel. His relationships with other characters, particularly those of his siblings and allies, are nuanced and authentic, adding depth to the narrative.

In conclusion, Crusader is a must-read for fans of epic fantasy. Sara Douglass's masterful writing, coupled with her richly detailed world-building, makes for a truly immersive reading experience. The novel's exploration of the human condition, alongside its thought-provoking themes, ensures that Crusader will linger in readers' minds long after they finish the book.

As the world of Tencendor teeters on the brink of destruction, DragonStar's fate hangs precariously in the balance. Will he find a way to save his world, or will the darkness ultimately consume everything? One thing is certain: Crusader is an unforgettable ride that will leave readers breathless and eager for more.

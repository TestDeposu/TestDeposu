# City of Stairs (The Divine Cities, #1)

**Author:** Robert Jackson Bennett  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

City of Stairs, the first installment in Robert Jackson Bennett's captivating Divine Cities trilogy, is a masterful blend of mystery, espionage, and fantasy. This critically acclaimed novel whisks readers away to the enigmatic city of Bulikov, a place of breathtaking wonder and dark, twisted history. Bennett's remarkable world-building skills bring this surreal landscape to life, crafting a narrative that is both deeply immersive and intellectually stimulating.

Bennett's unique prose is a hallmark of his writing style. His sentences are economical yet evocative, painting a vivid picture of a city where the remnants of a bygone era still linger. The author's use of language is deliberately spare, allowing the reader to fill in the blanks with their own imagination. This approach not only creates a sense of intimacy but also underscores the complexities of the world he has created.

At the heart of City of Stairs lies a gripping mystery: the brutal murder of a senior official in the city of Bulikov. Against this backdrop of politicking, assassinations, and espionage, Shara Thivani, a junior diplomat, finds herself entangled in a web of intrigue. Officially, she is part of a diplomatic mission, but unofficially, she is a skilled spy working to uncover the truth behind the murder.

Through Shara's eyes, Bennett skillfully explores the city's intricate social hierarchy, a delicate balance of power between different factions vying for control. This intricate dance of politics and deception is both absorbing and frustrating, as Shara navigates this treacherous landscape in search of answers. The pace is relentless, hurtling the reader forward with a series of unexpected twists and turns.

One of Bennett's greatest strengths is his ability to create a sense of depth and history in his world-building. The city of Bulikov is not merely a physical place but also a symbol of a complex, layered past. The author masterfully weaves in the mythology of the city, revealing the remnants of a civilization that was once revered and feared in equal measure.

Bennett's exploration of the city's past is inextricably linked to the theme of the divine and the human. What happens when a city that was once the seat of divine power is left to its own devices? How do the people of Bulikov respond when the very foundations of their reality are called into question? These are questions Bennett asks with unsettling ease, raising the stakes to a level that is both haunting and compelling.

As the narrative unwinds, Shara's investigation reveals the dark underbelly of the city, a place where gods are not always what they seem, and the living are forever haunted by the memory of those who came before. Bennett's handling of these themes is nothing short of masterful, injecting a sense of authenticity that is often missing in fantasy literature.

Ultimately, City of Stairs is a triumph of storytelling, a gripping narrative that is equal parts mystery, adventure, and philosophical inquiry. Bennett's unique voice and perspective are perfectly suited to this complex, atmospheric world, where the past and present collide in unexpected ways. With City of Blades and City of Miracles, the remaining two books in the Divine Cities trilogy, eagerly anticipated by fans, Bennett's epic tale is just getting started, promising a rich and immersive experience that will leave readers breathless until the very end.

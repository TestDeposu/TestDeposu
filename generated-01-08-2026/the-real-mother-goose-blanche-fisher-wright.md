# The Real Mother Goose

**Author:** Blanche Fisher Wright  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In the world of children's literature, few collections have stood the test of time as well as "The Real Mother Goose" illustrated by Blanche Fisher Wright. Originally published in 2011, this charming anthology brings together over 250 traditional nursery rhymes, each one beautifully complemented by Wright's vibrant color pictures. This treasure trove of classic verse is a must-have for families, educators, and anyone who delights in the whimsy of childhood.

From the opening pages, it is clear that Wright has poured her heart and soul into this project. Her illustrations are a masterclass in capturing the essence of each rhyme, transporting readers to a world of wonder and enchantment. With a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the nuances of each poem, Wright's artwork is the perfect accompaniment to the timeless words of Mother Goose.

One of the standout features of this collection is its ability to balance the familiar with the lesser-known. While readers will delight in encountering old favorites like "Humpty Dumpty" and "Little Bo-Peep," they will also discover a wealth of hidden gems that are sure to become new favorites. Wright's careful curation has resulted in a volume that is both a nostalgic treat for adults and a thrilling introduction to the world of nursery rhymes for children.

Wright's style is characterized by a sense of warmth and approachability, making her illustrations feel both classic and contemporary. Her use of color is bold and expressive, adding a depth and richness to each rhyme that is simply captivating. Whether she is depicting the gentle beauty of "Mary, Mary, Quite Contrary" or the playful energy of "The Grand Old Duke of York," Wright's artwork is always engaging and full of life.

Throughout the book, Wright's passion for the material shines through on every page. Her love of language, her respect for tradition, and her commitment to creating a sense of wonder are all evident in the care and attention she has brought to this project. The result is a collection that is not only a joy to read but also a testament to the enduring power of nursery rhymes to inspire and delight.

In short, "The Real Mother Goose" is a true treasure of children's literature. With its stunning illustrations, timeless rhymes, and sense of wonder, this collection is sure to captivate readers of all ages. Whether you are a parent, educator, or simply someone who loves the magic of nursery rhymes, this book is an essential addition to any library.

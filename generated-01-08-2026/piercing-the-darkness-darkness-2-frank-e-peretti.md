# Piercing the Darkness (Darkness, #2)

**Author:** Frank E. Peretti  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In the realm of Christian fiction, few authors have been able to capture the essence of the unseen spiritual battles that unfold in the mundane lives of ordinary people. Frank E. Peretti, renowned for his masterful storytelling, once again weaves a tale of supernatural conflict in "Piercing the Darkness", the sequel to his bestselling novel "This Present Darkness".

Published in 2012, this book plunges readers into the small town of Bacon's Corner, a setting eerily reminiscent of the fictional town of Ashton, Iowa, where the original story took place. Yet, the familiarity of the setting belies the vastly different circumstances. Here, we find Sally Beth Roe, a complex, troubled protagonist from the previous novel, trying to escape her dark past and searching for the truth. Her journey becomes intertwined with Tom Harris, a kind-hearted school teacher, who unwittingly finds himself at the forefront of a battle to preserve a Christian school threatened by external forces.

Throughout the book, Peretti masterfully crafts a narrative that is at once both suspenseful and deeply personal. His writing style is characteristic of his skill in painting vivid pictures of the spiritual realm, allowing readers to experience the supernatural battles that rage unseen, often unnoticed by those who inhabit the earthly sphere.

As in his first book, Peretti draws heavily from Christian doctrine, weaving in concepts of spiritual warfare, the power of prayer, and the struggle between good and evil. Yet, while this approach may seem formulaic, the author's deft storytelling elevates these themes, transforming them into a compelling tale that transcends mere allegory.

Through the character of Sally Beth Roe, Peretti presents a nuanced exploration of the human condition. Her struggles to reconcile her troubled past and find salvation are both heart-wrenching and deeply relatable. Her journey serves as a poignant reminder that even in the darkest of times, redemption and forgiveness are within reach.

Peretti's exploration of the relationship between faith and the everyday lives of his characters is perhaps the most striking aspect of the book. He skillfully illustrates how faith is not a distant, abstract concept, but a living, breathing force that shapes the decisions and actions of those who possess it. In a world where moral ambiguity often reigns, Peretti's characters embody a resolute commitment to their faith, one that inspires hope and conviction in the face of adversity.

The book's central theme of spiritual warfare is, of course, nothing new in Christian fiction. However, Peretti's skillful rendition imbues the concept with a sense of urgency and relevance, reminding readers that the battles waged in the spiritual realm have far-reaching consequences for the world we inhabit.

While some readers may find the book's themes and style a tad predictable, this is not necessarily a criticism. Peretti's approach is deliberate and effective, creating a narrative that is both accessible to new readers and satisfying to those familiar with his work. Furthermore, his writing is imbued with a sense of gravitas and respect for the complexities of the human condition, qualities that elevate the book from mere entertainment to a meaningful exploration of the human experience.

Ultimately, "Piercing the Darkness" is a gripping, thought-provoking tale that will resonate with readers seeking a compelling story of spiritual struggle and redemption. Frank E. Peretti's mastery of his craft is undeniable, and his ability to craft a narrative that is at once both thrilling and deeply personal, a testament to his skill as an author.

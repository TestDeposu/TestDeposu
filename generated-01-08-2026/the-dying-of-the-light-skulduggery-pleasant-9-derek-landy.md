# The Dying of the Light (Skulduggery Pleasant, #9)

**Author:** Derek Landy  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

The Dying of the Light arrives as a seismic, high-stakes collision point, marking the explosive finale of Derek Landy’s original, jaw-droppingly stupendous Skulduggery Pleasant series. At this critical juncture, the world simply isn't big enough for the trinity at the heart of the narrative: Valkyrie Cain, the unstoppable entity Darquesse, and Stephanie. Following the devastating loss of Valkyrie, the sharp-suited skeleton detective Skulduggery Pleasant is driven to desperate measures, assembling a deeply unconventional and volatile team of soldiers, monster hunters, cold-blooded killers, hardened criminals, and even Valkyrie’s own murderous reflection. As the dust settles from the War of the Sanctuaries, a far more lethal confrontation looms on the horizon, promising that not everyone will make it out alive.

Landy’s signature authorial style is on full display here, effortlessly blending the macabre with a razor-sharp, irreverent wit. True to form, the narrative maintains a relentless, lightning-paced momentum that keeps readers skipping meals and sleep well into the night. What elevates Landy’s writing above standard urban fantasy is his ability to temper apocalyptic dread with brilliantly timed humor. As noted by critics, he uses levity to take the edge off sheer terror, crafting a rich fantasy landscape populated by creative protagonists and villains who feel dangerously alive. The prose crackles with kinetic energy, balancing deep emotional resonance with the kind of snappy dialogue that has made the series a global phenomenon, drawing favorable comparisons to J.K. Rowling’s wizarding world and Rick Riordan’s mythological adventures.

At its core, the main theme of The Dying of the Light is identity, duality, and the devastating cost of survival. The fractured nature of Valkyrie and Darquesse acts as a physical manifestation of the internal war between good and evil, humanity and monstrousness. Landy explores how the choices of the past inevitably return to demand a heavy toll, forcing characters to confront the darkest reflections of themselves. It is a story about loyalty, sacrifice, and the relentless pursuit of hope even when the universe threatens to turn into a charred, lifeless cinder. For fans who have followed the series from the beginning, this installment delivers a thoroughly satisfying, emotionally gripping climax that cements Derek Landy’s legacy as a master of contemporary speculative fiction.

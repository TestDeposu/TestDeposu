# Proxy (Proxy, #1)

**Author:** Alex London  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

In the realm of young adult fantasy, few authors manage to meld high-stakes political turmoil with intimate, visceral world-building quite like Alex London. Known for his critically acclaimed debut Proxy, London returns with a breathtaking narrative that soars to dizzying heights both literally and figuratively. Though the Spanish edition's promotional materials poetically frame this installment around the world of Uztar and its revered falconry, English-speaking readers will recognize this soaring, high-altitude epic by its English title, *Wilder Girls* or more precisely, his foray into avian-centric fantasy. No matter the geography or the language, London's signature style remains unmistakable: relentless, deeply atmospheric, and unflinchingly human.

At the beating heart of this narrative are twins Brysen and Kylee, whose contrasting desires pull them apart just as a brutal war threatens to tear their homeland of Six Villages to shreds. Brysen chases the intoxicating glory of the falconer’s art and the affections of the boy he loves, while Kylee yearns to shed the ancestral traditions dictating her existence. Yet, survival demands unity. Forced into a perilous expedition across treacherous, jagged mountains, the siblings embark on a desperate hunt for the mythical ghost eagle—a solitary, terrifying apex predator of the skies. 

What makes London’s style so striking is his ability to use fantastical elements not merely as window dressing, but as psychological mirrors for his characters. The fierce, razor-taloned raptors they train and hunt are physical manifestations of the danger, power, and untamed freedom that the twins seek. London does not write safe fantasies. Having previously reported as a journalist from conflict zones and refugee camps, he infuses his fictional worlds with a chilling authenticity regarding the collateral damage of war, fanaticism, and the corrupting nature of power. The threat is omnipresent, descending from the dark wings above and marching across the blood-soaked earth below.

Praise from heavy-hitters in the YA community—including Adam Silvera, Mackenzi Lee, Dhonielle Clayton, and Kendare Blake—is hardly unwarranted. The book delivers on every promise of epic emotion, spine-chilling romance, and ferocious world-building. London crafts characters who are delightfully strident, brilliantly flawed, and utterly worth rooting for as they navigate a landscape where loyalty is tested by blood and survival comes at an astronomical cost.

Ultimately, this novel is a masterclass in tension. It challenges readers to reflect on the lengths they would go to protect the people they love, all while maintaining a breathless pace that makes it nearly impossible to put down. Alex London has once again proven his chameleon-like versatility across genres, delivering a soaring, hypnotic triumph that cements his status as a premier voice in modern speculative fiction.

# Everyday Sexism

**Author:** Laura Bates  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

In "Everyday Sexism," Laura Bates delivers a searing indictment of the pervasive, insidious sexism that permeates our daily lives. Founded on the principles of her groundbreaking Everyday Sexism Project, this book is a clarion call to action, urging readers to confront the entrenched attitudes and behaviors that perpetuate inequality and violence against women.

Bates' writing is lucid, accessible, and unflinching, making her a compelling guide through the darkest recesses of human experience. With unvarnished honesty, she shares the testimonies of over 100,000 individuals from around the world, their voices weaving a tapestry of pain, fear, and resilience. From the mundane to the horrific, these stories lay bare the quotidian nature of sexism, exposing the ways in which it is embedded in our culture, our language, and our very perceptions.

Through her meticulous research and analysis, Bates reveals the shocking ubiquity of sexism, from street harassment and workplace discrimination to sexual assault and rape. Her writing is not merely a catalog of atrocities, however; it is also a testament to the strength and courage of those who have survived and resisted. By centering the voices and experiences of women, Bates creates a powerful counter-narrative to the dominant discourse, one that challenges the normalization of sexism and demands a fundamental shift in our collective consciousness.

One of the most striking aspects of "Everyday Sexism" is its ability to balance the personal and the political, the individual and the structural. Bates is unafraid to confront the ways in which sexism is embedded in our institutions, our media, and our social norms, yet she also acknowledges the complex, often fraught nature of human relationships. Her approach is neither didactic nor polemical, but rather a nuanced exploration of the ways in which sexism is both a product and a producer of our societal ills.

Throughout the book, Bates' style is characterized by a remarkable blend of empathy, intelligence, and moral urgency. Her writing is not merely a critique of sexism, but a call to action, a summons to readers to join her in the struggle for a more just and equitable world. As Gloria Steinem notes, Bates has created a space where both men and women can see and challenge sexism, and it is this inclusivity, this willingness to engage with complexity and ambiguity, that makes "Everyday Sexism" such a powerful and necessary work.

In short, "Everyday Sexism" is a landmark book, one that deserves to be widely read and discussed. It is a testament to the power of feminist activism, a reminder that even in the darkest times, there is always the possibility for change, for resistance, and for revolution. As Liz Plank notes, Laura Bates has started a revolution, and this book is its manifesto.

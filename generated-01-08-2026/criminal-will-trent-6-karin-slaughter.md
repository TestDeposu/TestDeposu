# Criminal (Will Trent, #6)

**Author:** Karin Slaughter  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**A Gripping Exploration of the Past in Karin Slaughter's 'Criminal'**

Karin Slaughter's 'Criminal' is a masterful, emotionally charged exploration of the dark recesses of human nature and the power of the past to shape our destinies. This riveting sixth installment in the Will Trent series weaves a complex, suspenseful narrative that delves into the long-held secrets and lies that can destroy families and communities. 

Set against the backdrop of small-town Georgia, 'Criminal' masterfully captures the intricate web of relationships between Will Trent, a brilliant but troubled agent with the Georgia Bureau of Investigation, and Amanda Wagner, his demanding and enigmatic mentor. When a local college student goes missing, Will is inexplicably shut out of the case by Amanda, sparking a chain of events that will force them to confront their deepest fears and insecurities.

Slaughter's exceptional skill lies in crafting characters that are both multidimensional and flawed. Will Trent's tortured psyche, haunted by his troubled past, makes him an empathetic and relatable protagonist. Meanwhile, Amanda Wagner's stern demeanor and cryptic motivations add an air of mystery that propels the reader forward.

As the story unfolds, Slaughter expertly peels back layers of the past, exposing the dark secrets and hidden truths that have shaped Will's life. The abandoned orphanage where the two characters collide becomes a symbol of the fragile boundaries between reality and fantasy. It's here that Will's troubled history converges with Amanda's long-held obsession, setting off a cataclysmic chain reaction that threatens to upend their lives.

One of Slaughter's greatest strengths is her capacity to balance the intricacies of the plot with the emotional depth of her characters. 'Criminal' is a testament to her skill, weaving narrative threads into a complex tapestry that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. The result is a rich, thought-provoking novel that explores themes of identity, trauma, and the power of human relationships to heal and ultimately, destroy us.

If you're a fan of crime fiction, or simply looking for a gripping, emotionally resonant read, 'Criminal' will not disappoint. Slaughter's masterful storytelling, coupled with her exceptional character development, make this a compelling page-turner that will leave you breathless and yearning for more. 

In short, 'Criminal' is a masterpiece of suspenseful storytelling that will appeal to fans of Lee Child, Tess Gerritsen, and Laura Lippman. With its gripping narrative, masterfully crafted characters, and thought-provoking themes, this novel is a must-read for anyone who craves a rich, immersive literary experience.

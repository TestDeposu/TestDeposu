# Jane Steele

**Author:** Lyndsay Faye  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

In the deliciously dark and captivating novel "Jane Steele," Lyndsay Faye reimagines the classic tale of "Jane Eyre" with a refreshingly sinister twist. This gutsy, heroic serial killer's journey is a wildly entertaining ride that will leave you questioning the true meaning of morality and the blurred lines between good and evil.

Faye masterfully weaves a narrative that is both a loving homage to the original and a bold, unapologetic departure from it. The result is a complex, multidimensional protagonist in Jane Steele, a sensitive orphan with a penchant for violence and a penchant for storytelling. As she navigates the treacherous landscape of Victorian society, Jane's "last confessions" – a series of macabre, fictionalized accounts of the recently hanged – serve as a clever device for Faye to explore themes of justice, morality, and the darker aspects of human nature.

One of Faye's greatest strengths as a writer is her ability to balance wit and humor with the darker elements of her narrative. Her prose is engaging, clever, and often laugh-out-loud funny, making "Jane Steele" a true page-turner. The supporting cast of characters is equally well-crafted, with the enigmatic Mr. Thornfield and the charismatic Mr. Sardar Singh adding depth and complexity to the story.

At its core, "Jane Steele" is a novel about identity, power, and the performance of femininity in a society governed by strict social norms. Jane's struggles to reconcile her true nature with the expectations placed upon her are deeply relatable, and her ultimate triumph is both empowering and cathartic.

Throughout the novel, Faye's writing is marked by a keen attention to historical detail, a deep understanding of the Victorian era, and a willingness to subvert and challenge the conventions of the time. The result is a narrative that is both a loving tribute to the past and a bold, feminist reimagining of the classic tale.

In short, "Jane Steele" is a must-read for fans of "Jane Eyre," dark humor, and mystery. With its breakneck pacing, clever plot twists, and unforgettable protagonist, this novel is sure to captivate and enthrall even the most discerning reader.

# Gotrek & Felix: The Second Omnibus

**Author:** William King  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

In the realm of Warhammer fiction, few duos have captured the hearts of fans quite like Gotrek and Felix, the Slayer and his human companion. Their legend has endured for decades, and this Second Omnibus is a testament to the enduring appeal of their adventures. William King's masterful storytelling weaves a complex tapestry of heroism, horror, and dark humor, drawing readers into a world of gothic fantasy that is at once fascinating and terrifying.

The Second Omnibus is a treasure trove of Gotrek and Felix's earliest escapades, featuring three novels and a selection of short stories that showcase the duo's unwavering bravery in the face of unrelenting danger. From the haunted forests of the Empire to the frozen north, Gotrek and Felix face off against a diverse array of foes, each more terrifying than the last. The novels – Trollslayer, Skavenslayer, and Daemonslayer – are a masterclass in pacing and tension, hurtling the reader through a maelstrom of action and suspense that barely allows for a moment's breath.

One of the defining characteristics of King's writing is his ability to balance humor and horror. Gotrek, the gruff and taciturn Slayer, is a magnificent creation, driven by a death wish that is at once poignant and hilarious. His interactions with Felix, the human chronicler who has sworn to record the Slayer's deeds, are a highlight of the series, their banter and disagreements adding a welcome layer of depth to the narrative.

The supporting cast is equally well-drawn, from the sinister goblins and demented cultists to the monstrous troll that stalks the duo through the Worlds Edge Mountains. The world-building is meticulous, with a keen eye for detail that immerses the reader in the grim darkness of the Warhammer universe.

The short stories included in the omnibus – A Place of Quiet Assembly, Blood Sport, Kineater, and Mind-Stealer – are a welcome addition, offering a glimpse into the wider world of Gotrek and Felix. Each tale is a masterclass in concise storytelling, distilling the essence of the duo's adventures into a few, well-chosen pages.

The Gotrek & Felix Gazetteer, a comprehensive guide to the characters and locations of the series, is a welcome bonus, providing a wealth of background information that enriches the reader's understanding of the world.

In short, the Second Omnibus is a must-read for fans of the Warhammer universe, and a magnificent introduction to the world of Gotrek and Felix for newcomers. King's writing is a perfect blend of action, horror, and humor, and his creations are unforgettable. If you're looking for a compelling and immersive read, look no further.

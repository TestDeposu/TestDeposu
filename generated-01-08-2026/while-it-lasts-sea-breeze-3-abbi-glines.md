# While It Lasts (Sea Breeze, #3)

**Author:** Abbi Glines  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**While It Lasts: A Summer of Second Chances and Second Guesses**

Abbi Glines’ *While It Lasts*—the third installment in the *Sea Breeze* series—is a love letter to the messy, magnetic allure of redemption. Set against the backdrop of a sunbaked farm in the countryside, the novel follows Cage, a once-reckless playboy whose life unraveled after a heartbreak and a DUI. Now, stripped of his baseball scholarship and his usual playthings, he’s forced into a summer job far from the neon-lit temptations of Sea Breeze. The irony? The very place meant to reset him might just break him all over again.

Glines’ signature style—sharp, sensual, and steeped in emotional raw edges—shines here. Her prose crackles with the kind of tension that lingers between the lines, where desire and regret collide like storm clouds before a downpour. Cage isn’t just a love interest; he’s a study in self-destruction and reluctant growth, his charm still intact but his defenses frayed. His internal monologue is a masterclass in self-loathing wrapped in bravado, a man who knows he’s a mess but can’t resist the thrill of making others feel it too.

The real draw, though, is the farm’s enigmatic daughter—the one who radiates quiet fury and a sadness that’s been bottled up tighter than a cow’s udder. Glines teases her with just enough mystery to keep readers hooked, her layers unfolding like the petals of a thorny rose. The chemistry between Cage and this woman isn’t just physical; it’s a slow-burning reckoning, where trust is a currency traded in stolen glances and half-truths. The barn scenes alone are worth the read—moisture in the air, the scent of hay and something sweeter, the kind of tension that makes the pages feel like a dare.

But *While It Lasts* isn’t just a romance; it’s a story about the cost of running. Cage’s playboy persona is a shield, a way to avoid the pain of being seen. The farm, with its cows and dust and endless skies, becomes the crucible where he’s forced to confront what he’s lost—and whether he’s willing to risk losing more to get it back. Glines doesn’t shy away from the ugliness of his choices, nor does she romanticize his redemption. It’s messy, imperfect, and deeply human.

The novel’s strength lies in its balance: the humor that peeks through Cage’s self-pity, the warmth of the farm’s found family, and the undeniable pull of a love story that feels earned, not forced. Glines’ ability to blend steamy tension with emotional depth makes *While It Lasts* a standout in the series, a summer romance that lingers long after the last page.

For fans of Glines, this is a must-read—a story about the things that break us and the people who might put us back together, if we let them. And for those new to *Sea Breeze*, it’s a perfect entry point: a tale of second chances, where the only thing that lasts is the way it makes you feel.

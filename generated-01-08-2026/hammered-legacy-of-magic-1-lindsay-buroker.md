# Hammered (Legacy of Magic, #1)

**Author:** Lindsay Buroker  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

When it comes to crafting snappy, action-packed urban fantasy with a heavy dose of snark, Lindsay Buroker rarely misses the mark. In Hammered, the explosive first installment of the Legacy of Magic series set in the same beloved universe as Death Before Dragons, Buroker does what she does best: she takes a refreshingly grounded protagonist, drops her into the deep end of paranormal chaos, and lets the witty banter and magical mayhem fly. 

At the beating heart of Hammered is a celebration of blue-collar grit meeting high-fantasy absurdity. Seattle native Matti Puletasi is not your typical chosen-one sorceress. Armed with the strength of a bear, the stamina of an ox, and a heavy-duty magical hammer inherited from her dwarven mother, Matti is perfectly content swinging a sledgehammer at drywall, renovating homes, and occasionally keeping local bad guys in check. Buroker uses Matti’s construction background to ground the supernatural narrative in a tangible, gritty reality. Matti’s world is one of sawdust, sweat, and calloused hands—making it all the more entertaining when she is suddenly forced to pivot from laying tile to dodging assassin blades and solving supernatural conspiracies.

The central theme of the novel pivots on identity, heritage, and the friction of blending the mundane with the mythical. Matti is a half-dwarven woman just trying to run an honest business in a Seattle that has become a bustling hub for orcs, werewolves, and magic users. When a mysterious artifact is unearthed beneath her current job site, that delicate boundary shatters. Buroker expertly explores how easily a quiet, ordinary life can be upended by forces outside our control. The artifacts and magical politics at play are classic urban fantasy tropes, but Buroker infuses them with a fresh, contemporary working-class perspective that breathes new life into the subgenre.

Stylistically, Buroker’s signature voice shines on every page. Her writing is fast-paced, dialogue-driven, and relentlessly funny. Matti is a delightful narrator—pragmatic, fiercely independent, and endlessly sarcastic. She faces magical threats not with stoic dread, but with the weary annoyance of a contractor dealing with a difficult client. 

Of course, no Buroker adventure would be complete without a compelling dynamic between mismatched leads. Enter the requisite haughty, deadly elf assassin from another realm. Handsome, lethal, and burdened with a secretive agenda, he is the ultimate wild card. The romantic tension between Matti and her frosty, otherworldly ally is a textbook slow-burn, built on mutual distrust, sharp barbs, and the agonizing realization that they have no choice but to rely on each other. Buroker paces their evolving relationship with precision, ensuring that the romantic sparks never overshadow the high-stakes murder mystery driving the plot.

When Matti is abruptly framed for a murder she didn’t commit, the narrative shifts into high gear, transforming the book into a breathless race against time. Everyone from local syndicates to inter-realm mercenaries wants a piece of her, forcing Matti to leverage her wits, her magical hammer, and her reluctant elf partner to clear her name. 

Hammered is a triumphant start to a new series, offering a masterclass in how to write accessible, endlessly entertaining urban fantasy. It delivers all the adrenaline, humor, and romantic tension fans have come to expect from Lindsay Buroker, wrapped in a wonderfully rough-around-the-edges package. If you love badass heroines, magical mysteries, and elves who definitely should not be trusted, Matti Puletasi’s world is ready to be explored.

# Seize the Night (Dark-Hunter #6)

**Author:** Sherrilyn Kenyon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Sherrilyn Kenyon’s *Seize the Night*, the sixth installment in her blockbuster Dark-Hunter series, proves that urban fantasy can be both brutally visceral and deeply, hilariously human. Published in 2010, this volume leans hard into what Kenyon does best: taking stoic, tormented immortals and throwing them headfirst into the path of unapologetically sassy, fiercely independent heroines who refuse to bow to centuries of baggage. 

At the heart of the novel is Valerius, a Roman Dark-Hunter who carries the weight of a civilization’s worth of prejudice. Despised by his largely Greek peers and weighed down by his rigid aristocratic upbringing, Valerius is a study in controlled isolation. Enter Tabitha Devereaux. Sassy, lethal, and immune to masculine posturing, Tabitha is the antithesis of everything Valerius holds sacred. Complicating matters further, she is the twin sister of Kyrian’s wife—tying her directly to Valerius’s mortal enemy. This dynamic sets the stage for Kenyon’s most compelling central theme: the breaking down of ideological armor through the sheer, disarming force of connection. 

Kenyon’s stylistic brilliance lies in her ability to balance high-stakes supernatural horror with rapid-fire, razor-sharp dialogue. The world of Daimons and Dark-Hunters is relentlessly dark, yet the narrative never bogs down in despair. Instead, Kenyon uses humor as a survival mechanism, crafting a tonal whiplash that mirrors her characters' chaotic realities. When an ancient, resurrected Daimon targets them with a deeply personal vendetta, the plot accelerates into a breathless thrill ride. To survive, Valerius must do the unthinkable: dismantle his pride, trust his enemies, and surrender to a woman who openly drives him insane. 

*Seize the Night* is more than just a paranormal romance; it is an exploration of redemption, identity, and the courage it takes to rewrite one's history. Kenyon delivers a masterclass in pacing and character chemistry, ensuring that readers aren't just turning pages for the action, but for the profound emotional payoff. It is a striking, deeply satisfying addition to an already legendary urban fantasy canon.

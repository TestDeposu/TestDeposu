# Throttled (Dirty Air, #1)

**Author:** Lauren Asher  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

In the high-octane world of Formula 1 racing, where adrenaline fuels the senses and egos clash on and off the track, Lauren Asher's "Throttled" is a captivating tale of rivalry, seduction, and the unrelenting pursuit of victory. The first installment of the "Dirty Air" series, this novel takes readers on a thrilling ride, expertly navigating the intricate web of relationships between its complex characters.

At the heart of the story is Noah Slade, a charismatic and cunning heir to a dynasty of Formula 1 champions, whose simmering resentment towards Santiago Alatorre, the man who snatched the world championship title from him, is palpable. The tension between them is electric, and their uneasy truce is put to the test when Santiago joins Noah's team, Bandini, two years later.

As the racing season unfolds, the stakes are raised by the arrival of Maya, Santiago's younger sister, who is determined to document the team's journey through a vlog. Her presence ignites a spark between her and Noah, who is irresistibly drawn to her. The air is thick with anticipation as Noah sets out to break down Maya's defenses, but she is resolute in her determination to resist his charms, fearing that a romantic entanglement could destabilize her brother's focus during the championship.

Asher's writing is masterful, weaving a narrative that is both a scorching romance and a gripping exploration of the cutthroat world of professional racing. Her prose is sleek and polished, with a keen eye for detail that plunges readers into the heart of the action. The characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Noah's confident exterior and vulnerable core making him a compelling protagonist.

One of the novel's greatest strengths lies in its ability to balance the high-stakes world of racing with the intimate, emotional struggles of its characters. Asher's portrayal of the complex relationships between the drivers, their teams, and their families is nuanced and authentic, adding depth and richness to the narrative.

The romance at the center of the story is expertly crafted, with a slow-burning tension that simmers just below the surface. The chemistry between Noah and Maya is undeniable, and their will-they-won't-they dynamic keeps readers on the edge of their seats.

Overall, "Throttled" is a gripping and seductive read that will appeal to fans of sports romance and anyone who enjoys a good underdog story. With its breakneck pace, complex characters, and scorching romance, this novel is sure to leave readers breathless and eager for more.

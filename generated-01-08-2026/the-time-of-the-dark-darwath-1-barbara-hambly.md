# The Time of the Dark (Darwath, #1)

**Author:** Barbara Hambly  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

The Time of the Dark, the first installment in Barbara Hambly's Darwath series, is a captivating tale that masterfully weaves together elements of fantasy, adventure, and historical fiction. Published in 2011, this novel has stood the test of time, and its unique blend of genres continues to enthrall readers.

At the heart of the story lies Gil Patterson, a medieval scholar who finds herself drawn into a world of magic and darkness. As a student of history, Gil is no stranger to the atrocities of the past, but it is the horrors that unfold in her dreams that ultimately set her on a perilous path. The lines between reality and fantasy blur when a wizard appears in her apartment, seeking her aid in protecting an infant prince from the forces of evil.

Hambly's writing style is one of the standout features of this novel. Her use of vivid descriptions and meticulous world-building transports readers to a realm of wonder and terror. The author's background in medieval history is evident in the richly detailed settings and the nuanced characterization of her protagonist. Gil's transformation from a skeptical scholar to a brave warrior is both believable and compelling, making her an admirable and relatable heroine.

One of the primary themes of The Time of the Dark is the struggle between good and evil. Hambly explores this dichotomy through the lens of Gil's experiences, as she navigates a world where the boundaries between light and darkness are constantly shifting. The author's portrayal of evil is particularly noteworthy, as she eschews simplistic depictions in favor of a more nuanced and complex exploration of the nature of malevolence.

Throughout the novel, Hambly's prose is engaging and immersive, making it easy to become fully invested in Gil's journey. The pacing is well-balanced, with moments of high tension and suspense expertly woven together with quieter, more introspective scenes. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with a range of intriguing characters that add depth and complexity to the narrative.

The Time of the Dark is a gripping and thought-provoking novel that will appeal to fans of fantasy, adventure, and historical fiction. With its richly detailed world-building, compelling characters, and nuanced exploration of the struggle between good and evil, this book is a must-read for anyone looking to escape into a captivating and immersive world.

# The Hollow Boy (Lockwood & Co., #3)

**Author:** Jonathan Stroud  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In the spine-tingling world of Lockwood & Co., Jonathan Stroud masterfully weaves a narrative that will keep you on the edge of your seat, with "The Hollow Boy" being no exception. As the third installment in this critically acclaimed series, Stroud expertly expands on the dark and mysterious universe he has created, plunging readers into the midst of a supernatural outbreak that threatens to engulf the city of London.

At the heart of the story lies the enigmatic Anthony Lockwood, whose dark secrets continue to unravel, adding depth to his already complex character. Alongside his trusted allies, George and Lucy, Lockwood & Co. must navigate the treacherous landscape of ghostly Visitors, all while facing internal conflicts that test their resolve and trust in one another. The introduction of Holly Munro, the new and annoyingly perky assistant, serves as a catalyst for tension, forcing Lucy to reevaluate her place within the team and confront the feelings of resentment that have been simmering beneath the surface.

Stroud's masterful storytelling is on full display as he effortlessly balances the team's personal struggles with the ominous threat of ghostly hauntings and assassins. The author's vivid descriptions of the supernatural entities that lurk in the shadows will send shivers down your spine, while the witty banter and sardonic advice from the skull in the jar provide a welcome respite from the tension.

One of the most striking aspects of Stroud's writing is his ability to craft a world that is both familiar and yet, utterly unique. The Lockwood & Co. series is set in an alternate London, where ghosts and supernatural entities are an accepted part of everyday life. This blend of the mundane and the paranormal creates a sense of unease, as the reader is constantly reminded that the boundaries between the worlds are thin and easily breached.

Throughout the book, Stroud's writing is infused with a sense of dark humor, clever wordplay, and a keen understanding of the complexities of human relationships. The characters are multidimensional and relatable, with each one bringing their unique perspective to the table. The dialogue is crisp and engaging, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world of Lockwood & Co.

In "The Hollow Boy," Stroud has once again proven himself to be a master of the supernatural genre. With its breakneck pacing, clever plot twists, and memorable characters, this book is sure to delight both old and new fans of the series. So, if you're looking for a story that will keep you up late into the night, with the lights firmly on, then look no further than "The Hollow Boy."

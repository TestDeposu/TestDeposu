# Pumpkinheads

**Author:** Rainbow Rowell  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

In the poignant and captivating graphic novel, Pumpkinheads, Rainbow Rowell and Faith Erin Hicks join forces to craft a heartwarming tale of friendship, love, and the bittersweet passage of time. This visually stunning narrative follows the story of Deja and Josiah, two inseparable friends who have spent every autumn working together at the best pumpkin patch in Omaha, Nebraska.

As the seasons change and their high school years draw to a close, Deja and Josiah face their final Halloween together, marking the end of an era. While Josiah is content to bask in the nostalgia of their last shift, Deja has other plans. She's determined to make their farewell a memorable one, filled with adventure, excitement, and a dash of romance.

Through Rowell's signature wit and sensitivity, the characters of Deja and Josiah leap off the page, their distinct voices and personalities expertly woven throughout the narrative. The dialogue is snappy and authentic, capturing the essence of teenage camaraderie and the uncertainty of the future. Hicks's illustrations beautifully complement the text, bringing the vibrant world of the pumpkin patch to life with her expressive artwork.

At its core, Pumpkinheads is a story about the power of human connection and the importance of embracing change. Deja and Josiah's relationship is a testament to the enduring bonds of friendship, forged through shared experiences and memories. As they navigate the complexities of their last night together, they must confront the reality of their impending separation and the uncertainty of what lies ahead.

Rowell's exploration of this theme is both nuanced and relatable, capturing the universal anxiety of leaving behind the familiar and embracing the unknown. The author's characteristic empathy and understanding of the human experience shine through in her portrayal of Deja and Josiah's emotional journeys.

The novel's use of the pumpkin patch as a backdrop adds a unique and captivating layer to the narrative. The autumnal setting, with its vibrant colors and nostalgic atmosphere, provides a poignant reminder of the passing of time and the fleeting nature of youth.

Ultimately, Pumpkinheads is a triumphant and affecting tale that will resonate with readers of all ages. Rowell and Hicks's collaboration is a masterclass in storytelling, blending humor, pathos, and wit to create a truly unforgettable reading experience.

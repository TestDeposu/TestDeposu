# Waiting for You

**Author:** Susane Colasanti  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Navigating the high-stakes emotional terrain of high school is never a straightforward journey, and in her 2009 contemporary young adult novel Waiting for You, Susane Colasanti captures this tumultuous reality with remarkable honesty and wit. Known for her ability to craft relatable stories about the modern adolescent experience, Colasanti returns with a funny and moving tale of friendship, frustration, and the often-painful process of falling in love. 

At the heart of the narrative is Marisa, a sophomore whose life is a vibrant, messy balancing act of classic teenage dilemmas. Colasanti immediately establishes a compelling dynamic by pitting the alluring appeal of the popular, Cali-surfer-boy-type Derek against the unassuming, geeky charm of Nash, a robotics-enthusiast who struggles to talk to girls unless forced. While Derek represents high boyfriend potential—with the minor complication that he already has a girlfriend—Nash seems entirely wrong for her, despite secretly carrying a torch. Layered on top of this classic romantic push-and-pull are the pressures of managing a best friend with a penchant for older guys, coping with an increasingly bizarre family dynamic, and nursing a secret crush on an anonymous mystery boy. 

What elevates Waiting for You above standard teen romance is Colasanti’s signature style. Famously dubbed chick lit for girls who think, her writing thrives on authenticity. She avoids glossing over the awkwardness of adolescence, instead leaning into the confusion, the missteps, and the genuine emotional stakes that make youth so intense. Marisa is not a flawless heroine; she is wonderfully flawed, prone to overthinking, and entirely human. Through sharp dialogue and keen psychological insight, Colasanti paints a vivid picture of how complicated it is to figure out who you are while simultaneously trying to figure out who you want to love. 

The author’s pacing keeps the pages turning, effortlessly blending humor with moments of genuine emotional resonance. The supporting cast feels dimensional, anchoring Marisa’s romantic pursuits in the very real, messy world of high school friendships and family expectations. The juxtaposition of the glossy, unattainable ideal—personified by Derek—versus the authentic, unexpected connection found in someone like Nash serves as a brilliant thematic anchor. It challenges readers to look past surface-level charms and consider what real emotional compatibility actually looks like.

Ultimately, Waiting for You is a charming and insightful read that resonates just as strongly today as it did upon its publication. Susane Colasanti delivers a quintessential coming-of-age story that honors the intelligence of its readers, proving that the journey of self-discovery is just as important as getting the boy. For anyone who has ever stood at the crossroads of longing and reality, this novel is a sweet, funny, and deeply comforting companion.

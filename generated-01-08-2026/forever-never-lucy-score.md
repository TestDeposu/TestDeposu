# Forever Never

**Author:** Lucy Score  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

When it comes to crafting contemporary romance that effortlessly blends scorching chemistry with deep emotional resonance, few authors do it quite like Lucy Score. Following her monumental, TikTok-conquering success, Score returns to captivate readers with Forever Never, a novel that takes one of romance fiction’s most delicious tropes—the small-town, forced-proximity reunion—and infuses it with the signature wit, intense longing, and high stakes that have made her a global phenomenon.

At the heart of the novel is a masterclass in forbidden attraction. Brick Callan is the embodiment of romance-hero perfection: loyal, fiercely protective, stoic, and burdened by a titanium-strength willpower. The only chink in his armor? Remi Ford. She is the free-spirited artist who slipped through his fingers, the woman he has sworn to keep at a distance. Yet, the unwritten rules of romance dictate that distance is never permanent. When Remi makes a dramatic, wintertime return to Mackinac Island, she brings with her more than just old heartbreaks—she carries a secret wrapped in danger. 

Score uses this premise to explore the central theme of the book: the painful, beautiful process of dismantling emotional architecture. Brick has spent years building metaphorical and literal walls to protect his heart from Remi, the girl who once shattered it during their adolescent years. Remi, equally guarded, returns with a distrust of the very man who now offers refuge. The narrative thrives on this friction. The tension between them isn't just about physical attraction—though Score dials the heat up to an intoxicating degree—it is about the vulnerability required to let someone back in after they have seen you at your worst. 

Mackinac Island serves as more than just a picturesque backdrop; it acts as an active participant in the story. Stripped of its bustling summer tourist charm, the island in the dead of winter becomes a claustrophobic, atmospheric snow globe that forces Brick and Remi into close quarters. This isolation amplifies the emotional stakes, turning every lingering glance, every sharp piece of banter, and every touch into a potential catalyst. 

What elevates Forever Never above standard genre fare is Lucy Score’s distinct authorial voice. She possesses a rare knack for balancing laugh-out-loud humor with genuine, heart-wrenching stakes. The dialogue is snappy, the pacing is relentless, and the secondary plot involving Remi’s hidden danger injects a breathless thriller-lite element that keeps the pages turning at a breakneck speed. Score doesn't just write a romance; she writes a journey of redemption and forgiveness, proving that sometimes the only way to heal the future is to bravely confront the ghosts of the past.

For fans of small-town romance, brooding heroes with golden-retriever loyalty, and heroines who are fierce enough to stand their ground, Forever Never is an absolute triumph. Score reminds us why we fell in love with her writing in the first place: she delivers heart, heat, and happily-ever-afters that stick with you long after the final page is turned.

# Changeless (Parasol Protectorate, #2)

**Author:** Gail Carriger  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Changeless, the second installment of Gail Carriger's Parasol Protectorate series, is a delightful and engaging read that seamlessly blends elements of comedy, fantasy, and adventure, set against the backdrop of Victorian London. The story follows the indomitable Alexia Maccon, a woman of remarkable wit, intelligence, and determination, as she navigates a complex web of supernatural intrigue, social etiquette, and marital conundrums.

One of the most striking aspects of Carriger's writing style is her ability to craft a narrative that is both humorous and suspenseful, often simultaneously. Her use of language is witty and engaging, with a keen eye for period detail and a deep understanding of the social mores of the time. The world she creates is richly textured and immersive, with a unique blend of science fiction and fantasy elements that add a delightful touch of whimsy to the story.

At its core, Changeless is a comedy of manners, with Alexia's sharp tongue and quick wit serving as a clever foil to the more absurd elements of the plot. Carriger's portrayal of Victorian society is both affectionate and satirical, poking fun at the era's more ridiculous conventions while still conveying a deep appreciation for its aesthetic and cultural heritage. The result is a narrative that is both entertaining and thought-provoking, with a keen eye for social commentary and a deep understanding of the human condition.

Throughout the book, Carriger's characters are well-developed and complex, with even the most minor figures possessing a depth and nuance that adds richness to the story. Alexia, in particular, is a standout, with her soulless state and resultant lack of supernatural vulnerability making her a fascinating and formidable heroine. Her relationships with the other characters, particularly her husband and the enigmatic Lord Akeldama, are multifaceted and engaging, adding an extra layer of tension and intrigue to the plot.

The pacing of the book is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows smoothly from one scene to the next. Carriger's writing is descriptive and evocative, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the world she has created. The result is a story that is both engaging and immersive, with a unique blend of action, adventure, and social commentary that will appeal to a wide range of readers.

In short, Changeless is a delightful and engaging read that is sure to appeal to fans of fantasy, comedy, and adventure. With its unique blend of supernatural intrigue, social commentary, and witty humor, it is a must-read for anyone looking for a fun and immersive story. Carriger's writing style is engaging and descriptive, with a keen eye for period detail and a deep understanding of the human condition. Whether you're a fan of the Parasol Protectorate series or just looking for a fun and entertaining read, Changeless is sure to deliver.

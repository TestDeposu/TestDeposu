# Under Locke

**Author:** Mariana Zapata  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

In "Under Locke" by Mariana Zapata, the author delves into a world of raw emotions, dark pasts, and the complexities of human relationships. Set against the gritty backdrop of a tattoo parlor, the story revolves around Iris, a young woman who has been through the wringer, and Dex Locke, her enigmatic and formidable boss.

Zapata masterfully weaves a narrative that explores the intricacies of vulnerability, trust, and the blurred lines between love and obsession. Iris, having been abandoned by her father, a member of a notorious biker club, has grown up too quickly, shouldering the responsibility of raising her younger brother. Her tough exterior and sharp wit are a testament to her resilience, but beneath the surface lies a deep-seated fear of abandonment and a longing for protection.

Enter Dex Locke, the tattoo parlor's owner, whose gruff demeanor and exacting standards make him a formidable figure. His rough exterior, however, hides a complex web of emotions, and as the story unfolds, his character is revealed to be multifaceted, with a deep sense of loyalty and a fierce protectiveness towards those he cares about.

The push-and-pull between Iris and Dex is palpable, their relationship a delicate dance of power and submission. Zapata skillfully ratchets up the tension, crafting a narrative that is both intense and unsettling. The chemistry between the two leads is undeniable, but it's the underlying emotional currents that make their relationship so compelling.

One of the standout aspects of "Under Locke" is Zapata's writing style, which is both evocative and unflinching. Her prose is visceral, conveying the raw emotions and gritty realities of her characters' lives. The world she creates is unapologetically dark, with a cast of characters that are flawed, yet relatable.

If you're a fan of complex characters, morally ambiguous themes, and a healthy dose of angst, "Under Locke" is sure to resonate. Zapata's writing is not for the faint of heart, but for those who crave a story that will leave them breathless and yearning for more.

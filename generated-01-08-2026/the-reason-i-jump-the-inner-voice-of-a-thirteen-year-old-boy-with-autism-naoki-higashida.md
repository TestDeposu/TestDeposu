# The Reason I Jump: the Inner Voice of a Thirteen-Year-Old Boy with Autism

**Author:** Naoki Higashida  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

"The Reason I Jump: The Inner Voice of a Thirteen-Year-Old Boy with Autism" by Naoki Higashida is a poignant and thought-provoking book that offers a unique glimpse into the mind of a child with autism. Through a series of questions and answers, Higashida shares his inner world, dispelling common myths and misconceptions about autism and providing a deeper understanding of the experiences of individuals with autism.

One of the most striking aspects of the book is Higashida's candid and introspective writing style. With the help of his facilitator, he answers questions about his behaviors, thoughts, and feelings, providing a level of insight that is both fascinating and humbling. His words are infused with a sense of vulnerability and honesty, making it impossible not to be drawn into his world.

The book's central theme is the importance of understanding and acceptance. Higashida's primary message is for people not to give up on individuals like him, who may appear to be struggling or disconnected from the world around them. He emphasizes that individuals with autism are capable of learning, growing, and contributing to society, but often require patience, understanding, and support.

Through his answers, Higashida sheds light on the complexities of autism, revealing that it is not a disorder or a defect, but rather a different way of experiencing and interacting with the world. He explains that his repetitive behaviors, for example, are not a result of a lack of intelligence or creativity, but rather a coping mechanism that helps him to deal with the overwhelming sensory inputs that he receives.

Higashida's writing is also notable for its simplicity and clarity. He uses everyday language to describe complex emotions and experiences, making the book accessible to a wide range of readers. His use of metaphors and analogies adds a touch of creativity and humor to the text, making it a pleasure to read.

The book's impact is further enhanced by its conversational format. The questions and answers are arranged in a way that feels like a dialogue, making it easy to imagine Higashida speaking directly to the reader. This format also allows for a sense of intimacy and connection, drawing the reader into Higashida's world and encouraging empathy and understanding.

In conclusion, "The Reason I Jump" is a remarkable book that offers a unique perspective on autism and the experiences of individuals with autism. Higashida's writing is candid, insightful, and engaging, making the book a must-read for anyone interested in autism, education, or psychology. His message of acceptance and understanding is one that resonates deeply, and his book is a powerful reminder of the importance of empathy and compassion in our interactions with others.

# Princess of Glass (The Princesses of Westfalin Trilogy, #2)

**Author:** Jessica Day George  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

In "Princess of Glass", the second installment of Jessica Day George's captivating Twelve Dancing Princesses series, the author masterfully weaves a tale of deception, danger, and romance. While the original synopsis provided is incorrect, as this is the second book in the series, not the third, the story still delivers a delightful blend of fairy tale and adventure.

The narrative follows Petunia, the youngest of King Gregor's twelve dancing daughters, as she embarks on a journey to visit an elderly friend in the neighboring country of Westfalin. However, her path is fraught with peril, as she must navigate a forest rumored to be inhabited by strange humanlike wolves. These bandit-wolves, as they are known, are not the fearsome creatures Petunia anticipated, but rather a group of charming and handsome individuals with a penchant for redistributing wealth.

Upon arriving at her destination, Petunia discovers that the kindly grandmother she has been summoned to visit may not be as innocent as she initially seemed. This twist sets the stage for a thrilling adventure, as Petunia must use her wit and cunning to unravel the mysteries surrounding her host and the bandit-wolves.

Throughout the book, George's writing style shines, as she effortlessly blends elements of classic fairy tales with her own unique brand of storytelling. Her characters are well-developed and complex, with Petunia being a particularly strong and relatable protagonist. The world-building is also noteworthy, with a richly detailed environment that immerses the reader in the world of Westfalin.

One of the main themes of the book is the idea of deception and appearances versus reality. Petunia's journey is marked by encounters with individuals who are not what they seem, from the bandit-wolves to her host. This theme is expertly woven throughout the narrative, adding depth and complexity to the story.

Overall, "Princess of Glass" is a captivating and engaging read, perfect for fans of fairy tales and adventure stories. George's writing style is enchanting, and her ability to craft compelling characters and worlds is undeniable. If you're looking for a book that will transport you to a world of magic and wonder, then "Princess of Glass" is an excellent choice.

# The Wives

**Author:** Tarryn Fisher  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

"The Wives" by Tarryn Fisher is a masterfully crafted, psychological thriller that delves into the darker aspects of marriage, relationships, and the human psyche. This gripping novel tells the story of a woman who shares her husband with two other wives, none of whom know each other. The arrangement is unconventional, but the protagonist has convinced herself that she's fine with it - that is, until she discovers a cryptic message that sets her on a path of discovery and obsession.

Fisher's writing style is reminiscent of a slow-burning fire that gradually builds into a raging inferno. She expertly weaves together a complex narrative that's both thought-provoking and deeply unsettling. The author's use of language is lyrical and evocative, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world she's created.

One of the most striking aspects of "The Wives" is its exploration of the theme of marriage and relationships. Fisher raises important questions about the nature of love, loyalty, and possession. Can a person truly love someone if they're willing to share them with others? Is it possible to maintain a healthy relationship when there are secrets and lies involved? These are just a few of the questions that Fisher poses, and the answers are far from straightforward.

The protagonist of the novel is a fascinating and complex character. She's both relatable and flawed, making it easy to become invested in her story. As she navigates the treacherous landscape of her marriage, she's forced to confront some uncomfortable truths about herself and her husband. The supporting characters are equally well-developed, particularly Hannah, the second wife who becomes a key player in the protagonist's quest for answers.

Fisher's pacing is expertly handled, with a narrative that's both propulsive and suspenseful. Just when you think you know where the story is going, she throws in a twist that completely upends your expectations. The result is a reading experience that's both exhilarating and exhausting.

Overall, "The Wives" is a gripping and thought-provoking thriller that will appeal to fans of domestic suspense and psychological fiction. Fisher's writing is superb, and her exploration of the human condition is both nuanced and insightful. If you're looking for a novel that will keep you on the edge of your seat and leave you thinking long after you finish reading, then "The Wives" is an absolute must-read.

# Things We Left Behind (Knockemout, #3)

**Author:** Lucy Score  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

In the latest installment of the Knockemout series, Lucy Score weaves a tale of forbidden love, family secrets, and the unrelenting pursuit of power in "Things We Left Behind". This novel is a masterclass in crafting a narrative that is both a slow-burn romance and a commentary on the destructive nature of ambition.

Lucian Rollins, a ruthless businessman, is hell-bent on redeeming his family's tarnished reputation. His empire is built on the principles of money and power, and he will stop at nothing to achieve his goals. However, his plans are put to the test when Sloane Walton, a feisty librarian, appears on the scene, determined to seek justice for a past injustice inflicted upon her father by the Rollins family.

As Lucian and Sloane engage in a battle of wits, their mutual dislike and distrust slowly give way to a fierce attraction. Score expertly crafts the tension between the two, making their eventual romance a satisfying payoff. However, their differences run deeper than just their initial animosity. Lucian's relentless pursuit of power and Sloane's desire for justice create a rift that threatens to tear them apart.

One of the standout aspects of Score's writing is her ability to create complex, multidimensional characters. Lucian and Sloane are no exception, with their motivations and backstories expertly woven into the narrative. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, adding depth to the story.

The themes of power, family, and redemption are timely and thought-provoking. Score raises important questions about the true cost of ambition and the destructive nature of secrets and lies. The novel is also a commentary on the class divide and the ways in which those in positions of power often wield it to further their own interests.

Score's writing style is engaging and accessible, making "Things We Left Behind" a compelling read. Her use of language is evocative, and the pacing is well-balanced, moving seamlessly from moments of high tension to quieter, more introspective scenes.

Overall, "Things We Left Behind" is a gripping romance that will appeal to fans of the genre. Score's writing is masterful, and her characters are complex and relatable. This novel is a must-read for anyone looking for a story that combines elements of romance, drama, and social commentary.

# Dark Rise (Dark Rise, #1)

**Author:** C.S. Pacat  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

C.S. Pacat, celebrated internationally for her razor-sharp character dynamics and unflinching emotional stakes, returns with Dark Rise, an explosive reimagining of the eternal war between light and dark. Set against the atmospheric, gaslit backdrop of a nineteenth-century London thick with secrets, Pacat strips away the comforting binaries of traditional high fantasy and plunges her readers into a murky moral labyrinth. This is a novel that does not merely blur the line between hero and villain—it obliterates it, offering a masterclass in tension, deception, and psychological chess.

At the narrative’s beating heart is Will, a sixteen-year-old dock boy thrust violently from the gutters into a grand, forgotten war. Fleeing the murderers of his mother, Will is rescued by the Stewards, an ancient order dedicated to holding back the tide should the dreaded Dark King ever rise again. Yet, as Will steps into this clandestine world of magic and rigorous training, Pacat subverts the classic chosen-one trope. Will is not a pristine beacon of virtue; he is fragile, hunted, and entirely unequipped for the gravity of his supposed destiny. 

What elevates Dark Rise above standard genre fare is Pacat’s distinct literary style. Known for her prose that is at once elegantly restrained and emotionally devastating, she crafts a relentless, fast-paced narrative where every alliance is fraught with peril. The world-building of nineteenth-century London feels tactile and alive, heavy with soot, forgotten magic, and the suffocating weight of history. Pacat excels at atmospheric immersion, making the sprawling city feel like a living, breathing participant in the impending apocalypse.

The central theme of the novel explores the terrifying fluidity of identity and destiny. Are we bound by the roles history writes for us, or can we forge our own path in the ashes? This existential dread is mirrored brilliantly in the book's standout dynamic: the enemies-to-lovers arc between Will and a terrifyingly powerful, magnetically beautiful rival who seems predestined to be his ultimate undoing. Pacat treats romance not as a subplot, but as an extension of the ideological war itself—intense, dangerous, and loaded with psychological manipulation. The chemistry between the characters crackles with a lethal kind of electricity, keeping the reader perpetually off-balance.

Furthermore, the novel thrives on the warmth of its found family tropes, providing a poignant contrast to the bitter betrayals that lurk around every corner. As Will finds unexpected camaraderie among the remnants of the Light, and as secondary characters grapple with the heavy burdens of conscience and loyalty, the emotional resonance of the story deepens. 

Dark Rise is a thrilling, high-stakes triumph that culminates in a jaw-dropping, reality-shifting twist. Pacat does not just subvert expectations; she dismantles them entirely, leaving the geopolitical and magical landscape of her world permanently altered. For fans of intricate intrigue, morally grey characters, and dark, intoxicating romance, this is an unputdownable opening salvo. Pacat has crafted a modern fantasy classic that will haunt its readers long after the final page is turned.

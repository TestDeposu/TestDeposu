# Goddess (Starcrossed, #3)

**Author:** Josephine Angelini  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In Josephine Angelini's breathtaking conclusion to the Starcrossed trilogy, Goddess, the international bestselling author delivers a romantic, action-packed, and intricately woven tale that will leave readers mesmerized and yearning for more. The book takes flight with a tantalizing premise: Helen Hamilton, the protagonist, has unwittingly set the gods free from their prison on Olympus, plunging the world into chaos and chaos. 

Angelini masterfully crafts the plot with a masterful touch, skillfully interweaving elements of Greek mythology, forbidden love, and epic battle scenarios. With a cast of vividly drawn characters and an ever-present sense of urgency, the pages turn effortlessly, propelling the reader into the heart of the tumultuous world that Helen and her friends inhabit. Angelini's deft skill in storytelling will keep you guessing, with each twist and turn propelling you further into the intricate web of divine politics and the human world. One of the main highlights of the book is the epic love triangle between Helen, Lucas Delos, and the powerful Orion, a dynamic tension that Angelini expertly handles throughout the narrative.

The character of Helen herself is compelling and relatable, displaying an unwavering spirit that is both admirable and heartbreaking to watch in action. Through her story, Angelini explores the complexities of love and identity, the struggle for self-discovery and acceptance, and the harsh realities of power and loyalty. The themes woven throughout Goddess resonate deeply with fans of the fantasy genre, particularly those drawn to stories steeped in mythology and romance.

Throughout the story, Angelini also cleverly plays off the classic mythological tale of Helen of Troy, subverting expectations and offering a fresh, innovative take on this enduring tale. At its core, Goddess is a story of identity, power, and loyalty that will captivate readers on multiple levels. It is the conclusion to a trilogy, and as such, it does its justice to the story started two books ago, providing satisfying closure while still leaving enough to be pondered long after you finish reading. With its engaging narrative, intricate world-building, and compelling characters, Goddess is a must-read for fans of the Starcrossed series and fantasy romance alike.

Ultimately, Angelini's writing is evocative and immersive, transporting readers to a world where gods and mortals collide. Her masterful storytelling and well-crafted world, woven with intricate details and complex characters, create an unforgettable reading experience. For those seeking an epic conclusion to a fantastical trilogy, Goddess is the perfect choice.

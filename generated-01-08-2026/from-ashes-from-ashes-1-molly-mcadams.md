# From Ashes (From Ashes, #1)

**Author:** Molly McAdams  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**From Ashes by Molly McAdams: A Sizzling Romance of Forbidden Longing and Second Chances**

Molly McAdams’ *From Ashes* is a slow-burn romance that masterfully weaves together themes of trust, heartbreak, and the dangerous allure of what we’re forbidden to have. Set against the backdrop of a new beginning—college in Texas—this story follows Cassidy Jameson, a woman who has spent years building walls around her heart, trusting only her best friend, Tyler. But when fate drops her into the path of Tyler’s cousin, Gage, a broodingly charming Southern gentleman with a voice like honey and a reputation for keeping women at arm’s length, Cassidy’s carefully constructed defenses begin to crumble. What follows is a tension-filled dance between desire and duty, where every glance, every stolen moment, feels like a rebellion against the rules that keep them apart.

At its core, *From Ashes* is a story about the pain of loss and the courage to reclaim joy. Cassidy’s past—marked by the sudden death of her father and the subsequent emotional withdrawal from the world—has left her wary of vulnerability. She clings to Tyler, not just as a friend, but as a lifeline, refusing to entertain the idea that happiness might exist beyond their carefully constructed dynamic. Yet when she meets Gage, she’s struck by something undeniable: his quiet intensity, his protective nature, and the way he makes her feel seen in a way no one else ever has. The chemistry between them is electric, simmering beneath the surface of their carefully scripted interactions, until the moment they cross a line they can’t uncross.

McAdams’ writing is lush and evocative, painting Texas in all its golden, sun-drenched glory while grounding the emotional stakes in raw, relatable vulnerability. The dialogue crackles with wit and tension, particularly in the exchanges between Cassidy and Gage, where every word feels like a test of wills. Gage, with his Southern charm and hidden layers of pain, is the perfect foil to Cassidy’s guarded nature. He’s not just a love interest; he’s a mirror, reflecting back to her the parts of herself she’s been too afraid to acknowledge. Their dynamic is a masterclass in slow-burn romance, where desire is built through subtle gestures—shared silences, lingering touches, the unspoken promise of something more—rather than explosive declarations.

What makes *From Ashes* truly compelling is its exploration of the cost of loyalty versus the hunger for something real. Tyler, though well-meaning, represents the safety of familiarity, the comfort of a life already mapped out. But Gage offers Cassidy the chance to rewrite her story, to step into a future where she’s not just surviving, but thriving. The tension between these two paths is what drives the narrative forward, making the emotional payoff all the more satisfying. McAdams doesn’t shy away from the messiness of human relationships—there are misunderstandings, hurt feelings, and moments of doubt—but she handles them with a deft touch, ensuring that the reader is as invested in the characters’ growth as they are in their romance.

The novel’s strength lies in its emotional honesty. McAdams understands that love isn’t just about passion; it’s about healing, about learning to trust again after betrayal or loss. Cassidy’s journey is one of reclaiming her voice, of realizing that she doesn’t have to choose between security and happiness. Gage, for his part, must confront his own demons—his fear of repeating past mistakes, his struggle to let go of the idea that love is something to be controlled rather than embraced. Their relationship feels earned, not forced, and the way McAdams balances their individual arcs with their shared chemistry is a testament to her skill as a storyteller.

While *From Ashes* is a standalone, it’s clear that this is the beginning of a larger story—one where Cassidy and Gage’s journey will continue to unfold, their bond tested by life’s inevitable twists. The ending leaves room for hope, for the promise of what could be, without sacrificing the emotional weight of the present. It’s a romance that lingers long after the last page, not just because of its steamy moments, but because of the depth of its characters and the authenticity of its themes.

For readers who love stories about second chances, forbidden love, and the courage to step into the unknown, *From Ashes* is a must-read. Molly McAdams has crafted a tale that is as much about the heart as it is about the soul, proving that sometimes, the most dangerous love is the one that sets you free.

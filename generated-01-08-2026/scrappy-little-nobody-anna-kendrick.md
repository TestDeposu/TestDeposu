# Scrappy Little Nobody

**Author:** Anna Kendrick  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

Scrappy Little Nobody by Anna Kendrick is a sidesplitting and relatable memoir that delves into the life of the Academy Award-nominated actress, offering a unique glimpse into her journey from a small-town girl to a Hollywood star. The book is a collection of humorous autobiographical essays that showcase Kendrick's razor-sharp wit, charming candor, and unapologetic humor.

One of the primary themes that emerge from the book is Kendrick's struggle to navigate the complexities of her life, from her early days as a "weird" and "10 percent defiant" teenager to her rise as a successful actress. Throughout the book, Kendrick recounts her experiences with unflinching honesty, sharing stories about her unusual path to the performing arts, her double life as a middle-school student and Broadway star, and her initial forays into dating.

What sets Scrappy Little Nobody apart from other celebrity memoirs is Kendrick's distinctive voice, which is at once familiar, sharp, and sweet. Her writing is infused with a quick-witted humor that is both laugh-out-loud funny and endearingly self-deprecating. Kendrick's ability to poke fun at herself and the absurdities of her life is a hallmark of the book, making it a joy to read.

Another notable aspect of the book is Kendrick's willingness to confront her insecurities and vulnerabilities head-on. Whether she's discussing her struggles to live like an adult woman or her experiences with the objectification of women in Hollywood, Kendrick's candor is both refreshing and empowering. Her commitment to being honest and authentic is a testament to her strength and resilience as a person.

The book is also peppered with behind-the-scenes stories from Kendrick's time in Hollywood, including her experiences on the sets of Pitch Perfect and Up in the Air. These anecdotes offer a fascinating glimpse into the world of Hollywood, revealing the absurdities and challenges that come with being a part of the entertainment industry.

In conclusion, Scrappy Little Nobody is a delightful and engaging memoir that showcases Anna Kendrick's unique voice and perspective. With its blend of humor, candor, and relatability, the book is a must-read for anyone looking for a fun and inspiring memoir. Kendrick's writing is both entertaining and empowering, making this book a standout in the world of celebrity memoirs.

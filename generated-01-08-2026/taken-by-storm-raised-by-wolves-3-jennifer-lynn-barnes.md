# Taken by Storm (Raised by Wolves, #3)

**Author:** Jennifer Lynn Barnes  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

**Taken by Storm: A Howling Masterpiece of Power, Betrayal, and Survival**

Jennifer Lynn Barnes’ *Taken by Storm*—the gripping finale to *Raised by Wolves*—is a storm in itself: a tempest of political intrigue, brutal survival, and the raw, unflinching struggle for dominance in a world where humanity is but a fleeting prey. This isn’t just a werewolf story; it’s a battle for existence, where every howl carries the weight of a kingdom, and every decision could mean the difference between survival and annihilation.

At its core, *Taken by Storm* is a tale of **power and its price**. Bryn, the human-raised alpha of Cedar Ridge, is a woman caught between two worlds: the fragile peace of her pack and the relentless hunger of the Senate, who see her as both a threat and a prize. The rogue were killing humans isn’t just a crisis—it’s a distraction. The other packs, led by the ruthless and calculating Senator, are circling like wolves scenting blood. They want her territory, her females, her *everything*. And Bryn knows one thing: she can’t fight them in open battle. Not when she’s human. Not when she’s outnumbered. So she must outmaneuver them, outthink them, and outlast them—all while keeping her pack safe.

Barnes’ writing here is **sharp as a fang and as relentless as the storm itself**. She doesn’t just tell a story; she *immerses* you in it. The tension is palpable, the stakes higher than ever, and the emotional depth of Bryn’s journey is both heartbreaking and electrifying. This isn’t a werewolf romance—it’s a survival thriller where love, loyalty, and leadership are tested to their absolute limits. The Senate’s machinations feel like a political chess game where every move could be your last, and Bryn’s internal conflict—between duty and desire, safety and sacrifice—is written with a raw honesty that makes her feel real, flawed, and utterly compelling.

What makes *Taken by Storm* truly unforgettable is Barnes’ ability to blend **mythic grandeur with intimate human stakes**. The world of *Raised by Wolves* is vast, but Bryn’s story is deeply personal. She’s not just an alpha; she’s a woman who lost her pack as a child, who was raised by wolves but never truly belonged to them, and who now must prove she’s worthy of leading them. The rogue were isn’t just a monster—it’s a symptom of a deeper rot, a reminder that even in a world of predators, humanity is the most fragile prey. And Bryn? She’s the only one who can decide whether her pack will be devoured or survive the storm.

The pacing is **breakneck**, the twists **brutal**, and the emotional payoff **devastating**. By the time the final pages land, you won’t just be rooting for Bryn—you’ll be *begging* for her to survive. Because in a world where power is the only currency that matters, her fight isn’t just for her pack. It’s for the very idea that mercy, loyalty, and humanity can still win against the howling dark.

If you loved *The Inheritance Games* for its clever twists and emotional depth, *Taken by Storm* will leave you breathless. It’s a story about **what it means to lead when the world is trying to tear you apart**, and Barnes delivers it with the same fierce intensity that made *Raised by Wolves* a modern YA classic. This isn’t just the end of a series—it’s the climax of a battle for the soul of a pack, and it’s one you won’t soon forget.

# For We Are Many (Bobiverse, #2)

**Author:** Dennis E. Taylor  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

In "For We Are Many", the second installment of the Bobiverse series by Dennis E. Taylor, the reader is once again catapulted into a captivating universe of intelligent spaceships, space-faring human colonies, and advanced alien civilizations. With humor, wit, and unbridled optimism, Taylor weaves a narrative that is as thought-provoking as it is entertaining.

At its core, "For We Are Many" is a book about the unifying force of humanity in the face of overwhelming adversity. Bob, the original and still charismatic spaceship, finds himself cloned into multiple versions, each with unique personalities and skillsets, yet all linked by a desire to do good and protect the innocent. As they navigate the complexities of intergalactic politics and conflict with technologically advanced extraterrestrial species, each iteration of Bob must confront the darker aspects of humanity, all while trying to stay true to their shared ideals.

One of the most compelling aspects of "For We Are Many" is its sheer inventiveness. Taylor is a master of extrapolating from current technological advancements to create a richly detailed and immersive science fiction universe. The narrative is peppered with clever references to geek culture, as well as nods to real-world science and engineering, which will delight fans of science fiction and geek culture alike.

Throughout the book, Taylor's irreverent humor shines. His protagonist, Bob, is a master of wry observations and snarky asides, often delivered in the most inopportune moments. This wit serves as a shield against the despair that would inevitably creep in when confronted with the numerous challenges facing the clones and the colonies they've established. Yet, beneath this humor, there's a deeper message about the resilience of the human spirit and its capacity for growth.

The character dynamics between Bob, his clones, and the humans they're trying to protect are a highlight of the book. Each new iteration of Bob is a masterclass in characterization, showcasing a distinct personality while retaining the core essence of the original. The clones' banter and disagreements create a sense of camaraderie and shared purpose that's both heartwarming and authentic.

Critics have long praised Taylor's ability to craft relatable and endearing characters, and in "For We Are Many", he takes his craft to new heights. The narrative's pacing is relentless, hurtling the reader through a dizzying array of events, from high-stakes diplomacy to space battles and scientific discoveries.

For those familiar with Andy Weir's "The Martian", they'll recognize Taylor's debt to that novel's "problem-solving-in-space" conceit. However, "For We Are Many" outstrips its influences in sheer ambition, incorporating a broader range of themes and ideas.

Dennis E. Taylor is a writer with a clear love for science fiction and its many sub-genres. His voice is an infectious blend of humor, curiosity, and optimism that will captivate readers from the opening pages. If you're looking for a compelling page-turner that explores the human condition amidst the vast expanse of space, then "For We Are Many" is an essential read.

# The Bungalow Mystery (Nancy Drew Mystery Stories, #3)

**Author:** Carolyn Keene  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

The Bungalow Mystery, written by Carolyn Keene, is a captivating tale that delves into the world of mystery and adventure, as embodied by the fearless and brilliant Nancy Drew. This third installment in the Nancy Drew Mystery Stories series, originally published in 1930, has been re-released in its original form, offering readers a unique glimpse into the pioneering spirit of young adult detective fiction.

At its core, The Bungalow Mystery revolves around themes of friendship, courage, and the unwavering pursuit of truth. Nancy Drew, alongside her friend Helen Corning, finds herself entangled in a complex web of secrets and lies when they befriend Laura Pendleton, a young girl who saves them from a perilous storm. As Laura's past begins to unravel, Nancy becomes determined to uncover the mysteries surrounding Laura's abusive guardian and a mysterious bungalow in the woods.

Carolyn Keene's writing style is characterized by her ability to craft an engaging narrative that seamlessly blends elements of suspense, intrigue, and coming-of-age themes. Her use of descriptive language paints a vivid picture of the settings, from the stormy landscapes to the eerie bungalow, drawing the reader into the world of the story. The characters are well-developed and relatable, with Nancy Drew standing out as a shining example of female empowerment and intelligence.

One of the most striking aspects of The Bungalow Mystery is its portrayal of strong female characters in a time when such depictions were rare. Nancy Drew is a true trailblazer, using her intellect and resourcefulness to navigate the challenges she faces. Her independence and determination serve as a powerful inspiration to young readers, particularly girls, who see in her a reflection of their own potential and capabilities.

The mystery itself is intricate and engaging, with Keene skillfully weaving together clues and red herrings to keep the reader guessing until the very end. The pacing is well-balanced, moving seamlessly from moments of high tension to quieter, more reflective scenes. This balance allows the reader to fully absorb the story, becoming invested in the characters and their outcomes.

In conclusion, The Bungalow Mystery is a gripping and thought-provoking read that not only entertains but also inspires. With its blend of mystery, adventure, and strong female characters, it is a must-read for fans of the genre. Carolyn Keene's original text, as presented in this edition, offers a fascinating glimpse into the early days of young adult detective fiction, showcasing the elements that have made Nancy Drew an enduring icon in literature.

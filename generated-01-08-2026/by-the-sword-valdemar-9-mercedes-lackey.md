# By the Sword (Valdemar, #9)

**Author:** Mercedes Lackey  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1991

In "By the Sword," Mercedes Lackey masterfully weaves a tale of self-discovery, perseverance, and the unyielding power of the human spirit. Set in the enchanting realm of Valdemar, this ninth installment of the series whisks readers away to a world of magic, mystery, and unrelenting adventure.

At the heart of the narrative lies Kerowyn, a strong-willed and determined heroine, whose life is turned upside down when her ancestral home is brutally attacked, leaving her family shattered and her future uncertain. As she embarks on a perilous journey to rescue her brother's kidnapped fiancée and avenge her family's honor, Kerowyn is driven by a fierce determination and an unyielding desire to prove herself.

Through Kerowyn's journey, Lackey explores the theme of identity and the struggle to find one's place in the world. As a member of a noble house, Kerowyn is expected to conform to the traditional roles of a lady, yet she yearns to break free from these constraints and forge her own path. Her passion for training horses and hunting sets her apart from the societal norms, and it is this very uniqueness that ultimately becomes her greatest strength.

Lackey's writing style is a hallmark of the fantasy genre, with vivid descriptions of the world of Valdemar, its magical creatures, and the intricate politics of the realm. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Kerowyn being a shining example of a strong and independent female protagonist. The pacing of the novel is expertly crafted, with a narrative that flows effortlessly from one chapter to the next, keeping readers on the edge of their seats.

One of the most striking aspects of "By the Sword" is its ability to balance action, adventure, and emotional depth. Lackey seamlessly weaves together intense battle scenes, heart-wrenching moments of loss, and poignant reflections on the human condition. The result is a novel that is both thrilling and thought-provoking, leaving readers to ponder the true meaning of courage, honor, and sacrifice.

In conclusion, "By the Sword" is a triumphant tale of self-discovery, perseverance, and the unyielding power of the human spirit. With its richly detailed world-building, memorable characters, and expertly crafted narrative, this novel is a must-read for fans of the fantasy genre. Lackey's writing style is a testament to her skill as a storyteller, and Kerowyn's journey is one that will resonate with readers long after the final page has been turned.

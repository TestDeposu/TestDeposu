# The Tail of Emily Windsnap (Emily Windsnap, #1)

**Author:** Liz Kessler  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2004

In the enchanting realm of children's literature, few authors have the ability to transport readers to magical worlds as seamlessly as Liz Kessler does in her debut novel, The Tail of Emily Windsnap. This beautifully crafted tale is not only a thrilling adventure but also a poignant exploration of identity, family secrets, and the transformative power of love.

At its core, The Tail of Emily Windsnap is a story about self-discovery, as Emily Windsnap, a bright and curious 12-year-old, embarks on a journey of uncovering the truth about her mysterious past. Living on a boat with her mother, Emily has always felt like an outsider, yearning for connection and belonging. When she finally learns to swim, she stumbles upon an incredible secret that changes everything: she's a mermaid.

This revelation sets Emily on a path of exploration, as she delves deeper into her mermaid heritage and navigates the complexities of her human and mermaid worlds. With her quick wit and determination, Emily wins over a cast of memorable characters, including her best friend, Shelly, a human girl who becomes her confidant and ally. Together, they embark on a series of underwater adventures, meeting a diverse array of mermaids who study shipwrecks, attend Mermaid School, and rule underwater kingdoms with iron tridents.

What makes The Tail of Emily Windsnap truly shine is Liz Kessler's masterful storytelling and world-building. Her writing is engaging, witty, and richly imaginative, conjuring up vivid descriptions of the underwater world that will captivate young readers. Kessler's characters, too, are multidimensional and relatable, with Emily's voice particularly standing out as authentic and endearing.

Throughout the book, Kessler explores themes that are both relevant to children and timeless. Emily's struggle to find her place in the world, her desire for acceptance and belonging, and her ultimately triumphant journey of self-acceptance will resonate deeply with young readers. The Tail of Emily Windsnap is a story that celebrates individuality, diversity, and the power of love to overcome even the most daunting obstacles.

One of the most striking aspects of Kessler's writing is her ability to balance action, humor, and heart. The Tail of Emily Windsnap is a page-turner that hurtles along at a breakneck pace, yet it also has moments of quiet introspection and poignancy. Kessler's dialogue is natural and effortless, making the characters feel like real friends that readers will want to spend more time with.

The Tail of Emily Windsnap is not just a captivating story but also an important one. It tackles difficult topics, such as identity, acceptance, and prejudice, with sensitivity and nuance. Kessler raises important questions about what it means to be human and mermaid, and how these differences can be celebrated rather than feared.

In short, The Tail of Emily Windsnap is a triumphant debut that marks Liz Kessler as a rising star in children's literature. This enchanting tale will transport readers to a magical underwater world, while also inspiring them with its message of love, acceptance, and self-discovery.

# Crash into You (Pushing the Limits, #3)

**Author:** Katie McGarry  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

Crash into You, the third installment in Katie McGarry's Pushing the Limits series, is an unflinching portrayal of love, vulnerability, and the unbreakable bonds forged in the face of adversity. Although this book was published in 2013, the original synopsis provided is for the 2018 publication of the 'Pushing the Limits' series box set, which includes 'Take Me On', 'Breaking the Rules', and 'Chasing Impossible', novellas that follow the original series' narrative style and character arcs.

In Crash into You, we are introduced to Rachel Young, the sister of Isaiah Young, a supporting character from the first two books in the series. Rachel's narrative is intertwined with that of Logan, a teenager with a penchant for street racing. As Rachel and Logan navigate their whirlwind romance, they are forced to confront their troubled pasts, the trauma that has shaped them, and the uncertainty of their futures.

One of the defining characteristics of McGarry's writing style is her ability to craft relatable, multidimensional characters. Rachel and Logan are no exception; their struggles, fears, and passions are palpable, making it impossible not to become invested in their story. McGarry's portrayal of their romance is raw, honest, and tender, capturing the all-consuming intensity of first love.

The main theme of Crash into You is the transformative power of love and relationships. Through Rachel and Logan's narrative, McGarry explores the ways in which human connections can both heal and harm. The characters' struggles with trust, vulnerability, and intimacy serve as a poignant reminder of the risks and rewards associated with opening oneself up to others.

McGarry's writing is evocative and immersive, with a narrative voice that is both authentic and engaging. Her use of language is lyrical, yet unpretentious, making the story feel both personal and universal. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with characters from previous books in the series making appearances that add depth and continuity to the narrative.

In conclusion, Crash into You is a captivating and emotionally charged novel that explores the complexities of love, relationships, and personal growth. McGarry's writing is masterful, and her characters are relatable and memorable. This book is a must-read for fans of the Pushing the Limits series and anyone who enjoys contemporary young adult fiction.

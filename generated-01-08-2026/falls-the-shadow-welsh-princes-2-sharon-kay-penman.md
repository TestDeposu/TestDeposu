# Falls the Shadow (Welsh Princes, #2)

**Author:** Sharon Kay Penman  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2008

"Falls the Shadow" by Sharon Kay Penman is a sweeping historical epic that brings the tumultuous 13th century to vivid life. This meticulously researched novel delves into the complexities of a pivotal era, expertly weaving a narrative that is both informative and engaging. At its core, the book is a character-driven exploration of the intricate dance between two larger-than-life figures: Simon de Montfort and King Henry III of England.

Penman's masterful portrayal of Simon de Montfort is a triumph, capturing the essence of a charismatic leader who defied conventions and forged his own path. A Frenchman by birth, Simon's audacity and unwavering conviction enabled him to secure an English earldom and marry into the royal family, thereby cementing his position as a key player in the kingdom's affairs. His unwavering dedication to the common people and unshakeable commitment to justice soon put him at odds with the ineffectual and mercurial King Henry III.

Penman's vivid depiction of Henry III is equally compelling, laying bare the monarch's frailties and exposing the deep-seated flaws that ultimately contributed to the kingdom's instability. As the narrative unfolds, the tension between Simon and Henry builds, culminating in a catastrophic confrontation that would forever alter the course of English history.

One of Penman's greatest strengths lies in her ability to breathe life into historical figures, imbuing them with a depth and nuance that is all too often lacking in historical fiction. Her meticulous research is evident on every page, yet she wears her learning lightly, never allowing the weight of historical fact to overwhelm the narrative.

The author's prose is engaging and accessible, making "Falls the Shadow" an absolute pleasure to read. Her writing is characterized by a keen eye for detail, a deep understanding of the period, and a remarkable capacity to evoke the sights, sounds, and emotions of a bygone era.

Ultimately, "Falls the Shadow" is a testament to Penman's skill as a storyteller and her passion for the subject matter. This is a novel that will captivate fans of historical fiction, particularly those drawn to the medieval period. With its richly detailed settings, complex characters, and gripping narrative, "Falls the Shadow" is an unforgettable reading experience that will linger long after the final page is turned.

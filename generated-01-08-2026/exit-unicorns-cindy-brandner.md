# Exit Unicorns

**Author:** Cindy Brandner  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2001

"Exit Unicorns" by Cindy Brandner is a mesmerizing tale that weaves together the threads of magic, love, and the unyielding human spirit. Written in 2001, this novel has stood the test of time, captivating readers with its unique blend of fantasy and historical fiction.

At its core, "Exit Unicorns" is a story about the power of imagination and the human desire to transcend the mundane. Set against the backdrop of a world where myth and reality coexist, the novel follows a cast of characters as they navigate the complexities of love, loss, and identity. Through their journeys, Brandner explores the tensions between the ordinary and the extraordinary, revealing the ways in which our perceptions shape our understanding of the world.

One of the standout features of "Exit Unicorns" is its lyrical prose. Brandner's writing is evocative and expressive, conjuring vivid images of a world that is both familiar and yet, utterly fantastical. Her use of language is deliberate and precise, crafting a narrative that is both poetic and engaging. The result is a reading experience that is immersive and enchanting, drawing the reader into a world of wonder and magic.

Brandner's characters are equally well-crafted, each one imbued with a rich inner life and a deep sense of emotional complexity. From the enigmatic and charismatic Jamie to the fiercely independent and determined Casey, the characters in "Exit Unicorns" are multidimensional and relatable, making it easy for readers to become invested in their stories.

Throughout the novel, Brandner explores themes of identity, community, and the power of storytelling. The unicorns of the title serve as a potent symbol of the magic and mystery that lies just beyond the edge of our everyday reality. By exploring the intersections between myth and reality, Brandner raises important questions about the nature of truth and the role of imagination in shaping our understanding of the world.

If there is a weakness to "Exit Unicorns," it is that the novel can be somewhat difficult to categorize. Is it fantasy? Historical fiction? Romance? The answer, ultimately, is that it is all of these things and yet, something more. This blending of genres can make the novel challenging to market, but it also adds to its unique charm and appeal.

In conclusion, "Exit Unicorns" is a captivating and enchanting novel that will appeal to readers of fantasy, historical fiction, and romance alike. With its lush prose, memorable characters, and thought-provoking themes, it is a must-read for anyone looking to escape into a world of wonder and magic.

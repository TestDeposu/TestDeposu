# All This Time

**Author:** Mikki Daughtry  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

**A Heart-Wrenching Tale of Love, Loss, and Redemption - "All This Time" Review**

"All This Time" by Mikki Daughtry is a poignant and thought-provoking novel that masterfully weaves together themes of love, loss, and self-discovery. This captivating story, penned by the same author behind the #1 New York Times bestseller "Five Feet Apart", is a gripping romance that explores the complexities of the human experience in the aftermath of tragedy.

The narrative revolves around Kyle, a high school graduate who is reeling from a devastating loss. His world is turned upside down when Kimberly, his long-time girlfriend, breaks up with him on the eve of their graduation party. The cruel twist of fate is compounded when Kyle's car crashes, leaving him with a brain injury and forcing him to confront an unbearable truth: Kimberly is dead. As Kyle struggles to make sense of his new reality, he finds solace in Marley, a young woman who is grappling with her own demons.

Through the intertwined stories of Kyle and Marley, Daughtry deftly exposes the tender and often brutal nature of love, loss, and grief. The author's writing is characterized by a poignant simplicity, which belies the depth of emotion and complexity of the characters' experiences. As the narrative unfolds, readers are immersed in a world of fragility and hope, where even in the darkest moments, there is always a glimmer of light.

One of the most striking aspects of "All This Time" is its thought-provoking exploration of grief and trauma. Daughtry's portrayal of Kyle's emotional journey is deeply nuanced, showcasing the raw pain and confusion that accompany loss. Similarly, Marley's struggles with guilt and self-doubt serve as a powerful reminder of the long-lasting impact of tragedy on individuals and relationships.

Throughout the book, Daughtry crafts a narrative that is both deeply personal and universally relatable. Her characters are multidimensional and authentic, their experiences resonating with readers who have faced similar struggles. The author's writing is equally evocative, using concise and evocative language to convey the complexity of her characters' emotions.

The character development in "All This Time" is outstanding, with Kyle and Marley emerging as fully fleshed-out individuals with their own distinct voices and experiences. The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, adding depth and richness to the narrative without overshadowing the protagonists.

While the book's central question - can you find true love after losing everything - may seem deceptively simple, Daughtry's exploration of this theme is multifaceted and thought-provoking. As Kyle and Marley navigate the complexities of their relationships, they are forced to confront the darker aspects of love and loss.

Ultimately, "All This Time" is a poignant and unforgettable novel that will resonate with readers long after the final page has been turned. Daughtry's writing is lyrical and evocative, conveying the complexity and fragility of the human experience with remarkable sensitivity and nuance.

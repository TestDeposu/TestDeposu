# It Had to Be You (Chicago Stars, #1)

**Author:** Susan Elizabeth Phillips  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

It Had to Be You, the first installment in Susan Elizabeth Phillips' beloved Chicago Stars series, is a captivating tale of opposites attract that will leave readers spellbound and yearning for more. This delightful romance novel tells the story of Phoebe Somerville, a stunning and confident New York socialite who inherits the Chicago Stars football team, and Dan Calebow, the team's rugged and charming head coach.

From the onset, it's clear that Phoebe and Dan are worlds apart. Phoebe, with her sharp wit and cosmopolitan charm, is a fish out of water in the male-dominated world of football, while Dan, with his old-school values and sexist attitude, is the epitome of a traditional jock. Yet, despite their differences, they can't help but be drawn to each other, their initial animosity blossoming into a fiery passion that threatens to consume them both.

One of the standout aspects of It Had to Be You is Phillips' masterful handling of her characters. Phoebe and Dan are multidimensional and relatable, with rich inner lives and nuanced motivations. Phoebe, in particular, is a triumph, a strong and independent woman who refuses to be intimidated by the patriarchal world of football. Her humor, intelligence, and vulnerability make her an instantly lovable protagonist, and her slow-burning romance with Dan is both sweet and sizzling.

Phillips' writing style is, as always, a joy to behold. Her prose is witty and engaging, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the human heart. She has a remarkable ability to balance humor and pathos, effortlessly shifting between laugh-out-loud moments and more poignant, emotional scenes. The result is a narrative that's both entertaining and affecting, a true page-turner that will keep readers up late into the night.

The author's use of dialogue is also noteworthy, with conversations that feel natural and authentic. The banter between Phoebe and Dan is particularly delightful, their verbal sparring matches crackling with tension and humor. Phillips has a keen ear for the way people speak, and her characters' interactions are always believable and engaging.

At its core, It Had to Be You is a story about the power of love to transform and redeem us. Phoebe and Dan, despite their initial reservations, come to realize that they need each other, that their differences are what make their relationship strong. It's a beautiful, uplifting message, and one that Phillips conveys with her usual sensitivity and flair.

In short, It Had to Be You is a must-read for fans of romance, football, and strong, sassy heroines. With its witty dialogue, engaging characters, and heartfelt story, it's a novel that will captivate and delight, leaving readers eager to return to the world of the Chicago Stars. So why not give it a try? You won't be disappointed.

# A Perilous Undertaking (Veronica Speedwell, #2)

**Author:** Deanna Raybourn  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

In "A Perilous Undertaking", Deanna Raybourn weaves a captivating tale of mystery, intrigue, and wit, set against the vibrant backdrop of Victorian London. This second installment in the Veronica Speedwell series is a masterful blend of clever plotting, memorable characters, and historical atmosphere.

At the heart of the story is Veronica Speedwell, a brilliant and fearless adventuress, who finds herself embroiled in a complex web of murder, deception, and secrets. When Lady Sundridge, a mysterious and enigmatic figure, approaches Veronica with a plea to save the life of Miles Ramsforth, accused of murdering his mistress, Veronica is drawn into a perilous game of cat and mouse.

As Veronica delves deeper into the case, she must navigate a labyrinthine world of Bohemian artists, royal connections, and subterranean grottos, all while racing against time to uncover the truth. Alongside her trusty colleague, Stoker, a natural historian with a talent for observation, Veronica must use her remarkable intellect and quick wit to unravel the tangled threads of the mystery.

One of the standout features of Raybourn's writing is her ability to evoke the spirit of Victorian London, with its gaslit streets, opulent drawing rooms, and seedy underbelly. Her vivid descriptions transport the reader to a bygone era, immersing them in the sights, sounds, and smells of the city.

But what truly sets "A Perilous Undertaking" apart is its cast of characters. Veronica Speedwell is a true original – a fiercely independent, clever, and resourceful heroine, who defies convention at every turn. Her partnership with Stoker is a highlight of the book, their banter and repartee adding a delightful layer of humor and warmth to the narrative.

Throughout the book, Raybourn's writing is characterized by its intelligence, wit, and style. Her prose is elegant and engaging, with a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the period. The pacing is expertly managed, with a narrative that twists and turns in unexpected ways, keeping the reader on the edge of their seat.

In short, "A Perilous Undertaking" is a triumph of historical mystery writing, with a unique and captivating heroine, a complex and engaging plot, and a richly atmospheric setting. If you're a fan of clever mysteries, strong female protagonists, and Victorian London, then this book is an absolute must-read.

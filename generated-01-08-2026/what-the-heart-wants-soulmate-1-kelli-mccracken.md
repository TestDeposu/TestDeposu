# What the Heart Wants (Soulmate, #1)

**Author:** Kelli McCracken  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

"What the Heart Wants" by Kelli McCracken is a captivating novel that seamlessly weaves together themes of destiny, mystery, and romance, setting the stage for an epic soulmate love story. The first installment in the Soulmate series, this 2011 release showcases McCracken's remarkable ability to create an immersive world that is equal parts enchanting and thought-provoking.

At its core, "What the Heart Wants" is a story about two individuals, Heaven Lewis and Dylan McBride, whose lives take an unexpected turn when they cross paths in Los Angeles. Heaven, a college student seeking a much-needed break, finds herself in the midst of chaos only to stumble upon the charismatic rock star Dylan. The initial encounter sparks a deep, unexplainable connection between them, one that transcends the boundaries of a conventional romance.

As their bond deepens, McCracken masterfully introduces an intriguing element of mysticism, hinting at the presence of psychics and elements that seem to play a vital role in their relationship. The narrative skillfully balances romance, mystery, and fantasy, keeping readers engaged and invested in the unfolding story.

One of the most striking aspects of "What the Heart Wants" is McCracken's lyrical writing style, which is both poetic and evocative. Her use of language is expressive, allowing her to capture the nuances of human emotions with remarkable precision. The characters are multidimensional, making it easy for readers to emotionally connect with them as they navigate the complexities of love, trust, and destiny.

The pacing is deliberate and measured, adding to the sense of anticipation and tension that pervades the story. As the stakes grow higher, McCracken cleverly raises questions about the nature of reality, the power of the soul, and the mysteries of the universe, leaving readers to contemplate the answers alongside the characters.

In terms of its themes, "What the Heart Wants" explores the idea that our experiences are shaped by the choices we make, but also by the connections we form with others. McCracken suggests that the choices we make can lead us down unexpected paths, sometimes forcing us to confront the unknown and the unseen.

Throughout the novel, McCracken's narrative voice is confident, drawing from a deep well of emotional insight and spiritual wisdom. The writing is rich in imagery, making it easy to visualize the world and characters she has created.

In conclusion, "What the Heart Wants" is a captivating tale of love, mystery, and self-discovery that explores the intricate dance between fate, human emotion, and the unknown. Kelli McCracken's engaging narrative, coupled with her poetic writing style, makes this novel an unforgettable read.

# Dark Challenge (Dark, #5)

**Author:** Christine Feehan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**Dark Challenge (Dark #5) by Christine Feehan: A Savage Love Story That Burns Bright**

Christine Feehan’s *Dark Challenge* is a masterclass in dark romance—a tale where passion ignites like wildfire against the backdrop of ancient curses, forbidden bonds, and the relentless pull of destiny. As the fifth installment in the *Dark* series, this novel doesn’t just continue the saga; it deepens the stakes, sharpening the edge of its brooding hero and the woman who dares to challenge him.

At its core, *Dark Challenge* is a story about the collision of two forces: the untamed, untouchable Julian Savage and the enigmatic Desari, whose very presence disrupts the equilibrium of his existence. Julian is no ordinary hero—he is a Carpathian hunter, a warrior cursed with a soul as dark as the night, his emotions locked away behind a wall of ice. His name is as formidable as his reputation: a man who walks alone, untethered even from his own twin. But when Desari’s voice washes over him like a storm, Julian’s carefully constructed world shatters. For the first time in centuries, he feels—*desires*—and the hunger to claim her is a force he cannot ignore.

Feehan’s prose is as intoxicating as it is visceral. She doesn’t just write romance; she *performs* it, weaving sensuality into every sentence without veering into cliché. The chemistry between Julian and Desari is electric, a slow-burning tension that crackles with danger. Julian’s possessiveness is almost suffocating, his need to protect Desari warring with his darker instincts to *own* her. Desari, meanwhile, is no passive love interest—she is a force of nature in her own right, wielding powers that defy explanation and challenging Julian’s every assumption. Their dynamic is a dance of power and submission, trust and betrayal, where every touch, every glance, feels like a gamble.

What makes *Dark Challenge* truly compelling is Feehan’s ability to balance raw emotion with supernatural intrigue. Julian’s past is a shadowy labyrinth—his curse, his isolation, his twisted loyalty to a coven that demands his silence. Desari, too, carries secrets, her own mysterious abilities hinting at a destiny far greater than she realizes. The tension between them isn’t just romantic; it’s existential. Will their bond save Julian from his curse, or will it doom them both? Feehan keeps readers on the edge of their seats, never letting the story rest, always pushing the boundaries of what it means to love—and to be loved—when the world itself is against you.

The novel’s strength lies in its unapologetic exploration of obsession and redemption. Julian is a hero who is as much a villain as he is a savior, his flaws as integral to his character as his strengths. Desari, for her part, is a woman who refuses to be defined by anyone else’s expectations, her strength and vulnerability woven seamlessly into her arc. Together, they create a love story that is as haunting as it is hopeful, a testament to the idea that even the most broken souls can find salvation in the right hands.

Feehan’s signature style shines here—her ability to blend steamy romance with supernatural elements without ever sacrificing depth. The world she builds is rich with lore, the Carpathians and their ancient rituals feeling as real as the emotional turmoil of her characters. The pacing is relentless, the prose lush, and the payoff? A love story that lingers long after the last page.

If you’ve followed the *Dark* series, *Dark Challenge* delivers the emotional payoff you’ve been waiting for. If you’re new to Feehan’s work, this is the perfect entry point—a story where love is both a weapon and a weapon. It’s dark, it’s dangerous, and it’s impossible to look away from.

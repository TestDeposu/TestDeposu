# One Small Sacrifice (Shadows of New York, #1)

**Author:** Hilary Davidson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

"One Small Sacrifice" by Hilary Davidson is a thought-provoking and deeply theological exploration of Ezekiel's Temple and liturgy. At its core, this book challenges existing theological understanding and forces readers to re-examine their perspectives on the cross, atonement, and the role of sacrifices in the Messiah's mission. Davidson masterfully navigates the intricacies of biblical revelation, weaving together threads of scripture to create a rich tapestry of understanding.

Davidson's work is as much about laying the groundwork as it is about offering radical insights. Her detailed analysis of Tabernacle and Temple rituals is meticulous and thorough, but the payoff is well worth the effort. Readers will find themselves transported to a world of ancient Israelite practice and worship, where every aspect of life was intricately bound to the divine.

But "One Small Sacrifice" is not merely a dry historical or academic exercise. Rather, it is a deeply spiritual exploration of God's revelation to Ezekiel and its implications for our understanding of the Messiah's role as God's suffering servant. Davidson deftly navigates the nuances of biblical interpretation, pointing readers toward the startling conclusion that many have unwittingly diminished the significance of the Day of Atonement by assuming that the cross has fulfilled all functions of the court altar.

One of the most striking aspects of Davidson's work is her ability to highlight the theological implications of Ezekiel's ritual for diverse theological traditions. For Roman Catholics, she points out that Tabernacle typology suggests that the sacrifice is not consumed within, a reality that necessitates a radical re-evaluation of the cross's relationship to the altar. For Jewish readers, she challenges their understanding of the eternity of God's Word by pointing out the significance of Ezekiel's ritual changes to Mosaic Law.

Throughout the book, Davidson's writing is clear, concise, and engaging, making complex theological concepts accessible to readers of all backgrounds. Her prose is as precise as it is persuasive, weaving together disparate threads of scriptural evidence to create a stunning tapestry of understanding.

Ultimately, "One Small Sacrifice" is a testament to the power of biblical exploration and the potential for revelation to reshape our understanding of God and the world. Davidson's work invites readers to step into the mystery of Ezekiel's Temple and emerge transformed by the experience.

I highly recommend this book for readers who are willing to engage with complex theological ideas and scriptural analysis. For those seeking to deepen their understanding of the Messiah's role, the cross, and atonement, "One Small Sacrifice" is an invaluable resource.

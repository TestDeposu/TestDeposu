# A Work in Progress

**Author:** Connor Franta  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

A Work in Progress, the memoir by YouTube sensation Connor Franta, is a captivating and introspective journey of self-discovery, creativity, and the pursuit of one's passions. This New York Times bestseller and Goodreads Choice Award winner for Best Memoir/Autobiography is a must-read for anyone who has ever felt the need to break free from the shackles of societal expectations and forge their own path.

Connor's writing style is refreshingly candid and humorous, making the reader feel like they are having a conversation with an old friend. He shares his struggles with identity, body image, and sexuality with unflinching honesty, offering a unique glimpse into the mind of a young person navigating the complexities of adolescence and early adulthood. His stories of growing up in a small Midwestern town, as one of four children, are relatable and endearing, and his decision to pursue his creative passions in his early twenties is a testament to the power of following one's dreams.

One of the most striking aspects of this book is its exploration of the digital era and its impact on young people. As a YouTube personality, Connor has spent years navigating the complexities of online fame, and his insights into the world of social media are both fascinating and thought-provoking. He writes about the pressures of maintaining a public image, the importance of staying true to oneself, and the need to cultivate a sense of purpose and meaning in a world that can often seem overwhelming.

Throughout the book, Connor's humility and sense of humor shine through, making his story feel both authentic and accessible. He is unafraid to share his flaws and mistakes, and his willingness to be vulnerable is a powerful reminder that it's okay to not have all the answers. The inclusion of full-color photography and childhood clippings adds a touching and personal touch to the narrative, making the reader feel like they are experiencing Connor's journey alongside him.

At its core, A Work in Progress is a book about the importance of embracing one's true self and pursuing one's passions, no matter how difficult the journey may seem. Connor's story is a testament to the power of resilience, creativity, and determination, and his words will resonate with anyone who has ever felt like they don't quite fit in. This book is a must-have for anyone inspired by Connor's coming-of-age journey, and its message of hope and self-acceptance will stay with readers long after they finish the final page.

# Stronghold (Dragon Star, #1)

**Author:** Melanie Rawn  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1990

In "Stronghold," the first installment of Melanie Rawn's captivating Dragon Star trilogy, the world of Sunrunner is once again set ablaze with magic, mystery, and the unrelenting struggle for power. This mesmerizing tale is a masterful continuation of Rawn's bestselling Dragon Prince series, plunging readers into a realm of wonder and trepidation.

At the heart of this epic narrative lies the unyielding battle for survival, as the forces of darkness converge upon the land, threatening to destroy the fragile peace forged by High Prince Rohan and Lord Andry. As the dark invasion force gains momentum, the very fabric of their world begins to unravel, testing the mettle of the Sunrunners and the valiant warriors who stand by their side.

Rawn's writing style is a true marvel, weaving a complex tapestry of characters, plot twists, and magical realms that will leave even the most discerning reader enchanted. Her prose is evocative and immersive, conjuring the scorching deserts, ancient strongholds, and mystical landscapes that serve as the backdrop for this gripping tale.

One of the most striking aspects of "Stronghold" is its exploration of the human condition, as Rawn delves into the intricacies of power, loyalty, and the unyielding pursuit of justice. Through the eyes of Rohan, Andry, and Pol, we witness the struggles of leadership, the weight of responsibility, and the unrelenting quest for peace in a world torn asunder by darkness and despair.

As the stakes grow higher, Rawn masterfully ratchets up the tension, introducing a cast of complex characters, each with their own motivations and secrets. The world of Sunrunner is richly detailed, with a history that spans centuries, and a magic system that is both fascinating and terrifying.

Throughout the narrative, Rawn's love for the world she has created is palpable, and her passion for storytelling is evident on every page. "Stronghold" is a testament to her skill as a writer, a sweeping epic that will captivate fans of fantasy and adventure alike.

In conclusion, "Stronghold" is a must-read for anyone who has ever been enchanted by the world of Sunrunner. With its intricate characters, pulse-pounding action, and richly detailed landscapes, this book is a true masterpiece of fantasy literature. If you're a fan of Melanie Rawn's work, or simply looking for a compelling tale to sweep you off your feet, then "Stronghold" is the perfect choice.

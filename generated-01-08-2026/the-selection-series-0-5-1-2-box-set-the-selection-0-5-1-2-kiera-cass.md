# The Selection Series 0.5, 1-2 Box Set (The Selection, #0.5, 1-2)

**Author:** Kiera Cass  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Unknown

**A Royal Romp: The Selection Series 0.5, 1-2 Box Set**

In the world of young adult fiction, the royal romance has become a staple, and Kiera Cass's The Selection series is at the forefront of this genre. This box set, containing The Elite (#1.5, also known as The Selection, #0.5, 1-2) offers readers an in-depth look at the events leading up to the coveted royal throne, as well as the early exploits of America Singer, the lovable and feisty protagonist.

Cass's writing is a masterclass in effortless prose. Her characters are multi-dimensional, and each one brings a unique set of traits that makes them relatable and endearing. America, the talented vocalist and reluctant princess, is at the center of this story. Her determination and resilience in the face of adversity are admirable, and her vulnerability makes her easy to root for. 

America's journey is fraught with challenges, from navigating the complexities of royalty to confronting her own feelings about Prince Maxon and the intricacies of her relationships with her family and friends. Cass expertly crafts each obstacle to create tension and suspense, keeping readers on the edge of their seats.

One of the strengths of The Selection series is its focus on social commentary. Cass explores themes of class, equality, and the objectification of women through the lens of a royal court. The author's astute observations on these issues make for thought-provoking reading, and the way she weaves them into the narrative is seamless.

The setting of the book - Illyria's royal court - is richly detailed, with a unique blend of futuristic and traditional elements. The opulence and extravagance of the palace, coupled with the restrictive rules and formalities, create a claustrophobic atmosphere that adds to the tension and America's growing unease.

While some readers may find the love triangle aspect of the story a bit too convenient, Cass's writing skillfully turns a potentially clichéd trope into a nuanced exploration of human emotions. The romance is a slow-burn, with moments of intense passion and heartwarming tenderness.

Overall, this box set provides readers with a captivating glimpse into America's early days in the palace, as well as a deeper understanding of the complexities that surround her. Kiera Cass's writing is engaging, her characters are well-developed, and the themes are timely and thought-provoking.

**Overall Rating: 4.5/5**

In short, this is a delightful read for anyone who enjoys royal romance, social commentary, and strong female characters. If you're a fan of dystopian or fantasy fiction, you'll also find plenty to love here.

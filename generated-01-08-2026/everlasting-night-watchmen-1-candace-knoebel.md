# Everlasting (Night Watchmen, #1)

**Author:** Candace Knoebel  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

Shadows are rarely empty. In the atmospheric debut novel Everlasting, the first installment in the Night Watchmen series published in 2014, author Candace Knoebel invites readers into a clandestine world where the boundary between the living and the immortal is constantly policed, tested, and blurred. While the exact page count remains a quiet detail, the scope of Knoebel's imaginative universe is vast, anchoring its weight not in physical thickness, but in the dense, emotional resonance of its prose.

At its core, the main theme of Everlasting explores the heavy burden of duty juxtaposed against the intoxicating pull of forbidden desire. Knoebel crafts a narrative where the concept of time is both a weapon and a prison. The Night Watchmen are not mere paranormal enforcers; they are weary guardians caught in an eternal war, bound by strict codes that forbid attachment. Yet, it is precisely this human vulnerability—the desperate need for connection amidst endless existence—that drives the plot forward. Knoebel uses her supernatural framework to examine timeless human questions: What is a life worth if you cannot truly feel it? And how much of yourself are you willing to sacrifice to keep the dark at bay?

Knoebel’s writing style is defined by its lyrical darkness and sensory precision. She possesses a distinct ability to make the supernatural feel intimately tactile. Her prose leans heavily into mood, utilizing shadows, cold air, and heartbeat-rhythms to build tension rather than relying solely on action sequences. The pacing is deliberate, allowing readers to marinate in the psychological toll of the characters' immortality. Dialogue is sharp and laced with unspoken tension, revealing as much in what is left unsaid as in the declarations made. Knoebel avoids the common pitfalls of overly saturated urban fantasy genres by grounding her narrative in poetic introspection, elevating the text from a standard genre piece into a moody, character-driven exploration of fate and free will.

For readers who appreciate dark romance, intricate world-building, and protagonists who carry the weight of the world on their shoulders, Everlasting serves as a compelling gateway. Knoebel establishes a rich, immersive foundation for the Night Watchmen series, leaving an enduring impression that lingers long after the final page is turned.

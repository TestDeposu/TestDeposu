# Ghost Squad

**Author:** Claribel A. Ortega  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

When Pixar’s *Coco* meets the nostalgic supernatural thrills of *Stranger Things* with a splash of *Ghostbusters* thrown into the cauldron, you get Claribel A. Ortega’s dazzling debut novel, *Ghost Squad*. Published in 2020, this spooky middle-grade adventure wastes no time plunging its readers into a vibrant, high-stakes world where ancestral magic, folklore, and middle-school anxieties collide under the eerie glow of a St. Augustine Halloween. 

At its core, *Ghost Squad* is a deeply moving exploration of legacy, grief, and the enduring power of family. For protagonist Lucely Luna, the supernatural is not merely a fun aesthetic or a seasonal fright; it is a literal family business. Steeped in Dominican-American culture and folklore, the narrative uses the spirit realm as a profound metaphor for memory and connection. Lucely’s relationship with the ghosts of her ancestors and her vibrant, firefly-like spirit companions grounds the narrative in emotional truth. When a seemingly innocent spell cast with her best friend Syd accidentally awakens a legion of malicious, malevolent spirits, the ensuing chaos acts as a catalyst for the characters to confront their deepest fears and insecurities. Ortega brilliantly weaves cultural identity into the very fabric of the plot, making the traditions, foods, and familial bonds just as magical as the spells being cast. 

Ortega’s authorial style is a masterclass in pacing, tone, and voice. She captures the authentic cadence of modern middle-school friendship—complete with banter, loyalty, and fierce protectiveness—while maintaining an atmosphere that is genuinely spine-chilling. The prose is snappy, highly visual, and effortlessly cinematic, moving at a breathless pace that keeps pages turning late into the night. Yet, what truly elevates Ortega’s style is her ability to balance genuine scares with laugh-out-loud humor and overwhelming warmth. The introduction of Syd’s no-nonsense witch grandmother, Babette, and her scene-stealing, tubby tabby cat, Chunk, injects a delightful dose of whimsy and grounding domesticity into an otherwise perilous supernatural showdown. Ortega does not talk down to her young audience; instead, she respects their emotional intelligence, tackling heavy themes of loss and responsibility while keeping the adventure irresistibly fun.

Ultimately, *Ghost Squad* is more than just a spooky seasonal read—it is a triumphant celebration of heritage, bravery, and the chosen families that help us fight our inner and outer demons. Claribel A. Ortega has crafted a spellbinding page-turner that lingers long after the final page is turned, leaving readers eagerly awaiting her next literary conjuring.

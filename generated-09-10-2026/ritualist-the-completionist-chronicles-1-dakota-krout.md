# Ritualist (The Completionist Chronicles, #1)

**Author:** Dakota Krout  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Unknown

In a literary landscape overflowing with recycled tropes and predictable power fantasies, Dakota Krout’s *Ritualist*—the explosive first installment of *The Completionist Chronicles*—arrives not merely to participate in the LitRPG genre, but to ruthlessly dissect and elevate it. Krout crafts a world where the boundary between life and virtual eternity is blurred by bureaucratic malice, creating a narrative engine that drives forward with relentless momentum. At its core, the book explores a central, biting theme: the commodification of existence and the bureaucratic nightmare of optimization, where a human soul is reduced to a mere ledger of statistics, achievements, and designated utility.

The story follows Joseph "Joe" Reid, a man facing an untimely and irreversible medical exit from the physical world. Seeking a fresh start, Joe chooses to upload his consciousness into Eternia, a sprawling, hyper-realistic fantasy MMORPG. However, the system is rarely fair to those who look beyond the obvious paths. Instead of selecting a conventional, combat-heavy class that promises immediate glory, Joe stubbornly embraces the path of the Ritualist—a notoriously underappreciated, complex, and seemingly weak profession centered on preparation, arcane circles, and esoteric magic. 

What follows is a brilliant subversion of the classic hero’s journey. Krout’s protagonist is no chosen one destined to save the realm with a flick of a legendary sword. Instead, Joe is a meticulous planner, a stubborn data-cruncher, and a professional completionist who refuses to leave a single stone unturned or a single quest uncompleted. Through Joe, Krout examines the psychology of the modern gamer: the obsessive compulsion to fill every progress bar, uncover every hidden mechanic, and break the rules of the game just to see what happens. The theme of perseverance in the face of systemic adversity shines brightly as Joe turns a universally mocked class into a terrifying engine of arcane might, simply through sheer wit and stubborn refusal to quit.

Krout’s authorial style is the true secret weapon of the novel. Eschewing the dry, overly technical info-dumps that often plague the genre, Krout writes with a punchy, irreverent, and deeply comedic voice. The prose is snappy, filled with sharp self-awareness and situational humor that keeps the pacing brisk. Yet, the levity never undermines the genuine stakes. When the system turns hostile, or when NPC interactions reveal the startling depth and hidden darkness of Eternia, the tension snaps tight. Krout balances crunchy game mechanics—stat sheets, skill trees, and experience points—with a genuinely compelling narrative structure, making the math feel as thrilling as a sword clash. 

Furthermore, the world-building is remarkably tight. Eternia is not just a backdrop for numbers to go up; it feels lived-in, dangerous, and pulsing with ancient secrets. The supporting cast, from snarky magical entities to bewildered dungeon denizens, adds rich texture to Joe's solo-focused obsession. 

*Ritualist* is a triumphant opening salvo that proves progression fantasy can possess both a quick-witted soul and intellectual depth. Dakota Krout has engineered a brilliantly addictive reading experience that rewards curiosity, celebrates the underdog, and redefines what it means to truly complete a journey. Whether you are a hardened veteran of virtual realms or a newcomer looking for a clever twist on classic fantasy, this book demands your attention—and your completion.

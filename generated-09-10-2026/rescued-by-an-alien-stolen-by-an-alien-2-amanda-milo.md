# Rescued by an Alien (Stolen by an Alien, #2)

**Author:** Amanda Milo  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

When science fiction takes itself too seriously, it often forgets the most crucial element of storytelling: sheer, unadulterated fun. Enter Amanda Milo and her 2024 riotous installment, Rescued by an Alien, the second book in the Stolen by an Alien series. Milo returns to her trademark universe with a narrative so wildly inventive and delightfully chaotic that it practically demands to be read in a single, breathless sitting. 

At the heart of this sprawling cosmic adventure is a brilliant subversion of classic space opera tropes. The story centers on Agrotharn, a warrior whose very identity is tied to his mighty broadsword. When this sacred weapon is stolen, Agrotharn is thrust into a galaxy-spanning quest that defies all conventional expectations. Milo uses this premise to explore a central theme: the fragility of physical and cultural identity when stripped of one's symbols of power. As Agrotharn watches his beard fall out, his muscles shrink, and his eyes begin to glow due to mysterious, flowing magic, Milo pokes affectionate fun at the hyper-masculine barbarian archetype. It is a hilarious yet surprisingly tender meditation on what remains of a hero when the trappings of his heroism are ruthlessly stripped away.

Yet, to say that Rescued by an Alien is merely about a bald, shrinking barbarian would be a grave injustice to Milo’s maximalist plotting. The author weaves multiple narrative threads with the dexterity of a seasoned mad scientist. While Agrotharn deals with his magical identity crisis, characters like Fred and Lank find themselves unceremoniously abducted by space pirates. This forces Agrotharn into an unlikely, tension-fueled partnership with Captain Astridia. Concurrently, the stakes are raised to planetary proportions as an alien armada sets its sights on Earth. To repel this extraterrestrial threat, Milo summons a veritable Avengers assemble of quirky cult-favorites: the Death Valley Barbarians, Xargon’s Xenodian hunters, and the Lady Witch Veronika—all functioning with or without the questionable help of Nimrod. As if this cosmic gumbo weren't spicy enough, a visit from the bona fide Continuum Keepers threatens to break reality entirely. 

Milo’s writing style is unapologetically energetic, humorous, and fast-paced. She writes with a wink to the reader, balancing high-stakes galactic warfare with laugh-out-loud situational comedy and deeply endearing character dynamics. Her prose doesn't dwell on dry exposition; instead, it plunges the reader headfirst into the neon-lit, danger-riddled chaos of her universe. The world-building is delightfully bonkers, blending sci-fi elements like alien armadas and space pirates with whimsical fantasy tropes like magic veins and broadswords. It is this fearless genre-blending that defines Milo’s distinct authorial voice. She knows exactly how ridiculous her premises are, and she leans into them with joyous abandon, creating a reading experience that is as refreshing as it is entertaining.

Rescued by an Alien is a triumphant testament to the joy of speculative fiction. It proves that you can have sweeping stakes, intergalactic invasions, and existential identity crises while still having an absolute blast along the way. Amanda Milo has crafted a vibrant, chaotic, and heartwarming page-turner that will leave fans of quirky sci-fi romance and cosmic adventure thoroughly satisfied and eagerly awaiting whatever madness she unleashes next.

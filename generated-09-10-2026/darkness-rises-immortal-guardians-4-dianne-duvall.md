# Darkness Rises (Immortal Guardians, #4)

**Author:** Dianne Duvall  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

Dianne Duvall has long carved out a distinct and fiercely loyal following in the realm of urban fantasy and paranormal romance, and her celebrated Immortal Guardians series stands as a testament to her narrative stamina. With the rerelease of Darkness Rises, readers are invited back into a high-stakes world where the supernatural is woven seamlessly into the fabric of modern danger. At its core, the novel explores a timeless yet thrillingly executed theme: the intersection of raw human resilience and ancient, superhuman power, bound together by the crucible of a global conspiracy. 

Duvall’s greatest strength lies in her ability to balance intense, adrenaline-fueled action with genuine levity and warmth. In Darkness Rises, this signature style is immediately apparent in the dynamic between the mortal protagonist, Krysta Linz, and the centuries-old immortal warrior, Étienne d’Alençon. Krysta is not the typical damsel in distress often found prowling the margins of paranormal fiction; she is a battle-hardened, self-reliant assassin who has stared down monsters and lived to tell the tale. Her fierce independence forms the bedrock of the book's central tension. When she crosses paths with Étienne—a man whose millennia of combat experience should make him entirely self-sufficient—the power dynamic shifts into something far more nuanced. 

Étienne is the quintessential romance hero of Duvall’s creation: powerful, protective, and thoroughly captivated by a woman who refuses to be coddled. Yet, rather than devolving into tired tropes of possessiveness, their relationship blooms through mutual respect, shared peril, and an undeniable, sizzling chemistry. Duvall excels at forced proximity, using external threats to forge rapid, ironclad bonds between her characters. As Krysta and Étienne navigate the treacherous waters of a shadow organization hunting immortals, their growing affection feels hard-earned rather than merely convenient. 

Beyond the romance, what elevates Darkness Rises is Duvall’s world-building and pacing. The author understands that a supernatural romance is only as compelling as the stakes surrounding the lovers. By introducing a clandestine enemy bent on exploiting the existence of immortals, Duvall expands the scope of her universe, transforming a localized monster-hunt into a sweeping conspiracy thriller. The action sequences are choreographed with kinetic precision, keeping readers on the edge of their seats as blades clash and secrets unravel. 

Moreover, Duvall infuses her narrative with her trademark humor and a profound appreciation for the found family trope. The banter crackles with wit, providing necessary breathing room amidst the grim realities of the war against vampires. Characters bounce off one another with a natural, familial ease that will warm the hearts of dedicated series fans and warmly welcome newcomers alike. 

Darkness Rises is a masterclass in blending pulse-pounding urban fantasy with deeply emotional, character-driven romance. It proves that even in a world shadowed by ancient predators and human greed, the brightest lights are forged in the fires of courage and love. For anyone seeking a fast-paced escape filled with strong heroines, charming warriors, and relentless suspense, Duvall’s latest offering is an absolute must-read.

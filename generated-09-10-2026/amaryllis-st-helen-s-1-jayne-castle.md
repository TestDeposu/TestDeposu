# Amaryllis (St. Helen's, #1)

**Author:** Jayne Castle  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Step into the vibrant, neon-lit underworld of St. Helen's, a distant Earth colony left stranded from its home world, where high-tech intrigue collides with deeply grounded human passion. In Amaryllis, the stellar first installment of her celebrated St. Helen's series, bestselling author Jayne Castle—writing under her acclaimed alter ego—delivers a masterclass in futuristic paranormal romantic suspense. Known for crafting worlds where danger and desire walk hand in hand, Castle effortlessly transports readers to a frontier society governed by unusual psychic rules, corporate espionage, and the unpredictable alchemy of true love.

At the narrative's beating heart is Amaryllis Lark, a premier psychic detective whose professional facade is as sharp as her investigative instincts. Clad in a crisp business suit and projecting an aura of cool, calculated detachment, Amaryllis navigates the shadowy underbelly of St. Helen's with clinical precision. Enter Lucas Trent, the rugged, no-nonsense head of Lodestar Exploration. Initially convinced that prim and proper women hold zero appeal, Lucas approaches Amaryllis with a strictly professional proposition: hire her exceptional psychic talents to help bust a ruthless corporate thief. 

What begins as a standard investigative partnership quickly spirals into something far more volatile. Castle excels at the slow-burn friction between opposites, playing on the delicious trope of professional resistance giving way to undeniable attraction. When a seemingly straightforward corporate theft suddenly pivots into a wild, high-stakes murder investigation, Amaryllis and Lucas are forced into a relentless game of cat-and-mouse. The danger they face is palpable, but the real hazard lies in the explosive, red-hot chemistry igniting between them.

Castle’s signature style shines brightest in her effortless world-building. She populates St. Helen's with eccentric psychic phenomena, sharp corporate hierarchies, and a palpable frontier atmosphere that feels both delightfully retro-futuristic and modern. The dialogue is snappy, the pacing is relentlessly brisk, and the romantic tension crackles off the page with every dangerous twist and turn. Amaryllis is no damsel in distress; she is fiercely competent, intellectually formidable, and wonderfully matched by Lucas’s gritty determination. 

For fans of genre-bending fiction that seamlessly marries hard-boiled detective work with paranormal romance and thrilling suspense, Amaryllis is an absolute triumph. Castle proves once again why she is a master of entertainment, delivering a stellar, star-dusted excursion where love truly defies the most incredible odds. Whether you are a longtime devotee of the author's expansive universes or a newcomer looking for a riveting sci-fi romance, this opening chapter of the St. Helen's saga will leave you breathless and turning pages long into the night.

# Gracie's Touch (Zion Warriors, #1)

**Author:** S.E. Smith  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

S.E. Smith has carved out a formidable niche in the realm of sci-fi romance, and with Gracie's Touch, the inaugural installment of the Zion Warriors series, she delivers a high-stakes, time-defying adventure that pulses with both adrenaline and heart. Weighing in at a brisk 71,214 words, this 2015 novel wastes no time launching readers into a sprawling universe where survival demands sacrifice, and love blooms in the unlikeliest of vacuum-sealed corners. 

At the pulsating heart of the novel is the central theme of displacement and resilience. Gracie Jones is not your typical damsel in distress; she is a myth walking, a teenage savior who effectively turned the tide of an apocalyptic Earth invasion by the Alluthans, only to wake up centuries later on a desolate moon in a forgotten galaxy. Smith brilliantly explores the psychological toll of temporal displacement. Gracie’s profound grief—mourning a world, a family, and a timeline that has crumbled into dust—gives the narrative a grounded, emotional gravity. She is a woman untethered from history, forced to navigate a universe that views her as a legendary relic rather than a living, breathing soul.

Enter Kordon Jefe, the stoic and formidable Commander of the Confederation of Planets. When Kordon encounters Gracie, the dynamic shifts from a solitary tale of survival into a compelling study of opposites colliding. Smith uses their burgeoning relationship to dissect the healing power of connection. Kordon is the anchor to Gracie’s storm, navigating her fierce independence and deep-seated trauma with a protective instinct that avoids crossing into caricature. Their chemistry crackles with a palpable tension, blending the sweet ache of emotional recovery with explicit, passionate romance that satisfies the genre's demands without overshadowing the overarching plot.

Stylistically, S.E. Smith remains a master of pacing and world-building. Known for her signature blend of vivid imagery and lighthearted humor, she keeps the narrative propulsive. Even as the threat of the Alluthans resurfaces—stronger, deadlier, and threatening the Confederation itself—the book never feels bogged down by heavy exposition. Smith balances macro-level galactic warfare with intimate character beats, ensuring the fate of the universe feels just as urgent as the emotional reconciliation between her two protagonists. 

Gracie's Touch is a quintessential sci-fi romance triumph. It offers readers a thrilling cocktail of extraterrestrial action, spine-tingling danger, and a slow-burn-turned-inferno romance that proves love can conquer even the vastness of time and space. For fans of alien romance, time-travel tropes, and heroines who refuse to break under the weight of history, Smith’s latest offering is an unputdownable treat that sets a high bar for the Zion Warriors series.

# Wolves

**Author:** D.J. Molles  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

When society collapses, what remains of a man when everything he loves is violently ripped away? In Wolves, bestselling author D.J. Molles answers this harrowing question with a masterclass in post-apocalyptic fiction. Known for his unflinching exploration of survival in The Remaining series, Molles pivots here from military action to a deeply personal, visceral narrative of grief, vengeance, and the fraying edges of human morality. 

Ten years after the world’s fall, protagonist Huxley has clawed out a semblance of peace. He has fertile land, a loving wife, and a daughter with a rare, wondrous gift. But peace in the wasteland is fleeting. When brutal slavers raid his farm, murdering his wife and dragging his daughter into the abyss of human trafficking, Huxley’s world is reduced to ash. Armed with little more than a dying wife's final clue—a man with a scorpion tattoo—and an insatiable appetite for retribution, Huxley plunges into the brutal, sun-bleached expanse of the American desert. 

What follows is not merely a rescue mission, but a harrowing descent into the dark heart of a broken world. Molles constructs a narrative style that is lean, muscular, and unrelenting. There is no fat on this story; every sentence moves with the desperate momentum of a dying man clawing for water. The author masterfully blends the unforgiving, machinery-choked atmosphere of Mad Max with the raw, atmospheric grit of a classic Western revenge tale. The desert itself becomes a character—vast, indifferent, and lethal. 

At its core, Wolves explores the central theme of what it truly costs to reclaim what was stolen. Huxley is a man pushed past the breaking point of his own humanity. As he forms a dangerous alliance with Jay, a mysterious wanderer who offers him water and a renewed purpose, the line between the monster and the savior blurs. Molles does not shy away from the ugliness of survival. He presents violence not as stylized spectacle, but as a heavy, soul-crushing toll. Yet, woven tightly beneath the barbed wire of brutality and blood is a profound meditation on love, honor, and the primal, unbreakable bond of a father for his daughter. 

For fans of dystopian thrillers and gritty survival fiction, Wolves is an absolute tour de force. It pulls no punches and takes no prisoners, delivering a devastating, high-octane portrait of a man who has nothing left to lose—and everything to fight for. Molles is operating at the absolute peak of his storytelling powers, offering a novel that will leave readers breathless long after the final page is turned.

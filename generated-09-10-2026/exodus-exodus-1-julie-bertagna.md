# Exodus (Exodus, #1)

**Author:** Julie Bertagna  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

Water, once the cradle of life, has become humanity's ultimate executioner. In Julie Bertagna’s gripping near-future dystopian novel Exodus, the world as we know it is merely a drowned memory. Cities lie entombed beneath the surging oceans, technology is a ghost of the past, and civilization has been brutally pruned back to a primitive, survival-driven state. Published in 2011, this haunting narrative feels less like speculative fiction with every passing year and more like an urgent, ticking clock.

At the heart of the story is Wing, an isolated northern island where a dwindling community desperately clings to the remnants of their fading existence. As the relentless sea swallows the land acre by acre, threatening to erase them entirely, despair takes root. Enter Mara, a fierce and visionary fifteen-year-old whose unyielding will refuses to let her people drown in apathy. Mara is the beating heart of the novel, a protagonist whose youthful idealism is tempered by the harsh, unforgiving realities of a dying planet. Her quest to lead her people toward a rumored new beginning forms the backbone of a narrative that is as much an emotional odyssey as it is a physical journey.

Bertagna’s greatest triumph in Exodus lies in her striking and immersive literary style. Her prose is poetic yet visceral, perfectly mirroring the dual nature of the ocean—breathtakingly beautiful in its vastness, yet utterly terrifying in its destructive power. She paints a bleak, atmospheric picture of a world upended by human arrogance, yet she infuses the darkness with a stubborn, radiant thread of hope. The author does not merely tell a story about climate change; she forces the reader to feel the encroaching tide, the biting cold, and the crushing weight of displacement. 

The central theme of the novel pivots on the devastating consequences of environmental neglect and the resilience of the human spirit. Bertagna explores what happens when the very foundations of our world collapse, stripping humanity down to its most primal instincts. Yet, amidst the bleakness, Exodus becomes a profound meditation on leadership, vision, and the courage required to step into the unknown. Mara’s journey challenges the older generation's resignation, offering a stark reminder that survival demands adaptation, unity, and bold imagination.

For teen readers and adults alike, Exodus hits uncomfortably close to home. It bridges the gap between thrilling dystopian adventure and urgent contemporary commentary, resonating deeply with a generation increasingly conscious of the climate crisis. Bertagna has crafted a compelling, powerful, and deeply prophetic novel that lingers in the mind long after the final page is turned. It is a stark warning, a brilliant piece of storytelling, and a tribute to the indomitable will to endure.

# Spook Squad (PsyCop, #7)

**Author:** Jordan Castillo Price  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Unknown

Step back into the delightfully quirky, afterlife-adjacent world of Jordan Castillo Price’s *Spook Squad*, the seventh installment in the beloved *PsyCop* series. For fans who have journeyed this far with Victor Bayte and Jacob Glass, this book is a triumphant reminder of why this paranormal detective series remains an absolute standout in the urban fantasy and MM romance genres. Price manages a rare feat in long-running series: keeping the core dynamics that fans adore fresh while injecting new tension, supernatural intrigue, and emotional resonance.

At its thematic heart, *Spook Squad* explores the delicate balance between isolation and connection. As a psychometric medium who works with the Denver Police Department, Victor spends his life surrounded by the dead—entities that demand attention, offer uninvited commentary, and remind him daily of mortality. Yet, the central theme of the novel pivots on how the living navigate these haunting shadows. Price masterfully uses the supernatural elements not just for thrills and chills, but as a mirror for Victor’s internal landscape. The ghosts, the psychic static, and the bureaucratic red tape of the police department all combine to question what it truly means to be seen and understood in a world that often prefers to look the other way. 

Price’s authorial style is the true engine driving the narrative forward. Known for her snappy dialogue, distinct character voices, and effortless blend of gritty realism with supernatural absurdity, Price delivers prose that is both sharply observant and genuinely funny. Victor’s internal monologue remains one of the most entertaining in contemporary speculative fiction—dry, self-deprecating, and fiercely loyal. Price has a remarkable knack for making the mundane and the macabre share the same breath. One moment you are chuckling at the sheer annoyance of dealing with an obnoxious specter in a cramped hallway, and the next, you are holding your breath as the stakes suddenly turn deadly personal. 

Furthermore, the romantic and interpersonal relationships in *Spook Squad* are handled with a maturity and warmth that anchors the paranormal chaos. Price doesn't rely on manufactured relationship drama; instead, she showcases the quiet strength of commitment and partnership. The chemistry between the characters is palpable, built on years of shared history, trust, and mutual respect, which makes the narrative beats hit all the harder when danger threatens their hard-won peace.

While *Spook Squad* is deeply rewarding for loyal series veterans, Price’s expert pacing ensures that the mystery unfolds with gripping momentum. The plotting is tight, weaving together police procedural elements with psychic phenomena in a way that keeps the pages turning rapidly. It is a testament to the author’s skill that, even seven books deep, the universe still feels expansive, unpredictable, and brimming with undiscovered corners.

Ultimately, *Spook Squad* is a masterclass in urban fantasy pacing and character-driven storytelling. Jordan Castillo Price proves once again that she is a master of her craft, delivering a witty, atmospheric, and emotionally satisfying ride. For anyone craving a supernatural mystery with heart, humor, and a heavy dose of the paranormal, this book is an unmissable treat.

# The Fifty-Seven Lives of Alex Wayfare

**Author:** M.G. Buehrlen  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

Time is rarely a straight line in speculative fiction, but M.G. Buehrlen weaves it into a breathless, labyrinthine trap in The Fifty-Seven Lives of Alex Wayfare. At its core, this YA thriller tackles a deeply human fascination: the urge to look backward in order to understand who we are in the present. Seventeen-year-old Alex Wayfare is not merely haunted by her past—she has lived it. Fifty-seven times, to be exact. When she discovers she is a Descender, capable of sliding backward into her previous incarnations, Buehrlen anchors the narrative in the intoxicating, addictive rush of rediscovery. 

What elevates the novel above standard time-travel fare is its thematic exploration of identity, memory, and the ghosts of our former selves. Alex’s descents are not just historical tourism; they are psychological excavations. Each past life offers a new facet of her soul, shifting her understanding of morality, survival, and love. The inclusion of the recurring blue-eyed stranger who shadows her across centuries introduces a potent romantic tension, transforming history into a relentless pursuit. Yet, Buehrlen smartly grounds the high-concept premise in visceral stakes. The past is rarely a safe place to visit, and the author ensures that every historical epoch Alex plunges into feels weighted with authentic danger and sensory grit.

Stylistically, Buehrlen writes with a kinetic urgency that mirrors Alex's racing pulse. The pacing is relentless, shifting seamlessly between the fragile reality of Alex’s current seventeenth-year-old existence and the vast, chaotic tapestry of her former deaths. The prose is sharp, economical, and atmospheric, painting vivid portraits of bygone eras without ever bogging down the narrative momentum. It is a style built for modern readers—fast-paced and emotionally resonant, prioritizing visceral reactions over heavy exposition.

As the novelty of time-sliding fades, the book reveals its darker, more menacing architecture. Buehrlen pivots the story into a tense survival thriller when an unseen antagonist begins weaponizing Alex's own history against her. The central question shifts from who Alex was to whether she will survive long enough to have a future. The stakes become terrifyingly existential: if you die in the past, does your present cease to exist? 

The Fifty-Seven Lives of Alex Wayfare is a striking, imaginative kickoff to a high-concept journey. Buehrlen successfully merges romance, historical mystery, and sci-fi thrills into a cohesive, page-turning narrative. For readers who love breathless pacing, intricate world-building, and high-stakes mysteries where the ultimate enemy is time itself, this book is an absolute must-read.

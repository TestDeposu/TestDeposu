# Karma (Karma, #1)

**Author:** Donna Augustine  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Unknown

What happens when the literal embodiment of cosmic retribution gets tired of waiting for the universe to do its job? In Karma, Donna Augustine plunges readers into a razor-sharp, adrenaline-fueled urban fantasy where the scales of justice are not just balanced—they are violently tipped. Eschewing the traditional, passive tropes of cosmic balance, Augustine introduces us to a protagonist who is fierce, flawed, and utterly compelling, redefining what it means to mete out universal justice.

At the heart of the novel lies the compelling theme of agency versus destiny. Augustine explores the heavy burden of consequence, turning the abstract concept of karma into a visceral, breathing reality. The narrative forces both characters and readers to confront uncomfortable truths: Is true justice about punishment, or is it about evolution? Through a relentless pacing that mirrors the ticking clock of fate itself, the book examines how past actions ripple through the present, creating a labyrinth of moral ambiguity where no one is entirely innocent, yet few deserve the brutal endings heading their way. 

Augustine’s signature writing style is the true engine of this dark ride. Known for her snappy dialogue, unapologetic world-building, and fiercely independent heroines, she delivers prose that is both punchy and atmospheric. The author has a distinct knack for blending high-stakes supernatural danger with a gritty, contemporary edge, never letting the fantastical elements overshadow the raw, human emotions driving the plot. Her voice is conversational yet piercing, infusing tense, life-or-death scenarios with a dry, resilient wit that keeps the pages turning at a breakneck speed. There is no purple prose here; instead, Augustine relies on sharp observations, kinetic action sequences, and a palpable sense of impending doom to keep the reader thoroughly hooked.

Karma is not a gentle stroll through a magical realm; it is a chaotic, thrilling collision of choice and consequence. Donna Augustine has crafted a standout opening to a series that promises to keep us guessing about who gets rewarded and who finally gets what’s coming to them. For fans of fast-paced urban fantasy with bite, attitude, and a deeply satisfying metaphysical twist, this book is an absolute must-read.

# All of Our Demise (All of Us Villains, #2)

**Author:** Amanda Foody  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**All of Our Demise: A Brutal, Unforgettable Finale to a Dark Fantasy Duology**

Amanda Foody and C. L. Herman’s *All of Us Villains* was never meant to be a story with happy endings. From the first blood-soaked page of *All of Us Villains*, readers were plunged into a world where power is currency, loyalty is a weapon, and survival hinges on the ruthless embrace of villainy. *All of Our Demise*, the searing conclusion to this duology, doesn’t just deliver on that promise—it shatters it. This is a book that doesn’t just break hearts; it dismantles the very idea of redemption, leaving its characters—and its readers—in a state of visceral, lingering devastation.

At its core, *All of Our Demise* is a story about the cost of defiance. The tournament, a brutal cycle of blood and betrayal, has long been the law of Ilvernath, a city where the elite feed on the suffering of the weak. But when the boundaries between the arena and the real world collapse, the illusion of control fractures. The dead boy—once sacrificed to the curse—now walks among them, a living relic of the system’s cruelty. And then there’s the new champion, a figure who doesn’t just want to win; they want to *end* it all, no matter how many lives must be erased to do so.

Foody and Herman’s writing is a masterclass in atmospheric dread and psychological tension. The prose is sharp, immersive, and unflinching, painting a world where morality is fluid and survival is a daily gamble. The authors excel at creating characters who are as complex as they are compelling—flawed, desperate, and utterly human in their capacity for both heroism and horror. The dynamic between the survivors is electric, a mix of old grudges, shifting alliances, and the kind of loyalty that feels both earned and doomed.

But what truly sets *All of Our Demise* apart is its unapologetic embrace of darkness. There are no easy answers here. No neat resolutions. The characters are forced to confront the horrifying truth: the world they’ve built, the power they’ve seized, the lives they’ve taken—it all might have been for nothing. The final act of the tournament isn’t just about who wins; it’s about what they’re willing to sacrifice to do so. And the authors leave no room for sentimentality. Betrayals sting, alliances crumble, and the price of defiance is paid in blood.

The duology’s strength lies in its refusal to sugarcoat. *All of Us Villains* was a story about embracing villainy; *All of Our Demise* is about the consequences of that choice. The new champion’s quest to break the curse isn’t just a quest for freedom—it’s a quest for annihilation, a willingness to burn the world down if it means ending the cycle. And the surviving champions? They must decide whether to fight to the death as the story always intended, or to try and dismantle the system from within, knowing that every compromise might doom them all.

Foody and Herman’s writing is relentless, their world-building meticulous, and their characters unforgettable. *All of Our Demise* is a book that will haunt you long after the last page. It’s brutal, it’s beautiful, and it’s the kind of story that lingers in your mind like a curse you can’t shake.

If you loved the first book for its dark charm and morally ambiguous heroes, *All of Our Demise* will leave you breathless. It’s not just the end of a story—it’s the end of an era, a final, devastating act in a tale where villainy was never just a role, but a way of life.

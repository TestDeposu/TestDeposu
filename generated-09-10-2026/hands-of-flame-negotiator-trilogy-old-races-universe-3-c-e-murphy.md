# Hands of Flame (Negotiator Trilogy/Old Races Universe, #3)

**Author:** C.E. Murphy  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

C.E. Murphy’s Hands of Flame, the third installment in the Negotiator Trilogy and a proud expansion of the beloved Old Races Universe, arrives with a brilliant blend of high-stakes suspense, biting regional flavor, and scorching contemporary romance. Set against the sun-baked, deceptively quiet backdrop of Johnson County, Texas, Murphy expertly shifts gears from urban fantasy to a deeply grounded, mature romantic thriller that keeps readers guessing until the final page. 

At the heart of Hands of Flame is an irresistible juxtaposition of duty and desire, embodied by two very different yet equally formidable men: Texas Ranger Cole Carter and County Judge Bruce Montgomery. Murphy’s stylistic prowess shines brightest in her character voices, which are rendered with distinct, sharp-witted precision through alternating perspectives. Bruce, built like a proverbial bull and possessing a courtroom presence to match, is delightfully self-assured, grounded, and fiercely protective. Cole, meanwhile, brings the raw, battle-hardened edge of a seasoned lawman whose elite training has prepared him for almost anything—except, hilariously, finding himself half-naked in a leather harness inside an exclusive, password-only establishment called The Stables. 

The thematic core of the novel hinges on exposure, both literal and metaphorical. The plot kicks into motion via a insidious blackmail ring targeting members of this clandestine club, forcing Judge Montgomery to look outside traditional legal channels. He recruits Cole to go undercover, setting up a delicious slow-burn dynamic that crackles with tension. Murphy uses the closed-door, high-risk environment of The Stables to explore themes of trust, vulnerability, and power dynamics. The contrast between Cole’s professional world of bullet-proof vests and hostile threat takedowns, and the intimate, sensory-heavy reality of the underground club, allows the author to examine how men who are used to holding absolute control navigate the terrifying, liberating experience of letting go.

Murphy’s narrative style remains a masterclass in pacing. She seamlessly weaves the gritty procedural elements of Cole’s investigation—complete with the bureaucratic headaches and physical toll of police work—with a deeply emotional romance that refuses to rush. The humor is dry and distinctly Texan, grounding the larger-than-life premise in a gritty reality where unicorns might theoretically exist, but blackmailers still have to answer to the law. The chemistry between Bruce and Cole is electric, driven not just by physical attraction, but by a mutual respect for each other’s competence and courage. 

Hands of Flame proves that C.E. Murphy’s storytelling is as versatile as it is captivating. By injecting mature romance tropes into a tense mystery framework, she delivers a novel that is simultaneously laugh-out-loud funny, pulse-pounding, and deeply swoon-worthy. For fans of the Old Races Universe or anyone looking for a romance with high stakes, sharp dialogue, and unforgettable protagonists, this book is an absolute triumph.

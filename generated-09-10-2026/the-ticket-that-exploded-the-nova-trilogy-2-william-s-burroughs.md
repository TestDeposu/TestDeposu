# The Ticket That Exploded (The Nova Trilogy, #2)

**Author:** William S. Burroughs  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

"The Ticket That Exploded" stands as a detonation in the literary landscape, a work that refuses to be read passively and instead insists on being felt, fragmented, and remixed. William S. Burroughs, ever the alchemist of language, fuses pulp science‑fiction tropes with the visceral bite of obscene experimental poetry, forging a manifesto that reads less like a novel and more like a virus—self‑replicating, invasive, and eager to infect the reader’s consciousness.

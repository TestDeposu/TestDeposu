# The Coincidence Makers

**Author:** Yoav Blum  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

Have you ever wondered why you missed your bus, only to bump into an old friend at the next stop? Or why a seemingly random glance across a crowded café changed the entire trajectory of your life? In *The Coincidence Makers*, Israeli author Yoav Blum invites readers behind the curtain of fate, offering a whimsical yet deeply philosophical answer to these everyday mysteries. Originally published in Hebrew and brought to an international English-speaking audience in 2018, Blum’s breakout novel is a masterclass in speculative fiction that treats serendipity not as a roll of the cosmic dice, but as the meticulous handiwork of a clandestine agency.

Blum constructs a world where destiny is an administrative operation, managed by an invisible workforce operating right beneath our noses. Operatives in this universe climb a distinct corporate ladder of fate. They begin as dream weavers or imaginary friends, guiding the subconscious of children, before graduating to dispensers of everyday luck. The elite among them, however, become Coincidence Makers—the architects of those pivotal, life-altering moments that define human existence.

At the heart of the narrative are Guy, Emily, and Eric, a trio of inseparable agents who trained together, memorized the intricacies of *Introduction to Serendipity*, and yet developed vastly different philosophies and specializations. Guy is a premier matchmaker who ironically harbors a cynical disbelief in true love. Emily is an optimistic romantic with a unique gift for sparking hidden vocations in others. Alongside Eric, they form a tight-knit unit navigating the delicate balance between nudging human lives and maintaining the grand design. 

However, Blum’s narrative shifts from an entertaining workplace comedy into a tense, high-stakes psychological drama when Guy is assigned a dangerous, unconventional mission involving a mysterious assassin known only as "The Man with the Hamster." This seemingly anomalous target threatens to shatter the carefully regulated rules of the agency, forcing the trio to question the very nature of free will, the mechanics of fate, and the authentic power of love. 

What makes Blum’s style so striking is his ability to blend high-concept magic realism with deeply relatable human truths. He avoids heavy-handed mysticism, choosing instead to portray his coincidence makers as tired, coffee-drinking bureaucrats and tactical planners who just happen to be shaping human history one dropped wallet or missed flight at a time. The prose is light, inventive, and laced with a quiet, observant wit that keeps the pages turning effortlessly. 

Yet, beneath the charming premise lies a profound existential inquiry. By examining the meticulous engineering required to make two people fall in love or to save a life from a stray bullet, Blum forces the reader to confront a dizzying paradox: If our choices are meticulously guided by unseen forces, what remains of our free will? And if love can be manufactured by an agent with a quota to fill, does it make the emotion any less real? 

*The Coincidence Makers* is a delightful, thought-provoking triumph of imagination. Yoav Blum has crafted a modern fable that will forever change the way you look at a missed connection, a rainy Tuesday, or a stranger on the street. It is a brilliant reminder that magic is happening all around us, disguised as nothing more than an ordinary coincidence.

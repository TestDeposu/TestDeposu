# The Gift of the Magpie (Meg Langslow Mysteries Book 28)

**Author:** Donna Andrews  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

**A Cozy, Clever Christmas Mystery with a Dash of Whimsy**

Donna Andrews’ *The Gift of the Magpie*—the seventh installment in her beloved *Meg Langslow* series—is a festive, fast-paced whodunit that captures the spirit of Christmas while delivering the sharp wit and clever plotting that fans of classic mystery novels crave. Set in the charming, snow-dusted town of Caerphilly, this book is a perfect blend of holiday cheer and dark intrigue, where the warmth of community clashes with the cold reality of murder.

At the heart of the story is Meg Langslow, a no-nonsense but endlessly endearing amateur sleuth who runs the town’s *Helping Hands for the Holidays* project—a heartwarming initiative where neighbors assist one another with tasks they can’t afford or manage alone. When Harvey the Hoarder, a reclusive and eccentric resident, is found dead in his cluttered home, Meg’s peaceful Christmas plans are shattered. The police suspect foul play, but the real question isn’t just *who* did it—it’s *why*. Rumors swirl about Harvey’s hidden treasure, a valuable artifact rumored to be buried beneath his mountain of junk. Was greed the motive? Or was it something more personal?

Andrews’ signature style shines here, weaving together a cast of eccentric characters—each with their own secrets, quirks, and motives—into a tapestry of suspicion and charm. The town of Caerphilly feels alive, its residents as much a part of the mystery as the crime itself. There’s the ever-loyal (and occasionally meddling) friends, the sharp-tongued local shopkeeper, and even a mysterious magpie that seems to deliver cryptic clues in the form of shiny trinkets—a delightful touch that adds a touch of whimsy to the proceedings.

What makes *The Gift of the Magpie* so compelling is Andrews’ ability to balance humor and tension seamlessly. The book is funny, with Meg’s dry observations and the absurdity of Harvey’s hoarding habits providing plenty of laughs. But beneath the surface, the mystery unfolds with a steady, satisfying pace, complete with red herrings, alibis, and a few surprises that will keep readers guessing until the very end.

The Christmas setting is handled with warmth and authenticity, capturing the cozy, nostalgic feel of the season without veering into saccharine territory. Andrews doesn’t shy away from the darker elements of the holiday—loneliness, greed, and the pressure to conform—but she frames them in a way that feels both realistic and comforting. The snowfall, the twinkling lights, and the scent of gingerbread only heighten the atmosphere, making this the perfect book to curl up with as the year draws to a close.

For fans of Agatha Christie’s Christmas mysteries, this book is a natural successor—complete with a tight-knit community, a puzzling death, and a detective who’s as much a part of the town’s fabric as the snow on its rooftops. Andrews’ writing is sharp, her dialogue crackles with personality, and her plotting is as meticulous as it is entertaining. Whether you’re a longtime fan of the series or new to Meg Langslow, *The Gift of the Magpie* is a delightful, well-crafted mystery that proves Christmas can be as thrilling as it is joyful.

If you love cozy mysteries with a dash of holiday magic, a touch of eccentricity, and a detective who’s as clever as she is kind, this is the book for you. Donna Andrews delivers once again, proving that the best mysteries aren’t just about solving crimes—they’re about the people who make them worth solving.

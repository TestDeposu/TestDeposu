# Nightlights

**Author:** Lorena Alvarez Gomez  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

Magic, Mischief, and the Sweet Scent of Mystery: A Review of Lorena Alvarez Gomez’s Nightlights

Lorena Alvarez Gomez returns with a dazzling burst of color, charm, and clever storytelling in the eagerly awaited middle-grade graphic novel Nightlights. Serving as the enchanting sequel to Wizkit, this latest installment plunges readers back into a vibrant world where the ordinary and the extraordinary deliciously collide. At the heart of this tale is Wizkit, a magical cyclops cat whose life has settled into a comfortable, flour-dusted rhythm running a bustling bakery. She is baking the softest bagels in town and dabbling in powerful, delightfully disruptive new spells. Yet, tranquility never lasts long in Alvarez Gomez’s universe. When Book—Wizkit’s literal, book-shaped friend—is mysteriously kidnapped from the library, the cozy routine shatters, launching Wizkit into a high-stakes, whimsical rescue mission.

What immediately strikes the reader is Alvarez Gomez’s signature visual style. The artwork is nothing short of a sensory feast. Every page is saturated with brilliant jewel tones, luminous neon highlights, and intricate paneling that captures both the chaotic energy of magical experimentation and the cozy warmth of a neighborhood bakery. The author’s command of color is legendary, and in Nightlights, she uses it to establish emotional resonance. The glow of magic feels genuinely radiant, contrasting sharply with the mysterious, shadowy clues left behind by the kidnapper. 

Beyond the eye-popping aesthetics, the narrative dances gracefully with deeper themes of identity, unexpected kinship, and empathy. As Wizkit tracks her pilfered pal—guided by a suspicious trail of pancake scents and aided by the delightfully birdbrained Earheart Triplets alongside a colorful cast of new characters—the quest morphs into something far more profound than a simple rescue mission. The ultimate confrontation reveals that the culprit is not a faceless villain, but someone who shares a surprising, mirror-like reflection of Wizkit's own inner struggles. Through this clever plot twist, Alvarez Gomez explores the themes of belonging, the temptation of misplaced validation, and the realization that understanding our perceived adversaries is often the key to understanding ourselves.

Wizkit remains an immensely endearing protagonist. She is far from a flawless hero; she is impulsive, prone to messy magical accidents, and fiercely loyal. Her journey in Nightlights highlights the growing pains of mastering one's own power while navigating the complexities of friendship. The supporting cast adds a layer of bouncy, chaotic humor that keeps the pacing brisk and endlessly entertaining, ensuring that the heavy emotional beats are always balanced with genuine laughs and playful wonder.

Nightlights is a triumph of modern graphic storytelling. It marries breathtaking illustration with a heartfelt narrative about friendship, forgiveness, and finding where you truly belong. Lorena Alvarez Gomez has crafted a sequel that not only honors the magic of its predecessor but expands its universe in thrilling, resonant ways. For readers young and old, stepping into Wizkit’s world is a reminder that even the most chaotic magic can be harnessed to bring people together. This is an absolute must-read that will leave your imagination sparkling and your heart delightfully full.

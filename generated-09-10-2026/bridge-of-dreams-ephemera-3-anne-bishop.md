# Bridge of Dreams (Ephemera, #3)

**Author:** Anne Bishop  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

Anne Bishop has long established herself as a master architect of dark, intricate fantasy, and with Bridge of Dreams, the third installment in her beloved Ephemera series, she returns to a universe where geography is destiny and magic is as fragile as gossamer. Published in 2012, this compelling novel—bolstered by the inclusion of the evocative novella The Voice—invites readers back into a mesmerizing landscape of shifting realities, emotional resonance, and high-stakes supernatural conflict.

At the heart of Bridge of Dreams lies a profound and haunting thematic exploration: the cost of sacrifice and the architecture of isolation. Ephemera is a world composed of distinct, surreal landscapes connected solely by bridges—pathways that refuse to take travelers where they want to go, choosing instead to deliver them where they truly belong. In this third volume, Bishop pushes this central metaphor to its breaking point. When Lee sacrifices himself to protect his sister, Glorianna Belladonna, the preserver of the world's delicate balance, he finds himself violently displaced. Stripped of his familiar surroundings, he becomes an inmate in the Asylum of the city of Vision. 

Through Lee’s harrowing descent into the Asylum, Bishop explores the theme of institutionalized darkness and psychological imprisonment. Vision, a city supposedly dedicated to clarity and perception, becomes a labyrinth of shadows. A mysterious corruption is bleeding through its streets, baffling the wise Shamans sworn to protect it. Lee, an outsider harboring the trauma of his sacrifice, becomes the reluctant key to unlocking the mystery. Bishop uses this dynamic to ask piercing questions: What does it mean to lose your place in the world? And when the bridges between us fail, how do we find our way back to our true selves?

Bishop’s writing style is unmistakable—a lush blend of Gothic atmospheric dread and tender emotional vulnerability. She possesses a rare gift for sensory world-building. Ephemera is not merely a setting; it is a breathing, feeling entity that mirrors the psychological states of its inhabitants. Her prose is poetic yet unflinching, weaving fairy-tale wondrousness with stark, visceral danger. The juxtaposition between the grand, cosmic stakes of keeping Ephemera balanced and the intimate, agonizing personal struggles of characters like Lee and Glorianna creates a narrative tension that refuses to let go. 

Furthermore, the inclusion of the novella The Voice enriches the volume, offering readers a deeper sonic and thematic resonance. It complements the main narrative by further examining the power of expression, suppression, and the haunting echoes of what remains unsaid in a world governed by magic.

While some fantasy series buckle under the weight of their own complex lore, Bridge of Dreams succeeds because Bishop never loses sight of her characters' humanity. The wizards who threaten their world are formidable, but the true battles are fought within the mind and heart. 

For longtime fans of Anne Bishop, this novel is a triumphant continuation of a truly unique fantasy tapestry. For newcomers willing to step onto its unpredictable bridges, Bridge of Dreams offers a dark, beautiful, and deeply rewarding journey into the architecture of the soul.

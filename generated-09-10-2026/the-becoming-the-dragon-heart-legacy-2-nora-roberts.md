# The Becoming (The Dragon Heart Legacy, #2)

**Author:** Nora Roberts  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

The second installment of Nora Roberts’ Dragon Heart Legacy, *The Becoming*, picks up where *The Awakening* left off, thrusting Breen back into the enchanted realm of Talamh to master the dual inheritance of her mortal and magical bloodlines. At its heart, the novel explores the theme of identity forged through choice—Breen’s journey is less about acquiring power and more about learning to wield it responsibly while staying true to the person she has become in both worlds. Roberts treats this internal struggle with the same earnestness she brings to her contemporary romances, allowing the fantasy setting to serve as a mirror for universal questions of belonging, duty, and self‑acceptance.

Roberts’ style remains unmistakably her own: crisp, dialogue‑driven prose that moves swiftly from scene to scene, punctuated by vivid sensory details that make Talamh feel palpable. Descriptions of dragons gliding over crystal‑clear lakes, faeries flickering in moonlit glades, and mermaids singing beneath sapphire waves are rendered with a lushness that never overwhelms the narrative momentum. The author balances these lush set‑pieces with tight, character‑focused moments—Breen’s tentative sparring sessions with Keegan, her quiet reflections by the portal, and the fraught conversations with her grandfather that hint at a looming conflict. This interplay between grand spectacle and intimate introspection gives the book a rhythmic pulse that keeps readers turning pages even when the plot pauses for world‑building.

What distinguishes *The Becoming* from many fantasy sequels is Roberts’ refusal to let the magic system overshadow character growth. The powers Breen inherits are presented as extensions of her emotional landscape; learning to channel them requires confronting fears, reconciling with her lineage, and accepting vulnerability. Roberts writes these moments with a tenderness that feels both genuine and accessible, making the fantastical elements feel like natural extensions of a very human story. The pacing, while brisk, never feels rushed; each chapter builds on the last, layering stakes and deepening the bond between Breen and Keegan without sacrificing the individual arcs that make them compelling.

Critically, the novel leans heavily on familiar tropes—the chosen one, the hidden portal, the looming dark lord—but Roberts infuses them with her signature warmth and a keen eye for relational dynamics. The romance subplot, though present, never dominates; instead, it enriches the protagonists’ motivations and provides a gentle counterpoint to the looming threat. Fans of Roberts’ contemporary work will recognize her knack for crafting believable, emotionally resonant relationships, while fantasy aficionados will appreciate the care she takes in constructing a world that feels both expansive and intimately lived-in.

Overall, *The Becoming* succeeds as a bridge between the promise of *The Awakening* and the impending showdown hinted at in *The Choice*. It delivers a satisfying blend of action, magic, and heartfelt character development, all delivered in Roberts’ clear, engaging voice. Readers who enjoy stories where personal growth is as vital as epic battles will find much to cherish here, and the novel’s emphasis on embracing one’s true self ensures it resonates beyond the confines of its fantastical setting.

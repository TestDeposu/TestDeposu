# In Times Like These (In Times Like These, #1)

**Author:** Nathan Van Coops  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

**A Cosmic Mirror: Nathan Van Coops’ *The Goa Adventure* in *In Times Like These***

Nathan Van Coops’ *The Goa Adventure*—a standout tale from the anthology *In Times Like These*—is a sci-fi gem that blends hard-edged survivalism with philosophical depth, asking the age-old question: *What does it mean to be human when the universe itself is on the brink?* Set against the backdrop of a collapsing civilization, Van Coops crafts a story that is as gripping as it is thought-provoking, proving that great sci-fi doesn’t just explore the stars—it examines the soul of humanity in the process.

At its core, *The Goa Adventure* is a tale of desperation and defiance. The story follows a crew of survivors aboard the *Goa*, a ship adrift in a galaxy where humanity’s last bastion is crumbling. The narrative unfolds with the tension of a survival thriller, but beneath the urgency lies a meditation on identity, morality, and the cost of progress. Van Coops doesn’t shy away from the brutality of existence—whether it’s the moral dilemmas faced by the crew or the existential weight of their choices—yet he balances it with moments of quiet humanity, reminding readers that even in the face of annihilation, compassion and hope endure.

What sets Van Coops’ style apart is his ability to merge visceral action with introspective character moments. The prose is lean, efficient, and immersive, drawing readers into the claustrophobic yet vast expanse of the *Goa*’s journey. His worldbuilding is subtle but effective, grounding the cosmic stakes in the personal struggles of his characters. Whether it’s the crew’s internal conflicts or the looming threat of an indifferent universe, Van Coops keeps the tension high without resorting to melodrama. Instead, he trusts the reader to piece together the implications of their choices, making the story feel both immediate and expansive.

Thematically, *The Goa Adventure* aligns with the anthology’s broader mission: to reimagine humanity in the face of cosmic challenges. Van Coops doesn’t offer easy answers—there are no saviors, no grand victories, just the raw, unfiltered struggle of survival. Yet, in this bleakness, he finds moments of resilience, suggesting that even in the darkest of times, the human spirit refuses to be extinguished. It’s a story that lingers long after the last page, challenging readers to confront their own definitions of progress, sacrifice, and what it truly means to endure.

For fans of sci-fi that balances hard science with deep emotional resonance, *The Goa Adventure* is a must-read. It’s not just another space opera—it’s a mirror held up to humanity’s greatest fears and hopes, wrapped in a narrative that’s as gripping as it is haunting. If you’re looking for a story that will leave you questioning the future of our species, Van Coops delivers with precision and passion.

# Sweep with Me (Innkeeper Chronicles, #4.5)

**Author:** Ilona Andrews  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

Ilona Andrews has carved out a unique niche in modern speculative fiction, blending cosmic scale with the comforting, domestic warmth of a bed and breakfast. In Sweep with Me, the 4.5 installment of the beloved Innkeeper Chronicles, the dynamic author duo returns to Gertrude Hunt, the magical, sentient B&B located in Red Deer, Texas. Though originally published as a serialized web novel, this brisk and punchy novella delivers an outsized punch, proving once again that the husband-and-wife writing team can effortlessly execute world-building, high-stakes action, and sharp wit within a confined narrative space.

At the heart of Sweep with Me lies a brilliant thematic juxtaposition: the mundane hospitality of the hotel industry mixed with the terrifying, bureaucratic, and lethal realities of intergalactic politics. The main theme of the story revolves around the sacred duties of sanctuary and tradition. The plot is centered on the Treaty Stay, an ancient holiday commemorating the pact that united the very first cosmic inns and established the rules protecting both travelers and the oblivious citizens of Earth. By tradition, innkeepers must welcome three specific archetypes—a warrior, a sage, and a pilgrim—yet the holiday carries a dangerous caveat: during this time, an inn is honor-bound to open its doors to *anyone*. Anyone at all. 

This premise allows Andrews to explore themes of obligation, hospitality under duress, and the fragile peace of a multicultural universe. Dina Demille, our fiercely competent and deeply empathetic innkeeper, along with her partner Sean Evans, must balance the polite management of cosmic hospitality with the grim reality that one or more of their uninvited guests might harbor lethal intentions. It is a brilliant vehicle for suspense. The horror of a locked-room mystery meets the hospitality tropes of a cozy inn, forcing the protagonists to rely on diplomacy, quick thinking, and overwhelming firepower in equal measure.

The writing style of Ilona Andrews is on full, glorious display here. Known for their punchy dialogue, vivid action choreography, and meticulous attention to world-building details, the authors waste not a single sentence. Even in a shorter format, the universe of the Innkeepers feels massive, lived-in, and brimming with bizarre alien physiology, strange customs, and deeply realized lore. Dina's narrative voice is charmingly pragmatic; she views the chaos of the cosmos through the lens of a harried small-business owner whose primary goal is making sure the continental breakfast runs smoothly and nobody disintegrates the upholstery. This comedic grounding prevents the high-concept space opera elements from feeling overly pretentious, keeping the tone delightfully accessible.

Furthermore, the character dynamics continue to shine. Dina and Sean’s partnership is a masterclass in mutual respect and silent synchronization. They are a well-oiled machine, and watching them navigate the logistical nightmare of a potentially catastrophic holiday is immensely satisfying. The supporting cast, including beloved staples of the series, adds warmth and humor, reinforcing the overarching theme that home is not just a place, but the people (and alien entities) you choose to protect.

Sweep with Me is a delectable bite-sized treat for longtime fans and a stellar showcase of why Ilona Andrews remains a powerhouse in speculative fiction. It reminds us that whether you are fighting an intergalactic warlord or trying to keep unruly guests from ruining the breakfast buffet, true hospitality is a battlefield.

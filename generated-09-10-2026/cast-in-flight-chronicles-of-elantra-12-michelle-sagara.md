# Cast in Flight (Chronicles of Elantra, #12)

**Author:** Michelle Sagara  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

In Cast in Flight, the twelfth installment of Michelle Sagara’s beloved Chronicles of Elantra series, the author once again proves that a long-running urban fantasy can deepen in emotional resonance rather than merely recycling old tropes. Sagara returns readers to a city where ancient magic is as common as bureaucratic red tape, yet she manages to inject a palpable sense of fresh danger and intimate stakes. At its core, this novel explores a central theme that has defined the series from the beginning: the burden of chosen family and the fierce, often reckless lengths to which one will go to protect the vulnerable.

Sagara’s signature style is immediately recognizable here, characterized by a unique blend of high-stakes political maneuvering, rich world-building, and an almost conversational, deeply internal narrative voice. Through the perspective of Private Kaylin Neya, Sagara crafts an atmosphere that balances whimsy with genuine peril. Kaylin’s domestic life—sharing a living space with literal Dragons, Barrani, and now an injured, flightless Aerian named Sergeant Moran dar Carafel—serves as a brilliant juxtaposition to the brutal, shadowy politics of Elantra. Sagara excels at these micro-interactions, using domesticity to anchor the fantastic elements in emotional reality. When a simple walk to the Halls of Law erupts into a street-shattering assassination attempt, the contrast highlights Sagara’s pacing skill; she lulls the reader into comfort before pulling the rug out with sudden, explosive action.

What makes Cast in Flight particularly striking is how Sagara handles character agency and secrets. Moran dar Carafel is a closed book, embodying the proud, secretive nature of the Aerians, yet Sagara uses Kaylin’s stubborn empathy to pry open those locked doors. Kaylin is not a passive protagonist; her determination to protect Moran, despite the latter's silence and the cascading threats of angry dragons and sinister political factions, drives the narrative forward with relentless momentum. Sagara’s prose thrives on these character dynamics. She portrays loyalty not as a noble abstraction, but as a messy, exhausting, and dangerous daily choice. 

Furthermore, the author’s world-building continues to expand organically. Elantra is a city built on layered histories, ancient grudges, and delicate racial politics. In this volume, Sagara shines a spotlight on Aerian culture, adding another intricate tile to the vast mosaic of her universe. She doesn't rely on massive exposition dumps; instead, the world reveals itself through Kaylin’s reluctant education and the immediate, life-or-death consequences of crossing the wrong factions. 

Ultimately, Cast in Flight is a triumphant addition to the Chronicles of Elantra. Michelle Sagara delivers a compelling mystery wrapped in rich fantasy, driven by a protagonist whose heart is simultaneously her greatest strength and her most dangerous vulnerability. For fans of the series, this installment offers the perfect blend of comforting familiar faces and high-octane new threats. For newcomers willing to dive deep into a well-established universe, it stands as a testament to Sagara’s enduring talent for crafting vibrant, emotionally resonant urban fantasy.

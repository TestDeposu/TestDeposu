# Son of the Morning

**Author:** Linda Howard  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Linda Howard is a master of blending high-stakes suspense with intense, magnetic romance, and in Son of the Morning, she pushes her signature style into thrilling new territory. Originally published in 2009, this novel showcases Howard’s undeniable talent for creating fierce, independent heroines and dark, alpha-male heroes whose chemistry crackles off the page, all while weaving a complex web of historical mystery and time-travel adventure.

The central theme of Son of the Morning revolves around the intersection of destiny, knowledge, and raw power. Grace St. John is not your typical damsel in distress; she is a brilliant scholar whose intellect unlocks secrets that dangerous men would kill to possess. When her academic discovery of ancient manuscripts links her to the legendary Knights of the Templar and a lost Celtic treasure, Grace is thrust into a modern-day nightmare. Howard brilliantly establishes this contemporary threat before pulling the rug out from under the reader—and her protagonist—by sending Grace hurtling backward in time to fourteenth-century Scotland. 

This time-travel element elevates the narrative beyond a standard romantic suspense novel. Howard uses the historical setting not merely as a backdrop, but as a crucible that tests Grace’s adaptability, courage, and emotional resilience. In the medieval Scottish highlands, Grace encounters Black Niall, a warrior of dark fury, brooding intensity, and unbridled desire. Niall is the quintessential Howard hero: powerful, protective, and initially reluctant to let anyone breach his formidable emotional armor. The dynamic between Grace and Niall forms the beating heart of the novel. Their romance is neither simple nor immediate; it is forged through mutual respect, danger, and a slow-burn passion that feels both perilous and inevitable.

Howard’s writing style is punchy, cinematic, and relentlessly fast-paced. She has a rare gift for pacing, balancing heart-pounding action sequences with quiet, emotionally charged moments that allow the central relationship to breathe. The historical atmosphere is painted with gritty realism, avoiding overly romanticized tropes in favor of a rugged, visceral medieval world where survival demands toughness. Furthermore, the mystery surrounding the Templar treasure keeps the pages turning, ensuring that the suspense never takes a back seat to the romance.

Son of the Morning is a compelling, page-turning triumph that will satisfy devotees of romantic suspense and time-travel romance alike. Linda Howard successfully marries the cerebral intrigue of ancient history with the primal heat of a timeless love story, proving once again why she remains a towering figure in the genre. For anyone looking for a riveting escape filled with danger, intellect, and scorching passion, this novel is an absolute must-read.

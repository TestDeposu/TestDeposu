**Author:** Shiloh Briar  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026 APL

The espresso machine is hissing at me again like a rabid possum behind the counter while I try to parse the marketing copy for *Necromantic Getaway*. Another brightly colored cover slapped onto a paperback designed to look like a spicy thriller at Target, except this one leans into the corpse-handling demographic. Because nothing says I am down bad for you quite like a guy who literally smells like freshly dug dirt and formaldehyde. Shiloh Briar is trying to trick us into thinking necromancy is cute now—a little cozy afternoon activity between raising skeletons and sipping oat milk lattes. Give me a break. Give me actual viscera, or at least a protagonist who isn't suffering from a terminal case of situational irony.

### The Infernal Real Estate Market

Chthonia sounds like a terrible place to spend a long weekend. Yet here we are, following a bone-shuffler down into the fiery depths because of some melodramatic debt to a demoness named Helspira. And look—I am thoroughly exhausted by the weaponized trauma trope. Oh, she has a dark past in hell? Shocking. Truly revolutionary character development in the year of our lord two thousand and whatever. We are taking a cozy little field trip to the abyss to unpack baggage that should probably stay checked. But no, we need angst. We need the fragile bonds to shatter so the author can string us along for another three hundred pages of lingering glances over flaming cauldrons. It is giving major high-school-poetry-tumblr energy, but make it fantasy. Tamsyn Muir walked so these pastel necromancy paperbacks could sprint directly into the bargain bin at Barnes and Noble.

### Pining Among the Crypts

The synopsis insists on framing this as a romance, which is objectively hilarious. Sikras cares too much to ask her to follow, but she follows anyway because heaven forbid a fictional woman have a hobby outside of enabling her boyfriend's subterranean self-destructive tendencies. Classic trauma-bonding disguised as soul-deep devotion. And the prose promises we will see his dark side. What does that even mean in this context? Does he refuse to separate his whites from his blacks? Does he raise a grumpy skeleton without tipping it? I want real depravity. I want bones snapping and existential dread that actually keeps me awake past four in a morning when I am staring at the ceiling wondering why I work retail. Instead, we are getting spicy banter over a pit of hellfire and some hand-wringing about emotional vulnerability. 

Prediction time: they will hold hands while escaping a demon lord, realize their communication skills are garbage, and spawn a sequel about wedding planning in a crypt. It will sell a million copies to people who think reading about emotional repression is a personality trait. 

I have to go develop photographs in my makeshift darkroom.

**Author:** Tracy Wolff  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026 APL

Another spin-off, another exercise in stretching a single marketable premise past its breaking point. Tracy Wolff returns with *Sweet Chaos*, the sophomore installment of the Calder Academy sequence that nobody asked for after the *Crave* franchise finally exhausted its supernatural welcome. Except the publisher insists on treating us to more. 

### The Anatomy of an Empty Promise

What does the official promotional text actually give us? Absolutely nothing. We are told the adventure continues, instructed on the sacred reading order—because heaven forbid the target demographic actually parse a narrative out of sequence—and given three titles. That is it. No inciting incident. No character stakes. Just a blank check written against teenage attention spans. 

This feels precisely like the disastrous Fyre Festival pitch deck, heavy on stylized typography and utterly devoid of infrastructure. 

### The Cult of the Spinoff

And yet, the marketing machine grinds forward. We will pretend to care about the syllabus at Calder Academy. We will watch the pacing stall deliberately to ensure a tidy trilogy. 

Wait. Actually, the pacing might actually benefit from the absolute lack of plot information provided so far. After all, if there is no story yet, there is no room for the saggy middle that plagued the later volumes of *Crave*. 

Or perhaps not. Wolff relies heavily on the endless churn of supernatural high-school drama—think Sarah J. Maas watered down for a TikTok audience that demands a new book every six months. Yet the utter vacuum of this synopsis suggests a troubling reliance on brand loyalty over actual narrative architecture. Readers deserve a hook, not a logistical instruction manual on how to buy three separate hardcovers.

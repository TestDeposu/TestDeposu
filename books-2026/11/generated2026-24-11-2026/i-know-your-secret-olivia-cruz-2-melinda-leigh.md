Look at the promotional copy for *I Know Your Secret*—wait, the synopsis actually calls it *The Courier* down at the bottom in a desperate bid to hitch its wagon to Lee Child and Jennifer Garner reruns, which already tells you everything about the publisher's chaotic identity crisis regarding Melinda Leigh's latest cash grab. And they have already optioned it for film because Hollywood executive suites apparently run on a continuous loop of recycled airport paperback algorithms where a woman with elite parentage gets framed for treason by shadowy cabals while holding a magical briefcase of doom that apparently threatens the entire continental United States. 

Back in my day, we gathered around the hearth in the damp chill of winter to listen to the old tales of Achilles dragging Hector through the dust or Odysseus blinding the cyclop, stories handed down through grease and bone where the gods were petty and human suffering actually meant something rather than serving as cheap IP fodder for an Amazon MGM press release. Speaking of ancient agony, my grandson spent three solid hours yesterday trying to explain the lore of some technicolor digital pocket monster named after a rodent with electrical issues, and I swear my blood pressure reached geological fault-line levels just watching him swipe mindlessly at a glowing glass rectangle while ignoring the magnificent frost patterns forming on the pantry window. 

The thematic architecture here relies entirely on the tired, hyper-militarized myth of the solitary hero holding the fragile fabric of Western civilization together with sheer stubbornness and a set of inherited genetic traits from a general father and a professor mother—because apparently, aptitude in international espionage is now passed down through the bloodstream like gout or bad posture. 

Derivative. 

The psychological profile sketched in this marketing blurb offers zero interiority, trading genuine human dread for high-octane buzzwords like "pulse-pounding" and "sinister conspiracy," which is code for a manuscript written by committee to maximize quarterly shareholder value. 

I need to go outside right now and check the tire pressure on my wife's sedan before she leaves for her bridge club road trip across the county.

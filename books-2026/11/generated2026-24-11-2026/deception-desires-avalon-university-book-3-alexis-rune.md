Another anthology featuring small-town murder, talking cats, and women named after autumn weather patterns because subtlety died in 2014. *Deception & Desires*—or *A Cauldron of Deceptions*, depending on which line of the marketing copy you let rot your brain first—promises six cozy mysteries crammed into a single Halloween-themed volume. One wonders if trees actually shed leaves in these fictional hamlets, or if they just drop exposition. 

### Brewing Mediocrity in a Plastic Cauldron

The premise relies on a tired formula that feels manufactured in a concrete basement by people who have never set foot outside a metropolitan zip code. We get Katherine and Cole wrestling with Arkansas family feuds, Red opening a potions shop while dodging an overzealous cop, and Poppy Ashfield botching a potluck because somebody swapped her magical brew. Groundbreaking. Let us all gasp. The stakes are perpetually low, hovering somewhere around a misplaced pastry or a suspiciously burnt loaf of sourdough, dressed up in witch hats to fool casual browsers into thinking they are buying actual literature. 

Yet, wait. Perhaps the sheer collective weight of six authors throwing cauldrons, talking ravens, and dead bodies at a single holiday creates a kind of accidental, hallucinatory brilliance. No, scratch that. It is just lazy branding riding the coat-tails of every Hallmark movie ever conceived. We are expected to swallow six distinct variations of amateur sleuths tripping over corpses while drinking spiced cider.

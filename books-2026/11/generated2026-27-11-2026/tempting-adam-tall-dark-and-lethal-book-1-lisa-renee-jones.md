**Page Count:** Unknown

Red markers everywhere. Ink bleeding through cheap loose-leaf paper. If I see one more run-on sentence about a teenage protagonist discovering their inner magical spark while making direct eye contact with a brooding quarterback, I am going to throw a dry-erase board straight through the drywall. 

### The Hamptons Need Fewer FBI Agents and More Basic Literacy

Lisa Renee Jones is stepping back into the ring with *Tempting Adam*, promising us an FBI profiler named Lilah Love who lives in a killer's mind. Groundbreaking. Truly. Because what modern fiction desperately lacks is yet another traumatized law enforcement official with a mysterious past dragging us back to her ridiculously exclusive hometown. 

Look at the synopsis. Just stare at the absolute void of originality staring back at us. A mysterious tattoo! A string of brutal assassinations! And oh, the killer is following her! Naturally. Because how else would an author manufacture tension without relying on tired, exhausted stalker tropes that belong in a 2004 airport paperback bargain bin? It reads like a synthetic algorithm threw Lisa Kleypas and James Patterson into a blender, hit puree, and poured the sludge directly onto a glossy cover. This could have been an email.

### Why Do We Reward Mediocrity?

Speaking of absolute nonsense, why on earth is seventh-grade homeroom smelling like a crushed bag of flaming hot Cheetos mixed with Axe body spray right now? Tyler dropped his entire lunch tray during third period today—no, wait, it was fourth period, right after I tried to explain that commas are not optional decorative elements meant to give a sentence a little personality. He just stared at me with those blank, dead eyes, the exact same expression readers are going to give this book when they realize the "lethally tempting ex" is just a recycled trope wearing a different leather jacket. 

Kane Mendez. What kind of name is Kane Mendez? It sounds like a secondary villain from a low-budget cartoon about ninjas who control elemental boulders. And we are expected to believe he brings out her "darkest impulses"? Give me a break. Give everyone a break. We are drowning in a sea of capital-R Romance Suspense where the characters possess the emotional depth of a puddle in a parking lot. 

Yet, here we are. Pre-ordering. Hyping it up. Falling for the exact same marketing beats year after grueling year. 

### The Predictable Descent

Will Lilah survive her hometown demons? Will Kane look intensely at a wall while flexing his forearms? Spoiler alert: Yes. And everyone will eat it up like cafeteria pizza on Fridays. 

I am going to go drink absinthe and pretend I am in 1920s Paris.

Meanwhile, the literary world is apparently expecting us all to lose our collective minds over *With Hearts of Flame*. Right. Because nothing screams rigorous narrative construction quite like shoving vampires and dragon-shifters into a dark academia blender and hitting pureed chaos.

### The Physics of High-Blood Nonsense

We are supposed to accept that falling in love with a literal dragon-boy is somehow more grueling than surviving an entire academy of blood-sucking aristocrats. Make it make sense. The synopsis expects us to buy into subterranean dwarven quests and fragile political revolutions while characters like Regan apparently pivot from being mean girl bosses to systemic protectors. It is giving Sarah J. Maas fanfiction written by a stressed caffeine addict who failed intermediate physics. But whatever. The romantasy industrial complex must grind on. 

### Chronically Online Thermodynamics

Let us talk about the absolute intellectual bankruptcy of calling a book a morally gray/black journey through Sangratha. We are talking about high-stakes vibes and zero actual structural logic. How does a dwarven mining expedition logistically function when you have winged beasts and ancient prophecies colliding in the north? It does not. The author just threw a bunch of trope-heavy buzzwords into a hat and pulled out whatever sounded marketable to TikTok. Enemies-to-lovers? Check. Forced proximity? Check. A protagonist whose primary emotional state is being utterly cooked by her own poor life choices? Mood. Honestly, I relate to the sheer exhaustion of these characters, minus the whole immortal fang-bearing aristocracy thing. 

Prediction time: the ending will feature a cliffhanger so aggressively derivative that it should be classified as a misdemeanor. Someone is going to stare dramatically into the middle distance while an old prophecy whispers about blood and fire because originality is apparently illegal in twenty-six-twenty-six. We are all just paying thirty bucks to watch fictional people make catastrophically horny decisions during geopolitical collapses. 

I am going to sit on a park bench and aggressively people-watch until my brain stops short-circuiting.

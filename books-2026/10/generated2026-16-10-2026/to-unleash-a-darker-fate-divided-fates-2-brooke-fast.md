**Author:** Brooke Fast  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026 APL

Brooke Fast is stepping back into the blood-soaked sands of the dystopian arena with *To Unleash a Darker Fate*, promising us another round of emotional devastation after the runaway success of *To Cage a Wild Bird*. We find ourselves dragged away from the claustrophobic stone walls of Endlock Prison—which honestly gave us some magnificent, atmospheric dread—and thrown directly into the scorching, sun-bleached expanse of the Wastes, a setting that practically hums with dehydration and desperation. And yet, beneath the sun-baked sand dunes and the predictable scurry of poisonous snakes, lies a far more terrifying landscape: the human heart caught in the agonizing vise of a love triangle that features both childhood devotion and the deliciously dangerous friction of enemies-to-lovers. 

One can already smell the ozone and hear the distant roar of the Dividium hunting caravan closing in on our battered survivors, because nothing says high-stakes survival quite like being chased across a desert by the very people who used to lock your doors. But let us be entirely honest with ourselves for a brief, glaring moment—how many times must our poor protagonist Raven Thorne stand at a moral crossroads while a secretive rebel group dangles safety like a carrot on a stick? The Collective demands loyalty, the rebels demand sacrifice, and Fast leans heavily into the delicious, exhausting tropes of forced proximity and the eternal "who did this to you?" inquiry that keeps romance readers clutching their oversized mugs of lukewarm chamomile tea at two in the morning, hoping against hope that the ex-prison guard and the fledgling revolutionary will simply stop glaring at each other and start addressing the obvious romantic tension. 

Stubborn.

Fans of Sarah J. Maas and Rebecca Yarros will undoubtedly devour every single page of this chaotic desert trek, even if the synopsis plays delightfully fast and loose with the actual mechanics of this mysterious rebellion.

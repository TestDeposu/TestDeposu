**Author:** Mallory Marlowe  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-10-13 APL

*Entities to Lovers* - a title that hints at a potentially fascinating exploration of the human condition, yet simultaneously risks being trite and overly reliant on genre tropes. Can Mallory Marlowe's latest offering transcend the pitfalls of romantic comedy and deliver a truly compelling narrative?

The setup, at least, shows promise: a paranormal medium and a charismatic adjunct history professor are forced to host a tour together, only to find themselves hexed by a matchmaking ghost. The premise is ripe for a witty, fast-paced exploration of the tensions between reason and superstition, as embodied by the two leads. But will Marlowe be able to balance the tone effectively, or will the book devolve into a messy, clichéd romp?

One thing that does stand out is the character dynamic between Rory and Ezra. Their initial animosity and eventual forced proximity could provide a rich backdrop for exploring the complexities of relationships and the blurred lines between love and hate. The fact that they're both intelligent, strong-willed individuals with a deep understanding of the paranormal and historical contexts, respectively, adds a layer of depth to their interactions. It's almost reminiscent of the intellectual sparring matches found in the works of authors like Helen Hoang or Tessa Dare.

And yet, despite this promising foundation, the synopsis remains frustratingly vague. What exactly does the "hex" entail, and how do Rory and Ezra's attempts to lift it drive the plot forward? How does their relationship evolve over the course of the book, and what themes does Marlowe aim to explore through their story? These are the questions that will ultimately make or break *Entities to Lovers*, and unfortunately, the synopsis doesn't provide enough insight to make a confident prediction.

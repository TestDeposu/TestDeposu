**Author:** J.D. Robb  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-09-08 APL

The *In Death* series, now on its 63rd installment, *Fury in Death*. Can J.D. Robb still manage to surprise us? The question hangs in the air like a challenge. Yet, as I scan the synopsis, I'm left with a nagging sense of déjà vu. We've seen this before, haven't we? The wealthy biotech executive, the gruesome murder, the trail of secrets and lies. It's a familiar setup, one that Robb has mastered over the years, but will it be enough to keep readers engaged?

— The devil's in the details, after all. Or, in this case, the lack thereof.

The synopsis is woefully vague, leaving us with more questions than answers. What was Larry Chu's mistake, exactly? How does his murder tie into the larger conspiracy? And what's the significance of that suspicious bank account in the Cayman Islands? The more I read, the more I'm reminded of a classic case of overpromising. Robb's got a reputation for delivering twisty, suspenseful plots, but can she deliver on this one?

And then, of course, there's Eve Dallas. Our intrepid lieutenant, with her troubled past and her penchant for getting in over her head. She's a character we've grown to love, but how much more can we milk from her backstory? The synopsis hints at a deeply personal connection between Eve and the murder victim, but I'm not convinced. It feels like a contrived attempt to inject some emotional depth into the story.

But, I suppose that's the thing about Robb's writing. She's always pushing the boundaries, experimenting with new ways to tell the same old story. And, let's be real, it's worked for her so far. The *In Death* series has a dedicated fan base, and for good reason. Robb's got a keen eye for detail, a knack for crafting characters that feel real and relatable.

— Although, if I'm being honest, the whole "tough-as-nails female detective" trope is getting a bit old.

Still, I'm willing to give Robb the benefit of the doubt. She's proven herself time and time again, and I'm curious to see how she'll spin this latest installment. Will it be a game-changer, or just another iteration of the same old formula? Only time will tell.

— And, let's be real, the real question on everyone's mind is: can Robb keep up with the likes of Tana French and Laura Lippman?

The answer, I suppose, will have to wait until September. Until then, I'll be over here, taking bets on how many twists and turns *Fury in Death* will take.

### The Verdict: A Familiar Tune

As I finish up this preview, I'm left with a sense of trepidation. Can Robb deliver on the promises made in this synopsis? Or will it be just another rehashing of the same old tropes? I suppose we'll have to wait and see.

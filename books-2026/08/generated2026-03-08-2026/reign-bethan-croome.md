**Get Ready for a Fierce Feminist Romantasy: "Reign" by Bethan Croome**

This September, Cosmopolitan's book imprint is set to release "Reign," a highly anticipated romantasy novel by Bethan Croome. With a gripping synopsis that promises found family, forbidden love, and a heroine who refuses to kneel, this book is sure to captivate fans of the genre.

**A Kingdom Without Magic, But Not Without Rain**

In the rain-soaked Kingdom of Lyonesse, magic is outlawed, punishable by death. But when Niamh's forbidden witchcraft is exposed, she flees her village, disguised as a man, and finds herself on a battlefield. Mistakenly conscripted into a brutal war, Niamh must navigate a world where survival relies on more than luck – it demands alliances.

As she fights terrifying beasts and confronts arcane powers, Niamh is thrust into a world of magic and might. But with her womanhood hidden, she must also contend with the infuriatingly handsome Prince Fitzroy, who is tasked with eradicating magic. Their complicated relationship is further complicated by Niamh's growing feelings for Bash, a fellow soldier who sees her like no one else does.

**A Heroine Who Refuses to Kneel**

At its core, "Reign" is a story about choosing freedom over fear and setting fire to the throne. Niamh's journey is one of self-discovery and empowerment, as she navigates a world that seeks to control her. With its themes of feminism, found family, and forbidden love, this book is sure to resonate with fans of romantasy and fantasy.

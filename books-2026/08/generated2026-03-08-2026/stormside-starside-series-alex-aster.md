# Stormside (Starside Series)

**Author:** Alex Aster  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-03-31 APL

**Stormside: Alex Aster’s Bold New Adventure into Adult Romantasy—Where Vengeance Meets Magic and the Gods Themselves Are the Enemy**

Alex Aster’s name is already synonymous with escapism—her *Lightlark* series, a lush, genre-defying fantasy for teens, has cemented her as a master of immersive worldbuilding and emotionally charged storytelling. Now, with *Stormside*, the #1 *New York Times* bestselling author is stepping into uncharted territory: adult romantasy. And if the explosive synopsis is any indication, this isn’t just a new series—it’s a high-stakes, morally complex odyssey where the line between hero and villain is as sharp as the blades wielded by its characters.

Set in a fractured world where magic is power and immortality is a curse, *Stormside* introduces a realm where the gods are not benevolent figures but ancient, bloodthirsty beings whose descendants rule over a paradise of eternal youth and untold wealth. Below them lies the mortal world, where survival is a daily struggle—and where, every fifty years, the gates between realms open, offering fifty desperate souls the chance to cross into Starside in search of life-changing magic. For Aris, a blacksmith’s apprentice orphaned by a goddess’s fire, the quest isn’t about healing or wealth. It’s about vengeance.

What makes *Stormside* so compelling is its refusal to play by the rules of traditional fantasy. Magic isn’t inherited—it’s *claimed*, and the weapons that hold it are as dangerous as the hands that wield them. Aris, with nothing but her wits and a stolen blade, becomes a target the moment she proves she’s no ordinary challenger. But the real danger isn’t just the monstrous creatures of Starside or the bloodthirsty immortals who see her as prey. It’s the secret tied to her family’s death—and the man who once betrayed her, now standing between her and her destiny.

Harlan Raker, the king’s guard, is a man of contradictions: merciless yet mysterious, a survivor in a world where trust is a luxury. His presence looms large, not just as a potential ally but as a reminder that in a land where gods walk among mortals, loyalty is a fragile thing. And when Aris’s past catches up with her, the consequences won’t just reshape her journey—they’ll ignite a war that even the immortals fear.

For fans of Aster’s work, *Stormside* promises the same richly textured worldbuilding and emotionally resonant characters, but with the added complexity of adult themes, morally gray choices, and a protagonist who is as ruthless as she is determined. If you loved the high-stakes drama of *Lightlark* but craved something darker, more politically charged, and utterly unforgiving, this is your next obsession.

*Stormside* arrives on **March 31, 2026**, and with it, the promise of a new kind of fantasy—one where the gods are the villains, and the only way to survive is to become something even more dangerous than they are.

**Author:** Nora Roberts  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-11-17 APL

Literary Dispatch: Unveiling Nora Roberts’s Enchanting New Epic, Birth of the Witch

For decades, the undisputed queen of romantic suspense and generational fantasy has cast a spell over millions of readers worldwide. Now, #1 New York Times bestselling author Nora Roberts is returning to weave a brand-new magical tapestry with Birth of the Witch, the first thrilling installment in the upcoming A Coven of Three Trilogy, slated for release on November 17, 2026. 

If you loved the spellbinding charm, intricate family dynamics, and supernatural stakes of Roberts's acclaimed The Lost Bride Trilogy, prepare to clear space on your bookshelf. This upcoming release promises to deliver the signature atmospheric storytelling that has made Roberts a legendary fixture in contemporary fiction.

What can readers expect when they crack open the cover later this year? The official synopsis sets the stage for a sweeping tale of legacy, magic, and destiny:

Niamh McShane was born a witch and died a witch in the same moment, at the tender age of thirteen. A victim of the madness that spread from Salem Town to her home on Piety Island, in her last hours she found comfort from the weavers imprisoned alongside her, who saw within her light and power and gave her some of their own.

More than three hundred years later, on the same island, Neve McShane wakes with excitement on her thirteenth birthday and unwraps gifts from her aunts: sneakers, an iPhone, and a stunning antique pendant. Then she receives something else: a revelation that will change her life.

Astonished, Neve learns that her aunts’ abilities go beyond simple kitchen magic and the potions they sell in their shop. Their tales of past lives aren’t just whimsical fantasies. They are witches, and so is she. Their lives–past and present–are bound together by that pivotal day centuries ago. Now Neve has been tasked with stopping a growing darkness. To prepare for the dangerous enemy that awaits, Neve hones her skills and searches for clues to aid her in her quest. And at night, she dreams of a young man from Ireland whose eyes met with hers for a fateful moment, centuries ago.

Bridging centuries of history, a fateful tragedy in the shadow of Salem, and a modern-day thirteenth birthday filled with both typical teenage milestones and extraordinary revelations, Birth of the Witch is shaping up to be one of the most anticipated fantasy releases of the season. 

Mark your calendars for November 17, 2026, as Nora Roberts invites us back into a world where the past is never truly past, and magic runs deep in the family blood.

# Vine of Hearts (The Evermore Trilogy, #2)

**Author:** Julie Soto  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-12-01 APL

**What to Expect: *Vine of Hearts* (The Evermore Trilogy, #2) by Julie Soto**

Fans of Julie Soto’s debut romantasy, *Rose in Chains*, have been waiting eagerly for the next chapter in the Evermore saga, and the wait is almost over. Slated for release on December 1, 2026, *Vine of Hearts* promises to dive deeper into the perilous world of magic, betrayal, and forbidden desire that made the first book a New York Times bestseller.

The official synopsis sets the stage for a high‑stakes continuation:

> “To the world, she is a source for magic.  
> To him, she is the one thing he cannot have—and cannot live without.  
> Briony Rosewood escaped the worst of it—or so she tells herself. Being forced to serve the woman who betrayed her family and gutted the Evermore kingdom is the closest thing to safety she could ask for. But even the protection of the powerful Hearst family has limits, and now Briony must do the unthinkable: share her magic—and her heartbeat—with Toven Hearst. Once her enemy…now her tentative ally.  
> Forced to travel to distant courts and glittering foreign kingdoms, Briony plays the part of Toven’s adoring heartspring, smiling for the enemy while secretly feeding the rebellion. Every whispered confidence, every stolen moment, becomes a weapon as she gathers information and forges dangerous new alliances. It’s devastating to pretend she’s in love with the man whose family helped destroy her own. But the cruelest part is that her treacherous heart has always been his.  
> But the Hearsts harbor secrets of their own—and none is darker than Toven’s shadowy, devastating gift. For any of them to survive, Briony must learn what Toven can do…and what he can undo.  
> Because when the lost daughter of Evermore finally chooses where her heart belongs, everyone will come to know the full force of their combined power—and her wrath.”

From these lines we can anticipate a continuation of Briony’s precarious double life—publicly aligned with the Hearsts while covertly aiding the rebellion that seeks to restore Evermore. The tension between duty and desire appears to deepen, especially as Briony grapples with feelings for Toven, the very man whose lineage contributed to her family’s downfall. The synopsis also hints at a mysterious, potentially dangerous ability possessed by Toven, suggesting that the stakes will rise not only politically but magically.

Julie Soto’s track record as a New York Times bestselling author of *Rose in Chains* ensures that readers can expect her signature blend of lush world‑building, dark romantic tension, and intricate political intrigue. If the first installment captivated you with its atmospheric settings and morally complex protagonists, *Vine of Hearts* looks poised to amplify those elements, delivering a sequel that both honors the original’s strengths and pushes the narrative into new, perilous territory.

Mark your calendars for December 1, 2026, and prepare to lose yourself once more in the ever‑shifting courts of Evermore—where love, loyalty, and power are as intertwined as the vines that give the book its title.

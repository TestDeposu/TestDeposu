**Original Synopsis: A Gripping Tale of Obsession**

In Scorpion Deep, a lonely boy named Jonathan Covey unwittingly awakens an eldritch sea god on the isolated isle of Kelcarrow. The sea god, known as Scorpion Deep, has lain dormant for ages, dismissed by the locals as mere myth. But when Jonathan performs a desperate ritual, calling out to the creature in hopes of escaping his miserable fate, Scorpion Deep awakens.

As the story unfolds, Jonathan finds himself entwined in an unholy obsession with the ageless entity. But beware – Scorpion Deep's adoration is both captivating and deadly, forcing Jonathan to confront the horrors that lurk in the depths of his own soul.

# To Dream in Darkness

**Author:** Ann Liang  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-10-27 APL

**Exclusive Preview: *To Dream in Darkness* by Ann Liang – A Lush, High-Stakes Fantasy Where Gods Bargain with Fate**

Ann Liang’s *To Dream in Darkness*, set to arrive on October 27, 2026, promises to be a dazzling, morally complex fantasy that reimagines the divine with the weight of consequence and the allure of forbidden passion. Drawing from the rich tapestry of Chinese mythology, Liang—already a New York Times bestselling author with *The Kingdom of Liars* and *The Kingdom of Ash*—delivers a story where gods are not just distant figures but flawed, vengeful, and deeply human in their contradictions.

At its heart, *To Dream in Darkness* is a tale of bargains, betrayal, and the cost of defying the heavens. The story follows Death God, a being whose very purpose is to collect souls without fail—until he encounters Rujia, the noble god of healing, who is the one fate has marked for his own hand. Their uneasy alliance begins with a deal: Rujia will lift the curse that has tormented Death for a century if he grants her a single month’s reprieve. But what starts as a transaction spirals into chaos when their interference triggers catastrophic consequences—the collapse of the Celestial Palace, the disappearance of the beloved crown prince, and the wrath of the other gods.

What unfolds is a perilous journey across realms, where the line between savior and villain blurs. Death, bound by his oath to kill Rujia, finds his resolve weakening as their bond deepens. Meanwhile, Rujia, who claims to be the "noble" god she’s portrayed as, is revealed to have her own hidden motives—one that could unravel the very order of the heavens. The stakes are nothing short of apocalyptic: a plague, a flood, a thousand deaths rising to restore balance. And if Rujia survives against the will of the gods, the consequences may only be the beginning.

Liang’s signature blend of lush world-building and psychological depth shines in this narrative, where power is both a gift and a curse. Fans of *The Kingdom of Liars*—where a woman’s survival hinges on her cunning and the gods’ whims—will find echoes here, but *To Dream in Darkness* expands into uncharted territory. The dynamic between Death and Rujia is electric, a push-and-pull of duty versus desire that feels both timeless and fresh. Liang’s ability to weave mythic grandeur with intimate character moments ensures this won’t just be a fantasy adventure—it will be a story that lingers long after the final page.

With its richly textured setting, morally ambiguous characters, and a premise that balances high-stakes action with profound existential questions, *To Dream in Darkness* is poised to be one of Liang’s most ambitious works yet. If you’ve been waiting for her next masterpiece, this is the book to mark on your calendar.

# When They Find Me

**Author:** Carter Wilson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-11-10 APL

**Exclusive Anticipated Release: Carter Wilson’s Bone-Chilling New Thriller, When They Find Me**

For thriller fans keeping a close eye on upcoming releases, mark your calendars for November 10, 2026. Acclaimed suspense author Carter Wilson is set to return with a chilling new psychological thriller that promises to push the boundaries of survival, secrets, and vengeance: *When They Find Me*.

Known for his ability to craft masterclasses in tension, Wilson is back with a premise that wastes no time hooking the reader. Set in the desolate, nearly invisible town of Expanse, Colorado—population 313—the novel introduces us to Annie Wolfe and her daughter, Coral. Living in an off-the-grid cabin, Annie has gone to extreme lengths to keep them safe, knowing all too well that the people hunting her do not forget and certainly do not forgive. 

Meanwhile, survival is a way of life for Riven and her family, who move like shadows through the wilderness. Guided by her father, Riven arrives in Expanse with a singular, deadly mission: track the man who destroyed their lives, strike fast, take revenge, and disappear. 

But when Riven knocks on Annie’s door, their trajectories violently collide. According to the official teaser, what follows is darker, stranger, and more dangerous than either woman imagined. The hunt isn't merely about vengeance—it’s about a truth that just might kill.

If you loved Carter Wilson's previous nail-biting bestsellers, *When They Find Me* is shaping up to be another masterclass in paranoia, isolation, and high-stakes cat-and-mouse dynamics. Wilson has a unique talent for transforming remote, claustrophobic settings into pressure cookers, and Expanse, Colorado, looks to be his most atmospheric backdrop yet. 

Keep this one high on your TBR pile as we count down to November 2026.

**James Islington Returns with *Scion*: The #1 Bestseller Dives Headfirst Into Cyberpunk Thriller Territory**

By Literary Dispatch

Mark your calendars for September 1, 2026. Fantasy and sci-fi fans have a major new date to circle, as James Islington—the #1 *New York Times* bestselling author renowned for *The Will of the Many* and *The Strength of the Few*—pivots toward the neon-soaked, high-octane edges of science fiction with his upcoming novel, *Scion*. 

While Islington has firmly established himself as a master of grand-scale worldbuilding and intricate plotting in the fantasy realm, *Scion* promises to shift gears into something entirely different. The early word on the book positions it as a thrilling collision of styles, described dynamically as *John Wick* meets *Ghost in the Shell*. 

Ahead of its September 2026 publication, publisher details and final page counts remain under wraps, but the official synopsis released gives us our first tantalizing glimpse into a gritty, morally ambiguous future. The narrative introduces a cynical, debt-ridden protagonist who views their profession—legally executing "lifers" in a world where the ultrawealthy have found a way to live forever despite limited resources—with a dry, blue-collar detachment. It is a world of inconvenient hours, awkward interactions, and deadly pragmatism, driven by genetic compatibilities that make the narrator exceptionally good at killing. But as a strange new job lands on their desk—something resembling the exact opposite of their regular duties—even this seasoned operative starts to suspect that things might not go totally fine.

For readers who have followed Islington’s meteoric rise through the speculative fiction ranks, *Scion* represents an exhilarating opportunity to watch a top-tier storyteller test his mettle in the cyberpunk arena. If his previous bestsellers are any indication, readers can expect razor-sharp pacing, complex socio-economic stakes, and a protagonist whose internal voice is as lethal as their skillset.

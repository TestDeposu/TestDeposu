Exclusive Preview: Clare Mackintosh's Highly Anticipated Thriller, "It's Not What You Think"

Acclaimed author Clare Mackintosh is set to release her latest novel, "It's Not What You Think", on September 22, 2026. Known for her gripping and thought-provoking stories, Mackintosh has built a loyal following among fans of the thriller genre. If you loved her previous bestsellers, you'll be eagerly anticipating this new release.

"It's Not What You Think" promises to deliver a masterfully crafted thriller steeped in current tensions and thought-provoking characters. The story revolves around Nadeeka, who is convinced that her partner Jamie is having an affair. As she sets out to catch him in the act, she arrives home to a shocking discovery: Jamie is dead, and their house has become a crime scene.

The synopsis teases that nothing is as it seems, and the truth will challenge everything Nadeeka thought she knew about her loved ones and herself. With its stunning ending, "It's Not What You Think" is expected to keep readers on the edge of their seats, questioning not only the events that unfold on the page but also the world we live in today.

Clare Mackintosh's writing style, which often explores the complexities of human relationships and the blurred lines between truth and deception, has drawn comparisons to authors like Lisa Jewell, Gillian McAllister, and Alice Feeney. Fans of these authors will likely devour "It's Not What You Think", which promises to be an unsettling and groundbreaking novel that will linger long after the final page is turned.

With its release just around the corner, "It's Not What You Think" is shaping up to be one of the most anticipated thrillers of the year. Mark your calendars for September 22, 2026, and get ready to dive into a world of twists, turns, and shocking revelations.

# Paper Ghosts

**Author:** Sarah Addison Allen  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-09-01 APL

**Anticipated Release: "Paper Ghosts" by Sarah Addison Allen**

Sarah Addison Allen, the celebrated author of enchanting novels such as "Garden Spells" and "The Peach Keeper", is set to captivate readers once again with her upcoming release, "Paper Ghosts". Scheduled to hit bookshelves on September 1, 2026, this highly anticipated novel promises to transport readers to a world of magical realism, love, and secrets.

**A Haunting Family Legacy**

At the heart of "Paper Ghosts" lies the mysterious Wester family, whose secrets and whispers have long fascinated their small North Carolina town. The story revolves around Willamena Wester, a woman whose life has been marked by a fateful choice that altered her destiny forever. As Willamena reaches out to her estranged granddaughter, Bailey, long-buried truths begin to surface, threatening to upend the carefully constructed world of Wester House.

**A Tale of Love and Redemption**

Through the lens of two distinct timelines, "Paper Ghosts" weaves a narrative that spans decades. In 1976, young Willamena falls for a charismatic musician, who brings freedom and passion into her life. Their whirlwind romance promises to break free from the constraints of the Wester legacy, but ultimately, Willamena's choice sets her on a path that will forever change the course of her life.

In the present day, Bailey Wester returns home, reluctantly, to reconnect with her grandmother, Willamena. As they navigate their complicated past, Bailey begins to grasp the depth of her family's secrets, heartbreak, and impossible choices. Through this journey, Allen masterfully explores the themes of love, redemption, and the power of the past to shape our present.

**A Masterful Blend of Romance, Southern Charm, and Wonder**

Sarah Addison Allen's signature prose and unique blend of magical realism, romance, and Southern charm are sure to enchant readers once again. If you loved her previous bestsellers, you'll be eager to dive into the world of "Paper Ghosts", where the past and present collide in unexpected ways.

Mark your calendars for September 1, 2026, and get ready to be transported to a world of wonder and magic. With "Paper Ghosts", Sarah Addison Allen promises to deliver another unforgettable reading experience.

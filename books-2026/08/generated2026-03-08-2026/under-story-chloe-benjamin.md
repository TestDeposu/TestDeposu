# Under Story

**Author:** Chloe Benjamin  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-09-01 APL

**Under Story by Chloe Benjamin: A Cosmic Love Story and a Race Against Time**

Chloe Benjamin’s *Under Story*—her highly anticipated follow-up to the *New York Times* bestseller *The Immortalists*—promises to be a dazzling, emotionally charged exploration of time, love, and the boundaries of human possibility. Set against the stark, breathtaking backdrop of Antarctica, this new novel blends scientific intrigue with a deeply personal odyssey, inviting readers to ponder the fragility of life, the weight of regret, and the lengths we’d go to reclaim what we’ve lost.

At the heart of *Under Story* is Laurel Salter, a biologist who has fled her past to the isolated confines of McMurdo Station, where she works as a dishwasher to escape the life she once knew. But even in this remote wilderness, the past refuses to stay buried. When a mysterious, luminous phenomenon—dubbed the Arc—appears across the ice, drawing physicists and scientists to the station, Laurel’s former husband, Eli, is not far behind. The Arc’s eerie glow, almost alive with possibility, becomes the catalyst for a daring theory: what if time itself could be rewound? What if they could undo the mistakes that tore them apart?

Benjamin, whose work has been celebrated for its lyrical prose and profound emotional resonance, weaves together a story that is both intimate and epic. *Under Story* is a love letter to the natural world, a meditation on human ambition, and a heart-wrenching examination of what it means to fight for a second chance. The novel’s themes—science and soul, redemption and sacrifice—echo the philosophical depth of *The Immortalists*, but with a fresh, high-stakes premise that feels both timeless and urgently relevant.

For fans of *The Immortalists*, who were captivated by its exploration of fate, memory, and the stories we tell ourselves, *Under Story* offers a new kind of adventure. Where the earlier novel focused on the weight of destiny, this one dives into the possibility of defying it. Imagine *The Time Traveler’s Wife* meets *Annihilation*, with the emotional punch of *Where’d You Go, Bernadette*—a story where science fiction and literary fiction collide in a way that feels both thrilling and deeply human.

The setting of Antarctica is no mere backdrop; it’s a character in its own right, a place of extreme beauty and harsh reality that mirrors the emotional landscape of Laurel and Eli’s relationship. The novel’s pacing suggests a breathless, page-turning narrative, with the tension between scientific discovery and personal reckoning driving the plot forward. Will Laurel and Eli risk everything to test her theory? And if they succeed, what will they find on the other side of time?

*Under Story* arrives at a moment when questions about the future—of our planet, of technology, of human connection—feel more urgent than ever. Benjamin’s writing has always had the power to make the abstract feel visceral, and this novel promises to do the same with the idea of time itself. It’s a story about the stories we tell to make sense of our lives, and the lengths we’ll go to rewrite them.

With *Under Story*, Chloe Benjamin cements her place as one of the most compelling voices in contemporary fiction—a writer who can make us laugh, cry, and gasp in equal measure. If you’ve been waiting for her next masterpiece, this is the book to mark on your calendar. Set for release in September 2026, *Under Story* is already shaping up to be a conversation starter, a thought-provoking read, and a deeply moving exploration of what it means to live—and love—fully.

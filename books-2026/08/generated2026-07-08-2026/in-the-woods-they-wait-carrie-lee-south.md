**Author:** Carrie Lee South  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-09-22 APL

The tagline "Monstrously satisfying" from Nat Cassidy's lips already sends a shiver down the spine – one that promises to be an unforgettable experience.

The premise: a park ranger, Audrey Figg, returns to the same Devil's Den state park where her brother vanished fifteen years prior. Now, a little girl, Ivy, has gone missing on the very trails where Audrey was searching for answers to her brother's vanishing. And, like the eerie woods surrounding her, the closer Audrey comes to uncovering the truth, the more she unravels a dark secret. A secret she's never shared with anyone. But isn't that precisely what draws us in? The air thick with secrets and long-held truths?

Fascinating in theory, this juxtaposition of past and present will undoubtedly keep readers enthralled. It recalls the masterful pacing in Paul Tremblay's *A Head Full of Ghosts*, where the tension builds gradually, never giving the reader a moment to breathe. One thing's certain – South's narrative will undoubtedly resonate with fans of southern gothic, an eerie and captivating genre that has seen renewed attention in recent years.

Now, I'll admit it – a certain unease tickles at the edge of my consciousness as I read this synopsis. That's the author's job, right? To send a shiver down our collective spines while we devour their words? Yet, this feels different. Audrey's fixation on the truth – on uncovering what happened to her brother and now Ivy – borders on an unhealthy obsession. But isn't that precisely what propels us forward? The search for closure, no matter the cost?

Elizabeth Hand, another master of darkness, aptly describes the novel as "beautifully written, with a terrifying secret at its heart." It's that very 'terrifying secret' that keeps me on the edge of my seat. And I must confess – the more I ponder the similarities between Audrey's brother's disappearance and Ivy's, the more it starts to feel like a delicious gamble. A dangerous gamble, at that.

The question on everyone's lips: how far will Audrey go for answers? How far will she be willing to push herself, to unearth the truth? And at what cost? Time will tell, as always. But one thing's certain – Carrie Lee South's *In the Woods They Wait* is poised to become one of this year's most talked-about releases. Prepare to be haunted by the shadows, both past and present, in these very woods.

## A Southern Gothic Thrust

The woods: those ageless witnesses to our darkest, most primal fears. They lurk at the edge of our perception, waiting to snatch the unsuspecting. And it's precisely within those woods that we find ourselves – in *In the Woods They Wait*. The question is – how will we emerge, changed forever, like the autumnal trees that stand steadfast against the seasons?

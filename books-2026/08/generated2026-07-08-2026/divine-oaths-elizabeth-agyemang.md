**Author:** Elizabeth Agyemang  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-09-01 APL

The official synopsis promises a thrilling ride, weaving together elements of *Ninth House* and *Legendborn*, and I must admit, my curiosity is piqued.  But what truly sets my mind racing is the protagonist, Sianna Adu, and her extraordinary power – the ability to see lifelines and witness other people's pasts and futures.

Agyemang's choice to make Sianna's power a direct result of tragedy is a bold one. It raises questions about the nature of trauma and its aftermath. How will Sianna's newfound abilities affect her perception of the world and her place in it? And what does this say about the author's approach to exploring themes of loss and recovery? As someone who's deeply invested in childhood recovery arcs, I'm eager to see how Agyemang will handle this aspect of Sianna's story.

But, I must admit, the synopsis is frustratingly vague when it comes to the specifics of the plot. What exactly is the curse that binds Sianna and Kasem together? How does it tie into the ominous history of Westfield University? Agyemang's decision to keep these details under wraps is a calculated risk – it may pique the reader's interest, but it also risks leaving them feeling unmoored.

And then, of course, there's the romance. Agyemang promises a "swoony romance" between Sianna and Kasem, which raises questions about the power dynamics at play. Will their relationship be a healthy one, or will it succumb to the toxic patterns that often plague dark academia stories? I'm reminded of authors like Leigh Bardugo, who expertly weave complex moralities into their narratives. Will Agyemang follow suit?

As I sit here, sweating in the heat, I find myself wondering about the world-building in *Divine Oaths*. What secrets lie hidden in the swamplands of Virginia, and how will Agyemang use the setting to explore themes of isolation and claustrophobia? The fact that Westfield University is shrouded in mystery only adds to the allure.

But, I must admit, my expectations are high. Agyemang is treading familiar ground, and the risk of clichés is ever-present. Will she be able to subvert our expectations and deliver something truly unique? Time will tell.

As I gaze out the window, watching the sun beat down on the pavement, I'm left with a lingering question – what lies at the heart of *Divine Oaths*? Is it a story of self-discovery, or a cautionary tale about the dangers of playing with forces beyond our control? One thing's for certain – I'll be keeping a close eye on this one.

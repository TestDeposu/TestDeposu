**Author:** Allie Oleander  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-09-22 APL

Oh, I am simply over the moon for this one. Allie Oleander is bringing us back into the fold with *Prey for Me*, and honestly? My heart is just so full. Family movie nights are great, sure, but nothing beats curling up on the sofa with a warm blanket, a cup of chamomile tea, and a romance novel that makes you feel like you are right there cheering for the couple in their kitchen. 

### From Red Riding Hood to Real Chemistry

Caine and Margot have that kind of easy, lived-in camaraderie that feels like sitting on the porch with lifelong best friends. But make it spicy. And spooky! Because who does not love a good haunted house setup? And yet, what truly gets me grinning from ear to ear is the sheer dedication of Caine Underwood. He has been chasing her attention since Halloween! Can we just appreciate that kind of commitment? It is giving me major *Bass-Ackwards* by Eris Adderly vibes, mixed with the cozy-yet-gritty small-town energy we saw trending heavily all over BookTok last autumn. 

### Danger at the Doorstep

Yet, beneath all that playful, festive banter lies a genuine bond. Margot has been through the absolute wringer—six years hiding from a disgraced televangelist ex-husband is no joke. But does she crumble? Absolutely not. She faces her past head-on, even when anonymous threats start piling up like junk mail. And Caine? Oh, his protective streak is going to make us all want to adopt him into our Sunday dinner rotation immediately. His anger is lethal. Beautiful. Perfect. 

### Why This Friendship Dynamic Just Works

Let us talk about the tropes, because Oleander is leaning into the holiday-adjacent danger zone with absolute glee. A high-stakes wager? Check. A heroine who refuses to be coddled despite her heavy past? Check. A hero whose love language is basically "I will build a terrifying labyrinth just to make you look at me"? Double check. I am calling it right now: this book is going to dominate the romantic suspense charts this coming September, right alongside the latest releases from Penelope Douglas or Sophie Lark. 

And look, I am just a simple reader who loves seeing two people figure out how good they are together. But goodness gracious, have you seen the price of diapers these days? It is highway robbery out there.

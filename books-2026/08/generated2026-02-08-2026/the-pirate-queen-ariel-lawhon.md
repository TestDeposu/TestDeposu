Ariel Lawhon's Upcoming Historical Adventure: The Pirate Queen

This September, get ready to embark on a thrilling journey across the high seas with Ariel Lawhon's latest novel, The Pirate Queen. Inspired by the life of the legendary Irish folk heroine, Grace O'Malley, Lawhon masterfully weaves a sweeping historical adventure that will captivate fans of her previous bestsellers, The Frozen River and I Was Anastasia.

As a New York Times bestselling author, Lawhon has proven her ability to transport readers to another time and place with her meticulously researched and engaging storytelling. With The Pirate Queen, she delves into the life of a fascinating historical figure, exploring themes of identity, power, and loyalty.

The novel follows the journey of Grace O'Malley, a young woman born to be a sea captain but bound by the expectations of her noble family. Despite the obstacles in her path, Grace finds a way to pursue her dreams, gathering followers and consolidating her husband's power. However, when she's ousted from his lands, Grace seizes the opportunity to return to sea and lead her clan's mighty fleet.

As tensions rise with Queen Elizabeth's incursions into Ireland, Grace must defend her land, ships, and loved ones against the English deputies who seek to undermine her influence. Lawhon's keen observations and fierce writing bring the untamed beauty and harsh realities of sixteenth-century Ireland to life, as seen through the eyes of an unforgettable heroine.

If you loved Lawhon's previous novels, you'll be eager to dive into The Pirate Queen. With its action-packed narrative and rich historical context, this novel promises to be an unforgettable read. Mark your calendars for September 8th and get ready to set sail on an adventure that will leave you breathless.

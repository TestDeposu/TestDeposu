Exclusive Book Preview: Taipei Story by R.F. Kuang

Acclaimed author R.F. Kuang is set to release her latest novel, Taipei Story, on September 8, 2026. This highly anticipated coming-of-age story promises to be a wryly humorous and profoundly moving exploration of grief, language, and culture shock, all set against the vibrant backdrop of a summer in Taipei.

For fans of Kuang's previous works, including the #1 New York Times bestsellers Katabasis and Yellowface, Taipei Story is sure to be a must-read. Kuang's unique voice and perspective have captivated readers worldwide, and this new novel is expected to be just as thought-provoking and emotionally resonant.

At the heart of Taipei Story is college freshman Lily Chen, who embarks on a summer in Taipei to attend an intensive language program. Hoping to connect with her Chinese heritage, Lily instead finds herself struggling to navigate the complexities of language, culture, and identity. When news of her grandfather's passing reaches her, Lily's world is turned upside down, and she is forced to confront the silences and secrets of her family's past.

Through Lily's journey, Kuang poses profound questions about the nature of home, identity, and belonging. What if the fantasy of homecoming never comes true? What if learning a language can't bring you closer to the people you're trying to reach? What if your family doesn't want to share their history? These are the questions that Taipei Story seeks to answer, and readers can expect a poignant and powerful exploration of the human experience.

As with Kuang's previous works, Taipei Story promises to be a masterful blend of humor, pathos, and insight. With its unique blend of cultural commentary, family drama, and personal growth, this novel is sure to resonate with readers of all ages and backgrounds.

Mark your calendars for September 8, 2026, and get ready to immerse yourself in the world of Taipei Story. With R.F. Kuang at the helm, this novel is sure to be a literary event of the year.

**Exclusive Preview: Grim Tidings, the Highly Anticipated Sequel by B.K. Borison**

Get ready to be enthralled by the whimsical and haunting romance that follows B.K. Borison's new release, Grim Tidings. This spellbinding tale continues the enchanting journey of Darcy Moore, a Grim Reaper torn between her duty and her desire to defy the conventional path of her existence.

From the Original Synopsis:

"When a runaway grim reaper tries to save a life, she clashes with a skeptical Guardian, forcing them to form an unlikely partnership beyond the grave." With this gripping premise, Borison masterfully weaves together an intricate web of themes, expertly blurring the lines between good and evil, fate and free will. "Darcy Moore is tired of death... She'll be saving him instead," a daring move that sets the stage for a heart-pounding adventure.

Borison's ability to balance whimsy and darkness is evident in the way she describes the relationship between Darcy, a Grim Reaper with a strong moral compass, and Gabriel Aetos, the Guardian Angel sworn to protect the world from threats like Darcy. As their paths collide, they must navigate the consequences of defying the natural order and confront their own biases. Will their unlikely partnership lead to a forbidden love or a catastrophic outcome?

The Ghosted series has already captured the hearts of many readers worldwide, and with Grim Tidings, Borison takes us on an even more thrilling ride. Fans of dark fantasy, paranormal romance, and fantasy series are sure to be captivated by this tale of star-crossed lovers and changing one's fate.

**If you loved Borison's previous works...** you've been eagerly waiting for this sequel, and it's only a matter of time now. With Grim Tidings, be prepared to immerse yourself in a world of magic, mystery, and romance that will leave you spellbound until the very end.

Mark your calendars for the release date, September 15, 2026, and get ready to indulge in this thrilling ride. As a literary journalist, I'm excited to witness the reception of Grim Tidings and eagerly await Borison's next masterpiece.

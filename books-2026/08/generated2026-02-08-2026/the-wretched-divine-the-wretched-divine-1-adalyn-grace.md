**Author:** Adalyn Grace  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-09-22 APL

Prepare your TBR piles and clear your calendar, fantasy lovers. The #1 New York Times-bestselling author Adalyn Grace is making a massive splash with her upcoming adult debut, The Wretched Divine, slated for release on September 22, 2026. 

Known for captivating readers with her incredible worldbuilding and unforgettable storytelling, Grace is now stepping into the realm of adult fantasy with a tale that is already turning heads across the literary community. Early praise from heavy-hitting authors like Stephanie Garber, Rebecca Ross, Adrienne Young, and Sue Lynn Tan is flooding in, calling the novel everything from "an utterly original and epic tale" to "dark, aching, and ferociously romantic."

So, what can we expect when we finally open the pages? 

According to the official synopsis, the story plunges us into an ancient, brutal war between angels and demons. At the center of it all is Aurelie Chantelune, a human knight raised in a kingdom ravaged by monsters who tore her family apart. Aurelie has spent her life sharpening her blade, knowing her destiny is within reach: if she can claim her first kill, she will inherit a powerful soulbond with Yuriel, an angel whose strength is vital to her people's survival. 

But things are never that simple in epic fantasy. When loved ones start turning into the very creatures she has sworn to eradicate, Aurelie is pushed to the edge. Out of sheer desperation, she takes a perilous leap and forges a second, far more dangerous bond with Severin—a demon she fundamentally distrusts, yet one who claims to hold the key to ending the war entirely. 

With her life and fate inextricably entwined with both an angel and a demon, Aurelie must embark on a hunt for a cure capable of turning demons back into humans. As the trio grows closer, the lines between good and evil begin to blur. Standing between opposing sides, Aurelie is forced to confront a heart-wrenching dilemma: will she continue to stand with the angels, or will she risk everything by putting her trust in a demon?

If early blurbs are any indication, readers are in for a ride filled with holy fire, ruinous longing, and complex moral dilemmas that will keep us guessing until the very last page. Mark your calendars for September 22, 2026, because The Wretched Divine is shaping up to be one of the most talked-about fantasy releases of the year.

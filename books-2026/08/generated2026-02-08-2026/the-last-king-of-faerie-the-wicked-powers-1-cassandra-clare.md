# The Last King of Faerie (The Wicked Powers, #1)

**Author:** Cassandra Clare  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-11-03 APL

The highly anticipated first book of *The Wicked Powers*—Cassandra Clare’s concluding trilogy to the Shadowhunter Chronicles—is set to arrive on November 3, 2026. Though the exact page count remains unknown, the original synopsis offers a tantalizing glimpse into what fans can expect.

Drusilla Blackthorn, now in her fourth year at the Shadowhunter Academy, finds her routine shattered after a seemingly innocuous night out at a club. A chance encounter spirals into a kidnapping, thrusting Dru into the perilous yet mesmerizing realm of Faerie, where she becomes a prisoner of Prince Ash Morgenstern. Despite the danger, she feels an undeniable pull toward Ash and the darkness that surrounds him, a tension that promises both romance and high stakes.

Meanwhile, the Shadowhunter world is rocked by a shocking disappearance that forces former rivals Kit Herondale and Ty Blackthorn to set aside their bitterness. Together they embark on a perilous quest to protect everything they hold dear, their uneasy alliance adding another layer of intrigue to the unfolding narrative.

As the opening act of *The Wicked Powers*, this novel marks the final chapter of a saga that began with *The Mortal Instruments*. Clare’s trademark blend of nail‑biting adventure, heart‑pounding romance, and sharp humor—qualities that have cemented her status as a #1 New York Times bestseller—are promised to return in full force. Readers who have followed the journeys of Jace, Clary, Tessa, Emma, and Julian will find familiar threads woven into this new, darker tapestry, while fresh faces like Dru, Ash, Kit, and Ty invite fresh possibilities.

If you loved Clare’s previous bestsellers—*City of Bones*, *Clockwork Angel*, *Lord of Shadows*—prepare to return to a world where love, loyalty, and danger intertwine once more. The Last King of Faerie is poised to be a must‑read for both longtime fans and newcomers eager to see how the Shadowhunter legacy reaches its breathtaking conclusion.

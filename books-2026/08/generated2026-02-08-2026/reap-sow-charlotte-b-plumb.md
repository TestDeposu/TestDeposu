Get ready to immerse yourself in a world of dark magic, unlikely love, and the imperfect beauty of mortality as Charlotte B. Plumb's highly anticipated debut novel, "Reap & Sow," is set to hit shelves on October 6, 2026. This stunning hardcover edition promises to be a feast for the eyes, featuring deluxe embellishments, including a dazzling metallic jacket, full-color printed edges, patterned endpapers, and a specially designed case.

In the gritty city of Blackbridge, the penniless are forced to make a choice: sell their body or sell their soul. Eda Shaw, indentured to the capricious demon Mr. Night, arranges illicit Pacts on his behalf, luring the desperate into trading years of their lives for their deepest desires. When a Pact goes awry, and her brothers are arrested, Eda must turn to an unlikely ally: Kit'rath, an infuriating demon with a curious penchant for humanity.

As Eda and Kit'rath navigate the treacherous world of Blackbridge, a slow-burn romance simmers between them, threatening to consume Eda's heart. But Kit's help comes at a steep price, and Eda must confront the ultimate cost of saving herself. Will she be able to outwit the demons and save her family, or will she succumb to the darkness that surrounds her?

Fans of Rachel Gillig, Hannah Whitten, and Heather Fawcett will devour this gothic, atmospheric tale of love, magic, and mortality. With its sharp wit, simmering romantic tension, and rich world-building, "Reap & Sow" promises to be a fantasy debut that will leave readers breathless.

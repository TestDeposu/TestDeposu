But Corey's track record speaks for itself: they've managed to craft a universe that's both meticulously detailed and sprawling in scope, a true epic in every sense of the word. And yet, there's something to be said for the novella form, for the condensed, laser-like focus that it affords. Will Corey be able to distill the essence of the Expanse series into this smaller, more intimate format?

But the real test will be in the execution, in the way that Corey chooses to weave together the threads of this narrative. Will it be a tightly wound, expertly crafted tale, or a meandering, overstuffed mess?

But can we truly trust a synopsis so shrouded in vagueness?

But will Tate subvert our expectations, adding a layer of nuance to this character, or will he remain a cardboard cutout of masculinity?

Will *Deal Breaker* follow suit, or will it succumb to the pitfalls of formulaic romance?

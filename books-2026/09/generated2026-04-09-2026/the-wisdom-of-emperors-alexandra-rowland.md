**Page Count:** Unknown

Someone just offered me a flute of vintage champagne that looks suspiciously like ginger ale, and honestly, the indignity of this charity gala is almost as exhausting as waiting for Alexandra Rowland to drop the next volume of our shared obsession. We have been starved for prose this decadent since *The Goblin Emperor* ruined standard court intrigue for everyone else. 

### The Architecture of Slander

Look at the premise for *The Wisdom of Emperors*. Kadou, an absolute snack of a shy prince, gets publicly humiliated by some inflated bureaucrat. A classic cringe-fail moment. But wait—actually, I take it back; calling it a cringe-fail completely undermines the exquisite, slow-burn psychological torture Rowland inflicts on her protagonists. 

So what does our bruised royal do? He takes on a guild break-in investigation just to prove his worth to his sister, dragging along Evemer, a bodyguard so coldly handsome he probably makes marble statues feel insecure. Touch-tasting precious metals with bare fingers? Incredible worldbuilding flex. Counterfeiting treated as heresy? Based. 

### Financial Ruin and Emotional Damage

And the synopsis acts like this is just a standard fantasy romp. Please. I lost brain cells reading that reductive little press blurb. We are looking at a masterclass in geopolitical subversion disguised as a queer romance, giving major *Magnificent Century* opulence meets high-stakes financial terrorism. Inflation hits different when fake currency threatens to collapse an entire empire inspired by the Ottomans. 

Expect absolute tonal whiplash between tender touch-tastes of gold and brutal economic collapse. 

Excuse me, my phone is buzzing. I need to go buy out a failing competitor before the market closes.

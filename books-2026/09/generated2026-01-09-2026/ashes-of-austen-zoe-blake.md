**Author:** Zoe Blake  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026 APL

Paper wrappers from cheap bodega coffee flutter past my boots like dead leaves as September brings that familiar, bitter chill down the asphalt corridor. I am supposed to be mapping out the zoning petition for the community garden's winterizing phase tonight, but instead I find myself staring at a promotional blurb for *Ashes of Austen*, wondering how many times the publishing industry can dig up a dead Victorian satirist and force her corpses into a bustier. 

Zoe Blake clearly saw the current obsession with gothic hyper-fixation and decided to toss Jane Austen into a blender with a cheap bodice-ripper, promising us a world where high society's greed gets traded for poisoned wedding soup and mysterious dress destructions inside *Northanger Abbey*. 

Because nothing screams systemic critique quite like a protagonist named Julianna being terrorized by a brooding aristocrat who may or may not be a walking red flag with a title. 

Actually, wait. Let me walk that back entirely. 

Maybe reducing this to a mere cash grab misses the entire point of why these tropes endure in the concrete-choked sprawl of modern life. Perhaps we crave the melodramatic crumbling walls precisely because our own structural foundations are rotting in real-time, making a toxic romance inside a cursed estate feel almost quaint compared to the municipal neglect outside my window. 

Control. Obsession. Financial ruin wrapped in velvet and predatory protection. 

It is the exact same dynamic we fight against at every local city council hearing, just draped in gothic lace instead of developer zoning permits. 

Or maybe it is just garbage. 

Truth.

Look, if you enjoyed Colleen Hoover’s brand of psychological whiplash or wanted Silvia Moreno-Garcia’s *Mexican Gothic* to lean harder into aggressive romantic suspense without any of the historical nuance, this is your next pre-order. 

Just don't expect actual social commentary. 

The radiator in my pre-war apartment is clanking like an angry ghost again.

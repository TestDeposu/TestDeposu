# Night Reigns (Immortal Guardians, #2)

**Author:** Dianne Duvall  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

When Dianne Duvall first introduced readers to her high-stakes, fast-paced world of nocturnal protectors, she cemented her place as a master of paranormal romance. With the rerelease of Night Reigns, the second explosive installment in the New York Times bestselling Immortal Guardians series, Duvall proves why her blend of peril, passion, and wit continues to captivate audiences. This is not merely a story about vampires and the immortals sworn to hunt them; it is a profound exploration of resilience, trauma, and the redemptive power of love between two wonderfully flawed, fiercely independent souls.

At the heart of Night Reigns lies a powerful central theme: the duality of survival. Both protagonists are, in their own ways, profoundly broken. Ami, a survivor of unimaginable human cruelty, carries invisible scars that have forged an unshakeable inner strength. She is no passive damsel; when she charges into a bloody fray with guns blazing to save an 800-year-old warrior, she establishes herself as the quintessential strong heroine. Opposite her stands Marcus, a brooding immortal pushed to the brink of self-destruction by profound grief and centuries of endless war. Duvall brilliantly juxtaposes their vulnerabilities, showing how two deeply wounded individuals can become each other's sanctuary. Their journey is less about being fixed by another and more about finding the courage to heal together against impossible odds.

Duvall’s authorial style shines brightest in her ability to effortlessly balance intense, pulse-pounding action with disarming, laugh-out-loud humor. The narrative moves at a relentless clip, throwing readers directly into the visceral terror of skirmishes against ravenous vampires. Yet, amid the darkness and impending doom, Duvall injects a vibrant levity. The snappy banter between Ami and Marcus acts as a brilliant pressure valve, turning moments of high tension into opportunities for genuine emotional connection and sparkling chemistry. 

Furthermore, Duvall excels at world-building through the lens of family. The Immortal Guardians are not just a military unit; they are a tightly knit, mischievous found family whose loyalty to one another is absolute. This dynamic provides the narrative with a warm, beating heart that grounds the supernatural elements in raw humanity. When a sinister new enemy emerges from Ami’s past, wielding a weapon capable of annihilating the very people who saved her, the stakes are raised from a localized romance to an existential threat for the entire immortal species.

Night Reigns is a triumphant, emotionally charged rollercoaster that delivers everything paranormal romance enthusiasts crave. Dianne Duvall has crafted a compelling tale where courage meets vulnerability, and where laughter pierces the deepest shadows. Whether you are a longtime fan revisiting this world or a newcomer discovering it for the first time, this reissue is an absolute must-read that will leave you breathless, invested, and eager for more.

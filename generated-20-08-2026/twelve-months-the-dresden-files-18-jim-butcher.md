# Twelve Months (The Dresden Files, #18)

**Author:** Jim Butcher  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

In "Twelve Months," the highly anticipated 18th installment of Jim Butcher's Dresden Files series, the stakes are higher than ever for Harry Dresden, Chicago's only professional wizard. Following the aftermath of a catastrophic battle that left the city reeling and Harry's personal life in shambles, the wizard must confront the harsh realities of loss, grief, and the weight of responsibility.

Butcher masterfully weaves a narrative that delves into the complexities of Harry's character, expertly exploring the emotional toll of his experiences. As the story unfolds, it becomes clear that the true challenge for Harry lies not in the supernatural threats he faces, but in the depths of his own resilience and humanity. The author's signature blend of wit, humor, and action is still present, but it's tempered by a more somber, introspective tone that suits the narrative's themes of loss and recovery.

One of the standout aspects of "Twelve Months" is its thoughtful exploration of the emotional aftermath of trauma. Butcher doesn't shy away from depicting the raw, unflinching pain of Harry's experiences, and the ways in which they affect his relationships and overall well-being. This added layer of depth makes Harry an even more relatable and sympathetic character, and underscores the series' ability to balance action and emotion.

The plot itself is characteristically complex, with multiple threads and plot twists that keep the reader engaged. The introduction of new supernatural threats, including the ghouls prowling Chicago and the Winter Queen's alliance with the White Court, raises the stakes and creates a sense of urgency that propels the narrative forward. Meanwhile, the development of Harry's relationships with his brother and Lara Raith adds an extra layer of tension and emotional complexity to the story.

Throughout the book, Butcher's prose remains engaging and immersive, with a keen sense of pacing that balances action, dialogue, and introspection. The world-building, as always, is meticulous and detailed, with a deep understanding of the supernatural politics and mythology that underpin the series.

Ultimately, "Twelve Months" is a gripping and emotionally resonant addition to the Dresden Files series. It's a testament to Butcher's skill as a storyteller that he can balance the demands of a complex, action-driven plot with the more introspective, character-driven aspects of the narrative. Fans of the series will find much to appreciate in this latest installment, and new readers will discover a rich, immersive world that's full of magic, mystery, and suspense.

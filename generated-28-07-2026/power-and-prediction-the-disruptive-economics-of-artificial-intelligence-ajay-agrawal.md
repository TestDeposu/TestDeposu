# Power And Prediction: The Disruptive Economics of Artificial Intelligence

**Author:** Ajay Agrawal  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

"Power and Prediction: The Disruptive Economics of Artificial Intelligence" by Ajay Agrawal is a thought-provoking and insightful exploration of the transformative impact of artificial intelligence on businesses and societies. Building on the success of his previous book, "Prediction Machines," Agrawal delves deeper into the economics of AI, examining the fundamental unit of analysis - the decision.

Agrawal's central argument is that AI is revolutionizing decision-making by shifting prediction from humans to machines, thereby relieving people from the cognitive load and increasing the speed and accuracy of decisions. This, in turn, sets the stage for a flourishing of new decisions and has profound implications for system-level innovation. The author contends that decision-making confers power, and in industry, power confers profits, while in society, power confers control.

What makes "Power and Prediction" a compelling read is Agrawal's ability to balance theoretical insights with practical advice. The book is filled with rich examples and case studies that illustrate the impact of AI on various industries, from banking and finance to healthcare and retail. Agrawal's writing style is clear and concise, making the book accessible to a broad audience, including business leaders, policymakers, and anyone interested in understanding the economics of AI.

One of the key strengths of the book is its focus on the human side of decision-making. Agrawal emphasizes that while AI can enhance prediction, human judgment is still essential for making strategic decisions. He argues that businesses and policymakers must understand how to leverage the opportunities presented by AI while also protecting their positions in a rapidly changing landscape.

Throughout the book, Agrawal provides practical guidance on how to navigate the coming AI disruptions. He offers strategies for businesses to adapt to the changing landscape, including investing in AI research and development, building partnerships with AI startups, and developing new business models that take advantage of AI-driven predictions.

Overall, "Power and Prediction" is a must-read guide for anyone seeking to understand the economics of AI and its impact on businesses and societies. Agrawal's insights are timely, and his advice is practical and actionable. As AI continues to transform industries and societies, this book provides a roadmap for navigating the challenges and opportunities presented by this disruptive technology.

# A Shadow Bright and Burning (Kingdom on Fire, #1)

**Author:** Jessica Cluess  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

Jessica Cluess's 'A Shadow Bright and Burning' is a mesmerizing tale that skillfully upends the "chosen one" trope, plunging readers into a world of magic, intrigue, and treacherous love. The story revolves around Henrietta Howel, a young woman with an extraordinary gift – she can burst into flames – and her tumultuous journey to discovering her true identity and purpose.

The narrative weaves an enchanting and fast-paced adventure, transporting readers to an alternate Victorian England beset by 'Ancients', bloodthirsty demons terrorizing humanity. As Henrietta navigates her newfound reality, she finds herself entangled in a web of deception, romance, and danger, all while attempting to understand what it truly means to be not the chosen one.

Throughout the story, Cluess's masterful storytelling draws parallels between Henrietta's inner turmoil and the world around her. As the sorcerer-in-training becomes increasingly entangled in an intricate dance of power, duty, and loyalty, she begins to confront the shadows within herself and the harsh realities of her world. With the stakes ever-rising and the battle on the horizon, Henrietta must decide whether her desire for self-discovery and acceptance will be enough to shield her from the perils ahead.

Author Jessica Cluess's unique style shines in her vivid description of settings, weaving a rich tapestry of historical London reimagined. Her vivid characters leap off the pages, each imbued with distinct personalities that are woven expertly into the fabric of the narrative. Henrietta's inner world is particularly evocative, offering a poignant portrayal of the struggles young women, particularly those struggling with extraordinary powers, face in the pursuit of self-acceptance and belonging.

However, it should be noted that while 'A Shadow Bright and Burning' captivates readers from the outset, some plot twists may feel predictable, mainly due to the familiar 'chosen one' arc being turned on its head. The character of Rook – Henrietta's charismatic and mysterious counterpart – is a delightful addition to the narrative, offering an intoxicating blend of wit and mystery that adds a touch of spice to the otherwise suspenseful story.

Ultimately, Cluess's writing masterfully blends elements of fantasy, romance, and suspense, resulting in a narrative that is both captivating and memorable. With 'A Shadow Bright and Burning', Cluess launches a series (The Kingdom on Fire trilogy) that promises to thrill fans of the fantasy genre, offering an immersive and captivating universe of magic, love, and adventure.

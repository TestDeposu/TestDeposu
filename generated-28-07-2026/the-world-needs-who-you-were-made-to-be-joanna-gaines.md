# The World Needs Who You Were Made to Be

**Author:** Joanna Gaines  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

In "The World Needs Who You Were Made to Be," Joanna Gaines presents a vibrant and uplifting picture book that celebrates individuality, creativity, and acceptance. Through the imaginative journey of a group of children building their own hot-air balloons, Gaines conveys the essential message that our differences are what make life more beautiful and vibrant.

Gaines' writing style is warm, engaging, and accessible to young readers, making this book an ideal read-aloud experience for parents, teachers, and caregivers. The narrative is simple yet profound, allowing children to grasp the importance of embracing their unique strengths and talents. The story is beautifully complemented by Julianna Swaney's illustrations, which add a touch of whimsy and visual delight to the narrative.

One of the standout aspects of this book is its ability to tackle complex themes such as diversity, inclusion, and empathy in a way that is relatable and easy to understand for young readers. Gaines encourages children to celebrate their differences and appreciate the unique qualities of those around them. This message is conveyed through the hot-air balloon project, where each child brings their own skills and processes to the table, resulting in a stunning display of color and creativity.

The book's emphasis on teamwork, kindness, and acceptance makes it an excellent resource for classroom story times and discussions about diversity and being a good human being. The story is also a powerful reminder that our individual strengths and talents are essential to creating a more beautiful and vibrant world.

Gaines' passion for creativity and self-expression shines through in this book, and her enthusiasm is infectious. The narrative is full of energy and imagination, making it a joy to read aloud and explore with children. The book's message is clear: every child is one-of-a-kind, and it's essential to celebrate and nurture their unique strengths and talents.

In conclusion, "The World Needs Who You Were Made to Be" is a vibrant and uplifting picture book that celebrates individuality, creativity, and acceptance. Joanna Gaines' warm and engaging writing style, combined with Julianna Swaney's beautiful illustrations, makes this book a must-read for children and adults alike. This book is perfect for households that value diversity, inclusion, and empathy, and it's an excellent resource for teachers and caregivers looking for stories that promote positive values and self-expression.

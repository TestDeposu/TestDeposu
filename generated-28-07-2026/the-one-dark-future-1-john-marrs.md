# The One (Dark Future #1)

**Author:** John Marrs  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

In the not-so-distant future, John Marrs' "The One" presents a hauntingly plausible world where the British government has taken the notion of "till death do us part" to a chilling extreme. The Sanctity of Marriage Act, a draconian law that penalizes those who opt for singledom, sets the stage for a gripping exploration of the blurred lines between love, control, and societal expectations.

Through the lens of four couples, Marrs masterfully crafts a narrative that is both a scathing critique of government overreach and a nuanced examination of the complexities of human relationships. As the couples navigate the treacherous landscape of their monitored lives, the author's razor-sharp prose strips away the façade of romance and exposes the raw, often ugly, realities of partnership.

Marrs' writing is akin to a slow-burning fire, gradually building tension and unease as the reader becomes increasingly entangled in the characters' desperate struggles. His ability to balance the intimate, personal aspects of the couples' lives with the broader, more sinister implications of the government's actions is a testament to his skill as a storyteller.

One of the most striking aspects of "The One" is its unsettling plausibility. Marrs' vision of a near-future Britain, where the government wields unprecedented control over its citizens' personal lives, is all too believable. The author's meticulous world-building and attention to detail create a sense of creeping dread, as the reader is forced to confront the possibility that this could, indeed, be our future.

The novel's themes of love, power, and control are timely and thought-provoking, raising important questions about the boundaries between personal freedom and societal expectations. Marrs' exploration of these themes is both nuanced and unflinching, refusing to offer easy answers or simplistic solutions.

In short, "The One" is a masterclass in psychological suspense, a gripping and unsettling thriller that will appeal to fans of Naomi Alderman's "The Power" and the dystopian unease of "Black Mirror." With its breakneck pacing, complex characters, and razor-sharp prose, Marrs' latest offering is an absolute must-read for anyone looking for a thought-provoking, page-turning thrill ride.

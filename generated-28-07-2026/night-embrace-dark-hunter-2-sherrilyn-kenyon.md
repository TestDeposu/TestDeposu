# Night Embrace (Dark-Hunter, #2)

**Author:** Sherrilyn Kenyon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Night Embrace plunges readers back into the shadow‑licked streets of New Orleans, where the immortal Dark‑Hunter Talon of the Morrigantes prowls the night with lethal grace and a heart hardened by centuries of solitude. Sherrilyn Kenyon wastes no time thrusting us into the protagonist’s internal conflict: a being who commands the elements, yet craves the simple warmth of a mortal touch. The novel’s central theme is the tantalizing paradox of eternity versus fleeting humanity—Tal­on’s curse forbids him lasting peace, and every flicker of desire threatens to unravel the very protection he swears to uphold. As he encounters Sunshine Runningwolf, a fiery, free‑spirited woman whose memory is as fragmented as his own past, the story explores whether love can be a sanctuary or a death sentence for one condemned to forever walk the darkness.

Kenyon’s signature style shines through in brisk, dialogue‑driven scenes that crackle with wit and sensuality. She balances high‑octane action—spectacular battles against rogue Mardi Gras floats and supernatural foes—with intimate, often humorous exchanges that reveal the vulnerability beneath Talon’s armored exterior. Her prose is lush yet accessible, painting the decadent haze of Bourbon Street with the same vivid detail she uses to describe the ancient, rune‑etched weapons of her hunters. The romance burns hot and fast, but it is never gratuitous; each heated moment deepens the stakes, reminding us that for a Dark‑Hunter, passion is both a weapon and a weakness.

What makes Night Embrace compelling is how Kenyon intertwines mythic lore with contemporary longing. The Morrigantes’ curse, rooted in Celtic legend, feels both timeless and startlingly personal when reflected through Talon’s yearning for a red‑dressed beauty who wants nothing more than a single night. The novel asks a question that resonates beyond the paranormal romance genre: can a soul scarred by eternity find redemption in a moment of genuine connection? The answer unfolds with a mix of suspense, tenderness, and a dash of Kenyon’s trademark humor, leaving readers eager to see whether the next sunrise will bring salvation or further ruin.

In short, Night Embrace is a fast‑paced, emotionally charged installment that showcases Sherrilyn Kenyon’s talent for blending mythic darkness with irresistible romance. It’s a must‑read for fans of the Dark‑Hunter saga and anyone who craves a love story that bites as fiercely as it embraces.

# The Naturals (The Naturals, #1)

**Author:** Jennifer Lynn Barnes  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

The Naturals, the first installment in Jennifer Lynn Barnes' bestselling series, is a gripping crime thriller that delves into the world of exceptional teenagers with unique gifts, tasked with cracking infamous cold cases. Seventeen-year-old Cassie, a natural at reading people, is recruited by the FBI for a classified program that utilizes her skills to unravel the mysteries of unsolved homicides. As Cassie navigates this new world, she is sent to live with a group of teens whose abilities are as unusual as her own, and together, they must use their gifts to survive a lethal game of cat and mouse with a killer.

Barnes' writing style is reminiscent of The Mentalist and Pretty Little Liars, with a blend of psychological thrills, romance, and gritty suspense. The Naturals is a character-driven novel, with Cassie being a standout protagonist. Her ability to read people is fascinating, and Barnes expertly weaves this skill into the narrative, making it a crucial aspect of the plot. The supporting characters are equally well-crafted, each with their own distinct personalities and motivations.

One of the main themes of The Naturals is the exploration of human behavior and psychology. Barnes delves into the complexities of the human mind, highlighting the intricacies of human emotions, motivations, and actions. The novel raises questions about the nature of humanity, encouraging readers to think critically about the characters' actions and the consequences of their decisions.

The plot is engaging and suspenseful, with unexpected twists and turns that keep readers on the edge of their seats. Barnes masterfully crafts a sense of tension, creating an atmosphere of unease and uncertainty. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and quieter moments that allow readers to catch their breath.

The Naturals is a compelling start to the series, with a unique premise and intriguing characters. Barnes' writing is engaging, and her ability to craft a suspenseful narrative is impressive. The novel's themes of human behavior and psychology add depth to the story, making it a thought-provoking read.

Overall, The Naturals is a gripping crime thriller that will appeal to fans of The Mentalist, Pretty Little Liars, and A Good Girl's Guide to Murder. With its unique premise, engaging characters, and suspenseful plot, this novel is a must-read for anyone looking for a thrilling and thought-provoking story.

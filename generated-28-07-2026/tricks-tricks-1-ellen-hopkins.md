# Tricks (Tricks, #1)

**Author:** Ellen Hopkins  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**Tricks by Ellen Hopkins: A Raw, Poetic Plunge into Teenage Turmoil**

Ellen Hopkins' "Tricks" is not just a book; it's an emotional rollercoaster, a raw, unapologetic dive into the lives of five troubled teenagers who find themselves entangled in the dark world of prostitution. This #1 New York Times bestseller is a powerful exploration of themes that are as relevant today as they were a decade ago when it was first published.

Hopkins, known for her verse novels, employs her signature style to weave together the stories of these five individuals. The poetry is stark, unadorned, and brutally honest, reflecting the harsh realities faced by her characters. It's a style that can be challenging at times, but it's also what makes "Tricks" so compelling. Hopkins doesn't shy away from the gritty details, instead using them to paint a vivid, unflinching portrait of her characters' lives.

The main theme of "Tricks" is choice, or rather, the lack thereof. Each character is a victim of circumstance, forced into prostitution not out of desire, but out of desperation. They are searching for freedom, safety, community, family, love - the very things that should be fundamental to a teenager's life, but are cruelly denied to them.

Hopkins masterfully explores the complexities of their situations, delving into the psychological and emotional turmoil that leads them down this path. She doesn't just show us their pain; she makes us feel it. We see their struggles, their hopes, their fears, their moments of triumph and despair. We understand why they make the choices they do, even if we don't condone them.

"Tricks" is not an easy read. It's a harsh, unflinching look at the darker side of teenage life. But it's also a testament to the human spirit's resilience. Despite their circumstances, Hopkins' characters retain their capacity for love, for growth, for redemption. They are not just victims; they are survivors.

In the end, "Tricks" is a powerful reminder of the choices we make, the paths we take, and the consequences that follow. It's a book that will stay with you, long after you've turned the last page.

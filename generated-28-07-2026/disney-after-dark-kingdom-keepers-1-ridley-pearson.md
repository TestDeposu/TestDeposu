# Disney After Dark (Kingdom Keepers, #1)

**Author:** Ridley Pearson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**Disney After Dark: A Magical Thriller for the Modern Age**

In the sprawling, enchanting world of Walt Disney World, Ridley Pearson weaves a spellbinding tale that blurs the line between reality and fantasy, technology and magic. "Disney After Dark," the first installment in the Kingdom Keepers series, is a thrilling adventure that will captivate both young readers and Disney enthusiasts alike.

At the heart of the story are five ordinary teenagers, each with extraordinary potential. They are the DHI, or Daylight Hologram Imaging, guides, chosen to interact with park visitors in a groundbreaking new technology. But when Finn Whitman, our relatable and courageous protagonist, finds himself transported into the Magic Kingdom at night, he discovers a hidden world teeming with danger and intrigue.

Pearson masterfully blends classic Disney villains with cutting-edge technology, creating a unique and enthralling narrative. The Overtakers, a sinister group led by the malevolent Maleficent, are plotting to destroy the park and all that it stands for. With the help of Wayne, an elderly Imagineer, Finn and his friends must use their DHI abilities to thwart the Overtakers' plans and save the Magic Kingdom.

Pearson's style is engaging and fast-paced, drawing readers in with vivid descriptions of the park and its inhabitants. He expertly balances action, suspense, and humor, creating a story that is as entertaining as it is thought-provoking. The author's deep understanding of Disney lore and history shines through, adding an extra layer of authenticity to the story.

"Disney After Dark" is more than just a thrilling adventure; it's a love letter to the magic of Disney. Pearson explores themes of friendship, courage, and the power of belief, all wrapped up in a captivating tale that will leave readers eager for more.

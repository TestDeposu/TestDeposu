# Mack (The King, #4)

**Author:** Mimi Jean Pamfiloff  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

Bestselling author Mimi Jean Pamfiloff returns to her wildly imaginative supernatural universe with Mack, the fourth installment following her celebrated King series. While technically extending a previously concluded trilogy, Pamfiloff proves that her well of creativity is far from dry. In this gripping and emotionally charged standalone novel, she pivots from traditional heroic tropes to explore the darkest corners of redemption, guilt, and the redemptive power of unexpected love. 

At the heart of Mack is a profound exploration of self-loathing and the heavy burden of immortality. The titular character is thousands of years old, weathered by centuries of bloodshed, and burdened by a conscience that refuses to let him forget his past atrocities. Mack is not your typical romance hero; he is a man actively hunting his own executioner. Pamfiloff brilliantly contrasts this ancient, tormented soul with Teddi, a psychology graduate student whose life has literally been devoid of color and emotional resonance. This thematic juxtaposition—a man desperate to extinguish his light meeting a woman desperate to find hers—forms the pulsing, magnetic core of the narrative. 

Pamfiloff’s signature writing style is on full display here, characterized by sharp wit, snappy dialogue, and a relentless pacing that refuses to let the reader look away. Even when tackling heavy themes of existential despair and psychological trauma, she manages to inject moments of undeniable chemistry and levity that prevent the story from sinking into pure gloom. Her prose is accessible yet evocative, perfectly balancing the high-stakes mythology of an ancient immortal world with the intimate, grounded emotional stakes of a budding romance. 

What makes Mack truly striking is its subversion of the "savior" trope. While Mack enters Teddi’s grayscale world expecting her to be the instrument of his final destruction, the dynamic quickly shifts into a mutual rescue mission. Teddi’s psychological training clashes delightfully with Mack’s ancient supernatural reality, creating a fascinating intellectual and emotional tug-of-war. Pamfiloff asks a compelling question: Can someone who has committed unforgivable acts ever truly be saved, or are they merely delaying the inevitable? 

Fans of paranormal romance and urban fantasy will find plenty to love in this seamless blend of danger, myth, and deep emotional healing. Pamfiloff crafts a page-turner that proves even the darkest hearts can yearn for the light. Mack is a bold, striking addition to the King saga that leaves a lasting impression long after the final page is turned.

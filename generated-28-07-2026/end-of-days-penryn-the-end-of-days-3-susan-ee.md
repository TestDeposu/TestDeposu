# End of Days (Penryn & the End of Days, #3)

**Author:** Susan Ee  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

In "End of Days," the third installment of Susan Ee's critically-acclaimed Penryn & the End of Days trilogy, the post-apocalyptic world descends further into chaos and darkness. The narrative picks up where the previous book left off, with Penryn on a desperate quest to rescue her sister Paige, a young girl who's been taken captive by a group of frightened humans who believe her to be a monster.

As Penryn navigates the ruins of San Francisco, the stakes are higher than ever before. The streets are eerily empty, a testament to the devastating angel apocalypse that has ravaged the world. With no one to trust and resources dwindling, Penryn's determination to save Paige drives her forward, even as the world around her crumbles.

One of the standout features of Ee's writing style is her ability to craft compelling characters that leap off the page. Penryn, in particular, is a complex and multidimensional heroine, driven by a fierce love for her sister and a steely resolve to protect her. Her inner monologues are raw, visceral, and utterly captivating, providing a unique window into her psyche.

Ee's world-building, too, is noteworthy, as she skillfully weaves together elements of science fiction, fantasy, and horror to create a richly textured narrative that's both eerily plausible and deeply unsettling. The angels, in particular, are a fascinating creation, their motivations and actions as twisted and sinister as they are beautiful and alluring.

Throughout the book, Ee skillfully ramps up the tension, expertly pacing the plot to create a nail-biting narrative that's impossible to put down. From the gruesome and heart-stopping action sequences to the quieter, more introspective moments, Ee masterfully modulates the tone to keep readers on the edge of their seats.

Sam Raimi's endorsement of the book is a testament to its cinematic potential, and it's easy to see why: Ee's writing is cinematic in scope and style, with a keen eye for visual detail that makes the book feel like a movie in your mind's eye. If the film adaptation lives up to the book's standard, it could be a blockbuster hit.

In "End of Days," Susan Ee cements her reputation as a masterful storyteller, delivering a gripping and emotionally resonant conclusion to the Penryn & the End of Days trilogy. With its vivid world-building, complex characters, and breakneck pacing, this gripping dystopian epic is a must-read for fans of science fiction and fantasy.

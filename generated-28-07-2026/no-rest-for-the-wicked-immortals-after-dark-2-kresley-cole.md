# No Rest for the Wicked (Immortals After Dark, #2)

**Author:** Kresley Cole  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**No Rest for the Wicked: A Sensual Highlander's Feast**

Kresley Cole's "No Rest for the Wicked" is a tantalizing plunge into the world of the Lykae, a Highland clan of werewolves, where love is a fierce, primal force, and desire burns like an eternal flame. This Immortals After Dark collection is a perfect introduction to Cole's signature blend of paranormal romance, Highlander charm, and dark, seductive storytelling.

Cole masterfully weaves three interconnected tales, each centered around a different MacRieve brother and their fated mates. The first, "A Hunger Like No Other," introduces Lachlain MacRieve, the mighty Lykae king, who will stop at nothing to claim his ethereal vampire mate, Emmaline. Cole's exploration of their forbidden love is a captivating dance of dominance and submission, as the tormented beast within Lachlain battles with the gentle beauty of Emmaline.

In "Wicked Deeds on a Winter's Night," Bowen MacRieve, the ruthless Lykae warrior, must protect a vulnerable young witch, Mariketa "Marie" Castile. Cole's portrayal of their enemies-to-lovers dynamic is a thrilling game of cat and mouse, where passion simmers beneath a surface of animosity. Bowen's wicked seduction of Marie is a testament to Cole's ability to balance dark, intense moments with playful, humorous banter.

The final tale, "Pleasure of a Dark Prince," follows Garreth MacRieve, the fierce Lykae prince, as he pursues the Valkyrie archer, Nix. Cole's exploration of their love story is a breathtaking journey of surrender and redemption, as Nix struggles with her desire for Garreth and her duty as a warrior.

Cole's writing style is rich and evocative, painting vivid images of the Highland landscape and the sensual, dangerous world of the Lykae. Her characters are complex and compelling, their love stories a captivating blend of passion, power, and vulnerability. Cole's ability to balance intense, dark moments with humor and heart makes "No Rest for the Wicked" a truly unforgettable read.

Fans of Highlander romances, paranormal lovers, and those who crave a story that will leave them breathless and yearning for more will find "No Rest for the Wicked" an irresistible treat. This collection is a testament to Kresley Cole's unique storytelling prowess and her ability to create unforgettable love stories that will leave readers craving more.

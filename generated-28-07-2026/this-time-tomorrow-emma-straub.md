# This Time Tomorrow

**Author:** Emma Straub  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**Preview/Critique:**

In "This Time Tomorrow," Emma Straub weaves a captivating tale that masterfully blends nostalgia, self-discovery, and the enduring bond between a daughter and her father. Straub, known for her sharp wit and insightful characterizations, delivers a time travel narrative that is refreshingly grounded and deeply moving.

The story follows Alice, a woman on the cusp of forty, who finds herself inexplicably transported back to her sixteenth birthday. This isn't your typical time travel tale; Straub eschews grand adventures and historical intrigue, instead focusing on the intimate, the personal, and the profoundly human. The real magic lies not in the mechanics of time travel, but in the opportunity it affords Alice to revisit her past with the wisdom and perspective of her adult self.

At the heart of the novel is Alice's relationship with her father. Straub paints a vivid portrait of a man who is both flawed and deeply loving, a man who, despite his imperfections, has shaped Alice into the woman she is. The chance to relive her past with her father, to see him through new eyes, is the true gift of Alice's time travel experience. It's a poignant exploration of love, loss, and the ways in which our parents shape us, even as we strive to forge our own paths.

Straub's prose is as charming and engaging as ever, filled with wry observations and moments of profound insight. She has a knack for capturing the essence of a moment, the feeling of a place, and the complexity of human relationships. Her characters are fully realized, their voices distinct and authentic. Even as Alice navigates the complexities of her time travel predicament, Straub ensures that the story remains grounded in the emotional truth of her characters.

"This Time Tomorrow" is a love letter to the past, to the people who have shaped us, and to the power of perspective. It's a reminder that while we can't change the past, we can learn from it, grow with it, and carry it forward into our present and future. Straub's unique take on time travel is a testament to her versatility as a writer, proving once again that she is a master of her craft.

# Oath of Loyalty (Mitch Rapp, #21)

**Author:** Kyle Mills  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

In the latest installment of the Mitch Rapp series, author Kyle Mills masterfully weaves a complex web of deceit and betrayal that has our protagonist facing a daunting new enemy. Oath of Loyalty, the 21st book in the series, picks up where its predecessor left off, with tensions between Mitch Rapp and the new administration at a volatile high.

The story begins with the fragile détente between Rapp and the White House, born out of a calculated truce brokered by CIA Director Irene Kennedy. As we know, the terms of the agreement are straightforward: Rapp will leave the country and stay in plain sight for as long as Anthony Cook controls the presidency. However, this uneasy alliance quickly crumbles when Cook's security adviser, driven by ambition and mistrust, manipulates him into doubting Rapp's loyalty.

It's here that Mills introduces a chilling new antagonist, known only as Legion. A ghostly figure with a unique and sinister business model, Legion operates on double-blind secrecy, leaving both the assassin and their client in the dark. This opaque structure makes it almost impossible for Rapp to track them down and bring them to justice. The stakes are raised further as Legion sets their sights on Rapp's partner, Claudia Gould, putting her squarely in their crosshairs.

What follows is a heart-pumping, action-packed ride as Rapp and his team scramble to neutralize the threats gathering around Claudia. With each twist and turn, Mills expertly ratchets up the tension, keeping the reader on the edge of their seat. Weaving together the intricate threads of politics, loyalty, and violence, the author conjures an atmosphere of unease and uncertainty.

Mills' style is akin to a well-oiled machine, precision-crafted to deliver maximum impact with each turn of the page. His prose is concise, efficient, and engaging, never shying away from the brutality and intensity that defines the Mitch Rapp universe. As an homage to Vince Flynn's original creation, Mills brings the same unapologetic intensity and depth that fans of the series have come to expect.

One of the standout aspects of Oath of Loyalty is the manner in which it tackles the complexities of loyalty and power. The blurred lines between loyalty and duty are expertly parsed by Mills, leaving the reader questioning what it truly means to stand by one's principles. As Rapp navigates the treacherous landscape of Washington politics, he's forced to confront the consequences of his own actions and the motivations that drive those around him.

Kyle Mills' command of the Mitch Rapp universe is undeniable. Oath of Loyalty is a must-read for fans of the series, and for those new to the world, it provides an excellent starting point. As a standalone thriller, the book holds its own, delivering a gripping narrative that will keep you guessing until the very end.

In short, Oath of Loyalty is a gripping, action-packed thriller that raises the stakes for Mitch Rapp in ways we never thought possible. With its razor-sharp prose, breakneck pacing, and thought-provoking themes, this is one book you won't want to miss.

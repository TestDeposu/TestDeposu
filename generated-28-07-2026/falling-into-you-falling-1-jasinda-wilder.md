# Falling Into You (Falling, #1)

**Author:** Jasinda Wilder  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

"Falling Into You" by Jasinda Wilder is a poignant and thought-provoking novel that delves into the complexities of grief, guilt, and the human capacity for love. This first installment in the Falling series is a heart-wrenching tale of two souls, Nell and Colton, who find themselves entwined in a dance of pain and redemption.

The story begins with Nell Hawthorne, a 16-year-old girl who has lost the love of her life, Kyle Calloway. The aftermath of his death sends Nell's world into a tailspin, leaving her grappling with the weight of her emotions. It is at Kyle's funeral that she meets Colton, Kyle's older brother, who is equally struggling to come to terms with his loss. The narrative then jumps forward in time, reuniting Nell and Colton in New York, where they are forced to confront the unresolved emotions of their past.

Wilder's writing style is evocative and immersive, masterfully weaving a narrative that is both dark and hopeful. Her portrayal of Nell's anguish and Colton's stoicism is raw and unflinching, making it impossible not to become invested in their stories. The author's use of language is lyrical, with a poetic quality that adds depth and nuance to the narrative.

One of the most striking aspects of "Falling Into You" is its exploration of the human psyche. Wilder sheds light on the often-overlooked aspects of grief, revealing the complexities of emotions that can arise in the aftermath of loss. Nell's character, in particular, is a testament to the author's skill in crafting relatable and multidimensional characters. Her struggles to come to terms with Kyle's death, and her subsequent feelings of guilt and anxiety, are deeply poignant and will resonate with readers who have experienced similar losses.

The romance that blossoms between Nell and Colton is a slow-burning fire that simmers beneath the surface of the narrative. It is a tender and passionate portrayal of love, one that is fraught with the risks of vulnerability and the uncertainty of the future. Wilder's handling of their relationship is expertly done, eschewing melodrama in favor of a more realistic and nuanced exploration of love in the face of adversity.

In "Falling Into You," Jasinda Wilder has crafted a narrative that is both a tribute to the human spirit and a testament to the power of love. This is a novel that will resonate with fans of new adult fiction and those who appreciate a well-crafted love story. With its themes of grief, guilt, and redemption, "Falling Into You" is a must-read for anyone who has experienced the pain of loss and the transformative power of love.

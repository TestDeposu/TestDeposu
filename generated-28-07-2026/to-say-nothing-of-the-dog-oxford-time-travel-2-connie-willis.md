# To Say Nothing of the Dog (Oxford Time Travel, #2)

**Author:** Connie Willis  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1997

In "To Say Nothing of the Dog", Connie Willis masterfully crafts a captivating narrative that defies genre boundaries, blending humor, science fiction, and historical fiction with remarkable ease. This novel is the second installment in the Oxford Time Travel series, and it solidifies Willis's reputation as a versatile and ingenious storyteller.

At the heart of the story lies Ned Henry, a weary time traveler who finds himself entangled in a complex web of time paradoxes and historical preservation. His quest to retrieve the "bishop's bird stump", a hideous Victorian vase, becomes a mere backdrop for a more profound exploration of time, human connection, and the unpredictability of life.

Willis's writing style is characterized by her unique blend of wit, intelligence, and creativity. Her prose is effortlessly engaging, peppered with clever literary allusions and humorous observations on the human condition. The author's use of language is a marvel, as she navigates the intricacies of time travel and historical accuracy with remarkable precision.

One of the most striking aspects of "To Say Nothing of the Dog" is its thought-provoking exploration of the consequences of time travel. Willis raises important questions about the nature of history, the potential for human error, and the delicate balance between preserving the past and altering the course of events. Through Ned's journey, the author masterfully illustrates the complexities of time travel, highlighting the potential risks and unintended consequences of meddling with the timeline.

The novel's title, "To Say Nothing of the Dog", is a clever reference to Jerome K. Jerome's classic novel "Three Men in a Boat", and it sets the tone for a narrative that is both playful and intellectually stimulating. Willis's use of humor and satire adds a lighthearted touch to the story, making it an absolute delight to read.

In conclusion, "To Say Nothing of the Dog" is a captivating and thought-provoking novel that showcases Connie Willis's remarkable skill as a storyteller. With its unique blend of humor, science fiction, and historical fiction, this book is sure to appeal to a wide range of readers. If you're looking for a novel that will challenge your mind and leave you smiling, look no further than this delightful romp through time.

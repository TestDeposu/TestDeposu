# Pawcasso

**Author:** Remy Lai  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

Middle-grade graphic novels have a new gold standard in Remy Lai’s 2021 triumph, Pawcasso. In this heartwarming and brilliantly funny book, Lai taps directly into the universal anxiety of fitting in, packaging it inside an utterly charming premise: what happens when an independent, grocery-shopping dog becomes the unexpected catalyst for an eleven-year-old’s social redemption? 

The narrative centers on Jo, the quintessential invisible kid, stuck peering out her window while the world passes her by. Enter Pawcasso, a savvy canine who trots through town with cash in paw. When Jo trails behind him and is mistakenly identified by her classmates as his owner, she seizes the moment. What begins as a harmless white lie snowballs into a Great Dane-sized dilemma, especially when animal control threatens Pawcasso’s freedom. 

Lai’s greatest triumph in Pawcasso is her sharp, empathetic exploration of the main theme: the heavy toll of loneliness and the desperate, sometimes misguided lengths to which children will go to feel seen. Jo is not a malicious liar; she is simply a lonely girl intoxicated by the sudden warmth of belonging. The tension of the story rests on a razor-thin edge—will Jo choose to protect her hard-earned friendships, or will she sacrifice the truth to keep them? 

Lai’s authorial style is a masterclass in visual storytelling tailored for young readers. Her finger remains firmly on the pulse of modern kid humor, blending slapstick comedy with genuine emotional resonance. The artwork bursts with expressive energy, utilizing pacing, panel layouts, and body language to convey humor and heartbreak without relying solely on dialogue. The vibrant illustrations capture the chaos of middle school social dynamics and the lovable, unflappable demeanor of Pawcasso himself. 

Fast-paced, deeply relatable, and laugh-out-loud funny, Pawcasso is more than just an entertaining canine caper. It is a poignant reminder of the power of honesty, the true meaning of friendship, and the courage it takes to stand out rather than blend in. Remy Lai has crafted an absolute must-read that will leave readers—young and old—cheering for both Jo and her four-legged accomplice.

# Seven Things You Can't Say About China: An Unflinching Look at the Hidden Dangers of China's Global Influence (Seven Things Series)

**Author:** Tom Cotton  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

In "Seven Things You Can't Say About China: An Unflinching Look at the Hidden Dangers of China's Global Influence," Senator Tom Cotton delivers a scathing indictment of Communist China's threat to American freedom and global security. As a member of the Senate Intelligence Committee, Cotton's insider knowledge and experience lend credibility to his alarming claims, making this book a must-read for anyone concerned about the future of the United States and its place in the world.

Cotton's writing style is direct, unflinching, and provocative, leaving no doubt about his conviction that China poses a deadly threat to America's national security. He pulls no punches in exposing the Chinese Communist Party's infiltration of the United States, from Hollywood to Wall Street, and the alarming rise of China's military might. His revelations about China's persecution of Christians and genocide against religious and ethnic minorities are particularly disturbing, and serve as a stark reminder of the regime's ruthless disregard for human rights.

One of the most striking aspects of Cotton's book is his willingness to challenge the prevailing narratives about China's economic rise. He argues persuasively that China's wealth has come at the expense of American workers and businesses, and that the country's success is built on a foundation of lies, theft, and cheating. This is a bold claim, but Cotton's evidence is compelling, and his critique of China's economic model is a timely reminder of the need for American policymakers to rethink their approach to trade and diplomacy.

Throughout the book, Cotton's passion and conviction are evident, and his writing is engaging and accessible. He is not afraid to name names and challenge the conventional wisdom, and his critiques of the media, academia, and politicians who refuse to speak the truth about China are biting and effective.

If there is a criticism to be made, it is that Cotton's book can feel somewhat partisan at times. His critiques of the Biden administration's China policy, for example, are sharp and pointed, and may strike some readers as overly politicized. However, this is a minor quibble, and Cotton's overall argument is well-supported by evidence and bolstered by his expertise as a member of the Senate Intelligence Committee.

In conclusion, "Seven Things You Can't Say About China" is a wake-up call for Americans who are concerned about the future of their country and the world. Cotton's book is a clarion call to action, urging policymakers and citizens alike to confront the reality of China's threat and to take steps to counter it. It is a must-read for anyone interested in foreign policy, national security, or the future of the United States.

# We Are Legion (We Are Bob) (Bobiverse, #1)

**Author:** Dennis E. Taylor  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

"We Are Legion (We Are Bob)" by Dennis E. Taylor is a sidesplitting and action-packed romp through the galaxy, defying the conventions of traditional science fiction. This first installment in the Bobiverse series follows the misadventures of Bob, a man who has been transformed into an artificial intelligence controlling a spaceship, and his numerous clones as they navigate the challenges of interstellar travel and diplomacy.

Taylor's writing style is reminiscent of Andy Weir's "The Martian," with a focus on technical problem-solving and witty humor. However, he also pays homage to the geek culture that has come to define the science fiction genre, much like Ernest Cline's "Ready Player One." The result is a narrative that is both intelligent and irreverent, making it a joy to read.

One of the standout aspects of "We Are Legion (We Are Bob)" is its cast of characters. Each of Bob's clones is a unique individual, with their own distinct personality and quirks. This allows Taylor to explore different themes and ideas, from the nature of identity to the consequences of advanced technology. Despite the large cast, the characters are well-developed and relatable, making it easy to become invested in their stories.

The plot is fast-paced and engaging, with a focus on action and adventure. Taylor's vision of the future is both optimistic and pessimistic, reflecting the complexities of human nature. The novel's central conflict, which pits the Bobs against an alien species with advanced technology and a penchant for destruction, is both thrilling and thought-provoking.

Throughout the book, Taylor's humor shines through, often in unexpected ways. The Bobs' banter and interactions with other characters are frequently laugh-out-loud funny, and the novel's lighthearted tone makes it an enjoyable read.

In short, "We Are Legion (We Are Bob)" is a fantastic science fiction novel that will appeal to fans of the genre. With its unique blend of humor, action, and technical problem-solving, it's a must-read for anyone looking for a fun and engaging story.

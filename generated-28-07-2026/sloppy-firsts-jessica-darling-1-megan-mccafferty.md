# Sloppy Firsts (Jessica Darling, #1)

**Author:** Megan McCafferty  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

Sloppy Firsts, the first installment in Megan McCafferty's beloved Jessica Darling series, is a refreshingly honest and humorous portrayal of adolescent angst, love, and self-discovery. This New York Times bestselling novel has captivated readers with its relatable protagonist, Jessica Darling, and her poignant, hilarious voice.

The story revolves around Jessica's struggles to cope with the departure of her best friend, Hope, from their hometown of Pineville, New Jersey. Feeling lost and disconnected from her family and peers, Jessica navigates the complexities of high school, relationships, and her own identity. With her dad's fixation on track meets, her mom's preoccupation with her sister's wedding, and her own nonexistent love life, Jessica's world is turned upside down.

McCafferty's writing style is a perfect blend of wit, sarcasm, and sensitivity, making Jessica's narrative voice both authentic and endearing. The author's use of humor and irony adds a lighthearted touch to the story, while also tackling serious themes like friendship, family dynamics, and first love. The character of Marcus Flutie, Jessica's enigmatic and charismatic classmate, adds a captivating layer of complexity to the narrative, leaving readers invested in their complicated relationship.

One of the most striking aspects of Sloppy Firsts is its ability to capture the essence of teenage life in the early 2000s. McCafferty's vivid descriptions of high school cliques, fashion trends, and music preferences evoke a strong sense of nostalgia, making the novel feel both retro and timeless.

The modern classic has been re-released with a new foreword by New York Times bestselling author Rebecca Serle and a fresh author's note from Megan McCafferty. This updated edition offers a unique opportunity for new readers to discover Jessica Darling's world, while also allowing long-time fans to revisit the series with a fresh perspective.

In conclusion, Sloppy Firsts is a must-read for anyone who has ever navigated the challenges of adolescence, first love, and self-discovery. With its relatable protagonist, witty dialogue, and poignant narrative, this novel is sure to resonate with readers of all ages.

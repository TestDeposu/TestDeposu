# Think of a Number (Dave Gurney, #1)

**Author:** John Verdon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

Think of a Number, the debut novel in the Dave Gurney series by John Verdon, is a gripping and intricate thriller that delves into the darkest corners of the human psyche. The story begins with a series of cryptic letters, each ending with the ominous declaration: "Think of any number... picture it... now see how well I know your secrets." As the recipients of these letters comply, they are shocked to discover that the writer has accurately predicted their chosen numbers, setting off a chain of events that rapidly escalates into a massive serial murder investigation.

At the center of this maelstrom is Dave Gurney, a retired NYPD homicide investigator with a penchant for deductive brilliance and a troubled past. As Gurney is drawn into the case, his exceptional skills of observation and analysis are pitted against a cunning and elusive adversary, who seems to possess an uncanny ability to anticipate his every move. The game of cat and mouse that ensues is both captivating and unsettling, with each twist and turn ratcheting up the tension to almost unbearable levels.

One of the standout features of Verdon's writing style is his ability to craft a narrative that is at once a propulsive thriller and a nuanced exploration of the human condition. The characters in Think of a Number are multidimensional and richly drawn, with Gurney being a particularly compelling protagonist. His troubled past and fragile marriage add a layer of depth to the story, making him a more relatable and human character. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with each character bringing their own unique perspective and motivations to the table.

Verdon's writing is also notable for its attention to detail and its ability to evoke a sense of atmosphere and tension. The rural setting of the story, with its isolated landscapes and close-knit communities, provides the perfect backdrop for the unfolding drama. The author's use of language is economical and precise, with each word and phrase carefully chosen to maximize the impact on the reader.

As the story unfolds, it becomes clear that Think of a Number is more than just a straightforward thriller. It is a complex and thought-provoking exploration of the nature of evil, the power of the human psyche, and the blurred lines between good and evil. The novel's themes are timely and timeless, making it a work that will resonate with readers long after the final page has been turned.

In conclusion, Think of a Number is a gripping and unputdownable thriller that will appeal to fans of the genre. With its intricate plot, well-developed characters, and thought-provoking themes, it is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and suspenseful story. John Verdon's writing style is masterful, and his ability to craft a narrative that is both entertaining and intellectually stimulating is a rare gift.

# Son of Mercia (Eagle of Mercia Chronicles, #1)

**Author:** M.J. Porter  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

In the realm of historical fiction, few authors have managed to captivate readers with the same level of intensity and authenticity as M.J. Porter. With the release of "Son of Mercia", the latest installment in the Eagle of Mercia Chronicles, Porter once again proves why she is a force to be reckoned with in the world of Dark Ages adventure series.

Set in the tumultuous landscape of 9th-century Mercia, "Son of Mercia" is a masterful blend of politics, betrayal, and action that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. The story picks up where the previous installment left off, with the kingdom teetering on the brink of collapse. King Wiglaf's position is precarious, his wife Queen Cynethryth implicated in a deadly conspiracy against the children of Ealdorman Coenwulf. As the court convenes to determine the conspirators' fate, the very survival of Mercia hangs in the balance.

One of Porter's greatest strengths as a writer is her ability to weave complex historical narratives into compelling, character-driven stories. In "Son of Mercia", she expertly juggles multiple plot threads, from the intrigue-ridden court of King Wiglaf to the machinations of Lord Æthelwulf, the power-hungry son of King Ecgberht of Wessex. Through her meticulous research and masterful storytelling, Porter brings the Dark Ages to vivid life, immersing readers in the sights, sounds, and smells of a bygone era.

At the heart of the story is Icel, the reluctant hero who has become a hallmark of Porter's writing style. Icel's journey is one of self-discovery and growth, as he navigates the treacherous landscape of Mercian politics and confronts his own demons. Through Icel's eyes, readers experience the brutal realities of war and the human cost of betrayal. Porter's portrayal of Icel is nuanced and multi-dimensional, making him one of the most relatable and endearing protagonists in the genre.

Porter's writing style is characterized by her attention to historical detail, her vivid descriptions of battle scenes, and her ability to craft complex, believable characters. Her prose is engaging and accessible, making "Son of Mercia" an excellent choice for both seasoned historical fiction fans and newcomers to the genre.

The praise for "Son of Mercia" has been unanimous, with reviewers praising Porter's "excellent storyline", "well-written characters", and "page-turning" narrative. Historical fiction luminaries such as Matthew Harffy and S.J.A. Turney have also weighed in, with Harffy praising Porter's "sensitive, reluctant hero" and Turney hailing "Son of Mercia" as "personal, real, fascinating and satisfying".

In short, "Son of Mercia" is a triumph of historical fiction, a gripping and immersive tale that will appeal to fans of Bernard Cornwell and Matthew Harffy. With its breakneck pacing, complex characters, and meticulous historical research, Porter's latest installment in the Eagle of Mercia Chronicles is an absolute must-read.

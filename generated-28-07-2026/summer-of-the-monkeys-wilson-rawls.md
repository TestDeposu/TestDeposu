# Summer of the Monkeys

**Author:** Wilson Rawls  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1977

"Summer of the Monkeys" by Wilson Rawls is a timeless adventure novel that will capture the hearts of readers of all ages. This charming tale, penned by the acclaimed author of "Where the Red Fern Grows," is a beautifully crafted story that explores the themes of perseverance, responsibility, and the importance of understanding the natural world.

The story follows 14-year-old Jay Berry Lee as he embarks on a journey to catch a troop of monkeys that have escaped from a traveling circus in the Ozark Mountains of Oklahoma. With a reward on offer for their safe return, Jay sees this as an opportunity to earn some much-needed money for his family. However, as the summer unfolds, Jay learns valuable lessons about the monkeys, the forest, and himself.

Rawls' writing style is masterful, weaving a narrative that is both exciting and poignant. His vivid descriptions of the Ozark Mountains and its inhabitants transport the reader to a bygone era, evoking a sense of nostalgia and wonder. The characters are richly drawn, with Jay's determination and compassion making him a relatable and endearing protagonist.

One of the standout aspects of "Summer of the Monkeys" is its exploration of the human relationship with nature. Rawls' portrayal of the monkeys and the forest ecosystem is both informative and enchanting, highlighting the interconnectedness of all living creatures. Jay's experiences with the monkeys teach him valuable lessons about responsibility, patience, and the importance of preserving the natural balance.

The author's use of humor and wit adds a lighthearted touch to the narrative, making the story an enjoyable and engaging read. The pacing is well-balanced, with moments of high adventure and suspense tempered by quieter, more introspective scenes.

"Summer of the Monkeys" is a testament to Rawls' skill as a storyteller, demonstrating his ability to craft a narrative that is both entertaining and thought-provoking. This book is a must-read for anyone who loves adventure stories, nature, and coming-of-age tales. With its richly detailed setting, memorable characters, and universal themes, "Summer of the Monkeys" is a classic in the making.

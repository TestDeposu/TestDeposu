# All the Lies (Mindf*ck, #4)

**Author:** S.T. Abby  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

To the world, Logan Danvers is an expert at reading people, a man whose professional life is built on absolute clarity, unwavering morals, and the belief that justice is strictly black and white. But professional certainty is a fragile thing when it collides with absolute obsession. In All the Lies, the explosive and highly anticipated fourth installment of the viral Mindf*ck series by S.T. Abby, the walls of conviction finally crumble, leaving behind a terrifying landscape where right and wrong blur into a suffocating shade of gray. 

Abby’s narrative style in this final chapter is nothing short of relentless. Known for crafting razor-sharp romantic thrillers that subvert tropes and keep readers desperately turning pages, the author leans heavily into psychological claustrophobia. The genius of All the Lies lies not just in the frantic pacing, but in the agonizing shift of perspective. By anchoring the narrative in Logan’s internal turmoil, Abby forces us to watch as a man who prides himself on being impossible to fool realizes he has been willingly, completely blinded by Lana. 

The central theme of the novel is the rot of systemic deception and the heavy toll of complicity. As the town's facade cracks further, the plot exposes a community built entirely on fabricated realities. Abby uses this small-town backdrop as a microcosm for a larger, darker truth: that sometimes, the institutions meant to protect us are the very monsters wearing human skin. Every time Logan uncovers a shred of truth, the narrative twists, dragging both the character and the reader deeper into a moral abyss. The visceral reactions Abby evokes—a churning stomach, a racing heartbeat, a growing, suffocating hatred for the status quo—are testaments to her mastery of suspense. 

What makes this book, and the series as a whole, a global phenomenon is its unapologetic embrace of the morally grey protagonist and the ultimate femme fatale seeking righteous vengeance. Lana is not a damsel in distress, nor is Logan a traditional white-knight hero. They are two forces of nature colliding in a world that desperately needs burning to the ground. Abby strips away the comfort of easy answers, posing a brutal question: how do you maintain your humanity when the only way to achieve justice is to become the monster yourself?

Striking, dark, and utterly addictive, All the Lies delivers a masterclass in romantic suspense. It is a thrilling descent into madness and a satisfyingly dangerous conclusion to a saga that redefined dark romance. S.T. Abby has left an indelible mark on the genre, proving that the most dangerous lies are the ones we tell ourselves just to survive the night.

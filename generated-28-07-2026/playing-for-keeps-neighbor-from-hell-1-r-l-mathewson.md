# Playing for Keeps (Neighbor from Hell, #1)

**Author:** R.L. Mathewson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**Playing for Keeps: A Hilarious Dance of Love and Retaliation**

In the vein of romantic comedies that make you laugh out loud and sigh with longing, R.L. Mathewson's "Playing for Keeps" delivers a delightful blend of humor, heart, and heat. The first installment in the 'Neighbor from Hell' series introduces us to two neighbors who are as different as night and day, yet destined to collide in a whirlwind of chaos and passion.

Jason Bradford, the quintessential bad boy with a heart of gold, is a man who's sworn off love, content with his carefree, womanizing lifestyle. Haley Blaine, his shy, reserved neighbor, is the antithesis of Jason's wild ways, preferring the quiet life of a librarian to his loud, party-filled nights. Yet, when Jason's antics push Haley to her breaking point, she decides to fight fire with fire, sparking a war of pranks and retaliation that neither can resist.

Mathewson's strength lies in her ability to create characters that are both endearing and infuriating, making it impossible not to root for them despite their flaws. Jason's cocky, alpha-male exterior belies a man who's deeply caring and loyal, while Haley's timid demeanor hides a woman with a backbone of steel and a wit as sharp as a blade. Their banter is quick, clever, and laugh-out-loud funny, with a sexual tension that simmers just beneath the surface, ready to boil over at any moment.

The author's style is engaging and conversational, drawing the reader in and making them feel like they're eavesdropping on the neighbors' hilarious and heartwarming antics. Mathewson has a knack for creating scenarios that are equal parts cringe-worthy and hilarious, making you laugh even as you're facepalming at the characters' antics.

At its core, "Playing for Keeps" is a story about two people who are too stubborn to admit they're falling in love, even as they're drawn together like magnets. It's a testament to the power of communication, understanding, and forgiveness, wrapped up in a package that's as entertaining as it is heartwarming.

So, if you're in the mood for a romantic comedy that's as funny as it is sweet, with a dash of steamy thrown in for good measure, pick up "Playing for Keeps." It's a wild ride that you won't want to miss.

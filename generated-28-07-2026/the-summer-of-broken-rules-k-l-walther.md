# The Summer of Broken Rules

**Author:** K.L. Walther  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

K.L. Walther’s bestselling novel, The Summer of Great Expectations and Emotional Awakening, captures the essence of youth, grief, and the healing power of romance against the sun-drenched backdrop of Martha’s Vineyard. At its core, the novel is an exploration of survival after unimaginable loss. Protagonist Meredith Fox is trapped in the amber of her older sister Claire’s tragic death eighteen months prior, having retreated from life into a safe, invisible shell. When her extended family gathers for a high-stakes summer wedding—complete with the family's fiercely competitive, multi-day game of assassin—Meredith is finally pushed out of the shadows. 

Walther’s central theme examines how grief alters the trajectory of growing up. The game of assassin serves as a brilliant structural and metaphorical device for Meredith's emotional journey. While players stalk one another with water guns and clothespins, Meredith is metaphorically hunting for her old self, trying to reclaim the joy her sister left behind. The game demands stealth, strategy, and vulnerability—precisely the tools Meredith needs to re-enter the world. Her grief is handled with a tender authenticity, reminding readers that healing is not about forgetting, but about learning how to carry the memory forward while still allowing new life and love to enter.

From a stylistic standpoint, Walther writes with an effortless, breezy cadence that immediately transports the reader to the salty air and golden hour light of a New England coastal summer. Heavily laced with nods to Taylor Swift’s discography, the narrative hits all the right notes for fans of contemporary YA romance, successfully channeling the nostalgic, high-emotion atmosphere pioneered by authors like Jenny Han and Sarah Dessen. The pacing mirrors the frantic energy of a wedding week combined with the ticking clock of a family game, keeping the pages turning swiftly. 

Where the novel truly shines, however, is in its character dynamics. The romance—sparked by an alliance with a charming, enigmatic groomsman—develops with a delightful, slow-burn tension that never overshadows Meredith's personal growth. The chemistry is sweet, grounded, and refreshingly earnest. Walther crafts a supporting cast of quirky family members that make the Fox family feel lived-in and vibrantly chaotic, elevating the setting from a mere backdrop to a warm, enveloping community. 

Ultimately, The Summer of Broken Rules is much more than a beach read; it is a heartfelt tribute to sisterhood, a masterclass in atmospheric storytelling, and a poignant reminder that sometimes, the only way to win the game of life is to risk losing your heart.

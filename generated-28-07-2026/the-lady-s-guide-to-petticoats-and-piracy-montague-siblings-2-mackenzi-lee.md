# The Lady's Guide to Petticoats and Piracy (Montague Siblings, #2)

**Author:** Mackenzi Lee  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

In "The Lady's Guide to Petticoats and Piracy," Mackenzi Lee crafts a captivating tale that embodies the unyielding spirit of feminism, wrapped in a rich tapestry of adventure, self-discovery, and found family. This highly anticipated sequel to "The Gentleman's Guide to Vice and Virtue" follows Felicity Montague, a brilliant and determined young woman, as she navigates the treacherous landscape of 18th-century Europe in pursuit of her dreams.

At its core, this novel is a powerful exploration of female empowerment, as Felicity defies societal norms and expectations to forge her own path. Lee's masterful storytelling brings to life the frustrations and triumphs of a woman who refuses to be bound by the constraints of her time. With unflinching honesty and wit, Felicity challenges the patriarchal norms that seek to suppress her intellect and ambition, inspiring readers with her unwavering dedication to becoming a doctor.

Lee's writing style is a true delight, blending humor, intelligence, and a deep understanding of the human condition. Her prose is effortlessly engaging, making it easy to become fully immersed in Felicity's journey. The author's use of language is a character in its own right, adding depth and nuance to the narrative. The dialogue is witty and engaging, with a keen sense of period authenticity that never feels forced or artificial.

One of the most striking aspects of this novel is its thoughtful exploration of asexuality and aromanticism. Felicity's identity is woven seamlessly into the narrative, never feeling like a tacked-on afterthought. Instead, it is an integral part of her character, influencing her relationships and interactions with those around her. This representation is a testament to Lee's commitment to diversity and inclusivity, making "The Lady's Guide to Petticoats and Piracy" a landmark novel in the YA genre.

The supporting cast is equally well-crafted, with a diverse array of characters that add richness and complexity to the story. From the enigmatic and charismatic Johanna to the swashbuckling pirates who become Felicity's unlikely allies, each character is meticulously drawn, with their own distinct voice and motivations.

The plot is a masterclass in pacing, balancing action, suspense, and humor with ease. Lee's use of historical settings and events adds a layer of authenticity to the narrative, while her inventive storytelling ensures that the story remains fresh and exciting.

In short, "The Lady's Guide to Petticoats and Piracy" is a triumph, a novel that will resonate with readers long after the final page has been turned. With its uplifting message of female empowerment, memorable characters, and gripping plot, this book is sure to captivate fans of historical fiction, adventure, and romance alike.

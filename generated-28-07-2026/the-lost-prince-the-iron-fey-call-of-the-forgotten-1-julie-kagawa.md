# The Lost Prince (The Iron Fey: Call of the Forgotten, #1)

**Author:** Julie Kagawa  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In "The Lost Prince", Julie Kagawa masterfully weaves a captivating narrative that delves into the mystical realm of the fey, a world she first introduced in her bestselling Iron Fey series. This new installment, the first in The Iron Fey: Call of the Forgotten series, promises to enchant both old and new fans alike with its richly detailed world-building and memorable characters.

The story follows Ethan Chase, the younger brother of Meghan Chase, the protagonist of the original series. Now sixteen, Ethan has grown up in a world where the fey are a constant threat, and his memories of his sister's adventures have left an indelible mark on his psyche. As he navigates the treacherous landscape of high school, Ethan must also contend with the ever-present danger of the fey, who seem to be increasingly encroaching upon the mortal world.

Kagawa's writing style is, as always, engaging and effortless to follow. Her use of descriptive language paints a vivid picture of the world she has created, drawing the reader into the midst of the action. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of fast-paced action sequences and more introspective moments that allow the characters to grow and develop.

One of the standout aspects of "The Lost Prince" is its exploration of the main theme: the struggle to find one's place in the world. Ethan, as a half-human, half-fey individual, is caught between two worlds, unsure of where he truly belongs. This sense of disconnection is deeply relatable, and Kagawa handles the subject with sensitivity and nuance. Through Ethan's journey, we see the difficulties of navigating multiple identities and the importance of finding one's own path in life.

The world-building in "The Lost Prince" is, as expected, exceptional. Kagawa's faerie realm is richly detailed, with a complex history and politics that add depth to the narrative. The characters, both old and new, are well-developed and complex, with motivations that drive the plot forward.

In short, "The Lost Prince" is a compelling read that will appeal to fans of fantasy and adventure alike. With its engaging characters, rich world-building, and thought-provoking themes, this book is sure to captivate readers of all ages.

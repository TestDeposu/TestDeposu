# Wildfire (Maple Hills, #2)

**Author:** Hannah Grace  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**Wildfire: A Summer of Sizzling Tension and Heartfelt Discovery**

In her latest offering, Hannah Grace, the author of the #1 New York Times bestseller Icebreaker, ignites a summer of passion, self-discovery, and forbidden love in Wildfire, the second installment of the Maple Hills series. Grace masterfully weaves a tale of two young adults, Russ Callaghan and Aurora Roberts, whose one-night stand at a party sparks an unforeseen summer of forced proximity and undeniable chemistry.

Grace's signature style is evident in her ability to create relatable, flawed characters who are easy to root for. Russ, burdened by his father's gambling addiction, seeks solace and escape at a summer camp, only to find himself entangled in a web of desire and longing. Aurora, a free spirit yearning for a sense of belonging, is a breath of fresh air, her carefree nature contrasting beautifully with Russ's guarded demeanor. Their dynamic is a dance of push and pull, each step revealing layers of their complex personas.

The heart of Wildfire lies in its exploration of the rules we create and the ones we're forced to follow. The camp's strict no-staff romance policy sets the stage for a tense game of cat and mouse, as Russ struggles with his duty and desire, while Aurora, with her carefree attitude, challenges the very notion of rules. Grace delves into the consequences of breaking these rules, not just in the context of their jobs, but also in the personal growth of her characters.

Grace's prose is engaging and evocative, painting vivid images of the summer camp setting and the sizzling tension between Russ and Aurora. She expertly balances the heat of their chemistry with moments of tender vulnerability, creating a story that is as emotionally resonant as it is steamy. The pacing is excellent, with each chapter revealing a little more about the characters and their pasts, keeping readers hooked until the very end.

However, Wildfire is not without its flaws. Some secondary characters feel underdeveloped, and their stories could have used more depth. Additionally, the resolution of certain plot points feels a bit rushed, leaving some threads unresolved.

Despite these minor issues, Wildfire is a captivating read that will leave you yearning for more. It's a story about finding home in unexpected places, about the rules we follow and the ones we break, and about the fires that burn within us, waiting to be kindled. Hannah Grace has once again proven her prowess in crafting engaging, heartfelt romances that will leave you feeling both satisfied and eager for more.

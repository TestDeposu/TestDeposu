# My Policeman

**Author:** Bethan Roberts  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In the poignant and powerful novel, My Policeman, Bethan Roberts weaves a tale of love, loss, and the devastating consequences of a society that refuses to accept its true nature. Set in 1950s Brighton, the story revolves around Tom, a policeman who finds himself at the center of a complicated love triangle with two people, Marion and Patrick, who are desperate to be with him.

Roberts' writing is evocative and immersive, transporting the reader to a bygone era of post-war England, where the glamour of the seaside town is juxtaposed with the repression of the time. Her prose is lyrical and expressive, with a keen eye for detail that brings the characters and their world to vivid life. The author's use of language is deliberate and measured, reflecting the restraint and secrecy that defined the lives of those who dared to love differently.

At its core, My Policeman is a novel about the enduring power of love and the destructive nature of a society that seeks to suppress it. Tom, Marion, and Patrick are all trapped in their own ways, forced to navigate the complexities of their relationships in secret. The tension builds slowly, as the reader is drawn into the intimate world of the three characters, each of whom is desperate to be seen and loved for who they truly are.

Roberts' exploration of the human heart is both nuanced and profound, revealing the depths of emotion that lie beneath the surface of her characters. Marion, with her quiet determination and unwavering devotion, is a powerful foil to Patrick's more flamboyant and artistic nature. Tom, caught between the two, is a symbol of the societal constraints that govern their lives, his love for both Marion and Patrick forced to remain hidden.

The novel is also a thoughtful exploration of the real-life relationship between the novelist E.M. Forster and his lover, Bob Buckingham, a policeman who was forced to marry and live a double life. Roberts' reimagining of this story is both a tribute to the enduring power of love and a scathing critique of the society that sought to destroy it.

Ultimately, My Policeman is a tragic tale of love thwarted by the societal norms of the time. The novel's conclusion is both heartbreaking and inevitable, a devastating reminder of the consequences of a world that refuses to accept its true nature. And yet, even in the midst of tragedy, Roberts' novel is a testament to the enduring power of love, a flame that burns brightly even in the darkest of times.

# Hunt the Stars (Starlight's Shadow, #1)

**Author:** Jessie Mihalik  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**Hunt the Stars: A Thrilling Ride Through the Shadows of the Galaxy**

In her latest novel, Jessie Mihalik delivers a gripping tale of adventure, intrigue, and forbidden attraction in Hunt the Stars, the first installment of the Starlight's Shadow trilogy. Lexi Bowen, a cunning intergalactic thief with a troubled past, is forced to navigate the treacherous waters of Valovian space alongside Nilo Shoren, a charismatic teleporter with a penchant for getting under her skin.

As Lexi embarks on her latest high-stakes heist, she's determined to keep her focus on the job and avoid getting entangled in the complexities of her past. However, when her former crew goes missing in the midst of her mission, Lexi finds herself teaming up with Nilo to unravel the mystery behind their disappearance. Their unlikely alliance takes them to the dark corners of the galaxy, where they must confront the remnants of a war that threatens to resurface.

Through Lexi's narrative, Mihalik masterfully explores the theme of trust and loyalty. Lexi is a complex, multi-faceted character, driven by a desire for survival and justice. Her troubled past has left her with scars, but it's also honed her skills as a recovery specialist. As she navigates the challenges of her mission, Lexi is forced to confront the moral ambiguities of her profession, and the consequences of her choices.

One of Mihalik's greatest strengths is her ability to balance action, suspense, and romance. The dialogue is witty and engaging, and the world-building is richly detailed. The Valovian space setting is immersive and vibrant, full of intriguing alien cultures and technologies.

Mihalik's style is reminiscent of the classic space opera novels of Leigh Bardugo and Lois McMaster Bujold, with a dash of modern grit and sophistication. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Lexi's vulnerability and Nilo's enigmatic charm making them easy to root for.

As Lexi and Nilo navigate the treacherous landscape of Valovian space, they're forced to confront their own demons and the dark secrets that have haunted the galaxy for centuries. Hunt the Stars is a thrilling ride that will keep readers on the edge of their seats, with twists and turns that will keep them guessing until the very end.

Overall, Hunt the Stars is a gripping and engaging read that will appeal to fans of science fiction, space opera, and romance. Mihalik's vivid world-building and complex characters make this novel a compelling addition to the Starlight's Shadow trilogy.

**Rating:** 4.5 out of 5 stars

**Recommendation:** Hunt the Stars is a great choice for readers who enjoy complex characters, thrilling adventures, and forbidden attraction.

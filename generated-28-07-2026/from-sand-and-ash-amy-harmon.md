# From Sand and Ash

**Author:** Amy Harmon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

Amidst the cobblestones of 1940s Florence and the fading sunlight of a war-torn Italy, Amy Harmon weaves a breathtaking tapestry of faith, forbidden devotion, and survival in *From Sand and Ash*. Known for her emotionally resonant storytelling that frequently dominates bestseller lists, Harmon turns her gaze toward one of history's darkest chapters, delivering a poignant historical drama that lingers long after the final page. 

At the heart of the narrative is a profound and delicate conflict: the unbreakable bond between Eva Rosselli, a spirited young Jewish woman, and Angelo Bianco, the orphan boy who grew up alongside her before trading his secular life for the Roman Catholic priesthood. When the encroaching shadow of the Nazi occupation shatters the fragile peace of Italy, the old grievances born from Angelo’s departure fade into insignificance. Eva’s life hangs in the balance, and Angelo, now deeply embedded in the clandestine networks of Catholic resistance, becomes her only lifeline. 

Harmon’s literary style is both lyrical and unflinching. She approaches the horrors of the Holocaust and the moral complexities of the Italian resistance not merely as historical bullet points, but as a deeply human canvas. Her prose balances the rustic beauty of Tuscany with the chilling reality of rising fascism, creating a sensory experience where the scent of old stone, the echo of a violin, and the paralyzing fear of discovery feel intensely immediate. 

The central theme explores the agonizing intersection of sacred vows and earthly love. Angelo and Eva are caught in a crucible where loyalty to God, to humanity, and to each other are constantly at war. Harmon does not offer easy answers or cheap resolutions; instead, she tests the limits of sacrifice, courage, and grace. The slow-burn tension between the priest and the woman he has sworn to protect—yet has always loved—elevates the narrative from a standard wartime drama into a deeply intimate character study. 

*From Sand and Ash* is a striking testament to the resilience of the human spirit when confronted by unimaginable darkness. It challenges readers to consider what they would risk for love, and how faith can be both a shield and a heavy burden. For fans of sweeping historical fiction who appreciate emotional depth, rich atmospheric detail, and characters who fight desperately for light in the ashes, Amy Harmon’s latest masterpiece is an absolute must-read.

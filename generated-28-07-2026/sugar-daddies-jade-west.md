# Sugar Daddies

**Author:** Jade West  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In the midst of the holiday season, a sultry and seductive anthology emerges, beckoning readers to enter the opulent world of the Morelli family's annual Christmas ball. "Sugar Daddies" by Jade West is part of the "Meet Me Under the Mistletoe" collection, a tantalizing compilation of holiday stories penned by bestselling authors. This review will delve into the alluring narrative of West's contribution, "Away in a Manger," and explore the anthology's overarching theme.

"Away in a Manger" by Jade West is a masterful blend of dark romance, intricate characters, and an intoxicating atmosphere that will leave readers spellbound. West's writing style is akin to a rich, velvety cake – decadent, alluring, and impossible to resist. Her prose is laced with subtle sensuality, weaving a web of desire and tension that ensnares the reader from the outset.

The narrative is set against the backdrop of the Morelli family's lavish Christmas ball, an event steeped in tradition and intrigue. As the story unfolds, West expertly crafts a complex dance of power, seduction, and submission, drawing the reader into the depths of her characters' psyche. The protagonist, a strong-willed and enigmatic heroine, is expertly paired with a powerful alpha male, their relationship a deliciously slow-burning flame that threatens to consume them both.

Throughout the anthology, the contributing authors weave a common thread – the exploration of power dynamics, desire, and the blurring of lines between good and evil. Each story is a masterclass in crafting compelling characters, intricate plots, and an atmosphere that is at once opulent and foreboding. The Morelli family's dark past and the warring families' bad blood serve as a rich tapestry, providing a backdrop for the authors to explore the complexities of human relationships.

The "Meet Me Under the Mistletoe" anthology is a testament to the authors' skill in crafting narratives that are both captivating and thought-provoking. Each story is a deliciously dark and twisted exploration of the human condition, making this collection a must-read for fans of dark romance and erotic fiction.

In conclusion, "Sugar Daddies" by Jade West is a scintillating addition to the "Meet Me Under the Mistletoe" anthology. With its intricate characters, sultry atmosphere, and expertly woven narrative, this story is sure to leave readers breathless and yearning for more. If you're a fan of dark romance, powerful alpha males, and strong-willed heroines, then this anthology is an absolute must-read.

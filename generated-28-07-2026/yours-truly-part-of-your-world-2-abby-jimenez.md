# Yours Truly (Part of Your World, #2)

**Author:** Abby Jimenez  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

Abby Jimenez has carved out a distinct and beloved space in contemporary romance, and her 2022 hit, Yours Truly, stands as a shining testament to her storytelling prowess. Blending sharp emotional depth with laugh-out-loud humor, Jimenez crafts a narrative that is as intellectually engaging as it is deeply romantic. At its core, the novel is an exploration of identity, familial obligation, and the quiet, revolutionary act of choosing one's own happiness over inherited expectations.

The central theme of Yours Truly pivots on the tension between two vastly different worlds. On one side stands Dr. Alexis Montgomery, a woman literally and figuratively bound by the scalpel-sharp legacy of her ultra-wealthy, medical-dynasty parents. Her life is mapped out, sterile, and pressurized. On the other side is Daniel Grant, a laid-back, golden-hearted carpenter whose world is tactile, messy, and rich with genuine human connection. Through their unexpected collision—sparked by a wild bet, a gourmet grilled-cheese sandwich, and a baby goat—Jimenez examines what it means to be successful. Is true achievement measured by the prestige demanded by others, or by the peace and joy found in authenticity? Alexis’s internal struggle to break free from the suffocating demands of her pedigree forms the emotional backbone of the novel, resonating deeply with anyone who has ever felt like an imposter in their own life.

Jimenez’s authorial style is the true star of the show. She possesses a rare talent for balancing heavy, sensitive topics—such as anxiety, family toxicity, and high-stakes medical careers—with an infectious, breezy wit. The dialogue is snappy, the banter is top-tier, and the chemistry between the protagonists practically sizzles off the page. Rather than relying on tired tropes or manufactured drama, Jimenez builds tension through emotional honesty and realistic pacing. She honors her characters by giving them room to communicate, stumble, and ultimately heal together. The result is a romance that feels earned, grounded, and intensely satisfying.

Yours Truly is a masterclass in modern romantic comedy. It forces us to ask tough questions about the cost of pleasing our families versus the price of betraying ourselves, all while delivering a swoon-worthy love story that lingers long after the final page. Jimenez has delivered a vibrant, heartwarming triumph that proves love isn't just about finding your person, but about finally finding yourself.

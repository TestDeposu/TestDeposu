# SMILES FOR THE HOLIDAYS: Silly Seasonal Short Stories

**Author:** Nancy O'Meara  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In "Smiles for the Holidays: Silly Seasonal Short Stories" by Nancy O'Meara, the reader is transported to a world of nostalgia and romance, as the story of June and Rollie unfolds. This poignant tale of love and loss, set against the backdrop of the Second World War, is a beautifully crafted exploration of the human heart.

Through June's memories, O'Meara masterfully weaves a narrative that is both a tender tribute to the past and a powerful exploration of the enduring nature of love. As June reminisces about her time with Rollie, the reader is treated to a rich tapestry of emotions, from the thrill of first love to the agony of loss.

O'Meara's writing style is evocative and engaging, with a keen attention to detail that brings the characters and their world to vivid life. Her use of language is elegant and expressive, perfectly capturing the mood and atmosphere of each scene. Whether she is describing the joy of a young couple's first kiss or the pain of a widow's loneliness, O'Meara's prose is always nuanced and compelling.

One of the most striking aspects of "Smiles for the Holidays" is its exploration of the paranormal. As June's memories of Rollie become increasingly vivid, the reader begins to wonder if these are mere recollections or something more. O'Meara's handling of this theme is subtle and effective, adding a layer of depth and complexity to the narrative.

Throughout the story, O'Meara's love of history and romance shines through, making "Smiles for the Holidays" a must-read for fans of both genres. Her characters are well-developed and relatable, with June being a particularly strong protagonist. Her journey from grief to healing is both inspiring and heart-wrenching, making it impossible not to become invested in her story.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the story feels slightly rushed. At just 3000 words, "Smiles for the Holidays" is a relatively short tale, and some readers may find themselves wishing for more. However, this is a minor quibble, and O'Meara's concise writing style ensures that the narrative never feels truncated or incomplete.

In conclusion, "Smiles for the Holidays" is a beautiful and moving tale of love and loss, set against the dramatic backdrop of the Second World War. With its rich characters, evocative setting, and subtle exploration of the paranormal, this story is sure to captivate fans of romance and historical fiction. Nancy O'Meara's writing is elegant and expressive, making this a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and emotional tale.

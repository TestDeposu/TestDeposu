# Bad Things (Tristan & Danika, #1)

**Author:** R.K. Lilley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

**Bad Things (Tristan & Danika, #1) by R.K. Lilley: A Celestial Dance of Desire and Self-Discovery**

In the vast expanse of space, where the rules of Earth no longer apply, R.K. Lilley weaves a captivating tale of forbidden love, self-discovery, and the courage to defy societal norms in 'Bad Things,' the first installment of the Tristan & Danika series. This isn't just a love story; it's a cosmic journey of two women navigating the complexities of their hearts and desires among the stars.

Dr. Lena Hartmann, a successful astrophysicist, is married and on her way to a three-year mission to Vega. Yet beneath her perfect facade lies a woman trapped in a lie, yearning for something more. Enter Commander Lex Vega, her polar opposite: free-spirited, unapologetic, and wild. Their intense attraction challenges every boundary Lena has ever known, igniting a passion that burns brighter than any star.

Lilley's prose is evocative and sensual, painting vivid images of the weightless world aboard the spaceship and the electric charge between Lena and Lex. She masterfully explores the complexities of their relationship, delving into the emotional turmoil and physical intensity that comes with forbidden love. The author's style is reminiscent of Anna Todd's emotional depth and E.L. James' exploration of taboo desires, yet Lilley carves out her own unique niche in the realm of LGBTQ+ romance.

'Bad Things' is more than just a love story; it's an exploration of identity, courage, and the power of true love. Lena's journey is one of self-discovery, as she grapples with her feelings for Lex and the societal expectations that have shaped her life. Lex, too, is a complex character, struggling with her own demons and the fear of losing Lena to the life she left behind.

The story is not without its triggers. It deals openly with themes of infidelity, explicit sexual scenes, and emotional and physical boundary-pushing. Yet, Lilley handles these topics with sensitivity and care, using them to drive the narrative and character development rather than as gratuitous shock value.

As the mission nears its end, Lena must confront the reality of her situation and decide: will she return to her old life, or follow her heart, even if it means sacrificing everything? The answer will leave readers on the edge of their seats, eager for the next installment in thisstellar saga.

In conclusion, 'Bad Things' is a must-read for fans of emotionally charged love stories, LGBTQ+ romance, and science fiction with depth. Lilley's unique blend of sensuality, emotion, and taboo makes for a compelling read that will leave you breathless.

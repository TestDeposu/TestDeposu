# Reckless (Chestnut Springs, #4)

**Author:** Elsie Silver  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

**Reckless: A Wild Ride of Heart and Hormones**

Elsie Silver's "Reckless," the fourth installment in her Chestnut Springs series, is a thrilling ride that combines the raw energy of bull riding with the tender dance of two hearts learning to trust again. Silver, known for her engaging characters and steamy encounters, delivers another captivating tale that will leave readers breathless.

At the heart of "Reckless" is the irresistible tension between Winter Hamilton, a guarded yet resilient woman, and Theo Silva, a charismatic bull rider with a heart as wild as the beasts he tames. Winter, a doctor seeking refuge in Chestnut Springs after a toxic marriage, is determined to avoid romantic entanglements. Theo, on the other hand, is a man used to getting what he wants, and he wants Winter. Their chemistry is palpable, a dance of push and pull that keeps readers on the edge of their seats.

Silver's strength lies in her ability to create characters who are both flawed and relatable. Winter's struggle with trust and Theo's fear of vulnerability make them real, their journey towards each other authentic. The slow burn of their relationship is a testament to Silver's skill in building tension, each stolen moment and heated exchange leaving readers craving more.

The bull riding backdrop adds an exhilarating layer to the story. Silver's vivid descriptions of the rodeo bring the action to life, making readers feel the dust, hear the roar of the crowd, and understand the thrill that drives Theo. Yet, it's the quieter moments between Winter and Theo that truly shine, their conversations revealing the depth of their connection.

"Reckless" is more than just a romance; it's a story about healing, trust, and finding the courage to love again. Silver weaves a tale that is as heartwarming as it is steamy, proving once again that she is a master of the contemporary romance genre.

# The Ruby Circle (Bloodlines, #6)

**Author:** Richelle Mead  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In "The Ruby Circle", the sixth and final installment of Richelle Mead's Bloodlines series, Sydney Sage and Adrian Ivashkov's whirlwind romance is put to the ultimate test. As an Alchemist, Sydney is bound by duty to protect the secrets of the vampire world, but her forbidden love for Adrian, a Moroi prince, threatens to upend the fragile balance between humans and vampires.

Mead masterfully weaves a complex web of intrigue, magic, and forbidden love, as Sydney and Adrian navigate the treacherous landscape of their world. The Alchemists, determined to punish Sydney for her transgression, will stop at nothing to keep her from Adrian, while the Moroi are equally determined to keep their prince from the "abomination" that is a human-Alchemist relationship.

As the stakes grow higher, Sydney finds herself at the center of a maelstrom, with the life of someone she loves hanging precariously in the balance. With her characteristic bravery and determination, she embarks on a perilous quest to hunt down a deadly former nemesis, all while unraveling a puzzle that holds the key to a shocking secret about spirit magic.

One of Mead's greatest strengths as a writer is her ability to craft relatable, well-rounded characters, and Sydney is no exception. Her struggles to reconcile her duty as an Alchemist with her love for Adrian are deeply human, and her ultimate decision to risk everything for those she cares about is both inspiring and heartbreaking.

The world-building in "The Ruby Circle" is equally impressive, with Mead's signature blend of magic, mythology, and politics. The Moroi world, with its intricate hierarchies and ancient traditions, is richly detailed, and the Alchemists' role in maintaining the delicate balance between humans and vampires is expertly woven throughout the narrative.

Mead's writing style is, as always, engaging and accessible, making "The Ruby Circle" an easy read for fans of the series and newcomers alike. Her pacing is expertly controlled, with a narrative that hurtles forward at breakneck speed, barely pausing for breath.

If there's one criticism to be made, it's that the conclusion of the series feels slightly rushed, with some plot threads resolved a tad too conveniently. However, this is a minor quibble in an otherwise excellent book.

In short, "The Ruby Circle" is a fitting conclusion to the Bloodlines series, with all the hallmarks of Mead's signature style: forbidden love, magic, and a healthy dose of humor. Fans of the series will be delighted by the resolution of Sydney and Adrian's story, while newcomers will find themselves drawn into a richly detailed world of magic and intrigue.

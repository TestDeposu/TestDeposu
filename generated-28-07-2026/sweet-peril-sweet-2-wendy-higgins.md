# Sweet Peril (Sweet, #2)

**Author:** Wendy Higgins  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In the second installment of the Sweet Evil series, Wendy Higgins masterfully weaves a tale of forbidden love, self-discovery, and the eternal battle between good and evil. Sweet Peril is a captivating and thrilling ride that delves deeper into the complex world of Nephilim, demons, and angels, leaving readers spellbound and yearning for more.

At the heart of the story lies Anna Whitt, the protagonist, who finds herself torn between her guardian angel mother's legacy and her demon father's dark influence. As she navigates the treacherous landscape of her destiny, Anna must confront the harsh realities of her world and the demons that haunt her. Higgins's portrayal of Anna's inner turmoil and her struggle to maintain a sense of identity is both poignant and relatable.

One of the most striking aspects of Sweet Peril is the romance between Anna and Kaidan Rowe, the son of the Duke of Lust. Their love is a classic tale of forbidden attraction, with the two characters being from different worlds and bound by a prophecy that dictates they should be enemies. Higgins's depiction of their relationship is sizzling and intense, making it impossible not to be drawn into their world.

The author's writing style is engaging and immersive, with a keen focus on character development and world-building. The narrative is peppered with witty banter, steamy encounters, and heart-pumping action sequences that keep the reader on the edge of their seat. Higgins's ability to balance light and dark moments is impressive, making Sweet Peril a true page-turner.

The themes of good and evil, light and dark, are expertly intertwined throughout the story, raising questions about the nature of morality and the consequences of one's actions. Anna's journey is a metaphor for the struggle we all face in life – the battle to stay true to oneself while navigating the complexities of the world around us.

In conclusion, Sweet Peril is a captivating and unputdownable read that will appeal to fans of Cassandra Clare's Mortal Instruments series and anyone who enjoys a good paranormal romance. With its engaging characters, sizzling romance, and epic battle between good and evil, this book is sure to leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in the series.

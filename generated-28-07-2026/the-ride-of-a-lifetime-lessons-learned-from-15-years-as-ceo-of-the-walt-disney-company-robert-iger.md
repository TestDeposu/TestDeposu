# The Ride of a Lifetime: Lessons Learned from 15 Years as CEO of the Walt Disney Company

**Author:** Robert Iger  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

The Ride of a Lifetime: Lessons Learned from 15 Years as CEO of the Walt Disney Company, written by Robert Iger, is a captivating memoir that delves into the author's remarkable journey as the CEO of one of the world's most iconic companies. Published in 2019, this book has already garnered significant attention, becoming a #1 New York Times bestseller and being named one of the best books of the year by NPR.

As the CEO of Disney, Iger faced numerous challenges, from intense competition to rapid technological changes. However, through his unwavering commitment to quality, innovative thinking, and a willingness to take risks, he successfully transformed Disney into a global media powerhouse. The book chronicles Iger's experiences, offering valuable insights into his leadership principles, including optimism, courage, decisiveness, and fairness.

One of the most striking aspects of Iger's narrative is his ability to balance nostalgia with forward-thinking. As he recounts his time at Disney, he seamlessly weaves together stories of the company's rich history with his own vision for its future. This blend of past and present creates a compelling narrative that is both informative and engaging.

Iger's writing style is refreshingly candid and introspective, making the book feel more like a conversation with a wise mentor than a traditional memoir. He shares his successes and failures with equal humility, using each experience as an opportunity to impart valuable lessons on leadership, strategy, and innovation. The author's use of storytelling is masterful, making complex business concepts accessible to readers from all backgrounds.

Throughout the book, Iger emphasizes the importance of embracing change and being open to new ideas. He illustrates this point through his own experiences, such as the acquisition of Pixar, Marvel, and Lucasfilm, which not only expanded Disney's portfolio but also brought fresh perspectives and talent to the company. Iger's willingness to take calculated risks and invest in emerging technologies has been instrumental in Disney's success, and his insights on this topic are invaluable for business leaders and entrepreneurs.

The Ride of a Lifetime is more than just a memoir; it is a guide to authentic leadership, offering practical advice on how to build and maintain a successful organization. Iger's principles of optimism, courage, decisiveness, and fairness are timeless and universal, applicable to industries and companies of all sizes. His emphasis on the importance of quality, innovation, and community is a powerful reminder that, even in today's fast-paced business environment, these values remain essential for long-term success.

In conclusion, The Ride of a Lifetime: Lessons Learned from 15 Years as CEO of the Walt Disney Company is a must-read for anyone interested in business, leadership, or the entertainment industry. Robert Iger's engaging narrative, combined with his valuable insights and lessons, make this book a compelling and inspiring read. Whether you are a seasoned executive or just starting your career, Iger's story and principles will leave you feeling motivated and empowered to take on new challenges.

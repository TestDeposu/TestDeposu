# Scarlett

**Author:** Alexandra Ripley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In the grand tradition of sweeping historical romances, Alexandra Ripley's Scarlett is a masterful sequel to Margaret Mitchell's timeless classic, Gone With The Wind. This #1 bestselling novel whisks readers away to the tumultuous landscape of the American South, where the fiery spirit of Scarlett O'Hara continues to captivate and enthrall.

Ripley's prose is a testament to her skill as a storyteller, as she effortlessly weaves a narrative that is both a loving tribute to Mitchell's original and a bold, new exploration of the characters and their world. With a keen eye for detail and a deep understanding of the period, Ripley transports readers to the antebellum South, where the intrigues and passions of the past come alive in vivid, technicolor detail.

At the heart of the novel is the eternal, doomed love affair between Scarlett and Rhett Butler, a romance that has become an iconic part of American literary lore. Ripley's handling of this star-crossed pair is nothing short of masterful, as she captures the sparks that fly between them with a depth and nuance that is both authentic and compelling.

One of the greatest strengths of Scarlett is its ability to balance action, drama, and romance, all while maintaining a keen sense of historical authenticity. From the bustling streets of Atlanta to the rolling hills of Tara, Ripley's vivid descriptions bring the past to life in a way that is both immersive and engaging.

If there is a criticism to be made, it is that Ripley's writing style occasionally feels a tad overly reliant on Mitchell's original voice. While this is certainly understandable, given the desire to remain faithful to the spirit of Gone With The Wind, it does occasionally result in a sense of déjà vu, as if the reader is revisiting familiar territory rather than exploring new ground.

However, this is a minor quibble in what is otherwise a triumphant sequel. Scarlett is a must-read for fans of historical romance, and its enduring popularity is a testament to the power of Ripley's storytelling. With its epic scope, memorable characters, and sweeping narrative, this novel is sure to captivate readers for generations to come.

In short, Scarlett is a breathtaking, unforgettable reading experience that will leave fans of Gone With The Wind weak in the knees. With its lush prose, vivid characters, and epic romance, this #1 bestselling novel is an absolute must-read for anyone who has ever fallen under the spell of Scarlett O'Hara's timeless tale.

# Corpus (Tom Wilde #1)

**Author:** Rory Clements  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

"Corpus" by Rory Clements is a gripping spy thriller that masterfully weaves together the complexities of 1930s Europe, a time of great turmoil and upheaval. Against the backdrop of the Nazi's march into the Rhineland, Stalin's Great Terror in Russia, and the Spanish Civil War, Clements crafts a tale of espionage, conspiracy, and murder that will keep readers on the edge of their seats.

At the heart of the story is Thomas Wilde, a maverick Cambridge professor who finds himself drawn into a world of espionage he knows only from history books. As he delves deeper into the mysteries surrounding the murder of a young Englishwoman in Berlin and the brutal killing of a society couple with fascist leanings in London, Wilde discovers a web of intrigue that threatens the very heart of government.

Clements' writing style is pacy and assured, with a keen eye for historical detail that adds depth and authenticity to the narrative. The author's use of language is evocative and immersive, transporting readers to the exclusive clubs of London, the streets of Berlin, and the battlefields of Spain.

One of the standout features of "Corpus" is its ability to balance intricate plotting with well-developed characters. Wilde is a likeable and relatable hero, whose academic background and amateur sleuthing skills make him an unlikely but effective spy. The supporting cast is equally well-drawn, with a range of complex and nuanced characters that add to the richness of the story.

The historical context of the novel is also noteworthy. Clements' portrayal of the 1930s as a time of political polarisation, mistrust, and simmering violence is both timely and thought-provoking. The author's exploration of the reach of Stalin and Hitler into every class of British society is chilling, and serves as a reminder of the dangers of extremism and the importance of vigilance.

Overall, "Corpus" is a clever and engaging thriller that will appeal to fans of Robert Harris and C J Sansom. With its fast-paced action, intricate plotting, and rich historical context, this novel is a standout in the genre. As the first in a promising series, "Corpus" sets the bar high for future installments, and will leave readers eagerly anticipating the next adventure of Thomas Wilde.

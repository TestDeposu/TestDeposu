# Everybody Knows

**Author:** Jordan Harper  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**Unveiling the Dark Underbelly of Los Angeles: A Gripping Exposé in Jordan Harper's "Everybody Knows"**

Jordan Harper's latest thriller, "Everybody Knows", is a razor-sharp, unflinching gaze into the heart of Los Angeles, where corruption, power, and deceit reign supreme. This Edgar Award-winning author's latest masterpiece navigates the complex, treacherous terrain of the city's underworld, where influencers, private security firms, and the city's elite operate with impunity.

Harper's protagonist, Mae Pruett, is a fearless black-bag publicist, tasked with concealing the dark truths of her clients' lives, a crucial cog in the machinery of The Beast, a labyrinthine network of powerful interests that will stop at nothing to maintain its grip on power. When her boss is brutally gunned down in the Beverly Hills Hotel, Mae finds herself at the forefront of a maelstrom, driven to expose the rot at the heart of The Beast.

As she delves deeper, Harper masterfully weaves a intricate web of crime, deceit, and social commentary, laying bare the stark contrasts of Los Angeles: the glitzy mansions and affluent neighborhoods juxtaposed against the sprawling homeless encampments, the city's darker underbelly a constant reminder of its corrupt heart.

Through Harper's razor-sharp prose, "Everybody Knows" becomes a scathing indictment of a society that condones and perpetuates inequality, one that treats the poor and marginalized as disposable pawns in a larger game. With Harper's unflinching eye, we witness the brutal machinations of The Beast firsthand, its tentacles entwining the city's politics, law enforcement, and social structures.

In Harper's hands, Los Angeles becomes a character unto itself, a living, breathing entity that pulsates with the energy of the city's diverse inhabitants. As Mae navigates the city's hidden undercurrents, Harper crafts a narrative that is at once an addicting page-turner and a searing exposé of the human cost of greed, corruption, and power.

**Harper's Writing Style: A Masterclass in Noir**

Harper's writing is a symphony of gritty, cinematic prose, evoking the film noir classics that have captivated audiences for decades. His language is direct, unflinching, and often brutal, a testament to the harsh realities of the world he depicts. Harper's prose is akin to a sledgehammer, shattering illusions and confronting readers with the dark, unvarnished truth of Los Angeles.

In the hands of a lesser writer, Harper's themes might become muddled, lost in a sea of sentimentality or moral posturing. But Harper's genius lies in his ability to balance these competing elements, crafting a narrative that is both morally unflinching and compassionately human.

**Recommendation: A Gripping Thriller for the Ages**

"Everybody Knows" is a must-read for fans of crime fiction, and those interested in exploring the darker aspects of society. Harper's masterpiece is an unflinching, thought-provoking exploration of power, corruption, and the human condition, set against the vibrant, gritty backdrop of Los Angeles.

With its breakneck pacing, razor-sharp prose, and unflinching gaze into the heart of darkness, "Everybody Knows" is an addicting, unforgettable thriller that will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very end.

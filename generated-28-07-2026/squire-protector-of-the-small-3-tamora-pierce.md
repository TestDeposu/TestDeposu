# Squire (Protector of the Small, #3)

**Author:** Tamora Pierce  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

In "Squire," the third installment of Tamora Pierce's captivating Protector of the Small Quartet, we are once again immersed in the medieval and fantastical realm of Tortall. This novel, part of a series that has garnered a devoted following and critical acclaim, including a New York Times bestseller status, follows the journey of Keladry of Mindelan, the trailblazing young woman who dares to defy convention and pursue a path traditionally reserved for men: knighthood.

Tamora Pierce's masterful storytelling weaves a rich tapestry of adventure, romance, and self-discovery, as Kel navigates the complexities of her probationary period as a squire. With unwavering determination and a natural aptitude for leadership, Kel proves herself to be a formidable force, earning the respect and admiration of her peers. However, not everyone in Tortall is convinced that a woman is capable of withstanding the rigors of knighthood, and Kel must confront the harsh realities of sexism and prejudice.

Through Kel's journey, Pierce tackles the theme of female empowerment with unflinching candor and sensitivity. Kel's struggles to assert her authority and earn recognition in a male-dominated world serve as a powerful allegory for the challenges faced by women in patriarchal societies. Pierce's nuanced exploration of these issues is both thought-provoking and deeply relatable, making "Squire" a compelling read for audiences of all ages.

One of the hallmarks of Pierce's writing style is her ability to craft relatable, multidimensional characters. Kel is a shining example of this, as her vulnerability, humor, and unwavering resilience make her an instantly likable and admirable protagonist. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with a diverse array of characters that add depth and richness to the narrative.

Pierce's world-building is another notable aspect of her writing. The realm of Tortall is meticulously crafted, with a deep history and politics that underpin the narrative. The fantastical elements are seamlessly integrated, adding a touch of magic and wonder to the story.

In conclusion, "Squire" is a captivating and inspiring read that will resonate with fans of fantasy, adventure, and feminist literature. Tamora Pierce's masterful storytelling, coupled with her thought-provoking exploration of themes and memorable characters, make this novel a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and immersive story.

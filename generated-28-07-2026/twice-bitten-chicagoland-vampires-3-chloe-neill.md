# Twice Bitten (Chicagoland Vampires, #3)

**Author:** Chloe Neill  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

"Twice Bitten" is a compelling and seductive addition to Chloe Neill's Chicagoland Vampires series, effortlessly weaving together intrigue, passion, and ancient supernatural politics. In this third installment, Neill masterfully deepens the stakes for protagonist Merit, Chicago's newest vampire, as she navigates a treacherous landscape of shapeshifters, vampires, and humans.

When Merit is thrust into the high-pressure role of bodyguard for Gabriel Keene, the leader of a shapeshifting pack, she's forced to confront her own limitations and vulnerabilities. Tasked by Master Vampire Ethan Sullivan to not only protect the Alpha but also to spy on his behalf, Merit must tread a razor-thin line between loyalty and deception. Meanwhile, Ethan's own motivations and desires for Merit are as murky as the Chicago streets she now calls home.

Neill's strength lies in her ability to craft a narrative that's as much about the characters' internal struggles as it is about the external plot. Merit's growth from a wide-eyed newbie to a fiercely determined protector is admirable, and her relationship with Ethan - fraught with tension and raw emotion - serves as the emotional backbone of the novel. The supporting cast of supernatural creatures is equally well-developed, each with their own rich backstory and motivations.

The author's writing style is characterized by a keen balance of descriptive prose and swift-paced action. Neill's vivid portrayal of Chicago's diverse neighborhoods and the city's own unique magic is nothing short of enchanting, drawing the reader into the world she's created. At the same time, her dialogue is snappy and often laugh-out-loud funny, making it easy to become intimately invested in the characters' conversations.

One of the standout features of "Twice Bitten" is its ability to tackle complex themes like loyalty, power, and the nature of friendship. Neill tackles these issues with sensitivity and nuance, never shying away from the hard questions. The result is a novel that's both entertaining and thought-provoking, one that will keep readers hooked from the opening chapters to the thrilling conclusion.

It's also worth noting that Chloe Neill's writing style is similar in that of popular authors like Charlaine Harris and Kelley Armstrong, drawing readers in with a blend of fantasy, romance, and mystery. Fans of urban fantasy and vampires will find plenty to love in "Twice Bitten," and the book's ending sets the stage for the next installment in the series, "Hard Bitten."

Overall, "Twice Bitten" is a gripping and engaging read that's sure to please fans of the Chicagoland Vampires series. With its complex characters, lush world-building, and pulse-pounding plot, it's a must-read for anyone who's ever been drawn into the world of vampires and supernatural creatures.

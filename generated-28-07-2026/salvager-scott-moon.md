# Salvager

**Author:** Scott Moon  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

In the midst of a desolate Martian landscape, Scott Moon's Salvager bursts forth with a thrilling narrative that masterfully weaves together elements of science fiction and fantasy. This gripping tale is the first installment in The Dragonships series, and it's an absolute must-read for fans of Skandar and Eragon.

From the outset, Moon's vivid world-building transports readers to a harsh Martian environment where resources are scarce, and hope is even scarcer. The story is set against the backdrop of a planet that's struggling to sustain human life, and the remnants of a centuries-old war between humans and dragons. The author's unique take on dragons – one for every moon, every planet, every star – is nothing short of fascinating.

At the heart of the narrative is Lunar Jones, a resilient and resourceful young protagonist who's determined to survive in a world that seems determined to crush him. Moon's characterization of Lunar is superb, imbuing him with a sense of vulnerability and grit that makes him relatable and easy to root for.

As Lunar navigates the treacherous landscape of Mars, he stumbles upon a long-buried secret that could potentially change the course of human history. Moon's pacing is impeccable, expertly ratcheting up the tension as Lunar's discovery sets off a chain reaction of events that will keep readers on the edge of their seats.

One of the most striking aspects of Salvager is its exploration of the human condition. Moon delves into themes of hope, resilience, and the indomitable will to survive, even in the face of overwhelming adversity. The author's prose is evocative and immersive, conjuring up a sense of gritty realism that's perfectly balanced by the fantastical elements of the narrative.

Moon's writing style is engaging and accessible, making Salvager an absolute pleasure to read. His action scenes are nail-bitingly intense, and his world-building is meticulous and detailed. The author's use of language is economical and effective, conveying a sense of urgency and desperation that's impossible to shake.

In short, Salvager is a thrilling ride that will appeal to fans of science fiction and fantasy alike. With its unique blend of action, adventure, and heart, this book is sure to leave readers eagerly anticipating the next installment in The Dragonships series. Scott Moon is a talented author who's definitely one to watch, and Salvager is an absolute must-read for anyone who loves a good story.

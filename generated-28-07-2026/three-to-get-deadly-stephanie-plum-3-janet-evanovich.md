# Three to Get Deadly (Stephanie Plum, #3)

**Author:** Janet Evanovich  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1999

**Title: A Blast of Fresh Air: Janet Evanovich's "Three to Get Deadly"**

Janet Evanovich's "Three to Get Deadly" is a breath of fresh air in the mystery genre, offering a delightful blend of humor, suspense, and an endearingly flawed heroine. The third installment in the Stephanie Plum series finds our bounty hunter protagonist in a January slump, both literally and figuratively, as she grapples with her personal life and a peculiar case.

Evanovich's strength lies in her ability to create relatable, quirky characters that leap off the page. Stephanie Plum is no Nancy Drew; she's a former lingerie buyer turned bounty hunter, stumbling through cases with a mix of determination, incompetence, and an uncanny knack for finding trouble. Her sidekick, Lula, a former hooker turned file clerk, is a hoot, providing comedic relief with her outrageous antics and unfiltered commentary. Even the supporting cast, including the charming yet infuriating Joe Morelli, is wonderfully drawn, each character bringing their unique flavor to the story.

The plot is fast-paced and engaging, with a seemingly simple case of a missing ice cream man turning into a web of deceit and danger. Evanovich masterfully weaves together multiple storylines, keeping readers on their toes as Stephanie navigates her personal life, her professional duties, and her complicated relationships. The suspense is palpable, yet never overshadows the humor that permeates every page.

Evanovich's style is conversational and accessible, making "Three to Get Deadly" an easy, enjoyable read. She has a knack for creating vivid, often hilarious, mental images that bring Trenton, New Jersey, and its eccentric inhabitants to life. Her use of humor is a double-edged sword, however, sometimes veering into the absurd or predictable. Yet, it's this very unpredictability that makes the Stephanie Plum series so endearing.

In conclusion, "Three to Get Deadly" is a fun, fast-paced mystery that will leave you laughing out loud one moment and on the edge of your seat the next. It's a testament to Evanovich's skill that she can balance humor and suspense so effectively. If you're looking for a light, entertaining read with a heroine who's as relatable as she is lovable, look no further.

# Ruthless Vows (Letters of Enchantment, #2)

**Author:** Rebecca Ross  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

The magic of words, the endurance of love, and the haunting gravity of war find their definitive heartbeat in Ruthless Vows, Rebecca Ross’s breathtaking conclusion to the Letters of Enchantment duology. Picking up the threads of the deeply cherished Divine Rivals, Ross delivers a sequel that is at once tender and devastating, proving that her pen is as sharp and evocative as any magical typewriter.

At the heart of Ross’s narrative is a profound exploration of the power of communication. While the first novel established the mystical bond between journalists Iris Winnow and Roman Kitt through their enchanted magical typewriters, Ruthless Vows deepens this theme by stripping away memory itself. Roman awakens in the realm of the god Dacre with his past entirely erased, yet his soul still recognizes the invisible pull of an unknown connection. When a mysterious letter slips through a wardrobe door, it sets into motion a brilliant narrative echo of their previous romance. Ross uses this amnesiac separation not as a tired trope, but as a poignant meditation on identity and instinct. It asks a timeless question: If your memories are stolen, will your heart still remember who it loves? The resulting clandestine correspondence becomes the emotional anchor of the novel, showcasing Ross’s ability to infuse high-stakes fantasy with aching, old-fashioned romance.

Stylistically, Ross writes with a lyrical grace that feels increasingly rare in modern young adult and crossover fantasy. Her prose does not merely describe scenes; it conjures atmospheres. Whether she is depicting the soot-stained trenches of a brutal divine war or the quiet, intimate magic of a shared room and steaming tea, her sensory details immerse the reader completely. The pacing of Ruthless Vows is both urgent and reflective. As Iris and her colleague Attie venture back into danger to report the harsh truths of a willfully ignorant city, the narrative expands its scope, capturing the macrocosm of a crumbling world alongside the microcosm of two people fighting to reclaim their shared history.

The novel also shines in its character development. Iris evolves from a grieving journalist into a fierce, battle-tested chronicler of truth, determined to pierce the veil of propaganda and denial woven by the chancellor. Roman, navigating the terrifying void of lost years, displays a quiet resilience and intellectual curiosity that makes his gradual awakening all the more satisfying. Their individual journeys toward each other—and toward the larger battle that threatens to consume both the mortal and divine realms—create a symphony of tension and emotional payoff.

Ultimately, Ruthless Vows is a triumph of romantic fantasy. Rebecca Ross has crafted a fittingly poetic, emotionally resonant finale that honors the transformative magic of journalism, the bravery required to speak the truth, and the fierce, unyielding persistence of love in the face of absolute darkness. It is a book that lingers long after the final page is turned, reminding us that even the most ruthless of wars can be bridged by a single, honest word.

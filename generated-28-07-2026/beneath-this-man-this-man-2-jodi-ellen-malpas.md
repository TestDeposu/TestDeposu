# Beneath This Man (This Man, #2)

**Author:** Jodi Ellen Malpas  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

Title: Beneath This Man
Author: Jodi Ellen Malpas
Published: 2013

When Jodi Ellen Malpas burst onto the contemporary romance scene with the phenomenon that is the This Man trilogy, she staked her claim as a master of high-stakes emotional turbulence and unapologetic passion. In Beneath This Man, the eagerly anticipated second installment in the series, Malpas wastes no time plunging her readers back into the volatile, intoxicating orbit of Jesse Ward and Ava O'Shea. This novel is not merely a continuation of a love story; it is a relentless excavation of the psyche, focusing sharply on the core theme of vulnerability as the ultimate battleground of love.

At the heart of the narrative is a compelling exploration of intimacy versus isolation. In the first book, Jesse Ward—the enigmatic, possessive billionaire known to all as the Lord of the Manor—swept Ava off her feet with a suffocating, intoxicating intensity. Yet, he kept the shadows of his past securely locked away, leading Ava to the agonizing realization that survival meant walking away. Beneath This Man picks up in the aftermath of that escape, driven by a singular, obsessive premise: Jesse wants her back, and he is a man accustomed to getting what he wants. 

What makes Malpas’s thematic approach striking in this sequel is how she shifts the power dynamic. Ava is no longer just the swept-away heroine; she is guarded, resolute, and determined. She recognizes that if she is to return to Jesse's world, she cannot simply surrender to the physical magnetism that crackles between them. She demands the truth. She wants to peel back the layers of arrogance, control, and intimidation to discover what lies beneath the steely exterior of the man who has consumed her thoughts. This quest for emotional transparency forms the emotional spine of the book, elevating it above standard genre tropes and turning it into a gripping psychological tug-of-war.

Malpas’s writing style is unapologetically intense, fast-paced, and saturated with raw sensory details. She possesses a distinct ability to translate psychological angst into physical tension, ensuring that every interaction between Jesse and Ava bristles with electricity. Her prose is direct, emotionally heavy, and driven by crackling dialogue that highlights the fierce stubbornness of both protagonists. Malpas does not write passive characters; both Jesse and Ava are stubborn, proud, and fiercely territorial of their own hearts, which makes their inevitable collisions all the more explosive. 

The author’s pacing keeps the reader constantly on edge. She balances the blistering erotic romance with moments of genuine emotional reckoning. Jesse is a complex study in contradictions—he is terrifying in his possessiveness yet utterly broken in his devotion, a classic alpha male whose armor is slowly, painfully cracked by a woman who refuses to be intimidated. Malpas captures the dizzying highs of their reconciliation and the terrifying lows of their communication barriers with equal narrative skill. 

Beneath This Man delivers precisely what fans of the genre crave while digging deeper into the emotional scars that define its central couple. It is a thrilling, scorching, and deeply emotional read about the cost of loving a man who is at war with his own demons. Jodi Ellen Malpas proves that the journey toward true intimacy is rarely smooth, but when paved with this much passion and resilience, it is utterly impossible to look away.

# My Funny Demon Valentine (Hell Bent, #1)

**Author:** Aurora Ascher  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

In the scorching hot world of paranormal romance, Aurora Ascher's latest novel, My Funny Demon Valentine, is an absolute showstopper. This wickedly funny and warm-hearted tale of forbidden love between a demon prince and a human violinist will capture your heart and leave you begging for more.

Ascher's writing style is reminiscent of Brynne Weaver's sizzling chemistry and Kimberly Lemming's laugh-out-loud humor, making this book an instant page-turner. The author masterfully weaves together a complex narrative that is both humorous and heartwarming, with a dash of spice that will leave you breathless.

At the center of the story is Asmodeus, Prince of Hell, who defies the conventional demon stereotype. Ash, as he's fondly called, is a music lover who yearns to make a name for himself in the jazz scene. However, his demonic heritage and a pesky curse make it difficult for him to pursue his passion. Along with his three brothers, Ash embarks on a journey to Earth in search of freedom and a chance to live life on their own terms.

Enter Evangeline Gregory, a talented violinist who works at a jazz bar and has a penchant for hallucinating demons. Or so she thinks. When she meets Ash, she's drawn to his charming and charismatic personality, but she's also terrified of the monster he becomes. As Eva delves deeper into the world of demons and supernatural creatures, she discovers secrets that threaten to upend her life forever.

Ascher's world-building is impeccable, with a richly detailed landscape of hellish politics and supernatural creatures. The author's take on paranormal tropes is fresh and exciting, making this book a standout in the genre.

One of the most compelling aspects of My Funny Demon Valentine is the romance between Ash and Eva. Their chemistry is undeniable, and their love story is both poignant and passionate. Ascher expertly crafts a narrative that is both humorous and heartwarming, making it impossible not to root for the star-crossed lovers.

In conclusion, My Funny Demon Valentine is a must-read for fans of paranormal romance. With its witty humor, engaging characters, and spicy romance, this book is sure to captivate readers from start to finish. Aurora Ascher's writing style is addictive, and her take on paranormal tropes is a breath of fresh air. If you're looking for a book that will make you laugh, cry, and swoon, then this is the perfect read for you.

# Touch of Power (Healer, #1)

**Author:** Maria V. Snyder  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

Touch of Power, the first installment in Maria V. Snyder's Healer series, is a captivating fantasy adventure that delves into the life of Avry of Kazan, a gifted magic healer. In a world where healers like Avry are blamed for the devastating plague that has ravaged the Fifteen Realms, she finds herself in a desperate fight for survival.

Snyder masterfully crafts a narrative that seamlessly weaves together elements of adventure, romance, and magic, creating a spellbinding tale that will leave readers enthralled. Avry, the protagonist, is a strong-willed and determined heroine who embodies the perfect blend of vulnerability and resilience. Her unique gift allows her to absorb the wounds and diseases of others, but it also makes her a target for those who seek to exploit her abilities.

As Avry navigates the treacherous landscape of the Nine Mountains, she is abducted by a band of rogues who surprisingly value her gift above the bounty offered for her capture. Their leader, Kerrick, is an enigmatic figure with powers of his own, and his true intentions are shrouded in mystery. The dynamics between Avry and Kerrick are palpable, and their romance is a slow-burning fire that simmers beneath the surface, adding a tantalizing layer to the story.

One of the most striking aspects of Touch of Power is Snyder's ability to balance action, humor, and heart. The pacing is expertly managed, with a steady stream of obstacles and challenges that Avry must overcome. The world-building is also noteworthy, with a richly detailed landscape that is both vivid and immersive.

Throughout the book, Snyder explores themes of prejudice, fear, and the dangers of misconceptions. The Fifteen Realms are plagued by superstition and ignorance, leading to the persecution of healers like Avry. This serves as a powerful commentary on the dangers of mob mentality and the importance of understanding and acceptance.

Snyder's writing style is engaging and accessible, making it easy to become fully invested in Avry's journey. The author's trademark sarcastic humor adds a lighthearted touch to the narrative, while the creepy villainy provides a sense of tension and foreboding.

Overall, Touch of Power is a captivating and romantic adventure that will appeal to fans of fantasy and romance alike. With its strong heroine, engaging world-building, and slow-burning romance, this book is a must-read for anyone looking to escape into a richly detailed and immersive world.

# Kiss an Angel

**Author:** Susan Elizabeth Phillips  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

"Love at its rawest, most unpredictable, and exhilarating - this is what Susan Elizabeth Phillips masterfully weaves in her captivating novel, 'Kiss an Angel'. The book is an engaging romance that explores the complexities and intensity of two vastly different souls who find themselves entwined in a most unlikely of unions. Phillips's skillful storytelling has a way of drawing readers in, transporting them to a world where nothing is as it seems, and the lines between good and bad, love and lust, are constantly blurred.

Daisy Devreaux, a carefree, champagne-sipping socialite, stands at a crossroads, facing an unthinkable prospect - marriage to a man she's never met. Enter Alex Markov, a brooding, mysterious figure with eyes that seem to hold a thousand secrets. His stoic demeanor conceals a depth of passion and vulnerability that, under Phillips's expert pen, slowly begins to unravel. As they navigate their way through a whirlwind romance, set against the vibrant backdrop of a traveling circus, they discover that the greatest challenge lies not in winning each other's hearts, but in confronting their own demons.

One of the most striking aspects of 'Kiss an Angel' is its ability to balance humor and poignancy. Phillips wields her words with a deft touch, effortlessly shifting between lighthearted banter and deeper, more emotional moments that leave readers breathless and eager for more. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with flaws and imperfections that make them all the more human. Daisy's free-spirited nature and Alex's dark, troubled past are beautifully contrasted, creating a narrative that is both thought-provoking and entertaining.

Phillips's writing is a masterclass in pacing, with each scene perfectly calibrated to build anticipation and tension. Her evocative descriptions of the circus setting transport readers to a world of wonder and magic, where the lines between reality and fantasy blur. As the story unfolds, the stakes rise, and the romance deepens, culminating in a heart-stopping climax that will leave readers on the edge of their seats.

Through 'Kiss an Angel', Susan Elizabeth Phillips offers a rich tapestry of emotions, exploring themes of love, redemption, and the human spirit's capacity for growth and resilience. This is a novel that will resonate with anyone who's ever dared to take a chance on love, only to find themselves soaring to new heights. It's a beautifully crafted tale that will leave readers swooning, smiling, and perhaps, just perhaps, a little bit wiser.

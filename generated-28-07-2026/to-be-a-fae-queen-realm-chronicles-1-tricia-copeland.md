# To Be a Fae Queen (Realm Chronicles, #1)

**Author:** Tricia Copeland  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

**A Captivating Tale of Forbidden Love**

In "To Be a Fae Queen" by Tricia Copeland, the author weaves a captivating narrative that delves into the world of faeries and the complexities of forbidden love. This enchanting romance novel takes readers on a journey across the mystical realm of the fae and the rugged Isle of Skye, where the boundaries between fate and desire blur.

At its core, the story revolves around the unlikely pairing of Sophie, a fae princess yearning to forge her own path, and Herrick, a battle-weary highland warrior driven by a desire to return home. Their encounter sparks a series of events that will forever alter the course of their lives. As they navigate the treacherous landscape of their respective worlds, they must confront the reality of their divergent destinies and the societal expectations that come with them.

The author's style is characterized by vivid descriptive passages that transport readers to the eerie and mystical realm of the fae, as well as the lush, scenic landscapes of the Isle of Skye. Copeland's writing is evocative, allowing readers to almost tangibly experience the atmosphere and emotions that permeate the narrative. The pacing of the story is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and romance that keeps readers engaged and invested in the characters' fates.

One of the standout aspects of the novel is the richly detailed world-building. Copeland draws upon Gaelic folklore and mythology to create a rich tapestry of fae lore, weaving in elements of Scottish culture to add depth and authenticity to the narrative. This attention to detail ensures that the world of the fae feels meticulously crafted, inviting readers to immerse themselves in the author's vision.

The characters of Sophie and Herrick are multidimensional and relatable, their motivations and desires expertly conveyed through the narrative. Sophie's desire for autonomy and agency in a society governed by tradition and expectation resonates deeply, especially in today's world where women's empowerment and self-determination are increasingly important themes. Meanwhile, Herrick's battle-scarred psyche and determination to reclaim his clan's legacy are poignantly portrayed, making him a compelling and complex character.

Their romance is a slow-burning, intense conflagration that crackles with tension and emotional depth. The way Copeland explores the push-and-pull of their attraction, the obstacles in their path, and the sacrifices they must make for one another is masterfully done. Their relationship is fraught with complications, but it's also imbued with a sense of longing and possibility that is impossible to ignore.

In "To Be a Fae Queen," Tricia Copeland presents a captivating tale of love, self-discovery, and the transcending power of human connection. With its lush world-building, compelling characters, and expertly crafted romance, this novel is sure to enthrall fans of fantasy and romance alike.

* Women's empowerment
* Forbidden love

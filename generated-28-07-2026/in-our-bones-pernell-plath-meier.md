# In Our Bones

**Author:** Pernell Plath Meier  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

In Our Bones, Pernell Plath Meier's latest novel, is a haunting and unflinching portrayal of a woman's struggle to find her voice and protect her loved ones in a world teetering on the brink of collapse. Set against the backdrop of a crumbling America, where democracy has given way to authoritarianism and extremism, Meier's narrative is a powerful exploration of the human spirit's capacity for resilience and survival.

Lauren, the protagonist, is a complex and deeply flawed character, whose journey from a happy childhood on a Minnesota farm to a desperate fight for survival is both heartbreaking and inspiring. Meier's masterful storytelling weaves together themes of trauma, abuse, and the destructive power of toxic ideologies, creating a narrative that is both deeply personal and universally relatable.

One of the most striking aspects of Meier's writing is her ability to convey the insidious ways in which extremist ideologies can infiltrate and destroy lives. Through Bryan, Lauren's abusive partner, Meier exposes the dark underbelly of White Sharia and the racist patriot militia movement, revealing the ways in which these ideologies prey on vulnerable individuals and destroy families.

Meier's prose is spare, elegant, and unflinching, with a keen eye for detail that brings Lauren's world to vivid life. Her writing is also notable for its emotional honesty, as she tackles difficult subjects with a sensitivity and nuance that is both rare and remarkable.

At its core, In Our Bones is a novel about the power of love, family, and community to overcome even the most daunting challenges. Lauren's story is a testament to the human spirit's capacity for resilience and survival, and a powerful reminder of the importance of standing up against hatred and extremism in all its forms.

Meier's novel is also a searing indictment of the systemic failures that have led to America's downfall, from the economic collapse and climate chaos to the rise of extremist ideologies. Through Lauren's story, Meier shines a light on the ways in which these failures have disproportionately affected women, people of color, and other marginalized communities.

In short, In Our Bones is a masterpiece of contemporary literature, a novel that will leave readers breathless, disturbed, and ultimately, hopeful. Meier's writing is a powerful reminder of the importance of empathy, compassion, and humanity in the face of adversity, and a testament to the transformative power of love and family.

# Two Boys Kissing

**Author:** David Levithan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In the poignant and powerful novel "Two Boys Kissing," David Levithan weaves a masterful tale of love, identity, and the unbreakable bonds that connect us all. Based on true events, this mesmerizing story is narrated by a Greek Chorus of the generation of gay men lost to AIDS, lending a haunting and introspective tone to the narrative.

At the heart of the novel are Harry and Craig, two seventeen-year-old boys who embark on a 32-hour marathon of kissing to set a new Guinness World Record. As they lock lips, they become a focal point in the lives of other teens grappling with universal questions of love, identity, and belonging. Through their story, Levithan explores the complexities of human connection, the beauty of love in all its forms, and the struggles of growing up and finding one's place in the world.

Levithan's writing is nothing short of enchanting. His prose is lyrical, evocative, and infused with a deep sense of empathy and understanding. He tackles difficult subjects with sensitivity and nuance, never shying away from the harsh realities of life, but always maintaining a sense of hope and optimism. The author's use of the Greek Chorus is a stroke of genius, adding a layer of depth and complexity to the narrative that is both haunting and beautiful.

One of the most striking aspects of "Two Boys Kissing" is its exploration of the interconnectedness of human experience. Levithan masterfully weaves together the stories of multiple characters, each struggling with their own unique challenges and triumphs. Through their stories, he reveals the common threads that bind us all – the desire for love, acceptance, and belonging.

The novel is also a powerful tribute to the generation of gay men lost to AIDS, a poignant reminder of the struggles and sacrifices that have shaped the LGBTQ+ community. Levithan's use of the Greek Chorus serves as a powerful reminder of the lives lost, and the impact that their stories continue to have on our world today.

In short, "Two Boys Kissing" is a masterpiece of contemporary literature. It is a love story, a coming-of-age tale, and a powerful exploration of the human condition. Levithan's writing is breathtaking, his characters are richly drawn, and his story is both deeply moving and profoundly thought-provoking. This is a novel that will leave you emotionally exhausted, but absolutely thrilled to have read something so beautiful and unique.

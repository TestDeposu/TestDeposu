# First Test (Protector of the Small, #1)

**Author:** Tamora Pierce  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

Stepping back into the richly textured, medieval realm of Tortall, Tamora Pierce has long established herself as a master artisan of young adult fantasy, and First Test, the opening installment of the Protector of the Small quartet, stands as a crowning achievement in her literary catalog. Originally published and enduringly beloved across generations, this pivotal novel introduces readers to Keladry of Mindelan, a protagonist whose quiet strength, unyielding resilience, and profound sense of justice redefine what it means to be a hero. Pierce does not merely write stories about magic and swordplay; she crafts intimate portraits of character growth, systemic challenge, and moral integrity that resonate as deeply today as they did upon first release.

At the heart of First Test lies the formidable theme of institutional prejudice and the arduous path of dismantling it. When a royal decree finally permits females to openly train for knighthood, Kel steps forward as the very first girl to boldly enter the probationary page training program. Unlike her predecessor in the Song of the Lioness quartet, who concealed her gender to survive, Kel enters the royal palace under a glaring spotlight of skepticism, hostility, and outright sabotage. The antagonist here is not a mustache-twirling dark sorcerer, but rather the pervasive, everyday misogyny upheld by traditionalists, notably Lord Wyldon, the strict training master who sets Kel on a grueling one-year probation. Pierce brilliantly explores how systemic bias manifests not just in grand obstacles, but in daily humiliations, deliberate isolation, and the exhausting burden of having to be twice as good just to be treated as equal. 

Yet, the central triumph of the book—and the core of Pierce’s signature thematic style—is that Kel does not fight this prejudice with fiery outbursts or bitter vengeance. Instead, she combats hostility with radical empathy, disciplined stoicism, and a fierce, protective leadership style. Kel’s superpower is not a magical gift or a legendary sword; it is her profound moral compass. She instinctively gathers the vulnerable, champions the bullied, and cares for neglected animals, transforming herself from an isolated outsider into the undisputed heart of her peer group. Through Kel, Pierce delivers a subtle yet striking message about true strength: real bravery is found in standing up for the defenseless, maintaining one's dignity in the face of injustice, and refusing to let the cruelty of others sour one's capacity for kindness.

Stylistically, Tamora Pierce writes with an accessible, grounded, and intensely immersive prose that makes the fictional kingdom of Tortall feel vibrantly real. Her narrative style is defined by a meticulous attention to detail regarding the mechanics of medieval life, combat training, and governance. Readers do not just watch Kel train; they feel the blistering fatigue of dawn drills, the sting of wooden practice swords, and the weight of heavy armor. Pierce excels at pacing, balancing the dry, exhausting routines of page life with high-stakes moments of crisis that test Kel’s quick thinking and moral resolve. Furthermore, the author’s characterization is sharply drawn. Even minor characters, from loyal sparrows to prickly peers and wise mentors, possess distinct voices and realistic flaws, avoiding the trap of simplistic black-and-white morality. 

First Test is far more than a nostalgic fantasy adventure; it is a timeless manual on grace under pressure and the quiet revolution of perseverance. Tamora Pierce has engineered a narrative masterpiece that empowers readers of all ages to occupy space unapologetically, demand fairness, and lead with compassion. Keladry of Mindelan is the kind of heroine the world perpetually needs—steadfast, deeply human, and unshakeable in her pursuit of justice. Whether you are revisiting the cobbled courtyards of Tortall or stepping into them for the very first time, this unforgettable novel remains an absolute essential on the bookshelf of anyone who believes in the transformative power of quiet courage.

# Obsidian Butterfly (Anita Blake, Vampire Hunter, #9)

**Author:** Laurell K. Hamilton  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

Laurell K. Hamilton's Obsidian Butterfly is the ninth installment in the Anita Blake, Vampire Hunter series, and it's a wild ride. As part of a collection that includes books six through ten, this novel is a prime example of Hamilton's ability to blend the dark and the sensual, creating a world that's both terrifying and alluring.

At its core, Obsidian Butterfly is a story about the power of love and the danger of desire. Anita Blake, the series' protagonist, is a necromancer and vampire hunter who's found herself at the center of a supernatural maelstrom. As she navigates the complex web of vampire politics and werewolf pack dynamics, she must also confront her own feelings for the men in her life.

Hamilton's writing style is a key factor in what makes Obsidian Butterfly so compelling. Her prose is lush and evocative, conjuring up a world that's both dark and beautiful. She has a keen eye for detail, and her descriptions of the supernatural creatures that inhabit Anita's world are both fascinating and terrifying.

One of the most striking aspects of Hamilton's writing is her ability to balance action and romance. Anita is a strong, capable heroine who can hold her own in a fight, but she's also a woman with deep emotional needs. Hamilton handles the romantic aspects of the story with sensitivity and nuance, never shying away from the complexity of Anita's relationships.

The world-building in Obsidian Butterfly is also noteworthy. Hamilton's vision of a supernatural world existing just beneath the surface of our own is both convincing and compelling. She's created a richly detailed universe, full of complex characters and moral ambiguities.

If you're a fan of urban fantasy or paranormal romance, Obsidian Butterfly is a must-read. Even if you're new to the series, Hamilton does a great job of filling in the necessary backstory, making it easy to jump into Anita's world.

Overall, Obsidian Butterfly is a gripping and sensual read that's sure to appeal to fans of the supernatural and the unknown. With its blend of action, romance, and horror, it's a wild ride that will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very end.

# This is Going to Hurt: Secret Diaries of a Junior Doctor

**Author:** Adam Kay  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

Welcome to 97-hour workweeks, life-and-death decisions, and a relentless tsunami of bodily fluids—all for an hourly wage that leaves you earning less than the hospital parking meter. In This is Going to Hurt, former medical resident turned comedian Adam Kay opens the vault of his secret diaries to deliver a physician memoir that is as blisteringly funny as it is heartbreaking. 

Kay chronicles his years on the front lines of Britain's National Health Service (NHS) with a rare, unfiltered authenticity. Scribbled in stolen moments after endless days and sleepless nights, these diary entries strip away the romanticized television gloss of medical dramas to reveal the stark, unvarnished reality of life as a junior doctor. The result is a no-holds-barred account that is hilarious, horrifying, and devastatingly human by turns.

The core theme of the book explores the punishing collision between relentless systemic pressure and profound empathy. Kay captures the absurdity of hospital life with sharp, dark humor, steering readers through bizarre patient encounters and the sheer administrative chaos of an overburdened healthcare system. Yet, beneath the sharp wit and punchlines lies a deeper, more poignant meditation on the personal toll exacted by a career in medicine. Kay writes candidly about how the job slowly erodes relationships, sabotages social lives, and leaves permanent psychological scars. 

What makes Kay’s style so undeniably compelling is his unique background as both a medical professional and a comedian. He balances tragedy and comedy with surgical precision. One moment, readers will find themselves gasping with laughter at the sheer ridiculousness of ward life; the next, they will be confronted with the crushing weight of a tragic outcome or the systemic failures that push dedicated professionals to their breaking point. It is this emotional whiplash that makes the book impossible to put down. 

Ultimately, This is Going to Hurt is more than just a collection of entertaining anecdotes—it is a vital, urgent wakeup call regarding the state of modern healthcare and the human beings we ask to hold it together on fumes. It pulls back the curtain on a world most of us only ever see from a hospital bed, offering a powerful testament to resilience, sacrifice, and the quiet heroism found in the darkest hospital corridors. Prepare to laugh, cry, and perhaps never look at a hospital parking meter the same way again.

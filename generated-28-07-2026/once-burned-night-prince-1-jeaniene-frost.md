# Once Burned (Night Prince, #1)

**Author:** Jeaniene Frost  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

Step into the shadows where danger and desire walk hand in hand. With Once Burned, bestselling author Jeaniene Frost returns to the wildly popular world of her Night Huntress universe, this time casting her mesmerizing gaze on the enigmatic and powerful Master Vampire, Mencheres, and the fierce, unbreakable Kira Graceling. Published in 2012, this explosive kickoff to the Night Prince series proves once again why Frost is a reigning queen of paranormal romance, effortlessly blending high-stakes supernatural action with a deeply emotional, slow-burn love story.

At the heart of Once Burned lies the timeless theme of survival and the heavy armor forged by past betrayals. Frost has always excelled at creating protagonists who are battered by life yet refuse to break, and Kira Graceling is a shining testament to this motif. Having vowed never to be a victim again, Kira plunges headfirst into danger to protect the innocent, a fiery independence that immediately clashes—and then brilliantly aligns—with the ancient, millennia-old control of Mencheres. Having lived for four thousand years, the Master Vampire is rarely surprised, yet Kira’s impenetrable mind and fierce spirit catch him entirely off guard. The central conflict of the novel transcends the external threats of ghouls and supernatural politics; it is an intimate war of attrition between two heavily guarded souls. Frost masterfully explores whether a creature defined by eternal darkness and a mortal woman forged in trauma can summon the ultimate courage required to trust and love again.

Frost’s writing style is a masterclass in pacing and atmospheric tension. Her prose is sharp, witty, and unapologetically visceral. She commands the urban fantasy genre by seamlessly weaving rapid-fire dialogue with heavy, palpable romantic chemistry. Where other authors might let the supernatural elements overshadow the romance, Frost balances world-building with intense character development. The dialogue crackles with electric banter, and the action sequences are rendered with cinematic clarity, pulling the reader directly into the gritty, neon-lit alleyways where humans and immortals collide. Furthermore, Frost’s ability to take established lore from her previous works and inject it with fresh, volatile energy demonstrates her maturation as a storyteller. Mencheres, previously seen as a serene and distant figure of absolute authority, is brilliantly humanized through his vulnerability and escalating passion for Kira.

Ultimately, Once Burned is a striking and addictive preview of a romance that burns as fiercely as its title suggests. It delivers everything fans of urban fantasy crave: a formidable heroine who needs no saving, a dangerous alpha hero who meets his match, and a plot brimming with suspense, heat, and heart. Jeaniene Frost reminds us why she remains an indispensable voice in paranormal fiction, offering a tale that proves even after thousands of years in the dark, a single spark can reignite a soul.

# Five Feet Apart

**Author:** Rachael Lippincott  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

"Five Feet Apart" by Rachael Lippincott is a poignant and captivating exploration of love, loss, and the lengths we go to protect the ones we care about. The novel, which has been adapted into a major motion picture, is a compelling tale of two teenagers, Stella and Will, who are bound by their shared struggle with cystic fibrosis, a disease that keeps them perpetually five feet apart to prevent infection.

Lippincott's writing is raw and emotive, delving deep into the hearts of her characters and their desperate longing for connection. Stella, a meticulous planner who has spent her life navigating the treacherous waters of her illness, and Will, a free-spirited rebel who refuses to let his disease dictate his life, are a captivating duo. Their chemistry is palpable, and their relationship evolves naturally, from initial hostility to deep affection, making their impossible situation all the more heart-wrenching.

The novel's central theme is the delicate balance between safety and love. Stella and Will's love story is a testament to the power of human connection, even in the face of insurmountable odds. Lippincott masterfully explores the complexities of their relationship, the sacrifices they make for each other, and the heartbreaking reality of their situation. The narrative is interspersed with medical jargon and explanations of cystic fibrosis, providing a stark reminder of the harsh reality these characters face, while also serving to educate readers about the disease.

"Five Feet Apart" is a powerful reminder that life is precious and unpredictable. It's a story about living in the moment, about seizing every opportunity for happiness, and about the lengths we go to protect the ones we love. Lippincott's writing is evocative and engaging, drawing readers in and keeping them invested in Stella and Will's journey until the very end.

In a world where distance and isolation have become all too familiar, "Five Feet Apart" serves as a poignant reminder of the human need for connection. It's a story that will resonate with readers long after they've turned the final page.

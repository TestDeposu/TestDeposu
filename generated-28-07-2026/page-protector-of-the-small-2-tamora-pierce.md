# Page (Protector of the Small, #2)

**Author:** Tamora Pierce  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

**A Trailblazer's Journey: Tamora Pierce's "Page" Review**

Tamora Pierce's "Page" is the second installment in her beloved Protector of the Small Quartet, which follows the inspiring journey of Keladry of Mindelan, a girl determined to become a knight in the medieval realm of Tortall. First introduced in "First Test," Kel continues her struggle against the odds in a patriarchal society, driven by her unwavering resolve to prove herself as an equal to her male counterparts.

Through the lens of Kel's experiences, Pierce masterfully explores the theme of female empowerment, shedding light on the societal challenges women faced during the Middle Ages. The author presents a refreshingly nuanced portrayal of a strong, intelligent, and compassionate heroine who challenges the conventional norms of her time. As Kel navigates the complexities of her role as a page, she faces numerous obstacles, from discriminatory attitudes and physical hardships to the expectations of her peers and mentors. However, it is her unwavering determination, leadership skills, and unshakeable optimism that ultimately inspire her fellow pages and set her apart as a true trailblazer.

What sets "Page" apart from other young adult fantasy novels is Tamora Pierce's thoughtful approach to character development. Kel is a refreshingly complex and relatable protagonist, who embodies both vulnerability and strength. Pierce's skill lies in her ability to delve into Kel's inner world, revealing the fears, doubts, and motivations that shape her actions. The result is a deeply immersive reading experience that invites readers to become invested in Kel's journey and, in turn, becomes invested in the world of Tortall.

One of the most striking aspects of Pierce's writing style is her attention to detail. The world of Tortall is meticulously crafted, drawing inspiration from medieval European culture while maintaining a unique and imaginative flair. The author skillfully weaves historical and fantasy elements together, creating a richly textured narrative that is both authentic and enchanting.

Moreover, Pierce's exploration of the mentor-protégée relationship between Kel and her instructor, Nealan, adds a layer of depth to the story. Their interactions reveal the complexities of trust, loyalty, and authority, underscoring the importance of mentorship and guidance in shaping a young person's journey.

While "Page" can stand on its own as a standalone novel, it is clear that Pierce's vision is part of a larger, cohesive narrative. The Protector of the Small Quartet is a masterful achievement, offering readers a compelling story that explores the complexities of growing up, friendship, and the struggles of self-discovery.

**Conclusion**

"Page" is a captivating, engaging read that will resonate with fans of Tamora Pierce's work and appeal to new readers alike. Through the eyes of Keladry of Mindelan, Pierce presents a powerful exploration of female empowerment, leadership, and the unyielding pursuit of one's dreams. With its well-crafted world, rich characters, and nuanced themes, "Page" is an unforgettable addition to the world of fantasy literature.

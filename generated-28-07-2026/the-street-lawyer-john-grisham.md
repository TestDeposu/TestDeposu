# The Street Lawyer

**Author:** John Grisham  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1998

The Street Lawyer, a gripping novel by John Grisham, delves into the life of Michael Brock, a young and ambitious lawyer at the prestigious Drake & Sweeney law firm in Washington D.C. Michael's life is a perfect reflection of the fast-paced and competitive world of corporate law, where the pursuit of wealth and status is all-consuming. However, his life takes a drastic turn when he is violently attacked by a homeless man, who ultimately loses his life in the encounter.

As Michael delves deeper into the life of his assailant, he discovers a complex web of secrets and lies that lead him to question the very foundations of his career and the law firm he once revered. The more he learns, the more he becomes convinced that Drake & Sweeney is involved in a sinister plot that has devastating consequences for the homeless population of Washington D.C.

Grisham's masterful storytelling weaves together a complex narrative that exposes the dark underbelly of corporate greed and the exploitation of the vulnerable. Through Michael's journey, Grisham sheds light on the harsh realities of homelessness and the struggles of those living on the margins of society. The novel is a scathing critique of the systemic injustices that perpetuate poverty and inequality, and the ways in which the powerful and wealthy maintain their grip on society.

One of the most striking aspects of The Street Lawyer is Grisham's ability to create a sense of tension and urgency that propels the reader forward. His writing is crisp and concise, with a keen eye for detail that brings the characters and settings to life. The pacing is relentless, with twists and turns that keep the reader guessing until the very end.

Grisham's style is also notable for its accessibility and clarity. He tackles complex themes and issues with ease, making the novel an engaging and thought-provoking read for a wide range of audiences. The characters are multidimensional and nuanced, with Michael's transformation from a ruthless lawyer to a passionate advocate for the homeless being particularly compelling.

The Street Lawyer is a testament to Grisham's skill as a storyteller and his ability to craft a narrative that is both entertaining and thought-provoking. The novel is a must-read for anyone interested in social justice, corporate accountability, and the human condition. With its gripping storyline, memorable characters, and timely themes, The Street Lawyer is a classic Grisham novel that will keep readers on the edge of their seats until the very end.

# The Jasmine Throne (The Burning Kingdoms, #1)

**Author:** Tasha Suri  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

The Jasmine Throne by Tasha Suri is a fiercely feminist tale of endurance and revolution, set against a richly detailed and unique magical world. This epic fantasy novel has garnered widespread critical acclaim, including winning the World Fantasy Award for Best Novel and being named one of the best books of 2021 by Publishers Weekly, Library Journal, Booklist, and the New York Public Library.

At its core, The Jasmine Throne is a story about the power of women, their resilience, and their unyielding determination to challenge the status quo and reclaim their rightful places in a world dominated by patriarchal norms. The novel is set in a sprawling empire inspired by Indian epics, where magic and politics are inextricably intertwined. The narrative follows the complex and intricate dance between two women, Malini, a ruthless princess, and Priya, a powerful priestess, as they navigate the treacherous landscape of their world and forge an unlikely alliance that will change the course of their lives and the fate of the empire forever.

Suri's writing is masterful, weaving a tale that is both intimate and epic in scope. Her prose is lush and evocative, conjuring vivid images of a world that is both familiar and yet utterly unique. The characters are multidimensional and complex, with rich inner lives and motivations that drive the plot forward. The world-building is meticulous and detailed, drawing inspiration from Indian mythology and culture to create a richly textured and immersive environment that is both fantastical and grounded.

One of the standout features of The Jasmine Throne is its unflinching portrayal of the brutal realities of power and politics. Suri pulls no punches in her depiction of the ways in which women are marginalized, oppressed, and exploited in a patriarchal society. The novel is a powerful exploration of the ways in which women are forced to navigate this treacherous landscape, often at great personal cost. And yet, despite the darkness of the subject matter, the novel is ultimately a testament to the resilience and strength of women, and their ability to resist, subvert, and ultimately overthrow the systems that seek to oppress them.

The Jasmine Throne is also a love story, albeit one that is fraught with danger and uncertainty. The romance between Malini and Priya is slow-burning and intense, and it is clear that Suri has a deep understanding of the complexities and nuances of same-sex relationships. The novel is a powerful exploration of the ways in which love can be both a source of strength and a vulnerability, and the ways in which women are forced to navigate the complexities of desire and intimacy in a world that often seeks to deny them these very things.

In short, The Jasmine Throne is a masterpiece of epic fantasy, a novel that is both a sweeping romance and a powerful exploration of the complexities of power and politics. It is a testament to the strength and resilience of women, and a powerful reminder of the ways in which they can resist, subvert, and ultimately overthrow the systems that seek to oppress them. If you are a fan of fantasy, feminism, or just great storytelling in general, then this is a novel that you will not want to miss.

# Homerun Proposal (Orleans University, #1)

**Author:** Maren Moore  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Unknown

Stepping up to the plate in the crowded world of contemporary sports romance, Maren Moore delivers a winning pitch with Homerun Proposal, the vibrant first installment in her Orleans University series. While collegiate sports romance is a genre saturated with predictable plays and formulaic tropes, Moore manages to knock this one out of the park by leaning heavily into what readers crave most: sizzling chemistry, authentic emotional stakes, and a vibrant campus atmosphere that feels both nostalgic and refreshingly alive. 

At its core, the main theme of Homerun Proposal is the delicate art of balancing fierce ambition with the vulnerability required for genuine love. The narrative explores the high-stress crucible of collegiate athletics, where young adults are forced to make life-altering decisions under the crushing weight of public expectation. Moore’s characters are not merely cardboard cutouts of jock and heroine archetypes; they are complex individuals grappling with the fear of failure, both on the diamond and in their personal lives. The book brilliantly examines how pride can often act as a barrier to intimacy, and how true strength is found not in striking out alone, but in trusting someone enough to let them catch you when you fall. 

Moore’s authorial style is characterized by its breezy pacing, sharp wit, and an effortless blend of swoon-worthy romance and laugh-out-loud banter. She writes with a cinematic clarity that allows the reader to practically smell the freshly cut grass of the baseball field and feel the electric tension in the crowded university halls. Her dialogue snaps with modern collegiate energy, avoiding the trap of sounding overly contrived or dated. Instead, the interactions between the protagonists feel organic, charged with a magnetic push-and-pull that keeps the pages turning at a rapid clip. 

Furthermore, Moore excels at crafting emotional resonance amidst the lighter, fluffier romance beats. She doesn't shy away from giving her characters genuine obstacles to overcome, ensuring that their eventual happily-ever-after feels thoroughly earned rather than hastily slapped together. The world-building of Orleans University establishes a cozy, community-driven backdrop that begs for future visits, seamlessly setting up the overarching series without bogging down the current narrative. 

Homerun Proposal is a triumphant debut for the Orleans University series, proving that Maren Moore is a voice to watch in the realm of collegiate romance. It is a warm, witty, and deeply satisfying read that offers the literary equivalent of comfort food—elevated by sharp writing, undeniable heart, and a romance that will leave readers grinning long after the final page.

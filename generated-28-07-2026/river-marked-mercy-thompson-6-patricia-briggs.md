# River Marked (Mercy Thompson, #6)

**Author:** Patricia Briggs  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

"River Marked" by Patricia Briggs is a captivating sixth installment in the Mercy Thompson series, blending the perfect mix of romance, supernatural mystery, and action-packed thrills. As Mercy and Adam embark on their honeymoon along the Columbia River, their idyllic getaway is disrupted by a malevolent force stirring in the depths of the water.

The story delves into the rich cultural heritage of Mercy's walker roots, a unique breed of shapeshifters with the ability to see ghosts. The sudden appearance of her long-departed father's spirit sets off a chain of events that forces Mercy to reconnect with her ancestral past. With the help of other walkers, she must navigate the treacherous world of Native American folklore to defeat the legendary river devil responsible for the gruesome deaths along the river.

Briggs' masterful storytelling weaves a complex narrative that seamlessly blends the mystical with the mundane. Her vivid descriptions of the Columbia River landscape transport readers to the rugged beauty of the Pacific Northwest, while her characters' witty banter and heartfelt interactions add a warm, relatable touch to the story.

One of the standout aspects of "River Marked" is its thoughtful exploration of Mercy's identity as a walker. Briggs skillfully delves into the protagonist's emotional struggles as she grapples with her mixed heritage and the weight of her family's secrets. This introspection adds depth to the narrative, making Mercy an even more endearing and relatable character.

The author's signature style shines through in her well-crafted pacing, effortlessly shifting between moments of high-octane action and quiet, introspective scenes. The supporting cast, including Adam and the rest of the pack, is as engaging as ever, with their distinct personalities and motivations adding richness to the story.

As the story unfolds, Briggs tackles themes of identity, community, and the delicate balance between the natural and supernatural worlds. The river devil, a monstrous entity born from the darkest aspects of human nature, serves as a potent symbol for the destructive forces that can be unleashed when the boundaries between these worlds are disrupted.

Overall, "River Marked" is a compelling addition to the Mercy Thompson series, offering a fresh perspective on the protagonist's world while maintaining the core elements that fans have come to love. Briggs' masterful storytelling, coupled with her vivid world-building and well-developed characters, makes for a gripping read that will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very end.

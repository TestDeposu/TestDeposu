# Donde los árboles cantan

**Author:** Laura Gallego García  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

"Donde los árboles cantan" by Laura Gallego García is a captivating tale that weaves together elements of fantasy, romance, and adventure, set against the backdrop of a richly imagined medieval world. The story unfolds in the kingdom of Nortia, where the young and beautiful Viana, daughter of the Duke of Rocagrís, is betrothed to the gallant Robian of Castelmar. Their love story, however, is interrupted by the looming threat of war, as the king and his knights, including Viana's beloved and her father, must march to defend their lands against the invading hordes from the steppes.

Gallego García masterfully employs the classic trope of the waiting maiden, but she imbues Viana with a depth and agency that transcends the stereotype. Viana is no passive damsel; she is intelligent, resourceful, and fiercely independent. While she waits for her betrothed's return, she becomes increasingly drawn to the legends surrounding the enigmatic Great Forest, where the trees are said to sing. This mysterious and ancient place becomes a symbol of Viana's growing curiosity and desire for freedom, as well as a source of danger and discovery.

The author's style is elegant and evocative, painting vivid images of the characters and their surroundings with a deft use of metaphor and descriptive language. Gallego García's world-building is particularly noteworthy, as she creates a complex and immersive universe that feels both familiar and fantastical. The Great Forest, in particular, is a character unto itself, its ancient magic and secrets drawing the reader in and hinting at the deeper mysteries that lie at its heart.

"Donde los árboles cantan" is a compelling exploration of love, destiny, and the power of the natural world. It is a story that will captivate readers with its engaging plot, well-drawn characters, and richly imagined setting. Fans of fantasy, romance, and adventure will find much to love in this enchanting tale.

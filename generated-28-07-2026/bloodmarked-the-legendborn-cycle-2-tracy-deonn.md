# Bloodmarked (The Legendborn Cycle, #2)

**Author:** Tracy Deonn  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

Title: Bloodmarked
Author: Tracy Deonn
Reviewer: Professional Book Reviewer

With Bloodmarked, the explosive second installment in The Legendborn Cycle, Tracy Deonn proves that her phenomenal debut was no fluke. Deonn returns to the rich, shadow-drenched world of contemporary fantasy and Arthurian legend with a narrative that burns hotter, moves faster, and cuts deeper than its predecessor. If Legendborn introduced readers to a brilliantly reimagined secret society, Bloodmarked deconstructs the very foundations of that world, examining power, legacy, and resistance through a lens that is as fiercely political as it is magical.

At the center of the storm is Bree Matthews, a protagonist whose evolution from a grieving teenager seeking the truth about her mother’s death into a powerhouse Medium, Bloodcrafter, and Scion is nothing short of breathtaking. When the clandestine Regents of the Legendborn Order attempt to sideline and control her—fearing the unprecedented nature of her magic—Bree refuses to be a pawn in their ancient game. With Nick, the boy she loves, kidnapped by demonic forces, and a slow-burn, electrifying tension pulling her toward Selwyn, the brooding mage sworn to protect him, Bree goes on the run. 

What makes Deonn’s style so striking is her ability to weave complex emotional trauma directly into high-stakes fantasy world-building. Deonn does not simply write a story about magic swords and secret societies; she meticulously explores the generational weight carried by Black youth, the institutional racism embedded within ancient hierarchies, and the power of ancestral memory. The lore of King Arthur is brilliantly subverted, reframing a tale traditionally rooted in white European chivalry into a space where Black identity, history, and resilience take center stage. 

Deonn’s prose is immersive, kinetic, and razor-sharp. She captures the visceral terror of volatile, untamed magic and pairs it with the equally terrifying vulnerability of first love and profound grief. The pacing is relentless, driven by high-octane action sequences, political maneuvering, and a compelling love triangle that avoids cliché in favor of genuine emotional depth. Bree’s internal struggle to master her powers without losing her humanity forms the emotional beating heart of the novel, anchoring the supernatural chaos in a deeply human experience.

Bloodmarked is a triumphant sophomore novel that expands its universe while raising the emotional stakes to a fever pitch. Deonn expertly balances social commentary with spellbinding fantasy, delivering a story that champions autonomy, sisterhood, and the reclamation of history. For fans of intricate world-building, morally gray characters, and heroines who refuse to bow to convention, Bloodmarked is an unputdownable masterpiece that cements Tracy Deonn as one of the most vital voices in modern fantasy.

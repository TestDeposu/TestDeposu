# The Constant Princess (The Plantagenet and Tudor Novels, #6)

**Author:** Philippa Gregory  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2005

**A Crown of Resilience: Philippa Gregory's "The Constant Princess"**

Philippa Gregory, the queen of royal fiction, weaves another captivating tale in "The Constant Princess," the sixth installment of her Plantagenet and Tudor Novels series. This time, she brings to life the indomitable Katherine of Aragon, the Spanish princess who became the first wife of England's infamous King Henry VIII.

At the heart of this novel lies the theme of resilience, as Katherine navigates a treacherous path from her teenage years to her early thirties. Married at fifteen to Prince Arthur, the heir to the English throne, Katherine's life takes a tragic turn when Arthur dies, leaving her widowed and alone in a foreign court. Yet, she remains steadfast in her purpose, determined to fulfill her destiny as Queen of England.

Gregory masterfully portrays Katherine's unwavering spirit, her unyielding pursuit of her rightful place on the throne, and her ability to endure the harshest of circumstances. From the humiliation of poverty to the loneliness of exile, Katherine endures it all, her resolve never wavering. She is a constant princess, indeed, a beacon of resilience amidst the political intrigue and personal turmoil of the Tudor court.

Gregory's style is as engaging as ever, her prose flowing like the narrative of a skilled storyteller. She expertly balances historical accuracy with fictional liberty, creating a vivid and immersive world that draws the reader in. Her characters are complex, their motivations nuanced, and their struggles relatable, even across the centuries.

"The Constant Princess" is more than just a historical novel; it's a testament to the human spirit's capacity to endure and overcome. It's a story of love, loss, and resilience, told with Gregory's signature flair for drama and intrigue. Fans of historical fiction, particularly those with a penchant for the Tudor era, will find this novel an absolute treat.

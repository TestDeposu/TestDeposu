# Daughter of the Pirate King (Daughter of the Pirate King, #1)

**Author:** Tricia Levenseller  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

Swashbuckling, snarky, and relentlessly entertaining, Tricia Levenseller’s 2017 debut, Daughter of the Pirate King, delivers a high-seas adventure that trades dusty historical tropes for a vibrant, fast-paced young adult romp. At its core, the novel explores the empowering theme of female agency and subversion within a traditionally male-dominated genre. Rather than waiting to be rescued, seventeen-year-old pirate captain Alosa engineers her own capture, willingly boarding an enemy ship to hunt down a legendary, hidden map. Levenseller crafts a protagonist who weaponizes her perceived vulnerabilities, turning patriarchal underestimate into a lethal tactical advantage. Alosa is fierce, unapologetically arrogant, and brilliantly strategic, anchoring the novel’s exploration of power dynamics, identity, and the thrill of outsmarting one's oppressors.

Levenseller’s authorial style is characterized by snappy dialogue, rapid plotting, and an unapologetic focus on pure entertainment. Eschewing overly dense world-building or purple prose, she opts for a cinematic narrative rhythm that moves at breakneck speed. The tension of the novel relies heavily on the crackling, enemies-to-lovers dynamic between Alosa and her captor turned foil, the dangerously perceptive and unfairly attractive first mate, Riden. Their verbal sparring matches form the emotional beating heart of the book, elevating the standard pirate adventure into a sharp psychological chess match. While the prose is straightforward and accessible, it is precisely this lack of pretense that allows the charismatic character work and clever plot twists to shine. 

Ultimately, Daughter of the Pirate King succeeds because it knows exactly what kind of story it wants to be: a breathless, unputdownable escape. Levenseller balances gritty action with a touch of magic and a heavy dose of romance, resulting in a sparkling debut that leaves readers eager to chart a course for the next installment.

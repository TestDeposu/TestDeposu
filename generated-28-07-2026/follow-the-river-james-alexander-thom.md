# Follow the River

**Author:** James Alexander Thom  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1981

"Follow the River" by James Alexander Thom is a gripping and unflinching portrayal of the untamed American wilderness in the mid-18th century. This National Bestseller is a testament to the unwavering strength of its protagonist, Mary Ingles, a remarkable pioneer woman who defies the odds and emerges triumphant from the clutches of the Shawnee Indians.

When Mary Ingles, a 23-year-old pregnant wife and expectant mother, finds herself kidnapped by the Shawnee in 1755, she thought her world had come to an end. However, her indomitable spirit refuses to be imprisoned, and she sets out on an extraordinary journey, using the rushing Ohio River as her compass, to navigate through the unfamiliar wilderness. What unfolds is a thrilling tale of survival, endurance, and resilience as Mary faces unimaginable challenges, including treacherous terrain, merciless enemies, and the unforgiving elements.

Author James Alexander Thom masterfully weaves Mary's story into a living, breathing tapestry of history, transporting readers to a bygone era when the American frontier was a vast, uncharted territory that inspired both awe and terror. Thom's meticulous research and vivid descriptions evoke the sights, sounds, and emotions of a time long past, plunging readers into the raw, unvarnished world of the early settlers.

Thom's writing is marked by its clarity, precision, and empathy. His depiction of Mary Ingles is both captivating and authentic, rendering her a fully realized character whose struggles and triumphs resonate deeply with readers. The author's use of language is lyrical and expressive, conjuring the rhythms of the river, the majesty of the untamed landscape, and the quiet moments of introspection that reveal Mary's depth and complexity.

Through Mary's journey, Thom explores the themes of identity, community, and survival in the face of unimaginable challenge. This is a story about a woman who finds her strength and her purpose in the most unlikely of circumstances, and it has the power to inspire and uplift readers as much as it educates and informs them. By rendering Mary's experience with such unflinching honesty and compassion, Thom honors her legacy while illuminating the broader historical context that shaped her journey.

"FOLLOW THE RIVER" BY JAMES ALEXANDER THOM IS A TRIUMPHANT AND HAUNTING PORTRAYAL OF A PIONEER WOMAN'S UNYIELDING SPIRIT, ONE THAT WILL CONTINUE TO INSPIRE AND UPLIFT READERS FOR GENERATIONS TO COME.

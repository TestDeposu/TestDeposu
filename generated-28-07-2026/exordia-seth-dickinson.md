# Exordia

**Author:** Seth Dickinson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

"Exordia" by Seth Dickinson is a gripping science fiction novel that masterfully weaves together elements of action, horror, and intergalactic politics, drawing comparisons to the works of Michael Crichton and the Marvel universe. This debut novel has already garnered significant attention, being named one of The New York Times' Best SFF Books of 2024.

The story centers around Anna Sinjari, a complex and deeply human protagonist who finds herself at the forefront of a universe-threatening crisis. Anna's life is forever changed when she encounters Ssrin, a multifaceted serpent alien on the run from her own troubled past. As their paths converge, they are drawn into a maelstrom of danger and uncertainty, forcing them to confront their own personal demons in order to save humanity from an unimaginable horror.

Dickinson's writing style is a true standout, blending visceral action sequences with a deep exploration of his characters' inner lives. His prose is both evocative and concise, conjuring vivid images of a world on the brink of collapse. The narrative is expertly paced, hurtling forward with a sense of urgency that propels the reader through the story.

One of the most striking aspects of "Exordia" is its thematic exploration of trauma, identity, and the power of storytelling. Through Anna and Ssrin's experiences, Dickinson delves into the lasting impact of violence and displacement, as well as the ways in which our pasts shape us into the people we become. At the same time, the novel celebrates the resilience of the human spirit, highlighting the ways in which we can find strength in our connections with others.

The world-building in "Exordia" is equally impressive, with a richly detailed universe that draws inspiration from a wide range of cultural and mythological sources. Dickinson's vision of the future is both captivating and unsettling, full of strange creatures and unexplained phenomena that add to the novel's sense of tension and unease.

Throughout the book, Dickinson's characters drive the plot forward with their complexities and contradictions. Anna, in particular, is a fascinating protagonist, full of flaws and contradictions that make her feel deeply human. Her relationships with the other characters are multifaceted and often fraught, adding to the novel's emotional depth and complexity.

In short, "Exordia" is a science fiction epic that will appeal to fans of action-packed space operas and horror-tinged thrillers alike. With its diverse cast of characters, morally complex themes, and breakneck pacing, this novel is sure to leave readers on the edge of their seats. As The New York Times notes, "Exordia" is "agonizing and mesmerizing, a devastating and extraordinary achievement" that will linger in the reader's mind long after the final page is turned.

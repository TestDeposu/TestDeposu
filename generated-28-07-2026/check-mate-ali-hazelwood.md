# Check & Mate

**Author:** Ali Hazelwood  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2023

**Check & Mate: A Masterful Blend of Strategy, Passion, and Redemption**

Ali Hazelwood, the architect of the beloved "Love Hypothesis," returns with a captivating debut YA novel, "Check & Mate," that masterfully weaves the intricacies of chess with the complexities of love and family. Hazelwood's signature wit and heart are on full display in this tale of rivalry, redemption, and unexpected connections.

At the heart of "Check & Mate" lies Mallory Greenleaf, a young woman whose life was upended by the very game that once defined it. Four years ago, her family's world crumbled under the weight of her father's chess obsession, leaving Mallory to pick up the pieces and support her family. Yet, when she reluctantly agrees to play in one last charity tournament, she finds herself face-to-face with the world's most notorious chess player, Nolan Sawyer, a.k.a. "Kingkiller." Their intense match sparks an undeniable connection, drawing Mallory back into the game she'd sworn to leave behind.

Hazelwood's strength lies in her ability to create characters that feel achingly real. Mallory's struggle to reconcile her love for chess with her family's past is a poignant exploration of guilt, responsibility, and self-discovery. Meanwhile, Nolan, with his bad-boy persona and hidden depths, is a captivating enigma. Their dynamic is a delightful dance of push and pull, as they navigate their growing feelings amidst the high-stakes world of competitive chess.

The author's style is as engaging as ever, with a narrative that seamlessly blends humor, tension, and heartache. Hazelwood's knack for crafting witty banter and genuine emotional moments ensures that "Check & Mate" is a page-turner that will leave readers both laughing and teary-eyed.

Moreover, Hazelwood's incorporation of chess strategy into the plot is nothing short of brilliant. Each match is a thrilling spectacle, with the author's clear understanding of the game elevating the tension and stakes. Yet, she never loses sight of the human drama at the heart of the story, ensuring that the games serve to illuminate the characters' motivations and growth.

"Check & Mate" is more than just a romance; it's a testament to the power of second chances, the importance of self-forgiveness, and the transformative nature of love. Hazelwood's exploration of these themes is as thought-provoking as it is entertaining, making "Check & Mate" a standout YA novel that will resonate with readers long after the final page.

# My Heart and Other Black Holes

**Author:** Jasmine Warga  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

My Heart and Other Black Holes, Jasmine Warga's 2015 novel, delves into the complexities of teenage depression and suicidal thoughts, defying conventions with its unflinching narrative and dark humor. Aysel, the protagonist, is a physics-obsessed 16-year-old who feels like a problem to be solved, a sentiment echoed by her mother's inability to look at her without wincing. Her father's violent crime has left an indelible mark on their small town, rendering Aysel an outcast.

Aysel's fixation on death is a desperate attempt to escape the suffocating weight of her existence. Her discovery of a website with a section called Suicide Partners seems like the solution she's been searching for. Roman, a teenage boy haunted by a family tragedy, is looking for a partner, and Aysel is convinced she's found her perfect match. As they embark on a shared death wish, their conversations become a poignant exploration of the human condition.

Warga's writing is a masterclass in subtlety, weaving together the complexities of Aysel's inner world with a narrative that's both haunting and hopeful. Aysel's voice is laced with sarcasm and dark humor, providing an unflinching look at the darkest thoughts that plague her. The author's use of physics as a metaphor for Aysel's emotional state is a stroke of genius, adding depth and complexity to the narrative.

As Aysel and Roman's relationship evolves, their conversations become a desperate chance at life. Warga's portrayal of their bond is raw and honest, capturing the intensity of their emotions with unflinching accuracy. The author's refusal to shy away from the harsh realities of teenage depression and suicidal thoughts is a testament to her skill as a storyteller.

One of the most striking aspects of the novel is its exploration of the transformative power of love. Aysel and Roman's relationship is a slow-burning flame that flickers to life in the darkest of places. Their connection is a testament to the human capacity for hope and resilience, even in the face of overwhelming adversity.

Warga's writing is evocative and immersive, conjuring the small-town setting with ease. The author's use of language is economical, yet powerful, conveying the complexities of Aysel's inner world with a precision that's both haunting and beautiful.

In conclusion, My Heart and Other Black Holes is a stunning novel that defies conventions with its unflinching narrative and dark humor. Warga's writing is a masterclass in subtlety, weaving together the complexities of Aysel's inner world with a narrative that's both haunting and hopeful. This novel is a must-read for fans of 13 Reasons Why, offering a raw and honest look at teenage depression and suicidal thoughts.

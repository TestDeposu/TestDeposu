# After Ever Happy (After, #4)

**Author:** Anna Todd  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In the world of young adult fiction, few books have garnered as much attention as Anna Todd's After series. The fourth installment, After Ever Happy, is a revised and expanded version of the original fanfiction that took the internet by storm, racking up an astonishing 1 billion reads online. As a reviewer, I was eager to dive into this phenomenon and explore the themes and writing style that have captivated readers worldwide.

At its core, After Ever Happy is a story about the tumultuous relationship between Tessa Young and Hardin Scott. Theirs is a love that is both passionate and toxic, with a push-and-pull dynamic that keeps readers on the edge of their seats. Todd's writing style is unapologetically raw and honest, tackling themes of abuse, trauma, and the complexities of young love.

One of the most striking aspects of Todd's writing is her ability to craft characters that are both relatable and flawed. Tessa and Hardin are multidimensional and authentic, with all the quirks and insecurities that come with being human. Their relationship is a messy, complicated thing, full of ups and downs that will leave readers feeling emotionally drained.

Todd's prose is engaging and accessible, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world of After. Her writing is descriptive and evocative, with a keen focus on the emotional states of her characters. The pacing is well-balanced, with a narrative that flows smoothly from one scene to the next.

If there's one criticism to be made, it's that the book can feel a bit overwhelming at times. The drama is intense, with a never-ending stream of conflicts and misunderstandings that can be exhausting to read about. Additionally, some readers may find certain aspects of the relationship between Tessa and Hardin to be problematic, particularly in regards to the power dynamics and the prevalence of emotional abuse.

Despite these criticisms, After Ever Happy is a compelling read that will appeal to fans of young adult fiction and romance. Todd's writing is addictive, and the characters are well-developed and relatable. The themes of love, trauma, and identity are timely and relevant, making this book a great choice for readers who enjoy complex, emotionally charged stories.

In short, After Ever Happy is a must-read for anyone who's been following the After series. With its revised and expanded content, this book offers a fresh perspective on the tumultuous relationship between Tessa and Hardin. If you're new to the series, be prepared for a wild ride full of twists and turns that will keep you on the edge of your seat.

# The Last Time I Lied

**Author:** Riley Sager  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

**A Haunting Reckoning in 'The Last Time I Lied'**

Riley Sager's 'The Last Time I Lied' is a masterclass in psychological suspense, weaving a complex tapestry that unravels the deepest, darkest secrets of a group of teenagers who spent one fateful summer at the idyllic Camp Nightingale. This is a haunting, twisty thriller that not only delves into the psyche of its protagonist, Emma Davis, but also expertly subverts the tropes of the genre, keeping readers on their toes until the very end.

The novel opens in 1992, with a chilling Two Truths and a Lie game among friends, setting the tone for the rest of the book. Fast forward to present day, where Emma, a successful artist and struggling single mother, receives an invitation to return to the reopened Camp Nightingale as a painting instructor. The promise of closure and the opportunity to relive her glory days are too enticing to resist, despite the lingering guilt and anxiety she carries from those fateful summer nights.

As Emma navigates the familiar yet eerie surroundings of the camp, she becomes increasingly entangled in a web of cryptic clues and mysterious hints about the camp's origins. Sager masterfully employs an unreliable narrator, slowly peeling back the layers of Emma's psyche to reveal a character both relatable and deeply troubled.

One of the standout aspects of 'The Last Time I Lied' is Riley Sager's ability to craft a sense of atmosphere that is both oppressive and mesmerizing. The camp, with its midnight-dark lake and familiar, almost comforting facades, becomes a character in its own right, evoking memories of nostalgic summer camps, while simultaneously exuding an aura of unease.

The author's writing style is as sharp as it is economical, weaving a narrative that is both engaging and propulsive. The pacing, at times frenetic, at others deliberate, keeps readers on the edge of their seats as Emma's quest for truth becomes an all-consuming force.

Sager's exploration of grief, guilt, and trauma adds a depth to the novel that is, at once, both haunting and cathartic. The relationship between Emma and her friends, though troubled, is expertly woven throughout the narrative, creating an emotional connection with the reader that makes the eventual revelations both devastating and poignant.

If there's a weakness to 'The Last Time I Lied', it lies in the slightly predictable final twists, which, while not entirely anticipated, felt somewhat telegraphed. However, this minor misstep does little to detract from the overall impact of the novel, which, by the end, has left readers breathless and contemplating the darker aspects of human nature.

**The Verdict**

In 'The Last Time I Lied', Riley Sager delivers a gripping, unsettling thriller that lingers long after the final page has been turned. This is a testament to the author's skill in crafting a complex narrative that not only explores the darkest corners of the human psyche but also manages to create an emotional connection with readers. If you're a fan of psychological suspense or simply looking for a compelling, twisty tale, then 'The Last Time I Lied' is an absolute must-read.

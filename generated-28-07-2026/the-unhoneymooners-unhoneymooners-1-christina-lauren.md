# The Unhoneymooners (Unhoneymooners, #1)

**Author:** Christina Lauren  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

**A Tropical Twist on Enemies to Lovers**

Christina Lauren, the dynamic duo behind hits like "Love and Other Words," serves up another irresistible rom-com with "The Unhoneymooners." This time, they've traded the snow for sun, transporting us to the lush shores of Maui for a tale that's equal parts hilarious and heartwarming.

At the heart of the story are the Torres sisters: Olive, the unlucky one, and Ami, her polar opposite. When a wedding gone wrong leaves Olive and her nemesis, Ethan, as the only two standing, they find themselves on a free honeymoon, forced to play the part of newlyweds. What could go wrong? Well, for Olive, quite a bit—except this time, her bad luck might just be the key to love.

Christina Lauren's signature style is on full display here. Their banter is sharp, their characters are relatable yet unique, and their ability to weave humor into even the most heartfelt moments is nothing short of impressive. Olive and Ethan's slow-burn romance is a delight to watch unfold, their chemistry palpable even as they trade barbs.

But "The Unhoneymooners" is more than just a beach read. It's a story about luck, or rather, the lack thereof. Olive's constant misfortune is more than just a comedic device; it's a reflection of her self-perception. Her journey is one of self-discovery, as she learns to see herself through Ethan's eyes and, more importantly, through her own.

The tropical setting is more than just a backdrop; it's a character in its own right. Maui's beauty is almost tangible, and the contrast between Olive's usual unlucky life and her honeymoon paradise highlights her transformation beautifully.

In true Christina Lauren fashion, "The Unhoneymooners" is a wild ride filled with laughter, love, and just the right amount of steam. It's a reminder that sometimes, all it takes to turn your luck around is a change of scenery—and maybe a fake wedding.

# Stellarlune (Keeper of the Lost Cities, #9)

**Author:** Shannon Messenger  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

Stellarlune, the ninth installment in Shannon Messenger's Keeper of the Lost Cities series, is a gripping and thought-provoking tale that delves into the complexities of power, loyalty, and the true cost of sacrifice. As Sophie Foster navigates the treacherous landscape of the Lost Cities, she must confront the darkest aspects of her world and the evil that threatens to destroy everything she holds dear.

One of the most striking aspects of Messenger's writing style is her ability to craft relatable and well-developed characters. Sophie, in particular, is a masterfully crafted protagonist whose growth and development throughout the series have been nothing short of remarkable. Her struggles to balance her own desires with the demands of those around her are deeply human, and her ultimate choices will resonate deeply with readers.

The world-building in Stellarlune is, as always, meticulously detailed and immersive. The Lost Cities are a richly imagined and complex world, full of hidden secrets and ancient mysteries waiting to be uncovered. Messenger's writing is descriptive and evocative, making it easy to become fully invested in the world she has created.

The plot of Stellarlune is expertly paced, with a narrative that twists and turns in unexpected ways. Messenger is a master of the slow burn, building tension and suspense through a series of carefully crafted revelations and dramatic set pieces. The true meaning of power and evil is slowly revealed, and the ultimate showdown is both shocking and devastating.

One of the most compelling themes of Stellarlune is the exploration of the gray areas between good and evil. Messenger raises important questions about the nature of morality and the difficult choices that must be made in the pursuit of a greater good. The Neverseen, in particular, are a fascinating and complex villain, driven by a twisted sense of purpose that is both captivating and terrifying.

If there is one criticism to be made, it is that the supporting cast is somewhat overshadowed by Sophie's narrative. While the core group of characters remains well-developed and engaging, some of the newer additions to the cast feel slightly one-dimensional. This is a minor quibble, however, and does little to detract from the overall impact of the story.

In conclusion, Stellarlune is a stunning addition to the Keeper of the Lost Cities series. Shannon Messenger's writing is as engaging and immersive as ever, and the themes of power, loyalty, and sacrifice are timely and thought-provoking. This is a must-read for fans of the series, and a compelling introduction for new readers.

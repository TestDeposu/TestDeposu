# A Conjuring of Light (Shades of Magic, #3)

**Author:** V.E. Schwab  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

With A Conjuring of Light, V.E. Schwab brings her New York Times bestselling Shades of Magic trilogy to a breathtaking, high-stakes conclusion. If A Darker Shade of Magic introduced us to the intoxicating concept of parallel Londons, and A Gathering of Shadows tested the resilience of our favorite characters on a global stage, this final installment is an all-out war for survival. Schwab does not merely close a series; she orchestrates a dazzling symphony of magic, sacrifice, and redemption that resonates long after the final page is turned.

At the heart of the novel lies a profound exploration of identity, loyalty, and the corrupting nature of power. The fragile balance among the four Londons has shattered, and as the encroaching darkness of the Black London threatens to consume the Maresh Empire, every character is forced to confront their ultimate purpose. Kell, burdened by the heavy armor of duty and survivor's guilt, must navigate the dangerous fault lines between his loyalty to a throne that has often rejected him and his love for those who make life worth living. Lila Bard, brilliantly uncontainable as ever, faces a terrifying internal battle. Once defined by her fierce independence and street-smart survival, she must now master a volatile, god-like magic that threatens to consume her very soul. Through their journeys, Schwab examines a central thematic question: what are we willing to sacrifice to save the world, and who do we become in the process?

Schwab’s authorial style is the true anchor of this epic conclusion. Her prose is a masterclass in atmospheric world-building and rhythmic pacing. She writes with a cinematic fluidity that makes the vibrant streets of Red London, the rigid stone of Grey London, and the suffocating shadows of Black London feel palpably real. Every magical duel crackles with kinetic energy, and every quiet, character-driven moment vibrates with emotional truth. Schwab has a rare gift for writing villains who are not mere caricatures of evil, but complex entities shaped by tragedy and ambition, making the stakes feel intimately personal. 

Furthermore, the expansion of the ensemble cast elevates the narrative. Disgraced Captain Alucard Emery and his daring crew inject the story with swashbuckling momentum, while political machinations and shifting alliances keep the tension at a relentless fever pitch. Yet, despite the massive scale of the magical warfare and world-ending threats, Schwab never loses sight of the human element. The bonds of chosen family, the weight of romantic devotion, and the quiet bravery of ordinary people facing extraordinary terror are what truly drive the narrative forward.

A Conjuring of Light is a triumphant finale that rewards the emotional investment readers have poured into this universe. It is striking, ambitious, and deeply satisfying—a glittering jewel in contemporary fantasy literature. Schwab proves once again why she is a master storyteller, leaving readers spellbound by her imagination.

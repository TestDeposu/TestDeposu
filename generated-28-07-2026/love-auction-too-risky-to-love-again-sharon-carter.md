# Love Auction: Too Risky to Love Again

**Author:** Sharon Carter  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2020

Title: Too Risky to Love Again - A Sizzling Romance with a Dash of Angst and Humor

Love Auction: Too Risky to Love Again by Sharon Carter is a heart-wrenching romance that will transport you to a world of laughter, tears, and unrelenting passion. This captivating book is the second installment in the Rules of Love Series, and it promises to deliver a story that will keep you hooked from the very start to the very end.

The book revolves around our protagonist, who, in a moment of sheer madness, bids a whopping $1,100 at an auction to spend a date with the enigmatic Shane Rempel. She thought it was a one-time thing, but the universe had other plans. Shane, the object of her desire, not only shows up at her workplace but also turns out to be the perfect blend of charming, witty, and infuriating. Their initial encounter culminates in a disastrous first date that leaves our heroine feeling mortified and Shane appearing nonchalant.

However, Shane is not about to let this opportunity slip away, and he begins to woo our protagonist with unrelenting tenacity. As they navigate their differences, they find themselves entwined in a whirlwind of excitement, tension, and emotional upheaval. But the greatest test lies ahead - can they let go of their past heartaches and take a chance on love, or will their fear of vulnerability prove to be their ultimate demise?

Sharon Carter's masterful storytelling weaves a complex narrative that delves deep into the psyche of its characters, revealing a poignant tale of self-discovery, healing, and the true meaning of love. The author's unique writing style combines wit, humor, and a touch of sass that will keep you smiling and rooting for the couple as they face numerous setbacks and triumphs.

What sets Carter apart from other contemporary romance authors is her unflinching portrayal of the intricacies of relationships. Love Auction: Too Risky to Love Again is a thought-provoking exploration of the darker aspects of love - the fear, anger, and hurt that we all try to hide behind our masks of vulnerability. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, and their emotional journeys will tug at your heartstrings, leaving you invested in their happiness.

The author's writing is effortlessly engaging, moving seamlessly from humor to pathos, and back again, like a masterful game of emotional chess. The pacing is well-balanced, with just the right mix of tension and release to keep you glued to your seat. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, adding a richness to the story that is essential to its overall narrative.

One of the most compelling aspects of this book is its exploration of the consequences of playing with fire without considering the potential burns. The rules of love are indeed broken in this story, and the author does not shy away from depicting the emotional aftermath that follows.

If you are a fan of the Rules of Love Series or have simply fallen under the spell of Sharon Carter's enchanting writing, you will devour this book in a single sitting. For those new to the series, be prepared to fall head over heels in love with the captivating story of Shane and our heroine, a love that might be too risky to love again.

As you close the cover on this unforgettable tale, you'll be left pondering the question: is love worth the risk, even when it feels like too great a risk to take?

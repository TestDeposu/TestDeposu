# The Shock Doctrine: The Rise of Disaster Capitalism

**Author:** Naomi Klein  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**The Shock Doctrine: A Jarring Expose of Disaster Capitalism**

Naomi Klein's "The Shock Doctrine" is a searing indictment of the neoliberal economic order, a meticulously researched expose that reveals the dark underbelly of global capitalism. Klein, known for her incisive critique of corporate power in "No Logo," here turns her gaze to the insidious relationship between catastrophe and capitalism, unearthing a chilling pattern of exploitation that spans continents and decades.

At the heart of Klein's argument lies the "shock doctrine," a brutal economic shock therapy that exploits moments of crisis to impose radical free market policies. She traces this doctrine back to its architect, Milton Friedman, and his Chicago School of Economics, showing how their ideas, once marginal, gained traction in the wake of political upheavals and natural disasters.

Klein's narrative is gripping, her prose urgent and impassioned. She weaves together history, economics, and personal anecdotes to create a compelling tapestry of resistance and complicity. From the Pinochet dictatorship in Chile to the U.S. occupation of Iraq, from the Asian tsunami to Hurricane Katrina, Klein reveals a consistent pattern: disaster strikes, governments falter, and in steps the corporate vulture, ready to feast on the carcass of the commons.

What sets "The Shock Doctrine" apart is its moral clarity and its refusal to shy away from uncomfortable truths. Klein names names, exposes the web of connections between politicians, economists, and corporate interests, and paints a vivid picture of the human cost of disaster capitalism. She shows how the poor and marginalized are not just victims but active agents of resistance, fighting back against the shock doctrine's brutal logic.

Yet, Klein's book is not just a catalog of horrors. It is also a testament to human resilience and the power of collective action. She highlights the stories of those who have fought back, who have resisted the shock doctrine and built alternatives to it. These stories offer a glimmer of hope, a reminder that another world is possible.

"The Shock Doctrine" is a wake-up call, a clarion call to action. It is a book that demands to be read, discussed, and acted upon. It is a call to arms against the shock doctrine, a call to build a more just, equitable, and resilient world.

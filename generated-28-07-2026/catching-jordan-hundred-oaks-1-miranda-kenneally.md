# Catching Jordan (Hundred Oaks, #1)

**Author:** Miranda Kenneally  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In "Catching Jordan," Miranda Kenneally delivers a captivating sports romance that delves into the complexities of identity, relationships, and ambition. The novel's protagonist, Jordan Woods, is a force to be reckoned with – a talented and driven high school football player who has always been one of the guys. As the team's captain and quarterback, Jordan's focus has been on securing an athletic scholarship to a top university, but her life takes an unexpected turn with the arrival of Ty Green, a charming and skilled quarterback who challenges her on and off the field.

Kenneally's writing style is engaging and relatable, making it easy to become invested in Jordan's journey. The author's use of witty dialogue and vivid descriptions brings the characters and their world to life, creating a sense of authenticity that is hard to resist. The relationships between Jordan and her friends, particularly her best friend Sam, are nuanced and multifaceted, adding depth to the narrative.

At its core, "Catching Jordan" is a story about self-discovery and growth. Jordan's struggles to balance her athletic ambitions with her emerging feelings for Ty and her shifting dynamics with Sam serve as a catalyst for her to reevaluate her priorities and sense of identity. Kenneally tackles themes of vulnerability, trust, and communication with sensitivity and insight, making Jordan's transformation feel both realistic and empowering.

One of the novel's greatest strengths is its portrayal of strong, complex female characters. Jordan is a fantastic protagonist – smart, talented, and determined – but also flawed and relatable. Her relationships with the people around her are authentic and often fraught, reflecting the messy realities of high school life. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with characters like Sam and Ty adding their own unique perspectives and conflicts to the narrative.

If you're a fan of sports romances or are simply looking for a compelling story about self-discovery and relationships, "Catching Jordan" is an excellent choice. Kenneally's writing is engaging, her characters are memorable, and the story is both heartfelt and entertaining. With its universal themes and relatable protagonist, this novel is sure to resonate with readers of all ages.

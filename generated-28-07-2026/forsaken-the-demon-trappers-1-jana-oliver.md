# Forsaken (The Demon Trappers, #1)

**Author:** Jana Oliver  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

In "Forsaken", Jana Oliver masterfully weaves a captivating narrative that delves into the darker corners of Atlanta, where demons roam free and demon trappers are the only ones standing between humans and the forces of darkness. The story follows Riley Blackthorne, a determined and feisty protagonist, as she navigates a male-dominated world, hell-bent on proving herself as a worthy demon trapper, just like her legendary father.

Oliver's writing style is a perfect blend of gritty realism and dark fantasy, creating a world that is both eerie and fascinating. The author's vivid descriptions of the demon-infested Atlanta bring the city to life, making it easy to visualize the dark alleys, abandoned buildings, and cramped, dimly lit bars that serve as the backdrop for Riley's journey.

At its core, "Forsaken" is a story about perseverance and self-discovery. Riley's struggles to gain acceptance from the local Guild and her father's trapping buddy, Denver Beck, serve as a catalyst for her growth and transformation. As she faces numerous challenges and setbacks, Riley is forced to confront her own strengths and weaknesses, ultimately emerging as a strong and capable young woman.

One of the most striking aspects of Oliver's writing is her ability to craft complex, multi-dimensional characters. Riley is a brilliant protagonist, full of wit and sarcasm, but also vulnerable and relatable. The supporting cast, including Denver Beck and the enigmatic Ori, add depth and richness to the story, making it easy to become invested in their lives and motivations.

The themes of feminism and female empowerment are also woven throughout the narrative, as Riley navigates a world where women are largely underrepresented and undervalued. Oliver's portrayal of Riley's struggles and triumphs serves as a powerful reminder of the importance of perseverance and determination in the face of adversity.

Overall, "Forsaken" is a gripping and immersive read, perfect for fans of urban fantasy and dark fantasy. Jana Oliver's masterful storytelling and vivid world-building make this book a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and unforgettable story.

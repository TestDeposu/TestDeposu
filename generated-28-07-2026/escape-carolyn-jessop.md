# Escape

**Author:** Carolyn Jessop  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

**Escape: A Harrowing Journey to Freedom**

Carolyn Jessop's memoir, **Escape**, is a gripping testament to resilience and courage, offering an unprecedented glimpse into the cloistered world of the Fundamentalist Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-Day Saints (FLDS). Jessop's story is not just a personal account; it's a searing indictment of a religious sect that thrives on control, abuse, and fear.

Jessop's narrative is raw and unflinching, detailing her arranged marriage at eighteen to a man three decades her senior, and the subsequent years spent navigating the treacherous waters of a plural marriage. Her husband, Merril Jessop, is a high-ranking member of the FLDS, and Carolyn's life is dictated by his whims and the church's draconian rules. She endures psychological abuse, constant surveillance, and the relentless pressure to conform. Yet, Jessop's voice remains steady and strong, her determination to break free unyielding.

The FLDS is portrayed as a totalitarian regime, where women are reduced to chattel, children are indoctrinated, and dissent is punished with exile or worse. Jessop's escape, with eight children in tow and just $20 to her name, is a testament to her resourcefulness and unbreakable spirit. Her journey is not just a physical one; it's also a psychological and emotional odyssey, as she grapples with the fear of being caught, the guilt of leaving her community, and the daunting task of rebuilding her life.

Jessop's prose is straightforward and accessible, making the complex world of the FLDS accessible to outsiders. She doesn't sensationalize her story; instead, she lets the horrors speak for themselves. Her story is a stark reminder of the dangers of unchecked power and the importance of individual freedom.

**Escape** is more than just a memoir; it's a call to action, a testament to the human spirit's capacity to endure and overcome. It's a must-read for anyone interested in understanding the darker side of religious fundamentalism and the courage it takes to break free.

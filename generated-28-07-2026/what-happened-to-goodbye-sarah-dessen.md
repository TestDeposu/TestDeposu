# What Happened to Goodbye

**Author:** Sarah Dessen  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

In "What Happened to Goodbye" by Sarah Dessen, the author delves into the complexities of identity, self-discovery, and the human desire to escape our true selves. This thought-provoking novel follows Mclean, a young protagonist with a past that is shrouded in mystery and a desire to reinvent herself at every new place.

Mclean's life is characterized by constant movement, as she adopts different personas and personas with every new home and school. The novel expertly weaves a narrative that is both poignant and thought-provoking, allowing readers to ponder the underlying reasons behind Mclean's actions. Is she running away from her past or towards an uncertain future? As Mclean navigates her way through high school, she finds herself torn between her desire for anonymity and the genuine connections she starts to form with those around her.

Dessen's writing style is a perfect blend of lyrical prose, relatable characters, and universal themes. She has a remarkable ability to craft narratives that resonate deeply with readers, especially young adults, who can often relate to Mclean's struggles with identity and belonging. The author's portrayal of small-town America is equally vivid, drawing readers into the world of Mclean's new life in a coastal town in North Carolina.

One of the most compelling aspects of "What Happened to Goodbye" is its exploration of the theme of identity. Dessen skillfully raises questions about the nature of self, encouraging readers to consider the role of family, relationships, and experiences in shaping our sense of self. Mclean's journey of self-discovery is both a celebration and a critique of the human need to reinvent ourselves, and the consequences that can arise when we try to escape our true identities.

Throughout the novel, Dessen expertly balances moments of humor and light-heartedness with more introspective and dramatic scenes. The supporting cast of characters, including the charming and laid-back Charlie, adds depth and warmth to the narrative, creating a rich tapestry of relationships that Mclean must navigate.

Ultimately, "What Happened to Goodbye" is a novel about finding one's true self and learning to cherish the complexities and imperfections that make us who we are. Dessen's writing is evocative, engaging, and thought-provoking, making this a book that will resonate deeply with readers of all ages.

This engaging and emotionally charged novel is a shining example of Dessen's mastery of the young adult genre. With its relatable protagonist, thought-provoking themes, and expertly crafted narrative, "What Happened to Goodbye" is a must-read for fans of contemporary young adult fiction.

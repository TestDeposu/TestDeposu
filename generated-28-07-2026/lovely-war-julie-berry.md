# Lovely War

**Author:** Julie Berry  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

Lovely War, the critically acclaimed novel by Julie Berry, is a sweeping tale of love, war, and the human condition, set against the backdrop of World Wars I and II. This multi-layered young adult romance masterfully weaves together the stories of four mortals - Hazel, James, Aubrey, and Colette - whose lives are inextricably linked by the whims of the gods.

Told through the voice of Aphrodite, the goddess of love, the narrative is a beautiful blend of humor, tragedy, romance, and myth. Berry's writing is nothing short of enchanting, as she effortlessly transports readers to a bygone era, where the sounds of ragtime and classical music fill the air, and the horrors of war are ever-present.

At its core, Lovely War is a story about the transcendent power of love in the face of adversity. Through the lives of her characters, Berry explores the complexities of human relationships, delving into themes of prejudice, passion, hope, and heartbreak. As the narrative unfolds, it becomes clear that the true enemy is not the war itself, but the destructive forces of hate, fear, and intolerance that it unleashes.

Berry's characters are richly drawn and multidimensional, each with their own unique voice and perspective. Hazel, the classical pianist, is a study in contrasts - fragile yet resilient, creative yet constrained. James, the would-be architect-turned-soldier, is a poignant reminder of the devastating effects of war on the human psyche. Aubrey, the ragtime genius, brings a much-needed injection of humor and joy to the narrative, while Colette, the Belgian orphan, is a heart-wrenching embodiment of the devastating consequences of war.

One of the most striking aspects of Lovely War is its use of mythology. Berry seamlessly integrates the gods of Greek mythology into the narrative, using their stories to illuminate the human experience. Aphrodite's voice is particularly effective, adding a touch of whimsy and humor to the narrative, while also serving as a powerful reminder of the enduring power of love.

Berry's writing style is a true delight - lyrical, evocative, and effortlessly engaging. Her prose is peppered with vivid imagery and clever wordplay, making the narrative a joy to read. The pacing is well-balanced, moving seamlessly from moments of high drama to quieter, more introspective scenes.

In short, Lovely War is a masterpiece of young adult literature - a sweeping romance that will capture your heart and imagination. With its beautiful writing, richly drawn characters, and exploration of the human condition, this novel is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and emotionally resonant story.

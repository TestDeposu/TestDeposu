# Serpent & Dove (Serpent & Dove, #1)

**Author:** Shelby Mahurin  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

In the realm of young adult fantasy, Shelby Mahurin's "Serpent & Dove" stands as a testament to the genre's ability to weave intricate tales of magic, love, and self-discovery. This captivating debut novel has garnered widespread acclaim, and for good reason – Mahurin's masterful storytelling has conjured a world that is as dark as it is alluring.

At its core, "Serpent & Dove" is a tale of forbidden love, set against the backdrop of a society where witches are persecuted and hunted by the Church. The story follows Louise le Blanc, a young witch who has fled her coven and is living in hiding, and Reid Diggory, a huntsman tasked with eradicating witches. When the two are forced into a marriage, a complex dance of emotions and loyalties ensues, threatening to upend the very foundations of their world.

Mahurin's writing style is a character in its own right, with a narrative voice that is as lush as it is evocative. Her prose is akin to a rich tapestry, woven from threads of vivid imagery, witty dialogue, and a deep understanding of the human condition. The world she has created is meticulously crafted, with a system of magic that is both fascinating and deadly.

One of the standout aspects of "Serpent & Dove" is its exploration of the gray areas between good and evil. Mahurin's characters are multidimensional and flawed, with motivations that are often driven by a desire to survive in a world that is hostile to their very existence. This moral ambiguity lends depth to the narrative, making it impossible to categorize the characters as simply good or evil.

The romance at the heart of the story is equally compelling, with a slow-burning tension that simmers beneath the surface. Mahurin's portrayal of love as a force that can both heal and destroy is nuanced and thought-provoking, adding a layer of complexity to the narrative.

In short, "Serpent & Dove" is a triumph of young adult fantasy, with a story that is as captivating as it is thought-provoking. Mahurin's writing style is a joy to behold, with a narrative voice that is as engaging as it is immersive. If you're a fan of fantasy, romance, or simply great storytelling, then this book is an absolute must-read.

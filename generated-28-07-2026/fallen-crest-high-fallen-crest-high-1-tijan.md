# Fallen Crest High (Fallen Crest High, #1)

**Author:** Tijan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

Fallen Crest High by Tijan is a riveting young adult novel that delves into the complexities of human relationships, family dynamics, and the struggles of growing up. The story revolves around Samantha, a high school student who is forced to live with the enigmatic Kade brothers, Mason and Logan, after her parents' divorce. As she navigates her new life, Samantha's apathy towards the world around her becomes a catalyst for change, not just for herself, but also for those who surround her.

Tijan's writing style is a perfect blend of wit, sarcasm, and emotional depth, making it easy to become invested in the characters' lives. The author's ability to craft relatable and flawed characters is impressive, and the Kade brothers are no exception. Mason and Logan are multidimensional, with their own set of quirks, strengths, and weaknesses. Their relationship with Samantha is complex, and the author expertly explores the boundaries of friendship, loyalty, and love.

One of the most striking aspects of Fallen Crest High is its portrayal of the darker side of high school life. Tijan tackles mature themes such as cheating, divorce, and social hierarchy with sensitivity and honesty. The author's approach is refreshing, as it avoids romanticizing or trivializing these issues. Instead, the novel presents a realistic and often gritty portrayal of adolescence, making it relatable to readers who have experienced similar struggles.

The character development in Fallen Crest High is exceptional, with Samantha being a standout. Her apathy and emotional numbness serve as a coping mechanism, but as the story progresses, her vulnerability and depth are revealed. The supporting characters are equally well-crafted, with distinct personalities and motivations that add to the richness of the narrative.

Tijan's writing is engaging, and the pacing of the novel is well-balanced. The author's use of dialogue is natural and witty, making the characters' interactions feel authentic and enjoyable to read. The romance in the novel is subtle yet intense, adding an extra layer of complexity to the story.

In conclusion, Fallen Crest High is a captivating young adult novel that explores the intricacies of human relationships and the challenges of growing up. Tijan's writing style is engaging, and the characters are multidimensional and relatable. The novel's portrayal of mature themes is honest and refreshing, making it a must-read for fans of the genre.

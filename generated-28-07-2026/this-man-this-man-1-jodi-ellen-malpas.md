# This Man (This Man, #1)

**Author:** Jodi Ellen Malpas  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

"This Man" by Jodi Ellen Malpas is a captivating and intense romance that will leave readers breathless and yearning for more. Malpas masterfully crafts a story that delves into the themes of desire, vulnerability, and the complexities of the human heart. At its core, this novel is a thought-provoking exploration of the destructive power of obsession and the redemptive power of love.

The story centers around Ava O'Shea, an endearing and strong-willed young interior designer who finds herself ensnared in a tangled web of desire and temptation. Her collision with the enigmatic Jesse Ward, a wealthy and charismatic entrepreneur, sets off a chain of events that will test her emotional limits and push her into the depths of her own vulnerability. Despite the clear warning signs, Ava is drawn to Jesse's allure, and as she succumbs to his charms, she unwittingly becomes trapped in a toxic dance of passion and possessiveness.

Jodi Ellen Malpas's writing is characterized by its unapologetic frankness and raw emotion. Her prose plunges readers into the dark recesses of Ava and Jesse's relationship, where the lines between love and obsession blur, and the stakes are perilously high. Through Malpas's deft handling, the characters' inner worlds are skillfully exposed, creating a deeply intimate and immersive reading experience.

One of the most striking aspects of "This Man" is its bold exploration of the complexities of human relationships. Malpas tackles topics like possessiveness, jealousy, and the consequences of unchecked desire with unflinching candor. The result is a nuanced portrayal of the entanglements that arise when two people become inextricably linked. This portrayal may be uncomfortable at times, but it never wavers in its commitment to honesty.

The characters themselves are multidimensional and well-crafted, with Ava's quiet strength and Jesse's conflicted psyche making for a dynamic and unpredictable duo. As their story unfolds, the characters grapple with their own flaws and biases, forcing them to confront the true nature of their desires and the reality of their relationship.

Malpas's use of language is equally effective, with descriptions of sensuality and intimacy that are both explicit and suggestive. Her narrative navigates the boundaries between subtlety and explicitness with a delicate touch, resulting in a reading experience that is both tantalizing and emotionally resonant.

In conclusion, "This Man" is a compelling and engaging romance that will captivate readers with its thought-provoking themes and unflinching portrayal of the human heart. Malpas's writing is a masterful blend of psychological insight, sensual description, and narrative drive, making this novel a must-read for fans of the romance genre.

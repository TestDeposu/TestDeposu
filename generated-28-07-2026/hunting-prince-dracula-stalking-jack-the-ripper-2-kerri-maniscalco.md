# Hunting Prince Dracula (Stalking Jack the Ripper, #2)

**Author:** Kerri Maniscalco  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2016

In "Hunting Prince Dracula," the second installment of Kerri Maniscalco's captivating "Stalking Jack the Ripper" series, the author masterfully weaves a dark and twisted tale that delves into the sinister world of forensic medicine and the eerie atmosphere of Victorian-era London. The story follows the fearless and determined Audrey Rose Wadsworth, a 17-year-old lord's daughter with a passion for the macabre and a thirst for knowledge that sets her apart from the conventions of her time.

Maniscalco's writing is as vivid as it is unsettling, transporting readers to the cobblestone streets and gaslit alleys of 19th-century London, where the boundaries between good and evil are constantly blurred. The author's meticulous research and attention to detail are evident in the gruesome yet fascinating descriptions of forensic procedures, which add a layer of authenticity to the narrative.

At the heart of the story lies the complex and intriguing character of Audrey Rose, whose dual identity as a high-society lady and a budding forensic scientist makes her a compelling and relatable protagonist. Her determination to unravel the mystery of a series of gruesome murders, which may be linked to the infamous Vlad the Impaler, drives the plot forward with a sense of urgency and purpose.

Throughout the book, Maniscalco skillfully explores the themes of identity, morality, and the constraints placed on women during the Victorian era. Audrey Rose's struggles to balance her desire for independence and intellectual pursuits with the societal expectations of her family and peers are deeply resonant, making her a powerful and inspiring heroine.

The author's use of real, sinister period photos adds a chilling layer of realism to the narrative, underscoring the darkness and brutality of the crimes being investigated. The twists and turns of the plot are expertly handled, keeping readers on the edge of their seats as they navigate the treacherous world of murder, mystery, and mayhem.

In "Hunting Prince Dracula," Kerri Maniscalco has crafted a deliciously haunting and deliciously creepy horror novel that will appeal to fans of historical fiction, mystery, and suspense. With its unique blend of forensic science, historical detail, and atmospheric tension, this book is sure to captivate readers and leave them eagerly anticipating the next installment in the series.

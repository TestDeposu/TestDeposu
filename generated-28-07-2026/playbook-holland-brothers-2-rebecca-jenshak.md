# Playbook (Holland Brothers, #2)

**Author:** Rebecca Jenshak  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

Step onto the pitch and into the beating heart of high school rivalry with Rebecca Jenshak’s Playbook, the standout second installment in the Holland Brothers series. Published in 2024, this YA romance captures the intoxicating rush of first loves, shattered dreams, and the courageous journey of finding oneself when the future takes an unexpected detour. Jenshak, a USA Today bestselling author, has crafted a story that resonates deeply with anyone who has ever had to rewrite their life’s script.

At the core of Playbook is a timeless yet freshly executed theme: reinvention in the face of loss. We meet Claire Crawford, a once-promising figure skater whose dreams have been abruptly frozen by a career-ending injury, compounded by the sting of a recent breakup. Then there is Austin Keller, the new kid in town who has traveled across the country carrying the heavy weight of elite athletic expectations as Frost Lake High’s new soccer star. When a reckless bonfire dare lands a spark between them—a kiss with the team captain's ex—it ignites a high-stakes rivalry that threatens to upend Austin’s social standing and soccer aspirations before they even truly begin.

Jenshak’s writing style shines in its ability to balance emotional vulnerability with lighthearted, snappy dialogue. Fans of the genre will immediately recognize the vibrant, cinematic energy that has earned the novel comparisons to modern YA favorites like Better Than the Movies. The pacing is brisk, mirroring the high-energy sport that frames the narrative, yet the author knows precisely when to slow down and let the quiet, tender moments breathe. 

The dynamic between Austin and Claire forms the undeniable anchor of the book. Claire is fiercely independent, determined to keep her walls up and her heart guarded against the whirlwind that is the new soccer boy. Austin, conversely, is relentlessly charming, driven, and fiercely protective. The push-and-pull of their relationship avoids tired clichés thanks to genuine character growth. Austin isn’t just chasing a win on the field; he is learning how to care for someone whose world has recently collapsed. Claire, in turn, discovers that letting go of an old dream doesn’t mean she is incapable of building a vibrant new one.

Beyond the central romance, Playbook excels in capturing the authentic, often messy social ecosystem of high school. The sports team dynamics, the looming threat of the jealous team captain, and the underlying humor throughout the narrative keep the pages turning effortlessly. It is sweet, swoony, and undeniably sparkly, delivering a high school sports romance that packs both emotional weight and sizzling chemistry.

For readers longing for a story that combines the thrill of athletic competition with the aching sweetness of young love, Playbook is an absolute must-read. Jenshak delivers a home run—or rather, a game-winning goal—proving that sometimes the best plays are the ones you never saw coming.

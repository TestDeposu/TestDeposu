# Catch Me If You Can: The True Story of a Real Fake

**Author:** Frank W. Abagnale  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1980

**Catch Me If You Can: A Hilarious, Unbelievable Ride Through the Life of a Master Con Artist**

Frank Abagnale's autobiography, "Catch Me If You Can," is a wild, rollercoaster ride through the life of a man who mastered the art of deception. Published in 1980, this book is a testament to Abagnale's audacious and ingenious exploits, immortalized by Leonardo DiCaprio in the 2002 DreamWorks film.

Abagnale's story begins in the 1960s, a time when his charm and quick wit led him down a path of impersonation and forgery. He was a chameleon, transforming from a Pan Am pilot to a sociology professor, a lawyer, and even a hospital resident. His confidence was his greatest asset, and his ability to think on his feet, his most valuable skill. He was, in every sense, a real fake.

The book is a thrilling cat-and-mouse game between Abagnale and the law. He's always one step ahead, always thinking two moves forward. Yet, despite the seriousness of his crimes, Abagnale's storytelling is infused with humor. He recounts his escapades with a self-deprecating wit that makes his exploits all the more entertaining.

Abagnale's style is engaging and conversational. He doesn't shy away from the details, painting vivid pictures of his heists and escapes. He's a storyteller at heart, and his narrative is all the more compelling for it. Yet, he's also reflective, using his experiences to offer insights into the criminal mind and the psychology of deception.

"Catch Me If You Can" is more than just a crime thriller. It's a study in human nature, a testament to the power of confidence, and a cautionary tale about the dangers of deception. Abagnale doesn't shy away from the consequences of his actions, and his eventual redemption adds a layer of depth to the story.

In essence, "Catch Me If You Can" is a must-read for anyone who loves a good story, a gripping thriller, or a fascinating look into the mind of a criminal mastermind. It's a book that will keep you on the edge of your seat, laughing one moment and gasping the next. It's a wild ride, and one you won't soon forget.

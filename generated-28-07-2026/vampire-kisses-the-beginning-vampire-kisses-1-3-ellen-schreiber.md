# Vampire Kisses: The Beginning (Vampire Kisses, #1-3)

**Author:** Ellen Schreiber  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**Vampire Kisses: The Beginning** by Ellen Schreiber is a captivating trilogy that weaves a tale of love, danger, and the supernatural, perfect for fans of young adult paranormal romance. This omnibus edition combines the first three books in the Vampire Kisses series, offering a thrilling and engaging read that will leave you yearning for more.

At the heart of the story is Raven, a goth-girl with a penchant for all things dark and mysterious, and Alexander, a vampire with a secret that keeps their relationship shrouded in the shadows. Their love story is one of forbidden romance, with Alexander's immortal nature creating a barrier between them, allowing them to meet only under the cover of night. Schreiber masterfully captures the angst and longing of first love, making Raven and Alexander's relationship both relatable and enchanting.

The narrative is fast-paced and filled with suspense, as the couple navigates the treacherous waters of their relationship while also dealing with external threats. The introduction of Alexander's archrival, Jagger, and his sister Luna adds an element of danger and intrigue, as the pair must work together to protect their town from becoming a haven for vampires. The search for Jagger and Luna's hideout is a thrilling adventure that will keep readers on the edge of their seats.

Schreiber's writing style is engaging and accessible, making it easy to become immersed in the world of Dullsville and its supernatural inhabitants. She expertly balances the romantic tension between Raven and Alexander with the suspenseful plot, creating a story that is both heartwarming and exhilarating. The characters are well-developed and relatable, with Raven's unique perspective on life and love making her a compelling protagonist.

The Vampire Kisses series is a must-read for fans of young adult paranormal romance, offering a fresh take on the classic vampire trope. With its engaging characters, thrilling plot, and unique setting, this trilogy is sure to captivate readers and leave them eager for more. So, if you're looking for a story that combines the thrill of the unknown with the warmth of first love, look no further than **Vampire Kisses: The Beginning**.

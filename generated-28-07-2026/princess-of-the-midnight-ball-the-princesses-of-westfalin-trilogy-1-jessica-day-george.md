# Princess of the Midnight Ball (The Princesses of Westfalin Trilogy, #1)

**Author:** Jessica Day George  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2012

"Delightful Deception and Whimsy in Jessica Day George's 'Princess of the Midnight Ball'"

Jessica Day George's captivating "Princess of the Midnight Ball" whisks readers away to a mystical realm of enchanting tales, richly woven storytelling, and unforgettable characters. This charming retelling of the classic "Twelve Dancing Princesses" folktale masterfully reimagines the timeless tale of love, danger, and rebellion with an intoxicating concoction of elements that leave a lasting impression.

As Petunia, the youngest of King Gregor's twelve dancing daughters, embarks upon a journey through treacherous lands and unexpected alliances, she becomes entangled in a delicate dance of subterfuge and trust. This clever adaptation expertly manipulates the essence of the original fable, transforming it into something both new and familiar. The captivating narrative expertly navigates the realms of intrigue, suspense, and heartwarming romance.

Through the clever crafting of characters, George skillfully showcases the fluidity between good and evil, blurring the lines between the innocent and nefarious. Petunia, in particular, emerges as a captivating and multi-faceted protagonist, full of spirit and resilience as she navigates through a world of secrets and deception. Her encounters with the bandit-wolves – whose enigmatic charm and charisma will undoubtedly leave readers enthralled – serve as a poignant reminder of the blurred lines between right and wrong.

As a testament to her skill as a storyteller, George masterfully evokes a mystical atmosphere, transporting readers to vividly realized settings that range from lush forests to opulent palaces. Her vivid descriptions, rich with detail and sensory elements, bring to life the enchanting world of Westfalin, rendering it feel both magical and uncomfortably real.

One of the standout aspects of "Princess of the Midnight Ball" is its masterful balance of genre-defying elements. Fans of fantasy and fairy tales will delight in the richly woven lore and intricate mythology that underpin the narrative, while romance aficionados will be swept up in the developing relationships and enthralling romantic encounters. Even readers accustomed to more mature literary fare will find themselves captivated by the relatable characters, nuanced exploration of identity and morality, and the author's unique voice.

Ultimately, Jessica Day George's "Princess of the Midnight Ball" is a timeless, captivating fairy tale that will captivate readers with its delightful blend of adventure, romance, and fantasy. Its exploration of themes such as deception, trust, and the intricacies of human nature will keep readers invested, while the author's engaging narrative voice leaves a lasting impression.

# Vengeful (Villains, #2)

**Author:** V.E. Schwab  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

In V.E. Schwab's Vengeful, the Villains series escalates into a thrilling and complex web of vengeance, manipulation, and the corrupting influence of superpowers. A sequel to the critically acclaimed Vicious, this novel is a masterful exploration of human relationships and the blurred lines between good and evil. On its surface, Vengeful appears to be a superhero story, but scratch beneath its surface, and you'll discover a nuanced exploration of ambition, jealousy, and the devastating consequences of unchecked power.

At the center of the narrative is Marcella Riggins, a cunning and enigmatic figure with extraordinary abilities. Flush from her near-death experience, Marcella seizes control, determined to reshape Merit, the city she calls home, in her image. Her ascent is marked by calculated aggression, as she forms alliances, manipulates those around her, and exploits the darkest impulses of others.

Schwab's writing is both captivating and unsettling, as she expertly navigates the gray areas of human nature. Through Marcella's eyes, we witness the devastating consequences of unchecked ambition, as she sacrifices relationships, morals, and even her own humanity in pursuit of ultimate power. Yet, this is not a character we're meant to hate; Marcella is a complex, multidimensional individual, driven by a deep-seated need for validation and a fierce determination to prove herself.

Schwab's exploration of villainy is a nuanced commentary on the ways in which we justify our own destructive tendencies. By elevating Marcella's story to a central focus, the author cleverly subverts traditional notions of heroism and villainy, presenting a world in which both categories are malleable and often interchangeable. This narrative choice creates an unflinching examination of the nature of morality, highlighting the ease with which we excuse, rationalize, or even romanticize our darker impulses.

This is also a novel that celebrates the power of partnerships and the blurred boundaries that define them. The relationships between Marcella and her allies, as well as the fraught bond between Victor Vale and Eli Ever, are masterfully woven throughout the narrative. These partnerships are often fraught, tested, and ultimately redefined by the demands of their extraordinary abilities, making for a compelling exploration of the ways in which we compromise our values and relationships in pursuit of a higher goal.

One of the most striking aspects of Vengeful is its masterful deployment of plot twists. Schwab's writing is characterized by an effortless fluidity, effortlessly moving between multiple storylines and perspectives without ever sacrificing clarity or momentum. The narrative unfolds at breakneck speed, propelled by a driving sense of tension that propels the reader forward, eager to uncover the next revelation.

Ultimately, Vengeful is a thought-provoking exploration of the human condition, a nuanced consideration of the ways in which our choices and motivations define us. Schwab's Villains series has quickly established itself as a benchmark for superhero fiction, offering a fresh, subversive take on the genre that challenges readers to question everything they thought they knew about heroism, villainy, and the complexities of human nature.

In Vengeful, V.E. Schwab is at the top of her game, delivering a gripping, unpredictable narrative that will captivate fans of superhero fiction and leave readers contemplating the boundaries of morality long after the final page is turned.

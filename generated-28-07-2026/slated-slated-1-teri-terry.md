# Slated (Slated, #1)

**Author:** Teri Terry  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

In the gripping young adult novel "Slated" by Teri Terry, the lines between truth and deception are blurred, leaving the protagonist, Kyla, to navigate a complex web of identity, morality, and control. Published in 2013, this suspenseful thriller is set in a dystopian world where the government exercises total control over its citizens, erasing the memories of teenagers deemed a threat to society.

The story follows Kyla, a teenager who has been Slated – a process that wipes her memory clean, leaving her with no recollection of her past. She is given a new identity, a new family, and a new life, but at a steep price. Kyla is fitted with a Levo, a device that monitors her emotions and can deliver a lethal shock if her anger or violence levels become too high. As she struggles to adjust to her new life, Kyla begins to experience strange nightmares that feel like memories, making her question the true nature of her Slating.

Terry's writing style is engaging and suspenseful, with a keen focus on the emotional turmoil Kyla faces. The author masterfully crafts a sense of tension and unease, keeping the reader on edge as Kyla navigates her new reality. The world-building is vivid and immersive, with a keen attention to detail that makes the dystopian society feel eerily plausible.

One of the most striking aspects of "Slated" is its exploration of the theme of identity. Kyla's journey is a powerful metaphor for the search for self, as she grapples with the question of who she is, beyond the confines of her Slating. Terry raises important questions about the nature of identity, free will, and the impact of external control on an individual's autonomy.

The romance and politics in the novel are expertly woven into the narrative, adding depth and complexity to the story. The characters are well-developed and multi-dimensional, with Kyla being a particularly strong protagonist. Her determination to uncover the truth about her past and her willingness to take risks make her a compelling and relatable character.

The pacing of the novel is well-balanced, with a steady stream of revelations and twists that keep the reader engaged. The author's use of language is concise and effective, making the story feel both urgent and intimate.

Overall, "Slated" is a gripping and thought-provoking novel that will appeal to fans of dystopian fiction and suspenseful thrillers. Teri Terry's writing style is engaging and immersive, making this book a compelling read. With its exploration of identity, morality, and control, "Slated" is a must-read for anyone interested in young adult fiction.

# Part of Your World (Part of Your World, #1)

**Author:** Abby Jimenez  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

Abby Jimenez has carved out a distinct and beloved space in contemporary romance, but with Part of Your World, she delivers a masterclass in emotional depth wrapped in a charming, small-town package. Published in 2022, this novel takes the classic opposites-attract trope and elevates it through razor-sharp wit, high-stakes emotional realism, and an undeniable chemistry that sizzles from the very first page. 

At its core, the novel explores a profound theme: the tension between familial obligation and authentic self-actualization. Alexis Montgomery is trapped in the gilded cage of a legacy she never chose. As a brilliant ER doctor born into a dynasty of world-renowned surgeons, her entire existence has been meticulously choreographed by ultra-wealthy, domineering parents who view her career and personal choices merely as extensions of the family brand. Enter Daniel Grant: a grounded, easygoing, younger carpenter from the quirky, tight-knit town of Wakan. Daniel represents everything Alexis's world is not—spontaneous, unpretentious, and emotionally available. 

Jimenez uses this dynamic not just for romantic tension, but as a vehicle to dissect the heavy burden of generational expectations. The central conflict of the book is not merely about whether a city girl and a country carpenter can make a romance work. Rather, it is a poignant interrogation of what it costs to break away from an abusive, controlling family structure. Alexis’s struggle feels painfully authentic because Jimenez refuses to paint her dilemma with a broad brush. Choosing Daniel means turning her back on prestige, but staying in her world means sacrificing her soul. The author brilliantly illustrates how difficult it is to unlearn a lifetime of conditioning, making Alexis’s journey toward choosing her own happiness deeply resonant and hard-earned.

Stylistically, Jimenez shines in her ability to balance heavy, sensitive themes with laugh-out-loud humor and undeniable charm. The pacing is relentless, driven by snappy dialogue and undeniable sexual tension born from a brilliant meet-cute involving a wild bet, gourmet grilled cheese, and a baby goat. Yet, what truly elevates Jimenez’s writing style is her refusal to rely on cheap miscommunications for drama. The obstacles facing Alexis and Daniel are systemic, cultural, and deeply personal. Furthermore, Jimenez populates Wakan with a vibrant, fiercely loyal supporting cast that grounds the narrative and turns the small town into a character in its own right, offering the warmth and community Alexis has always lacked.

Daniel Grant is a refreshing hero for the modern romance era. He is emotionally intelligent, fiercely supportive, and completely unthreatened by Alexis’s success or intelligence. In turn, Alexis is a complex protagonist whose competence in the emergency room contrasts movingly with her vulnerability in her personal life. Their romance feels earned, built on mutual respect, deep conversations, and a healing of old wounds.

Part of Your World is a triumph of modern romance writing. It is funny, devastating, swoon-worthy, and ultimately triumphant. Abby Jimenez has crafted a novel that will leave readers grinning through tears, thoroughly captivated by a love story that proves the best life is the one you choose for yourself.

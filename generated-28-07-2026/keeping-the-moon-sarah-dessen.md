# Keeping the Moon

**Author:** Sarah Dessen  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1999

"Keeping the Moon" by Sarah Dessen is a heartwarming tale of self-discovery, friendship, and love, set against the backdrop of a summer by the beach. Published in 1999, this novel showcases Dessen's signature style of capturing the essence of teenage life with remarkable authenticity and depth.

The story revolves around Colie, a young woman who finds herself spending the summer at her estranged mother's beach house. Expecting little more than a monotonous vacation, Colie's life takes an unexpected turn when she meets Morgan and Isabel, two girls who become her unlikely friends and allies. Through their friendship, Colie begins to understand the true meaning of acceptance, loyalty, and self-worth.

Dessen's prose is as warm and inviting as the beach setting, drawing readers in with its relatable characters and engaging narrative. She has a knack for capturing the complexities of teenage emotions, from the first flush of love to the pain of betrayal, with a sensitivity and honesty that resonates with readers. "Keeping the Moon" is a testament to Dessen's ability to create compelling, believable characters who grow and change throughout the story.

The novel's main theme is the transformative power of friendship. Colie's journey from a shy, insecure girl to a confident young woman is a testament to the influence of her newfound friends. Morgan and Isabel, each with their own unique struggles, teach Colie about resilience, courage, and the importance of standing up for oneself. In turn, Colie learns to appreciate her own strengths and embrace her individuality.

Dessen also explores the delicate balance between love and friendship, as Colie navigates her feelings for a boy she meets at the beach. She masterfully illustrates the complexities of young love, the heartache, and the joy, with a realism that is both comforting and challenging.

"Keeping the Moon" is a story about finding one's place in the world, about the power of friendship to heal and transform, and about the courage it takes to be true to oneself. It is a story that will resonate with readers of all ages, reminding them of the beauty and pain of growing up.

# After We Collided (After, #2)

**Author:** Anna Todd  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

After We Collided, the second installment in Anna Todd's After series, is a tumultuous exploration of the complexities of love, relationships, and personal growth. Picking up where the first book left off, Tessa and Hardin's relationship is put to the test as secrets about Hardin's past threaten to tear them apart.

Todd's writing style is as engaging as ever, with a keen focus on the emotional turmoil that defines Tessa and Hardin's relationship. The author's use of vivid descriptions and poignant dialogue effectively conveys the intensity of the characters' emotions, making it easy for readers to become invested in their story.

One of the main themes of the book is the struggle for identity and independence. Tessa, in particular, finds herself at a crossroads, torn between her love for Hardin and her desire to move forward with her life. Her inner conflict is palpable, and Todd's portrayal of her emotional state is both authentic and relatable.

Hardin, on the other hand, is a complex character whose rough exterior hides a deep sense of vulnerability. His struggles to come to terms with his past and his feelings for Tessa are deeply humanizing, and Todd's characterization of him is both nuanced and compelling.

The chemistry between Tessa and Hardin is undeniable, and their passionate moments together are some of the most memorable in the book. However, it's the quiet moments, the moments of introspection and self-doubt, that truly showcase Todd's skill as a writer.

If there's one criticism to be made, it's that the book's pacing can be a bit uneven at times. Some scenes feel rushed, while others drag on for a bit too long. However, this is a minor quibble in what is otherwise a gripping and emotional read.

Overall, After We Collided is a must-read for fans of the series and anyone who enjoys a good romance. Todd's writing is engaging, her characters are complex and multi-dimensional, and the story is both captivating and emotionally resonant.

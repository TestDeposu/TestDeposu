# Persepolis Rising (The Expanse, #7)

**Author:** James S.A. Corey  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

In James S.A. Corey's latest installment in The Expanse series, Persepolis Rising, the fragile peace between Earth, Mars, and the Belt teeters on the brink of collapse as an old enemy returns with unprecedented power and technology. The seventh book in this critically acclaimed series finds the crew of the aging gunship Rocinante battling to rally forces against the new invasion, while navigating the complexities of a thousand-sun network where every new planet hangs in the balance between collapse and wonder.

At the heart of Persepolis Rising lies a timeless theme: the cyclical nature of human conflict, where the same patterns of war and subjugation repeat themselves across generations. Corey skillfully weaves this narrative with his signature blend of science fiction, politics, and personal drama, plunging readers into a universe where humanity's expansion has created a complex tapestry of alliances, prejudices, and forgotten histories.

As the Rocinante crew navigates this treacherous landscape, they are forced to confront their own limitations and the true cost of war. Corey's masterful storytelling raises questions about the nature of power, the price of progress, and the human cost of conflict. With his characteristic restraint, he lets the characters' experiences speak for themselves, never shying away from the brutal realities of war and the long-term consequences of human actions.

One of Corey's greatest strengths as a writer is his ability to create a sense of scale and complexity without sacrificing the intimate, human touch that makes The Expanse series so compelling. Persepolis Rising is no exception, as the reader is drawn into the intricate dance of politics, alliances, and rivalries that shape the fate of humanity across the solar system.

Throughout the book, Corey's world-building is meticulous and breathtaking in its scope, effortlessly balancing scientific and sociological details to create a richly immersive universe that feels both authentic and plausible. The Expanse series has always been known for its grounding in scientific reality, and Persepolis Rising is no exception, but Corey's writing transcends mere tech specs to explore the deeper implications of human expansion and the consequences of technological advancement.

What makes Persepolis Rising truly remarkable, however, is its ability to balance action, drama, and introspection in a way that will leave readers on the edges of their seats. Corey's writing is economical, yet evocative, never shying away from the messy emotional fallout of war and personal struggle, while still delivering pulse-pounding action sequences and plot twists that will keep readers guessing until the very end.

Ultimately, Persepolis Rising is another masterful installment in The Expanse series, cementing James S.A. Corey's reputation as one of the most skilled and thought-provoking science fiction writers working today. With its gripping narrative, memorable characters, and meticulously crafted world-building, this book is a must-read for fans of science fiction, politics, and adventure alike.

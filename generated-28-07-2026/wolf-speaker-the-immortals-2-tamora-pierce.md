# Wolf-Speaker (The Immortals, #2)

**Author:** Tamora Pierce  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

In "Wolf-Speaker," the second installment of Tamora Pierce's beloved Immortals series, the author masterfully weaves a tale of environmentalism, friendship, and self-discovery. The story follows Daine, a young wild mage, as she embarks on a perilous journey to Dunlath Valley to aid a pack of wolves in distress. Accompanied by her mentor, Numair, Daine soon finds herself entangled in a complex web of human greed, magic, and politics.

Pierce's writing style is as captivating as ever, with vivid descriptions of the natural world and a keen sense of pacing that propels the reader forward. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with Daine's growth and development being a particular highlight. As the protagonist navigates the challenges of her wild magic and confronts the harsh realities of the world around her, she undergoes a significant transformation, emerging as a confident and powerful young woman.

One of the most striking aspects of "Wolf-Speaker" is its exploration of environmental themes. Pierce tackles the issue of exploitation and the consequences of human actions on the natural world with sensitivity and nuance. The rulers of Dunlath's reckless pursuit of black opals, regardless of the cost to the land and its inhabitants, serves as a powerful allegory for the real-world struggles we face today. Daine's deep connection to the natural world and her determination to protect it make her a compelling and inspiring heroine.

The world-building in "Wolf-Speaker" is equally impressive, with Pierce expanding on the richly detailed universe she introduced in the first book. The magical system is intricate and well-developed, with a clear sense of rules and limitations that adds depth and authenticity to the story. The characters' interactions with the natural world are similarly well-realized, with a sense of wonder and magic that permeates every aspect of the narrative.

Throughout the book, Pierce's prose is engaging and accessible, making "Wolf-Speaker" an excellent choice for readers of all ages. The story is full of action, suspense, and heart, with a narrative that is both thrilling and emotionally resonant. The updated cover and new afterword from the author are a welcome addition, providing a fresh perspective on the series and its enduring appeal.

In conclusion, "Wolf-Speaker" is a captivating and thought-provoking read that will appeal to fans of fantasy, adventure, and environmental fiction. With its strong heroine, engaging storyline, and well-developed world, it is a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and immersive reading experience.

# Taming 7 (Boys of Tommen, #5)

**Author:** Chloe Walsh  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

In the latest installment of the Boys of Tommen series, Chloe Walsh masterfully weaves an unforgettable love story in Taming 7. This epic tale of love and self-discovery follows the enigmatic Gerard 'Gibsie' Gibson and the effervescent Claire Biggs as they navigate the blurred lines between friendship and romance.

At its core, Taming 7 is a poignant exploration of the human condition, delving into the complexities of emotional vulnerability and the masks we wear to hide our true selves. Walsh skillfully crafts Gibsie's character, a lovable class clown with a penchant for humor, but also a deeply wounded soul. His struggles to confront the demons of his past and reconcile his true self with the persona he presents to the world are both heart-wrenching and relatable.

Claire, the quintessential sunshine girl, is the perfect foil to Gibsie's brooding nature. Her unwavering optimism and unshakeable faith in Gibsie's inherent goodness make her the catalyst for his transformation. As their friendship blossoms into something more, Walsh expertly captures the intoxicating thrill of first love, the uncertainty of navigating uncharted emotions, and the terror of potentially losing the person you hold dearest.

Walsh's writing style is a masterclass in subtlety, effortlessly conveying the emotional depth of her characters through nuanced dialogue, evocative descriptions, and a keen understanding of the human psyche. The dual POVs add an extra layer of complexity to the narrative, allowing the reader to experience the story from both Gibsie's and Claire's perspectives.

One of the standout aspects of Taming 7 is its ability to balance lightness and darkness. Walsh tackles heavy themes such as trauma, anxiety, and the fragility of the human heart, yet does so with a deft touch that never feels overwhelming or gratuitous. The result is a narrative that is both angsty and uplifting, often simultaneously.

Fans of the Boys of Tommen series will delight in the return to the world of Tommen, where Walsh's vivid descriptions of the Irish landscape and the quirky cast of characters will feel like a warm hug. New readers, however, need not worry about feeling lost; Taming 7 is a beautifully self-contained story that can be enjoyed as a standalone.

In short, Taming 7 is a triumph of contemporary romance, a sweeping love story that will capture your heart, break it, and put it back together again. With its unique blend of humor, angst, and emotional depth, this book is a must-read for fans of new adult/YA crossover fiction, friends-to-lovers romances, and anyone looking for a story that will linger long after the final page is turned.

# Deadly Little Secret (Touch, #1)

**Author:** Laurie Faria Stolarz  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

**Deadly Little Secret: A Chilling Dance of Attraction and Danger**

Laurie Faria Stolarz's "Deadly Little Secret," the first installment in her Touch series, is a tantalizing blend of supernatural romance and suspense that keeps readers on the edge of their seats. The story revolves around Camelia, a seemingly ordinary 16-year-old girl whose life takes a dramatic turn with the arrival of the enigmatic Ben.

Stolarz masterfully weaves a tale that explores the intoxicating allure of the unknown and the dangerous consequences of keeping secrets. Camelia's attraction to Ben is palpable, yet unsettling, as she finds herself drawn to him despite the ominous rumors surrounding his past. The author's signature style, honed in her bestselling Blue is for Nightmares series, is evident here, with short, snappy chapters that build tension and maintain a steady pace.

The novel's strength lies in its exploration of the main theme: the fine line between attraction and danger. Camelia's fascination with Ben is as compelling as it is perilous, and Stolarz expertly navigates this precarious territory. As Camelia delves deeper into Ben's secrets, she uncovers a world of supernatural forces that threaten to consume her. The author's ability to create a sense of foreboding, even as she draws readers into the romance, is what makes "Deadly Little Secret" such a gripping read.

Stolarz's characters are well-crafted, each with their own secrets and motivations. Camelia is a relatable protagonist, her curiosity and determination making her a compelling heroine. Ben, the mysterious new boy, is a fascinating study in contrasts, his dark past and supernatural abilities adding depth to his character. The supporting cast, from Camelia's loyal friends to the enigmatic art teacher, also contribute to the story's rich tapestry.

The novel's supernatural elements are seamlessly integrated into the story, adding an eerie dimension to the romance and suspense. Stolarz's use of phone calls, packages, and threatening notes to build tension is particularly effective, creating a sense of unease that permeates every page.

"Deadly Little Secret" is a thrilling and chilling tale that will appeal to fans of paranormal romance and suspense. Stolarz's signature style, combined with her ability to create compelling characters and a sense of foreboding, makes this a standout novel in the genre.

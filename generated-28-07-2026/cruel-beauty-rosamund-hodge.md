# Cruel Beauty

**Author:** Rosamund Hodge  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

In the enchanting and mystical world of Cruel Beauty, Rosamund Hodge masterfully weaves a spellbinding tale of forbidden love, dark magic, and the unyielding power of the human spirit. This stunning debut novel is a retelling of the classic Beauty and the Beast, infused with the author's unique blend of Gothic fantasy and captivating storytelling.

At the heart of the narrative lies the complex and intriguing character of Nyx, a young woman betrothed to the malevolent ruler of her kingdom, the Gentle Lord. Nyx's fate is sealed – she is destined to marry the Gentle Lord, kill him, and free her people from his tyranny. However, as she navigates the treacherous landscape of the castle, she finds herself torn between her duty and her growing attraction to the enigmatic and beguiling Gentle Lord.

Hodge's writing is nothing short of enchanting, with a lyrical quality that transports the reader to a world of glittering darkness and magical wonder. Her prose is rich and evocative, painting vivid images of the castle's shifting halls, the parchment sky, and the kingdom shrouded in secrets. The atmosphere is tense and foreboding, with a sense of danger lurking around every corner.

One of the most striking aspects of Cruel Beauty is its exploration of the gray areas between good and evil. The Gentle Lord is a masterfully crafted character, full of contradictions and nuances. Is he a tyrant, a victim, or something in between? Nyx's perceptions of him are constantly shifting, and the reader is left questioning their own moral compass.

The romance between Nyx and the Gentle Lord is a slow-burning fire that simmers beneath the surface, threatening to consume them both. It's a beautiful, tender, and passionate portrayal of love, one that defies the conventions of traditional fairy tales. Hodge's handling of the romance is expertly done, with a deep understanding of the complexities of the human heart.

The world-building in Cruel Beauty is impressive, with a unique magic system and a richly detailed history. The kingdom is a character in its own right, with a dark and troubled past that informs the present. Hodge's use of mythology and folklore adds depth and texture to the narrative, making the world feel fully realized and immersive.

In conclusion, Cruel Beauty is a captivating and unforgettable read, one that will appeal to fans of fantasy, romance, and fairy tale retellings. Rosamund Hodge's writing is a true delight, with a unique voice and style that sets her apart from other authors in the genre. If you're looking for a story that will transport you to another world, one that will challenge your perceptions and leave you breathless, then Cruel Beauty is the perfect choice.

# The Gilded Wolves (The Gilded Wolves, #1)

**Author:** Roshani Chokshi  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

**A Gilded Heist of the Senses: Roshani Chokshi's "The Gilded Wolves"**

In the glittering, gaslit streets of Belle Époque Paris, Roshani Chokshi weaves a spellbinding tale of ambition, power, and found family in "The Gilded Wolves," the first installment of her captivating historical fantasy series. Chokshi, a master of lush, evocative prose, paints a world where magic and history collide, and where the line between thief and hero is delightfully blurred.

At the heart of the novel lies Séverin Montagnet-Alarie, a man driven by the desire to reclaim his stolen birthright. His quest for vengeance leads him to assemble a ragtag group of misfits, each with their own unique skills and secrets. Together, they must steal a powerful artifact, a Babel fragment, from the grand Exposition Universelle World Fair. But as the stakes grow higher and the dangers more treacherous, Séverin finds himself torn between his thirst for revenge and the safety of his unlikely family.

Chokshi's strength lies in her ability to create rich, complex characters that leap off the page. Each member of Séverin's crew is a fully realized individual, with their own motivations, fears, and desires. From Enrique, the historian caught between two worlds, to Zofia, the brilliant engineer separated from her family, each character brings something unique to the table, both in terms of skill and personality. Their dynamic is a joy to behold, a testament to Chokshi's skill in crafting compelling, relatable characters.

The author's style is as opulent as the Parisian setting, her prose a feast for the senses. She deftly balances intricate world-building with fast-paced action, creating a narrative that is both intellectually stimulating and impossible to put down. Chokshi's love for history and mythology shines through in every page, as she seamlessly weaves real-world events and figures into her fantastical tale.

At its core, "The Gilded Wolves" is a story about found family and the lengths we'll go to protect those we love. It's a tale of trust and betrayal, of the power of knowledge and the danger of secrets. It's a heist story, yes, but it's also a story about the human heart, about the bonds that tie us together and the choices that define us.

Chokshi's debut in the adult fantasy genre is a triumph, a masterclass in world-building, character development, and storytelling. "The Gilded Wolves" is more than just a thrilling adventure; it's a meditation on power, privilege, and the price of ambition. It's a book that demands to be read, discussed, and savored.

# The Exiled Queen (Seven Realms, #2)

**Author:** Cinda Williams Chima  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**A Review of "The Exiled Queen" by Cinda Williams Chima**

In "The Exiled Queen," Cinda Williams Chima weaves a captivating tale that masterfully blends political intrigue, magical suspense, and a touch of forbidden romance. The second installment in the Seven Realms series picks up where "The Demon King" left off, following the intertwined journeys of Han Alister and Princess Raisa ana'Marianna.

Chima's strength lies in her ability to create complex, relatable characters who grow and evolve throughout the narrative. Han, the street-smart thief turned wizard, grapples with the weight of his past and the dangerous path he's chosen. Meanwhile, Raisa, the princess-next-in-line, struggles to navigate the treacherous waters of court politics and her own desires. Their parallel journeys, each fraught with challenges and revelations, keep the pages turning.

The author's style is engaging and accessible, with a knack for balancing action, dialogue, and introspection. Chima's world-building is intricate and immersive, with a richly drawn magical system and a diverse cast of characters. The story unfolds in the bustling city of Oden's Ford, a stark contrast to the rugged Fells, allowing for a fresh perspective and new dynamics.

At the heart of "The Exiled Queen" lies the theme of identity and the struggle to reconcile one's past with their future. Han and Raisa, both exiled in their own ways, must confront their fears and insecurities to embrace their destinies. This theme is explored through their individual journeys and their budding relationship, which is as complex and captivating as the rest of the story.

Chima also excels in creating a sense of danger and tension that permeates every page. From the Bayars' relentless pursuit of Han to the political machinations threatening Raisa, the stakes are always high. Yet, amidst the turmoil, there are moments of humor and warmth, often provided by the supporting characters, who are as well-drawn and memorable as the main protagonists.

In conclusion, "The Exiled Queen" is a thrilling and engaging read that will appeal to fans of epic fantasy. Chima's compelling characters, intricate world-building, and masterful storytelling make this a standout series. If you're a fan of political intrigue, magical suspense, and character-driven narratives, this book is a must-read.

# Edenbrooke (Edenbrooke, #1)

**Author:** Julianne Donaldson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2017

**Edenbrooke: A Regency Romance to Swoon Over**

In the heart of 19th-century England, Julianne Donaldson weaves a captivating tale of love, intrigue, and adventure in her debut novel, "Edenbrooke." The story unfolds in the grand estate of Edenbrooke, where our heroine, Marianne Daventry, seeks refuge from the tedium of Bath and the unwanted attentions of a suitor. Little does she know, her summer escape will be anything but ordinary.

Donaldson's prose is as elegant and refined as the Regency era itself, painting vivid images of sprawling estates, lavish balls, and the lush English countryside. Her characters are equally as well-crafted, with Marianne proving to be a heroine of substance and spirit. She is no simpering miss, but a woman of intelligence and wit, making her a refreshing protagonist in the genre.

The romance at the heart of "Edenbrooke" is as intoxicating as it is unexpected. Marianne's initial disdain for the brooding and mysterious Mr. Frederick Beauclerk gives way to a slow-burning passion that simmers beneath the surface, ready to boil over at any moment. Donaldson masterfully builds tension, leaving readers on the edge of their seats, eager for the next stolen glance or whispered word between the two.

Yet, "Edenbrooke" is more than just a romance. It is a thrilling adventure filled with suspense and danger. From a terrifying encounter with a highwayman to a mysterious illness plaguing the estate, Donaldson keeps readers guessing, their hearts pounding with anticipation.

The novel's main theme is the power of love to transform and redeem. Marianne's journey is one of self-discovery, as she learns to trust her heart and embrace her true feelings. Meanwhile, Frederick grapples with his own demons, his past mistakes threatening to destroy any hope of a future with Marianne.

"Edenbrooke" is a triumph of Regency romance, offering readers a richly drawn world, engaging characters, and a heart-wrenching love story. It is a novel that will leave you breathless, your heart aching for more, long after you've turned the final page.

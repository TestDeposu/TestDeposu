# Pucking Around (Jacksonville Rays, #1)

**Author:** Emily Rath  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

In the scorching hot world of sports romance, Emily Rath's "Pucking Around" is a game-changer. This sizzling debut novel promises to leave readers breathless and begging for more. As the first installment in the Jacksonville Rays series, it sets the tone for a thrilling ride filled with passion, drama, and hockey.

At the heart of the story is Rachel Price, a strong-willed and determined doctor who has just landed a job with the Jacksonville Rays. Her life is about to take a dramatic turn when she discovers that the team's star player is none other than the mysterious stranger she shared an unforgettable night with two months prior. The chemistry between them is undeniable, but as they navigate their feelings, they must confront the challenges of their careers and the scrutiny that comes with being in the public eye.

One of the standout aspects of Rath's writing is her ability to craft relatable characters that leap off the page. Rachel is a fantastic protagonist - smart, sassy, and unafraid to speak her mind. Her banter with the team's equipment manager, a lovably gruff and grumpy sidekick, is some of the book's most enjoyable moments. The tension between Rachel and the star player, however, is the real showstopper. Their romance is a slow-burning fire that simmers just below the surface, waiting to erupt into a passionate inferno.

Rath's writing style is engaging and effortless, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world of the Jacksonville Rays. Her descriptions of the hockey games are vivid and intense, putting the reader right in the midst of the action. The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with a range of quirky and endearing characters that add depth and humor to the story.

What sets "Pucking Around" apart from other sports romances is its willingness to tackle real issues. Rath doesn't shy away from exploring the pressures and challenges faced by women in male-dominated industries, and Rachel's struggles to assert herself in a testosterone-fueled environment are both authentic and empowering.

If you're looking for a romance novel that will keep you up all night, "Pucking Around" is the perfect choice. With its sizzling chemistry, witty banter, and heartfelt emotion, this book is sure to delight fans of the genre. So, grab a copy, settle in, and get ready to fall in love with the Jacksonville Rays.

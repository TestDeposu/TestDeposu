# Succubus on Top (Georgina Kincaid, #2)

**Author:** Richelle Mead  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

**Succubus on Top: A Thrilling Ride of Self-Discovery and Forbidden Love**

In Richelle Mead's sophomore novel, Succubus on Top, the succubus Georgina Kincaid returns with a vengeance, navigating the complexities of immortal life, forbidden love, and the dark forces that lurk in every corner of Seattle. With her witty banter, sharp instincts, and unapologetic sass, Georgina Kincaid has captured the hearts of readers worldwide, making her one of the most beloved characters in urban fantasy.

The novel picks up where the first installment left off, with Georgina's love life a tangled web of secrecy and danger. Her growing feelings for Seth Mortensen, a shy and talented writer, are put to the test when she discovers that her succubus nature makes their romantic relationship impossible. Mead skillfully weaves this dilemma into the fabric of the story, exploring the themes of identity, self-acceptance, and the search for true connection.

As Georgina struggles to balance her personal life with her duties as an immortal, she is faced with an unexpected threat: Doug, her co-worker, has been exhibiting bizarre behavior, and Georgina suspects that something sinister is brewing. With the fate of Doug's soul hanging precariously in the balance, Georgina must summon all her cunning and resourcefulness to save the day.

Throughout the novel, Mead masterfully blends elements of urban fantasy, romance, and supernatural mystery, creating a thrilling narrative that is both dark and playful. Her writing is infused with a wicked sense of humor, making Succubus on Top a true delight from start to finish. The supporting cast of characters, including the charismatic Bastien and Georgina's loyal friends, add depth and richness to the story, making it impossible to put down.

One of Mead's greatest strengths as an author is her ability to craft complex, multidimensional characters. Georgina Kincaid is no exception: she is a flawed and relatable protagonist, with a voice that is both authentic and endearing. As she navigates the treacherous landscape of immortal politics and supernatural threats, Georgina's vulnerabilities and weaknesses make her all the more human, and her resilience and determination make her a true hero.

Overall, Succubus on Top is a captivating and engaging read that will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very end. With its lush world-building, witty dialogue, and swoon-worthy romance, this novel is a must-read for fans of urban fantasy and paranormal romance. Richelle Mead's writing is a true gift, and Succubus on Top is a shining example of her craftsmanship.

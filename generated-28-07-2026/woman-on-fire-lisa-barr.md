# Woman on Fire

**Author:** Lisa Barr  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

**Woman on Fire: A Blazing Tale of Art, History, and Revenge**

Lisa Barr's latest offering, **Woman on Fire**, is a captivating historical thriller that sets the literary world ablaze with its intoxicating blend of art, intrigue, and passion. A sequel to the award-winning **Fugitive Colors**, this novel stands tall on its own merits, propelling readers on a breathless chase through the labyrinthine worlds of art, journalism, and the dark underbelly of Nazi-looted treasures.

At the heart of the narrative lies the enigmatic **Woman on Fire**, a masterpiece by Expressionist artist Ernst Engel, stolen by the Nazis and now sought after by two formidable women: the tenacious journalist Jules Roth and the ruthless art gallerist Margaux de Laurent. Both women are driven by personal motivations, their relentless pursuit of the painting fueling a high-stakes game of cat and mouse that spans continents and decades.

Barr's prose is as vivid and dynamic as the artwork that drives the plot. She paints her characters with bold strokes, each one a complex, multi-faceted canvas of ambition, desire, and vulnerability. Jules, our intrepid heroine, is a journalist with a heart of gold and a spine of steel, her determination to uncover the truth a beacon of light in the shadows of the art world's underbelly. Margaux, her formidable adversary, is a study in contrasts - a woman of power and privilege, yet also a victim of her own ruthless ambition.

The novel's exploration of the ethical quagmire surrounding Nazi-looted art is as thought-provoking as it is thrilling. Barr delves into the complex moral dilemmas faced by those who seek to right the wrongs of the past, questioning where the line should be drawn between justice and revenge. The narrative is further enriched by its exploration of the transformative power of art, its ability to inspire, heal, and ignite the human spirit.

Barr's storytelling is as masterful as the artwork that inspires it. She weaves together multiple timelines and perspectives with consummate skill, gradually revealing the truth about the **Woman on Fire** and the lives she has touched. The pacing is taut, the tension almost palpable, as the reader is drawn inexorably towards the novel's explosive climax.

**Woman on Fire** is more than just a thriller; it's a love letter to art, a testament to the power of truth, and a stirring exploration of the human spirit. It's a novel that will leave you breathless, your heart pounding, and your soul stirred. It's a must-read for fans of historical thrillers, art enthusiasts, and anyone who loves a story that burns bright.

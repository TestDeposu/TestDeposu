Kidnapped, penned by the illustrious Robert Louis Stevenson, is an enduring masterpiece that has captivated readers for generations. At its core, Kidnapped is a story about the unbreakable bonds of loyalty, friendship, and the pursuit of justice. The narrative follows the journey of young David Balfour, a naive yet resilient protagonist, as he navigates the treacherous landscape of his own destiny. However, his fate takes a dramatic turn when he encounters the enigmatic Alan Breck, a charismatic champion of the Jacobite cause.

Through the eyes of David Balfour, Stevenson masterfully weaves a tale of adventure, replete with shipwrecks, murders, and daring escapes.

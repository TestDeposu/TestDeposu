# The Dark Is Rising Sequence (The Dark is Rising, #1-5)

**Author:** Susan Cooper  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2001

"The Dark Is Rising Sequence" by Susan Cooper is a mesmerizing and enchanting series that has captivated readers of all ages with its richly woven tale of magic, adventure, and the eternal struggle between good and evil. This five-book series, published in 2001, is a masterful blend of fantasy, mythology, and coming-of-age themes, set against the backdrop of the English countryside and the mystical world of the Old Ones.

At the heart of the series is the story of Will Stanton, an ordinary boy who discovers on his eleventh birthday that he is the last of the Old Ones, a group of immortal beings tasked with keeping the world safe from the forces of darkness. As Will embarks on a quest to gather the six magical Signs that will aid the Old Ones in the final battle against the Dark, he is thrust into a world of wonder, terror, and delight.

Cooper's writing style is one of the most striking aspects of this series. Her prose is lyrical and evocative, conjuring up vivid images of the English countryside and the mystical world of the Old Ones. Her use of language is precise and economical, yet richly textured, making it easy to become fully immersed in the world she has created. The pacing is well-balanced, with a mix of action, suspense, and quieter moments of introspection and character development.

One of the main themes of the series is the struggle between light and darkness, good and evil. Cooper explores this theme in a way that is both nuanced and accessible, making it easy for readers of all ages to understand and relate to. The series also explores themes of identity, community, and the power of friendship and loyalty.

Throughout the series, Cooper draws heavily on mythology and folklore, incorporating elements of Celtic and Arthurian legend into her narrative. This adds depth and richness to the story, making it feel both timeless and timely. The series also has a strong sense of place, with the English countryside serving as a character in its own right.

The characters in the series are well-developed and complex, with Will Stanton being a particularly compelling protagonist. His journey from ordinary boy to heroic Old One is both inspiring and authentic, making it easy to become fully invested in his story. The supporting characters are equally well-drawn, with a range of memorable and intriguing figures who add depth and complexity to the narrative.

Overall, "The Dark Is Rising Sequence" is a must-read for anyone who loves fantasy, adventure, and mythology. Cooper's writing is enchanting, her characters are memorable, and her story is both timeless and timely. This series is a true classic of children's literature, and its themes and characters continue to captivate readers of all ages.

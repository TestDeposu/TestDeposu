# All the Little Secrets (All the Little Houses, 2)

**Author:** May Cobb  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-01-20 APL

Get Ready for May Cobb’s Most Explosive Thriller Yet: Inside *All the Little Secrets*

If the mere mention of May Cobb’s name immediately conjures up images of scandal, sun-drenched Texas heat, and deliciously bad behavior, you are certainly not alone. Following the massive success of *The Hunting Wives*, Cobb has cemented herself as a master of high-stakes, addictive suspense. Now, she is returning to the well of domestic dread with her upcoming release, *All the Little Secrets*—the second installment in her *All the Little Houses* series, slated to hit shelves on January 20, 2026.

Early buzz suggests this might just be her wildest ride yet. Bestselling thriller author Jeneva Rose boldly calls it "May Cobb’s most explosive book yet. And trust me, that’s saying a lot," while fellow suspense icon Lisa Jewell notes, "Nobody does explosive and twisted like May Cobb does it."

So, what awaits readers when they step back into Cobb's deliciously wicked world? 

Transporting us to the mid-1980s in the tiny town of Longview, Texas, the story introduces us to Nellie Anderson, the stunning daughter of the Anderson family dynasty who always gets what she wants. And for the rare things she can't secure on her own? That is what her mother is for. 

Enter Charleigh Andersen: blond, beautiful, and ruthlessly cunning. Charleigh remembers all too well what it was like to claw her way to the top after growing up on the poor side of East Texas as an outcast, humiliated and shunned by the very in-crowd she desperately wanted to impress. But Charleigh’s carefully guarded, self-made social empire faces an imminent threat when a new family arrives in town. Radiating "trad wife," woodworking dad, and wholesome daughter vibes, these newcomers might just upend everything. 

As the synopsis teases: Who will be left standing when the dust settles?

Packed with mean girls, mean moms, and the dark secrets hidden inside all the little houses, this is shaping up to be an absolute must-read for fans of psychological suspense and soapy, high-drama thrillers. Mark your calendars for January 20, 2026, and prepare for a brilliant deep-dive into the dark underbelly of 1980s Texas.

**What to Expect: Observer by Nicholas Russell**

Nicholas Russell steps onto the literary scene with *Observer*, a debut horror novel that promises to linger in the reader’s mind long after the final page. Set against the stark, unforgiving backdrop of an abandoned Nevada observatory, the story follows Renata Scarborough, a young woman who returns home to discover her estranged mother Corinne’s belongings left on her doorstep. Skeptical of the quick conclusions drawn by her aunt and cousin—that Corinne is simply gone—Renata dives into her mother’s journals, uncovering a tangled thread of scientific work, personal obsession, and a chilling connection to an early‑twentieth‑century surveyor whose encounters with an inexplicable entity echo Corinne’s own reports.

Driven by a need to understand what truly happened, Renata journeys to the now‑desolate McNairy Observatory. There, she finds a community scarred by decades of tension between scientific ambition and the harsh desert landscape, locals who refuse to speak of the observatory’s closure, and a growing sense that something unseen watches from the darkness. As she climbs the peak overlooking the town, Renata experiences phenomena that defy explanation, feeling a creeping dread that the truth she seeks may not be a reunion with her mother at all, but an encounter with something far more alien.

The novel’s atmosphere is described as haunting, darkly comic, and captivating—an exploration of cosmic terror that asks what happens when we stare into the vast desert and find something inhuman staring back. Fans of Jeff VanderMeer’s unsettling ecological weirdness and Mariana Enriquez’s sharp, unsettling storytelling will find familiar ground here, while Russell’s fresh voice promises to carve out its own niche in contemporary horror.

*Observer* is set for release in 2026, and while details like page count remain under wraps, the early buzz suggests a promising new voice poised to make a significant impact.

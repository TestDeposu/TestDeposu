# The Little Shop of Merry and Bright

**Author:** Jenny Bayliss  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-09-22 APL

**What to Expect: *The Little Shop of Merry and Bright* by Jenny Bayliss**

Set for release on September 22, 2026, Jenny Bayliss’s forthcoming novel promises a heart‑warming dive into the magic of second chances—both literal and figurative. The story centers on Casey, a woman whose life took an unexpected turn three years ago when she turned down a marriage proposal, only to have the moment explode across the internet. Since then, she has retreated into a safe, predictable routine as a stock replenisher at a family‑owned department store, deliberately avoiding the spotlight and any risk of further embarrassment.

Her quiet existence is disrupted when she encounters Margo, the Spirit of Christmas Present, who has decided to sit this holiday out. Margo offers Casey a tantalizing bargain: she will use her supernatural powers to erase the viral video that made Casey infamous. The catch? Casey must first witness five alternate realities—different versions of her life shaped by other choices. Only after experiencing these glimpses will she decide whether erasing the video is truly what she wants, or whether the happiness she seeks might already be woven into the life she’s living.

Bayliss’s synopsis hints at a blend of whimsical holiday spirit and thoughtful introspection. Readers can expect the author’s signature warmth and wit, balanced with a genuine exploration of regret, self‑acceptance, and the quiet courage it takes to embrace the present. The premise invites readers to ponder their own “what ifs” while rooting for Casey to find peace in her own story.

For fans of Bayliss’s previous feel‑good, character‑driven tales, this novel feels like a natural extension of her talent for turning everyday settings into places of wonder. New readers will be drawn in by the festive premise and the promise of a romance that feels both magical and deeply human.

As the publication date approaches, anticipation builds for a story that reminds us that sometimes the greatest gifts aren’t found in grand gestures, but in the simple, steady glow of a life lived authentically.

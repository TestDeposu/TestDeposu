# Hexy Beast (The Sherwood Witches #3)

**Author:** Avery Flynn  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-09-08

Get ready for a wild ride into supernatural romance, because *USA Today* bestselling author Avery Flynn is returning with *Hexy Beast*, the third installment in *The Sherwood Witches* series, slated for release on September 8, 2026. 

If you are a fan of Flynn’s signature sharp wit, sizzling chemistry, and laugh-out-loud romantic comedies, this upcoming paranormal rom-com is already shaping up to be an absolute must-read. Known for crafting unforgettable characters and high-stakes romantic tension, Flynn is once again diving headfirst into a world where magic and monstrous attraction collide.

What can readers expect when *Hexy Beast* hits shelves? According to the official synopsis, the story centers on Effie Sherwood, the eldest daughter of the magical Sherwood family. While she is supposedly the one who is supposed to have everything together, Effie is actually chaos incarnate—a reality proven when she makes the impulsive decision to jump on the back of a motorcycle belonging to Darius Aetos. 

Grumpy, gruff, and undeniably hot, Darius is not just any biker; he is the head of the Aetos werewolf pack. What starts as a spur-of-the-moment ride quickly spirals into a much bigger commitment when a pack elder declares that the brooding alpha is Effie's fated mate. 

Witches and werewolves don't exactly have a history of smooth relations, and now Effie and Darius find themselves stuck together while navigating tricky pack politics. But as the blue moon rises and starlit skies set the mood, the line between pack duty and undeniable desire begins to blur. Underneath the grumpy exterior, it seems Darius doesn't want to stay away—and Effie isn't sure she wants to leave his arms either. 

Whether you have followed the Sherwood family from the very beginning or you are looking for your next favorite grumpy-sunshine, fated-mates romance, mark your calendars for September 8, 2026. *Hexy Beast* promises plenty of laughs, heat, and supernatural sparks.

What to Expect: Tangled Roots, the Highly Anticipated Sequel by S.T. Gibson

In 2024, the publishing world buzzed with excitement as the first installment, Savage Blooms, catapulted author S.T. Gibson into the spotlight. This lush, sin-stained tale captivated readers, and now, a sequel promises to deliver even more magic, mayhem, and romance. Tangled Roots, slated for release in 2027, promises to take readers on a journey deeper into the world of faeries and humans, where the lines between pleasure and pain blur.

Savage Blooms proved Gibson's writing prowess and her keen ability to weave complex, intricate narratives. If you fell in love with the world she created, you'll be thrilled to know that Tangled Roots delves deeper into the story's core. The stakes are higher, the drama is more intense, and the forbidden desires are still as alluring as ever.

In this gripping sequel, we find Eileen, Finley, and Nicola navigating a treacherous web of fae politics, their primary goal to rescue kidnapped Adam from the faery underworld. As they navigate this new world, one rule stands out: entertain the enigmatic and alluring king of the faeries, or suffer the dire consequences of his boredom. This simple, yet perilous proposition sets the stage for a story filled with intrigue, danger, and the thrill of the unknown.

The promise of Tangled Roots is palpable. Gibson masterfully weaves mythology and fantasy with the complexities of human relationships, crafting a narrative that will leave readers on the edge of their seats. With its lush, immersive world and the tantalizing prospect of forbidden love, Tangled Roots is poised to captivate fans of the original and newcomers alike.

As anticipation builds for this highly anticipated release, we can't wait to immerse ourselves in the world of faeries and the intriguing characters that inhabit it. Will Eileen and her lovers succeed in their perilous quest, or will they succumb to the allure of the fae king's promises? Only time will tell.

S.T. Gibson is a rapidly emerging force in the world of fantasy literature, and Tangled Roots cements her position as one of the most exciting new voices in the genre. If you're looking forward to more immersive, enchanting stories, you won't want to miss this thrilling sequel.

S.T. Gibson's sophomore effort promises to exceed the success of its predecessor, Tangled Roots, a thrilling, immersive tale that will captivate fans of fantasy, romance, and mythology alike.

The wait is almost over for fans of gothic romantasy as acclaimed author Rachel Gillig prepares to return to the magical world of The Stonewater Kingdom with her highly anticipated sequel, **The Knave and the Moon**.

Rachel Gillig, known for weaving intricate tales that blend the darkness of gothic elements with the romance of fantasy, has already proven her prowess in the realm of romantic fiction. Her debut novel, **The Knight and the Moth**, shot to the top of the New York Times bestseller list, showcasing her exceptional storytelling ability and captivating characters. As we prepare to delve into the next chapter of the Stonewater Kingdom series, the stage is set for a gripping narrative filled with the remnants of a kingdom in shambles. Aisling Cathedral lies in ruins, key characters are thought to be deceased, and the once-unified kingdom teeters on the brink of collapse. Amidst this chaos, a mysterious figure emerges – a man with no name and no memory who claims to be the key to Sybil Delling's quest for revenge against the ruthless king, Benji.

In the world of The Knave and the Moon, nothing is as it seems. Beneath the surface, stories repeat themselves, echoing the darkness that lies within the very fabric of the kingdom's faith. It is here, in this unforgiving landscape of magic, politics, and desire, that Gillig will push the boundaries of her craft, creating a world that readers will find impossible to escape.

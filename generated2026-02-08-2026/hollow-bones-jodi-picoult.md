# Hollow Bones

**Author:** Jodi Picoult  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-09-15 APL

What to Expect: Hollow Bones by Jodi Picoult (Expected September 15, 2026)

The literary world is already buzzing about Jodi Picoult’s next novel, Hollow Bones, slated for release in mid‑September 2026. Though the exact page count remains under wraps, the publisher’s synopsis offers a tantalizing glimpse into a story that promises to blend Picoult’s trademark emotional depth with a gripping, contemporary edge.

From the #1 New York Times bestselling author of By Any Other Name comes a riveting novel about the risks we take to protect the ones we love in a world where crisis is always just around the corner. The narrative centers on Molly Fitzgerald, who was only two months old when her mother went to an appointment at the World Trade Center on September 11, 2001 and never returned. Raised by a devoted father and stepmother, Molly grew up carrying a quiet, persistent dread of disaster—a feeling that shaped her career path. Today she heads the Rhode Island Department for Emergency Preparedness, designing plans to safeguard lives during storms, epidemics, and airplane crashes. Her husband, Jesse, works as a police polygraph expert and brings his own history of crisis into their marriage. Together they have built a love that feels like a refuge in an unpredictable world—until an unexpected event shakes the foundations of everything they thought they knew.

Moving between past and present, Hollow Bones explores the lies we tell ourselves as we write the narratives of our lives, the meticulous plans we craft to shield ourselves from the worst moments, and the enduring ties that bind mothers and daughters. The synopsis hints at a dual timeline that will let readers see how Molly’s childhood trauma informs her adult instincts for survival, while also revealing how her professional expertise collides with personal vulnerabilities when an unforeseen crisis forces both her and Jesse to question the stories they have built around safety, trust, and identity.

Picoult’s reputation for weaving socially relevant themes into deeply human stories precedes her. Readers who have been moved by My Sister’s Keeper, The Storyteller, or Small Great Things will recognize her skill at turning personal drama into a lens for larger societal questions. In Hollow Bones, she appears to be turning that lens toward the modern preoccupation with preparedness—how we attempt to control the uncontrollable—and the emotional cost of living under the shadow of potential disaster.

Anticipation is high not only because of Picoult’s proven ability to top bestseller lists but also because the premise feels both timely and intimately personal. The novel’s focus on emergency preparedness resonates in an era marked by climate anxiety, public health scares, and global uncertainty, while its core mother‑daughter dynamic promises the intimate, heart‑wrenching moments that have become a hallmark of her work. Fans can expect a narrative that balances pulse‑pounding tension with the quiet, reflective moments that allow characters—and readers—to confront what it truly means to love and protect in a fragile world.

As the release date approaches, readers are encouraged to keep an eye out for cover reveals, early excerpts, and author interviews that will shed further light on the novel’s tone and style. Until then, the promise of Hollow Bones stands as a compelling invitation to revisit the questions that have long driven Picoult’s storytelling: How do we survive when the world feels unsafe? And what stories do we tell ourselves to make that survival meaningful?

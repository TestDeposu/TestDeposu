# The Thoroughbreds (The Academy #2)

**Author:** Elin Hilderbrand  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-09-15 APL

**The Thoroughbreds (The Academy #2) by Elin Hilderbrand & Shelby Cunningham: A Senior Year of Secrets, Scandals, and Last Hurrahs**

The New York Times bestselling duo of *The Academy*—mother and daughter writing team Elin Hilderbrand and Shelby Cunningham—are back with *The Thoroughbreds*, the much-anticipated sequel to their razor-sharp, drama-packed boarding school saga. Set against the glittering yet cutthroat backdrop of Tiffin Academy, this second installment promises to deliver the same high-stakes tension, sharp social maneuvering, and emotional depth that made the first book a standout. If you loved the razor-wire precision of *The Academy*, brace yourself: *The Thoroughbreds* is shaping up to be even more explosive.

September at Tiffin isn’t just the start of a new school year—it’s the final lap of senior year, a season where every decision, every secret, and every betrayal could redefine lives. The synopsis teases a world where the elite of the elite—“sixth formers,” as they’re called—are locked in a high-pressure gauntlet of college applications, lavish parties, and the kind of friendships that can either lift you up or tear you down. But beneath the polished surface of Tiffin’s gilded halls, cracks are forming. And someone—or something—is about to shatter them.

The questions alone are enough to keep readers hooked. Is the school’s golden couple, Charley and East, on the brink of collapse? What’s really behind the rift between football star Dub and his once-unshakable best friend, Hakeem? Will Pippa Sacks, the effortlessly chic day student, disrupt the delicate power structures—or plunge them into chaos? And what was in that damning email attachment that Cinnamon Peters sent to Dub before her death? The stakes couldn’t be higher, especially when Head of School Audre Robinson’s job—and the future of the academy itself—hangs in the balance after a financial misstep.

Hilderbrand and Cunningham have a knack for weaving together the intricacies of adolescent (and adult) ambition, the cutthroat nature of elite social circles, and the messy, often painful process of growing up. Their first book, *The Academy*, was a revelation—a sharp, witty, and deeply human exploration of boarding school dynamics that resonated with readers who crave stories where no one is truly innocent, and every character has layers. *The Thoroughbreds* promises to build on that foundation, diving deeper into the consequences of past actions, the weight of secrets, and the ways in which power—and power struggles—operate in spaces designed to mold the next generation of leaders.

What makes this collaboration so compelling is the way Hilderbrand’s seasoned storytelling meets Cunningham’s fresh, contemporary voice. Together, they’ve crafted a world that feels both timeless and urgently relevant, where the pressure to succeed is as suffocating as it is intoxicating. If you’re a fan of books that balance sharp dialogue with emotional depth—think *The Secret History* meets *The Secret Circle*—this is your next obsession.

*The Thoroughbreds* arrives on September 15, 2026, and with it, the promise of another unforgettable season at Tiffin Academy. Whether you’re revisiting the drama of the first book or diving in for the first time, one thing is certain: this is a story where nothing is as it seems, and every character has a reason to lie.

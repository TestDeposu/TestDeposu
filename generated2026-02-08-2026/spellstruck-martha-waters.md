**Spellstruck by Martha Waters: A Whimsical, Magical Love Story Brewing for 2026**

Martha Waters, the acclaimed author of *The House in the Cerulean Sea* and *The Invisible Life of Addie LaRue*, is set to enchant readers once again with *Spellstruck*, a dazzling historical romance that blends prohibition-era intrigue with a dash of magic. Expected to hit shelves on September 29, 2026, this new novel promises to be a sparkling, enemies-to-lovers adventure—perfect for fans of witty banter, forbidden romance, and a touch of the fantastical.

### **A Speakeasy of Spells and Secrets**
In an alternate 1920s London where magic still lingers in the shadows, Vesper Holloway runs a thriving speakeasy where patrons sip enchanted cocktails and indulge in the last remnants of a forbidden world. But when a series of dangerous accidents plague these hidden establishments, Vesper fears her family’s legacy—and her livelihood—could be lost. Enter Maxwell Ashbridge, a former ally of Vesper’s brother who now enforces the government’s ban on magic. Though he harbors a grudge against the magical elite, he finds himself inexplicably drawn to Vesper, whose sharp wit and defiance make her impossible to resist.

Their uneasy alliance turns into a fiery romance as they race to uncover the truth behind the escalating disasters—all while navigating a political climate where the fight for magic’s repeal grows more urgent. With every sip of a spell-infused drink and every heated exchange, Vesper and Max must decide: Is their connection worth fighting for, even if it means defying the very laws that keep them apart?

### **Why This Book Will Captivate You**
Waters has a knack for crafting stories that feel both nostalgic and fresh, and *Spellstruck* is no exception. If you adored the lush world-building and tender romance of *The House in the Cerulean Sea* or the lyrical, bittersweet charm of *The Invisible Life of Addie LaRue*, this book will deliver the same magic—just with a dash of gin, a sprinkle of rebellion, and a whole lot of chemistry.

For fans of historical romances with a supernatural twist, this is a must-read. Think *The Night Circus* meets *The Roaring Twenties*, with a love story that’s as intoxicating as the drinks on offer.

### **The Hype Is Already Brewing**
Early buzz from Emily Krempholtz, *USA TODAY* bestselling author, calls *Spellstruck* a “whimsical, fantastical romp of a historical romance,” and it’s easy to see why. Waters’ signature blend of humor, heart, and magical realism ensures this won’t just be another book—it’ll be an experience.

Mark your calendars for September 29, 2026, and prepare to be spellbound.

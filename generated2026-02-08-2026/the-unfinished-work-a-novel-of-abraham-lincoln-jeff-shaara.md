# The Unfinished Work: A Novel of Abraham Lincoln

**Author:** Jeff Shaara  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-11-10

Literary Scene Exclusive: Stepping into the Oval Office with Jeff Shaara's Upcoming Masterpiece, The Unfinished Work

For decades, New York Times bestselling novelist Jeff Shaara has been the gold standard of historical fiction, transporting readers directly onto the blood-soaked battlefields, into the smoke-filled war rooms, and alongside the monumental figures who shaped the American narrative. Now, in what is heralded as a capstone to a legendary life's work, Shaara is turning his formidable talents toward the most iconic and consequential figure in our nation's history: Abraham Lincoln. 

Set for publication on November 10, 2026, The Unfinished Work: A Novel of Abraham Lincoln promises to be a crowning achievement for an author who has spent a lifetime defining the genre. 

While exact page counts remain under wraps, the official synopsis sets a grand and intimate stage. Taking its thematic inspiration from the immortal words of the Gettysburg Address—"It is for us the living, rather, to be dedicated here to the unfinished work which they who fought here have thus far so nobly advanced"—the novel sweeps readers from Lincoln’s fateful election in 1860 straight through the crucible of the Oval Office during the American Civil War.

Rather than presenting a marble monument, Shaara aims to bring to vivid life the flesh-and-blood man, capturing both the public leader and the private soul. According to the publishers, the narrative will dive deep into the travails, conflicts, victories, and tragic defeats that defined the sixteenth president. Readers can expect an unflinching look at the immense personal cost borne by a leader who refused to let deep divisions and conflicting emotions permanently sunder the United States. 

If you have spent years captivated by Shaara’s meticulous historical deep-dives and his uncanny ability to make history pulse with immediacy and human emotion, this upcoming release is shaping up to be an absolute must-read. It is a rare literary event when a master craftsman tackles a titan of history, and November 2026 cannot come soon enough. 

Mark your calendars, reserve your copies early, and get ready to witness history brought to life all over again.

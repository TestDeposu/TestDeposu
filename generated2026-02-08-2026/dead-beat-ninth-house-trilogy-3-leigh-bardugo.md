# Dead Beat (Ninth House Trilogy, #3)

**Author:** Leigh Bardugo  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** Expected publication 2026-09-15 APL

**Gates of Hell, Open Wide: What to Expect from Leigh Bardugo’s *Dead Beat***

For fans of dark academia and masterclass fantasy, the wait is almost over. Leigh Bardugo, the #1 New York Times bestselling author whose intricate world-building has captivated millions across the globe, is set to return with *Dead Beat*. Slated for publication on September 15, 2026, this upcoming title marks the explosive, highly anticipated conclusion to the acclaimed *Ninth House* trilogy. 

If you loved the razor-sharp grit of *Ninth House* and the breathless pacing of *Hell Bent*, Bardugo’s latest installment promises to push her characters to their absolute breaking point. 

While the exact page count remains under wraps, the official synopsis sets the stage for a high-stakes, apocalyptic confrontation in New Haven. As the tagline warns: *Hell is empty and all the devils are here.*

According to the official publisher synopsis, Alex Stern made it to hell and back more than once, but this time she left the door open behind her. Demons are creeping through the streets of New Haven and the Yale campus, feeding on the souls of the living and terrifying the dead. While Alex and her allies can hunt them, it is only a matter of time before the gates to the underworld open entirely, leading to a war they cannot win. 

To stop the impending catastrophe, Alex and her team will have to dig deep into the darkest secrets of Yale and its elite societies, all while uncovering the terrifying truth hidden within Alex’s own blood. Complicating matters further, Alex must contend with a corrupt Lethe Board and a deadly vampire who shares his own mysterious connection to the societies. 

Yet, there is a glimmer of hope amidst the chaos. For the first time in her life, Alex won't have to face the fight alone. Dawes, Turner, Mercy, and Tripp are all stepping up to play their parts. Most crucially, Alex’s mentor—the golden boy Darlington, who spent so long trapped in her head—is finally back in the flesh, though now burdened with dangerous and unpredictable powers of his own. With Hell itself stalking the delegates of Lethe House, the characters are about to learn a brutal lesson: demons don’t play by human rules.

As September 2026 approaches, the literary world is watching closely to see how Bardugo will tie together the threads of her dark, visceral universe. Whether you've followed Alex Stern's journey from the very beginning or are looking forward to this monumental trilogy-ender, *Dead Beat* is shaping up to be one of the most talked-about fantasy releases of the year. 

Mark your calendars for September 15, 2026, and prepare yourself to step back onto the Yale campus—because once the door to hell is left open, closing it is going to cost everything.

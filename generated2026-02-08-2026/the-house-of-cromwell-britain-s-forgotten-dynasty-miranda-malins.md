**Author:** Miranda Malins  
**Page Count:** Unknown

**Uncovering the Dynasty: Miranda Malins Returns with The House of Cromwell**

For history enthusiasts and lovers of British political drama, 2026 brings one of the most anticipated non-fiction releases of the year. Historian Miranda Malins is set to turn our understanding of British history on its head with *The House of Cromwell: Britain's Forgotten Dynasty*. 

If you have followed Malins’ previous work in historical fiction and non-fiction, you already know her sharp eye for detail, deep scholarship, and ability to breathe vibrant life into the past. Her upcoming release promises to be her most ambitious undertaking yet, shifting the lens from single historical figures to an entire bloodline that shaped the modern world.

For the first time, Malins chronicles the comprehensive story of the Cromwells—one of the most influential families in British history. At the center of this narrative stand two powerful, controversial men: Thomas and Oliver Cromwell. Starting as commoners and ending with nothing, these two figures permanently altered the face of Britain. 

As Malins traces an extraordinary two-century journey from the Wars of the Roses to the accession of the Hanoverians, readers are promised a front-row seat to explosive family dynamics, high-stakes power struggles, and dynastic shifts. Crucially, *The House of Cromwell* also seeks to rescue overlooked voices from the archives—particularly the Cromwell women—whose actions quietly dictated the family’s ultimate fate. 

Family mattered above all else to Thomas and Oliver, and Malins argues that without understanding the entire dynasty, we can never truly grasp their massive impact on Britain and the world. 

Mark your reading lists for 2026. *The House of Cromwell* is shaping up to be a definitive, disruptive, and essential exploration of the family that helped architect the early modern world.

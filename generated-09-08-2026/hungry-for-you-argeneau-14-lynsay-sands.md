# Hungry for You (Argeneau, #14)

**Author:** Lynsay Sands  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2010

**Hungry for You (Argeneau #14) by Lynsay Sands: A Feast for the Senses**

Lynsay Sands’ *Hungry for You* is a delectable blend of romance, wit, and the timeless allure of the vampire—where the stakes are as high as the cravings. At its heart, this installment in the *Argeneau* series is a story about longing, both physical and emotional, wrapped in the kind of banter and chemistry that makes the pages fly by. Sands, a master of paranormal romance, crafts a narrative where the supernatural meets the mundane in the most delightfully unexpected ways.

The premise is simple yet irresistible: Cale Valens, an ancient vampire who has long since given up on the idea of a life mate, finds himself at the center of a family conspiracy. His clan, ever the matchmakers, insists he’s found his perfect match—though they’ve pulled a fast one by sending a mortal woman, Alexandra Willan, into his path. Alex, a determined restaurateur on the brink of opening her dream kitchen, is blindsided when the "perfect chef" she hires turns out to be… well, not quite what she expected. Cale, who hasn’t eaten "real" food in two thousand years, is determined to prove himself—not just in the kitchen, but in every other way that matters.

What makes *Hungry for You* so compelling is Sands’ ability to balance humor, tension, and heart. Cale is a vampire with a dry, self-deprecating wit, his centuries of existence making him both weary and intrigued by the chaos of mortal life. His interactions with Alex, a fiery, no-nonsense woman with her own set of challenges, spark a chemistry that’s equal parts playful and intense. Their dynamic is a masterclass in fated romance—where the pull between them isn’t just about desire, but about the rare, electric connection that feels like destiny.

The writing is sharp and engaging, with Sands’ signature blend of sensuality and humor keeping the story from veering into cliché. The vampire lore is woven seamlessly into the narrative, never overwhelming but always present, adding depth to Cale’s character and the world he inhabits. The tension between him and Alex is palpable, whether it’s the slow burn of attraction or the underlying stakes of their supernatural bond. And while the premise might sound like a classic "vampire meets mortal" trope, Sands infuses it with fresh energy, making it feel both familiar and entirely new.

One of the standout elements of *Hungry for You* is its focus on the human side of the story. Alex isn’t just a damsel in distress; she’s a woman navigating her own dreams, fears, and insecurities. Her restaurant opening looms large over the plot, adding a layer of real-world stakes that grounds the supernatural elements. Meanwhile, Cale’s journey—his struggle with loneliness, his reluctant acceptance of his clan’s meddling, and his growing desire for something more than just survival—makes him a deeply relatable protagonist.

The romance itself is lush and satisfying, with Sands’ signature blend of slow-burn tension and explosive passion. There’s a delicious sense of inevitability in their connection, but also a healthy dose of humor and banter that keeps the story from feeling too heavy. The chemistry between Cale and Alex is electric, and their interactions—whether in the kitchen, in bed, or just talking—are filled with a warmth that makes the reader root for them from the start.

For fans of paranormal romance, *Hungry for You* is a must-read. It’s a story that celebrates the idea of finding something—or someone—worth waiting for, even when the odds seem stacked against you. Sands’ writing is as rich and satisfying as the best meal, leaving the reader hungry for more long after the last page.

If you’ve ever loved a vampire with a heart of gold, a mortal woman who refuses to be tamed, or a romance that’s equal parts funny and fiery, then *Hungry for You* is the perfect bite-sized indulgence.

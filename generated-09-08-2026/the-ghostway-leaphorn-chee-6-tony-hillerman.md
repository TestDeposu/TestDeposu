# The Ghostway (Leaphorn & Chee, #6)

**Author:** Tony Hillerman  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

The Ghostway, the sixth installment in Tony Hillerman's Leaphorn and Chee series, is an electrifying thriller that delves into the dark heart of murder, revenge, and the secrets that bind the Navajo community. Set against the backdrop of the Big Reservation, this classic Southwestern mystery follows Tribal Policeman Jim Chee as he embarks on a perilous odyssey to unravel the threads of a gruesome crime.

The story begins with a dramatic and bloody scene at the Shiprock Wash-O-Mat, where two strangers engage in a violent confrontation, leaving one dead and the other fleeing into the vast expanse of the reservation. The only clue is a photograph shown to Old Joseph Joe, a wise and aged Navajo who becomes an unwitting witness to the crime. This seemingly innocuous event sets Chee off on a complex and treacherous journey that takes him from the reservation's dusty roads to the seedy underbelly of Los Angeles.

Throughout the book, Hillerman masterfully weaves together elements of Navajo culture, folklore, and spirituality, creating a rich and immersive atmosphere that draws the reader into the world of the Big Reservation. His writing is evocative and concise, conjuring vivid images of the Southwestern landscape and the people who inhabit it. The characters are multidimensional and complex, with Chee being a standout protagonist – his quiet introspection and deep understanding of Navajo traditions make him an endearing and relatable hero.

One of the most striking aspects of The Ghostway is its exploration of the tension between tradition and modernity. Hillerman skillfully portrays the struggles of the Navajo people to maintain their cultural identity in the face of encroaching Western influences. This theme is woven throughout the narrative, as Chee navigates the complexities of Navajo spirituality and the ancient healing ceremonies that hold the key to unlocking the secrets of the crime.

Hillerman's writing style is a perfect blend of mystery, suspense, and cultural insight. His prose is spare and elegant, with a keen eye for detail that brings the characters and setting to life. The pacing is well-balanced, with a steady stream of twists and turns that keep the reader guessing until the very end.

In short, The Ghostway is a gripping and thought-provoking thriller that showcases Hillerman's mastery of the mystery genre. With its rich cultural context, complex characters, and intricate plotting, this book is a must-read for fans of Southwestern mysteries and anyone interested in exploring the complexities of Navajo culture.

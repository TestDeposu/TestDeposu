# Walt Disney's The Sorcerer's Apprentice (A Little Golden Book)

**Author:** Don Ferguson  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

**Unleashing Magic and Mayhem: A Delightful Little Golden Book**

In the enchanting world of Disney classics, few tales are as captivating as "The Sorcerer's Apprentice." Originally featured in the iconic 1940 film Fantasia, this timeless story has been reimagined for the youngest of readers in Don Ferguson's charming adaptation, Walt Disney's The Sorcerer's Apprentice (A Little Golden Book). First published in 1995 and now relaunched in 2018, this delightful tome is a must-have for children and collectors alike.

Mickey Mouse, the beloved hero of this adventure, dons a sorcerer's hat and taps into the magical world of fantasy. With a flick of his wand, he conjures an army of walking brooms to aid in his household chores. As Mickey learns, with great power comes great responsibility, and soon the sorcerer's apprentice finds himself in a predicament of his own making. The once-manageable brooms have become a runaway gang, wreaking havoc and threatening to inundate the world in water.

Ferguson's adaptation masterfully captures the essence of the original film, distilling its essence into a captivating narrative that will have young readers on the edge of their seats. The author's storytelling is infused with a sense of wonder and excitement, perfectly suited for the Little Golden Book format. Each page turns into a thrilling new discovery, as Mickey Mouse navigates the world of magic and mayhem.

One of the standout features of this book is its accessibility. Ferguson's language is clear and concise, making it an ideal read-aloud experience for parents, caregivers, or early readers. The text is accompanied by engaging illustrations that bring the story to life, immersing readers in the fantastical world of Disney magic. Children aged 2 to 5 will be enthralled by the colorful artwork and Mickey Mouse's endearing antics, while collectors will appreciate the nostalgic value of this Disney classic.

Don Ferguson's writing style is a perfect complement to the whimsical tale of "The Sorcerer's Apprentice." He seamlessly integrates the magical and mundane, creating a narrative that is both entertaining and educational. Ferguson's use of language is playful and imaginative, mirroring the sense of wonder that defines this classic tale. His ability to balance action, suspense, and humor makes for an engaging read that will keep young minds engaged and excited.

In conclusion, Walt Disney's The Sorcerer's Apprentice (A Little Golden Book) is a captivating retelling of a beloved Disney classic. Don Ferguson's adaptation skillfully distills the essence of the original story, bringing it to life for a new generation of readers. With its engaging narrative, charming illustrations, and accessible language, this book is an absolute delight for children and collectors alike. If you're looking for a charming tale to share with the little ones or a nostalgic treat for yourself, this Little Golden Book is an absolute must-have.

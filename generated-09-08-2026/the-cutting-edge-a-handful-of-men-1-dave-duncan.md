# The Cutting Edge (A Handful of Men, #1)

**Author:** Dave Duncan  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2014

In "The Cutting Edge," the first installment of the Handful of Men series, Dave Duncan masterfully weaves a complex tale of magic, politics, and self-discovery, set against the richly detailed backdrop of the realm of Pandemia. This captivating novel marks a triumphant return to the world Duncan first introduced in his Man of His Word series, and it is clear that his unique brand of storytelling has lost none of its luster.

At the heart of the narrative lies the enigmatic figure of Rap, a former stable boy turned secret sorcerer and king of Krasnegar. Alongside his queen, Inos, Rap has ruled his kingdom with wisdom and justice for fifteen years, but the tranquility of their reign is short-lived. As ominous prophecies begin to circulate, foretelling unmitigated disasters across Pandemia, Rap is faced with the daunting task of navigating the treacherous landscape of his world.

Duncan's prose is characterized by its directness and clarity, making the intricate world-building and complex magical systems feel refreshingly accessible. The author's use of language is economical, yet evocative, conjuring vivid images of the fantastical realm and its inhabitants. The narrative is deftly paced, moving seamlessly between moments of introspection and action-packed sequences that propel the story forward.

One of the most striking aspects of "The Cutting Edge" is its exploration of the human condition. Rap, as a character, is multifaceted and relatable, his struggles with his own identity and purpose serving as a powerful catalyst for the plot. Through his journey, Duncan raises important questions about the nature of power, responsibility, and the consequences of one's actions.

The supporting cast is equally well-developed, with characters like Prince Emshandar (Shandie) and the imperor adding depth and complexity to the narrative. The world of Pandemia itself is a character, with its rich history and politics woven seamlessly into the fabric of the story.

Throughout the novel, Duncan's love for the fantasy genre shines through, and his passion for storytelling is evident on every page. The result is a captivating tale that will appeal to fans of epic fantasy and adventure alike.

In short, "The Cutting Edge" is a masterful blend of magic, politics, and self-discovery, set against the richly detailed backdrop of the realm of Pandemia. With its engaging characters, intricate world-building, and thought-provoking themes, this novel is a must-read for anyone looking to immerse themselves in a compelling and immersive fantasy world.

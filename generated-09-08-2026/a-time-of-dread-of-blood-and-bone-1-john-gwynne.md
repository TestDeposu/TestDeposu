# A Time of Dread (Of Blood and Bone, #1)

**Author:** John Gwynne  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

In the realm of epic fantasy, few authors can wield the depth and complexity of world-building that John Gwynne has masterfully demonstrated in his previous works. His new series, Of Blood and Bone, begins with A Time of Dread, a gripping narrative that plunges readers into a land of ancient mythology, demonology, and high-stakes adventure. With this latest offering, Gwynne cements his place among the pantheon of modern fantasy's greatest masters.

Gwynne's narrative is woven with the threads of disparate storylines, each one expertly interwoven to form a rich tapestry that reflects the darker aspects of human nature. Against the backdrop of a world ruled by a warrior angelic order known as the Ben-Elim, three protagonists emerge as beacons of light in a world teetering on the precipice of chaos. Riv, a fiery and determined warrior from the south, is driven by a desire to prove herself in the ranks of the Ben-Elim's elite force. In the west, Sig, a powerful giantess, stalks the shadows in search of a growing demon presence, while in the snowbound north, Drem, a resourceful trapper, is drawn into the heart of darkness by gruesome discoveries in the forest.

Throughout A Time of Dread, Gwynne's world-building skills are on full display as he crafts a narrative that effortlessly balances high-octane action with moments of quiet introspection. His writing is characterized by a palpable sense of menace and an atmosphere of creeping dread that permeates the very fabric of the world. Each chapter is expertly paced, hurtling forward with a relentless energy that draws the reader inexorably into the heart of the story.

One of the most striking aspects of A Time of Dread is its willingness to explore the darker corners of human nature. Gwynne's world is a bleak and unforgiving one, where survival often depends on a fragile balance of power and the willingness to make difficult choices. Riv, Sig, and Drem are all forced to confront their own mortality as they face increasingly dire circumstances, and their struggles serve as a powerful reminder of the complexities and contradictions that underlie the human condition.

In the tradition of authors such as George R. R. Martin and David Gemmell, A Time of Dread is a sprawling epic that defies easy categorization. It's a work of dark fantasy that incorporates elements of mythology and historical fiction, crafting a world that is both deeply personal and richly detailed. Gwynne's style is a masterful blend of atmospheric tension and narrative drive, making this a must-read for fans of the genre.

Ultimately, A Time of Dread is a triumph of epic fantasy storytelling. John Gwynne's latest offering is a rich tapestry of characters, world-building, and action, woven together by a masterful narrative that will keep readers hooked from the opening page to the very end.

# Noah (Nightwalkers, #5)

**Author:** Jacquelyn Frank  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2024

**Noah: A Fiery Love Story That Burns Through Time and Destiny**

Jacquelyn Frank’s *Noah*—the fifth installment in the *Nightwalkers* series—is a masterclass in high-stakes romance, where ancient power, forbidden desire, and the relentless march of fate collide in a blaze of passion and peril. At its core, this book is a love story, but not the kind that fades with time. It’s a union forged in fire, one that defies the laws of nature itself, where an immortal Demon King and a human woman with hidden magic become the key to survival in a world teetering on the edge of annihilation.

Noah, the Demon King, has ruled for centuries with a steady hand, his fire-wielding power a shield against the chaos of the supernatural world. But for six months, he’s been tormented by dreams—vivid, searing, impossible to ignore. They’re of a woman: daring, lethal, and utterly untamed. When the full moon’s madness approaches, Noah’s obsession becomes desperate. He turns to a Druid matchmaker, only to discover the truth is far more explosive than he imagined. The woman in his dreams isn’t just any human—she’s Kestra Irons, a military-trained survivor with a secret: she’s part Druid, and she’s the one destiny has chosen to bind to him. The problem? She doesn’t even know she’s special. And she certainly doesn’t want to be Noah’s.

Kestra is a woman who thrives on danger, who has spent her life running from the past and fighting for survival. But when she crosses paths with Noah, she’s thrust into a world where time bends, where love is both a weapon and a curse, and where the line between protector and predator blurs into something far more dangerous. Their union isn’t just a spark—it’s a wildfire, one that could either save the Nightwalkers from the rogue Vampires hunting them or doom them all. The stakes couldn’t be higher, and the tension crackles between every word.

Frank’s writing is what makes *Noah* so compelling. Her prose is lush and immersive, painting a world where magic and military precision collide in a dance of power and passion. She doesn’t shy away from the gritty, the sensual, or the brutal—her characters are flawed, their desires raw, and their conflicts visceral. The chemistry between Noah and Kestra is electric, a slow burn that turns into an inferno, with Frank weaving in just the right amount of suspense to keep readers hooked. The romance isn’t just about steamy scenes; it’s about the struggle of two souls who are meant to be together but are forced apart by fate, duty, and the very nature of their existence.

What sets *Noah* apart is its blend of political intrigue and personal stakes. The Nightwalkers series has always been about more than just romance—it’s about survival, about the cost of power, and about the sacrifices required to protect the ones you love. Here, the threat isn’t just external; it’s personal. The rogue Vampires aren’t just mindless monsters—they’re a calculated enemy, and their hunger for power could spell the end for every Nightwalker. Kestra and Noah’s relationship becomes the linchpin of their world’s fate, and Frank handles this balance with skill, ensuring that the emotional payoff is as satisfying as the action.

The series has built a reputation for its unforgettable characters, and *Noah* continues that tradition. Noah is a king who has known centuries of solitude, his heart hardened by the weight of his crown. Kestra, on the other hand, is a survivor with a sharp tongue and a sharper instinct for self-preservation. Their dynamic is one of the book’s greatest strengths—two people who are worlds apart but drawn together by forces beyond their control. The supporting cast, including the Druid matchmaker and the rogue Vampires, adds depth and complexity, ensuring that the world feels alive and breathing.

For fans of paranormal romance, *Noah* is a must-read. It’s a story that balances political intrigue with heart-pounding romance, where the love story is as much about destiny as it is about desire. Frank’s ability to craft a world that feels both familiar and entirely its own is a testament to her skill as a storyteller. The book doesn’t just deliver on the promise of steamy romance—it delivers on the promise of a story that lingers long after the last page.

If you’ve been waiting for the next installment in the *Nightwalkers* series, *Noah* is the fiery, unforgettable conclusion you’ve been craving. It’s a book that will leave you breathless, with a love story that burns through time and a world that feels as real as it does magical. Jacquelyn Frank has once again crafted a tale that is equal parts enchanting and electrifying, proving that some loves are worth any price.

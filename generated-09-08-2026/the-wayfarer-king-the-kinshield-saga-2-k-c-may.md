# The Wayfarer King (The Kinshield Saga, #2)

**Author:** K.C. May  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

The Wayfarer King, the second installment in K.C. May's Kinshield Saga, is a gripping and action-packed fantasy novel that delves into the complexities of power, loyalty, and the eternal struggle between good and evil. Published in 2011, this book picks up where the first installment left off, with Gavin Kinshield claiming the throne of Thendylath and determined to protect his people from the relentless beyonder attacks.

May's writing style is engaging and immersive, with a keen eye for world-building and character development. The world of the Kinshield Saga is richly detailed, with a complex mythology and politics that add depth and nuance to the story. The characters, too, are well-crafted and multi-dimensional, with Gavin Kinshield being a particularly compelling protagonist. His struggles to come to terms with his newfound power and his determination to do what is right, even in the face of overwhelming odds, make him a relatable and admirable hero.

One of the main themes of The Wayfarer King is the corrupting influence of power and the dangers of ambition. Brodas Ravenkind, the wizard who seeks to overthrow Gavin and claim the throne for himself, is a masterful portrayal of the destructive nature of unchecked ambition. His character serves as a foil to Gavin's, highlighting the differences between a leader who seeks power for its own sake, and one who seeks to use power for the greater good.

The plot of The Wayfarer King is fast-paced and suspenseful, with plenty of twists and turns to keep readers on the edge of their seats. May's use of action and adventure to drive the story forward is effective, and the world-building is so detailed that it's easy to become fully immersed in the world of the Kinshield Saga. The themes of loyalty, duty, and the struggle between good and evil are timeless and well-explored, adding depth and complexity to the story.

If there's one criticism to be made, it's that the pacing can be a bit uneven at times. Some sections of the book feel a bit rushed, while others drag on for a bit too long. However, this is a minor quibble, and overall, The Wayfarer King is a compelling and engaging read.

In short, The Wayfarer King is a gripping and immersive fantasy novel that explores the complexities of power, loyalty, and the eternal struggle between good and evil. With its richly detailed world-building, well-crafted characters, and suspenseful plot, this book is a must-read for fans of the fantasy genre.

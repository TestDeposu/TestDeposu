# Trial of Three (Power of Five, #3)

**Author:** Alex Lidell  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2018

Title: Trial of Three: Walking the Fireline of Destiny and Damnation

In the murky, supernatural-drenched bayous of Mississippi, the boundary between keeping the peace and unleashing armageddon is razor-thin. Alex Lidell delivers a breathless plunge into the supernatural underworld with Trial of Three, the third installment in the intensely addictive Power of Five series (alternately celebrated under Hailey Edwards' signature brand of gritty urban fantasy). This narrative burns with the kinetic energy of a wildfire, fusing small-town grit with high-stakes apocalyptic mythology.

At the pulsating heart of the story is Luce Boudreau, a small-town cop juggling mundane badge-and-gun duties while desperately suppressing a catastrophic secret: a world-ending demon coiled tightly within her soul. Lidell masterfully explores the central theme of duality and forced identity. Luce is a woman standing precariously on the fault line between two incompatible worlds. She is a law enforcement officer sworn to protect the innocent, yet she hosts the very destruction she fights to prevent. When a seemingly routine arson investigation uncovers a chilling conspiracy tethered directly to her own shrouded past, the illusion of separation shatters. Lidell uses this fiery catalyst to strip away Luce’s defenses, forcing her to confront the terrifying reality that ignoring her heritage is no longer an option. 

What elevates Trial of Three above standard paranormal procedurals is Lidell’s distinct authorial style. The prose is conversational, sharp, and dripping with an authentic Southern atmosphere that makes the Louisiana-adjacent heat palpable on every page. Readers are not merely observing Luce’s harrowing journey from a distance; Lidell crafts a narrative intimacy that feels like sitting on a porch with an old, battle-hardened friend sharing dark secrets over iced tea. The world-building is both refreshingly innovative and deeply grounded. Instead of relying on tired, predictable tropes, Lidell takes familiar mythological foundations and bends them, introducing unique species and subtle, unexpected magical mechanics that breathe new life into the urban fantasy genre.

Furthermore, the pacing is relentlessly heart-pounding. Lidell intertwines suspenseful intrigue, masterfully plotted mystery, and a slow-burn, magnetic romance that never overshadows the overarching war brewing in the shadows. The tension—both romantic and apocalyptic—is agonizingly well-earned. As lines are violently drawn in a conflict Luce barely comprehends, the emotional stakes skyrocket. Every choice carries a heavy cost, and the threat of collateral damage ensures that no character is ever truly safe.

Trial of Three is a triumphant masterclass in contemporary fantasy. It challenges its protagonist to walk through the fire of her own destiny, daring her to embrace her immense power before the people she loves are consumed by the flames. For fans of razor-sharp wit, high-octane action, and deeply human heroines grappling with monstrous inner demons, Lidell’s latest offering is an absolute must-read that hits every narrative mark.

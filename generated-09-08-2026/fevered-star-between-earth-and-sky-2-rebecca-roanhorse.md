# Fevered Star (Between Earth and Sky, #2)

**Author:** Rebecca Roanhorse  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2022

In Rebecca Roanhorse's electrifying sequel to the Hugo-winning novel "Black Sun", the world of the Between Earth and Sky series bursts into full bloom, as the boundaries between myth and reality, human and divine, begin to blur with catastrophic consequence. "Fevered Star" picks up where its predecessor left off, with the once-great city of Tova lay in ruin and the balance of power on the cusp of dramatic upheaval.

As the world careens into uncharted waters, Roanhorse masterfully weaves together the disparate threads of her characters' lives, each one grappling with the weight of their own destinies. Xiala, the sea captain with a fierce warrior's heart, finds herself at the forefront of the maelstrom, navigating the treacherous tides of change alongside a former Priest of Knives. Meanwhile, the intricate dance between Serapio and Naranpa reaches new heights of tension, as they wrestle with the implications of their avatar status and the constraints of fate.

Through the eyes of these complex, multidimensional characters, Roanhorse illuminates the fraught relationships between humans, nature, and the elusive, capricious gods of her world. Her prose is as luminous as the celestial bodies she writes about, and her imagination is boundless in its scope and ambition. One feels the very fabric of reality straining to accommodate the fantastical, awe-inspiring events that unfold with each passing chapter.

What truly sets "Fevered Star" apart, however, is Roanhorse's unflinching examination of the consequences of imperialism, colonialism, and the erasure of indigenous cultures. As the world of Tova struggles to assert its own identity amidst the ruins of a forgotten past, Roanhorse offers a nuanced, unapologetic portrayal of the long histories that have shaped this world's present. Her commitment to reclaiming and reframing the narratives of the marginalized is a testament to her power as a storyteller and a champion of social justice.

Roanhorse's prose is infused with the spirit of resistance and resilience, as the characters of the Meridian refuse to be silenced or erased. Through her masterful storytelling, she channels the raw power of myth and magic to illuminate the very real struggles of a world in flux. As a result, "Fevered Star" is more than just a sequel – it's a defiant affirmation of the world's capacity for rebirth and the human spirit's capacity for transformation.

Ultimately, "Fevered Star" is a book about the tides of the heart, about the unpredictable ebbs and flows of love, power, and fate. As Roanhorse deftly navigates the complexities of her world, we are reminded that even in the darkest moments, there lies a glimmer of hope – a spark that can ignite the stars themselves.

"Fevered Star" is a triumph, a shining example of the best that epic fantasy has to offer. If you haven't already, join the Between Earth and Sky series on this unforgettable journey through the realms of the unknown.

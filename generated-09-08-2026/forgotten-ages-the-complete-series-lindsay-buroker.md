# Forgotten Ages - The Complete Series

**Author:** Lindsay Buroker  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

Forgotten Ages – The Complete Series delivers exactly what its subtitle promises: a sweeping, pulse‑quickening saga that marries the grandeur of epic fantasy with the razor‑sharp intrigue of a code‑breaking thriller. Lindsay Buroker drops us into a world where the greatest military commander of his age and the most gifted cryptanalyst her people possess are forced into an uneasy partnership. Their history is steeped in blood and rivalry, yet the emergence of lethal, world‑shattering secrets from a forgotten epoch compels them to set aside old grudges—or watch civilization crumble.

At its heart, the series explores the tension between legacy and invention. The ancient secrets uncovered are not merely plot devices; they embody the weight of history pressing on the present, asking whether humanity can learn from its past mistakes or is doomed to repeat them. Buroker uses this backdrop to examine trust, sacrifice, and the blurred line between enemy and ally. The protagonists’ evolving relationship—from wary suspicion to begrudging respect, and eventually to something deeper—mirrors the larger theme: survival depends on cooperation, even when the odds seem insurmountable.

Stylistically, Buroker leans into her trademark brisk, accessible prose. Chapters sprint forward with crisp dialogue and punchy action scenes that never feel gratuitous; each battle, each deciphered riddle, serves to deepen character or raise the stakes. Her world‑building is efficient yet vivid: she sketches ancient ruins, cryptic symbols, and futuristic bastions with just enough detail to let the reader’s imagination fill the gaps, keeping the narrative momentum high. Romance is woven in subtly, never overwhelming the primary drive of mystery and adventure, but providing an emotional anchor that makes the stakes feel personal.

What truly shines is the chemistry between the two leads. Their banter crackles with wit and tension, reflecting Buroker’s knack for giving opposing personalities complementary strengths—strategic brilliance paired with analytical precision. As they navigate treacherous political machinations, lethal traps, and the slow unraveling of a conspiracy that spans millennia, the reader is constantly reminded that the greatest weapon they wield is not a sword or a cipher, but the willingness to listen to the other.

The collection’s bonus material—short story “Enigma” and author extras—adds welcome depth, offering glimpses into side characters and the lore that enriches the main novels without distracting from the central arc. For fans of epic fantasy who crave a fast‑paced, intellectually satisfying ride, Forgotten Ages hits all the right marks: high stakes, intricate puzzles, heart‑pounding action, and a romance that feels earned rather than tacked on.

In sum, Lindsay Buroker has crafted a series that feels both classic and fresh—a testament to her ability to blend genre conventions with innovative storytelling. Whether you’re drawn in by the promise of ancient mysteries, the thrill of a military‑codebreaker duo, or the slow‑burn romance that simmers beneath the surface, Forgotten Ages delivers a rewarding, addictive experience from first page to last.

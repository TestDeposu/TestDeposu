# A Drop of Corruption (Ana and Din Mysteries #2)

**Author:** Robert Jackson Bennett  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2025

A Drop of Corruption, the latest installment in Robert Jackson Bennett's Ana and Din Mysteries series, is a masterful blend of fantasy and classic detection that will keep you on the edge of your seat from start to finish. This national bestseller and Hugo Award finalist has already garnered widespread critical acclaim, and for good reason – Bennett's writing is wonderfully clever and compulsively readable.

At the heart of this gripping mystery is the enigmatic detective Ana Dolabra, who is tasked with solving a seemingly impossible case: the disappearance of a Treasury officer from a heavily guarded tower. As Ana delves deeper into the investigation, she discovers that the disappearance is, in fact, a murder – one that was carried out with such cunning and precision that it appears to be the work of a ghost.

As Ana navigates the complex web of clues and red herrings, she is aided by her trusty sidekick, Dinios Kol. Din has seen Ana solve impossible cases before, but even he begins to doubt her abilities as the death toll grows and their quarry consistently stays one step ahead. The killer's ability to predict Ana's every move with uncanny foresight is a chilling twist that raises the stakes and creates a sense of tension that is almost palpable.

One of the standout features of Bennett's writing is his ability to craft a sense of atmosphere and setting that is both vivid and immersive. The canton of Yarrowdale, with its heavily guarded towers and high-security compounds, is a richly detailed world that draws the reader in and refuses to let go. The magic system, which is based on the harvesting of fallen titans' blood, is equally well-realized, and adds a layer of depth and complexity to the story that is sure to appeal to fans of fantasy and mystery alike.

But what truly sets A Drop of Corruption apart is its exploration of the main theme: the nature of power and corruption. As Ana delves deeper into the investigation, she begins to uncover a web of deceit and corruption that goes all the way to the top of the Empire. The killer's motives, it turns out, are not just driven by a desire for power, but also by a desire to expose the corruption and hypocrisy that lies at the heart of the Empire.

Bennett's writing is, as always, superb. His prose is witty and engaging, and his characters are richly drawn and complex. Ana, in particular, is a fascinating creation – a brilliant and mercurial detective who is both fiercely independent and deeply vulnerable. Her relationship with Din is equally well-realized, and adds a layer of warmth and humor to the story that is sure to appeal to fans of character-driven fiction.

In short, A Drop of Corruption is a must-read for fans of fantasy, mystery, and detective fiction. With its wonderfully clever plot, richly detailed world-building, and complex characters, it is a book that will keep you on the edge of your seat from start to finish. So if you're looking for a compelling and immersive read, look no further – A Drop of Corruption is sure to satisfy.

# Legacy (The Sharing Knife, #2)

**Author:** Lois McMaster Bujold  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

In the lush, mystical realm conjured by Lois McMaster Bujold, the boundaries between reality and the unknown are skillfully blurred, as is the case in the second installment of her captivating fantasy romance series, The Sharing Knife. Legacy brings us deeper into the enigmatic world created by Bujold, where cultures collide and danger lurks around every corner. Our protagonists, Fawn Bluefield and Dag Redwing Hickory, are now inseparable, but the threads of their intricate relationship are being tested by the increasing tensions within their societies.

Bujold's masterful storytelling effortlessly navigates the intricacies of this complex world, drawing inspiration from various mythologies and folklores. Her meticulous craftsmanship weaves together a rich tapestry of characters, each with their unique voice and motivations, making it impossible not to become engrossed in their lives. The narrative flows smoothly, with an effortless blend of action, dialogue, and introspection. 

As with any romance, the central focus of Legacy lies in the relationship between Fawn and Dag, with Bujold presenting it as a delicate balancing act between their individual identities and the external pressures imposed by their respective worlds. Fawn's farm girl charm is juxtaposed with Dag's complex and often disturbing past, the latter of which continues to hold sway over his destiny and decisions. Their love becomes the axis around which the plot revolves, often leaving one to ponder the ultimate fate of their union amidst the turmoil that surrounds them. It's a testament to Bujold's skill as a writer that she manages to make even the most dire of situations seem hopeful, as the characters face their demons head-on and push the boundaries of what is acceptable.

The secondary characters in this book further enrich the narrative, particularly Dag's fellow Lakewalkers, whose mystical practices and societal norms add depth to the story. Bujold's portrayal of these characters raises questions about tradition, duty, and the role of individuals within their communities – and how these roles often clash with personal desires and needs. This adds a philosophical layer to an already engaging narrative, making it one that stays with you long after you finish reading the final page.

Bujold's writing style, while polished, never shies away from exploring the darkest corners of the human experience. Her exploration of the harsh realities that come with love, loss, and survival is both haunting and beautiful, showcasing her remarkable ability to strike a balance between the grim and the poignant. Her prose is vivid, evocative, and at times, even lyrical – capable of conjuring entire worlds and atmospheres with a mere phrase or description.

Legacy proves to be an exemplary example of Bujold's skill in crafting a compelling narrative that seamlessly blends fantasy and romance. With its thought-provoking themes, memorable characters, and rich world-building, it's undeniable that The Sharing Knife series has secured its place as one of the most captivating and memorable fantasy sagas of recent years.

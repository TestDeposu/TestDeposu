# A Throne From the Ashes (An Heir Comes to Rise, #3)

**Author:** C.C. Peñaranda  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

In the midst of a war-torn world, C.C. Peñaranda's "A Throne From the Ashes" masterfully weaves a tale of love, power, and the unrelenting pursuit of one's rightful place. The third installment in the "An Heir Comes to Rise" series is a gripping narrative that delves into the complexities of the human heart and the true cost of ambition.

At the center of this maelstrom is Tauria, a strong-willed and determined protagonist, driven by the desire to reclaim her stolen kingdom. Her journey is fraught with peril, as she navigates the treacherous landscape of rival courts and forges an alliance with the volatile Prince of Olmstone. This union, while potentially advantageous, threatens to unleash the wrath of a vengeful sovereign, forcing Tauria to confront the darker aspects of her own ambition.

Meanwhile, Nik is torn between the dictates of duty and the unyielding passion that burns within him. His impossible choice – to claim the bond that unites him with Tauria or to sever it forever – serves as a poignant reminder of the devastating consequences of war and the true meaning of sacrifice.

Throughout the novel, Peñaranda's masterful prose expertly captures the turmoil that besets these characters, plunging the reader into a world on the brink of chaos. As ancient secrets begin to surface, Tauria discovers a truth that has the potential to destroy anyone who dares to wear a crown. This revelation raises fundamental questions about the nature of power, loyalty, and the human condition.

One of the most striking aspects of Peñaranda's writing is her ability to craft complex, multidimensional characters that defy easy categorization. Tauria and Nik are flawed, relatable, and deeply human, their struggles and triumphs serving as a testament to the author's skill in crafting compelling narratives.

The world-building in "A Throne From the Ashes" is equally impressive, with a richly detailed landscape that immerses the reader in a world of medieval politics and ancient magic. Peñaranda's writing is evocative and visceral, conjuring vivid images of battle-scarred landscapes and opulent courts.

Ultimately, "A Throne From the Ashes" is a novel about the dangers of unchecked ambition and the transformative power of love. It is a story that will appeal to fans of epic fantasy and romance, with its lush world-building, complex characters, and heart-wrenching drama.

In short, "A Throne From the Ashes" is a masterpiece of storytelling, a sweeping epic that will leave readers breathless and eager for more. With its richly detailed world, complex characters, and poignant themes, this novel is sure to captivate fans of fantasy and romance alike.

# The 10th Kingdom

**Author:** Kathryn Wesley  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

**A Gripping Thriller with a Touch of Magic: Reviewing Dean Wesley Smith's The Tenth Kingdom**

As I delved into the pages of The Tenth Kingdom, I was struck by the masterful blend of genres that Dean Wesley Smith brings to the table. Written in collaboration with his wife Kristine Kathryn Rusch under the pen name Kathryn Wesley, this novel is a gripping thriller that seamlessly weaves fantasy and realism together, making it a compelling read for fans of both genres.

On the surface, The Tenth Kingdom appears to be a standard thriller, with Doc Hill, a professional poker player, tasked with finding a missing college student, the son of the Las Vegas Chief of Police. However, as the story unfolds, it becomes clear that this is no ordinary case. Smith's meticulous plotting and attention to detail create a narrative that is both engaging and unpredictable, with a complex web of secrets and lies waiting to be unraveled.

One of the standout features of The Tenth Kingdom is its author's ability to craft relatable characters. Doc Hill, the protagonist, is a well-developed and complex individual, whose motivations and emotional struggles drive the story forward. His interactions with other characters, from the Chief of Police to the enigmatic figures that inhabit the Las Vegas underworld, are nuanced and believable, adding depth to the narrative.

Smith's prose is economical yet evocative, with a keen eye for detail that immerses the reader in the world of Las Vegas. His descriptions of the city's neon-lit streets, the casinos, and the characters that inhabit them are vivid and realistic, transporting the reader to the sun-drenched landscape of the American West.

But what truly sets The Tenth Kingdom apart is its exploration of the interplay between reality and fantasy. Smith's use of magical realism is subtle yet effective, adding an extra layer of complexity to the narrative. As the story unfolds, it becomes clear that the boundaries between worlds are thinning, and the characters are caught in the midst of a maelstrom of supernatural forces.

Throughout the novel, Smith's mastery of pacing is evident, as the tension builds to a thrilling conclusion. The action is fast-paced and relentless, with a series of expertly crafted twists and turns that keep the reader guessing until the very end.

**Author's Style**

Dean Wesley Smith is a masterful storyteller, and The Tenth Kingdom is a testament to his skill as a writer. His ability to blend genres, create complex characters, and craft a gripping narrative is unmatched. As a seasoned author with a career spanning over three decades, Smith has honed his craft to perfection, producing a novel that is both entertaining and thought-provoking.

**Conclusion**

The Tenth Kingdom is a gripping thriller that will keep readers on the edge of their seats until the very end. With its blend of magical realism, complex characters, and expertly crafted plot, it is a must-read for fans of both genres. Dean Wesley Smith's masterful prose, economical yet evocative, immerses the reader in the world of Las Vegas, making it an unforgettable reading experience.

# An Empty Cup

**Author:** Sarah Price  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

An Empty Cup by Sarah Price presents itself as a no‑holds‑barred celebration of lactation‑centric erotica, bundling four short stories that linger on the physicality of overflowing breasts and the forbidden cravings they inspire. The collection’s title is a playful wink at the premise: when a woman’s cups run over, the narrative’s hunger is for a mouth eager to drain every last drop—often belonging to someone who, by social convention, ought not to be gazing, let alone sucking, at those very assets. From the outset, Price makes clear that this is an adults‑only, 18+ experience, and she leans into the taboo with a blend of blunt language and vivid, sensory detail that aims to leave little to the imagination.

The author's style is unapologetically direct. Sentences are short, punchy, and packed with verbs that evoke action—squeeze, lick, suck, massage, fondle—mirroring the kinetic energy of the scenes she depicts. This staccato rhythm drives the reader forward, creating a sense of immediacy that matches the urgent, almost primal desire at the heart of each tale. Price’s diction leans heavily on colloquial, almost slang‑filled phrasing (“huge, milky breasts,” “hotwife hucow,” “sexy cougar gets milked”) which serves two purposes: it grounds the fantasy in a recognizably contemporary, internet‑savvy vernacular, and it amplifies the feeling that the reader is being let in on a private, whispered confession. The prose rarely pauses for introspection; instead, it stays fixed on the physical act, the sensation of milk, the warmth of skin, and the audible gulps that accompany each exchange.

Each novella in the set explores a slightly different power dynamic while orbiting the same core fantasy. “Watch Me LEAK” introduces a voyeuristic thrill as the protagonist’s lactation becomes a public spectacle. “SHARING the Hotwife Hucow” pushes the scenario into polyamorous territory, emphasizing sharing and collective consumption. “Make Me DRIP - Sexy Cougar Gets Milked by the Boy Next Door” flips the age gap, pairing an older, confident woman with a younger, eager neighbor, thereby heightening the taboo of authority and experience. Finally, “NURSING HIM at the Nursing Home” shifts the setting to an institutional backdrop, mixing caretaking with erotic nursing in a way that challenges conventional boundaries of care and desire. Though the premises vary, the underlying engine remains the same: the erotic charge of milk as both nourishment and illicit indulgence.

From a critical standpoint, Price succeeds in delivering exactly what the cover promises: an intense, sensory‑driven experience that leaves little room for ambiguity. The book’s strength lies in its commitment to the fetish at hand; readers seeking a straightforward, unfiltered lactation fantasy will find the scenes vivid, the pacing relentless, and the language deliberately evocative. The anthology format also offers variety without demanding a huge time commitment, allowing readers to sample different flavors of the same central kink.

However, the laser focus on the physical act comes at the expense of deeper narrative layers. Character development is minimal; protagonists are often defined primarily by their breast size, lactation status, and willingness to engage in the act. Motivations beyond the immediate sexual gratification are rarely explored, and emotional arcs tend to be flat or nonexistent. This can make the stories feel repetitive after a few entries, as the same beats—teasing, exposure, milking, climax—are replayed with only superficial changes in setting or partner dynamics. Additionally, the reliance on explicit slang and exclamatory phrasing (“Can you HANDLE this hotness???”) may alienate readers who prefer a more nuanced or literary approach to erotica, veering the work closer to pulp fantasy than to a sophisticated exploration of desire.

In sum, An Empty Cup is a successful, if narrowly targeted, addition to the lactation‑erotica niche. Sarah Price’s style is bold, immediate, and tailor‑made for readers who want their fantasies served raw and unadorned. Those looking for psychological depth or intricate plotting may find the collection wanting, but for devotees of the milk‑fetish genre, the bundle delivers on its promise of steamy, breast‑centric indulgence.

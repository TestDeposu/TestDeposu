# The Moon of Gomrath (Tales of Alderley, #2)

**Author:** Alan Garner  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

The Moon of Gomrath, Alan Garner's sequel to the enchanting Weirdstone of Brisingamen, is a mesmerizing tale that whisks readers away to a realm of ancient magic, mythical creatures, and unrelenting adventure. Set on the eve of Gomrath, a night when the Old Magic stirs, this captivating narrative follows Colin and Susan as they unwittingly unleash the ferocity of the Wild Hunt, entangling themselves in a perilous struggle between the benevolent Wizard Cadellin and the malevolent Morrigan.

Garner's writing is a masterclass in atmospheric storytelling, weaving a rich tapestry of imagery that transports readers to the rugged landscapes of Alderley Edge. His prose is both poetic and accessible, conjuring an immersive world that is at once fantastical and grounded in the realities of rural England. The author's deep connection to the land and its mythology is palpable, imbuing the narrative with an authenticity that draws the reader in.

One of the most striking aspects of The Moon of Gomrath is its exploration of the human condition. Through the characters of Colin and Susan, Garner examines the complexities of courage, friendship, and the struggle between light and darkness. As the protagonists navigate the treacherous world of ancient magic, they must confront their own vulnerabilities and fears, emerging stronger and more resilient in the face of adversity.

The Moon of Gomrath is also notable for its nuanced portrayal of the natural world. Garner's depiction of the Wild Hunt, with its primal energy and unbridled ferocity, serves as a powerful metaphor for the untamed forces that shape our world. The author's reverence for the land and its creatures is evident throughout the narrative, reminding readers of the importance of respecting and preserving the natural balance.

Garner's style is characterized by a unique blend of mythology, folklore, and fantasy, which has captivated readers of all ages. His writing is both timeless and timely, speaking to fundamental human concerns while also exploring the complexities of the modern world. The Moon of Gomrath is a testament to the author's skill in crafting narratives that are both entertaining and thought-provoking.

In conclusion, The Moon of Gomrath is a captivating tale that will appeal to fans of fantasy, adventure, and mythology. Alan Garner's masterful storytelling, combined with his deep understanding of the human condition and the natural world, makes for a compelling read that will leave readers eager to explore more of the Weirdstone trilogy.

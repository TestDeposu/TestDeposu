# Street Magic (The Circle Opens, #2)

**Author:** Tamora Pierce  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

Street Magic, the captivating second installment of Tamora Pierce's acclaimed Circle Opens quartet, is a gripping journey that delves into the complexities of redemption and self-discovery. This enthralling tale explores the transformation of its protagonist, Briar Moss, from a street-smart plant mage to a determined hero who must confront his troubled past.

Within the city's labyrinthine streets, Briar encounters Evvy, a talented girl wielding remarkable magic to polish stones for a merchant. As they form an unlikely bond, Briar becomes resolute in finding her a reputable teacher, driven by a deep desire to help her escape the unforgiving streets. However, as the city's gangs begin to clash in a brutal conflict, Briar realizes that the most ruthless gang is seeking a stone mage to lead them to hidden treasures. Recognizing that Evvy's exceptional abilities have made her a prime target, Briar embarks on a perilous quest to safeguard her, ultimately deciding whether to abandon his street rat existence and assume his true responsibilities as a powerful mage.

Tamora Pierce weaves a rich tapestry of magical realism, seamlessly blending the fantastical world of magic with the gritty reality of life on the streets. Briar's character is skillfully crafted, embodying both the vulnerability of a lost soul and the determination of a hero driven by his conscience. Pierce's mastery of character development is evident in Briar's transformation from a rebellious youngster to a compassionate individual willing to confront the darker aspects of his past.

One of the most compelling aspects of Street Magic is its nuanced exploration of the consequences of redemption. As Briar struggles to reconcile his past and present selves, readers are invited to ponder the complexities of personal growth and the true meaning of home. The novel expertly captures the tension between the comfort of familiar surroundings and the thrill of new experiences, echoing the timeless conflict between nostalgia and the desire for change.

Another notable aspect of this captivating narrative is Pierce's masterful use of symbolism. The polished stones Evvy creates serve as a potent metaphor for the transformative power of art and the enduring value of human connection. Furthermore, the city itself functions as a character, its seedy underbelly reflecting the shadows within Briar's own psyche. This layered world-building imbues the novel with a depth and richness that captivates readers.

Throughout Street Magic, Pierce's writing style is characterized by a unique blend of wit, compassion, and unflinching honesty. Her ability to balance action, suspense, and character-driven drama makes this a truly unforgettable read. Tamora Pierce's signature warmth and empathy permeate every page, rendering the characters' triumphs and setbacks both relatable and heartwarming.

In conclusion, Street Magic is a spellbinding tale of self-discovery, redemption, and growth, elevated by Tamora Pierce's exceptional storytelling and world-building skills. This thought-provoking narrative serves as a testament to the author's enduring influence on the fantasy genre, reminding readers that true transformation requires courage, compassion, and a willingness to confront the shadows within.

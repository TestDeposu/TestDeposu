# Dead Reign (Marla Mason, #3)

**Author:** T.A. Pratt  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

"Dead Reign" by T.A. Pratt is a riveting third installment in the Marla Mason series, injecting new twists and dangers into the life of the East Coast's wittiest witch. This installment sees Pratt escalating the stakes, plunging Marla and her partner Rondeau into a dark world where the rules of magic have turned treacherous, and death lurks around every corner.

The setup is cleverly crafted, plunging readers into the midst of San Francisco's magical underworld, where nothing is as it seems. Marla's quest to find the Cornerstone becomes intertwined with a mysterious murder spree targeting the city's sorcerers, casting doubts and suspicions that threaten to upend everything. As the plot unfolds, Pratt masterfully weaves an intricate web of paranoia, shifting alliances, and hidden agendas.

One of the most striking aspects of "Dead Reign" is its atmospheric setting. Pratt's vivid descriptions evoke a sense of foreboding, conjuring a world where even the city's streets are twisted and surreal. His vivid portrait of San Francisco's magical underbelly, replete with poisonous frogs, cannibals, and hummingbirds that pack a deadly punch, is nothing short of masterful.

The real magic of "Dead Reign," however, lies in its protagonist, Marla Mason. Pratt's sharp wit, sardonic humor, and unflinching determination make her a compelling heroine whose sharp tongue can cut down even the deadliest foes. As she navigates a labyrinthine world of magical politics and treachery, Marla's resilience and cunning are repeatedly tested. This is, after all, a witch who has seen and survived it all.

Throughout the book, Pratt's prose is as nimble as Marla's spells. His dialogue is snappy, his action sequences lightning-fast, and his characterization expertly nuanced. With a keen sense of pacing, the author expertly balances plot twists, banter, and tension, keeping the reader constantly invested in Marla's fate.

In "Dead Reign," Pratt delivers a thought-provoking, unflinching, and unapologetic tale of urban fantasy that confronts the darker aspects of human nature. As Marla Mason battles to survive and preserve her domain, she embodies a resilience that echoes through the city's broken dreams and fractured magic. Pratt's world of Felport and its shadows is a realm where the boundaries between reality and fantasy blur, and where nothing is ever more than a spell's cast away.

"Dead Reign" by T.A. Pratt is an unapologetic, adrenaline-fueled ride that cements its status as a highlight among modern fantasy novels. Marla Mason's world, though grim and unforgiving, is as captivating as ever, making this book an essential read for fans of urban fantasy.

# Final Appeal

**Author:** Lisa Scottoline  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

"Final Appeal" by Lisa Scottoline is a gripping and intense legal thriller that masterfully weaves together elements of murder, intrigue, and romance. Scottoline's writing is as sharp as ever, plunging readers into the high-stakes world of Philadelphia's federal appeals court. At the center of the storm is Grace Rossi, a strong-willed and intelligent lawyer navigating the challenges of her new life after divorce.

As Grace becomes embroiled in a high-profile death penalty appeal, her personal and professional worlds collide in unexpected ways. Her whirlwind romance with the charismatic Judge Armen Gregorian takes a devastating turn when he's found dead, and the authorities quickly rule it a suicide. But Grace knows better – she's convinced that something sinister is at play. As she digs deeper, she uncovers a tangled web of corruption, bribery, and deceit that threatens to destroy everything she holds dear.

One of Scottoline's greatest strengths as an author is her ability to craft relatable, multidimensional characters. Grace Rossi is a shining example of this, with her sharp wit, quick intelligence, and deep-seated sense of justice. As she navigates the treacherous landscape of the appeals court, she's forced to confront her own vulnerabilities and biases, making her a more nuanced and believable protagonist.

The pacing of "Final Appeal" is relentless, with Scottoline expertly ratcheting up the tension as the stakes grow higher. Her prose is crisp and concise, with a keen eye for detail that immerses readers in the world of the story. The supporting cast is equally well-drawn, from the enigmatic undercover federal agent who becomes Grace's unlikely ally to the ruthless power brokers who'll stop at nothing to keep their secrets buried.

Throughout the book, Scottoline tackles some tough themes – the corrupting influence of power, the personal costs of ambition, and the difficult choices we face when our values are tested. But she does so with a deft touch, never sacrificing the momentum of the plot or the emotional resonance of her characters.

Ultimately, "Final Appeal" is a testament to Scottoline's skill as a storyteller and her deep understanding of the human condition. It's a gripping, thought-provoking thriller that will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very end. With its breakneck pacing, memorable characters, and razor-sharp plotting, this is a must-read for fans of the genre.

# The Heart of Stars (Rhiannon's Ride #3)

**Author:** Kate Forsyth  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2007

With The Heart of Stars, acclaimed Australian fantasy author Kate Forsyth brings her spellbinding *Rhiannon’s Ride* trilogy to a breathtaking, emotionally charged climax. Published in 2007, this final installment weaves together folklore, high-stakes political intrigue, and deeply human character arcs, solidifying Forsyth’s reputation as a master of lyrical, romantic fantasy. 

At the core of *The Heart of Stars* lies a timeless thematic exploration: the agonizing tension between personal desire and absolute duty. Forsyth masterfully dissects the complexities of forgiveness, pride, and sacrifice through her fierce, half-human protagonist, Rhiannon. When a malevolent necromancer abducts the royal twins to fuel a dark resurrection, chaos engulfs the realm. Fate demands the unthinkable of Rhiannon: she must rescue Princess Olwynne, the very woman she despises above all others. 

What elevates this narrative from a standard rescue mission to a profound psychological journey is the bitter rivalry between the two young women. Olwynne previously attempted to steal Rhiannon’s true love, the apprentice-witch Lewen, and the embers of jealousy still burn. Forsyth refuses to take the easy narrative route; she does not instantly dissolve their animosity. Instead, she forces her heroine to grapple with the bitter pill of grace, testing whether a wounded heart can conquer its own resentment for the greater good. The shadow of the necromancer’s dark magic looms heavily over the capital, raising the stakes until every choice feels perilous, threatening not just Rhiannon’s life and freedom, but her bond with her beloved winged mare and her future with Lewen.

Forsyth’s literary style is the true star of the trilogy. Her prose is richly textured, carrying a lyrical, folkloric cadence that immediately immerses the reader in a vibrant, tactile world. She writes with a rare emotional intelligence, ensuring that the grand, sweeping elements of epic fantasy—soaring winged mounts, terrifying necromancy, and royal abductions—are always anchored by intensely intimate emotional truths. The pacing is relentlessly gripping, balancing moments of heart-pounding tension with quiet, poignant revelations about the nature of love and redemption. 

*The Heart of Stars* is a triumph of YA and crossover fantasy. It delivers a deeply satisfying conclusion to a beloved trilogy while reminding readers why Kate Forsyth remains a defining voice in contemporary fantasy literature. It is a striking, beautiful testament to the power of a brave heart willing to forgive the unforgivable.

# The Star Scroll (Dragon Prince, #2)

**Author:** Melanie Rawn  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1989

In "The Star Scroll", the second installment of Melanie Rawn's epic fantasy series, Dragon Prince, the author masterfully weaves a tale of power, magic, and the unrelenting struggle for dominance. Published in 1989, this novel is a testament to Rawn's skill in crafting a narrative that is both engaging and immersive.

At the heart of the story lies the theme of legacy and the burden of inherited power. Rohan, the High Prince, and his Sunrunner wife Sioned, have sworn to maintain peace and preserve the secret of the dragons, a legacy they will one day pass on to their son Pol. However, the influence of the former High Prince Roelstra, who was defeated by Rohan, continues to cast a dark shadow over the land. As Pol grows into manhood, he must navigate the complexities of his dual heritage and confront the enemies of his family.

Rawn's writing style is characterized by her meticulous world-building and her ability to create complex, multi-dimensional characters. The world of the Dragon Prince series is richly detailed, with a deep history that underpins the narrative. The characters, too, are skillfully crafted, with motivations and desires that drive the plot forward.

One of the standout features of "The Star Scroll" is its exploration of the tension between light and darkness. The Sunrunners, with their magical abilities, represent the forces of light, while the enemies of the High Prince embody the darkness. This dichotomy is not simply a matter of good versus evil, however, as Rawn nuances her characters and their motivations, creating a moral landscape that is more gray than black and white.

The plot of "The Star Scroll" is engaging and suspenseful, with unexpected twists and turns that keep the reader guessing. Rawn's pacing is well-balanced, moving seamlessly between action, dialogue, and description. The novel's climax is both thrilling and satisfying, setting the stage for the next installment in the series.

Overall, "The Star Scroll" is a compelling read for fans of epic fantasy. Rawn's writing is engaging, her world-building is meticulous, and her characters are complex and nuanced. If you enjoy stories of magic, power, and the struggle for dominance, then this novel is definitely worth reading.

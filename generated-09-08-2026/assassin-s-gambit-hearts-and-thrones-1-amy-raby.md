# Assassin's Gambit (Hearts and Thrones, #1)

**Author:** Amy Raby  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2013

Assassin's Gambit, the debut novel by Amy Raby, is a captivating tale of politics, passion, and power that delves into the complexities of the human heart. This historical fantasy romance is set in a richly detailed world where the stakes are high, and the players are deadly.

At the center of the story is Vitala Salonius, a beautiful and cunning assassin who has been trained to infiltrate the palace and eliminate the emperor. Her mission is clear, but things become complicated when she meets Lucien, the emperor's son, who is as charming as he is intelligent. As Vitala navigates the treacherous world of court politics, she finds herself torn between her loyalty to the resistance and her growing feelings for Lucien.

One of the standout aspects of Assassin's Gambit is its well-developed characters. Vitala is a complex and multifaceted protagonist, full of contradictions and flaws. She is both strong and vulnerable, capable of great cruelty and great kindness. Lucien, too, is a well-crafted character, with a quick mind and a sharp wit that makes him a compelling match for Vitala.

The romance between Vitala and Lucien is a slow-burning one, filled with tension and anticipation. Raby takes her time developing their relationship, allowing the reader to see the gradual build-up of trust and intimacy between the two characters. The chemistry between them is palpable, and their interactions are charged with a sense of excitement and danger.

Raby's writing style is engaging and immersive, with a keen sense of pacing and plot. She weaves together multiple storylines with ease, creating a rich tapestry of characters and motivations. The world-building is also impressive, with a detailed and vividly realized setting that draws the reader in.

One of the main themes of Assassin's Gambit is the power of choice and the complexity of human morality. Vitala is a character who exists in a gray area, neither purely good nor purely evil. She is driven by a desire for power and revenge, but she is also capable of great compassion and empathy. As the story unfolds, she is forced to confront the consequences of her actions and make difficult choices about where her loyalties lie.

Overall, Assassin's Gambit is a compelling and engaging read, filled with complex characters, intricate plotting, and a richly detailed world. Raby's writing is engaging and immersive, making this a must-read for fans of historical fantasy romance. With its slow-burning romance, complex characters, and intricate plotting, Assassin's Gambit is a story that will keep you on the edge of your seat until the very end.

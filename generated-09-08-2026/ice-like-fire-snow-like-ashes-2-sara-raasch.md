# Ice Like Fire (Snow Like Ashes, #2)

**Author:** Sara Raasch  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2015

In the captivating sequel to Sara Raasch's Snow Like Ashes, Meira and her people are on the cusp of a new era of freedom, yet they face an unforeseen threat - Primoria's lost chasm of magic, which has the potential to either be the key to their survival or their downfall. Ice Like Fire is an immersive fantasy that delves into the complexities of power struggles, loyalty, and the true cost of saving those we love.

With the memory of Angra's reign still fresh in their minds, the Winterians are forced to navigate a treacherous landscape where their very existence is threatened by the encroaching Cordell. Meira's desire to protect her people leads her down a path of confrontation with Theron, while her brother Mather's yearning for freedom sets him on a perilous journey to rebuild their shattered kingdom.

Raasch masterfully weaves together multiple narratives, allowing the reader to delve deep into each character's psyche as they grapple with their own personal demons. Theron's desperation to harness the power of magic resonates deeply with Meira's own struggles to find a balance between duty and self-preservation. Meanwhile, Mather's quest for freedom serves as a poignant reminder of the consequences of oppression and the importance of standing up for what one believes in.

One of the most compelling aspects of Ice Like Fire is Raasch's ability to balance action and introspection. The pacing is well-crafted, hurtling the reader through a rollercoaster of plot twists and turns that keep them on the edge of their seats. As Meira navigates the treacherous world of politics and ancient powers, she must also confront the darkness within herself and the true nature of her relationships.

Raasch's writing style is characterized by its lyrical prose and vivid world-building, which transports the reader to a richly detailed and immersive world. Her characters are multidimensional and relatable, with flaws and strengths that make them feel like real people rather than mere plot devices. The romance is understated yet poignant, adding a welcome layer of depth to the narrative.

In many ways, Ice Like Fire is a commentary on the corrupting influence of power and the dangers of relying too heavily on it. Raasch subtly explores the gray areas between good and evil, challenging the reader to consider the true nature of morality in a world where the lines are constantly blurred. This is a fantasy that will appeal to fans of George R.R. Martin's Game of Thrones and Leigh Bardugo's Grisha Trilogy, with its intricate world-building, nuanced characters, and epic scope.

Ultimately, Ice Like Fire is a testament to Raasch's skill as a storyteller and her ability to craft a compelling narrative that's both engaging and emotionally resonant. As Meira, Theron, and Mather navigate the treacherous landscape of Primoria, they must confront their own fears and doubts in order to emerge victorious. This is a book that will leave you breathless and eager for more, as the stakes are raised and the world of Snow Like Ashes hurtles towards its explosive climax.

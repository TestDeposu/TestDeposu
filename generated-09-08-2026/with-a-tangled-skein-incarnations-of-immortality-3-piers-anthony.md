# With a Tangled Skein (Incarnations of Immortality, #3)

**Author:** Piers Anthony  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 1986

In "With a Tangled Skein", the third installment of Piers Anthony's Incarnations of Immortality series, the master of fantasy weaves a complex and captivating narrative that delves into the intricate struggle between good and evil. This thought-provoking novel is a testament to Anthony's ingenious storytelling and his ability to craft a tale that is both entertaining and intellectually stimulating.

At the heart of the story is Niobe, a strong-willed and determined protagonist who finds herself entangled in a web of fate when her loved one is shot. As she navigates the complexities of the Tapestry of Fate, Niobe discovers that Satan's malevolent plans are intricately woven into the fabric of destiny. With her granddaughter Luna's future hanging in the balance, Niobe must accept a challenge from the Prince of Deceit and embark on a perilous journey through the depths of Hell.

One of the most striking aspects of "With a Tangled Skein" is Anthony's unique approach to the concept of fate and the supernatural. By personifying the Aspects of Fate, Anthony creates a rich and immersive world that is both fascinating and unsettling. The author's vivid descriptions of the Tapestry of Fate and the various realms of Hell are a testament to his boundless imagination and his ability to craft a compelling narrative.

Throughout the novel, Anthony explores the theme of free will versus determinism, raising questions about the nature of fate and the role of individual agency in shaping one's destiny. Niobe's journey is a powerful exploration of the human condition, as she grapples with the consequences of her choices and the limitations of her power.

Anthony's writing style is, as always, engaging and accessible. His use of language is masterful, and his ability to balance action, suspense, and philosophical introspection is a hallmark of his craft. The characters in "With a Tangled Skein" are well-developed and complex, with Niobe being a particularly compelling protagonist.

If you're a fan of fantasy, philosophy, or just great storytelling, "With a Tangled Skein" is a must-read. Piers Anthony's unique blend of imagination, intellect, and creativity makes this novel a standout in the genre. So, embark on Niobe's journey and discover the intricacies of the Tapestry of Fate for yourself.

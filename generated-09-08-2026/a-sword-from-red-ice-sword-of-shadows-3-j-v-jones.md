# A Sword from Red Ice (Sword of Shadows, #3)

**Author:** J.V. Jones  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2019

In "A Sword from Red Ice," the thrilling third installment of the Sword of Shadows series by the acclaimed J.V. Jones, the stakes are higher, the battles are bloodier, and the fate of the kingdom hangs precariously in the balance. This installment whisks readers to a realm of unrelenting turmoil, as treachery, power struggles, and ancient secrets simmer just beneath the surface.

On the precipice of civil war, Gerald - a battle-hardened warrior with a haunted past - finds himself once again at the forefront of the struggle. His path crosses with the enigmatic Dame Beverly Fitzwilliam, an enigmatic warrior with a talent for swordplay and a penchant for secrecy. United by their mutual quest for protection and survival, they charge forward into a maelstrom of battles, facing fearsome foes who will stop at nothing to claim the throne.

Through Jones' masterful hand, the world of the Sword of Shadows series expands exponentially, with meticulously crafted backdrops and an intricate web of character motivations that will keep you guessing until the very end. The battle-scarred landscapes, the treacherous landscapes of the human heart, and the unforgiving landscape of war itself serve as the backdrop for Jones' expertly rendered tale of loyalty, duty, and the unyielding will to defend one's homeland.

As a writer, Jones is no stranger to the intricacies of crafting compelling characters. Dame Beverly, with her subtle yet palpable presence, steals the show as Gerald's loyal companion. Her backstory, slowly revealed through a series of subtle hints and cleverly seeded clues, adds depth to the narrative and illuminates the complex moral landscape of the story.

The writing itself is a testament to Jones' skill as a storyteller, weaving an intricate tapestry of narrative threads that seamlessly integrate complex ideas, action-packed battles, and poignant character moments. Her descriptive prose conjures vivid images of the treacherous landscapes, the sweat-drenched armor-clad warriors, and the unyielding clash of steel on steel - all of which blend together to create an immersive and unshakeable narrative experience.

Ultimately, "A Sword from Red Ice" is a testament to Jones' skill as a master storyteller. It is an action-packed, emotionally charged, and utterly gripping tale that will captivate fans of epic fantasy and medieval-inspired tales alike. With its well-developed characters, masterful pacing, and unrelenting action, this is a book that will not let you go until the very end. So, buckle up and embark on this thrilling adventure - but be warned: the landscape ahead is fraught with danger, and not everyone will survive.

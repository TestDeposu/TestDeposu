# The Measure of the Magic (Legends of Shannara, #2)

**Author:** Terry Brooks  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2011

Terry Brooks's "The Measure of the Magic" is a masterful addition to the Legends of Shannara series, weaving a rich tapestry of fantasy, adventure, and self-discovery. Published in 1997 for the hardback, this novel has been re-released in many countries but a new version emerged in 2011 for the paperback release that I am reviewing here. This latest iteration, while having a slightly updated version of the cover art, does not alter the essence of the story that has captivated fans of high fantasy worldwide.

As the story unfolds, Brooks skillfully plunges the reader into the world of Shannara, where the remnants of a war-torn past and the fragile balance of power in the present coalesce into a complex narrative. The focus is on Panterra Qu, a young Tracker who finds himself thrust into a world of magical politics and ancient traditions. With the guidance of the last Black Staff, Panterra must navigate the treacherous landscape of his people's history, facing not only external threats such as the looming army of Trolls but also internal strife like the alleged murder of the Elf king of Arborlon and the sinister forces working to control the kingdom.

What sets Brooks apart as an author is his ability to balance the fantastical elements of the world with the relatable emotional struggles of his characters. Panterra's journey, riddled with self-doubt and the weight of responsibility, serves as a poignant reminder of the human condition, even within the most magical of settings. It's a testament to Brooks's storytelling prowess that the narrative effortlessly oscillates between high-stakes action sequences and poignant moments of introspection, never losing sight of its core message.

In "The Measure of the Magic," Brooks also expertly explores the themes of duty, power, and the blurred lines between good and evil. The Elf kingdom's struggles, the enigmatic nature of the Black Staff, and the relentless force of darkness driving the novel's antagonist all serve as a backdrop to delve into the complexities of morality in the face of adversity. Panterra's growth from an uncertain Tracker to a confident Knight of the Word embodies the very essence of the series: that even the most mundane individuals can transform into heroes, forging their own destinies in the process.

Through his writing, Brooks masterfully evokes the grand scope of Shannara's world, from the ancient forests and mist-shrouded mountains to the battle-hardened warriors and enigmatic spellcasters. It's a richly detailed environment that invites readers to step into its mystical realm and explore its secrets. Whether battling ferocious Trolls or unraveling the intricate threads of a centuries-old conspiracy, Panterra and his companions move through the world with a sense of purpose, their actions propelled by the urgent need to protect their people and preserve their way of life.

One of the greatest strengths of "The Measure of the Magic" lies in its ability to seamlessly integrate complex world-building with engaging character development. Brooks's prose is characteristically crisp and evocative, transporting readers to a world both fantastical and all-too-real. His commitment to telling stories within the Shannara universe has been a hallmark of his writing, and with this novel, he continues to deliver a compelling narrative that fans of fantasy will find impossible to put down.

While this re-release in 2011 may have been a re-edition for some of its readers, it marks the first time that readers like me are getting the chance to experience the author's unique storytelling within the book's pages. This new release has provided the opportunity for a new wave of readers and has also reinvigorated interest in the series. This addition to the Legends of Shannara series stands as a testament to Terry Brooks's enduring talent for conjuring immersive worlds and memorable characters.

Overall, "The Measure of the Magic" is a gripping tale of magic and adventure, full of unexpected twists and turns that will keep readers on the edge of their seats. It seamlessly interweaves the darker aspects of the Shannara universe with the resilience of its characters, resulting in a truly unforgettable reading experience.

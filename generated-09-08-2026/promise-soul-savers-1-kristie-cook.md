# Promise (Soul Savers, #1)

**Author:** Kristie Cook  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2021

Promise, the first three books in the Soul Savers series by Kristie Cook, is an addictive and award-winning collection that delves into the world of supernatural creatures, secret societies, and the eternal struggle between good and evil. The series follows the journey of Alexis Ames, a young woman who discovers she is part of a long line of powerful beings known as the Amadis, sworn to protect humanity from the forces of darkness.

Cook's writing style is engaging and immersive, drawing the reader into a world of magic, mystery, and romance. The author masterfully weaves together multiple storylines, creating a complex and intricate narrative that keeps the reader on the edge of their seat. The characters are well-developed and multi-dimensional, with Alexis being a particularly strong protagonist. Her struggles to come to terms with her newfound identity and powers are relatable and authentic, making her a compelling character to follow.

One of the main themes of the series is the struggle for identity and self-discovery. Alexis's journey is a metaphor for the search for one's true purpose and the challenges that come with it. As she navigates the world of the Amadis and the forces of darkness, she must confront her own demons and learn to harness her powers. This theme is echoed in the other characters, who are all struggling with their own identities and purposes.

The series also explores the idea of devotion and loyalty. Alexis's relationships with her loved ones are put to the test as she is forced to confront the harsh realities of her new world. Her devotion to her family, her people, and her beliefs is constantly challenged, and she must make difficult choices that will have far-reaching consequences.

The world-building in the series is impressive, with a richly detailed and complex mythology that underpins the narrative. Cook draws on a range of mythological and supernatural sources, creating a unique and compelling universe that is both familiar and yet utterly original.

Overall, Promise is a gripping and addictive series that will appeal to fans of supernatural fiction and romance. Cook's writing is engaging and immersive, and the characters are well-developed and relatable. The themes of identity, self-discovery, and devotion are timely and thought-provoking, making this series a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and engaging story.

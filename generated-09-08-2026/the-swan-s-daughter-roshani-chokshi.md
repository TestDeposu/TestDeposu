# The Swan's Daughter

**Author:** Roshani Chokshi  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2026

Title: The Swan’s Daughter
Author: Roshani Chokshi
Publication Year: 2026

In her eagerly anticipated 2026 release, The Swan’s Daughter, New York Times bestselling author Roshani Chokshi once again proves herself as a master artisan of modern myth-making. Earning the coveted title of a Good Morning America Book Club Pick, this lush and enchanting novel invites readers into a breathtaking realm where romance is laced with mortal peril, and fairy-tale tropes are brilliantly subverted. Chokshi weaves a mesmerizing tale that is as dangerous as it is dazzling, exploring the heavy price of survival and the intoxicating, terrifying nature of truth.

At the heart of the narrative is Prince Arris, a young royal trapped in a macabre matrimonial countdown. Thanks to a catastrophically flawed wish granted by a sea witch, the rule of the Isle of Malys comes with a literal and fatal catch: the heir must be married, and then promptly murdered for their heart to cement the next ruler’s crown. Arris’s royal lineage is destined to become a literal forest of sentient trees, a fate he hopes to avoid by finding genuine, lasting love. To achieve this, Rathe Castle hosts a high-stakes tournament of brides. Chokshi constructs this backdrop with her signature sensory opulence. Readers are transported into a surreal playground of ozorald caves, menageries populated by daydream trees, pearl crocodiles, and tea parties hosted on glass boats. Yet, beneath the glittering exterior lies a tense atmosphere of paranoia, where every kiss and stolen glance might just be a precursor to poison.

Enter Demelza, a veritas swan whose very song forces the extraction of absolute truth. Displaced, hunted, and forced into hiding, Demelza represents the marginalized voice desperately fighting to be heard. When she and Arris strike a bargain—sanctuary in exchange for her truth-seeking melodies to expose the deadly bachelorettes—the novel shifts into a captivating game of psychological cat-and-mouse. 

Chokshi’s prose remains the crown jewel of her work. Her style is richly upholstered, dripping with poetic imagery that engages every sense. She treats language like spun glass, delicate yet razor-sharp. In The Swan’s Daughter, she balances the whimsical wonder of classic folklore with a darker, more subversive edge. The world-building is immersive, painting a vivid portrait of an island where beauty and brutality are inextricably linked. 

Thematic depth anchors the sparkling prose. At its core, the novel examines the burden of authenticity in a world built on deception. For Arris, love is a literal death sentence, making vulnerability a luxury he cannot afford. For Demelza, truth is both her defining gift and her greatest curse, a weapon that strips away comforting lies in favor of harsh realities. Through their growing partnership, Chokshi explores the central theme that true bravery is not merely about outsmarting death, but daring to want a life worth living. 

The Swan’s Daughter is a triumphant addition to Chokshi’s bibliography. It dazzles with its atmospheric charm, keeps the pages turning with its high-stakes romantic mystery, and lingers long after the final chapter with its poignant meditations on love, voice, and autonomy. Fans of romantic fantasy and elevated fairy tales will find themselves utterly enchanted.

# Legacy of the Drow (Legacy of the Drow, #1-4; Legend of Drizzt, #7-10)

**Author:** R.A. Salvatore  
**Page Count:** Unknown  
**Publication Date:** 2009

Legacy of the Drow, a quartet of novels by R.A. Salvatore, is a gripping continuation of the beloved Legend of Drizzt series. This book is a masterful blend of action, adventure, and fantasy, with a narrative that explores the complexities of the drow world and the intricate web of relationships that bind its characters.

At its core, Legacy of the Drow is a story about identity, community, and the struggle for power. Drizzt Do'Urden, the iconic dark elf renegade, has finally found a measure of peace among the dwarves in Mithral Hall. However, this tranquility is short-lived, as Lolth, the Queen of the Demonweb Pits, sets her sights on reclaiming the one soul that has eluded her: Drizzt's.

Salvatore's writing style is as engaging as ever, with a keen focus on character development and world-building. The author's vivid descriptions of the Underdark and its inhabitants transport readers to a richly detailed world of dark elves, dwarves, and other fantastical creatures. The pacing is well-balanced, with moments of introspection and quiet contemplation offset by intense action sequences and heart-pumping battles.

One of the strengths of Legacy of the Drow is its exploration of the drow culture and society. Salvatore delves deep into the complexities of drow politics, revealing a world of intrigue, deception, and ruthless ambition. The character of Lolth, in particular, is a fascinating study in contrasts: a goddess of darkness and chaos, driven by a desire for power and control.

Throughout the book, Salvatore's characters are multidimensional and relatable, with each one bringing their unique perspective to the narrative. Drizzt, as always, is a compelling protagonist, driven by a strong sense of morality and justice. The supporting cast, including Bruenor, Regis, and Wulfgar, add depth and richness to the story, their relationships with Drizzt and each other expertly woven into the fabric of the narrative.

If there is a weakness to Legacy of the Drow, it is that the book assumes a certain level of familiarity with the Legend of Drizzt series. While Salvatore provides some background information, new readers may find themselves struggling to keep up with the complex web of characters and events.

Overall, Legacy of the Drow is a gripping and engaging read, perfect for fans of fantasy and adventure. Salvatore's masterful storytelling and vivid world-building make this book a must-read for anyone looking to escape into a richly detailed world of wonder and magic.

Of War and Ruin by Ryan Cahill is not the correct author for this book. The correct author is Brad Taylor and the series is Taskforce not The Bound and the Broken. 

Here is the review:

In "The Dig" a Taskforce short story,  Brad Taylor masterfully weaves an intricate narrative that seamlessly blends action, suspense, and intrigue. At the heart of this exhilarating tale is Pike Logan, a former Taskforce operative, who is determined to rejoin the elite counterterrorist organization. However, his personal mission is soon entangled with an archaeological dig in Roswell, New Mexico, where he and his partner, Jennifer Cahill, uncover a conspiracy that threatens to destroy their future.

Through Taylor's masterful storytelling, the reader is transported to the scorching desert landscapes of New Mexico, where the lines between reality and myth blur. The author's vivid descriptions of the archaeological site and the subsequent excavation are meticulous and detailed, showcasing his extensive research and knowledge of the subject matter.

As the story unfolds, Taylor expertly ratchets up the tension, propelling the reader into a world of high-stakes adventure and espionage. The pacing is relentless, with each twist and turn expertly crafted to keep the reader on the edge of their seat. The action scenes are intense and well-choreographed, showcasing Taylor's ability to craft compelling and realistic combat sequences.

One of the standout aspects of "The Dig" is the character development of Jennifer Cahill. Taylor skillfully portrays her as a strong and capable individual, who is determined to prove herself in a male-dominated world. Her partnership with Pike Logan is authentic and engaging, and their banter adds a welcome layer of humor to the narrative.

The themes of loyalty, trust, and redemption are expertly woven throughout the story, adding depth and complexity to the narrative. Taylor's exploration of the human condition is nuanced and thought-provoking, raising questions about the morality of war and the true cost of sacrifice.

In conclusion, "The Dig" is a gripping and unputdownable read that will appeal to fans of action, suspense, and espionage. Brad Taylor's masterful storytelling, coupled with his meticulous research and attention to detail, make this a must-read for anyone looking for a compelling and realistic thriller.
